## Translation Philosophy

**Primary:** New American Standard Bible (NASB) — most literal word-for-word English translation

**For verification:** Strong's Concordance — every key term should be checked in Hebrew/Greek original

**Key translation traps to address on screen:**
- Jesus vs Joshua — both are Yeshua (יֵשׁוּעַ) in Hebrew
- "Was" vs "became" in Genesis 1:2 (hayah — הָיְתָ֥ה)
- "Formless and void" — tohu va bohu — carries judicial desolation meaning
- "Sons of God" — Bene Elohim — always angelic in other OT appearances
- "Blameless" (tamim) in Noah's description — same word as unblemished sacrifice
- "Utterly destroy" (herem) — consecrated destruction, not hate-driven genocide
- Lucifer — Latin translation of Hebrew Heylel (הֵילֵל) — "shining one"

## Visual Tone Notes for AI Generation

- **Gap War / Pre-Adamic Earth:** Cosmic scale, massive celestial battle, ruined landscape, darkness and void
- **Garden of Eden:** Lush, otherworldly, light-filled but with an undertone of something watchful
- **Gen1 Humans:** Magnificent, terrifying beauty — not soft or humanly pretty — closer to the cherub descriptions of Ezekiel 1
- **The Serpent:** Intelligent-looking, upright perhaps, serpentine but carrying presence — not a cute cartoon snake
- **Cain in Nod:** Desolate landscape, strange inhabitants, ancient city construction, a marked man among beings he fears
- **The Watchers descending:** Celestial beings arriving on a mountain (Hermon), fire and light, awe not romance
- **The Flood:** Catastrophic, not peaceful — the violence of a world ending
- **Temple Glory Departing:** Slow, mournful, step by step — golden light moving through stone corridors, pausing, lifting
- **Temple Mount today:** Modern conflict zone above ancient prophecy — satellite view transitioning to ground level
- **Dead Sea:** Receding shoreline, salt crusts, desolation — then cut to Ezekiel's vision of it coming alive
