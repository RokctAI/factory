# Palestinians, Canaanites, and the Land That Is Still Cursed

## Core Argument
The Palestinian people are not primarily descended from Ishmael or from any Abrahamic line. The populations that have continuously inhabited the land of Canaan — across Philistine, Canaanite, and later Arab-Muslim identity — trace ancestrally to the Canaanites who were never removed when God commanded their removal. The conflict over that land is not political at its root. It is the continuation of a 3000-year-old incomplete obedience — and the land cannot produce its promise while the curse of Canaan is still embedded in it.

---

## Clarifying the Family Tree

The confusion usually collapses the Middle East into one Abrahamic family. The distinctions matter:

| People | Primary Ancestry | Connected to Abraham via |
|---|---|---|
| Jewish people (modern) | Judah + Benjamin | Isaac → Jacob → Judah |
| Arabs broadly | Ishmael's descendants | Abraham → Ishmael |
| Palestinians (Gaza/West Bank) | Canaanite/Philistine stock | Pre-Abrahamic Canaan |
| Jordanians/Syrians | Mixed — Edomite, Moabite, Canaanite | Various |

The Palestinian people are largely descended from the ancient inhabitants of Canaan — the Philistines, Jebusites, Amorites, and other Canaanite groups who remained in the land after Israel's conquest and were never fully removed. Over centuries they absorbed Arab Muslim identity through conquest and conversion but their ancestral bloodline is Canaanite, not Ishmaelite.

---

## The Philistines — The Most Direct Line

The Philistines occupied Gaza and the coastal plain — precisely the territory of modern Gaza. They were not Semitic by origin. Scholars trace them to the Aegean/Mediterranean (often called "Sea Peoples").

**Genesis 10:13-14** (NASB) — The Philistines descended through **Mizraim**, Ham's son:
> "Mizraim became the father of Ludim and Anamim and Lehabim and Naphtuhim and Pathrusim and Casluhim (from which came the Philistines) and Caphtorim."

Ham → Mizraim → Casluhim → **Philistines**

The same Hamitic bloodline that produced Canaan also produced the Philistines through a parallel branch. Both are under Ham's curse. Both occupied the promised land. Both were supposed to be removed.

**Goliath was a Philistine** — and he was a giant (1 Samuel 17). The Nephilim connection runs through the Philistines as it does through the Canaanites.

---

## Islam as Religious Identity — Not Bloodline

**This is the critical distinction:**

Islam is a religion founded in the 7th century AD by Muhammad, who was from the Quraysh tribe of Arabia — connected to Ishmael's descendants in the Arabian peninsula. Islam spread through conquest, trade, and conversion across North Africa, the Middle East, and beyond.

The Palestinians of Gaza and the West Bank did not become Canaanites by becoming Muslim. They became Muslim through the Arab conquests of the 7th century. Their religion changed. Their bloodline did not.

Similarly, Jews of the Diaspora who converted to Judaism (like the Edomites under John Hyrcanus) changed their religion. Their bloodline did not change.

**Religion and bloodline are two separate data streams** — the Bible tracks both separately and so should we.

## The Curse That Never Lifted

**Numbers 14:34** (NASB) — God's sentence on Israel for refusing to take the land:
> "According to the number of days which you spied out the land, forty years—a year for each day—you will bear your guilt forty years and know My opposition."

They eventually entered. But they did not finish the removal.

**Judges 2:20-23** (NASB) — God's response to their failure:
> "The anger of the LORD burned against Israel, and He said, 'Because this nation has transgressed My covenant which I commanded their fathers and has not listened to My voice, I also will no longer drive out before them any of the nations which Joshua left when he died, in order to test Israel by them, whether they will keep the way of the LORD to walk in it as their fathers did, or not.'"

The Canaanite populations were left as a test. And Israel failed the test — repeatedly, generationally, for centuries.

The curse on Canaan (Genesis 9:25) was never officially lifted. No ritual, no event, no prayer in scripture marks its removal. It was supposed to be resolved through Israel's obedience and God's presence settling in the land. Neither happened fully.

## The Land Vomiting Its Inhabitants

**Leviticus 18:24-28** (NASB):
> "Do not defile yourselves by any of these things; for by all these the nations which I am casting out before you have become defiled. For the land has become defiled, therefore I have brought its punishment upon it, so the land has spewed out its inhabitants. But as for you, you are to keep My statutes and My judgments and shall not do any of these abominations, neither the native, nor the alien who sojourns among you; for the men of the land who have been before you have done all these abominations, and the land has become defiled; so that the land will not spew you out, should you defile it, as it has spewed out the nation which has been before you."

The land has a spiritual metabolism. It responds to what happens on it. The Canaanites defiled it. It vomited them out. God warned Israel it would do the same to them — and it did. Twice. Assyria and Babylon.

The land is still in this condition. The defilement was never fully addressed. The promise — milk and honey, flourishing, rest — was never activated because the curse was never removed.

---

## Who Has the Land Claim?

This is not a question that can be answered purely politically. Biblically:

- **God owns the land** — Leviticus 25:23: "The land shall not be sold permanently, for the land is Mine; for you are but aliens and sojourners with Me."
- The land was given to **all twelve tribes** — only two have a traceable modern identity
- The Canaanites were given a removal command — never completed
- The land has been fought over by Assyrians, Babylonians, Persians, Greeks, Romans, Byzantines, Arab Caliphates, Crusaders, Ottomans, British, and now modern Israel and Palestinians

The legal title in human terms has changed hands over twenty times. The biblical title was conditional on obedience that was never sustained.

**The honest answer:** The land is still under a disputed spiritual condition that human politics cannot resolve. The biblical resolution requires:
1. The return and recognition of all twelve tribes
2. The removal of the curse
3. God's presence returning to the land
4. The land healing and producing its promise

None of those conditions are currently met.

## Translation Notes
- **פְּלִשְׁתִּים (Pelishtim)** — Philistines — possibly meaning "migrants" or "invaders," ironically the same charge modern Palestinians and Israelis level at each other
- **כְּנַעַן (Kena'an)** — Canaan — connected to the verb meaning "to be subdued/humbled" — the curse language is embedded in the name itself
- The modern Arabic name for the region **فِلَسْطِين (Filastin)** derives directly from the Latin *Palaestina* which derives from the Greek *Philistia* — named after the Philistines by the Romans after 135 AD to erase Jewish connection to the land. The name itself carries 3000 years of history.

## Episode Angle
The news covers this conflict as if it began in 1948 or 1967. The Bible locates the origin in the tent of Noah, runs through the failed conquest under Joshua, and has been building ever since. The Canaanites didn't disappear — they became the Philistines, they became the populations Israel never removed, they became Muslim when the Arab conquests swept through. The religion changed. The bloodline didn't. And the land — promised to be flowing with milk and honey once the curse was removed — is still producing conflict, not abundance, because that removal never happened. This is not a political episode. This is a theological one. God promised a specific outcome for a specific land under specific conditions. None of those conditions have been fully met. The land is still waiting.
