# The Third Temple — The Occupied Site and Lucifer's Final Move

## Core Argument
The Third Temple must be built according to prophecy — but its current site is occupied by one of Islam's holiest structures. This is not accidental. The same pattern that placed the serpent in Eden, that put Canaanites in the promised land, that brought pagan worship into Solomon's temple — is operating here. The temple cannot be built in the current political environment without a figure who appears to solve the unsolvable. And that figure is not who Jerusalem will think he is.

---

## Why a Third Temple Is Prophesied

**Daniel 9:27** (NASB):
> "And he will make a firm covenant with the many for one week, but in the middle of the week he will put a stop to sacrifice and grain offering; and on the wing of abominations will come one who makes desolate, even until a complete destruction, one that is decreed, is poured out on the one who makes desolate."

For sacrifice and grain offering to stop — they must first restart. A functional temple with a functioning priesthood must exist. Sacrifice has not occurred in Jerusalem since 70 AD when the second temple was destroyed by Rome.

**2 Thessalonians 2:3-4** (NASB):
> "Let no one in any way deceive you, for it will not come unless the apostasy comes first, and the man of lawlessness is revealed, the son of destruction, who opposes and exalts himself above every so-called god or object of worship, so that he takes his seat in the temple of God, displaying himself as being God."

**"The temple of God"** — a physical building in which this figure sits. It must exist before this event can occur.

**Matthew 24:15** (NASB) — Jesus quotes Daniel directly:
> "Therefore when you see the ABOMINATION OF DESOLATION which was spoken of through Daniel the prophet, standing in the holy place (let the reader understand)..."

Jesus treats this as a future event even from His own time, pointing to a specific physical location — the holy place.

## What Currently Occupies the Site

**The Dome of the Rock** — built 688-691 AD by Umayyad Caliph Abd al-Malik. Sits on the Temple Mount (Haram esh-Sharif). The third holiest site in Islam, believed to be where Muhammad ascended to heaven on the Night Journey (Isra and Mi'raj).

**The Al-Aqsa Mosque** — built around the same period on the southern end of the same platform.

**The Temple Mount** — controlled by the Jordanian Islamic Waqf since 1967 despite Israeli sovereignty over Jerusalem. Non-Muslims may visit but not pray. The political arrangement is one of the most delicate in modern geopolitics.

**Demolishing or displacing these structures** would almost certainly trigger immediate conflict with the entire Muslim world. No Israeli government can do this unilaterally without triggering a war far larger than any previous Middle East conflict.

## The Location Debate

Scholars disagree on the precise location of the Holy of Holies within the Temple Mount:

**Traditional view:** The Dome of the Rock sits directly over the Foundation Stone (Even HaShetiyah) — which sat inside the Holy of Holies. Building a Third Temple requires either removing the Dome or building around it.

**Asher Kaufman's theory (1983):** The temple was located approximately 100 meters northwest of the Dome of the Rock — over a small domed structure called the "Dome of the Spirits" or "Dome of the Tablets." Building there would not require touching the Dome of the Rock.

**Tuvia Sagiv's theory:** The temple was located south of the Dome of the Rock.

The disagreement itself is suspicious. If the location were unambiguous, the political solution would be clearer. The confusion preserves the stalemate.

## Who Could Enable the Temple — Daniel's Covenant

**Daniel 9:27** — "he will make a firm covenant with the many for one week"

The "many" likely includes multiple parties — Israel, surrounding nations, possibly major world powers. The covenant is specifically described as enabling the restart of temple sacrifice. This is a peace deal that solves the Temple Mount problem.

**The one who brokers this deal:**
- Appears to solve the Middle East's most intractable conflict
- Enables Jewish temple worship to resume
- Is received as a political and possibly messianic figure
- Has a seven-year window
- Breaks the covenant at the midpoint (3.5 years)
- Enters the temple and declares himself God

**Lucifer's strategy made explicit:**
He does not prevent the temple. He enables it — under conditions that serve his purpose. The temple built through his broker is the throne he has been positioning for since Eden. The invitation is not forced. It is offered willingly by people who believe they are finally getting what God promised.

## The Ezekiel Connection — Glory Returned From the East

**Ezekiel 10:18-19** — God's glory departed eastward to the Mount of Olives (see 09_gods_glory_departs.md).

**Ezekiel 43:1-5** (NASB) — the glory returns:
> "Then he led me to the gate, the gate facing toward the east; and behold, the glory of the God of Israel was coming from the way of the east. And His voice was like the sound of many waters; and the earth shone with His glory... and the glory of the LORD came into the house by the way of the gate facing toward the east. And the Spirit lifted me up and brought me into the inner court; and behold, the glory of the LORD filled the house."

The glory left east. The glory returns from the east. Through the eastern gate.

**The Eastern Gate (Golden Gate) is currently sealed.** It was walled up by the Ottoman Sultan Suleiman the Magnificent in 1541 AD — reportedly because of a prophecy that the Messiah would enter through it. A Muslim cemetery was placed in front of it, as Jewish religious law prohibits priests from walking through a cemetery.

The gate through which prophecy says the Messiah returns — is sealed — with a cemetery in front of it — by a decision made 500 years ago by a ruler who apparently understood the significance well enough to try to prevent it.

## The Curse of the Land and the Temple

**The land cannot produce its promise** (milk and honey) while the Canaan curse remains active (see 14_milk_and_honey.md).

**God's presence returning to the temple** was the mechanism for lifting that curse and healing the land.

**Ezekiel 47:1-9** (NASB) — the river from the Third Temple:
> "Then he brought me back to the door of the house; and behold, water was flowing from under the threshold of the house toward the east... and wherever the river goes, it will bring life."

**Verse 8**: The river flows into the Arabah and the sea — the Dead Sea — and the Dead Sea becomes fresh.

The Third Temple, when God's genuine presence fills it, literally heals the land. Transforms the most dead body of water on earth into a living sea. Produces the milk and honey that was always the promise.

But that temple — the Millennial temple of Ezekiel's vision — is not the one Daniel's covenant builder constructs. There is a false temple before the true one. And the two are separated by the most turbulent period in human history.

## Translation Notes
- **שִׁקּוּץ שֹׁמֵם (shiqqutz shomem)** — "abomination of desolation" — shiqqutz means detestable/abominable thing, shomem means desolating/appalling. The combination indicates something so spiritually repulsive it empties or desolates the sacred space.
- **שָׁבוּעַ (shavua)** — "week" in Daniel 9:27 — a unit of seven. Most interpreters understand this as a week of years = 7 years. The midpoint is 3.5 years.
- **The Eastern Gate** — Hebrew **שַׁעַר הַקָּדִים (sha'ar ha-kadim)** — its sealing is not prophecy itself but its re-opening is associated in Jewish tradition with Messianic arrival.

## Episode Angle
The Third Temple is coming. Scripture is clear. What is less discussed is that the first version of the Third Temple — the one Daniel describes — is not built by God. It is built by a covenant that a deceiver brokers. Lucifer's final move is not to prevent the temple. It's to occupy it. The door principle from the garden — he cannot enter what hasn't invited him — means someone will open that door willingly. And the people who open it will believe they are finally fulfilling God's prophecy. The land is still cursed. The site is still occupied. The Eastern Gate is still sealed. But the threads are converging. And when they finally produce the man who seems to solve all of it — that is precisely the moment to be most afraid.
