Hey — if frame structures feel complicated, here is the calm version: squares wobble, triangles do not.

Push a square of struts and it squashes into a diamond.

Push a triangle and it stays the same.

Add one strut across a square to make two triangles.

That is why roofs, bridges, cranes and pylons are full of triangles.

Open the practice set and look for the triangles. You've got this!
