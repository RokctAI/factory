Hey — if indigenous homes feel like lots of new names, here is the calm version: every one starts with a frame of poles.

Push thin poles into the ground in a circle, bend them over and tie them.

Cover the frame: reed mats for the Nama matjieshuis, thatch grass for Zulu and Xhosa homes, sometimes with mud walls.

The frame gives the strength. The covering gives the shelter.

Open the practice set and think: frame first, cover second. You've got this!
