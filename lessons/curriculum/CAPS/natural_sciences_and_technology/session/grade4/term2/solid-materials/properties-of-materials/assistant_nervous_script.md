Hey — if ten property words feel like a lot, here is the calm version: they come in five pairs.

Hard or soft. Stiff or flexible. Strong or weak. Light or heavy. Waterproof or absorbent.

Then just match the job: a raincoat must be waterproof, a towel must be absorbent, a chair must be strong.

Open the practice set and think of the testing lab. You've got this!
