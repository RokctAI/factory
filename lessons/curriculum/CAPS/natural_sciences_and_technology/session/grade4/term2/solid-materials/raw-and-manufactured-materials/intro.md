Today a Grade 4 class in Vereeniging sets up a table called Where Does It Come From? On it are a glass jar, a clay mug, a plastic bottle, a newspaper, a wool jersey and a leather shoe. Next to each, they place the raw material it came from: sand, clay, coal and oil, wood, wool and hide. We learn the difference between raw materials and manufactured materials, and how people change one into the other.
