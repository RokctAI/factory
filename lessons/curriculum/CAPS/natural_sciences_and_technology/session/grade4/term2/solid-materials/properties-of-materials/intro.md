Today a Grade 4 class in George, a rainy town on the Garden Route, runs a materials testing lab. They test wood, glass, plastic, paper, cotton cloth, rubber, metal and foam. They learn five pairs of properties: hard or soft, stiff or flexible, strong or weak, light or heavy, and waterproof or absorbent. Then they choose the best material for jobs like a raincoat, a towel and a school bag.
