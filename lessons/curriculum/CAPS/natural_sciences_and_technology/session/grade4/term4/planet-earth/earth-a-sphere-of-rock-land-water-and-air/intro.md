Today we stand at Cape Agulhas, the southern tip of Africa, where a Grade 4 class from Bredasdorp looks out over the sea with a globe in their hands. We learn that the Earth is a huge sphere made of rock, that its surface has land, water and air, that most of it is covered by oceans and seas, that the land is made up of continents and islands, and that a thin layer of air wraps around the whole planet.
