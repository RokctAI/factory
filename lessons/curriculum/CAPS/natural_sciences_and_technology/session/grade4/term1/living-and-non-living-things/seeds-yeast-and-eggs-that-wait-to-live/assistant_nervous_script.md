Hey — if beans, yeast and eggs all being alive sounds strange, here is the calm version: they are just asleep.

A dry bean wakes up with water and warmth and grows a root.

Dried yeast wakes up with warm water and sugar and makes bubbles.

A fertilised egg kept warm grows a chick.

Too much heat kills them all.

Open the practice set and ask: what would wake this one up? You've got this!
