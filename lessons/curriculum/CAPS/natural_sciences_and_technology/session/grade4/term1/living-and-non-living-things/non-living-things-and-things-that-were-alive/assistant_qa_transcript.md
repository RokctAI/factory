### subtopic_1 — Things That Were Never Alive
**Assistant:** Is a shell living, once living or never living?

**Tutor:** An empty shell is once living. A shell is made by a living sea animal, such as a snail, a mussel or an oyster. The animal has a soft body, and it builds the hard shell around itself, little by little, from things it takes out of the sea water. The shell grows as the animal grows. While the animal is inside and alive, the shell is part of a living thing. When the animal dies, its soft body rots away or is eaten, and the empty shell washes up on the beach. The shell cannot feed, grow or move any more, so it is not living. But it came from a living animal, so we put it in the once-living group, not with the sand and rocks. Be careful on the beach, though: a shell that is heavy and closed, or that has something moving inside, may still have its animal living in it.

### subtopic_2 — Things That Were Once Alive
**Assistant:** Why does a plastic bottle last so much longer than a dead log?

**Tutor:** A dead log is made of wood, which came from a living tree. In nature there are many tiny living things, like fungi and bacteria, as well as termites and beetles, that feed on dead wood and leaves. As they feed, they break the wood down into smaller and smaller pieces, until it becomes part of the soil. This is called rotting, or decomposing. It is nature's way of recycling, because the soil then feeds new plants. Plastic is made by people in factories, mostly from oil. Very few living things can feed on it, so it does not rot. A plastic bottle can lie on a beach for hundreds of years, slowly breaking into tiny pieces that sea animals may swallow by mistake. That is why the class is cleaning up the beach, and why we should put plastic in the bin or recycle it.

### subtopic_3 — Sorting into Three Groups
**Assistant:** If a seed is alive, is an apple on the kitchen table alive too?

**Tutor:** This is a tricky one. An apple is the fruit of an apple tree, and while it hangs on the tree it is part of a living plant. Once it is picked, the fruit itself slowly starts to change. It can still take in air and give off gas for a while, which is why apples can ripen in a bowl, but it cannot grow or reproduce on its own. The seeds inside the apple, however, are alive and resting, just like a dry bean. If you planted an apple seed in soil and gave it water and warmth, it could grow into a new apple tree. So we can say that the seeds are living, and the fruit around them is part of a once-living plant. When in doubt, go back to the seven life processes and ask which ones the thing can still do on its own.
