Hey — if sorting living and non-living things feels tricky, here is the calm version: just use three baskets.

Living: it does all seven life processes now.

Once living: it came from something alive, like wood, a shell or a feather.

Never living: it was never alive, like sand, plastic or metal.

Moving is not enough. A fire moves, but it is not alive.

Open the practice set and drop each thing into a basket. You've got this!
