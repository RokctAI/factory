Today a Grade 4 class in Kimberley builds model shelters for the school's pet rabbits. Before they start, they study two kinds of structures. Shell structures are hollow with a strong outer skin, like an egg, a snail shell or a plastic kennel. Frame structures are made of parts joined together like a skeleton, like a bird cage or a kraal fence. We learn about the shapes, sizes and materials of animal shelters and how to choose the best one.
