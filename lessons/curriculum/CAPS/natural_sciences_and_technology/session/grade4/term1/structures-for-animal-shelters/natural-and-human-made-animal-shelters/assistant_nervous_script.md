Hey — if animal shelters feel like a long list, here is the calm version: two groups.

Natural shelters are made by animals: nests, shells, hollow trees, wasp nests, burrows.

Human-made shelters are built by people: kennels, cages, kraals, stables.

Every shelter keeps off the weather and keeps the animal safe.

Open the practice set and ask: who built this one? You've got this!
