Hey — if cuttings and seeds feel confusing, here is the calm version: two ways to make a new plant.

A cutting is a piece of stem. Push it into damp soil and it grows new roots.

A seed has a baby plant inside. Give it water and warmth and it germinates.

The root comes out first, then the shoot.

Seeds do not need light to start.

Open the practice set and picture the warm jar of beans. You've got this!
