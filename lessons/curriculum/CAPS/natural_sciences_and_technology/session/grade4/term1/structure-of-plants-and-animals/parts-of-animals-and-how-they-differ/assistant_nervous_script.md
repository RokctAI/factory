Hey — if animal parts feel like a long list, here is the calm version: head, body, tail, limbs.

Limbs are anything for moving: legs, wings, fins, flippers.

Sense organs tell the animal what is around it: eyes, ears, nose, tongue, skin.

Then animals just look different: big or small, fur, feathers, scales, shells or wet skin.
