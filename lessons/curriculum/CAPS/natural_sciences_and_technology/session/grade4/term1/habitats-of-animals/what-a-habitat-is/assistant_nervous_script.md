Hey — if habitats sound like a big word, here is the calm version: a habitat is just an animal's home address.

Grassland: open and grassy, with zebra and blesbok.

Forest: tall trees and shade, with the Knysna turaco.

River: fresh water, with fish, frogs and hippos.

Sea: salty water, with dolphins, sharks and seals.

Open the practice set and match each animal to its address. You've got this!
