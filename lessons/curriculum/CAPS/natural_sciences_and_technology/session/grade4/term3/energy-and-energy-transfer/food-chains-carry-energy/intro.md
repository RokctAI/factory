Today a Grade 4 class from Phalaborwa goes on a game drive in the Kruger National Park. They see zebra grazing, a lion resting in the shade, a grasshopper in the grass, a lizard on a rock and an eagle circling overhead. We learn how energy passes from the Sun to plants, from plants to animals, and from animals to other animals in a food chain, and what the arrows in a food chain mean.
