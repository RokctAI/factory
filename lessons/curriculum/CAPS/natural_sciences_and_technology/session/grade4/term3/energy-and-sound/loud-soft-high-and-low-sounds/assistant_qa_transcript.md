### subtopic_1 — Loud and Soft Sounds
**Assistant:** Why do men usually have lower voices than children?

**Tutor:** Your voice comes from your vocal cords, two small folds inside your voice box in your throat. When air from your lungs passes between them, they vibrate and make a sound. The pitch of your voice depends on how fast your vocal cords vibrate. Children have short, thin vocal cords, which vibrate quickly and make higher voices. As boys grow into teenagers, their voice box grows bigger and their vocal cords become longer and thicker. Longer, thicker vocal cords vibrate more slowly, so the voice becomes deeper. That is why a teenage boy's voice sometimes cracks or squeaks while it is changing. Girls' vocal cords grow too, but less, so women's voices usually stay higher than men's. It is the same rule as the guitar strings: thick and long make low sounds, and thin and short make high sounds. You can change your own pitch a little by tightening or relaxing your vocal cords, which is how singers reach high and low notes.

### subtopic_2 — High and Low Sounds
**Assistant:** How do the people who make marimbas get each key to play the right note?

**Tutor:** Marimba makers use the rule that long, thick keys vibrate slowly and make low notes, while short, thin keys vibrate faster and make higher notes. They start by cutting wooden bars of different lengths, from long to short. Then they tune each key carefully. If a key sounds too low, they shave a little wood off the end, making it shorter, which raises its note. If a key sounds too high, they carefully carve a little wood from the underside in the middle, which makes it vibrate more slowly and lowers the note. They strike the key, listen, carve, and strike again, often comparing it with a tuning device, until the note is just right. Under each key hangs a tube or a box called a resonator. The resonators are also cut to the right length so that the air inside vibrates with the key and makes the note louder and richer. Many South African schools have marimba bands that use instruments made this way.

### subtopic_3 — Making Music with Bottles
**Assistant:** Can very loud sounds also be very high or very low?

**Tutor:** Yes. Volume and pitch are completely separate, so any combination is possible. A police siren is loud and high. A big truck's engine or a thunderclap can be loud and low. A mosquito buzzing near your ear is soft and high. A cat's purr is soft and low. Think about the drum and the bottles. Sipho can play the big drum softly or loudly, but it always has a low pitch, because the size of the drum decides the pitch. He can play the small drum softly or loudly too, but it always sounds higher. Hitting harder only changes the volume. On the guitar, Aviwe can pluck the thin string gently or strongly, but its note stays high. To change the pitch, she must tighten or loosen the string, or press it to make it shorter. So remember: energy and how big the vibrations are decide the volume, and how fast the vibrations are decides the pitch.
