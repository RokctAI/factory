Hey — if hearing damage sounds scary, here is the calm version: you can protect your ears easily.

Very loud sounds can hurt the tiny hairs inside your ears, and they do not grow back.

So turn the volume down. Take quiet breaks. Move away from loud speakers. Wear earplugs in very loud places.

Ringing ears mean: give them a rest.

Open the practice set and think about your own ears. You've got this!
