Today the Grade 4 class in Thohoyandou in Limpopo prepares for their Heritage Day concert. The school marimba band practises, drummers beat their drums, a learner plucks a guitar, another blows a kudu horn and the little ones shake rattles. We learn that a musical instrument is a system, with parts that work together, that it needs movement as its input energy, and that its main output energy is sound.
