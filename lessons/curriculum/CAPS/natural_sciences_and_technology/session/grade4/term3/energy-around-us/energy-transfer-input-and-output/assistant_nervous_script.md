Hey — if input and output sound technical, here is the calm version: what goes in, and what comes out?

Energy comes from a source, like the Sun, the wind or a power station, and travels to where it is needed.

A machine takes in input energy and gives out output energy.

Kettle: electricity in, heat out. Torch: battery in, light out.

Some energy is always wasted as heat or sound.
