Today a Grade 6 class in Phalaborwa in Limpopo, a town built next to one of the biggest open-pit copper mines in South Africa, builds a tester circuit with a gap in it. They clip a nail, a coin, a spoon, foil, a pencil, an eraser and other objects into the gap one by one. We learn that conductors let electricity flow through them, that most metals are good conductors, and why copper is used for wires.
