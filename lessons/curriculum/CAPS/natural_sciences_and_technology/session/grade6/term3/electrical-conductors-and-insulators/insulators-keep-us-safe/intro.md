Today a power line technician visits a Grade 6 class in Ermelo in Mpumalanga, wearing thick rubber gloves and boots and carrying tools with plastic handles. She shows them the glass and ceramic discs that hold power lines on pylons, and a cut plug with plastic all around the metal. We learn that insulators do not let electricity flow through them, that most non-metals such as plastics are insulators, and how insulators keep us safe.
