Today a Grade 6 class from Ogies in Mpumalanga, where huge cooling towers rise above the grasslands, follows a lump of coal on its journey through a coal-fired power station. Back in class, their teacher makes steam from a kettle spin a paper pinwheel, and a bicycle dynamo light a bulb. We learn how burning coal makes steam, the steam turns a turbine, and the turbine turns a generator that produces electricity.
