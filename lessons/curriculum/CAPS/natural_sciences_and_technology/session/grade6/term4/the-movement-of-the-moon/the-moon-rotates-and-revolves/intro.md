Today a Grade 6 class in Ladysmith in KwaZulu-Natal shares the Moon diaries they kept for a month, with a drawing of the Moon for every clear night. Then they act out the Moon's movements with a chair, a learner and a lamp. We learn that the Moon revolves around the Earth in about a month, that it rotates once in about twenty-eight days, and that the Earth and Moon travel around the Sun together.
