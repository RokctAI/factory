Today a Grade 6 class in Thohoyandou in Limpopo meets a learner whose mother was born on 29 February, so her real birthday only comes once every four years. To find out why, the class acts out the Earth travelling around the Sun on the netball court. We learn that planets revolve around the Sun, that one revolution of the Earth takes 365¼ days, which is a year, and why we have leap years.
