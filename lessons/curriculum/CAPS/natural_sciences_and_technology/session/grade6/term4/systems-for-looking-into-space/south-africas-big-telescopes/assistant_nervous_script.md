Hey — if all the telescope names feel confusing, here is the calm version: one mirror, many dishes.

SALT at Sutherland has a giant mirror that catches faint light.

MeerKAT near Carnarvon has sixty-four dishes that catch radio waves, so phones must be off.

The SKA will be the biggest radio telescope in the world.

Open the practice set and take one telescope at a time. You've got this!
