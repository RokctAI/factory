Hey — if a whole term feels big, here is the calm version: three stations.

Station 1: particles are packed in solids, sliding in liquids and spread out in gases. Mixtures can be separated.

Station 2: a solute dissolves in a solvent to make a solution. Evaporation gets the solute back.

Station 3: keep water clean, then purify it to make it safe.

Open the practice set and visit one station at a time. You've got this!
