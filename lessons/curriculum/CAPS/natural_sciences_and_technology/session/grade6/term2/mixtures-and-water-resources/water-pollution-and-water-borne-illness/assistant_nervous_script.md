Hey — if pollution sounds scary, here is the calm version: three kinds.

Pollution you can see: plastic, litter, oil and mud.

Pollution you can't see: dissolved fertiliser, poisons, soap and salt.

Germs from sewage, which cause water-borne illnesses like cholera.

Clean water, toilets and handwashing keep us safe.

Open the practice set and sort each pollutant into one of the three kinds. You've got this!
