Today a Grade 6 class from Graskop visits the old mining town of Pilgrim's Rest in Mpumalanga, where they try panning for gold in a stream, just like the miners of long ago. Back at school, a bag of beans, rice, sand and stones has split open. We learn that a mixture is made of at least two different substances, and how to separate visible mixtures by hand sorting, sieving, filtering, settling and decanting.
