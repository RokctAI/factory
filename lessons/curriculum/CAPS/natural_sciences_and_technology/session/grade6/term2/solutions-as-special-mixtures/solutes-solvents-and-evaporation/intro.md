Today a Grade 6 class from Velddrif on the West Coast visits the salt pans near their town, where sea water lies in wide, shallow ponds until the Sun dries it out and leaves crunchy white salt behind. Back in class, they weigh salt and water and leave saucers of salt water on the windowsill. We learn the words solute, solvent and soluble, and how a dissolved solute can be recovered by evaporation.
