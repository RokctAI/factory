Today a Grade 6 class in Clanwilliam, the home of rooibos tea in the Western Cape, stirs sugar into cups of water and sand into others, and compares them. Then a nurse shows them the sugar and salt drink that clinics use to help children who have diarrhoea. We learn that a solution is a special mixture that looks the same all the way through, made when a solid dissolves in a liquid.
