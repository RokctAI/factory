Hey — if starch sounds tricky, here is the calm version: it is the plant's lunch box.

Plants make glucose, a sugar.

The extra glucose is changed into starch and packed away.

The food can be stored in leaves, stems, roots, flowers, fruits and seeds.

A drop of iodine turns blue-black on starch.

Open the practice set and pack the lunch box one plant part at a time. You've got this!
