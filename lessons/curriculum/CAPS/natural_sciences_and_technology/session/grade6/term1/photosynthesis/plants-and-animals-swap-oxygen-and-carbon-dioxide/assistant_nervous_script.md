Hey — if gases feel invisible and confusing, here is the calm version: it is a swap.

Plants take in carbon dioxide and give out oxygen.

Animals and people take in oxygen and give out carbon dioxide.

Each one gives the other what it needs.

Oxygen relights a glowing splint. Carbon dioxide turns limewater milky.
