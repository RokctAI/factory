Hey — if all these disease names feel heavy, here is the calm version: too little or too much.

Too little protein: kwashiorkor. Too little food: marasmus.

Too little vitamin C: scurvy. Too little iron: anaemia.

Too much sugar, salt or fat: tooth decay, high blood pressure, obesity and diabetes.

A balanced diet and exercise help keep us well.
