### subtopic_1 — What a Diet Is
**Assistant:** Can a vegetarian have a balanced diet without eating meat?

**Tutor:** Yes, a vegetarian can have a very healthy, balanced diet, as long as they plan it well. The main nutrient that meat gives is protein, along with iron. Vegetarians can get protein from beans, lentils, peas, soya, peanuts and other nuts, and many also eat eggs, milk, maas and cheese. Iron can come from beans, lentils and dark green leaves like spinach and morogo, especially if they are eaten with fruit or vegetables rich in vitamin C, like tomatoes or oranges, which help the body take in the iron. Some vegetarians eat no animal foods at all, not even eggs or milk. They are called vegans, and they need to plan even more carefully, because one vitamin, called vitamin B12, comes almost only from animal foods. Vegans often get it from specially fortified foods or from tablets. So it is not meat itself that the body needs, but the nutrients that meat contains, and those can come from other foods too.

### subtopic_2 — The Balanced Diet
**Assistant:** Why is breakfast called the most important meal of the day?

**Tutor:** While you sleep, your body keeps working. Your heart beats, you breathe, and your body repairs itself. It uses up energy all night. By the morning, it may have been ten or twelve hours since you last ate. Breakfast refuels the body. Starchy foods like porridge or bread give energy that lasts through the morning, and milk or an egg gives protein. Studies have found that children who eat breakfast usually find it easier to concentrate and remember things in class, and they are less likely to buy unhealthy snacks at break because they are starving. Children who skip breakfast are more likely to feel tired, grumpy or dizzy, and some get headaches. Breakfast does not have to be fancy. A bowl of soft porridge or mabele with milk, or a slice of brown bread with peanut butter and a glass of water, is a good start. Many schools also give learners a meal during the day for this reason.

### subtopic_3 — Water and Fibre
**Assistant:** How do I know if I am drinking enough water?

**Tutor:** Your body gives you some simple signals. The first is thirst. If you feel thirsty, your body is already a little short of water, so it is a good idea to drink before you get very thirsty, especially on hot days and when you play sport. The second signal is the colour of your urine. When you drink enough water, your urine is pale yellow, like weak lemon juice. If it is dark yellow and there is not much of it, you need to drink more. Other signs that you need water are a dry mouth, a headache and feeling tired or dizzy. Children need more water when the weather is hot, like a summer afternoon in Limpopo, and when they are running around. Water from fruit and vegetables, soup, milk and tea also counts, but plain clean water is best, because it has no sugar. Keep a bottle of water with you at school and take small sips during the day.
