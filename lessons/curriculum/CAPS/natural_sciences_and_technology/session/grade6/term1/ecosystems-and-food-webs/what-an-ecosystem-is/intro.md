Today a Grade 6 class in Bloemfontein builds an ecosystem wall from six big photographs their teacher took on her travels around South Africa: the Orange River, the sea at False Bay, a wetland in Limpopo, the Free State grassland, the Knysna forest and the desert of the Richtersveld. We learn that an ecosystem is an area where living and non-living things depend on each other, and we explore water and land ecosystems.
