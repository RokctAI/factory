Hey — if ecosystem sounds like a big word, here is the calm version: eco means home.

An ecosystem is a home where living and non-living things depend on each other.

Living: plants and animals. Non-living: sun, air, water and soil.

Water ecosystems: rivers, the sea and wetlands.

Land ecosystems: grasslands, forests and deserts.
