Hey — if food webs look like a tangle of arrows, here is the calm version: follow the food.

Plants make food. They are producers.

Animals eat. They are consumers: herbivores, carnivores and omnivores.

The arrow points from the food to the eater.

Many chains together make a web.

Fungi and bacteria break down dead things and feed the soil.

Open the practice set and follow one arrow at a time. You've got this!
