Today a Grade 6 class from Rustenburg goes on a game drive in the Pilanesberg National Park in North West. They see zebras grazing, a cheetah resting in the shade, baboons eating fruit, vultures circling, and a dung beetle rolling a ball of dung across the road. We learn how producers, consumers and decomposers are linked in food webs, and how decomposers return nutrients to the soil.
