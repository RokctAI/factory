Today a Grade 6 class from Howick in the KwaZulu-Natal Midlands spends a morning at a farm dam with a small vlei next to it. They measure the water temperature, dig in the mud, watch dragonflies, frogs and a heron, and draw lines between everything they find. We learn how living things depend on air, water, sunlight and soil, how they depend on each other, and how one change can affect the whole ecosystem.
