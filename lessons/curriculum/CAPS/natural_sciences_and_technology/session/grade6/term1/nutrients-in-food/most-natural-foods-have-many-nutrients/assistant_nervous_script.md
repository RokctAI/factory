Hey — if this seems confusing after learning food groups, here is the calm version: most foods are a mix.

An egg has protein, fat, vitamins and minerals.

Milk has protein, fat, sugar and calcium.

We group a food by what it has the most of.

Iodine finds starch. Brown paper finds fat.
