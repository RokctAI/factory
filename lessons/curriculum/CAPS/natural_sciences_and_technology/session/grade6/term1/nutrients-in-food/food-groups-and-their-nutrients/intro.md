Today a Grade 6 class in Mamelodi, Pretoria, spends a morning in their school kitchen, where the volunteer cooks prepare the free school lunch for more than a thousand learners. On the menu this week are pap, rice, samp and beans, pilchards, chicken, cabbage, butternut and fruit. We learn about the main nutrients in food: carbohydrates, proteins, fats and oils, and vitamins and minerals, and what each one does for the body.
