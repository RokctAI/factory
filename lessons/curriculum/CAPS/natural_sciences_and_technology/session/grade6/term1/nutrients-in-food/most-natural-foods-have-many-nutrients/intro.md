Today a Grade 6 class in Mthatha holds a Food Detective Day. Each group brings a natural food from home: an egg, a glass of milk, a handful of peanuts, a cup of dried beans, an avocado, a mealie and an orange. They read food labels and do two simple tests. We learn that most natural foods contain more than one nutrient group, and that we group a food by the nutrient it has the most of.
