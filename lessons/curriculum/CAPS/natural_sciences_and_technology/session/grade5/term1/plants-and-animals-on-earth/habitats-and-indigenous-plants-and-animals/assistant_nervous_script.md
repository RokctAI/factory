Hey — if all these habitats feel like a lot, here is the calm version: every living thing has a home place, called a habitat.

Indigenous means it has always lived here naturally.

Fynbos has proteas. The Karoo has succulents and meerkats.

The bushveld has elephants. The grassland has blesbok.

Wetlands have hippos. The sea has dolphins and kelp.
