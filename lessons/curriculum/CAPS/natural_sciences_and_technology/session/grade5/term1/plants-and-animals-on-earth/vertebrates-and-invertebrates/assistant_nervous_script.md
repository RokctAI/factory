Hey — if sorting animals feels tricky, here is the calm version: ask just one question. Does it have a backbone?

Yes means vertebrate: fish, amphibians, reptiles, birds and mammals.

No means invertebrate: worms, insects, spiders, crabs and snails.

A hard shell on the outside does not count as a backbone.

Open the practice set and ask the backbone question every time. You've got this!
