Hey — if all these bone names feel like a lot, here is the calm version: the skeleton has three big jobs.

It holds the body up.

It protects the soft parts: the skull for the brain, the backbone for the spinal cord, and the ribs for the heart and lungs.

It helps us move, with arms, legs and hips.

Open the practice set and remember: hold, protect, move. You've got this!
