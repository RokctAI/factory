Today a Grade 5 class from Bethlehem in the Free State visits the Golden Gate Highlands National Park, where huge golden cliffs glow in the afternoon sun. Looking closely, they see that the cliffs are made of layers, stacked like the pages of a book. We learn how sedimentary rock forms: rock breaks down, the pieces are carried away and laid down in layers, and over millions of years they are squashed and hardened into new rock.
