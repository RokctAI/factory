Hey — if two kinds of fossils feel tricky, here is the calm version: body or trace?

Body fossils are parts of the living thing: teeth, bones, shells, leaves and seeds.

Trace fossils are signs it left behind: footprints, nests, eggs, burrows and droppings.

Teeth show what it ate. Footprints show how it moved.

Open the practice set and ask each time: body or trace? You've got this!
