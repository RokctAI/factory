Today a Grade 5 class from Krugersdorp visits Maropeng, the visitor centre of the Cradle of Humankind in Gauteng, where some of the world's most important fossils of early human relatives were found. Along the way, they learn about a fish that was thought to have died out millions of years ago, and about the dinosaurs that once walked across South Africa. We learn why South Africa has one of the richest fossil records in the world.
