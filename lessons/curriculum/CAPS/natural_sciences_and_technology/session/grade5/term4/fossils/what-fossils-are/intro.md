Today a Grade 5 class from Graaff-Reinet visits the small Karoo village of Nieu-Bethesda, where a fossil centre shows animals that lived there long before the dinosaurs. A palaeontologist, a scientist who studies fossils, shows them a skull turned to stone. We learn what fossils are, how a dead plant or animal can become a fossil in sedimentary rock, and how fossils tell us the history of life on Earth.
