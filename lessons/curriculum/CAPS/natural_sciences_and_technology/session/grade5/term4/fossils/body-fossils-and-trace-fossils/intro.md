Today a Grade 5 class from Vredenburg on the West Coast visits the West Coast Fossil Park, where the bones and teeth of ancient bears, short-necked giraffes and sabre-toothed cats have been found. Then they learn about human footprints, about 117,000 years old, found in rock at nearby Langebaan Lagoon. We learn the difference between body fossils and trace fossils, and what each kind can tell us.
