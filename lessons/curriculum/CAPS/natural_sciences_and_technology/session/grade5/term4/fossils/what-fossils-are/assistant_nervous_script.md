Hey — if fossils sound like an ancient mystery, here is the calm version: buried, pressed, turned to stone.

A fossil is what is left of a plant or animal from long ago, kept in rock.

It forms when the living thing is buried quickly in mud or sand.

Over millions of years, the mud becomes rock and the bones become stone.

Older fossils are in deeper layers. Fossils tell the story of life.
