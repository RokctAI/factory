Hey — if soil formation sounds slow and complicated, here is the calm version: a slice of cake.

Top layer: dark topsoil, with roots and humus.

Middle layer: paler subsoil, with stones.

Bottom layer: rock.

Heat, cold, ice, roots, water and wind break rock into grains. Grains plus humus make topsoil, very slowly.
