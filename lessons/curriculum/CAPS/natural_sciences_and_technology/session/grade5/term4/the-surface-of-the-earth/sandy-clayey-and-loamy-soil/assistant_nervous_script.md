Hey — if soil types feel mixed up, here is the calm version: three buckets.

Sandy: big, gritty grains. Water runs straight through.

Clayey: tiny, sticky grains. It holds water and cracks when dry.

Loamy: a mix with humus. Best for growing plants.

And all soil has grains, air, water, dead remains and living things.

Open the practice set and picture the three buckets. You've got this!
