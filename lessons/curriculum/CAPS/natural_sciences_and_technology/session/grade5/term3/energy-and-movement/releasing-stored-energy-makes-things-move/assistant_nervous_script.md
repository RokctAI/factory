Hey — if energy changes sound confusing, here is the calm version: stretch it, then let it go.

Stretching or twisting stores energy.

Letting go turns the stored energy into movement.

Bungee cords, bows, catapults, wind-up toys and trampolines all do this.

More twists in the cotton reel, further it goes.
