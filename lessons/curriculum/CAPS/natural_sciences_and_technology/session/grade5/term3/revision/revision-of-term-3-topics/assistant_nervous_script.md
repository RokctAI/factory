Hey — if a whole term feels big, here is the calm version: three tables on a trail.

Table 1: fuels store energy. Burning gives heat and light. Fire needs fuel, heat and oxygen. Use fire safely.

Table 2: cells store energy. A complete circuit lights a bulb. Power stations make mains electricity. Use it safely.

Table 3: stretch, twist or squash elastic and springs to store energy. Let go, and things move.

Open the practice set and visit one table at a time. You've got this!
