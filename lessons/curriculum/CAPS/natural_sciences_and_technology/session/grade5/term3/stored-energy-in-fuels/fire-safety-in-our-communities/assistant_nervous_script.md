Hey — if fires sound scary, here is the calm version: knowing what to do keeps you safe.

Keep candles in sand and matches up high.

Never store paraffin in a cooldrink bottle.

If there is a fire: shout, get out, crawl under smoke, stay out.

Call 10177 or 112.

Clothes on fire? Stop, drop and roll.

Open the practice set and practise the safe steps. You've got this!
