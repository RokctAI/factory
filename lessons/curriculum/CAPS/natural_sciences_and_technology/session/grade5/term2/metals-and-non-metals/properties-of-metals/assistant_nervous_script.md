Hey — if all these new words feel strange, here is the calm version: metals have a few properties to remember.

Shiny, hard and strong.

Malleable: you can hammer it into a new shape.

Ductile: you can pull it into wire.

They melt only when very hot.

They are dug out of the Earth as ores.

Open the practice set and take one property at a time. You've got this!
