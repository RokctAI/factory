Today a Grade 5 class in Kroonstad gets ready for a school braai. On the table are charcoal, a box of matches, pencils, a lump of yellow sulfur from the science cupboard and the metal braai grid. We learn about non-metals: that solid non-metals such as carbon and sulfur are dull and brittle, how they differ from metals, and how to test a material to find out which group it belongs to.
