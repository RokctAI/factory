Hey — if a whole term feels big, here is the calm version: four shelves in a shop.

Shelf 1: metals are shiny, malleable and ductile. Non-metals are dull and brittle.

Shelf 2: only iron rusts, only some metals are magnetic, and each metal suits its job.

Shelf 3: mix and set, cook, cool, or fire to make new materials.

Shelf 4: processed materials are strong, waterproof, fire resistant and colourful.

Open the practice set and visit one shelf at a time. You've got this!
