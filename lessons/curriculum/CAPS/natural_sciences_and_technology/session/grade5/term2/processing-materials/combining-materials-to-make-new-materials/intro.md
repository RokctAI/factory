Today we spend a busy Saturday with a family in Khayelitsha. Uncle Sipho mixes concrete for a new room, Gogo bakes bread, the children make jelly for a birthday and glue paste for a school project, and a cousin shows off the plaster cast on her arm. We learn how materials can be combined to make new materials, by mixing and setting, mixing, mixing and cooking, mixing and cooling, and mixing, drying and firing.
