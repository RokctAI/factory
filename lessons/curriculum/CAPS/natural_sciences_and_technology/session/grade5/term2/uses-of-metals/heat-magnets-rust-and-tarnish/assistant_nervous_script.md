Hey — if heat, magnets and rust feel like a lot, here is the calm version: four quick facts.

Metals carry heat quickly, so pot handles are wood or plastic.

Only some metals are magnetic: iron, steel, nickel and cobalt.

Only iron and steel rust. Paint and oil stop it.

Silver and copper tarnish, and polish brings back the shine.
