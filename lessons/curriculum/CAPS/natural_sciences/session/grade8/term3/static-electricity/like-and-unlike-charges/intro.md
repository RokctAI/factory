Today we investigate the forces between electric charges. Hang two balloons on threads, rub both on a woollen jersey, and they swing apart; bring the jersey near and each swings towards it. We establish the law of electric charges, that like charges repel and unlike charges attract, and show that the force grows with the amount of charge and weakens quickly with distance. We explain the puzzle of a charged comb lifting neutral paper and a balloon sticking to a wall, using induced charge separation. We learn how a gold-leaf electroscope detects charge and tells positive from negative. Finally we see these forces at work in photocopiers, electrostatic spray painting in South African car factories, and the precipitators that clean ash from power station smoke.
