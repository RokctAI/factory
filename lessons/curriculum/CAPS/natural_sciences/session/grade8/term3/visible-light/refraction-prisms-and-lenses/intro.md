Today we explain refraction, the bending of light as it passes from one transparent material into another. A pencil standing in a glass of water looks snapped at the surface, a swimming pool looks shallower than it really is, and a coin hidden behind the rim of a cup appears when water is poured in. We see that light bends because it changes speed, towards the normal when it slows down and away from the normal when it speeds up, and investigate this with a glass block. We explain apparent depth, mirages on hot Karoo roads and twinkling stars. We meet convex lenses, which converge light to a focus and can magnify, and concave lenses, which diverge it. Finally we find lenses at work in spectacles, cameras, projectors, microscopes and telescopes.
