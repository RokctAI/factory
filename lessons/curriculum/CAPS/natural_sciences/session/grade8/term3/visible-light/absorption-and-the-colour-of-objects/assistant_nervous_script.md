Hey — if colour felt confusing, here is the calm version: the colour you see is the light that bounces back to you.

White light is all the colours mixed. When it hits an object, the object soaks up some colours and bounces back others. A red apple bounces back red and soaks up the rest. A leaf bounces back green.

White things bounce back all colours. Black things soak up all colours and bounce back almost nothing.

A coloured filter lets its own colour through and soaks up the others. Filters only take colours away.

In coloured light, an object can only bounce back colours that are there. Under a red light, a white shirt looks red, a red cap looks red, but a green or blue shirt looks black, because it has nothing to bounce back.

The primary colours of light are red, green and blue. Screens mix them to make every colour.

Dark surfaces soak up more light energy, so they get hotter in the sun. That is why solar heater panels are black.
