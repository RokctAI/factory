Hey — if reflection felt full of new words, here is the calm version: light bounces like a ball, at equal angles.

Draw a line at right angles to the mirror where the light hits. That is the normal. The angle between the incoming ray and the normal is the angle of incidence. The angle between the outgoing ray and the normal is the angle of reflection. The law of reflection says they are equal.

Always measure from the normal, not from the mirror.

A flat mirror makes an image that seems to be behind the glass. It is virtual, upright, the same size, and as far behind as you are in front. Left and right are swapped.

Smooth surfaces, like a mirror or still water, reflect light in neat parallel lines and make a clear image: regular reflection. Rough surfaces, like paper, scatter light in all directions: diffuse reflection. That is why you can see a page from any angle.

Uses: car mirrors, periscopes, road sign reflectors, telescopes.
