Today we follow light into the eye. Walk from bright sunshine into a dark cinema and at first you see almost nothing; minutes later shapes appear, but only in grey. Step back outside and you are dazzled. We name the parts of the eye and their jobs, from the cornea and iris to the lens, retina and optic nerve. We see how the cornea and lens focus an upside-down image on the retina, how the lens changes shape for near and distant objects, and how the pupil responds to light. We meet the receptor cells, rods for dim light and cones for colour and detail, which turn light into nerve impulses that travel to the brain. Finally we look at short and long sight, cataracts and colour vision, and how to care for our eyes.
