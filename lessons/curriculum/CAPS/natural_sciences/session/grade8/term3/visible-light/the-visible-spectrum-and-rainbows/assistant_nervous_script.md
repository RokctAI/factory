Hey — if the spectrum and rainbows felt like a lot, here is the calm version: white light is all the colours mixed together, and bending light can pull them apart.

White light is a mixture of colours. In order: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet. Remember ROY G BIV.

When light goes from air into glass or water at a slant, it slows down and bends. That is refraction. Each colour bends by a slightly different amount: violet bends the most, red the least. So the colours spread out. That is dispersion. A prism does this; it does not add colour, it separates it.

A rainbow is sunlight being bent, bounced and bent again inside raindrops. The Sun must be behind you and the rain in front of you. Red is on the outside of the bow. A fainter second bow has its colours the other way round.

Beyond red is infrared, which we feel as heat. Beyond violet is ultraviolet, which causes sunburn.

For any explain question, use the words refraction and dispersion, and say violet bends most.
