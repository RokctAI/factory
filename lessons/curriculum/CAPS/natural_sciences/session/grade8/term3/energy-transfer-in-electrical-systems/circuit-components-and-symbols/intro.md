Today we meet the parts that circuits are built from and learn the language used to draw them. Two learners given the same written description build two different circuits, but given a circuit diagram they build identical ones in minutes. We look at cells and batteries, their terminals and voltages, and why joining cells in a row adds their push. We see why wires are copper with plastic insulation, why thicker wires carry larger currents, and how different switches work. We meet fixed and variable resistors, heating elements, LEDs, fuses, ammeters and voltmeters. Finally we learn the standard circuit symbols and the rules for drawing a clear, correct circuit diagram, including the direction of conventional current from the positive terminal to the negative.
