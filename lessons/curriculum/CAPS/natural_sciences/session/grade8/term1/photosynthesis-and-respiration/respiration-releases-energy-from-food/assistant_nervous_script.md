Hey — if respiration felt like a tangle of lungs, equations and seeds in jars, here is the calm version: it is a slow fire, and the fire is not the truck.

The fire. Glucose plus oxygen gives carbon dioxide plus water plus energy. That happens in every living cell, in you, in a tree, in a fish, all day and all night. No flames, because it happens in tiny steps inside the mitochondria. The energy is what moves you, grows you, heals you and keeps you at 37 degrees.

The truck. Breathing is just the delivery: air in, oxygen into the blood, carbon dioxide out. It is physical. Respiration is chemical and happens in the cells. A tree respires without any lungs at all.

The evidence. Limewater turns milky for carbon dioxide; living seeds in a dark jar do it, boiled seeds do not. A thermometer in sprouting seeds shows the heat.

And plants do both: they photosynthesise only in the light, and respire always. The two equations are each other backwards.
