Hey — if producers, consumers, decomposers, herbivores, carnivores and omnivores all ran together, here is the calm version: it is a restaurant with a kitchen, some eaters and a cleaning crew.

The kitchen is the producers: green plants and algae, cooking food from sunlight. Nobody else can cook. The eaters are the consumers, sorted by menu: herbivores eat plants, carnivores eat animals, omnivores eat both. Among the meat-eaters, a predator hunts, a scavenger eats what is already dead, an insectivore eats insects. The cleaners are the decomposers, bacteria and fungi, who break the dead down into minerals for the soil, with earthworms and dung beetles shredding the big bits.

Two traps. A mushroom is a cleaner, not a kitchen. A vulture is an eater, not a cleaner.

Teeth tell you the diet, and the diet gives the label, never the size. And the arrow in a diagram goes from the food to the eater, the way the energy flows.
