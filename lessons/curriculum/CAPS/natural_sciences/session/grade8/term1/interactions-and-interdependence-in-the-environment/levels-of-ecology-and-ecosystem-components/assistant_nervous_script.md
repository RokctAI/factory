Hey — if all those words, population, community, ecosystem, biome, biotic, abiotic, felt like a vocabulary test from nowhere, here is the calm version: six boxes and two columns.

The boxes. One living thing is an organism. All of one kind in one place at one time is a population. Everything alive in the area is a community. Add the sun, rain, soil and air and it is an ecosystem. A whole climate region is a biome. The whole living planet is the biosphere. Each box sits inside the next.

The columns. Biotic is anything alive or that was alive, including the dead leaf and the dung. Abiotic is anything that never was: water, rock, light, temperature. Two columns, every time.

A habitat is just a home, described by what the place is like. And when the home changes with night or winter, living things hide, leave, sleep or have bodies built for it, and you always finish with how that helps them survive.

Open the practice set, draw the boxes, fill the columns. You've got this!
