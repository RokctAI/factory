Hey — if this topic felt like a list of recipes, here is the calm version: each one is a reaction that makes a useful new substance.

Fermentation is tiny living things breaking down sugar without oxygen. Yeast turns sugar into alcohol and carbon dioxide. That is umqombothi bubbling in its pot, and bread rising in the oven. The sorghum is sprouted first because sprouting turns its starch into sugar the yeast can use.

Lactic acid bacteria turn sugar into lactic acid. That is how milk becomes thick, sour amasi and porridge becomes mageu. The acid stops harmful germs, so the food keeps longer.

Smelting iron: charcoal and bellows make a very hot furnace, and gas from the charcoal pulls oxygen away from the iron oxide in the ore. Iron is left behind.

Soap: fat plus a liquid made from wood ash. Leather: animal skin plus bark tannins.

For any question, name the process, give the word equation, and point to a sign of a reaction, like bubbles or a new smell.
