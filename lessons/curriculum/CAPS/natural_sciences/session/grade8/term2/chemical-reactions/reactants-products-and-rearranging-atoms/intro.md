Today we look inside a chemical reaction. Light a gas stove and methane meets oxygen in a steady blue flame; the gas seems to vanish, yet every atom that enters the flame leaves it again, joined to new partners as carbon dioxide and water. We name the reactants and products and write word equations for burning, rusting and fizzing reactions. We use particle diagrams to show that a reaction only rearranges atoms, and count them on both sides of the arrow. We see that old bonds must break, which needs energy, and new bonds form, which releases energy, giving exothermic and endothermic reactions. Finally we learn the signs that a reaction has happened, and test the law of conservation of mass with a sealed bottle of vinegar and baking soda.
