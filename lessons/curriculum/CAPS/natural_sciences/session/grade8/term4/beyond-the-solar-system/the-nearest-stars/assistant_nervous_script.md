Hey — if the nearest stars lesson felt confusing, here is the calm version: the Sun is a star, and the next nearest stars are much, much further away.

The Sun is the nearest star. It is a ball of hot gas making its own light, about 150 million kilometres away, or about eight light minutes.

The next nearest stars are in the Alpha Centauri system, next to the Southern Cross. It looks like one star but it is three. The closest of the three, Proxima Centauri, is about 4.24 light years away.

A light year is a distance: how far light travels in one year.

If the Sun were a soccer ball in Johannesburg, the nearest star would be another soccer ball about as far away as Cairo.

Star colour tells temperature: red is coolest, blue-white is hottest. A star can look bright because it is powerful or because it is close.

Never look straight at the Sun.
