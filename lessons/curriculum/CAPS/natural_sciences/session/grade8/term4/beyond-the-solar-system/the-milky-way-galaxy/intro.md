Today we travel beyond the Solar System to the vast system of stars we live in: the Milky Way Galaxy. On a dark winter night in the Karoo, a faint, glowing band stretches across the sky; the |xam San told that a girl threw ashes and embers into the sky to light the way home. We learn that a galaxy is a huge collection of stars, gas and dust held together by gravity, and that the Milky Way is a spiral galaxy of hundreds of billions of stars, about one hundred thousand light years across. We find out where the Sun sits, about twenty-six thousand light years from the centre, and why living inside the flat disc makes the galaxy look like a band across our sky. Finally we see that the whole galaxy turns, carrying the Sun around once every two hundred and thirty million years.
