Hey — if light years felt confusing, here is the calm version: we measure huge distances by how long light takes to travel them.

Light travels about 300 000 kilometres every second. Nothing is faster.

Distance equals speed times time.

A light minute is how far light goes in one minute: about 18 million kilometres. The Sun is about 8 light minutes away.

A light hour is about 1 080 million kilometres. Neptune is about 4 light hours from the Sun.

A light year is how far light goes in one year: about 9.5 trillion kilometres. The nearest star after the Sun is about 4.24 light years away.

A light year is a distance, not a time, even though it says year.

When you convert, always change minutes or hours into seconds first, then multiply by 300 000.
