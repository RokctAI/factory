Today we meet the Sun's family. The ring of hills around Parys in the Free State, the Vredefort Dome, is the scar of an asteroid that struck the Earth two billion years ago, a leftover fragment from the birth of the Solar System. We name the eight planets in order and compare the four small rocky inner planets with the four giant outer planets, with a key feature of each. We explore moons, from our own to volcanic Io and ocean-hiding Europa. We find the asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter, define a dwarf planet and see why Pluto was reclassified in 2006. Finally we travel out to the icy Kuiper Belt and the distant Oort Cloud, home of the comets, and explain meteors, meteoroids and meteorites.
