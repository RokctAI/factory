Today we study the Sun as a star. On a summer beach in Durban the sand burns your feet, and all that heat and light has crossed 150 million kilometres of space in about eight minutes, after a journey of many thousands of years out of the Sun's core. We see how big the Sun is, what it is made of, and why its glowing gas is called plasma. We explain nuclear fusion, the squeezing of hydrogen nuclei into helium at millions of degrees, and how a tiny loss of mass becomes an enormous amount of energy. We follow that energy outward through the Sun and across space to Earth, where it powers almost all life. Finally we look at sunspots, solar flares, the solar wind and the aurora, and the space weather they cause.
