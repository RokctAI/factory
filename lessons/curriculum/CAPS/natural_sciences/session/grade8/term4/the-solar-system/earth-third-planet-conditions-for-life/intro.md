Today we ask why the Earth, the third planet from the Sun, is the only world known to support life. Venus, just inside our orbit, is almost the same size as the Earth but hotter than a pizza oven under a thick blanket of carbon dioxide. Mars, just outside, is a frozen desert with air too thin to breathe. We learn where the Earth sits, about 150 million kilometres from the Sun, and what the habitable zone is. We see why liquid water is essential, and why the Moon, at the same distance, has none. We explore how the atmosphere keeps the planet warm through the natural greenhouse effect, how the ozone layer blocks harmful ultraviolet rays, and how the Earth's magnetic field shields us from the solar wind. By the end you will be able to list the conditions that make our planet a home.
