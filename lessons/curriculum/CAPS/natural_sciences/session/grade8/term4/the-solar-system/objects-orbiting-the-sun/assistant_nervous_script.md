Hey — if the Solar System felt like too many names, here is the calm version: the Sun and everything its gravity holds in orbit.

Eight planets, in order: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune. My Very Energetic Mother Just Served Us Nachos.

The first four are small and rocky. The last four are giants of gas and ice. Jupiter is the biggest, Saturn has the famous rings.

Moons orbit planets. Earth has one, Mars two, and the giants have dozens.

Between Mars and Jupiter is the asteroid belt, full of rocky leftovers.

A dwarf planet is round but shares its orbit with lots of other objects. Ceres and Pluto are dwarf planets. Pluto stopped being called a planet in 2006.

Beyond Neptune is the icy Kuiper Belt, and much further out the Oort Cloud. Comets come from there. Their tails always point away from the Sun.

A meteor is a streak of light from space dust burning up; a meteorite is a piece that lands.
