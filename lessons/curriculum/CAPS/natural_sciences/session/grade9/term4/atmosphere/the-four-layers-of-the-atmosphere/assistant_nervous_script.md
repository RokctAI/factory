Hey, four layers and one zig-zag pattern. Here is the calm version.

From the ground up:

1. Troposphere (0 to about 12 km)
Temperature DOWN (about 6,5 °C per km).
Heated from the ground.
Weather, clouds, three quarters of the air.

2. Stratosphere (12 to 50 km)
Temperature UP.
Ozone layer absorbs ultraviolet.
Calm air, jets fly low in it.

3. Mesosphere (50 to 85 km)
Temperature DOWN, to about −90 °C, the coldest.
Meteors burn up.

4. Thermosphere (above 85 km)
Temperature UP, over 1 000 °C.
Very thin, so it would not feel hot.
Space station, auroras.

Pattern: down, up, down, up.

Boundaries: tropopause, stratopause, mesopause.

Trap to avoid: pressure and density do NOT zig-zag. They just keep decreasing.

Second trap: the warming is NOT from being closer to the Sun. It is from gases absorbing radiation.

Write the four names in order on your page before every question: T, S, M, T, from the ground up.
