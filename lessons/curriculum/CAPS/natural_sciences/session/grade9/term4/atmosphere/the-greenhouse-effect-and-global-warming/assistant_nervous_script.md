Hey, it is just a blanket that has got too thick. Here is the calm version.

How it works:
1. Sunlight passes through the air.
2. Ground and sea warm up.
3. Warm surface gives off infrared (heat) radiation.
4. Greenhouse gases absorb some infrared and send part back down.
5. Earth stays warm.

Numbers:
Without it: about −18 °C.
With it: about 15 °C.
Difference: 33 °C.

Greenhouse gases: water vapour, carbon dioxide, methane, nitrous oxide (and CFCs).
NOT nitrogen or oxygen.

Enhanced greenhouse effect: people add extra gas.
Carbon dioxide: about 280 to about 420 ppm (burning coal, oil, gas; cutting forests).
Methane: cattle, landfills, coal mines.

Result: global warming, about 1,2 °C so far.
Effects: droughts, floods, heat waves, sea level rise.

Fixes: solar and wind, saving energy, solar geysers, public transport, trees.

Trap to avoid: the ozone hole is NOT the cause of global warming. Different problem.

Easy check: if an answer mentions sunlight being trapped, fix it. Sunlight goes in; infrared is trapped.
