Hey, a star is just a cloud that gravity squeezed until it switched on. Here is the calm version.

Star: huge ball of hot gas that makes its own light.
Planet: only reflects light.

Step 1: Nebula.
A cloud of mostly hydrogen gas and dust.

Step 2: Gravitational collapse.
Gravity pulls a dense patch together.
A shock wave from a supernova can trigger it.

Step 3: Protostar.
Gas falls in, heats up, spins faster.
A disc forms around it (planets come from the disc).
No fusion yet.

Step 4: Fusion switches on.
Core reaches about 10 million °C.
Hydrogen fuses into helium.
Huge energy released.

Step 5: Star is born.
Outward push from fusion balances inward pull of gravity.

Too small? It becomes a brown dwarf, a failed star.

Trap to avoid: stars do NOT burn like fire. Fusion is a nuclear reaction, not burning.

Remember the order with N, G, P, F: nebula, gravity, protostar, fusion.
