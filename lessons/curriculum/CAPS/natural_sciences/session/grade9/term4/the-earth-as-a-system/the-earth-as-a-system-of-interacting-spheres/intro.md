Stand outside on a summer afternoon on the Highveld and you can watch the whole planet working together. The Sun heats the ground and the air, water evaporates from dams and leaves, clouds build up into towering thunderstorms, rain falls onto the soil, rivers carry mud downhill, and plants and animals use the water to live and grow. Scientists describe the Earth as a system made of four great parts, or spheres: the lithosphere of rock and soil, the hydrosphere of water, the atmosphere of air, and the biosphere of living things. Today we find out what each sphere contains, and, more importantly, how they constantly interact, exchanging matter and energy so that a change in one sphere causes changes in the others. We will follow water and carbon as they cycle between the spheres and see how people, as part of the biosphere, are now changing the whole Earth system.
