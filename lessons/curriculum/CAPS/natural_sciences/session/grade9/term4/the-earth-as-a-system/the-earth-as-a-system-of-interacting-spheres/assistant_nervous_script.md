Hey, this lesson is about seeing how the Earth fits together. Here is the calm version.

A system: parts that work together and affect each other.

Four spheres:
Lithosphere: rock and soil, the crust plus the top of the mantle.
Hydrosphere: all water. About 97 per cent is salty ocean water.
Atmosphere: air. About 78 per cent nitrogen, 21 per cent oxygen.
Biosphere: all living things and where they live.

Interactions: one sphere affects another.
Volcano: lithosphere to atmosphere.
Photosynthesis: biosphere and atmosphere.
Rain eroding soil: hydrosphere and lithosphere.

Cycles: water and carbon move round between spheres and are never used up.
The Sun is the main energy source.

People are part of the biosphere and affect all the others, for example mining, pollution and clearing land.

Remember the size: all four spheres are squeezed into a thin outer zone of the planet, which is why they affect each other so much.
