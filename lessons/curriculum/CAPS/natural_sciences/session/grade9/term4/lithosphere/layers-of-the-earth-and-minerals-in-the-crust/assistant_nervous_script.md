Hey, the inside of the Earth is just four layers. Here is the calm version.

Crust: thin rocky skin. Under oceans 5 to 10 km (basalt). Under continents about 35 km on average (granite).
Mantle: hot solid rock that flows very slowly. Down to about 2 900 km.
Outer core: liquid iron and nickel. Makes the magnetic field.
Inner core: solid iron and nickel, very hot but solid because of huge pressure.

Deeper means hotter and more pressure.
We know this from earthquake waves.

Lithosphere = rigid outer shell = continental crust + oceanic crust + rigid top of the mantle. About 100 km thick. Broken into tectonic plates that move a few centimetres a year.

Mineral: natural solid with a fixed chemical makeup and crystal structure.
Rock: a mix of minerals. Ore: a rock worth mining.
Most common elements in the crust: oxygen, then silicon.

Remember: the lithosphere is not just the crust. It is the crust plus the stiff top of the mantle.
