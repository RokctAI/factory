Hey, this lesson is about weighing benefits and costs. Here is the calm version.

What South Africa mines:
Platinum and chrome: Bushveld. Gold: Witwatersrand. Coal: Mpumalanga.
Iron ore and manganese: Northern Cape. Diamonds: Kimberley and the west coast.

Benefits: jobs, export income, taxes, raw materials.

Impacts on land: habitat loss, pits and dumps, subsidence and sinkholes.
Impacts on water: mines use lots of water, and acid mine drainage.
Acid mine drainage: pyrite + air + water make sulfuric acid; it dissolves toxic metals; it can last decades.
Impacts on air: dust from dumps; pollution from coal power stations (Highveld).
Impacts on people: accidents, silicosis from silica dust (and TB), migrant labour, towns losing jobs when mines close.

Reducing impacts: environmental impact assessment, rehabilitation (topsoil back, replant), dust control, treating acid water with lime, recycling.

Remember: mining brings both benefits and harm. Good answers mention both and say how harm can be reduced.
