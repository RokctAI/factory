Today we look at what a healthy diet is made of and why. Food supplies seven kinds of components: carbohydrates and fats for energy, proteins for growth and repair, vitamins and minerals in tiny amounts to keep the body's chemistry working, water, and fibre. Each one has a job, good South African sources, and a disease that appears when it is missing. We will learn the food tests for starch, glucose, protein and fat, calculate the energy in food from a label using 17 kilojoules per gram for carbohydrates and proteins and 37 for fats, build a balanced plate using the South African food guidelines, and look at malnutrition in both directions: children who are stunted or suffer kwashiorkor, and the rise of obesity and diabetes. Grade 10 Life Sciences returns to nutrition and the molecules of life.
