Hey — the digestive system has a lot of parts, but it is really one tube and four helpers. Here is the calm version.

Five processes in order: ingestion, taking food in; digestion, breaking big molecules into small ones, by chewing and churning and by enzymes; absorption, small molecules passing into the blood in the small intestine; assimilation, cells using them; egestion, pushing out undigested food. Egestion is not excretion: excretion removes waste made by cells.

The tube in order: mouth, pharynx, oesophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine, rectum, anus. Peristalsis, waves of muscle, moves food along.

The helpers, which food never passes through: salivary glands make saliva, the liver makes bile, the gall bladder stores bile, the pancreas makes pancreatic juice.

The villi of the small intestine give a huge surface for absorption. The large intestine takes back water.

Health: tooth decay from sugar and bacteria; diarrhoea causes dehydration, so give oral rehydration solution, one litre of water, eight level teaspoons of sugar and half a teaspoon of salt; most ulcers come from a germ; appendicitis needs a hospital.

Open the practice set and follow the tube. You've got this!
