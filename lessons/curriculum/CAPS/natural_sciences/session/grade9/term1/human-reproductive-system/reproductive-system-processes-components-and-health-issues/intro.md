Today we open the human reproductive system as a whole. Every one of us began as two cells, one from each parent, each carrying exactly half the instructions for a person: 23 chromosomes in the sperm, 23 in the egg, 46 in the single cell they make together. We will see why sexual reproduction uses these special half-cells, called gametes, name the main parts of the male and female systems and what each one is for, follow the processes in order from making gametes to the birth of a baby, and look at the health issues that affect the system: infections, infertility and the cancers that screening and vaccination can prevent. The next lessons zoom in on puberty, the organs in detail, the menstrual cycle and pregnancy, and protection. In Grade 10 and 12 Life Sciences this becomes meiosis and human reproduction.
