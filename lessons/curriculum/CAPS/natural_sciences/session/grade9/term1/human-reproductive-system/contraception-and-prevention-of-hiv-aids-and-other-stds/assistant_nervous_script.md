Hey — this lesson is about health and choices, and you can learn it calmly as biology. Here is the short version.

Every contraceptive cuts one link of the chain. Hormone methods, the pill, injection and implant, stop the egg being released. Condoms stop sperm reaching the egg. The copper loop stops implantation. Abstinence stops everything.

Out of 100 couples in a year: nothing, about 85 pregnancies; withdrawal about 20; condoms about 13; pill about 7; injection about 4; implant or loop fewer than 1.

Only abstinence and condoms also protect against infections. So dual protection means a condom plus a reliable method.

HIV is a virus that destroys helper white blood cells. Untreated, after years it leads to AIDS. It passes only through blood, semen, vaginal fluids and breast milk, never by hugging, cups or mosquitoes. A test is the only way to know. ART, taken daily, controls it for life.

Bacterial STDs, syphilis, gonorrhoea, chlamydia, are cured with antibiotics. Viral ones, HIV and herpes, are controlled, not cured. Many have no symptoms.

Remember: know, protect, test, treat.
