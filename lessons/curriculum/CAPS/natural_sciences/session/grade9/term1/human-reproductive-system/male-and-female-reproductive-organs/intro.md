Today we take the male and female reproductive organs one at a time, the way a diagram question does. We will follow a sperm cell from the testis where it is made, through the epididymis, the sperm duct and the urethra, collecting fluid from three glands on the way, and follow an egg cell from the ovary into the oviduct and towards the uterus. Then we compare the two gametes themselves: a sperm about a twentieth of a millimetre long with a head, a middle piece packed with mitochondria and a tail, and an egg about a tenth of a millimetre across, the largest cell in the human body and unable to move on its own. Every organ gets its function, every gamete its adaptations, and we finish with how to label both diagrams for full marks. Grade 12 Life Sciences builds directly on these diagrams.
