Hey — two diagrams full of new names can look like a lot. Here is the calm version, one road at a time.

The sperm's road: testis makes sperm and testosterone, hanging in the scrotum where it is a few degrees cooler. Epididymis stores and matures them. The sperm duct moves them with muscle squeezes. The seminal vesicles and prostate add fluid, and that mix is semen. The urethra carries it out through the penis.

The egg's road: the ovary releases an egg at ovulation. The funnel of the oviduct catches it, and cilia carry it towards the uterus. Fertilisation happens in the oviduct. The uterus, with its thick lining, is where the baby grows. The cervix is the neck; the vagina is the birth canal.

The gametes: the sperm is small, with a head holding 23 chromosomes, a middle piece full of mitochondria and a tail. The egg is big, cannot move, has a food store and 23 chromosomes. Every egg carries X; the sperm carries X or Y, so the sperm decides the sex.

For diagrams: ruled label lines that do not cross and touch the exact part.
