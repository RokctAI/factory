Today we look at how pregnancy and sexually transmitted diseases are prevented, as a matter of biology and of health. Every contraceptive works by stopping one step in the chain you already know: stopping ovulation, stopping sperm from reaching the egg, or stopping implantation. The methods differ greatly in how reliable they are, and only one kind, the condom, also protects against infections. We will then study HIV, the virus that attacks the white blood cells that defend the body, and AIDS, the late stage it leads to without treatment, in a country where about one person in eight lives with HIV. We will see exactly how HIV is and is not passed on, how testing and antiretroviral treatment work, and how syphilis, gonorrhoea, chlamydia, herpes and HPV are prevented and treated. Grade 10 to 12 Life Sciences returns to immunity and to these diseases.
