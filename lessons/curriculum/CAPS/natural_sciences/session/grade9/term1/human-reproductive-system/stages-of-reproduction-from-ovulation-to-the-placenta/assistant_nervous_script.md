Hey — lots of days and hormones in one lesson, I know. Here is the calm version, in order.

The cycle, about 28 days: days 1 to 5, the lining of the uterus is shed, that is the period. Days 6 to 13, oestrogen thickens the lining again. Around day 14, ovulation releases an egg. Days 15 to 28, progesterone keeps the lining thick. No embryo, progesterone falls, the lining breaks down, back to day 1.

Fertilisation happens in the oviduct: 23 chromosomes from the sperm plus 23 from the egg gives a zygote with 46. It divides into a ball of cells on its way down and implants in the uterine lining after about a week.

Pregnancy is about 40 weeks. Embryo for the first eight weeks, then foetus, floating in amniotic fluid. The placenta and umbilical cord bring oxygen and food in and take carbon dioxide and urea out, by diffusion. The two bloods never mix. Alcohol, smoking, drugs and some viruses can still cross.

Birth: contractions, the cervix opens to about 10 cm, the baby is pushed out, then the placenta follows.
