Hey — this topic can feel awkward or confusing at first, and that is completely normal. It is just biology, the same way the heart and lungs are, and here is the calm version.

Gametes are sex cells with half the usual chromosomes: the sperm has 23, the egg has 23, and when they join at fertilisation the new cell, the zygote, has 46 again.

The testes make sperm and testosterone. The prostate and seminal vesicles add fluid to make semen, and the urethra carries it out. The ovaries release usually one egg about every 28 days and make oestrogen and progesterone. The egg is fertilised in the oviduct, not the uterus. The uterus is where the baby grows, the cervix is its neck, and the vagina is the birth canal.

The processes in order: make gametes, transfer sperm, fertilisation, implantation, pregnancy, birth. If no egg implants, the lining breaks down as the period.

Health: bacterial infections can be cured with antibiotics, viral ones like HIV only controlled. The HPV vaccine and Pap smears prevent cervical cancer. Infertility can be the man's or the woman's.
