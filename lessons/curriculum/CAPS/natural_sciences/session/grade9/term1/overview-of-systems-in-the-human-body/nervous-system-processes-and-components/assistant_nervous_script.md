Hey — if neurons, cerebrum and reflex arcs felt like a wiring diagram you could not read, here is the calm version: a marshal, radios and one shortcut.

The marshal is the central nervous system, brain and spinal cord. The radios are the nerves, the peripheral nervous system, carrying electrical pulses. Sensory neurons bring news in from receptors in the eyes, ears, nose, tongue and skin. Motor neurons carry orders out to effectors, the muscles and glands. Sensory in, motor out.

Three brain departments: cerebrum thinks, remembers and chooses movements; cerebellum keeps balance and coordination; medulla runs heartbeat and breathing. The spinal cord relays messages and runs reflexes.

Every pathway is five parts in order: receptor, sensory neuron, central nervous system, motor neuron, effector. For the hot pot: skin receptor, sensory neuron, spinal cord, motor neuron, arm muscle. That is a reflex arc, fast and automatic, no decision, brain told afterwards.

Health: concussion from knocks, paralysis from spinal injury, meningitis in the membranes, stroke from lost blood supply, and alcohol slowing every message so reactions lengthen. Helmets, seat belts, sleep.
