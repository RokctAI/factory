Today we meet the system in charge: the nervous system. The brain and spinal cord form the central nervous system, and the nerves that run from them to every part of the body form the peripheral nervous system. Sense organs detect a stimulus, sensory neurons carry the message to the central nervous system as electrical impulses at up to 120 metres per second, the brain or spinal cord processes it, and motor neurons carry the instruction to a muscle or gland, which responds. We will map the parts of the brain and their jobs, follow a reflex that snatches your hand from a hot pot before you feel the pain, and look at the health of the system: concussion, spinal injury, meningitis, stroke and the effect of alcohol on reaction time. In Grade 12 Life Sciences the nervous system returns as a full topic.
