Today we follow the body's transport system: the blood, the heart that pumps it and the vessels that carry it. About five litres of blood circulate in an adult, and the heart drives them round roughly 100 000 times a day through two linked circuits: the right side pumps blood to the lungs to collect oxygen, the left side pumps it to the rest of the body. We will name the four components of blood and their jobs, trace blood through the four chambers of the heart, compare arteries, veins and capillaries by structure and function, and read a blood pressure of 120 over 80. Then the health issues: high blood pressure, heart attack, stroke, anaemia, and the choices about smoking, diet and exercise that decide them. Grade 10 Life Sciences continues this as transport systems in mammals.
