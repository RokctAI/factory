Hey — the Periodic Table looks crowded, but you only need to read it in a few simple ways. Here is the calm version.

Symbols: capital first letter, small second. Co is cobalt; CO is carbon monoxide. Some are Latin: Fe iron, Na sodium, K potassium, Cu copper, Ag silver, Au gold, Pb lead.

Groups are the 18 columns: same group, similar behaviour. Group 1 alkali metals, Group 17 halogens, Group 18 noble gases. Periods are the 7 rows, changing from metals on the left to non-metals on the right.

The staircase line: metals to the left, shiny, conducting, bendable; non-metals to the right, dull, poor conductors, brittle, many gases; semi-metals like silicon right on the line. Hydrogen is a non-metal.

Counting: atomic number equals protons. In a neutral atom, electrons equal protons. Neutrons equal mass number minus atomic number. Sodium, 11 and 23: protons 11, electrons 11, neutrons 12.

If a question feels confusing, find the element's block first, write down its two numbers, and only then decide what the question is asking for. Most marks come from those two numbers.
