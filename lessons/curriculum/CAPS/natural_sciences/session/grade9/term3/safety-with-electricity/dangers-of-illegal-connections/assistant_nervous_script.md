Hey, this lesson is mostly common sense backed by the science you already know. Here is the calm version.

An illegal connection is electricity taken without permission: a cable hooked onto a line, a wire run from a neighbour, or a bypassed meter.

Why it is dangerous to people:
No breaker, no earth leakage unit, no earth wire, so nothing switches off a fault.
Bare cables on the ground can electrocute anyone, especially children.
Overhead lines can carry 11 000 V or more, and high voltage can jump through air.
Thin overloaded cables overheat and start fires.

Why it is bad for everyone:
Transformers get overloaded and burn out, so the whole area loses power.
Load reduction may switch areas off at peak times.
Paying customers carry the cost.
It is a crime.

What you do: treat every loose cable as live, keep away (ten metres from fallen lines), never climb poles, and report it to Eskom or the municipality.
