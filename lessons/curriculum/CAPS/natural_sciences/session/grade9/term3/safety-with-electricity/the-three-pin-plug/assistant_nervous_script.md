Hey — this lesson is three wires, three colours and three jobs. Here is the calm version.

Live: brown, or red in old wiring. Carries current to the appliance at 230 V. The dangerous one.

Neutral: blue, or black in old wiring. Completes the circuit back to the supply. Close to 0 V.

Earth: green and yellow, or green in old wiring. Safety wire. Connects metal casings to the ground. Carries current only in a fault.

How earth protects you: if the live wire touches a metal casing, current rushes down the low-resistance earth wire to the ground. The breaker trips and the earth leakage unit switches off. The casing does not stay live.

Switches, fuses and breakers go in the live wire, so switching off really disconnects the appliance from 230 V.

The earth pin is the longest: it connects first and disconnects last.

Double-insulated appliances, marked with a square inside a square, need only two pins.

Check plugs for cracks and burn marks, pull by the plug not the cord, and keep them dry.
