Today we look at a danger that affects many South African communities: illegal connections to the mains supply. An illegal connection is any connection made to the electricity network without permission from Eskom or the municipality, such as a cable hooked onto an overhead line, a wire run from a neighbour's house, or a meter that has been bypassed. These connections have no proper insulation, no earth wire, no circuit breaker and no earth leakage unit. They kill people, especially children who touch bare wires lying on the ground, and they start fires in homes and informal settlements. They also overload transformers, which then fail and leave whole areas without power, and the cost of stolen electricity is carried by everyone who pays. We will use what you know about current, short circuits and protective devices to explain exactly why these connections are so dangerous, and what you can do to stay safe and report them.
