Today we open up the 3-pin plug and learn what each wire does. The live wire, coloured brown in modern cables and red in older ones, carries the current to the appliance at 230 volts and is the dangerous one. The neutral wire, coloured blue, or black in older cables, completes the circuit back to the supply and is close to 0 volts. The earth wire, with green and yellow stripes, or plain green in older cables, is the safety wire: it connects the metal casing of an appliance to the ground, so that if a fault makes the casing live, the current rushes safely to earth and the protection switches off, instead of passing through you. The earth pin is the longest, so it connects first and disconnects last. We will also see why switches and fuses always go in the live wire, and why some appliances need only two pins.
