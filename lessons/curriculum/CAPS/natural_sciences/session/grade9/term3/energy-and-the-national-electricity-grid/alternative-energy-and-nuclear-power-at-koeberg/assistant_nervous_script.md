Hey, this lesson is a tour of energy sources, and each has a simple story. Here is the calm version.

Renewable (replaced naturally): sun, wind, water.
Non-renewable (runs out): coal, oil, gas, uranium.

Solar panels: light → electricity, no moving parts. Northern Cape.
Wind turbines: moving air spins blades and a generator. Cape coasts.
Hydroelectric: water from a dam falls through turbines. Orange River.
Problem with sun and wind: they depend on weather and time of day.

Koeberg near Cape Town: Africa's only nuclear power station. Uranium nuclei split (fission) in a chain reaction; control rods keep it steady. Heat → steam → turbine → generator. No carbon dioxide while running, but expensive and makes radioactive waste.

Waste: low-level waste is buried at Vaalputs in the Northern Cape. Spent fuel is cooled in pools at Koeberg, then stored in steel casks.

For each source, learn: renewable or not, the energy transfer, one pro and one con.
