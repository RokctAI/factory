Hey, this lesson is mostly common sense with a little maths. Here is the calm version.

Energy used = power (kW) × time (h). Both matter.
A fridge (0,15 kW for 10 h = 1,5 kWh) can beat a kettle (2 kW for 0,5 h = 1 kWh).

Energy audit: list appliances, work out kWh per day, add up, find the biggest.
In most homes the geyser is the biggest user, then cooking and heating. Lights and electronics are small.

Why? Water needs a lot of energy to heat up. One geyser full of hot water is about 8 kWh.

Solar water heater: black collector under glass, facing north, heats water with sunlight. Saves about 60 to 80 per cent of water-heating energy.
Other options: heat pump, gas, solar panels, insulation, hotbox.

Payback time = cost ÷ savings per month.
Energy labels: A is efficient, G uses the most.

Remember: high power does not always mean high energy. Always ask how long it is on.
