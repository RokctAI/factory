Hey, electricity cost is just three steps. Here is the calm version.

We pay for energy, in kilowatt-hours (kWh). One kWh = one unit on the meter.
1 kWh = a 1 kW appliance running for 1 hour = 3 600 000 J.

Step 1: power in kW (divide watts by 1 000), time in hours (divide minutes by 60).
Step 2: units = kW × hours.
Step 3: cost = units × price per unit.

Kettle: 2 kW × 0,5 h = 1 kWh a day. At R2,50 that is R2,50 a day.
Geyser: 3 kW × 3 h = 9 kWh a day, 270 kWh a month, R675.

Working backwards: units bought = money ÷ price. R200 ÷ R2,50 = 80 units.

Remember: a kilowatt is how fast; a kilowatt-hour is how much. Cost depends on power and time together.

Traps to watch: 100 W is 0,1 kW, and 30 minutes is 0,5 hours, not 0,3 hours.
