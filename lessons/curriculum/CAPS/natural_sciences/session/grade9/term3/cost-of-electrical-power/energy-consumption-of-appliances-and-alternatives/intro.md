Which appliance in your home uses the most electricity? Many people guess the television or the fridge, but in most South African homes with an electric geyser, the answer is the geyser, often by a long way. Today we become energy detectives. We will compare how much energy different appliances use in a day by combining their power ratings with the time they are used, add up a whole household's use, and find out where most of the energy, and most of the money, really goes. Then we look at alternatives that use the Sun's free energy or use electricity more cleverly: solar water heaters on the roof, heat pumps, solar panels, insulation and even the simple hotbox that keeps food cooking without any electricity at all. You will see that a few good choices can save a family hundreds of rands a month and help reduce the demand on the national grid.
