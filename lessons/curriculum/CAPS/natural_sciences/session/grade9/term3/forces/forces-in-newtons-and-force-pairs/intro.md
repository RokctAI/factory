Today we begin Energy and Change with forces. A force is a push, a pull or a twist that one object exerts on another. Forces can start things moving, stop them, speed them up, slow them down, change their direction or change their shape. We measure force in newtons, written N, using a spring balance, also called a newton meter. To get a feel for the unit, a 100 gram apple pulls down on your hand with a force of about 1 newton. We draw forces as arrows, with the length showing the size and the arrowhead showing the direction. Then comes one of the most important ideas in physics: forces always act in pairs. When you push on a wall, the wall pushes back on you just as hard. That is how you walk, how a swimmer moves forward and how a rocket climbs into space. Grade 10 to 12 Physical Sciences builds all of mechanics on these ideas.
