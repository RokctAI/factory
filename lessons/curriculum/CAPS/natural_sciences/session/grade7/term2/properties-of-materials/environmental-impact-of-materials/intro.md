Today we follow the life stories of three ordinary things: a steel can, a plastic bottle and a lump of coal. We see how mining scars land and causes acid mine drainage, how smelting metals and burning coal release carbon dioxide and pollute the air, especially on the Mpumalanga Highveld, and why plastics that never rot break into microplastics that harm turtles, seabirds and our beaches. We weigh up mining's benefits and costs, meet the waste pickers who collect most of South Africa's recycling, and learn to reduce, reuse and recycle, use energy wisely and switch to cleaner sources.
