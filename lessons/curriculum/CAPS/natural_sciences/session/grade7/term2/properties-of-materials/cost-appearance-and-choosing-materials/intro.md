Today we help a family in Mthatha choose a roof for a new rondavel: thatch, corrugated iron or clay tiles. We learn that physical properties decide whether a material can do the job, but cost, availability, colour, texture, durability and safety decide which one is chosen. We see why wiring is copper and not gold, why cable theft is so costly, and why recycled aluminium saves energy; why white roofs keep homes cool, and how colour and texture do real work. We finish by weighing up the three roofs and learning to explain trade-offs and justify a choice.
