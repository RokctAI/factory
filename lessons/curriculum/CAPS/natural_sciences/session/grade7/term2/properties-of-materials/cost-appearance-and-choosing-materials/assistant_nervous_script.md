Hey — if this lesson feels like there is no single right answer, you are right, and that is fine. Here is the calm version.

First check the properties: can the material do the job?

Then ask: can we afford it and get it? How long will it last? How does it look and feel? Is it safe?

Colour and texture can do real work: white roofs stay cool, rough soles grip, smooth tiles wipe clean.

For a choice question, list what is needed, compare the options, and give your reasons.
