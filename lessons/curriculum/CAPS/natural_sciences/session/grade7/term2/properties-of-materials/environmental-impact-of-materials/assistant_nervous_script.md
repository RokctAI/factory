Hey — if this lesson feels heavy, here is the calm version: every material has a life story.

Metals: mining damages land and water, and making them uses coal and releases carbon dioxide. Recycling saves a lot.

Plastics: made from fossil fuels, they do not rot, and they break into microplastics that harm sea life.

Fuels: burning coal dirties the air and heats the planet.

The fixes: reduce first, then reuse, then recycle. Use less energy and more Sun and wind.

Open the practice set and follow one material's story. You've got this!
