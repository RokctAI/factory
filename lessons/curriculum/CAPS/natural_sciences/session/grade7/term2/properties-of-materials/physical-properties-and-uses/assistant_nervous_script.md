Hey — if properties of materials feel like a long list, here is the calm version: what does the job need?

Melting point: will it survive the heat? Iron pots yes, plastic no.

Conductors let electricity or heat through; insulators block it. Cables need both: copper inside, plastic outside.

Strong is not the same as hard: glass is hard but brittle. Density is heavy-for-its-size; things less dense than water float.

Pick the material that matches everything the job needs. Open the practice set and pick a few materials. You've got this!
