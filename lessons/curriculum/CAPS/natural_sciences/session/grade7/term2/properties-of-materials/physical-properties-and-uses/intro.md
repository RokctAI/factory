Today we choose materials like engineers, starting with a cast-iron potjie pot on the coals. We see how melting and boiling points decide what survives heat, from chocolate melting in the mouth to tungsten glowing in a bulb, and why water boils at a lower temperature in Johannesburg. We test conductors and insulators of electricity with a simple circuit and of heat with a metal spoon and a wooden one. Then we add strength, hardness, flexibility, elasticity and density, find out why aeroplanes are aluminium and not steel, and learn to match several properties to every job.
