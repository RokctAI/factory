Today we start the topic of acids and bases with the tongue and with a rule that keeps everyone safe. We learn how taste buds detect sweet, salty, sour and bitter, plus savoury umami, why the tongue map is a myth and why flavour is mostly smell. We connect sour tastes to acids in lemons, vinegar and amasi, and bitter, soapy tastes to bases like soap and baking soda. Then we learn why tasting is never a scientific test, how to store household chemicals safely, the laboratory rules, first aid for splashes, and why bleach must never be mixed with other cleaners.
