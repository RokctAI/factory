Hey — if acids and bases feel confusing, here is the calm version: two opposite teams and a middle.

Acids: sour, turn blue litmus red. Lemon, vinegar, amasi; battery acid is the fierce one.

Bases: bitter and soapy, turn red litmus blue. Baking soda, soap, toothpaste; drain cleaner is the fierce one.

Neutral: neither, like pure water. Litmus stays the same.

Acid plus base makes a salt and water: neutralisation.
