Today we learn the safe way to sort acids, bases and neutrals: colour-changing indicators. We meet litmus, a dye from lichens, and its rules: blue turns red in an acid, red turns blue in a base, and neither changes in a neutral, which is why both colours are needed. We set up a fair, safe testing table with goggles, labels and a clean rod for each substance, record results for lemon juice, vinegar, soapy water, baking soda and sugar water, and learn what litmus cannot tell us. We finish by making a natural indicator from red cabbage and meet the colour tricks of beetroot, flowers and turmeric.
