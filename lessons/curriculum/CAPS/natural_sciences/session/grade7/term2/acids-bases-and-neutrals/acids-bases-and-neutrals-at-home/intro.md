Today we tour a South African kitchen and bathroom to meet the acids, bases and neutral substances we use every day. Acids are the sour team, from gentle citric acid in lemons and lactic acid in amasi to fierce battery acid, and they turn blue litmus red. Bases are the soapy team, from baking soda and toothpaste to caustic drain cleaner, and they turn red litmus blue. Neutral substances like pure water leave litmus unchanged. We finish with neutralisation, the reaction that makes a salt and water and explains antacids, toothpaste and farmers liming acidic soil.
