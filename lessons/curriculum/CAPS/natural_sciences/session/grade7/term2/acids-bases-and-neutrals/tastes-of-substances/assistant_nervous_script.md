Hey — this lesson is mostly about tastes you already know, plus one big safety rule.

Four main tastes: sweet like honey, salty like chips, sour like lemon, bitter like strong coffee. Plus umami, the savoury one.

Sour usually means acid. Bitter and soapy usually means base. Sugar is neutral.

And the golden rule: never taste anything to find out what it is. Keep chemicals in labelled bottles, never in cooldrink bottles, and never mix cleaners.

Open the practice set and take it calmly. You've got this!
