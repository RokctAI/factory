Today we pull mixtures apart. We define a mixture as substances mixed but not chemically joined, each keeping its own properties, and meet solutions, solutes, solvents and the difference between soluble and insoluble. Then we start separating, one difference at a time: hand sorting for big pieces, sieving by size, magnets for iron and steel, and the panning that found gold at Pilgrim's Rest, using density. We finish with filtration, learn the words filtrate and residue, discover what a filter cannot remove, and plan how to separate a bowl of beans, iron filings, sand and salt.
