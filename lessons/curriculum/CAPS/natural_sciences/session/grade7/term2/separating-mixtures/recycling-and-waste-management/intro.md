Today we open a black bin bag from a home in Soshanguve and treat it as what it really is: a mixture. We climb the waste hierarchy, reduce, reuse, recycle, recover, and dispose only as a last resort, and see why sorting at the source keeps materials valuable. At a recycling plant, hand sorting, screens, magnets, eddy currents, air blowers and optical sensors pull the mixture apart, and we learn why cans and glass can be recycled forever but paper only a few times, and how compost is made. We finish with hazardous waste, e-waste and the real costs of dumping and burning rubbish.
