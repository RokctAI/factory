Hey — if waste management sounds like a lot, here is the calm version: rubbish is a mixture, and sorting turns it into a resource.

The ladder: reduce, reuse, recycle, recover, and throw away only as a last resort.

Recycling plants use the same separation tricks we learned: hands, sieves, magnets and air.

Metals and glass recycle forever; paper only a few times. Food scraps make compost.

Batteries, bulbs, medicines and old phones need special drop-off points.

Open the practice set and sort a few items. You've got this!
