Today we separate what filters cannot. At the salt pans of the West Coast, evaporation leaves crystals of salt as the Sun and wind remove the water, and slow crystallisation gives the sugar from KwaZulu-Natal's mills. With a lid on a boiling pot and a laboratory condenser we learn distillation, which keeps the pure liquid and is used for desalination, fuel refining and buchu oil. Then a black felt-tip dot climbs a paper strip and splits into hidden colours in paper chromatography, the method used to check food colourings and catch drug cheats. We finish by matching each mixture to the right method.
