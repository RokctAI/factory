Hey — if three new methods feel like a lot, here is the calm version: what do you want to keep?

Want the solid that is dissolved, like salt? Evaporate the water. The water is lost, the salt stays.

Want the liquid, like pure water? Distil: boil it, then cool the steam so it drips back into liquid.

Want to see the colours in an ink? Chromatography: a dot on a pencil line, water below it, colours climb at different speeds.

Open the practice set and pick the right method for each mixture. You've got this!
