Hey — if separating mixtures sounds tricky, here is the calm version: use one difference at a time.

A mixture is things mixed but not joined, so each part is still itself.

Big pieces: pick them out. Different sizes: sieve. Iron or steel: magnet. Very heavy, like gold: pan it.

Insoluble solid in a liquid: filter. What comes through is the filtrate; what stays is the residue.

Remember: a filter cannot remove dissolved salt or germs.

Open the practice set and plan a separation. You've got this!
