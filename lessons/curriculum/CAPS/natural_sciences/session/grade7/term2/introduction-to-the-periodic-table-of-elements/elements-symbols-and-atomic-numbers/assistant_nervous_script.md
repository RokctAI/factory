Hey — if symbols and numbers feel like a code, here is the calm version: every square has four facts.

The name. The symbol: capital first letter, small second letter. Watch the Latin ones: Na sodium, K potassium, Fe iron, Cu copper, Ag silver, Au gold, Pb lead.

The atomic number: the number of protons, the element's ID. The table goes 1, 2, 3, all the way to 118.

The seat: rows are periods, columns are groups. Group 1 alkali metals, group 17 halogens, group 18 noble gases.

Open the practice set and read a few squares. You've got this!
