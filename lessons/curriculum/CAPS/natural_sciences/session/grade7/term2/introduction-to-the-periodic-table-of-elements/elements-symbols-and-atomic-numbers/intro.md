Today we learn to read every square of the Periodic Table. Each element has a name, some ancient and some honouring places and people, and a symbol with a capital first letter and a small second letter, with a few sneaky ones from Latin like Na for sodium, Fe for iron and Pb for lead. We discover that the atomic number counts the protons in an atom and acts as the element's identity card, which is why no chemist could turn lead into gold, and we learn the first twenty elements. Finally we find each element's seat by period and group and meet the alkali metals, halogens and noble gases.
