Hey — if the Periodic Table looks like a scary wall of boxes, here is the calm version: it is a map.

An element is one kind of atom. Compounds are elements joined.

Mendeleev arranged the elements in 1869, left gaps and predicted the missing ones. When they were found, everyone believed him.

On the map: rows are periods, columns are groups. Left of the staircase is metals, right is non-metals, on the staircase is semi-metals. Hydrogen sits top left but is a non-metal.
