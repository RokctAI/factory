Hey — if these properties blur together, here is the calm version: metals, opposites, and in-betweens.

Metals shine, conduct heat and electricity, bend into sheets and stretch into wires, and ring. Only some are magnetic, and mercury is a liquid.

Non-metals are mostly the opposite: many are gases, and the solids are dull, brittle and do not conduct. Graphite is the surprise: it conducts.

Semi-metals, like silicon, are in between, and that is why they make computer chips.
