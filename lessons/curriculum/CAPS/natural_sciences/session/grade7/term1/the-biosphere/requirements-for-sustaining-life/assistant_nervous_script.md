Hey — if this topic feels like a long list, here is the calm version: a survival backpack with five things in it.

Energy, which nearly always comes from the Sun through plants. Gases: oxygen to release energy, carbon dioxide for plants to make food. Water, for everything a cell does. Soil, to hold plants and give them minerals. And the right temperature, usually between zero and forty-five degrees.

When one thing is short, it holds life back. In a desert that thing is water, and we call it the limiting factor.

Remember the forest and the desert: same Sun, same air, very different water. Open the practice set and try the backpack questions. You've got this!
