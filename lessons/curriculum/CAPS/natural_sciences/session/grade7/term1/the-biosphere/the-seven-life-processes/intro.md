Today we ask what it really means to be alive. We meet the seven life processes through a goat on a hillside: nutrition, respiration, excretion, growth, reproduction, sensitivity and movement. We separate respiration from breathing and excretion from passing faeces, and we watch a mealie plant do all seven slowly, bending towards light and sending roots down. Then we test a car and a fire, which copy a few processes but are not alive, and sort things into living, once living and never living. We finish with a sleeping seed that proves being alive does not mean being busy.
