Today we discover why living things are built the way they are. We define a habitat and an adaptation, and sort adaptations into body structures, inside body workings and behaviours, all inherited rather than chosen. The gemsbok of the Kalahari shows all three as it survives weeks without drinking, and the quiver tree stores water behind a waxy skin. Then we travel to rivers and estuaries, the freezing Drakensberg and the yellowwood forests, and finish with camouflage and the bright colours that warn predators away.
