Today we pack a survival backpack for every living thing. We find five requirements: energy, which almost always starts as sunlight captured by plants; gases, oxygen for respiration and carbon dioxide for photosynthesis; water, which makes up about sixty per cent of your body; soil, which anchors plants and supplies minerals; and a favourable temperature, usually between zero and forty-five degrees. Then we compare the Tsitsikamma forest with the Richtersveld desert to discover the limiting factor, and see how pollution and erosion can empty the backpack.
