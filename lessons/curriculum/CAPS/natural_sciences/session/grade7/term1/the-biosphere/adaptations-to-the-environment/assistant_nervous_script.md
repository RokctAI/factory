Hey — if adaptations feel like a mountain of examples, here is the calm version: every body tells you where it lives.

An adaptation is an inherited feature that helps an organism survive in its habitat. Three kinds: body parts, inside workings and behaviours.

Desert: save water. The gemsbok sweats little, wees little, eats at night. Succulents store water under a waxy coat; spines lose less water.

Water: gills and webbed feet. Cold: fur, burrows, cushion plants. Forest: race for light.

One trap to avoid: animals do not adapt because they want to. Adaptations come from parents and build up over many generations.
