Today we look at the whole Earth through one handful of soil. We meet the lithosphere of rock and soil, the hydrosphere of every drop of water, and the atmosphere, mostly nitrogen with about one fifth oxygen. Then we find the biosphere, not as a fourth layer but as the thin zone where the other three meet and life exists, including dead leaves and the decomposers that recycle them. We follow water, carbon and soil as they move between the spheres, see how overgrazing on a hillside can fill a dam with mud, and finish with the layouts that earn marks for definitions, examples and interactions.
