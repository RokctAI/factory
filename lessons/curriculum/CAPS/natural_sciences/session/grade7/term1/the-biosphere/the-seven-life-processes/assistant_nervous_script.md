Hey — if the life processes feel like a list to cram, here is the calm version: think of a goat.

It eats: nutrition. It releases energy from food in its cells: respiration. It gets rid of waste its body made, like urine and carbon dioxide: excretion. It grows. It has kids: reproduction. It hears a dog: sensitivity. It walks away: movement.

Two traps. Respiration is not breathing; it happens in cells. And dung is not excretion, because the body never made it.

Plants do all seven too, just slowly: they bend to light and turn to the Sun.

A car moves and uses fuel, but it cannot grow, reproduce or live by itself, and it has no cells. So it is not alive.
