Hey — if four spheres sounds like a lot, here is the calm version: rock, water, air and life.

Litho means stone, so the lithosphere is rock and soil. Hydro means water, so the hydrosphere is all the water, including groundwater, ice and clouds. The atmosphere is the air, mostly nitrogen, about one fifth oxygen.

Bio means life. The biosphere is not a fourth layer on top. It is the thin zone where rock, water and air meet and living things are found, and it includes dead leaves and dead animals, because decomposers recycle them.

The spheres keep swapping things: water through the water cycle, gases through plants and animals, soil through weathering and erosion.

If you can sort a handful of soil into its four spheres, you have the whole idea. Open the practice set and try it. You've got this!
