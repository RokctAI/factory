Hey — if pollination feels like a lot of detail, here is the calm version: pollen needs a courier.

Pollination is pollen moving from an anther to a stigma. Same plant: self-pollination. Another plant: cross-pollination, which gives more varied babies.

Wind flowers are plain: no bright petals, no nectar, dangling anthers and feathery stigmas.

Animal flowers advertise: bright petals, scent and nectar, with sticky pollen that rubs onto visitors.

And remember why it matters: bees help produce about a third of our food.
