Today we learn how humans reproduce, starting with puberty, the years when hormones like oestrogen and testosterone change a child's body into one able to reproduce, at different ages for different people. We name the male organs that make sperm and the female organs that release eggs and hold a growing baby, and compare a tiny swimming sperm cell with a large egg. Then we follow the monthly menstrual cycle, see how fertilisation in the oviduct leads to implantation and pregnancy, and learn how the placenta feeds the baby and why alcohol in pregnancy is so harmful.
