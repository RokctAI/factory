Hey — if flower parts feel like a list of strange words, here is the calm version: rings from the outside in.

Sepals: the green jacket that protected the bud. Petals: the advertising.

Stamens are male: a filament holding up an anther, the pollen factory.

The carpel is female: a sticky stigma, the landing pad; a style, the neck; and an ovary, the nursery with ovules inside.

Later, ovules become seeds and the ovary becomes the fruit.

Open the practice set and label a flower in your head. You've got this!
