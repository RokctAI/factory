Today we get straight, factual answers to questions about protecting health. We define contraception and see why abstinence is the only completely effective choice, how condoms, used correctly every time, block both pregnancy and infections, and why other methods need a condom too. We learn what sexually transmitted infections are, how HIV attacks the immune system and can lead to AIDS if untreated, how it is and is not spread, and how testing and antiretroviral treatment let people live long lives. We finish with the consequences of early sexual activity, the age of consent, and how to say no and get help.
