Hey — if fertilisation and seeds feel like a lot of steps, here is the calm version: land, tunnel, join, grow, travel.

Pollen lands on the stigma. It grows a tube, like a tunnel, down to an ovule. A male cell joins the egg cell: that is fertilisation.

Then the ovule becomes a seed and the ovary becomes the fruit. Petals fall off because their job is done.

Seeds travel away from the parent so they are not crowded: by animals, wind, water, or by the plant popping its pods.
