Hey — if this topic feels awkward or confusing, that is okay. Here is the calm version.

Puberty is when hormones change a child's body into an adult body that could reproduce. It happens at different ages for everyone, and that is normal.

Testes make sperm. Ovaries release an egg about once a month. The uterus is where a baby can grow.

Each month the uterus builds a soft lining. If no egg is fertilised, the lining is shed: that is a period. If a sperm joins the egg in the oviduct, that is fertilisation, and the new cells settle in the uterus: pregnancy.

The placenta feeds the baby, so what the mother takes in reaches the baby too.
