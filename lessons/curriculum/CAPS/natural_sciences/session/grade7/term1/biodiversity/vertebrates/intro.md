Today we meet the animals with backbones at a river in Mpumalanga: a yellowfish, a frog, a crocodile, a fish eagle and an impala. We sort the five vertebrate classes, fish, amphibians, reptiles, birds and mammals, using four questions: what covers the body, how the animal breathes, whether it is warm-blooded or cold-blooded, and how it has its young. On the way we hear how a living coelacanth was found off East London, watch a tadpole change into a frog, and catch out the tricky customers: the whale, the bat, the penguin and the crocodile.
