Hey — if classification feels like a lot of names, here is the calm version: five boxes.

Bacteria: tiny single cells with no nucleus. Protista: tiny cells that do have a nucleus, like amoeba. Fungi: mushrooms, mould and yeast, which absorb food and are never green. Plants: green, make their own food. Animals: eat other living things and usually move.

Sort by reliable features, like nucleus or not and how the organism gets food, not by where it lives. That is why a whale is a mammal.

Two traps: mushrooms are not plants, and viruses are not in any box.
