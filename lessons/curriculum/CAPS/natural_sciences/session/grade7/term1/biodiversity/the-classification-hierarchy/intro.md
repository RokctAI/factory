Today we climb down the ladder of classification from Kingdom to Species. Like an address that narrows from country to one house, the seven levels, Kingdom, Phylum or Division, Class, Order, Family, Genus and Species, get smaller and more alike at every step, and King Philip Came Over For Good Soup keeps them in order. We trace the lion all the way down to Panthera leo, learn the two-part scientific names introduced by Carl Linnaeus and why they beat common names in a country with many languages, and play a game to decide how closely related two animals are.
