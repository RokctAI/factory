Today we walk up a damp valley in the Magaliesberg and sort the plant kingdom by how plants reproduce. Mosses and ferns make no seeds at all; they release dust-like spores and need damp places because their sex cells must swim. Seed plants pack a baby plant, a food store and a tough coat into every seed. The gymnosperms, pines, our national yellowwood and the threatened cycads, carry naked seeds on cones, while the angiosperms, the flowering plants, wrap their seeds inside fruits, which to a scientist include tomatoes, bean pods and mealie grains.
