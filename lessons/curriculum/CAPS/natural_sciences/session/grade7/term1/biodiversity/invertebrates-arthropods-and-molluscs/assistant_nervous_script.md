Hey — if invertebrates feel like a crowd of creepy-crawlies, here is the calm version: armour or soft body.

Arthropods wear armour, an exoskeleton, and have jointed legs. Count to classify. Six legs and three body parts: insect. Eight legs and two body parts: arachnid, like a spider. Ten or more legs and gills: crustacean, like a crab.

Molluscs are soft, with a muscly foot and usually a shell. Snail: one shell. Mussel: two. Octopus: none.

The trap: a crab's hard shell does not make it a mollusc. It has jointed legs, so it is an arthropod.
