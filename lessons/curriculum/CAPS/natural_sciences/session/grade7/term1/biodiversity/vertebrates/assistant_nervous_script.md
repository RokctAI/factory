Hey — if five classes of vertebrates feel like a lot, here is the calm version: four questions.

What covers it? Scales and slime: fish. Wet skin: amphibian. Dry scales: reptile. Feathers: bird. Fur: mammal.

How does it breathe? Gills or lungs.

Warm-blooded or cold-blooded? Only birds and mammals make their own heat.

How does it have babies? Jelly eggs in water, shelled eggs on land, or live babies with milk.

And never classify by where it lives. A whale is a mammal; a bat is a mammal; a penguin is a bird.

Open the practice set and run the four questions on a few animals. You've got this!
