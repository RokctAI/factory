Hey — if plant groups feel confusing, here is the calm version: spores or seeds, then seeds with or without a wrapper.

Mosses and ferns make spores, tiny specks with no food. They need damp places because their sex cells swim.

Seed plants make seeds: a baby plant, a food store and a coat, like a packed lunchbox.

Gymnosperms keep their seeds naked on cones and have no flowers: pines, yellowwoods, cycads.

Angiosperms have flowers, and their seeds grow inside fruits. A fruit is anything from a flower holding seeds, even a bean pod.
