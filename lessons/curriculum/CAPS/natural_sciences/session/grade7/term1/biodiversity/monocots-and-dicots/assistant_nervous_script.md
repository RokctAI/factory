Hey — if monocots and dicots feel like a long table, here is the calm version: one or two, then five quick checks.

Monocot: one seed leaf, like a mealie. Dicot: two, like a bean that splits in half.

Leaf veins: side by side like train tracks for monocots, a branching net for dicots.

Roots: a beard of thin roots for monocots, one main tap root for dicots.

Flowers: threes for monocots, fours or fives for dicots.

Use several checks together and you will be right. Open the practice set and sort a few plants from the garden. You've got this!
