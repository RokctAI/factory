Today our Grade 7 class becomes the experiment. We learn that genes on forty-six chromosomes come half from each parent, shuffled into a new mix for every child, and survey inherited features such as height, eye colour, hair type, blood group, tongue rolling and earlobes. We plan a fair survey with a clear question, careful measurement against a wall and respect for everyone's privacy. Then we turn the data into the right picture, a histogram with touching bars for height and separate bars for categories, calculate a range and a percentage, and write a conclusion that stays within the evidence.
