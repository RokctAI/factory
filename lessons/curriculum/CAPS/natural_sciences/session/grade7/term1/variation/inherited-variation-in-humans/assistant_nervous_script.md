Hey — if this topic mixes biology and maths, here is the calm version: genes, then a fair survey, then the right graph.

You get half your genes from each parent, shuffled differently for each child. Features like blood group, eye colour and tongue rolling are inherited.

To survey your class: ask a clear question, measure everyone the same way, get permission and use numbers instead of names.

Height changes smoothly, so its bars touch. Groups like can or cannot roll your tongue get separate bars.

And percentages are simple: 21 out of 30 is 21 divided by 30, which is 70 per cent.
