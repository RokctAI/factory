Hey — if variation feels abstract, here is the calm version: same kind, never identical.

A species is a group that can have babies that can also have babies. A mule cannot, so horses and donkeys are different species.

Variation means differences within one species. Smooth differences like height are continuous and make a hill-shaped graph. Separate groups like blood groups are discontinuous and make separate bars.

Differences come from genes, half from each parent, and from life: food, sun, exercise.
