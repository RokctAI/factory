Today a learner in Bloemfontein drops a stone and a feather and watches astronauts bounce on the Moon, and we explore gravity, the invisible pull between all objects with mass. We learn that gravity grows stronger with more mass and weaker with more distance, and we separate mass, which never changes, from weight, which does. Then we use a stopper on a string to show how gravity and sideways motion combine to hold the Moon in orbit around the Earth and the Earth in orbit around the Sun.
