Today we visit the coalfields of Mpumalanga and the gas fields off Mossel Bay to discover where fossil fuels come from. We follow ancient swamp plants as they become peat and then coal under heat and pressure over millions of years, and tiny sea organisms as they become oil and gas, rising through rock until trapped. We learn why coal, oil and gas are called stored solar energy, why they are non-renewable resources, and why burning them causes pollution and climate change.
