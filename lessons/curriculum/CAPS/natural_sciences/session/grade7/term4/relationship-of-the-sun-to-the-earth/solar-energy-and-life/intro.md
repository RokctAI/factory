Today we stand in a Free State mealie field to follow the Sun's energy into living things. We learn what the Sun is and how its light and heat warm the Earth, drive rain and winds, and make life possible. We see how green plants use chlorophyll to capture sunlight in photosynthesis, storing it as chemical energy in grains, stems and roots, and then trace that energy along food chains and webs to cattle, farmers and hawks, discovering why only a tenth passes on at each step.
