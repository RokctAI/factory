Today a busy hair salon full of hairdryers, kettles, lights and fans helps us sort energy outputs into useful energy, the form we want, and wasted energy, usually heat and sound escaping into the surroundings. We see that the same form can be useful in one device and wasted in another, and use the conservation law to show why useful output is always less than input. Then we explore why petrol engines waste so much, how LED bulbs and good habits cut waste, and why standby chargers still use power.
