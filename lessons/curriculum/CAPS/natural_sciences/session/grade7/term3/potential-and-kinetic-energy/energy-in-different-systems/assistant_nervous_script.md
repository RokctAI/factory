Hey — if systems sound complicated, here is the calm version: energy in, energy changed, energy out.

Machines: height, stretch and movement swap around, and a little leaks as heat.

Heating: particles move faster.

Electricity: batteries or power stations make electrical energy that becomes light, heat or movement.

Living things: plants store sunlight; animals turn food into movement and warmth.
