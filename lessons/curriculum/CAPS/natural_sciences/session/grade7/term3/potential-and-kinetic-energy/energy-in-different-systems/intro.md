Today we spend a morning on a sugar-cane farm to find stored and moving energy in four kinds of system. In mechanical systems, a bicycle trades speed for height on a hill, a wind-up toy releases spring energy, and a swing slowly stops as friction leaks energy away. In thermal systems, heating makes particles move faster, and in electrical systems, batteries and a coal power station's chain of changes light our homes. In biological systems, plants store sunlight as chemical energy and animals, including the cane cutter, turn it into movement and warmth, with energy thinning out along food chains.
