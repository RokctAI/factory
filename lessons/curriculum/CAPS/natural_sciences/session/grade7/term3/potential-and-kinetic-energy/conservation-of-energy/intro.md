Today we ride a roller coaster to meet the biggest rule about energy: it cannot be created or destroyed, only changed in form or passed on. We follow height and speed swapping on the hills, find out why each hill must be lower, and see that "used up" energy has really spread out as heat. Then we trace energy inside a phone and a wind-up radio with energy-flow diagrams, follow a long chain from the Sun to a moving taxi, and discover why nobody can ever build a machine that runs forever.
