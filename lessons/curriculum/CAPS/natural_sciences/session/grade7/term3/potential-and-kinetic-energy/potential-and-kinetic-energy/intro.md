Today we watch a boy on a swing to meet the two big families of energy. Potential energy is stored: gravitational in a raised rock or a full dam, elastic in a stretched catapult, and chemical in fuels, batteries and food. We read food labels in kilojoules and learn why fats are so energy-dense. Kinetic energy is the energy of movement, and it grows with speed and mass, so doubling a car's speed gives it about four times the energy, which is why speeding kills. We finish by following energy back and forth between potential and kinetic on the swing.
