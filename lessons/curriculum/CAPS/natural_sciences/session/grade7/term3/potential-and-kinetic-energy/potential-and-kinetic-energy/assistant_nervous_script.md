Hey — if potential and kinetic sound like big words, here is the calm version: stored and moving.

Potential energy is stored: high up, stretched, or in chemicals like food and batteries.

Food energy is in kilojoules on the label. Fatty foods have the most.

Kinetic energy is movement. Faster or heavier means more of it.

On a swing: at the top, most stored energy; at the bottom, most movement energy.
