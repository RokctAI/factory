Today we park a black car and a white car in the Upington sun to explore the third way heat travels, radiation. Radiant heat moves as invisible waves in straight lines and needs no particles at all, which is how the Sun's energy crosses empty space to reach Earth. We compare radiation with conduction and convection, test painted and foil-covered cans to show that dark, dull surfaces absorb and emit well while shiny surfaces reflect, and then put the ideas to work in solar water heaters, solar cookers and shiny survival blankets.
