Today we cook a potjie to find out what heating really is and how heat travels through solid things. Heat is energy that always flows from hotter to colder, until both reach the same temperature, and cold never flows at all. We watch heat creep up a metal spoon by conduction, particle bumping particle, and see why metals, with their free electrons, are the best conductors. Then we sort conductors from insulators, discover why trapped air is such a good insulator, and learn why a metal bench feels colder than a wooden one at the same temperature.
