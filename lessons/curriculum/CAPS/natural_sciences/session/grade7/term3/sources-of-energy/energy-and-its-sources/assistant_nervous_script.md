Hey — if energy feels like a big abstract word, here is the calm version: energy makes things happen.

We measure it in joules. It changes form: gas to heat, battery to light, food to movement.

Non-renewable sources will run out: coal, oil, gas and uranium. Fossil fuels also make smoke and carbon dioxide.

Renewable sources come back: sunlight, wind, flowing water and biofuel. They are cleaner, but they depend on the weather.
