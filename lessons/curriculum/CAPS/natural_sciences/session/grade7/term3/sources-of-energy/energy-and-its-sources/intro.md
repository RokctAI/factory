Today we start the Energy and Change strand on a load-shedding evening, when a family switches from coal-powered electricity to a gas cooker, batteries and a small solar light. We define energy as what makes things work, move, live, heat up and light up, measure it in joules and kilojoules, and watch it change from one form to another. Then we sort energy sources: non-renewable coal, oil, gas and uranium, which took millions of years to form or must be mined and will run out, and renewable sunlight, wind, water and biofuel, which nature refills, comparing their advantages and disadvantages for South Africa.
