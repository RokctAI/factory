Today a family packs for a cold winter rugby match, and we use their cool box, flask and baby's beanie to learn how heat is lost and gained by conduction, convection and radiation, and why wind makes a cold day feel colder. We discover that the best insulators trap still air, take apart a cool box and a vacuum flask to see how each slows heat flow in both directions, and look at ceiling insulation, layers of clothing, blankets, and the fluffed-up feathers of birds on a frosty morning.
