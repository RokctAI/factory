Today we compare a thick-walled, thatched rondavel in the Eastern Cape with a tin-roofed block house next door to see how buildings gain and lose heat through roofs, walls, windows and gaps. We learn practical ways to conserve energy, from ceiling insulation and sealed gaps to thick walls with thermal mass and north-facing windows shaded by overhangs. Then we explore the wisdom of indigenous South African homes, including rondavels, beehive huts, Karoo corbelled stone houses and Nama reed matjieshuise, which stay comfortable using only local materials.
