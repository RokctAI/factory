Today we are mastering the two drawings the fluvial section keeps returning to: a river seen from the side, and a river seen from above. From the side it becomes the longitudinal profile, with its graded curve, its base levels and its interruptions; from above it becomes the meander, the loop that tightens until the river amputates it and leaves an oxbow lake behind. We will work through the machinery that drives a meander's growth, tell the full oxbow story the way an eight-line paragraph question demands it, and finish with the identification skills that turn sketches, photographs and contour patterns into marks.
