Today we are working through the two dramas that dominate Grade twelve fluvial geomorphology: a river that gets its youth back, and a river that steals another river's water. Rejuvenation and river capture carry the theory marks in this section, and both are stories about energy — a river's energy budget suddenly rewritten. We will cover rejuvenation's causes and its four signature features, then river capture's mechanism and its five-piece vocabulary, then what both processes mean for people, and finally the identification skills that let you read these landforms off long profiles, cross-sections and the topographic map.
