Today we are covering the urban half of settlement geography, in a world where more than half of humanity — and about two thirds of South Africans — now lives in urban settlements. We will move through four blocks: how urban settlement began and how the world urbanised, the precise vocabulary of urban growth that examiners test as definitions, site, situation and the functional classification of towns from central places to gap towns, and finally the internal anatomy of a city — the urban hierarchy and the land-use zones. Every zone is described in words, as a journey you can hear, because that is exactly how the exam will describe them to you.
