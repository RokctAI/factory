Today we are completing the fluvial section with the management half of the syllabus, because South Africa is the thirtieth driest country on Earth and almost every drop its cities drink falls first on a catchment. We will cover the two special drainage patterns that ignore the rock beneath them — superimposed and antecedent drainage — then why drainage basins and catchment areas matter and why they are so often poorly managed. From there we work through the four great human impacts on our rivers, with eutrophication at the head of the list, and close with management strategies and the required South African case study of a catchment management strategy in action.
