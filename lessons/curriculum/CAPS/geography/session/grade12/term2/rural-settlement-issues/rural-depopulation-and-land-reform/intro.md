Today we are tackling the rural settlement question with the highest mark weighting — not shapes or patterns, but people leaving. We will work through the machinery of rural-urban migration: the push and pull factors that drive it, rural depopulation with its causes, consequences and counter-strategies, the social justice issues that keep rural poverty in place, and the three-legged land reform programme of tenure, redistribution and restitution that the examiners test every single year. The running example is the one South Africa knows best: the emptying villages of the Eastern Cape and the young workers they send to Gauteng and Cape Town.
