Today we take a practice run through the theory sections of both Geography papers at examination pace: the sixty-mark question architecture, the fifteen-mark objective opener, and one full walked subquestion each from climate, geomorphology, settlement and economic geography. Every question comes with its mark allocation named, because the allocation is the instruction — two marks means one developed fact, and the hidden eight-mark paragraph is always four ideas at two marks each in full sentences. Answer each question in your head before the walkthrough gives it away, and by the end the papers stop being a mystery and become a rubric you have already met.
