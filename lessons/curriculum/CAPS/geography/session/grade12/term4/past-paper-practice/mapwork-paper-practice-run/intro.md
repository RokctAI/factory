Today we are running a real national examination at examination pace: the mapwork paper of November 2018, set on the one to fifty thousand topographic map of Pietermaritzburg and its orthophoto extract. Every question is walked with its mark allocation named, and the centrepiece calculations — the average gradient from contour to trig station and the vertical exaggeration of a cross-section — are worked exactly as the marking guidelines rewarded them. Since no map sheet is in front of you, we build each map moment on the board as it comes, so keep the terrain assembling in your mind. The habit this session drills is the one that banks Section B: formula before numbers, working before answers, units always.
