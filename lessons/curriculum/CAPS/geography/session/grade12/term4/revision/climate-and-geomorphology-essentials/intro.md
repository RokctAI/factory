Today we compress a year of Climate and Weather and Geomorphology into the essentials the examiners return to every November — the two theory pillars of Paper 1, worth sixty marks each. We line up the three weather systems and the three anticyclones, walk a valley through a winter night, then follow drainage basins, patterns, profiles, fluvial landforms, rejuvenation and river capture through to catchment management. The method is deliberate: core mechanism first, exam vocabulary second, then the question shapes the DBE actually sets. Keep a pen moving while you listen, because revision that only listens is revision half done.
