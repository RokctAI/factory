Today we build the conversion every mapwork calculation stands on: turning a distance measured on paper into a distance on the real ground. Scale and distance opens the calculation section of the matric mapwork question, and the memorandum pays line by line — the formula, the multiplication, the unit ladder, and the unit itself. We will read what one to fifty thousand actually means, work the standard problem of 8,6 centimetres into 4,3 kilometres in full, extend the method to winding roads, orthophoto scales and the reverse calculation, and finish with the sense checks that catch a slipped decimal before the marker does. Master this one conversion and gradient, cross-sections and area all stand on ground you have already made solid.
