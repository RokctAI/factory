Today we master two mapwork calculations that sit beside gradient and scale in every matric paper: magnetic declination, which corrects the compass against the map, and area, which turns two measured distances into square units. Each carries three to four marks, each follows a fixed recipe, and each punishes one specific careless step — the minutes that must roll over into degrees, and the centimetres that must be converted before they are multiplied. We will work both calculations in the exact form examiners print them: updating an eleven-year-old declination note to the current year, and turning a measured block on the orthophoto into square metres. Learn the recipes and these become the steadiest marks on your mapwork paper.
