Today we are taking apart the most violent weather system on Earth: the tropical cyclone, the storm that flattened Beira as Idai and crossed the Mozambique Channel twice as Freddy. We will work through it exactly as the examiners do — the four birth conditions, the staged growth from disturbance to full cyclone, the anatomy of eye, eye wall and rain bands with the weather sequence they deliver, and the case study, satellite identification and management strategies that close the question. Hold one idea underneath everything: a tropical cyclone is a machine that converts warm ocean water into wind, and every fact in this session follows from that single conversion.
