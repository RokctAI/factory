Today we build the mid-latitude cyclone from the ground up — the great winter rain-maker of the Western Cape and the most heavily examined weather system in the matric paper, whether the question calls it a frontal depression, extra-tropical cyclone or temperate cyclone. We will establish where and why these storms are born on the polar front, walk the three-stage life cycle from wave to occlusion, follow the exact weather sequence a cold front drags across a town, and finish with the synoptic-chart and satellite skills that turn all of it into marks. Keep one picture in mind throughout: two enormous air masses, warm and cold, fighting along a boundary south of the country, and a wave on that boundary growing into a spinning storm.
