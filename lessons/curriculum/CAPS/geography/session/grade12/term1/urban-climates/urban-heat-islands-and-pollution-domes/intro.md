Today we are studying how a city manufactures its own climate: Johannesburg is measurably warmer than the farmland around it, rains a little harder on its downwind side, and wears a lid of dirty air on winter mornings. We will work through urban climates exactly as the examiners frame them — why urban and rural climates differ element by element, the urban heat island with its definition, causes, effects and reduction strategies, and the pollution dome and plume with theirs. The command words to train for are define, account for and suggest strategies, and every section of this session builds toward those three moves.
