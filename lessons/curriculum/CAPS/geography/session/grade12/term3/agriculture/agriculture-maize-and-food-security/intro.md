Today we are working through agriculture, the maize case study and food security — a sector that earns barely three per cent of GDP yet carries three of term three's most reliable question machines. We will argue both sides of the small-percentage paradox, compare the two farming worlds that share one country, walk maize from the Highveld triangle to the export terminal, and take the food security definition apart clause by clause. Hold one constraint in mind throughout: only about twelve per cent of South Africa's land is arable, and that single fact explains half of everything that follows. Master this session and the agriculture question becomes marks you collect, not marks you fight for.
