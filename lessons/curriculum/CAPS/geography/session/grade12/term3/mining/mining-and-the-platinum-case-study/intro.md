Today we are covering mining and the prescribed platinum case study — the industry modern South Africa was literally assembled around, from diamonds at Kimberley to gold on the Witwatersrand to the world's richest platinum deposits in the Bushveld. We will justify the claim that mining is the foundation of the economy, walk the Bushveld Igneous Complex with its three limbs and famous reefs, weigh platinum's contribution against its hindering factors, and finish with the mapwork skills that let you spot a mine on any topographic sheet. This is a topic where Paper 1 theory and Paper 2 mapwork pay you twice for the same knowledge, so every diagram we build today earns marks in two places.
