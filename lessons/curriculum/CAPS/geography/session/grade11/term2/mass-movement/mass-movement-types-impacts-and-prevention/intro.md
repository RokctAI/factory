Today we study mass movement — rock, soil and debris moving downslope under gravity alone, from creep too slow to watch to mud flows at highway speed. We begin with the tug-of-war between driving and resisting forces and water's double role in it, then classify the six types by speed and water content so any exam photograph sorts itself instantly. The KwaZulu-Natal floods of April 2022 are our case study — the numbers, and the vulnerability that turned a natural process into a national catastrophe — before we close with the four families of prevention, from gabions and drains to GIS hazard maps and early warning. This topic carries both essay and mapwork marks, and it teaches you to read a real hillside before the rain comes.
