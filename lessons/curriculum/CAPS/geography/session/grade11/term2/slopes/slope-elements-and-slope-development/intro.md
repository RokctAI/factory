Today we study the slope itself as the unit of landscape — the profile behind every mesa, cuesta and dome this term has described. We set the stage with South Africa's three-part build of plateau, Great Escarpment and marginal zone, then learn the examination's favourite diagram: the four slope elements from convex crest through cliff and straight talus to concave pediment, each with its shape and its numbers. Slope retreat then sets the profile walking backwards to build the Karoo's pediplain, and we close with the GIS toolkit — resolution, raster and vector — that turns elevation data into slope maps at the press of a button. Master the four-part profile and its motion and half the term's landforms explain themselves.
