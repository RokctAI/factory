Today we are looking at South Africa's conventional energy sources — the coal that generates roughly eighty percent of our electricity, the small hydro contribution a dry country can manage, and nuclear power as the case study for whether conventional sources can carry our future. Energy questions are a fixture of the resources and sustainability section, and examiners want processes explained in order and both columns of every argument weighed. We will map the energy mix, walk the full energy chain inside a coal-fired power station, balance coal's advantages against its heavy environmental bills, and end at the fork in the road where Koeberg and the idea of an energy mix wait. Master this and the whole energy debate becomes marks you can argue with confidence.
