Today we close the energy topic where the syllabus points it: management — how South Africa steers its energy use toward a greener economy and sustainable lifestyles. This is a favourite for evaluation questions, because it assigns responsibilities by name to government, business and individuals, and examiners reward answers that give each role its specific tools. We will build the case for managing energy at all, then work through government's laws and plans, business's audits and rooftop solar, and the household choices that add up to the evening peak. The thread running through it all is the topic's master idea: the cheapest, cleanest kilowatt-hour is the one never used.
