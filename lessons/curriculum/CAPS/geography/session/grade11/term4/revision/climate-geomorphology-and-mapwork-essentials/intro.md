Today we revise the physical geography of the whole Grade 11 year: the atmosphere's energy engine and global circulation, Africa's climate with its droughts and desertification, and the geomorphology of horizontal, inclined and massive rock, down to the slope elements and mass movement. These topics carry the physical paper, and the examiners test them through diagrams and the 1:50 000 map sheet, so every landform we revise is also a contour pattern you must recognise at a glance. We will rebuild the circulation model in order — heating, pressure, cells, deflection — then read rock arrangement into landform families, walk the four slope elements, and close on the map where the paper opens. Command these essentials and the physical questions become revision, not surprises.
