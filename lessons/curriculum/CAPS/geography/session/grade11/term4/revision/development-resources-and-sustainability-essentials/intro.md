Today we revise the human geography of the Grade 11 year: what development means and how it is measured, the trade and aid rules that decide who accumulates, the conventional and non-conventional energy powering South Africa, and the soil on which everything ultimately stands. These topics feed the essay questions where examiners reward candidates who can define sustainability word for word and then apply it to money, power stations and topsoil alike. We will work the indicator toolkit from GDP to HDI, argue trade and aid both ways, weigh coal against sun and wind, climb down the erosion ladder from splash to donga, and close by walking the circle that joins the whole year. Hold that circle and the human paper has nothing left to surprise you with.
