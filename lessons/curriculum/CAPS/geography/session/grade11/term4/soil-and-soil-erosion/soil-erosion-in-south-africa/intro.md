Today we open the resources theme with the resource everything else stands on: soil, and how South Africa is losing it. Soil erosion is a reliable exam topic because it links physical process, human history and management — the causes sorted into human, animal and physical families, the evidence written in dongas and silted dams, and the remedies from contour ploughing to gabions. We will follow the water-erosion sequence from raindrop splash to gully, weigh the effects on people and environment, and finish with the GIS toolkit the syllabus attaches to this topic. Hold onto one asymmetry throughout: centuries to build a few centimetres of topsoil, one storm to strip it.
