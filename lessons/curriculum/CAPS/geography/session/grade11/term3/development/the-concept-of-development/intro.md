Today we build the foundation for the whole development term: what development actually means, and why it is never just money. We separate development from economic growth, standard of living from quality of life, and learn the measuring tools examiners demand — GDP and GNP, per capita corrections, the Gini coefficient that exposes how the cake is sliced, and the Human Development Index with its three subjects of health, education and income. We finish by replacing the old rich-North-poor-South boxes with a continuum, and placing South Africa on it honestly with evidence on both sides. These definitions and indicators carry direct marks in every development question this term, so this is the session to get word-perfect.
