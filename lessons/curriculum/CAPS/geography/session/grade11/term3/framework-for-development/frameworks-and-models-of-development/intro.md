Today we move from measuring development to explaining it, with the frameworks and models every essay in this section leans on. We start with the five aspects a full answer must touch — economic, social, sustainable, appropriate-scale and spatial — including the Brundtland definition worth quoting word for word. Then the two great models: core-periphery, the magnet that explains the map at global, national and local scale, and Rostow's five-stage staircase that claims to explain the timeline. Both earn marks twice — once described, once criticised — and South Africa, where apartheid engineered core and periphery by law, is the sharpest test case for each. Learn to run both models over real ground and evaluation marks stop being a mystery.
