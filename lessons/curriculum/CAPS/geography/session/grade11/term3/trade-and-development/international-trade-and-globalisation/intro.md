Today we are working through international trade and globalisation — who sells what to whom, and why the rules of that exchange decide which countries accumulate wealth and which stay stuck supplying raw materials. This topic anchors the trade and development section of your Paper 1 essay questions, where examiners reward learners who can argue both columns: the gains globalisation delivers and the costs it imposes. We will define exports, imports and the terms of trade, unpack trading relationships from free trade to fair trade, weigh globalisation's impact, and finish with a critical examination of the export-led path the Asian Tigers climbed. By the end you will be able to argue South Africa's position on that ladder with evidence.
