Today we are examining what happens when the rain fails for years: drought, defined properly against the local normal and graded through its four deepening stages, and desertification, the man-made land degradation that drought exposes. We trace the causal chain from overgrazing to the runaway erosion spiral, count the costs from failed harvests to drought migration, and study Cape Town's Day Zero crisis with the numbers examiners expect. The session closes with the three families of management — early warning, water discipline and land restoration — carrying named programmes like Working for Water and the Great Green Wall, so every drought question meets a full toolkit.
