Today we are classifying population movements and explaining migration — the vocabulary that separates a daily commuter from a true migrant, and the push-and-pull machinery behind every decision to move. We build the three sorting axes examiners test — where, why, and how long — and then apply them to two case studies CAPS treats as essential: the internal stream from the rural Eastern Cape to Gauteng, and the international stream into South Africa, above all from Zimbabwe. By the end you will be able to argue consequences for both the place left behind and the place arrived at, which is exactly the essay structure the marks reward.
