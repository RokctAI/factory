Today we are turning a whole country into a handful of numbers and one picture: the population indicators — birth rate, death rate, fertility, infant mortality and life expectancy — and the population pyramid that shows a nation's age and sex structure at a glance. You will learn each definition precisely, the traps hidden inside the rates, and the number 2,1 that decides whether a population grows or shrinks. Then we build and read the pyramid itself — base, slope, notches and bulges — and match the three classic shapes to real countries before reading South Africa's own transitional pyramid. Pyramid interpretation is one of the most reliably examined skills in this topic, so every habit here converts directly into marks.
