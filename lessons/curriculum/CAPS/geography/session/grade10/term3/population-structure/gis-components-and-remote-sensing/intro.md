Today we are opening the toolbox behind every population map you have seen this term: the Geographical Information System that stores and analyses georeferenced data, and remote sensing that collects so much of it from satellites and aircraft. We define a GIS properly, work through its five components and the layer principle that makes overlay analysis possible, then distinguish satellite images from aerial photographs and weigh their strengths and limits. The session closes with South African applications examiners love — tracking informal settlements, planning the census, and reading flood damage from space — so the definitions become tools you can actually apply.
