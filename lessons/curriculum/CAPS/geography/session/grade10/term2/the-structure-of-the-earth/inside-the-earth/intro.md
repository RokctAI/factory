Today we are going inside the earth — crust, mantle and core, the two discontinuities between them, and the two kinds of crust whose density difference explains subduction itself. Nobody has ever drilled deeper than about twelve kilometres of the six thousand three hundred and seventy-one to the centre, so the session also answers the question examiners love most: how we know, through the seismic shadow zones that prove a liquid outer core and a solid centre. Master the layers, the evidence and the four classic traps, and next week's plate tectonics becomes almost self-explanatory.
