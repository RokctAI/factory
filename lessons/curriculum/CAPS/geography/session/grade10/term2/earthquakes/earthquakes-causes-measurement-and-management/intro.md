Today we are studying earthquakes — where the stored energy comes from, how it travels, how we measure it, and why the same shaking kills a few hundred people in one country and a quarter of a million in another. We begin with elastic rebound and the difference between focus and epicentre, follow the three seismic waves to the triangulation trick that locates any epicentre on the planet, and then separate the two scales learners confuse most: Richter, which measures the energy released, and Mercalli, which measures what people felt. The session closes with the highest-mark question in the topic — vulnerability and preparedness, and why buildings, not shaking, decide who survives.
