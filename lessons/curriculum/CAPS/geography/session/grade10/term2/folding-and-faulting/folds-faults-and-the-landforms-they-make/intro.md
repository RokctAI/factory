Today we are studying folding and faulting — what happens to rock when tectonic forces squeeze it, stretch it or slide it. Rock that bends gives us anticlines, synclines and fold mountains; rock that snaps gives us faults, block mountains and rift valleys, and the exam tests both by name, by diagram and by example. Our worked case is South Africa's own Cape Fold Mountains, crumpled into parallel ranges while Gondwana was being assembled, with the wine and fruit valleys of the Western Cape lying between the folds. By the end you will describe every structure in the order markers reward: the force, the response, the shape, the landform.
