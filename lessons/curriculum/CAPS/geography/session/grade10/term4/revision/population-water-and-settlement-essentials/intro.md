Today we are revising the human geography of the Grade 10 year in one session: population distribution, density and the vital rates, the demographic transition and the pyramid, migration and urbanisation, and the water topic that binds them — floods and water management in one of the driest countries on earth. Every definition is stated in the examiner's preferred wording, and the calculations for density and natural increase are rehearsed until they are reflex. We close with the chain that links all of it: people move for work, settle on the land left over, and the leftover land is so often the river's own — which is why population, settlement and water are one story, not three topics.
