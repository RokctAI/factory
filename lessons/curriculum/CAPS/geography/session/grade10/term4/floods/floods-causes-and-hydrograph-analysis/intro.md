Today we are explaining floods — the most common natural disaster in South Africa — and learning to read the geographer's instrument for them: the flood hydrograph. We separate the physical causes, from rainfall intensity and cut-off lows to slope, soil and catchment shape, from the human causes, above all the sealed city and settlement on floodplains. Then we build the hydrograph piece by piece — base flow, rising limb, peak discharge, lag time, falling limb — and use it for the comparison examiners favour: urban against rural, and flash flood against regional flood, anchored in Laingsburg 1981 and KwaZulu-Natal 2022. Master the three readings of a hydrograph and every flood question becomes a graph you can talk through.
