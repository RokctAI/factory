Today we are studying the heating of the atmosphere — the engine behind every wind, cloud and front you will ever be examined on. We will track one hundred units of insolation through reflection, absorption and the fifty that warm the ground, then define the four transfer processes — radiation, conduction, convection and advection — and see why the atmosphere is heated from below. From there we build the greenhouse mechanism step by step, separate the natural effect from the enhanced warming we caused, and finish with the five factors that decide why Upington bakes while Sutherland freezes.
