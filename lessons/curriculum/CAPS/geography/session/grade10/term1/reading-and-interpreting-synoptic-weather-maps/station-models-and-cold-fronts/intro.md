Today we are reading the synoptic weather chart — the map behind every forecast, and a guaranteed source of marks from Grade 10 all the way to the cyclone questions of matric. We will work one complete examination task: a station reporting eighteen degrees, a dew point of sixteen, six-oktas of cloud and a fresh north-westerly, plotted on a chart carrying a cold front. Along the way we decode isobars and pressure cells, take the station model apart symbol by symbol, find the front by its teeth and its evidence, and describe the cold, showery, clearing weather it leaves behind — in observations, not impressions.
