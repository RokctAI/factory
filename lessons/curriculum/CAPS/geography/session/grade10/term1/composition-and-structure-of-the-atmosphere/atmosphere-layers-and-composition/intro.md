Today we are studying the composition and structure of the atmosphere — the measured mixture of gases above your head and the four layers it is arranged into. Every weather and climate question from now to matric stands on this foundation: the percentages of dry air, the temperature reversals that separate troposphere from stratosphere, mesosphere and thermosphere, and the ozone shield that decides how much ultraviolet reaches the ground. We will work through the gas figures, build the layer diagram and its zigzag temperature graph, follow the CFC story from fridge to Antarctic hole, and finish with the exam method that turns all of it into marks.
