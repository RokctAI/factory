Today we are following water through the atmosphere — humidity, condensation and the three types of rainfall. Rain is not a mystery but a sequence: air takes up vapour, rises, cools to its dew point, condenses onto nuclei and finally lets the water go, and every exam question on this topic tests one link in that chain. We will work the relative humidity and condensation level calculations with real numbers, learn to name any cloud from four Latin words, and finish with the three lifting mechanisms — mountain, hot ground and cold front — that decide where and when South Africa gets its rain.
