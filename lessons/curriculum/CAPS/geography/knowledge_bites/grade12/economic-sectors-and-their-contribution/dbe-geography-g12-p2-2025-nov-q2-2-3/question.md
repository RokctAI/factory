## Question (1 mark)

Complete the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option in COLUMN B (write only the letter Y or Z). Refer to the table of contributions (%) made to the gross domestic product (GDP) during 2024: Transport 11; Agriculture 2,9; Mining 8,4; General government services 10,6; Electricity and gas 2,8; Construction 3; Trade, catering and accommodation 14,7; Manufacturing 15; Finance and real estate 31,7.

… makes the largest contribution to the tertiary sector.  [Y Finance and real estate / Z Construction]

## Method

column matching

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

(Reasoning: Construction is a secondary activity, so it cannot contribute to the tertiary sector at all. Finance and real estate is a service — tertiary — activity, and at 31,7% it is the largest single contribution in the whole table.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Y (Finance and real estate)
