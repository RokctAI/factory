## Question (1 mark)

Complete the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option in COLUMN B (write only the letter Y or Z). Refer to the table of contributions (%) made to the gross domestic product (GDP) during 2024: Transport 11; Agriculture 2,9; Mining 8,4; General government services 10,6; Electricity and gas 2,8; Construction 3; Trade, catering and accommodation 14,7; Manufacturing 15; Finance and real estate 31,7.

Primary economic activities accounted for … of the gross domestic product (GDP).  [Y 8,4% / Z 11,3%]

## Method

column matching

## Memo working

Primary economic activities extract raw materials directly from nature — in the table these are Agriculture and Mining. Add their contributions: Agriculture 2,9% + Mining 8,4% = 11,3%. (Option Y, 8,4%, counts mining only, so it is too small.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Z (11,3%)
