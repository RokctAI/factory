## Question (1 mark)

According to the extract, what is the percentage of informal trading that takes place in Johannesburg? (1 x 1)

(Context: extract 'Regulating trading in the informal sector' — trading in the informal sector has always been a part of South Africa's economy, 30% of which occurs in Johannesburg. The city's informal trading by-laws let the municipality declare any place an area where informal trading is restricted or prevented; the restrictions include traders sleeping overnight as it exposes them to criminal activity, and non-compliance can draw a fine of up to R500. Car window washers and other informal traders are part of a large group of poor South Africans who have been economically excluded; human rights lawyers have said certain regulations could be 'punishing people just for being poor'.)
