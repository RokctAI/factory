## Question (2 marks)

Refer to the table of the number of informal traders in the Richards Bay CBD per month for 2024: January 35; February 22; March 38; April 24; May 22; June 42; July 35; August 29; September 46; October 49; November 58; December 60.

Account for the large number of informal traders during the month of December. (1 x 2)

## Method

data interpretation

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

(Reasoning: December's 60 traders is the highest count of the year — the festive holiday season brings more shoppers, tourists and spending power, so more traders set up to sell.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Holiday season increases shoppers (2)
- More tourists hence more potential customers (2)
- Buying power of locals increase due to extra income/bonuses (2)
- People have additional income (during festive season) (accept examples) (2)
