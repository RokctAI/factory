## Question (1 mark)

Complete the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option in COLUMN B (write only the letter Y or Z). Refer to the table of contributions (%) made to the gross domestic product (GDP) during 2024: Transport 11; Agriculture 2,9; Mining 8,4; General government services 10,6; Electricity and gas 2,8; Construction 3; Trade, catering and accommodation 14,7; Manufacturing 15; Finance and real estate 31,7.

The … sector was the greatest contributor to the gross domestic product (GDP) in 2024.  [Y secondary / Z tertiary]

## Method

column matching

## Memo working

Group the table's activities by sector and add their percentages. Tertiary (services): Transport 11 + General government services 10,6 + Trade, catering and accommodation 14,7 + Finance and real estate 31,7 = 68%. Secondary (processing and manufacturing): Manufacturing 15 + Construction 3 + Electricity and gas 2,8 = 20,8%. The tertiary sector's 68% far exceeds the secondary sector's 20,8%.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Z (tertiary)
