## Question (1 mark)

Complete the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option in COLUMN B (write only the letter Y or Z).

A factor that advantages international trade  [Y trade tariffs / Z well-developed infrastructure]

## Method

column matching

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

(Reasoning: trade tariffs are barriers that make imports more expensive and so hinder trade; well-developed infrastructure — ports, railways, roads — makes moving goods easier and cheaper, which favours international trade.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Z (well-developed infrastructure)
