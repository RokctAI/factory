## Question (1 mark)

Complete the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option in COLUMN B (write only the letter Y or Z).

The sketch below illustrates …  [Y balance of trade / Z domestic trade]  (Sketch: a balance scale with 'Imports' in the pan on the left and 'Exports' in the pan on the right, evenly balanced.)

## Method

column matching

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

(Reasoning: the balance of trade is the difference between the value of a country's imports and its exports — exactly what a scale weighing imports against exports illustrates.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Y (balance of trade)
