## Question (1 mark)

Complete the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option from COLUMN B. Write only the letter (Y or Z). (Refer to the table of contributions (%) made to the gross domestic product (GDP) during 2024: Transport 11; Agriculture 2,9; Mining 8,4; General government services 10,6; Electricity and gas 2,8; Construction 3; Trade, catering and accommodation 14,7; Manufacturing 15; Finance and real estate 31,7.)

… is a secondary economic activity.  [Y Transport / Z Manufacturing]

## Method

column matching

## Memo working

Classify each option by sector. Secondary activities take raw materials and process them into finished products. Manufacturing is the textbook secondary activity — factories turning inputs into goods. Transport moves people and goods, which is a service, so it belongs to the tertiary sector. The secondary activity is manufacturing, option Z.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Z (Manufacturing)
