## Question (1 mark)

Complete the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option in COLUMN B (write only the letter Y or Z).

… is an economic benefit of international trade.  [Y Access to foreign currencies / Z Closing down of local industries]

## Method

column matching

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

(Reasoning: exporting goods earns foreign exchange — a benefit; the closing down of local industries because of cheap imports is a disadvantage of international trade.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Y (Access to foreign currencies)
