## Question (1 mark)

Refer to the photograph of an informal trader's fruit and vegetable stall on the pavement of the main road in Richards Bay.

Why is the informal trader in the photograph found on the main road? (1 x 1)

## Method

explain reason

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

(Reasoning: the main road carries the most passing foot and vehicle traffic, so a stall there is within easy reach of the greatest number of potential customers.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

More accessible to potential customers (1)
