## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to two synoptic sketch maps of southern Africa. Sketch A: a high (H) over the interior plateau, an H off the south-west coast and an H off the south-east coast, with a small low (L) on the south coast between them — a coastal low. Sketch B: a low (L) over the interior, a large elongated high off the south-west coast ridging around the south coast, and an H off the east coast. Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) The anticyclones in sketch A and B change their positions seasonally because of the …  A rotation of the Earth.  B frictional drag.  C shifting of the ITCZ.  D Coriolis force.

## Method

synoptic sketch mcq

The whole global pressure-belt system migrates with the overhead sun. As the Inter-Tropical Convergence Zone shifts north in winter and south in summer, the subtropical anticyclones shift with it — which is why their positions differ between the two sketches.
