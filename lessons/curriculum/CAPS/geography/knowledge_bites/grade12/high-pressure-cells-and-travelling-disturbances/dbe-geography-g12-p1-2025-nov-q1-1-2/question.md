## Question (1 mark)

(Synoptic weather map interpretation: complete the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option from COLUMN B. Write only Y or Z.) Air movement associated with high-pressure cells  [Y convergence / Z divergence]

## Method

column matching

In a high-pressure cell air subsides from above and then spreads outwards away from the centre at the surface — that outward spreading is divergence. Convergence, where surface air flows inwards towards a centre, belongs to low-pressure cells.
