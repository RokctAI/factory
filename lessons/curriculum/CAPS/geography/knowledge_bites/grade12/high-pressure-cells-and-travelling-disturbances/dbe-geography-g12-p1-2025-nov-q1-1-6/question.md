## Question (1 mark)

(Synoptic weather map interpretation: complete the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option from COLUMN B. Write only Y or Z.) A … is found between two air masses of different moisture contents.  [Y moisture front / Z inter-tropical convergence zone]

## Method

column matching

The key words are "different moisture contents". A moisture front is the boundary between moist air (usually drawn in from the Indian Ocean) and dry air over the interior — the two air masses differ in moisture, not mainly in temperature. The inter-tropical convergence zone is where the trade winds of the two hemispheres converge near the equator, so it does not fit the definition.
