## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to two synoptic sketch maps of southern Africa. Sketch A: a high (H) over the interior plateau, an H off the south-west coast and an H off the south-east coast, with a small low (L) on the south coast between them — a coastal low. Sketch B: a low (L) over the interior, a large elongated high off the south-west coast ridging around the south coast, and an H off the east coast. Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) … is the concept used to describe the elongation of the isobars associated with the South Atlantic anticyclone in sketch B.  A Backing  B Divergence  C Ridging  D Convergence

## Method

synoptic sketch mcq

When a high-pressure cell stretches out so that its isobars elongate around the south coast, the process is called ridging — the South Atlantic High ridges in behind cold fronts around the southern coastline.
