## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to two synoptic sketch maps of southern Africa. Sketch A: a high (H) over the interior plateau, an H off the south-west coast and an H off the south-east coast, with a small low (L) on the south coast between them — a coastal low. Sketch B: a low (L) over the interior, a large elongated high off the south-west coast ridging around the south coast, and an H off the east coast. Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) The low-pressure system in sketch B forms due to … over the land.  A low evaporation  B intense heating  C cold dense air  D heavy rainfall

## Method

synoptic sketch mcq

Sketch B is a summer pattern. Intense heating of the interior land surface makes the air expand and rise, creating a thermal (heat) low over the plateau.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

B (intense heating)
