## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to a synoptic weather map showing South African berg winds: an H with a 1016 hPa isobar lies over the interior; an L is shown to the north-west and a coastal L off the south/south-east coast, with tightly packed isobars of 1012, 1008, 1004, 1000 and 996 around the coastal low near Cape Town. Station models give temperature/dew point pairs: Windhoek 12/8, interior stations 18/10 and 20/12, Maputo 21/12, Durban 27/10, East London 34/10, Cape Town 12/12. A caption below the map reads: 'Extremely hot and uncomfortable conditions are expected across the Eastern Cape. Temperatures are expected to rise above 30 °C.') Name ONE South African city indicated on the synoptic weather map that is experiencing berg wind conditions. (1 x 1)

## Method

synoptic map reading

A berg wind is hot, dry air that descends from the interior plateau to the coast, so look for a coastal station with an unusually high temperature and a wide gap between temperature and dew point. East London reads 34 over 10 — extremely hot with very dry air — and Durban reads 27 over 10, also hot and dry. Cape Town at 12 over 12 is cool and saturated, so it is not experiencing berg winds. Either East London or Durban earns the mark.
