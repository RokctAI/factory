## Question (2 marks)

Give evidence from the synoptic weather map to support your answer to QUESTION 1.5.1. (1 x 2)  (Synoptic weather map of southern Africa showing berg wind conditions: a high (1016 hPa) over the interior, a low to the north-west and a coastal low off the south/south-east coast with tightly packed isobars near Cape Town; station models give temperature/dew point pairs including East London 34/10, Durban 27/10 and Cape Town 12/12; the caption warns of extremely hot conditions above 30 degrees C across the Eastern Cape. In QUESTION 1.5.1 the city experiencing berg wind conditions was identified as East London or Durban.)

## Method

data evidence

## Memo working

A berg wind station on the coast shows unusually hot, dry air. On this map East London reads 34/10 and Durban 27/10: the air temperature sits far above the dew point, which means dry air, low humidity and a large temperature range. The pressure pattern supports this too - a Kalahari high over the interior drives air down the escarpment towards a coastal low. Any ONE piece of evidence earns the 2 marks.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Large temperature range (2); High air temperatures/Air temperature of 34 °C/27 °C (2); Low humidity/dry air/clear sky (2); (Accept - presence of Kalahari HP and coastal LP) (2) [ANY ONE]
