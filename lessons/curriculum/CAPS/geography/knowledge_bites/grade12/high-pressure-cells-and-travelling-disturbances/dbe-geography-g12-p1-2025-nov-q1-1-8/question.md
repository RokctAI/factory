## Question (1 mark)

(Synoptic weather map interpretation: complete the statement with the correct option. Write only Y or Z. Refer to a sketch map of South Africa with a circled L over the central interior. Two station models are plotted: at Cape Town, on the south-west coast, a filled — overcast — station circle with a wind shaft carrying two full feathers; at Durban, on the east coast, a filled circle with a plain wind shaft.) Rainfall with 20 knots of wind is likely at …  [Y Durban (Dbn) / Z Cape Town (CT)]

## Method

column matching

Read the station models. On a wind shaft each full feather represents 10 knots, so Cape Town's shaft with two full feathers shows a 20-knot wind, and its fully shaded station circle shows overcast conditions, which fit rainfall. Durban's shaft carries no feathers at all, so there is no 20-knot wind there. Cape Town is therefore the station where rainfall with 20 knots of wind is likely.
