## Question (1 mark)

(Synoptic weather map interpretation: complete the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option from COLUMN B. Write only Y or Z.) The general direction of movement of a coastal low-pressure system is …  [Y easterly / Z westerly]

## Method

column matching

A coastal low is a shallow low-pressure system that travels around the South African coastline, moving from the west coast around the southern coast towards the east coast. Because it progresses towards the east, its general direction of movement is easterly.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Y (easterly)
