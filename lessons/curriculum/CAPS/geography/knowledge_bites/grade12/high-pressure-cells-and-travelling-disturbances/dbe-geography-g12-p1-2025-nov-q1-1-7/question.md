## Question (1 mark)

(Synoptic weather map interpretation: complete the statement with the correct option. Write only Y or Z. Refer to a sketch map of South Africa: a band of bold dashed parallel lines — a moisture front — runs north-west to south-east across the interior. One long curved arrow blows in from the Indian Ocean in the north-east towards the dashed band, and a second arrow blows in from the south-west towards the band. Two highs with concentric isobars are shown: an H off the south-west coast, the South Atlantic High, and an H off the east coast, the South Indian High.) The conditions represented in the sketch below will lead to …  [Y line thunderstorms / Z berg winds]

## Method

column matching

The sketch shows moist Indian Ocean air and drier air from the south-west converging along a north-west to south-east moisture front over the interior. Where the two air masses meet, the moist air is forced to rise, building a long band of cumulonimbus clouds along the front — that produces line thunderstorms. Berg winds are hot, dry offshore winds descending from the plateau in winter and have nothing to do with a moisture front.
