## Question (8 marks)

The warm and dry conditions can influence veld fires. In a paragraph of approximately EIGHT lines, suggest sustainable strategies that can be put in place to reduce the negative impact of veld fires. (4 x 2)  (Synoptic weather map of southern Africa showing berg wind conditions: a high (1016 hPa) over the interior, a low to the north-west and a coastal low off the south/south-east coast with tightly packed isobars near Cape Town; station models give temperature/dew point pairs including East London 34/10, Durban 27/10 and Cape Town 12/12; the caption warns of extremely hot conditions above 30 degrees C across the Eastern Cape.)

## Method

paragraph strategies

## Memo working

Write a paragraph of about eight lines choosing ANY FOUR sustainable strategies, each worth 2 marks. Strong points from the memo: create firebreaks or buffer zones around settlements and farmland; build water storage facilities for fire-fighting; educate the community about strategies they could implement; make emergency services accessible; provide access to fire-fighting equipment; implement early warning systems; create emergency assembly points; create awareness campaigns; build lookout towers; install sprinklers; plan evacuation routes; remove flammable alien vegetation.
