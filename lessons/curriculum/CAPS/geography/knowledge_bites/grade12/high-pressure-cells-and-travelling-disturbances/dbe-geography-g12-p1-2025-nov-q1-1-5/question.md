## Question (1 mark)

(Synoptic weather map interpretation: complete the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option from COLUMN B. Write only Y or Z.) The elongation of isobars extending outwards from a high-pressure cell is referred to as …  [Y diverging / Z ridging]

## Method

column matching

When a high-pressure cell stretches a tongue of higher pressure outwards — seen on a synoptic map as isobars elongating away from the cell, for example the South Atlantic High extending around the south coast — that elongation is called ridging. Divergence describes the outward air movement within a high, not the elongated shape of its isobars.
