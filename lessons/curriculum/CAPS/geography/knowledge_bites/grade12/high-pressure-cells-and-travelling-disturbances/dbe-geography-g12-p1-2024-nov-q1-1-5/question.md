## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to two synoptic sketch maps of southern Africa. Sketch A: a high (H) over the interior plateau, an H off the south-west coast and an H off the south-east coast, with a small low (L) on the south coast between them — a coastal low. Sketch B: a low (L) over the interior, a large elongated high off the south-west coast ridging around the south coast, and an H off the east coast. Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) The South Indian anticyclone in sketch B is likely to feed more moisture over the east coast in comparison with sketch A because it …  A is a blocking high.  B has a larger fetch (distance).  C diverges dry air.  D has a smaller fetch (distance).

## Method

synoptic sketch mcq

In sketch B the South Indian anticyclone sits further out over the ocean, so its air travels a longer distance across the warm Indian Ocean before reaching land. A larger fetch gives the air more time to absorb moisture, feeding wetter air onto the east coast.
