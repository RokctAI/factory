## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to two synoptic sketch maps of southern Africa. Sketch A: a high (H) over the interior plateau, an H off the south-west coast and an H off the south-east coast, with a small low (L) on the south coast between them — a coastal low. Sketch B: a low (L) over the interior, a large elongated high off the south-west coast ridging around the south coast, and an H off the east coast. Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) Sketch A shows typical … conditions.  A summer  B spring  C winter  D autumn

## Method

synoptic sketch mcq

In winter the subtropical anticyclones shift north and strengthen, so a Kalahari high sits over the interior plateau and highs dominate off both coasts, with only a small coastal low squeezed onto the south coast between them — exactly the pattern in sketch A.
