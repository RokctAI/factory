## Question (1 mark)

(Synoptic weather map interpretation: complete the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option from COLUMN B. Write only Y or Z.) The name of the air pressure cell that dominates the interior of South Africa in winter  [Y heat low / Z Kalahari high]

## Method

column matching

In winter the interior plateau cools down and the subtropical high-pressure belt shifts northwards, so a strong high-pressure cell — the Kalahari High — settles over the interior of South Africa and dominates its winter weather. The heat low is the opposite case: a summer feature that forms when intense surface heating makes air rise over the interior.
