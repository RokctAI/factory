## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to a source on line thunderstorms over South Africa: 'During summer, thunderstorms can form a line that can extend for hundreds of kilometres. These line thunderstorms can persist for many hours and produce damaging winds and hail, which impacts negatively on the natural environment.' A sketch map of South Africa shows a north-west to south-east moisture front across the interior with cumulonimbus clouds along it at A and B. Wind A blows in from a high off the south-west coast towards the moisture front; wind B blows in from a high off the east coast over the Indian Ocean. An inset diagram grades rainfall outward from the moisture front: heavy rain nearest the front, then moderate rain, then light rain furthest away.) According to the extract, in which season do line thunderstorms commonly occur? (1 x 1)

extract reading

This is a direct reading question: the extract's opening words state that line thunderstorms form during summer.
