## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to two synoptic sketch maps of southern Africa. Sketch A: a high (H) over the interior plateau, an H off the south-west coast and an H off the south-east coast, with a small low (L) on the south coast between them — a coastal low. Sketch B: a low (L) over the interior, a large elongated high off the south-west coast ridging around the south coast, and an H off the east coast. Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) Berg winds are most likely to develop in sketch A because of the presence of the … and …  (i) Kalahari anticyclone  (ii) coastal low  (iii) South Indian anticyclone  (iv) heat low  A (i) and (ii)  B (ii) and (iii)  C (i) and (iv)  D (iii) and (iv)

## Method

synoptic sketch mcq

A berg wind needs a pressure gradient from the interior towards the coast: the Kalahari anticyclone over the plateau pushes air down the escarpment towards a coastal low. The descending air warms adiabatically and arrives hot and dry. Both features are present in sketch A.
