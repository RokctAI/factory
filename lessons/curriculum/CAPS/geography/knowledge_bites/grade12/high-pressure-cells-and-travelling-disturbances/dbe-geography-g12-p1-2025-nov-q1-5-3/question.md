## Question (4 marks)

Explain the processes that lead to berg winds being warm and dry. ## Memo working

Deal with temperature and moisture separately - the memo requires both. Temperature: air from the interior plateau descends down the escarpment towards the coast; as it sinks it is compressed and heated adiabatically at about 1 degree C per 100 m. Moisture: as the descending air warms, its relative humidity drops and moisture is evaporated, so the wind reaches the coast warm and dry.
