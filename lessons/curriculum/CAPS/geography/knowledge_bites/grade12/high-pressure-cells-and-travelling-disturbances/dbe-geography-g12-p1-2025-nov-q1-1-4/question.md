## Question (1 mark)

(Synoptic weather map interpretation: complete the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option from COLUMN B. Write only Y or Z.) The … is known as a blocking high when it is in the path of the mid-latitude cyclone.  [Y South Indian high / Z Kalahari high]

## Method

column matching

Mid-latitude cyclones travel from west to east south of the country. When the South Indian High sits to the south-east, directly in a cyclone's path, it blocks the cyclone's eastward movement and forces it to slow or divert — that is why it is called a blocking high. The Kalahari High sits over the interior plateau, not in the cyclone's path.
