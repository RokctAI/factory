(Refer to two synoptic sketch maps of southern Africa. Sketch A: a high (H) over the interior plateau, an H off the south-west coast and an H off the south-east coast, with a small low (L) on the south coast between them — a coastal low. Sketch B: a low (L) over the interior, a large elongated high off the south-west coast ridging around the south coast, and an H off the east coast. Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) The inversion layer that forms over the escarpment in sketch B will favour the formation of … and …  (i) frost  (ii) rainfall  (iii) fog  (iv) hail  A (i) and (ii)  B (i) and (iii)  C (ii) and (iv)  D (iii) and (iv)

## Method

synoptic sketch mcq

In the summer pattern of sketch B the inversion layer lifts above the escarpment, so moist air from the Indian Ocean can penetrate onto the interior plateau, where strong uplift builds thunderstorms. The memo pairs rainfall and hail as the favoured formations.
