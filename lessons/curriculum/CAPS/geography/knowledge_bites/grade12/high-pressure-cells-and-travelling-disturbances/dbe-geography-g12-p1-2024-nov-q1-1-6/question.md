## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to two synoptic sketch maps of southern Africa. Sketch A: a high (H) over the interior plateau, an H off the south-west coast and an H off the south-east coast, with a small low (L) on the south coast between them — a coastal low. Sketch B: a low (L) over the interior, a large elongated high off the south-west coast ridging around the south coast, and an H off the east coast. Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) The low-pressure system in sketch A will move in a … direction.  A northerly  B westerly  C easterly  D southerly

## Method

synoptic sketch mcq

The low in sketch A is a coastal low. Coastal lows travel along the South African coastline from west to east ahead of approaching disturbances, so it moves in an easterly direction.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C (easterly)
