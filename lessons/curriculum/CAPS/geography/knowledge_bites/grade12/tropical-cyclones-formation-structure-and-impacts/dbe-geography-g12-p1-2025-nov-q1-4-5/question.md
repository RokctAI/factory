## Question (4 marks)

(Refer to the infographic on Tropical Cyclone Dikeledi. Fact file: on 8 January 2025 the central pressure was 996 hPa with a maximum wind speed of 75 km/h; on 13 January 2025 the central pressure was 976 hPa with a maximum wind speed of 150 km/h. The satellite images show the system east of Madagascar on 8 January and as a well-developed cyclone with a distinct eye in the Mozambique Channel, west of Madagascar, on 13 January.) Explain why Tropical Cyclone Dikeledi intensified from 8 January to 13 January 2025. (2 x 2)

## Method

process explanation

Track what changed between the two dates. The system crossed from Madagascar into the Mozambique Channel, moving over warmer water: more evaporation and more latent heat released, so more energy fed the storm. Over the open ocean there is also less frictional drag than over land, so wind speeds increased — from 75 km/h to 150 km/h. With the extra energy, the central pressure dropped from 996 hPa to 976 hPa, deepening the system. Any two of these explanations, at two marks each, earn the four marks.
