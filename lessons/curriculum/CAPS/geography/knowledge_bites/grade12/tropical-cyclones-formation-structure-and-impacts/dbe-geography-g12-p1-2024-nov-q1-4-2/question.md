## Question (2 marks)

(Refer to an infographic on Tropical Cyclone Filipo: 'The tropical storm made landfall in Mozambique on 12 March after strengthening off the coast of southeast Africa, bringing strong winds and heavy rain to Mozambique. On 13 March, Tropical Storm Filipo moved back over the ocean and developed into a tropical cyclone on 14 March.' A map shows the plotted path: from 10 March 12:00 UTC over the Indian Ocean it tracks west/south-west, crosses the Mozambique coast, passes near Maputo at 06:00 UTC on 13 March, then curves back south-east over the Indian Ocean. An inset synoptic sketch of the mature stage shows closed isobars of 984 and 980 hPa spiralling around the eye. A table gives wind speeds along the path: 10 Mar 12:00 UTC 63 km/h; 11 Mar 12:00 UTC 95 km/h; 11 Mar 18:00 UTC 116 km/h; 12 Mar 06:00 UTC 95 km/h; 12 Mar 18:00 UTC 74 km/h; 13 Mar 06:00 UTC 84 km/h; 13 Mar 18:00 UTC 106 km/h; 14 Mar 06:00 UTC 127 km/h.) Give evidence from the map and table of information that Tropical Cyclone Filipo had strengthened from 10 to 11 March. (2 x 1)

## Method

data evidence

Read the table across 10 to 11 March: wind speed climbed from 63 km/h to 95 km/h and then 116 km/h. Read the map for the cause: over that period the storm stayed out over the open Indian Ocean, exposed to more moisture to feed it.
