## Question (4 marks)

(Refer to an infographic on Tropical Cyclone Filipo: 'The tropical storm made landfall in Mozambique on 12 March after strengthening off the coast of southeast Africa, bringing strong winds and heavy rain to Mozambique. On 13 March, Tropical Storm Filipo moved back over the ocean and developed into a tropical cyclone on 14 March.' A map shows the plotted path: from 10 March 12:00 UTC over the Indian Ocean it tracks west/south-west, crosses the Mozambique coast, passes near Maputo at 06:00 UTC on 13 March, then curves back south-east over the Indian Ocean. An inset synoptic sketch of the mature stage shows closed isobars of 984 and 980 hPa spiralling around the eye. A table gives wind speeds along the path: 10 Mar 12:00 UTC 63 km/h; 11 Mar 12:00 UTC 95 km/h; 11 Mar 18:00 UTC 116 km/h; 12 Mar 06:00 UTC 95 km/h; 12 Mar 18:00 UTC 74 km/h; 13 Mar 06:00 UTC 84 km/h; 13 Mar 18:00 UTC 106 km/h; 14 Mar 06:00 UTC 127 km/h.) Give TWO reasons for the decrease in wind speed from 06:00 to 18:00 on 12 March. (2 x 2)

## Method

explain reason

Between 06:00 and 18:00 on 12 March the map shows Filipo crossing onto land over Mozambique. Over land the storm loses its warm-water moisture supply, surface friction increases, less latent heat is released, and the system begins to dissipate.
