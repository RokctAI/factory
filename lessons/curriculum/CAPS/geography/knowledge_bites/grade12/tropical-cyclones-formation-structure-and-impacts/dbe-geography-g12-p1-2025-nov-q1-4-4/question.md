## Question (4 marks)

(Refer to satellite images C and D of the infographic on Tropical Cyclone Dikeledi. Image C, taken on 8 January 2025, shows a loosely organised spiralling cloud mass east of Madagascar with no clear eye. Image D, taken on 13 January 2025, shows a well-developed circular cyclone with a distinct dark eye in the Mozambique Channel west of Madagascar; a dashed curve marks the 'Forward left-hand quadrant'.) Refer to the satellite images C and D. Describe the weather conditions associated with the forward left-hand quadrant (dangerous semicircle) in satellite image D. (2 x 2)

## Method

describe conditions

In the Southern Hemisphere the forward left-hand quadrant is the dangerous semicircle: there the cyclone's rotating winds blow in the same direction as the whole system is moving, so their speeds add together. Describe the resulting weather: hurricane-force, very destructive winds, torrential rainfall with thunderstorms, and the associated hail and lightning. Any two conditions, at two marks each, earn the four marks.
