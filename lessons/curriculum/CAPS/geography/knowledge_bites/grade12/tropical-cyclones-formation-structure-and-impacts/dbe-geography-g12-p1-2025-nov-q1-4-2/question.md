## Question (2 marks)

(Refer to the fact file on Tropical Cyclone Dikeledi: conditions recorded on 8 January 2025 were a central pressure of 996 hPa and a maximum wind speed of 75 km/h. The answer to QUESTION 1.4.1 was the immature stage.) Give TWO reasons in the fact file to support your answer to QUESTION 1.4.1. (2 x 1)

## Method

data evidence

Quote the fact file itself. The central pressure of 996 hPa is still only moderately low — a mature tropical cyclone drops much lower — and the maximum wind speed of 75 km/h is gale force, below hurricane strength. Both readings are the immature-stage evidence the fact file provides; the memo also accepts that the system had already been given a name, which happens at the immature stage.
