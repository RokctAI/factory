## Question (4 marks)

(Refer to location map B of the infographic on Tropical Cyclone Dikeledi: a sketch map of south-east Africa and Madagascar with latitude lines marked at the Equator, 5°S and 20°S; Dikeledi is labelled with a curved arrow just north-east of Madagascar between 5°S and 20°S, over the Indian Ocean.) Refer to location map B. Explain why Tropical Cyclone Dikeledi developed between 5° and 20° south of the equator. (2 x 2)

## Method

process explanation

Two ingredients of tropical-cyclone formation come together in this latitude band. First, rotation: the Coriolis force is zero at the equator but strong enough between about 5 and 20 degrees south to deflect the inflowing air and set the system spinning. Second, energy: the tropical ocean here is warm, driving a high evaporation rate; when that vapour condenses it releases latent heat, which powers the storm and deepens the low pressure at its centre. Any two of these linked points, fully explained, earn two marks each.
