## Question (1 mark)

(Match the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option in COLUMN B; write only the letter Y or Z.) Greater profits are associated with this settlement pattern  [Y dispersed / Z nucleated]

## Method

column matching

A dispersed pattern means farmsteads stand far apart, each on its own large piece of land — typical of large commercial farms. Large landholdings allow large-scale, mechanised production and therefore greater profits. A nucleated pattern, with dwellings clustered together, is linked to smaller plots and more subsistence-style farming.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)
