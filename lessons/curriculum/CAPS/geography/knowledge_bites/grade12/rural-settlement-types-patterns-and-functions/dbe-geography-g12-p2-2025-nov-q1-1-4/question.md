## Question (1 mark)

Complete the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option from COLUMN B. Write only the letter (Y or Z).

A … has mostly rural functions and some urban functions.  [Y farmstead / Z village]

## Method

column matching

## Memo working

A farmstead is a single farm dwelling with purely rural (farming) functions — it has no urban functions at all. A village is the step in the rural hierarchy where urban functions first appear: it is still mostly rural, but adds a few urban functions such as a school, a shop or a church. "Mostly rural functions and some urban functions" is exactly the village, option Z.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Z (village)
