## Question (1 mark)

(Match the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option in COLUMN B; write only the letter Y or Z.) Exact piece of land that a settlement is built on  [Y situation / Z site]

## Method

column matching

Separate the two paired concepts: the site is the actual, physical piece of land a settlement occupies — its soil, slope, drainage. The situation is the settlement's position relative to its surroundings, such as routes, markets or other towns. "Exact piece of land" points to the physical ground itself, so it is the site.
