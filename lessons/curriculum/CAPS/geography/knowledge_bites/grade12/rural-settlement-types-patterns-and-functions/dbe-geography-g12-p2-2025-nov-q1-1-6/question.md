## Question (1 mark)

Complete the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option from COLUMN B. Write only the letter (Y or Z).

The sketch below depicts a … settlement.  [Y wet-point / Z dry-point]  (Examiner's sketch, key: bold line = river, square = settlement, thin line = contour line — contour lines labelled 60 and 80 run parallel to a river, and the settlement squares are clustered right along both banks of the river.)

## Method

column matching

## Memo working

A wet-point settlement is sited to be near water, typically right at a river or spring; a dry-point settlement is sited to stay away from water, for example on higher ground above a floodplain. In the sketch the settlement squares hug both banks of the river rather than sitting up on the higher contours at 60 and 80 — the site was clearly chosen for access to the water. That is a wet-point settlement, option Y.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Y (wet-point)
