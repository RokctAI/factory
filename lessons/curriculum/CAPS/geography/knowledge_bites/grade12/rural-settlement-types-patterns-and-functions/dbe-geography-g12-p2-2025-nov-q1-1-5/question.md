## Question (1 mark)

Complete the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option from COLUMN B. Write only the letter (Y or Z).

The pattern of the settlement below is …  [Y nucleated / Z dispersed]  (Examiner's sketch, scale 1 : 30 000, key: square = settlement, line = road — a few isolated settlement squares spread far apart, each connected by roads that meet at a central junction.)

## Method

column matching

## Memo working

Ask what the buildings are doing relative to each other. In a nucleated pattern the buildings cluster tightly together around a point. In the sketch the opposite is happening: only a few settlement squares appear, and they are spread far apart from one another, each standing on its own and linked to the others only by roads. Isolated, spread-out dwellings define a dispersed pattern, option Z.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Z (dispersed)
