## Question (1 mark)

Complete the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option from COLUMN B. Write only the letter (Y or Z).

Settlements are classified as rural, based on …  [Y the number of functions / Z the total population]

## Method

column matching

## Memo working

Geographers separate rural from urban settlements by function, not by headcount. A rural settlement offers only a small number of functions, and those functions are dominated by primary activities such as farming. Total population cannot be the classifier: a small place can still be urban if its functions are urban, and a large farming area remains rural. So the number of functions, option Y, completes the statement.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Y (the number of functions)
