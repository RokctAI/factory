## Question (1 mark)

(Match the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option in COLUMN B; write only the letter Y or Z.) The shape of this settlement is influenced by a river  [Y circular / Z linear]

## Method

column matching

Think of what a river does to a settlement's outline: buildings string themselves out in a long, narrow line along the banks, following the water. A feature that runs in a line — a river, road or railway — produces a linear (elongated) settlement, not a circular one.
