## Question (1 mark)

Complete the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option from COLUMN B. Write only the letter (Y or Z).

The actual physical position where a rural settlement is located  [Y site / Z situation]

## Method

column matching

## Memo working

Keep the pair of concepts apart: the site is the actual land the settlement is built on — the physical position, with its soil, slope and water; the situation is that settlement's position in relation to its surroundings, such as nearby towns, rivers and routes. The statement says "the actual physical position", which is the definition of site, option Y.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Y (site)
