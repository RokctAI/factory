## Question (1 mark)

Complete the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option from COLUMN B. Write only the letter (Y or Z).

A loose grouping of farmsteads is called a …  [Y town / Z hamlet]

## Method

column matching

## Memo working

Work up the rural settlement hierarchy: a single farmstead is one farm dwelling; a loose grouping of a few farmsteads with almost no functions is a hamlet; a village is bigger and adds some urban functions; a town is already an urban settlement. "A loose grouping of farmsteads" therefore describes a hamlet, option Z — a town is far too high up the hierarchy.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Z (hamlet)
