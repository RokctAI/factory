## Question (1 mark)

(Match the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option in COLUMN B; write only the letter Y or Z.) The situation of a commercial livestock farm is influenced by …  [Y topography / Z markets]

## Method

column matching

Situation is about a settlement's position relative to features around it. Topography — the shape of the land itself — belongs to the site. A commercial livestock farm sells its produce, so its situation is judged by its position relative to markets.

(see marking guidelines)
