## Question (1 mark)

Complete the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option from COLUMN B. Write only the letter (Y or Z).

The shape of the settlement below is …  [Y linear / Z round]  (Examiner's sketch, key: bold line = river, square = settlement — settlement squares strung out in a line following the winding course of the river on both banks.)

## Method

column matching

## Memo working

Shape describes the outline the settlement makes on the landscape. A round settlement grows evenly outwards from a centre. Here the settlement squares do not spread outwards — they are strung out one after another along the winding river, so the settlement is long and narrow, following a line feature. A settlement stretched along a river, road or railway is linear in shape, option Y.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Y (linear)
