## Question (1 mark)

(Match the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option in COLUMN B; write only the letter Y or Z.) The largest rural settlement type  [Y village / Z hamlet]

## Method

column matching

Order the rural settlement types by size: an isolated farmstead is the smallest, then a hamlet — a handful of buildings with few or no services — and then a village, which is larger and offers basic services such as a school, shop or church. Of the two options given, the village is the largest.

(see marking guidelines)
