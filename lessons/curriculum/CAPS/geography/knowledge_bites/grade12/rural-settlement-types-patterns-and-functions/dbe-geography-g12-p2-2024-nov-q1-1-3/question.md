## Question (1 mark)

(Match the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option in COLUMN B; write only the letter Y or Z.) Settlements located close to a water source in arid areas  [Y wet-point / Z dry-point]

## Method

column matching

A wet-point settlement is deliberately placed close to water where water is scarce — for example around a spring, well or river in a dry region. A dry-point settlement is the opposite: it is placed on land that stays dry where water is abundant or flooding threatens. In an arid area, locating close to a water source makes it a wet-point settlement.
