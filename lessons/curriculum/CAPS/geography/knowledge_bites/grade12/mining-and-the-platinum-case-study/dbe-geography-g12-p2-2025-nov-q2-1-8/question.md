## Question (1 mark)

Refer to the line graph showing the number of people employed in gold mines in South Africa between 1995 and 2025 (employment declines gently from near 120 000 in 2015 to below 100 000 by 2025).

An economic factor that has caused employment numbers to decrease from 2015 onwards is/are …

A HIV and Aids.  B fluctuating prices of gold.  C environmental degradation.  D strikes and protests.

## Method

mcq

## Memo working

Again filter by the type of factor demanded — economic. HIV and Aids is a social (health) factor, environmental degradation is environmental, and strikes and protests are social/labour factors. The economic factor is the fluctuating price of gold: when the price swings or falls, revenue becomes uncertain, marginal shafts become unprofitable and mines shed workers. The answer is B.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

B (fluctuating prices of gold)
