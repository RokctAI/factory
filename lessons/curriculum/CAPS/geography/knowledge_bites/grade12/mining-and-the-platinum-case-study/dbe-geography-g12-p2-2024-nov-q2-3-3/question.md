## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to the line graph 'South Africa's coal exports and employment', 2016 to 2023: the employees line starts at about 87 000 in 2016, peaks at about 92 500 in 2018, declines to a low of about 76 000 around 2021/2022, then recovers to about 84 000 by 2023.) According to the graph, between which years was the smallest number of employees recorded in the coal mining industry? (1 x 1)

## Method

graph reading

Trace the employees line — the one read against the left-hand axis — and find its lowest dip. After peaking in 2018 the line falls steadily and bottoms out at about 76 000, in the stretch between 2021 and 2022, before recovering. So the smallest number of employees was recorded between 2021 and 2022.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Between 2021 and 2022 (1)
