## Question (1 mark)

Choose the answer and write only the letter (A–D). (This question is based on gold mining in South Africa.)

The province that produces the most gold in South Africa is …

A Limpopo.  B Mpumalanga.  C KwaZulu-Natal.  D Gauteng.

## Method

mcq

## Memo working

South Africa's gold comes from the Witwatersrand Basin, the gold-bearing reef on which Johannesburg was founded. That reef lies in Gauteng, which remains the country's leading gold-producing province. Limpopo and Mpumalanga are associated with platinum and coal respectively, and KwaZulu-Natal is not a gold province. The answer is D.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

D (Gauteng)
