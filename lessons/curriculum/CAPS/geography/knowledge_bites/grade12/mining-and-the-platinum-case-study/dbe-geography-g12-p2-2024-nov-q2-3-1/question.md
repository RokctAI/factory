## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to a line graph titled 'South Africa's coal exports and employment', covering 2016 to 2023. The left vertical axis shows the number of employees, 60 000 to 100 000; the right vertical axis shows exports in billions of rands, 50 to 250. The exports line runs roughly flat around 50-80 billion rands from 2016 to 2021, then rises very steeply to about 250 billion in 2023. The employees line starts at about 87 000 in 2016, peaks at about 92 500 in 2018, declines to a low of about 76 000 around 2021/2022, then recovers to about 84 000 by 2023.) According to the graph, what amount of coal, in billions, was exported in 2023? (1 x 1)

## Method

graph reading

Find 2023 at the far right of the horizontal axis, then follow the exports line — the one keyed 'Rands in billions' — up to its end point. Read its value off the right-hand axis, which is the axis for exports: the line finishes at the top of that axis, at 250 billion rands.
