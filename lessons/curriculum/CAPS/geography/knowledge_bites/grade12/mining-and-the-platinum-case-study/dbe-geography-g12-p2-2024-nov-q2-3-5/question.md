## Question (4 marks)

(Refer to an extract on coal mining in South Africa: 'The high-quality coal reserves in South Africa have been decreasing over the years. Shallow and high-quality resources in the Emalahleni (Witbank) coal field have been depleted. Mining companies are required to find other more cost-effective methods to extract the deeper, low quality coal seams of the basin.') According to the extract, the high-quality coal reserves in South Africa have been decreasing over the years. Explain the negative impact of decreasing high-quality coal reserves for the future supply of power in South Africa. (2 x 2)

## Method

impact explanation

Link the coal supply directly to electricity generation: South Africa's power stations burn coal, so less high-quality coal means less reliable generation. Follow the chain of consequences — demand starts to outstrip supply, load-shedding becomes more frequent, power stations must burn lower-quality coal or shut down for maintenance, and the cost of electricity rises. There is also the expensive fallback of investing in alternative or renewable energy sources. Explain any two of these impacts for two marks each.
