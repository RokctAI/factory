## Question (1 mark)

Refer to the line graph showing the number of people employed in gold mines in South Africa between 1995 and 2025. (The line starts near 385 000 in 1995, falls steeply to about 250 000 by 2000, continues down to roughly 200 000 in 2005, about 160 000 to 170 000 in 2010, near 120 000 in 2015, then declines gently to below 100 000 by 2025.)

The number of people employed in South African gold mines decreased the most between …

A 1995 and 2000.  B 2000 and 2005.  C 2005 and 2010.  D 2010 and 2015.

## Method

graph mcq

## Memo working

Compare the drop across each five-year interval rather than the overall level. From 1995 to 2000 the line plunges from about 385 000 to about 250 000 — a fall of roughly 135 000, the steepest segment on the whole graph. The later intervals each lose only about 30 000 to 50 000 jobs. The biggest decrease is therefore in the first interval, option A.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A (1995 and 2000)
