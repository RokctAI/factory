## Question (2 marks)

(The answer to QUESTION 2.3.3 was that the smallest number of coal mining employees was recorded between 2021 and 2022.) Give ONE possible reason for the small number of employees (answer to QUESTION 2.3.3). (1 x 2)

## Method

explain reason

Ask what could shrink a mining workforce around 2021/2022 or in the industry generally. Think along several lines: the coal itself running out (depletion of seams), the economy (recession), health shocks (pandemics), labour unrest (strikes), machines replacing people (mechanisation), policy uncertainty (threats of nationalisation), the shift to environmentally friendly energy reducing coal demand, load-shedding disrupting operations, or mine accidents. Any one well-stated reason earns the two marks.
