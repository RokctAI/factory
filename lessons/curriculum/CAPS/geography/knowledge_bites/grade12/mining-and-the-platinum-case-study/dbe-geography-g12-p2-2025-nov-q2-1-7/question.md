## Question (1 mark)

Choose the answer and write only the letter (A–D). (This question is based on gold mining in South Africa.)

A physical (natural) factor causing employment numbers to decrease in South African gold mines is/are …

A threats of nationalisation.  B depletion of gold in mines.  C unskilled labourers.  D less foreign investment in mines.

## Method

mcq

## Memo working

The qualifier is "physical (natural)". Threats of nationalisation are political, unskilled labourers are a social/human factor, and less foreign investment is economic. The only factor rooted in nature itself is the depletion of gold in the mines — the ore body is finite, and as the gold runs out mines close and jobs disappear. The answer is B.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

B (depletion of gold in mines)
