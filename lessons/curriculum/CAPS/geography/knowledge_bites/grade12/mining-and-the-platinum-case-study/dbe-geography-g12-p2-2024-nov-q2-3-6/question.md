## Question (6 marks)

Explain why the coal mining industry is important to the economy of South Africa. (3 x 2)

## Method

explain importance

Work through the standard economic-contribution checklist and expand each point. Jobs: coal mining creates employment, which reduces dependency on the state and raises buying power. National income: the industry contributes to GDP, pays taxes, and coal exports earn foreign exchange. Linkages: coal is the country's main source of electricity on which every sector depends, it serves as a raw material for other products, and the multiplier effect stimulates other industries and infrastructure development. A bare fact without expansion earns only one mark, so tie each fact to its economic consequence. Any three expanded points earn the six marks.
