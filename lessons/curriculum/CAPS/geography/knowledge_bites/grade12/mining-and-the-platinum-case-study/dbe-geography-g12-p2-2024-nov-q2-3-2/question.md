## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to the line graph 'South Africa's coal exports and employment', 2016 to 2023: the exports line runs roughly flat around 50-80 billion rands from 2016 to 2021, then rises very steeply to about 250 billion rands in 2023.) What has been the general trend in profits from the export of coal in recent years? (1 x 1)

## Method

graph trend

A trend question asks for the direction of change, not a value. Look at the recent years on the exports line: from 2021 onwards it climbs steeply, from roughly 80 billion to about 250 billion rands. The direction is clearly upward, so the general trend is an increase. Quoting figures that show the increase also earns the mark.
