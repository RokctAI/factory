## Question (1 mark)

Map interpretation (refer to F in block C2 and G in block D4 on the 1 : 50 000 topographical map 2829DB LADYSMITH; F is in the centre of the town): (a) The temperature at F in the centre of the town is 2 °C higher than at G. Name the concept that describes this difference. (1 x 1)

## Method

concept identification

A built-up city centre that is measurably warmer than its surroundings is the textbook definition of the urban heat island effect.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)
