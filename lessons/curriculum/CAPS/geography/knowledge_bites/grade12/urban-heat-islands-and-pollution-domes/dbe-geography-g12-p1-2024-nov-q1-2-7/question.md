## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to two sketches of an urban skyline under a pollution dome. Sketch A: a high arched dome over the urban area with arrows pointing upwards from the buildings — pollutants rising and dispersing. Sketch B: a low flattened dome close over the buildings with arrows pointing downwards — pollutants trapped near the surface. Write only A or B.) Denser concentration of pollutants over the urban area

## Method

sketch matching

In sketch B the flattened night-time dome traps pollutants beneath the inversion, so their concentration over the urban area is at its densest.
