## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to two sketches of an urban skyline under a pollution dome. Sketch A: a high arched dome over the urban area with arrows pointing upwards from the buildings — pollutants rising and dispersing. Sketch B: a low flattened dome close over the buildings with arrows pointing downwards — pollutants trapped near the surface. Write only A or B.) Represents a pollution dome at night

## Method

sketch matching

At night the urban surface cools, the air becomes stable and an inversion forms close to the ground, so the pollution dome flattens and pollutants are pressed down towards the surface — sketch B.
