## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to two sketches of an urban skyline under a pollution dome. Sketch A: a high arched dome over the urban area with arrows pointing upwards from the buildings — pollutants rising and dispersing. Sketch B: a low flattened dome close over the buildings with arrows pointing downwards — pollutants trapped near the surface. Write only A or B.) Pollutants are dispersed

## Method

sketch matching

Sketch A shows arrows rising from the buildings: daytime heating makes the air unstable, so pollutants are carried upwards, escape the dome and disperse.
