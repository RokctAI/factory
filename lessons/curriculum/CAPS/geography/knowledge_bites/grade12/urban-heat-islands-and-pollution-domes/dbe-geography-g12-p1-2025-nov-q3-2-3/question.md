## Question (2 marks)

Refer to area 8 on the orthophoto map. Explain why area 8 will experience lower temperatures than the surrounding built-up area. (1 x 2)

(Based on the 1 : 10 000 orthophoto map 3318 DD 18 STELLENBOSCH; area 8 is a vegetated, natural area within the town.)

## Method

explain reason

## Memo working

This is urban-climate reasoning in reverse: the built-up area is warm for the usual heat-island reasons, so a green space inside it stays cooler. The vegetation in area 8 cools the air through evapotranspiration and provides shade; its natural surfaces absorb less heat than tarmac and concrete; there are fewer buildings to absorb and re-radiate heat, fewer human activities generating heat, and better air movement to cool the area. A full two-mark answer must complete the explanation (for example "presence of vegetation results in evapotranspiration") — naming the factor alone (for example just "presence of vegetation") earns one mark at most.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Presence of vegetation results in evapotranspiration (2); Presence of vegetation provides shade (2); Natural surfaces absorb less heat (2); Less buildings/built up areas to absorb heat (2); Fewer human activities that generate heat (2); Better air movement cools the area (2) [ANY ONE] [PART MARKING: Presence of vegetation (1); Natural surfaces (1); Less buildings/built up areas (1); Fewer human activities (1); Better air movement (1); MAXIMUM ONE MARK]
