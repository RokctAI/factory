## Question (2 marks)

Map interpretation (refer to F in block C2, in the centre of Ladysmith, on the topographical map): (b) Give ONE reason why the high density of buildings at F generally contributes to the higher temperatures. (1 x 2)

## Method

explain reason

Densely packed buildings trap outgoing heat between their walls, block the wind that would carry warm air away, and replace natural surfaces with artificial ones that absorb and re-radiate heat.
