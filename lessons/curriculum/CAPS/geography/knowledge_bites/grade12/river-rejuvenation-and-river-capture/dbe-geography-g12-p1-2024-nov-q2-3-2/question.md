## Question (2 marks)

Determine the stream order of X in drainage pattern A. (In pattern A, streams radiate outwards from a central high-lying area; the stream marked X is joined by a short unbranched headwater.) (1 x 2)

## Method

stream order

Stream ordering starts at 1 for an unbranched headwater stream. When two first-order streams join, the stream below the junction becomes second order. X lies below the point where a short unbranched (first-order) headwater joins another first-order stream, so X is a second-order stream.
