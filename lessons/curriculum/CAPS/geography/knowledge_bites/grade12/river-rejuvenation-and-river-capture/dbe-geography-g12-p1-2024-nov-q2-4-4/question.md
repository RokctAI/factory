## Question (2 marks)

Describe the change that river rejuvenation has made to the landscape at B. (In the sketch, B points to the deepened, narrow inner valley cut below the old flood plain.) (1 x 2)

## Method

describe landform change

At B the rejuvenated river has cut a new, deeper and narrower valley into the floor of its old valley, producing the classic valley-in-a-valley form.
