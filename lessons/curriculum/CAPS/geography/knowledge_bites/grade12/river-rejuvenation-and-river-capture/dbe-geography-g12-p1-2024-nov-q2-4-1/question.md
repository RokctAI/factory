## Question (2 marks)

Define the term river rejuvenation. (Refer to a sketch on river rejuvenation: a block diagram of a river valley with stepped benches labelled 'River terraces' flanking both sides; A points to a break/step in the river's long profile where the river drops into a newly incised inner valley; B points to the deepened, narrow inner valley cut below the old flood plain.) (1 x 2)

## Method

definition

Rejuvenation means the river is 'made young again': something (such as a drop in base level or uplift) gives the river extra energy, and it switches back to vertical erosion, cutting down into its own old valley floor.
