## Question (4 marks)

Why are human activities limited in areas where drainage patterns A (radial) and B (trellis) are found? (2 x 2)

## Method

explain reason

Both patterns signal difficult terrain: radial drainage means a steep central upland, and trellis drainage means folded ridge-and-valley country. Steep, uneven land with thin, erodible soils is expensive to develop and risky to farm or build on.
