## Question (1 mark)

The physical (natural) impact of river capture on the captor stream is that the … and …  (i) drainage basin decreases  (ii) river rejuvenation takes place  (iii) deposition increases  (iv) water flows faster  A (i) and (iv)  B (i) and (iii)  C (ii) and (iv)  D (ii) and (iii)  (Two block diagrams on river capture. Sketch A, BEFORE RIVER CAPTURE: river X on the left, lower and more deeply incised, and river Y on the right flow roughly parallel, separated by a ridge. Sketch B, AFTER RIVER CAPTURE: the headwaters of river Y now bend sharply at an elbow of capture into river X's valley; the lower part of river Y is left as a smaller stream in its old wide valley. Choose the answer A-D.)

## Method

fluvial mcq

## Memo working

The captor stream gains water and energy: its erosive power is renewed (river rejuvenation takes place) and the extra volume makes the water flow faster. Its drainage basin grows rather than decreases, and erosion, not deposition, increases - so (ii) and (iv) are correct. Answer C.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C ((ii) river rejuvenation takes place and (iv) water flows faster)
