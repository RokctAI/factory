## Question (1 mark)

Identify the feature at A. (In the river rejuvenation sketch, A points to a break/step in the river's long profile where the river drops into a newly incised inner valley.) (1 x 1)

## Method

feature identification

A marks a sharp break or step in the river's long profile, where the smooth old profile meets the newly incised one. That break is the knickpoint, often visible as a waterfall.
