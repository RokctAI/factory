## Question (1 mark)

A river that has its water diverted into another river is referred to as a …  A captured stream.  B stream piracy.  C captor stream.  D headward stream.  (Two block diagrams on river capture. Sketch A, BEFORE RIVER CAPTURE: river X on the left, lower and more deeply incised, and river Y on the right flow roughly parallel, separated by a ridge. Sketch B, AFTER RIVER CAPTURE: the headwaters of river Y now bend sharply at an elbow of capture into river X's valley; the lower part of river Y is left as a smaller stream in its old wide valley. Choose the answer A-D.)

## Method

fluvial mcq

## Memo working

Keep the terms apart: stream piracy names the whole process, the captor stream is the river that steals the water, and the captured stream is the river that loses its water to another. A river that has its water diverted into another river is the captured stream - answer A.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A (captured stream)
