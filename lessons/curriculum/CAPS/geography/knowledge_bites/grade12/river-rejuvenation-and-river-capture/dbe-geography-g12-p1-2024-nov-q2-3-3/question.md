## Question (2 marks)

Give a reason for the direction in which streams flow in drainage pattern A (streams radiating outwards in all directions from a central high-lying area). (1 x 2)

## Method

explain reason

In a radial pattern the streams rise on a central high-lying area, and water can only flow downslope, so the streams radiate outwards from that central high point in all directions.
