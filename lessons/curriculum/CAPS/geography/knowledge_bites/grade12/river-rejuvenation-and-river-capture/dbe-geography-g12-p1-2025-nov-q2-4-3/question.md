## Question (4 marks)

Refer to photograph B and cross-section E-F. How does the process of river rejuvenation lead to the formation of incised meanders? (2 x 2)

(Photograph B shows an incised meander: a river loops around a high, steep-sided wooded spur in a deep gorge; the cross-section E-F drawn across the valley shows a deep valley with steep sides and a relatively flat floor.)

## Method

process explanation

## Memo working

Rejuvenation gives the river renewed erosive energy, so it starts cutting downwards again. This vertical (downward) erosion happens while the river keeps its existing meandering course, so the meander loops are cut deeper and deeper into the landscape. The result is the deep, steep-sided channel seen in cross-section E-F: the meander has become incised into a gorge-like valley.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

River rejuvenation results in vertical (downward) erosion (2); Results in a deeper stream channel/valley (due to vertical erosion) (2)
