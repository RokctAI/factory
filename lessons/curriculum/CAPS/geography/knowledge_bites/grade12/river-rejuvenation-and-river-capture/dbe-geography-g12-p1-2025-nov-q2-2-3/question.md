## Question (1 mark)

Evidence that river capture has taken place in sketch B:  A River Y has more water flowing in it.  B River Y has captured the headwaters of river X.  C River X has captured the headwaters of river Y.  D River X has a smaller drainage basin.  (Two block diagrams on river capture. Sketch A, BEFORE RIVER CAPTURE: river X on the left, lower and more deeply incised, and river Y on the right flow roughly parallel, separated by a ridge. Sketch B, AFTER RIVER CAPTURE: the headwaters of river Y now bend sharply at an elbow of capture into river X's valley; the lower part of river Y is left as a smaller stream in its old wide valley. Choose the answer A-D.)

## Method

fluvial mcq

## Memo working

In sketch B the headwaters of river Y now bend at the elbow of capture into river X's valley - X has taken Y's headwaters. So the evidence is C: River X has captured the headwaters of river Y.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C (River X has captured the headwaters of river Y)
