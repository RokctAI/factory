## Question (4 marks)

Why are some river terraces not suitable for farming? (2 x 2)

## Method

explain reason

Terraces stand above the new river level, so they are dry, often narrow benches with thin soils on unstable, uneven edges - awkward and expensive land to irrigate, reach and work with machinery.
