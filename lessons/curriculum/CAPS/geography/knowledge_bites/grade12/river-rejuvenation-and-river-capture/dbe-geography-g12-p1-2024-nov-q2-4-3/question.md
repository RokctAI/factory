## Question (2 marks)

How does feature A (the break/step in the river's long profile) indicate that river rejuvenation has taken place? (1 x 2)

## Method

explain reason

The knickpoint is direct evidence of rejuvenation: it marks a sudden change in gradient where the new, steeper profile is eroding vertically headwards into the old, gentler profile.
