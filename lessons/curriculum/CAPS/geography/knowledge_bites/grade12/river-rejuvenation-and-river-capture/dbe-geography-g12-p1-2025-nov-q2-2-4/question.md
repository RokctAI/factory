## Question (1 mark)

The cause of river capture, as shown in sketch B, is …  A headward erosion by river Y.  B lateral erosion by river X.  C headward erosion by river X.  D vertical erosion by river Y.  (Two block diagrams on river capture. Sketch A, BEFORE RIVER CAPTURE: river X on the left, lower and more deeply incised, and river Y on the right flow roughly parallel, separated by a ridge. Sketch B, AFTER RIVER CAPTURE: the headwaters of river Y now bend sharply at an elbow of capture into river X's valley; the lower part of river Y is left as a smaller stream in its old wide valley. Choose the answer A-D.)

## Method

fluvial mcq

## Memo working

River X sits at a lower level and erodes headward - its source cuts backwards through the ridge until it intersects river Y's channel and diverts the headwaters. The cause is headward erosion by river X - answer C.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C (headward erosion by river X)
