## Question (2 marks)

How does the joining of the tributaries to the mainstream differ in drainage patterns A and B? (Pattern A: streams radiate outwards from a central high-lying area. Pattern B: parallel main streams with short tributaries joining at right angles in a rectangular/lattice arrangement.) (1 x 2)

## Method

compare patterns

Listen for the angle at the junctions: in the radial pattern A the tributaries meet the main streams at sharp, acute angles, while in the trellis pattern B the short tributaries join the parallel main streams at right angles.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The tributaries join the main river at acute angles at A and at right angles at B (2)
