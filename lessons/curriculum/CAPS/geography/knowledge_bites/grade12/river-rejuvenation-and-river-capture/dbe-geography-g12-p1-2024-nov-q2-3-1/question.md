## Question (1 mark)

Identify drainage pattern A. (Refer to drainage patterns A and B: in pattern A, streams radiate outwards in all directions from a central high-lying area, like spokes of a wheel; one stream in the lower left is marked X with a short unbranched headwater joining it. In pattern B, parallel main streams have short tributaries joining them at right angles, forming a rectangular/lattice arrangement.) (1 x 1)

## Method

pattern identification

In pattern A the streams run outwards in all directions from a central high point, like the spokes of a wheel. That is the radial drainage pattern, typical of a dome or volcanic cone.
