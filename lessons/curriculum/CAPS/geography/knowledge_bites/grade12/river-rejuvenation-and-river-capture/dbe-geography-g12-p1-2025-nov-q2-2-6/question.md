## Question (1 mark)

River X has more erosive power after river capture because of …  A a greater volume of water and turbulent stream flow.  B a greater volume of water and laminar flow.  C headward erosion and turbulent stream flow.  D rejuvenation and laminar flow.  (Two block diagrams on river capture. Sketch A, BEFORE RIVER CAPTURE: river X on the left, lower and more deeply incised, and river Y on the right flow roughly parallel, separated by a ridge. Sketch B, AFTER RIVER CAPTURE: the headwaters of river Y now bend sharply at an elbow of capture into river X's valley; the lower part of river Y is left as a smaller stream in its old wide valley. Choose the answer A-D.)

## Method

fluvial mcq

## Memo working

The captor gains the captured headwaters, so its volume increases; a greater volume flowing in the same channel makes the flow more turbulent, and turbulent flow picks up and drags more load. Answer A - a greater volume of water and turbulent stream flow.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A (a greater volume of water and turbulent stream flow)
