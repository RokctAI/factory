## Question (4 marks)

State TWO conditions associated with the underlying rock that contributed to the formation of trellis drainage pattern B. (2 x 2)

## Method

rock structure conditions

A trellis pattern develops on folded, tilted rock where bands of hard and soft rock alternate. Main streams follow the soft-rock valleys while short tributaries cut down the hard ridges at right angles.
