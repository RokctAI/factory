## Question (1 mark)

Rivers flowing over areas with a … are more likely to be the captor stream.  A gentle gradient and soft rock  B steep gradient and low rainfall  C steep gradient and soft rock  D heavy rainfall and resistant rock  (Two block diagrams on river capture. Sketch A, BEFORE RIVER CAPTURE: river X on the left, lower and more deeply incised, and river Y on the right flow roughly parallel, separated by a ridge. Sketch B, AFTER RIVER CAPTURE: the headwaters of river Y now bend sharply at an elbow of capture into river X's valley; the lower part of river Y is left as a smaller stream in its old wide valley. Choose the answer A-D.)

## Method

fluvial mcq

## Memo working

The captor needs the most erosive energy: a steep gradient gives fast flow and strong headward erosion, and soft rock erodes easily, letting it cut back through the ridge. Answer C - steep gradient and soft rock.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C (steep gradient and soft rock)
