## Question (1 mark)

The part of the river that has less water after river capture is known as the … stream.  A captor  B non-perennial  C perennial  D misfit  (Two block diagrams on river capture. Sketch A, BEFORE RIVER CAPTURE: river X on the left, lower and more deeply incised, and river Y on the right flow roughly parallel, separated by a ridge. Sketch B, AFTER RIVER CAPTURE: the headwaters of river Y now bend sharply at an elbow of capture into river X's valley; the lower part of river Y is left as a smaller stream in its old wide valley. Choose the answer A-D.)

## Method

fluvial mcq

## Memo working

After capture the beheaded lower section of river Y has lost its headwaters, so it carries far less water than the wide valley it occupies - it is a misfit stream. Answer D.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

D (misfit)
