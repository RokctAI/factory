## Question (4 marks)

Refer to the river terraces in the sketch (stepped benches flanking both sides of the rejuvenated river valley). How do river terraces form? (2 x 2)

## Method

process explanation

Picture the old flood plain: renewed vertical erosion slices a new valley into it, so remnants of that old flood plain are left standing as paired flat 'steps' or benches above the new channel - those steps are the river terraces.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Vertical erosion occurs on the flood plain (2)
- A new river valley is formed (2)
- This creates a step-like feature (known as a terrace) (2)
- The old flood plain is now the river terrace (2)
