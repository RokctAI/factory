## Question (2 marks)

Calculate the difference in height between trigonometrical station F in block C3 and trigonometrical station G in block D3. (2 x 1)

(Based on the 1 : 50 000 topographical map 3318DD STELLENBOSCH. The map sheet is a separate physical sheet; heights as read for the memo: trigonometrical station F = 1167,5 m, trigonometrical station G = 928,0 m.)

## Method

height difference calculation

## Memo working

Read the height printed next to each trigonometrical station on the map: station F in block C3 is 1167,5 m above sea level and station G in block D3 is 928,0 m. The difference in height is the higher value minus the lower value: 1167,5 - 928,0 = 239,5 m. One mark is for the correct subtraction of the two map-read heights, one mark for the answer.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

1167,5 m - 928,0 m (1) = 239,5 m (1)
