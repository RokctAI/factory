## Question (1 mark)

The natural feature found at 33°58'29"S; 18°54'15"E is a …

A trigonometrical station.
B perennial river.
C saddle.
D spot height. (1 x 1)

(Based on the 1 : 50 000 topographical map 3318DD STELLENBOSCH: at these coordinates the contour pattern shows a lower area between two higher points — a saddle. Note that a trigonometrical station and a spot height are map symbols, not natural features.)

grid reference reading

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C (saddle) (1)
