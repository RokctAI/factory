## Question (1 mark)

Match the average gradient (answer to QUESTION 3.1.3) with sketch A or B below. (1 x 1)

(Two small slope-profile sketches: A - a short, steep concave slope profile dropping quickly to the base. B - a longer, gentler stepped slope profile descending gradually. The answer to QUESTION 3.1.3 was a gradient of 1 : 6,33.)

## Method

gradient matching

## Memo working

A gradient of 1 : 6,33 means the land rises 1 m for every 6,33 m travelled horizontally — a steep slope. Of the two sketches, the short, steep concave profile A matches a steep gradient; the long, gentle stepped profile B would correspond to a much larger ratio (a gentler slope).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A (1)
