## Question (5 marks)

Calculate the average gradient between contour line O (height 820 m) and the trigonometrical station at P on the topographic map. Show ALL calculations. Marks will be awarded for calculations. Formula: Average gradient = vertical interval (VI) / horizontal equivalent (HE).

## Method

gradient calculation

## Memo working

VI = 1057,9 m - 820 m = 237,9 m; HE = 3,9 cm x 50 000 = 195 000 cm = 1 950 m; gradient = 237,9 / 1 950 = 1 : 8,2. Memo accepts measurement 3,8-4 cm (HE 1 900-2 000 m) and final answers 1 : 7,9 to 1 : 8,4.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

1 : 8,2
