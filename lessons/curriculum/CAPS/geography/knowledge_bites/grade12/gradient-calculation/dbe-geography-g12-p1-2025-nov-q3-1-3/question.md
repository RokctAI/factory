## Question (4 marks)

Calculate the average gradient from 6 in block E5 to 7 in block D5 on the orthophoto map. Use the following information: Vertical interval (VI) = 60 m. Formula: Vertical Interval (VI) / Horizontal Equivalent (HE) (4 x 1)

(Based on the 1 : 10 000 orthophoto map 3318 DD 18 STELLENBOSCH. The map sheet is a separate physical sheet; the distance between points 6 and 7 measured off the map for the memo is 3,8 cm, with 3,7 cm - 3,9 cm accepted.)

## Method

gradient calculation

## Memo working

The vertical interval is given: VI = 60 m. The horizontal equivalent is the real ground distance between the two points. Measure the map distance — 3,8 cm (1) — and convert it using the orthophoto scale of 1 : 10 000, where 1 cm on the map is 100 m on the ground: HE = 3,8 x 100 = 380 (1) m [measurement range 3,7 cm - 3,9 cm, so 370 m - 390 m]. Substitute into the formula: Gradient = VI/HE = 60/380 (1) (mark for correct substitution). Divide both sides by 60 to express it as one metre of climb per so many metres of distance: 1 : 6,33 (1) [accepted range 1 : 6,16 - 1 : 6,50].

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

1 : 6,33 (1) [accepted range 1:6,16 - 1:6,50]
