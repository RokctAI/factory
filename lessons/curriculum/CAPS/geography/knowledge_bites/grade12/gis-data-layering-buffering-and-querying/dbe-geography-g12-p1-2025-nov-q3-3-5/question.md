## Question (1 mark)

Which photograph, A or B, has fewer pixels? (1 x 1)

(Two aerial images of the Danie Craven Stadium, extracted from block D1/D2 on the orthophoto map: A is a coarse, blocky, pixelated image in which individual large pixels are visible and detail is blurred; B is a sharp, detailed satellite image in which the stadium, stands, field markings and surrounding trees are clear.)

## Method

gis concepts

## Memo working

Fewer pixels over the same area means each pixel must be larger, so the image looks blocky and blurred — low resolution. That is image A. Image B, with many small pixels, is sharp and detailed — high resolution.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A (1)
