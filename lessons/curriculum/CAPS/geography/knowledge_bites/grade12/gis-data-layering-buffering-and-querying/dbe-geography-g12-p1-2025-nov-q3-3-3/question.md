## Question (2 marks)

Refer to area 9 in block A3 on the orthophoto map. Using data manipulation, explain how you would manipulate the scale at area 9 to make the image clearer. (1 x 2)

(Based on the 1 : 10 000 orthophoto map 3318 DD 18 STELLENBOSCH.)

## Method

gis application

## Memo working

Enlarging the scale (zooming in) means each ground feature is drawn bigger on the screen or page, so the detail at area 9 becomes easier to see. Remember that a larger scale is a scale showing a smaller area in more detail — for example 1 : 5 000 is larger than 1 : 10 000.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Make the scale larger (2)
