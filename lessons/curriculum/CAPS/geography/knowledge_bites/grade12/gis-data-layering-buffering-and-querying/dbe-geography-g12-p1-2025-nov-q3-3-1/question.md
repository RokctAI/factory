## Question (1 mark)

The number 310, labelled M on the main road in block A3 on the topographical map, is referred to as … data.

A spatial
B vector
C attribute
D numerical (1 x 1)

(Based on the 1 : 50 000 topographical map 3318DD STELLENBOSCH: 310 is the route number printed on the main road.)

## Method

gis concepts

## Memo working

In GIS terms, the road's position and shape on the map are spatial data, but the route number 310 describes a property of the road — information about the feature, not its location. Descriptive information attached to a spatial feature is attribute data.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C (attribute) (1)
