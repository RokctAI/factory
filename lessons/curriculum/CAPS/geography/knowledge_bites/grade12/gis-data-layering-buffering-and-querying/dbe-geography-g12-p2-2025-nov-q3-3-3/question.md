## Question (3 marks)

Refer to the GIS sketch of three stacked map layers: the top layer, labelled 'Vegetation layer' A, shows shaded woodland patches and a row of trees drawn as a line of dots; a plus sign links it to the middle layer B, which shows a single feature labelled 'Non-perennial water'; an equals sign links to the bottom layer, labelled 'Data layering' C, which is an empty frame. Also refer to block E3 on the 1 : 50 000 topographical map 2529CC eMALAHLENI (WITBANK).

Redraw layer C in the sketch in your ANSWER BOOK. Apply the data layers of A and B to the sketch. (3 x 1)

## Method

layer composite sketch

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

(Reasoning: data layering means overlaying the layers, so the redrawn frame C must show everything from layer A and layer B together, each feature kept in its original position.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A single redrawn frame combining, in their original positions:

- the woodland patches from layer A (1)
- the row of trees (the line of dots) from layer A (1)
- the non-perennial water feature from layer B (1)

(Marking: 1 mark for the row of trees; 1 mark for the woodland; 1 mark for the non-perennial water.)
