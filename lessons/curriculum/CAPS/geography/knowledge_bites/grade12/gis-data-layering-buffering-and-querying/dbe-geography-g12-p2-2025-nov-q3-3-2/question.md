## Question (1 mark)

Refer to the GIS sketch of three stacked map layers: the top layer, labelled 'Vegetation layer' A, shows shaded woodland patches and a row of trees drawn as a line of dots; a plus sign links it to the middle layer B, which shows a single feature labelled 'Non-perennial water'; an equals sign links to the bottom layer, labelled 'Data layering' C, which is an empty frame. Also refer to block E3 on the 1 : 50 000 topographical map 2529CC eMALAHLENI (WITBANK).

Name the data layer at B (in the sketch) as it is represented on the topographical map. (1 x 1)

## Method

layer identification

(see marking guidelines)

(Reasoning: layer B shows non-perennial water; water features — rivers, dams, pans — are represented on a topographical map as the drainage layer.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)
