## Question (2 marks)

Give a reason for your answer to QUESTION 3.3.5. (1 x 2)

(QUESTION 3.3.5 asked which of two aerial images of the Danie Craven Stadium has fewer pixels; the answer was A, the coarse, blocky image in which individual large pixels are visible and detail is blurred.)

## Method

explain reason

## Memo working

Connect pixel count to what the eye sees. With fewer pixels covering the same area, each pixel is large, so the image shows less detail and appears blurry, unclear or distorted — in other words, the clarity of the photograph is poor because the resolution is low. Any one of these observations, fully stated, earns the two marks.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The clarity of the photograph is poor (2); There is low resolution (2); It shows less details (2); Image is blurry/unclear/distorted (2); Large pixels (2) [ANY ONE]
