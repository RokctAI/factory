## Question (1 mark)

(Valley climates. Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) … forms when the dew point temperature of air is below freezing point on the valley floor.  A Mist  B Fog  C Frost  D Smog

## Method

photo mcq

The deciding phrase is "below freezing point". Cold katabatic air ponds on the valley floor at night; when the air is cooled to its dew point and that dew point is below zero degrees Celsius, the water vapour is deposited directly as ice crystals on the ground and on plants — that is frost. Mist and fog are droplets of liquid water in the air, and smog is fog mixed with pollution, so none of them require freezing temperatures.
