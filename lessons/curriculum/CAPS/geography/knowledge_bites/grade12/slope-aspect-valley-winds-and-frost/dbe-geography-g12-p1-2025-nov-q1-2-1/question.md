## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to a photograph on slope aspect: a valley labelled SOUTH at the top left and NORTH at the top right. Slope A, on the left, is densely covered in trees; slope B, on the right, is largely bare grassy hillside with only scattered trees. Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) Slope aspect refers to …  A upslope movement of air in a valley.  B climate on a smaller scale.  C a zone of warmer air in the valley.  D slope direction in relation to the sun.

## Method

photo mcq

This is a definition question. Slope aspect is the direction a slope faces in relation to the sun's rays — it determines how much direct sunlight the slope receives. Upslope air movement is an anabatic wind, climate on a smaller scale is a microclimate, and a zone of warmer air in the valley is the thermal belt, so only option D matches the definition.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)
