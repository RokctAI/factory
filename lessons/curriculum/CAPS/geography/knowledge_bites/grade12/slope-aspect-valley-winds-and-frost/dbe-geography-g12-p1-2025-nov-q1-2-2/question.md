## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to a photograph on slope aspect: a valley labelled SOUTH at the top left and NORTH at the top right. Slope A, on the left, is densely covered in trees; slope B, on the right, is largely bare grassy hillside with only scattered trees. Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) The photograph represents a valley in the … Hemisphere.  A Northern  B Eastern  C Southern  D Western

## Method

photo mcq

Use the labelled directions together with the vegetation contrast. In the Southern Hemisphere the midday sun is in the northern sky, so the slope that catches the direct sun is warmer and drier while the slope turned away from the northern sun stays cooler and moister. Matching the SOUTH and NORTH labels in the photograph against which slope is densely vegetated and which is bare gives the pattern of a Southern Hemisphere valley, as the marking guidelines confirm.
