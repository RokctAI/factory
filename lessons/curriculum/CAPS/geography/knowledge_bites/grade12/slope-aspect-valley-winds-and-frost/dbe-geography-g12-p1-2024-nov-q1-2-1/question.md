## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to two V-shaped valley cross-section sketches. Sketch A: arrows labelled 'Cold air' point down both valley slopes towards the valley floor. Sketch B: arrows labelled 'Warm air' point up the valley slopes away from the valley floor. Write only A or B.) Represents an anabatic wind

## Method

sketch matching

An anabatic wind is a daytime up-slope valley breeze: the sun heats the valley slopes, the air in contact with them warms and rises up the slopes. Sketch B shows warm air moving up the slopes away from the valley floor.
