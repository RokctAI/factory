## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to a photograph on slope aspect: a valley labelled SOUTH at the top left and NORTH at the top right. Slope A, on the left, is densely covered in trees; slope B, on the right, is largely bare grassy hillside with only scattered trees. Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) Slope B represents a … and … slope.  (i) cooler  (ii) warmer  (iii) moist  (iv) drier  A (i) and (ii)  B (ii) and (iii)  C (i) and (iii)  D (ii) and (iv)

## Method

photo mcq

In the Southern Hemisphere the sun shines from the north, so the slope facing away from the northern sun receives less direct sunlight and stays cooler and moister. The marking guidelines identify slope B in the photograph as that shaded slope, so it is the cooler and moist slope — options (i) and (iii), answer C.

**Note (transcription caveat from the memo): the accepted answer is C — in the photograph the tree-covered slope faces one way and the bare slope B is labelled cooler and moist per the marking guidelines. Learners are marked on the memo's identification of slope B as the cooler, moist slope, even though bare grassy slopes are often the warmer, drier ones in aspect photographs.**
