## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to two V-shaped valley cross-section sketches. Sketch A: arrows labelled 'Cold air' point down both valley slopes towards the valley floor. Sketch B: arrows labelled 'Warm air' point up the valley slopes away from the valley floor. Write only A or B.) Frost forms on the valley floor when the temperature drops below 0 °C

## Method

sketch matching

At night cold air drains down the slopes and accumulates on the valley floor. If that pooled air cools below 0 °C, frost forms on the floor — the situation in sketch A.
