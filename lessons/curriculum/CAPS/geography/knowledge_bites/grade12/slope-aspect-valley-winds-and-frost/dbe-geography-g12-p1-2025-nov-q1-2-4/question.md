## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to a photograph on valley winds: a mountain valley at night, with a crescent moon drawn at the top right. Broad white arrows labelled A point down the valley slopes from both sides towards the valley floor, where a train and trees are visible. Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) The arrows at A indicate a/an … wind.  A anabatic  B katabatic  C upslope  D onshore

## Method

photo mcq

Two clues: it is night, and the arrows move down the slopes towards the valley floor. At night the slopes lose heat through terrestrial radiation; the air in contact with them cools, becomes denser and drains downslope. A downslope night-time valley wind is a katabatic wind. Anabatic and upslope both describe the daytime up-valley breeze, and an onshore wind is a sea breeze at the coast.
