## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to two V-shaped valley cross-section sketches. Sketch A: arrows labelled 'Cold air' point down both valley slopes towards the valley floor. Sketch B: arrows labelled 'Warm air' point up the valley slopes away from the valley floor. Write only A or B.) Cold air forces the warm air to rise which forms an inversion layer

## Method

sketch matching

In sketch A cold, dense air drains down the slopes (a katabatic wind) and pools on the valley floor, pushing the warmer, lighter air up above it. Warm air lying on top of cold air is a temperature inversion.
