## Question (1 mark)

(Valley climates; the answer to QUESTION 1.2.6 was frost. Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) The negative physical (natural) impact of the answer to QUESTION 1.2.6 is that it …  A reduces visibility.  B increases pollution.  C increases rainfall.  D destroys vegetation.

## Method

photo mcq

The question asks for the negative impact of frost on the physical, natural environment. Frost freezes the water inside plant tissue, killing crops and natural vegetation — so it destroys vegetation. Reduced visibility is an effect of mist or fog, increased pollution relates to smog trapped under an inversion, and frost has no rain-making effect.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

D (destroys vegetation)
