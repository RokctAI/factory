## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to a photograph on valley winds: a mountain valley at night, with broad white arrows labelled A pointing down the valley slopes from both sides towards the valley floor. The arrows at A indicate a katabatic wind. Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) The main conditions for the formation of the wind at A are … and …  (i) terrestrial radiation  (ii) solar radiation  (iii) low temperatures  (iv) high temperatures  A (i) and (iii)  B (i) and (iv)  C (ii) and (iii)  D (ii) and (iv)

## Method

photo mcq

A katabatic wind forms at night. The ground loses the day's heat through terrestrial radiation, so temperatures on the slopes drop. The cold, dense air next to the slopes then sinks and drains down to the valley floor. The two required conditions are therefore terrestrial radiation and low temperatures — options (i) and (iii). Solar radiation and high temperatures drive the opposite, daytime anabatic wind.
