## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to two V-shaped valley cross-section sketches. Sketch A: arrows labelled 'Cold air' point down both valley slopes towards the valley floor. Sketch B: arrows labelled 'Warm air' point up the valley slopes away from the valley floor. Write only A or B.) Occurs during the day due to insolation

## Method

sketch matching

Insolation is incoming solar radiation, so this is a daytime process: the heated slopes warm the air, which rises up the valley sides as an anabatic wind — sketch B.
