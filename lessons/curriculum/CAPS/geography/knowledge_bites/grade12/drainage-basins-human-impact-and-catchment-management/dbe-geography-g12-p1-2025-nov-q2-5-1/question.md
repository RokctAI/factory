## Question (1 mark)

Refer to the extract below on catchment and river management.

IMPACT OF SETTLEMENTS ON RIVER MANAGEMENT: "Dense settlements that locate along rivers have a negative impact on rivers. The main reason is that these settlements generally receive poor service provision. These poor communities also do not have the means to pay for the provision of services, such as waste removal. When large amounts of waste pile up in the settlement, the community is more likely to dispose of the waste in the river. This leads to further pollution, eventually leading to serious health risks for the community."

According to the extract, why are dense settlements along rivers associated with pollution? (1 x 1)

## Method

extract reading

## Memo working

The answer must come straight from the extract. The extract gives a chain of causes: the settlements receive poor service provision; the communities cannot pay for services such as waste removal; so waste piles up and is disposed of in the river. Any one link in that chain, quoted or paraphrased from the extract, earns the mark.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Poor service provision (1); Do not have means to pay for waste removal services (1); Piled up waste in the settlement is disposed of in the river (1) [ANY ONE]
