## Question (4 marks)

Explain the negative impact of poor river management on the health of rivers. (2 x 2)

## Method

impact explanation

## Memo working

Think of the river as a living system and trace what neglect does to it. Poorly managed rivers receive pollution and waste, so water quality drops. That degraded water damages the habitat of aquatic life and can destroy the organisms themselves, which in turn breaks food chains and food webs and disrupts whole ecosystems, causing a loss of biodiversity. Nutrient-rich pollution triggers eutrophication (algal blooms that starve the water of oxygen), and unmanaged catchments deliver increased sedimentation that chokes the channel. Any two of these impacts, fully explained, earn the four marks.
