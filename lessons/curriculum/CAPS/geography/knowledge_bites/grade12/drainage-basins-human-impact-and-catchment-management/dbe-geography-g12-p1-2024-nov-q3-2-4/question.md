## Question (2 marks)

Map interpretation (refer to the weir at I in block A3 on the 1 : 50 000 topographical map 2829DB LADYSMITH): A weir is used to regulate (control) the flow of water in a river. Explain why the construction of the weir was necessary. (1 x 2)

## Method

map interpretation

A weir controls flow. At I the river is near a confluence, with cultivated land, a built-up area, a railway line and a caravan park downstream, so regulating the flow reduces the flood risk to them.
