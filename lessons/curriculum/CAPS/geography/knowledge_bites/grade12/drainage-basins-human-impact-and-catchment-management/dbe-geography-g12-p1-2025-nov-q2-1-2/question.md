## Question (1 mark)

The ... in area B separates one stream from another.  [Y watershed / Z interfluve]  (Block diagram of a drainage basin from the mountains to the sea. Key: tree symbols = vegetation; solid line = permanent river; dashed line = periodic river. Labels: A - main permanent river from mountains to sea; B - high mountain ridge between two tributaries; C - clump of vegetation on the lower plain beside the river; D - solid-line tributary; E - circled point where a tributary joins another stream; F - circled point on a single stream with no junction; G - steep mountain slope; H - strongly meandering section on the flat lower plain; I - dashed-line river. Complete the statement by choosing Y or Z.)

## Method

column matching

## Memo working

B is the high ridge between two tributaries inside the same drainage basin. A ridge separating individual streams of the same system is an interfluve; a watershed separates one whole drainage basin from another. The answer is Z.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Z (interfluve)
