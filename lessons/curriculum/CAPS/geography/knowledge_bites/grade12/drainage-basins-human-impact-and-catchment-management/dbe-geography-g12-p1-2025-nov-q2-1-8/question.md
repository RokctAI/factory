## Question (1 mark)

The water table at I will resemble …  [Y / Z]  (Block diagram of a drainage basin from the mountains to the sea. Key: tree symbols = vegetation; solid line = permanent river; dashed line = periodic river. Labels include I - a dashed-line river. Options, from a figure with key dashed line = rainy season water table, dotted line = dry season water table: Y - a valley cross-section in which the rainy-season and dry-season water tables lie close together just below the surface, with the stream channel cutting below both; Z - a valley cross-section in which the rainy-season water table lies just below the valley floor and the dry-season water table lies much deeper, widely separated from it. Complete the statement by choosing Y or Z.)

## Method

column matching

## Memo working

I is a dashed-line river, so it is periodic: it flows only in the rainy season. That happens where the water table intersects the channel in the wet season but drops far below it in the dry season - a large seasonal drop, as sketch Z shows. In sketch Y the water table stays near the surface all year, which would feed a permanent river. The answer is Z.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Z (large seasonal drop in the water table, matching the periodic river at I)
