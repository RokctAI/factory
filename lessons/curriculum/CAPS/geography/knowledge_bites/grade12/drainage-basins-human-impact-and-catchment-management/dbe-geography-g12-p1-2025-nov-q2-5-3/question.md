## Question (2 marks)

Explain how the rise in the water level of the river will negatively affect the houses (in the photograph) due to their location. (1 x 2)

(The photograph shows an informal settlement with shacks built right up to the eroded banks on both sides of a river.)

## Method

impact explanation

## Memo working

Link the hazard to the location. The houses stand right on the river banks, so when the water level rises they are the first to be affected: being so close to the river, they could easily flood; and the rising water can undermine the already eroded banks, so the banks could collapse and destroy the houses. A full two-mark answer must make the located consequence explicit — a partial statement (only "houses are close to the river" or only "river banks could collapse") earns one mark at most.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Houses are close to the river and could easily flood (2); River banks could collapse destroying houses (accept examples) (2) [ANY ONE] [PART MARKING: Houses are close to the river (1); River banks could collapse (1); MAXIMUM ONE MARK]
