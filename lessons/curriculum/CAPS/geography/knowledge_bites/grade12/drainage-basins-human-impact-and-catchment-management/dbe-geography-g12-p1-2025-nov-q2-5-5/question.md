## Question (6 marks)

Suggest strategies that the municipality can put in place to ensure the sustainability of the river in the photograph. (3 x 2)

(The photograph shows an informal settlement with shacks built right up to the eroded banks on both sides of a river; the channel and banks are strewn with litter and solid waste.)

## Method

suggest strategies

## Memo working

Strategies must be realistic actions a municipality can take, and they group naturally. Managing the people and the land: relocate the people, create a buffer zone along the banks, and discourage building on the river bank. Changing behaviour: awareness campaigns, education on river management, community involvement, incentives, and encouraging recycling. Enforcement: implement legislation and impose fines. Managing the river environment directly: plant more vegetation, provide more refuse-removal facilities, build proper sanitation/drainage systems or water-treatment plants, maintain infrastructure, and monitor and test the river continuously. Any three well-stated strategies earn the six marks.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Relocate the people (2); Create a buffer zone (2); Create awareness campaigns (accept examples) (2); Educate people on river management (accept examples) (2); Encourage people not to build houses on the river bank (2); Implement legislation (2); Impose fines (2); Plant more vegetation (accept examples) (2); Provide more refuse removal facilities (accept examples) (2); Continuous monitoring/testing (2); Encourage community involvement (accept examples) (2); Provide incentives to people (accept examples) (2); Encourage the recycling of waste (2); Build proper sanitation/drainage systems/water treatment plants (2); Maintain infrastructure (2) [ANY THREE]
