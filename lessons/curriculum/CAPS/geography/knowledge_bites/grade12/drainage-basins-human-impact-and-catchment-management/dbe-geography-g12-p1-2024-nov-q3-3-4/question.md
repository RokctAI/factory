## Question (2 marks)

Geographical Information Systems (GIS) (refer to 12 in block C2 on the orthophoto map): Define the term buffering. (1 x 2)

## Method

definition

Buffering in GIS means demarcating a zone of a chosen width around or along a geographical feature, for example a strip along a river.
