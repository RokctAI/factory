## Question (4 marks)

How does the human activity (answer to QUESTION 2.5.2, agriculture) negatively impact the health (water quality) of a catchment area? (2 x 2)

## Method

impact explanation

Trace the chain from farm to river: fertiliser-rich run-off enters the water, nutrient levels become unbalanced, eutrophication uses up oxygen, and biodiversity, habitats and water quality all decline.
