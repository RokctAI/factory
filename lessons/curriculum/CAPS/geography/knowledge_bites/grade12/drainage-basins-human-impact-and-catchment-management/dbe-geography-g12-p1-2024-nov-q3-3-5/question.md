## Question (2 marks)

Geographical Information Systems (GIS): How would buffering at 12 (in block C2 on the orthophoto map, along the Klip River) protect the quality of the water in the Klip River? (1 x 2)

## Method

gis application

A buffer along the Klip River at 12 keeps development and pollution sources back from the banks, so less polluted run-off and eroded soil reach the river and its water quality and biodiversity are protected.
