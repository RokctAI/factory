## Question (2 marks)

Give TWO reasons from the photograph to support your answer to QUESTION 2.5.1. (2 x 1)

(The photograph shows an informal settlement with shacks built right up to the eroded banks on both sides of a river; the channel and banks are strewn with litter and solid waste. QUESTION 2.5.1 asked why dense settlements along rivers are associated with pollution — the extract's answer being poor service provision, no means to pay for waste removal, and waste being disposed of in the river.)

## Method

photo evidence

## Memo working

The evidence must be visible in the photograph. The most direct sign is the solid waste itself — litter strewn across the channel and banks. The photograph also shows no infrastructure for removing waste, and no sign of potable (safe drinking) water services. Any two of these visible observations earn the marks.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Evidence of solid waste (accept examples) (1); No infrastructure to remove waste (accept examples) (1); No evidence of potable water (1) [ANY TWO]
