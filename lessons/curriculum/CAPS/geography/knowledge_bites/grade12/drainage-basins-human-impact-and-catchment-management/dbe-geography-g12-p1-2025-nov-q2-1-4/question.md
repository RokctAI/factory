## Question (1 mark)

The river at D is a ... river.  [Y permanant / Z periodic]  (Block diagram of a drainage basin from the mountains to the sea. Key: tree symbols = vegetation; solid line = permanent river; dashed line = periodic river. Labels: A - main permanent river from mountains to sea; B - high mountain ridge between two tributaries; C - clump of vegetation on the lower plain beside the river; D - solid-line tributary; E - circled point where a tributary joins another stream; F - circled point on a single stream with no junction; G - steep mountain slope; H - strongly meandering section on the flat lower plain; I - dashed-line river. Complete the statement by choosing Y or Z.)

**Note:** The question paper prints option Y as 'permanant' - this spelling error appears in the original paper; the memo confirms the intended word is 'permanent'.

## Method

column matching

## Memo working

The key shows solid lines are permanent rivers and dashed lines are periodic rivers. D is drawn as a solid line, so it is a permanent river - answer Y.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Y (permanent)
