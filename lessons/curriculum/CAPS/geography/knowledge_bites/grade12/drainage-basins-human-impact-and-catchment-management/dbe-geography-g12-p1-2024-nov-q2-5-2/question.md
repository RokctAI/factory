Name ONE human activity in the extract that has a negative impact on South Africa's catchment areas. (The extract states that agricultural run-off can carry chemicals into river catchments, creating an imbalance in nutrient levels.) (1 x 1)

## Method

extract reading

Read the extract for a named activity with a negative impact: it says agricultural run-off carries chemicals into river catchments, so the activity is agriculture.
