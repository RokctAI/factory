## Question (1 mark)

The area at C will have a ... infiltration rate.  [Y low / Z high]  (Block diagram of a drainage basin from the mountains to the sea. Key: tree symbols = vegetation; solid line = permanent river; dashed line = periodic river. Labels: A - main permanent river from mountains to sea; B - high mountain ridge between two tributaries; C - clump of vegetation on the lower plain beside the river; D - solid-line tributary; E - circled point where a tributary joins another stream; F - circled point on a single stream with no junction; G - steep mountain slope; H - strongly meandering section on the flat lower plain; I - dashed-line river. Complete the statement by choosing Y or Z.)

## Method

column matching

## Memo working

C is a clump of vegetation. Plant cover slows water down and root channels open up the soil, so more water soaks in instead of running off. Infiltration at C is therefore high - answer Z.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Z (high)
