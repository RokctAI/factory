## Question (2 marks)

What is river management? (Refer to an extract on catchment and river management: river catchments are under severe pressure in some parts of South Africa; the fertile ground surrounding them is ideal for agricultural activities; toxins and heavy metals can leach into catchment areas and endanger flora and fauna; agricultural run-off can carry chemicals into river catchments, creating an imbalance in nutrient levels.) (1 x 2)

## Method

definition

River management is about looking after the water resource itself: the monitoring and controlling of the water resources within a drainage basin or catchment area.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Monitoring and controlling (management) of water resources in a drainage basin/catchment area (2) [CONCEPT]
