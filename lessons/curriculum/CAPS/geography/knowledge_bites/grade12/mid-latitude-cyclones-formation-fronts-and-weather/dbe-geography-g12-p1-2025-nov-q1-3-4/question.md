## Question (4 marks)

(Refer to photographs C and D on the impact of mid-latitude cyclones: C shows palm trees bent by gale-force winds along a wet coastal road; D shows vehicles driving through deep floodwater in a street. The stage named in QUESTION 1.3.3 is the mature stage.) Explain how the weather conditions illustrated in photographs C and D developed during the stage named in QUESTION 1.3.3. (2 x 2)

## Method

process explanation

Link each photograph to a mature-stage process. The bent trees come from the wind: in the mature stage the pressure gradient around the deep low is at its steepest, driving gale-force winds. The flooded street comes from the rain: the cold air behind the cold front undercuts the warm air ahead of it, forcing rapid uplift that builds cumulonimbus clouds and releases heavy rainfall. A full answer must mention both the gale-force or strong winds and the heavy rainfall, each explained by its process, for two marks apiece.
