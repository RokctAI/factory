## Question (4 marks)

(Refer to sketch B on the mid-latitude cyclone: a synoptic plan of a wave cyclone with a cold front E-F, marked with triangles, trailing south-west from the low centre, a warm front to the north-east, a warm-air arrow from the north-east, a cold-air arrow behind the cold front, and isobars labelled 1004 and 1008.) In the ANSWER BOOK, draw a fully labelled cross-section of a cold front (E-F) in sketch B. Clearly indicate the following: (a) Correct cross-section  (b) General direction of movement of the mid-latitude cyclone  (c) Cloud type  (d) Sector (4 x 1)

## Method

labelled sketch

Picture the cross-section as a slice through the cold front from E on the left to F on the right. Draw a steep frontal boundary: the cold, dense air forms a blunt wedge that undercuts the warm air. On the cold side of the front, label the cold sector with cumulus cloud; on the warm side, label the warm sector. Along the steep front itself, draw a towering cumulonimbus cloud with upward arrows showing the rapid uplift of warm air. Finally, add an arrow along the base pointing towards the east and label it easterly movement, because mid-latitude cyclones travel from west to east. One mark each for the correct cross-section shape, the direction of movement, a cloud type and a sector label.
