## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to photographs C and D on the impact of mid-latitude cyclones: C shows palm trees bent by gale-force winds along a wet coastal road; D shows vehicles driving through deep floodwater in a street.) Name the stage of development of the mid-latitude cyclone associated with weather conditions in photographs C and D. (1 x 1)

## Method

stage identification

The photographs show the cyclone at its most violent: gale-force winds strong enough to bend palm trees and rainfall heavy enough to flood streets. A mid-latitude cyclone produces its most intense weather when both fronts are fully developed and the pressure gradient is steepest — the mature stage.
