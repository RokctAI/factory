## Question (4 marks)

(Refer to sketch B on the mid-latitude cyclone: a synoptic plan of a wave cyclone with a cold front trailing south-west from the low centre, a warm front to the north-east, warm air flowing in from the north-east and cold air behind the cold front.) Refer to sketch B. Explain how the difference in temperature of the air masses behind the cold front and ahead of the warm front will result in the formation of a cold front occlusion. (2 x 2)

## Method

process explanation

Reason through the temperatures step by step. The air behind the advancing cold front is colder than the cool air ahead of the warm front. Because the cold front moves faster, it catches up with the warm front; being the coldest and densest air present, the air behind the cold front slides under both the warm sector and the cool air ahead of the warm front. The warmer, less dense air is lifted completely off the ground over the colder, denser air — producing a cold front occlusion. Two well-explained statements at two marks each earn the four marks.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- The air behind the cold front is colder than the air in front of the warm front (2)
- The cold air behind the cold front undercuts the warmer air in front of the warm front (2)
- The warmer (less dense air) is uplifted over the colder (denser) air (2)
