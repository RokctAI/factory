## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to a cross-section of a mid-latitude cyclone. A bold arrow labelled 'DIRECTION OF MOVEMENT' runs from west on the left to east on the right. On the left is a steeply curved front A with a towering cumulonimbus cloud, heavy rain shafts and rapid uplift, with air behind it at 12 °C. On the right is a gently sloping front B with small scattered clouds and light rain, with air below it at 8 °C.) In which general direction do mid-latitude cyclones move? (1 x 1)

## Method

diagram interpretation

Mid-latitude cyclones form and travel in the westerly wind belt between roughly 30 and 60 degrees of latitude, so the whole system is steered from west to east — the cross-section's direction-of-movement arrow confirms this.
