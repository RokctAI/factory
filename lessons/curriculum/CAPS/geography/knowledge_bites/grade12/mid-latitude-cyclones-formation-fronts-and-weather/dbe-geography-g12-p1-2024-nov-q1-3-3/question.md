## Question (4 marks)

(Refer to a cross-section of a mid-latitude cyclone. A bold arrow labelled 'DIRECTION OF MOVEMENT' runs from west on the left to east on the right. On the left is a steeply curved front A with a towering cumulonimbus cloud, heavy rain shafts and rapid uplift, with air behind it at 12 °C. On the right is a gently sloping front B with small scattered clouds and light rain, with air below it at 8 °C.) How does front A give rise to the formation of cumulonimbus clouds? (2 x 2)

## Method

process explanation

Front A is the cold front. Follow the sequence verbally: the dense cold air drives in underneath the warm air ahead of it; the steep frontal slope forces that warm air upward rapidly; and the rising air cools to dew point, so condensation builds towering cumulonimbus cloud.
