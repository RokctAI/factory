## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to sketch A on the mid-latitude cyclone: a plan view at 60°S where warm air moving east meets cold air moving west along the boundary between the two air masses — the polar front.) Give ONE reason why the boundary between the two air masses in sketch A would form a wave. (1 x 1)

## Method

explain reason

The polar front is only a smooth line while the two air flows stay undisturbed. Any disturbance — unstable air, friction with the surface, an upper-air jet stream, a mountain barrier, or differences in temperature or wind speed across the front — lets warm air bulge poleward while cold air pushes equatorward, kinking the boundary into a wave. Any one such reason earns the mark.
