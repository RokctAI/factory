## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to sketches on the mid-latitude cyclone. Sketch A is a plan view with latitudes 30°S, 60°S and 90°S marked: arrows labelled WARM AIR move east above a bold horizontal line labelled 'BOUNDARY BETWEEN TWO AIR MASSES' at 60°S, and arrows labelled COLD AIR move west below it.) What is the name of the boundary between two air masses, as shown in sketch A? (1 x 1)

## Method

diagram interpretation

Read the latitudes on the sketch: the boundary lies at about 60 degrees south, where warm subtropical air moving east meets cold polar air moving west. The permanent boundary between these two contrasting air masses is the polar front — the zone along which mid-latitude cyclones develop.
