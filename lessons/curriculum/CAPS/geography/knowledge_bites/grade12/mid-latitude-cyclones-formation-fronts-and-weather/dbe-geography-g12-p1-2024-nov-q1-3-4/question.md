## Question (8 marks)

(Refer to a cross-section of a mid-latitude cyclone. A bold arrow labelled 'DIRECTION OF MOVEMENT' runs from west on the left to east on the right. On the left is a steeply curved front A with a towering cumulonimbus cloud, heavy rain shafts and rapid uplift, with air behind it at 12 °C. On the right is a gently sloping front B with small scattered clouds and light rain, with air below it at 8 °C.) In a paragraph of approximately EIGHT lines, explain strategies that can be put in place to manage the negative environmental impact of the heavy rainfall associated with mid-latitude cyclones. (4 x 2)

## Method

paragraph strategies

Group the strategies so they are easy to recall aloud. First, protect and restore vegetation cover — natural vegetation, afforestation, wetlands and buffer zones — so water infiltrates instead of running off. Second, engineer the flow: drainage systems, dams or weirs, artificial levees, gabions, widened or canalised river channels. Third, shield vulnerable slopes: retaining walls, sandbags, catch nets and canopies where rockfalls threaten, contour ploughing and terracing. Fourth, prepare people: environmental-conservation education and awareness or early-warning systems. Any four well-explained strategies at two marks each earn the eight marks.
