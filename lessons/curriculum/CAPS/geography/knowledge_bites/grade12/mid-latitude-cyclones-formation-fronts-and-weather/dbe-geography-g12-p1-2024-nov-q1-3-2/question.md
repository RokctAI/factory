## Question (2 marks)

(Refer to a cross-section of a mid-latitude cyclone. A bold arrow labelled 'DIRECTION OF MOVEMENT' runs from west on the left to east on the right. On the left is a steeply curved front A with a towering cumulonimbus cloud, heavy rain shafts and rapid uplift, with air behind it at 12 °C. On the right is a gently sloping front B with small scattered clouds and light rain, with air below it at 8 °C.) Give a reason for your answer to QUESTION 1.3.1. (1 x 2)

## Method

explain reason

The steering mechanism is the belt of prevailing westerly winds of the mid-latitudes: the cyclone is embedded in the westerlies and carried along with them.
