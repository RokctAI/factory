## Question (3 marks)

Mapwork on the 1 : 50 000 topographical map 2529CC eMALAHLENI (WITBANK) and the 1 : 10 000 orthophoto map 2529 CC 15 eMALAHLENI (WITBANK). eMalahleni is a coal-mining city on the Highveld of Mpumalanga, with opencast coal mines, power stations and a steel mill nearby. (The physical map sheets are separate; map-read inputs come from the memo's accepted values.)

Refer to the topographical map. Calculate the magnetic declination of eMalahleni (Witbank) for 2025. Use the following information:

Difference in years: 11

Mean annual change: 5' W  (3 x 1)

(The mean magnetic declination of 18°19' west of true north is read from the map margin; the 11-year difference implies a base year of 2014.)

## Method

magnetic declination calculation

## Memo working

Total change over the period: 11 years x 5' per year = 55' (1) westwards (a westward change is added).

Magnetic declination for 2025 = mean magnetic declination + total change = 18°19' + (1) 55' = 18°74'.

Convert 74 minutes to 1°14' (60' = 1°): 18°74' = 19°14' west of true north (1).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

19°14' west of true north (1)
