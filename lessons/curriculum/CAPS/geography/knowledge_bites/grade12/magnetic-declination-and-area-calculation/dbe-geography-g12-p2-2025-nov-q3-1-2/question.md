## Question (4 marks)

Mapwork on the 1 : 50 000 topographical map 2529CC eMALAHLENI (WITBANK) and the 1 : 10 000 orthophoto map 2529 CC 15 eMALAHLENI (WITBANK). eMalahleni is a coal-mining city on the Highveld of Mpumalanga, with opencast coal mines, power stations and a steel mill nearby. (The physical map sheets are separate; map-read inputs come from the memo's accepted values.)

Refer to the orthophoto map. Calculate the area of the demarcated feature 6 in blocks B4, C3 and C4 in m². Use the following information:

Measured breadth (orthophoto map distance) = 1,8 cm

Formula: Area = Length (L) x Breadth (B)  (4 x 1)

(The length is measured off the map by the candidate; the memo's accepted measurement is 3,5 cm, range 3,4 cm - 3,6 cm.)

## Method

area calculation

## Memo working

On the 1 : 10 000 orthophoto map, 1 cm on the map represents 10 000 cm = 100 m on the ground.

Length: 3,5 (1) cm [measurement range 3,4 cm - 3,6 cm] x 100 = 350 (1) m [range 340 m - 360 m].

Breadth (given): 1,8 cm x 100 = 180 (1) m.

Area = Length x Breadth = 350 m x 180 m = 63 000 m² (1) [range 61 200 m² - 64 800 m²].

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

63 000 m² (1) [accepted range 61 200 m² - 64 800 m²]
