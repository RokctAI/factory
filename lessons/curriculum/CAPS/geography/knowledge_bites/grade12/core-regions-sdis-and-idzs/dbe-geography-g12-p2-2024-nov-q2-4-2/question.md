## Question (2 marks)

(Refer to an extract on the Gauteng (PWV) core industrial region, which states among other things: 'About 10 000 businesses involved in the province's manufacturing sector employ over half a million people.') Quote evidence from the extract that shows why 31% of South Africa's labour force is found in the Gauteng (PWV) core industrial region. (1 x 2)

## Method

extract quotation

'Quote' means copy the exact words from the extract — do not paraphrase. You need a phrase that explains why so much of the labour force is concentrated here, so look for wording about employment or the number of businesses. Either the phrase about the manufacturing sector 'employing over half a million people' or the phrase about the 'about 10 000 businesses involved in the province's manufacturing' works, quoted word for word.
