## Question (4 marks)

Explain TWO challenges faced by the Gauteng (PWV) core industrial region. (2 x 2)

## Method

explain challenges

Organise the possibilities into a few families and pick the two you can explain best. Location: Gauteng is inland, far from harbours, so transport costs are high and it depends heavily on road transport while rail deteriorates. Services: load-shedding cuts production and water shortages threaten industry. Space and costs: limited land for expansion, high petrol prices, rising operating costs. People and politics: labour strikes, crime, lack of skills, and political issues that dent business confidence. Environment: rising pollution. Whichever two you choose, state the challenge and explain its effect on industry for the full two marks each.
