## Question (1 mark)

(Match the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option in COLUMN B; write only the letter Y or Z.) The main industry associated with the West Coast SDI is a/an …  [Y automotive plant / Z steel plant]

## Method

column matching

Attach the right anchor industry to the right corridor. The West Coast SDI is anchored on Saldanha Bay, whose flagship development is the Saldanha steel plant, fed by iron ore railed in on the Sishen-Saldanha line. Automotive plants are the anchor of the Eastern Cape corridors (Coega and East London), not the West Coast — so the steel plant is correct.
