## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to an infographic on the Gauteng (PWV) core industrial region with these statistics: 20% of South Africa's population; 31% of SA's labour force in all economic sectors; 22% contributed to the mining sector; 38% contribution to the GDP of South Africa; 45% of South Africa's manufacturing capacity.) Refer to the statistics and state the percentage that the Gauteng (PWV) core industrial region contributes to the GDP of South Africa. (1 x 1)

## Method

infographic reading

Scan the list of statistics for the one labelled with GDP — be careful not to grab a neighbouring figure like the 31% labour force or the 45% manufacturing capacity. The entry reading 'contribution to the GDP of South Africa' gives 38%.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

38 (%) (1)
