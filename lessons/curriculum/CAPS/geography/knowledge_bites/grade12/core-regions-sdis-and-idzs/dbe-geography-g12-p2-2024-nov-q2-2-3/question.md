## Question (1 mark)

(Match the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option in COLUMN B; write only the letter Y or Z.) A positive economic impact of spatial development initiatives (SDIs) is …  [Y improved quality of life / Z upgraded infrastructure]

## Method

column matching

Sort the two options by category before choosing. Improved quality of life describes people's wellbeing — that is a social impact. Upgraded infrastructure — roads, railways, ports, power — is productive capital that supports business activity, which makes it the economic impact the statement asks for.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Z (upgraded infrastructure)
