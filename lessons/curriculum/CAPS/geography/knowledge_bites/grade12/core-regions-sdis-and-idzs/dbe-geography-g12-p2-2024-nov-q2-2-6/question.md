## Question (1 mark)

(Match the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option in COLUMN B; write only the letter Y or Z.) The South-western Cape core industrial region is restricted by …  [Y few mineral resources / Z small local markets]

## Method

column matching

Weigh each option against the region's real geography. The South-western Cape is anchored on Cape Town, a metropolitan area with a substantial local market — so a small market is not its restriction. What the region genuinely lacks is mineral wealth: it sits far from the coalfields and mineral deposits of the interior, so raw materials and cheap energy must be brought in over long distances. Few mineral resources is the restriction.
