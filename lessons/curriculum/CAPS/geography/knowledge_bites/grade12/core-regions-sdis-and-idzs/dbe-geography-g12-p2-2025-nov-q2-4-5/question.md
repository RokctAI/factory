## Question (6 marks)

Refer to the extract and map on the Dube Trade Port Industrial Development Zone (IDZ). The extract reports that in August 2024 major automotive (car) companies pledged investment and set up business in the IDZ; the automotive industry would join the electronic, high-value manufacturing, food processing and fibre optics sectors at the Dube Trade Port (IDZ); Auto Investment Holdings Group and Mahindra (South Africa) revealed a new assembly plant (construction expected to be completed in 2025; currently 90 staff on the assembly line); and Ogihara South Africa — a joint venture between Toyota Tsusho Africa, Ogihara (Thailand) Corporation and Toyota South Africa Motors — pledged R1,2 billion to build a parts-manufacturing facility. The map shows the Dube Trade Port on the Durban coast in the same vicinity as King Shaka International Airport, 30 minutes from Africa's busiest cargo port, Durban Harbour, and 41 km from the Durban-Pinetown industrial region.

Explain how the variety of manufacturing industries in the Dube Trade Port (IDZ) would have a positive impact on the economy of the province. (3 x 2)
