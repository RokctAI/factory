## Question (1 mark)

(Match the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option in COLUMN B; write only the letter Y or Z.) SDIs are generally located in … areas.  [Y underdeveloped / Z developed]

## Method

column matching

Think about why spatial development initiatives exist at all: they channel government and private investment into corridors with untapped economic potential, precisely because those areas were neglected and lack development. If an area were already developed, it would not need an SDI — so SDIs are located in underdeveloped areas.
