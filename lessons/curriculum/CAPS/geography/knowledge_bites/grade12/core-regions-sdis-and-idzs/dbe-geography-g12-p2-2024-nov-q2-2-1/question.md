## Question (1 mark)

(Match the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option in COLUMN B; write only the letter Y or Z.) The main purpose of an industrial development zone (IDZ) is to promote …  [Y exports / Z imports]

## Method

column matching

Recall what an IDZ is designed for: a purpose-built industrial estate located next to a port or international airport, offering incentives to attract investment in manufacturing aimed at foreign markets. The whole point of placing it at a port is to ship finished goods out of the country — so an IDZ promotes exports, not imports.
