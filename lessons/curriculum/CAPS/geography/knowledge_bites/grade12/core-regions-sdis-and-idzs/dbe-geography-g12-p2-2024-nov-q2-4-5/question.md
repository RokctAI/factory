## Question (4 marks)

Suggest ONE advantage and ONE disadvantage of industries moving towards high value-added production in the Gauteng (PWV) core industrial region. (2 x 2)

(Context: from the paper's infographic on the Gauteng (PWV) core industrial region — the province's economy is moving away from traditional heavy industry and low value-added production, meaning basic production that uses traditional technology and manual labour, towards high value-added production, meaning production that uses advanced technology and is highly efficient, for example robotics operating machinery, particularly in information technology, telecommunications and other high-tech industries.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

**Advantages (any ONE, 2 marks):**

- Higher profits (2)
- Efficient production process (2)
- Upskilling of people (accept examples) (2)
- Increased investment (2)
- Increased foreign income (2)
- Access to a larger market (2)
- Diversifies production (2)
- Provides opportunities in specialised sectors (2)

**Disadvantages (any ONE, 2 marks):**

- Less job opportunities for unskilled workers (2)
- Skilled labour will be sourced from other countries/regions (2)
- Buyers will have to pay more for goods (2)
- Businesses in low value production will be negatively affected (2)
- High cost for skills development (accept examples) (2)
- Production losses due to load-shedding (2)
- Requires a large capital outlay (2)
- Existing infrastructure struggles to meet demands (2)
- High maintenance cost (2)
