## Question (4 marks)

(Refer to a sketch map of major roads that link the main industrial areas in the Gauteng (PWV) core industrial region: boxed nodes PRETORIA, WITWATERSRAND and VEREENIGING are linked vertically by major-road arrows, with further major roads radiating outwards to surrounding centres such as Rustenburg, Brits, Emalahleni (Witbank), Middelburg, Bronkhorstspruit, Potchefstroom, Klerksdorp, Delmas, Secunda/Evander, Mabopane and Polokwane.) Refer to the sketch and explain the role of the major roads in supporting industrial development in the Gauteng (PWV) core industrial region. (2 x 2)

## Method

sketch interpretation

Read the sketch as a network: the three industrial nodes are tied together and connected to a ring of surrounding towns. Then explain what a dense road network does for industry — it moves raw materials, finished goods and workers efficiently, gives factories access to major markets, offers the shortest, most cost-effective routes between centres, spreads traffic to reduce congestion and speed up deliveries, and makes the region attractive to investors. Explain any two roles for two marks each.
