## Question (1 mark)

(Match the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option in COLUMN B; write only the letter Y or Z.) A social challenge experienced in the Saldanha Bay IDZ is …  [Y unskilled workforce / Z poor harbour facilities]

## Method

column matching

Again separate social from physical or economic. Harbour facilities are infrastructure, not a social matter — and Saldanha Bay in fact has a deep natural harbour, which is why the IDZ is there. A social challenge concerns the people of the area: the local workforce lacks the skills the IDZ's industries need, so the unskilled workforce is the social challenge.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Y (unskilled workforce)
