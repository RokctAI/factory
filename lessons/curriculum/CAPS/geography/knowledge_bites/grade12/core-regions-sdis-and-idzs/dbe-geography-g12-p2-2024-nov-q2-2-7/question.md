## Question (1 mark)

(Match the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option in COLUMN B; write only the letter Y or Z.) A physical factor that promotes industries in the South-western Cape core industrial region is …  [Y overseas markets / Z coastal location]

## Method

column matching

The keyword is physical — a natural or locational characteristic of the place itself. Overseas markets are an economic factor: they concern trade, not the physical site. The region's coastal location, with its natural harbours at Cape Town and Saldanha, is a feature of the physical geography that promotes industry by enabling shipping and imports of raw materials.
