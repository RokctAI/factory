## Question (2 marks)

State TWO social causes of urban blight/decay. (2 x 1)

## Method

recall

## Memo working

The question asks for social causes — conditions in people's lives that let an area decay, not the decay itself. Unemployment and poverty leave residents unable to maintain buildings or pay for services; a lack of affordable housing pushes people into overcrowded or illegally occupied buildings; abandoned buildings attract illegal occupation; landlords lose interest in upgrading; and poor service delivery lets the environment run down. State any TWO for one mark each.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Unemployment (1)
- Poverty (accept examples) (1)
- Lack of affordable housing (1)
- Abandoned buildings (accept examples) (1)
- Illegal occupation (accept examples) (1)
- Lack of interest of landlords to upgrade (1)
- Poor service delivery (accept examples) (1)
