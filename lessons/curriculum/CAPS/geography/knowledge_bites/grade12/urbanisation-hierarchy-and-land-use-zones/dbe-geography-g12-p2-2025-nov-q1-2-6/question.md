## Question (1 mark)

Choose the answer and write only the letter (A–D).

The cartoon below depicts the negative impact of …  (Cartoon, key: hatched = building, textured = tree — buildings drawn as monstrous shapes with teeth advancing over and devouring the last remaining trees.)

A urban blight.  B urban renewal.  C counter-urbanisation.  D urban expansion.

## Method

cartoon mcq

## Memo working

Read what the cartoonist is showing: buildings, drawn as monsters with teeth, are advancing outwards and swallowing the last trees. The built-up area is growing outwards at the expense of natural vegetation. That is the negative impact of urban expansion. Urban blight would show decaying buildings, urban renewal shows upgrading, and counter-urbanisation is people moving out of cities — none of these matches buildings consuming trees. The answer is D.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

D (urban expansion)
