## Question (4 marks)

Why is the land use at B2 (high-income residential area) not located close to the land use at A (heavy industry)? (2 x 2)

## Method

explain reason

## Memo working

The core idea is that the two land uses are incompatible: expensive housing and heavy industry cannot share a neighbourhood. Argue from the residents' side — separation preserves the aesthetics of the suburb, maintains the high value of land and property, reduces the impact of the industry's pollution, alleviates traffic congestion from industrial transport, and reduces health risks. The memo equally accepts the mirror-image points argued from the industry at A (it would decrease aesthetic appeal and property values, increase pollution, congestion and health risks nearby). Any TWO points, each for 2 marks.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Two land uses are incompatible (2)
- Preserves the aesthetics of the land use (accept examples) (2)
- Maintains high value of land/property (2)
- To reduce the impact of pollution (accept examples) (2)
- Alleviates traffic congestion (2)
- Reduces health risks (accept examples) (2)
