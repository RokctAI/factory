## Question (1 mark)

(Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) The range associated with the purchase of a motor vehicle is greater than the purchase of bread because it is:  (i) More expensive  (ii) Purchased on a daily basis  (iii) Not purchased frequently  (iv) Less expensive  A (i) and (iii)  B (ii) and (iv)  C (i) and (ii)  D (iii) and (iv)

## Method

urban hierarchy mcq

A motor vehicle is a high-order good: it is expensive and bought rarely, so buyers are willing to travel far for it — a large range. Bread is a low-order convenience good: cheap and bought almost daily, so its range is short. The true statements about the motor vehicle are (i) more expensive and (iii) not purchased frequently; statements (ii) and (iv) describe bread instead.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)
