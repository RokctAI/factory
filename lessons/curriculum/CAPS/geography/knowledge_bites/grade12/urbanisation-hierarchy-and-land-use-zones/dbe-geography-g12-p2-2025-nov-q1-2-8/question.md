## Question (1 mark)

Refer to the table below based on the level and rate of urbanisation in South Africa (adapted from STATISTA.COM). Year / Level (%) / Rate (%): 2020 — 67,35 and 0,55; 2021 — 67,85 and 0,50; 2022 — level missing, rate 0,49; 2023 — 68,82 and 0,48; 2024 — 69,30, rate missing.

The level (%) of urbanisation in 2022 AND the rate (%) of urbanisation in 2024 is … and …

(i) 68,34  (ii) 69,34  (iii) 0,51  (iv) 0,47

A (i) and (iii)  B (i) and (iv)  C (ii) and (iii)  D (ii) and (iv)

## Method

data mcq

## Memo working

Fill each gap from the pattern in its column. The level of urbanisation climbs steadily every year, so the missing 2022 value must lie between 67,85 (the 2021 level) and 68,82 (the 2023 level): 68,34, option (i), fits; 69,34 does not, because it would overshoot even the 2024 level of 69,30. The rate of urbanisation falls every year — 0,55; 0,50; 0,49; 0,48 — so the missing 2024 rate must continue the decline below 0,48: 0,47, option (iv); 0,51 would be a jump back up. The pair (i) and (iv) is option B.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

B ((i) 68,34 and (iv) 0,47)
