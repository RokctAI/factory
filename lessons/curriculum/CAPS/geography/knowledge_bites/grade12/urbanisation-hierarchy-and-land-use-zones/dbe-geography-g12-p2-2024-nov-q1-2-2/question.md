## Question (1 mark)

(Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) A … has the smallest number of settlements.  A conurbation  B city  C town  D megalopolis

## Method

urban hierarchy mcq

In any urban hierarchy, the higher the order of the settlement, the fewer of them exist: there are many towns, fewer cities, fewer conurbations still, and only a handful of megalopolises in the world. The megalopolis is the highest order offered, so it occurs in the smallest number.
