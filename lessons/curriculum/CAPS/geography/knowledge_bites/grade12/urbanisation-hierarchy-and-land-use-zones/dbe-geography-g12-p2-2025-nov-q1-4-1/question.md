## Question (2 marks)

Refer to the urban profile depicting urban land-use zones (a skyline cross-section with zones labelled A — industrial zone, B1 and B2 — residential zones, C — transition zones, D — CBD with the tallest buildings in the centre, and E — rural-urban fringe).

What is an urban land-use zone? (1 x 2)

## Method

definition

## Memo working

This is a concept question, so give the full definition: within an urban settlement, land is divided up and zoned so that each part serves one specific function — business in the CBD, housing in the residential zones, factories in the industrial zone, and so on. An urban land-use zone is therefore land that has been zoned (set aside) for a specific function in an urban settlement.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Land that has been zoned for a specific function (in an urban settlement) (2) [CONCEPT]
