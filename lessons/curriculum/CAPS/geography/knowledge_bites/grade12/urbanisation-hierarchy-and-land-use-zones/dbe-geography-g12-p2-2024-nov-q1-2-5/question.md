## Question (1 mark)

(Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) The … of a city covers a larger area than a town.  A range  B sphere of influence  C threshold population  D service area

## Method

urban hierarchy mcq

The phrase "covers a larger area" signals the sphere of influence — the whole area from which a settlement draws its customers and to which it provides goods and services. A city offers more high-order functions than a town, so people travel to it from much further away, giving it a larger sphere of influence. Range is a distance, and threshold population is a number of people — neither "covers an area".
