## Question (1 mark)

(Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) A … is an urban settlement type made up of a major city and towns.  A metropolis  B conurbation  C city  D megalopolis

## Method

urban hierarchy mcq

Match the composition to the term: a metropolis is one major (dominant) city together with the surrounding towns that function with it. A conurbation, by contrast, forms when several cities merge, and a megalopolis when conurbations merge. "A major city and towns" describes the metropolis.
