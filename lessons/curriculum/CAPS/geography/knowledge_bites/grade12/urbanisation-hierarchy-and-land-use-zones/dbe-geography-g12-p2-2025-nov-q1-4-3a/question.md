## Question (2 marks)

Refer to the urban profile showing land-use zones and the photographs that show examples of land use in these zones. (Zone B2, a residential zone drawn next to the rural-urban fringe, is linked to an aerial photograph of a leafy suburb with large houses, gardens and wide streets.)

(a) Give evidence that the land use in B2 is a high-income residential area. (2 x 1)

## Method

photo evidence

## Memo working

Use what is visible in the photograph and where B2 sits on the profile. In the photo: large plots with large houses at low density, gardens and trees making the area aesthetically appealing, wide streets, garages showing residents own cars, striking architecture and high security walls. On the profile: B2 lies close to the rural-urban fringe, far from the heavy industries at A and away from the CBD — the quiet, clean edge of the city that wealthy residents can afford to commute from. Any TWO pieces of evidence, one mark each.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Close to the rural-urban fringe (1)
- Large plots (1)
- Low density of buildings/large houses (1)
- Far away from the (heavy) industries (1)
- Architecture of buildings (1)
- Residents own cars (garages) (1)
- Wide roads/streets (1)
- Gardens and trees/Aesthetically appealing (1)
- Away from the CBD (1)
- High security walls (1)
