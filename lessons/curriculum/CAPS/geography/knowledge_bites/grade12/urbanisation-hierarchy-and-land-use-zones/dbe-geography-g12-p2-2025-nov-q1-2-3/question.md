## Question (1 mark)

Choose the answer and write only the letter (A–D).

A … provides mostly low-order goods and services to the surrounding rural population.

A city  B metropolis  C conurbation  D town

## Method

mcq

## Memo working

Place the options on the urban hierarchy. City, metropolis and conurbation all sit high on the hierarchy — they serve huge spheres of influence and offer high-order goods and services. The settlement that serves the surrounding rural population with mostly low-order (everyday, frequently bought) goods and services is the one lowest on this list: the town. The answer is D.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

D (town)
