## Question (2 marks)

(Refer to photographs and an extract on the upgrading of informal settlements: a 'before' photograph shows a dense informal settlement of makeshift shacks built of scrap materials with litter and a haphazard layout; an 'after' photograph shows the same type of area upgraded with neat rows of corrugated-iron structures and overhead electricity lines. The extract notes that informal settlements are generally located on any vacant land that is available and are characterised by a lack of basic services, pollution, overcrowding and poor waste management, and argues that upgrading programmes — rather than relocation — bring social and economic benefits while residents remain on site.) Define the term informal settlement. (1 x 2)

## Method

definition

Build the definition from two ideas: what is built and on what basis the land is held. Informal settlements are structures (often shacks of mixed materials) erected on land the occupants have no legal claim to — typically vacant land — making them unplanned, temporary and without basic services.
