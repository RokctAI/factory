## Question (8 marks)

(Refer to photographs and an extract on the upgrading of informal settlements: a 'Before' photograph shows a dense informal settlement of makeshift shacks; an 'After' photograph shows the same type of area upgraded into neat rows of corrugated-iron structures with overhead electricity lines. The extract argues that rather than uprooting and relocating residents, upgrading programmes bring social benefits: neighbourhood infrastructure improves while low-income residents remain on site, income-generating opportunities benefit local companies and residents, and young people within these settlements are often directly employed in upgrading projects.) In a paragraph of approximately EIGHT lines, explain how the upgrading (improvement) of informal settlements would have a positive social impact for people living in these settlements. (4 x 2)

## Method

paragraph explanation

The key word is social — impacts on people's daily lives, wellbeing and community, not on profits. Build the paragraph around quality of life: upgraded services like water, sanitation and electricity, new facilities and better transport links improve convenience and accessibility; a cleaner, healthier environment raises life expectancy; and because residents stay on site, community networks survive, which keeps crime lower and builds a sense of belonging. Add the people-development angle: locals learn new skills on upgrading projects and local appointments create jobs. Each fact must be expanded — a bare fact without expansion earns only one of its two marks — and four well-explained points earn the full eight.
