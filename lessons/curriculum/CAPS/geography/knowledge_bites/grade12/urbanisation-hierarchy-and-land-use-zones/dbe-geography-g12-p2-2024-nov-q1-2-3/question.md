## Question (1 mark)

(Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) A … is the largest settlement type found in South Africa.  A megalopolis  B conurbation  C metropolis  D city

## Method

urban hierarchy mcq

The key words are "found in South Africa". South Africa has no megalopolis — that order is reached only by regions like the north-eastern USA. The largest settlement type South Africa does have is the conurbation: separate cities and towns that have grown and merged into one continuous built-up area, as in Gauteng, where Johannesburg, Ekurhuleni and Pretoria have fused.
