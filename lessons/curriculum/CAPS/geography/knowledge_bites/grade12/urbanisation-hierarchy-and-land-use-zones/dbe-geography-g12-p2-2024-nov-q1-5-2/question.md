## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to the extract on informal settlements in QUESTION 1.5: 'Informal settlements are characterised by a lack of basic services, pollution, overcrowding and poor waste management that impact negatively on the environment and poses a health risk.') State ONE factor in the extract that has a negative impact on the health of residents in informal settlements. (1 x 1)

## Method

extract reading

The instruction "in the extract" means the answer must be lifted from the passage itself, not from general knowledge. The extract names four health-threatening conditions in a single sentence — lack of basic services, pollution, overcrowding and poor waste management. Stating any one of them earns the mark.
