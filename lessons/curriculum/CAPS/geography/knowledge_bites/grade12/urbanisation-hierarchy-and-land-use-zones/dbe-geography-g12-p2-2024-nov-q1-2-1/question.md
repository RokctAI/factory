## Question (1 mark)

(Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) The smallest urban settlement in an urban hierarchy is a …  A city.  B metropolis.  C town.  D megalopolis.

## Method

urban hierarchy mcq

Run up the urban hierarchy from small to large: town, then city, then metropolis, then conurbation, then megalopolis. Of the four options offered, the town sits lowest on the ladder, so it is the smallest urban settlement listed.
