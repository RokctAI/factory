## Question (1 mark)

Choose the answer and write only the letter (A–D).

Plettenberg Bay in the photograph below is classified as a/an … urban settlement and its dominant economic function is …  (Photograph of a crowded beach with holiday-makers, umbrellas and a large beachfront hotel behind.)

(i) gateway  (ii) specialised  (iii) tourism  (iv) mining

A (i) and (iii)  B (i) and (iv)  C (ii) and (iii)  D (ii) and (iv)

## Method

photo mcq

## Memo working

Take the two blanks one at a time. The photograph — a crowded holiday beach with a beachfront hotel — shows a town that lives off one dominant activity, so by classification it is a specialised urban settlement, (ii); a gateway town controls entry to a region, which is not what the photo shows. That dominant activity is clearly tourism, (iii), not mining, (iv). The pair (ii) and (iii) is option C.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C ((ii) specialised and (iii) tourism)
