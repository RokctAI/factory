## Question (1 mark)

Refer to the urban profile showing land-use zones and the photographs that show examples of land use in these zones. (Zone A, the industrial zone on the far edge of the profile, is linked to a photograph of an industrial plant with tall smoking chimneys, silos and steel structures.)

(b) Is the land use at A a heavy or a light industry? (1 x 1)

## Method

photo classification

## Memo working

Classify by what the photograph shows. Heavy industry works with bulky raw materials in large plants — think tall chimneys pouring out smoke, silos and massive steel structures — and creates noise and pollution, which is why it is pushed to the urban edge. Light industry sits in small, clean buildings making small consumer goods. The chimneys, silos and steel structures at A mark it unmistakably as heavy industry.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Heavy (industry) (1)
