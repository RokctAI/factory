## Question (1 mark)

Choose the answer and write only the letter (A–D).

… refers to the uncontrolled growth of urban areas.

A Rate of urbanisation  B Urban growth  C Urban sprawl  D Level of urbanisation

## Method

mcq

## Memo working

The key word is "uncontrolled". Rate of urbanisation is the speed at which the urban percentage changes; level of urbanisation is the percentage of people living in urban areas at a moment; urban growth is any increase in an urban area's population or size. Only urban sprawl carries the idea of unplanned, uncontrolled outward spread of the built-up area into surrounding land. The answer is C.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C (Urban sprawl)
