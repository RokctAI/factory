## Question (1 mark)

Choose the answer and write only the letter (A–D).

… is the maximum distance that people will travel to buy goods or obtain services.

A Sphere of influence  B Range  C Market  D Threshold population

## Method

mcq

## Memo working

These central-place terms are easily confused, so pin each to its definition. The sphere of influence is the whole area a settlement serves; the threshold population is the minimum number of customers a service needs to survive; the market is simply the customers themselves. The maximum distance people are prepared to travel for a good or service is, by definition, the range. The answer is B.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

B (Range)
