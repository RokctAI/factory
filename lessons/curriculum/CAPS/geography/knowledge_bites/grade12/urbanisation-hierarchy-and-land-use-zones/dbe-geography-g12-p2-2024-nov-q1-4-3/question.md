## Question (2 marks)

(Refer to the urban profile sketch in QUESTION 1.4, with the CBD's tall, closely packed tower blocks at the centre of the profile.) How has the demand for land influenced the density of buildings in the CBD? (1 x 2)

## Method

explain reason

Follow the chain from demand to density: the CBD is the most accessible point of the city, so competition for its limited land is intense and land values are highest. Because so many businesses want that scarce land, every stand is built up as fully as possible — the result is a high building density.
