## Question (4 marks)

Why are both the transition zone and rural-urban fringe referred to as zones of change? (2 x 2)

## Method

explain reason

Ask what is actually changing in each zone. Both are borderlands: the transition zone sits between the CBD and the residential areas, the rural-urban fringe between the city and the countryside. In both, land uses mix and replace one another — invasion and succession as one activity takes over from another, urban renewal in the ageing transition zone, and urban expansion or sprawl at the fringe. Any two explained reasons earn two marks each.
