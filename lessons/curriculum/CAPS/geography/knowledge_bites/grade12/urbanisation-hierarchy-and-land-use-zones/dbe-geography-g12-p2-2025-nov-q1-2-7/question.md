## Question (1 mark)

Choose the answer and write only the letter (A–D).

… is a process whereby an increasing percentage of a country's population lives in urban rather than rural areas.

A Urbanisation  B Population growth  C Urban growth  D Rural-urban migration

## Method

mcq

## Memo working

The definition hinges on the word "percentage". Population growth is an increase in total numbers; urban growth is the increase in an urban area's own population or extent; rural-urban migration is one cause — people physically moving to cities — but not the process itself. The process in which the urban share of a country's population rises is urbanisation. The answer is A.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A (Urbanisation)
