## Question (6 marks)

Suggest economic reasons why the rural-urban fringe would be an attractive location for commercial activities. (3 x 2)

## Method

suggest reasons

Keep every reason economic — about costs, land and markets. At the edge of the city, land is cheaper and there is plenty of open space to build on; the urban market is still close by; transport routes serve the area, cutting transport costs; there is less crime-related cost than in the inner city; and raw materials from the surrounding rural area are within easy reach. Any three reasons at two marks each.
