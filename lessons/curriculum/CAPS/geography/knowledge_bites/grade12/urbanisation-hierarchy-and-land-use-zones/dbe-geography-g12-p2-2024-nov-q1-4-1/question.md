## Question (2 marks)

(Refer to a sketch of an urban profile: a side view of an urban area 25 km wide showing, from left to right, the CBD with tall multi-storey tower blocks, the transition zone with older mixed low-rise buildings, a residential zone with rows of houses, an industrial zone with factories, and the rural-urban fringe with a small house and farmland. Building height decreases outwards from the CBD.) Define the concept urban profile. (1 x 2)

## Method

definition

The sketch itself is the clue: it shows the city as if sliced through and viewed from the side, with building heights rising towards the CBD. That is exactly what an urban profile is — the side (cross-sectional) view of an urban area. Saying only "side view" without tying it to an urban area earns part marks.
