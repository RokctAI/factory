## Question (1 mark)

Refer to the extract and photograph on urban blight/decay in Wynberg, Cape Town. (The photograph shows a run-down street: a dilapidated building with broken windows, graffiti sprayed across its walls, and rubbish and discarded belongings piled on the pavement.)

What evidence in the photograph suggests that urban blight/decay has taken place? (1 x 1)

## Method

photo evidence

## Memo working

Urban blight is the physical deterioration of buildings and their surroundings, so point to something visibly deteriorated in the photograph itself: the neglected, dilapidated building; its broken windows; the graffiti covering the walls; or the litter and rubbish piled in the street. Any ONE observable piece of evidence earns the mark — it must come from the photo, not the extract.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Buildings are neglected/dilapidated (accept examples) (1); Broken windows (1); Graffiti on the walls (accept examples) (1); Litter/rubbish in the streets (1) [ANY ONE]
