## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to the urban profile sketch in QUESTION 1.4: from the CBD's tall tower blocks the buildings become progressively lower through the transition, residential and industrial zones out to the rural-urban fringe.) Comment on the height of buildings in the CBD in comparison to those in the rural-urban fringe. (1 x 1)

## Method

sketch interpretation

Read the skyline of the profile from the centre outwards: the CBD carries the tall multi-storey blocks because land there is most expensive and building upwards maximises its use, while at the rural-urban fringe the buildings are low. Any single accurate comparison — taller in the CBD, lower at the fringe, or height decreasing away from the CBD — earns the mark.
