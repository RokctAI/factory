## Question (1 mark)

Choose the answer and write only the letter (A–D).

A … trade and transport town/city is shown in the photograph below.  (Aerial photograph of a harbour city: cranes, quays and ships in a large port in the foreground with high-rise CBD buildings behind.)

A gap  B junction  C break-of-bulk-point  D specialised

## Method

photo mcq

## Memo working

Match the photograph to the type of trade-and-transport settlement. The photo shows a working harbour — cranes, quays and ships — in front of the city. A harbour is where cargo is transferred from one mode of transport to another: goods leave the ships and are broken into smaller loads for rail and road. A settlement that grows at such a transfer point is a break-of-bulk point. A gap town sits at a pass through mountains and a junction town where routes meet — neither fits a port, and "specialised" is a classification by function, not a trade-and-transport type. The answer is C.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C (break-of-bulk-point)
