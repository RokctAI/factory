## Question (8 marks)

Refer to the extract on urban blight/decay in Wynberg, Cape Town, where a committed group of business owners has invested time, effort and money in attempts to implement urban renewal.

In a paragraph of approximately EIGHT lines, explain the positive impact of urban renewal on the economy of Wynberg. (4 x 2)

## Method

paragraph impacts

## Memo working

The paragraph must stay economic: every sentence should link renewal to money, jobs or investment in Wynberg. Build the chain of reasoning — upgraded buildings and streets attract more businesses and investors; a safer, more attractive area draws more customers and tourists; that new spending sets off the multiplier effect, creating more job opportunities and upskilling workers; property values rise and infrastructure improves, which attracts yet more investment. Write it as a flowing paragraph of about eight lines making any FOUR of these points, each worth 2 marks.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Attracts more businesses/investors (accept examples) (2)
- Attracts more customers (accept examples) (2)
- Attracts more tourists (2)
- Influences the multiplier effect (accept examples) (2)
- Creates more job opportunities (accept examples) (2)
- Upskills workers (2)
- Increases the value of properties (2)
- Improves infrastructure (accept examples) (2)
