## Question (4 marks)

(Refer to photographs and an extract on the upgrading of informal settlements: a 'Before' photograph shows a dense informal settlement of makeshift shacks built of scrap materials with litter and a haphazard layout; an 'After' photograph shows the same type of area upgraded into neat rows of corrugated-iron structures with overhead electricity lines and cleaner, ordered surroundings. The extract explains that informal settlements are generally located on any vacant land, are characterised by a lack of basic services, pollution, overcrowding and poor waste management, and that in spite of this their number is growing.) Explain TWO economic reasons for the increase in informal settlements. (2 x 2)

## Method

explain reason

The question asks for economic reasons, so every point must be about money — income, costs, budgets or finance. Reason from two purses. From the household's purse: unemployment and low wages leave people unable to afford formal housing or rent, banks are reluctant to lend and interest rates are high, while building or renting a shack on vacant land is far cheaper and often closer to work, saving transport costs. From the municipality's purse: the housing budget simply cannot keep up with the growing demand for formal housing. Choose any two reasons and explain each for two marks.
