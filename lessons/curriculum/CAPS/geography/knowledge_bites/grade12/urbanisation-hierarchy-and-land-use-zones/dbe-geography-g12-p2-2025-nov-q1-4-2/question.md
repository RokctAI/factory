## Question (2 marks)

Name TWO urban land-use zones where commercial functions are mainly found. (2 x 1)

## Method

recall

## Memo working

Commercial functions are the buying-and-selling activities — shops, offices, services. Their prime home is the Central Business District, the most accessible point of the city; the transition zone around the CBD also carries commerce that has spilled out of the centre. The memo further accepts the residential zone (neighbourhood shopping centres) and the rural-urban fringe (out-of-town malls and office parks). Name any TWO zones for one mark each.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Central Business District (CBD) (1)
- Transition (zone) (1)
- Residential (zone) (1)
- Rural-urban fringe (1)

[ANY TWO]
