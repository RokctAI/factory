## Question (1 mark)

(Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) A university has a … range and a … sphere of influence than a school.  A smaller; larger  B smaller; smaller  C larger; larger  D larger; smaller

## Method

urban hierarchy mcq

A university is a high-order function: people are prepared to travel very far to attend one, so its range — the maximum distance people will travel for the service — is larger than a school's. Because students come from that much wider area, the university also serves a larger sphere of influence. Both blanks therefore take "larger".
