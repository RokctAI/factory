## Question (1 mark)

Refer to the extract on land restitution: the Moletele community's lime-producing farm at Hoedspruit, Limpopo — "The Moletele community opted to take their land back and this has resulted in a successful land restitution programme … a joint venture project of the Moletele communal property association (holding a 51% stake) and the established commercial fruit producer Komati Fruit Group."

According to the extract, which option (model) of land restitution applied to the Moletele community? (1 x 1)

## Method

extract reading

## Memo working

Land restitution offers claimants a choice: take the land itself back, or accept financial compensation instead. The extract answers this directly in its opening line — "The Moletele community opted to take their land back". Quote that option; the community chose the return of the land, not a payout.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

They opted to take their land back (1)
