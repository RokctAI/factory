## Question (4 marks)

How does the movement of young adults to urban areas have a negative social impact on the rural community? (2 x 2)

## Method

impact explanation

Focus on the people left behind, and keep the impacts social rather than purely economic. When the young adults leave, the rural community loses its most active age group: births decline and the population ages, families are split, the remaining old and young become vulnerable, services shrink, and the community loses skills and identity. Any two impacts, clearly stated, earn two marks each.
