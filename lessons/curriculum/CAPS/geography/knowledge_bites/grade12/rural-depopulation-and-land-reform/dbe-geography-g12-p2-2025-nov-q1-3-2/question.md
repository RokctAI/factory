## Question (1 mark)

Refer to the bar graph showing the percentage of rural population in South Africa from 2017 to 2024 (values: 2017 — 34,66; 2018 — 34,16; 2019 — 33,65; 2020 — 33,14; 2021 — 32,65; 2022 — 32,15; 2023 — 31,67; 2024 — 31,18).

Give the general trend in the rural population (percentages) as depicted between 2017 and 2024 in the graph. (1 x 1)

## Method

trend identification

## Memo working

Compare the first and last bars: the rural population percentage falls from 34,66 in 2017 to 31,18 in 2024, and every year in between is lower than the year before. A steady fall like this is described as a downward or decreasing trend — one describing word is all the mark needs.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Downward (Decreases) (1) (Accept: from 34,66 to 31,18) (1)
