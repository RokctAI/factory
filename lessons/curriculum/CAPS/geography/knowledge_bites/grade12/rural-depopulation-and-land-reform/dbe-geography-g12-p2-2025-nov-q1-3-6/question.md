## Question (6 marks)

Suggest strategies that can be implemented after land restitution has taken place to reduce rural depopulation (as indicated in the graph of the falling rural population percentage). (3 x 2)

## Method

suggest strategies

## Memo working

The question links the two stimuli: land has been returned (as at Moletele), so what would keep people in the rural area instead of leaving? Think about what made Moletele succeed — a partnership with an established commercial producer, skills transfer, jobs and bursaries — and generalise: train and upskill the new landowners, give technical and financial support, promote commercial farming, create employment, form business partnerships, involve stakeholders in decisions, and improve infrastructure and services so rural life stays attractive. Any THREE strategies, each for 2 marks.
