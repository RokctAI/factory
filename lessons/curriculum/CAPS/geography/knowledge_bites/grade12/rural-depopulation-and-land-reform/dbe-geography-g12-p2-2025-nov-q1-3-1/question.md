## Question (1 mark)

Refer to the bar graph showing the percentage of rural population in South Africa from 2017 to 2024 (values: 2017 — 34,66; 2018 — 34,16; 2019 — 33,65; 2020 — 33,14; 2021 — 32,65; 2022 — 32,15; 2023 — 31,67; 2024 — 31,18).

According to the graph, state the rural population percentage of South Africa for 2024. (1 x 1)

## Method

graph reading

## Memo working

Go to the last bar on the graph, the one labelled 2024, and read off its value: 31,18. This is a direct reading question — quote the figure exactly as the graph gives it, with the percentage.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

31,18 (%) (1)
