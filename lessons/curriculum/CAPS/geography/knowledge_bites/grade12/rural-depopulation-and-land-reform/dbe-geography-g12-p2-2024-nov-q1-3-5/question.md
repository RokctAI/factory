## Question (6 marks)

Suggest strategies that can be introduced in rural areas to reduce the movement of young adults to urban areas. (3 x 2)

## Method

suggest strategies

Ask what pulls young adults to the city — jobs, services, opportunity — and then propose ways of providing those same things in the rural area itself: attract investment and create employment, develop skills and entrepreneurship, improve services and infrastructure, promote tourism, and support young farmers through land reform and financial assistance. Three workable strategies at two marks each give the six marks.
