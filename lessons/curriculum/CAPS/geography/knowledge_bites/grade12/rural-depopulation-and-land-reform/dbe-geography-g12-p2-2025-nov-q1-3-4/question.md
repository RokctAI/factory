## Question (2 marks)

Refer to the extract on land restitution (the Moletele community's citrus farm at Hoedspruit, a successful land restitution programme in Limpopo). What is land restitution? (1 x 2)

## Method

definition

## Memo working

"Restitution" means giving back. Land restitution is the land-reform programme that returns land to the people it was forcefully taken from — either the land itself goes back to its rightful owners, or the dispossessed are compensated for it. Either formulation, stated as a full definition, earns the concept mark.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Returning the land to its rightful owners (2); Compensation for people for land forcefully taken away (2) [ANY ONE]
