## Question (1 mark)

(Match the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option in COLUMN B; write only the letter Y or Z.) Land returned to the original owners  [Y land restitution / Z land tenure]

## Method

column matching

Keep the three land-reform terms distinct: land restitution returns land (or compensation) to people who were dispossessed of it — the original owners. Land tenure is about the legal right under which land is held or occupied, and land redistribution transfers land to broaden access. "Returned to the original owners" is the definition of restitution.
