## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to a line graph of percentage level of poverty from 1994 to 2024: a dashed line for young adults who remain in rural areas declines gently from about 81% to about 72%, while a solid line for young adults who migrate to urban areas falls steeply from about 79% to about 31%.) According to the graph, the percentage level of poverty decreased by a smaller amount in (rural/urban) areas. (1 x 1)

## Method

graph reading

Compare the size of each line's drop over the 30 years. The rural line falls from about 81% to about 72% — a drop of only about 9 percentage points. The urban line falls from about 79% to about 31% — a drop of nearly 48 percentage points. The smaller decrease is clearly the rural line.
