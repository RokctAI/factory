## Question (4 marks)

Explain the social impact that the general trend (answer to QUESTION 1.3.2 — the decreasing rural population) will have on the rural community. (2 x 2)

## Method

impact explanation

## Memo working

The trend is rural depopulation: people, especially the young and economically active, are leaving the countryside. Reason from that loss of people to what happens socially to those who stay behind. Fewer people means shops, schools and clinics lose support, so facilities and services decline; with fewer workers and customers, employment opportunities shrink and poverty deepens; the population becomes unbalanced (mostly the elderly and children remain), family ties are broken and there is less social interaction; quality of life and infrastructure deteriorate, and crime and other social ills can increase. Give any TWO impacts, each explained for 2 marks.
