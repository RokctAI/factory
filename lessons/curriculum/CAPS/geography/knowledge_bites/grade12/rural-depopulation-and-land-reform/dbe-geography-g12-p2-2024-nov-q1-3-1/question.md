## Question (2 marks)

(Refer to a sketch and graph on the levels of poverty caused by rural-urban migration: a city skyline with a curved arrow labelled 'Young Adults' migrating towards it, above a line graph of percentage level of poverty from 1994 to 2024 — a dashed line for young adults who remain in rural areas declines gently from about 81% to about 72%, while a solid line for young adults who migrate to urban areas falls steeply from about 79% to about 31%.) Define the term rural-urban migration. (1 x 2)

## Method

definition

A definition question needs both the action and the direction: name the movement of people, and state that it runs from rural areas to urban areas. Naming only the movement from rural areas, without the urban destination, earns part marks only.
