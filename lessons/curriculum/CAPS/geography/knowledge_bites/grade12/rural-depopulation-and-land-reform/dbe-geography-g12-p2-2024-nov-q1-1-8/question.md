## Question (1 mark)

(Match the statement in COLUMN A with the correct option in COLUMN B; write only the letter Y or Z.) An economic challenge of land reform  [Y poverty / Z legal disputes]

**Note: the official marking guidelines accept BOTH options (Y and Z) for this question — either letter earns the mark.**

## Method

column matching

Both options can be argued as economic challenges of land reform: poverty persists where beneficiaries lack capital, skills or support to farm productively, and legal disputes over claims are costly and delay productive use of the land. The marking guidelines recognised this and credited either answer.
