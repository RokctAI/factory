## Question (1 mark)

Give a reason for your answer to QUESTION 3.1.5. (1 x 1)

(QUESTION 3.1.5 asked whether L in block C2 is visible from H in block C4 on the 1 : 50 000 topographical map; the answer was No.)

## Method

explain reason

## Memo working

Check the relief along the straight line between the two points. The contours show higher ground rising between L and H, so the line of sight is blocked — the two points are not intervisible. Naming the specific obstruction from the map (for example a hill, ridge or mountain slope between them) is accepted.
