## Question (1 mark)

Mapwork on the 1 : 50 000 topographical map 2529CC eMALAHLENI (WITBANK) and the 1 : 10 000 orthophoto map 2529 CC 15 eMALAHLENI (WITBANK). eMalahleni is a coal-mining city on the Highveld of Mpumalanga, with opencast coal mines, power stations and a steel mill nearby. (The physical map sheets are separate; map-read inputs come from the memo's accepted values.)

Refer to the topographical map. You would be driving in a/an … direction on the main road from F in block C3 to Valley Glen in block A5.

A north-easterly
B north-westerly
C south-easterly
D south-westerly  (1 x 1)

## Method

direction mcq

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

(Reasoning: on the map grid the block letters run from north to south and the block numbers from west to east, so travelling from block C3 to block A5 means moving north — C up to A — and east — 3 across to 5 — which is a north-easterly direction.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A (north-easterly) (1)
