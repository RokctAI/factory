## Question (2 marks)

Give evidence from the map to support your answer to QUESTION 3.2.4. (1 x 2)

(QUESTION 3.2.4 asked for the general night-time wind direction around the row of trees labelled J in block E3 on the 1 : 50 000 topographical map; the memo accepts north/NW/NNW/north-east — the wind blowing from that side towards the south.)

## Method

map evidence

## Memo working

Two lines of map evidence support the direction. First, the row of trees is a windbreak, and windbreaks are planted perpendicular to the prevailing wind — on the map the trees stand on the north/north-east side of the orchards and vineyards, so the wind they shield against must come from that side. Second, at night air on the mountain slopes cools, becomes dense and drains downslope as a katabatic (mountain) wind — flowing down from the high ground towards the valley, which fits the same direction. Any one piece of evidence, fully stated, earns the two marks.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Row of trees (windbreaks) are on the north/north east of the orchards or vineyards (2); Wind blows down the mountain slopes (katabatic wind) (2); Wind breaks are planted perpendicular to the prevailing wind (2) [ANY ONE]
