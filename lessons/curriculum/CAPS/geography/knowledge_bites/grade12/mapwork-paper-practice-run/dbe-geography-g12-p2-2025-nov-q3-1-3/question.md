## Question (1 mark)

Mapwork on the 1 : 50 000 topographical map 2529CC eMALAHLENI (WITBANK) and the 1 : 10 000 orthophoto map 2529 CC 15 eMALAHLENI (WITBANK). eMalahleni is a coal-mining city on the Highveld of Mpumalanga, with opencast coal mines, power stations and a steel mill nearby. (The physical map sheets are separate; map-read inputs come from the memo's accepted values.)

Why is the measurement of the breadth on the topographical map smaller than that on the orthophoto map? (1 x 1)

## Method

scale comparison

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

(Reasoning: the topographical map's scale of 1 : 50 000 is five times smaller than the orthophoto map's 1 : 10 000, so the same ground feature is drawn five times shorter on the topographical map.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Scale of topographical map is (5x) smaller (1)
- Scale of orthophoto map is (5x) larger (1)
