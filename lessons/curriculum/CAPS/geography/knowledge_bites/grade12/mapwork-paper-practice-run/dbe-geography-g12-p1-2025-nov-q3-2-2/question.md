## Question (1 mark)

Stellenbosch experiences seasonal rainfall. Give evidence from block A5 on the topographical map to support this statement. (1 x 1)

(Based on the 1 : 50 000 topographical map 3318DD STELLENBOSCH.)

## Method

map evidence

## Memo working

Seasonal rainfall means rain falls mainly in one season, so water must be stored for the dry months and some streams stop flowing. On the map, block A5 shows exactly that evidence: stored perennial water in dams and reservoirs (built to carry supply through the dry summer), and non-perennial rivers (channels that only flow in the rainy season). Any one of these map features earns the mark.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Perennial water/Dams (1); Reservoirs (1); Non-perennial rivers (1) [ANY ONE]
