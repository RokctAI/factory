## Question (1 mark)

Map interpretation (refer to the dam H in block B1 on the 1 : 50 000 topographical map 2829DB LADYSMITH): The dam wall indicates that the river is flowing in a ... direction.  A south-westerly  B north-easterly  C north-westerly  D south-easterly (1 x 1)

## Method

flow direction mcq

A dam wall is always built across the downstream end of the dam, so the water backs up behind (upstream of) the wall. Read the position of the wall at H relative to the water body: the river flows from the open water towards and past the wall, which gives the flow direction.
