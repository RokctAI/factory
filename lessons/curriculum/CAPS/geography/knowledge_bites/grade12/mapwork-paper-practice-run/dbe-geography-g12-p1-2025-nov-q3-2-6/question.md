## Question (2 marks)

Refer to the topographical map. Stellenboschberg is a watershed. Give evidence from the map to support this statement. (1 x 2)

(Based on the 1 : 50 000 topographical map 3318DD STELLENBOSCH.)

## Method

map evidence

## Memo working

A watershed is the high ground that separates one drainage basin from another, so the evidence to look for is rivers rising on the high ground and flowing away from it on opposite sides. On the map, many river systems flow away from Stellenboschberg in opposite directions — towards the north-east on one side and the south-west on the other — which is exactly what a watershed produces.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Many river systems flow in a north easterly and south westerly direction (2); Many river systems are flowing (away) in opposite directions from the Stellenboschberg (2) [ANY ONE]
