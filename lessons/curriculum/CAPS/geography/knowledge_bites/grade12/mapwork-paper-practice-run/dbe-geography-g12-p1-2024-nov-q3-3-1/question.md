Geographical Information Systems (GIS): The vertical aerial photograph used to make the orthophoto map has a (high/low) resolution. (1 x 1)

## Method

gis concepts

Orthophoto maps are made from large-scale vertical aerial photographs that show fine detail, so the photograph has a high resolution.
