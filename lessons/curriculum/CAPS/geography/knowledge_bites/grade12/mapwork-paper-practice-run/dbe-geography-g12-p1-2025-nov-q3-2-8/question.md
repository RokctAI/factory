## Question (2 marks)

Give a reason for your answer to QUESTION 3.2.7. (1 x 2)

(QUESTION 3.2.7 asked whether the photographed river — a boulder-strewn, turbulent mountain stream with small waterfalls — is located at K in block D1 or L in block C2 on the 1 : 50 000 topographical map; the answer was L.)

## Method

explain reason

## Memo working

Match what the photograph shows to what the map shows at L. The turbulent white water, boulders and small waterfalls are all upper-course features: L lies in the mountains where the stream flows over a steep gradient with high velocity across an uneven riverbed, producing the rapids seen in the photograph. Any one of these reasons, fully stated, earns the two marks.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

L is found in the upper course (2); Rapids are characteristic of the upper course (2); The flow of water is turbulent (2); The stream flows over steep gradient (2); High velocity (2); Uneven riverbed (2) [ANY ONE]
