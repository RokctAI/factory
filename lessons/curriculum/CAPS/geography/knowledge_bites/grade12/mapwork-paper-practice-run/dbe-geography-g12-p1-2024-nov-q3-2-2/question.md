## Question (2 marks)

Map interpretation (refer to blocks D2 and D3 on the 1 : 10 000 orthophoto map 2829 DB 6 LADYSMITH): Explain how aspect of slope determined the location of Rose Park at 7 on the orthophoto map. (1 x 2)

## Method

aspect interpretation

Ladysmith is in the Southern Hemisphere, so north-facing slopes receive more direct insolation and are warmer. Rose Park at 7 sits on the north(east)-facing slope - the warmer, more favourable aspect for settlement.
