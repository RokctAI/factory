## Question (1 mark)

Multiple choice: The slope at X on the inner bank of a meander is also referred to as a/an ... slope. (Sketch of a meandering river: arrow X points to the gently sloping deposition bank on the inside of a meander bend; the outer bank opposite is steep.)  A steep  B undercut  C concave  D slip-off

## Method

fluvial mcq

On the inside of a meander bend the water flows slowest, so the river deposits material there and builds a gentle bank. That gentle depositional bank is called the slip-off slope; the steep, eroded outer bank is the undercut slope. X sits on the inner bank, so it is the slip-off slope.
