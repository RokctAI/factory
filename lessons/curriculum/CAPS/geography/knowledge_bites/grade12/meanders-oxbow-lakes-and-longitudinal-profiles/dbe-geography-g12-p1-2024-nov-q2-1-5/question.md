## Question (1 mark)

(Match the statement with the correct option. Write only Y or Z.) Causes reduction of surface run-off  [Y impermeable rocks / Z dense vegetation]

## Method

column matching

Dense vegetation intercepts rainfall and its roots open the soil, promoting infiltration, so less water runs off the surface. Impermeable rocks do the opposite — they block infiltration and increase run-off.
