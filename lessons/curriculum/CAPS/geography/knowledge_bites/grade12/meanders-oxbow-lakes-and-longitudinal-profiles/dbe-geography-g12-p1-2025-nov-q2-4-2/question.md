## Question (2 marks)

Account for the gentle gradient of the inner bank at C in photograph A. (1 x 2)  (Photograph A - aerial photo of a strongly meandering river on a flat flood plain; a line C-D crosses one meander bend from the inner bank at C to the outer bank at D, and the cross-section shows a gently sloping bank at C rising to a steep undercut bank at D.)

## Method

explain reason

## Memo working

C is the inner bank (slip-off slope) of the meander bend. Water on the inside of the bend moves slowly, so it has too little energy to carry its load and deposition takes place, building a gently sloping bank. Either the deposition point or the slow velocity point earns the 2 marks.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Due to deposition (2); Slow movement/velocity of water (2) [ANY ONE]
