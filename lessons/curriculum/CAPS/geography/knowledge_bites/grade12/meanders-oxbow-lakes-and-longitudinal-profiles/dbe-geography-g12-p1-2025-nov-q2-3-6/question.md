## Question (2 marks)

Refer to cross-profiles C and D. Differentiate between the shapes of the cross-profiles at C and D. (1 x 2)  (On the graded longitudinal profile in sketch B, two valley cross-profiles are drawn: C near the source, a narrow deep V-shape, and D near the mouth, a wide shallow flat-floored shape.)

## Method

compare profiles

## Memo working

Mention both profiles to earn the marks. Profile C, near the source, is a closed V-shape: narrow, deep and steep-sided. Profile D, near the mouth, is an open U-shape: very wide, shallow and gently sloping.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Profile C is a closed V-shaped (narrow, deep and steep sided) whereas profile D is an open U-shaped (very wide and gently sloping) (2) [MUST MENTION BOTH PROFILES]
