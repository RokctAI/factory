## Question (1 mark)

(Match the statement with the correct option. Write only Y or Z.) Promotes a rapid rise in the water table  [Y gentle gradient / Z steep gradient]

## Method

column matching

On a gentle gradient water moves slowly across the surface, giving it time to infiltrate the soil. More infiltration recharges the groundwater, so the water table rises quickly.
