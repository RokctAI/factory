## Question (1 mark)

Meanders are more likely to form in the (upper/lower) stage/course of a river. (1 x 1)  (Photograph A - aerial photo of a strongly meandering river on a flat flood plain; a line C-D crosses one meander bend from the inner bank at C to the outer bank at D, and the cross-section shows a gently sloping bank at C rising to a steep undercut bank at D. Photograph B - an incised meander looping around a high, steep-sided wooded spur in a deep gorge, with cross-section E-F showing a deep steep-sided valley.)

## Method

choice selection

## Memo working

Meanders develop where the gradient is gentle and lateral erosion dominates over vertical erosion - conditions found on the flood plain of the lower course. The answer is Lower.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Lower (1)
