## Question (1 mark)

(Match the statement with the correct option. Write only Y or Z.) The confluence is located at Y/Z  (Y — a sketch of a tributary joining a main stream with the circled point on the tributary upstream of, before, the junction; Z — the same sketch with the circled point exactly at the point where the tributary meets the main stream.)

## Method

column matching

A confluence is the exact point where two streams join, so the correct circle is the one placed on the junction itself, not upstream of it.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Z (point where the two streams meet)
