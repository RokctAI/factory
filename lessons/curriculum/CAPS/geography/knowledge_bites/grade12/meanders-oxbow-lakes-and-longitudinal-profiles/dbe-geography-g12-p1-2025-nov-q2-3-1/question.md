## Question (2 marks)

What is a longitudinal profile? (1 x 2)  (Two longitudinal river profiles. Sketch A - ungraded profile from source to mouth: a waterfall over resistant rock near the source, a dam with a dashed line marked temporary base level, and a dashed line at sea level marked permanent base level. Sketch B - smooth, concave graded profile from source to mouth with permanent base level at the sea; two valley cross-profiles are drawn on it: C near the source, a narrow deep V-shape, and D near the mouth, a wide shallow flat-floored shape.)

## Method

definition

## Memo working

A longitudinal profile shows the river along its length: it is a side view of a river from its source to its mouth, plotting the drop in height along the channel. The concept 'side view from source to mouth' earns the full 2 marks; 'a side view of the river' alone earns part marks (1).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A side view of a river from source to mouth (2) [CONCEPT] [PART MARKING: A side view of the river (1)]
