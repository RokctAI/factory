## Question (2 marks)

Give evidence in the sketch to support your answer to QUESTION 2.3.2. (2 x 1)  (Two longitudinal river profiles. Sketch A - ungraded profile from source to mouth: a waterfall over resistant rock near the source, a dam with a dashed line marked temporary base level, and a dashed line at sea level marked permanent base level. Sketch B - smooth, concave graded profile from source to mouth with permanent base level at the sea. In QUESTION 2.3.2 sketch B was identified as the graded profile.)

## Method

diagram evidence

## Memo working

Point to what is absent and to the shape, for any TWO of: there are no temporary base levels or knickpoints; there are no obstructions such as a waterfall or dam; the profile is a smooth concave shape from source to mouth.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

No temporary base levels/knickpoints (1); No obstructions (accept examples) (1); Smooth concave shape (1) [ANY TWO]
