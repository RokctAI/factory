## Question (1 mark)

(Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) The fluvial landform/feature depicted in the sketch is a … (Cross-section sketch of a stream flowing over alternating soft rocks and resistant outcrops of rock in the stream bed, producing broken, turbulent, stepped flow tumbling over the outcrops.)  A waterfall.  B valley.  C rapid.  D cliff.

## Method

fluvial mcq

Where harder rock outcrops interrupt the bed but the water still tumbles over them in broken steps rather than falling vertically, the feature is a rapid.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C (rapid)
