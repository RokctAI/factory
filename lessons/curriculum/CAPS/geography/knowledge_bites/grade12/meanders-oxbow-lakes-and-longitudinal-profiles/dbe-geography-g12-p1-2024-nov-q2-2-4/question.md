## Question (1 mark)

(Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) The flat, low-lying area along the banks of a river in the lower course is a/an …  A meander.  B delta.  C flood plain.  D alluvial fan.

## Method

fluvial mcq

In the lower course the river deposits sediment across the flat land beside its channel each time it floods, building up a flood plain.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C (flood plain)
