## Question (2 marks)

Why is the sea indicated as the permanent base level? (1 x 2)  (Two longitudinal river profiles. Sketch A - ungraded profile from source to mouth: a waterfall over resistant rock near the source, a dam with a dashed line marked temporary base level, and a dashed line at sea level marked permanent base level. Sketch B - smooth, concave graded profile from source to mouth with permanent base level at the sea.)

## Method

definition

## Memo working

Base level is the level below which a river cannot erode. The sea is the permanent base level because it is the lowest, ultimate level to which a river can erode - a river cannot cut below sea level at its mouth.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

It is the lowest (ultimate) level to which a river can erode (2) [CONCEPT]
