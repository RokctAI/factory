## Question (1 mark)

(Match the statement with the correct option. Write only Y or Z.) Seepage of water into the ground  [Y infiltration / Z run-off]

## Method

column matching

Infiltration is the seepage of surface water down into the soil; run-off is water flowing across the surface instead.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Y (infiltration)
