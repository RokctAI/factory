## Question (1 mark)

(Match the statement with the correct option. Write only Y or Z.) The upper level of ground water  [Y water table / Z through flow]

## Method

column matching

The water table is the upper surface of the zone of saturated ground water; through flow is water moving sideways through the soil, not a level.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Y (water table)
