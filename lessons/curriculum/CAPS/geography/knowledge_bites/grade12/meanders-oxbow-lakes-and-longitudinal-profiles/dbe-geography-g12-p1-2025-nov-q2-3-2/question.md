## Question (1 mark)

Which ONE of the sketches above shows a graded longitudinal profile? (1 x 1)  (Two longitudinal river profiles. Sketch A - ungraded profile from source to mouth: a waterfall over resistant rock near the source, a dam with a dashed line marked temporary base level, and a dashed line at sea level marked permanent base level. Sketch B - smooth, concave graded profile from source to mouth with permanent base level at the sea; two valley cross-profiles are drawn on it: C near the source, a narrow deep V-shape, and D near the mouth, a wide shallow flat-floored shape.)

## Method

diagram interpretation

## Memo working

A graded profile is a smooth, concave curve with no interruptions - the river has removed all irregularities. Sketch B is the smooth concave profile, while sketch A still has a waterfall and a dam breaking the curve. The answer is B.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

B (1)
