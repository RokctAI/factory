## Question (1 mark)

(Match the statement with the correct option. Write only Y or Z.) Area drained by a river and its tributaries  [Y catchment area / Z drainage basin]

## Method

column matching

A drainage basin is defined as the whole area drained by a river and all its tributaries, bounded by the watershed.
