## Question (1 mark)

Map interpretation (refer to the section of the Klip River from 8 in block A2 to 9 in block A5 on the 1 : 10 000 orthophoto map 2829 DB 6 LADYSMITH): Identify the fluvial landform/feature. (1 x 1)

## Method

feature identification

Follow the Klip River from 8 to 9 on the orthophoto: the channel swings in broad, looping bends across flat land - that winding form is a meander.
