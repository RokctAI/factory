## Question (2 marks)

Map interpretation (refer to block C5 on the 1 : 10 000 orthophoto map 2829 DB 6 LADYSMITH, where 10 marks the inner bank and 11 the outer bank of a river bend): Why will more erosion take place at 11 than at 10? (1 x 2)

## Method

meander erosion explanation

In a river bend the fastest water swings to the outside. Point 11 is on the outer (undercut) bank where fast water erodes; point 10 is on the inner (slip-off) bank where slow water deposits.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Water flows faster on the outer bank /11/ undercut slope (2)
- Water flows slower on the inner bank /10/ slip-off slope (2)
