## Question (1 mark)

Multiple choice: Deltas form at the river mouth when the following conditions occur:  (i) Shallow sea bed  (ii) Steep sea bed  (iii) Strong currents  (iv) Small tidal range  A (i) and (ii)  B (ii) and (iii)  C (iii) and (iv)  D (i) and (iv)

## Method

fluvial mcq

A delta needs deposited sediment to accumulate at the mouth. That happens where the sea bed is shallow, so sediment can build up to the surface, and where the tidal range is small, so the sea does not sweep the sediment away. Steep sea beds and strong currents prevent delta formation, so conditions (i) and (iv) apply.
