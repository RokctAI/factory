## Question (1 mark)

Multiple choice: A/An ... forms when the meander loop is cut off from the main stream.  A delta  B rapid  C ox-bow lake  D flood plain

## Method

fluvial mcq

When a meander's neck narrows and the river eventually cuts straight through it, the old loop is sealed off by deposition and left as a horseshoe-shaped body of standing water - an ox-bow lake.
