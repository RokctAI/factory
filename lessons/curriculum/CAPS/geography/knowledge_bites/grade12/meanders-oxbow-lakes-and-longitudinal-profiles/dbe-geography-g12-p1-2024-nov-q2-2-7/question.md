## Question (1 mark)

Multiple choice: The benefit of natural levees is ... on the flood plain. (Sketch of a river on its flood plain with raised banks labelled 'Natural levees' on either side of the channel; flood plain deposits layered beside the channel.)  A increased deposition  B reduced flooding  C reduced fertility  D increased flooding

## Method

fluvial mcq

Natural levees are raised banks of coarse sediment deposited right next to the channel during floods. Because they stand higher than the flood plain behind them, they help contain the river in its channel, so the benefit is reduced flooding on the flood plain.
