## Question (8 marks)

Refer to photograph A. In a paragraph of approximately EIGHT lines, explain how an oxbow lake is formed. (4 x 2)

(Photograph A is an aerial photo of a strongly meandering river on a flat flood plain with farmland; a cross-section drawn across one meander bend shows a gently sloping inner bank at C rising to a steep, undercut outer bank at D.)

## Method

paragraph process

## Memo working

Work through the life of one meander bend. On the outer bank — the undercut slope, where the water flows fastest — continuous lateral erosion keeps eating into the bank. On the inner bank — the slip-off slope, where the water is slow — deposition builds the bank outwards. Erosion on the outside and deposition on the inside make the neck of land between the two limbs of the meander narrower and narrower. During a flood the river takes the shortcut and cuts straight through the narrow meander neck. The abandoned meander loop is then sealed off from the new straight channel by deposition, leaving a horseshoe-shaped body of standing water: an oxbow lake.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Continuous (lateral) erosion takes place on the outer bank/under-cut slope (2); Deposition on the inner bank/slip off slope (2); Meander neck narrows (2); River floods and cuts through meander neck (2); Meander loop is separated from the main stream (by deposition resulting in an ox-bow lake) (2) [ANY FOUR]
