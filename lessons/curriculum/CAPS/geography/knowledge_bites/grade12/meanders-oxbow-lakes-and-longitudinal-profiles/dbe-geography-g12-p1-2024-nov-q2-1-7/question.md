## Question (1 mark)

(Match the statement with the correct option. Write only Y or Z.) High-lying area separating two tributaries in the same river system  (Key: hatched band = high-lying area; arrowed lines = streams. Y — the hatched high-lying area lies between two SEPARATE river systems, each stream with its own tributaries flowing away on either side; Z — the hatched high-lying area lies between two tributaries of the SAME river which join downstream.)

## Method

column matching

High ground between two tributaries of the same river is an interfluve; high ground separating two different river systems is a watershed. The statement specifies the same river system, so the answer is the sketch where the tributaries join downstream.
