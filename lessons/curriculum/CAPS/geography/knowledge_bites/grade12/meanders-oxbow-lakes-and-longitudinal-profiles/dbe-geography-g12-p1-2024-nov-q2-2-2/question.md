## Question (1 mark)

(Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) A … forms at the base of a waterfall.  A braided stream  B delta  C gorge  D plunge pool

## Method

fluvial mcq

The energy of water plunging over a waterfall scours out a deep basin in the riverbed at its base — a plunge pool.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

D (plunge pool)
