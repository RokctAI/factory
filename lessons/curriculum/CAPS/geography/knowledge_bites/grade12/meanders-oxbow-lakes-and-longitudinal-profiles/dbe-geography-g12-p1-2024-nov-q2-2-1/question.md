## Question (1 mark)

(Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) … is responsible for the shape of the river valley below. (Block diagram of a narrow, steep-sided V-shaped valley with a stream in the bottom.)  A Deposition  B Lateral erosion  C Weathering  D Vertical erosion

## Method

fluvial mcq

A narrow, steep-sided V-shape is the signature of the upper course, where the river's energy is spent cutting downward into its bed — vertical erosion dominates.
