## Question (1 mark)

Choose the answer and write only the letter (A–D). (This question is based on sugar cane farming in KwaZulu-Natal.)

… is a positive social factor of sugar cane production in KwaZulu-Natal.

A Earning foreign exchange  B Provision of employment  C Stimulation of industrial production  D Contribution to the GDP

## Method

mcq

## Memo working

The trap is the word "social". Earning foreign exchange, stimulating industrial production and contributing to the GDP are all economic benefits. The benefit that changes people's lives directly — giving households income, dignity and a livelihood — is the provision of employment, and that is the social factor. The answer is B.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

B (Provision of employment)
