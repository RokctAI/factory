## Question (1 mark)

(Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) … is an economic advantage for a farmer practising large-scale farming.  A The use of machinery  B Larger profits  C A large labour force  D The practising of monoculture

## Method

agriculture mcq

Sort the options into methods versus outcomes. Machinery, a large labour force and monoculture are ways of farming — inputs and practices, which all cost money. An economic advantage is the money outcome the farmer gains: producing in bulk lowers the cost per unit (economies of scale) and yields larger profits.
