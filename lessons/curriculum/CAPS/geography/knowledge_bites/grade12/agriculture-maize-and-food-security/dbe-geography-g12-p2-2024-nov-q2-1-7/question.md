## Question (1 mark)

(Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) A factor favouring cattle farming in South Africa is …  A heavy rainfall.  B high temperatures.  C available grazing land.  D climatic variation.

## Method

agriculture mcq

Ask what cattle actually need: large areas of natural pasture. Much of South Africa's interior is grassland and semi-arid veld — too dry or marginal for crops but well suited to grazing, which is exactly why livestock farming dominates there. Heavy rainfall and consistently high temperatures do not describe these regions, and climatic variation is a challenge to farmers, not a favouring factor.
