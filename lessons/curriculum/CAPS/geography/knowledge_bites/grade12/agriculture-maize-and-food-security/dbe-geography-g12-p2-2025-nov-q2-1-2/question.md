## Question (1 mark)

Choose the answer and write only the letter (A–D). (This question is based on sugar cane farming in KwaZulu-Natal.)

… is/are a negative economic factor hindering sugar cane farming in KwaZulu-Natal.

A Floods  B Lack of access to capital  C Shortage of large-scale farmers  D Crime

## Method

mcq

## Memo working

Sort the options by type of factor. Floods are a physical (natural) factor; crime is a social factor. That leaves the economic candidates, and the one that genuinely hinders sugar cane farmers — especially small-scale growers — is the lack of access to capital: without finance they cannot buy inputs, machinery or land. The answer is B.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

B (Lack of access to capital)
