## Question (1 mark)

(Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) Distribution of dairy products is an activity in the … economic sector.  A primary  B secondary  C tertiary  D quaternary

## Method

economic sectors mcq

Follow the product along the chain: dairy farming is primary, processing milk into dairy products is secondary, and moving and selling the finished products is a service. Distribution — transport, wholesale and retail — provides a service rather than producing or processing goods, so it belongs to the tertiary sector.

C (tertiary)
