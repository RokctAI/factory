## Question (1 mark)

(Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) The main difference between small-scale and large-scale farming is the …  A size of farm holdings.  B use of research technology.  C types of farmers.  D impact on the market.

## Method

agriculture mcq

The names themselves carry the answer: "scale" refers to the size of the operation, so the defining difference is the size of the land holding being farmed. Technology use, farmer type and market impact all follow from that size — a large holding can afford research technology and supplies bigger markets — but they are consequences, not the main difference itself.
