## Question (1 mark)

(Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) Challenges faced by small-scale cattle farmers in South Africa are:  (i) High rate of unemployment  (ii) Animal diseases  (iii) Semi-arid land  (iv) Export market  A (i) and (ii)  B (ii) and (iii)  C (i) and (iv)  D (iii) and (iv)

## Method

agriculture mcq

Test each item as a direct challenge to the farmer's herd and land. Animal diseases kill or weaken livestock, so (ii) qualifies. Semi-arid land limits grazing and water for the cattle, so (iii) qualifies. A high rate of unemployment is a broad social problem, not a specific farming challenge, and the export market is an opportunity rather than a challenge — especially for small-scale farmers who rarely export. The pairing of (ii) and (iii) is therefore correct.
