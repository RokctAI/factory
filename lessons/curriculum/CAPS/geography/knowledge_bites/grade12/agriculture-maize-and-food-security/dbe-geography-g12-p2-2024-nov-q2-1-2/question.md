## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to four photographs labelled P, Q, R and S on small- and large-scale farming: P shows a combine harvester working a large grain field; Q a herd of beef cattle in open grazing; R a centre-pivot mechanised irrigation boom watering a large cultivated field; S workers cultivating a small plot by hand with hand tools.) Photograph … below illustrates small-scale farming.  A P  B Q  C R  D S

## Method

photograph mcq

Small-scale farming means a small plot worked mainly with manual labour and simple tools, usually for the household or a local market. Test each photograph against that: P (combine harvester) and R (centre-pivot irrigation) show heavy mechanisation on large fields, and Q shows a large herd on open grazing — all large-scale signatures. Only S, workers cultivating a small plot by hand, fits small-scale farming.
