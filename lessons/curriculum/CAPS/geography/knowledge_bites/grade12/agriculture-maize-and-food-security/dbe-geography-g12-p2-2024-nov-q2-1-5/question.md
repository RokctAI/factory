## Question (1 mark)

(Choose the answer and write only the letter A-D.) Cattle farming is an activity in the … economic sector.  A primary  B secondary  C tertiary  D quaternary

## Method

economic sectors mcq

Place the activity on the production chain. The primary sector takes raw products directly from nature — farming, fishing, forestry, mining. The secondary sector processes those raw products, the tertiary sector provides services, and the quaternary sector deals in specialised knowledge. Raising cattle produces raw animal products straight from the land, so it is a primary activity.
