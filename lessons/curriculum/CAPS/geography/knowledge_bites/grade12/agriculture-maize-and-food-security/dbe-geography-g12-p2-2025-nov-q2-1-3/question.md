## Question (1 mark)

Choose the answer and write only the letter (A–D). (This question is based on sugar cane farming in KwaZulu-Natal.)

TWO advantages of sugar cane farming for the economy of South Africa is/are … and …

(i) contributing to the gross domestic product (GDP)  (ii) fluctuating prices  (iii) development of infrastructure  (iv) retrenchment of workers

A (i) and (iv)  B (ii) and (iv)  C (i) and (iii)  D (iii) and (iv)

## Method

mcq

## Memo working

First strike out anything that is not an advantage: fluctuating prices, (ii), create uncertainty, and retrenchment of workers, (iv), destroys jobs — both are negatives. The genuine advantages are contributing to the GDP, (i), and the development of infrastructure such as roads, railways and mills, (iii). The only option pairing (i) with (iii) is C.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C ((i) contributing to the GDP and (iii) development of infrastructure)
