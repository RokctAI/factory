## Question (1 mark)

(Refer to the photograph of small-scale farming identified in QUESTION 2.1.2: photograph S, workers cultivating a small plot by hand with hand tools.) A characteristic of small-scale farming evident in the photograph is …  A the use of hybrid seeds.  B that it is only export-orientated.  C the high capital investment.  D the use of manual labour.

## Method

photograph mcq

The phrase "evident in the photograph" restricts you to what can actually be seen. Hybrid seeds cannot be seen in a photograph, and export orientation and high capital investment are features of large-scale farming in any case. What is visible is people working the plot by hand with hand tools — the use of manual labour.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

D (the use of manual labour)
