## Question (1 mark)

Choose the answer and write only the letter (A–D). (This question is based on sugar cane farming in KwaZulu-Natal.)

Climatic factors that favour KwaZulu-Natal as the highest producer of sugar cane in South Africa is/are … and …

(i) high temperatures  (ii) low rainfall  (iii) high rainfall  (iv) low temperatures

A (i) and (ii)  B (iii) and (iv)  C (i) and (iii)  D (ii) and (iv)

## Method

mcq

## Memo working

Sugar cane is a tropical crop: it needs heat and plenty of water through its long growing season. KwaZulu-Natal's warm, humid subtropical east coast supplies exactly that — high temperatures, (i), and high rainfall, (iii). Low rainfall or low temperatures would stunt the cane, so any option containing (ii) or (iv) falls away. The answer is C.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C ((i) high temperatures and (iii) high rainfall)
