## Question (1 mark)

Mapwork (1 : 50 000 topographical map 2829DB LADYSMITH): The mean magnetic declination for the year ... was 21°03' west of true north.  A 2000  B 1995  C 2001  D 2024 (1 x 1)

## Method

map margin reading

The magnetic declination note in the map margin states the mean magnetic declination as 21°03' west of true north for a stated year - on this sheet that year is 2001.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C (2001) (1)
