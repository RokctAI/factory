## Question (1 mark)

The grid reference of the reservoir at F in block B1 on the topographical map is ...

A 26°10'12''S; 29°02'55''E.
B 29°02'55''S; 26°10'12''E.
C 29°01'55''S; 26°10'25''E.
D 26°10'25''S; 29°01'55''E.

(1 x 1)

(Mapwork context: 1 : 50 000 topographical map 2926AA BLOEMFONTEIN-NOORD (NORTH); Bloemfontein lies at about 29°05'S; 26°09'E.)

## Method

grid reference mcq

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

(Note from the transcription: B is the only option with a valid S-latitude/E-longitude order for the Bloemfontein sheet, which lies at about 29°S; 26°E — read latitude south first, then longitude east.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

B (29°02'55''S; 26°10'12''E) (1)
