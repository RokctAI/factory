## Question (1 mark)

Mapwork (1 : 50 000 topographical map 2829DB LADYSMITH): The town closest to Ladysmith is ...  A Glencoe.  B Harrismith.  C Newcastle.  D Colenso. (1 x 1)

## Method

map reading mcq

Read the map margin and surrounds: of the four options, Colenso lies closest to Ladysmith (neighbouring-town names and distances are indicated on the map sheet); Glencoe, Newcastle and Harrismith are all considerably further away.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

D (Colenso) (1)
