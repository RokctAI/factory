(Mapwork context: 1 : 10 000 orthophoto map 2926AA 10 BLOEMFONTEIN-NOORD (NORTH). Measured clockwise from true north at spot height 1427, block B4, to spot height 1431, block A3 — the target lies up and to the left of the start point, that is to the north-west.)

true bearing measurement
