## Question (1 mark)

Mapwork (1 : 50 000 topographical map 2829DB LADYSMITH): The feature located at grid reference (coordinates) 28°31'32"S and 29°47'22"E is ...  A cultivated land.  B perennial water.  C a road.  D a reservoir. (1 x 1)

## Method

grid reference reading

Work degrees, then minutes, then seconds: from the corner values of sheet 2829DB, move south to 28°31'32" and east to 29°47'22". At that position the map shows perennial water (a stretch of permanent water), so option B is correct.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

B (perennial water) (1)
