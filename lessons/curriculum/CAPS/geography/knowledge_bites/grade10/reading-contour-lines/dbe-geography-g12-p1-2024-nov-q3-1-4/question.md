## Question (2 marks)

Mapwork (1 : 50 000 topographical map 2829DB LADYSMITH): Determine the total annual change for 2024. (The map margin gives the mean magnetic declination as 21°03' west of true north for 2001, with a mean annual change of 8' per year.) (2 x 1)

## Method

magnetic declination calculation

Total change equals the number of years elapsed multiplied by the mean annual change. Count the years from the declination year (2001) to the target year (2024), which gives 23 years, then multiply by 8 minutes per year and convert the minutes into degrees and minutes.

## Memo working

23 (1) years x 8' = 184' / 3°04' (1). [Years: 2024 - 2001 = 23. The mean annual change of 8' is read from the map margin (map sheet not included in repo; value as used in the memo). Direction of change not required by memo.]

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

3°04' (184')
