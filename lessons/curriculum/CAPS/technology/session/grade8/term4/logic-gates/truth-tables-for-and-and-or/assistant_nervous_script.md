Hey — if truth tables look like maths homework, they are really just a checklist with four lines.

Two inputs, four rows: 00, 01, 10, 11. Count in binary so you miss none.

AND: one 1 at the bottom. OR: one 0 at the top. They differ only in the two middle rows, where just one input is on.

Then test every row on the bench, predicted against observed, tick by tick. And if a table has one 1 at the bottom, it is AND, series switches; one 0 at the top, OR, parallel.

Open the practice set and write both tables from memory. You've got this!
