Hey — if logic gates sound like computer science, here is the whole thing with two switches and a bulb.

A gate has inputs that are on or off and an output that follows a rule.

AND: two switches in series. Both closed, lamp lights; anything else, dark. Guards dangerous things: microwave door AND start.

OR: two switches in parallel. Either closed, lamp lights; both open, dark. Raises alarms: any door OR any window.

Open the practice set and build the AND gate, then rewire it as OR. You've got this!
