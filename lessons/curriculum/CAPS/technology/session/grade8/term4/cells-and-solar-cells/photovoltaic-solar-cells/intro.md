Today we study the cell that has no chemicals to run out: the photovoltaic or solar cell, which turns sunlight directly into electricity. South Africa has some of the best sunshine in the world, and solar panels now sit on rooftops from Sandton to shacks in Khayelitsha and in solar farms of a million panels in the Northern Cape. We learn how a solar cell works, how cells are built into panels and systems with batteries and inverters, and we weigh the advantages and disadvantages honestly: free fuel and no smoke against night, clouds, cost and the problem of storage. By the end you will be able to explain what a solar cell does, describe a working solar system, and argue both sides of the case for solar in South Africa with evidence.
