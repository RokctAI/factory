Hey — if solar feels like a subject everyone has opinions about and nobody explains, here is the clean version.

Light knocks electrons loose in silicon and a built-in push sends them round a circuit. About half a volt per cell, so panels stack cells in series. Shade on a few cells chokes the lot.

For night you need a battery, a controller to protect it, and an inverter for 230 volt appliances. Rainy days are the weak spot.

For: free fuel, no smoke, lasts decades, works anywhere, now cheap. Against: nothing at night, storage is costly, up-front cost, mined materials, land.
