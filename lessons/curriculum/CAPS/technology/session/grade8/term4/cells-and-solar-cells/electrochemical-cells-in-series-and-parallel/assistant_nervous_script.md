Hey — if series and parallel keep swapping places in your head, here is the fix.

A cell's volts are set by its chemistry, about 1.5 for a dry cell. Capacity is how many hours it lasts.

Series, end to end: volts add, hours stay the same. Three cells, 4.5 volts, one cell's worth of hours. One weak or backwards cell spoils the stack.

Parallel, side by side: volts stay, hours add. Three cells, 1.5 volts, three times the hours. Cells must match.

Ask: what volts does it need, how long must it run? Open the practice set and work out two cells in series, then in parallel. You've got this!
