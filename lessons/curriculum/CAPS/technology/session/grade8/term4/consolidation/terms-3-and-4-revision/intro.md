Today we gather the year's last two terms into one revision session and prepare for the end-of-year examination. Term 4 gave us circuits and components, the energy realities of rural and informal settlements, electrochemical and solar cells, the four ways South Africa generates electricity and the grid that carries it, Ohm's law and resistors, AND and OR gates with their truth tables, and the panic button project from brief to poster. Term 3 gave us gears, mechanical advantage, the mine headgear and the tender presentation. We revisit each topic in compressed form, pull out the facts and skills that examiners test, work through the question types the paper uses, and finish with the habits that turn knowledge into marks. By the end you will have a one-page summary of each term in your head and a plan for the examination.
