Hey — if dams and windmills seem simpler than they turn out to be, here is the core.

Hydro: water falls through a pipe and spins a turbine; power is head times flow; clean, fast, ninety percent efficient, but dams drown valleys and South Africa lacks big rivers.

Pumped storage: pump water up at night with cheap surplus, let it down at the evening peak. It stores energy; it does not make it. Three schemes, nearly 2700 megawatts.

Wind: wing-shaped blades, power rises with the cube of wind speed, cheapest new power, but variable, so pair it with storage.
