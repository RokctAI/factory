Hey — if power stations feel like a lot of pipes and a lot of politics, here is the simple core.

Every thermal station: heat boils water, steam spins a turbine, the turbine spins a generator, out it goes through transformers. Only the fire changes.

Coal: cheap, ours, dirty, old. Gas: cleaner, flexible, imported. Nuclear: clean to run, dear to build, waste to store. Solar thermal: mirrors, free fuel, stored heat for the evening.

Compare them all on the same list: fuel, flexibility, pollution, water, waste, safety, cost, jobs.
