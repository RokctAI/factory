Hey — if 400 000 volts and 50 hertz sound like a lot of numbers, here is the thread that ties them.

DC flows one way; AC swings back and forth, 50 times a second here, because generators spin.

Wires waste power as heat with the square of the current, so send power at high volts and low amps. Transformers change AC volts: more turns on the secondary, volts up; fewer, volts down; amps go the other way.

Station to 400 kilovolts, pylons across the country, substations step down to 132, 11, then 230 at your house. Frequency at 50 hertz means supply equals demand.

Open the practice set and label the voltages along the journey. You've got this!
