Hey — if building circuits feels like guesswork that sometimes lights up, here is the method that makes it work every time.

Read the drawing, lay the parts out the same way, connect one lead at a time with the switch open, check, then close. Nothing? Loose clip or flat cell, nine times in ten.

Series: parts share the volts, dim, and all die together. Parallel: each gets full volts, bright, and survives alone.

To draw a circuit, follow the wire from plus, dots where wires branch.

Open the practice set and build circuit one, a battery, switch and lamp, then draw it. You've got this!
