Today we work both ways between the drawing and the bench. We build circuits from diagrams, a lamp and switch, a buzzer with its own switch, a motor that reverses, an LED with its resistor, and we draw diagrams from circuits that are handed to us already wired, so that the symbol language and the real components become one skill. We add components in series and in parallel branches and watch what changes, we practise the bench habits that keep cells, components and fingers safe, and we troubleshoot circuits that do not work. By the end you will be able to take any simple circuit diagram, build it correctly, and take any simple circuit, draw it correctly.
