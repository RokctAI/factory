Hey — if circuits that do not work and symbols that look like hieroglyphs are putting you off, two habits fix both.

Trace the loop with your finger: plus, through every part, back to minus, every clip on metal. Dead circuit? Find the gap. Cells and LEDs have a right way round.

Never let a wire skip a part; that is a short, and it gets hot. Fuses and breakers are there for when it happens anyway.

Symbols: cell is long-short lines, lamp a circle with a cross, switch a gap, resistor a box, LED a triangle with arrows. Draw them on a ruled rectangle.

Open the practice set and draw a cell, a switch and a lamp in one loop. You've got this!
