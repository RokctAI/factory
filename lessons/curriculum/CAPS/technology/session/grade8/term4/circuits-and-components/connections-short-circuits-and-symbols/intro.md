Today we learn to connect circuits correctly and to draw them in the language every electrician and engineer reads. A circuit works only when it is a complete path from one terminal of the supply, through the components, back to the other, with components that care about direction connected the right way round; and it is dangerous when a conductor bypasses the components and makes a short circuit, letting a large current flow with nothing to limit it. We learn the rules for correct connections, what a short circuit is, how fuses and circuit breakers protect against it, and the accepted symbols for cells, batteries, switches, lamps, resistors, buzzers, motors, LEDs and more, so that a circuit can be drawn and read without a picture. By the end you will be able to draw any simple circuit as a diagram and spot a short before it happens.
