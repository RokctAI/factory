Today we open the term on electrical systems and control by revising the parts every circuit has. An electrical system, like the mechanical systems of Term 3, has an input, a process and an output, and the components sort into the same three groups: input devices that supply electrical energy, cells, generators and solar panels; output devices that turn electrical energy into light, heat, sound or movement, lamps, heaters, buzzers and motors, with resistors that limit the current; and control devices, the switches that decide when current flows. We revise what each does, how it converts energy, and where it appears in homes, torches and the panic-button system you will design in Week 7. By the end you will be able to sort any component into its group and say what it does to the energy.
