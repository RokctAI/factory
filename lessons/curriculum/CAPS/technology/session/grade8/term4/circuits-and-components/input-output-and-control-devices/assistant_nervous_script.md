Hey — if a bench of components looks like a jumble, three boxes sort all of it.

In: cell, generator, solar panel. They make the electricity, from chemicals, movement or light.

Out: lamp, heater, buzzer, motor. They use it, for light, heat, sound or movement. The resistor limits current so nothing burns.

Control: switches. Push, toggle, slide, key, or ones worked by magnets, heat or water.

Open the practice set and sort six components into the three boxes. You've got this!
