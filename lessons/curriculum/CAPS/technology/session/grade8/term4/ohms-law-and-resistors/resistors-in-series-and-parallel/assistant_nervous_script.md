Hey — if series and parallel resistors feel like a trap, here is the rule of thumb that never fails.

In a line, series: resistances add, current drops, the lamp dims. Two 100s make 200.

Side by side, parallel: more paths, total resistance drops below the smallest, current rises, the lamp brightens. Two 100s act like 50.

Read the stripes: digit, digit, zeros. Brown black brown is 100. Draw resistors as rectangles, dots only where three lines meet.

Open the practice set and draw three resistors in series, then in parallel. You've got this!
