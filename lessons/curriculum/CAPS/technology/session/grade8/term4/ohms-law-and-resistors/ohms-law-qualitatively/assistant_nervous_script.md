Hey — if volts, amps and ohms feel like three words for the same thing, here is how they split apart.

Volts push. Amps flow. Ohms squeeze.

Add cells in series with the same lamp: more push, more flow, brighter lamp. Add resistors with the same cells: more squeeze, less flow, dimmer lamp. That is Ohm's law in words.

Ammeter goes in the loop; voltmeter goes across a part. Never put the ammeter across a cell.
