Today we discover the rule that links the three quantities in every circuit. Voltage is the push, current is the flow, and resistance is how hard the circuit makes the flow; Ohm's law says that for a given resistance the current rises in step with the voltage, and for a given voltage the current falls as resistance rises. We investigate it the Grade 8 way, qualitatively: adding cells in series and watching a lamp brighten, adding resistors and watching it dim, recording what we see and stating the pattern in words before anyone asks us for a formula. By the end you will be able to explain each of the three quantities, predict what happens to the current when voltage or resistance changes, and describe the investigation that shows it.
