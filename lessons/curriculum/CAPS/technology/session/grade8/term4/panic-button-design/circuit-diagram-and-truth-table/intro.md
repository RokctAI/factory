Today the panic button becomes a circuit. Last lesson's specification said that any one of three buttons must set off the alarm, and that sentence tells us which logic gate the system needs; from it we draw a circuit diagram with the standard symbols for cells, push switches, a buzzer and a lamp, we write the eight-row truth table that the circuit must satisfy, and we build the circuit and test every row against the table. We also meet the design refinements that the specification demands, a lamp as well as a buzzer, a reset switch, and the problem of what happens when the user lets go. By the end you will have a correct circuit diagram and a verified truth table for the panic button, the two documents the project is marked on.
