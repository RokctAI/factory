Today we tell the world about the panic button. An advertising poster has a few seconds to make a passer-by stop, understand what a product does and why they need it, and remember how to get it, and it does this with a headline, a picture, a short list of benefits and a clear layout rather than with paragraphs. We work out who the poster is for and what single message it must carry, design it step by step from rough thumbnails to a finished A3 sheet, and judge it against the rules of honest advertising and the needs of readers who may have poor eyesight or little English. By the end you will have planned and produced a poster that sells the panic button truthfully to the people who need it, completing the term's design project.
