Hey — if a design brief sounds like paperwork before the fun part, it is actually the part that makes the fun part work.

Investigate: who needs help, where, when, and what help is nearby. Ask what the user would say.

Brief: problem, users, purpose. Three sentences. Name the need, not the circuit.

Specifications are testable musts: any one of three buttons, big and low, loud and bright, battery powered, a reset. Constraints are limits: the kit, the budget, low voltage, two sessions.

Open the practice set and write Gogo's brief in your own three sentences. You've got this!
