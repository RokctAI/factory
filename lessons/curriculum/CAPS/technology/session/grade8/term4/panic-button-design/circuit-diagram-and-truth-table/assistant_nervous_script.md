The spec says any one button sets off the alarm. That is OR. OR is switches in parallel. Three push switches side by side.

Buzzer and lamp in parallel at the output, LED with its resistor. A toggle in parallel as the reset. Dots at every junction.

Eight rows for three inputs; 0 in the first, 1 in the rest. Build it, test every row, tick them off.
