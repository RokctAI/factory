Hey — if writing a balanced report sounds like being asked to have no opinion, here is the trick: you have one, you just earn it first.

Fair means need-based, not identical. Power is limited, so sharing it is a trade-off between many people with real claims.

Gather numbers with sources and voices from more than one side, asked the same questions. Protect anyone who could be harmed by being named.

Write: question, intro, facts, views, discussion, conclusion, sources. Neutral words, every opinion attributed, your view only at the end, clearly marked.

Open the practice set and write your title as a question and your five interview questions. You've got this!
