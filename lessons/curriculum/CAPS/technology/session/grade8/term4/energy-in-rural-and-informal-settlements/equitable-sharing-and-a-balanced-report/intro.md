Today we bring the two previous lessons together and turn them into a piece of writing. The question is how a country shares a limited power supply fairly between a city suburb, a rural village and an informal settlement, between households that pay and households that cannot, between people and industry, and in a time of load shedding, between everyone at once. We examine what equitable sharing means, how to gather evidence and voices from more than one side, and how to write a balanced report that presents the facts, argues fairly and reaches a reasoned conclusion. By the end you will be able to plan and write a short balanced report on an energy question in your own community, the task CAPS sets for this week.
