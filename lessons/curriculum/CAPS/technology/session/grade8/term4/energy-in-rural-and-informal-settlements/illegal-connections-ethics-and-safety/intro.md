Today we tackle a subject that is argued about in every municipality in the country: illegal electricity connections. A tangle of cables runs from a streetlight pole or a neighbour's meter box across a road and into a settlement, and it powers lights, stoves and televisions for people who have no legal supply. We examine what these connections are and why people make them, the real dangers they create for the people using them and for everyone nearby, and the ethical questions they raise about need, theft, fairness and responsibility. By the end you will be able to describe the technical hazards precisely, argue more than one side of the ethical question honestly, and propose responses that address causes rather than only symptoms.
