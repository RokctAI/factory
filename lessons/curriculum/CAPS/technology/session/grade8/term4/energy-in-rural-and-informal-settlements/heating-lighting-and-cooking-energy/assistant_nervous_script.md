Hey — if this topic feels far from your own kitchen, or uncomfortably close to it, here is the shape of it.

Every home needs warmth, light and cooking heat. Some get them from the grid; many get them from wood, paraffin, candles, gas and small solar, and most use several at once.

Each option has a cost up front, a cost per week, a safety risk, a health effect and an environmental footprint. Judge all five together.

The cheap options are the dangerous ones; the safe ones need money first. That is why the problem is about economics as much as technology.

Open the practice set and make the table for the three options you know best. You've got this!
