Today we look at a question that millions of South Africans answer every evening: how do you heat a home, light a room and cook a meal when grid electricity is unreliable, unaffordable or simply not there? We study the energy needs of households in rural villages and informal settlements, the sources they actually use, from firewood and paraffin to candles, gas, solar lanterns and the grid, and we compare those sources fairly on cost, safety, health, convenience and the environment. By the end you will be able to describe the energy situation of a household that is not like your own, explain why people choose what they choose, and judge each option with evidence rather than assumption.
