Hey — if judging your own work and standing up in front of the class both sound scary, here is the calm version.

Evaluating: take your list of musts, test each one, write met or not met and what you saw. Then three lines: what worked, what did not and why, what you would change. Honest beats perfect.

Presenting: tell the story in order, problem to improvements. Everyone says one part. Show the model turning. Point at the drawing instead of reading it. If something breaks, say why; you already know.

Open the practice set and turn two specifications into tests. You've got this!
