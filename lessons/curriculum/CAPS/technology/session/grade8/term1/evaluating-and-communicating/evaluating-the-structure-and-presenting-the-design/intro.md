Today we judge the work and then show it. Evaluation means testing the finished model against the specifications and constraints we wrote in Week 6, recording honestly what it does well and where it falls short, and proposing improvements. We also practise evaluating the complex structures we investigated, listing advantages and disadvantages in a way that is fair and tied to purpose. Then we plan the presentation: how a team shows its plans, drawings, model and evaluation to the class in a few minutes so that everyone understands the design. By the end you will know how to be your own honest critic and your own clear spokesperson.
