Hey — if all the rules of technical drawing feel like a lot, here is the calm version. There are three ideas.

Lines: dark for what you see, light for guides, dashed for hidden, dash-dot for centres.

Scale: paper to real. 1:2 means half size, so divide real sizes by 2 to draw them, but still write the real number.

Dimensions: millimetres, no unit, number above a thin line with arrowheads, outside the drawing, each size once.

That is the whole language. Everyone who builds anything speaks it.

Open the practice set and dimension one rectangle. You've got this!
