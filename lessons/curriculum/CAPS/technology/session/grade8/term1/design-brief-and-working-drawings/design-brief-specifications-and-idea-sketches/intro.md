Today the Design step of the PAT begins. We turn the scenario into a design brief, one or two clear sentences that say who needs what and why. Then we list the specifications, which say what the structure and mechanism must do, and the constraints, which say what limits we must work within: size, materials, time and cost. Finally we pick up a pencil and produce several quick initial idea sketches, because the best way to find a good idea is to draw three and compare them. By the end your team will have a brief, a list and a page of ideas to choose from.
