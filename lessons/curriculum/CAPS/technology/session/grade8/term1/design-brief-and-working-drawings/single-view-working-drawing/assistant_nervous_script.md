Hey — if a full working drawing feels like a big ask, here is the calm version. It is one face of your frame, drawn in a fixed order.

Pick the front. Pick a scale that fits the page, probably half size.

Draw everything faintly first, measure it, check it. Then press harder on the lines you can see.

Dashed for hidden, dash-dot through the holes.

Write the real sizes in millimetres outside the drawing, once each, with the big sizes first.

Box in the corner: name, scale, date.

Open the practice set and draw the front of a simple box frame to 1:2. You've got this!
