Hey — if a blank page and the word "design" feel frightening, here is the calm version.

First, one sentence: who needs what, what kind of thing, where. That is your brief. Do not say how.

Second, two lists. Musts: what it has to do. Limits: how big, what materials, how long, how much. Make the musts things you can test.

Third, three rough sketches of different ideas, with labels. No ruler, no rubbing out, five minutes each.

Then tick which idea meets the most musts and limits. Write why. Done.

Open the practice set and write one brief from the scenario given. You've got this!
