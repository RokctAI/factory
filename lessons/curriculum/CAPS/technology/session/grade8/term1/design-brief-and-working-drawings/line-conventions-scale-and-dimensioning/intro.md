Today we learn the language that every drawing in Technology speaks. Lines come in types: dark outlines for edges you can see, feint construction lines to guide the drawing, dashed lines for hidden detail and thin chain lines for centres. Scale lets us draw a large structure small enough for the page or a tiny part large enough to see. Dimensions written in millimetres tell the maker exactly how big every part must be. By the end you will be able to read and draw a technical drawing that anyone in the world could build from.
