Hey — if a whole term's project feels like a mountain, here is the calm version.

You are building a frame with something moving inside. A crank you turn, and gears or a cam or a pulley that make something happen.

You do it in five steps, one or two weeks each: find out, plan, build, test, present. You already know these five from Grade 7.

Keep every sketch and note in your portfolio; that is where the marks are.

Read the scenario, underline the needs, and the rest follows one week at a time.

Open the practice set and match each step to its week. You've got this!
