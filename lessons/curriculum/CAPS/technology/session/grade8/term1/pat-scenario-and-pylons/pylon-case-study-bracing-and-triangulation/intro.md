Today we investigate the tall steel towers that march across the veld carrying power lines: electrical pylons. We look at why they are built as open frames of thin steel rather than solid posts, how the X-shaped cross-bracing and the triangles inside them keep them stiff in a high wind, and why different pylon designs are used in different places. Then we practise the investigation skill of weighing up a complex structure, listing its advantages and disadvantages fairly. By the end you will be able to look at any big frame structure and explain what keeps it standing.
