Hey — if a forty-metre steel tower seems far from your cardboard model, here is the calm version.

A pylon is a frame. The loads go down the corner legs, so everything else is left out. Open frame, less steel, wind goes through.

Every panel has an X. Two diagonals make four triangles, and triangles cannot fold. That is the whole secret of stiffness.

Wide base at the bottom keeps it standing. That is stability.

To evaluate it: list the good, list the bad, then say which wins for the job.

Open the practice set and find the triangles in one pylon picture. You've got this!
