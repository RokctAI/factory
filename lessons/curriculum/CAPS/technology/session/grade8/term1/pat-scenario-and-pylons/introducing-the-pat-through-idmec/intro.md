Today the big project of the term begins. The Practical Assessment Task, or PAT, asks your team to design and build a structure that houses a mechanism: a frame that stands up and holds a crank, gears, a cam or a pulley that actually moves. We walk through the scenario, see how the five IDMEC steps map onto the weeks ahead, and find out how the mechanical systems from the last three weeks and the structures work that is coming will join together in one model. By the end you will know exactly what the PAT asks of you and how it will be marked.
