Today we pull the whole term together before the test. We revise the mechanisms: mechanical advantage, the wedge, the wheel and axle, gears and their ratios, cams and cranks. We revise the structures: pylons and triangulation, tension and compression in trusses, shear and torsion, spanning structures and the three ways things fail. And we revise the design skills that ran through the PAT: the brief, specifications and constraints, idea sketches, line conventions, scale, dimensioning, the working drawing and the isometric. By the end you will have a map of the term and know exactly which skills the test will ask you to show.
