Today we look back before we look forward. Grade 7 Technology gave you the design process, three classes of lever, pneumatics and hydraulics, frame structures, simple circuits and electromagnets. We revisit each of those ideas quickly, see how they connect, and then prepare for the baseline assessment that your teacher uses to find out where the class stands before the Grade 8 work begins. By the end you will know what to expect and how to show your best thinking.
