Hey — if a whole year of Grade 7 feels like too much to remember for one assessment, here is the calm version.

Five steps: investigate, design, make, evaluate, communicate. Say them in order once.

Three levers: pivot in the middle is first class, load in the middle is second class, effort in the middle is third class.

One brace turns a wobbly rectangle into two stiff triangles.

A circuit is a complete loop: cell, wire, switch, lamp.

That is most of what the baseline asks. It is a check-up, not a verdict.

Open the practice set and label one lever and one frame. You've got this!
