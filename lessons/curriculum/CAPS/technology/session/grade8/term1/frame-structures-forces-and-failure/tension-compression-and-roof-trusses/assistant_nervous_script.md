Hey — if tension and compression sound like physics words, here is the calm version.

Tension is pulled. Compression is pushed. A rope can only be pulled. A pile of bricks can only be pushed. That is all the words mean.

Look at a roof truss. The sloping rafters are pushed by the roof: compression. The flat beam along the bottom is pulled to stop the rafters sliding apart: tension. The post in the middle hangs from the top and holds the beam up: tension.

One post in the middle: king post, for a house. Two posts: queen post, for a hall.

Open the practice set and label one truss with pushes and pulls. You've got this!
