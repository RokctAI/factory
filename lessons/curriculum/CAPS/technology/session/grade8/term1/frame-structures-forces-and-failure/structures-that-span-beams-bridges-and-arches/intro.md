Today we cross gaps. A structure that spans carries a load across an opening with nothing underneath: a lintel over a door, a beam under a floor, a bridge over a river. We study five ways of doing it, the beam and column bridge, the I-beam, the suspension bridge, the cable-stayed bridge, the arch and the cantilever, and see how each one turns the downward pull of the load into forces its materials can carry. We visit South African bridges from the Nelson Mandela Bridge in Johannesburg to the Bloukrans arch on the Garden Route. By the end you will be able to name the type of any bridge you see and explain how it stands.
