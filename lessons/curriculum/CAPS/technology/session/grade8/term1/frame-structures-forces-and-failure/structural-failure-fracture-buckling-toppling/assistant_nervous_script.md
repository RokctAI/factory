Hey — if a failed model feels like a disaster, here is the calm version: there are only three ways it can fail, and each one tells you exactly what to fix.

Did it snap? Not strong enough. Thicker piece, bigger glue joint.

Did it sag or bow? Not stiff enough. Deeper beam, tube, triangles, a brace in the middle.

Did it fall over? Not stable enough. Wider base, weight low down.

Look at the broken model, pick the one that happened, apply the matching cure. That is what engineers do too.

Open the practice set and match each failure to its fix. You've got this!
