Today we study how structures fail, because knowing the three ways something can collapse is the best guide to designing something that will not. A structure can fracture, snapping because its material is not strong enough. It can bend or buckle, bowing out of shape because it is not stiff enough. Or it can topple, falling over because it is not stable enough. Each failure has a different cause and a different cure. By the end you will be able to look at a failed model, name what went wrong and say how to fix it.
