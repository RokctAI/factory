Hey — if five kinds of bridge seem a lot to remember, here is the calm version. Each one is a different way to get a load across a gap.

Beam: a plank across. Bends. Stand it on its edge to make it stiff.

Suspension: hang the road from big draped cables. Cables pull.

Cable-stayed: cables straight from road to tower, like a harp.

Arch: a curve that is squashed all round. Stone loves it.

Cantilever: a diving board, fixed at one end.
