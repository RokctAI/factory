Today we look up at the roof. Every pitched roof in South Africa is held up by trusses, triangular frames of timber or steel whose members are either being stretched or being squashed. We learn the words tension and compression, find out which members of a truss carry which force, and name the parts: rafter, tie beam, king post, queen post, strut and tie. By the end you will be able to look at the roof of your school hall and explain what every piece of timber is doing.
