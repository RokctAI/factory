Hey — if four forces feel like too many, here is the calm version. Each one is a single action you have done with your hands.

Pull a rope: tension. Push a brick: compression. Cut with scissors: shear. Wring a cloth: torsion.

Along, along, across, around.

Shear hides in bolts and nails. Torsion hides in anything that spins.

On a drawing, two straight arrows out is tension, two in is compression, two sideways opposite is shear, two curved opposite is torsion.

Open the practice set and match each picture to its hand action. You've got this!
