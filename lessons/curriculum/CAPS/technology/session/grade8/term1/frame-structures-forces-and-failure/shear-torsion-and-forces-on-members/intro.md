Today we complete the set of forces that act on the members of a structure. We already know tension, which pulls, and compression, which pushes. Now we add shear, which slices, as when scissors cut paper or a bolt is cut through by two plates sliding past each other, and torsion, which twists, as when you wring out a cloth or turn a screwdriver. We find where each force appears in real structures, from the pins of a bridge to the drive shaft of a bakkie, and practise identifying all four on a drawing with the correct arrows. By the end you will be able to name the force acting on any member you are shown.
