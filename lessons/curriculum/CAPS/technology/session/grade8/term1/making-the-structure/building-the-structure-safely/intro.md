Today the model gets built. Making is the step where the working drawing becomes cardboard, dowel, string and glue, and where the quality of the planning shows. We start by listing the parts, the materials and the tools and deciding the order of work. We go through the safety rules for cutting, bending wire and gluing, because a making lesson with a cut finger is a making lesson lost. Then we build the frame and fit the mechanism, checking as we go that axles run true and joints are strong. By the end you will know how a team turns a drawing into a working model in two lessons without accidents.
