Hey — if the making lesson feels like chaos waiting to happen, here is the calm version.

Make a list of the parts and an order of work before you cut anything. Everyone gets a job.

Knife: mat under, steel ruler beside, cut away from you, fingers back. Wire: pliers and goggles. Glue gun: handle only.

Build the base, then the uprights, then the braces. Then the axle, then test gently.

Check at each step. Fix small problems while they are small.

Open the practice set and write the first five steps of your order of work. You've got this!
