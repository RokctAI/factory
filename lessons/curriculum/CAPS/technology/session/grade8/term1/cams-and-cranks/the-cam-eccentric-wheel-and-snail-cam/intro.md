Today we meet the cam, the mechanism that turns spinning into bobbing. A cam is a wheel with a special shape that pushes a rod, called the follower, up and down as it turns. We look at two shapes: the eccentric wheel, a round disc mounted off centre that gives a smooth rise and fall, and the snail cam, shaped like a shell, that lifts slowly and drops suddenly. We see cams in sewing machines, toy animals that nod their heads and the engine of every car. By the end you will be able to draw both cams and predict how their followers move.
