Today we pick up the pencils and the colours. An artist's impression is a drawing that shows what a finished object will look like, with colour, shading and texture to make it look solid and real, so that other people can picture the idea. We learn the difference between an artist's impression and a working drawing, practise the three techniques that make a flat sketch look three-dimensional, and then apply them to the cam and the crank we studied in the last two lessons. By the end you will have a drawing of each mechanism that looks as if you could pick it up.
