Today we study the crank, the bent handle that you turn on a bicycle pedal, a fishing reel, a window winder or an old-fashioned car starter. We discover that a crank is really a second-class lever bent into a circle, which is why a longer crank arm makes turning easier. Then we add a connecting rod and watch the crank do the opposite of a cam: it turns back-and-forth motion into rotary motion, and rotary motion back into back-and-forth. By the end you will understand the mechanism inside every engine and every hand-cranked machine.
