Hey — if you think you cannot draw, here is the calm version: an artist's impression is three simple tricks, not talent.

Colour: a different colour for each part. Shading: pick top left for the light, keep pale surfaces facing it and dark surfaces away from it. Texture: wavy lines for wood, a bright stripe for metal.

Draw the outline lightly first, then colour, then shade, then texture.

No measurements. Those go on the working drawing.

Open the practice set and shade one cylinder with the light from the top left. You've got this!
