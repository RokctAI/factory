Hey — if cams feel like a strange new word, here is the calm version.

A cam is a wheel with a bump on a spinning shaft. A rod rests on it. The shaft spins, the rod bobs up and down. Round and round becomes up and down. That is all a cam does.

Two shapes to know. A plain disc with the hole off centre: smooth, even bobbing. That is the eccentric wheel. A shell shape: slow lift, sudden drop. That is the snail cam, and it only turns one way.

Open the practice set and match each cam to its motion. You've got this!
