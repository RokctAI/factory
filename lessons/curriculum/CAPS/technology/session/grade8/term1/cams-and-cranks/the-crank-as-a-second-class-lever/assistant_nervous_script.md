Hey — if the word crank sounds technical, you have been using one all your life: a bicycle pedal.

A crank is a handle on an arm sticking out from an axle. Turn the handle, the axle turns.

Why is it a second-class lever? Pivot at the axle centre, your hand at the handle, the load at the axle surface in between. Load in the middle, like a wheelbarrow. So it always makes you stronger, and a longer arm makes you stronger still.

Add a rod and a slider and round-and-round becomes back-and-forth. That is a piston.

Open the practice set and label the pivot, load and effort on a bicycle pedal. You've got this!
