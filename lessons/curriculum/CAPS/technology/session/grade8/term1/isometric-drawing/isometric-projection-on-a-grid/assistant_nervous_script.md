Hey — if isometric drawing sounds like a new language, here is the calm version. It is three directions and a sheet of dots.

Up is up. Width goes up to the right. Depth goes up to the left. That is all.

On the dotted paper, start at a bottom corner, count the dots in each of the three directions, and join the dots to make a box.

Braces are drawn last, corner to corner.

Write the real sizes along the edges and the scale in the corner.

Open the practice set and draw one box, 4 by 3 by 2 units. You've got this!
