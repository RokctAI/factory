Today we open up the gearbox. Spur gears are wheels with teeth around the edge, and when two of them mesh, turning one turns the other. We discover the first rule of gears: two meshing gears always turn in opposite directions. Then we add a third gear in between, the idler, and see that it brings the driver and the driven gear back into step so that they turn the same way. By the end you will be able to predict the direction of every gear in a simple train.
