Hey — if gears look like a tangle of teeth, here is the calm version.

Two coins on a desk, edges touching. Roll one, the other rolls the opposite way. That is every pair of gears.

The one you turn is the driver. The one it turns is the driven gear. Opposite directions, always.

Want them to turn the same way? Put a third gear between them. That is the idler. It flips the direction back and changes nothing else.

Open the practice set and draw arrows on a three-gear train. You've got this!
