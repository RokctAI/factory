Today we revise the idea that sits under all of Grade 8 mechanical systems: a well-designed machine gives a mechanical advantage. We start with a worker lifting a drum onto a bakkie, meet the six simple machines that engineers have used for thousands of years, and then open up a bicycle, a hand drill and a can opener to see that every complex machine is really a team of simple ones working together. By the end you will be able to spot the simple machines hidden inside the things you use every day.
