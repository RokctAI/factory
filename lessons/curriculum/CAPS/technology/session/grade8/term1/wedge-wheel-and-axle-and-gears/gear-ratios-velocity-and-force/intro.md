Today we find out what happens when two meshing gears are different sizes. We watch a bicycle in low gear crawl up a hill and in high gear race along the flat, and we learn that the number of teeth on each gear decides both how fast the driven gear turns and how much force it delivers. We discover the trade at the heart of every gearbox: a gear train that makes the output faster always makes it weaker, and one that makes it stronger always makes it slower. By the end you will be able to predict the speed and force of a gear pair from its tooth counts.
