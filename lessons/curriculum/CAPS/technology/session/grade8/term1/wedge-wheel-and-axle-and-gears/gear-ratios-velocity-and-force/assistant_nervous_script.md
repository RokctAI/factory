Hey — if gear ratios look like maths you were not warned about, here is the calm version.

Teeth are steps. A small gear turning a big gear takes many turns to get the big one round once. So the big one is slower, and slower always means stronger.

Big gear turning a small gear: the small one spins fast, and fast always means weaker.

That is the whole rule. More teeth on the driven gear, slower and stronger. Fewer teeth, faster and weaker.

Open the practice set and decide, for each pair, faster or stronger. You've got this!
