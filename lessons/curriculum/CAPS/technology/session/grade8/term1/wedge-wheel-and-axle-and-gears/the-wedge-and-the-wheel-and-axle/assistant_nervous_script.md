Hey — if wedges and wheels feel like two more things to memorise, here is the calm version.

A wedge is just a ramp that moves. Axe into a log: push down, log splits sideways. Thin edge, easier cut.

A wheel and axle is a big wheel on a thin rod. Turn the big wheel, the thin rod turns with force. Tap, doorknob, screwdriver. Drive the thin rod instead and the big wheel goes fast: a bicycle.

That is the whole lesson: a moving ramp, and a lever that goes round.

Open the practice set and find one wedge and one wheel and axle in your home. You've got this!
