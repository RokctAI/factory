Today your team finds out whether the solution works, and says so honestly. Evaluation is the step of the design process that most teams rush and that assessors weigh most heavily, because it is where thinking shows. We learn what objective, fair, accurate and in-depth evaluation means, we test the model against every specification and constraint from Week 6 with real numbers, and we go back to the brief to ask the hardest question: does this solution actually reduce the negative impact it was designed against, for the people and the place in the brief? By the end your team will have an evaluation report with results, a verdict and recommendations for improvement.
