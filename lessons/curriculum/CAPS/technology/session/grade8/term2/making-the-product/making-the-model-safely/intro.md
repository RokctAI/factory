Today the drawings become an object. Over the next two weeks your team makes the model or prototype of the solution you designed, and this session sets up the making so that it goes safely and well: a plan of the steps in order, a list of materials and tools, the safety rules for the knives, hot tools and adhesives you will use, and the techniques of marking out, cutting, scoring, forming and joining that give a clean result. We also set up the record of the make, the photographs and notes that go in the portfolio. By the end your team will have a making plan and the first parts cut.
