Today we move from structures to the materials they are made of. In Term 1 we met tension and compression in the members of a truss; now we look at what five forces, tension, compression, bending, torsion and shear, actually do to a piece of material, and why a material that resists one force well may fail quickly under another. We learn the words engineers use to describe a material's response, strength, stiffness, brittleness and toughness, and we set up simple classroom tests that show each force at work on a strip of card, a chalk stick and a piece of string. This is the knowledge you will use next week to understand reinforced concrete, plywood and steel sections.
