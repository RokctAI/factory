Hey — if the letters I, L and T and all those flanges feel like alphabet soup, here is the plain version.

Stand a ruler on its edge: suddenly it is strong. Depth is strength. The middle of a beam does nothing, so engineers hollow it out and pile the metal top and bottom. That is an I-beam.

L for corners and frames. T where one side must be flat. Tubes for squashing and twisting.

Open the practice set and say which shape you would pick for a floor beam and why. You've got this!
