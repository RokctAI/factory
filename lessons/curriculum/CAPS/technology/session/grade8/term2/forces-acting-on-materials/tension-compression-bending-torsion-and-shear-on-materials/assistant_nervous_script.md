Hey — if five forces and six material words feel like too many, here is the short version.

Five actions: pull, squash, bend, twist, cut. Those are tension, compression, bending, torsion, shear. Bend a ruler and you can see it: outside stretched, inside squashed.

Then one rule: never say "strong" without saying which force. Concrete is strong squashed, weak pulled.
