Today we finish the forces topic with the cleverest trick in structural engineering: changing a member's shape so that it carries more load with less material. Last session we adapted materials by combining them; today we adapt them by shaping them. We work out why a ruler on edge is so much stiffer than a ruler laid flat, follow that idea to the I-beam, the angle iron and the T-bar you see on every building site and in every steel gate, and learn to choose a section for the force it must carry. By the end you will be able to explain why the middle of a beam can be mostly air.
