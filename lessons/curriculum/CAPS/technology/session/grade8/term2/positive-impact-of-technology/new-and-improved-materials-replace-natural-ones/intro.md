Today we start a new term on materials and the impact of technology. We look back at the natural materials people have always used, wood, stone, clay, leather, cotton, wool and natural rubber, and ask what their limits were. Then we meet the new and improved materials that replaced them in many jobs: plastics, synthetic fibres, steel, concrete, glass fibre and composites. We finish by asking how those changes made life better, from lighter buckets to safer helmets. By the end you will be able to explain why a new material replaced a natural one in at least three everyday products.
