Today we look at the other side of the material story: materials designed to be kind to the environment. We find out what biodegradable means, why a banana peel disappears in weeks while a plastic bottle lasts for centuries, and how new materials such as bioplastics, paper-based packaging and bamboo are made to break down or to come from renewable sources. We learn how to judge whether a material is environmentally friendly by looking at where it comes from, how it is made, how long it is used and what happens to it afterwards. By the end you will be able to compare two materials and say which is the greener choice and why.
