Hey — if green labels and long words feel confusing, here is the calm version.

Biodegradable: living things can eat it and turn it into compost. Banana peel yes, plastic bottle no.

Before you trust the label, ask how long, where, and what is left.

Environmentally friendly is four questions: where from, how made, how long used, what happens after.

To compare two materials, ask the four questions of each and count the wins.

Open the practice set and compare a paper cup with a polystyrene one. You've got this!
