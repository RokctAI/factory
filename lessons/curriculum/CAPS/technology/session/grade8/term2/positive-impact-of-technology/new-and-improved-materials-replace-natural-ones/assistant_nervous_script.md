Hey — if a term about materials sounds dry, here is the calm version: it is the story of why your bucket is plastic and your pot is steel.

Natural materials, wood, clay, leather, cotton, each have a weak spot: rot, smash, crack, slow to dry.

New materials fix the weak spot. Plastic does not rot or smash. Steel does not rot. Plywood does not warp. Nylon dries fast.

Result: lighter, stronger, cheaper, safer things that more people can have.
