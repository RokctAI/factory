Today your team draws. The brief from last session asked for a solution without saying what it should be; now you develop two genuinely different ideas as free-hand sketches, each clear enough that a stranger could understand how it works and what it is made of. We learn the sketching techniques that make an idea readable, outlines, labels, exploded and cut-away views, notes on material and forces, and then we compare the two ideas against the specifications and constraints and choose the one the team will draw properly and build. By the end you will have two annotated sketches in your portfolio and a reasoned choice between them.
