Today the second mini-PAT of the term begins in earnest. Your team investigated a product with a negative impact and chose the harm to design against; now you turn that into a design brief and decide which route you will take: adapting the material so the product does less harm, or designing a new or changed product that reduces the impact. We write a brief that names the harm, the user and the setting, we draw up specifications and constraints that a solution must meet, and we see how the forces knowledge of the last three weeks feeds into the choice of material. By the end your team will have a one-page brief that the sketches, the isometric drawing and the model of the coming weeks will all answer.
