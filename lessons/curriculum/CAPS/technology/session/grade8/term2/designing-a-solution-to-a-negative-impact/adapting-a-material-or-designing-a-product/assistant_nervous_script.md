Hey — if writing a design brief feels like writing an essay, here is the small version.

Four lines: what is the harm, who is hurt, where does the fix have to work, what is the fix for. Do not say what the fix is yet.

Then two roads: change the stuff it is made of, or change the thing itself. Look where the harm sits to choose.

Then a list of rules you can test with yes or no, each with a reason.

Open the practice set and rewrite "design a cardboard box" as a problem instead of a solution. You've got this!
