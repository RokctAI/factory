Today the chosen idea becomes a proper drawing. In Term 1 you learned isometric projection on a grid; now you use it to draw your team's solution in three dimensions, to scale, with dimensions and notes, so that the model can be built from it. We revise the three isometric axes and the rules of the grid, then draw the solution step by step from a bounding box, carving away and adding until the shape is right. We finish with the parts of the drawing that trip people up: circles that become ellipses, sloping faces, hidden detail, and the dimensioning and labelling that turn a drawing into instructions. By the end you will have a scaled isometric drawing of your solution ready for the making stage.
