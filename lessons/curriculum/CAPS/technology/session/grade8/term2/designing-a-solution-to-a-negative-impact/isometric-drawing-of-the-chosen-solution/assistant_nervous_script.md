Hey — if isometric drawing feels like geometry class, the grid has already done the hard part.

Three lines from one corner: up, 30 degrees left, 30 degrees right. Count triangles for lengths. Draw a faint box the size of your object, then carve the details by counting along the edges.

Circles become ovals. Slopes you find from their ends. Write real sizes in millimetres, not drawing sizes.

Open the practice set and draw a 4 by 3 by 2 box on the grid, then carve a notch out of one corner. You've got this!
