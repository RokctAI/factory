Hey — if you think you cannot draw, good news: this lesson does not need you to.

Faint box, firmer outline, tilt it so you can see depth. Then write all over it: what each part is, what it is made of, where it gets squashed or bent. The words do the work.

Draw two different ideas. Then make a table of your rules and tick which idea meets each one. Choose the winner and write why.

Open the practice set and list four things every sketch must have written on it. You've got this!
