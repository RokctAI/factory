Today we turn the term's story around. The first weeks showed how new materials and products improved lives; now we investigate the harm that technological products can also do, to people, to society and to the environment. We learn a method for investigating one product thoroughly, from the glass bottle to the cell phone to the two-minute noodle packet, tracing its impact through its whole life. Then we practise a class discussion in which solutions that counteract the harm are proposed, compared and defended. By the end you will have chosen the product your team will work on for the rest of the term.
