Hey — if investigating the damage a product does feels like a huge research project, here is the calm version.

Pick one thing from the table. Follow it through five stages: where it came from, how it was made, how it got here, how it is used, where it goes. At each stage ask: earth, people, me?

Collect a few facts you can actually get: a count, a photo, a label, a price.

Then think of fixes in six directions: material, design, reuse, recycling, rules, behaviour. Ask of each: less harm, same benefit, will it work?

Open the practice set and list the five life stages for a cold-drink bottle. You've got this!
