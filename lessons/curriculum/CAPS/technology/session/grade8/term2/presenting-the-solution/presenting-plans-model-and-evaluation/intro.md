Today your team tells the story of its solution to people who were not there. Communication is the last step of the design process and the one that decides whether a good idea goes anywhere: a client, a funder or a community has to understand the problem, trust the evidence and believe the solution. We learn what a design presentation must do, how to structure five minutes around the steps of the design process using the portfolio you already have, the brief, the sketches, the isometric drawing, the model and the evaluation, and how to deliver it with clear visuals, a steady voice and honest answers to questions. By the end your team will have a presentation plan and a rehearsal behind it.
