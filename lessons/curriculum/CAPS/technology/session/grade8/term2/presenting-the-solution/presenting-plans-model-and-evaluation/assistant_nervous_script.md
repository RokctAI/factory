Hey — if standing up in front of the class makes your stomach drop, remember that you are not performing; you are showing people something you made.

Hold up the model first. Tell the problem as a story with one number. Then walk through what you did in order: brief, two ideas, drawings, making, testing. Say the test that failed and your fix before anyone asks.

Each person talks about the part they did. Cards, not pages. Rehearse twice with a timer.

Open the practice set and write your opening thirty seconds, with one number from your investigation in it. You've got this!
