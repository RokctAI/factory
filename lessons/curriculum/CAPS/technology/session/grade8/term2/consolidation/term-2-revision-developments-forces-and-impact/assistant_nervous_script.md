Hey — if the whole term feels like a pile, it is really three small piles.

Nets: draw the base, hang the sides, hang the lid, add flaps. Dashed folds, solid cuts. Count six faces.

Forces: ask what the load is doing to the part. Pulled, squashed, bent, twisted, slid. Steel goes where concrete is pulled. I-beams for bending.

Impact: one good thing, one bad thing with its life stage, one fix. Three sentences.

Open the practice set and draw the net of an open tray from memory. You've got this!
