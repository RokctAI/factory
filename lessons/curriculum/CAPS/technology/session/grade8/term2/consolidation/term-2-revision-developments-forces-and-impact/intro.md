Today we pull the term together before the examination. Term 2 ran three threads side by side: the graphic skill of drawing developments, the forces that act on materials and the shapes and composites that resist them, and the impact, positive and negative, of materials on the environment. This session revises each thread with worked examples of the kind the examination asks, a development of an open box with glue flaps, a question identifying forces on a loaded member and choosing a section for it, and a balanced paragraph on the impact of a material. We finish with the exam habits that lose or save marks: reading the command word, drawing with instruments where asked, and writing both sides of an impact question.
