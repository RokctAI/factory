Hey — if drawing a box flat sounds like a puzzle, here is the calm version. Open a cereal box and look.

Four sides in a row, top and bottom stuck on, a thin tab for the glue. That is it.

To draw one: four rectangles in a row in the order they go round, then the top and bottom on the long sides. Dashed lines for folds, dark for cuts, sizes in millimetres.

Add a tab down the end and flaps for the lid. Count six faces. Fold a paper copy to check.

Open the practice set and draw the net of a cube. You've got this!
