Today we take a box apart on paper. A development, also called a net, is the flat shape that folds up into a 3D container: every face laid out side by side, joined along the edges that will become folds. We open a cereal box to see its development, learn the rules for drawing one accurately with fold lines, cut lines and glue tabs, and practise with a cube, a rectangular box and a simple tray. By the end you will be able to draw a development that folds into exactly the container you intended.
