Today we make the package we designed. The development on paper becomes a flat blank cut from card, which is scored along its fold lines, folded into shape and glued at its tabs. We learn the techniques that separate a crisp professional box from a crumpled one: marking out accurately, cutting against a steel ruler, scoring folds so they bend sharply, and gluing seams that stay closed. We work safely with knives and glue, and we finish by testing the package with the real product inside. By the end you will have a finished package and know what to check before you call it done.
