Today we design packaging on purpose. Every package has jobs to do: protect the product, contain it, carry information, make it easy to handle and persuade someone to buy it. We discover that the product decides the design, because an egg, a bag of maize meal, a cell phone and a bottle of cough syrup each need something completely different. We learn how to choose the material by matching its properties to the product's needs, and we start the design of a package of our own, from brief to development. By the end you will be able to justify every choice in a package design by pointing to the product inside it.
