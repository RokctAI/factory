Hey — if turning a drawing into a real box feels like it will go wrong, here is the calm version. Four steps, in order.

Mark out: trace or measure the development onto the card, long folds along the grain.

Cut: only the outside edge and any slots. Steel ruler, mat, light passes, hand back. Never cut a fold line.

Score and fold: press a blunt tool along every fold line, bend each one once, then wrap the tube, glue the tab thinly, hold it square, then the base, lid last.

Test: product in, shake, stack, open, close.

Open the practice set and put the making steps in order. You've got this!
