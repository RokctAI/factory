Hey — if writing a report sounds like an essay you cannot start, here is the calm version.

The story is short. Plastic bags were free, light and never rot, so they ended up on every fence. In 2003 South Africa banned thin ones and charged for the rest. Litter fell, then use crept back. Biodegradable bags have problems of their own; the bag you reuse is the real fix.

The report is six headings: introduction, background, findings, discussion, recommendation, sources. Two or three sentences under each. Facts, not feelings.

Open the practice set and write the introduction sentence. You've got this!
