Today we investigate a case study you can see from any taxi window: the plastic shopping bag. We find out why a bag that costs almost nothing to make became one of the most visible pollution problems in South Africa, why bags were once called the national flower, and what the country did about it in 2003 with thicker bags and a levy. We look at biodegradable alternatives and ask whether they work. Then we learn how to write a short investigation report with findings and a recommendation. By the end you will be able to report on the plastic bag problem like a technologist, with evidence on both sides.
