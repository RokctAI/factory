Hey — if a lesson about cardboard sounds dull, here is the calm version: it is a loop you can see every day.

Boxes get flattened, collected and pulped in water back into fibre soup. The soup is spread, squeezed and dried into new board. Brown boxes, egg trays, cereal cartons.

Each trip shortens the fibres, so after five to seven trips fresh fibre is added.

It saves trees, water, energy and landfill, and gives thousands of waste pickers a living. Greasy and waxed paper spoils the batch.

Open the practice set and put the recycling steps in order. You've got this!
