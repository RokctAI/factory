Today we follow a cardboard box on its second life. Waste paper and cardboard collected from homes, offices and shops is pulped, cleaned and pressed into new board, egg trays, cup holders, and the brown boxes that carry almost everything we buy. We find out how the process works, what products come out of it, why recycling paper saves trees, water and energy, and where it reaches its limits. We also meet the people who make the system work, from the waste pickers with their trolleys to the mills in KwaZulu-Natal and Mpumalanga. By the end you will be able to explain the paper recycling loop and judge its value.
