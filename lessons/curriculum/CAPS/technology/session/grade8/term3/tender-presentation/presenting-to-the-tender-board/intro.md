Today the companies face the tender board. Each team presents its research, plans, flow chart, model and budget to a board made up of the teacher and invited guests, demonstrates the model lifting its load, and answers the board's questions; the board then scores the bids against the criteria and awards the tender. This session prepares the presentation: what a board looks for and how it differs from a class audience, how to structure seven minutes around the six documents of the bid, how to run a live demonstration so that it works, how to answer questions from people who may know more than you, and how the board decides. By the end your company will have a presentation plan, a demonstration checklist and a rehearsal behind it.
