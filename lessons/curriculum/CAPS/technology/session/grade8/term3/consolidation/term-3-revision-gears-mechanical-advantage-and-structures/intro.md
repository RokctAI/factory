Today we gather the term for the test. Term 3 built one project from gear theory to a tender presentation, and the examination will ask for the pieces separately: which way gears turn and how fast, the mechanical advantage of a lever or a gear train from ratios, drawing a gear system and a systems diagram, reading a working drawing, and the design process steps from brief to evaluation. This session revises each with the kinds of question that appear, works the standard calculations in both directions, and rehearses the drawing conventions and the vocabulary. We end with the exam habits that save marks on a paper that mixes calculation, drawing and writing.
