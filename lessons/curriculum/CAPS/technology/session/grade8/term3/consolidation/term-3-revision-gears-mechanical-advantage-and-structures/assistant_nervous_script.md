Hey — if the whole term feels like a lot to revise, it is three short lists.

Gears: opposite ways; driven teeth over driver teeth; idlers flip direction only; stages multiply; bevels turn corners.

MA: load over effort, or effort arm over load arm; for gears, same as the ratio; above one is force, below one is speed.

Project: circles with tooth counts, three boxes with numbers, front-plan-side drawings, and the design steps from brief to evaluation.

Open the practice set and find the ratio and output speed of 15 teeth at 900 rpm driving 45 teeth. You've got this!
