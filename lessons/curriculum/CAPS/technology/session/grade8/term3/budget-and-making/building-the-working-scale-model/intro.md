Today the plan becomes a working model. Over the two double periods of making, your company builds the headgear from its working drawing and flow chart: the base, the tower faces built flat and stood up, the back-leg, the sheave beam, and then the winder with its gear train, drum, rope, brake and guides. This session sets out the safe working practices for saws, knives, drills and hot glue, the techniques that make a card-and-dowel tower square, plumb and braced, and the method for installing the gear train and testing the lift against the specifications, recording every result and change. By the end your model will lift a load up its shaft, and your making record will show how it got there.
