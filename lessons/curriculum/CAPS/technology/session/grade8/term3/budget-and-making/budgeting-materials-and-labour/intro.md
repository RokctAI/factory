Today your company puts a price on the headgear. A tender is won or lost on its budget as much as on its drawing, and the board will reject a bid whose budget is incomplete, unrealistic or dishonest. We learn what a realistic budget contains, how to take quantities of steel, concrete and components off the parts list and the working drawing and price them at South African rates, how to cost the people and machines that build it, and how to add a contingency for what nobody foresaw. We budget the model as well, in rand and cents, as a check on the method. By the end your company will have a one-page budget for the full-size headgear and a shopping list for the model.
