Today your company plans the build so that the two double periods of making go to plan. From the parts list and the working drawing we write the list of materials to collect and tools to sign out, we break the construction into steps in a logical order where nothing is built before the thing it stands on, we draw the steps as a flow chart with decision points for tests, and we fit the steps into the time available with jobs allocated to people. The flow chart goes into the bid, because the tender board will ask how the company intends to build. By the end your team will have a materials list, a tools list, a numbered sequence of steps, a flow chart and a timetable, and nothing left to decide when the knives come out.
