Hey — if ratios make your eyes glaze, gears come down to one picture.

Teeth pass one for one where two gears meet. So the gear with more teeth turns slower. Count teeth: 40 over 10 is 4, so the big one turns once while the small one turns four times.

Slower always means stronger. Small driving big: slow and strong, MA above one. Big driving small: fast and weak, MA below one.

And meshed gears always turn opposite ways.

Open the practice set and work out how many turns a 12-tooth driver makes for one turn of a 36-tooth gear. You've got this!
