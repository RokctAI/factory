Hey — if bevel gears sound like a new subject, they are just gears that turn a corner.

Teeth on a cone instead of a cylinder. Put two cones tip to tip and the shafts meet at 90 degrees: flat shaft in, upright shaft out.

Same ratio rule as always: driven teeth over driver teeth. Small driving big still slows down and strengthens.

Look at a hand drill or an egg beater and you have seen one.

Open the practice set and work out the ratio of a 10-tooth bevel driving a 30-tooth bevel. You've got this!
