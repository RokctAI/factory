Today the term's project begins with a scenario. A platinum mining company on the Bushveld reef is inviting tenders for a new mine shaft headgear, the tall structure over a vertical shaft that carries the winding rope and lifts miners and ore to the surface. Your team is an engineering company bidding for that tender. Over the term you will investigate gear systems, learn to calculate mechanical advantage, draw gear trains and systems diagrams, research real South African headgear, design and draw your own, budget it, build a working scale model and present it to the tender board. This session sets the scene, explains what a headgear does and why it is both a structure and a mechanism, and maps the term so you know where each lesson fits.
