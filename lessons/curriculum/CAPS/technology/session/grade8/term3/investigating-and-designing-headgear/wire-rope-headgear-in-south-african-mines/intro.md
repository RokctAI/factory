Today the design work begins with investigation. Before a team sketches a headgear it studies the ones that exist: the wire-rope winding systems that have lifted miners and ore from South African shafts for more than a century. We learn how to investigate a real structure from photographs, drawings, visits and interviews, and how to record what we find. We trace how a drum winder and a friction winder work, what the rope, drum, sheave and conveyance each do, and what the law requires for safety. Then we look at the structural forms that real headgear take and the features worth borrowing for your own design. By the end your team will have an investigation record that the brief and the sketches can draw on.
