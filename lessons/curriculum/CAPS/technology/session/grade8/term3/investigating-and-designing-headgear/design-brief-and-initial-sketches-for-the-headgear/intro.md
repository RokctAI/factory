Today your company writes its brief and puts its first ideas on paper. The tender notice, the investigation record and the gear calculations now come together: the brief states the problem the headgear must solve in the words of the client, the specifications and constraints turn the tender's requirements into measurable targets for the model, and the initial idea sketches explore two or three different towers and winder layouts before any is chosen. By the end your team will have a one-page brief with its specification table and a set of annotated free-hand sketches ready for the engineering companies' evaluation in Week 6.
