Today the companies judge one another. In the simulation, each team acts as an engineering company evaluating the other teams' sketched headgear ideas against the specifications, scoring them, and writing criticism that the designing team can use; then each team takes the criticism of its own three ideas and selects the one it will carry into its bid. We learn how a structured evaluation with criteria and scores differs from a vote, how to criticise a design without insulting the designer, how to receive criticism without defending everything, and how to make and record a decision. By the end your company will have chosen its tender design with written reasons and a list of improvements borrowed from the review.
