Hey — if designing a gear train sounds like engineering degree stuff, it is four small sums.

Weigh the cage. Find what the motor can lift on its own. Divide: that is the MA you need. Add a bit for friction.

Then pick gear pairs whose ratios multiply to that number. 60 over 12 is 5; two of them is 25.

Check it is not too fast, and that the drum turns the right way.

Open the practice set and work out the overall ratio of two stages of 48 over 12. You've got this!
