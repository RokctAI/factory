Hey — if "mechanical advantage" sounds like a formula you will forget, it is just two divisions that give the same answer.

Load divided by effort. Or effort arm divided by load arm, both measured from the fulcrum. Crowbar: 1.2 metres over 0.2 metres is 6, and 600 newtons over 100 newtons is 6.

Above one: easier. Below one: faster. That is all the number means.

Open the practice set and find the MA of a wheelbarrow with hands 1.5 metres and load 0.5 metres from the wheel. You've got this!
