Hey — if drawing gears sounds like drawing a hundred teeth, relax: you draw circles.

One circle per gear, size from the teeth, number written beside it. Two gears that mesh have circles that just touch; the distance between centres is the two radii added.

Arrows flip at every mesh. Write the ratio: driven teeth over driver teeth.

Compass, light lines first, then firm.

Open the practice set and draw a 20T gear meshing with a 40T gear at 1 millimetre per tooth. You've got this!
