Hey — if "systems diagram" sounds abstract, it is three boxes and two arrows.

Left box: what goes in, with its speed. Middle box: the gears and their ratio. Right box: what comes out, with its new speed.

MA 4 to 1: 400 in, 100 out, four times the force. Faster train: 100 in, 400 out, a quarter of the force. Flip the direction at each mesh.

Ruler for the boxes, numbers on the arrows, a title saying what it does.

Open the practice set and fill in the output box for a 12T driving a 36T at 300 rpm. You've got this!
