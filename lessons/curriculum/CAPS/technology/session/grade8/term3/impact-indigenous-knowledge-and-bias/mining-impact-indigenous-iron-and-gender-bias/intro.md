Today the headgear project pauses to look at the people and the land around the shaft. Mining built South Africa's cities and it also left acid water running from old workings, dust blowing off dumps, a history that began long before the modern era with indigenous iron smelters, and a workforce that for a century excluded women by law. Your company must investigate and report on one of four topics, acid mine drainage, dust pollution from mine dumps, indigenous iron mining before the modern era, or gender bias in mining careers, and the report joins the bid, because a tender board expects a company that knows the impact of what it builds. This session gives the background on all four and shows how to write a report that is evidence-based and fair.
