Today the textile tests become a specification. We write the design brief for the fabric of an emergency shelter, the roof and walls that will keep a family of six dry, warm and as safe as possible from fire for a month, and we turn last week's results into measurable requirements: how waterproof, tested how; how it must behave in a flame; how heavy per square metre; how it must stand up to sun, wind and folding; how its seams are to be sealed; and what it may cost. We separate the specifications for the textile from the constraints on the shelter programme, and we write them so that a supplier could quote and a tester could check. By the end you will have a one-page textile brief that your shelter sketches next lesson must be covered in.
