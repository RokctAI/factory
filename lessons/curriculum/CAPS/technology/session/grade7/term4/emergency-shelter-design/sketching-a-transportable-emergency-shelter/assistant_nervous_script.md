Hey — if designing a whole shelter sounds like too much for one page, here is the calm version. Fold the page. Left, a dome of bent rods. Right, a tent with a ridge pole. Write the sizes: 21 square metres, 1.8 metres high. Draw the cover on with its eyelets and the floor turned up.

Then label the safety list onto it: door, two vents, stove place, lamp hook, guy ropes, pitched roof.

Tick and cross each idea against the lists, build both out of straws, push them, and choose the one that stands.

Open the practice set and picture the straw dome springing back. You've got this!
