Hey — if the list of requirements feels endless, here is the calm version. The task: a sturdy, waterproof, easy-to-erect shelter for six for a month. Sturdy means wind. Waterproof means roof, walls and floor. Easy means two adults, an hour, a mallet.

Then the safety that is easy to forget: two metres between shelters, a safe stove place, vents high and low so nobody is poisoned, a door that fastens, toilets downhill of the water.

Every item becomes a test for your model later.

Open the practice set and picture the camp plan with the gaps between shelters. You've got this!
