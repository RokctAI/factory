Today we look at the moment a roof disappears. A shack fire in Khayelitsha at two in the morning, a river rising through Ladysmith, the ground shaking in a mining town, a street emptied by violence: four different disasters, one need, somewhere to sleep tonight that is dry, warm and safe. We study each scenario for what it destroys, how fast, how many people it displaces and what they bring with them, and we see how the impact of technology, good and bad, runs through every one: the paraffin stove that starts the fire, the drainage that failed, the building that fell, the tent that arrives. By the end you will be able to describe the scenarios that create the need for emergency shelters and state what the shelter must do in each.
