Hey — if deciding what a hundred strangers should eat feels too big, here is the calm version. Ask five questions of every food: keeps, nourishes, travels, cooks easily, accepted. Processing is what makes a food pass: dry it, mill it, tin it, ferment it, fortify it.

The list almost writes itself: maize meal, rice, beans, pilchards, oil, tinned tomatoes, onions, cabbage. Then soften it for babies and grandmothers and watch the firewood.
