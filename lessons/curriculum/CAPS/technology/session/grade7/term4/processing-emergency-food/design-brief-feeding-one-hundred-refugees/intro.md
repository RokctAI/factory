Today we write the design brief for feeding one hundred refugees for a day, and we plan one nutritious, tasty meal that a camp kitchen can actually cook. We take the needs from week one, the food list from last lesson and the figures aid workers use, and we turn them into a brief with specifications and constraints: how much energy, which food groups, what quantities of each item, for which people, cooked with what fuel and water. Then we list the ingredients of one meal, work out the quantities for one hundred, and check it for nutrition, taste and camp conditions. By the end your team will have a brief and a costed, quantified meal plan ready to cook a portion of next week.
