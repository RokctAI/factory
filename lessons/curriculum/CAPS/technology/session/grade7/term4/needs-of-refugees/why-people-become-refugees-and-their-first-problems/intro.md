Today we begin the last term with people, not machines. We ask what turns an ordinary family into refugees: war, persecution, floods, drought, earthquakes, shack fires and political violence, and we look at who arrives at a camp in the first days, babies and grandparents, the injured, the healthy, people who speak many languages. We list the problems those first days bring, no shelter, no clean water, no food, no toilets, lost relatives and fear, and we practise the investigation skill of separating what people need from what they merely want. By the end you will be able to describe why people become refugees, who they are, and what the first problems are that technology must solve.
