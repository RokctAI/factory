Today we turn the three biggest needs, shelter, food and water, into specifications that a designer can work to. How much water does one person need each day, and for what? How much food, of what kind, for a baby, a teenager and a grandmother? What must a shelter do for a family of six for a month, in rain, wind and cold? We use the figures that aid organisations work with, we think about the mix of people from last lesson, and we practise writing each need as a measurable requirement. By the end you will have a one-page table of shelter, food and water requirements for a camp of one hundred people that will feed straight into this term's design briefs.
