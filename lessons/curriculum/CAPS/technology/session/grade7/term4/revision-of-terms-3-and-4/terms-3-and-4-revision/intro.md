Today we revise the second half of the year for the end-of-year examination, in the order it was taught. From term three: magnetism, simple circuits and electromagnets, the crank and the pulley, mechanical advantage, and the drawing and planning skills of oblique projection and flow charts. From term four: the needs of refugees, emergency food and its processing, protective textiles, the scenarios that create the need for shelters, health and safety, indigenous materials, waterproofing and burning, and the shelter brief and sketches. For each we fix the definitions, the facts and the skills the paper tests, and we practise the kinds of question it asks. By the end you will be able to find your way round both terms and answer a question from any part of them.
