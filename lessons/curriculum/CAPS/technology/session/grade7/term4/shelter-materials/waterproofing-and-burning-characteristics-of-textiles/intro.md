Today we test the two properties that decide whether a shelter textile is safe: does it keep water out, and what does it do in a flame. We investigate the chemicals that waterproof a textile such as canvas, wax, oil, silicone, acrylic and PVC coatings, and how they work, and we test treated and untreated samples under a drip and a spray. Then, in the teacher's demonstration with tongs and a candle, we watch cotton, polyester, nylon, wool and treated samples meet a flame and record whether they catch, melt, drip, char or go out. By the end you will be able to explain how textiles are waterproofed, describe the burning characteristics of common fabrics, and choose a shelter textile that is both waterproof and as safe as possible from fire.
