Hey — if chemicals and burning tests sound scary, here is the calm version. Water: either make each fibre repel drops, with wax, oil or silicone, which breathes but leaks under pressure; or paint the whole cloth with a plastic film, acrylic or PVC, which blocks everything but breathes nothing. Seams always leak until you seal them.

Fire, demonstrated by the teacher: cotton burns, plastics melt and drip, PVC smokes, wool and treated cloth char and go out.

Put it all in a table, safety first, and choose.

Open the practice set and picture the drops beading on the waxed canvas. You've got this!
