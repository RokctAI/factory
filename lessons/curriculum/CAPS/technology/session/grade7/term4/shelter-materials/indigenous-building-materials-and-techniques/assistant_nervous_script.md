Hey — if this feels like a history lesson, here is the calm version: it is an engineering lesson in disguise. A dome of bent sticks is one of the strongest shapes there is. Thatch is a thick layer of trapped air that insulates. Mud walls store heat. A low door keeps the wind out. Mats breathe in heat and shut in rain.

Three words: readily available, appropriate, environmentally friendly. One honest weakness: thatch burns.

Borrow the dome, the overlaps, the overhang, the tie-down and the mound, not the thatch.

Open the practice set and picture the bent saplings lashed into a dome. You've got this!
