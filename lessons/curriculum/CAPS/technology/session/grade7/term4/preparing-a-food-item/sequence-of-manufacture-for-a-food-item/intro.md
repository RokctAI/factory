Today we plan the cooking the way a factory plans production. One item from the camp meal, the pilchard and tomato relish or the soft enriched porridge, is broken into its sequence of manufacture: what is collected, washed, opened, chopped, heated, stirred, tested and served, in what order, by whom, with what, and how long each step takes. We draw it as a flow chart with the shapes from term three, we build in the hygiene and safety steps that a camp kitchen cannot skip, and we add the two tests, taste and temperature, that decide whether the food is ready. By the end you will have a flow chart your team can cook from next period without a wasted minute or an unsafe step.
