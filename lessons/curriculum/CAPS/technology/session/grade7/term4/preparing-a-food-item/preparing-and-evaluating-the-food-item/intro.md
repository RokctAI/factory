Today we cook. Following last lesson's flow chart, the team prepares its item, the relish or the enriched porridge, for ten portions, with hands washed, roles assigned and the three readiness tests applied before anything is served. Then we evaluate it the way the camp would: flavour, is it seasoned and appetising; texture, is it right for adults and for the soft-food group; and nutritional value, does the portion carry its share of staple, protein, fat and vegetables and roughly its share of the day's energy. We score it against criteria agreed beforehand, we record what went well and what to change, and we write the evaluation that completes the task. By the end you will have cooked, tested and evaluated a camp meal item with evidence.
