Today we investigate the clothes that keep dangerous jobs survivable. A fire fighter walks into heat that would blister skin in seconds; an NSRI volunteer goes into a cold sea at night; a welder, a miner and a traffic officer each face their own danger. We look at what each job demands of a textile, heat resistance, waterproofing, flotation, visibility, strength, and we see how fibres and fabrics, natural and synthetic, woven and knitted, coated and layered, are chosen and combined to meet those demands. We test a few samples ourselves. By the end you will be able to name the properties protective textiles need, match fibres and fabrics to jobs, and explain how layering turns several ordinary materials into one protective garment.
