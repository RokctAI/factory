Hey — if shell, frame and solid feel like three more terms to confuse, here is the calm version: egg, coat hanger, brick.

Egg: a thin curved skin, strong because of its shape. That is a shell. Helmets, bottles, domes.

Coat hanger: thin bars joined at corners, mostly air, strong because of its shape and its triangles. That is a frame. Pylons, roof trusses, cell phone towers.

Brick: thick and heavy, strong because there is a lot of it. That is a solid. Walls, dams.

Most real things mix them: a house has solid walls, a frame roof and a shell tank.
