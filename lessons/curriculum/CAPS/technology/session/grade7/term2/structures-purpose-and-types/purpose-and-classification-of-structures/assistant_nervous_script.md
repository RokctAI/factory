Hey — if structures feel like a vague new topic, here is the calm version: a structure is anything that keeps its shape and holds something up, and it does one of four jobs.

Contain, like a tank. Protect, like a house. Support, like a chair or a mast. Span, like a bridge. Many do more than one.

Then sort by who made it. Nature: trees, nests, webs, shells. People: houses, bridges, towers, chairs. A wooden chair is man-made, because people made it.

Look around the room right now. The chair, the window frame, the roof, the cup on the desk: all structures, all doing a job.
