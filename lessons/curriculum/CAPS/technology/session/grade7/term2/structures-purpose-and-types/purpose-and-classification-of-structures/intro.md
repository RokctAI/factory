Today we begin the term on structures by asking what a structure is and what it is for. We look around a township street in Soweto: a house, a water tank, a bridge over the stormwater canal, a tree, a bird's nest and a cell phone mast all stand up against gravity and wind and do a job. We learn the four purposes of structures, to contain, protect, support and span, and we sort them into natural and man-made. By the end you will see structures everywhere and be able to say what each one is doing.
