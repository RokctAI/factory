Today we revise everything from the first half of the year for the mid-year test. We walk back through two projects, the Jaws of Life rescue device and the cell phone tower, and pull out every idea they taught: the design process, levers and hydraulics, briefs and fitness for purpose, drawing conventions, the purposes and types of structures, triangulation, stability, stiffening by tubing and folding, reinforcing, and the impact of technology on society and the environment. We practise the question types a test uses, we sort out the confusions that cost marks, and we finish with a one-page summary in our heads.
