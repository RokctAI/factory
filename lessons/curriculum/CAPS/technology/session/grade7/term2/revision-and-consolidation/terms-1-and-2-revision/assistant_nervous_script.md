Hey — if revising two whole terms feels like too much, here is the calm version: you built two things, and every idea on the test is attached to one of them.

The claw: the design process, the brief, levers and their three classes, load over effort, hydraulics firm and pneumatics springy, drawings dark and feint.

The tower: structures that contain, protect, support or span; shell, frame, solid; stable, strong, rigid as three different words; tubes, folds and triangles; gussets and webs; the view, the community, the environment.

Then the pairs people mix up, one sentence each: strong and rigid, specification and constraint, load and effort, working and oblique.

You did all of this with your hands. Open the practice set and walk through it project by project. You've got this!
