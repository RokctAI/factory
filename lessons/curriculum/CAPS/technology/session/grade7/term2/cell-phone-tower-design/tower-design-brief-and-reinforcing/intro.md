Today we start designing our own cell phone tower. We write a design brief with specifications for a new tower for a particular community, drawing on everything we have investigated: the four purposes, frame structures, triangulation, stability, stiffening and the needs of society. Then we plan how the tower will be reinforced, with triangular webs, gussets and internal cross-bracing, and how it might be disguised so that it does not spoil the view. By the end you will have a written target for your model tower and a list of the tricks that will keep it standing.
