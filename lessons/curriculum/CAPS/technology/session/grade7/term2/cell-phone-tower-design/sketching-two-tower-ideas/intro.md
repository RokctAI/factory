Today we draw. With the brief and specifications written, we produce two genuinely different design ideas for the cell phone tower as free-hand 3D sketches in oblique projection, labelled and annotated, each showing how it will be triangulated, reinforced, made stable and softened for the view. We practise drawing a tapered lattice tower on squared paper, using feint construction lines, 45 degree depth lines and dark outlines, and we learn how to make two ideas different in ways that matter rather than only in looks. By the end you will have the two sketches the PAT requires and a way of comparing them.
