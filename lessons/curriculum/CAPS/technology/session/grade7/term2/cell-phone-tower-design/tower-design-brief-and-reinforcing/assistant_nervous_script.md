Hey — if writing a brief for a whole tower feels bigger than the rescue device, here is the calm version: same four sentences, same two lists, plus one sentence about the view.

Who needs it and why. What you will make. Who it is for and the main limit. One way to spare the view.

Specifications with numbers: height, base size, load it holds, how far the top may move, weight limit, every face triangulated.

Then plan the fixes before you build: webs in the corners, gussets on the joints, X's on the faces, string through the inside against twist.

Pick a disguise and say what you strengthened to carry it.

Open the practice set and sort the specifications from the constraints. You've got this!
