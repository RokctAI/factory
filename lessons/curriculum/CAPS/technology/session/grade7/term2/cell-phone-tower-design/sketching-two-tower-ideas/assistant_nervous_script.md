Hey — if drawing a whole lattice tower in 3D feels impossible, here is the calm version: it is a trapezium with depth and some lines inside.

Feint trapezium: base, two legs leaning in, short top. Lines back at 45 degrees from the right corners, half the depth. Join them. Darken the legs. Add horizontals, then a zigzag or X in each panel. Platform on top, block below. Label it.

For idea two, change something that matters: three legs instead of four, K bracing instead of X, folded legs instead of tubes, a heavy base, a tree canopy.

Under each, two sentences: what is good, what might fail.
