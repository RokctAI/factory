Today we make weak materials strong without adding any weight. A single sheet of A4 paper droops over the edge of a desk, but rolled into a tube it holds a textbook, and folded into a zigzag it holds even more. We investigate why, using the words strength, stiffness, stability and rigidity properly, and we see the same tricks in drinking straws, corrugated roofs, car bodies and the tubes of a cell phone tower. By the end you will know two ways to stiffen a structural material and be able to prove them with a sheet of paper.
