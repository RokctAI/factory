Today we stiffen a frame rather than a material. We build a square from four card strips pinned at the corners and watch it fold flat with one push. We add one diagonal and it locks solid. We build a cube, a tower section and a bridge truss, and we discover where triangles are needed, how many, and which members get pulled and which get pushed. By the end you will be able to take any wobbly frame and make it rigid with the fewest possible extra pieces, which is exactly what your cell phone tower will need.
