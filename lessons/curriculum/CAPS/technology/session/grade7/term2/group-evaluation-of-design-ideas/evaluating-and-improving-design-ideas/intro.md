Today your sketches meet other people's eyes. In groups we evaluate each member's two tower ideas for advantages and disadvantages, fairly and against the brief, using a simple table and the language of the term: stability, strength, rigidity, triangulation, materials, visual impact. Then each of us takes the group's comments back to our own sketches and adapts and improves the design, recording what changed and why. By the end you will know how to give criticism that helps, how to receive it without defending, and how a design gets better when more than one person looks at it.
