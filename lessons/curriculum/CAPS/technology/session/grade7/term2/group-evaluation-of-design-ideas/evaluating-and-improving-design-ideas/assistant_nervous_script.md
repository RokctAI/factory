Hey — if having your sketch judged by the group feels scary, here is the calm version: it is a table, some rules, and a chance to make your tower better.

The table: specifications down the side, sketches across the top, tick or cross or question mark with a reason from the term. Not taste, the brief.

The rules for speaking: the design, not the person; what works first; point to the brief; suggest. The rules for listening: hear it all, write it down, ask, thank, decide later.

Then fix things one comment at a time, with a reason for each, and keep the old sketch next to the new one so the improvement shows.

Every designer in the world works this way. Open the practice set and run through the table. You've got this!
