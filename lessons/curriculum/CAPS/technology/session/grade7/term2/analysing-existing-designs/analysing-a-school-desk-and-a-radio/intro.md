Today we learn to take an existing product apart with our eyes. We examine the school desk you are sitting at, listing its features and asking what each one is for, who it suits and what it costs, and then we write the design brief its designer must have started from. We do the same for an FM radio and a cell phone. Reverse-engineering the brief from the product is a skill designers use constantly, and it trains the eye for the tower brief you will write in two weeks. By the end you will never look at an ordinary object the same way again.
