Hey — if comparing landlines and mobiles feels like a trick question, here is the calm version: two systems, same job, different wires.

Landline: your voice goes as electricity along a copper wire to an exchange. Steady, private, works in a power cut, but needs a wire to every house and cannot move.

Mobile: your voice goes as radio waves to a tower in a cell. Goes anywhere, one tower serves thousands, carries internet and banking, but needs a charged battery, a signal and a powered tower.

Compare them heading by heading: cost, reliability, convenience, coverage, safety, impact. Then say who each one suits. There is no single winner, and saying so is the right answer.
