Hey — if analysing a product sounds like something only experts do, here is the calm version: look, ask why, say what is wrong, and write the brief.

Look: list every feature of the desk as a plain fact. Tubes, sloping top, laminate, shelf, fixed seat, plastic feet.

Ask why: tubes are cheap and strong, the slope helps writing, the laminate wipes clean, the fixed seat stops theft.

Say what is wrong: tall learners are squashed, the frame rusts. That counts as much as the good points.

Write the brief: who needed it, what it must do, what limits there were.

Then do the same for a radio and a phone. Same four moves every time.
