Today we compare two technologies that do the same job in very different ways: the landline telephone, carried on copper wires to a fixed point in a house, and the mobile phone, carried through the air from tower to tower. We trace how each system works, from the handset to the exchange or the tower, and we weigh their advantages and disadvantages for cost, reliability, convenience, safety and reach. South Africa skipped most of the landline age and went straight to mobile, and by the end you will understand why, and what was gained and lost along the way.
