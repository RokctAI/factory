Today we get the tower ready to build. First we list every resource we will need, materials and tools, with quantities and reasons, so that nothing is missing when the making lesson starts. Then we draw a 2D working drawing of one face of the cell phone tower, to scale, with every dimension a maker would need: the height, the base width, the platform width, the levels of the horizontals and the positions of the diagonals. By the end you will have a parts list and a drawing that a classmate could build your tower from without asking you a single question.
