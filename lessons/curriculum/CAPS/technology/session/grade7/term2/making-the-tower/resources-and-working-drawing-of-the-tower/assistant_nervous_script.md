Hey — if a resource list and a working drawing sound like paperwork before the fun part, here is the calm version: they are what make the fun part work.

The list: go through your sketch part by part and write what you need, how many and why. Then the tools. Then weigh a sample and check you are under 150 grams.

The drawing: one face of the tower, flat, at 1 to 2. Centre line, base, platform, legs, levels, diagonals. Real sizes in millimetres, each once. Letter the parts and match them to a table.

Then the best bit: cover the drawing with plastic and build the faces right on top of it, so they all come out the same.

Open the practice set and work through the list and the drawing steps. You've got this!
