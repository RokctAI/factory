Hey — if impact on society and the environment feels like an essay topic with no right answer, here is the calm version: two columns.

Left column, the good: a clinic that can call an ambulance, a school with internet, money sent home, a shop taking cards. Right column, the bad: a mast in the view, health worries, screen time, theft, smelted steel, diesel smoke, birds in the wires.

Then two questions. Who gets the good and who gets the bad? And can design shrink the bad: solar instead of diesel, a disguised mast, shared towers?

Designers find the needs by asking people, and they list both columns honestly. That is the whole method.

Open the practice set and fill in the two columns. You've got this!
