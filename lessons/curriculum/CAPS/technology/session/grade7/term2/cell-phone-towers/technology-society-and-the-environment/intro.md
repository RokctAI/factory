Today we step back from steel and triangles and ask who technology is for. A cell phone tower changes a community: a grandmother in a rural village can now phone the clinic, a spaza shop can take card payments, a learner can look up homework, but the tower also sits in someone's view, uses electricity and worries some people. We look at how designers find out what a society needs, how a technology's impact spreads to people who never asked for it, and how the environment pays. By the end you will have a way of weighing the good and the bad of any technology, not just towers.
