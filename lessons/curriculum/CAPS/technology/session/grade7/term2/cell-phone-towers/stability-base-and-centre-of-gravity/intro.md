Today we look at the design problems a cell phone tower must solve beyond simply standing up. Why do neighbours complain about towers, and what can designers do about it? What keeps a tall thin thing from toppling in the wind? We meet the ideas of stability, base size and centre of gravity with a broom, a traffic cone and a stack of books, and we look at how towers are reinforced with gussets, webs and bracing. By the end you will know the three ways to make anything more stable and why a tower is shaped like a wide-footed, narrow-headed giant.
