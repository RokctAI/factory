Hey — if the parts of a tower and the word triangulation feel like a lot, here is the calm version: look up and count.

Legs carry the weight down. Flat bars hold the legs apart. Slanting bars make triangles. Antennas on top, concrete at the bottom, wide base, narrow top.

Why triangles? A rectangle leans sideways like a cardboard box folding. A triangle cannot lean without a side stretching. So the slanting bars lock the tower stiff, and that is why it can be mostly air and still stand in a storm.

Pylons, farm windmills and mine headgear all use the same trick.
