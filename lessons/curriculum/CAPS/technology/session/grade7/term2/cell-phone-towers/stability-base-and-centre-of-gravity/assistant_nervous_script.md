Hey — if centre of gravity and stability sound like physics you have not done yet, here is the calm version: it is just the rule for why things fall over.

Every object has a balance point. It stays up while a line straight down from that point lands inside its base. Tilt a cone, it rocks back. Stand a broom on its handle end, it topples.

Three ways to stop toppling: wider base, weight lower down, or bolt it to the ground. A tower does all three.

Strengthen the weak spots with gussets at corners, webs between bars and an X of bracing inside.

And remember the neighbours: a tower nobody wants to look at is a design problem too.

Open the practice set and tilt the objects in your head. You've got this!
