Today we investigate the structure this whole term is built around: the cell phone tower. We stand at the foot of a lattice mast outside a township and look up, counting legs, horizontal bars and diagonals, and asking why the diagonals are there. Then we look at its cousins, electricity pylons, old farm windmills and the headgear over gold mine shafts, and find the same pattern in every one: triangles. By the end you will be able to read a tower like a page and explain why triangulation is the secret of tall, light, strong frames.
