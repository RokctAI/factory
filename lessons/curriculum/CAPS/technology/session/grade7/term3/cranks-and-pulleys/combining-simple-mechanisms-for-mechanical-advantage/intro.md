Today we open up a complicated machine and find that it is only simple machines holding hands. A bicycle, a crane, a wind-up toy and a hand drill all turn out to be levers, wheels, pulleys and gears joined so that the output of one becomes the input of the next, and the mechanical advantage of each stage multiplies through the chain. We trace the input, the process and the output through a real machine, we work out why combining mechanisms lets a small hand move a heavy load, and we see where the crane you are designing fits in. By the end you will be able to break a complex machine into its simple mechanisms and explain how their advantages combine.
