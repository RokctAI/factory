Hey — if "second-class lever" sounds like a trap, here is the calm version. A crank is a handle on an arm on an axle. Freeze it: the axle is the pivot, the handle is where you push, the string on the drum is the load, and the load sits between the other two. Load in the middle is second class, same as a wheelbarrow.

Longer handle, easier turning: handle length over drum radius is the advantage. 90 over 15 is six.

For the crane: 60 to 90 millimetre handle, cotton-reel drum, handle at right angles, and a peg so it cannot unwind.

Open the practice set and picture the well bucket coming up. You've got this!
