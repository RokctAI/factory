Hey — if a bicycle full of parts looks like too much to explain, here is the calm version: every machine is simple mechanisms in a row. Ask what goes in, what each part does, what comes out.

A lever doubles your push, a pulley system triples it, and in a chain they multiply to six. You pay with distance: your hand moves six times as far as the load.

Your crane is just crank, drum, pulley, magnet. The crank does the multiplying; the pulley only changes direction.

Open the practice set and picture the egg beater spinning. You've got this!
