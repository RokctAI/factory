Hey — if pulleys feel like a puzzle, here is the calm version. A pulley is a wheel with a groove and a rope in it, a wheel and axle that turns a rope round a corner. Fixed to something: changes direction, same force. Hanging on the load: two ropes share the load, half the effort, twice the distance.

Count the ropes holding the load. That number is the advantage.

Your crane needs one fixed pulley at the tip, light and grooved, so the string drops straight down to the magnet.

Open the practice set and picture the flag going up as the rope comes down. You've got this!
