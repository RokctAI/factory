Hey — if electricity feels invisible and scary, here is the calm version: a cell pushes, wires carry, a switch controls, a bulb does the job. Join them in a ring and the bulb lights.

Break the ring anywhere and it stops. That is really the whole rule.

Metals let current through; plastic and rubber do not. A buzzer works one way only. Never join a wire straight across a cell.

To draw it: a rectangle, a long and short line for the cell, a hinged gap for the switch, a circle with a cross for the bulb.

Open the practice set and picture the loop. You've got this!
