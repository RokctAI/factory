Hey — if winding wire round a nail sounds fiddly, here is the calm version: current in a wire makes a magnetic field. Coil the wire and the field gets stronger. Put iron in the middle and it gets hundreds of times stronger.

Press the switch, the nail is a magnet. Let go, it is a plain nail again. That is the whole trick, and it is why a crane can drop its load.

Stronger: more turns, more current, iron core. Weaker: loose turns both ways, or a copper core.
