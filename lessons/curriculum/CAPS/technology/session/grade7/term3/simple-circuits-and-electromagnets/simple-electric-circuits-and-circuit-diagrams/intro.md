Today we make electricity do a job on the bench. A cell, two wires, a switch and a small bulb or buzzer are enough to build a complete circuit, watch it fail when one connection is loose, and fix it. Then we learn to draw what we built using the standard symbols that every electrician and engineer in the world recognises, so that a diagram drawn in Durban can be built in Dakar. By the end you will be able to build a working circuit in a group, explain why it must be a closed loop, name each component and draw a correct circuit diagram.
