Today we make a magnet out of a nail and switch it on and off. We wind insulated copper wire round an iron nail, connect the ends to a cell through a switch, and watch paper clips leap to the nail while the switch is closed and drop the instant it opens. Then we find out what makes the electromagnet stronger: more turns, more current, and most of all the iron core in the middle. By the end you will be able to build an electromagnet, explain how it works, name three ways to strengthen it and say why it is the perfect lifting device for a scrap crane.
