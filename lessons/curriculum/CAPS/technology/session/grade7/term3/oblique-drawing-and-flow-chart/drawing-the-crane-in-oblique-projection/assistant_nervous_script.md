Hey — if a full working drawing in one sitting sounds like a lot, here is the calm version. Sheet landscape, border, title block, scale 1:2. Table of halves. Then draw in order: base, mast, arm, pulley, magnet, winch. Light lines, then dark edges.

Dashed for anything hidden, chain through the centres. Sizes once each, the real numbers. Circle the winch and draw it bigger as DETAIL A.

Then let a friend read it as if building it.

Open the practice set and picture the base parallelogram with the mast standing on it. You've got this!
