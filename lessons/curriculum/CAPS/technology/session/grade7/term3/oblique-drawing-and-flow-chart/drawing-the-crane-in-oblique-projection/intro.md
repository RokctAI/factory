Today the chosen crane becomes a working drawing. On squared paper at a scale of 1:2 we draw the crane in cabinet oblique: base first, then mast, arm, winch and the hanging electromagnet, with the hidden axle shown dashed, the drum's centre line in chain, and every size the maker needs written once in millimetres. We add a detail of the winch at a larger scale and a title block, and we check the drawing against the brief and the sketch. By the end you will have the drawing your team will build from in weeks eight and nine, and the one the PAT marks for communication.
