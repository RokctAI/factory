Today we sharpen the drawing skills from term one before we draw the crane. Oblique drawing shows a true front face with the depth running back at 45 degrees, so a box or a crane looks solid on flat paper. We revise the line types that give a drawing its grammar, thick outlines, thin construction lines, dashed hidden detail, chain centre lines, the scale that lets a 350 millimetre crane fit on an A4 page, and the dimensioning rules that tell the maker every size without a ruler on the drawing. By the end you will be able to draw a simple box in oblique at a stated scale with correct line types and dimensions, which is exactly what the crane drawing will need.
