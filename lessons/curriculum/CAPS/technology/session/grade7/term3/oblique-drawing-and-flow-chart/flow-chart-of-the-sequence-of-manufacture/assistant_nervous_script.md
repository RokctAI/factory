Hey — if a flow chart sounds like something for computers, here is the calm version: it is a recipe drawn with shapes. Oval to start, a rectangle for each step with a verb, a diamond for every question with a yes arrow and a no arrow, oval to end.

For the crane: glue the slow parts first, wind the coil while they dry, test the magnet before you build it in, test the whole crane before you call it done, and loop back to fix anything that says no.

Then read it aloud with your team and tick it as you go.
