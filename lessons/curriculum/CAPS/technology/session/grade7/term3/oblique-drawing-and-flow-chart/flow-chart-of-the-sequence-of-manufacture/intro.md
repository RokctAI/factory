Today we plan the build before we touch the card. A flow chart is a drawing of a process, boxes for steps, diamonds for decisions and arrows for the order, and we use it to lay out exactly how the crane and its electromagnet will be made in four periods: what is cut first, what is wound while glue dries, where to stop and test, and what to do if the test fails. We learn the standard shapes, we write the steps in the right order, and we build in the two tests that save a model. By the end you will have a flow chart your team can follow on making day without a single argument about what comes next.
