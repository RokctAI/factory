Today the electromagnet stops being a drawing and becomes a working part. We wind the coil on the soft iron core, strip and connect the wire, wire the cell, push switch and indicator bulb exactly as the circuit diagram shows, and then test against the specification: ten clips lifted, all dropped within a second of letting go. We work safely with cells and a craft knife, we record every test, and we fix the three faults that stop most electromagnets working. By the end your team will have a tested, labelled electromagnet with its circuit, ready to hang from the crane, and a results table for the evaluation.
