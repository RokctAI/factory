Hey — if making day feels like the moment everything can go wrong, here is the calm version. Nail that lets go. Wire wound tight, one way, 100 turns. Ends scraped bright. Cell, switch, coil, bulb in a loop, switch last. Press: light on means coil on.

Test three times: ten clips up, all down in a second. Weak? Change one thing: turns, cell, direction, bare patches.

Write everything down. A recorded failure is worth more marks than an unrecorded success.

Open the practice set and picture the clips leaping to the nail. You've got this!
