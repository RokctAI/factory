Today the crane itself is built: base, mast, arm, winch and pulley, from the working drawing and in the order of the flow chart, with the tested electromagnet hung from the string and the circuit fixed to the base. We cut and roll card into stiff tubes, triangulate the frame, mount the drum and crank so the string winds true, fit the pulley at the arm's tip, and then run the whole-crane test: lift the clips 150 millimetres, swing or move them 200 millimetres to the bin, drop them, and stay standing. By the end your team will have a working model that sorts ferrous from non-ferrous metal, and the test results to prove it.
