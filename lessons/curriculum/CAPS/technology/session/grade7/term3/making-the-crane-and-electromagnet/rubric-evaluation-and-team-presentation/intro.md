Today we judge the cranes fairly and then speak for our own. First the class agrees a rubric, a table of criteria taken straight from the specifications with a points scale for each, so that every team's model is measured by the same yardstick and not by who shouts loudest. Each team then evaluates another team's crane with the rubric, test by test, and writes feedback that is honest and useful. Finally each team presents its sketches, working drawing and working model, explaining how magnetic sorting works, how its electromagnet could be made stronger and why recycling scrap metal matters. By the end you will be able to build a rubric, evaluate objectively, and present your design work with evidence.
