Today we gather the whole of term three into one revision sweep, in the order the term ran. Magnetism: poles, attraction and repulsion, ferrous and non-ferrous metals, domains and how a magnet is made and destroyed. Simple circuits: cell, switch, conductor, bulb, the symbols and the series loop. Electromagnets: current makes a field, the coil, the core, how to strengthen and why it can be switched off. Cranks and pulleys: the crank as a second-class lever, the pulley as a wheel and axle, fixed and movable. Mechanical advantage: load over effort, the distance trade and how advantages multiply. By the end you will be able to answer an examination question on any of them and connect each to the crane.
