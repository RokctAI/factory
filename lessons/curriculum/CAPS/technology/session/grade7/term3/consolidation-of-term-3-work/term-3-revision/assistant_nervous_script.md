Hey — if a whole term in one lesson feels like a wall, here is the calm version, four shelves. Magnets: two poles, like repel, only ferrous metals stick, domains line up. Circuits: a closed loop of cell, switch, wire, bulb, drawn with symbols. Electromagnets: coil plus iron core, switch it off, strengthen with turns, current, core. Mechanisms: advantage is load over effort, crank is a second-class lever, pulley is a wheel and axle, count the ropes.

Every shelf has the crane on it, so if you forget a fact, picture the crane and ask which part it is.

Open the practice set and work shelf by shelf. You've got this!
