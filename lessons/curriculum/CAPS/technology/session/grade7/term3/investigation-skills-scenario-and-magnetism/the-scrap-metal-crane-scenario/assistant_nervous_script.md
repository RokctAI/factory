Hey — if a whole term's project feels huge, here is the calm version: a scrap dealer has mixed metal and needs the steel pulled out. That is the need.

A crane with a magnet is the answer we are going to build. A crane has a base, a body, an arm, a cable over a pulley and a magnet on the end. It lifts, swings and lowers. A counterweight stops it tipping.

The magnet must switch off to drop the steel, so it will be an electromagnet. Everything else this term is just building those pieces one at a time.

Open the practice set and picture the yard and the crane. You've got this!
