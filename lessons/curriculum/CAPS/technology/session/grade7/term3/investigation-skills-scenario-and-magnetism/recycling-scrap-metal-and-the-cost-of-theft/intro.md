Today we step back from the bench and look at the people around the scrap yard. Where does the metal come from, who collects it, and what happens to it after the dealer sells it? We follow a steel can from the bin to a new car part, we meet the gleaners who push trolleys of scrap across town to earn a living, and we face the other side of the trade: stolen copper cable that leaves a suburb without electricity and stolen manhole covers that leave a hole in the road. By the end you will be able to explain why metal recycling matters, tell honest gleaning from theft, and say what a responsible dealer must do.
