Hey — if this lesson feels more like social studies than technology, here is the calm version: metal recycles as good as new, so scrap is worth money and saves ore, energy and landfill.

Gleaners collect most of it from bins and sell it by the kilogram. Honest, skilled work.

Thieves cut copper cable and steal manhole covers, which blacks out suburbs and kills people. Dealers must record every seller and refuse burnt cable and covers, or they are guilty too.

Open the practice set and picture the two trolleys at the gate. You've got this!
