Today we meet the problem that drives the whole term. A scrap-metal dealer on the edge of town receives a jumble of old metal every day: car parts, fence wire, bent gutters, pots, cans and cable. Some of it is steel and some is copper, aluminium or brass, and the two groups must be separated before they can be sold for recycling. The dealer wants a crane with a magnet that lifts only the metals a magnet attracts. We read the scenario carefully, pull out the real need, and then study photographs of cranes to collect ideas for our own design. By the end you will be able to state the problem in your own words and point out the parts of a crane that make it work.
