Hey — if a tray of metals feels like a lot to remember, here is the calm version: a magnet pulls iron, and steel is mostly iron. That is almost the whole list.

Copper, aluminium, brass and lead are ignored. Metals with iron are ferrous; the rest are non-ferrous.

The tin can and galvanised wire are steel with a thin coat, so they stick. Trust the test, not the name.

Write it in a table, test the same way each time, and finish with the sentence: a magnet separates ferrous from non-ferrous scrap.

Open the practice set and picture the nail jumping to the magnet. You've got this!
