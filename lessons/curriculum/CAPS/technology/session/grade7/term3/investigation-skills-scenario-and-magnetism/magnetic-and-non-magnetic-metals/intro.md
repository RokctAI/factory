Today we test the one fact the whole scrap-yard idea depends on: a magnet attracts some metals and ignores others. We bring a tray of metal objects to the bench, a steel nail, a copper wire, an aluminium can, a brass key, a two-rand coin, a tin can and a stainless steel spoon, and we test each with a magnet and record the result. By the end you will know which metals are magnetic, what they have in common, why a tin can sticks but a cooldrink can does not, and how to write up a fair materials test.
