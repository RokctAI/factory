Hey — if two sketches and a circuit sounds like a lot for one period, here is the calm version. Fold the page. Left: a mast with a swinging arm. Right: an arm that tips up and down. Label the parts, write what they are made of, tick them against the brief.

The circuit is one loop: cell, switch, coil, bulb. Four symbols, a ruler, square corners, switch open.

Same crane in two colours does not count. No switch does not count. Everything else is just neat labelling.

Open the practice set and picture the string running up the mast and along the arm. You've got this!
