Hey — if the words brief, specification and constraint are swimming together, here is the calm version. The brief is one sentence: what you are making, who it is for, what it must do. A specification is what the crane must do, with a number you can test: lift ten clips, drop them in one second. A constraint is a limit on you: only card, string and a nail, on an A4 base, in four periods.

Ask "is this about the crane or about me?" Crane: specification. Me: constraint.

Open the practice set and picture the dealer's mixed pile with the steel coming out. You've got this!
