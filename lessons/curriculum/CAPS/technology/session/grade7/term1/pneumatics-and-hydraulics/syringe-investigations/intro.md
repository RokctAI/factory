Today we do the experiments. Two syringes, a length of clear tubing, a jug of water and a careful notebook. First we join two equal syringes, fill them with air, and push; then we fill them with water and push again. Then we join a small syringe to a large one and repeat both. We feel the difference between squashy air and firm water, measure how far each plunger moves, and discover with our own hands how the size of the pistons changes the force. These results go straight into the investigation marks for the term's project.
