Hey — if pneumatics and hydraulics sound like big engineering words, here is the calm version: it is pushing through a pipe.

Pneumatics: pushing with air. Bus doors that hiss, tyre pumps. Hydraulics: pushing with liquid. Jacks, car brakes, the Jaws of Life.

The trick: push a small piston, and the pressure spreads through the fluid to a bigger piston. Bigger face, bigger force. Four times the area, four times the force, but it moves a quarter as far.

Air squashes and springs back. Water does not squash at all. So for firm, heavy, exact jobs you use liquid. That is why our rescue device uses water in syringes.
