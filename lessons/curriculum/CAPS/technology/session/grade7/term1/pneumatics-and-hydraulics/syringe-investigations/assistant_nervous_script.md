Hey — if the syringe experiments feel like a lot of steps, here is the calm version: there are only four tests, and they tell one story.

Equal syringes, air: the far plunger is late and spongy. Equal syringes, water: it moves at once, the same distance, the same force.

Small into big, water: half the distance, double the force. Big into small: double the distance, half the force.

Why? Pressure spreads through the fluid, a bigger face collects more force, and water does not squash while air does.

Fill under water so there are no bubbles. Keep the tube short. Write everything in a table, even the runs that went wrong.

Open the practice set and go through the four tests one by one. You've got this!
