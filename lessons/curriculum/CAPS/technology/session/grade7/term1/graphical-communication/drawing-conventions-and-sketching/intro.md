Today we learn to talk with a pencil. Graphics in Technology are not art for the wall; they are the language designers use to develop an idea in their own heads and pass it to someone else without a word being spoken. We learn the conventions that make a drawing readable anywhere in the world: dark outlines, feint construction lines, dashed hidden detail, scale and dimensions. Then we practise free-hand sketching, the quick, loose drawing that catches an idea before it escapes. By the end you will know why a drawing with the wrong line types is as confusing as a sentence with the wrong words.
