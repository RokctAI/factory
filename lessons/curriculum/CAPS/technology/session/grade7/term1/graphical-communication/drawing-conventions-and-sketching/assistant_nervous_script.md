Hey — if drawing conventions feel fussy, here is the calm version: they are just a few rules so that your drawing means the same thing to everyone.

Edges you can see: dark solid lines. Guide lines: feint. Edges you cannot see: dashed. Middle of a circle: long dash, short dash.

Scale tells how big the paper picture is compared with the real thing. The numbers always give the real size, in millimetres, no mm written.

Free-hand sketches are quick and a bit rough. No ruler. Feint first, dark after, labels on the parts. If a line is wrong, draw another beside it.

Open the practice set and name the line types on the pictures. You've got this!
