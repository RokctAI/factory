Today we pull the whole term into one picture. We retrace the rescue device project from the crash on the N1 to the cardboard jaws gripping a block, and on the way we revise every idea we met: the design process and IDMEC, levers and their three classes, mechanical advantage, pneumatics and hydraulics, design briefs with specifications and constraints, fitness for purpose, and the drawing conventions for working and oblique drawings. We practise the kinds of questions a test asks, sort out the confusions that cost marks, and leave with a one-page summary in our heads.
