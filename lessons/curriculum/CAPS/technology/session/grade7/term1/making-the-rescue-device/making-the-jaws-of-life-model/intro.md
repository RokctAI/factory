Today we build. With the working drawing and the parts list on the bench, we make a simple working model of a Jaws of Life machine from plastic tubing, syringes and cardboard. We plan the order of work, mark and cut accurately, make the pivots and joints, fill the hydraulic system without bubbles, assemble, and test. We learn the safe way to use a craft knife and a cutting mat, how to keep card stiff, and what to do when a part does not fit. Then we evaluate the model against the brief and get ready to communicate what we made.
