Hey — if the making lessons feel like a rush where everything could go wrong, here is the calm version: write the order first, then do one step at a time.

Mark out, cut, glue two layers, punch holes, build the block, fit the arms, pin the rod, fill with water, mount the syringe, test. Ten steps, in that order.

Knife on a mat, light passes, cap it when you put it down. Never point a syringe at anyone.

Stiff arms: two layers, ridges crossed. Good joints: punched holes, no slack. No bubbles: fill under water.

When something does not work, that is normal. Fix it and write down what you did. That is where the marks are.

Open the practice set and go through the ten steps. You've got this!
