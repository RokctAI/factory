Hey — if drawing the whole rescue device feels like a mountain, here is the calm version: you measure, you decide, you draw it twice.

Measure the syringe. Decide the base, the arm length and where the pivot goes, and write one reason for each. Pivot near the tips means grip; pivot further back means a wider opening.

Oblique drawing: side view as the front, feint lines, 45 degree lines back on the grid, dark over the edges, label the parts.

Working drawing: one view, scale 1 to 2, every size in real millimetres, each once, plus a parts list.
