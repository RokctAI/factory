Hey — if the Jaws of Life lesson feels like a lot of new words, here is the calm version: think of one crashed car on a wet road.

Someone is trapped. The door is bent shut. Hands cannot open it. A hammer would hurt them. A flame is dangerous near petrol. So the rescuers need a tool that pushes very hard but very slowly.

That tool is the Jaws of Life. Oil is pumped into it, the oil pushes a piston, and the arms are levers. Hydraulics and levers. That is all the technology is.

Your job this term is not to cut steel. It is to understand the problem, write it in one sentence, and build a small model with syringes and cardboard that shows the same idea.

Open the practice set and try writing the problem statement in your own words. You've got this!
