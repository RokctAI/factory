Today we learn the method that every Technology project follows: the design process, remembered by the letters IDMEC. We investigate, design, make, evaluate and communicate. We watch the process at work in two everyday examples, a learner building a better lunch box and a spaza shop owner fixing a leaking roof, and we see why the steps loop back on each other instead of running in a straight line. We finish by matching each step to the parts of the rescue device project.
