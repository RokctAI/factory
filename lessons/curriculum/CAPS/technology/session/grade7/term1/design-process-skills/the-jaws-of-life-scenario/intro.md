Today we start the year's first big project by meeting a real problem. On a wet night on the N1 a car has rolled, the doors are crushed shut and a passenger is trapped inside. Emergency workers arrive with a tool called the Jaws of Life, which cuts and spreads twisted metal in minutes. We look at what the tool must do, why ordinary tools fail, and how this scenario becomes the problem that our own rescue device will solve. We finish by writing down the needs the problem gives us, the first step of every design.
