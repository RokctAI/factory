Today we meet the oldest machine of all, the lever, and the three words that describe every lever: load, effort and pivot. We lift a heavy rock with a plank and a stone, move the stone closer to the rock and feel the push get easier, and we learn the name for that gain, mechanical advantage. We see why a long handle beats a short one, why you cannot get something for nothing, and how the distances from the pivot decide whether a lever helps you or works against you. No moment calculations, just the idea and the feel of it.
