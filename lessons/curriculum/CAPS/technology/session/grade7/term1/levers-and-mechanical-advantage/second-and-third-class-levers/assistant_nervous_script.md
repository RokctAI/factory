Hey — if second and third class levers feel like a muddle, here is the calm version: just ask what is in the middle.

Load in the middle: second class. Wheelbarrow, nutcracker, bottle opener. These always make you stronger. Put the load close to the pivot and they help even more.

Effort in the middle: third class. Fishing rod, broom, tweezers, your own arm. These never make you stronger, but the far end moves fast and far. That is the point of them.

Neither of these flips your push the other way. Only first class, with the pivot in the middle, does that.

Open the practice set and sort the tools by their middle part. You've got this!
