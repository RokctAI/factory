Hey — if the three classes of lever feel confusing, here is the calm version for the first one: the pivot sits in the middle.

Seesaw. Crowbar on a brick. Scissors at the screw. Push one side down, the other side goes up. That is first class.

Now slide the pivot. Near the load: you get stronger. In the middle: same force, opposite direction. Near your hands: you push harder but the far end moves fast.

One rule covers all of it: the longer side carries the smaller force.

For the rescue jaws, put the pivot near the tips so a small push becomes a strong grip.

Open the practice set and sort the examples by where the pivot sits. You've got this!
