Today we meet the other two classes of lever. In a second-class lever the load sits in the middle, as in a wheelbarrow or a nutcracker, and the lever always makes you stronger. In a third-class lever the effort sits in the middle, as in a fishing rod, a pair of tweezers or your own forearm, and the lever never makes you stronger but gives you speed and reach instead. We load a wheelbarrow at a building site, crack a nut, cast a line and lift a brick with a bent arm, and by the end we can sort any lever into its class with one question: what is in the middle?
