Today we investigate levers that work in pairs and in chains. We take scissors, pliers, a nutcracker, an office punch, a stapler and tweezers out of a kitchen drawer and sort them by class with our hands, feeling where each one is strong and where it is weak. Then we look at linkages with more than one pivot, from a folding clothes airer to the arms of the Jaws of Life, and discover how joining levers together can multiply force, change direction and turn one movement into another. This is the practical investigation that your rescue device design will grow from.
