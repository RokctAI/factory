Hey — if linked levers and linkages sound complicated, here is the calm version: most tools are two levers stuck together at one pivot, and you already know how to sort levers.

Scissors and pliers: pivot in the middle, first class. Nutcracker and punch: load in the middle, second class. Stapler and tweezers: fingers in the middle, third class. Pick each one up and feel where it is strong; near the pivot is always stronger.

A linkage is just more levers and more pivots joined up, like a folding clothes airer. Where the movement gets bigger, the force gets smaller, and the other way round.

Your rescue jaws are two first-class levers pushed by a syringe. Put the pin near the tips for grip.

Open the practice set and sort the drawer. You've got this!
