Today we look closely at the first class of lever, the one with the pivot in the middle, between the effort and the load. We start on a seesaw, move to a crowbar prising up a paving slab, and then to a pair of scissors. We discover that a first-class lever can make you stronger, leave your force unchanged or even make you weaker, depending only on where the pivot sits. We finish by seeing why the arms of a rescue spreader are first-class levers and why the pivot position matters so much in our own design.
