Today we turn the problem into a plan. A design brief is a short, clear statement of what we are going to make, for whom and why, followed by specifications that say what it must do and constraints that say what limits us. We write one for the rescue device, step by step, then sketch initial ideas, choose the best and pick the materials. We see how a good brief makes every later decision easier, and how a vague one leads to a model that nobody can judge. This is the design step of IDMEC beginning in earnest.
