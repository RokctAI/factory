Hey — if eight design considerations feel like too many to remember, here is the calm version: they are the questions you would ask before buying anything for someone you love.

Who is it for? What is it for? Does it do that? Is it worth the money? Could it hurt them? Does it fit their body? Will they like how it looks? Who else pays, the neighbours or the planet?

Users, purpose, function, cost-effectiveness, safety, ergonomics, aesthetics, impact. That is the list.

A paraffin stove cooks fine but burns homes, so it is not fit for purpose. Working is not enough.
