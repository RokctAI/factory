Hey — if writing a design brief feels like an essay you cannot start, here is the calm version: it is four sentences and two lists.

Sentence one: the problem and who has it. Two: what you will make and who it is for. Three: what it will do and why. Four: the main limits.

List one, specifications: things the product must do that you can test with a ruler, a weight or a count. List two, constraints: the rules of the project, like materials, size and time.

Then draw three ideas that work differently, tick them against your specifications, pick the winner and say why.

Open the practice set and sort the statements into specifications and constraints. You've got this!
