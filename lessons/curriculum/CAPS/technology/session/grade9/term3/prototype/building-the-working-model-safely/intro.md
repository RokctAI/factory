This is the Make week: the team builds the working model of its device, neat, to scale and safely, following the circuit diagram, the orthographic drawing and the exploded view. The circuit is soldered onto a strip board or built on a small breadboard glued into the box; the LED, switch and sensor are mounted; the battery holder is fitted; the box is closed and labelled. Safety comes first with hot irons, sharp tools and small parts, and neatness is marked because a tidy model is a reliable model. We plan the build, learn to solder a clean joint, assemble to the drawings, test against the specifications and finish the model ready for the presentation.
