Hey — if build day feels like the day it all goes wrong, here is the calm version: in order, and tested as you go.

Lay the parts out and tick them. Pin up the drawings.

Box first, while it is empty. Then the board. Test the board before it goes in. Then fit everything, close, label.

Soldering: tip on lead and pad, two seconds, feed solder to the joint, pull away, hold still. Bright and smooth is good. Dull and lumpy is not.

Check polarity before every solder. Cut the tracks the diagram needs.

Glasses on, iron in its stand, cut away from faces.

Test each specification and write it down. Fix what fails. That is the whole week.
