The term ends with the PAT handed in and the content revised for the examination. This session goes back through the term's three strands in order. Circuits: symbols, series and parallel, AND and OR switches, conventional current. Ohm's law: the three quantities and their meters, the investigation and its graph, the calculations in all three forms. Components: diodes and LEDs with their resistors, resistor colour codes, the four switch types, the transistor as switch and amplifier, the sensors, the capacitor, and the two standard circuits. Each rule is restated in a line, the key calculations are redone with fresh numbers, and the errors that cost marks are named.
