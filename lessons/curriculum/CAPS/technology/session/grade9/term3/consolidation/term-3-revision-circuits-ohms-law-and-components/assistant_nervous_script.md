Hey — if the whole term feels like too much to revise, here is the calm version: three strands, a few rules each.

Circuits: a complete loop, symbols, series shares volts, parallel shares current, series switches are AND, parallel switches are OR.

Ohm's law: volts across, amps in line, V equals I times R, convert milliamps, straight line through zero, gradient is resistance, five-line sums.

Components: each one has a job. Diode one way. LED lights, needs a resistor. Transistor: small current controls big. Sensors change resistance. Capacitor stores for a time.

Four traps: backwards parts, no base resistor, sensors driving loads, explaining without naming jobs.

You built all of this. Revising is just putting it in a short list.
