Before designing a circuit for the PAT device we revise the language of circuits from Grade 8: the standard symbols for cell, battery, lamp, switch, resistor, LED, buzzer and wire, and the two ways of connecting components. In series, components sit one after another in a single loop, so the same current passes through each and the cells' voltages add. In parallel, components sit side by side on separate branches, so each gets the full voltage and one can fail without stopping the others. We draw both with cells and lamps, build them on a board, and see why torches use series cells while house lights are wired in parallel.
