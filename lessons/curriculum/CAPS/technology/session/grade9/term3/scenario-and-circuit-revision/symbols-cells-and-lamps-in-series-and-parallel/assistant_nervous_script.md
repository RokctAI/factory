Hey — if circuits from Grade 8 have gone fuzzy, here is the calm version: one loop or many loops.

Symbols: cell is a long line and a short fat line, lamp is a circle with a cross, switch is a gap, resistor is a box. Draw with straight lines and dots at joins.

Series is one loop. Same current everywhere. Cells add up. Lamps share the volts, so more lamps means dimmer. One goes, all go.

Parallel is side by side. Every branch gets the full volts. All bright. One goes, the rest stay on. Cells in parallel do not add volts, they just last longer.

Trace the loop with your finger. If it is complete, current flows.

That is all of it.
