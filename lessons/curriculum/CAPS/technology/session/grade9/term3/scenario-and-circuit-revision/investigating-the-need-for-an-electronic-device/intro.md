Term 3 is the second practical assessment task: a team designs and builds a device with an electronic circuit inside it. Before any wire is cut we investigate the need. The scenario might be a warning light for a water tank that is running empty, a night light for a child's room that switches on by itself, a moisture alarm for a seedling tray, or a doorbell for a hard-of-hearing grandparent that flashes instead of ringing. Today we learn to read such a scenario, find out who the device is for and where it will live, look at devices that already exist, and write down what the circuit will have to sense, decide and show. This investigation carries marks in the PAT.
