Hey — if logic sounds like computer science, here is the calm version: it is just where you put the switches.

Switches in a row: both must be closed. AND. That is every safety interlock: lid down and button pressed.

Switches side by side: either one will do. OR. That is every alarm: any door opens, light comes on.

Truth table: two switches, four rows. AND lights one row. OR lights three.

Current is drawn from positive to negative. Everyone agrees, so draw it that way.

Read the words: both, all, only when means series. Any, either, or means parallel.

Build it, test it, tick the table. Done.
