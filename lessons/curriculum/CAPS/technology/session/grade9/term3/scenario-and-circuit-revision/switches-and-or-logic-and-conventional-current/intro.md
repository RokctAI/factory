Switches do more than turn a lamp on and off. Put two switches in series and the lamp lights only when both are closed: that is AND logic, the rule behind a machine that runs only when the guard is shut and the start button is pressed. Put two switches in parallel and the lamp lights when either is closed: that is OR logic, the rule behind a car's interior light that comes on when any door opens. Today we build both, write their truth tables, and settle the convention that current in diagrams is drawn flowing from positive to negative. These ideas return when the PAT device needs a sensor and a manual override together.
