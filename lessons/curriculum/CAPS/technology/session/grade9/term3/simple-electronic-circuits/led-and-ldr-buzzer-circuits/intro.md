Two complete circuits today, drawn with every symbol and value and then built. The first is the indicator circuit of the term: a 4.5 volt battery, a switch, a 470 ohm resistor and an LED in series, the circuit that proves a PAT device's battery and output. The second is a sensing circuit: a light-dependent resistor controlling a buzzer on a 3 volt battery through a transistor, so that the buzzer sounds when the light falls, a simple alarm for a drawer or a cupboard. We draw each to standard, trace it to predict what it does, build it on a breadboard, test it, and compare the result with the prediction, which is exactly what the PAT Make stage requires.
