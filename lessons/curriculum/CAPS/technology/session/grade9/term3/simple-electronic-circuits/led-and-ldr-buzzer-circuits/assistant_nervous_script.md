Hey — if building a real circuit feels like the moment it all goes wrong, here is the calm version: draw, trace, build, check, test.

LED circuit: battery, switch, 470 ohms, LED, in a loop. Long leg toward plus. Trace it: about 5 milliamps, lit.

LDR buzzer: resistor and LDR in a line from plus to minus, the middle goes through a resistor to the base, buzzer from plus to collector, emitter to minus. Dark makes the buzzer sound.

Breadboard: rows of five, rails at the sides. Put each part where the diagram says. Before power, touch every part and match it to the diagram.

Test. Not working? LED backwards first. Then rows. Then transistor legs.

Everyone's first build has one slip. Finding it is the skill.
