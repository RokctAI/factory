Hey — if seven components on one diagram looks like too much, here is the calm version: it is the LDR buzzer circuit with two extras.

Sensor and variable resistor in a line from plus to minus. Middle goes through a resistor to the base. LED and 470 ohms from plus to collector. Emitter to minus. That is the circuit you already built.

Extra one: a switch in the plus lead, for on and off.

Extra two: a capacitor from base to minus, plus leg on the base, to stop flicker and add a pause.

Warm the thermistor, the LED comes on after a second. Cool it, off after a second. Turn the knob to set the temperature.

Swap the thermistor and the knob: on when cold instead.

Name each part's job and you have the whole explanation.
