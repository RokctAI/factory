Hey — if the dead LED in the bag was yours, here is the calm version: everyone kills one LED. Here is how not to kill the next.

A diode is a one-way valve. Triangle shows the way current goes. Backwards, it blocks.

An LED is a diode that glows. Long leg to positive. It drops about 2 volts and has almost no resistance, so straight on a battery it burns out.

Put a resistor in series. A few hundred ohms. 470 is the PAT value. It takes the spare volts and keeps the current small.

If it does not light, it is probably backwards, not dead. Flip it.

Every LED gets its own resistor. That is the whole rule.
