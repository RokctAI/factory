Hey — if your circuit refuses to light and you feel stupid, here is the calm version: half the room's circuits do not light first time.

The circuit is a loop: battery, switch, lamp, back to battery. Any gap and it is dark.

Find the gap in order. Battery: lamp straight on the terminals. Lamp: swap it. Switch: bridge it. Leads: look for a clip on plastic.

Voltmeter across a part. Ammeter in the loop. Never the ammeter across the battery.

The PAT circuit is this same loop with an LED and a sensor. Get this one right and the rest follows.
