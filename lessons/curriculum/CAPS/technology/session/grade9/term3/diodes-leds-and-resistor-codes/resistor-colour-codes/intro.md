The resistor saved the LED last week; today we learn to tell one resistor from another. A resistor's job is to resist current, and its value in ohms decides how much. Large resistors have the value printed on them, but the small ones in the PAT box are too small for numbers, so they carry coloured bands instead: three bands give the value, two digits and a multiplier, and a fourth band gives the tolerance, how far the real value may stray from the marked one. We learn the colour sequence, read the 470 ohm resistor from the PAT circuit and a dozen others, check them with a meter, and practise choosing the nearest standard value.
