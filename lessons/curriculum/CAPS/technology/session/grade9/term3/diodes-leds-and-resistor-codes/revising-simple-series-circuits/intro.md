Before adding new components we make sure the basic circuit is solid: a cell or battery, a switch and a lamp in one loop. This is the circuit inside every torch and the skeleton of the PAT indicator. We draw it with symbols, build it on a board, and learn to find faults in it methodically when it does not light, which is the most useful skill in the term. We also meet the two meters that will matter for Ohm's law later, the voltmeter across a component and the ammeter in the loop, and see how the simple circuit grows into the PAT circuit by swapping the lamp for an LED and resistor and the switch for a sensor.
