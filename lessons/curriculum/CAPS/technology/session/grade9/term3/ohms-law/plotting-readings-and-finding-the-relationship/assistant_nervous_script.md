Hey — if graphs make you tense, here is the calm version: three crosses, one ruler, one division.

Volts up, amps across. Label them. Even scales starting at zero.

Plot the three means as neat crosses. Check each one.

Ruler through the origin, tilt until it is close to all three. One straight line. Never join the dots.

Straight line through zero means proportional. That is Ohm's law.

Gradient: pick two far-apart points on the line, volts difference over amps difference. About 4.4 over 0.044 is 100. That is the resistor.

Three sentences: straight line so proportional; about 100 ohms matching the bands; cells lost a bit of voltage inside.

You can do this.
