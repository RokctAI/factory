The table from the practical becomes a graph today. Current goes on one axis, voltage on the other, each pair of mean readings is plotted as a point, and a single straight line is drawn through the points and the origin. If the points lie close to that line, Ohm's law is confirmed for the resistor: current is directly proportional to voltage. The steepness of the line gives the resistance, which should come out near the resistor's marked 100 ohms, and the conclusion states the relationship in words and numbers. We learn to choose scales, plot carefully, draw a line of best fit rather than joining the dots, and write a conclusion that the data supports.
