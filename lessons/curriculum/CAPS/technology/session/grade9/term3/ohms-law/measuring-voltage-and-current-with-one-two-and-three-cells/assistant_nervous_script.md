Hey — if practicals make you nervous because your numbers never come out perfect, here is the calm version: they are not supposed to.

Build the loop: cells, switch, resistor. Voltmeter across the resistor. Ammeter in the loop, in a gap. Set the ranges first.

One cell, read both meters, write both. Two cells, same. Three cells, same.

Each cell adds about 1.5 volts and about 15 milliamps for 100 ohms. About. 1.45 and 14 is fine.

Write what the meter says. Repeat each one. Divide volts by amps; it should hover near 100.

If a row is odd, measure it again and write a note. Do not rub it out.

Real numbers wobble. That is what makes them real.
