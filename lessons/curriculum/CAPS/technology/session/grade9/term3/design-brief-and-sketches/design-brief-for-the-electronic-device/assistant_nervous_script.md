Hey — if writing the brief feels like paperwork getting in the way of building, here is the calm version: it is the page that stops arguments later.

One sentence: what the device does, for whom, where. Do not name the circuit.

Specifications: what it must do, with a number or a test on each. Detect below one fifth. Visible at ten metres. Six months of battery. A test button.

Constraints: kit, 80 rand, weeks 3 to 9, no mains.

Evaluation: say now how you will test each one.

One page, everyone reads it, everyone signs. Then you build, and when someone says make it beep, you point at the page.
