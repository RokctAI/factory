The investigation told us who needs what. Now the team writes it down as a design brief: one sentence naming the device, its job, its user and its setting, followed by specifications, the measurable things the device must do and have, and constraints, the limits the team cannot change. For an electronic device the specifications include the input to sense, the output to show, the supply voltage, the battery life and the enclosure, and the constraints include the components available in the kit, the budget and the time. The brief is written by the whole team and signed off before any sketching, because everything that follows is judged against it.
