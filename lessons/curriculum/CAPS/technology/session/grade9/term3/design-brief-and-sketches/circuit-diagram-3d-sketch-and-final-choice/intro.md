With the brief agreed, each team member sketches an idea, and each idea has two drawings: a circuit diagram in standard symbols showing the electronics, and a 3D sketch showing the device as the user will see it, with the circuit's parts placed inside. One idea might be a float switch and a bright LED on a long wire; another, two probes and a transistor driving a flashing LED; a third, a reed switch with a magnet on a float. The team then lays the ideas side by side, checks each against every numbered specification, and chooses a final solution, often combining the best parts. Today covers both drawings and the choice.
