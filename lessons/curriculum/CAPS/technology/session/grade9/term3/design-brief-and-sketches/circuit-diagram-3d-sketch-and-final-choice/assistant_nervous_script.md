Hey — if drawing the circuit and the box and then choosing feels like three jobs at once, here is the calm version: insides, outside, tick list.

Insides: a circuit diagram with proper symbols, every value written, LED and battery the right way round. Trace it: does the LED light when the tank is low?

Outside: a pencil sketch of the box on the sill and the sensor on the tank. Every part from the diagram has a place in the sketch.

Tick list: specifications down the side, ideas across. Tick, cross, note. Take the best bits.

Write why, redraw clean, add costs.

Your idea does not have to win. The best combination does.
