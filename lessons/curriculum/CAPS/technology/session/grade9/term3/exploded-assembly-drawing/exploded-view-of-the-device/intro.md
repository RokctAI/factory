The circuit is designed and the box is sketched; before the model is built we draw how it goes together. An exploded view is a 3D drawing in which the parts of an object are pulled apart along a line, each in the position it will take when assembled, with thin lines showing how they slide back together. It is the drawing in every flat-pack furniture box and every repair manual. For the PAT device it shows the lid, the circuit board, the battery holder, the LED, the switch, the screws and the box base, in order, so that the team and the examiner can see every part and how it fits. We learn to choose the axis, order the parts, draw them in isometric and label them.
