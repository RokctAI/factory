Hey — if the exploded view sounds like an art project, here is the calm version: stack the parts on a line.

Draw a faint vertical line. Base at the bottom. Battery holder above it. Board above that. Lid above that. LED and button out of the lid. Screws above their holes.

Draw each one in isometric, the right way up, same size. Thin dashed lines joining them through the holes.

Number each part in a little circle, base first. Make a list that matches: number, name, how many, material, size.

Title block. A note if something must be done first.

Ask: could a stranger put it together from this? Then you are done.
