Hey — if sensors sound like a long list to memorise, here is the calm version: they are all resistors that change.

LDR: light makes the resistance drop. Cover it, it climbs.

Thermistor: heat makes the resistance drop for the usual NTC kind, rise for the PTC kind.

Touch and moisture: two contacts, and a finger or wet soil bridges them.

Test every one with a meter on ohms and write three readings.

None of them can light an LED alone. They feed the base of a transistor.

Pick the one whose reading changes most at the thing you want to detect.

That is all of it.
