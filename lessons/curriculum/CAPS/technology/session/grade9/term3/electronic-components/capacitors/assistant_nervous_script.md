Hey — if the capacitor feels like one component too many, here is the calm version: it is a tiny bucket for charge.

Two plates, an insulator between. Battery fills it. Disconnect, it holds. Connect an LED, it empties into the LED.

Size is capacitance, in microfarads mostly.

Fill it through a resistor and it takes time: ohms times farads is seconds. That is how timers work.

Small discs go either way. Big cans have a stripe on the minus leg and must go the right way, or they pop. Check the volts on the can.

Drain a big one through a resistor before you touch it.

That is the whole component.
