Hey — if standing up in front of the class is the worst part of the PAT, here is the calm version: one minute, one card, one page to point at.

The team tells the story in order. You tell the part you led. Your card has the first line and the page number.

Show things: the drawing, the table, the model. Pointing at a page is easier than remembering words.

If the model fails, say so, show the test record, explain. That is fine.

Speak a bit slower than feels natural.

Then write what you did, in the first person, specifically. I drew this. I tested that. I drilled this. That is your mark.

Rehearse twice and the fear mostly goes.
