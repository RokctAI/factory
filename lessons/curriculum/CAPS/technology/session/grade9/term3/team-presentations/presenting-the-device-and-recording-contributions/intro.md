The device is built; now the team presents it. Five minutes is short, so the presentation is planned as one story: the need, the brief, the ideas and the choice, the circuit, the drawings, the cost, the model working, and what the team would improve. Every member speaks, the drawings and the model are shown rather than described, and the demonstration is rehearsed so that the LED lights on cue. Alongside the team presentation, each learner records their own contribution to the PAT, honestly and specifically, because the PAT marks are individual as well as shared. Today we plan, rehearse and write.
