Ohm's law is now a tool, and today we use it on the circuits of the PAT. We set out calculations properly, formula, substitution, answer with unit, and apply the law to resistors in series, where the resistances add and the current is shared, and to resistors in parallel, where the combined resistance is less than the smallest and the voltage is shared. Then the real problems: the series resistor for an LED on 4.5, 6 and 9 volt supplies, the current drawn by two LEDs on separate resistors, and how long a battery will last. Each problem is a few lines, and each line earns a mark in the examination.
