Hey — if the calculations feel like a wall of numbers, here is the calm version: five lines every time.

Known and wanted. Formula. Numbers in. Calculate. Answer with unit, then ask if it makes sense.

Convert milliamps to amps first. Divide by 1000.

Series: add the resistances, same current, volts shared. LED resistor: battery minus 2, divided by the current.

Parallel: each branch gets the full volts, do each alone, add the currents.

Battery life: capacity divided by current. Your device draws nothing when the tank is full, so it lasts for ages.

Every one of these is three lines of arithmetic. You already know the arithmetic.
