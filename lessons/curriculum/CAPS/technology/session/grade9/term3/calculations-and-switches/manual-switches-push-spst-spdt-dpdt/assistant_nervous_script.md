Hey — if the switch names look like alphabet soup, here is the calm version: count poles and throws.

Poles: how many circuits at once. Throws: how many places each can connect to.

Push-to-make: on while you press. Test button, doorbell.

SPST: plain on-off. Light switch. Your silence switch.

SPDT: one wire goes to either of two. Choose this lamp or that lamp.

DPDT: two wires each go to either of two. Cross the wires and a motor reverses.

To choose: how many circuits, how many positions, stay or spring back, how many amps.

Draw them open. That is the whole topic.
