Hey — if the ratchet feels like fiddly machinery, here is the calm version: it is a one-way gate for a wheel.

Saw teeth on a wheel. A little arm pressing on them. Turn one way, the slopes lift the arm, click click. Turn back, the arm hits a steep face and jams.

It does not add force. It only says which way is allowed.

Boat winch, ratchet spanner, cable tie, handbrake. Same idea every time.

To draw it: teeth, arrow for the free way, crossed arrow for the blocked way, pawl, spring. Label one tooth.

To evaluate it: does it hold, does it release, can the pawl slip, is it hard steel.

Make one from card. The click tells you it works.
