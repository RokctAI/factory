Today we begin pulleys with the simplest one: a single pulley fixed to a beam, with a rope over it, used to lift a load. We find that it does not make the load lighter at all, the mechanical advantage is exactly 1, but it does something else very useful: it changes the direction of the pull, so that you can lift a load upwards by pulling downwards, using your weight and standing safely on the ground. We measure it, explain it, and look at where fixed pulleys are used, from flagpoles to wells to cranes.
