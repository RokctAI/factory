Hey — if the zigzag rope looks confusing, here is the calm version: count the ropes under the load.

Top block fixed, bottom block on the load, one rope between them. The rope has the same tension everywhere. Every section pulling up on the bottom block shares the load equally. Three sections, each holds a third, you pull a third. Advantage 3.

The rope to your hand from the top block does not count.

You pull as many metres as there are sections, for one metre up. Force down, distance up, work the same.

Measured values come out a bit worse because of friction and the bottom block's weight. That is normal; call it efficiency.

Draw it, arrow every section, count the up arrows. That is the whole skill.
