Today we apply Pascal's principle to a real machine, the hydraulic press, and we do the calculations. A press has a small cylinder where the effort goes in and a large cylinder where the load is squeezed, and because the pressure is the same in both, force divided by area is equal on both sides. From that one equation we can find the output force, the input force or an area, given the others. We work several examples step by step, learn to check an answer for sense, and see where presses are used, from car workshops to brick factories.
