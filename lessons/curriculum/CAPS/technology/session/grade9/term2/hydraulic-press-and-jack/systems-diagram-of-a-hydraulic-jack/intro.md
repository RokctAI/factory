Today we describe the hydraulic jack in a new way, as a system. A system has an input, a process and an output, and a systems diagram shows them as boxes joined by arrows, with the processes in the order they happen. We draw the jack's systems diagram, from the hand on the handle through the pump, the valves and the ram to the lifted car, and then we add feedback and control, the parts that let a user or a machine adjust what is happening. Systems diagrams will be used again for the machine design later in the term and for the recycling plant in Term 4.
