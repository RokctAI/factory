Hey — if systems diagrams look like a new kind of drawing to learn, here is the calm version: three boxes and arrows.

Input: your hand on the handle, oil, the car. Process, in order: lever, pump, valves, pressurised oil, ram. Output: the car up.

Release is a branch back to the oil store.

Feedback: an arrow from the output back to you, because you watch and stop pumping. The overload valve is the automatic one.

It is a map of what happens, not a picture of the jack.

Open the practice set and put the boxes in order. You've got this!
