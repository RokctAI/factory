Hey — if press calculations look like algebra with a trap in it, here is the calm version: one equation, one check.

F1 over A1 equals F2 over A2. Same pressure both sides.

Want the big force: multiply by the area ratio. Want the small force: divide by it. Mass times 10 gives newtons.

Check: big area, big force. If the ram comes out weaker than your push, something flipped.

150 newtons on 4, ram of 100: 150 times 25 is 3 750.
