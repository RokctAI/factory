Today we take a real product, the hydraulic bottle jack found in most car boots and workshops, and we do two things with it: understand how it works, with its pump, one-way valves, ram and release valve, and then evaluate its design against five criteria, fitness for purpose, cost, aesthetics, safety and ergonomics. This is the same kind of evaluation we did on existing ramps in Term 1, applied to a mechanism, and it prepares us to judge our own machine designs later in the term.
