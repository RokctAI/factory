Hey — if the second half of the term feels like a pile of gadgets, here is the calm version: two counting rules and three verbs.

Pulleys: count the ropes holding the load up. That is the advantage. Pull that many times the rope. Measured is a bit less.

Controls: ratchet allows one way, brake stops, cleat holds. None of them adds force.

Gears: count teeth, driven over driver. Small driving big is slow and strong. Idler flips direction only. Bevel turns a corner. Rack makes straight motion. Worm gives a big self-locking reduction.

Four errors to avoid: hand rope counted, control said to add force, ratio upside down, idler thought to change the ratio.

You have met every one of these. Today is just sorting them into a short list.
