The term's first half was fluids: syringes joined by tubing, air that squashes and water that does not, Pascal's principle, the trade between force and distance, the hydraulic press and the jack. This revision session pulls it together before the mid-year examination. We go back over each idea in the order it was taught, restate the rules in one line each, redo the key calculations with fresh numbers, and sort out the errors that lose marks: mixing up input and output, forgetting units, saying a jack makes energy, and drawing a systems diagram as a picture. Everything here has been covered; today it is tidied and tested.
