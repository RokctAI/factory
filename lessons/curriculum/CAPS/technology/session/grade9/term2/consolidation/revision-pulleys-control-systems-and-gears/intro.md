The second half of the term was mechanisms that move with ropes and teeth, and the controls that govern them. This revision session goes back through pulleys, control systems and gears in the order they were taught, restating each rule in a line, redoing the calculations with new numbers and clearing up the errors that cost marks. Pulleys: count the load-bearing ropes. Controls: the ratchet allows one direction, the brake stops, the cleat holds. Gears: count the teeth, driven over driver, and know which pair turns a corner, which makes a straight line, and which locks itself. Everything has been taught; today it is tidied for the examination.
