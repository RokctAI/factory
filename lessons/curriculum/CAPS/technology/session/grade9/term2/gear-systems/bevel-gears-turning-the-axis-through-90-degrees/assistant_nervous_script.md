Hey — if bevel gears look like a whole new topic, here is the calm version: they are spur gears bent round a corner.

Teeth on a cone instead of a cylinder. Two cones point to point, shafts at 90 degrees.

Ratio is still driven teeth over driver teeth. Equal: 1 to 1, just turns the corner. Small driving big: slower, stronger. Big driving small: faster, weaker.

Eggbeater, hand drill, bakkie axle, windmill pump. All turning motion round a corner.

Draw two cones meeting at their points, shafts at right angles, write the ratio.

Nothing new in the maths. Only the angle changed.
