The last gear of the week gives the biggest change in one step. A worm gear is a screw-like shaft, the worm, meshing with a toothed wheel, the worm wheel, with the two shafts at right angles. Each full turn of the worm advances the wheel by just one tooth, so a 40-tooth wheel turns once for every 40 turns of the worm: a 40 to 1 reduction and a 40-fold force increase from a single pair. The worm also cannot usually be turned by the wheel, so the system holds its position on its own. We meet it in guitar tuning pegs, the hoist on a winch, gate motors and the hand-cranked lifts in many workshops.
