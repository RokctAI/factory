Gears so far have turned one shaft with another. Today's gear turns a shaft into a straight push or pull. The rack and pinion is a round gear, the pinion, meshing with a straight bar of teeth, the rack. Spin the pinion and the rack slides along; push the rack and the pinion spins. It is what slides a driveway gate open at a Johannesburg complex, what turns a car's steering wheel rotation into the side-to-side movement of the front wheels, and what moves the drill head on a pillar drill. We see how it works, where it is used, calculate how far a rack moves per turn, and evaluate it.
