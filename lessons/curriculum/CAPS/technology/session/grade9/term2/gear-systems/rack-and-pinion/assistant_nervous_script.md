Hey — if the rack and pinion seems like another gear to memorise, here is the calm version: a gear and a flat gear.

The rack is a bar of teeth. The pinion is a small gear on it. Turn the pinion, the rack slides. Round becomes straight.

The gate at a complex: rack on the gate, motor beside it. Steering: wheel turns a pinion, pinion slides a rack, rods turn the wheels.

Maths: one turn moves the rack one tooth gap for each pinion tooth. 12 teeth, 10 millimetres: 120 millimetres per turn.

Big pinion: more travel per turn, more effort. Small: less, lighter.

Judge it: travel, no slip, guards, stops for obstacles, release for load shedding.

That is the whole thing.
