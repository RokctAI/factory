Hey — if the worm gear sounds like something out of a factory manual, here is the calm version: a screw turning a wheel.

The worm is a screw. The wheel's teeth sit in the thread. One turn of the screw, one tooth of the wheel.

So the ratio is just the wheel's teeth. 40 teeth, 40 to 1. Slow and strong.

The wheel cannot turn the screw back. So whatever you lift or tighten stays there. Guitar peg, winch, gate motor.

It loses a lot to friction and it is slow. That is the price.

Draw a circle with teeth and a cylinder across the top. Write the ratio. Done.
