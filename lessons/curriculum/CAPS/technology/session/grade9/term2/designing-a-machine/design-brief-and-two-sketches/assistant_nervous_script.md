Hey — if writing a brief and sketching feels like a lot, here is the calm version: one sentence, some rules, two pictures, one choice.

The sentence: what, for whom, where. No mechanism named.

Specifications: what it must do, with numbers. Constraints: budget, materials, time.

Two sketches, really different. A frame with a winch and pulleys. A scissor platform with a crank and worm gear. Label the parts. Show it in use.

Then go down the specifications and tick each sketch. Write which one wins and why. Combine if that is best.

Pencil is fine. Reasons matter more than neatness.
