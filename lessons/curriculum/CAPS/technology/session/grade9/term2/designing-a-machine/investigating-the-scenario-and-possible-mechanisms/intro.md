We have spent the term collecting mechanisms: syringes and cylinders, pulleys and tackles, ratchets, brakes and cleats, spur, bevel, rack and worm gears. Now we start the design task that puts them to use. The scenario is a machine needed in a real South African setting, for example a device to lift a heavy drum onto a bakkie, to raise a water tank onto a stand, or to lift a wheelchair user onto a stage. Today we investigate: what exactly must the machine do, who will use it, what mechanisms could do each part of the job, and what controls will keep it safe. Nothing is drawn yet; everything is found out and written down.
