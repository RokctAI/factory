With the investigation written up, we design. First the design brief: one clear sentence saying what will be designed and for whom, followed by specifications, the things the machine must do and must have, and constraints, the limits it must stay within. Then two different sketches of machines that could meet the brief, each using mechanisms and controls from the term, labelled so a reader can see how they work. Finally a choice: one of the two is selected, or the best parts of both combined, with reasons that point back to the brief. This is the same design process used in Term 1 for the ramp, now applied to a machine.
