Hey — if two syringes and a tube seem too simple to matter, here is the calm version: it is the whole of hydraulics in your hand.

Push one plunger, the other comes out. Same size, same distance, same force. A push sent round a corner.

Air inside: spongy, springs back. Pneumatic. Water inside: solid, instant. Hydraulic. Because gas squashes and liquid does not.

Test it fairly: one change at a time, no bubbles, same pushes, write it down.

Open the practice set and sort air from water. You've got this!
