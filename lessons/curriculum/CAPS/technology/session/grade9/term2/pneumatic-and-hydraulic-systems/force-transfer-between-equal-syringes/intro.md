Today we begin Term 2 with the simplest fluid power system there is: two syringes of the same size joined by a tube. Push the plunger of one and the plunger of the other moves out. We find out what travels along the tube, force or fluid or both, and we compare the system filled with air, which is pneumatic, with the same system filled with water, which is hydraulic. Then we investigate force transfer between the equal syringes as a fair test, recording what we feel and what we measure, so that in the following sessions we can explain the hydraulic jack and the brakes of a car.
