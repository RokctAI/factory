Hey — if the particle talk feels like a different subject, here is the calm version: it is one picture.

Gas: particles far apart, empty space. Push and the space closes up. Squashes. Springs back.

Liquid: particles touching. Push and there is nothing to close up. Does not squash.

So air stores energy, lags and cushions. Water moves instantly and holds solid.

Big exact force: liquid. Fast soft action: air.
