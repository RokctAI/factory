Today we explain the difference we felt in the first session: why air squashes and water does not. We go down to the particles, far apart in a gas and touching in a liquid, and see why pushing on a gas uses up empty space while pushing on a liquid meets the molecules themselves. Then we trace what compressibility does inside a working system, storing energy, delaying movement, making a pedal spongy, and we finish by deciding which fluid suits which machine, from a bicycle pump to a car's brakes.
