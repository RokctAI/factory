Today we change one thing from last session: the two syringes are no longer the same size. Push a small master into a large slave and the slave pushes with a far greater force than you applied, but moves a shorter distance. Swap them, pushing a large master into a small slave, and the slave moves much further but pushes with less force. We investigate both arrangements, measure the forces and distances, and discover the rule that governs every hydraulic machine: force multiplied means distance divided, and the product of the two stays the same.
