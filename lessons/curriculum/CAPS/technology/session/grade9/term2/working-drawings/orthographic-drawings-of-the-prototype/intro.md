The chosen machine exists as a sketch and a paragraph. Before anything is cut, it needs working drawings: first angle orthographic projection, the three flat views that fix every size and position, which we learnt in Term 1 for the stairs and ramp. Today we draw the lifting machine, front view, top view and side view, placed correctly on the page, to a stated scale, with dimensions, hidden detail shown in dashed lines and a parts list. A working drawing lets someone who has never seen the sketch build the model exactly as designed, and it is what the final evaluation will be checked against.
