Hey — if orthographic drawing still feels fiddly from Term 1, here is the calm version: three flat pictures in fixed places.

Front in the middle. Top below it. Side to the right. That is first angle. Symbol in the title block.

Pick a scale that fits: 1 to 10 turns 1200 into 120. Faint lines first, then project down and across. Darken the outlines.

Dashed for hidden. Long-short dashes for centres.

Dimensions in true millimetres, each once, where clearest.

Parts list: number, name, quantity, material, size. Balloons point to them.

Ask: could a stranger build it from this? If yes, you are done.
