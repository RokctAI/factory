Hey — if Pascal's principle sounds like a grand law, here is the calm version: squeeze a sealed liquid and the squeeze goes everywhere, the same amount, every direction.

Pressure is push divided by area. Pin: small area, big pressure. Thumb: big area, small pressure.

In a sealed liquid that pressure is the same everywhere. Push on a big area and you get a big force. Push on four slaves and they all move.

One brake pedal, four wheels, same pressure, straight stop.
