Hey — if practicals with weighing and recording feel fussy, here is the calm version: three plates, one scale, one look a day.

Drying works because germs need water. Take it out and they stop.

Salting works because salt pulls water out of the food and the germs, and leaves the food too salty for them anyway.

The practical: six equal slices. Two plain, two salted, two drying in the sun. Weigh them. Every day, weigh and look. Write it down.

Plain ones go mouldy. Salted ones sit in brine, firm. Dried ones shrink and go leathery.

Do not taste anything. Throw it all away at the end.

Same place, same slices, same time each day. That is a fair test.
