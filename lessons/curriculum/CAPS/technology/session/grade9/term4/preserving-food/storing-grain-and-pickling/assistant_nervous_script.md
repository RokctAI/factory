Hey — if food science feels far from Technology, here is the calm version: it is the same idea as rust, with living spoilers instead of oxygen.

Food spoils because bacteria, moulds, insects and rats eat it. They need moisture, warmth, air and a way in.

Take one away and the food keeps.

Grain: dry it, keep it cool, seal it, raise it off the ground. A basket on stilts, a sealed pit, a steel silo, a lidded bucket. Same four ideas.

Pickling: make it sour. Pour vinegar on it, or let good bacteria sour it in brine. Keep everything under the liquid.

That is the whole lesson. The practical next time dries and salts.
