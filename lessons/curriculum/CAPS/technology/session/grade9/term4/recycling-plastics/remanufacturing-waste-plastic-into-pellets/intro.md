What happens to a PET bottle after the waste picker sells it? This session follows it through a recycling plant, from a bale of crushed bottles to a bag of clean pellets ready to be moulded into something new. The steps are sorting, washing, shredding into flake, drying, melting and extruding into strands that are chopped into pellets, with quality checks along the way. We then draw the whole thing as a systems diagram, the input, process and output boxes with feedback, which is a tool you will use for your own design project. Recycling is a manufacturing process with its own machines, workers and economics, and this is a look inside.
