The term's design task begins here: identify a need that a plastic item of your own design can satisfy. Good design starts not with an object but with a problem, something that is awkward, missing or broken in your home, school or community, and this session shows how to find one and describe it properly. We work through observing, asking, and listing needs; writing a design brief and specifications that say exactly what the item must do; and choosing which recycled plastic and which making method fit. By the end you should have a problem statement, a brief and a short specification for an item you will draw in orthographic projection in two weeks' time.
