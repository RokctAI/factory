A bag of recycled pellets is only useful once it becomes a crate, a bin, a pipe or a chair. This session follows the pellet into the moulding factory and looks at the four main ways a thermoplastic is shaped: injection moulding for crates and chairs, blow moulding for bottles and drums, extrusion for pipes and sheet, and rotational moulding for water tanks. We see how each works, what it is good at, and how a designer shapes a product so that it can be moulded well, with even wall thickness, draft angles and no undercuts. This matters for your own design, since a plastic item you draw later this term must be something a mould could actually make.
