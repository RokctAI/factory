Hey — a blank design project is the scariest kind of homework, so here is a way in.

Do not think of a product. Walk around and watch people. Where do they struggle? Tap with no cup, tangled cables, phone propped on a book. Pick one small thing.

Write who has the problem, what it is, where, and why it matters. Two sentences.

Write a brief: design something that does this, for these people, from recycled plastic. Do not say what it is.

List specs you can test: size, material, cost, must survive outdoors, no sharp edges.

Pick a plastic that fits, and a way the school can make it.

That is the whole session. One real problem, written down clearly.
