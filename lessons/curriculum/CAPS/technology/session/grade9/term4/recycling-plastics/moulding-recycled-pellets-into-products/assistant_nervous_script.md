Hey — four machines and a list of rules can feel like a lot. Here is the compact version.

Pellets get melted and shaped four ways. Injection: squirt into a mould, solid parts like crates. Blow: inflate a tube in a mould, bottles. Extrusion: push through a shaped hole, pipes. Rotational: tumble powder in a hot hollow mould, tanks.

Pick by shape and how many you need.

Design rules so the part comes out right: same wall thickness everywhere, a little taper on the sides, nothing that hooks it into the mould, round the corners.

That is enough to design something a factory could actually make.
