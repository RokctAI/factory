Hey — if chemistry words make you tense, here is the calm version: rust is iron plus air plus water, and we keep the air and water off.

Paint: a skin. Clean the metal first, prime, paint. Cheap, any colour. Chip it and rust starts there and creeps under.

Galvanising: a skin of zinc from a hot dip. Zinc is more eager to corrode than iron, so when it is scratched the zinc corrodes instead of the steel. That is why it beats paint at chips and lasts decades.

Limits: grey, factory only, dearer at first, zinc runs out eventually.

That is all of it. Look at a fence and a gate and you will see both.
