Term 4 is about processing: changing materials to make them last or to make them useful. We begin with metals, which South Africans fight to protect from rust on every farm gate, roof and bakkie. Iron and steel corrode when oxygen and water reach them, flaking into rust that weakens the metal. Two of the oldest defences are painting, which seals the surface from air and water, and galvanising, which coats steel in zinc that both seals it and corrodes in its place. We look at why rust happens, how each method works, where each is used across the country and why a galvanised roof outlasts a painted one.
