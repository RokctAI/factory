Hey — if the plating cell sounds like a chemistry exam, here is the calm version: minus gets the metal.

Blue solution of the coating metal's salt. Copper strip on plus. Key on minus. Low volts.

Copper leaves the strip, travels through the solution, lands on the key. Strip shrinks, key gets coated.

Three rules: key spotless, current low, key facing the strip and not touching.

Do it: sand the key, wash it, do not touch it, wire it, wait ten minutes. Pink coat.

Swap the leads on a second key and nothing happens. That proves the direction.

Gloves and glasses, wash your hands. That is the whole practical.
