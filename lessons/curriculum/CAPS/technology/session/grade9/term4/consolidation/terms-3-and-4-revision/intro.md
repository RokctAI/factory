The end-of-year examination covers Terms 3 and 4, so this session gathers both into one revision map. Term 3 was electrical systems and control: circuits, series and parallel, Ohm's law, resistors, LEDs, switches and sensors, logic, and the electronic device you designed and built. Term 4 was processing: preserving metals and food, the properties of plastics, reduce, reuse and recycle, the recycling plant and its systems diagram, moulding, plastics in cars and homes, and the plastic item you specified and drew. We revise the key facts of each, the diagrams and drawings the exam expects, and the technique for the kinds of questions it asks, so that what you know turns into marks.
