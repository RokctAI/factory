Hey — technical drawing can feel like a test of neatness. It is really a test of three rules.

One: positions. Front view first, top view below it, side view from the right drawn on the left. Line them up.

Two: lines. Thick for edges you see, dashed for hidden, chain for centres.

Three: dimensions. Each one once, outside the shape, in millimetres, holes with a diameter sign, corners with R.

For a plastic item add a cut-through view showing the wall thickness, a slight taper on the sides and rounded corners. Title block in the corner.

Then check it with the list. Rulers and a sharp pencil do the rest.
