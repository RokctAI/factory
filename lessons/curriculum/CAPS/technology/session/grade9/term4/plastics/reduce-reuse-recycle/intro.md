South Africa produces more than two million tonnes of plastic waste a year, and much of it ends in landfills, on roadsides, in rivers and on beaches, where it lasts for centuries. This session is about the three Rs in their proper order: reduce, use less plastic in the first place; reuse, use the same item again and again; and recycle, collect and remelt it into something new. We look at why the order matters, what each R looks like in a township, a school and a factory, the role of the waste pickers who collect most of what South Africa recycles, and the honest limits of recycling, since only some plastics are worth collecting and every recycling pass degrades the material a little.
