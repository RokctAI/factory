Plastics are everywhere in a South African home and shop: the bucket, the kettle body, the chair, the bread bag, the car bumper, the electrical plug. They are chosen because of their properties, the things a material is good or bad at. This session sorts plastics into the two big families, thermoplastics that soften when heated and thermosets that set hard for good, and then works through the properties that matter: strength, flexibility, toughness, lightness, electrical and heat insulation, resistance to water and chemicals, and how plastics behave in sunlight and fire. By the end, you should be able to look at an object and say why that plastic was chosen for it.
