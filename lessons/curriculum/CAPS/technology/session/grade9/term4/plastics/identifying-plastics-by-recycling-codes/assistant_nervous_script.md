Hey — if seven codes sounds like a lot to memorise, here is the calm version: three numbers matter most.

Plastics are different materials that do not mix when melted. So they must be sorted.

Turn the item over. Find the triangle and the number.

1 is clear cooldrink bottles. 2 is milk bottles. 5 is margarine tubs and caps. Those three get recycled here and are worth money.

4 is soft bags, worth a bit if clean. 3, 6 and 7 are pipes, polystyrene and everything else; keep them out of the bag.

Rinse it, cap off, sort it, squash it, bag it.

Practise with a dozen containers from home and it sticks in ten minutes.
