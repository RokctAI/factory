Hey — if this feels like a guilt lesson, it is not. It is a tools lesson.

The problem: plastic lasts centuries, we make a lot, most is not recycled, it ends in drains, rivers and animals.

Three tools in order. Reduce: use less, cloth bag, refillable bottle. Reuse: same thing again, bottle to water bottle, bucket to planter. Recycle: collect, sort, wash, melt, remould, and only for clean PET, HDPE, polypropylene, LDPE.

Why that order: each step down costs more and gets back less.

Who does it: waste pickers and buy-back centres, so keep your clean recyclables separate.

That is the lesson. One bag a week less is already doing it.
