Hey — plastics feel like a long list of strange names, so here is the short shape of it.

Two families. Thermoplastics soften when heated and can be remelted; that is nearly everything, bottles, bags, chairs, pipes. Thermosets set once and char if heated; plugs, pot handles, melamine trays.

Properties: light, tough, bendy, do not conduct electricity or heat well, do not rust, easy to mould. Weak in heat, sunlight and fire.

Matching: pick the plastic whose strengths fit the job. Clear and strong, PET bottle. Bendy, bread bag. Tough and outdoors, polypropylene chair. Stays hard when hot, thermoset plug.

That is really all. You can do this.
