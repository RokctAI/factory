The rest of the term is plastics, the materials of the modern world and the problem of the modern landfill. Plastics are not one material but a family of many, each with its own properties, and they can only be recycled if they are sorted by type, because mixing them ruins the recycled product. The small triangle with a number stamped on the bottom of a bottle or tub is the recycling code, 1 to 7, and it names the plastic. We learn what the seven codes mean, which common items carry each, which are recycled in South Africa and which are not, and then we sort a bag of household plastic the way a recycling depot in Johannesburg or Cape Town does.
