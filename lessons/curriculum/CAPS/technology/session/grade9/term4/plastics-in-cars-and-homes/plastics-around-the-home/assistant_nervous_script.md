Hey — a whole house of plastic sounds like a huge list, but it is one pattern repeated.

In the walls for decades: PVC pipes, conduit, window frames. No rust, no paint.

In the rooms for years: polypropylene for kettles, chairs, buckets, containers; HDPE for bottles and boards; ABS for the fridge liner and the TV; nylon bristles; foam mattresses.

On the table for a day: PET bottles, LDPE bags.

Safety: polypropylene in the microwave, not polystyrene or melamine. Keep plastic out of the sun and away from the stove. Plastic burns, so smoke alarm.

For each thing: right plastic, lasts long enough, can be recycled after. That is all the audit asks.
