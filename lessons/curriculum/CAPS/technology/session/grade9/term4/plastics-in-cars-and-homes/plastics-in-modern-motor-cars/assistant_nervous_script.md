Hey — a car has a thousand parts, but you only need the pattern.

About a tenth of a car's weight is plastic. Why: lighter saves fuel, moulds any shape, never rusts, softer in a crash.

Where: polypropylene bumpers and trim, polycarbonate lamp lenses, a foam-backed dashboard, an HDPE fuel tank, nylon under the bonnet, PVC on the wires. Body, chassis and engine stay steel because they carry the loads.

The catch: when the car is scrapped, the steel recycles easily and the plastic mostly does not, so makers now label parts and make them clip apart.

Material, property, end of life. That is the whole method.
