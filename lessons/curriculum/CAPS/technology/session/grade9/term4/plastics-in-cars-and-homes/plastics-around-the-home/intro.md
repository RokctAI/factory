Walk through a South African home with a notebook and count the plastic: the water pipes in the wall, the light switches, the kettle, the fridge lining, the chairs, the bucket, the shower curtain, the toothbrush, the bread bin, the window frames of a newer house. This case study audits a home room by room, names the plastics and the properties that chose each, and sorts them into things that last decades, like pipes and frames, and things used once, like packaging. It ends with the questions a designer asks about safety, heat near food, and what each item becomes when it is thrown away, which feeds straight into the design task.
