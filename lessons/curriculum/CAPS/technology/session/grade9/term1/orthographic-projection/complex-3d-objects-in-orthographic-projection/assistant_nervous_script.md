Hey — if slopes and holes in three views feel like a trick, here is the calm version: every feature is true in one view and lines in the others.

Slope: real angle in the view along it. Flat strip elsewhere.

Hole: circle with crossed centre lines in the view down its axis. Two dashed lines in the other two.

Tools: 45 set square for chamfers and the mitre line. Compass set to the radius. Hard pencil feint, soft pencil dark.

Reading views: pick a feature, find it in all three.

Open the practice set and trace one hole through three views. You've got this!
