Today we learn the drawing method that engineers and architects use for working drawings: first angle orthographic projection. Instead of one picture of an object, we draw three flat views, front, top and side, each looking straight at a face, and we place them in a fixed pattern so that any reader knows which view is which. We start with simple cubes and blocks, project one view from another with feint lines, and learn the rule that in first angle the top view sits below the front view and the side view sits on the opposite side from the face it shows.
