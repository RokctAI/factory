Today we move from simple cubes to objects with sloping faces, holes and slots, drawn in first angle orthographic projection with instruments. We learn how a slope appears true in one view and foreshortened in another, how a round hole becomes a circle in one view and a pair of dashed lines in the other two, and how to use set squares, a compass and the 45 degree mitre line to keep everything aligned. We finish by practising the reverse skill: looking at three views and building the solid object in our minds.
