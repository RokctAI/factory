Today we put the last three sessions together and draw the stairs and wheelchair ramp from the design brief in first angle orthographic projection, to scale, with correct line types and dimensions. We choose the views and the scale, set out the front view of four steps beside the ramp, project the plan below it, draw the side view on the right with the step profile, add the handrails and hidden detail, and dimension the whole drawing so that a builder could set out the real thing. By the end you will have a method for drawing any staircase and ramp.
