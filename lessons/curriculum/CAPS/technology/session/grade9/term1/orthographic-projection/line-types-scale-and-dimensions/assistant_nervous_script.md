Hey — if lines, scales and dimensions feel like three separate rulebooks, here is the calm version: they are one language.

Five lines: dark for what you see, feint for helpers, dashed for hidden, wavy for a break, chain for centres and cuts.

Scale: 1:50 means divide the real size by 50 to draw it. 7 200 becomes 144. Write the scale on the drawing.

Dimensions: always the real size, in millimetres, once each, outside the shape, number above the line.

Open the practice set and label the lines on one drawing. You've got this!
