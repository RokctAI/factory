Today we meet the Term 1 project: a flight of stairs and a wheelchair ramp for a school building whose entrance sits above the ground. We find out why both are needed, then learn the numbers that a design brief for them must contain: the number of steps, the riser height, the width of the ramp, its gradient and the handrail. By the end you will be able to read a brief for stairs and a ramp and know what each figure means for the person who will use it.
