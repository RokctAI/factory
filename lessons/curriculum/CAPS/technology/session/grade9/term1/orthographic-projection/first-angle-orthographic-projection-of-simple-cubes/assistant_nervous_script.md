Hey — if three views of one block feel like three times the trouble, here is the calm version: one rule and one pattern.

Rule: look straight at a face and draw it true. Front gives width and height. Top gives width and depth. Side gives depth and height.

Pattern for first angle: top view under the front view, left side view on the right.

Join them with feint lines and leave the lines in. Edge-on faces become a single line. Hidden edges go dashed.

Open the practice set and place the three views of one cube. You've got this!
