Hey — if a ten-week project feels huge, here is the calm version: five steps, in order, each with its own job.

Investigate: measure the rise, measure the space, ask the users, list the options. Weeks 1 to 4.

Design: brief, two sketches, choose, draw. Weeks 5 and 6.

Make: build the model. Weeks 7 and 8.

Evaluate: test it against your own checklist. Communicate: present the bid in week 10.

Write everything in the file. Open the practice set and put the five steps in order. You've got this!
