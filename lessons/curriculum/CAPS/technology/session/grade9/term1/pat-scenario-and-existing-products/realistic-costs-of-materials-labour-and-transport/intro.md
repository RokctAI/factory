Today we learn where the money goes when something is built. Every building job has three kinds of cost: the materials, the labour of the people who do the work, and the transport that brings materials and people to the site. We learn how to find realistic prices from hardware stores and suppliers, how to measure quantities from a drawing, and how to put the two together into an estimate. We work through a concrete ramp from start to finish so that the budgeting exercise in week 9 holds no surprises.
