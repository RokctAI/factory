Hey — if analysing other people's ramps feels pointless, here is the calm version: every ramp in town is a free experiment.

Measure it: rise over length gives the gradient. 1 in 12 passes. 1 in 6 fails. Width, rails, landing.

Materials: concrete lasts, steel rusts, timber rots.

Safety: odd steps, missing rails, no top landing.

Cost: to build and to keep.

Write a table: what you saw, the standard, the lesson. Open the practice set and judge one ramp. You've got this!
