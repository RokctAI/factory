Today the Term 1 PAT begins properly. We meet the problem situation, a school entrance raised above the ground that shuts out wheelchair users and makes everyone else climb a makeshift step, and we lay the design process, IDMEC, over the ten weeks of the term so that we know what happens when. Then we start the Investigate stage: looking at the site, measuring the rise, watching the users, and thinking about the kinds of structure, stairs, ramps, landings and rails, that could solve the problem.
