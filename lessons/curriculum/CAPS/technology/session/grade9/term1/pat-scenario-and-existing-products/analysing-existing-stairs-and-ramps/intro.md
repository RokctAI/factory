Today we learn to analyse existing stairs and ramps the way a designer does: against fitness for purpose, the suitability of their materials, their safety and their cost. We look at real entrances, a shopping mall ramp, a clinic step, a church stair, a school ramp built as an afterthought, and ask of each what works, what fails and why. The point is not to criticise for its own sake but to collect lessons, so that our own design borrows the good and avoids the bad.
