Today we turn the cost knowledge from week 4 into the budget for our tender: a complete costing of the real stairs and ramp, including materials, labour and transport, with allowances, laid out so that a tender board can read it. We practise on a short budgeting exercise first, then take the quantities from our own working drawing and the prices we collected, and build the tender price line by line. We finish by checking the total against sensible benchmarks, looking for savings that do not break the specifications, and preparing the budget page for the presentation.
