Today we open Grade 9 Technology by looking back before we look forward. Grade 8 gave you a design process called IDMEC, a set of drawing conventions, the forces that act on structures, gears and mechanical advantage, and the basics of electrical circuits. All of that is the foundation for this year's work on a staircase and wheelchair ramp, hydraulics, electronics and processing. We revise the main ideas in one sweep, then explain what the baseline assessment is for and how to approach it so that it works for you rather than against you.
