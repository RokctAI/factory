Today the term's work goes in front of the tender board. Each team presents its bid for the stairs and ramp: the problem it investigated, the ideas it considered and the one it chose, the working drawings, the budget and the model. We learn what a board is listening for, how to order a presentation so that it tells a clear story in a few minutes, how to divide the speaking so that every member contributes, how to answer questions without bluffing, and how to sit on the board ourselves and judge other teams fairly against the same criteria.
