Hey — if brief, specifications and constraints sound like three words for one thing, here is the calm version: they answer three different questions.

Brief: what, for whom, where, why. One short paragraph. Facts of the site, not the answer.

Specifications: what must it be? Numbers you can test. 1 in 12. Risers 150 to 180. Rails at 900.

Constraints: what limits you? Space, money, materials, time, law.

Considerations: what values do you judge by? Safety, cost, looks, people, planet.
