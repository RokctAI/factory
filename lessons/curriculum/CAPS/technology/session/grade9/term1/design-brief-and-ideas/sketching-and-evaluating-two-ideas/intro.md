Today we generate and judge ideas. Each team sketches two genuinely different solutions for the stairs and ramp, freehand in 3D with notes, so that there is a real choice to make. Then we build an evaluation instrument, a scorecard made from the specifications and design considerations in the brief, and the group applies it to both ideas in the same way. The winner is chosen on the scores, not on whose idea it was, and then developed further before it goes to the working drawing.
