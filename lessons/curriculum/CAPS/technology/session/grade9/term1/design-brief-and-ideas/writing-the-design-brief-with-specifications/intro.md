Today we write the design brief for the stairs and wheelchair ramp, the document that turns everything we investigated into a clear statement of the job. We learn what a brief is, a short description of the problem and the solution to be designed, and what goes with it: specifications, which say what the product must do and be, measurably; constraints, which say what limits the design; and design considerations, the values such as safety and cost against which the final design will be judged. By the end you will have a brief that a stranger could design from.
