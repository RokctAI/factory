Today we consolidate Term 1. The PAT is done and the tender awarded; now we gather what the term taught into a form that will serve for tests and for the rest of the year. We revise first angle orthographic projection and the five line types, scale and dimensioning. We revise static and dynamic loads, even and uneven loads, tension, compression, bending and torsion, and the properties of materials including corrosion. And we revise the design process as it played out: brief, specifications, evaluation, working drawing, flow chart, budget and tender. By the end you will have a one-page summary of the term in your own hand.
