Hey — if six material words sound like a vocabulary test, here is the calm version: each one answers a different question.

How heavy for its size? Density. Does it scratch? Hardness. Does it bend under load? Stiffness, and flexible is the opposite. Does it snap or dent? Brittle snaps, tough dents.

Does it rust? Iron plus air plus water. Paint is a barrier that scratches. Galvanising is zinc that protects even when scratched. Cap the tube ends.
