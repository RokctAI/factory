Today we build. The Make stage of the PAT asks for a neat scale model of the chosen stairs and ramp, built to the working drawing, following the flow chart, using safe working practices. We choose model materials that behave like the real ones at small scale, convert every dimension to model size, learn the rules for working safely with craft knives, hot glue, saws and sanding dust, and then build in the order the flow chart gives, checking the gradient, the risers and the rail heights before anything is glued for good.
