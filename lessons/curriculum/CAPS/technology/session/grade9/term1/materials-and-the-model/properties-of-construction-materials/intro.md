Today we learn the words engineers use to describe how a material behaves, so that we can choose the right one for each part of the stairs and ramp. Density is how heavy a material is for its size; hardness is how well it resists scratching and denting; stiffness is how little it bends under load; flexibility is the opposite. We look at why some materials snap and others dent, and then at corrosion, the slow destruction of metals by air and water, and the ways of preventing it: painting, galvanising, choosing stainless steel or keeping water away.
