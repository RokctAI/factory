Hey — if building the model feels like the part where everything can go wrong, here is the calm version: table, chart, check.

Table: every drawing size divided by 20. Cut from it, not from memory.

Chart: base, ramp, steps, landing, posts, rails, paint. Check before glue: 360 for 1 in 12, risers all 7.5, posts 45.

Safe: knife on a mat, cut away, cap it. Glue gun on its stand, cold water ready. Tidy bench.

Neat: sharp blade, thin glue, hold square.

Open the practice set and order the build steps. You've got this!
