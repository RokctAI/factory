Today the Final Idea becomes two documents that the Make stage will depend on: a working drawing of the stairs and ramp in first angle orthographic projection, and a flow chart that lays out the making of the model step by step. We draw the final design to scale with correct views, line types and dimensions, check it line by line against the specifications, and then plan the build as a sequence of boxes and arrows with decisions and checks, so that two weeks of making go smoothly.
