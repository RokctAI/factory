Hey — if tension, compression, bending and torsion feel like four separate theories, here is the calm version: two forces and two combinations.

Pull is tension. Steel loves it, concrete cracks. Push is compression. Concrete loves it, long thin steel buckles.

Bend a beam: top pushed, bottom pulled, both at once. Steel goes on the pulled side.

Twist is torsion. Tubes resist it. A rectangle folds; a diagonal makes triangles that cannot.

Open the practice set and label each force on one picture. You've got this!
