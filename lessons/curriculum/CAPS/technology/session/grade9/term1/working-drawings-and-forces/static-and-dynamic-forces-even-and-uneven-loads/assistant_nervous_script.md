Hey — if static, dynamic, even and uneven sound like four words to memorise, here is the calm version: two questions.

Does the load move? Still means static, like the slab's own weight or a parked chair. Moving, hitting or repeating means dynamic, like a crowd running down the stairs, and it hits several times harder.

Is the load spread out? Even means shared across the slab. Uneven means bunched on a wheel or a post, and that spot takes it all.

Thicker slab, mesh, deep posts, strong rails. Open the practice set and sort the loads. You've got this!
