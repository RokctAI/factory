Today is a full practice run through a chemistry Paper 2, seven questions in the paper's own order: organic naming and isomers, a reaction scheme, stoichiometry with percentage purity, rates and collision theory, the equilibrium constant, titration with pH, and the galvanic cell. The goal is examination pace — read, plan, execute, audit, move on.
