Hey, it's Mandy — Paper 2 in one lesson feels like the whole year compressed, and it is, so let me shrink it to pocket size.

Organic is one page: the family tree of names and the reaction compass. Read the name tag — longest chain, lowest number, groups alphabetical. Point the compass — seats swapped is substitution, seats filled is addition, seats created is elimination — and quote the conditions on the arrow.

Equilibrium is a seesaw with a stubborn opponent: whatever you do, it leans the other way, and only temperature can touch the factory setting, Kc. Answer every shift in three sentences — disturbance, opposing reaction, new position.

Acids are two definitions and one warning: donor and acceptor by Lowry-Brønsted, and strong is never the same word as concentrated — fraction ionised against amount dissolved.

Electrochemistry is one machine, two gears: galvanic pushes electrons on its own, electrolytic is pushed. An ox, red cat — oxidation at the anode, reduction at the cathode, in both, forever. And mind the double negative when the emf calculation meets zinc.

Every one of these calculations — the RICE table, the pH log, the titration ratio — you have already done successfully this year. Revision is just reminding your hands.
