Today we are working through momentum, impulse and collisions — the topic that opens the Grade 12 year and anchors the first section of Paper 1. We will define momentum and impulse in the exact wording the marking guideline expects, then build the two calculations that carry everything: the impulse-momentum theorem for a single object, and conservation of momentum for two objects colliding in one dimension. Every quantity here is a vector, so a stated positive direction sits at the top of every solution, and the signs do the real work. Get the signs and the definitions right, and this becomes some of the most predictable marks in the paper.
