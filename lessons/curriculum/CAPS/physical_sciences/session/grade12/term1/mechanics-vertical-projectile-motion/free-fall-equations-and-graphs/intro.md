Today we are mastering vertical projectile motion — the most predictable question in Paper 1, because only one force is involved and only three equations are needed. We will fix the sign convention that every solution must open with, work the standard calculations for objects dropped and thrown upward, and then turn to the graphs of position, velocity and acceleration, where the topic's real marks live. By the end you will read gradients and areas off a velocity-time graph as fluently as you substitute into an equation, and the bouncing-ball sawtooth will hold no fear.
