Today we are studying chemical equilibrium — the chemistry of reactions that refuse to finish, settling instead into a stalemate where forward and reverse reactions run at exactly equal rates. We will pin down the definitions of open, closed and reversible systems with exam precision, drive Le Chatelier's principle through the three standard disturbances, and learn to read concentration-time graphs by their jumps, curves and flat sections. The session closes with the equilibrium constant K c: the four-line calculation, what the number means, and the one thing that can ever change it. Paper 2 devotes a full question to this topic nearly every year, and it rewards precise definitions above all.
