Today we begin the second great block of Paper 1 mechanics: work and energy, where instead of tracking forces instant by instant we follow energy flowing in and out of a moving object over a whole journey. We will define work precisely and let the cosine sort every force into positive, negative or zero work, then build net work from the free-body diagram. The payoff is the work-energy theorem — net work equals the change in kinetic energy — proved on flat ground and on an incline, where the weight must be split into its components. Master the sign discipline here and questions about forces, distances and speeds all translate into one four-line energy audit.
