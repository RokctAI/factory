Today we open the full energy ledger: mechanical energy — the sum of kinetic and gravitational potential energy — and what happens to it in a world with friction. We will separate conservative forces from non-conservative ones, state the conservation principle with its conditions, and then put the equation W nc equals delta E k plus delta E p to work on slides, ramps and projectiles, reading its sign like a bank statement. The session finishes with power, the rate of doing work, from the stairs practical to the borehole pump and the cruising truck. These are among the most reliably repeated questions in Paper 1, and the method is the same four steps every time.
