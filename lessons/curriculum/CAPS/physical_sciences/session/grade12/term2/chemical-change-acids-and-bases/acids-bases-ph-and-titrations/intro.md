Today we are tackling acids and bases — the most calculation-rich territory in Paper 2, covering pH, the ionisation constants, and the titration every matric must be able to run on paper and at the bench. We will set the two definitions, separate strong from concentrated with the precision the examiner demands, and build the pH toolkit from the ionic product of water. The session finishes with the four-line titration calculation, the choice of indicator, and why the salt left at equivalence takes after its stronger parent. This is last fortnight's equilibrium chemistry wearing acid-base clothing, and the hardest exam questions are built on exactly that connection.
