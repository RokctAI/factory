Today we are explaining the Doppler effect — why an ambulance siren seems to drop in pitch the instant it passes you, when the siren itself never changed. This earns a dedicated question in Paper 1 every year, so we will fix the definition and the wavefront picture, then master the one equation and its signs until approach-raises, recede-lowers audits every answer automatically. From there we work the standard calculations forwards and in reverse, and follow the same physics from ultrasound in the maternity ward to the red-shifted galaxies that prove the universe is expanding. One street-corner observation, scaled from a heartbeat to the whole sky.
