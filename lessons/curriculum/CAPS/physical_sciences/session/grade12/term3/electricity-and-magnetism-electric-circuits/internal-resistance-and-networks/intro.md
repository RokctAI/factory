Today we are working on internal resistance and electric networks — the reason a car's headlights dim the moment the engine cranks, and one of the most reliable mark-earners in Paper 1. Every real battery resists its own current, so the emf it promises is never quite the terminal voltage it delivers, and the equation that splits that budget governs the whole topic. We will rebuild series and parallel combinations, define emf and internal resistance properly, then run the full circuit routine the examiners love: collapse the network, solve the loop, and walk back in branch by branch. By the end, a voltage-current graph will read you a battery's constants the way a doctor reads a chart.
