Today we take chemistry into the medium where most of it actually happens: water. We will see why the polar water molecule pulls ionic solids apart, which solutions conduct electricity and which stay dark, and how two clear solutions can meet and snow a solid precipitate out of nothing. The whole session runs on one detective story — an unlabelled bottle of clear solution whose hidden ion we will name using two reagents and a fizz. These dissolution equations, the electrolyte distinction and the ion tests are exam staples, and they set up the reaction-type language Grade 11 chemistry assumes.
