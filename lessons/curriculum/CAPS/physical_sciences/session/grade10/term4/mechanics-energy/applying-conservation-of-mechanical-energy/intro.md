Today we put the principle of conservation of mechanical energy to work on the four situations CAPS names explicitly: vertical throws, the swinging pendulum, roller coasters and inclined planes. The centrepiece is a 200 kilogram roller-coaster car cresting a 25 metre hill at 3 metres per second, and the question every version of this topic asks — how fast is it at the bottom? One equality answers all of it while ignoring the shape of the track entirely, which is the superpower we are here to earn. Along the way we will disarm the two traps the marking reports complain about every year, including the starting kinetic energy that so many candidates throw away.
