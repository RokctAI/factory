Today we assemble the most powerful bookkeeping tool in Grade 10 physics: mechanical energy, and the law that keeps its total fixed. You already own the two ingredients — gravitational potential energy and kinetic energy — and this session merges them into one quantity governed by one principle: without friction or air resistance, the total never changes. Our working problem is a 2 kilogram ball dropped from a 20 metre rooftop, and by the end we will read off its speed at the top, halfway down and at the ground without touching a single equation of motion. The definition, the conditions and the ledger habit we build here carry straight through to the biggest calculations in the matric paper.
