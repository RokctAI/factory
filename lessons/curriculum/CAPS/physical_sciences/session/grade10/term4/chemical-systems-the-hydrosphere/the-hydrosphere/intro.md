Today we study the hydrosphere — all the water of planet Earth, in every form and every hiding place — and we point the whole year's chemistry at one system. We will inventory the water and its startling proportions, follow it through the atmosphere, the rocks and the living world, weigh honestly what a dam gives and what it takes, and finish by testing and purifying water with the very ion tests you already know. This topic is marked on application and interpretation rather than rote equations, so the skill we practise throughout is thinking with water. It also explains something worth knowing about your own country: why a world drenched in water still leaves South Africa counting every drop.
