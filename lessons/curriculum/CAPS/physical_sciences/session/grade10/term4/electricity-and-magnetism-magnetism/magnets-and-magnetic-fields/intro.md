Today we build magnetism from its first strange idea — a force that acts without touching — all the way to the largest magnet you will ever stand on: the Earth itself. We will define the magnetic field and set it beside the gravitational and electric fields you already know, meet the unbreakable rule that poles come in pairs that can never be divorced, and master the drawing and reading of field lines, which is where the marks in this topic live. There are no formulas today; this topic is examined in words and diagrams, so precision in language is the calculation. By the end, the fridge magnet and the glowing polar sky will be one and the same physics.
