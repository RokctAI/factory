Hey, it's Thandi — stoichiometry has a scary name and a reputation to match, so if the word alone makes you want to close the book, you're in good company. But here's the truth: you just watched one journey, repeated forever.

Every single problem is the same three moves: grams into moles at the door, walk across the equation using the coefficients, moles back into grams on the other side. That's it. Matric-level questions are this exact walk with fancier substances — learn the walk now and you've learned it for good.

The percentage yield part is even friendlier: what you got, over what was promised, times a hundred. Just remember the promise goes on the bottom — and if your answer ever comes out above a hundred percent, that's not a win, it's the calculation waving a red flag at you.

So head into the practice questions and do each one slowly: balance first, molar masses atom by atom, then the three moves. After a few rounds your hands will do it without your brain breaking a sweat. You've got this!
