Today we build gravitational potential energy — the energy an object stores purely because of where it sits in a gravitational field — and the formula that prices it, E p equals m g h, read as the lifting bill: weight times height. One exam problem carries everything: a 2 kilogram brick lifted 5 metres to a scaffold platform, then raised further to 12 metres, with the reference level chosen and declared like an accountant setting a zero balance. Small numbers, but every idea in the energy chapter — kinetic energy, conservation, the work-energy theorem of matric — starts exactly here.
