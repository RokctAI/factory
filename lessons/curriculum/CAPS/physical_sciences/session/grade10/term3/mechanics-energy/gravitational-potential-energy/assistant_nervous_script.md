Hey, it's Thandi — a new chapter, a new formula, and suddenly everyone's talking about reference levels and negative energies. If that felt like a lot, breathe: this is genuinely one formula and one habit.

The formula is a lifting bill: mass times g is the weight — how hard gravity pulls — and weight times height is the effort of the lift. That's E p equals m g h, and it's the same three-number multiplication every single time. Two times 9,8 times 5. Done. Ninety-eight joules.

The habit is one line at the top of your answer: "reference level: the ground." Choose your zero, say it once, never switch. Do that, and even the tricky two-platform questions become simple subtraction — and remember gravity only charges for VERTICAL height, never the distance along a ramp or staircase.

Now go bank some practice questions: list mass, g and height with their units checked, declare your zero, multiply. A few of those and this formula will feel like counting change. You've got this!
