The habit: square the speed FIRST, on its own line. Write "v squared equals 400" before anything else touches your page. That single line kills the biggest mark-eater in the whole energy chapter — the forgotten square that shrinks answers twenty times.

Then it's just: half the mass, times that square. Half of 1 000 is 500, times 400 is 200 000 joules. Big numbers are normal here — cars carry fortunes of energy, and that's the whole road-safety point: double the speed, four times the energy, every time, because two squared is four.

One more pocket rule: kilometres per hour must become metres per second — divide by 3,6 — BEFORE you square. Then go run the practice questions: convert, square on its own line, half the mass, multiply. Four steps, same order, every time — and watch this topic become free marks. You've got this!
