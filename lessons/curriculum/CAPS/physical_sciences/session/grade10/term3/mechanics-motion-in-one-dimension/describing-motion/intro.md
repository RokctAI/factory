Today we are building the vocabulary of motion — frame of reference, position, distance, displacement, speed and velocity — the six ideas every mechanics question quietly assumes you own. One short walk carries the whole session: 40 metres east, 30 metres west, 50 seconds in total, and from it we will pull four different answers, two of which surprise almost everyone the first time. The scalar-vector split we drill here is the single most examined distinction in this topic, and it decides marks all the way to matric. Get this alphabet right and everything that follows in mechanics is just spelling.
