Today we are working on acceleration and the three motion graphs — the tools that turn any journey into a picture you can read marks straight off. One car's trip carries the whole session: speeding up, cruising, then braking, and we will pull both accelerations and the total displacement out of a single velocity-time graph using nothing but a gradient and an area. Along the way we will meet the classic traps — the rising line that gets misread as moving away, and the units that divide off the axes. Learn to read these pictures properly and half of Grade 10 mechanics becomes looking, not calculating.
