Today we lay the first stone of all mechanics: the difference between vectors and scalars, and the arithmetic each kind obeys. Two exam tasks organise the session — classifying displacement, speed, force, energy and velocity, and then finding the resultant of 30 newtons east meeting 50 newtons west, where the reflex answer of 80 newtons turns out to be exactly wrong. We will build the direction test, the sign-convention method, and the habit of answering with magnitude and direction every single time. These look like small questions on paper, but every mechanics and electricity topic through matric stands on getting them right.
