Today we meet the family of waves that need no medium at all: the electromagnetic spectrum. This session explains what an electromagnetic wave is made of, why every member from radio waves to gamma rays crosses a vacuum at exactly the same speed, and how to keep the seven bands in order with wavelength and frequency running opposite ways. We then put the two governing equations to work — c equals f lambda and E equals h f — converting FM frequencies and ultraviolet wavelengths in full scientific notation. Master the order, the two formulas and the prefix conversions, and the uses-and-dangers questions become pure logic instead of memory work.
