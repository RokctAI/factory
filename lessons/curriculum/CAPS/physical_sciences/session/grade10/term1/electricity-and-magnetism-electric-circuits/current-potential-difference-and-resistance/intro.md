Today we take the charge that electrostatics left standing still and set it moving: this session builds the four foundation ideas of every circuit — current, potential difference, the meters that measure them, and resistance. You will learn the two defining equations, I equals Q over delta t and V equals W over Q, and use each of them both ways with real numbers and units. We then fix the strict connection rules for ammeters and voltmeters, and finish inside the wire itself, where colliding electrons explain why length, thickness and temperature control resistance. These definitions and rules are guaranteed marks in every circuits question from Grade 10 to matric.
