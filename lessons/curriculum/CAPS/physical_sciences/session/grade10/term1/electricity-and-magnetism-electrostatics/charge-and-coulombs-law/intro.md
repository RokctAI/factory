Today we start electricity where it truly begins: with charge itself. This session pins down the coulomb, the rule that charge only ever comes in whole multiples of the electron's charge, the three ways an object becomes charged, and the conservation principle that lets identical spheres share charge with their signs intact. We finish with Coulomb's law, turning "like charges repel" into an answer in newtons — and drilling the inverse square, where doubling the distance cuts the force to a quarter. The marks in this topic are lost to missed prefixes and unsquared distances, so we work every calculation in full, conversions first.
