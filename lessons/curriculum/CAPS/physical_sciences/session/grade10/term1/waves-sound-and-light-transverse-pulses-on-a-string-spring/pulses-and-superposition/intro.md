Today we isolate the building block of every wave in this subject: the pulse — one disturbance travelling through a medium. This session pins down the transverse pulse, where particles move at right angles to the travel, and the fact examiners love most: the medium does not travel with the pulse, only the energy does. We define amplitude properly, measured from the rest position, and then meet the principle of superposition — adding displacements with their signs when pulses overlap, constructively or destructively. Remember that pulses pass through each other unchanged, and even a momentarily flat rope still holds both of them.
