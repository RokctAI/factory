Today we build the short list of quantities that describes every wave you will meet this year: amplitude, wavelength, period and frequency, all tied together by the wave equation v equals f lambda. This session moves from a single pulse on a rope to a full travelling wave, defines each quantity with its symbol and SI unit, and works the standard calculations in the three-line form the memo rewards — formula, substitution, answer with unit. We finish on the traps: the crest-to-trough amplitude error, the period-frequency swap, unconverted centimetres, and reading a graph's horizontal axis before measuring anything. Discipline with symbols and units is where this topic's marks live.
