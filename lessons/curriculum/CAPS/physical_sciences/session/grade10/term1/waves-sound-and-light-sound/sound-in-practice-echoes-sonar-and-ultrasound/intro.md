Today we put the physics of sound to work: echoes, sonar and ultrasound, all running on one equation, v equals f lambda, plus the echo rule with its famous factor of two. We work the full sonar problem — depth from echo timing, wavelength from the wave equation — and see why the two calculations use completely different logic. Then nature's own sonar: the bat's five-millimetre waves that turn a moth into a mirror, the dolphin's two-centimetre clicks, and the hospital scanner that sees an unborn baby safely because ultrasound is not ionising radiation. Keep the round trip halved and the kilohertz converted, and this whole topic pays out.
