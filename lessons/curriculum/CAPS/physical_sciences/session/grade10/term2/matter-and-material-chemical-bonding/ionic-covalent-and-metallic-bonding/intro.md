Today we answer one question three ways: why is salt a brittle crystal, wax a soft solid, and copper a bendable conductor? The answer is the three types of chemical bond — covalent sharing between non-metals, ionic transfer between a metal and a non-metal, and the metallic sea of delocalised electrons. We build the Lewis diagrams that draw each bond, the charge-balancing rules that write every ionic formula, and the formula-mass additions that close nearly every bonding question in the exam. Get the three pictures straight and the whole of Paper 2 chemistry has its foundations poured.
