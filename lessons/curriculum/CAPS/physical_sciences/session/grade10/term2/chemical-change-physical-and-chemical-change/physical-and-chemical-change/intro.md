Today we draw the line that runs through the whole of chemistry: the boundary between physical and chemical change, decided by one question — is a new substance formed? This session works both sides of that line at particle level: state changes, dissolving and grinding where the particles survive intact, against burning, rusting and cooking where bonds break and atoms regroup. We arm you with the five observable signs of a chemical change, the conservation laws of mass and energy that hold across both kinds, and the tricky cases examiners love — electrolysis, the fizzing tablet, and the candle running both changes at once.
