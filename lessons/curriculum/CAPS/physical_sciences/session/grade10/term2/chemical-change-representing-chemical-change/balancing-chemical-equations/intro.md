Today we learn the craft that opens nearly every chemistry calculation from now to matric: balancing chemical equations, driven by the law of conservation of mass. Our working example is the combustion of propane — braai gas — where three elements must be tracked across the arrow and an unbalanced equation quite literally lies about missing atoms. You will learn the one commandment, change coefficients and never subscripts, the strategic order carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, and the closing audit that proves the balance rather than hoping for it. We finish by clearing fractions the honest way and reducing to the smallest whole-number set, because those coefficients become next term's mole ratios.
