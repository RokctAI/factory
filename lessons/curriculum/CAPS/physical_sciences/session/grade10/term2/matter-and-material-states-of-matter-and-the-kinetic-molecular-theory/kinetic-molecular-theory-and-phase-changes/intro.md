Today we build the model that explains every physical property you have ever observed: the kinetic molecular theory. Five short statements about particles, motion, energy and forces will let us explain solids, liquids and gases, name all six changes of state, and read the heating curve of water — two kilograms of ice at minus twenty degrees heated all the way to steam. The exam question at the centre of it appears every year: why does the temperature stop rising while the heating continues? Answer that in the language of particles, energy and forces, and the whole topic is yours.
