Term 4 closes the chemistry year with the second great family of reactions: acid-base chemistry moves protons, but redox chemistry — the topic that carries Grade 12 electrochemistry, batteries and electrolysis — moves electrons. Today we finish the proton story with the deliberate design of salts from their acid and base parents, then build the electron bookkeeping system called oxidation numbers. From there we define oxidation and reduction exactly, name the agents without inverting them, and close with the half-reaction method for balancing redox equations from the table of standard reduction potentials. Master the bookkeeping now and next year's electrochemistry becomes arithmetic you already know.
