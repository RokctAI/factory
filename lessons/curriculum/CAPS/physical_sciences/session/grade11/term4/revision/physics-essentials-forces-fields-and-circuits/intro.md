This session is the physics revision sweep for the final examination: the year's mechanics, fields and electricity reorganised into the four toolkits that earn the marks. We drill the free-body method that solves every force problem, revise gravity and Coulomb's law together as the inverse-square twins, run the collapse-solve-expand drill on a full resistor network with its power audit, and walk the electromagnetism chain from field to flux to induced emf. Nothing here is new — everything is the exam-day version, compressed, ordered, and closed with the trap museum of the year's five favourite mark-destroyers.
