This session is the chemistry revision sweep for the final examination: the year's quantitative chemistry and acids and bases reorganised into the four toolkits that earn the marks. We run the mole conversions for mass, solution and gas through one hub, price any gas state with the ideal gas equation, cross the stoichiometry bridge with its limiting reagents and honesty percentages, and finish with the acid-base machinery from proton donor to titration. Nothing here is new — everything is the exam-day version, compressed, ordered, and drilled through worked examples chosen to expose the classic traps.
