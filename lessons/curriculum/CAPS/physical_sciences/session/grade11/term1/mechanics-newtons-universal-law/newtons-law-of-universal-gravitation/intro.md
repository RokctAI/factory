Today we are working with Newton's law of universal gravitation — the single equation that explains a falling apple, the Moon's orbit, and why you would weigh less on Mars. We will state the law in the wording the marker wants, work the standard substitutions with their powers of ten intact, and use the inverse-square structure to answer ratio questions without touching a calculator. Then we derive the gravitational acceleration of any planet from its mass and radius, and settle the mass-versus-weight distinction that is examined every single year, ending with what weightlessness really is: free fall, not the absence of gravity.
