Today we are tackling connected bodies and pulleys — two objects joined by a string, where the exam asks for both the acceleration of the system and the tension in the string. The whole topic runs on one reliable two-step method: treat the pair as a single object first, because the tension cancels out of that equation, then return to one body alone to extract the tension. We will run the method through the three standard arrangements — crates on a flat floor, a block on a table tied over a pulley to a hanging mass, and an incline with a pulley — adding friction along the way. Master the two steps and the sign convention, and these long Paper 1 questions become routine bookkeeping.
