Today we are mastering Newton's three laws — the statements that carry the largest single section of the Grade 11 physics paper. We will learn each law in the exact wording the marker wants, build the four-step free-body method that turns any force question into an equation, and work the standard applications: a crate dragged across a rough floor, a passenger on a scale in an accelerating lift, and a rocket leaving the pad. Along the way we will kill the classic misconceptions — that a force is needed to keep things moving, that the scale reads your true weight, and that weight and the normal force form an action-reaction pair. Reason these laws out properly once, and the marks follow for the rest of mechanics.
