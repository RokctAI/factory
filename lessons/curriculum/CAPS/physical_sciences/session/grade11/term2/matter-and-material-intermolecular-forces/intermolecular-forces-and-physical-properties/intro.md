Today we are looking between the molecules, not inside them — at the intermolecular forces that decide why water is liquid at room temperature while heavier carbon dioxide is a gas, and why nail polish remover vanishes in seconds while oil sits all day. We will separate these weak attractions from the strong covalent bonds within molecules, classify the whole family — London forces, dipole-dipole attractions, hydrogen bonding and ion-dipole forces — and then use them to explain the four properties Paper 2 asks about relentlessly: boiling point, melting point, vapour pressure and solubility. The showcase comparison of water against hydrogen sulfide, and the machinery behind like dissolves like, both live in this session.
