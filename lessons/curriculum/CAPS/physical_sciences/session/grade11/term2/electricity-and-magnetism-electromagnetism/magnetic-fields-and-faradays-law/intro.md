Today we are learning the two discoveries that power the entire national grid: a current makes a magnetic field, and a changing magnetic field makes a current. We will master the Right Hand Rule for straight wires, loops and solenoids, define magnetic flux as B A cosine theta with the angle measured against the normal, and state Faraday's law in the wording the marker rewards. The session builds to the calculation the paper always sets — the emf induced in a multi-turn coil when the field through it changes — together with the opposition rule that makes induced currents fight the change that creates them. Get flux first, change second, law last, and this topic pays reliably.
