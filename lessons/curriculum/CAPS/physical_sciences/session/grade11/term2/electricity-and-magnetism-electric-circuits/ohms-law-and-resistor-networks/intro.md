Today we are connecting emf, potential difference and current with the single most used equation in electricity — Ohm's law — and then putting it to work on whole networks of resistors. We will state the law with its constant-temperature condition, meet the filament that breaks it, and master the two combination rules: plain addition for series, the reciprocal sum for parallel. The heart of the session is the four-step walk through a mixed circuit — collapse, total current, spend the volts, split the amps — with the balance checks that catch slips before they cost marks. We finish with power and energy, turning kilowatts and hours into the rands and cents on a municipal bill.
