Today we are treating every chemical reaction as an energy transaction: breaking bonds costs energy, forming bonds pays it out, and the balance of that deal is the heat of reaction, delta H. We will divide all reactions into exothermic and endothermic, climb the activation energy hill that even downhill reactions must pay, and meet the activated complex at its summit. Then we put the entire story into one picture — the potential energy graph, with and without a catalyst — because that graph is exactly what this topic's exam questions are drawn on.
