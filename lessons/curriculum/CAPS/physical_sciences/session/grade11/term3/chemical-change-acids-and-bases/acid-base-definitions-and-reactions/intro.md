Today we are answering the question underneath all acid-base chemistry: what actually makes a substance an acid or a base. We start with the Arrhenius definitions and their famous failures, then upgrade to Bronsted-Lowry, where everything becomes the story of a single proton changing hands. From there we extract conjugate acid-base pairs, meet the ampholytes that play both roles, master the three reactions of acids every paper demands, and lock in the four indicator colours that make the invisible chemistry visible. Definitions, equations and colours from this session are guaranteed marks in the chemical change section of the exam.
