Every mark in the Chemical Change section of the paper passes through one idea: the mole. Today we rebuild the mole at Grade 11 depth and add the three conversion tools the rest of the year depends on — molar mass for solids, molar volume for gases, and concentration for solutions. We close with percentage composition and the route from an empirical formula to a molecular formula, worked in full with the numbers the examiners actually use. Four tools, one habit: every quantity becomes moles before anything else happens to it.
