## Question (1 mark)

Carbon monoxide, CO(g), reacts with oxygen, O2(g), to form carbon dioxide, CO2(g), in a sealed container according to the balanced equation: 2CO(g) + O2(g) → 2CO2(g). The graph below shows the concentration of O2(g) versus time. (Graph of concentration of O2(g) in mol·dm⁻³ versus time in s: the curve starts at 0,27 mol·dm⁻³ at t = 0, falls steeply to 0,15 mol·dm⁻³ at 10 s, continues falling less steeply and flattens to 0,05 mol·dm⁻³ from about 40 s to 60 s.) At which time, 10 s or 30 s, is the reaction rate higher?

## Method

rate from graph

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) The rate at an instant is the magnitude of the gradient of the concentration-time curve at that instant. At 10 s the curve is still falling steeply; by 30 s it has almost flattened. The steeper gradient at 10 s means the higher rate.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

10 s
