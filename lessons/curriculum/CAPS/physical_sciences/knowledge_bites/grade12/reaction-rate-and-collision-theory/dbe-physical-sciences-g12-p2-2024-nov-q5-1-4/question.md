## Question (2 marks)

EXPERIMENT II: Experiment I is repeated using a 2 mol·dm⁻³ HCℓ solution. Redraw the above graph (NO numerical values need to be shown) in your ANSWER BOOK and label the curve A. On the same set of axes, draw the curve that will be obtained for Experiment II. Label this as curve B. (Context: Experiment I used 1 mol·dm⁻³ HCℓ with a 0,5 g Aℓ strip; the graph shows volume of H2 in dm³ versus time in minutes.)

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Reason it out before sketching: doubling the acid concentration increases the rate, so curve B climbs more steeply than curve A from the very start. But the acid is in excess in both experiments and the same 0,5 g of aluminium limits the reaction, so the total hydrogen produced is unchanged — curve B levels off at exactly the same final volume as curve A, just reaching it sooner.
