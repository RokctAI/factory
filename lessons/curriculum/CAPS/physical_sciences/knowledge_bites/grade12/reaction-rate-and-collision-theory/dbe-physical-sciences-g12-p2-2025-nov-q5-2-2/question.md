## Question (5 marks)

Carbon monoxide, CO(g), reacts with oxygen, O2(g), to form carbon dioxide, CO2(g), in a sealed container according to the balanced equation: 2CO(g) + O2(g) → 2CO2(g). The graph shows the concentration of O2(g) versus time. (Graph of concentration of O2(g) in mol·dm⁻³ versus time in s: the curve starts at 0,27 mol·dm⁻³ at t = 0, falls steeply to 0,15 mol·dm⁻³ at 10 s, continues falling less steeply and flattens to 0,05 mol·dm⁻³ from about 40 s to 60 s.) The reaction takes place in a 3 dm³ container. Calculate the average rate (in mol·s⁻¹) at which CO2(g) is formed in the first 10 s.

## Method

average rate calculation

## Memo working

Step 1 — read the change in oxygen concentration off the graph over the first 10 s: [O2] falls from 0,27 mol·dm⁻³ at t = 0 to 0,15 mol·dm⁻³ at t = 10 s. Rate = −Δc(O2)/Δt = −(0,15 − 0,27)/(10 − 0) = 0,012 mol·dm⁻³·s⁻¹.

Step 2 — convert from concentration per second to moles per second using the container volume: rate(O2) = cV per second = (0,012)(3) = 0,036 mol·s⁻¹.

Step 3 — use the mole ratio from the balanced equation, n(O2) : n(CO2) = 1 : 2, so CO2 forms twice as fast as O2 is used: rate(CO2) = 2 x 0,036 = 0,072 mol·s⁻¹.

Marking criteria: (a) calculate the change [O2]final − [O2]initial (accept 0,265 to 0,27 for [O2]initial); (b) substitute 10 s in rate formula; (c) multiply rate/concentration of O2 by 3 dm³; (d) use mole ratio n(O2) : n(CO2) = 1 : 2; (e) final correct answer 0,072 mol·s⁻¹.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Rate(CO2) = 0,072 mol·s⁻¹ [accepted RANGE: 0,06 - 0,072]
