## Question (2 marks)

The reaction between pure aluminium, Aℓ(s), and EXCESS hydrochloric acid, HCℓ(aq), is used to investigate the factors that affect the rate of a reaction. The balanced equation for the reaction is: 2Aℓ(s) + 6HCℓ(aq) → 2AℓCℓ3(aq) + 3H2(g). Define the term reaction rate.

## Method

define reaction rate

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) A definition question marked 2 or 0: the answer must link a change in some measurable quantity of reactant or product (concentration, amount, moles, volume or mass) to time — 'per unit time'. Any one of the memo's formulations earns both marks. (Memo note: give the mark for 'per unit time' only if in context of reaction rate.)

ANY ONE: Change in concentration of products/reactants per (unit) time. Change in amount/number of moles/volume/mass of products or reactants per (unit) time.
