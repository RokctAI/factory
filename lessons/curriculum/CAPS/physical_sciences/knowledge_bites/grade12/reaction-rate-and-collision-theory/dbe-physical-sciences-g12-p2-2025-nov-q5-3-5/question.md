(Context: curve X is the Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution curve for the reaction — a graph of number of particles versus kinetic energy, a single curve starting at the origin, rising to a peak and tailing off. (see marking guidelines) Verbal description of the required sketch: curve Y has the same shape as curve X and also starts at the origin; because more gas particles are now in the container, the peak of curve Y is HIGHER than the peak of curve X; because the temperature is unchanged, the peak of curve Y sits at the SAME kinetic energy as the peak of curve X. Marking criteria: both curves start at the origin and have correct shape with peaks at the same kinetic energy; peak of curve Y higher than curve X at the same kinetic energy. Both curves not labelled: 0/2. Curves intersecting anywhere besides the origin: max 1/2.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Curve Y: same shape as curve X, starting at the origin, with its peak HIGHER than curve X and at the SAME kinetic energy (more particles, same temperature).
