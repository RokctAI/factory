## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: A hypothetical endothermic reaction is given below: A(g) + B(g) ⇌ AB(g). The activation energy for the reverse reaction is 50 kJ·mol⁻¹. Which ONE of the following is possible for the reaction above? A The heat of the reaction is +70 kJ·mol⁻¹. B The activation energy for the forward reaction is 50 kJ·mol⁻¹. C The energy of the activated complex is 40 kJ·mol⁻¹. D The activation energy for the forward reaction is 40 kJ·mol⁻¹.

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Use the key relationship ΔH = EA(forward) − EA(reverse). The reaction is endothermic, so ΔH must be positive, which forces EA(forward) to be GREATER than EA(reverse) = 50 kJ·mol⁻¹. Option A: ΔH = +70 kJ·mol⁻¹ gives EA(forward) = 50 + 70 = 120 kJ·mol⁻¹ — perfectly possible. Option B: EA(forward) = 50 would make ΔH = 0, not endothermic. Option D: EA(forward) = 40 would make ΔH = 40 − 50 = −10 kJ·mol⁻¹, exothermic. Option C: the activated complex sits 50 kJ·mol⁻¹ above the products and more than 50 kJ·mol⁻¹ above the reactants, so an energy of only 40 kJ·mol⁻¹ is inconsistent with the diagram. Only A is possible.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A
