## Question (1 mark)

Give a reason for the answer to QUESTION 5.2.1. (In QUESTION 5.2.1 the change made to obtain curve Y from curve X on the Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution was identified as an increase in temperature.)

## Method

maxwell boltzmann interpretation

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Point at the feature of the graph that proves it: curve Y peaks at a higher kinetic energy (the peak has shifted to the right), meaning the average kinetic energy of the particles has increased and a larger fraction of particles now has high kinetic energy — which is exactly what an increase in temperature does.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Curve Y has a peak/maximum at a higher kinetic energy / peak shifted to the right. OR The (average) kinetic energy (of the particles) increases / more particles with higher kinetic energy / larger area with higher kinetic energy.
