## Question (1 mark)

EXPERIMENT III: Experiment I is repeated using 0,5 g pure powdered Aℓ. How will the volume of H2(g) produced in Experiment III compare to that in Experiment I? Choose from GREATER THAN, LESS THAN or EQUAL TO. (Context: Experiment I used a 0,5 g Aℓ strip in excess HCℓ(aq).)

## Method

surface area yield reasoning

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Powdering the aluminium increases its surface area, which speeds the reaction up — but rate and yield are different things. The same 0,5 g of aluminium reacts with the same excess of acid, so exactly the same amount of hydrogen is produced in the end; it just forms faster.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Equal to.
