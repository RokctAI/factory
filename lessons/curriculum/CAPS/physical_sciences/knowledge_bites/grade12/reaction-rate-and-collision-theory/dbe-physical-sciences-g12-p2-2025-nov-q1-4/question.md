## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: Magnesium ribbon of mass 2 g reacts with excess hydrochloric acid of concentration 0,1 mol·dm⁻³ at 20 °C: Mg(s) + 2HCℓ(aq) → MgCℓ2(aq) + H2(g). Which ONE of the following changes will NOT increase the initial rate of the reaction? A Using 2 g of powdered magnesium B Increasing the temperature of HCℓ to 30 °C C Using a longer piece of the magnesium ribbon D Doubling the volume of the hydrochloric acid used

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Work through the collision-theory factors one by one. Powdering the same 2 g of magnesium (A) increases the surface area, so more effective collisions per second — rate increases. Raising the temperature to 30 °C (B) increases the kinetic energy of the particles and the fraction with energy above the activation energy — rate increases. A longer piece of ribbon of the same mass (C) is a thinner, more spread-out piece, which also exposes more surface area — rate increases. Doubling the volume of the acid (D) changes neither its concentration (still 0,1 mol·dm⁻³) nor any other rate factor, and the acid was already in excess — so the initial rate stays the same.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

D
