## Question (1 mark)

The reaction between sulphur dioxide, SO2(g), and oxygen, O2(g), takes place in a sealed container according to the balanced equation: 2SO2(g) + O2(g) ⇌ 2SO3(g), ΔH = −196 kJ·mol⁻¹. Was there a net release or net absorption of energy during the REVERSE reaction?

## Method

exothermic endothermic reasoning

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) The negative ΔH tells us the forward reaction is exothermic — it releases energy. The reverse reaction is the mirror image energetically, so it must absorb the same amount of energy: net absorption.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Absorption
