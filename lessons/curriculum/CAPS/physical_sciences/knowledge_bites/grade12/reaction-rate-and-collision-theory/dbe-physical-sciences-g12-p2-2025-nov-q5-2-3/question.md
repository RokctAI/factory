## Question (1 mark)

Carbon monoxide, CO(g), reacts with oxygen, O2(g), to form carbon dioxide, CO2(g), in a sealed container according to the balanced equation: 2CO(g) + O2(g) → 2CO2(g). The graph shows the concentration of O2(g) versus time. (Graph of concentration of O2(g) in mol·dm⁻³ versus time in s: the curve starts at 0,27 mol·dm⁻³ at t = 0, falls steeply to 0,15 mol·dm⁻³ at 10 s, continues falling less steeply and flattens to 0,05 mol·dm⁻³ from about 40 s to 60 s.) Which reactant is in excess, CO or O2?

## Method

excess reactant from graph

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) The reaction stops (the curve flattens) while the O2 concentration is still 0,05 mol·dm⁻³, not zero. Oxygen is left over after the CO is used up, so O2 is the reactant in excess.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

O2/oxygen
