## Question (2 marks)

Define the term reaction rate.

## Method

define reaction rate

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) (Memo note: give the mark for 'per unit time' only if in context of reaction rate.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Change in concentration of products/reactants per (unit) time. (Also accepted: change in amount/number of moles/volume/mass of products or reactants per (unit) time; amount/number of moles/volume/mass of products formed / reactants used per (unit) time; rate of change in concentration/amount/number of moles/volume/mass (2 or 0).)
