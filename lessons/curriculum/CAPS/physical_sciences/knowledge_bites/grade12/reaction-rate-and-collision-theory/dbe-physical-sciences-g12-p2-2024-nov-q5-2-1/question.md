## Question (1 mark)

Curve X is the Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution curve for a reaction under a set of reaction conditions. A change was made to one of the reaction conditions to obtain curve Y. (Graph: number of particles versus kinetic energy; curve X has a higher, earlier peak; curve Y is lower and shifted to higher kinetic energy.) What change was made to obtain curve Y?

## Method

maxwell boltzmann interpretation

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Read the shape change: curve Y's peak is lower and sits at a higher kinetic energy than curve X's, with the distribution spread further to the right. That is the signature of raising the temperature — the same number of particles is redistributed towards higher kinetic energies. (A catalyst would not move the distribution at all; it only lowers the activation energy.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

An increase in temperature.
