## Question (3 marks)

A galvanic cell is represented by the following cell notation: Pb(s) | Pb²⁺(aq) || Fe³⁺(aq), Fe²⁺(aq) | Pt(s). Write down the balanced net ionic equation for this cell.

## Method

net ionic equation

## Memo working

The anode (left of the cell notation) is where oxidation occurs: Pb → Pb²⁺ + 2e⁻. The cathode (right) is where reduction occurs: Fe³⁺ + e⁻ → Fe²⁺, doubled to transfer the same two electrons. Adding the half-reactions gives the net ionic equation. Marking criteria: reactants, products, balancing. Ignore ⇌ and phases.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Pb(s) + 2Fe³⁺(aq) → Pb²⁺(aq) + 2Fe²⁺(aq)
