## Question (2 marks)

It is observed that dilute hydrochloric acid does not react with copper, Cu(s), at 25 °C. Explain this observation by referring to the relative strengths of the reducing agents.

## Method

reducing agent strength reasoning

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Cu/copper is a weaker reducing agent than hydrogen/H2 (and will not reduce H⁺/hydrogen ion to H2). OR Cu/copper is too weak a reducing agent to reduce H⁺/hydrogen ion (to H2).
