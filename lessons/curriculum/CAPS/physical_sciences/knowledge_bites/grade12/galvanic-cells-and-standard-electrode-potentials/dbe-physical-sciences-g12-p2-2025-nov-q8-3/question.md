## Question (3 marks)

Write down the cell notation for this cell. (Context: the galvanic cell above — an aluminium electrode in Aℓ3+(aq) solution and a zinc electrode in Zn2+(aq) solution, connected by a voltmeter and a salt bridge, operating initially under standard conditions.)

## Method

cell notation

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Verbal reasoning: aluminium is the stronger reducing agent, so Aℓ is oxidised at the anode; the anode half-cell is written on the left, the salt bridge as a double vertical line, and the cathode half-cell (Zn2+ reduced to Zn) on the right.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Aℓ(s) | Aℓ3+(aq) || Zn2+(aq) | Zn(s). (Also accepted with concentrations 1 mol·dm⁻³, or without phases: Aℓ | Aℓ3+ || Zn2+ | Zn.)
