## Question (3 marks)

Will dilute nitric acid, HNO3(aq), react with copper, Cu(s), at 25 °C? Choose from YES or NO. Explain the answer in terms of the relative strengths of the oxidising agents.

## Method

oxidising agent strength reasoning

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Yes. NO3⁻/nitrate ion/nitric acid is a stronger oxidising agent than Cu²⁺/copper(II) ion (therefore Cu/copper will be oxidised to Cu²⁺/copper(II) ion).
