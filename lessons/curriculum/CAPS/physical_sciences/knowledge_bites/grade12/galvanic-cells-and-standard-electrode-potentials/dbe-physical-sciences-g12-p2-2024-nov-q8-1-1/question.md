## Question (2 marks)

Dilute hydrochloric acid, HCℓ(aq), reacts with magnesium, Mg(s), at 25 °C according to the following balanced equation: Mg(s) + 2HCℓ(aq) → MgCℓ2(aq) + H2(g). Use oxidation numbers for EACH of the reactants and explain why this reaction is a redox reaction.

## Method

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The oxidation number of H changes from +1 to 0 AND the oxidation number of Mg changes from 0 to +2. OR Mg⁰ → Mg²⁺ oxidation number increases; H⁺ → H2⁰ oxidation number decreases.
