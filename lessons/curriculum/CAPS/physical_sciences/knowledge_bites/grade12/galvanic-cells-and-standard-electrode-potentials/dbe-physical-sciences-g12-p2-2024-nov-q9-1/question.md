## Question (5 marks)

A strip of silver is added to a 1 mol·dm⁻³ solution of Pb(NO3)2 at 25 °C. (Diagram: a beaker containing Pb(NO3)2(aq) with a silver strip immersed in it.) Consider the reaction below: 2Ag(s) + Pb²⁺(aq) → 2Ag⁺(s) + Pb(s). By means of a calculation, determine whether this reaction is SPONTANEOUS or NON-SPONTANEOUS.

**Note: the paper prints the product as '2Ag⁺(s)'; the phase should read (aq). Transcribed verbatim — flagged as a source typo.**

## Method

cell potential spontaneity

## Memo working

OPTION 1: E°cell = E°reduction − E°oxidation = –0,13 – (0,80) = –0,93 V; the negative cell potential means the reaction is non-spontaneous. OPTION 2: Pb²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Pb, E° = –0,13 V; 2Ag → 2Ag⁺ + 2e⁻, E° = –0,80 V; overall Pb²⁺ + 2Ag → Pb + 2Ag⁺, E° = –0,93 V; non-spontaneous. (Accept any other correct formula from the data sheet; unconventional abbreviations, e.g. E°cell = E°OA − E°RA, with correct substitutions: 3/4 of the formula-related marks.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

E°cell = –0,93 V, therefore non-spontaneous
