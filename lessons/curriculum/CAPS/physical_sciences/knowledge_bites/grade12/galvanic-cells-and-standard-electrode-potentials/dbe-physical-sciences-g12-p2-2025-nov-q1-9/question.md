## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: A galvanic cell consists of the following half-cells: Fe3+(aq)|Fe2+(aq) and Ni2+(aq)|Ni(s). Which ONE of the following statements is CORRECT for this cell? A Ni2+ is reduced and Fe2+ is oxidised. B Ni2+ is oxidised and Fe3+ is reduced. C Pt is the electrode at the cathode. D Ni is the electrode at the cathode.

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Compare the standard electrode potentials from the table: E°(Fe3+/Fe2+) = +0,77 V is higher than E°(Ni2+/Ni) = −0,27 V, so Fe3+ is the stronger oxidising agent. In the spontaneous cell, Fe3+ is reduced to Fe2+ at the CATHODE, and Ni is oxidised to Ni2+ at the ANODE. That eliminates A (reversed direction), B (Ni2+ cannot be oxidised at the anode; it is Ni metal that is oxidised) and D (the nickel electrode is the anode, not the cathode). The Fe3+|Fe2+ half-cell contains only ions in solution — no solid conductor — so it needs an inert platinum electrode, and that platinum electrode is the cathode.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C
