## Question (2 marks)

The diagram below represents a cell that operates initially under standard conditions. (Diagram: a galvanic cell with a voltmeter V connecting an Aℓ electrode in Aℓ3+(aq) solution and a Zn electrode in Zn2+(aq) solution, joined by a salt bridge.) Define the term electrolyte.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A substance whose (aqueous) solution contains ions (2 or 0) / a substance that dissolves in water to give a solution that conducts electricity (through movement of ions) / a substance that dissociates to form ions in water / in molten state.
