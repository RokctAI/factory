## Question (2 marks)

A hot gas of a single element, with some atoms in an excited state, forms a spectrum of specific coloured lines on a black background (a line emission spectrum). Explain the presence of the coloured lines in the spectrum.

## Method

emission spectrum explanation

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Coloured lines represent (associated) frequencies/wavelengths/energy of the emitted (photons) when atoms/electrons move to a lower energy level.
