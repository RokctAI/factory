## Question (3 marks)

Light of frequency 1,045 x 10¹⁵ Hz is incident on different metals. The maximum kinetic energy of the emitted electrons is 2,53 x 10⁻¹⁹ J for sodium and 3,50 x 10⁻¹⁹ J for caesium. How does the work function of sodium compare to that of caesium? Choose from SMALLER THAN, GREATER THAN or EQUAL TO. Explain the answer.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Greater than. The maximum kinetic energy of electrons from sodium is smaller; E is constant / the same frequency (light) is used, so since Ek(max) is smaller, W0 is larger.
