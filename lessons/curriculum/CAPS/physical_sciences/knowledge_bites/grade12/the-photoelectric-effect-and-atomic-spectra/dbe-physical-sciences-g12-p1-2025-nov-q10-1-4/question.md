## Question (2 marks)

When light of frequency 1,045 x 10¹⁵ Hz is incident on metal M, no electrons are emitted. Light of the same frequency, but with a higher intensity, is now incident on metal M. Will electrons now be ejected? Choose from YES or NO. Give a reason for the answer.

## Method

photoelectric intensity reasoning

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

No. The intensity of light does not have any effect on the ejection of electrons for metal M OR the frequency of light has not changed / the energy of a photon has not changed.
