## Question (1 mark)

Red light, blue light and green light are shone simultaneously on a sheet of potassium. Each colour of light consists of a single frequency. Two maximum kinetic energies of the ejected electrons are possible, namely 6,96 x 10⁻²⁰ J and 2,65 x 10⁻²⁰ J. Each ejected electron has only one of these maximum kinetic energies (diagram: red, blue and green light incident on a potassium sheet). Which colour of light is responsible for ejecting electrons that have a maximum kinetic energy equal to 2,65 x 10⁻²⁰ J?

## Method

photoelectric frequency reasoning

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Green
