## Question (2 marks)

The energy levels of a certain atom are E1 = 1,3 x 10⁻¹⁹ J, E2 = 5,1 x 10⁻¹⁹ J and E3 = 5,7 x 10⁻¹⁹ J. Is it possible for a photon with an energy of 2,5 x 10⁻¹⁹ J to be emitted from this atom? Choose from YES or NO. Give a reason for the answer.

## Method

energy level transition reasoning

## Memo working

The possible downward transitions give photon energies E3 − E1 = 4,4 x 10⁻¹⁹ J, E2 − E1 = 3,8 x 10⁻¹⁹ J and E3 − E2 = 0,6 x 10⁻¹⁹ J. None of these equals 2,5 x 10⁻¹⁹ J.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

No. The energy does not correspond to any transition in this atom.
