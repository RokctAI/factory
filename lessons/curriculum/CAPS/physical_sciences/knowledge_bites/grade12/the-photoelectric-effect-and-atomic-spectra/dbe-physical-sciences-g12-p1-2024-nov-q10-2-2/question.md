## Question (2 marks)

Red light, blue light and green light, each of a single frequency, are shone simultaneously on a sheet of potassium. Only two maximum kinetic energies of ejected electrons are observed: 6,96 x 10⁻²⁰ J and 2,65 x 10⁻²⁰ J. In Question 10.2.1 it was found that GREEN light ejects the electrons with maximum kinetic energy 2,65 x 10⁻²⁰ J. Explain this answer.

## Method

photoelectric explanation

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Only green and blue light eject electrons / red light does not eject electrons. Green has a lower frequency than blue / green has longer wavelength than blue / photons of blue light have more energy than photons of green light, and therefore green ejects electrons with the lower kinetic energy. (fgreen < fblue / blue has a higher frequency than green.)
