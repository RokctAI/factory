## Question (2 marks)

Define the term photoelectric effect.

## Method

define photoelectric effect

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The process whereby electrons are ejected from a metal surface when light (of suitable frequency) is incident/shining on that surface. (Memo instruction: if any of the underlined key words/phrases in the correct context is omitted, deduct 1 mark.)
