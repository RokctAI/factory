## Question (4 marks)

An energy diagram (NOT drawn to scale) shows the electron energies in different energy levels, E1, E2 and E3, of a certain atom: E1 = 1,3 x 10⁻¹⁹ J; E2 = 5,1 x 10⁻¹⁹ J; E3 = 5,7 x 10⁻¹⁹ J, with energy increasing upwards. An electron of this atom moves from E3 to E1. Photon X is emitted. Calculate the frequency of photon X.

## Method

photon energy frequency

## Memo working

The energy of the emitted photon equals the energy difference between the two levels: E3 − E1 = 5,7 x 10⁻¹⁹ − 1,3 x 10⁻¹⁹ = 4,4 x 10⁻¹⁹ J.

Set this equal to the photon energy E = hf: 4,4 x 10⁻¹⁹ = (6,63 x 10⁻³⁴)f, so f = 6,64 x 10¹⁴ Hz. (Memo: accept E3 − E1 or E1 − E3 in the substitution; f must be positive.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

f = 6,64 x 10¹⁴ Hz
