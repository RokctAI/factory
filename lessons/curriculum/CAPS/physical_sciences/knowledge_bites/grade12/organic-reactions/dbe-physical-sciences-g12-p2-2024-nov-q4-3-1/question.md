## Question (3 marks)

Write down the STRUCTURAL FORMULA of compound W. (From the flow diagram: cracking reaction I gives W and T, where T is C4H8; reaction II converts W into CH3CH2CH2CH2Br.)

## Method

structural formula

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Work backwards from reaction II: it turns W into CH3CH2CH2CH2Br, a haloalkane made by substituting ONE hydrogen of an alkane with bromine. Undo the substitution — put the H back in place of the Br — and W must be the straight-chain four-carbon alkane butane, C4H10 (CH3CH2CH2CH3). Draw it in full, showing every C–H bond on the four-carbon chain.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

[Structural formula of butane: a four-carbon chain CH3CH2CH2CH3 drawn in full showing all H atoms.] Marking criteria: correct stem, i.e. 4 C atoms; whole structural formula correct.
