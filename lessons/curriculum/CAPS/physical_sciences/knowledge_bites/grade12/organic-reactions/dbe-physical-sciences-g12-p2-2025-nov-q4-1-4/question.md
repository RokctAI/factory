## Question (1 mark)

Study the three organic reactions I, II and III below. Compounds T and W are organic compounds. R and S are inorganic substances. Consider only the major products. (Reaction scheme: I — HBr(g) + T → W; II — W + NaOH(aq) → CH3CH(OH)CH2CH3 + R; III — CH3CH(OH)CH2CH3 → (with S above the arrow) T + H2O(ℓ).) Write down the NAME or FORMULA of S.

## Method

identify catalyst

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Reaction III converts the alcohol butan-2-ol into the alkene T plus water — a dehydration (elimination of water). Dehydration of an alcohol requires an acid catalyst written above the arrow: concentrated sulphuric acid (H2SO4) or phosphoric acid (H3PO4). That catalyst is S.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

(Concentrated) sulphuric acid / H2SO4 / phosphoric acid / H3PO4
