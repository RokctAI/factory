## Question (2 marks)

Study the flow diagram. Reaction I is a CRACKING REACTION forming two organic compounds, W and T, as the ONLY products. (Flow diagram: organic compound U goes through Reaction I to give organic compound W plus organic compound T, which is C4H8. W goes through Reaction II to give CH3CH2CH2CH2Br. CH3CH2CH2CH2Br goes through Reaction III, an elimination, to give organic compound R. Reaction IV links compound R and organic compound S; Reaction V links compound T and organic compound S.) Define the term cracking reaction.

## Method

define cracking

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) A definition question: the two marks sit on the two key ideas — starting from longer chain hydrocarbon/alkane molecules, and breaking them down into shorter (more useful) molecules. Both ideas must appear in the correct context. (Memo instruction: if any of the underlined key phrases in the correct context is omitted, deduct 1 mark.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The chemical process/reaction in which longer chain hydrocarbon/alkane molecules are broken down to shorter (more useful) molecules.
