## Question (1 mark)

Compound E, C3H8(g), reacts with oxygen, O2(g), according to the balanced equation: C3H8(g) + 5O2(g) → 3CO2(g) + 4H2O(g). Initially 8 cm³ of compound E and 50 cm³ of oxygen were injected into a container of adjustable volume and allowed to react. [Diagram: sealed syringe-like container holding the reaction mixture.] 2.3.1 Write down the name for this type of reaction.

## Method

identify reaction type

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) A hydrocarbon reacting with oxygen to form carbon dioxide and water is the classic signature of combustion, which is an oxidation reaction. Either name earns the mark.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Combustion/oxidation
