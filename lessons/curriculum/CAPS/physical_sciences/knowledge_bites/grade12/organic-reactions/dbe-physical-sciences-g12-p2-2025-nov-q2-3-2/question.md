## Question (5 marks)

Calculate the TOTAL volume of the GASES present in the container at the end of the reaction. (Refers to QUESTION 2.3: C3H8(g) + 5O2(g) → 3CO2(g) + 4H2O(g); initially 8 cm³ of C3H8 and 50 cm³ of O2 were injected into a container of adjustable volume.)

## Method

gas volume stoichiometry

## Memo working

V(O2) used = 5V(C3H8) = (5)(8) = 40 cm³. V(CO2) formed = 3V(C3H8) = (3)(8) = 24 cm³. V(H2O) formed = 4V(C3H8) = (4)(8) = 32 cm³. V(O2) unused = 50 − 40 = 10 cm³. Total volume of gas in the container = 10 + 24 + 32 = 66 cm³. Step by step: because all species are gases at the same temperature and pressure, the mole ratio in the balanced equation is also the VOLUME ratio. The 8 cm³ of propane needs five times its volume of oxygen, 40 cm³, so oxygen is in excess and propane is the limiting reagent — all 8 cm³ of propane reacts. The products are three volumes of CO2 (24 cm³) and four volumes of water VAPOUR (32 cm³) per volume of propane. Do not forget the leftover oxygen: 50 − 40 = 10 cm³ remains. Adding the three gases still present gives 10 + 24 + 32 = 66 cm³. Marking criteria: (a) volume O2 and C3H8 used; (b) volume CO2 and H2O produced; (c) volume O2 remaining; (d) addition of the three volumes; (e) correct final answer 66 cm³ or 0,066 dm³. (Mole-based calculations using Vm = 22,4 dm³ also accepted.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Total volume of gases = 66 cm³ (or 0,066 dm³)
