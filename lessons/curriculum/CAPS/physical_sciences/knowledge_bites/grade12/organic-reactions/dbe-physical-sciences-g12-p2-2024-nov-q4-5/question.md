## Question (1 mark)

Write down the TYPE of elimination in reaction III. (From the flow diagram: reaction III is an elimination converting CH3CH2CH2CH2Br into organic compound R.)

## Method

identify elimination type

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Reaction III removes H and Br from adjacent carbon atoms of the haloalkane CH3CH2CH2CH2Br, releasing HBr and leaving a double bond. Eliminating a hydrogen halide from a haloalkane is called dehydrohalogenation — or, since the halogen here is bromine, dehydrobromination.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Dehydrohalogenation / Dehydrobromination
