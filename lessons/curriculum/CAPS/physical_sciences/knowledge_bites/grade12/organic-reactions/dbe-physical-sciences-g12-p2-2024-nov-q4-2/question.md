## Question (2 marks)

Is the product in reaction II a PRIMARY, SECONDARY or TERTIARY haloalkane? Give a reason for the answer. (From the flow diagram: reaction II converts compound W into CH3CH2CH2CH2Br.)

## Method

haloalkane classification

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) The product of reaction II is CH3CH2CH2CH2Br, 1-bromobutane. Classification depends on the carbon carrying the halogen: count how many other carbon atoms are bonded to it. Here the bromine sits on the end (terminal) carbon of the chain, and that carbon is bonded to only ONE other carbon atom — so the haloalkane is primary.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Primary. The halogen/bromine/functional group (-X) is bonded to a C atom that is bonded to one other C atom. Accept: the Br/bromine (atom) is bonded to the first/last/terminal C atom.
