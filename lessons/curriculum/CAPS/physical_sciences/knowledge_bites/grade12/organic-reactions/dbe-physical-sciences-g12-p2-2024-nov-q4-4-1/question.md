## Question (1 mark)

For reaction II, write down the NAME or FORMULA of the inorganic reactant. (From the flow diagram: reaction II converts compound W, butane, into CH3CH2CH2CH2Br.)

## Method

halogenation reagent

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Reaction II puts a bromine atom onto an alkane. The inorganic reactant that supplies the bromine in a substitution (halogenation) of an alkane is molecular bromine, Br2 — one Br replaces an H on the chain and the other leaves as HBr.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Br2 / Bromine
