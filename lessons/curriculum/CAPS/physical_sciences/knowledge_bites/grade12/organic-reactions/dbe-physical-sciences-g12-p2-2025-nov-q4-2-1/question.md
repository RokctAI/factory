## Question (2 marks)

A compound with the formula C10H22 undergoes a cracking reaction according to the equation: C10H22(ℓ) → 2X(g) + C4H10(g). The mixture of the two products is bubbled in bromine water, Br2(aq), in a darkened room. Define the term cracking.

## Method

define cracking

(see marking guidelines) (Memo instruction: if any one of the underlined key phrases in the correct context is omitted, deduct 1 mark.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The chemical process/reaction in which longer chain hydrocarbon/alkane molecules are broken down to shorter (more useful) molecules.
