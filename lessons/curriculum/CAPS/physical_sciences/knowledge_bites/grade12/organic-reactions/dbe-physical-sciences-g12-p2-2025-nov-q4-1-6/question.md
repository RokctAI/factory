## Question (1 mark)

Study the three organic reactions I, II and III below. Compounds T and W are organic compounds. R and S are inorganic substances. Consider only the major products. (Reaction scheme: I — HBr(g) + T → W; II — W + NaOH(aq) → CH3CH(OH)CH2CH3 + R; III — CH3CH(OH)CH2CH3 → (with S above the arrow) T + H2O(ℓ).) Compound W can be converted to compound T in one step. State, besides heat, ONE other reaction condition for this conversion.

## Method

elimination reaction conditions

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Converting the haloalkane W (2-bromobutane) directly to the alkene T (but-2-ene) is an elimination — dehydrohalogenation. The conditions that favour elimination over substitution are a CONCENTRATED strong base (such as concentrated NaOH or KOH, typically in ethanol) together with heat. Since heat is already given, the required condition is the concentrated strong base.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Concentrated strong base / concentrated NaOH/KOH/LiOH
