## Question (1 mark)

Write down the MOLECULAR formula of compound U. (From the flow diagram: cracking reaction I breaks U into W and T as the ONLY products; W is butane, C4H10, from QUESTION 4.3.1, and T is C4H8.)

## Method

molecular formula

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) In a cracking reaction no atoms are lost — the original molecule simply splits. Since W (C4H10) and T (C4H8) are the ONLY products, add the atoms back together: carbons 4 + 4 = 8 and hydrogens 10 + 8 = 18, so U is C8H18 (octane).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C8H18 (POSITIVE MARKING FROM QUESTION 4.3.1)
