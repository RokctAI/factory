## Question (1 mark)

For reaction II, write down ONE reaction condition. (From the flow diagram: reaction II is the substitution of compound W, butane, with bromine to give CH3CH2CH2CH2Br.)

## Method

reaction conditions

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Halogenation of an alkane is a substitution that only proceeds when the halogen molecules are split into reactive atoms. That requires an energy input: ultraviolet light, sunlight or heat. Any ONE of these conditions earns the mark.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

UV / (Sun)light / Heat
