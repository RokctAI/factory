## Question (2 marks)

A compound with the formula C10H22 undergoes a cracking reaction according to the equation: C10H22(ℓ) → 2X(g) + C4H10(g). Write down the STRUCTURAL FORMULA of X.

## Method

structural formula from equation

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Balance the atoms in C10H22 → 2X + C4H10. Carbons: 10 = 2×(carbons in X) + 4, so X has 3 carbons. Hydrogens: 22 = 2×(hydrogens in X) + 10, so X has 6 hydrogens. X is therefore C3H6, propene. Draw the full structural formula: a three-carbon chain CH2=CH-CH3 with the double bond between the first and second carbons, showing every bond to every hydrogen. (Marking criteria: (a) correct functional group; (b) whole structure correct.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Propene: full structural formula of a three-carbon chain CH2=CH-CH3 with the double bond between the first and second carbons.
