## Question (2 marks)

Study the three organic reactions I, II and III below. Compounds T and W are organic compounds. R and S are inorganic substances. Consider only the major products. (Reaction scheme: I — HBr(g) + T → W; II — W + NaOH(aq) → CH3CH(OH)CH2CH3 + R; III — CH3CH(OH)CH2CH3 → (with S above the arrow) T + H2O(ℓ).) Write down TWO names for the type of reaction in reaction I.

## Method

identify reaction type

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) In reaction I the alkene T (but-2-ene) reacts with HBr: the H and Br atoms add across the double bond, so the double bond becomes a single bond and one product forms. Adding atoms across a double bond is an addition reaction; because the molecule added is a hydrogen halide (HBr), it is specifically hydrohalogenation, or hydrobromination for bromine.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Addition; hydrohalogenation/hydrobromination
