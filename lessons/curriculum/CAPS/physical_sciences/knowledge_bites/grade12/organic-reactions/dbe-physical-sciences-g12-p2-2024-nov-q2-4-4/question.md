## Question (2 marks)

Write down the IUPAC name of compound X. (Refers to QUESTION 2.4.1: a carboxylic acid reacting with organic compound X, in the presence of a catalyst, to form the ester F, CH3COO(CH2)2CH3.)

## Method

iupac naming

## Memo working

Marking criteria: correct chain length and functional group, i.e. propanol; everything else correct: IUPAC name completely correct including numbering. Working: compound F is the ester CH3COO(CH2)2CH3 (propyl ethanoate). The acid part supplies the CH3CO– group (ethanoic acid), so the other reactant X must be the alcohol that supplies the three-carbon propyl group with its –OH on the end carbon: propan-1-ol.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Propan-1-ol / 1-propanol (memo note: 'Propanol' gets the first criterion mark only)
