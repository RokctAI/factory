## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: The organic compound C2H4 is converted to an alkane as shown by the reaction below: C2H4 → Alkane. Which ONE of the following is a suitable catalyst for this reaction? A Lead B Platinum C Hydrogen D Iron

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) C2H4 is ethene, an alkene. Converting an alkene to an alkane means adding hydrogen across the double bond — hydrogenation. Hydrogenation needs H2 as the reactant plus a metal catalyst, and the catalyst used at this level is platinum (palladium or nickel also work). Option C is a trap: hydrogen is consumed in the reaction, so it is a reactant, not a catalyst. Lead and iron are not hydrogenation catalysts.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

B
