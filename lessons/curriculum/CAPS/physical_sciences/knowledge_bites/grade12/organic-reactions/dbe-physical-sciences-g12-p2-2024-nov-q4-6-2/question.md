## Question (3 marks)

Write down the balanced equation for reaction V, using STRUCTURAL formulae and the correct reagent shown above. (Reagents available: Br2, H2SO4(conc.), NaOH(conc.), HBr, H2. From the flow diagram: reaction V links compound T, the C4H8 positional isomer of R, and organic compound S, the bromobutane formed in reaction IV.)

## Method

elimination reaction equation

## Memo working

Marking criteria: NaOH; whole structural formula of alkene correct (functional group on second/first C atom); NaBr + H2O. If condensed, semi-structural or molecular formula: max 1/5. For extra product or reactant, deduct 1 mark. Working: reaction V must turn the haloalkane S into the alkene T, so it is an elimination (dehydrohalogenation) — the reagent for that from the list is concentrated NaOH. Taking S as 2-bromobutane, elimination of HBr gives but-2-ene, CH3–CH=CH–CH3 (T must be the positional isomer of R, but-1-ene), plus NaBr and H2O as the inorganic products. (Memo alternative: with S drawn as the terminal bromide, the alkene product is but-1-ene.) Draw the haloalkane and alkene in full structural formulae.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

[Balanced equation in structural formulae: 2-bromobutane (CH3–CHBr–CH2–CH3 drawn in full) + NaOH → but-2-ene (CH3–CH=CH–CH3 drawn in full) + NaBr + H2O; memo alternative shows the haloalkane with Br on the terminal C giving but-1-ene.]
