## Question (2 marks)

Write down the STRUCTURAL FORMULA of compound F. (F: CH3COO(CH2)2CH3.)

## Method

structural formula

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Compound F, CH3COO(CH2)2CH3, is the ester propyl ethanoate: the ethanoate (acid) part CH3–C(=O)– is joined through the ester oxygen to the propyl (alcohol) part –CH2–CH2–CH3. Draw the full structural formula showing every C–H bond, the C=O double bond and the ester C–O–C linkage.
