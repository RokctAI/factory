## Question (2 marks)

Write down the IUPAC name of compound T. (From the flow diagram: T is the C4H8 product of cracking reaction I, and compounds R and T are positional isomers.)

## Method

iupac naming

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) T has molecular formula C4H8, an alkene with a four-carbon chain, so the stem is butene. R and T are positional isomers — same chain, same functional group, different position of the double bond. Since R is but-1-ene (from the elimination in reaction III), T is but-2-ene. The memo applies positive marking from the earlier answers, so but-2-ene or but-1-ene is accepted, but the locant must be given: 'butene' alone loses a mark.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

But-2-ene / 2-butene / but-1-ene / 1-butene ('Butene' alone: deduct 1 mark)
