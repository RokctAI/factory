## Question (1 mark)

Write down the type of reaction. (Refers to QUESTION 2.4.1: a carboxylic acid reacting with organic compound X, in the presence of a catalyst, to form the ester F, CH3COO(CH2)2CH3.)

## Method

identify reaction type

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) A carboxylic acid and an alcohol combine to form an ester and water. This is esterification, which is a condensation reaction because a small molecule (water) is eliminated when the two reactants join.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Esterification / Condensation
