## Question (2 marks)

Study the three organic reactions I, II and III below. Compounds T and W are organic compounds. R and S are inorganic substances. Consider only the major products. (Reaction scheme: I — HBr(g) + T → W; II — W + NaOH(aq) → CH3CH(OH)CH2CH3 + R; III — CH3CH(OH)CH2CH3 → (with S above the arrow) T + H2O(ℓ).) Write down the STRUCTURAL formula of compound T.

## Method

structural formula alkene

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) T is the major product of the acid-catalysed dehydration of butan-2-ol in reaction III. By Zaitsev's rule the major alkene forms towards the more substituted carbon, giving but-2-ene rather than but-1-ene. Draw the full structural formula: a four-carbon chain CH3-CH=CH-CH3 with the double bond between the second and third carbons, showing every bond to every hydrogen. (Marking criteria: (a) correct functional group; (b) whole structure correct. More than one functional group / wrong functional group: 0/2. Correct condensed formula: max 1/2.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

But-2-ene: full structural formula of a four-carbon chain CH3-CH=CH-CH3 with the double bond between the second and third carbons.
