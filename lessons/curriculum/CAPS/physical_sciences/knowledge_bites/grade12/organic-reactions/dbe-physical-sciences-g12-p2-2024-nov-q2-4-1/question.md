## Question (1 mark)

Compound F [CH3COO(CH2)2CH3] is formed when a carboxylic acid reacts with another organic compound, X, in the presence of a catalyst. Write down the NAME or FORMULA of the catalyst.

## Method

esterification catalyst

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Compound F is an ester, and esters are formed when a carboxylic acid reacts with an alcohol. This esterification reaction requires concentrated sulphuric acid as catalyst.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

H2SO4 / Sulphuric acid
