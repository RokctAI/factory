## Question (5 marks)

Compounds R and T are positional isomers. The inorganic reagents shown below are available for reactions IV and V: Br2, H2SO4(conc.), NaOH(conc.), HBr, H2. Write down the balanced equation for reaction IV, using STRUCTURAL FORMULAE and the correct inorganic reagent shown above. (From the flow diagram: reaction III, an elimination, converts CH3CH2CH2CH2Br into compound R; reaction IV links compound R and organic compound S.)

## Method

addition reaction equation

## Memo working

Marking criteria: functional group of alkene on first C atom; whole structural formula of alkene correct; HBr; functional group of haloalkane correct; whole structural formula of haloalkane correct (halogen on second/first C atom). If condensed, semi-structural or molecular formula: max 1/5. For extra product or reactant, deduct 1 mark. Working: reaction III eliminates HBr from 1-bromobutane, so R is the alkene but-1-ene, CH2=CH–CH2–CH3. Reaction IV must turn the alkene R into the haloalkane S, which is an addition reaction — the only reagent on the list that adds across a double bond to give a bromoalkane is HBr. Adding HBr across the double bond of but-1-ene gives 2-bromobutane (Br on the second carbon, the major product) or 1-bromobutane (Br on the first carbon); the memo accepts either. Both reactant and product must be drawn in full structural formulae showing every bond.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

[Balanced equation in structural formulae: but-1-ene (CH2=CH–CH2–CH3 drawn in full) + HBr → 2-bromobutane (CH3–CHBr–CH2–CH3 drawn in full) OR 1-bromobutane (CH3CH2CH2CH2Br drawn in full).]
