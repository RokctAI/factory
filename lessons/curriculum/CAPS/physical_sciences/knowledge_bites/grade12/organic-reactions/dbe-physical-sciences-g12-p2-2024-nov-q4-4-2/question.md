## Question (1 mark)

For reaction II, write down the type of reaction. (Choose from SUBSTITUTION, ADDITION or ELIMINATION.) (From the flow diagram: reaction II converts compound W, butane, into CH3CH2CH2CH2Br.)

## Method

identify reaction type

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Compound W is butane, a saturated alkane — there is no double bond to add across, so addition is out. In reaction II a hydrogen atom on the chain is replaced by a bromine atom, which is the definition of a substitution reaction (halogenation of an alkane).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Substitution
