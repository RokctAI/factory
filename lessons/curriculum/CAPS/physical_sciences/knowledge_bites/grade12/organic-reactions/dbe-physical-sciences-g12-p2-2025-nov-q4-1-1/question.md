## Question (2 marks)

Study the three organic reactions I, II and III below. Compounds T and W are organic compounds. R and S are inorganic substances. Consider only the major products. (Reaction scheme: I — HBr(g) + T → W; II — W + NaOH(aq) → CH3CH(OH)CH2CH3 + R; III — CH3CH(OH)CH2CH3 → (with S above the arrow) T + H2O(ℓ).) Write down the IUPAC name of compound W.

## Method

reaction scheme iupac naming

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Work backwards from the compound that is named in the scheme: CH3CH(OH)CH2CH3 is butan-2-ol. Reaction III is the acid-catalysed dehydration of butan-2-ol, and the major product (Zaitsev's rule) is but-2-ene, so T is but-2-ene. Reaction I adds HBr across the double bond of but-2-ene; the bromine attaches at carbon 2, giving W as 2-bromobutane. This is consistent with reaction II, in which W undergoes hydrolysis with NaOH(aq) back to butan-2-ol.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

2-bromobutane
