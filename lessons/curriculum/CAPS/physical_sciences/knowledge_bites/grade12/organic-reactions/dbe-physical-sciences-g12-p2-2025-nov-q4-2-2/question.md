## Question (1 mark)

A compound with the formula C10H22 undergoes a cracking reaction according to the equation: C10H22(ℓ) → 2X(g) + C4H10(g). The mixture of the two products is bubbled in bromine water, Br2(aq), in a darkened room. State ONE change, besides a change in temperature, that will be observed when the mixture is bubbled in Br2(aq).

## Method

bromine water test observation

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) One of the cracking products, X, is an alkene (balancing the equation gives X = C3H6, propene). An alkene reacts with bromine water by addition even in the dark, using up the red-brown Br2, so the observable change is that the bromine water loses its colour.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Decolourisation / colour fades / becomes lighter in colour / colourless
