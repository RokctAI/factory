## Question (1 mark)

Study the three organic reactions I, II and III below. Compounds T and W are organic compounds. R and S are inorganic substances. Consider only the major products. (Reaction scheme: I — HBr(g) + T → W; II — W + NaOH(aq) → CH3CH(OH)CH2CH3 + R; III — CH3CH(OH)CH2CH3 → (with S above the arrow) T + H2O(ℓ).) Write down the NAME or FORMULA of R.

## Method

reaction scheme identify product

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) In reaction II the haloalkane W (2-bromobutane) is hydrolysed by dilute NaOH(aq): the OH group replaces the Br atom to form butan-2-ol. The displaced bromine leaves with the sodium as the inorganic salt sodium bromide, so R is NaBr.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

NaBr / sodium bromide
