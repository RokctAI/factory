## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: The following reaction reaches equilibrium in a closed container: H2(g) + S(s) ⇌ H2S(g), ΔH < 0. The reaction rate versus time graph for the reaction is given below. [Graph: two reaction-rate curves at equilibrium (equal, constant); at time t1 both rates jump vertically to higher values, then converge to a new common constant rate.] What change was made to the system at time t1? A The pressure was increased at a constant temperature. B The volume of the container was increased. C The temperature was increased. D More hydrogen was added to the system.

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) At t1 BOTH the forward and the reverse rate jump upward instantaneously. Increasing the pressure at constant temperature (by decreasing the volume) raises the concentrations of both gases, H2 and H2S, at the same instant, so both rates jump — and because there is one mole of gas on each side (the sulfur is a solid), the equilibrium position is unaffected and the rates settle at a new, higher common constant value. Test the distractors: increasing the volume (B) would make both rates drop, not jump; increasing the temperature (C) changes the rates through the rate constants, not through a sudden concentration jump, and for this exothermic reaction would shift the equilibrium; adding hydrogen (D) would make only the forward rate jump at t1.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A
