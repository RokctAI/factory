## Question (3 marks)

Explain the answer to QUESTION 6.2.1 by using Le Chatelier's principle. (Context: for the decomposition NH4HS(s) ⇌ NH3(g) + H2S(g), Kc increases with temperature — 7,5 x 10⁻² at 200 °C, 18 x 10⁻² at 250 °C, 40 x 10⁻² at 300 °C; in Q6.2.1 the decomposition was identified as ENDOTHERMIC.)

## Method

le chatelier kc explanation

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

With an increase in the temperature the Kc value increases; the concentration of the products increases (OR concentration of reactants decreases OR the forward reaction is favoured); (according to Le Chatelier's principle) an increase in temperature favours the endothermic reaction.
