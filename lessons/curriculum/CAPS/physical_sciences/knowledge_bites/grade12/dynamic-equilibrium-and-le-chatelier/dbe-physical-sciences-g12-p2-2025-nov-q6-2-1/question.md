## Question (1 mark)

Initially 70 g of NH4HS(s) is placed in a 3 dm³ container at 250 °C. The container is sealed and the reaction is allowed to reach equilibrium according to the balanced equation: NH4HS(s) ⇌ NH3(g) + H2S(g). The Kc values for the decomposition of NH4HS(s) at different temperatures are given (table: Kc = 7,5 x 10⁻² at 200 °C; Kc = 18 x 10⁻² at 250 °C; Kc = 40 x 10⁻² at 300 °C). Is the decomposition of NH4HS(s) an EXOTHERMIC or ENDOTHERMIC reaction?
