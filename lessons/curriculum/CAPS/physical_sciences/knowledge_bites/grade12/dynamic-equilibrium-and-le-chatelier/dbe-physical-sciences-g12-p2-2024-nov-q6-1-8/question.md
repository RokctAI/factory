## Question (3 marks)

The reaction of carbon monoxide gas, CO(g), with oxygen gas, O2(g), reaches equilibrium in a closed container at constant temperature T °C, according to the balanced equation: 2CO(g) + O2(g) ⇌ 2CO2(g), ΔH < 0. (Graph: concentration in mol·dm⁻³ versus time in minutes for three gases labelled X, Y and Z, with disturbances at times t1, t2 and t3.)

Use Le Chatelier's principle to explain the answer to QUESTION 6.1.7. (In QUESTION 6.1.7, the temperature change made at t3 was DECREASED.)

## Method

le chatelier explanation

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Concentration of products/Y/CO2 increases (OR concentration of reactants/Z/X/CO/O2 decreases OR the forward reaction is favoured). The forward reaction is exothermic. A decrease in temperature favours the exothermic reaction.
