## Question (1 mark)

A few drops of concentrated hydrochloric acid, HCℓ(conc.), are added to the equilibrium mixture. What effect does this addition have on the mass of Ca(OH)2(s)? Choose from INCREASES, DECREASES or REMAINS THE SAME. (Context: the equilibrium Ca(OH)2(s) ⇌ Ca2+(aq) + 2OH⁻(aq) is established at 25 °C in a saturated calcium hydroxide solution.)

## Method

equilibrium shift prediction

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

(Mass) decreases.
