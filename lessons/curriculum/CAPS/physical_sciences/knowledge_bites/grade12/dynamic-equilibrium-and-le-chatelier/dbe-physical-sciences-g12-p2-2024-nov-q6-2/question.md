## Question (9 marks)

Carbon monoxide gas, CO(g), reacts with water vapour, H2O(g), at T °C. The reaction reaches chemical equilibrium according to the balanced equation: CO(g) + H2O(g) ⇌ CO2(g) + H2(g). Initially, 0,6 moles of CO(g), 0,6 moles of H2O(g), 0,1 moles of carbon dioxide gas, CO2(g), and 0,1 moles of hydrogen gas, H2(g), were mixed and sealed in a 2 dm³ flask. If the equilibrium constant, Kc, for this reaction at T °C is 4, calculate the mass of CO(g) present in the flask at equilibrium.

**Note: the official memo works this question by two routes that round differently — a mole-based ICE table (x = 0,37 mol, giving m = 6,44 g) and a concentration-based ICE table (x = 0,18 mol·dm⁻³, giving m = 6,72 g). Any final answer in the accepted range 6,44 – 6,72 g earns the marks.**

## Method

kc ice table calculation

## Memo working

ICE table (moles): initial CO 0,6; H2O 0,6; CO2 0,1; H2 0,1. Change: –x, –x, +x, +x (mole ratio 1:1:1:1). Equilibrium: 0,6–x; 0,6–x; 0,1+x; 0,1+x; divide each by the 2 dm³ volume for concentrations. Substitute into Kc = [CO2][H2]/[CO][H2O]: 4 = ((0,1+x)/2)²/((0,6–x)/2)², so x = 0,37. Then n(CO)eq = 0,6 – 0,37 = 0,23 mol, and from n = m/M: 0,23 = m/28, so m(CO)eq = 6,44 g. (Memo also gives full concentration-based variants: initial concentrations 0,3/0,3/0,05/0,05 mol·dm⁻³ give x = 0,18, [CO]eq = 0,12 mol·dm⁻³, n(CO)eq = 0,24 mol, m = 6,72 g; the range 6,44 – 6,72 g covers rounding.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

m(CO)eq = 6,44 g (accepted range 6,44 – 6,72 g)
