## Question (1 mark)

The reaction of carbon monoxide gas, CO(g), with oxygen gas, O2(g), reaches equilibrium in a closed container at constant temperature T °C, according to the balanced equation: 2CO(g) + O2(g) ⇌ 2CO2(g), ΔH < 0. (Graph: concentration in mol·dm⁻³ versus time in minutes for three gases labelled X, Y and Z, with disturbances at times t1, t2 and t3; at t1, oxygen gas was added — the letter X represents O2(g).)

Give a reason for the answer to QUESTION 6.1.5. (In QUESTION 6.1.5, the gas represented by the letter Z was identified as CO(g) / carbon monoxide.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The concentration of Z (CO) decreased with a decrease in the concentration of X (O2). OR The concentration of Z (CO) increased with an increase in the concentration of X (O2). OR Z (CO) behaves like X (O2) / follows the same trend as X (O2). OR Z (CO) and X (O2) are both reactants / Y (CO2) is the product. OR The reverse reaction is favoured to increase the number of moles.
