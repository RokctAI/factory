## Question (2 marks)

Equilibrium is established at 25 °C in a saturated calcium hydroxide solution according to the reaction: Ca(OH)2(s) ⇌ Ca2+(aq) + 2OH⁻(aq). State Le Chatelier's principle.

## Method

state le chatelier

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

When the equilibrium in a closed system is disturbed, the system will re-instate a new equilibrium by favouring the reaction that will cancel/oppose the disturbance.
