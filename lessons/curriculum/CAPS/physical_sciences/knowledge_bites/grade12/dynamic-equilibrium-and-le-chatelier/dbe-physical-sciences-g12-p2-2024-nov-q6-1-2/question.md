## Question (1 mark)

Use the graph to answer: At t1, oxygen, O2(g), was added to the container. Write down the letter that represents O2(g). Choose from X, Y or Z. (Context: 2CO(g) + O2(g) ⇌ 2CO2(g), ΔH < 0 at equilibrium in a closed container; the graph shows concentration versus time for gases X, Y and Z with disturbances at t1, t2 and t3.)

## Method

equilibrium graph interpretation

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Adding a gas to the container makes its concentration jump vertically at the moment of addition, before the system gradually adjusts back towards a new equilibrium. Only curve X shows that sudden upward step at t1, so X is the oxygen.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

X
