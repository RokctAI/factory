## Question (2 marks)

The reaction of carbon monoxide gas, CO(g), with oxygen gas, O2(g), is investigated. The reaction reaches equilibrium in a closed container at constant temperature T °C, according to the balanced equation: 2CO(g) + O2(g) ⇌ 2CO2(g), ΔH < 0. Changes to the conditions of equilibrium are made at different times. (Graph: concentration in mol·dm⁻³ versus time in minutes for three gases labelled X, Y and Z, with disturbances at times t1, t2 and t3; X has the highest concentration, then Y, then Z.) Define the term chemical equilibrium.

## Method

define chemical equilibrium

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) A definition question marked 2 or 0: the first accepted formulation hinges on the two rates being equal — the forward and reverse reactions both still run (it is dynamic), but at the same rate. The alternative formulation describes the observable consequence: the concentrations of reactants and products remain constant.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

(The dynamic equilibrium when) the rate of the forward reaction equals the rate of the reverse reaction. (2 or 0) OR The stage in a chemical reaction when the concentrations of the reactants and products remain constant.
