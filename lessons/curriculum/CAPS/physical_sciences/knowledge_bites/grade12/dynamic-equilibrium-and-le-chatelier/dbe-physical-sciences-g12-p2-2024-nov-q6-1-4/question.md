## Question (1 mark)

Give a reason for the answer to QUESTION 6.1.3. (In QUESTION 6.1.3 the pressure change at t2 was identified as a DECREASE, made by changing the volume of the container. Context: 2CO(g) + O2(g) ⇌ 2CO2(g), ΔH < 0.)

## Method

equilibrium graph interpretation

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) The evidence is on the graph at t2: every curve steps down at once, so the concentrations of all the gases decreased — only an increase in volume (a decrease in pressure) does that to all species simultaneously. Consistent with this, the system then shifts towards the side with more moles of gas (the reverse reaction is favoured) to oppose the pressure decrease.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The concentrations of (all) the gases decreased. / The reverse reaction was favoured. Accept: all concentrations decreased.
