## Question (2 marks)

Explain the answer to QUESTION 6.1.2 by using Le Chatelier's principle. (Context: a few drops of concentrated HCℓ were added to the equilibrium Ca(OH)2(s) ⇌ Ca2+(aq) + 2OH⁻(aq) in a saturated calcium hydroxide solution at 25 °C; in Q6.1.2 the mass of Ca(OH)2(s) DECREASES.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The decrease in the amount/concentration of OH⁻ ions favours the reaction that increases the amount/concentration of OH⁻ ions (OR the acid/HCℓ/H+ reacts with the OH⁻ ions); the forward reaction is favoured (OR the amount/concentration of the products increases).
