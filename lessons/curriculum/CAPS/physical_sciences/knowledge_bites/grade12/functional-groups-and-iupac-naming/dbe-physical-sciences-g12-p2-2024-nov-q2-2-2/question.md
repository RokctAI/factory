## Question (3 marks)

Write down the IUPAC name of compound E. [E: CH3C(CH3)2CCCH3]

## Method

iupac naming

## Memo working

Marking criteria: correct stem, i.e. pentyne; substituent (dimethyl) correctly identified; IUPAC name completely correct including numbering, sequence, hyphens and commas. Working: the longest chain through the triple bond is five carbons, so the stem is pentyne. Number from the end nearest the triple bond so that the triple bond gets the lowest locant: the triple bond starts at carbon 2, and both methyl groups sit on carbon 4, giving 4,4-dimethylpent-2-yne.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

4,4-dimethylpent-2-yne / 4,4-dimethyl-2-pentyne
