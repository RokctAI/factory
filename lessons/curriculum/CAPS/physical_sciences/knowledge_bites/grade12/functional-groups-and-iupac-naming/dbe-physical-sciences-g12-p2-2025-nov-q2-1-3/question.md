## Question (1 mark)

Write down the LETTER(S) that represent(s) TWO compounds that belong to the same homologous series. (Refers to the table of compounds A to G in QUESTION 2.1: A 3-methylbutan-2-one, a ketone; B 2,6-dichloro-2,5-dimethylheptane; C CH3CH(CH3)CH2CHO, an aldehyde; D C4H10O, an alcohol; E C3H8; F pentane; G 3-ethyl-2-methylhex-2-ene.)

## Method

identify homologous series

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Compounds in the same homologous series share the same general formula and differ by a CH2 unit. Compound E is C3H8, which fits CnH2n+2 — propane, an alkane. Compound F is pentane, C5H12, also an alkane. Both are saturated hydrocarbons of the alkane series, two CH2 units apart. None of the other pairs share a series: A is a ketone, C an aldehyde, B a haloalkane, D an alcohol and G an alkene.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

E and F
