## Question (3 marks)

Write down the IUPAC name of compound A. [Structural formula: CH3-C(=O)-CH(CH3)-CH3.]

## Method

iupac naming

## Memo working

Marking criteria: correct stem, i.e. butanone; substituents (methyl) correctly identified; IUPAC name completely correct including numbering, sequence, hyphens and commas. Working: the longest chain through the carbonyl carbon is four carbons with the C=O inside the chain, so the compound is a ketone with stem butanone. Number from the end nearest the carbonyl so that the carbonyl carbon gets the lowest locant — position 2. The methyl branch then sits on carbon 3, giving 3-methylbutan-2-one.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

3-methylbutan-2-one / 3-methylbutanone (ACCEPT: 3-methyl-2-butanone / methylbutanone)
