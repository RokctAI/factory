## Question (3 marks)

Write down the IUPAC name of compound G. [Structural formula: CH3CH2CH2-C(CH2CH3)=C(CH3)-CH3 as drawn in the table.]

## Method

iupac naming

## Memo working

Marking criteria: correct stem, i.e. hexene; substituents (ethyl and methyl) correctly identified; IUPAC name completely correct including numbering, sequence, hyphens and commas. Working: choose the longest chain that runs THROUGH the double bond — taking the propyl side into the chain gives six carbons, so the stem is hexene. Number from the end that gives the double bond the lowest locant: starting at the methyl end, the double bond sits between carbons 2 and 3, so hex-2-ene. The methyl branch is on carbon 2 and the ethyl branch on carbon 3. Citing substituents alphabetically (ethyl before methyl) gives 3-ethyl-2-methylhex-2-ene.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

3-ethyl-2-methylhex-2-ene / 3-ethyl-2-methyl-2-hexene
