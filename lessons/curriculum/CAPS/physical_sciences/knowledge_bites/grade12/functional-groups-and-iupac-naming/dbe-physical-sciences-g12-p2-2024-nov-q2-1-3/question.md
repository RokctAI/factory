## Question (1 mark)

Write down the LETTER that represents an unsaturated compound. (Refers to the table of compounds A to H in QUESTION 2.1.1: A butanal; B 3,3-dibromo-4,4-dimethylhexane; C butanone; D C4H10O; E CH3C(CH3)2CCCH3; F CH3COO(CH2)2CH3; G C4H8O2; H CH3C(CH3)2CH2CH3.)

## Method

identify functional group

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) An unsaturated compound contains at least one multiple bond between carbon atoms. Compound E, CH3C(CH3)2CCCH3, contains a carbon-carbon triple bond (it is an alkyne), so it is unsaturated. Compound B has halogen substituents but only single carbon-carbon bonds, so it is saturated, as are the other compounds in the table.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

E
