## Question (3 marks)

Write down the IUPAC name of compound B. [Structural formula: CH3-C(Cℓ)(CH3)-CH2-CH2-CH(CH3)-CH(Cℓ)-CH3 as drawn in the table.]

## Method

iupac naming

## Memo working

Marking criteria: correct stem, i.e. heptane; substituents (dichloro and dimethyl) correctly identified; IUPAC name completely correct including numbering, sequence, hyphens and commas. Working: the longest chain is seven carbons, so the stem is heptane. There are two chlorine atoms and two methyl groups on the chain. Numbering from the end nearest the carbon that carries both a chlorine and a methyl group gives chlorines on carbons 2 and 6 and methyls on carbons 2 and 5 — locant set {2,2,5,6}. Numbering from the other end would give methyls on 3 and 6 — locant set {2,3,6,6}, which is higher at the first point of difference. So the correct name is 2,6-dichloro-2,5-dimethylheptane, with substituents cited alphabetically (chloro before methyl).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

2,6-dichloro-2,5-dimethylheptane
