## Question (1 mark)

Write down the LETTERS that represent these functional isomers. (Refers to the table of compounds A to H in QUESTION 2.1.1: A butanal; B 3,3-dibromo-4,4-dimethylhexane; C butanone; D C4H10O; E CH3C(CH3)2CCCH3; F CH3COO(CH2)2CH3; G C4H8O2; H CH3C(CH3)2CH2CH3.)

## Method

identify isomers

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Compound A, butanal (CH3CH2CH2CHO), and compound C, butanone, both have the molecular formula C4H8O, but A is an aldehyde while C is a ketone — the same molecular formula with different functional groups, which is exactly the definition of functional isomers.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A and C
