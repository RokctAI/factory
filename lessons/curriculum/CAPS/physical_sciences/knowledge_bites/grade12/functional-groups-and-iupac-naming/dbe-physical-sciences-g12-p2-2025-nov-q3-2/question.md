## Question (2 marks)

Identify the TWO homologous series to which these compounds belong. (Refers to QUESTION 3: compounds A and B share the molecular formula C4H8O2, are straight chain molecules, and belong to two different homologous series.)

## Method

identify homologous series

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) The molecular formula C4H8O2 fits the general formula CnH2nO2 — two oxygens and one degree of unsaturation from a C=O group. The two homologous series in the grade-12 syllabus that match this general formula are the carboxylic acids (for example butanoic acid, CH3CH2CH2COOH) and the esters (for example methyl propanoate or ethyl ethanoate). Since A and B belong to two DIFFERENT series with the same molecular formula, they must be the ester and the carboxylic acid — functional isomers of one another.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Ester and carboxylic acid
