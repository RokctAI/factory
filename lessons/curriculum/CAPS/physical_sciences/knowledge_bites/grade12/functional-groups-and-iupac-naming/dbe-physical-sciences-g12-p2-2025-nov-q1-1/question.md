## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: Which ONE of the following is the name of the functional group of propan-1-ol? A Carboxyl B Carbonyl C Hydroxyl D Formyl

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) The suffix -ol tells you propan-1-ol is an alcohol, and every alcohol is defined by the hydroxyl group, –OH, bonded to a saturated carbon. The distractors name other oxygen-containing groups: carboxyl (–COOH) belongs to carboxylic acids, carbonyl (C=O) to ketones and aldehydes, and formyl (–CHO) is the aldehyde end-group. Only hydroxyl matches an alcohol.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C
