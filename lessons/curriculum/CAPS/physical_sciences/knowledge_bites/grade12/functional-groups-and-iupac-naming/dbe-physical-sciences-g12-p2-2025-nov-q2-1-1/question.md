## Question (1 mark)

The letters A to G in the table below represent organic compounds: A [structural formula: a four-carbon chain CH3-C(=O)-CH(CH3)-CH3, i.e. carbon 1 is CH3, carbon 2 carries a double-bonded O, carbon 3 carries an H and a methyl branch drawn downward, carbon 4 is CH3]; B [structural formula: a seven-carbon (longest chain) molecule drawn as a six-carbon horizontal chain whose fifth carbon carries a two-carbon branch drawn upward; carbon by carbon along the longest chain: C1 = CH3, C2 carries a Cℓ (drawn below) and a methyl group (drawn above), C3 = CH2, C4 = CH2, C5 carries an H and a methyl group (the sixth carbon of the horizontal row), C6 = the upward branch carbon carrying a Cℓ (drawn left) and an H, C7 = CH3, i.e. 2,6-dichloro-2,5-dimethylheptane]; C CH3CH(CH3)CH2CHO; D C4H10O; E C3H8; F Pentane; G [structural formula drawn as a horizontal row CH3-CH2-C=C-CH3 in which the left doubly-bonded carbon also carries a propyl chain (CH2-CH2-CH3) drawn downward and the right doubly-bonded carbon also carries a methyl group drawn upward, i.e. CH3CH2-C(C3H7)=C(CH3)-CH3, 3-ethyl-2-methylhex-2-ene]. 2.1 Write down the LETTER(S) that represent(s) EACH of the following: 2.1.1 An alcohol

## Method

identify functional group

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) An alcohol carries the hydroxyl group, –OH, and a saturated alcohol fits the general formula CnH2n+2O. Compound D, C4H10O, matches with n = 4 — butanol. Check the others: A is a ketone (double-bonded O inside the chain), B is a haloalkane (chlorine atoms), C ends in –CHO so it is an aldehyde, E (C3H8) and F (pentane) are alkanes, and G contains a C=C double bond, so it is an alkene. Only D can be the alcohol.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

D
