## Question (1 mark)

The letters A to H in the table represent organic compounds: A [structural formula: CH3CH2CH2CHO, a four-carbon chain whose last carbon carries a double-bonded O and an H, i.e. butanal]; B [structural formula: a five-carbon horizontal chain, H3C–C(*)–CBr2–CH2–CH3, where the second carbon (*) carries an ethyl group (two vertical carbons) and a methyl group, and the third carbon carries two Br atoms, i.e. 3,3-dibromo-4,4-dimethylhexane]; C Butanone; D C4H10O; E CH3C(CH3)2CCCH3; F CH3COO(CH2)2CH3; G C4H8O2; H CH3C(CH3)2CH2CH3. Write down the LETTER that represents an alcohol.

## Method

identify functional group

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) An alcohol contains a hydroxyl (–OH) group and fits the saturated general formula CnH2n+2O. Compound D, C4H10O, matches with n = 4 (butanol). The other compounds belong to different homologous series: A is an aldehyde, B a haloalkane, C a ketone (butanone), E an alkyne, F an ester, G (C4H8O2) fits a carboxylic acid or ester, and H is an alkane.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

D
