## Question (4 marks)

Write down the STRUCTURAL FORMULAE of two STRAIGHT CHAIN positional isomers of compound D (C4H10O).

## Method

structural formulae positional isomers

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Compound D, C4H10O, is a four-carbon alcohol. Positional isomers keep the same molecular formula and the same functional group but move that group to a different position on the chain. On a STRAIGHT four-carbon chain the hydroxyl group can sit on carbon 1 or on carbon 2, giving butan-1-ol and butan-2-ol. Draw each as a full structural formula: every carbon shown with all four bonds, every hydrogen shown, and the O-H drawn out. Memo marking criteria per molecule: hydroxyl group on the correct C-atom; whole structure correct. If more than one functional group or a wrong functional group is drawn: 0/2 for that molecule. Condensed structural formulae: maximum 2/4. Bond between O and H not shown: accept.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Butan-1-ol: full structural formula of a four-carbon straight chain with the hydroxyl (OH) group on the first carbon, all other bonds to H. Butan-2-ol: full structural formula of a four-carbon straight chain with the hydroxyl (OH) group on the second carbon, all other bonds to H.
