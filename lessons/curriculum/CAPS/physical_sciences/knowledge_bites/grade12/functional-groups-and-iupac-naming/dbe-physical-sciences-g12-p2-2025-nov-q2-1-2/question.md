## Question (1 mark)

Write down the LETTER(S) that represent(s) TWO compounds that are functional isomers of one another. (Refers to the table of compounds A to G in QUESTION 2.1: A 3-methylbutan-2-one, a ketone; B 2,6-dichloro-2,5-dimethylheptane; C CH3CH(CH3)CH2CHO, an aldehyde; D C4H10O; E C3H8; F pentane; G 3-ethyl-2-methylhex-2-ene.)

## Method

identify functional isomers

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Functional isomers have the SAME molecular formula but belong to DIFFERENT homologous series. Count atoms: compound A, the ketone CH3-C(=O)-CH(CH3)-CH3, has molecular formula C5H10O; compound C, the aldehyde CH3CH(CH3)CH2CHO, also has molecular formula C5H10O. Same molecular formula, but one is a ketone and the other an aldehyde — two different homologous series — so A and C are functional isomers of one another.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A and C
