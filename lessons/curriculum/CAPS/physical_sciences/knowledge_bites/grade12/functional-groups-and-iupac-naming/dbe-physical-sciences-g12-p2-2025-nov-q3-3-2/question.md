## Question (2 marks)

Write down the IUPAC name for a possible compound B. (Refers to QUESTION 3: compounds A and B share the molecular formula C4H8O2 and belong to two different homologous series; from the vapour pressures, A is butanoic acid, so B — vapour pressure 9,7 kPa — is the ester.)

## Method

iupac naming

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Compound B is the ester with molecular formula C4H8O2. An ester's name has two parts: the alkyl group from the alcohol side, then the -oate stem from the acid side, with the total carbon count adding to four. The possibilities are methyl propanoate (1 + 3 carbons), ethyl ethanoate (2 + 2 carbons) and propyl methanoate (3 + 1 carbons). Any ONE of these earns the marks; the memo scores this 2 or 0, so the name must be completely correct.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Methyl propanoate / propyl methanoate / ethyl ethanoate (2 or 0)
