## Question (1 mark)

Write down the LETTER that represents a compound with a formyl group. (Refers to the table of compounds A to H in QUESTION 2.1.1: A butanal; B 3,3-dibromo-4,4-dimethylhexane; C butanone; D C4H10O; E CH3C(CH3)2CCCH3; F CH3COO(CH2)2CH3; G C4H8O2; H CH3C(CH3)2CH2CH3.)

## Method

identify functional group

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) The formyl group is the aldehyde group, –CHO: a carbon at the end of the chain carrying both a double-bonded oxygen and a hydrogen. Compound A, CH3CH2CH2CHO (butanal), ends in exactly this group, so A is the compound with a formyl group.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A
