## Question (3 marks)

Write down the IUPAC name of compound B. [Structural formula: CH3CH2–CBr2–C(CH3)2–CH2CH3 as drawn in the table.]

## Method

iupac naming

## Memo working

Marking criteria: correct stem, i.e. hexane; correct substituents (bromo and methyl) identified; IUPAC name completely correct including numbering, sequence, hyphens and commas. Working: the longest chain is six carbons, so the stem is hexane. Two bromine atoms sit on one carbon and two methyl groups on the neighbouring carbon. Numbering from either end gives the same locant set {3,3,4,4}; the tie is broken by giving the lowest locants to the substituent cited first alphabetically (bromo before methyl), so the bromines get position 3.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

3,3-dibromo-4,4-dimethylhexane
