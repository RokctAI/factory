## Question (1 mark)

Two bottles contain compounds A and B with the same molecular formula, C4H8O2. These compounds are straight chain organic molecules that belong to two different homologous series. [Diagram: two bottles labelled A (C4H8O2) and B (C4H8O2).] The boiling points are used to distinguish between the two compounds. 3.1 Define the term homologous series.

## Method

define homologous series

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) This is a bookwork definition — learn it word for word. A homologous series is a family of organic compounds sharing one general formula, with each member differing from the next by a single CH2 unit. Either accepted formulation below earns the mark, but a vague answer (for example "compounds with similar properties") earns nothing: the memo marks it 1 or 0.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A series of organic compounds that can be described by the same general formula. OR: A series of organic compounds in which one member differs from the next by a CH2 group. (1 or 0)
