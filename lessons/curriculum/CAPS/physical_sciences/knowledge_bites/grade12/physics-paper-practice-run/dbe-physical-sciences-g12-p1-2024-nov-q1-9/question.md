## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: The diagram shows a simplified electric motor. The rotation of the coil is observed from the battery. [Diagram: rectangular coil between magnetic poles N (left) and S (right), connected via a split-ring commutator to a battery.] Which ONE of the following statements is CORRECT while the motor is in operation? The coil and the ... A slip rings rotate anti-clockwise. B slip rings rotate clockwise. C commutator rotate clockwise. D commutator rotate anti-clockwise.

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) First eliminate by component: a DC motor uses a split-ring commutator, not slip rings — slip rings belong to AC machines. The diagram shows a split-ring commutator connected to a battery, so options A and B fall away immediately. That leaves the direction. Applying the motor rule (left-hand rule for conventional current) to the current direction and the field from the N pole on the left to the S pole on the right, the forces on the two sides of the coil in this diagram turn the coil anti-clockwise as seen from the battery. The coil and the commutator rotate together, anti-clockwise.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

D
