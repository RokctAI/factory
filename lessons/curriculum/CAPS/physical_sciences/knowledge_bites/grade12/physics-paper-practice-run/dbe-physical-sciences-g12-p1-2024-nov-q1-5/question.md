## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: A ball falling vertically downwards from point A strikes the ground with velocity v and bounces, reaching a maximum height at point B, as shown in the diagram. [Diagram: point A above point B; B is the lower maximum-rebound height above the ground.] Which ONE of the combinations below is CORRECT for the direction of the impulse on the ball upon striking the ground and the magnitude of the velocity with which the ball leaves the ground? A Upward; Greater than v B Downward; Greater than v C Upward; Less than v D Downward; Less than v

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Impulse equals the change in momentum. The ball arrives moving downwards and leaves moving upwards, so its momentum changes from downward to upward — the change in momentum, and therefore the impulse the ground exerts on the ball, points upward. For the speed: the ball rebounds only to point B, which is lower than the drop height A. A lower rebound height means less kinetic energy leaving the ground than arriving, so the ball leaves the ground with a speed less than v.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C
