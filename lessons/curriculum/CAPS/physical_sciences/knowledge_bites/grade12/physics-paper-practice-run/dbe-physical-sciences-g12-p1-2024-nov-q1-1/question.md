## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: Several forces are acting on a moving object. Which ONE of the following statements is CORRECT when these forces are in equilibrium? A The velocity of the object is increasing. B The object is moving at a constant velocity. C The kinetic energy of the object is decreasing. D The object has a non-zero acceleration.

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Forces in equilibrium mean the net force is zero. By Newton's first law, a zero net force on a moving object means zero acceleration, so the object keeps moving at a constant velocity. That rules out A (velocity increasing needs a net force), C (decreasing kinetic energy means the speed is changing, which needs a net force) and D (a non-zero acceleration needs a non-zero net force).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

B
