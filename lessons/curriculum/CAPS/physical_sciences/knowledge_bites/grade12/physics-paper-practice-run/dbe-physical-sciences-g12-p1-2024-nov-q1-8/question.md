## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: The kilowatt-hour (kWh) is a unit of ... A power. B electric current. C electrical energy. D potential difference.

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) A kilowatt is a unit of power (energy per unit time), and an hour is a unit of time. Multiplying power by time gives energy: kilowatt x hour = energy. So the kilowatt-hour is the unit used for electrical energy — it is what a household electricity meter counts.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C
