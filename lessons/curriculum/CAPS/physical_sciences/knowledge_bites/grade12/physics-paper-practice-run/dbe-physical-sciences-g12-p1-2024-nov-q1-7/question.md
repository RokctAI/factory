## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: Two small identical spheres, each with mass m and charge +Q, are placed in a vertical cylinder. The spheres remain stationary when their centres are r metres apart, as shown in the diagram. Ignore ALL frictional effects. [Diagram: vertical cylinder with one charged sphere (+Q, mass m) floating a distance r above an identical sphere (+Q, mass m) at the bottom.] Which ONE of the following expressions can be used to CORRECTLY calculate the distance r? A √(kQ²/mg) B √(kmg/Q²) C √(Q²/kmg) D √(mg/kQ²)

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) The upper sphere floats in equilibrium, so the upward electrostatic repulsion from the identical charge below balances its weight. Coulomb's law gives the repulsion as kQ·Q/r² = kQ²/r², and the weight is mg. Setting them equal: kQ²/r² = mg. Solve for r: r² = kQ²/(mg), so r = √(kQ²/mg).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A
