## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: A ball moving horizontally has constant momentum p and kinetic energy K. The ball collides with a wall and bounces back horizontally. Immediately after the collision, the ball has momentum ½p. The mass of the ball remains constant. [Diagram: ball moving with momentum p towards a wall; after bouncing it moves away with momentum ½p.] Which ONE of the following is the kinetic energy of the ball immediately after the collision? A ¼K B ½K C 2K D 4K

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Link kinetic energy to momentum: Ek = ½mv² and p = mv, so Ek = p²/(2m). Kinetic energy is proportional to the square of the momentum when the mass is constant. Before the collision, K = p²/(2m). After the collision the momentum magnitude is halved to ½p, so the new kinetic energy is (½p)²/(2m) = ¼ · p²/(2m) = ¼K. Halving the momentum quarters the kinetic energy.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A
