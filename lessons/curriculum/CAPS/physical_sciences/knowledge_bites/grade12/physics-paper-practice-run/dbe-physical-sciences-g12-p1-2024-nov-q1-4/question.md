## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: A force F acts on a box as the box moves from rest down a rough incline at a constant acceleration. The force is parallel to the incline, as shown in the diagram. [Diagram: box on an incline with force F directed up the slope, box moving down the slope.] Choose the option that CORRECTLY completes the following statement: The work done by the gravitational force is ... the work done by the frictional force and the work done by F. A equal to the sum of B less than the sum of C greater than the sum of D equal to the difference between

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Apply the work-energy theorem: Wnet = ΔEk. The box starts from rest and accelerates down the slope, so its kinetic energy increases and the net work is positive. The motion is down the slope, while both friction and F point up the slope, so friction and F each do negative work. Gravity is the only force doing positive work. For the net work to come out positive, the positive work done by gravity must be larger in magnitude than the combined magnitude of the work done by friction and by F — greater than their sum, not equal to it.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C
