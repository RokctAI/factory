## Question (1 mark)

Compounds D and E are compared. Which compound has the lower boiling point? (From the vapour-pressure table at 20 °C: D propanoic acid, molar mass 74 g·mol⁻¹, vapour pressure 0,32 kPa; E butanal, molar mass 72 g·mol⁻¹, vapour pressure 12,2 kPa.)

## Method

boiling point comparison

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) The two compounds have almost the same molar mass, so the comparison comes down to the strength of the intermolecular forces. Butanal (E) has the much higher vapour pressure (12,2 kPa versus 0,32 kPa), which tells you its intermolecular forces are weaker — an aldehyde has only dipole-dipole and London forces, while a carboxylic acid also has hydrogen bonding. Weaker forces need less energy to overcome, so butanal boils at the lower temperature.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

E / butanal
