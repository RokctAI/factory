## Question (1 mark)

The vapour pressures of compounds A, B and C are given in random order below: 79 kPa, 146 kPa, 58 kPa. Write down the vapour pressure of compound C. (Refers to the table in QUESTION 3.1: A pentane, B 2-methylbutane, C 2,2-dimethylpropane, all with molar mass 72 g·mol⁻¹.)

## Method

vapour pressure branching

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Compounds A, B and C are chain isomers of C5H12, all with molar mass 72 g·mol⁻¹. Compound C, 2,2-dimethylpropane, is the most branched, so it has the smallest surface area, the weakest London forces between its molecules, and therefore evaporates most easily. It must have the HIGHEST vapour pressure of the three values given: 146 kPa.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

146 (kPa). Accept: 146 000 Pa.
