## Question (1 mark)

Two bottles contain compounds A and B with the same molecular formula, C4H8O2. These compounds are straight chain organic molecules that belong to two different homologous series (an ester and a carboxylic acid). At a given temperature the vapour pressure of compound A is 0,071 kPa and that of compound B is 9,7 kPa. Write down the strongest type of Van der Waal's forces between the molecules in compound B.

## Method

identify intermolecular forces

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Compound B has the much higher vapour pressure, so its molecules are held by the weaker intermolecular forces — B is the ester. An ester has polar C=O and C-O bonds but no O-H bond on which to hydrogen bond, so its strongest intermolecular force is the dipole-dipole force.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Dipole-dipole forces
