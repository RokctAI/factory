## Question (2 marks)

The vapour pressures of different organic compounds are determined at 20 °C. The vapour pressures of compounds A, B and C are NOT shown in the table. [Table: A Pentane, molar mass 72 g·mol⁻¹, vapour pressure not shown; B 2-methylbutane, 72, not shown; C 2,2-dimethylpropane, 72, not shown; D Propanoic acid, 74, 0,32 kPa; E Butanal, 72, 12,2 kPa.] Define the term vapour pressure.

## Method

define vapour pressure

(see marking guidelines) (Memo instruction: if any of the underlined key phrases in the correct context is omitted, deduct 1 mark.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The pressure exerted by a vapour at equilibrium with its liquid in a closed system.
