## Question (2 marks)

Two bottles contain compounds A and B with the same molecular formula, C4H8O2. These compounds are straight chain organic molecules that belong to two different homologous series; the strongest intermolecular forces are hydrogen bonds between molecules of compound A and dipole-dipole forces between molecules of compound B. Which compound, A or B, has a higher boiling point? Give a reason for the answer by referring to the strength of the intermolecular forces.

## Method

boiling point imf reasoning

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Boiling point rises with the strength of the intermolecular forces: stronger forces need more energy to overcome before molecules can escape into the vapour phase. Hydrogen bonds between the molecules of A are stronger than the dipole-dipole forces between the molecules of B, so A boils at the higher temperature.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A. The hydrogen bond is stronger than the dipole-dipole force. (OR: the dipole-dipole force is weaker than the hydrogen bonds / compound A has stronger intermolecular forces (than B) / compound B has weaker intermolecular forces (than A).)
