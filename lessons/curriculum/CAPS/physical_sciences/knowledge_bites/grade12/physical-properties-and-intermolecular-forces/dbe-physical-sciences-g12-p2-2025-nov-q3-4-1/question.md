## Question (1 mark)

Two bottles contain compounds A and B with the same molecular formula, C4H8O2. These compounds are straight chain organic molecules that belong to two different homologous series (an ester and a carboxylic acid). At a given temperature the vapour pressure of compound A is 0,071 kPa and that of compound B is 9,7 kPa. Write down the strongest type of Van der Waal's forces between the molecules in compound A.

## Method

identify intermolecular forces

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Compound A has the much lower vapour pressure, so its molecules are held by the stronger intermolecular forces — A is the carboxylic acid (butanoic acid), which has an O-H group and therefore forms hydrogen bonds.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Hydrogen bonds
