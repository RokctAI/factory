## Question (2 marks)

The following are the vapour pressures of these compounds at a given temperature: Compound A 0,071 kPa; Compound B 9,7 kPa. Write down the STRUCTURAL FORMULA of compound A. (Refers to QUESTION 3: compounds A and B share the molecular formula C4H8O2, are straight chain molecules, and belong to two different homologous series — the ester and the carboxylic acid.)

## Method

structural formula from properties

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Link vapour pressure to intermolecular forces: the LOWER the vapour pressure, the STRONGER the intermolecular forces holding the molecules in the liquid. Compound A's vapour pressure (0,071 kPa) is far lower than compound B's (9,7 kPa), so A has the stronger intermolecular forces. Between a carboxylic acid and an ester of the same molecular formula, the carboxylic acid has the stronger forces because its O-H bond allows hydrogen bonding between molecules; an ester has no O-H and cannot hydrogen-bond with itself. So A is the straight-chain carboxylic acid with formula C4H8O2: butanoic acid. Draw the full structural formula: a four-carbon chain in which the fourth carbon is the carboxyl carbon, showing the double-bonded O and the O-H. Memo marking criteria: (a) functional group correct; (b) whole structure correct.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Butanoic acid: full structural formula of a four-carbon chain CH3-CH2-CH2-COOH, with the carboxyl carbon showing the double-bonded O and the O-H.
