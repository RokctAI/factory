## Question (3 marks)

The voltage versus time graph for an AC generator is given. (Graph: voltage in V versus time in s; one full cosine-shaped cycle starting at a positive maximum, passing through zero, reaching a negative minimum and returning to the positive maximum at t = 4 s.) Redraw this graph in the ANSWER BOOK and label it as A. On the same set of axes, draw the graph for ONE rotation of the coil when the speed of rotation is doubled. Label this as graph B.

## Method

generator graph sketch

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) (Memo criteria: amplitude of graph B bigger than that of graph A; period of B is half of A; correct shape and one cycle — a cosine graph. Doubling the rotation speed doubles the rate of change of flux, so the induced maximum voltage increases, and one rotation now takes half the time.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Graph B: a cosine graph with amplitude bigger than that of graph A and period half of A (one full cycle in 2 s).
