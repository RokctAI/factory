## Question (3 marks)

The graph of the generator's induced current against time shows a peak (maximum) current of 1,2 A. Calculate the root-mean-square current delivered by the generator.

## Method

rms current calculation

## Memo working

Read the maximum current off the graph (Imax = 1,2 A), then apply the rms relation: Irms = Imax/√2 = 1,2/√2 = 0,85 A.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Irms = 0,85 A
