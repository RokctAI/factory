## Question (2 marks)

An AC generator operates a fan. (Diagram: a rectangular coil rotates between two magnet poles, the right-hand pole labelled Z; the coil ends connect via two slip rings and brushes to an external circuit containing a voltmeter V and the fan.)

Define the term rms potential difference.

## Method

define rms potential difference

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) (Memo note: if DC is written first, energy or heating effect is omitted, or alternating current is defined: 0/2.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The AC potential difference/voltage that produces/dissipates the same amount of energy as an equivalent DC potential difference/voltage.
