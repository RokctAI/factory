## Question (2 marks)

The graph shows how the induced current varies with time after a change was made to the operation of the generator (graph: same shape as before — repeating positive humps — but the peak current is now 0,6 A instead of 1,2 A, and one full hump takes 0,04 s, double the original period). Fully describe the change that was made.

## Method

generator graph interpretation

## Memo working

Compare the two graphs verbally: the period has doubled from 0,02 s to 0,04 s, so the coil completes each rotation in twice the time, and the peak current has halved from 1,2 A to 0,6 A, consistent with the induced emf being proportional to the rotation speed. Both changes point to the same single cause: the coil is being rotated at half the original speed.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Speed of rotation halved. Accept: frequency was halved / period was doubled. (If only 'speed of rotation slower / frequency decreases / period increased': 1/2.)
