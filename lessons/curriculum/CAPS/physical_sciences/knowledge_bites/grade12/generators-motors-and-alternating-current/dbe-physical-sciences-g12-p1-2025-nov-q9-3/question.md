## Question (5 marks)

An AC generator operates a fan. The resistance of the fan is 60 Ω and the maximum potential difference produced by the generator is 311,11 V. Calculate the cost of operating the fan for 1,5 hours if the cost of electricity is R3,33 per kWh.

## Method

rms power cost calculation

## Memo working

First convert the maximum potential difference to the rms value: Vrms = Vmax/√2 = 311,11/√2 = 219,99 V.

Next find the average power dissipated in the fan: Pave = Vrms²/R = (219,99)²/60 = 806,59 W, which is 0,80659 kW.

Finally, cost = power x time x tariff = 0,80659(1,5)(3,33) = R4,03. (Equivalently, energy E = PΔt = 0,80659 x 1,5 = 1,21 kWh, and cost = E x tariff = 1,21(3,33) = R4,03.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Cost = R4,03
