## Question (2 marks)

The graph of a DC generator's induced current against time shows identical positive humps: the current rises from 0 to a peak of 1,2 A and returns to 0 at 0,02 s, then repeats the same hump between 0,02 and 0,04 s. Calculate the frequency at which the coil rotates in the generator.

## Method

frequency from period

## Memo working

Read the period off the graph — one complete hump takes 0,02 s — then take the reciprocal: f = 1/T = 1/0,02 = 50 Hz. (Memo alternative: f = number of waves/time = 2/0,04 = 50 Hz.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

f = 50 Hz
