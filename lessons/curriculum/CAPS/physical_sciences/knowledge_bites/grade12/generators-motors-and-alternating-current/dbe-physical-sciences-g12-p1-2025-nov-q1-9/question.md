## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: How does the commutator in a DC motor ensure that the coil rotates continuously in ONE direction? A By reducing friction B By converting AC to DC C By reversing the direction of the current in the coil D By maintaining electrical contact between the external and the internal circuits

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C (by reversing the direction of the current in the coil every half rotation, so the turning force on the coil keeps acting in the same rotational direction)
