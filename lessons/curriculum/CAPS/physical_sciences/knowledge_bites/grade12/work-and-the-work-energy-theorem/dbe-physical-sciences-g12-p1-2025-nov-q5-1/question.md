## Question (2 marks)

A crate of mass 3 kg is at rest at point A on a frictionless horizontal surface. A constant horizontal force F of 12 N acts on the crate and moves it from point A to point B. The crate then moves down a rough plane, inclined at 20° to the horizontal. The distance from point A to point B is 7 m and from point B to point C is 6,8 m. (Diagram: horizontal surface from A to B with force F = 12 N pushing the 3 kg crate to the right; at B the surface becomes a plane inclined at 20° below the horizontal going down to C.) State the work-energy theorem in words.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The net/total work done (on an object) is equal to the change in the object's kinetic energy. OR: The work done (on an object) by a resultant/net force is equal to the change in the object's kinetic energy.
