## Question (4 marks)

(Context: a 6 kg crate is pulled along a rough horizontal surface by a constant force F applied at 30° above the horizontal, against a constant frictional force of 10 N.) Draw a labelled free-body diagram showing ALL the forces acting on the crate as it moves.

## Method

free body diagram

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Four forces act on the moving crate. Represent the crate as a dot and draw one labelled arrow for each: the normal force N pointing straight up from the surface; the weight w pointing straight down; the applied force F pointing at 30° above the horizontal in the direction of motion; and the kinetic friction f pointing horizontally, opposite to the motion. Draw F itself as a single arrow at the angle — do not replace it with its horizontal and vertical components on the free-body diagram.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

[Free-body diagram: dot with normal force N upward, weight w downward, applied force F at 30° above the horizontal (to the right), friction f horizontally opposite to the motion (to the left). Mirror image accepted.] Accepted labels: w = Fw/Fg/mg/58,8 N/gravitational force/weight; f = Ff/fk/(kinetic) friction; N = FN/normal force; F = FA/applied force. Notes: mark awarded for label and arrow; additional forces max 3/4; no arrows max 3/4; forces not touching the dot max 3/4; components drawn for F max 3/4.
