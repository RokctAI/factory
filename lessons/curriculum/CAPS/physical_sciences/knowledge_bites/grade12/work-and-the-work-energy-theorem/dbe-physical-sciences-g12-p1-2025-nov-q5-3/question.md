## Question (3 marks)

Force F is removed when the crate reaches point B. The crate then experiences a constant frictional force of 21 N as it moves down the incline. Draw a free-body diagram showing ALL the forces acting on the crate as it moves down the incline. (The 3 kg crate moves down a plane inclined at 20° to the horizontal.)

free body diagram

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Three forces on the crate: w (weight, vertically down; accepted labels Fw/Fg/mg/29,4 N/gravitational force), N (normal force, perpendicular to the incline), f (kinetic friction, 21 N, up the incline opposing the motion). (Memo note: each mark is awarded for label and arrow; any additional force(s), missing arrows, or forces not making contact with the dot: maximum 2/3.)
