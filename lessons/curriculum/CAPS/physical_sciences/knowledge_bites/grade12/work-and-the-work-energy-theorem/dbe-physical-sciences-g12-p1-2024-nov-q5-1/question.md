## Question (2 marks)

A constant force F is applied at an angle of 30° to the horizontal on a crate of mass 6 kg that is initially at rest. A constant frictional force of 10 N acts on the crate as it moves from rest at point A along a horizontal surface to point B. The distance between point A and point B is 1,5 m. The speed of the crate at point B is 2 m·s⁻¹. [Diagram: 6 kg crate on a horizontal surface pulled by force F at 30° above the horizontal over the 1,5 m from A to B.] 5.1 Define the term work done by a force.

## Method

define work done

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) This is a definition recall. The key ideas that must appear are: the force F, the displacement Δx, and the angle θ between the force and the displacement — combined as W = FΔxcosθ; or, in words, the product of the force and the displacement in the direction of the force. (Memo instruction: if any of the underlined key words/phrases in the correct context is omitted, deduct 1 mark.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The work done on an object by a constant force F is FΔxcosθ, where F is the magnitude of the force, Δx the magnitude of the displacement and θ the angle between the force and the displacement. OR The work done on an object is the product of the force and the displacement of the object in the direction of the displacement.
