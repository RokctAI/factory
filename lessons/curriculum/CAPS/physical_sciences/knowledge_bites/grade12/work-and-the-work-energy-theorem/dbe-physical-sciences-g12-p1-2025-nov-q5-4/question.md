## Question (5 marks)

Using ENERGY PRINCIPLES ONLY, determine whether the crate will pass point C. (The 3 kg crate leaves point B with a kinetic energy of 84 J — from Q5.2 — and moves down a rough plane inclined at 20° to the horizontal against a constant frictional force of 21 N; the distance from B to C along the incline is 6,8 m.)

## Method

work energy theorem incline

## Memo working

POSITIVE MARKING FROM 5.2. Option 1: apply Wnet = ΔEk over the distance Δx the crate travels before stopping. WFg + Wfk = Ekf − Eki, so mg sin θ Δx cos 0° + fk Δx cos 180° = 0 − 84, i.e. (3)(9,8)sin 20°Δx − 21Δx = −84, giving Δx = 7,68 m. Since 7,68 m > 6,8 m, the crate passes C. Option 2: with Δx = 6,8 m: (3)(9,8)sin 20°(6,8)cos 0° + (21)(6,8)cos 180° = Ekf − 84, so Ekf = 9,58 J > 0 and the crate passes C. (Memo options 3/4 use Wnc = ΔEk + ΔEp with h = 6,8 sin 20° = 2,326 m, giving the same result. Criteria: substitution for WFg; substitution for Wf; substitution for ΔEk; final answer; conclusion. NOTE: cos 70° or sin 20° accepted throughout.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The crate will pass point C (it can travel Δx = 7,68 m > 6,8 m down the incline before stopping; equivalently Ekf at C = 9,58 J > 0 J, or vf = 2,52 m·s⁻¹ > 0).
