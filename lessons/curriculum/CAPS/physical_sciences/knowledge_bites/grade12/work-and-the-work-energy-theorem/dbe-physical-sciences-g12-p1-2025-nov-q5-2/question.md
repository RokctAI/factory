## Question (3 marks)

Using ENERGY PRINCIPLES ONLY, calculate the kinetic energy of the crate at point B. (A 3 kg crate starts at rest at point A on a frictionless horizontal surface; a constant horizontal force F of 12 N moves it 7 m from A to B.)

## Method

work energy theorem

## Memo working

Apply the work-energy theorem Wnet = ΔEk, i.e. FΔxcos θ = Ekf − Eki (any form of the theorem). The 12 N force acts along the 7 m displacement, and the crate starts from rest: (12)(7)cos 0° = Ekf − 0, so Ekf = 84 J.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Ekf = 84 J
