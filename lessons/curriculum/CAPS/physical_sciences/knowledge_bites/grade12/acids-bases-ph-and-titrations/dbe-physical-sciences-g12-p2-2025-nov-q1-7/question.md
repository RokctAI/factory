## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: The products for the reaction between a metal oxide and an acid are … A a salt and water. B a salt and hydrogen gas. C a salt and carbon dioxide. D a salt, water and carbon dioxide.

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) A metal oxide is a base, and a base reacting with an acid is a neutralisation reaction: acid + metal oxide → salt + water. The distractors describe other reaction families: a metal (not a metal oxide) plus an acid gives a salt and hydrogen gas, while a metal carbonate plus an acid gives a salt, water and carbon dioxide.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A
