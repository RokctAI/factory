## Question (2 marks)

A hydrochloric acid solution, HCℓ(aq), is titrated with a potassium carbonate solution, K2CO3(aq) — a strong acid against a weak base. METHYL ORANGE is used as the indicator. Explain why methyl orange is the most suitable indicator for this titration by referring to the pH at the equivalence point.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The titration's equivalence point/colour change is in a pH range less than 7 / solution is acidic / the reaction of a strong acid and weak base has its equivalence point at pH less than 7. The end point of this titration is within the pH range in which methyl orange/indicator changes colour / methyl orange changes colour at a pH less than 7.
