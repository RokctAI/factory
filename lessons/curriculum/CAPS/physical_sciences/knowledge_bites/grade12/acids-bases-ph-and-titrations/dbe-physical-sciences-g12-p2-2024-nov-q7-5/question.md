## Question (5 marks)

Calculate the concentration of the K2CO3 solution. (From QUESTION 7.3: 25 cm³ of 0,1 mol·dm⁻³ HCℓ(aq) is neutralised by 20,05 cm³ of K2CO3(aq) in Run 1 and 20,15 cm³ in Run 2 — average 20,1 cm³ — according to K2CO3(aq) + 2HCℓ(aq) → 2KCℓ(aq) + CO2(g) + H2O(ℓ).)

## Method

titration concentration calculation

## Memo working

OPTION 1: caVa/cbVb = na/nb: (0,1 x 25)/(cb x 20,1) = 2/1; c(K2CO3) = 0,0622 mol·dm⁻³. OPTION 2: n(HCℓ) = cV = (0,1)(25 x 10⁻³) = 2,5 x 10⁻³ mol; the mole ratio gives n(K2CO3) = ½n(HCℓ) = 1,25 x 10⁻³ mol; then 1,25 x 10⁻³ = c(20,1 x 10⁻³); c(K2CO3) = 0,0622 mol·dm⁻³. Note: the average volume 20,1 cm³ must be used; if 20,05 or 20,15 is used, deduct 1 mark.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

c(K2CO3) = 0,0622 mol·dm⁻³ (0,06) (accepted range 0,06 to 0,0625 mol·dm⁻³; the criteria list the final answer as 0,0625 mol·dm⁻³)
