## Question (3 marks)

Write a balanced equation to explain the answer to QUESTION 7.1.4. (Context: sodium hydrogen phosphate, Na2HPO4(s), dissolved in water gives a BASIC solution.)

## Method

hydrolysis equation

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Memo note: marks for reactants; products; balancing. Ignore → versus ⇌ and phases.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

HPO4²⁻(aq) + H2O(ℓ) ⇌ H2PO4⁻(aq) + OH⁻(aq). (Also accepted: HPO4²⁻ + 2H2O ⇌ H3PO4 + 2OH⁻, and the equivalent equations written with Na2HPO4/NaH2PO4/NaOH.)
