## Question (1 mark)

A hydrochloric acid solution, HCℓ(aq), of concentration 0,1 mol·dm⁻³ is titrated with a potassium carbonate solution, K2CO3(aq), of unknown concentration. (Titration results table, K2CO3(aq) in burette: Run 1 — volume of HCℓ(aq) used 25 cm³, initial burette reading 6,5 cm³, final burette reading p, volume of K2CO3(aq) used 20,05 cm³. Run 2 — volume of HCℓ(aq) used 25 cm³, initial burette reading q, final burette reading 48,3 cm³, volume of K2CO3(aq) used 20,15 cm³.)

Determine the value of q.

## Method

burette reading arithmetic

## Memo working

The volume dispensed from a burette is the final reading minus the initial reading, so the initial reading q = final reading – volume of K2CO3(aq) used: q = 48,3 – 20,15 = 28,15 cm³.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

28,15 (cm³)
