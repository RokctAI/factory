## Question (1 mark)

A hydrochloric acid solution, HCℓ(aq), of concentration 0,1 mol·dm⁻³ is titrated with the prepared potassium carbonate solution, K2CO3(aq), of unknown concentration. The balanced equation for the reaction is: K2CO3(aq) + 2HCℓ(aq) → 2KCℓ(aq) + CO2(g) + H2O(ℓ). (Titration results table, K2CO3(aq) in burette: Run 1 — volume of HCℓ(aq) used 25 cm³, initial burette reading 6,5 cm³, final burette reading p, volume of K2CO3(aq) used 20,05 cm³. Run 2 — volume of HCℓ(aq) used 25 cm³, initial burette reading q, final burette reading 48,3 cm³, volume of K2CO3(aq) used 20,15 cm³.)

Determine the value of p.

## Method

burette reading arithmetic

## Memo working

The volume dispensed from a burette is the final reading minus the initial reading, so the final reading p = initial reading + volume of K2CO3(aq) used: p = 6,5 + 20,05 = 26,55 cm³.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

26,55 (cm³)
