## Question (2 marks)

Hydrated potassium carbonate, K2CO3·xH2O, is a WEAK BASE. A solution is prepared by dissolving some of this solid in water. Define the term weak base.

## Method

define weak base

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Weak bases dissociate/ionise incompletely/partially in water to form a low concentration of hydroxide/OH⁻ ions.
