## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: Which ONE of the following is a CORRECT GENERAL FORMULA for the carboxylic acids? A CnH2n+1O2 B CnH2nO2n C CnH2nO2 D CnHnO2

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Test each general formula against a known carboxylic acid. Ethanoic acid, CH3COOH, has molecular formula C2H4O2: two carbons, four hydrogens, two oxygens. With n = 2, formula CnH2nO2 gives exactly C2H4O2 — a match. The other options fail the same test: A gives an odd hydrogen count (C2H5O2), B gives too many oxygens (C2H4O4), and D gives too few hydrogens (C2H2O2).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C
