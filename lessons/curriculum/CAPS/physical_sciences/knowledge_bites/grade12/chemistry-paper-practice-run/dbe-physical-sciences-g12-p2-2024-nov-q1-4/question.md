## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: The potential energy diagram is for the hypothetical chemical reaction X + Y ⇌ Z. [Diagram: potential energy versus course of reaction; the products lie LOWER than the reactants (exothermic) and the activation-energy hump for the forward reaction is smaller than that of the reverse reaction.] Which ONE of the following combinations of values for the heat of the reaction and the activation energies can be obtained for this reaction? (ΔH(forward); EA(forward); EA(reverse), all in kJ·mol⁻¹) A -400; 300; 100 B -200; 300; 100 C +400; 100; 300 D -200; 100; 300

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) The products lie lower than the reactants, so the reaction is exothermic and ΔH must be negative — this eliminates option C. The forward activation-energy hump is smaller than the reverse one, so EA(forward) must be less than EA(reverse) — this eliminates A and B, which both have EA(forward) = 300 greater than EA(reverse) = 100. Check option D for consistency using ΔH = EA(forward) − EA(reverse) = 100 − 300 = −200 kJ·mol⁻¹, which matches the stated ΔH of −200.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

D
