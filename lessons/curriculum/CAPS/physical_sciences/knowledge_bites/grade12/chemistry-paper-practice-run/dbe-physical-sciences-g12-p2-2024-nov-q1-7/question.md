## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: The table shows the ionisation constants, Ka, at 25 °C: butanoic acid 1,5 x 10⁻⁵; ethanoic acid 1,8 x 10⁻⁵. Consider the following statements for these two acids when they have equal concentration at 25 °C: (i) Both are weak acids. (ii) Butanoic acid is a stronger acid than ethanoic acid. (iii) The butanoic acid solution has a lower concentration of hydronium ion, H3O+(aq), than the ethanoic acid solution. Which of the above statements are TRUE? A (i) and (ii) only B (i) and (iii) only C (ii) and (iii) only D (i), (ii) and (iii)

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Statement (i): both Ka values are very small (order of 10⁻⁵), so both acids ionise only partially in water — both are weak acids. TRUE. Statement (ii): butanoic acid has the SMALLER Ka (1,5 x 10⁻⁵ versus 1,8 x 10⁻⁵), so it is the weaker acid, not the stronger one. FALSE. Statement (iii): at equal concentration, a smaller Ka means less ionisation, so the butanoic acid solution has a lower concentration of H3O+ ions. TRUE. Statements (i) and (iii) only — option B.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

B
