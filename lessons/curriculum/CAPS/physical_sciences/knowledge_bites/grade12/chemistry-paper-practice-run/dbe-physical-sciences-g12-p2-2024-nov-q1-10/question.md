## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: An electrolytic cell is set up to electroplate an iron rod with nickel. [Diagram: electrolytic cell with a battery, a nickel electrode and an iron rod in a solution.] Consider the following statements: (i) The iron rod is the negative electrode. (ii) The metal ions in the solution undergo reduction. (iii) The anode is pure nickel. Which of the above statements are TRUE? A (i) and (ii) only B (i) and (iii) only C (ii) and (iii) only D (i), (ii) and (iii)

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) In electroplating, the object to be plated (the iron rod) is made the cathode, connected to the negative terminal of the battery — statement (i) is TRUE. At this cathode the Ni2+ ions in the solution gain electrons and are reduced to nickel metal, which deposits on the rod — statement (ii) is TRUE. The anode is pure nickel, which is oxidised and dissolves to replenish the Ni2+ ions in the solution — statement (iii) is TRUE. All three statements are true — option D.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

D
