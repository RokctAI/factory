## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: Consider the following reaction at equilibrium: 2SO2(g) + O2(g) ⇌ 2SO3(g), ΔH = −188 kJ·mol⁻¹. Which ONE of the changes to the reaction conditions below will increase the yield of SO3(g)? A The addition of O2(g) B The addition of a catalyst C An increase in temperature D An increase in the volume of the container at a constant temperature

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Apply Le Chatelier's principle. Adding O2(g) (option A) increases a reactant concentration, so the equilibrium shifts toward the products and the yield of SO3 increases. A catalyst (option B) speeds up the forward and reverse reactions equally and does not change the yield. Increasing the temperature (option C) favours the endothermic reverse reaction, because the forward reaction is exothermic (ΔH = −188 kJ·mol⁻¹). Increasing the volume (option D) decreases the pressure and shifts the equilibrium to the side with more gas moles — the reactant side (3 moles versus 2) — decreasing the yield.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A
