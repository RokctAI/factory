## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: Which ONE of the following pairs of acids and bases, all of the same concentration, react to give the highest pH at the equivalence point in a titration at 25 °C? A HCℓ and NH3 B HCℓ and NaOH C HNO3 and KOH D CH3COOH and NaOH

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) At the equivalence point the solution contains only the salt formed by the acid and the base. A strong acid with a strong base (options B and C) gives a neutral salt solution with pH 7. A strong acid with a weak base (option A) gives a salt whose ammonium ion hydrolyses to make the solution acidic, pH below 7. A weak acid with a strong base (option D, CH3COOH and NaOH) gives the ethanoate ion, which hydrolyses as a weak base, making the equivalence-point solution basic — the highest pH of the four.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

D
