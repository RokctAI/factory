## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: Study the reactions below. Reaction 1: CH3CH2CHOHCH3 --(H2SO4 conc.)--> Compound P (major product) + H2O. Reaction 2: Compound P --(hydrogenation)--> Compound Q. Which ONE of the following combinations is the CORRECT IUPAC names of compounds P and Q? A But-1-ene; Butane B But-2-ene; Butane C But-1-ene; Butan-2-ol D But-2-ene; Butan-2-ol

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Reaction 1 is the dehydration of butan-2-ol with concentrated H2SO4 (an elimination reaction). By Zaitsev's rule the major product P is the more substituted alkene, but-2-ene, not but-1-ene. Reaction 2 is hydrogenation: adding H2 across the double bond of but-2-ene saturates it to the alkane butane, so Q is butane. That combination is option B.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

B
