## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: Initially, an equal number of moles of hydrogen gas, H2(g), and iodine gas, I2(g), are mixed in a closed container. The reaction reaches equilibrium at a constant temperature according to the balanced equation H2(g) + I2(g) ⇌ 2HI(g). Which ONE of the following is ALWAYS TRUE at equilibrium? A [H2] = [I2] B [HI] = [I2] C [HI] = 2[H2] D [H2] = [I2] = [HI]

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) H2 and I2 react in a 1:1 mole ratio. The container starts with equal moles of each, and every bit of reaction removes them in equal amounts, so their concentrations stay equal at every instant — including at equilibrium: [H2] = [I2]. The HI concentration depends on how far the reaction has proceeded before equilibrium is reached, so the relations in options B, C and D are not always true.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A
