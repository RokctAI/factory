## Question (4 marks)

(Context: trolley B, of mass 2 kg, leaves a spring launch on a frictionless horizontal track, then moves up a frictionless inclined plane, rising to a maximum vertical height of 1,5 m.) Calculate the speed of trolley B at the bottom of the inclined plane.

## Method

conservation of mechanical energy

## Memo working

The incline is frictionless, so mechanical energy is conserved between the bottom and the top of the incline. Option 1: (Ep + Ek)top = (Ep + Ek)bottom. At the top the trolley is momentarily at rest at height 1,5 m, so all the energy is potential: (2)(9,8)(1,5) + 0. At the bottom all of it is kinetic: 0 + ½(2)v². Setting them equal: (2)(9,8)(1,5) = ½(2)v², so v² = 2(9,8)(1,5) = 29,4 and v = 5,42 m·s⁻¹. Option 2: Wnc = ΔEk + ΔEp with zero non-conservative work: 0 = [0 – ½vi²] + [(9,8)(1,5 – 0)], giving the same vi = 5,42 m·s⁻¹. (Memo also gives Wnet = ΔEk with FgΔycos180°, ΔEk = –ΔEp, and vf² = vi² + 2aΔx options.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

v = 5,42 m·s⁻¹
