## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: A motor fixed to the ground is used to lift a box vertically upwards at a CONSTANT VELOCITY. (Diagram: motor on the ground with a cable lifting a box vertically.) Consider the statements below for the upward motion of the box. (i) The rate at which work is done by the motor on the box increases. (ii) The rate at which work is done by the motor on the box is constant. (iii) The mechanical energy of the box increases. (iv) The mechanical energy of the box is constant. Which of the statements above are CORRECT? A (i) and (iii) only B (ii) and (iv) only C (ii) and (iii) only D (i) and (iv) only

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C ((ii) and (iii) only: at constant velocity the power P = Fv is constant, while the potential energy — and so the mechanical energy — of the box increases)
