## Question (6 marks)

A stationary listener records the wavelength of the sound emitted by the siren of a police car travelling at a constant velocity away from the listener (wavelength recorded by the listener λL = 0,40 m; wavelength emitted by the siren λS = 0,38 m; speed of sound in air 343 m·s⁻¹; from Question 6.3.1 the siren frequency is fS = 902,63 Hz). Calculate the magnitude of the velocity of the car.

## Method

doppler equation moving source

## Memo working

POSITIVE MARKING FROM QUESTION 6.3.1. First convert the listener's wavelength to the frequency the listener hears: v = fλ, so 343 = f(0,4), giving fL = 857,5 Hz. Then apply the Doppler equation for a source moving away from a stationary listener: fL = v/(v + vs) × fS, so 857,5 = (343/(343 + vs))(902,63), giving vs = 18,05 m·s⁻¹. (Memo notes: if wavelengths are substituted instead of frequencies, max 2/6; if the wavelengths are swopped, max 3/6.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

vs = 18,05 m·s⁻¹ (accepted range 18,05 – 18,45 m·s⁻¹)
