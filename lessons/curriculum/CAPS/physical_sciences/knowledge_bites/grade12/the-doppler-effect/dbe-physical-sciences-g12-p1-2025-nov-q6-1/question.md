## Question (2 marks)

An investigation is performed to determine the relationship between the velocity of a moving sound source and the frequency of the sound detected. The siren of an ambulance produces sound with a constant frequency. The ambulance, with its siren on, moves towards a stationary sound detector (Part A) and away from the stationary sound detector (Part B) at constant velocities. The experiment is repeated for different velocities of the ambulance. The detected frequency is measured for EACH velocity. (Graph: detected frequency in Hz versus velocity of the ambulance in m·s⁻¹; two straight lines start from the same point on the frequency axis — the upper line, Part A, rises and passes through 1 298 Hz at 25 m·s⁻¹, while the lower line, Part B, falls and passes through 1 115 Hz at 25 m·s⁻¹.)

STATE the Doppler effect.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The change in frequency (or pitch) of the sound detected by a listener, because the sound source and the listener have different velocities relative to the medium of sound propagation. OR: An (apparent) change in (observed/detected) frequency (pitch) as a result of the relative motion between a sound source and an observer.
