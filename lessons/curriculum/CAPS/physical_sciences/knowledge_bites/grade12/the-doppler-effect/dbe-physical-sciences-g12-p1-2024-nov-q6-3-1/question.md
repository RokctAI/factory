## Question (3 marks)

A stationary listener records the wavelength of the sound emitted by the siren of a police car travelling at a constant velocity (the wavelength emitted by the siren is λS = 0,38 m; the speed of sound in air is 343 m·s⁻¹). Calculate the frequency of the sound emitted by the siren.

## Method

wave equation

## Memo working

Use the wave equation with the source wavelength: v = fλ, so 343 = f(0,38), giving f = 902,63 Hz. (Memo notes: if c = fλ is used, penalise 1 mark; if 340 m·s⁻¹ is used for the speed of sound, penalise 1 mark for the answer.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

f = 902,63 Hz
