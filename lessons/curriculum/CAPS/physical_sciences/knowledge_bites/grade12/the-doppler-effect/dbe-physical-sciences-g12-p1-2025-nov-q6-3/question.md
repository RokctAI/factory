## Question (2 marks)

An ambulance siren produces sound with a constant frequency. In Part B of an investigation, the ambulance moves away from a stationary sound detector at constant velocities, and the detected frequency is measured for each velocity. (Graph: for Part B, detected frequency versus velocity of the ambulance is a straight line that falls as velocity increases, passing through 1 115 Hz at 25 m·s⁻¹.)

What conclusion can be made for Part B of this experiment?

## Method

conclusion from graph

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) (Memo note: if 'inversely proportional' is indicated, 0/2 — state the trend, do not claim inverse proportionality.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

As the velocity of the ambulance increases, the detected frequency decreases.
