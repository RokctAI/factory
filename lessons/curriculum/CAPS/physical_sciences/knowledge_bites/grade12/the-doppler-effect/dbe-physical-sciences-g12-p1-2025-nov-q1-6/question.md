## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: A star's spectrum, observed from Earth, is red shifted. Which ONE of the following statements is CORRECT? A The speed of light is increasing. B The star is moving towards Earth. C The frequency of each spectral line has increased. D The wavelength of each spectral line has increased.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

D (the wavelength of each spectral line has increased — a red shift means the star is moving away from Earth, stretching the observed wavelengths towards the red end of the spectrum)
