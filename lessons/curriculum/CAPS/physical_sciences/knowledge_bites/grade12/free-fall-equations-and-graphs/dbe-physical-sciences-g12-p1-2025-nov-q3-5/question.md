## Question (4 marks)

Sketch a velocity versus time graph for the motion of the ball from the time it was projected until it reached the maximum height after it bounced. Show the following NUMERICAL VALUES on the graph: the initial velocity, time p and velocity q. (The ball was projected upwards at 15 m·s⁻¹ from a 5 m building, reached its highest point at p = 1,53 s, struck the ground at q = 17,97 m·s⁻¹ after 3,36 s, and bounced to a maximum height of 3 m.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Taking upwards as positive: a straight line starting at v = 15 m·s⁻¹ with constant negative gradient, cutting the time axis at t = 1,53 s and reaching −17,97 m·s⁻¹ when the ball strikes the ground; then a second parallel straight line for the motion after the bounce starting at a positive velocity smaller than 17,97 m·s⁻¹ and ending at v = 0. (Mirror image accepted with downwards positive. Memo criteria: line starts at ±15 m·s⁻¹ with opposite-sign final velocity; line cuts the time axis at the time from Q3.2; correct velocity from Q3.3 when the ball is on the ground; second line has smaller magnitude than the Q3.3 value, is parallel to the first and ends at v = 0. Accept if no contact time is shown. POSITIVE MARKING FROM QUESTIONS 3.2 AND 3.3.)
