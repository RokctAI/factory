## Question (3 marks)

The ball bounces to a maximum height of 3 m after it struck the ground. Is the collision with the ground ELASTIC or INELASTIC? Explain the answer WITHOUT the use of calculations. (The ball was projected upwards at 15 m·s⁻¹ from the top of a 5 m building and struck the ground at about 17,97 m·s⁻¹.)

## Method

elastic inelastic reasoning

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Inelastic. The height decreased/is smaller. The velocity with which the ball strikes the ground is not the same as the velocity with which it left the ground / kinetic energy is converted to heat and sound energy / kinetic energy is NOT conserved / the kinetic energy is not the same before and after the ball strikes the ground. (ACCEPT: kinetic energy is lost. Memo note: do not accept any calculation as an explanation.)
