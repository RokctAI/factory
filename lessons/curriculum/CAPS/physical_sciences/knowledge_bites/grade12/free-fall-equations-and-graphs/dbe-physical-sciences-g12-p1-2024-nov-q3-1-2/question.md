## Question (3 marks)

(Context: ball A is thrown vertically upwards at 12 m·s⁻¹ from the top of a building; 2 s later ball B is thrown vertically downwards at 5,4 m·s⁻¹ from the same rooftop; both strike the ground at t = 4 s.) Using EQUATIONS OF MOTION ONLY, calculate the value of Z, as shown in the graphs. [Z is the starting position of the balls, i.e. the height of the top of the building above the ground.]

## Method

equations of motion displacement

## Memo working

POSITIVE MARKING FROM QUESTION 3.1.1 — use the t = 4 s found there. With upwards positive, substitute either ball's full flight into Δy = viΔt + ½aΔt². Option 1, ball A over the full 4 s: Δy = (12)(4) + ½(–9,8)(4)² = 48 – 78,4 = –30,4 m. The displacement is 30,4 m downwards, so the roof is 30,4 m above the ground: Z = 30,4 m. Option 2, ball B over its (4 – 2) = 2 s of flight: Δy = (–5,4)(2) + ½(–9,8)(2)² = –10,8 – 19,6 = –30,4 m, giving the same Z = 30,4 m. (Memo also gives options using vf² = vi² + 2aΔy with vf = –27,2 m·s⁻¹ or –25 m·s⁻¹, and Δy = ((vi + vf)/2)Δt.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Z = 30,4 m
