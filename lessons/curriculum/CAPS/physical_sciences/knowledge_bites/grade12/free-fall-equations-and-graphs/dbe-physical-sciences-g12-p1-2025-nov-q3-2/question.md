## Question (3 marks)

Using EQUATIONS OF MOTION ONLY, calculate the value of p. (A ball is projected vertically upwards at 15 m·s⁻¹ from the top of a 5 m high building; p is the time at which the magnitude of its velocity is zero, i.e. at the highest point. Ignore friction.)

## Method

equations of motion time

## Memo working

Take upwards as positive. At the highest point the velocity is zero, so from vf = vi + aΔt: 0 = 15 + (−9,8)Δt, giving Δt = 1,53 s. (Also accepted: −15 = 0 + (−9,8)Δt over the downward leg, or 3,06 s for the full up-down flight divided by 2. Marking criteria: formula with Δt; correct substitution; correct final answer 1,53 s. NOTE for 3.2 and 3.3: if energy principles used, 1/3 for the final answer.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

p = 1,53 s
