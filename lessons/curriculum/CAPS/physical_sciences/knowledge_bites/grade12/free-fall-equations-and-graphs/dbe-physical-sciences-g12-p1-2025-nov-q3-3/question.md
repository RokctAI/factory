## Question (3 marks)

The ball strikes the ground 3,36 seconds after it was thrown upwards. Calculate q. (The ball was projected vertically upwards at 15 m·s⁻¹ from the top of a 5 m high building; q is the magnitude of its velocity at t = 3,36 s, the moment it strikes the ground. Ignore friction.)

## Method

equations of motion final velocity

## Memo working

POSITIVE MARKING FROM QUESTION 3.2. Option 1 (from the launch point A to the ground, upwards positive, displacement −5 m): vf² = vi² + 2aΔy; vf² = (15)² + 2(−9,8)(−5); vf = 17,97 m·s⁻¹. Option 2 (using the flight time): vf = vi + aΔt = (15) + (−9,8)(3,36) = −17,93, so the magnitude is 17,93 m·s⁻¹. Other accepted routes via the highest point or the top of the building give 17,94 to 18,33 m·s⁻¹. (Marking criteria: formula with vf; correct substitution; correct final answer.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

q = 17,97 m·s⁻¹ [accepted RANGE: 17,93 - 18,33]
