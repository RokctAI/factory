## Question (4 marks)

(Context: ball A is thrown vertically upwards at 12 m·s⁻¹ from a rooftop at t = 0; ball B is thrown vertically downwards at 5,4 m·s⁻¹ from the same rooftop at t = 2 s; both strike the ground at t = 4 s.) On the same set of axes, sketch the velocity-time graphs for ball A and ball B while they are in free fall. Label the graphs A and B for ball A and ball B respectively. Clearly indicate the following on the graphs: the initial velocity of each ball; the time at which each ball is thrown; the time at which the balls strike the ground.

**Note: the transcribed memo sketch description says line A crosses zero "at 2 s", but from the given data ball A's velocity reaches zero at t = 12/9,8 = 1,22 s. The memo's own marking criteria are consistent with this (they require only that B starts after the time-axis intercept of A); the 2 s in the sketch description appears to reflect the not-to-scale memo drawing. Sketch A's zero crossing at about 1,22 s.**

## Method

velocity time graph sketch

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Both balls are in free fall, so both velocity-time graphs are straight lines with the same slope, –9,8 m·s⁻² (taking upwards as positive) — the lines must be parallel. Line A starts at +12 m·s⁻¹ at t = 0, slopes down through zero (at t = 12/9,8, about 1,22 s, where ball A is momentarily at its highest point) and continues into negative values until t = 4 s. Line B starts later, at t = 2 s, at –5,4 m·s⁻¹, and runs parallel to A, lying to the right of A, also ending at t = 4 s. Mark the initial velocities +12 and –5,4, the starting times 0 and 2 s, and the common landing time 4 s. The downwards-positive mirror image of all of this is also accepted.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

[Sketch (upwards positive): straight line A starting at +12 m·s⁻¹ at t = 0 with constant negative slope, crossing zero at 2 s (turning point implied) and ending at 4 s; straight line B starting at –5,4 m·s⁻¹ at t = 2 s, parallel to A and to the right of A, ending at 4 s. Downwards-positive mirror image also accepted.] Marking criteria: correct initial velocities with correct shape (straight lines, both positive or both negative slopes); correct starting times with B starting after the time-axis intercept of A; both graphs end at 4 s (accept t); graphs parallel with B to the right of A. NB: no labels A and B, deduct 1 mark.
