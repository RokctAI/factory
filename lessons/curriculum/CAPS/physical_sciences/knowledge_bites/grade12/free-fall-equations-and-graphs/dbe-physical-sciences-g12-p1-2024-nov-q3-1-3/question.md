## Question (4 marks)

(Context: ball A is thrown vertically upwards at 12 m·s⁻¹ from the top of a building that is Z = 30,4 m above the ground.) Using EQUATIONS OF MOTION ONLY, calculate the value of Y, as shown in the graphs. [Y is the maximum position reached by ball A above the ground.]

## Method

equations of motion max height

## Memo working

POSITIVE MARKING FROM QUESTIONS 3.1.1 and 3.1.2. Y is measured from the ground, so first find how far ball A rises above the roof, then add the height of the roof. At the top of the flight the velocity is zero, so use vf² = vi² + 2aΔy with upwards positive: 0 = (12)² + 2(–9,8)Δy, so 19,6Δy = 144 and Δy = 7,35 m above the roof. Add the building height from Question 3.1.2: Y = 7,35 + 30,4 = 37,75 m. (Memo options also allow finding Δt = 1,22 s from vf = vi + aΔt then Δy = ((vi + vf)/2)Δt = 7,32 m, or Δy = viΔt + ½aΔt² = 7,35 m.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Y = 7,35 + 30,4 = 37,75 m (accepted range 37,72 – 37,75 m)
