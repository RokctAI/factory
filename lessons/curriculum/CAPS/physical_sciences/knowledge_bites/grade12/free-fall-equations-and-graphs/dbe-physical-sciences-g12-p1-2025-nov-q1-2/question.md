## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: A hot-air balloon is moving upwards at a constant velocity. A small stone is dropped from THE BALLOON. Which ONE of the following graphs represents the motions of the balloon and the stone? Ignore the effects of friction on the stone. (Four velocity in m·s⁻¹ versus time in s graphs, each showing a line for the balloon and a line for the stone: A — balloon is a horizontal line at a positive velocity, stone starts above the balloon line at a higher positive velocity and decreases linearly; B — balloon is a horizontal line at a positive velocity, stone starts on the balloon line at the same positive velocity and decreases linearly, crossing the time axis to negative values; C — balloon is a horizontal line at a positive velocity, stone starts at zero and increases linearly above the balloon line; D — balloon is a horizontal line at a positive velocity, stone starts at zero and increases linearly, labelled above the balloon line.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

B (the stone leaves the balloon with the balloon's upward velocity, then its velocity decreases linearly under gravity, crossing the time axis to negative values)
