## Question (2 marks)

A ball is projected vertically upwards from the top of a building that is 5 m high. Ignore the effects of friction. (Diagram: building of height 5 m with a ball projected vertically upwards from the top. A table shows the magnitude of the velocity of the ball at THREE different times during its motion: at time 0 s the magnitude of the velocity is 15 m·s⁻¹; at time p the magnitude is 0; at time 3,36 s the magnitude is q.) Define the term free fall.
