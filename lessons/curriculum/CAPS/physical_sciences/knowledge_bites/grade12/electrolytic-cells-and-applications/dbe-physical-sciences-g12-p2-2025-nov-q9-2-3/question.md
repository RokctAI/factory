## Question (6 marks)

Calculate the change in mass of electrode R after T hours. (Context: in the copper-refining cell, electrode R is the unrefined copper anode with zinc as the only impurity; after T hours the amount of Cu2+(aq) ions in the electrolyte changed by 0,05 moles and 0,15 moles of Cu(s) were deposited on electrode Q.)

## Method

electrolysis mass change calculation

## Memo working

Electrode R is the impure copper anode, so it loses mass in two ways: zinc impurity is oxidised into the electrolyte, and copper is oxidised to replace the Cu2+ being deposited.

Step 1 — zinc lost: n(Zn2+) formed = n(Cu2+) decrease = 0,05 mol; m(Zn) = nM = (0,05)(65) = 3,25 g Zn lost from R.

Step 2 — copper lost: the cathode gained 0,15 mol Cu but the electrolyte only lost 0,05 mol Cu2+, so the anode supplied the difference: Cu oxidised at R = 0,15 − 0,05 = 0,1 mol; m(Cu) = nM = 0,1(63,5) = 6,35 g Cu lost from R.

Step 3 — total change in mass = 6,35 + 3,25 = 9,6 g (decrease).

Marking criteria: (a) use ratio n(Cu2+) : n(Zn2+) = 1 : 1; (b) formula n = m/M; (c) substitute 65 and n(Zn2+); (d) subtraction of moles; (e) substitute 63,5 and n(Cu); (f) final correct answer 9,6 g.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Change in mass of electrode R = 9,6 g (decrease in mass). (NO RANGE)
