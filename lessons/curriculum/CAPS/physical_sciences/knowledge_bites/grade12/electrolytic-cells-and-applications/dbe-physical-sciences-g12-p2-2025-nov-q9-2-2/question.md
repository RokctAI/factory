## Question (3 marks)

Will the amount of Cu2+(aq) ions INCREASE or DECREASE? Explain the answer in terms of the relative strengths of the oxidising agents present. (Context: the electrolytic cell above refines copper; the unrefined copper anode contains zinc as the only impurity, so both Cu2+ and Zn2+ ions enter the electrolyte while copper is deposited on electrode Q.)

## Method

oxidising agent strength reasoning

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Decrease. More copper(II) ions/Cu2+ are reduced than formed / only copper(II) ions/Cu2+ are reduced; the copper(II) ion/Cu2+ is a stronger oxidising agent than the zinc(II) ion/Zn2+ (OR Zn2+ is a weaker oxidising agent than Cu2+).
