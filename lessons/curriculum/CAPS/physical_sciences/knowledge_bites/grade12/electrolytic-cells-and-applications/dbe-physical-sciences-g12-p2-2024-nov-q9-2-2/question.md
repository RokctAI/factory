## Question (2 marks)

Write down the PREDOMINANT oxidation half-reaction that takes place in this cell. (The cell: an electrolytic cell with two carbon electrodes in concentrated NaCℓ(aq) connected to a power source.)

## Method

oxidation half reaction

## Memo working

Memo notes: Cℓ2 + 2e⁻ ← 2Cℓ⁻ (2/2); 2Cℓ⁻ ⇌ Cℓ2 + 2e⁻ (1/2); Cℓ2 + 2e⁻ ⇌ 2Cℓ⁻ (0/2); 2Cℓ⁻ ← Cℓ2 + 2e⁻ (0/2). Ignore if charge omitted on electron. If charge (-) omitted on Cℓ⁻ (e.g. 2Cℓ → Cℓ2 + 2e⁻): max 1/2.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

2Cℓ⁻ → Cℓ2 + 2e⁻
