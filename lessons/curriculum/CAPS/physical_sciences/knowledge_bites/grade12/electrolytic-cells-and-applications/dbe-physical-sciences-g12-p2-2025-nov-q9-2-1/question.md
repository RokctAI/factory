## Question (1 mark)

When the switch is closed, it is found after T hours that the amount of Cu2+(aq) ions in the electrolyte changed by 0,05 moles and 0,15 moles of Cu(s) were deposited on electrode Q. How will the concentration of the zinc ions in the electrolyte be affected during the refining of the copper? Choose from INCREASES, DECREASES or REMAINS THE SAME. (Context: the electrolytic cell above is used for the refining of copper; the unrefined copper contains zinc as the only impurity.)
