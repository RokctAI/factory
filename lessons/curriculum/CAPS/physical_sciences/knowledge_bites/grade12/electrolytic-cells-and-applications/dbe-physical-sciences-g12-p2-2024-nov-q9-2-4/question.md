## Question (2 marks)

Explain the answer to QUESTION 9.2.3 by referring to the relative strengths of the oxidising agents involved. (In QUESTION 9.2.3, the products at the cathode of an electrolytic cell containing concentrated NaCℓ(aq) were identified as hydroxide ions/NaOH and hydrogen/H2.)

## Method

oxidising agent strength reasoning

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Water/H2O is a stronger oxidising agent (than Na⁺/sodium ion) and water/H2O will be reduced. OR Na⁺/sodium ion is a weaker oxidising agent than water/H2O and water/H2O will be reduced.
