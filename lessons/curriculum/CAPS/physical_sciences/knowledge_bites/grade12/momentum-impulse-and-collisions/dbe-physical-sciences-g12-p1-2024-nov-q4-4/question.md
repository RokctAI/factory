## Question (2 marks)

(Context: trolleys A, 1,5 kg, and B, 2 kg, are pushed apart from rest by a compressed spring on a frictionless horizontal track; trolley B moves off to the right at 5,42 m·s⁻¹ and trolley A's change in momentum is 10,84 kg·m·s⁻¹ to the left.) Calculate the speed of trolley A after t seconds.

## Method

conservation of momentum

## Memo working

POSITIVE MARKING FROM QUESTIONS 4.2 and 4.3.1. Route one, from trolley A's change in momentum: ΔpA = mvf – mvi, so with right positive, –10,84 = 1,5(vf – 0), giving vf = –7,23 m·s⁻¹ — that is, 7,23 m·s⁻¹ to the left, so the speed is 7,23 m·s⁻¹. Route two, conservation of momentum for the system: the total momentum is zero before release, so Σpi = Σpf gives 0 = 1,5vf + (2)(5,42), and again vf = –7,23 m·s⁻¹, speed 7,23 m·s⁻¹. The lighter trolley moves off faster, in the inverse ratio of the masses.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

v = 7,23 m·s⁻¹
