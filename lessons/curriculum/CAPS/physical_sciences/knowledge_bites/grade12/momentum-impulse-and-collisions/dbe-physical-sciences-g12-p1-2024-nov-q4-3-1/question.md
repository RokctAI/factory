## Question (3 marks)

(Context: trolleys A, 1,5 kg, and B, 2 kg, are released from rest with a compressed spring between them on a frictionless horizontal track; trolley B moves off to the right at 5,42 m·s⁻¹.) For the t seconds that the spring takes to return to its natural length: Calculate the change in momentum of trolley B.

## Method

change in momentum

## Memo working

POSITIVE MARKING FROM QUESTION 4.2 — use the 5,42 m·s⁻¹ found there. Change in momentum is final momentum minus initial momentum: Δp = mvf – mvi. Trolley B starts at rest, so vi = 0, and leaves the spring at 5,42 m·s⁻¹ to the right: Δp = 2(5,42 – 0) = 10,84 kg·m·s⁻¹ to the right. Momentum is a vector, so the direction must be stated. (Taking right as negative instead gives 2(–5,42 – 0) = –10,84 kg·m·s⁻¹, the same change expressed with the opposite sign convention.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Δp = 10,84 kg·m·s⁻¹ to the right
