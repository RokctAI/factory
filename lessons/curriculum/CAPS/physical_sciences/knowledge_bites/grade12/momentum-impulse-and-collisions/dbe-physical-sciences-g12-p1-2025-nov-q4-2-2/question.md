## Question (4 marks)

Calculate the magnitude of the initial velocity of the ball. (The 150 g = 0,15 kg cricket ball is struck with an average net force of 225 N — from Q4.2.1; the pf versus Δt graph passes through (0,01; 0) and (0,03; 4,5).)

## Method

impulse momentum change

## Memo working

POSITIVE MARKING FROM 4.2.1. Use the impulse-momentum theorem FnetΔt = Δp = mvf − mvi (any form). At the point (0,03; 4,5): (225)(0,03) = 4,5 − (0,15)vi, so 6,75 = 4,5 − 0,15vi and vi = −15 m·s⁻¹; the magnitude is 15 m·s⁻¹. (Also accepted: (225)(0,01) = 0 − (0,15)vi; or via pi = FnetΔt subtracted from pf; or via a = Fnet/m = 1500 m·s⁻² with vf = 30 m·s⁻¹.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

vi = 15 m·s⁻¹
