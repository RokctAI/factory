## Question (3 marks)

Calculate the average net force acting on the ball. (A 150 g cricket ball is struck by a bat with a constant average net force; the graph of final momentum pf in kg·m·s⁻¹ versus contact time Δt in s is a straight line through the points (0,01; 0) and (0,03; 4,5).)

## Method

impulse momentum gradient

## Memo working

Option 1: The gradient of a pf versus Δt graph equals the net force. Gradient = Fnet = Δpf/Δ(Δt) = (4,5 − 0)/(0,03 − 0,01) = 225 N. (Memo options 2/3: apply FnetΔt = Δp at the two graph points — Fnet(0,01) = 0 − pi and Fnet(0,03) = 4,5 − pi — and solve simultaneously, which also gives Fnet = 225 N to the right / opposite to the original direction of the ball.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Fnet = 225 N to the right / opposite to the original direction of the ball
