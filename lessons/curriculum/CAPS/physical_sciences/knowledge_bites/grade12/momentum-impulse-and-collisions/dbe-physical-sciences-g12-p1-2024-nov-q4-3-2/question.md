## Question (1 mark)

(Context: trolleys A, 1,5 kg, and B, 2 kg, are pushed apart from rest by a compressed spring on a frictionless horizontal track; trolley B's change in momentum is 10,84 kg·m·s⁻¹ to the right.) For the t seconds that the spring takes to return to its natural length: Write down the change in momentum of trolley A.

## Method

newton third law momentum

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) By Newton's third law the spring pushes the two trolleys with equal and opposite forces for the same time t, so the trolleys receive equal and opposite impulses. Equivalently, total momentum is conserved and starts at zero, so trolley A's change in momentum must exactly cancel trolley B's. Trolley A therefore gains momentum of the same magnitude, 10,84 kg·m·s⁻¹, but directed to the left. No calculation is needed — write it down.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

10,84 kg·m·s⁻¹ left / opposite direction. (POSITIVE MARKING FROM QUESTION 4.3.1.)
