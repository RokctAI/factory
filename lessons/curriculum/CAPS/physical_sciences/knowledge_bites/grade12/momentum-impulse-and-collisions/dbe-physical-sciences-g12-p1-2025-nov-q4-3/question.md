## Question (2 marks)

Redraw the graph in the ANSWER BOOK and label it as A. On the same set of axes, draw the graph that will be obtained when a ball with a bigger mass is used, without changing the initial velocity and average net force. Label this as B. (Graph A is the straight line of final momentum pf versus contact time Δt through (0,01; 0) and (0,03; 4,5) from the cricket-ball experiment.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Graph B is a straight line parallel to graph A (same gradient = same net force) shifted to the right: it cuts the Δt-axis at a larger contact time because the initial momentum has a larger magnitude.
