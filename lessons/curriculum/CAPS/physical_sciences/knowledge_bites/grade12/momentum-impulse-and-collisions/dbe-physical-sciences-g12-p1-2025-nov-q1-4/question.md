## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: Objects P and Q, with masses m and 2m respectively, have the same momentum. (Diagram: small sphere P and larger sphere Q.) The velocity of P is … A equal to the velocity of Q. B half the velocity of Q. C twice the velocity of Q. D four times the velocity of Q.

## Method

multiple choice

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C (twice the velocity of Q: for equal momentum p = mv, halving the mass doubles the velocity)
