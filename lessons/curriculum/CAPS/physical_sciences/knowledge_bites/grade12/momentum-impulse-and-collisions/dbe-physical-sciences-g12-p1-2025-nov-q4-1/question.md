## Question (2 marks)

Cricketers at an academy conduct an experiment to determine the relationship between the contact time and the final momentum of a ball for a constant average net force. A cricket ball of mass 150 g is thrown horizontally with a certain initial velocity and is struck by a bat so that the ball moves in the opposite direction. The average net force acting on the ball is horizontal. (Diagram: bat striking a ball; initial velocity vi towards the bat, final velocity vf away from the bat.) The experiment is repeated using the same ball. The average net force and the initial velocity of the ball are kept constant. The contact time between the bat and the ball is changed EACH time. The results are plotted as a straight-line graph of final momentum pf in kg·m·s⁻¹ versus contact time Δt in s, passing through the points (0,01; 0) and (0,03; 4,5). Define the term impulse.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The product of the resultant/net force acting on an object and the time the (net) force acts on the object. OR: The product of the resultant/net force and the contact time.
