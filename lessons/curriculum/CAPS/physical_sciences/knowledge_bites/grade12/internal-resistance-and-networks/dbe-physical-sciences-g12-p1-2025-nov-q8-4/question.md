## Question (6 marks)

A car battery of unknown emf (Ɛ) and internal resistance (r) is connected to two identical parallel headlights (12 Ω each, switched by S1) and a starter motor (0,1 Ω, switched by S2). With S1 open and S2 closed, the motor current is 120 A. With S1 closed and S2 open, the total headlight current is 2,24 A. Calculate the emf of the battery.

## Method

emf internal resistance simultaneous

## Memo working

First combine the two parallel headlights: 1/Rp = 1/12 + 1/12, so Rp = 6 Ω.

With S1 closed and S2 open, the emf equation is Ɛ = I(R + r) = 2,24(6 + r). With S1 open and S2 closed, the same battery gives Ɛ = 120(0,1 + r). The emf is the same in both cases, so equate: 2,24(6 + r) = 120(0,1 + r), which gives r = 0,012 Ω.

Substitute back: Ɛ = 2,24(6 + 0,012) = 13,46 V (or Ɛ = 120(0,1 + 0,012) = 13,47 V). (Memo option 2 uses Ɛ = 12 + 120r and Ɛ = 13,42 + 2,24r, from V = 13,42 V across the headlights, giving 13,45 - 13,47 V. POSITIVE MARKING FROM QUESTIONS 8.2 AND 8.3.2.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Ɛ = 13,46 V [accepted RANGE: 13,42 V - 13,47 V]
