## Question (3 marks)

A car battery is connected to two identical parallel headlights, L1 and L2 (12 Ω each, in series with switch S1 and ammeter A1), and to a starter motor M (0,1 Ω, in series with switch S2 and ammeter A2). Switch S1 is now closed and switch S2 is opened. The power dissipated by each headlight is 15 W. Calculate the current passing through L1.

## Method

power current resistor

## Memo working

The power dissipated in a resistor is P = I²R. For headlight L1: 15 = I²(12), so I² = 1,25 and I = 1,12 A. (Also accepted: P = V²/R gives V = 13,42 V across the headlight, then P = VI or V = IR gives I = 1,12 A.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

I = 1,12 A
