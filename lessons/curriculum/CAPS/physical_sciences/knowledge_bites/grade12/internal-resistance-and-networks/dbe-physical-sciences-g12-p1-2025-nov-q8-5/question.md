## Question (5 marks)

A car battery with internal resistance r is connected to two parallel headlights (12 Ω each, in series with switch S1 and ammeter A1) and a starter motor (0,1 Ω, in series with switch S2 and ammeter A2). Both switches are now closed. How will the reading on ammeter A1 be affected? Choose from INCREASES, DECREASES or REMAINS THE SAME. Explain the answer WITHOUT the use of a calculation.

## Method

circuit analysis after change

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) (Memo note: a calculation given as explanation scores 1/5. Memo also accepts, for a maximum of 3/5: Increases — the initial reading of A1 is 0 A when switch S1 is open, and with both switches closed current now flows through the circuit — only if the explanation refers to the situation of Question 8.2.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Decrease. Total/external resistance decreases; total current increases; V(internal) increases; voltage across the light bulbs decreases / V(external) decreases.
