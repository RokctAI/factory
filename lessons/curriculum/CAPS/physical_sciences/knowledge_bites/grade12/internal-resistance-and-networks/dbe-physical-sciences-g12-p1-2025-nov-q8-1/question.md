## Question (2 marks)

Two identical headlights, L1 and L2, and a starter motor, M, of a car are connected to a battery. The resistance of each headlight is 12 Ω, while the resistance of the starter motor is 0,1 Ω. The emf (Ɛ) and internal resistance (r) of the battery are unknown. The ammeters and the connecting wires have negligible resistance, while the voltmeter has a high resistance. Switches S1 and S2 are initially open. (Circuit: headlights L1 and L2 in parallel with each other, in series with switch S1 and ammeter A1 on the left side; the starter motor M in series with switch S2 and ammeter A2 on the right side; a voltmeter V connected across the battery.)

Define the term emf.
