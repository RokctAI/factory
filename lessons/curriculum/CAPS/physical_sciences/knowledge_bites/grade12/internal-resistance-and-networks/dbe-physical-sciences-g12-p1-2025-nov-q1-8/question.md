## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: Three identical light bulbs, X, Y and Z, are connected in a circuit. Switch S is initially closed. Ignore the internal resistance of the battery. (Circuit: battery connected to bulb X in series; after X the circuit splits into two parallel branches, one containing bulb Y and the other containing switch S in series with bulb Z.) Switch S is now opened. How will the brightness of bulbs X and Y be affected? A Brightness of bulb X: Increases, Brightness of bulb Y: Decreases B Brightness of bulb X: Decreases, Brightness of bulb Y: Increases C Brightness of bulb X: Increases, Brightness of bulb Y: Increases D Brightness of bulb X: Decreases, Brightness of bulb Y: Decreases

## Method

multiple choice

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

B (opening S removes the Z branch, so the total resistance increases and the current through X decreases — X dims; but Y now carries the full circuit current instead of sharing it with Z, so Y brightens)
