## Question (3 marks)

Light bulbs L1 (36 W ; 20 V) and L2 (48 W ; 32 V) are connected in a circuit (battery with internal resistance 0,6 Ω; R2 = 4 Ω in series with a parallel section; branch 1 contains ammeter A2 and bulb L2, branch 2 contains resistor R1 in series with bulb L1; ammeter A1 is in the main circuit; from Question 8.2.1 the current through L2 is 1,5 A). If both light bulbs operate as RATED, calculate the reading on ammeter A1.

## Method

parallel branch currents

## Memo working

POSITIVE MARKING FROM QUESTION 8.2.1. A1 reads the total current, the sum of the two branch currents. For L1 at its rating: P = VI, so 36 = 20I, giving I = 1,8 A (or via R = 20²/36 = 11,11 Ω). Then ITOT = 1,5 + 1,8 = 3,3 A.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

ITOT = 1,5 + 1,8 = 3,3 A
