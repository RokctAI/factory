## Question (4 marks)

Light bulbs L1 (36 W ; 20 V) and L2 (48 W ; 32 V) are connected in a circuit (battery with internal resistance 0,6 Ω; R2 = 4 Ω in series with a parallel section; branch 1 contains ammeter A2 and bulb L2, branch 2 contains resistor R1 in series with bulb L1; from Question 8.2.2 the current through L1's branch is 1,8 A). If both light bulbs operate as RATED, calculate the resistance of resistor R1.

## Method

series parallel resistance

## Memo working

POSITIVE MARKING FROM QUESTION 8.2.2. OPTION 1: The two parallel branches share the same potential difference, which equals L2's rated 32 V. In L1's branch: VR1 + VL1 = V(parallel), so VR1 + 20 = 32, giving VR1 = 12 V. Then V = IR with the branch current: 12 = 1,8R1, giving R1 = 6,67 Ω. (Memo option 2: 32 = 1,8R gives the whole branch resistance R = 17,77 Ω; RL1 = 20²/36 = 11,11 Ω; then R1 = 17,77 – 11,11 = 6,67 Ω.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R1 = 6,67 Ω
