## Question (2 marks)

L1 and L2 are two light bulbs that have the following ratings: L1: 36 W ; 20 V and L2: 48 W ; 32 V. The two bulbs are connected in a circuit (circuit diagram: battery with emf ε and internal resistance r = 0,6 Ω; ammeter A1 in the main circuit; resistor R2 = 4 Ω in series with the parallel section; the parallel section has two branches — branch 1 contains ammeter A2 and bulb L2, branch 2 contains resistor R1 in series with bulb L1; the conducting wires and ammeters have negligible resistance). Define the term power.

define power
