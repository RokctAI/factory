## Question (4 marks)

Light bulbs L1 (36 W ; 20 V) and L2 (48 W ; 32 V) are connected in a circuit (battery with emf ε and internal resistance r = 0,6 Ω; R2 = 4 Ω in series with the parallel section containing L2 in one branch and R1 with L1 in the other; from Question 8.2.2 the total current is 3,3 A and from Question 8.2.3 R1 = 6,67 Ω). If both light bulbs operate as RATED, calculate the emf of the battery.

## Method

emf internal resistance

## Memo working

POSITIVE MARKING FROM QUESTIONS 8.2.2 and 8.2.3. OPTION 1: The series resistor's potential difference is V2 = ITOT·R2 = 3,3(4) = 13,2 V. The external voltage is V2 plus the 32 V across the parallel section, so ε = Vext + Ir = (13,2 + 32) + 3,3(0,6) = 47,18 V. (Memo options 2/3: combine resistances first — Rp = (21,33)(11,11 + 6,67)/(21,33 + 11,11 + 6,67) = 9,69 Ω; Rext = 9,69 + 4 = 13,69 Ω; then ε = I(R + r) = 3,3(13,69) + 3,3(0,6) = 47,18 V.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

ε = 47,18 V (accepted range 47,16 – 47,19 V)
