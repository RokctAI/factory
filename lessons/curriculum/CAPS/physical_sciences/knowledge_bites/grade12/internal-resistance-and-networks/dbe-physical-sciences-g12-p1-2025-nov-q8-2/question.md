## Question (3 marks)

A car battery is connected to two parallel headlights (12 Ω each, in series with switch S1 and ammeter A1) and to a starter motor M of resistance 0,1 Ω (in series with switch S2 and ammeter A2). A voltmeter is connected across the battery. Switch S1 remains open while switch S2 is closed. The reading on ammeter A2 is 120 A. Calculate the reading on the voltmeter.

## Method

ohms law

## Memo working

With S1 open, no current flows through the headlights; the entire external circuit is the starter motor. The voltmeter across the battery therefore reads the potential difference across the motor. Apply Ohm's law to the motor: R = V/I, so 0,1 = V/120, giving V = 12 V.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

V = 12 V
