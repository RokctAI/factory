## Question (3 marks)

Light bulbs L1 (36 W ; 20 V) and L2 (48 W ; 32 V) are connected in a circuit (battery with internal resistance 0,6 Ω; R2 = 4 Ω in series with a parallel section; branch 1 contains ammeter A2 and bulb L2, branch 2 contains resistor R1 in series with bulb L1). If both light bulbs operate as RATED, calculate the reading on ammeter A2.

## Method

power rating current

## Memo working

A2 reads the current through L2, which operates at its rating of 48 W at 32 V. OPTION 1: P = VI, so 48 = 32I, giving I = 1,5 A. (Memo option 2: P = V²/R, so 48 = 32²/R, giving R = 21,33 Ω; then P = I²R or V = IR gives I = 1,5 A.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

I = 1,5 A
