## Question (1 mark)

With switch S1 closed and switch S2 open, each of the two identical parallel headlights carries a current of 1,12 A. Write down the reading on ammeter A1 (the ammeter in the main branch feeding both headlights).

## Method

parallel branch currents

## Memo working

Ammeter A1 carries the total current of the two identical parallel branches, so its reading is twice the current in one headlight. (Memo: POSITIVE MARKING FROM QUESTION 8.3.1.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A1 = (1,12)(2) = 2,24 A
