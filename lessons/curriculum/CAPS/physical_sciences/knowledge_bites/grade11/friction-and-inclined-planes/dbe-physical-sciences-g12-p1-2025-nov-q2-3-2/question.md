## Question (5 marks)

The horizontal component of force F is 15 N. Calculate the coefficient of static friction. (A 5 kg block at rest on a rough horizontal surface experiences maximum static friction while a constant force F of 18 N pulls at θ = 33,56° above the horizontal — the angle found in Q2.3.1.)

## Method

static friction coefficient

## Memo working

POSITIVE MARKING FROM QUESTION 2.3.1. Start from fs(max) = µsN. The block is in equilibrium, so the maximum static friction balances the horizontal component of F: fs(max) = Fx = 15 N. The upward component of F reduces the normal force: N = mg − Fsin θ = (5)(9,8) − 18sin 33,56° = 39,05 N. Then 15 = µs(39,05), so µs = 0,38. (Marking criteria: formula fs(max) = µsN; correct substitution of fs(max); correct calculation of Fy; subtraction for the calculation of N from Fg, i.e. (5)(9,8) − (18sin 33,56°); correct final answer 0,38. NOTE: if the N calculation is not shown, 3/5.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

µs = 0,38
