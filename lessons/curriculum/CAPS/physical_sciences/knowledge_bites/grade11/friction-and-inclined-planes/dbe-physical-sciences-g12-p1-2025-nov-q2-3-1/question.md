## Question (2 marks)

The horizontal component of force F is 15 N. Calculate θ. (A constant force F of magnitude 18 N acts on a 5 kg block on a rough horizontal surface, pulling at an angle θ above the horizontal.)

## Method

force components

## Memo working

The horizontal component of a force at angle θ is Fx = Fcos θ. Substitute the values: 15 = 18 cos θ, so cos θ = 15/18 and θ = 33,56°. (Memo alternatives: 9,95 = 15 tan θ, θ = 33,56°; or 9,95 = 18 sin θ, θ = 33,56°. NOTE: if answer only, 1/2.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

θ = 33,56°
