## Question (4 marks)

Angle θ is decreased while the magnitude of force F remains constant. How will the friction acting on the block be affected? Choose from INCREASES, DECREASES or REMAINS THE SAME. Explain the answer. (A 5 kg block on a rough horizontal surface is pulled by F = 18 N at angle θ above the horizontal and experiences maximum static friction.)

## Method

friction normal force reasoning

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Increases. The vertical component of F decreases; the normal force increases; µs remains constant and fs(max) is directly proportional to N. (Memo also accepts: Decreases — the horizontal component of F increases, the block will move, Fnet will increase and fk < fs(max); or Remains the same — only if explained with a relevant calculation in which the candidate chooses µk < µs and a θ that together with the µk value gives an answer of 15 N for fk.)
