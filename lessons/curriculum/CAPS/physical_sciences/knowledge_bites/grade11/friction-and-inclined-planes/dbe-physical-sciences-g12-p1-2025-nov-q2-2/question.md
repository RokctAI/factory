## Question (4 marks)

Draw a labelled free-body diagram showing ALL the forces acting on the block. (A block of mass 5 kg is at rest on a rough horizontal surface; a constant force F of magnitude 18 N acts on the block at an angle θ above the horizontal, and the block experiences maximum static friction.)

## Method

free body diagram

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Four forces on the block: w (weight, vertically down; accepted labels Fg/Fw/weight/mg/49 N/gravitational force), N (normal force, vertically up), F (applied force 18 N at angle θ above the horizontal), fs(max) (static friction, horizontal, opposite to the horizontal component of F). (Memo note: each mark is awarded for label and arrow; fk is not accepted as a label; any additional force(s), missing arrows, or forces not making contact with the dot: maximum 3/4.)
