## Question (3 marks)

P is a +2 nC point charge. X is a point 6 cm away from charge P (diagram: charge P, +2 nC, with point X 6 cm to its right). Calculate the magnitude of the electric field at X.

## Method

electric field point charge

## Memo working

Convert the distance to metres (6 cm = 0,06 m) and the charge to coulombs (2 nC = 2 x 10⁻⁹ C), then apply the point-charge field equation: E = kQ/r² = (9 x 10⁹)(2 x 10⁻⁹)/(0,06)² = 5 x 10³ N·C⁻¹.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

E = 5 x 10³ N·C⁻¹ (5 000 N·C⁻¹)
