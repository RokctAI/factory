## Question (2 marks)

Sphere X carries a charge of +3 x 10⁻⁷ C. Draw the electric field pattern due to the charge on sphere X.

## Method

electric field pattern sketch

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) (Memo criteria: correct direction; field lines radially outward. Deduct 1 mark if two or more field lines are inside the sphere, do not touch the sphere, or cross each other. If more than one charge is drawn: 0/2.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Field lines radially outward from the positive sphere, with arrows pointing away from the sphere.
