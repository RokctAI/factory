## Question (3 marks)

Sphere X, carrying a charge of +3 x 10⁻⁷ C, is at point A. Point P is r metres to the left of point A, and the magnitude of the electric field at point P is 1,08 x 10⁶ N·C⁻¹.

Show, by means of a calculation, that r = 0,05 m.

## Method

electric field point charge

## Memo working

The electric field of a point charge is E = kQ/r². Substituting the known field and charge: 1,08 x 10⁶ = (9 x 10⁹)(3 x 10⁻⁷)/r². Solving for r²: r² = (9 x 10⁹)(3 x 10⁻⁷)/(1,08 x 10⁶) = 2,5 x 10⁻³, so r = 0,05 m.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

r = 0,05 m
