## Question (3 marks)

P is a +2 nC point charge. Point charge S, with a charge of −2 nC, is placed 4 cm to the right of charge P (diagram: P, +2 nC, and S, −2 nC, 4 cm apart). Draw the resultant electric field pattern due to charges P and S.

## Method

electric field pattern sketch

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Sketch the field pattern of two unlike charges: field lines leave the positive charge P and enter the negative charge S, curving between them, with additional outer lines. Marking criteria: correct direction of field lines; correct shape of the electric field lines between the charges and on the outside of the charges; no field lines crossing each other; field lines must touch the charge but not go inside the charge. (Note: field pattern of two like charges 0/3; only one charge drawn 0/3.)
