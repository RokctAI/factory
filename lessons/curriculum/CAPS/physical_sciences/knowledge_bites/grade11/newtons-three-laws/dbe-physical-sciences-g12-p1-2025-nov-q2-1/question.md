## Question (2 marks)

A block of mass 5 kg is at rest on a rough horizontal surface. When a constant force F of magnitude 18 N acts on the block at an angle θ to the horizontal, the block experiences maximum static friction. (Diagram: 5 kg block on a horizontal surface with force F = 18 N pulling upward at angle θ above the horizontal.) State Newton's Second Law of Motion in words.

## Method

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

When a net/resultant force acts on an object, the object will accelerate in the direction of the force and the acceleration is directly proportional to the force and inversely proportional to the mass of the object. (Also accepted, as an all-or-nothing 2 or 0: The net/resultant force acting on an object is equal to the rate of change of momentum of the object, in the direction of the net force.)
