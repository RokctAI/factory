## Question (2 marks)

Hence, determine the net force applied on the object during the experiment (graph of 1/a versus m, gradient 2).

## Method

newtons second law application

## Memo working

From F_net = ma: 1/a = m/F_net, so gradient = 1/F_net = 2, giving F_net = 1/2 = 0,5 N. (Memo option 2: F_net = ma = (1)(1/2) = 0,5 N.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

F_net = 0,5 N
