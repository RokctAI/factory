## Question (1 mark)

P is a +2 nC point charge and S, a −2 nC point charge, is 4 cm to the right of P. A third point charge T, placed 2 cm to the right of S, experiences a net electrostatic force of 2,5 x 10⁻⁴ N to the left. What is the polarity of charge T? Choose from POSITIVE or NEGATIVE.
