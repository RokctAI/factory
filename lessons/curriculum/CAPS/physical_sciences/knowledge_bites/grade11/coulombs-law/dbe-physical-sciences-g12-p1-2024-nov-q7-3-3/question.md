## Question (5 marks)

P is a +2 nC point charge and S, a −2 nC point charge, is 4 cm to the right of P. A third point charge T, placed 2 cm to the right of S, experiences a net electrostatic force of 2,5 x 10⁻⁴ N to the left. Calculate the magnitude of charge T.

## Method

coulombs law net force

## Memo working

POSITIVE MARKING FROM Q7.1 IN TERMS OF CONVERSIONS. OPTION 1: Write Coulomb's law F = kQ1Q2/r² for each force on T. S is 2 cm (0,02 m) from T, so FST = (9 x 10⁹)(2 x 10⁻⁹)QT/(0,02)²; P is 6 cm (0,06 m) from T, so FPT = (9 x 10⁹)(2 x 10⁻⁹)QT/(0,06)². The forces oppose each other, so Fnet = FST – FPT: 2,5 x 10⁻⁴ = (9 x 10⁹)(2 x 10⁻⁹)QT/(0,02)² − (9 x 10⁹)(2 x 10⁻⁹)QT/(0,06)², giving QT = 6,25 x 10⁻⁹ C. (Memo option 2: work with fields at T — ES = (9 x 10⁹)(2 x 10⁻⁹)/0,02² = 45 000 N·C⁻¹; Enet = EP – ES = 5 x 10³ − 45 000 = –4 x 10⁴ N·C⁻¹; then F = Eq: –2,5 x 10⁻⁴ = (–4 x 10⁴)QT, giving QT = 6,25 x 10⁻⁹ C.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

QT = 6,25 x 10⁻⁹ C
