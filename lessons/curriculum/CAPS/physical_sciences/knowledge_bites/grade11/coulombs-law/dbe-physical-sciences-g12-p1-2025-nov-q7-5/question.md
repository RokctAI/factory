## Question (2 marks)

Sphere X carries a charge of +3 x 10⁻⁷ C and sphere Y, fixed 0,05 m away, carries a charge of −5 x 10⁻⁷ C. Sphere Y is brought into contact with sphere X, and is placed back in its original position. How will the magnitude of the force that sphere X now exerts on sphere Y be affected? Choose from INCREASES, DECREASES or REMAINS THE SAME.
