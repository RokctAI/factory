## Question (2 marks)

Multiple choice: Three point charges, Q1, Q2 and Q3, are fixed in a straight line. Q1 is r metres from Q2, while Q3 is 2r metres from Q2. (Diagram: Q1, Q2 and Q3 on a straight line; distance Q1 to Q2 is r; distance Q2 to Q3 is 2r, so Q1 to Q3 is 3r.) The magnitude of the charge on Q2 is q. The net electrostatic force on charge Q1 is zero. What is the magnitude of charge Q3 in terms of q? A (1/9)q B (1/3)q C 3q D 9q

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

D (9q: for zero net force on Q1, Coulomb's law gives kqQ1/r² = kQ3Q1/(3r)², and since Q3 sits 3r from Q1 — nine times weaker per unit charge — Q3 must be 9q)
