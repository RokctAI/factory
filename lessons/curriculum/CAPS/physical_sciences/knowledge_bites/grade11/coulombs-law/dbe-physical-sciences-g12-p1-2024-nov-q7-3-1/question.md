## Question (2 marks)

P is a +2 nC point charge and S, a −2 nC point charge, is 4 cm to the right of P. A third point charge T is placed 2 cm to the right of S. Point charge T experiences a net electrostatic force of 2,5 x 10⁻⁴ N to the left (diagram: P, +2 nC; S, −2 nC, 4 cm to the right of P; and T, 2 cm to the right of S). State Coulomb's law in words.

## Method

state coulombs law

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The magnitude of the electrostatic force exerted by one point charge on another point charge is directly proportional to the product of the magnitudes of their charges and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them.
