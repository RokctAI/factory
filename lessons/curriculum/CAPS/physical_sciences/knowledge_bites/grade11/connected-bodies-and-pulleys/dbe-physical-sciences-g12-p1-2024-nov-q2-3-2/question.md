## Question (5 marks)

(Context: a crate of mass 8,5 kg on a rough horizontal table is connected by a light inextensible string over a frictionless pulley to a hanging mass; the system now accelerates.) Calculate the magnitude of the acceleration represented by Y on the graph if the coefficient of kinetic friction between the crate and the table is 0,40. [Y is the acceleration at a hanging mass of 7,4 kg.]

## Method

connected bodies newton second law

## Memo working

Treat each body separately and apply Newton's second law to each, remembering the string gives both the same acceleration a and the same tension T. For the crate on the table: Fnet = ma, so T – fk = ma, where kinetic friction fk = µkN = 0,4(8,5)(9,8) = 33,32 N. That gives T – 0,4(8,5)(9,8) = 8,5a — equation one. For the hanging mass: Fnet = ma downwards, so mg – T = ma, giving 7,4(9,8) – T = 7,4a — equation two. Add the two equations so T cancels: 7,4(9,8) – 33,32 = (8,5 + 7,4)a, that is 39,2 = 15,9a, so a = 2,47 m·s⁻². (Memo note: T can be calculated first, 54,32 N.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Y = a = 2,47 m·s⁻²
