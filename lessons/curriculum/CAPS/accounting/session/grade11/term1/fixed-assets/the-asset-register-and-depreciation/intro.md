Today we turn fixed assets into a system: the fixed asset register that holds one page of detail per asset behind the ledger's totals, and depreciation raised to Grade 11 level, where assets arrive part-way through the year. We will build the true cost of an acquisition — invoice plus delivery plus installation — separate capital from revenue expenditure, work both depreciation methods, and master the part-year fraction that markers reward on its own. We close with fully depreciated assets and the controls and ethics that keep valuable, portable property honest. Every disposal question in the next lesson reads its figures straight off a register page, so this is the spine of the whole topic.
