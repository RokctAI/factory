Today we take on the disposal of fixed assets — what must happen in the books when a vehicle or piece of equipment is sold, traded in, scrapped or taken home by the owner. Every disposal gathers three pieces in one place — the cost, the accumulated depreciation and the proceeds — and the balancing figure is a profit or loss that belongs on the income statement. We will drill the four-step procedure that never varies, work a full diminishing-balance example through the Asset Disposal account, handle the four kinds of disposal and the timing rules, and close with the register and the controls that stop an asset simply walking out. This is one of the most reliably examined procedures in Grade 11, and the part-year depreciation in step one is where the marks live.
