Today the business gets a second owner, and the books must answer a question a sole trader never faces: whose money is whose? We split each partner's interest into a fixed capital account and a moving current account, read what the partnership agreement promises each partner, and then run the full set of year-end adjustments — the six revised from Grade 10 plus the two new to Grade 11, the provision for bad debts and capitalised interest on a loan. We finish with the reversals that open the new year clean, so every expense and income is counted exactly once. These adjustments feed directly into the final accounts and appropriation in the next session, and they are tested every single year.
