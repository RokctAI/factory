Today we close the partnership's year and share its profit — the step that separates partnership accounting from everything closed off in Grade 10. We run the closing sequence through the trading account and the profit and loss account to a net profit of R421 000, then execute the partnership agreement rand by rand in the appropriation account: interest on capital, salaries, the bonus, and the remaining profit split in ratio. From there the earnings land on each partner's current account, written up in full, and we finish with the accounting-equation analysis examiners attach to every partnership transaction. The single most valuable rule of the session: partners' salaries and interest on capital are appropriations of profit, never expenses.
