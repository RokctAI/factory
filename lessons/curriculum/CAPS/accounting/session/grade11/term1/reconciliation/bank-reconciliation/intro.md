Today we test the trust Grade 10 placed in the Bank account. Two independent records exist of the same money — the business's cash journals and the bank's monthly statement — and they will not agree, so we learn the three-way sort that governs the whole topic: timing differences, our omissions, and errors. We work Bushveld Hardware's February statement item by item, journalise what belongs in our books, correct the balance to the figure the statements will carry, and then build the Bank Reconciliation Statement that bridges our number to the bank's. One question decides every add and subtract in that bridge — what will the bank still do to this balance — and reasoning it, rather than reciting it, is what the examination rewards.
