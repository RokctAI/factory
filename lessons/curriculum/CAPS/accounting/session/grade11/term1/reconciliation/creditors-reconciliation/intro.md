Today we repeat the reconciliation discipline on a different outsider: the supplier. Their monthly statement says what their books think we owe; our creditors ledger says what our books think we owe; and the two will differ for the same three reasons the bank taught us — timing, omissions, and errors — with one crucial twist: the supplier can be wrong too, so a statement is a claim to be tested, never a command to be paid. We work Bushveld Hardware's account with Limpopo Timber item by item, correct our own books where they are short, build the Creditors Reconciliation Statement that bridges the two balances, and finish with the traps and the control argument. This is the routine that stops a business quietly paying money it does not owe.
