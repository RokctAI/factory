Today we take the unit cost from the last lesson and turn it into the sharpest decision tool in this course: break-even analysis. The question is the one every owner actually loses sleep over — how many units must be sold before the business stops losing money — and the answer comes through contribution per unit, the amount each sale tosses onto the pile of fixed costs. We will formalise fixed and variable behaviour, build the four-line contribution statement, compute Kagiso Footwear's break-even point in units and in rand, and then move the line with what-ifs on price and costs. Learn why the answer always rounds up, and the calculation, interpretation and discussion marks are all yours.
