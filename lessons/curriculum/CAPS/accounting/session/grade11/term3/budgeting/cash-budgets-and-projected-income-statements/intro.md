Today we build the two forecasts every business keeps for the same months — the projected Statement of Comprehensive Income, which predicts profit on the accrual basis, and the cash budget, which predicts the bank balance on the cash basis — and we learn why they constantly disagree. The engine of the topic is the debtors' collection schedule: one month's receipts drawing on three different months' sales, with the settlement discount applied only to the portion that earns it and the bad-debt slice sent to the other document entirely. We work a full quarter, explain how a business can be profitable and cash-strapped in the same month, and close with variance analysis and the ethics of forecasts — because a budget nobody checks is a wish, and a budget built wrong on purpose is a lie.
