Today the line at the bottom of every South African till slip stops being invisible: Value Added Tax — why it exists, how it collects at every stage of the chain, and the four calculations the examination demands. We will separate standard-rated, zero-rated and exempt supplies with the one question that never fails — may the seller reclaim its own input tax — and drill the examiners' favourite trap, extracting VAT from an inclusive amount with fifteen over one hundred and fifteen. We finish with returns, discounts and bad debts, and the ethics of a tax that is trust money: collected from customers on the state's behalf and never the vendor's to spend. The arithmetic is short; the classification is where the marks are won or lost.
