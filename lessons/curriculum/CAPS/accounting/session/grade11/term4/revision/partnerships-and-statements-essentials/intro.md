Today we revise the centrepiece of the grade 11 year: the partnership, rebuilt end to end through one business — Zola and Sipho, trading as ZS Traders. We run the pair of accounts each partner owns, divide R240 000 of profit through the appropriation account until it empties to the rand, draw the financial statements with the four-step asset disposal inside them, and finish by reading the indicators the way a creditor, a lender and a partner each would. Every mark in a partnership question comes from keeping two distinctions sharp — capital against current, and expenses against appropriations — so this session drills both until they are reflex.
