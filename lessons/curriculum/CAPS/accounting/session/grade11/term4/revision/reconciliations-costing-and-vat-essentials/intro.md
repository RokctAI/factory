Today we revise the grade 11 toolkit — the four skills that surround any set of books. Reconciliation forces two records of one truth to meet on the same figure; costing finds the contribution per unit and the break-even line where production starts paying; the stock systems and forward documents value the shelf and write the months ahead; and VAT is the tax a business collects without ever owning it. Each station carries a fully worked example, from the bank balance of R10 400 proved from both directions to the R600 of VAT hiding inside a R4 600 slip. These four are the examiners' favourite short questions, and by the end each one should be a reflex.
