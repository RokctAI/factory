Today we learn to read financial statements the way an examiner rewards: turning figures into indicators and indicators into comments. We revise the profitability percentages, then add Grade 11's four operating-cycle clocks — stock turnover rate, stock holding period, debtors' collection period and creditors' payment period — and the risk-and-reward pair, the debt-equity ratio and return on average partners' equity. Every figure comes from the statements built this term, and every indicator is only as good as its comparison: last year, the policy, the norm, the rival. The session closes with the four-part comment discipline — figure, comparison, cause, advice — because interpretation carries more marks than calculation, and it is marked to exactly that pattern.
