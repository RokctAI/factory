Truth: you already knew this system before class. The exercise book under the counter? That's the Debtors Ledger. "Write it on my page"? That's a debit to a personal account. Today just added the official papers — invoice for the promise, credit note for the change of heart — and one summary line so the owner sees the whole street at a glance.

Two anchors for the practice questions. Anchor one: every credit event goes to TWO places — the person's page (daily) and the summary totals (monthly). Say "page AND totals" until it's automatic. Anchor two: when things come back, follow the GOODS — shirt back on the shelf, cost column writes; kettle stays with the customer, cost column sleeps.

And the month-end lock — pages adding up to the control line — is your friend, not a trap: when it clicks, you KNOW your month is right. Go work the questions page by page. You've got this!
