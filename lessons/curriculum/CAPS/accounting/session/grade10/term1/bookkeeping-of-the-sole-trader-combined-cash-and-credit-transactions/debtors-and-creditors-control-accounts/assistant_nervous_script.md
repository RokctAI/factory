Hey, Thandi here — don't let those journal abbreviations scare you off.

DJ, CRJ, DAJ... I know it looks like alphabet soup, but every single entry in that control account came down to one tiny question: did the debtors end up owing us more, or less? Sales on credit? More — debit side. They paid us, returned goods, or we wrote off a bad debt? Less — credit side. That's genuinely the whole game.

And balancing the account is nothing new — it's the exact same balancing you've done with every ledger account since Grade 8. The only fresh idea today is the check at the end: the control balance must match the list of debtors' balances. If that clicked even a little, you're ready.

So grab some scrap paper, redo the R48 200 example slowly, and then hit the practice questions. Getting one wrong just shows you which side of that one question — more or less? — to look at again. You can absolutely do this.
