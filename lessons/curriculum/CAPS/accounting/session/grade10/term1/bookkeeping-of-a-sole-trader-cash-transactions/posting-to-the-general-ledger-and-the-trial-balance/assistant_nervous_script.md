Hey, it's Thandi — posting day is the one where Accounting suddenly feels like a LOT of moving pieces, so if you're overwhelmed, you're exactly on schedule. Everyone is, the first time.

Here's the calming truth: nothing new happened today. Every "posting" was just a column total you already computed, walking to a drawer you already know, landing on a side you already learned in the double-entry lesson. Asset up, left. Income, right. Expense, left. The month was already sorted — today it just went home.

And you have TWO safety nets stacked: the journals' handshake caught errors before posting, and the trial balance weighs the whole cabinet after. When those scales sit level at R56 900 both sides, that's not luck — that's every entry you made all month agreeing with itself. Very few school subjects hand you proof like that.

So take the practice questions drawer by drawer: post the total, say the side out loud, balance the account, then weigh everything. If the scales tip, read the gap — equal, halvable, or divisible by nine — before re-adding. You've got this, and the level scales will tell you so.
