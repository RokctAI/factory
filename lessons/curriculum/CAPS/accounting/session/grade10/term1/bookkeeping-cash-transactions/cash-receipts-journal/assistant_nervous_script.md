Hey, it's Thandi — first real journal today, and if all those columns felt like a spreadsheet ambush, breathe. Columns are your friends; they were literally invented to make the writing LESS.

Hold the diary picture: the CRJ is just a money-IN diary. One line per arrival: date, proof, who, how much. The columns are only sorting — regulars get their own lane (Sales), visitors get named in Sundries. If you can sort washing into piles, you can sort receipts into columns.

Two things deserve a sticky note. One: cost of sales comes from DIVIDING by 1,25 — never from taking 25% off. Say "reverse the mark-up, divide" until your pen believes it. Two: the handshake — Sales plus Sundries must equal Bank. Run it at the end of every practice question; when it matches, you KNOW you're right, and that feeling beats hoping every time.

The practice questions are a month of little arrivals waiting to be sorted. Take them line by line, shake hands at the end, and walk away certain. You've got this!
