Today we step back from single entries and study the whole machine: the accounting cycle — the fixed six-stage route every transaction travels, from source document through journals, ledger and trial balance to the final accounts and financial statements. Every reporting question in Paper 1 walks this exact path, and examiners love testing the order and what each stage produces. We will walk the route stage by stage with a real month of trading, prove why no stage can happen without the one before it, then ride a single R150 till slip through all six stops. By the end the sequence will be memorised not as a list but as a journey you can retrace from any point.
