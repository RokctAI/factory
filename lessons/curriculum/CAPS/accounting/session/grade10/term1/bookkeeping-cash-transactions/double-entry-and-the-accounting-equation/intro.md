Today we lay the foundation the whole of Accounting stands on: the accounting equation — assets equal owner's equity plus liabilities — and the double-entry principle that keeps it balanced at every moment. Every journal, ledger and financial statement you will ever prepare is just this equation in motion, which is why examiners test it from Grade 10 straight through to matric. We will prove the design with four real transactions — capital deposited, an asset swap, a credit purchase and a payment to a creditor — then total everything and verify that the equation holds to the rand. By the end you will own a five-step routine that turns any transaction into two correct entries, every time.
