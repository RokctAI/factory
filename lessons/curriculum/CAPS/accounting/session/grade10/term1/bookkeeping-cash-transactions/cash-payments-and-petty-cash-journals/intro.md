Today we take on the Cash Payments Journal — the book of first entry for every rand that leaves the business bank account — together with its small companion, the Petty Cash Journal and the imprest system. Payments are where errors cost twice and where money leaks if control is loose, so the prescribed columns and the month-end cross-cast are marks the examiners guard jealously. We will record a full month of payments for a sole trader, walk straight into the drawings trap and out again, then prove the journal with totals before running the petty cash tin by voucher and float. Master the money-out side and the whole bookkeeping cycle starts balancing itself.
