Bookkeeping proper starts with the Cash Receipts Journal — the book of first entry that records every rand a business receives, in date order, backed by a numbered source document. The format itself carries marks in CAPS: the analysis of receipts column, the double truth of every cash sale under the perpetual inventory system, and the month-end cross-cast that proves the journal before it posts. We will record a full month for a sole trader — cash sales with cost of sales stripped out of a 25 percent mark-up, capital and rent income through the sundries column — then total and prove the whole journal. Get this format under your hands now and every journal that follows is the same machine with different lanes.
