Today the journals come home: we post the Cash Receipts Journal and Cash Payments Journal to the general ledger, balance the accounts, and prove the whole month with a trial balance. This is the engine room of the accounting cycle, and CAPS marks it on format — the right total to the right account, the right side, folio references intact, balances brought down correctly. We will post Thabo's Trading's actual month figure by figure, including the double posting of cost of sales, then build the trial balance and learn exactly what its level columns prove and what they can never prove. By the end you will also read an unbalanced trial balance's difference like a fingerprint and know which error to hunt.
