Today we consolidate Accounting's working vocabulary before the bookkeeping of the coming weeks begins: the sole trader, assets, liabilities and owner's equity, debit and credit, the pipeline from journal to ledger to trial balance to final accounts, and the two kinds of discount. We then master the system every Grade 10 to 12 record runs on — the perpetual inventory system, where the Trading Stock account is updated at every transaction and each sale writes both the selling price and the cost of sales. We also draw the clean contrast between financial and managerial accounting, the report for outsiders versus the plan for insiders. Precision with these terms now saves marks all year.
