Hey, it's Thandi — six principles feels like a lot, I know. Deep breath.

Here's the trick: you don't need to memorise six definitions word for word. You need to recognise six *situations*, like recognising faces. Land stuck at its old price? Historical cost. Being cautious about bad news? Prudence. "Too small to bother"? Materiality. Owner's personal stuff touching the business? Entity rule. "The business will keep going"? Going concern. Expenses shuffled into the right year? Matching.

Notice that every one of them is basically common sense with a name tag on. You already think this way — you'd never count your friend's money as your own, and that's the entity rule right there.

So try the practice questions now, while the six faces are fresh. When a scenario stumps you, ask the lesson's question: "what is the accountant refusing to do?" The answer practically introduces itself. You've got this — go match some faces.
