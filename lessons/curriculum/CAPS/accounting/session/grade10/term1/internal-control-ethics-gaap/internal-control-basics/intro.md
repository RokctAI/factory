A business can have honest owners, good products and full tills — and still bleed money through error, theft and carelessness; the discipline that stops the bleeding is internal control. Today we master its exam-precise definition and four objectives, the towering principle of division of duties, and the five components the syllabus names: the control environment, risk assessment, control activities, information systems and communication, and monitoring. Then we train the real exam skill — reading a scenario, naming the weakness, explaining the risk and recommending a specific control. These five components return in every grade of Accounting, so learn them properly once and they serve you until matric.
