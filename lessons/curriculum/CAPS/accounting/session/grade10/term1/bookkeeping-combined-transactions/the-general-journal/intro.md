Today we open bookkeeping's oldest and most flexible book: the General Journal, the book of first entry for everything the specialised journals cannot take — bad debts, interest on overdue accounts, the owner taking stock home, and corrections of errors. Because this book can record anything, it carries the tightest format rules of all: debit line first, credited account indented below, and a narration that explains the entry or loses the mark. We will work the classic Grade 10 entries in full — a R900 write-off, interest in both directions, drawings of stock at cost — then fix a posting error in pen, correcting only the broken leg. Finish this session and all six journals are yours: every transaction in any business now has exactly one front door.
