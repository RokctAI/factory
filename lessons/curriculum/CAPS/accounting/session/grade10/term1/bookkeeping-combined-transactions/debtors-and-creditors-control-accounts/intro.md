Today we build one of the most examinable tools in bookkeeping: the Debtors Control account, and its mirror image, the Creditors Control account. The principle is detail versus totals — personal accounts in the Debtors Ledger hold the people, the control account holds the street — and at month end the two independent records must agree to the rand, which is why this topic is the backbone of reconciliation questions from Grade 10 to matric. We will post a real June — opening balance, credit sales, receipts, returns and a bad debt — balance the account to R28 900, then test that balance against the list of debtors' balances and read a disagreement for its clue. One question drives every entry: did this event make debtors owe us more, or less?
