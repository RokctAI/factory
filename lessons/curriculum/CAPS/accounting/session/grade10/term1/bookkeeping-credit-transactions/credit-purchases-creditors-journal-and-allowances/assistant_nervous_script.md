Hey, it's Thandi — if your brain is protesting "but we JUST learned this, why does everything point the other way now?", that's not confusion — that's the lesson working. It IS the same system, pointing the other way.

So don't learn creditors from scratch. Learn one flip: debtors owe US (asset, grows debit); WE owe creditors (liability, grows credit). Every other habit you already own carries straight over — pages per name, one summary line, daily detail, monthly totals, and the lock at month end. One flip, zero new machinery.

Two small anchors for the practice questions. The documents: invoice IN creates the debt; debit note OUT shrinks it when goods go back. And the missing column: no cost-of-sales on this side, ever — buying is one truth, only selling needs two.

If a question turns you around mid-answer, just ask: who owes whom here? Point the arrow, and every side chooses itself. Go run the mirror — you've got this!
