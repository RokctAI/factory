Today we revise everything that happens when the financial year ends — the adjustments that make the figures true, the final accounts and the two statements they feed, the ratios that read the results, and the controls, ethics and GAAP principles that guard the books all year. This is the heart of the year-end examination: a right number in the wrong place loses the mark, so the prescribed formats matter as much as the arithmetic. We work one business, Thabo's Trading, from trial balance to balanced statement of financial position, then read its health through the gauges and close with unit cost and the cash budget. By the end, the whole year-end toolkit — adjusting, closing, reading, guarding, costing, planning — is revised and yours.
