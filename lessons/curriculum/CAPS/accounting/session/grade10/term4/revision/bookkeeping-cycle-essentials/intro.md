Today we rebuild the year's recording machine end to end, because examiners reward the learner who sees one machine, not ten separate topics. One small month of transactions runs through every station: the accounting equation and the debit-credit compass behind each entry, the six journals with their source documents, the posting that gathers the month into the ledger, the trial balance that weighs it at twenty thousand a side, and the control accounts that count the same street twice. Along the way we name exactly what each check proves and what it cannot see — the blind spots the examiner loves to probe. Master this machine and every examination question becomes the same month with different numbers.
