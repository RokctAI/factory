Hey, it's Thandi — last topic of the year, and if the budget family blurred together — cash, capital, zero-based, medium, long — here's the sorting trick: each one answers ONE question. Can we pay our way this month? Cash budget. When do we buy big things? Capital budget. Does every cost still deserve its place? Zero-based. Where are we going in a few years? Medium and long term. Match question to budget and the concept marks are yours.

The calculations live in the cash budget, and its skeleton never changes: in, out, difference, opening, closing — and each closing walks into the next month as the opening. Five lines per month. The arithmetic is literally addition and subtraction; the marks hide in two rules. Rule one: cash ONLY — credit sales count when the money ARRIVES, a month late, and depreciation never appears because no rand leaves. Rule two: label your estimates — "40% of December's sales" written next to the figure is a mark in the bank.

And honestly? You already know this tool. If you've ever watched someone plan the month before payday — rent first, then food, then transport, then what's left — you've seen a cash budget run live. Tonight just gave it columns.

One topic, one skeleton, two rules. Finish the practice quarter and you've finished Grade 10 Accounting — the whole toolkit, yours. You've got this!
