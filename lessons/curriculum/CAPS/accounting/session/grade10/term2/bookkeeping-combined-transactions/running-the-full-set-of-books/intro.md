Term one built six journals, two subsidiary ledgers, a general ledger and a trial balance — each in isolation; today we run them together as one system on a full combined month. The skill examiners now demand is routing: reading any transaction — cash sale, credit purchase, return, receipt, write-off — and sending it through the right journal to the right ledgers without hesitation. We journalise Thabo's Trading month event by event, post it, balance every account, and prove the whole thing with a level trial balance and agreeing control accounts. Then we thread the internal-control and ethics questions through the same month, because integrated scenarios are where the marks concentrate.
