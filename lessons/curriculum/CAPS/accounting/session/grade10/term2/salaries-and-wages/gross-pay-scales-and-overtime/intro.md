The books have watched money move for customers and suppliers; now they must pay the people who do the work. Today is the earning side of salaries and wages: the difference between selling your hours and selling your year, the salary scale read notch by notch, and the overtime multipliers — time and a half for evenings and Saturdays, double time for Sundays and public holidays. We run a full wage week at Thabo's Trading to gross pay, line by labelled line, and place the salaried administrator on her scale. Master these calculations now, because every payroll question in the examination begins with a gross figure you must compute, not copy.
