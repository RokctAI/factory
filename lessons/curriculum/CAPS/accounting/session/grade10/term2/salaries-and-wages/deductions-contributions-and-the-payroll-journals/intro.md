Last lesson ended at gross pay — but nobody takes gross home, so today the payroll machinery completes its work. We run the five deductions that shrink gross to net — PAYE, pension, UIF, medical aid and union fees — through the administrator's R8 800 month, then add the employer's contributions on top, including the one-sided Skills Development Levy, to reach the true total cost of employing her. From there we build the Salaries Journal and Wages Journal column by column, reason the double entry, and follow each rand to its final destination through the CPJ. We close with the four ethical matters the syllabus attaches to payday: the contract, nepotism, pay in line with responsibilities, and the trade union.
