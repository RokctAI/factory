Today we build the largest and most examined adjustment of them all: depreciation. Fixed assets work for years and every working year wears them down, so matching and prudence insist the books say so — through three figures you must keep strictly apart: cost price, accumulated depreciation and carrying value. We calculate on both methods — straight-line, the level slice of original cost, and diminishing balance, the shrinking slice of carrying value — then handle an asset bought partway through the year with the pro rata months fraction, and maintain the asset register that keeps every figure auditable. This calculation appears in every Accounting paper from now to matric; build it correctly once.
