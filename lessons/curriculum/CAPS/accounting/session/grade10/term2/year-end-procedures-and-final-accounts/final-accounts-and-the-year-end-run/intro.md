The accounting cycle's daily and monthly clocks have run all year; today the yearly clock strikes. Year-end procedures are the bridge between a trial balance and the financial statements, and the bridge has a fixed sequence: pre-adjustment trial balance, adjustments, post-adjustment trial balance, final accounts, and only then the statements. We walk that sequence at Thabo's Trading — aligning stationery and stock with year-end fact, then computing gross profit in the Trading account and net profit in the Profit and Loss account before transferring it to Capital — with the GAAP principle named at every step. The order is the control: each step certifies the one after it, and examiners mark exactly that discipline.
