Years away? Non-current. Within the year? Current. That single test files every item on both walls — the equipment, the loan, the stock, the telephone bill. You're not memorising a map; you're asking one question per item and letting the answers stack into the format by themselves.

The three family names sound formal but read literally: trade and OTHER receivables — the debtors plus the little adjustment children owed TO us; trade and OTHER payables — the creditors plus the children owed BY us; inventories — everything on shelves, stock and the stationery parcel. Say the names literally and every adjustment twin files itself.

The scary "will it balance?" moment — remember it's not luck. If your year's entries were doubles, the page MUST level. And when it doesn't, the culprits are famous: forgotten drawings, a lost adjustment twin, the loan at its old figure. Check those three before panicking.
