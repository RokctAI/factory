Today the year's bookkeeping steps into public view: the Statement of Comprehensive Income, the formal report that tells the earning story in a format every accountant in the country recognises. We sort nominal accounts from balance sheet accounts, then build the prescribed shape line by line — sales to gross profit, the operating block to operating profit, and the financing tail down to net profit for the year — posting each adjusted figure with its working shown in brackets. Format carries marks in this topic as surely as arithmetic does, so we finish by reading the completed statement the way its three audiences do: the owner, the bank and the accountant.
