Today we move from how well the business earns to whether it survives: liquidity, solvency and return on owner's equity. Three questions drive the session — can the bills of the coming year be paid as they knock, do total assets cover total debts at all, and was the owner's money better in this business than in the bank? We will compute the current ratio, the acid-test ratio, the solvency ratio and the return on average equity straight from the statement of financial position, then judge each against its norm the way examiners expect. By the end you will hold the full nine-gauge dashboard of the term's analysis and know how to comment in both directions — too low is dangerous, but too high can be idle money.
