Today grade 12 leaves the partnership behind and takes on the company — a separate legal person under the Companies Act, owned by shareholders who may never set foot in it and run by directors who may own almost none of it. That separation creates the transactions unique to companies: shares issued at no par value and bought back at the average issue price, income tax paid provisionally against a year-end charge, dividends declared in cents per share on the shares in issue on the day, and loans whose capitalised interest grows the debt before instalments shrink it. We work every entry through Thabiso Limited's year, because the financial statements ahead are built directly on top of these transactions.
