Today the financial statements stop being records and start answering questions: analysis and interpretation of a company's results. Grade 11's ratios return in company clothing, and grade 12 adds the shareholder's own toolkit — return on shareholders' equity, return on total capital employed, earnings and dividends per share, the pay-out rate and net asset value. One company, Khanya Limited with its million shares, carries every calculation, and each indicator gets the sentence after it that earns the second half of the marks. The discipline you leave with is worth a large slice of the final paper: quote the figure, compare it to something, and judge with a reason — every single time.
