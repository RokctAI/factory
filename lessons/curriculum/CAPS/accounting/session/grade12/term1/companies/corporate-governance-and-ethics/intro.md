Today we are working with the machinery that makes a company trustworthy: corporate governance and ethics. A company runs on other people's money, so we start with the split between the shareholders who own it and the directors who run it, then take on South Africa's King Code and its four demands — responsibility, accountability, fairness and transparency. From there we move to the professional code of conduct, conflicts of interest, insider trading and share-price manipulation, and finally the three layers of checking: internal control, internal audit and the independent auditor. Every idea ends in the examination's favourite question — something has gone wrong, and you must name the principle broken and recommend the remedy.
