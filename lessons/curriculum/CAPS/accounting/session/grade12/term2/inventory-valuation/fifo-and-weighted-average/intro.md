Today we are valuing stock when prices move — the question that decides cost of sales, gross profit and the balance sheet in one stroke. We will work specific identification, first-in-first-out and the weighted average method to the rand on one set of figures, and prove the law that whatever value stands on the shelf has by that exact amount been kept out of cost of sales. Then we bring in the discipline around the numbers: physical stock counts, the internal controls that keep deficits small, and the GAAP principles and ethics that stop stock becoming an instrument of manipulation. Inventory valuation is a guaranteed examination question, usually with the arithmetic and the ethics stitched together.
