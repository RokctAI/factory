## Question (3 marks)

Comment on how the 'rights issue' improved the solvency of the company to a favourable position. Refer to the effect of the rights issue on the assets, equities and liabilities of the company, assuming that the company's bank balance was favourable.

(The context: Piranna Ltd, declared insolvent in March 2025, implemented a rights issue — new shares issued to existing shareholders only. It offered five million shares at a 30% discounted price of R27 per share while the shares were trading at approximately R39 per share; shareholders responded positively and the company raised sufficient cash to move out of insolvency.)

## Method

rights issue solvency effect

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

The reasoning: trace the rights issue through the accounting equation. The cash received increases assets; the new shares issued increase equity by the same amount; and no debt is created, so liabilities stay the same. With assets rising while liabilities are unchanged, assets move back above liabilities and the solvency position becomes favourable.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Assets will increase as more cash is received (1 mark).
- Equity will increase as more shares have been issued (1 mark).
- Liabilities will remain unchanged (1 mark).
