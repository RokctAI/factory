## Question (5 marks)

Calculate the value of closing stock using the first-in-first-out (FIFO) method.

(Sunglass City — same information as Q2.1.1. Closing stock is 840 units. Purchases and returns during the year: May 2023: 855 units at R3 200; August 2023: 800 units at R3 750; November 2023: 745 units at R4 200; December 2023: return of 20 units at R4 200.)

## Method

fifo closing stock

## Memo working

Under FIFO the oldest stock is sold first, so the 840 units on hand at year-end are valued at the most recent purchase prices, working backwards from November.

November purchase net of the December return: [745 - 20] = 725 units x 4 200 = 3 045 000 (two marks).

The remaining (840 - 725) = 115 units come from the August purchase: 115 x 3 750 = 431 250 (one method mark + one mark).

Closing stock: 3 045 000 + 431 250 = R3 476 250. (Memo: for the method mark, both unit figures must add up to 840.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R3 476 250 (one part correct; both figures must be added)
