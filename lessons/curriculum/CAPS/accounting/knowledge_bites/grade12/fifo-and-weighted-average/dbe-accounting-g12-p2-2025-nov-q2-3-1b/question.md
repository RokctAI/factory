## Question (2 marks)

Explain how this decision had a positive result for the business. Provide ONE point, with figures.

(MADRID ELECTRIC FANS SUPPLIERS — same information as Q2.3.1a: the mark-up on electric fans was increased from 60% to 80%, lifting the selling price from R200 to R270. Electric fans 2025 vs 2024: total sales R137 700 vs R112 000; gross profit R61 200 vs R42 000; units sold 510 vs 560.)

## Method

pricing decision effect comment

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

To reason it out: test whether the price increase paid off despite fewer units being sold. Gross profit rose from R42 000 to R61 200 — up R19 200, or 45,7%. Total sales rose from R112 000 to R137 700 — up R25 700, or 22,9% — even though units sold fell from 560 to 510. The extra margin per unit more than compensated for the lost volume.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Any relevant point (1 mark) with figure/s (1 mark): Gross profit has increased (from R42 000) to R61 200 / by R19 200 / by 45,7%; Despite selling fewer units (510 vs. 560), total sales increased (from R112 000) to R137 700 / by R25 700 / by 22,9%, showing that the price increase boosted overall revenue.
