## Question (2 marks)

Bryan has noticed that some of the stock of flavoured juices has exceeded their shelf life (sell-by date) of 65 days. Identify the value of stock that Bryan is referring to, using the FIFO stock valuation method.

(SUPERSAVERS SUPERMARKET — same information as Q2.1.1: closing stock of 980 units consists of 818 February 2025 units at R42 and 162 units at R37, being the January 2025 purchase of 150 units plus 12 units left from December 2024.)

## Method

fifo aged stock value

## Memo working

Under FIFO the units still on hand are the newest ones, so the oldest units in closing stock are the 12 December 2024 units — by 28 February 2025 they are older than the 65-day shelf life. Their value: 12 x 37 = 444. (Equivalent routes: 96 200 - 95 756, or 5 994 - 5 550.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

444 (one part correct)
