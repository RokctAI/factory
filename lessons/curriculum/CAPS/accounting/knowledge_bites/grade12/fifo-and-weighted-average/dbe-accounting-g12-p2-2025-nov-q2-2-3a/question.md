## Question (4 marks)

Clear View model: Although the Clear View model had units returned, Moses is very pleased with his decision to introduce it as a new model. Provide TWO points (with figures) to explain why Moses is pleased.

(BANDURA PRINTERS — same information as Q2.2.1: Clear View was introduced in February 2025; 90 units purchased at R35 000, 15 returned, 65 sold at R49 000 for R3 185 000; closing stock 10 units, R350 000. Rotrim sold 79 units for R1 714 300 over 12 months; Punchies sold 100 units for R3 108 000 over 12 months.)

## Method

new model performance comment

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

To reason it out: compare Clear View against the other two models on profitability and speed of sales. Profit per unit: 49 000 - 35 000 = R14 000, giving total gross profit of 65 x 14 000 = R910 000. Pace: 65 units in only 5 months (February to June) is 13 per month, against roughly 7 per month for Rotrim (79 over 12 months) and 8 for Punchies (100 over 12 months). Sell-through: 65 of the 75 available units (90 - 15 returns) is 86,7%. Its sales of R3 185 000 are the highest of the three models, only 10 units (R350 000) are left, and its stockholding period is only 23,1 days over the 5 months since introduction.

**Note: the official memo contains documented slips in this item. Its stockholding bullet quotes a Rotrim stockholding period of 124,7 days, which cannot be reproduced from the given figures (closing-stock basis 635 500 / 1 224 500 x 365 = 189,4 days; average-stock basis 164,0 days), and it attaches 41 units/R780 500 to Rotrim and 35 units/R635 500 to Punchies — the reverse of the values computed in Q2.2.1 and Q2.2.2a (Rotrim is 41 units/R635 500; Punchies is 35 units/R780 500). The memo answer below is transcribed verbatim; the other comparison points recompute correctly (Punchies 127,8 days; Clear View 23,1 days).**

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Compare the Clear View printer with the other printers (2 marks); figures (2 marks). Any TWO of:

- Each printer is sold at a profit of R14 000 (total gross profit R910 000), higher than that of the other two printers.
- Sold an average of 13 printers per month, surpassing Rotrim (7) and Punchies (8).
- Sold 86,7% of available printers, compared to (65,8%) of Rotrim or (74,1%) of Punchies / sold 65 units in 5 months, compared to Rotrim (79 units) and Punchies (100 units) over 12 months.
- Achieved the highest sales of R3 185 000, exceeding Rotrim (R1 714 300) and Punchies (R3 108 000).
- Only 10 (R350 000) units left, compared to (41 units) (R780 500) for Rotrim and (35 units) (R635 500) for Punchies.
- Stockholding period is only 23,1 days compared to (127,8 days) of Punchies and (124,7 days) of Rotrim.
