## Question (7 marks)

SUPERSAVERS SUPERMARKET: The information relates to flavoured juices (2-litre bottles) that Supersavers Supermarket trades with. The first-in-first-out (FIFO) stock valuation method is applicable. The business is managed by Bryan Mills. The financial year ended on 28 February 2025. Calculate the value of the closing stock on 28 February 2025, using the FIFO method.

INFORMATION:

A. Balances (columns: DATE | NUMBER OF UNITS | TOTAL (R)): 1 March 2024, 630 units; 28 February 2025, 980 units, total unknown.

B. Purchases and returns during the year (columns: NUMBER OF UNITS | UNIT PRICE (R) | TOTAL (R)): Purchases 10 500 units, R358 250 in total — March-November 2024: 6 900 at 32 = 220 800; December 2024: 2 600 at 37 = 96 200; January 2025: 150 at 37 = 5 550; February 2025: 850 at 42 = 35 700. Returns: 32 units, R1 344.

C. Sales: 10 118 units were sold at R47 per bottle: R475 546.

## Method

fifo closing stock

## Memo working

Under FIFO the oldest stock is sold first, so the 980 units on hand at year-end are made up of the most recent purchases. The returns of 32 units were at R42 each (1 344 / 32), so they come off the February 2025 batch.

Start with February 2025: 850 - 32 returns = 818 units at R42 = (818 x 42) = 34 356 (three marks). The remaining 162 units [980 - 818, or the January 150 plus 12 from December] are valued at R37: (162 x 37) = 5 994 (two method marks + one mark). Closing stock: 34 356 + 5 994 = 40 350.

(Memo: for the two method marks both quantities must add up to 980. OR (35 700 - 1 344) (one mark + two marks) + (5 550 + 444) (one mark + two method marks). Ensure that only one option is marked consistently.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R40 350 (one part correct; both amounts added)
