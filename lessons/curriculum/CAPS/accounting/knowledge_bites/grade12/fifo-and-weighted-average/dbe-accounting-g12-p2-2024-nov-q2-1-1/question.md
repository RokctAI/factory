## Question (5 marks)

SUNGLASS CITY: Nosisa is the owner of Sunglass City, selling one type of popular branded sunglasses. The financial year ended on 29 February 2024. (NOTE: Where comments or explanations are required, you should quote calculations, figures and/or trends and give a reason or an explanation in EACH case.) Calculate the weighted average cost of ONE pair of sunglasses.

INFORMATION:

A. Stock records (one 'unit' refers to one pair of sunglasses) (columns: UNITS | TOTAL (R)): Opening stock (1 March 2023) 620 units, R1 719 000; Closing stock (29 February 2024) 840 units, total unknown.

B. Purchases and returns during the financial year (columns: UNITS | UNIT PRICE (R) | TOTAL (R)): May 2023: 855 at 3 200 = 2 736 000; August 2023: 800 at 3 750 = 3 000 000; November 2023: 745 at 4 200 = 3 129 000; December 2023: (20) at 4 200 = (84 000) — a return; TOTALS: 2 380 units, R8 781 000.

C. Sales: 2 050 pairs of sunglasses were sold during the year at R5 600 per pair.

## Method

weighted average unit cost

## Memo working

Weighted average cost per unit is the total cost of all stock available for the year divided by the total units available. Total cost: opening stock plus net purchases: [1 719 000 + 8 781 000] = 10 500 000 (two marks). Total units: opening units plus net purchased units: [620 + 2 380] = 3 000 (two marks). Weighted average cost: 10 500 000 / 3 000 = R3 500.

(Memo: numerator and denominator must be marked as such / all amounts are stand-alone. Do not award marks for any operation done on 10 500 000 and 3 000. Workings in square brackets constitute one part, cannot be separated.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R3 500 (one part correct)
