## Question (6 marks)

Refer to Information B. Calculate the correct value of the closing stock of men's informal shirts.

(The information was taken from the accounting records of Violet Limited. The financial year ended on 28 February 2025. The company trades with women's and men's clothing.)

Information B — Trading stock: It was discovered that the closing stock of one specific brand of men's informal shirts was valued according to the first-in-first-out stock valuation method instead of the weighted-average stock valuation method. This must be corrected. The records applicable to this brand are: stock on 1 March 2024 was 300 units with a total cost of R97 500; purchases during the year were 4 800 units with a total cost of R1 605 000; returns were 100 units with a total cost of R42 500; closing stock on 28 February 2025 was 820 units, shown at a total cost of R285 500 (the incorrect first-in-first-out value).

## Method

weighted average closing stock

## Memo working

Step 1 — total cost of stock available on the weighted-average basis: 97 500 + 1 605 000 - 42 500 = 1 660 000 (one mark plus one method mark).

Step 2 — total number of units available: 300 + 4 800 - 100 = 5 000 units (one mark plus one method mark).

Step 3 — weighted-average cost per unit: 1 660 000 divided by 5 000 = R332 (four marks up to this point).

Step 4 — correct value of the closing stock: 332 x 820 units = 272 240.

(Memo: numerator and denominator must be marked as such; do not award marks for any stand-alone figures, e.g. no ticks if only 97 500/300 is written.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

272 240 (one part correct if x by 820)
