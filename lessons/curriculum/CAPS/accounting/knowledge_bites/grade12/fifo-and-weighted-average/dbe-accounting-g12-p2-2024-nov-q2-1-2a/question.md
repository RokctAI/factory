## Question (4 marks)

Nosisa suspects that some sunglasses were stolen during the year. Calculate the number of sunglasses stolen.

(Sunglass City — same information as Q2.1.1: opening stock 620 units; net purchases 2 380 units; sales 2 050 units; closing stock counted at 840 units.)

## Method

unit reconciliation missing stock

## Memo working

Reconcile the units: everything available for sale must either be sold, still on hand, or missing. Units available: [620 + 2 380] = 3 000. Subtract what was sold and what was counted at year-end: 3 000 - 2 050 - 840 = 110 units missing.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

110 (one part correct)
