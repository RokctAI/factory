## Question (1 mark)

MANAGEMENT OF INVENTORIES: You are provided with information from the books of Madrid Electric Fans Suppliers. The business is owned by Robin Madrid. They sell electric fans and fridges to households in Middelburg. The financial year ended on 30 September 2025. Electric fans: Identify the decision that Robin implemented about the electric fans.

INFORMATION (columns: ELECTRIC FANS 2025 | ELECTRIC FANS 2024 | FRIDGES 2025 | FRIDGES 2024):

- Total sales: R137 700 | R112 000 | R2 418 750 | R5 161 500
- Gross profit: R61 200 | R42 000 | R806 250 | R1 720 500
- Selling price per unit: R270 | R200 | R5 625 | R6 975
- Cost price per unit: R150 | R125 | R3 750 | R4 650
- Units available for sale: 750 | 600 | 960 | 820
- Units sold: 510 | 560 | 430 | 740
- Mark-up %: 80% | 60% | 50% | 50%

## Method

identify pricing decision

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

To reason it out: run down the electric fans column and spot what changed between 2024 and 2025. The mark-up percentage jumped from 60% to 80%, which shows in the selling price rising from R200 to R270 per unit. Either way of stating it — the higher mark-up or the higher selling price — identifies the decision.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Decision (figures not required): Increased the mark-up % (from 60% to 80%) / by 33,3% / by 20% points; OR Increased the selling price per unit (from R200) to R270 / by R70 / by 35%.
