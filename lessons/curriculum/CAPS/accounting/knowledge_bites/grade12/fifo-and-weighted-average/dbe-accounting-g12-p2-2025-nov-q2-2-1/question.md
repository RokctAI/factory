## Question (4 marks)

BANDURA PRINTERS: Bandura Printers sells different models of printers. The business is owned by Moses Banda. Moses decided to introduce a new model (Clear View) in February 2025. The printers are valued using the specific identification method. The financial year ended on 30 June 2025. Rotrim model: Calculate the value of the closing stock on 30 June 2025 using the specific identification method.

INFORMATION:

A. Opening stock on 1 July 2024 (columns: MODEL | NUMBER OF UNITS | UNIT PRICE (R) | TOTAL (R)): Rotrim 30 at 15 500 = 465 000; Punchies 25 at 22 300 = 557 500; total 1 022 500.

B. Purchases and returns (columns: DATE | MODEL | NUMBER OF UNITS | UNIT PRICE (R) | TOTAL (R)): Purchases — Sept. 2024 Rotrim 90 at 15 500 = 1 395 000; Nov. 2024 Punchies 110 at 22 300 = 2 453 000; Feb. 2025 Clear View 90 at 35 000 = 3 150 000. Returns — Feb. 2025 Clear View 15 at 35 000 = (525 000). Net purchases 6 473 000.

C. Sales for the year ended 30 June 2025 (columns: MODEL | NUMBER OF UNITS | UNIT PRICE (R) | TOTAL (R)): Rotrim 79 at 21 700 = 1 714 300; Punchies (units and price not given) 3 108 000; Clear View 65 at 49 000 = 3 185 000; total sales 8 007 300.

D. Closing stock on 30 June 2025 (columns: MODEL | TOTAL (R)): Rotrim ?; Punchies 780 500; Clear View 350 000.

## Method

specific identification closing stock

## Memo working

Under specific identification each model is tracked at its own cost — every Rotrim unit costs R15 500. Units on hand: opening 30 + purchased 90 - sold 79 = 41 units (two marks: one mark for 30 + 90, one mark for - 79). Value: 41 x 15 500 (one mark) = 635 500.

(Memo alternative: 465 000 + 1 395 000 - 1 224 500 [79 x 15 500] = 635 500. Ensure that only one option is marked consistently.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

635 500 (one part correct if multiplied by R15 500)
