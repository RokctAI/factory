## Question (4 marks)

Formal shirts: Calculate how long (in days) it will take Mike to sell the closing stock of the formal shirts.

(Slim-Line Boutique — same information as Q2.2.1a: formal shirts were introduced and sold only from 1 November 2023; closing stock 125 units; total units sold 855; cost price R680 per shirt; cost of sales R581 400.)

**Note (from the marking guideline):** formal shirts traded only from 1 November 2023, hence the 120-day (or 121-day) trading period in the formula — not a full 365-day year.

## Method

stockholding period closing stock

## Memo working

The stock holding period on closing stock is closing stock divided by cost of sales, multiplied by the length of the trading period. Because the formal shirts only traded from 1 November 2023 to 29 February 2024, that period is 120 days (or 121 days), not 365.

In units: 125 / 855 x 120 days (two marks; OR 121 days) = 17,5 days (17,7 with 121 days).

OR in rands: [R680 x 125] = 85 000 (one mark) / 581 400 x 120 days (two marks; OR 121 days).

(Memo: numerator and denominator must be marked as such / all amounts are stand-alone. Do not mix rands with units.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

17,5 / 17,7 days (one part correct; accept 18 days)
