## Question (2 marks)

Comment on whether the mark-up percentage would benefit the business or not.

(Slim-Line Boutique — follows on from Q2.2.1a: the trousers carry an 84% mark-up, against 55% on casual shirts and 25% on formal shirts, and their stock holding period is 186 days.)

## Method

markup evaluation

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Any valid explanation (part marks for partial or unclear responses):

- Customers will not buy / pay higher price for the product.
- Price-sensitive customers may choose competitors offering similar trousers for less, leading to lower sales.
- Regular customers may be discouraged from making repeated purchases.
- A decrease in sales volume could negatively affect cash flow.
- Stock holding period of 186 days / 6,2 months indicates lower demand which resulted in high stock levels / slow sales can lead to stock being out of fashion.
