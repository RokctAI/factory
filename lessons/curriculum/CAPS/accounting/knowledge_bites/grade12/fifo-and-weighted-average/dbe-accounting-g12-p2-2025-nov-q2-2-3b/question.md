## Question (2 marks)

Moses wants to reduce the number of units being returned to the suppliers. Give ONE suggestion on how this can be achieved.

(BANDURA PRINTERS — same information as Q2.2.1: 15 of the 90 Clear View printers purchased in February 2025 were returned to the supplier.)

## Method

reduce returns suggestion

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

To reason it out: returns to suppliers happen because units arrive or leave the storeroom in a defective state. The fixes are to check condition at the point of purchase, use a more reliable supplier, secure guarantee or warranty terms, or prevent in-store damage through staff training and correct storage.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Any relevant suggestion:

- Ensure that the printer is in working condition when it is bought.
- Change supplier to boost reliability and customer satisfaction of Clear View.
- Request guarantee / warranty terms from suppliers.
- Train staff on handling procedures / store printers correctly to prevent damages.
