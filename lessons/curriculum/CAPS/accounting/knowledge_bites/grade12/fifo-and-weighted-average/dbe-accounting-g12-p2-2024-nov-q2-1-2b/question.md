## Question (2 marks)

Calculate the rand value of the sunglasses stolen, using the weighted-average stock valuation method.

(Sunglass City — follows on from Q2.1.1, weighted average cost R3 500 per pair, and Q2.1.2a, 110 pairs stolen.)

## Method

weighted average value of missing stock

## Memo working

Value the missing units at the weighted average cost per unit: R3 500 (see 2.1.1) x 110 (see 2.1.2 above) = 385 000.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R385 000 (two method marks or nothing, if amount calculated is 2.1.1 x 2.1.2)
