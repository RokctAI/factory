## Question (5 marks)

Punchies model: Calculate the stockholding period (in days) using the closing stock.

(BANDURA PRINTERS — same information as Q2.2.1: Punchies opening stock 25 units at R22 300 = R557 500; purchases 110 units at R22 300 = R2 453 000; closing stock R780 500, which is 35 units.)

## Method

stockholding period closing stock

## Memo working

Stockholding period = closing stock / cost of sales x 365. Cost of sales for Punchies: opening stock plus purchases minus closing stock: [557 500 + 2 453 000 - 780 500] = 2 230 000 (three marks). Then 780 500 (one mark) / 2 230 000 x 365/1 = 127,8 days.

(Memo alternative in units: 35 units (one mark) / 100 units (three marks) [25 + 110 - 35] x 365/1. 'x 365' does not constitute 'one part correct'. Numerator and denominator must be marked as such. Do not award a mark for any operation done to R780 500 or 35 units. Ensure that only one option is marked consistently.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

127,8 days (one part correct; must x 365; days not necessary; accept 128 days / 127 days)
