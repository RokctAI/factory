## Question (2 marks)

Comment on the stockholding period. Provide ONE point, with figures. The stockholding period for the year ended 30 June 2024 was 69 days.

(BANDURA PRINTERS — same information as Q2.2.1; the Punchies stockholding period calculated in Q2.2.2a is 127,8 days.)

## Method

stockholding period comment

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

To reason it out: compare the two years first — the period has almost doubled, from 69 days to 127,8 days (an increase of 58,8 days). Then explain why holding Punchies stock this long is a problem: the new Clear View model may be making the older models outdated, slow-moving stock piles up, storage costs and theft risk rise, and cash is tied up in stock instead of being available elsewhere.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Comparison with figures (1 mark); comment (1 mark): It has increased (from 69 days) to 127,8 days (see 2.2.2) / by 58,8 days. Other printer models may become outdated due to improved technology on the Clear View printer / stock moving slowly (piling) due to delayed sales / there will be increased storage costs decreasing profitability / increased risk of printers being stolen / cash tied up in stock instead of being available for other expenses.
