## Question (2 marks)

Fridges: Robin changed the brand of fridges that he was selling. Provide ONE point, with figures, to show that this was not a good idea.

(MADRID ELECTRIC FANS SUPPLIERS — same information as Q2.3.1a. Fridges 2025 vs 2024: total sales R2 418 750 vs R5 161 500; gross profit R806 250 vs R1 720 500; selling price per unit R5 625 vs R6 975; units available 960 vs 820; units sold 430 vs 740.)

## Method

brand change evaluation

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

To reason it out: compare the fridge columns year on year — every indicator worsened after the brand change. Units sold dropped from 740 to 430 (down 310, or 41,9%). Sales more than halved, from R5 161 500 to R2 418 750 (down R2 742 750, or 53,1%), and gross profit fell in step, from R1 720 500 to R806 250 (down R914 250, or 53,1%). Unsold stock ballooned from 80 units (820 - 740) to 530 units (960 - 430) — an increase of 450 units, or 562,5% — and the sell-through rate collapsed from 90,2% (740 of 820) to 44,8% (430 of 960). Any one of these, with figures, earns the marks.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Any relevant point (1 mark) with figures (1 mark): Number of units sold has decreased (from 740) to 430 / by 310 / by 41,9%; Sales have decreased (from R5 161 500) to R2 418 750 / by R2 742 750 / by 53,1%; Gross profit has decreased (from R1 720 500) to R806 250 / by R914 250 / by 53,1%; Units on hand / unsold increased (from 80) to 530 / by 450 / by 562,5%; Sold 90,2% (740 out of 820) in 2024 as compared to 44,8% (430 out of 960) in 2025.
