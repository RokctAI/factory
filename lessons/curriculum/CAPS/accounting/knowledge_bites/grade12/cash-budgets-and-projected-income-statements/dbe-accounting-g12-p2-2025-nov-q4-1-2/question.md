## Question (13 marks)

Calculate the amounts denoted by (i) to (iv) in the extract of the Projected Statement of Comprehensive Income: (i) Bad debts in January 2026; (ii) Salary: sales manager for January 2026; (iii) Salaries: assistants for December 2025; (iv) Advertising for December 2025. (From QUESTION 4.1, Vivan Traders. Extract from the Projected Statement of Comprehensive Income — December 2025: sales 1 995 000, bad debts 78 600, salary: sales manager 29 000, salaries: assistants (iii), advertising (iv); January 2026: sales 2 030 000, bad debts (i), salary: sales manager (ii), salaries: assistants 88 000, advertising 182 700. Additional information: October 2025 credit sales were R1 417 500; debtors pay 30% in the month of sales, 50% the next month, 14% two months after, and the remainder is written off as irrecoverable in the third month after sales. The sales manager is paid for 20 days per month at a fixed daily rate and will take four days' unpaid leave in January 2026. All assistants will receive a 60% bonus in December, and their annual salary increase of 10% on 1 January 2026. Advertising is budgeted at a fixed percentage of the monthly sales.)

**Note (documented paper/memo conflict):** The paper's Information C states that the assistants receive the 60% bonus "in December 2026", although the budget period ends 31 January 2026 and the statement extract covers December 2025. The memo applies the bonus to the December 2025 salaries — its primary answer for (iii) is 128 000 (80 000 + 48 000) — while also accepting 80 000 for one method mark. The paper wording appears to be a misprint for December 2025.

## Method

projected income statement amounts

## Memo working

(i) Bad debts written off in January 2026 come from October 2025 credit sales: the collection pattern accounts for 30% + 50% + 14% = 94%, so the irrecoverable remainder is 6%. Bad debts = 1 417 500 x 6% = 85 050 (OR 1 417 500 - 1 332 450). (ii) The sales manager earns a fixed daily rate for 20 days: daily rate = 29 000 / 20 = 1 450; four days' unpaid leave costs 1 450 x 4 = 5 800, so January salary = 29 000 - 5 800 = 23 200 (OR 29 000 x 16/20). (iii) January's 88 000 already includes the 10% increase, so the December base salary is 88 000 x 100/110 = 80 000; adding the 60% December bonus of 80 000 x 60% = 48 000 gives 128 000 (OR 80 000 x 1,6 OR 88 000 x 160/110). (Memo alternative answer: 80 000, one method mark — see the note above.) (iv) Advertising is a fixed percentage of sales: 182 700 / 2 030 000 = 9%, so December advertising = 1 995 000 x 9% = 179 550.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

(i) R85 050 (one part correct); (ii) R23 200 (one part correct); (iii) R128 000 (one part correct) OR R80 000 (one method mark); (iv) R179 550 (one part correct)
