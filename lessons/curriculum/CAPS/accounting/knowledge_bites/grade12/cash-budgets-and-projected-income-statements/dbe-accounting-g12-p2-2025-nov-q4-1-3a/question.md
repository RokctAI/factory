## Question (3 marks)

Calculate the following for January 2026: Cash purchases of trading stock. (From QUESTION 4.1, Vivan Traders: total sales for January 2026 are R2 030 000; the mark-up is 60% on cost; stock is replaced in the month of sales and a fixed-base stock level is maintained; 20% of stock is bought for cash.)

## Method

cash purchases of stock

## Memo working

Because stock is replaced in the month of sale at a fixed base level, January purchases equal January cost of sales. With a mark-up of 60% on cost, cost of sales = sales x 100/160: 2 030 000 x 100/160 = 1 268 750. Cash purchases are 20% of that: 1 268 750 x 20% = 253 750. In one step: 2 030 000 x 20/160 [100/160 x 20/100] = 253 750 (OR 1 268 750 - 1 015 000, total purchases less the 80% credit portion).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R253 750 (one part correct)
