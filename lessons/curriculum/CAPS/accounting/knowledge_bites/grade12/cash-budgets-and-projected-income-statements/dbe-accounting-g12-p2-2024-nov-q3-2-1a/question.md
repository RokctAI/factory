## Question (4 marks)

JESARY SUPERSPARES: The information relates to the budget period ending 31 December 2024. The business is owned by Bluey Summers. Creditors' Payment: Calculate the credit purchases for November 2024. (Information: 60% of total sales is cash sales; goods are sold at a mark-up of 25% on cost; trading stock on hand is maintained through monthly purchases; the business buys merchandise only on credit; the Cash Budget extract shows cash sales of R103 200 for November 2024.)

## Method

credit purchases from cash sales

## Memo working

Cash sales of 103 200 are 60% of total sales, so total sales for November are 103 200 x 100/60 = 172 000 (two marks). Goods are sold at a mark-up of 25% on cost, so the cost of sales is 172 000 x 100/125 = 137 600. Because stock is replaced through monthly purchases and all merchandise is bought on credit, credit purchases equal the cost of sales: 137 600. (Memo option 2: work in the other order — [103 200 x 100/125] = 82 560 (two marks) x 100/60 (one mark). Workings in square brackets constitute one part, cannot be separated.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

137 600 (one part correct)
