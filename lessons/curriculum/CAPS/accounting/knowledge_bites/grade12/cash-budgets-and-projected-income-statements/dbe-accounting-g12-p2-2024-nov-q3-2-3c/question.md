## Question (4 marks)

Comment on the control over delivery expenses and packing materials for October 2024. Quote figures. (Information I compares October 2024 budgeted with October 2024 actual: total sales R160 000 vs R221 000; delivery expenses R24 000 vs R33 150; packing materials R14 400 vs R26 520.)

## Method

expense control comment

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) — Because these are variable costs, judge control by comparing each expense to sales, not by the rand overspend alone. Delivery expenses: budgeted at 24 000 on sales of 160 000, which is 15% of sales; actual 33 150 on sales of 221 000 is still 15% of sales — the increase of R9 150 (38,1%) simply tracks the 38,1% rise in sales, so control is effective. Packing materials: budgeted at 14 400 on 160 000, which is 9% of sales; actual 26 520 on 221 000 is 12% of sales — the overspend of R12 120 (84,2%) far outstrips the 38,1% sales growth, so this cost is not well controlled.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Delivery expenses (2 marks; must be compared to sales; do not accept comparison to September figures): Effective control — budgeted at 15% of sales, actual is 15% of sales (100% utilised); OR overspent by R9 150 / (from R24 000) to R33 150 / by 38,1%, while total sales also increased by 38,1% only / actual sales of R221 000 exceeds the budgeted sales (R160 000). Packing material (2 marks): Not well controlled — actual exceeded budgeted by 33,3% / budgeted for 9% on sales and 12% has been spent; OR overspent by R12 120 (from R14 400) to R26 520 / by 84,2%, while total sales increased by 38,1% only.
