## Question (3 marks)

(v) Salaries for November 2024. (Information: the business has 9 employees who earn the same monthly salary; 5 of the employees will receive a bonus of 70% of the normal monthly salary during December 2024; the Cash Budget shows salaries of R143 750 for December 2024.)

## Method

salaries from bonus month

## Memo working

In December the payroll covers 9 normal salaries plus 5 bonuses of 0,7 of a salary each, i.e. 9 + (5 x 0,7) = 12,5 salary units, so 143 750 represents 12,5 units. November has only the 9 normal salaries: 143 750 x 9/12,5 = 103 500. (Memo option 2: one salary unit is 143 750 / 12,5 = 11 500, so the bonus portion is [8 050 x 5] = 40 250 (one mark + one mark) and November salaries are 143 750 - 40 250 = 103 500. Memo option 3: [143 750 x 4/12,5] = 46 000 (one mark) x 9/4 (one mark).)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

103 500 (one part correct)
