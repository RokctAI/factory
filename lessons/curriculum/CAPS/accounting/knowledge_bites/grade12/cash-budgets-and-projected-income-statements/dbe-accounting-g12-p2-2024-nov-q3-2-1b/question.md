## Question (5 marks)

Complete the Creditors' Payment Schedule for December 2024. (Information: creditors are paid 75% in the month after the date of purchase to qualify for a 3% discount, and the balance is paid in the month thereafter; credit purchases were R147 200 for September, R132 800 for October, and R137 600 for November — as calculated in question 3.2.1a.)

## Method

creditors payment schedule

## Memo working

Each month's credit purchases are settled in two slices: 75% in the following month, but only 97% of that slice is actually paid because of the 3% discount, and the remaining 25% in the month after that. For the December column: October purchases contribute their final 25% slice, 132 800 x 25% = 33 200; November purchases (137 600, see 3.2.1a) contribute the discounted slice, 137 600 x 75% x 97% = 100 104 (must be x [75% x 97%]; three marks if credit purchase not shown). December payment total: 33 200 + 100 104 = 133 304. (For reference, the memo's November column is: September 147 200 x 25% = 36 800; October 132 800 x 75% x 97% = 96 612; total 133 412. A December entry for September purchases would be superfluous and loses the method mark on the total.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

December column: October purchases 33 200; November purchases 100 104; total December payment 133 304 (one part correct)
