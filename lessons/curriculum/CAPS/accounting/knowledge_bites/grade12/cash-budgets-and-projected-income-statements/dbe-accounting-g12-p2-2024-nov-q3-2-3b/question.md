## Question (4 marks)

The actual credit sales were higher than the budgeted credit sales for October 2024. Explain why Bluey should not be concerned. Provide TWO points with figures. (Information I compares October 2024 budgeted with October 2024 actual: number of customers 540 vs 729; total sales R160 000 vs R221 000; credit sales R64 000 vs R145 000; gross profit R60 000 vs R82 875.)

## Method

budget vs actual comment

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) — Higher-than-budgeted credit sales are not a warning sign here because the whole business grew in October: the customer base expanded from 540 to 729 (up 189, or 35%), total sales rose from 160 000 to 221 000 (up 61 000, or 38%), and gross profit improved from 60 000 to 82 875 (up 22 875, or 38,1%). Any two of these points, each supported with figures, earn the marks. Comparisons must be against the October budget, not September.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Any TWO valid explanations with figures: The number of customers grew (from 540) to 729 / by 189 / by 35%; Gross profit has improved (from R60 000) to R82 875 / by R22 875 / by 38,1%; Total sales has improved (from R160 000) to R221 000 / by R61 000 / by 38%. Do not accept comparison to September figures.
