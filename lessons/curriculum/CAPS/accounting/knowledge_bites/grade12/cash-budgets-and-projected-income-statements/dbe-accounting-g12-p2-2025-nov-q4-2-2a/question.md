## Question (2 marks)

Loss of sales: The number of units sold in October has decreased. Give a reason, with figures, for this decrease. (From QUESTION 4.2, Warmbad Traders' Cash Budget extract: units sold — September actual 124, October actual 61; selling price per unit — September R1 600, October R2 480.)

## Method

sales decrease reason

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) — Look for what changed between September and October alongside the drop in units from 124 to 61: the selling price per unit was raised from R1 600 to R2 480, an increase of R880 per unit, which is 880/1 600 = 55%. Customers resisted the sharply higher price, so fewer stoves were sold.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Any valid point, with figures: The selling price increased (from R1 600) to R2 480 / by R880 / by 55%.
