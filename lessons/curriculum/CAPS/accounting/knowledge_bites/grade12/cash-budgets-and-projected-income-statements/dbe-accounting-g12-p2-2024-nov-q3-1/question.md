## Question (6 marks)

Show the amounts for the transactions below in the appropriate columns for the Cash Budget and the Projected Statement of Comprehensive Income for December 2024 in the ANSWER BOOK.

Example: Monthly insurance premium for December 2024, R5 000, will be paid.

3.1.1 Expected cash sales for December 2024, R132 000. Cost of sales, R101 000.

3.1.2 Drawings by the owner budgeted for December 2024, R40 000. This includes R34 000 cash and the remainder for merchandise.

3.1.3 A fixed deposit of R245 000 will mature on 31 December 2024. A direct deposit of R264 600 (inclusive of interest) will be made into the current banking account of the business on this date. Interest is not capitalised.

## Method

classify budget vs income statement

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

The test for each item: the Cash Budget records only actual money flowing in or out, while the Projected Statement of Comprehensive Income records only incomes and expenses.

Example: the insurance premium is both a cash payment and an expense, so it appears in both: Cash Budget payment 5 000; Projected Statement expense 5 000.

3.1.1: Cash sales bring in money, so the Cash Budget shows a receipt of 132 000. The Projected Statement shows the sales income of 132 000 and the matching cost of sales expense of 101 000 (or the net effect of 31 000).

3.1.2: Only the cash portion of drawings, R34 000, is a cash outflow for the Cash Budget. The R6 000 merchandise portion is not a cash flow, and drawings never appear in the Statement of Comprehensive Income — they reduce equity, not profit.

3.1.3: The full deposit of R264 600 lands in the bank, so it is a Cash Budget receipt. But only the interest portion, 264 600 - 245 000 = 19 600, is income in the Projected Statement — the R245 000 is the return of the fixed deposit capital, not income.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

3.1.1: Cash Budget receipt 132 000; Projected SOCI income 132 000 and expense 101 000 (OR net effect 31 000, two marks). 3.1.2: Cash Budget payment 34 000. 3.1.3: Cash Budget receipt 264 600; Projected SOCI income 19 600. (Example: Cash Budget payment 5 000; Projected SOCI expense 5 000.) -1 per line for superfluous entry (max -2); only if a mark is earned on that line.
