## Question (4 marks)

(ii) Interest on loan for December 2024. (Information: part of the loan from Diamond Bank will be paid on 1 December 2024 — the Cash Budget shows a loan repayment of R60 000 in December; interest at 14% p.a. is paid at the end of each month and is not capitalised; the Cash Budget shows interest on loan of R3 500 for November 2024.)

## Method

loan interest after repayment

## Memo working

The R60 000 repayment on 1 December reduces the monthly interest. The interest saved each month on 60 000 at 14% p.a. is 60 000 x [14/100 x 1/12] = 700, so December interest is 3 500 - 700 = 2 800. (Memo option 2: first find the loan balance — November interest of 3 500 at 14% p.a. implies a loan of 300 000; after the repayment the balance is [300 000 - 60 000] = 240 000 (one mark + one method mark), and one month's interest is 240 000 x [14/100 x 1/12] (one mark) = 2 800.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

2 800 (one part correct)
