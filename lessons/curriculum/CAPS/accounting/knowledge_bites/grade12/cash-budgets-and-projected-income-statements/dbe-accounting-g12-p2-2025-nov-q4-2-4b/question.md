## Question (2 marks)

Give ONE reason why the employees will not be satisfied with the revised remuneration policy. Quote figures. (From QUESTION 4.2, Warmbad Traders: the business has three employees; from October 2025 Dozer introduced commission on sales with a reduced monthly salary. Cash Budget actual figures — September: salaries R38 400, commission R3 900; October: salaries R27 000, commission R4 500.)

## Method

remuneration dissatisfaction reason

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) — Compare total pay under the old and new policy. October total earnings = salaries 27 000 + commission 4 500 = 31 500, i.e. R10 500 per employee across the three employees. That is below September's salaries alone of 38 400 (R12 800 per employee) — a drop of R6 900 in total (R2 300 each, about 18%). Against September's full salaries plus commission of 38 400 + 3 900 = 42 300, October's 31 500 is R10 800 lower (R3 600 per employee).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Any ONE valid point (with figures):

- Total earnings for October of R31 500 (R10 500 per employee) are lower than September salaries of R38 400 (R12 800 per employee) / by R6 900 (by R2 300 per employee) / by 18%.
- Total salaries and commission in September of R42 300 are more than total salaries and commission in October of R31 500 / by R10 800 (R3 600 per employee).
