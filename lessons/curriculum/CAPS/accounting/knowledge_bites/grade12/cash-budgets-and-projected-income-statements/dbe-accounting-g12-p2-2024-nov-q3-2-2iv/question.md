## Question (3 marks)

(iv) Insurance for December 2024. (Information: the Cash Budget shows total insurance of R17 500 for November 2024; the current monthly insurance premium on the existing computers is R4 500, and this premium will increase by 28% from 1 December 2024 to get additional cover on the new computers; the renewal period of other items insured only comes into effect next year.)

## Method

insurance premium increase

## Memo working

Only the computer premium changes; the rest of the November insurance stays the same. The increase on the computer premium is [4 500 x 28/100] = 1 260, so December insurance is 17 500 + 1 260 = 18 760. (Memo option 2: new computer premium [4 500 x 128/100] = 5 760 (one mark) plus the unchanged other insurance [17 500 - 4 500] = 13 000 (one mark), giving 18 760.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

18 760 (one part correct)
