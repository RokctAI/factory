## Question (6 marks)

VIVAN TRADERS: Information is provided for the budget period ending 31 January 2026. Complete the Debtors' Collection Schedule for January 2026. (The ANSWER BOOK pre-prints the schedule with credit sales October 2025 R1 417 500, November 2025 R1 443 750, December 2025 R1 496 250; the December 2025 collection column is completed as 198 450, 721 875 and 430 920 with total receipts 1 351 245; the January 2026 credit sales row and collection column are to be completed. From the information: total sales are November R1 925 000, December R1 995 000, January R2 030 000; credit sales account for 75% of total sales; debtors pay 30% in the month of sales subject to a 4% discount, 50% in the month following the month of sales, and 14% two months after the month of sales; the remaining amount is written off as irrecoverable in the third month after sales.)

## Method

debtors collection schedule

## Memo working

Work down the January 2026 column, one sales month at a time. From November 2025 credit sales of 1 443 750 (1 925 000 x 75%), January is two months after the sale, so collect 14%: 1 443 750 x 14% = 202 125. From December 2025 credit sales of 1 496 250 (1 995 000 x 75%), January is the month after the sale, so collect 50%: 1 496 250 x 50% = 748 125. January 2026 credit sales are 2 030 000 x 75% = 1 522 500; of these, 30% pay in the month of sale and receive the 4% discount, so the cash received is 1 522 500 x 30% x 96% = 438 480. Receipts from debtors for January = 202 125 + 748 125 + 438 480 = 1 388 730. (Memo cautions: if the credit sales of R1 522 500 are not shown but the R438 480 is correct, award three marks; a collection entered against October 2025 in the January column is superfluous — October's 198 450 was already collected in December — and loses the method mark on the total.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

January 2026 column: collections from November 2025 sales 202 125; collections from December 2025 sales 748 125; January 2026 credit sales 1 522 500 with collections 438 480; Receipts from debtors R1 388 730 (one part correct)
