## Question (4 marks)

Calculate the amount of the fixed deposit which will mature on 31 January 2026. (From QUESTION 4.1, Vivan Traders: a fixed deposit, including the quarterly interest, matures on 31 January 2026; the interest rate is 8% p.a., receivable quarterly; interest is not capitalised; the Projected Statement of Comprehensive Income shows interest on fixed deposit of R5 440 in January 2026 and R0 in December 2025.)

## Method

fixed deposit from interest

## Memo working

The R5 440 received in January is one quarter's interest (interest is receivable quarterly and is not capitalised). Convert it to a full year's interest: 5 440 x 12/3 (or x 4) = 21 760. The annual rate is 8%, so the capital is 21 760 x 100/8 = 272 000. Equivalently, one quarter at 8% p.a. is 2% of the capital, so 5 440 / 2% = 272 000. (Memo alternative: treating 5 440 as one month's interest, 5 440 x 12/1 x 100/8 = 816 000 — one part correct plus one method mark.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R272 000 (one part correct)
