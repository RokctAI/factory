## Question (2 marks)

Provide a calculation to show the amount of cash that was lost in October 2025 sales when compared to September 2025 sales. (From QUESTION 4.2, Warmbad Traders: units sold — September actual 124, October actual 61; selling price per unit in October R2 480.)

## Method

lost sales calculation

## Memo working

The business sold 124 - 61 = 63 fewer units in October. Value the shortfall at the October selling price: 63 x 2 480 = 156 240. Equivalently, compare the two months' sales at the October price: 124 x 2 480 = 307 520 against 61 x 2 480 = 151 280, a difference of 156 240.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R156 240 (one part correct)
