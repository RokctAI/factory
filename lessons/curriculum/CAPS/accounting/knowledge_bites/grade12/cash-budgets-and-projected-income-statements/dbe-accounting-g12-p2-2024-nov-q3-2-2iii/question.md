## Question (3 marks)

(iii) Deposit to be paid on 30 November 2024 for the new computers installed. (Information: new computers will be operational from 30 November 2024; a 20% cash deposit must be paid on 30 November 2024 and the balance repaid in 36 equal monthly instalments from 31 December 2024; the Cash Budget shows the December instalment as R9 800.)

## Method

deposit from instalments

## Memo working

The 36 instalments settle the balance after the deposit, which is 80% of the full price: [9 800 x 36] = 352 800. The deposit is 20% against that 80%, so deposit = 352 800 x 20/80 [i.e. x 100/80 x 20/100] = 88 200. (Memo option 2: gross up to the full price first — [352 800 x 100/80] = 441 000 (one mark) - 352 800 (one mark) = 88 200. Memo option 3: 441 000 (one mark) x 20/100 (one mark).)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

88 200 (one part correct)
