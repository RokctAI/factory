## Question (2 marks)

State TWO benefits that Warmbad Traders will forfeit by failing to meet its financial obligations with suppliers in October 2025. (From QUESTION 4.2, Warmbad Traders: creditors are paid one month after purchase, subject to a 4% discount; payments to creditors in October were budgeted at R306 000 but only R61 200 was actually paid.)

## Method

forfeited supplier benefits

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Any TWO valid points:

- Forfeit the early repayment discount.
- Lose the advantage of trade discounts for loyal customers / forfeit bulk-buying discounts and priority deals.
- Credit score or rating will decrease, which will affect future credit applications / the reputation of the business will be tarnished.
- Future credit limit will be affected.
- Suppliers might switch to a cash-only policy, affecting the business's cash flow.
