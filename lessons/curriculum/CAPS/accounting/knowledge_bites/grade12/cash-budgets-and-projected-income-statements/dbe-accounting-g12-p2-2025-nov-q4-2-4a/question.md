## Question (2 marks)

Remuneration to employees: Give ONE reason for Dozer awarding his employees with commission during September 2025. Quote figures. (From QUESTION 4.2, Warmbad Traders' Cash Budget extract for September 2025: units sold budgeted 90, actual 124; selling price R1 600 per unit; commission on sales budgeted R0, actual R3 900; cash surplus budgeted R63 800, actual R98 950.)

## Method

commission award reason

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) — The employees outperformed the budget: 124 units sold against the 90 budgeted, i.e. 34 extra units (34/90 = 37,8% more), which added 34 x R1 600 = R54 400 of sales and lifted the cash surplus from the budgeted R63 800 to R98 950 (an increase of 55,1%). The commission rewarded this performance.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Any ONE valid point (1 mark) with figure/s (1 mark):

- Number of units sold increased (from 90) to 124 / by 34 / by 37,8%.
- Sales increased by R54 400 (34 x R1 600) / by 37,8%.
- Cash surplus increased (from R63 800) to R98 950 / by 55,1%.
