## Question (2 marks)

Calculate the following for January 2026: Payments to creditors. (From QUESTION 4.1, Vivan Traders: cost of sales for November 2025 was R1 203 125 on total sales of R1 925 000; stock is replaced in the month of sales; 20% of stock is bought for cash and the rest on credit; creditors are paid in full in the second month after the month of purchases.)

## Method

payments to creditors

## Memo working

Creditors are paid in full in the second month after purchase, so January 2026 pays for November 2025 credit purchases. November purchases equal November cost of sales of 1 203 125 (stock replaced in the month of sale), of which 80% was on credit: 1 203 125 x 80% = 962 500 (OR 1 925 000 x 80/160, working straight from sales with the 60% mark-up on cost).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R962 500 (one part correct)
