## Question (2 marks)

Calculate the following amounts: (i) Rent expense for December 2024. (Information: the Cash Budget extract shows rent expense of R29 000 for November 2024; rent will increase by 6% during December 2024.)

## Method

percentage increase

## Memo working

The December rent is the November rent grown by 6%: 29 000 x 106/100 (i.e. x 1,06) = 30 740. (Memo option 2: 29 000 + 1 740, where 1 740 is 29 000 x 6%.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

30 740 (two marks or nothing)
