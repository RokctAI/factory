## Question (3 marks)

Calculate the following financial indicator for the year ended 29 February 2024: Interim dividend per share. (The information relates to Eybers Ltd. Given: The interim dividend of R416 000 was paid on 1 September 2023. On 1 March 2023 there were 1 200 000 shares in issue and 400 000 more shares were issued on 30 June 2023 — the interim dividend was therefore paid on 1 600 000 shares. The 34 000 shares repurchased on 30 November 2023 were still in issue on the interim date.)

## Method

dividends per share

## Memo working

Dividend per share = dividend amount divided by the number of shares that received it, expressed in cents. The interim dividend was paid on 1 September 2023, when the shares in issue were the original 1 200 000 plus the 400 000 new shares:

416 000 / 1 600 000 x 100 = 26 cents; denominator [1 200 000 + 400 000] (one mark)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

26 cents (one part correct; must be in cents, assume it is cents if not specified; accept R0,26)
