## Question (4 marks)

Calculate the total amount that Grant spent on the shares he acquired through the rights issue. NOTE: No shares were repurchased during the financial year. (The information relates to Winston Ltd. Given: ordinary share capital increased from R28 800 000 to R31 770 000 — an increase of R2 970 000 for the 600 000 rights-issue shares — and Grant took up 324 000 of those shares — see Q3.4a and Q3.5a/b.)

## Method

rights issue value

## Memo working

Step 1 — price per rights-issue share: the whole rights issue raised 2 970 000 for 600 000 shares, so the issue price was 2 970 000 / 600 000 = R4,95 (see 3.4).

Step 2 — Grant's spend: 324 000 (see above) x R4,95 = 1 603 800.

(Memo alternative: 2 970 000 (see 3.4, one method mark) - 1 366 200 (276 000 x 4,95; one mark + one method mark) = 1 603 800. Note: award method mark if calculations are provided for R4,95.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R1 603 800 (one part correct)
