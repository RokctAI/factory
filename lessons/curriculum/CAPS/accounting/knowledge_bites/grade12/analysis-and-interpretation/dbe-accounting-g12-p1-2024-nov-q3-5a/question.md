## Question (3 marks)

Grant Waters' % shareholding, rights issue and shares repurchased: Calculate the number of shares that Grant bought through the rights issue on 31 October 2023. (The information relates to Winston Ltd. Given: Grant Waters is the majority shareholder and owned 1 620 000 of the issued shares on 1 March 2023. On 31 October 2023, each shareholder was offered 10 shares for every 50 shares that they owned, at a reduced price. All shareholders exercised this right.)

## Method

rights issue share count

## Memo working

The rights issue gives 10 new shares for every 50 held. First find how many "blocks of 50" Grant owned, then multiply by 10:

1 620 000 / 50 = 32 400 (two marks) x 10 = 324 000 (mark awarded if x 10). Check numerator / denominator.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

324 000 shares
