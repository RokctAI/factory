## Question (4 marks)

Share price: Comment on the shareholders' satisfaction with the performance of their share price. Quote TWO financial indicators to support your response.

(The information, on 28 February 2025: for Shorts Ltd, net asset value per share 270 cents and market price on the JSE 256 cents; for Lynn Ltd, net asset value per share 958 cents and market price 976 cents.)

## Method

nav vs market price comment

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

The reasoning: compare each company's market price with its net asset value (NAV) per share. When the market price trades below NAV, the market values the share at less than the underlying net assets — the share is undervalued and shareholders are dissatisfied (Shorts Ltd). When the market price trades above NAV, demand for the share is strong and shareholders are satisfied (Lynn Ltd). Marks: comparison of NAV and market price (2 marks); figures with trends (2 marks).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Shorts Ltd: NAV of 270 cents is higher than the MP of 256 cents / by 14 cents.
- Lynn Ltd: MP of 976 cents is higher than the NAV of 958 cents / by 18 cents.

For four marks: In Shorts Ltd shares are undervalued at 14 cents, while in Lynn Ltd shares are overvalued at 18 cents.
