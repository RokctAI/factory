## Question (4 marks)

Shorts Ltd — Liquidity: Explain how the credit policy has improved the liquidity of the company. Quote TWO financial indicators with trends.

(The information relates to Shorts Ltd for the year ended 28 February 2025, with the prior year in brackets: debtors' collection period 31 days (42 days); creditors' payment period 90 days (71 days); current ratio 2,4 : 1 (2,2 : 1); acid-test ratio 1,8 : 1 (1,4 : 1).)

## Method

credit policy liquidity comment

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

The reasoning: the credit policy has two sides — collecting from debtors and paying creditors. Cash now comes in 11 days sooner from debtors and goes out 19 days later to creditors, so cash stays available in the business for longer, improving liquidity. The current and acid-test ratios measure liquidity itself, not the credit policy, so they do not answer this question. Marks: financial indicators (2 marks); figures with trends (2 marks).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Debtors' collection period has improved (from 42 days) to 31 days / by 11 days.
- Creditors' payment period improved (from 71 days) to 90 days / by 19 days.

Do not accept current ratio and acid-test ratio.
