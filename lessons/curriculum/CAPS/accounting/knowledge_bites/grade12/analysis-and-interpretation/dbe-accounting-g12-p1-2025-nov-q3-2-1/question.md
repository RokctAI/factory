## Question (4 marks)

Shorts Ltd — Operating efficiency: The CEO believes that the company's pricing policy, when compared to its competitors, is well managed. Quote TWO financial indicators/financial data with trends to support his statement.

(The information relates to Shorts Ltd for the year ended 28 February 2025, with the prior year in brackets: sales R77 m (R48 m); mark-up 50% (65%); % operating expenses on sales 17,8% (18,2%); % net profit after tax on sales 26,4% (22%).)

## Method

quote pricing policy indicators

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

The reasoning: lowering the mark-up from 65% to 50% made prices more competitive; the pay-off shows in sales jumping from R48 m to R77 m, and the higher volume more than compensated for the thinner margin, since the % net profit after tax on sales still rose from 22% to 26,4%. Marks: financial indicators/financial data (2 marks); figures with trends (2 marks).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Any TWO of:

- Mark-up % decreased (from 65%) to 50% / by 23,1% / by 15% points.
- % net profit after tax on sales increased (from 22%) to 26,4% / by 20% / by 4,4% points.
- Sales have improved (from R48 m) to R77 m / by R29 m.

Do not accept operating expenses on sales.
