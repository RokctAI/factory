## Question (3 marks)

Calculate the following financial indicator for the year ended 29 February 2024: % operating expenses on sales. (The information relates to Eybers Ltd. Given, from the Statement of Comprehensive Income: Sales R8 240 600; Operating expenses R1 360 950.)

## Method

operating expenses on sales percentage

## Memo working

The indicator compares operating expenses with sales, expressed as a percentage:

1 360 950 / 8 240 600 x 100 = 16,5%

(Memo notes: the 'x 100' does not constitute 'one part correct'. Numerator and denominator must be marked as such. Do not award marks for any operations done to stand-alone figures, e.g. no tick if anything else is added to R1 360 950.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

16,5% (one part correct; calculated as a %; % sign not necessary; accept 17%; do not accept 0,165/0,17)
