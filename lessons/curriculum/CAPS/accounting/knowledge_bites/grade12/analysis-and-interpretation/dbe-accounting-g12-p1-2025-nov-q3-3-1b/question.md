## Question (4 marks)

A potential investor believes that Lynn Ltd has a better dividend pay-out policy when compared to Shorts Ltd. Explain why you would agree with her. Provide TWO points with figures.

(The information, for the year ended 28 February 2025 with the prior year in brackets: for Shorts Ltd, earnings per share 130 cents (165 cents), dividends per share 162 cents (81 cents), dividend pay-out rate 124,6% (49,1%); for Lynn Ltd, earnings per share 510 cents (528 cents), dividends per share 275 cents (260 cents), dividend pay-out rate 53,9% (49,2%).)

## Method

dividend payout policy comparison

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

The reasoning: a pay-out rate above 100% means a company is distributing more than it earned — Shorts Ltd paid 162 cents per share out of earnings of only 130 cents, dipping into past reserves. Lynn Ltd paid out just over half its earnings and retained the rest for growth, which is the healthier, more sustainable policy. Marks: financial indicator with figures (2 marks); explanation (2 marks).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Shorts Ltd:

- They did not retain any funds but had used 24,6% (162c of 130c) of funds from the previous financial year / it increased (from 49,1%) to 124,6%.
- This was to distract shareholders from the decrease in the profitability (EPS) of the company in the current financial year; hiding company share performance in the market / shares undervalued; they wanted to make the shareholders happy.

Lynn Ltd:

- They have retained 46,1% (235c of 510c) of its earnings / it increased (from 49,2%) to 53,9%.
- This indicates that they have plans for future expansion purposes which will benefit shareholders in the future / allowed reinvestment in profitable projects.
