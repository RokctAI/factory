## Question (3 marks)

Lynn Ltd: The debt-equity ratio indicates that additional loans were acquired during the financial year. The directors are of the opinion that this was a good decision. Quote ONE financial indicator to support their opinion.

(The information, for the year ended 28 February 2025 with the prior year in brackets: Lynn Ltd's debt-equity ratio 0,6 : 1 (0,4 : 1); % return on total capital employed 21,8% (19,2%); interest rate on loans 12,5%.)

## Method

gearing rotce comment

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

The reasoning: borrowing is a good decision when the return earned on the capital exceeds the cost of borrowing. Lynn Ltd's return on total capital employed (ROTCE) rose after taking the extra loans and sits well above the 12,5% interest rate on loans — the company is positively geared, so the borrowed funds earn more than they cost. Marks: financial indicator with figures (2 marks); explanation (1 mark).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- ROTCE has increased (from 19,2%) to 21,8% / by 2,6% points / by 13,5%.
- ROTCE is higher than the interest rate (12,5%), the company is positively geared / company has effectively used the borrowed funds to generate higher profits.
