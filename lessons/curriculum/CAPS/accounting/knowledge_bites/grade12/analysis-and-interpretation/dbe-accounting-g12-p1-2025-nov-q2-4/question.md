## Question (2 marks)

The directors should be concerned about the cash resources of the company for the year ended 28 February 2025. Give ONE reason (with figures) for this concern.

(The information relates to Mustang Ltd: per the Cash Flow Statement, cash and cash equivalents fell from a favourable 415 000 at the beginning of the year to an unfavourable (757 000) at the end, a net change of (1 172 000).)

## Method

cash resources comment

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

A full response pairs a reason (1 mark) with the supporting figure or figures (1 mark), taken from the completed Cash Flow Statement.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Reason (1 mark) with figure/s (1 mark), ONE of:

- The cash and cash equivalents have changed (from a favourable balance of R415 000) to an unfavourable balance of R757 000 (see Q2.2).
- The net cash outflow of R1 172 000 (see Q2.2) indicates that the company's operations did not generate enough cash to cover its needs.
