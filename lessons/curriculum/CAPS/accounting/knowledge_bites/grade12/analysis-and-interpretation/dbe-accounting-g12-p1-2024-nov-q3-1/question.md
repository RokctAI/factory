## Question (4 marks)

Profitability: Quote TWO financial indicators with figures and trends to indicate that the company is managing its expenses well. (The information relates to Winston Ltd for the year ended 29 February 2024. Given financial indicators, 2024 versus 2023: mark-up percentage 45% versus 40%; % operating expenses on sales 14% versus 19%; % operating profit on sales 18% versus 12%; % net profit before tax on sales 19% versus 11%. NOTE: Where comments or explanations are required, you should quote financial indicators and trends with figures and give a reason or an explanation for the financial indicators quoted.)

## Method

quote profitability indicators

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Reasoning: expense management shows up in the indicators that involve expenses. If expenses are under control, the % operating expenses on sales falls, and the profit percentages that come after expenses (% operating profit on sales, % net profit before tax on sales) rise. Quote the indicator by name, its trend and both figures. Mark-up % does not measure expense management — it measures pricing on cost — so it earns no marks here.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Any TWO of:

- % operating expenses on sales has decreased / improved (from 19%) to 14% / by 5%-points / by 26,3%
- % operating profit on sales has increased / improved (from 12%) to 18% / by 6%-points / by 50%
- % net profit before tax on sales has increased / improved (from 11%) to 19% / by 8%-points / by 72,7%

Do not accept mark-up %. (Financial indicators: 2 marks; figures and trends: 2 marks)
