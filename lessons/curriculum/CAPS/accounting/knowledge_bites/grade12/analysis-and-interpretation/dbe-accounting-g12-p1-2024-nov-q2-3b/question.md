## Question (4 marks)

Calculate the following financial indicator for the year ended 29 February 2024: Stock turnover rate. (The information relates to Eybers Ltd. Given: Cost of sales R5 060 000; Trading stock R174 000 on 29 February 2024 and R193 000 on 28 February 2023.)

## Method

stock turnover rate

## Memo working

Stock turnover rate = cost of sales divided by average stock. Average stock is half of opening plus closing stock:

Average stock: 1/2 (174 000 + 193 000) = 183 500 (two marks for the average)

5 060 000 / 183 500 = 27,6 times

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

27,6 times (one part correct if average stock (x 1/2) is used; accept 28 times)
