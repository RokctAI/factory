## Question (4 marks)

Apart from the factors and financial indicators covered in the previous questions, identify and explain TWO other points indicating that the company is well managed by the CEO. Quote figures. (The information relates to Winston Ltd for the year ended 29 February 2024. Given: solvency ratio 3,8 : 1 in 2024 versus 2,8 : 1 in 2023; current ratio 2,5 : 1 versus 1,6 : 1; acid-test ratio 1,9 : 1 versus 1,2 : 1.)

## Method

liquidity solvency indicators comment

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Reasoning: the earlier questions already used the profitability, dividend, gearing and shareholding indicators, so the "other" points must come from the remaining indicators — solvency and the two liquidity ratios. Quote the indicator with both figures and explain what the improvement means.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Any TWO (financial indicators with figures and trend, 2 marks; explanation, 2 marks):

- Solvency ratio increased / improved (from 2,8 : 1) to 3,8 : 1 — the business has a stronger financial position
- Current ratio increased / improved (from 1,6 : 1) to 2,5 : 1 — the business's ability to pay its short-term debts has strengthened
- Acid-test ratio increased / improved (from 1,2 : 1) to 1,9 : 1 — the business has enough cash to cover its short-term debts
