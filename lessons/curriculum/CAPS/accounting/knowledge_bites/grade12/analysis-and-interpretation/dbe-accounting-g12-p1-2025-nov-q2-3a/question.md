## Question (3 marks)

Calculate the following financial indicators for the year ended 28 February 2025: Net asset value per share.

(The information relates to Mustang Ltd. Ordinary shareholders' equity was 7 140 120 on 28 February 2025, and there were 498 000 ordinary shares in issue on that date.)

## Method

net asset value per share

## Memo working

Step 1 — net asset value per share is the ordinary shareholders' equity divided by the number of shares in issue, expressed in cents.

Step 2 — 7 140 120 / 498 000 x 100/1 = 1 433,8 cents.

(Memo: the 'x 100' does not constitute 'one part correct'. Numerator and denominator must be marked as such. Do not award marks for any operations done to stand-alone figures, e.g. no tick if anything else is added to R7 140 120 / 498 000 / 518 400.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

1 433,8 cents (one part correct; calculated as R/c; R/c not necessary; accept R14,34 / R14,33 / 1 433c / 1 434c)
