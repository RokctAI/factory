## Question (5 marks)

Calculate the following financial indicators for the year ended 28 February 2025: Dividend pay-out rate.

(The information relates to Mustang Ltd: net profit after tax was 518 400; dividends paid per the Cash Flow Statement for the year was R252 000; shareholders for dividends was 162 000 on 29 February 2024 and 129 480 on 28 February 2025; the directors paid an interim dividend on 31 July 2024 and declared a final dividend on 28 February 2025.)

## Method

dividend payout rate

## Memo working

Step 1 — the interim dividend: the R252 000 paid during the year settled last year's final dividend of 162 000 plus the interim dividend, so the interim dividend is 252 000 - 162 000 = 90 000 [two marks].

Step 2 — the final dividend declared is the closing shareholders for dividends balance, 129 480 [one mark].

Step 3 — total dividends for the year: (90 000 + 129 480) = 219 480.

Step 4 — dividend pay-out rate: 219 480 / 518 400 x 100/1 = 42,3%.

(Memo: the 'x 100' does not constitute 'one part correct'. Numerator and denominator must be marked as such.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

42,3% (one part correct; do not accept 0,42)
