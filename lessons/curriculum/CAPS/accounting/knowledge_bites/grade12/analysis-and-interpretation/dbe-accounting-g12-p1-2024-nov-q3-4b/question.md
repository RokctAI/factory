## Question (6 marks)

Explain how the financing decisions affected the risk and gearing of the company. Quote TWO financial indicators with figures. (The information relates to Winston Ltd for the year ended 29 February 2024. Given: debt-equity ratio 0,2 : 1 in 2024 versus 0,5 : 1 in 2023; % return on average capital employed 24% versus 15%; interest rate on loans 12%.)

## Method

gearing risk comment

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Reasoning: gearing is judged with the debt-equity ratio (risk) and the return on average capital employed (whether borrowing pays off). Although the loan grew, the share issue grew equity even more, so debt-equity actually improved from 0,5 : 1 to 0,2 : 1 — low financial risk. ROTCE of 24% is double the 12% interest rate on loans, so the company is positively geared: borrowed money earns more than it costs.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Debt/equity ratio: decreased / improved (from 0,5 : 1) to 0,2 : 1 / by 0,3 : 1; the company still has a low financial risk.
- Return on average capital employed (ROTCE): increased (from 15%) to 24% / by 9%-points / by 60%; the company has positive gearing / ROTCE (24%) is greater than the interest rate on loan (12%).
