## Question (5 marks)

Calculate Grant's % shareholding after the rights issue on 29 February 2024. (The information relates to Winston Ltd. Given: 3 000 000 shares were in issue on 1 March 2023; the rights issue on 31 October 2023 offered 10 shares for every 50 owned and all shareholders exercised the right; no other shares were issued or repurchased during the year. Grant owned 1 620 000 shares before the rights issue and bought 324 000 through it — see Q3.5a.)

## Method

percentage shareholding

## Memo working

Step 1 — Grant's shares after the rights issue: 1 620 000 + 324 000 (see above) = 1 944 000.

Step 2 — total shares after the rights issue: every shareholder took up 10-for-50, so the total also grew by one fifth: denominator [3 000 000 + 600 000] = 3 600 000 (one mark + one method mark).

Step 3 — percentage: (1 620 000 + 324 000) / 3 600 000 x 100 = 54%.

(Memo alternative: work with the other shareholders — (1 380 000 + 276 000) / 3 600 000 x 100 = 46% (two marks); 100% - 46% = 54%.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

54% (one part correct)
