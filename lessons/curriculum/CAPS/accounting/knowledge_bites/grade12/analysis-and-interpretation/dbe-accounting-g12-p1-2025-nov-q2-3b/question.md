## Question (5 marks)

Calculate the following financial indicators for the year ended 28 February 2025: Acid-test ratio.

(The information relates to Mustang Ltd on 28 February 2025: Trade and other receivables totalled 3 167 000, all debtors' control; cash and cash equivalents consisted of petty cash of 18 000; trade and other payables totalled 1 609 380; and there was a bank overdraft of 775 000.)

## Method

acid test ratio

## Memo working

Step 1 — the acid-test ratio compares current assets excluding inventories with current liabilities: (receivables plus cash) to (payables plus bank overdraft).

Step 2 — (3 167 000 + 18 000) : (1 609 380 + 775 000) (one mark each) = 3 185 000 : 2 384 380.

Step 3 — divide both sides by the current liabilities: 3 185 000 / 2 384 380 = 1,3, so the ratio is 1,3 : 1.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

1,3 : 1 (one part correct; in form x : 1; accept 1,34 : 1)
