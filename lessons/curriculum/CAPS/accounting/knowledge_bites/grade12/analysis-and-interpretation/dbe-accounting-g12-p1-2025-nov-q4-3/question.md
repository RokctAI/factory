## Question (2 marks)

Refer to Information C. Explain the meaning of the term insolvent.

(Information C — article: Piranna Ltd was declared insolvent in March 2025. Companies that are insolvent need drastic measures to improve their financial situation.)

## Method

define insolvent

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

The reasoning: solvency compares total assets with total liabilities. A company is insolvent when that comparison turns negative — its liabilities exceed its assets, so even selling everything would not settle what it owes.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Any valid explanation:

- The liabilities exceed the assets / company does not have enough money or assets to pay what you owe.
- The company will not be able to settle its liabilities with its assets if it is liquidated / financially unable to meet obligations.
- The company has a negative equity.
