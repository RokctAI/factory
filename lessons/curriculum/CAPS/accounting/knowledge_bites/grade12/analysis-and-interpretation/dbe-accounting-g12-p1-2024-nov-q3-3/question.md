## Question (4 marks)

Earnings and returns: Explain whether the shareholders should be satisfied or not with the earnings per share and returns on average shareholders' equity of the company. Quote TWO financial indicators with figures. (The information relates to Winston Ltd for the year ended 29 February 2024. Given: earnings per share 112 cents in 2024 versus 104 cents in 2023; return on average shareholders' equity 20% versus 11%; interest rate on fixed deposits 7,5% versus 6,5%.)

## Method

eps and roshe comment

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Reasoning: quote each indicator with its trend and figures, then judge it against a benchmark. EPS is benchmarked against its own prior year — it grew, so profitability per share improved. ROSHE is benchmarked against the return of an alternative investment, such as the interest rate on fixed deposits (7,5%) — earning 20% on shareholders' funds beats that comfortably.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- EPS: increased (from 104 cents) to 112 cents / by 8 cents / by 7,7%; shareholders should be happy as it shows an increase in the profitability of the company.
- ROSHE: increased (from 11%) to 20% / by 9%-points / by 81,8%; shareholders should be satisfied because ROSHE (20%) is greater than the interest rate on fixed deposit (7,5%).
