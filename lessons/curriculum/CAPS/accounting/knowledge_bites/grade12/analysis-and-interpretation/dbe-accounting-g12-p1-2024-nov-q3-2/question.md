## Question (3 marks)

Dividend pay-out policy: Comment on the dividend pay-out policy of the company. Explain why this is a responsible change in policy. Provide ONE point. (The information relates to Winston Ltd for the year ended 29 February 2024. Given: dividend pay-out rate 60% in 2024 versus 86% in 2023; dividends per share 67 cents versus 89 cents; earnings per share 112 cents versus 104 cents.)

## Method

dividend payout policy comment

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Reasoning: first state the trend with figures — the pay-out rate dropped from 86% to 60%, so the company now retains 40% of its earnings instead of 14%. Then explain why retaining more profit is responsible: retained earnings fund expansion and protect cash flow, which benefits shareholders in the long run.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Figures and trend (1 mark): It decreased (from 86%) to 60% / by 26%-points / by 30,2%.

Explanation (2 marks, part mark for unclear/incomplete answers), any ONE of:

- Funds have been retained for future expansion purposes
- Shareholders will benefit in the future as funds have been set aside
- Protect the cash flow of the business
