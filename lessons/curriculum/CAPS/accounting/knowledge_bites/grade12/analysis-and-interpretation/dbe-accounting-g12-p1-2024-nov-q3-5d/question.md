## Question (4 marks)

Grant intends to convince the board of directors to repurchase shares from him in the next financial year at R12 per share. Explain why the other board members would probably vote against this transaction proposed by Grant. Provide TWO points (with figures). (The information relates to Winston Ltd. Given: market value per share 950 cents; net asset value per share 564 cents; Grant's shareholding after the rights issue 54% — see Q3.5b.)

## Method

share repurchase evaluation

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Reasoning: test the proposed R12 (1 200 cents) against the two value benchmarks for a share — the market price and the net asset value — and consider the governance angle of a majority shareholder pushing a deal that benefits himself.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Any TWO valid points with figures (part mark for unclear/incomplete answers):

- The repurchase price of (R12 / 1 200 cents) exceeds the market value per share of 950 cents / by 250 cents
- The repurchase price of (R12 / 1 200 cents) exceeds the NAV per share of 564 cents / by 636 cents
- He is taking advantage of his status as the majority shareholder, 54% (see above) / this constitutes self-enrichment due to his position as the majority shareholder by 54%
