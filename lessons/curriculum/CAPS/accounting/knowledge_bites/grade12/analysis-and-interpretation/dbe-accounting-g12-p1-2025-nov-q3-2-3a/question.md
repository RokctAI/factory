## Question (4 marks)

Shareholding of Lewis Clark in Shorts Ltd: Refer to Information A, B and C. Provide a calculation to show the effect which the repurchase of shares had on Lewis's % shareholding.

(The information: Lewis Clark, the CFO, is a shareholder of Shorts Ltd. He convinced the board to repurchase 400 000 shares from a dissatisfied shareholder at R3 each. Shorts Ltd had 1 783 250 shares in issue on 29 February 2024 and, after the repurchase of 400 000 shares, 1 383 250 shares in issue on 28 February 2025. Lewis's shareholding was 40% on 29 February 2024.)

## Method

percentage shareholding after repurchase

## Memo working

Step 1 — Lewis's number of shares (unchanged by the repurchase): [1 783 250 x 40%] = 713 300 (one mark).

Step 2 — his percentage of the smaller number of shares now in issue: 713 300 / 1 383 250 (one mark) x 100 = 51,6%.

(Memo option 2: the other shareholders now hold 669 950 (two marks) / 1 383 250 x 100 = 48,4% (one mark); 100% - 48,4% = 51,6% (three marks).)

The effect: the repurchase pushed Lewis's shareholding from 40% up to 51,6%, making him the majority shareholder.

(Memo: 'x 100' does not constitute 'one part correct'. Numerator and denominator must be marked as such. Do not award marks for any operations done to stand-alone figures, e.g. no tick if anything else is added to 713 300 / 1 383 250.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

51,6% (one part correct; % sign not necessary; accept 51% / 52%)
