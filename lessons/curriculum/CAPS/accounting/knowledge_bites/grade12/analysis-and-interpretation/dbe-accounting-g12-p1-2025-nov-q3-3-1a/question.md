## Question (4 marks)

Shorts Ltd and Lynn Ltd — Returns and dividends pay-out policy: The CEOs of both companies will discuss their companies' performance. Comment on what they should mention regarding % returns. Quote ONE financial indicator with figures.

(The information, for the year ended 28 February 2025 with the prior year in brackets: % return on shareholders' equity was 6% (14,7%) for Shorts Ltd and 16,1% (14,5%) for Lynn Ltd; interest on fixed deposit was 7,1% for Shorts Ltd and 6,7% for Lynn Ltd.)

## Method

returns comparison comment

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

The reasoning: compare each company's return on shareholders' equity (ROSHE) with its trend and with the alternative return shareholders could earn on a fixed deposit. Shorts Ltd's return collapsed to below the fixed-deposit rate; Lynn Ltd's return improved to well above it. Marks: financial indicator with figures (2 marks); explanation/comment (2 marks).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Shorts Ltd:

- ROSHE has decreased (from 14,7%) to 6% / by 8,7% points / by 59,2%.
- Directors will acknowledge this decrease and indicate that they are working on improving it in the new financial year; the return from ROSHE is less than what could have been earned from fixed deposit.

Lynn Ltd:

- ROSHE has increased (from 14,5%) to 16,1% / by 1,6% points / by 11%.
- Directors will remind the shareholders that they are earning more than the interest of fixed deposit; investing more in the company has better returns than they would earn from a fixed deposit.
