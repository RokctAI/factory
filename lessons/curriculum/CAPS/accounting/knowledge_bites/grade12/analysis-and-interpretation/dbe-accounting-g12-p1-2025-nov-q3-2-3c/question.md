## Question (2 marks)

Calculate the additional amount that the company paid for the shares repurchased, compared to the share price on the JSE.

(The information: Shorts Ltd repurchased 400 000 shares at R3 each; the market price of the share on the JSE on the last day of the financial year was 256 cents.)

## Method

repurchase premium over market price

## Memo working

Step 1 — the premium per share above the JSE price: R3,00 - R2,56 = R0,44.

Step 2 — the additional amount for all the repurchased shares: 400 000 x 0,44 = 176 000.

(Memo option 2: total paid minus total at market price: 1 200 000 - 1 024 000 = 176 000.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

176 000 (one part correct)
