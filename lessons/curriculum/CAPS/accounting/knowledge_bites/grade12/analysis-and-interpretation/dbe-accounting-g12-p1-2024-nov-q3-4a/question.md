## Question (4 marks)

Financing strategies and gearing: The directors of Winston Ltd decided to buy new fixed assets in 2024 to enhance the company. Identify the TWO main sources (over R1m) that were used to finance this purchase. Provide figures. (Given, from the Notes and Statement of Financial Position, 29 February 2024 versus 28 February 2023: fixed assets purchased R8 235 000 versus R4 180 000; fixed assets sold R710 000 versus R0; fixed deposit R550 000 versus R250 000; ordinary share capital R31 770 000 versus R28 800 000; loan R12 913 000 versus R9 400 000.)

## Method

identify financing sources

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Reasoning: look for the balances that increased by more than R1 million during the year. Ordinary share capital rose by 31 770 000 - 28 800 000 = 2 970 000 (shares issued through the rights issue), and the loan rose by 12 913 000 - 9 400 000 = 3 513 000. The fixed assets sold (710 000) and the fixed deposit movement are below R1m, so they do not qualify.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Shares issued (sold), R2 970 000 (from 28 800 000) to 31 770 000 / by 10,3%
- Increase in loan, R3 513 000 (from 9 400 000) to 12 913 000 / by 37,4%

Do not accept: sale of fixed assets / cash and cash equivalents. (Source: 2 marks; figures: 2 marks)
