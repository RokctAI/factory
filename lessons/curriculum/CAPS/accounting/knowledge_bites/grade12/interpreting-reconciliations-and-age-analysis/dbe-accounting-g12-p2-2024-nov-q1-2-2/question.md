## Question (14 marks)

Complete the correct Debtors' List on 31 August 2024. The pre-adjustment figures have been entered.

(MATSAMO TRADERS — same information as Q1.2.1. Pre-adjustment debtors' balances: N Naidoo 63 000; S Sibiya 69 000; V Esterhuizen 114 000; J Nyoka 95 700; W du Plessis 11 500; Total 353 200. Errors and omissions: (i) credit sale invoice to N Naidoo, R4 500, not entered; (ii) credit sale to S Sibiya, R18 000, posted to V Esterhuizen's account; (iii) debtors' allowances column in the DAJ undercast by R11 000; (iv) EFT of R5 700 by W du Plessis correctly journalised but posted as R7 500 to his account; (v) credit sales to V Esterhuizen, R5 200, recorded as R2 500 in the Debtors' Journal and posted as such; (vi) goods returned by J Nyoka, R3 450, posted to the wrong side of his account in the Debtors' Ledger.)

## Method

debtors list corrections

## Memo working

Correct each debtor's personal account for the errors that touch the Debtors' Ledger. Note that error (iii), the DAJ column undercast, affects only the control account total, not any individual debtor.

N Naidoo: the omitted invoice must be added: 63 000 + 4 500 = 67 500.

S Sibiya: his R18 000 sale was posted to the wrong account, so add it back: 69 000 + 18 000 = 87 000.

V Esterhuizen: remove the R18 000 that belongs to Sibiya, then add the understated sale: 114 000 - 18 000 + 2 700 (see 1.2.1(v)) = 98 700 (one part correct).

J Nyoka: the returns of R3 450 were debited instead of credited, so the correction is double the amount: 95 700 - 3 450 - 3 450 (i.e. -6 900, two marks) = 88 800 (one part correct).

W du Plessis: reverse the wrong posting of R7 500 and post the correct R5 700: 11 500 + 7 500 - 5 700 (i.e. +1 800, two marks) = 13 300 (one part correct).

Total: 67 500 + 87 000 + 98 700 + 88 800 + 13 300 = 355 300 (one part correct) — this agrees with the corrected Debtors' Control balance of R355 300 from Q1.2.1, which is the whole point of the reconciliation.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

N Naidoo 67 500; S Sibiya 87 000; V Esterhuizen 98 700; J Nyoka 88 800; W du Plessis 13 300; Correct total of Debtors' List 355 300 (one part correct)
