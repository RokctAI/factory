## Question (10 marks)

BANK RECONCILIATION: The information relates to Duma Stores on 31 July 2025. The business is owned by Duma Mathupha. They use the official Bank Statement, received on the 28th of each month, to reconcile their Cash Journals. Complete the table provided in the ANSWER BOOK by showing the entries that must be recorded in the Cash Journals on 31 July 2025. (The ANSWER BOOK pre-prints a two-column table headed Cash Receipts Journal R162 000 and Cash Payments Journal R55 200.)

INFORMATION:

A. Extract from the Bank Reconciliation Statement on 30 June 2025: outstanding deposits dated 29 June 2025, R132 000, and 30 June 2025, R18 600; outstanding EFTs: EFT 124 (Typek Stationers) R5 310 and EFT 125 (Cola Stores) R9 990.

B. Provisional totals in the Cash Journals on 31 July 2025 before receiving the Bank Statement: Cash Receipts Journal R162 000; Cash Payments Journal R55 200.

C. Items on the Bank Statement that do not agree with the July 2025 Cash Journals (columns: Date | Details | Debit (R) | Credit (R)): 29 June deposit, credit 132 000; 30 June deposit, credit 8 600; 3 July EFT 124 (Typek Stationers), debit 3 150; 5 July EFT 125 (Cola Stores), debit 9 990; 10 July direct deposit (Q Ngcobo), credit 16 500; 15 July debit order (Prime Insurers), debit 2 700; 15 July debit order (Prime Insurers), debit 2 700; 25 July interest, debit 975; July service fees, debit 605; 28 July A-Z Suppliers, debit 12 000. Notes: (i) the internal auditor discovered that the supporting cash slips for the deposit on 30 June 2025 amounted to R18 600 — the shortfall is irrecoverable because the cashier at that time has resigned; (ii) EFT 124 on 3 July 2025 (Typek Stationers) is correct on the Bank Statement; (iii) the direct deposit on 10 July 2025 by Q Ngcobo (a tenant) was recorded incorrectly in the Cash Payments Journal; (iv) the debit order on 15 July 2025 to Prime Insurers for the business insurance was processed twice due to insufficient funds in June — the amounts covered payments for both June and July 2025 to bring the account up to date; (v) a bank transfer on 28 July 2025 in favour of A-Z Suppliers was overstated by R11 000 by the bank clerk — the bank was notified and will correct the error in August 2025.

D. Entries in the Cash Journals for July 2025 that were not reflected on the July 2025 Bank Statement: deposit on 30 July 2025 R41 850; EFT 207 R7 650; EFT 208 R10 800.

E. Duma, the owner, used a business debit card on 29 July 2025 for the payment of R13 000 to Star Motors for repairs to the company vehicle, but he neglected to submit the transaction slip to the bookkeeper.

F. The Bank Statement on 28 July 2025 showed a favourable balance of R119 080.

## Method

cash journal supplementary entries

## Memo working

Work through each difference and decide whether it belongs in the Cash Receipts Journal or the Cash Payments Journal.

Cash Receipts Journal (on the provisional R162 000): 2 160 (two marks) corrects EFT 124 — it was recorded at R5 310 in June but the Bank Statement shows the correct amount of R3 150, so the overstatement of 5 310 - 3 150 = 2 160 comes back in. Then 16 500 (one mark) + 16 500 (one mark) = 33 000 corrects the tenant Q Ngcobo's direct deposit that was recorded in the CPJ: one 16 500 cancels the wrong CPJ entry and the other records the receipt itself.

Cash Payments Journal (on the provisional R55 200): 10 000 (two marks) is the missing cash on the 30 June deposit (cash slips R18 600 versus only R8 600 banked; irrecoverable, so it is written off); 2 700 (one mark) + 2 700 (one mark) = 5 400 for the Prime Insurers debit orders covering June and July; 13 000 (one mark) for the owner's debit card payment to Star Motors; 975 (one mark) interest charged by the bank; 605 (one mark) service fees.

(Memo: -1 foreign items, max -1, only if marks are scored elsewhere for the same figure; -1 superfluous items, max -1; ensure that the candidate does not get full marks for including any incorrect figure/s. Note that the A-Z Suppliers bank error of R11 000 is the bank's mistake — it belongs in the Bank Reconciliation Statement, not in the journals.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Cash Receipts Journal (on R162 000): 2 160; 33 000 [16 500 + 16 500]. Cash Payments Journal (on R55 200): 10 000; 5 400 [2 700 + 2 700]; 13 000; 975; 605
