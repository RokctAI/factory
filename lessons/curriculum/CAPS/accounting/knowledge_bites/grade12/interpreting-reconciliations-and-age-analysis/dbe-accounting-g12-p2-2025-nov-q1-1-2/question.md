## Question (7 marks)

Prepare the Bank Reconciliation Statement on 31 July 2025. (The ANSWER BOOK offers a choice of two templates: a two-column method with Debit and Credit columns, OR a one-column method. Choose only ONE.)

(DUMA STORES — same information as Q1.1.1: Bank Statement balance on 28 July 2025 favourable R119 080; outstanding deposit of 30 July 2025 R41 850; outstanding EFT 207 R7 650 and EFT 208 R10 800; the bank overstated the A-Z Suppliers transfer by R11 000 and will correct it in August; the owner's debit card payment of R13 000 on 29 July 2025 does not yet appear on the Bank Statement.)

## Method

bank reconciliation statement

## Memo working

The reconciliation starts from the Bank Statement balance and adjusts for everything the bank has not yet seen, plus the bank's own error.

Two-column method — Credit side: Balance as per Bank Statement 119 080 (one mark); Outstanding deposit 41 850 (one mark); Incorrect EFT 11 000 (one mark, the bank's overstatement of the A-Z Suppliers transfer); total 171 930. Debit side: Outstanding EFT No. 207 7 650 (one mark); Outstanding EFT No. 208 10 800 (one mark); Debit card on 29 July 2025 13 000 (one mark, dated after the statement cut-off of 28 July); Balance as per bank account 140 480 (one method mark, could be Cr); totals 171 930 on both sides (the Dr and Cr totals must be the same).

One-column method: 119 080 + 41 850 - 7 650 - 10 800 + 11 000 - 13 000 = 140 480 (same mark allocation; candidates must present the appropriate sign, positive or negative).

(Memo: -1 presentation, max -1, for inappropriate/no details; -1 foreign items, max -2, if marks awarded elsewhere; for the two-column method figures must be in the correct column to earn marks; in awarding the method mark for the balancing bank account figure, ensure that candidates do not get full marks for including any incorrect figure/s; be alert to candidates who start with the bank account balance or vice versa.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Balance as per Bank Statement 119 080 (favourable); Outstanding deposit 41 850; Outstanding EFTs: No. 207 (7 650), No. 208 (10 800); Incorrect EFT +11 000; Debit card on 29 July 2025 (13 000); Balance as per bank account 140 480 (favourable)
