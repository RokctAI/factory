## Question (11 marks)

CREDITORS' RECONCILIATION: PG Stores is owned by Portia Gina and she buys goods on credit from Matsulu Suppliers. The statement of account received from Matsulu Suppliers reflected a balance of R287 600 for June 2025. Use the table in the ANSWER BOOK to indicate how the relevant balances will change when preparing the Creditors' Reconciliation. Indicate the amount, as well as a (+) for increase and a (-) for decrease. (The ANSWER BOOK pre-prints a table with rows (i) to (vi) and the incorrect balances: Creditors' Ledger Account of Matsulu Suppliers 270 140 | Statement of Account 287 600.)

INFORMATION:

A. Creditors' Ledger of PG Stores — Matsulu Suppliers (columns: Date | Details | Debit (R) | Credit (R) | Balance (R)): 1 June balance b/d 135 000; 12 June Invoice 320, credit 162 000; 14 June EFT, debit 67 500; 19 June Debit Note 785, debit 13 680; 20 June Invoice 412, credit 50 000; 23 June Invoice 471, credit 100 500; 24 June Debit Note 1106, credit 16 200; 28 June Invoice 681, credit 42 000; 29 June EFT and discount, debit 154 380; closing balance 270 140.

B. When comparing the Creditors' Ledger to the statement of account received from Matsulu Suppliers, the following differences were noted: (i) Debit Note 785 was incorrectly recorded in the Creditors' Ledger — the statement shows the correct amount of R17 280; (ii) Invoice 471 was incorrectly recorded on the statement from Matsulu Suppliers as R40 500; (iii) the transaction on 24 June 2025 in the Creditors' Ledger is for merchandise returned to Matsulu Suppliers; (iv) the statement showed interest charged at R1 080 — the correct amount of interest is R1 410, and no entry was made into the Creditors' Ledger; (v) the statement reflects transactions up to 25 June 2025; (vi) all other transactions recorded in the Creditors' Ledger appeared correctly in the statement of account received from Matsulu Suppliers.

## Method

creditors reconciliation table

## Memo working

Correct each side for the errors that belong to it, then show that both corrected balances agree.

Creditors' Ledger Account of Matsulu Suppliers (start at 270 140): (i) Debit Note 785 was under-recorded at 13 680 instead of 17 280, so a further [-17 280 + 13 680] = -3 600 (two marks). (iii) the 24 June return of R16 200 was entered in the credit column instead of the debit column, so the correction is double the amount: -16 200 (one mark) - 16 200 (one mark) = -32 400. (iv) the interest of R1 410 was never recorded in the ledger: +1 410 (one mark). Corrected balance: 270 140 - 3 600 - 32 400 + 1 410 = 235 550.

Statement of Account (start at 287 600): (ii) Invoice 471 appears at 40 500 instead of 100 500, so [100 500 - 40 500] = +60 000 (two marks). (iv) interest of 1 080 must be corrected to 1 410: +330 (one mark). (v) the statement runs only to 25 June, so Invoice 681 of 28 June must be added: +42 000 (one mark). (vi) the EFT and discount of 29 June must be deducted: -154 380 (one mark). Corrected balance: 287 600 + 60 000 + 330 + 42 000 - 154 380 = 235 550.

Totals 235 550 in both columns (one part correct for both totals; must include opening balances; could be different amounts).

(Memo: accept brackets for a negative sign; if no sign, assume positive; accept correct identifiable amounts presented in wrong line/s; foreign entries -1 per line if a mark has been scored on that line.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Creditors' Ledger Account of Matsulu Suppliers: balance 270 140; (i) -3 600; (iii) -32 400; (iv) +1 410; total 235 550. Statement of Account: balance 287 600; (ii) +60 000; (iv) +330; (v) +42 000; (vi) -154 380; total 235 550
