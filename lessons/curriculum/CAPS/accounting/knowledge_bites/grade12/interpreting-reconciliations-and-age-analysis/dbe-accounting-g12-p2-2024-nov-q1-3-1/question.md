## Question (4 marks)

DEBTORS' AGE ANALYSIS: The information relates to Timo Traders. Their credit terms are 60 days and it is expected at least 90% of debtors will adhere to these terms. Provide a calculation or figures to show why Timo Traders should not be satisfied with the figures in the Debtors' Age Analysis.

INFORMATION:

Debtors' Age Analysis on 31 August 2024 (columns: Total | Current month | 30 days | 60 days | 90 days | 90+): R240 000 | R118 000 | R36 000 | R38 000 | R29 200 | R18 800

## Method

age analysis percentage within terms

## Memo working

With 60-day credit terms, debts in the current month, 30-day and 60-day columns are within terms; the 90-day and 90+ columns are overdue. Compare the within-terms percentage to the 90% target.

Within terms: [240 000 - 48 000] OR [118 000 + 36 000 + 38 000] = 192 000 (two marks) / 240 000 (one mark) x 100 = 80% OR 49,2% + 15% + 15,8%. This is below the expected 90%.

OR overdue: [29 200 + 18 800] = 48 000 (two marks) / 240 000 (one mark) x 100 = 20% OR 12,2% + 7,8% — more than the 10% that would be acceptable.

('x 100' does not count as one part correct.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

80% within credit terms (one part correct; accept 10% below target; do not accept 'R') OR 20% overdue (one part correct; do not accept 'R')
