## Question (5 marks)

MATSAMO TRADERS: The information relates to Matsamo Traders for August 2024. The business sells goods for cash and on credit. Calculate the correct balance of the Debtors' Control account on 31 August 2024. Provide figures and a plus (+) or minus (-) sign for each correction. If there is no change to the balance, indicate with (0).

INFORMATION:

A. Balances on 31 August 2024 before taking the errors and omissions below into account:

(i) Debtors' Control Account balance in the General Ledger, R359 100

(ii) List of debtors' balances as per Debtors' Ledger (columns: Debtors | Amount (R)): N Naidoo 63 000; S Sibiya 69 000; V Esterhuizen 114 000; J Nyoka 95 700; W du Plessis 11 500; Total 353 200.

B. Errors and omissions:

(i) No entry was made for the credit sale invoice issued to N Naidoo, R4 500.

(ii) Merchandise sold on credit to S Sibiya for R18 000 was incorrectly posted to the account of V Esterhuizen.

(iii) The debtors' allowances column in the DAJ was undercast by R11 000.

(iv) An EFT payment of R5 700 was made by W du Plessis. This was correctly recorded in the relevant journal, but was posted as R7 500 to his account in the Debtors' Ledger.

(v) Credit sales to V Esterhuizen, R5 200, were incorrectly recorded as R2 500 in the Debtors' Journal and posted as such to the Debtors' Control Account and Esterhuizen's account in the Debtors' Ledger.

(vi) Goods returned by J Nyoka, R3 450, were posted to the wrong side of his account in the Debtors' Ledger.

## Method

debtors control corrections

## Memo working

Start with the Debtors' Control balance before errors and omissions: R359 100. For each error, ask whether it affects the Debtors' Control account in the General Ledger (an error in a journal entry or a journal column total), or only an individual debtor's personal account in the Debtors' Ledger (a posting error).

(i) The credit sale invoice was never entered anywhere, so the control account must increase: +4 500.

(ii) The sale was posted to the wrong debtor's personal account — this moves the amount between accounts inside the Debtors' Ledger only, so no change to control: 0.

(iii) The debtors' allowances column total of the DAJ posts to the control account; an undercast of R11 000 means allowances were understated, so the balance decreases: -11 000.

(iv) The EFT was correctly recorded in the journal; only the posting to the personal account was wrong, so no change to control: 0.

(v) Credit sales of R5 200 were recorded as R2 500 in the journal, so the control account is understated: +2 700 ([+5 200 - 2 500], two marks for both figures; R2 700 must be positive).

(vi) Wrong side of the personal account in the Debtors' Ledger only, so no change to control: 0.

Correct balance: 359 100 + 4 500 - 11 000 + 2 700 = 355 300. (Memo: a superfluous entry loses the method mark on the total.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Correct balance of Debtors' Control Account: 355 300 (one part correct; should include R359 100)
