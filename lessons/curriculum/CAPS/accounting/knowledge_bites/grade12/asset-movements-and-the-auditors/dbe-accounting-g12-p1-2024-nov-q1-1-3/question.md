## Question (5 marks)

Calculate: Depreciation on vehicles on 29 February 2024. (The information relates to Ivory Park Ltd for the financial year ended 29 February 2024. Given: Vehicles are depreciated at 20% p.a. on the diminishing-balance method. On 1 September 2023 an old vehicle — cost price R240 000, accumulated depreciation R73 600 up to 28 February 2023 — was traded in on a new vehicle costing R320 000. Depreciation on the old remaining vehicles was R132 720.)

## Method

diminishing balance depreciation pro rata

## Memo working

Three separate depreciation amounts must be added together, and the two vehicles that changed hands are each depreciated for only part of the year (pro rata by months).

Step 1 — new vehicle, owned from 1 September 2023 to 29 February 2024, which is 6 months. On a new asset the diminishing-balance rate applies to cost: 320 000 x [20/100 x 6/12] = 32 000 (one part correct).

Step 2 — vehicle traded in, owned from 1 March 2023 to 1 September 2023, also 6 months. Diminishing balance means the rate applies to the carrying value at the start of the year: [240 000 - 73 600] = 166 400; then 166 400 x [20/100 x 6/12] = 16 640 (one part correct).

Step 3 — remaining old vehicles: 132 720 (given).

Step 4 — total: 32 000 + 16 640 + 132 720 = 181 360.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Depreciation on vehicles = R181 360 (one part correct; should include R132 720)
