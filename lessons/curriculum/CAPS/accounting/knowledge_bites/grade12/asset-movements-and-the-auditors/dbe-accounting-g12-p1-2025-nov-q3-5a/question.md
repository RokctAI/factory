## Question (2 marks)

Audit report of Lynn Ltd: Explain why the shareholders should be concerned about this audit report. Provide TWO points.

(Extract from the audit report of Lynn Ltd — Audit Opinion: "In our opinion, the financial statements fairly represent the financial position of the company, except for matters mentioned below. The audit evidence revealed that supporting documentation of some expenditures were missing and could not be traced.")

## Method

qualified audit report concerns

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

The reasoning: the phrase "except for" signals a qualified audit opinion — the auditors could not verify some expenditures because documentation was missing. That points to weak internal controls and possible mismanagement, which undermines the trust shareholders, investors and lenders place in the financial statements.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Any TWO valid points:

- This is a qualified report / this implies bad report.
- This report will affect the company's image or reputation.
- There is lack of internal control processes (poor recording).
- Possibility of mismanagement of funds or fraud.
- Reduces confidence in directors' integrity.
- It can reduce market confidence and cause a fall in share price / it will create doubt to possible new investors.
- It can lower the company's creditworthiness / contribute to a lower credit rating.
