## Question (4 marks)

Calculate: Cost price of vehicles on 29 February 2024. (The information relates to Ivory Park Ltd for the financial year ended 29 February 2024. Given: Vehicles at cost was R1 250 000 on 28 February 2023. On 1 September 2023 an old vehicle with a cost price of R240 000 was traded in on a new vehicle costing R320 000.)

## Method

asset cost reconciliation

## Memo working

Reconstruct the vehicles-at-cost balance: start with the opening cost, take out the cost of the vehicle that left, and add the cost of the vehicle that came in.

Step 1 — opening balance: 1 250 000 (one mark).
Step 2 — remove the cost of the vehicle traded in: minus 240 000 (one mark).
Step 3 — add the cost of the new vehicle: plus 320 000 (one mark).

1 250 000 - 240 000 + 320 000 = 1 330 000 (one method mark)

(Memo alternative: shown as a Vehicles ledger account — debit 1 250 000 and 320 000, credit 240 000, balance 1 330 000. If a working is shown as a final answer, award working mark only.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Cost price of vehicles = R1 330 000 (one part correct)
