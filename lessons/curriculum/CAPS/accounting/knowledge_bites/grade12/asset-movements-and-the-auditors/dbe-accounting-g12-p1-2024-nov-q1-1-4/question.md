## Question (5 marks)

Calculate: Profit/Loss on vehicle traded in on 1 September 2023. (The information relates to Ivory Park Ltd for the financial year ended 29 February 2024. Given: The old vehicle cost R240 000, its accumulated depreciation was R73 600 up to 28 February 2023, plus R16 640 depreciation for the six months to 1 September 2023 — see Q1.1.3. The trade-in value received was R153 660.)

## Method

asset disposal carrying value vs proceeds

## Memo working

Profit or loss on disposal is the selling price (here the trade-in value) compared with the carrying value on the date of disposal.

Step 1 — accumulated depreciation on the date of disposal: 73 600 (one mark) + 16 640 (one method mark, see 1.1.3) = 90 240.

Step 2 — carrying value on 1 September 2023: 240 000 - 90 240 = 149 760 (three marks).

Step 3 — compare with the trade-in value: 153 660 - 149 760 = 3 900. The proceeds are higher than the carrying value, so it is a profit.

(Memo alternative: an Asset Disposal account — debit 240 000 (one mark); credit 90 240 (one mark + one method mark) and 153 660 (one mark); profit 3 900 (one method mark). If a working is shown as a final answer, award working mark/s only. NOTE: The relevant amounts calculated in 1.1.1 to 1.1.4 must be transferred to QUESTION 1.2, the Statement of Comprehensive Income.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Profit on vehicle traded in = R3 900 profit (one part correct)
