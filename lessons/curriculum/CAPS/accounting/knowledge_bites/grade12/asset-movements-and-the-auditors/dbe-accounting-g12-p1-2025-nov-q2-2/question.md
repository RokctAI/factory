## Question (23 marks)

Complete the Cash Flow Statement for the year ended 28 February 2025. NOTE: Certain figures are provided in the ANSWER BOOK. Missing amounts in shaded areas MUST NOT be filled in. (The ANSWER BOOK pre-prints Interest paid (316 250) and Dividends paid (252 000); the lines for Cash generated from operations and Change in fixed deposit are among the shaded/pre-printed areas.)

(The information relates to Mustang Ltd for the financial year ended 28 February 2025.)

Information, summarised:

- A. Extract from the Statement of Comprehensive Income: Cost of sales 12 800 000; Interest on loan 316 250; Net profit before tax 720 000; Taxation for the year 201 600; Net profit after tax 518 400.
- B. Extract from the Statement of Financial Position (28 February 2025 versus 29 February 2024): Ordinary shareholders' equity 7 140 120 versus 5 500 000; Mortgage loan: Merchant Bank 2 880 000 versus an unknown opening balance; Bank overdraft 775 000 versus 0.
- C. Fixed assets: Land and buildings at cost 5 600 000 in both years; Equipment at cost unknown versus 745 000; Vehicles at cost 2 900 000 versus 2 520 000; Accumulated depreciation on equipment unknown versus 233 000; Accumulated depreciation on vehicles unknown versus 85 000. Equipment: an old fridge was sold for cash at carrying value on 30 June 2024 — per the Fixed Assets Register its cost was R55 000 and its carrying value was R16 000 on 1 March 2024; equipment is depreciated at 15% p.a. at cost. Vehicles: a new delivery vehicle was bought for cash on 1 December 2024; no vehicles were sold during the year.
- D. Trade and other receivables: Debtors' control 3 167 000 versus 1 303 500; SARS: Income tax 0 versus 32 000.
- E. Cash and cash equivalents: Bank 0 versus 405 000; Petty cash 18 000 versus 10 000; totals 18 000 versus 415 000.
- F. Trade and other payables: Creditors' control 1 450 000 versus 1 323 500; Accrued expenses 14 600 versus 0; Shareholders for dividends 129 480 versus 162 000; SARS: Income tax 15 300 versus 0.
- G. Shares and dividends: the directors paid an interim dividend on 31 July 2024; 50 000 new shares were issued on 1 November 2024 at R20 per share; 2 000 shares were repurchased at 40% more than the average share price of R11 per share; the directors declared a final dividend on 28 February 2025; there were 498 000 ordinary shares in issue on 28 February 2025; dividends paid per the Cash Flow Statement for the year was R252 000.
- H. Financial indicator: debt-equity ratio unknown on 28 February 2025 versus 0,6 : 1 on 29 February 2024.

## Method

cash flow statement

## Memo working

Operating activities — Taxation paid: (201 600 - 32 000 - 15 300) OR -201 600 + 32 000 + 15 300 = (154 300) (one part correct): the year's tax expense, less the SARS receivable of 32 000 already paid over last year, less the 15 300 still owed to SARS at year-end. (Accept ledger format or use of brackets as workings; for use of reversed signs ensure that only one option is marked consistently.) Interest paid (316 250) and Dividends paid (252 000) are given. [Operating-activities block: 4 marks.]

Investing activities — Fixed asset purchased: vehicles at cost rose from 2 520 000 to 2 900 000 with no vehicle sales, so purchases = (2 900 000 - 2 520 000) = (380 000). Sale of fixed asset: the fridge was sold at carrying value on 30 June 2024, four months into the year, so its carrying value at sale is 16 000 (one mark) - 2 750 [depreciation of 55 000 x (15% x 4/12), one mark + one method mark] = 13 250 OR 55 000 - 41 750. [Investing-activities block: 6 marks.]

Financing activities — Sale of shares: (50 000 x R20) = 1 000 000. Shares repurchased: the price is 40% above the R11 average share price, so (2 000 x R15,40) = (30 800) OR 22 000 + 8 800 (one mark each). Change in loan: the opening loan comes from the 2024 debt-equity ratio of 0,6 : 1, that is 0,6 x 5 500 000 = 3 300 000, so the change is (3 300 000 - 2 880 000) = (420 000) repaid (one part correct). [Financing-activities block: 9 marks.]

Final block — Net change in cash and cash equivalents (1 172 000) (one part correct). Cash and cash equivalents at beginning 415 000. Cash and cash equivalents at end (757 000): petty cash 18 000 minus bank overdraft 775 000. [Final block: 4 marks.]

(Memo: starred figures — one part correct and must indicate correct operation and correct use of brackets. If no brackets, assume answer is an inflow of cash — award marks for workings only. If the correct amount is provided without the brackets, and without workings, award all marks allocated for workings only. -1 presentation, max -1; misplaced items award marks for workings only.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Operating activities: Interest paid (316 250) [given]; Taxation paid (154 300); Dividends paid (252 000) [given]. Investing activities: Fixed asset purchased (380 000); Sale of fixed asset 13 250. Financing activities: Sale of shares 1 000 000; Shares repurchased (30 800); Change in loan (420 000). Net change in cash and cash equivalents (1 172 000); Cash and cash equivalents at beginning 415 000; Cash and cash equivalents at end (757 000)
