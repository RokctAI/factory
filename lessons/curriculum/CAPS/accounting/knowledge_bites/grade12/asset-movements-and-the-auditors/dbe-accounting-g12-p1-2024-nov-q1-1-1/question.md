## Question (2 marks)

Calculate: Depreciation on equipment on 29 February 2024. (The information relates to Ivory Park Ltd for the financial year ended 29 February 2024. Given: Equipment at cost was R852 000 on both 28 February 2023 and 29 February 2024 — no equipment was bought or sold during the year. Accumulated depreciation on equipment was R344 700 on 28 February 2023 and R472 500 on 29 February 2024.)

## Method

accumulated depreciation difference

## Memo working

Because no equipment was bought or sold during the year, the only thing that changed the accumulated depreciation balance was the current year's depreciation charge. So the year's depreciation is simply the closing accumulated depreciation minus the opening accumulated depreciation:

472 500 - 344 700 = 127 800

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Depreciation on equipment = R127 800
