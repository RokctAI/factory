## Question (10 marks)

VALUE-ADDED TAX: The following information relates to the financial records of Zaba Stores for the VAT period ended 31 August 2024. 15% VAT is applicable. Certain items are zero-rated. Analyse the transactions under the headings provided in the ANSWER BOOK. Indicate the effect of EACH answer on the amount payable to SARS. Refer to the example completed in the ANSWER BOOK.

INFORMATION:

Example: The owner took stock for private use, cost price R5 200 (excluding VAT).

A. Received discount of R17 250 when settling the account of the supplier.

B. Merchandise amounting to R139 200 (VAT exclusive), including zero-rated items for R9 200, were sold on credit.

C. Bought merchandise on credit from the supplier. The invoice received totalled R23 000 (VAT inclusive). Trade discount of 10% was incorrectly omitted from the invoice.

## Method

vat transaction analysis

## Memo working

Two habits carry this question: use 15/100 on VAT-exclusive amounts and 15/115 on VAT-inclusive amounts, and then ask whether the VAT increases or decreases the amount payable to SARS.

Example: drawings of stock cancel input VAT already claimed: 5 200 x 15/100 = R780, increase.

A: The settlement discount received of R17 250 is VAT-inclusive, so the VAT portion is 17 250 x 15/115 = 2 250. Less was paid to the supplier than the input VAT claimed assumed, so the amount payable to SARS increases (+).

B: Only the standard-rated portion of the sales attracts output VAT: [139 200 x 15/100] = 20 880 - [9 200 x 15/100] = 1 380 -> 19 500 OR [139 200 - 9 200] = 130 000 (two marks) x 15/100 (mark if x 15/100) OR 149 500 (one mark) - 130 000 (one mark). Output VAT increases the amount payable (+).

C: First correct the invoice for the omitted 10% trade discount, then extract the VAT from the inclusive amount: 23 000 (one mark) x [90/100 x 15/115] (one mark) = 2 700 OR 20 000 (one mark) x [90/100 x 15/100] (one mark) OR 20 700 - 18 000. This is input VAT on purchases, so the amount payable decreases (-).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A: VAT amount 2 250, Increase / +. B: VAT amount 19 500 (one part correct), Increase / +. C: VAT amount 2 700 (one part correct), Decrease / -. (Example: 5 200 x 15/100 = R780, Increase.) Allow the use of +/- sign for effects; + (increase) / - (decrease).
