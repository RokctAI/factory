## Question (8 marks)

Prepare the Ordinary Share Capital Note. (The ANSWER BOOK pre-prints the final line: 2 750 000 shares in issue at end of year.)

(The information was taken from the accounting records of Violet Limited. The financial year ended on 28 February 2025.)

Information: Ordinary share capital was R17 875 000 on 28 February 2025 and R11 395 000 on 29 February 2024. Shares and dividends (the relevant entries have already been recorded): the company had an authorised share capital of 9 000 000 shares; 750 000 new shares were issued on 31 March 2024; 150 000 shares were repurchased on 31 January 2025, with EFT payments totalling R1 710 000 made in favour of those shareholders; the directors declared a final dividend of 90 cents per share on the 2 750 000 shares in issue on 28 February 2025.

## Method

ordinary share capital note

## Memo working

Step 1 — shares in issue at the beginning of the year, working backwards from the end: 2 750 000 + 150 000 repurchased - 750 000 issued = 2 150 000 shares (one part correct), at the opening balance of R11 395 000.

Step 2 — shares issued during the year: 750 000 shares (one mark); the amount is the balancing figure 17 875 000 + 975 000 - 11 395 000 = 7 455 000 (one part correct).

Step 3 — shares repurchased: the average share price after the new issue is (11 395 000 + 7 455 000) divided by 2 900 000 shares = R6,50 per share (one mark); the repurchase removes 150 000 x R6,50 = (975 000) from share capital. Only the average share price comes out of share capital — do not accept the full EFT amount of R1 710 000, because the amount paid above the average price is debited to retained income, not share capital. (Memo: ignore brackets; if the answer of 975 000 is correct, award two marks even if R6,50 is not shown.)

Step 4 — shares in issue at the end of the year: 2 750 000 shares at R17 875 000 (given).

(Memo: -1 presentation/details, max -1.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

2 150 000 shares in issue at beginning, 11 395 000; 750 000 shares issued during year, 7 455 000; (150 000) shares repurchased (ASP: R6,50), (975 000); 2 750 000 shares in issue at end, 17 875 000
