## Question (16 marks)

LOCKSMITH MANUFACTURERS: The business manufactures security jackets. Their financial year ended on 28 February 2025. Complete the Factory Overhead Cost Note. (The ANSWER BOOK pre-prints the line: Other factory expenses 737 010.)

INFORMATION:

A. Extract from balances (columns: 28 Feb. 2025 (R) | 29 Feb. 2024 (R)): Factory equipment 5 400 000 | ?; Accumulated depreciation on factory equipment ? | 1 680 000; Work-in-progress stock 93 000 | 125 000.

B. Summary of totals for the year ended 28 February 2025 (R): Prime cost 6 025 000; Water and electricity 915 000; Insurance 78 000; Rent expense 240 400; Advertising 380 000; Other factory expenses 737 010.

C. Additional information and adjustments: 75% of the water and electricity must be allocated to the factory — the remainder must be shared equally between the other departments. Insurance includes an additional premium of R10 800 paid for the period of 1 December 2024 to 30 November 2025; this expense is allocated between the factory and the sales department in the ratio 3 : 2. Locksmith Manufacturers rent their premises from Dynamic Properties at a fixed rate per m2; due to expansion they had to rent additional space for the factory only, from 1 October 2024, at the same fixed rate per m2 — floor space for the year: 1 Mar. 2024 to 30 Sept. 2024: factory 200 m2, other departments 40 m2, total 240 m2; 1 Oct. 2024 to 28 Feb. 2025: factory 225 m2, other departments 40 m2, total 265 m2. Provide for depreciation on factory equipment at 20% p.a. on the diminishing-balance method. NOTE: New factory equipment costing R684 000 was installed on 1 November 2024; this was properly recorded.

## Method

factory overhead cost note

## Memo working

Take each overhead in turn and allocate the factory's share.

Water and electricity: (915 000 x 75%) = 686 250 (two marks).

Insurance: first remove the prepaid portion of the additional premium — R10 800 covers 1 December 2024 to 30 November 2025, so 9 months (1 March to 30 November 2025) are prepaid: 10 800 x 9/12 = 8 100. Insurance expense for the year: [78 000 - 8 100] = 69 900 (one mark + one mark); factory share 3/5 (one mark + one mark): 69 900 x 3/5 = 41 940 (one part correct). (Memo alternatives: 46 800 - 4 860, OR 69 900 - 27 960.)

Rent expense: the rate per m2 is fixed, so weight the floor space by months. Factory m2-months: 200 x 7 + 225 x 5 = 2 525 (two marks); total m2-months: 240 x 7 + 265 x 5 = 3 005 (two marks). Factory share: (240 400 x 2 525/3 005) = 202 000 (one part correct). (Memo alternatives: 112 000 + 90 000, OR 240 400 (one mark) - 38 400 (three marks).)

Depreciation on factory equipment (20% p.a. diminishing balance): new equipment, 4 months in use: 684 000 x 20/100 x 4/12 = 45 600; old equipment carrying value: (5 400 000 - 684 000) - 1 680 000 = 3 036 000, so 3 036 000 x 20/100 = 607 200. Total depreciation: 45 600 + 607 200 = 652 800 (one part correct).

Other factory expenses: 737 010 (given).

Total factory overhead cost: 686 250 + 41 940 + 202 000 + 652 800 + 737 010 = 2 320 000 (one part correct; must include other factory expenses; if advertising is included, lose the mark on the final total — advertising is a selling and distribution cost, not a factory overhead).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Water and electricity 686 250; Insurance 41 940; Rent expense 202 000; Depreciation: factory equipment 652 800; Other factory expenses 737 010; Total 2 320 000 (one part correct; must include other factory expenses)
