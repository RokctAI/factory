## Question (8 marks)

Refer to Information C. Complete the Factory Overhead Cost Note. (Information C: the bookkeeper calculated the factory overhead cost as R1 132 700. Adjustments — a clerk was paid a salary of R9 200 per month, recorded entirely as an administration cost, but 20% must be allocated to the office and the rest to production; rent of R126 000 was shared by the bookkeeper on floor areas of 600 m2 total, with factory 400 m2, office 80 m2, sales 120 m2 — however, since the beginning of the year the annual rent increased to R157 500 as the factory grew, with adjusted floor areas of 800 m2 total: factory 600 m2, office 80 m2, sales 120 m2, which the bookkeeper did not consider.)

## Method

factory overhead note adjustments

## Memo working

Start with the bookkeeper's (incorrect) factory overheads of 1 132 700. Factory indirect labour: the clerk's annual salary is (9 200 x 12) = 110 400, and 80% belongs to production: 110 400 x 80/100 = 88 320 to add. Factory rental correction: the correct factory share is [157 500 x 600/800] = 118 125, while the bookkeeper recorded [126 000 x 400/600] = 84 000, so add the difference of 34 125. Corrected total: 1 132 700 + 88 320 + 34 125 = 1 255 145.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

1 255 145 (one part correct; should include R1 132 700)
