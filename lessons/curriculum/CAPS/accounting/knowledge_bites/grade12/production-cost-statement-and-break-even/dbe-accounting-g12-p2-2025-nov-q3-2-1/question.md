## Question (4 marks)

Picaso Manufacturers produces cellphone screen covers and cases; the financial year ends on 31 August each year. CELLPHONE CASES: Calculate the break-even point on 31 August 2025. (From the information table, cellphone cases for the year ended 31 August 2025: 28 750 units produced and sold; selling price R265 per unit; total fixed cost R80 per unit; total variable cost R146 per unit.)

## Method

break even point

## Memo working

Break-even point = total fixed costs divided by contribution per unit. First convert the fixed cost per unit into total fixed costs: 28 750 units x R80 = 2 300 000. Then find the contribution each unit makes towards covering fixed costs: selling price minus variable cost per unit = 265 - 146 = 119. Break-even point = 2 300 000 / 119 = 19 327,7, rounded up to 19 328 units. (Numerator and denominator must be marked as such.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

19 328 units (one part correct; do not accept less than 1; accept 19 327,7)
