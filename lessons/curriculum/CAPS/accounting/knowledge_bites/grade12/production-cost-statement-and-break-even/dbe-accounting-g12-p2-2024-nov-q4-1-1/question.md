## Question (4 marks)

FLOWERPOTS MANUFACTURERS: The business manufactures large concrete flowerpots. Their financial year ended on 29 February 2024. Refer to Information A. Calculate the direct/raw material cost. (Information A: 4 800 units were produced and sold during the financial year; each complete pot requires 1,4 packets of direct materials (concrete mix); the business allows for wastage of 10% of direct materials; the cost price of concrete mix was maintained at R125 per packet for the financial year.)

## Method

direct material cost with wastage

## Memo working

Packets needed for production: [4 800 x 1,4] = 6 720. Add the 10% wastage allowance: 6 720 x 1,1 = 7 392 packets (three marks). Cost at R125 per packet: 7 392 x R125 = 924 000. (Memo option 2: (6 720 (two marks) + 672 (one mark)) x R125. Memo option 3: 4 800 (one mark) x 1,54 (two marks) x R125, where 1,54 = 1,4 x 1,1 packets per pot including wastage. Workings in square brackets constitute one part, cannot be separated.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

924 000 (mark if x 125)
