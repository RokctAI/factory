## Question (3 marks)

CELLPHONE SCREEN COVERS — Variable costs per unit: Identify the variable-cost item (with figures) that has been poorly controlled. Give advice on how to improve control over this cost item. (From QUESTION 3.2, Picaso Manufacturers, cellphone screen covers variable costs per unit, 2024 to 2025: direct material cost R24 to R38; direct labour cost R22 to R20; selling and distribution cost R9 to R10.)

## Method

identify poorly controlled variable cost

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) — Compare each variable-cost component year on year: direct labour fell (R22 to R20) and selling and distribution rose only slightly (R9 to R10), but direct material cost jumped from R24 to R38 per unit — an increase of R14,00, which is 14/24 = 58,3%. That outsized increase marks it as the poorly controlled item.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Cost (1 mark) and trend with figures (1 mark); advice (1 mark):

- Direct materials cost per unit increased by R14,00 (from R24,00 to R38,00) / by 58,3%.
- Advice (any one): train workers to minimise wastage; reduce wastage by buying quality materials / use accurate cutting machinery to ensure precise phone cover shapes; change to a cheaper supplier / buy from local suppliers / buy in bulk to earn discount; increase supervision.
