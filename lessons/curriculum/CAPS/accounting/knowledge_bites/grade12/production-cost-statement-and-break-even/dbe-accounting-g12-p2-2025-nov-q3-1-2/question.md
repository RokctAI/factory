## Question (4 marks)

Calculate the cost of production of finished goods for the year ended 28 February 2025. (From QUESTION 3.1, Locksmith Manufacturers: prime cost for the year R6 025 000; total factory overhead cost R2 320 000 as calculated in Q3.1.1; work-in-progress stock R125 000 on 29 February 2024 and R93 000 on 28 February 2025.)

## Method

cost of production of finished goods

## Memo working

Cost of production of finished goods is the total cost of goods completed in the factory this year: prime cost plus factory overhead cost, adjusted for the change in work-in-progress stock. Start with the prime cost of 6 025 000. Add the factory overhead cost of 2 320 000 (see 3.1.1) to get total manufacturing cost. Then adjust for work-in-progress: the stock still on the factory floor decreased from 125 000 to 93 000, so an extra 32 000 of earlier work was completed and released into finished goods this year — add it. 6 025 000 + 2 320 000 + 32 000 [125 000 - 93 000] = 8 377 000.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R8 377 000 (one part correct)
