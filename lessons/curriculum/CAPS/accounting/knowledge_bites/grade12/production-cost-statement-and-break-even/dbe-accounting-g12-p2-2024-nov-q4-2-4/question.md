## Question (4 marks)

Jabu wishes to increase his profit by R300 000 during the next financial year, while maintaining the costs. Calculate the price that he would have to charge his customers in order to produce 400 extra units. (Information: the variable cost per unit for 2024 is R755.)

## Method

required selling price for target profit

## Memo working

Each of the 400 extra units must carry its own variable cost of R755 plus its share of the extra profit target. The profit required per extra unit is [300 000 / 400] = 750 (two marks); add the variable cost per unit of 755 (one mark) to get the required price of R1 505. (Memo option 2: total amount the 400 units must bring in is (R300 000 (one mark) + R302 000 [400 x 755] (one mark)), divided by 400 (one mark) = R1 505. Workings in square brackets constitute one part, cannot be separated.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R1 505 (one part correct)
