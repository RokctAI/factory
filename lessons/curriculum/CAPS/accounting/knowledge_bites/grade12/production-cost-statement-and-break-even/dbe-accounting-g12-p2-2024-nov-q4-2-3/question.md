**Note (memo caveat):** The memo lists three acceptable cost rows (direct material cost, total fixed cost, selling and distribution cost) although the question asks for TWO main costs — any two of the three, each with a matching strategy, earn the marks. Direct material cost per unit decreased from R520 to R450, consistent with a positive effect on the selling price.

## Question (4 marks)

Identify the TWO main costs that would have a positive effect on the selling price. In each case, provide a strategy that Jabu would have implemented. (Information, cost per unit 2024 vs 2023: direct material cost R450 vs R520; direct labour cost R185 vs R132; selling and distribution cost R120 vs R110; total fixed cost R1 820 000 in both years while units produced rose from 2 910 to 3 640.)

## Method

cost control strategies

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) — Look for the costs that fell per unit or stayed flat while output grew, since those allow a keener selling price. Direct material cost dropped from R520 to R450 per unit — achievable strategies include switching to a cheaper or local supplier, bulk buying at lower prices, or cutting wastage through better supervision, better-trained workers or technology. Total fixed cost stayed at R1 820 000 even though more units were produced — economies of scale spread the same fixed cost over more watches. Selling and distribution cost (R110 to R120 per unit) qualifies where paired with a strategy such as more reliable distribution to increase customer satisfaction, or additional advertising / free delivery that contributed to increased sales.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Main costs (2 marks) with strategies (2 marks): Direct material cost — change to a cheaper supplier / local suppliers / bulk purchase at lower price; less wastage due to improved supervision / better trained workers / technology used. Total fixed cost — economies of scale, total fixed cost remained the same (R1 820 000) even though more units were produced. Selling and distribution cost — offer more reliable distribution systems to increase customer satisfaction; additional advertising / free delivery contributed to increased sales.
