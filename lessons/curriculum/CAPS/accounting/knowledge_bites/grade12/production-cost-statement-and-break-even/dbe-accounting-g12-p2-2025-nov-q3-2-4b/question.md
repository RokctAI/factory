## Question (3 marks)

Identify the variable-cost item (with figures) that has been well controlled. Explain how this item was well controlled. (From QUESTION 3.2, Picaso Manufacturers, cellphone screen covers variable costs per unit, 2024 to 2025: direct material cost R24 to R38; direct labour cost R22 to R20; selling and distribution cost R9 to R10. NOTE from the paper: no workers were retrenched and no overtime was worked.)

## Method

identify well controlled variable cost

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) — The only variable-cost component that decreased is direct labour: from R22 to R20 per unit, a saving of R2,00, which is 2/22 = 9,1%. Because the paper states that no workers were retrenched and no overtime was worked, the explanation must point to efficiency, not job cuts or overtime changes.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Cost (1 mark) and trend with figures (1 mark); reason (1 mark; do not accept 'no overtime worked' or retrenchment):

- Direct labour cost per unit decreased by R2,00 (from R22,00 to R20,00) / by 9,1%.
- Reason (any one): due to proper supervision workers were more efficient / labour hours were scheduled according to production needs; better trained / qualified workers; there were no power interruptions e.g. load shedding.
