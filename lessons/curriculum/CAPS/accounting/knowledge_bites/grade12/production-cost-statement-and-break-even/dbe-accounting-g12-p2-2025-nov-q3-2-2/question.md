## Question (2 marks)

Comment on whether Dawn should be satisfied with the number of units produced and sold. Provide ONE point, with figures. (From QUESTION 3.2, Picaso Manufacturers, cellphone cases: 28 750 units produced and sold in 2025 against a break-even point of 19 328 — see Q3.2.1; in 2024 the figures were 25 000 units against a break-even point of 20 000.)

## Method

units vs breakeven comment

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) — Compare the units produced and sold with the break-even point: 28 750 - 19 328 = 9 422 units above break-even, each earning the full contribution of R119, so profit is made on 9 422 units (9 422 x 119 = R1 121 218). In 2024 the margin was only 25 000 - 20 000 = 5 000 units, so the position improved.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Valid explanation with comparison of units produced and break-even point, with figures (2 marks). Yes, Dawn should be satisfied — any ONE of:

- Units produced (28 750) are above the break-even point of 19 328 (see 3.2.1) / exceed the break-even point by 9 422 units / the business makes a profit on 9 422 units (R1 121 218).
- More units were produced and sold, resulting in a higher profit: 9 422 units above break-even in 2025 compared to 5 000 units in 2024.

For ONE mark only: units produced increased (from 25 000) to 28 750 / by 3 750 units; or the break-even point decreased (from 20 000) to 19 328 (see 3.2.1) / by 672 units.
