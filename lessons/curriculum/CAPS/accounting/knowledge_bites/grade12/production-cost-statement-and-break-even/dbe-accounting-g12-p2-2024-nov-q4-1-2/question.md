## Question (7 marks)

Refer to Information B. Calculate the direct labour cost. (Information B: direct labour budgeted for the year amounted to R1 117 200; the business has 7 direct workers, each required to work 1 680 hours per year and paid the same hourly rate; not yet recorded — one worker resigned on 31 October 2023 (this worker did not work overtime), and three of the direct workers worked overtime of 60 hours each during the year at 1,6 times the normal hourly rate. The financial year ended on 29 February 2024.)

## Method

direct labour cost adjustments

## Memo working

Start from the budgeted direct labour of 1 117 200. Resigned worker: the year runs to 29 February 2024, so the worker who left on 31 October 2023 is unpaid for 4 of the 12 months. One worker-month is 1/84 of the budget (7 workers x 12 months), so subtract 1 117 200 x 4/84 = (53 200) (one part correct; ignore bracket). (Memo alternatives: per-worker budget [1 117 200 / 7] = 159 600, then 159 600 x 4/12; or 159 600 - 106 400 [159 600 x 8/12]; or hours [1 680 x 4/12] = 560 x the hourly rate of 95, where 95 = [1 117 200 / 1 680] / 7.) Overtime: 180 hours (3 x 60) x 95 (one mark + one method mark) x 1,6 = 27 360 (one part correct). (Memo option 2: (3 x 60 x 1,6) = 288 (two marks) x 95 (one method mark).) Total: 1 117 200 - 53 200 + 27 360 = 1 091 360.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

1 091 360 (one part correct; check operation; resigned worker subtracted and overtime added; must include R1 117 200)
