## Question (3 marks)

Dawn is concerned about a fixed-cost item that has been poorly controlled, despite the decrease in fixed cost per unit in 2025. Identify the fixed-cost item (with figures) that has been poorly controlled. Give a possible reason for this concern. (From QUESTION 3.2, Picaso Manufacturers, cellphone cases fixed costs per unit: total fixed cost R92 in 2024 down to R80 in 2025; factory overhead cost R50 down to R34; administration cost R42 up to R46.)

## Method

identify poorly controlled fixed cost

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) — Scan the two fixed-cost components: factory overhead cost per unit fell from R50 to R34 (well controlled), but administration cost per unit rose from R42 to R46, an increase of R4,00 or 4/42 = 9,5%. The overall fixed cost per unit still decreased only because the factory overhead saving outweighed the administration increase.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Cost (1 mark) and trend with figures (1 mark); reason for concern (1 mark):

- Administration cost per unit increased by R4,00 (from R42,00 to R46,00) / by 9,5%.
- Possible reason: abuse of administration cost items / increase in office or administrative expenses / additional administrative staff.
