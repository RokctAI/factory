## Question (17 marks)

Complete the Cash Flow Statement of Eybers Ltd for the year ended 29 February 2024. (Given: SARS: Income tax was 44 675 Cr on 28 February 2023 and 66 650 Dr on 29 February 2024; income tax for the year was R462 000. Bank overdraft was R142 680 and cash R36 000 on 28 February 2023. On 1 March 2023 there were 1 200 000 shares in issue; 400 000 shares were issued at R10 per share on 30 June 2023; on 30 November 2023, 34 000 shares were repurchased at R1,90 above the average share price; ordinary share capital was R8 160 000 on 28 February 2023. Dividends: final dividend for 2023 of 17 cents per share was paid during this year; the interim dividend of R416 000 was paid; the final dividend of R328 860 was declared on 29 February 2024. Loan: monthly instalments of R97 120, including interest, were paid; interest capitalised amounted to R685 440. Net change in cash and cash equivalents was R296 460 inflow.)

## Method

cash flow statement

## Memo working

Operating activities. Taxation paid: last year's amount still owing to SARS, plus this year's income tax expense, plus the amount overpaid by year end (the closing debit balance means SARS was paid too much): 44 675 + 462 000 + 66 650 = (573 325) (one part correct; OR -44 675 - 462 000 - 66 650). Dividends paid: cash actually paid this year is last year's final dividend plus this year's interim dividend — the final dividend declared this year is only paid next year: (1 200 000 x 17/100) = 204 000 + [744 860 - 328 860] = 416 000, giving (620 000) (one part correct). [Operating-activities block: 8 marks.]

Financing activities. New shares issued: 400 000 x R10 = 4 000 000. Shares repurchased: the average share price is (8 160 000 + 4 000 000) / 1 600 000 = R7,60, so the buy-back price is 7,60 + 1,90: (34 000 x 7,60) = 258 400 + (34 000 x 1,90) = 64 600 (see 2.1), giving (323 000) (one part correct; both figures added). Loan repaid: total instalments 97 120 x 12 = 1 165 440, of which 685 440 was interest, so the capital portion is (480 000) (if no bracket, award one mark). [Financing-activities block: 6 marks.]

Cash and cash equivalents at the beginning of the year: 36 000 - 142 680 = (106 680) (if no bracket, award one mark). Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the year: -106 680 + 296 460 = 189 780 (operation) [3 marks].

(Memo notes: one part correct, reflected as an outflow (brackets); -1 poor presentation (max -1); be alert to alternative workings such as ledger account or use of brackets or signs reversed; for use of reversed signs, ensure that only one option is marked consistently; if no brackets, assume answer is an inflow of cash — award marks for workings only; if a workings amount is shown as a final figure, award the mark/s allocated, ignore the brackets; if correct figure is shown without brackets or workings, award all working marks only, not final mark. The memo's printed layout leaves the Cash generated from operations, Interest paid and investing-activities lines blank — pre-printed/not examined here.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Operating activities: Taxation paid (573 325); Dividends paid (620 000). Financing activities: New shares issued 4 000 000; Shares repurchased (323 000); Loan repaid (480 000). Net change in cash and cash equivalents 296 460; Cash and cash equivalents at the beginning of the year (106 680); Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the year 189 780
