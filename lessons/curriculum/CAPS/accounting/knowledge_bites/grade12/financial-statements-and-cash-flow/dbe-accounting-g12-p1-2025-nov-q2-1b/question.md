## Question (4 marks)

Calculate the following amounts in respect of the Reconciliation of Profit before Taxation and Cash Generated from Operations Note on 28 February 2025: Change in payables. NOTE: Indicate inflow or outflow of cash for answers calculated. (The information relates to Mustang Ltd for the financial year ended 28 February 2025.)

Information — Trade and other payables: Creditors' control was 1 450 000 on 28 February 2025 and 1 323 500 on 29 February 2024; Accrued expenses were 14 600 and 0; Shareholders for dividends were 129 480 and 162 000; SARS: Income tax was 15 300 and 0; the totals were 1 609 380 and 1 485 500.

## Method

change in payables

## Memo working

Step 1 — only the trading payables belong in this note: creditors' control and accrued expenses. Shareholders for dividends is dealt with in dividends paid, and SARS: Income tax in taxation paid.

Step 2 — payables at the end of the year: [1 450 000 + 14 600] = 1 464 600 (one mark for each component).

Step 3 — change in payables: 1 464 600 - 1 323 500 (one mark) = 141 100 (one part correct; current year - previous year or vice versa; if workings are not shown, award full marks for the answer; do not award a mark for any operation done on stand-alone figures for each year).

Step 4 — payables increased, which holds cash back in the business, so this is an inflow (award marks for answer/detail if not in the relevant column).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

141 100 Inflow
