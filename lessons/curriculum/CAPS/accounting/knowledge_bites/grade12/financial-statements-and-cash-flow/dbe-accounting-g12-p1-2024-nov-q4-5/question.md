## Question (3 marks)

State THREE possible consequences for the independent external auditor if he was influenced by the directors of the company to deliberately provide the shareholders with an incorrect audit report.

## Method

auditor consequences

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Reasoning: think of the three directions the consequences come from — the profession (IRBA can deregister him), the law and his employer (disciplinary action, fines, even imprisonment), and the people he misled (shareholders can sue; his and his firm's reputation suffers).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Any suitable relevant response, accept one-word answers (3 x 1 mark):

- As employee: deregistered / struck off the roll by IRBA (accounting body) / loss of professional licence / revocation of his certificate
- Disciplinary hearing / suspended (during investigation) / fired by his employer / fined / imprisonment (arrested)
- Sued by shareholders (company) for negligence (financial loss) / bad reputation to his name
- For the auditing company (auditor's employer): lose clients / removed as auditors by clients / loss of employment to staff as client base decreases / acquiring contracts will be challenging in the future / bad image of the company
