## Question (36 marks)

Prepare the Statement of Financial Position on 28 February 2025. NOTE: Show ALL workings in brackets. Certain figures are provided in the ANSWER BOOK. Missing amounts in shaded areas must NOT be filled in. (The ANSWER BOOK pre-prints the format with Current assets 5 798 000, Trade and other receivables opening with 735 000, and Trade and other payables opening with 878 500; the lines for Fixed assets at carrying value, Cash and cash equivalents, Total assets, and the fixed-asset figure are among the shaded/blank areas.)

(The information was taken from the accounting records of Violet Limited. The financial year ended on 28 February 2025.)

Information, summarised:

- A. Balances on 28 February 2025 (with 29 February 2024 comparatives where given): Fixed deposit: Time Bank unknown; Trading stock 4 598 000; Debtors' control 735 000; Provision for bad debts unknown; Ordinary shareholders' equity unknown (16 200 000 in 2024); Ordinary share capital 17 875 000 (11 395 000 in 2024); Retained income unknown (4 805 000 in 2024); Mortgage loan: Delta Bank 3 200 000; Creditors' control 878 500; Bank (unfavourable) unknown.
- B. Trading stock: the closing stock of one brand of men's informal shirts, shown at R285 500 on the first-in-first-out method, must be corrected to the weighted-average method (the corrected value, from Q1.1, is R272 240).
- C. Fixed deposit: Time Bank: a fixed deposit of R320 000 matured on 31 July 2025; the remaining 60% of the fixed deposit will mature on 31 December 2028.
- D. A debtor who had overpaid his account by R5 000 in the Debtors' Ledger requested that the overpayment be transferred to his account in the Creditors' Ledger; this transfer has not yet been recorded. The provision for bad debts must be adjusted to 4% of the outstanding debtors.
- E. Extract from the Statement of Comprehensive Income for the year (already adjusted): Commission income 169 400; Rent income 430 500; Directors' fees 1 998 750. The adjustments taken into account were: commission income of R6 400 for February 2025 still outstanding; rent for March and April 2025 received and recorded, with the rent increased by 10% on 1 December 2024; two directors earned the same monthly fee all year and were paid in full, while a third director, Jack Jele, employed on 1 June 2024 at a monthly fee 25% lower than the others, was paid up to 31 March 2025.
- F. Mortgage loan: Delta Bank: fixed monthly repayments of R45 000, including capitalised interest, were made and correctly recorded for the year; the monthly repayments stay the same until the loan is repaid in approximately four years; interest for the next financial year is expected to be R290 000.
- G. Shares and dividends (already recorded): authorised share capital 9 000 000 shares; 750 000 new shares issued on 31 March 2024; an interim dividend paid on 1 September 2024; 150 000 shares repurchased on 31 January 2025 with EFT payments totalling R1 710 000; a final dividend of 90 cents per share declared on the 2 750 000 shares in issue on 28 February 2025.
- H. The SARS assessment for the year indicated a tax refund of R70 000; the net profit after tax was accurately calculated to be R4 721 700.
- I. Financial indicators calculated on 28 February 2025 after all of the above: current ratio 1,3 : 1; % return on average shareholders' equity 24%.

## Method

statement of financial position with adjustments

## Memo working

Non-current assets — Fixed deposit: Time Bank: the R320 000 that matured on 31 July 2025 is a current asset, and it represents 40% of the deposit, so the non-current portion is (320 000 x 60/40) OR 800 000 - 320 000 = 480 000 (2 marks).

Current assets (pre-printed total 5 798 000) — Inventories: 4 598 000 (one mark) - 13 260 (one mark) [that is, - 285 500 + 272 240, the corrected stock from Q1.1, one mark + one method mark; if the Q1.1 answer is greater than the closing stock, the sign should be positive] = 4 584 740 (one part correct).

Trade and other receivables: 735 000 + 5 000 (transfer of the overpaid debtor's credit balance to the Creditors' Ledger) - 29 600 (provision for bad debts, 4% x 740 000) + 6 400 (commission income accrued) + 48 750 (directors' fees prepaid: 30,75 monthly fees make up the 1 998 750 expense, so a monthly fee is 65 000; Jack Jele's fee is 75% x 65 000 = 48 750, and he was paid for March 2025 in advance) + 70 000 (SARS income tax refund) = 835 550 (one part correct). [Assets section: 12 marks.]

Shareholders' equity: the average equity is 4 721 700 x 100/24 = 19 673 750, so closing equity is [(4 721 700 x 100/24) x 2] = 39 347 500 (three marks) - 16 200 000 = 23 147 500 (one part correct). Ordinary share capital 17 875 000 (see Q1.2). Retained income 5 272 500 = shareholders' equity minus ordinary share capital; can be negative. [Equity block: 7 marks.]

Non-current liabilities — Mortgage loan: Delta Bank: 3 200 000 + 290 000 - 540 000 [the year's repayments of 45 000 x 12; the capital portion repayable next year is 540 000 - 290 000 = 250 000, three marks] = 2 950 000 (one part correct). [5 marks.]

Current liabilities: from the current ratio of 1,3 : 1, total current liabilities = 5 798 000 / 1,3 = 4 460 000. Trade and other payables: 878 500 + 5 000 (transfer) + 77 000 (rent income received in advance: 12,3 months' rent make up the 430 500 income, so the rent was 35 000 per month before the 10% increase and 38 500 after; March and April 2025 at 38 500 each = 77 000) = 960 500 (one part correct). Current portion of loan: (540 000 - 290 000) = 250 000 (see non-current liabilities). Shareholders for dividends: (2 750 000 x 90/100) = 2 475 000. Bank overdraft 774 500 (balancing figure: 4 460 000 - 960 500 - 250 000 - 2 475 000; can be negative). [Current liabilities block: 10 marks.]

(Memo: inspect — current portion of loan and shareholders for dividends may be included in Trade and other payables. -1 foreign items, max -2; -1 presentation, max -1; misplaced items award marks for workings only.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Fixed deposit: Time Bank 480 000; Current assets 5 798 000; Inventories 4 584 740; Trade and other receivables 835 550; Shareholders' equity 23 147 500; Ordinary share capital 17 875 000; Retained income 5 272 500; Non-current liabilities: Mortgage loan Delta Bank 2 950 000; Current liabilities 4 460 000; Trade and other payables 960 500; Current portion of loan 250 000; Shareholders for dividends 2 475 000; Bank overdraft 774 500. (Fixed assets at carrying value, Cash and cash equivalents and Total assets are shaded and not completed.)
