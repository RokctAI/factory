## Question (8 marks)

Prepare the Retained Income Note for the year ended 29 February 2024. (The information relates to Eybers Ltd for the financial year ended 29 February 2024. Given: Retained income on 28 February 2023 was R573 720. On 30 November 2023, 34 000 shares were repurchased at R1,90 per share above the average share price. Dividends: an interim dividend of R416 000 was paid on 1 September 2023; total dividends paid and declared for the year were R744 860; the dividend pay-out rate for the year was 40% after all transactions were correctly recorded.)

## Method

retained income note

## Memo working

Step 1 — Net profit after tax: the pay-out rate says total dividends are 40% of net profit after tax, so work backwards: 744 860 / 40% (dividend pay-out rate) = 1 862 150 (one part correct) OR 744 860 x 100/40.

Step 2 — Repurchase of shares: only the premium above the average share price comes out of retained income: 34 000 x R1,90 = (64 600) (ignore brackets).

Step 3 — Ordinary share dividends (744 860): Interim 416 000 (given, paid); Final = total dividends minus interim = 744 860 - 416 000 = 328 860 (declared).

Step 4 — Balance at the end of the year: 573 720 + 1 862 150 - 64 600 - 744 860 = 1 626 410 (one part correct; check operation; shares repurchased and ordinary share dividends must be subtracted). (-1P if no details, max -1. Memo alternative version: Net profit after tax (1 458 167 x 1,175) = 1 713 346 with closing balance 1 477 606, all other lines identical.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Balance at the beginning of the year 573 720; Net profit after tax 1 862 150; Repurchase of shares (64 600); Ordinary share dividends (744 860): Interim dividend 416 000, Final dividend 328 860; Balance at the end of the year 1 626 410
