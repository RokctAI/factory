## Question (3 marks)

Calculate the following amounts in respect of the Reconciliation of Profit before Taxation and Cash Generated from Operations Note on 28 February 2025: Change in receivables. NOTE: Indicate inflow or outflow of cash for answers calculated. (The information relates to Mustang Ltd for the financial year ended 28 February 2025.)

Information — Trade and other receivables: Debtors' control was 3 167 000 on 28 February 2025 and 1 303 500 on 29 February 2024; SARS: Income tax was 0 on 28 February 2025 and 32 000 on 29 February 2024; the totals were 3 167 000 and 1 335 500.

## Method

change in receivables

## Memo working

Step 1 — only the trading receivable (debtors' control) belongs in this note; the SARS: Income tax amount is dealt with separately in the taxation paid calculation.

Step 2 — change in debtors' control: 3 167 000 - 1 303 500 = 1 863 500 (ignore working and brackets).

Step 3 — debtors increased, which ties up cash, so this is an outflow (award marks for answer/detail if not in the relevant column).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

1 863 500 Outflow
