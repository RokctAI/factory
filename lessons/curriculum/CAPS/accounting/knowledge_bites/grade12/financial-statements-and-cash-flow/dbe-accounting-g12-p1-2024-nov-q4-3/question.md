## Question (4 marks)

What kind of audit evidence does the independent external auditor examine to verify the fixed assets of a company? Provide TWO points.

## Method

audit evidence fixed assets

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Reasoning: for fixed assets the auditor tests existence (do the assets physically exist?), valuation (cost and depreciation correctly recorded?), ownership (title deeds, registration certificates) and proper procedure (procurement and disposal). Name the specific evidence checked, not just "check the register".

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Any suitable relevant response (2 x 2 marks):

- Confirm the physical existence of items listed on the fixed assets register
- Check if calculations of depreciation are correct / check the correct depreciation rate was used
- Check the invoice/receipt against the cost price written on the fixed asset register if they are the same
- Check if correct procedure followed in procurement of new fixed assets / check if correct procedures were followed in disposing of fixed assets
- Check the registration certificate / title deed to confirm ownership

Responses for one mark: Check the fixed asset register; Check the logbook; Verify source documents.
