## Question (39 marks)

Complete the Statement of Comprehensive Income of Ivory Park Ltd for the year ended 29 February 2024. NOTE: Some amounts are provided in the ANSWER BOOK.

(Given balances at 29 February 2024, in rand: Cost of sales 4 780 900; Trading stock 654 500; Debtors' control 516 600; Provision for bad debts 29 520 on 28 February 2023; Audit fees 79 000; Service fee income 1 757 700; Sundry operating expenses 119 900; Bad debts 24 780; Packing material 66 550; Salaries and wages including contributions 1 425 600; Insurance 100 800; Rent income 158 100; Interest on loan 149 400. Adjustments: (ii) mark-up is 60% on cost, but goods costing R115 000, included in cost of sales, were sold to internal employees at a mark-up of only 40% on cost; (iii) physical stocktaking showed trading stock R647 100 and packing material used during the year R58 700; (iv) provision for bad debts must be adjusted to 5% of outstanding debtors; (v) monthly insurance premiums were fixed for the past three years and were paid up to 31 May 2024; (vi) rent for an office has been received up until 30 April 2024, and the rent was decreased by 8% on 1 December 2023; (vii) one employee was omitted from the February 2024 Salaries Journal — his net monthly salary was R19 340 after 35% deductions for pension and personal tax and R2 500 deducted for medical aid, and the employer contributes 10% of gross salary towards pension; (viii) two directors received a combined annual fee of R1 065 200 after their monthly fees were increased by R5 800 each on 1 August 2023, and a third director joined on 1 November 2023 at the same monthly fee as the others excluding the R5 800 increase; (ix) net profit after tax was R992 160 after all adjustments, with income tax at 28% of net profit. From Q1.1: depreciation on equipment 127 800, depreciation on vehicles 181 360, profit on vehicle traded in 3 900.)

## Method

income statement with adjustments

## Memo working

Sales: normally sales would be cost of sales times 1,6 because of the 60% mark-up: [4 780 900 x 1,6] = 7 649 440. But goods costing R115 000 earned only a 40% mark-up instead of 60%, so sales are lower by 20% of that cost: [115 000 x 0,2] = 23 000. Sales = 7 649 440 - 23 000 = 7 626 440 (one part correct; both figures must be added/subtracted). (Memo alternative: [4 665 900 x 1,6] = 7 465 440 (two marks) + [115 000 x 1,4] = 161 000 (two marks).)

Cost of sales: (4 780 900). Gross profit = Sales - Cost of sales = 2 845 540 (6 marks to here).

Other income (operation) 1 902 000, made up of: Service fee income 1 757 700. Profit on sale of asset 3 900 (see 1.1.4; if calculated as a loss in 1.1.4, award method mark under operating expenses). Rent income: R158 100 includes two months (March and April 2024) received in advance at the reduced rate. Working: nine months at the old rent plus five months at 92% of the old rent equals 158 100, so the old monthly rent is 11 625 and the reduced rent is 10 695; deduct the two prepaid months: 158 100 - 21 390 [10 695 x 2] = 136 710 (one part correct) (Memo alternative: 104 625 + 32 085). Provision for bad debts adjustment: the new provision is 5% of 516 600 = 25 830, which is 3 690 lower than 29 520, so 3 690 (should be shown as an income).

Gross operating income = Gross profit + Other income = 4 747 540 (9 marks in this block).

Operating expenses (operation) (3 371 340), made up of: Audit fees 79 000. Sundry operating expenses 119 900. Bad debts 24 780. Packing material used 58 700. Trading stock deficit: 654 500 - 647 100 = 7 400 (should be shown as an expense). Insurance: R100 800 covers 15 months (up to 31 May 2024) at a fixed premium of 6 720 per month, so three months are prepaid: 100 800 - 20 160 [100 800 x 3/15 OR 6 720 x 3] = 80 640 (one part correct; OR 100 800 x 12/15). Salaries and wages: the omitted employee's gross salary is found from his net pay — 65% of gross minus the R2 500 medical aid equals 19 340, so gross = [21 840 + 11 760] = 33 600; add the employer's 10% pension contribution of 3 360: 1 425 600 + 33 600 + 3 360 = 1 462 560 (one part correct). Directors' fees: the combined fee of 1 065 200 equals 24 base monthly fees plus 14 months of the R5 800 increase, so the base monthly fee is 41 000; the third director worked four months (November to February): 1 065 200 + 164 000 [41 000 x 4] = 1 229 200 (one part correct). Depreciation [127 800 + 181 360, see 1.1.1 and 1.1.3; both figures should be added to award method mark] = 309 160 (18 marks in this block).

Operating profit = Gross operating income - Operating expenses = 1 376 200.

Interest income: 151 200 (balancing figure, accept a negative figure). Profit before interest expense = Net profit before tax + interest expense = 1 527 400. Interest expense (149 400). Net profit before tax = Net profit after tax + income tax = 1 378 000 (also 992 160 / 0,72). Income tax = Net profit after tax x 28/72 = (385 840). Net profit after tax 992 160 (given).

(Penalties: -1 foreign items, max -2, i.e. Statement of Financial Position items; -1 presentation, max -1.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Sales 7 626 440; Cost of sales (4 780 900); Gross profit 2 845 540; Other income 1 902 000; Gross operating income 4 747 540; Operating expenses (3 371 340); Operating profit 1 376 200; Interest income 151 200; Profit before interest expense 1 527 400; Interest expense (149 400); Net profit before tax 1 378 000; Income tax (385 840); Net profit after tax 992 160
