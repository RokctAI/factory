## Question (4 marks)

What are the implications of this audit report for the following: Directors; Shareholders. State ONE point in each case. (Valiant Ltd received an unqualified audit report — see Q4.2.)

## Method

audit report implications

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Reasoning: an unqualified (clean) report reflects well on those who prepared the statements and reassures those who invested. For directors, think of reputation and reappointment; for shareholders, think of confidence in their investment.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Directors (any suitable relevant response, 2 marks):

- Will be reappointed by the shareholders
- Improves their credentials / reputation

Shareholders (any suitable relevant response, 2 marks):

- Satisfied that their investment is safe
- It will encourage further investment in the company
