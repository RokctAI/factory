Hey — if multiples and factors blur together, here is the calm version: multiples go up, factors go down.

Multiples of 7 are the 7 times table, going on and on: 7, 14, 21, 28.

Factors of 24 are the numbers that fit into 24 exactly: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24.

Find factors in pairs. 1 and 24, 2 and 12, 3 and 8, 4 and 6. Stop when the pairs start repeating.

One fact, two views. 4 times 6 is 24, so 24 is a multiple of 6 and 6 is a factor of 24.
