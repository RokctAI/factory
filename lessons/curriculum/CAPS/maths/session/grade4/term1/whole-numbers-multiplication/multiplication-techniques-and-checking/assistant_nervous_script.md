Hey — if multiplying bigger numbers feels like guesswork, here is the calm version: make the numbers friendly first.

Estimate. 48 times 5 is nearly 50 times 5, so about 250.

Break it down: 40 times 5 and 8 times 5, which is 200 plus 40, which is 240.

Or halve one and double the other. 16 times 25 becomes 8 times 50, which is 400.

Then check the other way: divide your answer by one number and you should get the other. 240 divided by 5 is 48.

Open the practice set and use two techniques on every sum. You've got this!
