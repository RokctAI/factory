Hey — if the tables feel like a mountain of numbers, here is the calm version: there are far fewer facts than you think.

Times means groups of. Picture the egg boxes.

Times 2 doubles. Times 10 adds a zero. Times 5 is half of times 10. Times 4 is double the double.

Nines: digits add to 9, and your fingers can do it.

Every fact has a twin: 6 times 7 and 7 times 6 are the same.

Forgot one? Use a neighbour and adjust: 7 times 6 is 35 plus 7.

Open the practice set and drill the few hard ones. You've got this!
