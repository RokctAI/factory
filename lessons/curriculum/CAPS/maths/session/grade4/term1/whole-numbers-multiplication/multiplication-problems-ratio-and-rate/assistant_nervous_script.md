Hey — if ratio and rate sound like new monsters, here is the calm version: they are both just multiplying.

Each, every, per: those words mean multiply. 24 boxes at R35 each is 24 times 35.

Ratio compares two of the same kind, like 2 cups of flour to 3 vetkoek. To make more, multiply both numbers by the same amount.

Rate compares two different kinds, like 60 kilometres per hour. To use it, multiply by how many hours.

Per means for each one.

Open the practice set and multiply your way through. You've got this!
