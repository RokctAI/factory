Hey — if big multiplications look impossible, here is the calm version: split the number into pieces you can handle.

24 times 6? Split 24 into 20 and 4. 6 times 20 is 120. 6 times 4 is 24. Add: 144.

Two two-digit numbers? Draw a grid. Split both numbers, fill all four boxes, add them up.

Guess the answer first with round numbers so a missing box cannot sneak past.

Line the products up by place when you add.

Open the practice set and fill in every box. You've got this!
