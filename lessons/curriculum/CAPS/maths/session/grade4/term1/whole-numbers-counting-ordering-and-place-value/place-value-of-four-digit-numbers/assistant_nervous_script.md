Hey — if place value feels like a puzzle, here is the calm version: every digit has a seat, and the seat decides its worth.

From the right: units, tens, hundreds, thousands. Each seat is worth ten of the one beside it.

In 4 736 the 7 sits in the hundreds seat, so it is worth 700. Not 7. Seven hundred.

Expanded notation just lists the seats: 4 000 plus 700 plus 30 plus 6.

Zero is an empty seat that still counts. 2 305 has no tens, and the zero keeps the 2 and the 3 where they belong.

Open the practice set and give every digit its seat. You've got this!
