Today we put four-digit numbers in order, compare them with the signs for greater than, less than and equal to, and show the same number in words, in digits and in expanded form. We use the prices of three second-hand bicycles, the heights of South African mountains and the house numbers in a street to decide which number is bigger and why. We also sort numbers up to 1 000 into odd and even and learn the one-digit test that does it instantly.
