Hey — if rounding makes you nervous, here is the calm version: look at one digit, and that digit decides.

Rounding to the nearest 10? Look at the units digit. Nearest 100? Look at the tens digit. Nearest 1 000? Look at the hundreds digit. Always the digit just to the right of the place you want.

0, 1, 2, 3, 4 means round down. 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 means round up.

3 468 is about 3 470, about 3 500, about 3 000. Three questions, three answers.

Use rounding to guess an answer before you work it out, so you can spot a slip.
