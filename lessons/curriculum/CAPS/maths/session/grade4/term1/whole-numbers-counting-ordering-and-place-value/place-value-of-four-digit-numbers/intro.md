Today we look inside four-digit numbers to see what every digit is worth. We use a school fund-raising total of R4 736, a bag of 2 305 marbles and the distance from Cape Town to Musina, about 2 000 kilometres, to see thousands, hundreds, tens and units at work. We learn to say the value of a digit, not just its name, and we see why a zero in the middle of a number is doing a very important job.
