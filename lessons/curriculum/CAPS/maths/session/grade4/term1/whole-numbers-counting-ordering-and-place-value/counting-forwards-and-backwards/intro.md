Today we warm up the counting we learned in Grade 3 and stretch it all the way to 10 000. We count forwards and backwards in 2s, 3s, 5s, 10s, 25s, 50s and 100s, starting from any number, not only from zero. We use a taxi fare board, the seats in a stadium and the stickers on a shop shelf to see why counting in steps is faster than counting one by one, and we learn how to spot the step size when a number pattern is given to us.
