Hey — if counting in big steps feels like too much, here is the calm version: every jump is the same size.

Count in 25s like money: 25, 50, 75, 100, then the endings start again.

Count in 100s and only the hundreds digit moves, until a thousand fills up and the thousands digit clicks over.

Going backwards past a thousand? 3 050 back 100 is 2 950. The thousands digit drops, the hundreds digit becomes 9.

Gap in a sequence? Find the jump from two neighbours, check it on another pair, then fill the gap.

Open the practice set and count your way through it. You've got this!
