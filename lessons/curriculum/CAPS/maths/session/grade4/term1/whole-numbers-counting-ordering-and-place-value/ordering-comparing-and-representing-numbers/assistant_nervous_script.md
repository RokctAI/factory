Hey — if comparing big numbers makes you freeze, here is the calm version: start on the left.

Count the digits. More digits wins. Same number of digits? Look at the first digit, then the next, until one is bigger.

The sign opens its mouth towards the bigger number.

Ascending goes up like a staircase, descending comes down.

Odd or even? Look at the last digit only. 0, 2, 4, 6, 8 is even. Anything else is odd.

Open the practice set and line the numbers up. You've got this!
