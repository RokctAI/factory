Hey — if word problems about money and measuring make you freeze, here is the calm version: four steps, every time.

Read it and picture it. What is happening, and what is being asked?

Write the number sentence with a box for what you do not know. Altogether means add. How much more, or left, means take away. Change is paid minus spent.

Work it out one way, check it another way.

Write the answer with its unit, rands or metres or litres, and read the question once more.

Then ask: could this really happen in the story? If a tank needs more water than it can hold, go back.

Open the practice set and walk the four steps. You've got this!
