Hey — if four-digit sums look like a wall of numbers, here is the calm version: deal with one column at a time.

Line up the digits so units sit under units. Start on the right.

Adding: if a column makes 10 or more, write the units digit and carry the ten to the next column. 8 plus 5 is 13: write 3, carry 1.

Taking away: if the top digit is too small, exchange one from the column on the left. 2 minus 4? Make it 12 minus 4, and the 6 next door becomes 5.

Zeros on top? Take 1 off first, subtract, then add the 1 back.

Open the practice set and go column by column. You've got this!
