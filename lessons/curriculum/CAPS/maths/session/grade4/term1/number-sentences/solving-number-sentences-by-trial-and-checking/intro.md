Today we solve number sentences that have a missing number. We use three tools: inspection, which means seeing the answer at once; trial and improvement, which means guessing, checking and guessing better; and substitution, which means putting our answer back into the sentence to see if it is true. We use a bag of marbles, the change from a R100 note and a stack of chairs in the school hall to practise, and we learn why the inverse operation is the fastest way to find a box.
