Today we learn to turn a story problem into a number sentence. A number sentence is a sum written with numbers, operation signs and an equals sign, and it shows exactly what we are going to work out. We use a spaza shop till, a school bus and a bag of oranges shared at a soccer practice to see how the words add, take away, times and share become the signs plus, minus, multiply and divide, and how to use a box or a letter for the number we do not yet know.
