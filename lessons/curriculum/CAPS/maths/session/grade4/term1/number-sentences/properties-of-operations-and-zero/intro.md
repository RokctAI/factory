Today we meet three properties that make calculating easier and one special fact about zero. The commutative property lets us swap numbers when we add or multiply. The associative property lets us group them differently. The distributive property lets us split a number into parts, multiply each part and add the results. We use the seats in a minibus, trays of eggs and a packet of sweets to see each property at work, and we see why adding zero changes nothing at all.
