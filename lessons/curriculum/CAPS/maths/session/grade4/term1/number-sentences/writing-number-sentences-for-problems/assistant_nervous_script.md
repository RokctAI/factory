Hey — if word problems make your mind go blank, here is the calm version: write the story as a sentence before you touch the numbers.

Find what is missing. Draw a box for it, right where the gap in the story is.

Pick the sign from the story. Altogether means plus. Left means minus. Groups of means times. Shared equally means divide.

Two steps? Brackets around the part that happens first.

Then read your sentence back as a story. If it sounds right, it is right.

Open the practice set and write the sentence first every time. You've got this!
