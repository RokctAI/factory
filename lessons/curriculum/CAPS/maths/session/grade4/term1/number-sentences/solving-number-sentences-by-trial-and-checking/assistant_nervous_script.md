Hey — if a missing number makes you panic, here is the calm version: the two sides of the sentence must balance.

Know the fact? Just see it. Box plus 7 equals 12, so the box is 5.

Don't know it? Guess, check, improve. Too small, go bigger. Too big, go smaller.

Or undo it. Plus is undone by minus, times is undone by divide. Box plus 17 equals 45, so the box is 45 minus 17, which is 28.

Then put your answer back in and see the sentence come true.
