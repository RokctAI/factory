Hey — if the long words commutative, associative and distributive scare you, here is the calm version: they just say what you are already allowed to do.

Commutative: swap the order. 4 times 3 is 3 times 4. Only for plus and times.

Associative: group differently. 7 plus 8 plus 2: do 8 plus 2 first to make 10.

Distributive: split a number, multiply each part, add. 5 times 23 is 5 times 20 plus 5 times 3, which is 115.

And zero: adding it changes nothing. 348 plus 0 is 348.

Open the practice set and use the three freedoms to make every sum easier. You've got this!
