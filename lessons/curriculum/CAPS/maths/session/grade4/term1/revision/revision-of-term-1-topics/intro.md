Today we pull the whole of Term 1 together before the test. We revisit counting, ordering and place value, rounding, number sentences, the properties of operations, addition and subtraction with four-digit numbers, multiplication, and multiples and factors. We use one running story, a school fete in Mahikeng, so that every skill appears where it is actually used, and we practise the checking habits that turn a good answer into a certain one.
