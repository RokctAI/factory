# Part 1 — Expert

This session covers converting between millilitres and litres, using whole numbers and fractions, and solving problems in contexts involving capacity and volume. The anchor is a soccer tournament in Mthatha where a community kitchen serves soup from a 5 litre pot and juice from 2 litre bottles into 250 ml cups. The governing idea is that 1 litre is 1 000 millilitres, so litres become millilitres by multiplying by 1 000 and millilitres become litres by grouping in thousands, and that fractions of a litre are fractions of 1 000 ml. Converting whole and mixed capacities, finding fractions of a litre in millilitres, and adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing capacities in context are examinable.

## Subtopic: Changing Litres and Millilitres

1 litre is 1 000 millilitres. So 2 l is 2 000 ml, 3 l is 3 000 ml and 5 l is 5 000 ml. To change whole litres into millilitres, multiply by 1 000. On paper, that means writing three zeros after the number of litres.

Many capacities are a mix of litres and millilitres, like 2 l 400 ml. To write this in millilitres, change the litres and add the millilitres: 2 000 ml plus 400 ml is 2 400 ml. A jug of 1 l 750 ml of juice holds 1 750 ml.

Going the other way, group the millilitres into thousands. 4 600 ml has 4 thousands and 600 left over, so it is 4 l 600 ml. 8 000 ml is exactly 8 l. 3 075 ml is 3 l 75 ml. The thousands digit gives the litres, and the last three digits give the millilitres.

Be careful with zeros. 1 l 50 ml is 1 050 ml, not 1 500 ml. The 50 ml has to sit in the tens and units places of the millilitres. Saying it aloud helps: one thousand and fifty millilitres.

The questions for this section are with you now: changing whole litres to millilitres, writing litres and millilitres as millilitres, and going back from millilitres to litres and millilitres.

## Subtopic: Fractions of a Litre

Fractions of a litre are fractions of 1 000 ml. Half a litre is half of 1 000 ml, which is 500 ml. A quarter of a litre is 1 000 divided by 4, which is 250 ml, exactly one measuring cup. Three quarters of a litre is 3 times 250, which is 750 ml.

Other fractions work the same way: divide 1 000 by the bottom number and multiply by the top. One fifth of a litre is 1 000 divided by 5, which is 200 ml. Two fifths is 400 ml. One tenth of a litre is 1 000 divided by 10, which is 100 ml.

Mixed amounts with fractions are common in shops. One and a half litres is 1 000 ml plus 500 ml, which is 1 500 ml. Two and a half litres is 2 000 ml plus 500 ml, which is 2 500 ml. Two and a quarter litres is 2 000 ml plus 250 ml, which is 2 250 ml.

Going back, look for the fraction you know. 250 ml is a quarter of a litre. 750 ml is three quarters. 1 500 ml is one and a half litres. Milk and water are often sold in 500 ml bottles, which hold half a litre.

The questions for this section are with you now: half, quarter, three quarters, fifths and tenths of a litre in millilitres, and mixed amounts like one and a half litres.

## Subtopic: Solving Capacity Problems

Adding capacities: the kitchen has 2 l 500 ml of orange juice and 750 ml of guava juice, mixed in one container. In millilitres: 2 500 ml plus 750 ml is 3 250 ml, which is 3 l 250 ml. Changing to millilitres first makes the adding the same as adding whole numbers.

Subtracting capacities: the soup pot holds 5 l, and 1 l 800 ml is served to the first team. 5 000 ml minus 1 800 ml is 3 200 ml, so 3 l 200 ml of soup is left. Check by adding: 3 200 plus 1 800 is 5 000.

Multiplying capacities: 12 players each get a 250 ml cup of juice. That is 12 times 250 ml, which is 3 000 ml, or 3 l. Dividing capacities: 2 l of juice shared equally into 8 cups is 2 000 ml divided by 8, which is 250 ml in each cup.

How many cups? Each litre fills 4 cups of 250 ml, so a 5 l container fills 5 times 4, which is 20 cups. Check: 20 times 250 ml is 5 000 ml, which is 5 l. Read the question for the unit it wants, and always write the unit with your answer.

The error museum, four exhibits. One: writing 1 l 50 ml as 1 500 ml; the 50 ml goes in the tens and units places, so it is 1 050 ml. Two: thinking a quarter of a litre is 25 ml or 400 ml; it is 1 000 divided by 4, which is 250 ml. Three: adding 2 l 500 ml and 750 ml as 2 l 1 250 ml and stopping there; change to millilitres, 3 250 ml, which is 3 l 250 ml. Four: giving an answer without a unit; always say whether it is millilitres or litres.

The questions for this section are with you now: adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing capacities in soup and juice problems, counting cups, and giving the answer with a unit.

Half a litre is 500 ml. A quarter is 250 ml, one cup. Three quarters is 750 ml.

One fifth: 1 000 divided by 5, 200 ml. One tenth: 100 ml.

One and a half litres is 1 500 ml. Two and a quarter is 2 250 ml.

## Subtopic: Capacity Stories

Change to millilitres, then add. Juice 2 500 ml and 750 ml: 3 250 ml.

Take away the same way. 5 000 ml of soup, 1 800 ml served: 3 200 ml left.

Times and share: 12 cups of 250 ml is 3 l. 2 000 ml into 8 cups is 250 ml each.
