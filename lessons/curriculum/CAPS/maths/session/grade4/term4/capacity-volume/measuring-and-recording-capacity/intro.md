Today we measure how much liquid a container can hold. This is called its capacity, and we measure it in millilitres and litres. We use measuring spoons, measuring cups and jugs, we estimate before we measure, and we learn to read the scale on a jug. We help a family in Upington make ginger beer for a Heritage Day braai, and then record, compare and order the capacities of the bottles, cans and buckets we find.
