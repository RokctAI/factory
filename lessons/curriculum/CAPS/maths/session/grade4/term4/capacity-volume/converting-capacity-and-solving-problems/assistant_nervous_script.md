Hey — if changing units scares you, here is the calm version: 1 000 millilitres is 1 litre.

Litres to millilitres: times 1 000. 4 l is 4 000 ml.

2 l 400 ml is 2 400 ml. Watch the zeros: 1 l 50 ml is 1 050 ml.

Half a litre is 500 ml. A quarter is 250 ml, one cup.

For problems, change to millilitres, work it out, then write the unit.

Open the practice set and picture the cups filling up. You've got this!
