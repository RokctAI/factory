Hey — if changing units of length worries you, here is the calm version: 10, 100, 1 000.

10 millimetres in a centimetre. 100 centimetres in a metre. 1 000 metres in a kilometre.

Going to a smaller unit? Multiply. 4 m is 400 cm.

Half a metre is 50 cm. A quarter is 25 cm.

For problems, use the smaller unit, then write the unit with your answer.

Open the practice set and measure your way through it. You've got this!
