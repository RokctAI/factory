Today we revise the shape, data and measurement work from the whole year before the final test. We revisit 2D shapes, 3D objects, symmetry and tessellations, collecting data and drawing graphs, chance with coins and dice, and measuring time, length, mass and capacity, perimeter, area and volume. We use one setting, a class trip to a park near Knysna, and we practise the checking habits that make every answer certain.
