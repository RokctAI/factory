Today we read, tell and write the time. We read analogue clocks with hands and digital clocks with numbers, and we learn the two ways of writing time: the 12-hour way with a.m. and p.m., and the 24-hour way used on bus timetables and phones. We follow a learner in Pietermaritzburg through one day, from waking up to going to bed, and we learn to change between the two ways of writing each time.
