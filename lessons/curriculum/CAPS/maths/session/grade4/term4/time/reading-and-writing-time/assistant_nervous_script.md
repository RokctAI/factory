Hey — if clocks feel confusing, here is the calm version: short hand hours, long hand minutes.

Count the minutes in fives. The 3 is 15, the 6 is 30, the 9 is 45.

The hour is the number the short hand has just passed.

a.m. is the morning half of the day. p.m. is the afternoon and evening.

For the 24-hour way, add 12 to afternoon and evening hours.

Open the practice set and read each clock slowly. You've got this!
