Hey — if stopwatches and calendars feel fiddly, here is the calm version: seconds add up to minutes, days add up to weeks.

60 seconds make a minute. Shortest time wins a race.

Thirty days has September, April, June and November.

February has 28, or 29 in a leap year. The rest have 31.

Count in sevens to find the day of a date.

Open the practice set and take it one week at a time. You've got this!
