Hey — if time problems feel like a muddle, here is the calm version: count on in easy jumps.

Remember the chain: 60 seconds, 60 minutes, 24 hours, 7 days.

To find how long, jump to the next whole hour, then whole hours, then the rest.

Never subtract clock times like ordinary numbers.

When minutes reach 60, make an hour.

Open the practice set and draw a number line for each one. You've got this!
