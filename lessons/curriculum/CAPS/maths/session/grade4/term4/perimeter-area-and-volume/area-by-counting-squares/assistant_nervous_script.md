Hey — if area sounds new and strange, here is the calm version: count the squares inside.

Area is the surface inside a shape. Perimeter is the edge.

Cover it with equal squares, no gaps, no overlaps.

Count them, or count in rows. The answer is in square units.

For a leaf, count full squares and add one for every two halves.

Open the practice set and count your squares carefully. You've got this!
