Today we go on a shape hunt. We look for lines, 2D shapes, 3D objects and lines of symmetry in nature, in the everyday world around us, and in the art and crafts of South Africa's cultures. We see hexagons in honeycomb, symmetry in leaves and butterflies, rectangles and triangles in buildings and road signs, and bold lines, triangles and symmetry in Ndebele house painting, Zulu beadwork and Basotho litema wall patterns.
