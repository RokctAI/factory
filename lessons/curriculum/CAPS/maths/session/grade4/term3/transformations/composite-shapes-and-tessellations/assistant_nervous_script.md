Hey — if putting shapes together sounds tricky, here is the calm version: fit, count, fold, cover.

Fit shapes edge to edge to make a new shape.

Count only the outside sides. A house shape has 5.

Fold to test symmetry. Every piece needs a partner.

Cover a surface with no gaps and no overlaps: that is a tessellation.

Squares fit. Circles leave gaps.

Open the practice set and build each shape in your mind. You've got this!
