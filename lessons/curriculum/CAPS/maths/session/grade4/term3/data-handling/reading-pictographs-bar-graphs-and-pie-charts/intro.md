Today we become graph readers. We read pictographs and bar graphs carefully, starting with the title, the key and the scale, and we answer questions like how many, how many more and how many altogether. We also meet pie charts, circles cut into slices, and read them as halves, quarters and eighths of a group. We use a graph of tuck shop sales in Bethlehem and a pie chart of the sports chosen by 40 learners.
