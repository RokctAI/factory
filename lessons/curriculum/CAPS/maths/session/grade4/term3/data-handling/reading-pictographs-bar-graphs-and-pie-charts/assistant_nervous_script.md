Hey — if graphs look busy, here is the calm version: read the outside first.

Title, labels, scale or key. Then the bars or pictures.

Read straight across from the top of a bar to the scale.

A pie chart is the whole group cut into slices. A quarter of 40 is 10.

How many more? Subtract. How many altogether? Add.

Open the practice set and read each graph from the outside in. You've got this!
