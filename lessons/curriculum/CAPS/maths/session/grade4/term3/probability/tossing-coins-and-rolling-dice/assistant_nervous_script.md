Hey — if chance feels random and confusing, here is the calm version: list it, try it, count it.

A coin has 2 outcomes: heads or tails.

A dice has 6 outcomes: 1 to 6.

Each one is equally likely, and the coin does not remember.

Toss or roll many times and tally every result.

Expect close to fair, not exactly fair.

Open the practice set and give chance a few tries. You've got this!
