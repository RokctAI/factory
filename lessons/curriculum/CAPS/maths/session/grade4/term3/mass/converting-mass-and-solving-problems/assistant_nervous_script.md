Hey — if changing units scares you, here is the calm version: 1 000 grams is 1 kilogram.

Kilograms to grams: times 1 000. 3 kg is 3 000 g.

2 kg 300 g is 2 300 g. Watch the zeros: 2 kg 30 g is 2 030 g.

Half a kilogram is 500 g. A quarter is 250 g.

For problems, change to grams, work it out, then write the unit.

Open the practice set and weigh it all in grams first. You've got this!
