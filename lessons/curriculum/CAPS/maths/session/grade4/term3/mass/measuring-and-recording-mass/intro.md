Today we measure how heavy things are. How heavy something is, is called its mass, and we measure it in grams and kilograms. We learn which unit to use for light and heavy things, how to estimate before we measure, and how to read a balance, a kitchen scale and a bathroom scale. We use the groceries in a gogo's kitchen in Ladysmith, from a packet of yeast to a bag of mielie meal, and then record, compare and order the masses we find.
