Today we compare and order fractions. We learn three quick ways to tell which of two fractions is bigger: when the top numbers are both 1, when the bottom numbers are the same, and when the top numbers are the same. We also use one half as a measuring stick and the fraction wall to order fractions from smallest to biggest. We use a fun run in Durban and bottles of juice to see which fraction of the way, or of the bottle, is more.
