Hey — if fractions and division feel like two different things, here is the calm version: they are the same sharing.

1 roti shared among 4 children: one quarter each. 1 divided by 4.

3 rotis shared among 4: three quarters each.

A fraction of a group: divide by the bottom, times by the top.

Leftover food? Cut it into halves or quarters and share it.
