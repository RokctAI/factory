Today we start fractions. A fraction is a part of a whole that has been cut into equal parts. We learn the names halves, thirds, quarters, fifths, sixths, sevenths and eighths, and we learn to read and draw fractions in pictures. We use a melktert cut at a bakery, a slab of chocolate and a strip of paper folded into equal parts, and we learn to compare two fractions just by looking at their pictures.
