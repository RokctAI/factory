Hey — if equivalent fractions feel like magic, here is the calm version: more cuts, same pizza.

Half a pizza is still half when you cut it into 2 quarters or 4 eighths.

So 1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8. Same amount, new name.

To make one, times the top and the bottom by the same number.

Three quarters into eighths: 4 times 2 is 8, 3 times 2 is 6. Six eighths.

Never add to both. That changes the amount.
