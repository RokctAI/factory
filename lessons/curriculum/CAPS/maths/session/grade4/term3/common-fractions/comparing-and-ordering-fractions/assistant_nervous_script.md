Hey — if comparing fractions muddles you, here is the calm version: ask which number is the same.

One on top? The bigger bottom gives the smaller piece. One third beats one fifth.

Same bottom? More pieces win. Five eighths beats three eighths.

Same top? Bigger pieces win. Two thirds beats two fifths.

Neither the same? Check each one against a half.

Open the practice set and line them up, one pair at a time. You've got this!
