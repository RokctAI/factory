Hey — if fractions look scary, here is the calm version: a fraction is just a fair share.

Cut the whole into equal parts. The bottom number counts the parts.

The top number counts the parts you take.

3 blocks out of a slab of 8 is three eighths.

The parts that are left make the rest of the whole.

More parts means smaller parts. One half is bigger than one eighth.

Open the practice set and name each picture one part at a time. You've got this!
