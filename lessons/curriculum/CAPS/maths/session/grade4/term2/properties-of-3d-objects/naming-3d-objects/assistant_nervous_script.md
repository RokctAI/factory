Hey — if 3D objects feel confusing, here is the calm version: box, ball, tin, pyramid.

Flat shapes are 2D, you can draw them. Solid objects are 3D, you can hold them. The flat sides of a solid are its faces.

Box: a rectangular prism, six rectangle faces. A dice is a cube, a box with all square faces.

Tin: a cylinder, two circle faces and a curved surface.

Ball: a sphere, curved all over, no faces.

Pyramid: a flat base and triangles meeting at a point.

Open the practice set and name each object with those four pictures in mind. You've got this!
