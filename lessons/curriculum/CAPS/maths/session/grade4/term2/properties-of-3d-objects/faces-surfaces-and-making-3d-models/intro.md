Today we look more closely at what 3D objects are made of. We describe each object by the shapes of its faces and by whether its surfaces are flat or curved, we sort and compare objects using those properties, and then we build our own models. We cut out polygons, fold and tape them, and watch flat shapes turn into a box or a pyramid. We use a shoe box, a party hat, a tin and a ball to practise, and we learn how a flat pattern called a net becomes a solid.
