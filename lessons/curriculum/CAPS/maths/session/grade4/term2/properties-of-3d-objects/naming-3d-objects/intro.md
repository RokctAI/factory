Today we move from flat shapes to solid objects. Solid objects are called 3D objects because they have three dimensions: length, width and height. We learn to recognise and name four kinds: rectangular prisms like a cereal box, spheres like a soccer ball, cylinders like a tin of beans, and pyramids like the ones in Egypt. We find them in the kitchen, on the sports field and in buildings, and we learn the difference between a solid object and the flat shapes on its faces.
