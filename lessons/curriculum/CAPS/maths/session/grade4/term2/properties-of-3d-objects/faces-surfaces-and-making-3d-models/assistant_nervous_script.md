Hey — if describing 3D objects feels like too many parts to track, here is the calm version: list the faces and count the surfaces.

Faces are the flat shapes. A box has 6 rectangles. A cube has 6 squares. A square pyramid has 1 square and 4 triangles.

Surfaces are flat or curved. A tin has 2 flat and 1 curved. A ball has 0 flat and 1 curved.

Sort into flat only, curved only, or both, and say why.

Building a model is the best check. Six squares taped at the edges fold into a cube. One square and four triangles make a pyramid.

Open the practice set and picture the faces of each object. You've got this!
