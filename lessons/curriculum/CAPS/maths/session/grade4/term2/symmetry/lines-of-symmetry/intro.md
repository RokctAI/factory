Today we look for symmetry, the kind of balance where one half of a shape is the mirror image of the other. We learn what a line of symmetry is, how to test for one by folding or with a mirror, how to draw the lines of symmetry on squares, rectangles, triangles and other shapes, and how to describe a shape's symmetry in words. We use a butterfly, the letters of the alphabet and the South African flag to practise.
