Today we revise Terms 1 and 2 before the mid-year test. We bring together whole numbers, number sentences, the four operations with their checks, multiples and factors, division, geometric and numeric patterns, 2D shapes, 3D objects and symmetry. We use one setting, a school sports day in Polokwane, so that each skill appears where it is really used, and we practise the habits that earn marks: estimate first, show working, check with the inverse, and read the question to the end.
