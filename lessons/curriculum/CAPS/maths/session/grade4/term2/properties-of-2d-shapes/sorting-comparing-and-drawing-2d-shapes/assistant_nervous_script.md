Hey — if sorting shapes feels like a muddle, here is the calm version: ask two questions.

Straight sides or curved sides? Triangles, squares and hexagons are straight. Circles are curved. A semicircle is both.

How many sides? Three, four, five, six. Tall or wide does not matter.

Put each shape in exactly one place and say why it belongs there.

Drawing? Use the grid. Count the squares along the lines, not the lines themselves. Ruler for any slanted side.

Open the practice set and sort the shapes with your two questions. You've got this!
