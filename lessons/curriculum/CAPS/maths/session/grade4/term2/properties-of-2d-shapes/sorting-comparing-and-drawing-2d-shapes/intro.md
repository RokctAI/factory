Today we sort flat shapes into groups and compare them, using two questions: are the sides straight or curved, and how many sides are there? We put shapes into sorting tables and sorting circles, and we learn to describe exactly why a shape belongs in one group and not another. Then we pick up a pencil and draw shapes accurately on grid paper, using the squares to make sides straight and corners square. We use a bag of cut-out shapes, a tiled floor and a quilt pattern to practise.
