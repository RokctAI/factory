Today we name the flat shapes around us. Flat shapes are called 2D shapes because they have two dimensions, length and width, with no thickness. We learn what makes a shape a polygon, we name polygons by their number of sides from triangles up to heptagons, we see the difference between regular and irregular shapes, and we meet the circle, which is not a polygon at all. We find these shapes in road signs, floor tiles, coins and the shapes of our own classroom.
