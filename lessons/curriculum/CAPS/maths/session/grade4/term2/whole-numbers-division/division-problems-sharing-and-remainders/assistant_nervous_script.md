Hey — if division word problems feel like a trap, here is the calm version: divide first, then read the story again.

Shared equally or in groups of means divide. R936 among 8 is R117 each.

Got a remainder? Ask what it means. People who all need a seat: round up. Full bags only: round down. What is left: say the remainder.

A rate is an amount for one, so divide the total by the number of units. 480 kilometres in 6 hours is 80 kilometres per hour.

Sharing in a ratio? Add the parts, divide to find one part, multiply out.
