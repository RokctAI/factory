Hey — if dividing big numbers feels like falling off a cliff, here is the calm version: just ask how many times it fits.

84 divided by 4? Which number times 4 is 84? Break 84 into 80 and 4. 20 and 1. So 21.

Three digits? Take away big chunks. 372 divided by 6: take 300, that is 50 sixes. Take 60, that is 10 sixes. Take 12, that is 2 sixes. Add the chunks: 62.

Something left over? That is the remainder, and it must be smaller than the number you divided by.

Guess first so a wild answer cannot sneak past.
