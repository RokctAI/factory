Hey — if division feels like a guess, here is the calm version: estimate, break it up, multiply back.

Guess how big first. 144 divided by 8 is between 10 and 20.

Break the number into pieces the divisor fits. 144 is 80 and 64, so 10 and 8, so 18.

Or build up: 7 times 30 is 210, 42 to go, 7 times 6 is 42, so 30 plus 6 is 36.

Then multiply back. 8 times 18 is 144. If there was a remainder, add it on.
