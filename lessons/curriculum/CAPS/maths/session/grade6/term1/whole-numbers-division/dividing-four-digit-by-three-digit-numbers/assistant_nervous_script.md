Hey — if long division looks like a staircase of numbers, here is the calm version: you just ask how many times does it fit, again and again.

Estimate first so you know roughly what the answer is.

Write out the multiples of the divisor so you can look them up.

Divide, multiply, subtract, bring down. Repeat.

Check by multiplying back, and add any remainder.

Open the practice set and share out the concert hall seats. You've got this!
