# Part 1 — Expert

This session revises the Term 1 topics: whole numbers to nine digits, including place value, ordering, prime numbers and rounding; addition and subtraction with techniques for checking and the properties of operations; multiplication of four-digit by three-digit numbers, multiples, factors and prime factors; division of four-digit by three-digit numbers with remainders; multiple operations with brackets; and ratio and rate. The anchor is a school fun fair in Kimberley that raised R245 368 this year and R238 950 last year, with 12 485 visitors on Saturday and 9 876 on Sunday, 125 stalls paying R240 each, and 3 024 boerewors rolls packed in trays of 24. The governing idea is that each topic gives you a tool, and that estimation and inverse operations check every tool. The whole of Term 1 is examinable in the Term 1 test.

## Subtopic: Numbers: Place Value, Primes and Rounding

Place value: in R245 368, the 2 is in the hundred thousands place and is worth 200 000, the 4 is worth 40 000 and the 5 is worth 5 000. In expanded notation the number is 200 000 plus 40 000 plus 5 000 plus 300 plus 60 plus 8. In words it is two hundred and forty-five thousand, three hundred and sixty-eight. Remember that Grade 6 numbers go up to nine digits: in 62 027 503, the 6 is worth 60 000 000.

Comparing and ordering: R245 368 and R238 950 both have six digits and both start with 2. The ten thousands are 4 and 3, so R245 368 is bigger: 245 368 > 238 950. More digits always means a bigger number, and you compare from the left.

Prime numbers: a prime has exactly two factors, 1 and itself. The fun fair numbers its stalls. Stall 37 is a prime number, because no number except 1 and 37 divides into it. Stall 39 is not prime, because 39 is 3 times 13. Remember that 1 is not prime and 2 is the only even prime. There are 25 primes up to 100.

Rounding: look at the digit just to the right of the rounding place. 245 368 to the nearest 10 is 245 370, because the units digit is 8. To the nearest 100 it is 245 400, because the tens digit is 6. To the nearest 1 000 it is 245 000, because the hundreds digit is 3. To the nearest 5, units of 8 round up, so it is 245 370.

The questions for this section are with you now: place value, comparing, primes and rounding.

## Subtopic: Adding and Subtracting with Checks

Column addition: 12 485 visitors on Saturday and 9 876 on Sunday. Line up the units. 5 plus 6 is 11, write 1 carry 1. 8 plus 7 plus 1 is 16, write 6 carry 1. 4 plus 8 plus 1 is 13, write 3 carry 1. 2 plus 9 plus 1 is 12, write 2 carry 1. 1 plus 1 is 2. The total is 22 361 visitors. Check with an estimate: about 12 000 plus 10 000 is 22 000.

Column subtraction: how much more was raised this year? R245 368 take away R238 950. Units: 8 take away 0 is 8. Tens: 6 take away 5 is 1. Hundreds: 3 take away 9 cannot be done, so borrow from the thousands: 13 take away 9 is 4, and the thousands 5 becomes 4. Thousands: 4 take away 8 cannot be done, so borrow from the ten thousands: 14 take away 8 is 6, and the 4 becomes 3. Ten thousands: 3 take away 3 is 0. Hundred thousands: 2 take away 2 is 0. The answer is R6 418. Check by adding: 6 418 plus 238 950 is 245 368.

Other techniques: break down and build up, count on, or round and compensate. 245 368 plus 99 999 is 245 368 plus 100 000 take away 1, which is 345 367.

The properties of operations: in addition you can swap the order and change the grouping, but not in subtraction. Adding 0 changes nothing.

The questions for this section are with you now: adding and subtracting, techniques and properties.

## Subtopic: Multiplying and Dividing

Multiplication: 125 stalls each pay R240. Double and halve: 125 times 240 is 250 times 120, which is 500 times 60, which is R30 000. For four-digit by three-digit numbers, use long multiplication with placeholder zeros in the tens and hundreds rows.

Multiples and factors: the factors of 24 are 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12 and 24. Prime factors break a number into primes: 126 is 2 times 3 times 3 times 7. The lowest common multiple of 6 and 8 is 24, and the highest common factor of 84 and 120 is 12.

Division: the 3 024 boerewors rolls are packed in trays of 24. 3 024 divided by 24: 24 times 100 is 2 400, leaving 624. 24 times 20 is 480, leaving 144. 24 times 6 is 144, leaving nothing. So 100 plus 20 plus 6 is 126 trays. Check: 126 times 24 is 3 024. If a story has a remainder, decide whether to leave it over, round up or ignore it.

Order of operations: brackets first, then multiplication and division, then addition and subtraction. 3 024 divided by 24 plus 6 is 126 plus 6, which is 132.

Ratio and rate: lemonade is made in the ratio 1 cup of syrup to 5 cups of water, so 8 cups of syrup need 40 cups of water. A rate uses per: R240 per stall.

The error museum, four exhibits. One: saying the 5 in 245 368 is worth 5; it is worth 5 000. Two: forgetting to borrow, so 245 368 take away 238 950 becomes 13 618; borrow and get 6 418. Three: leaving out a placeholder zero in long multiplication. Four: adding before dividing in 3 024 divided by 24 plus 6; divide first to get 132.

The questions for this section are with you now: multiplying, dividing, factors, order of operations, ratio and rate.
