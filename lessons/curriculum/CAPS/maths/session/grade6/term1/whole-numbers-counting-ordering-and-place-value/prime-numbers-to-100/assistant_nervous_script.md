Hey — if prime numbers sound fancy, here is the calm version: a prime is a number that only makes one long row.

Try to split the number into equal rows. If only 1 row works, it is prime.

Test with 2, 3, 5 and 7. If none of them fit exactly, the number under 100 is prime.

1 is not prime, and 2 is the only even prime.

Watch out for sneaky ones like 51 and 91.
