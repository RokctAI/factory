Hey — if nine-digit numbers look scary, here is the calm version: it is just three small numbers standing in a row.

Split the number into groups of three from the right.

Read each group like a normal number, then say million or thousand.

To find what a digit is worth, look at its seat and fill the rest with zeros.

When you write a number, every empty seat gets a 0.

Open the practice set and read the census number aloud. You've got this!
