Hey — if long numbers make your eyes swim, here is the calm version: compare them like a race, from the front.

First count the digits. The longer number wins.

If they are the same length, start on the left and look for the first digit that is different.

The open mouth of > or < always eats the bigger number.

On a number line, find the two marks your number sits between.
