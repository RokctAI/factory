Hey — if multiplying big numbers in your head sounds impossible, here is the calm version: you can make the numbers friendlier first.

Double one number and halve the other. The answer does not change.

Swap or regroup to find a 10, 100 or 1 000.

Times 1 changes nothing. Times 0 gives 0.

Check with an estimate and with division.

Open the practice set and count the bakery rolls. You've got this!
