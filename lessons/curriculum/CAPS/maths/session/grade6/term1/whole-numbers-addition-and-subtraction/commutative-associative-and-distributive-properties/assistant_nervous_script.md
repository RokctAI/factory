Hey — if the property names sound like long words, here is the calm version: they are just rules for making sums easier.

Commutative means you can swap the order when you add or multiply.

Associative means you can choose which two to add first.

Distributive means you can multiply a number in parts and add the parts.

Adding 0 changes nothing, and subtraction does not like to be swapped.
