Hey — if graphs look busy, here is the calm version: read one part at a time.

For a bar, follow the top across to the scale.

For a pie, the whole circle is everyone, and each slice is a fraction.

Half of 40 is 20, a quarter is 10, an eighth is 5.

Then ask: who made this graph, and is the scale fair?
