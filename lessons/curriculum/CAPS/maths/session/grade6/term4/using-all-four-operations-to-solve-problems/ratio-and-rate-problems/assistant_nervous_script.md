Hey — ratio and rate sound alike, but here is the calm way to tell them apart.

Ratio compares things of the same kind, like cups to cups.

Rate compares different kinds, like rands to rolls, and uses the word per.

To share in a ratio, add the parts, find one part, then multiply.
