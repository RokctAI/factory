Hey — word problems can feel like a lot at once, so let's take them one step at a time.

First, read the story and find the question.

Next, choose the operation: total means add, change means subtract, many at the same price means multiply, sharing means divide.

Make the units match: kilograms to grams, litres to millilitres.

For a fraction of an amount, divide by the bottom number and multiply by the top number.
