Hey — if millilitres and litres feel muddled, here is the calm version: it is the same idea as grams and kilograms.

1 000 ml is 1 litre, and 1 000 litres is 1 kilolitre.

A cup is about 250 ml, and 4 cups make a litre.

On a jug, work out what each mark is worth.

Bend down and read it at eye level.
