Hey — if 24-hour time looks strange, here is the calm version: in the morning it looks almost the same as normal.

After noon, just add 12 to the hour.

2 p.m. becomes 14:00, and 9 p.m. becomes 21:00.

To go back, take away 12 and write p.m.

Midnight is 00:00, the start of a new day.
