Hey — if multiplying decimals sounds scary, here is the calm version: you do not need to do any long working at all.

Times 10: every digit moves one place to the left.

Times 100: every digit moves two places to the left.

If a place is empty, put a 0 in it.

Do not just add a zero to the end of a decimal; that does not change it.

Open the practice set and price the koeksister orders. You've got this!
