Hey — if fractions, decimals and percentages all at once feel like a lot, here is the calm version: they are three names for the same amount.

Half is 1/2, which is 0,5, which is 50%.

To switch, make the bottom number 100.

To compare, turn them all into decimals.

For money problems, line up the commas and check your answer.
