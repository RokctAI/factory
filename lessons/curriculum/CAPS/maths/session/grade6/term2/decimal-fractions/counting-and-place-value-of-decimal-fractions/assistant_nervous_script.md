Hey — if decimals look like strange numbers with a comma in the way, here is the calm version: the comma just separates the wholes from the pieces.

The first place after the comma is tenths. The second is hundredths.

Every digit has a seat, and a 0 can hold an empty seat.

When you count past 9 tenths, you reach the next whole number.

Think of rand and cents: R3,68 is 3 rand and 68 cents.
