Hey — if equivalent fractions seem confusing, here is the calm version: they are just different names for the same amount.

To make one, multiply the top and bottom by the same number.

To simplify, divide the top and bottom by the same number.

Keep going until nothing bigger than 1 divides both.

Bigger numbers can still mean the same amount: 3/4 is 75/100.
