Hey — if percentages sound grown-up and scary, here is the calm version: percent just means out of a hundred.

A percentage is a fraction with 100 at the bottom.

To turn a fraction into a percentage, make the bottom number 100.

To find 10 percent, divide by 10. Build other percentages from that.

25 percent is a quarter and 50 percent is a half.
