Hey — if adding fractions with different bottoms looks impossible, here is the calm version: you just make the pieces the same size first.

Check if one bottom number goes into the other.

Change the smaller one to match, multiplying top and bottom.

Then add or take away the tops. The bottom stays the same.

With mixed numbers, do the wholes and the parts separately.

Open the practice set and measure out the koeksister sugar. You've got this!
