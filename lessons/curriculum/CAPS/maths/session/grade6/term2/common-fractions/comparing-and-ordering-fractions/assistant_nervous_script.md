Hey — if comparing fractions feels upside down, here is the calm version: you are just comparing pieces.

Same bottom number? Count the pieces.

Same top number? Look at the size of the pieces. Fewer cuts mean bigger pieces.

Different everything? Change them so the bottom numbers match.

For tenths and hundredths, one tenth is ten hundredths.

Open the practice set and see which farm has picked the most apples. You've got this!
