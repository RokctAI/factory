Hey — if converting units feels confusing, here is the calm version: ask one question first. Am I going to a bigger unit or a smaller one?

To a smaller unit, like km to m, multiply. You need more pieces.

To a bigger unit, like m to km, divide. You need fewer pieces.

Remember 10, 100 and 1 000.

Before you add lengths, make the units the same.
