Hey — if all the length units feel muddled, here is the calm version: there are only four, and they go from small to big.

Millimetres, centimetres, metres, kilometres.

10 mm is 1 cm, 100 cm is 1 m, and 1 000 m is 1 km.

When you measure, start at the 0 mark.

When you compare, change to the same unit first.
