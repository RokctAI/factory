Hey — if symmetry seems hard to see, here is the calm version: just imagine folding the shape.

If the two halves fit exactly on top of each other, the fold line is a line of symmetry.

A mirror on the line works the same way.

For regular shapes, count the sides: that is how many lines there are.

When you complete a drawing, flip it; do not just copy it.
