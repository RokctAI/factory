Hey — if area feels confusing next to perimeter, here is the calm version: perimeter goes around, area fills in.

Area counts the squares inside a shape.

For a rectangle, count the rows and the squares in each row, then multiply.

Length times width, and the answer is in square units.

For an L-shape, split it into rectangles and add.
