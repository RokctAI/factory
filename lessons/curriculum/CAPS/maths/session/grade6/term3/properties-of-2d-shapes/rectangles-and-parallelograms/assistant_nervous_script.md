Hey — if rectangles and parallelograms seem like the same thing, here is the calm version: they are cousins in the same family.

Both have opposite sides that are equal and parallel.

A rectangle has four square corners.

Push a rectangle sideways, and it becomes a parallelogram with slanted corners.

The sides stay the same; only the angles change.
