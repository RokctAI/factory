Hey — if six angle names feel like too many, here is the calm version: you only need two benchmarks.

A right angle is a quarter turn, a square corner.

A straight angle is a half turn, a straight line.

Smaller than right is acute. Between right and straight is obtuse.

Bigger than straight is reflex, and all the way round is a revolution.
