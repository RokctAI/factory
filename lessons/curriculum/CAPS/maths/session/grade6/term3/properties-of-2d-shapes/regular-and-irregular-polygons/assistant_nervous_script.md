Hey — if all the shape names sound alike, here is the calm version: count the sides and the name follows.

3 is a triangle, 4 a quadrilateral, 5 a pentagon, 6 a hexagon, 7 a heptagon and 8 an octagon.

Regular means every side and every angle is the same.

If even one is different, it is irregular.

A stop sign is a regular octagon, and a honeycomb cell is a regular hexagon.

Open the practice set and go on the shape walk. You've got this!
