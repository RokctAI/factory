Today we put the four basic shapes side by side — the square, the rectangle, the triangle and the circle — and use a formula for the distance around each one and for the space inside it. Perimeter is a length, measured in units; area counts squares, measured in square units; and choosing the right formula, substituting carefully and writing the right unit is most of the work. By the end you will calculate perimeter and area for all four shapes, find a missing side or height from a given area, use pi as 3,14, as twenty-two sevenths or from the calculator as the question asks, and leave answers in terms of pi when an exact value is wanted.
