Today we convert areas between square millimetres, square centimetres, square metres and square kilometres — and discover why the conversion factors are not the familiar 10, 100 and 1 000. One centimetre is 10 millimetres, but one square centimetre is 100 square millimetres, because the square is 10 millimetres in both directions. By the end you will explain where each area conversion factor comes from, convert in either direction by multiplying or dividing, convert by first changing the lengths when the dimensions are known, choose a sensible unit for an area, and catch the most common error in the topic: using a length factor for an area.
