Today enlargements move onto the coordinate plane, and a simple pattern appears: when a figure is enlarged or reduced from the origin by a scale factor, every coordinate of every vertex is multiplied by that factor. We investigate why, using the lines from the origin through each vertex, and test what the rule does to side lengths, directions and areas. By the end you will enlarge or reduce a figure from the origin by any scale factor, find the scale factor from the coordinates of a figure and its image, decide whether an image really is an enlargement from the origin, and connect the coordinates to the perimeter and area rules of the previous lesson.
