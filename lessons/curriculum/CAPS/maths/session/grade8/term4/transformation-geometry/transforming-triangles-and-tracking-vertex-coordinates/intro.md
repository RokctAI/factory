Today a whole triangle moves instead of a single point, and the work is to move it one vertex at a time and keep track of where every corner lands. A reflection, a translation or a rotation about the origin is applied to each vertex by its rule, the image is labelled with primes so every corner can be matched to its original, and the new triangle is checked: same side lengths, same angles, same area. By the end you will transform a triangle by any of the Grade 8 rules, record the vertex coordinates in a table, name a transformation from a triangle and its image, and use orientation — the order of the corners going round — to tell a reflection from a rotation.
