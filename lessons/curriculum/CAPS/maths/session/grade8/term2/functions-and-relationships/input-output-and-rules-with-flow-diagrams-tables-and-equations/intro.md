Today we revise the input–rule–output idea with flow diagrams, tables and formulae, and extend it to equations. A flow diagram that says multiply by three, then subtract two turns an input of four into an output of ten; the same rule is the formula y equals three x minus two, the same rule fills a table, and when the output is known and the input is not, the rule becomes an equation, three x minus two equals ten, to be solved. By the end you will find outputs from inputs, inputs from outputs, and the rule from a table of pairs, moving freely between the four forms.
