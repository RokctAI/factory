Today we solve equations by doing the same thing to both sides: adding the additive inverse to remove a term, multiplying by the multiplicative inverse to remove a coefficient, and using the laws of exponents when the unknown is squared or cubed. Three x minus four equals twenty becomes three x equals twenty-four by adding four to both sides, then x equals eight by dividing both sides by three. x squared equals forty-nine gives x equal to seven or negative seven, and two to the power x equals thirty-two gives x equal to five. By the end you will solve one- and two-step equations formally, with the letter on both sides, with fractions, and with powers, writing every step and checking every answer.
