Today we solve equations by inspection — by looking at the equation, asking what number makes it true, and checking. If x plus seven equals fifteen, the question is what number plus seven gives fifteen; eight does, and substituting confirms it. If three x minus four equals twenty, three x must be twenty-four, so x is eight. By the end you will solve one-step and simple two-step equations by reading them as questions, use trial and improvement when the answer is not obvious, and check every solution by substitution.
