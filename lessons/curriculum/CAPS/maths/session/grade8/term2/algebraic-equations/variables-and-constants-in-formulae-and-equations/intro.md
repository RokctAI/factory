Today we look closely at formulae and equations to tell their variables from their constants. In the perimeter formula P equals two l plus two b, the letters P, l and b vary from rectangle to rectangle while the twos are fixed; in the taxi equation C equals eight k plus twelve, k and C vary while eight and twelve are set by the tariff. By the end you will identify every variable and constant in a formula, say which variables are inputs and which the output, recognise constants that are written as letters, and read a formula as a sentence about how one quantity depends on others.
