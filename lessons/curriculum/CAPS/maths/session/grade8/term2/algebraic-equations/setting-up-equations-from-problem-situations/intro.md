Today we turn situations into equations and read equations back as situations. A taxi charges a twelve rand flag fee plus eight rand per kilometre, and a trip costs sixty rand: the equation is eight k plus twelve equals sixty, and reading it back, each part of the equation is a part of the trip. By the end you will choose a variable, build an equation from a word problem in one or two steps, and interpret an equation someone else wrote — what the letter stands for, what each term means, and what the equals sign claims.
