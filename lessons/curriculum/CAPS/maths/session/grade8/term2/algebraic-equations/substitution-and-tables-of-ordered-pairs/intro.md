Today we use substitution to turn an equation in two variables into a table of ordered pairs. If y equals two x plus one, then x equal to zero gives y equal to one, x equal to one gives three, x equal to two gives five, and the table of pairs — zero and one, one and three, two and five — is the equation made visible, one point at a time. By the end you will complete such tables from an equation, find a missing x from a given y, recognise the pattern in the y values, and prepare the ordered pairs that the graphs topic will plot later this term.
