Today we divide in algebra: a single term by a number or a single term, and a two- or three-term expression by a number or a single term. Twelve x cubed divided by three x is four x squared — divide the coefficients, subtract the exponents. Six x squared minus ten x, all divided by two x, is three x minus five — every term on top is divided by the term below. By the end you will divide any monomial, binomial or trinomial by a monomial, simplify fractions with letters in them, and avoid the error of dividing only the first term.
