Today we simplify expressions by adding and subtracting like terms, and we see which laws make that legal. Five x squared minus three x plus two x squared plus x minus four collects to seven x squared minus two x minus four: the x squared terms together, the x terms together, the constants together. The commutative and associative laws let us rearrange and regroup, the distributive law explains why three x plus four x is seven x, and the laws of exponents tell us that x squared and x cannot be collected. By the end you will simplify any such expression and keep every sign in its place.
