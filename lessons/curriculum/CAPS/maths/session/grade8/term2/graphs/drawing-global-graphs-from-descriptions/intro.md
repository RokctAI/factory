Today we go from a described situation to a sketched graph. A description such as the bath fills steadily for five minutes, someone sits in it for ten, then it drains in three becomes a graph of depth against time with a rising straight line, a long horizontal, and a steeper falling line. By the end you will choose and label axes, break a description into parts, decide for each part whether the graph rises, falls or stays level and whether it is straight or curved, judge relative steepness, mark maxima and minima, choose dots or a line, and produce a sketch an examiner can read.
