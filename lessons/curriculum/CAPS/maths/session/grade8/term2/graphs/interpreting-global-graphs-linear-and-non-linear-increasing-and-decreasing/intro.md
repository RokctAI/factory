Today we read graphs for their overall shape rather than for exact numbers: is the graph a straight line or a curve, and is it going up, going down or staying level? A graph of distance against time for a car on a highway at steady speed is a straight line rising; a bath filling, then being used, then emptying gives a graph that rises, stays flat and falls. By the end you will describe any global graph as linear or non-linear and as increasing, decreasing or constant over each part, read a story from a graph, and match a situation to its graph.
