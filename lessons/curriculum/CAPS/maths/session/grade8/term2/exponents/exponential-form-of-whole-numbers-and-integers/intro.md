Today we revise exponential form for whole numbers and extend it to integers. Two to the power five is a short way of writing two times two times two times two times two, which is thirty-two, and the same shorthand works for negative bases as long as the brackets are respected: negative two to the power five is negative thirty-two, while negative two to the power four is positive sixteen. By the end you will write repeated products in exponential form, expand and evaluate powers of integers, compare powers correctly, and tell the difference between bracket negative three bracket squared and negative three squared, which is the mistake that costs the most marks in this topic.
