Today we learn to write very large numbers in scientific notation: a number between one and ten, multiplied by a power of ten. The distance from the Earth to the Sun, about one hundred and fifty million kilometres, becomes one comma five times ten to the power eight. By the end you will convert large numbers into scientific notation and back, compare numbers written in scientific notation by looking at the exponent first, and avoid the classic mistake of a first factor that is not between one and ten.
