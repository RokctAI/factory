Today we establish the laws of exponents by working them out from expanded form, not by memorising them. When you multiply powers of the same base you add the exponents; when you divide you subtract them; a power of a product is the product of the powers; and any non-zero number to the power zero is one. By the end you will be able to simplify two to the power three times two to the power four as two to the power seven, five to the power six divided by five squared as five to the power four, and bracket two times three bracket to the power four as two to the power four times three to the power four, and you will know why each law is true.
