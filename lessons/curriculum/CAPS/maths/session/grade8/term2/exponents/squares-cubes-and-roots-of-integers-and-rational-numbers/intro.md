Today we calculate squares, cubes, square roots and cube roots of integers, fractions and decimals in one connected method. Squaring a fraction squares the top and the bottom, so two thirds squared is four ninths; taking the square root of a fraction takes the root of the top and the bottom, so the square root of twenty-five over sixty-four is five eighths. Decimals follow by counting decimal places: nought comma three squared is nought comma zero nine, and the square root of nought comma zero nine is nought comma three. By the end you will handle any of these, with negative signs included, and you will know why the square root of nought comma zero nine is not nought comma zero three.
