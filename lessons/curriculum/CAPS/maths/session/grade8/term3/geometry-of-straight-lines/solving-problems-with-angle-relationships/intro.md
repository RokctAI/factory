Today we put the angle pairs to work. A diagram with one or two given angles and several unknowns is solved by chaining relationships — straight line, vertically opposite, corresponding, alternate, co-interior — one reasoned step at a time, and when the unknowns are expressions such as 2x plus 10 the relationship becomes an equation to solve. By the end you will plan a route through a multi-step diagram, write each step as statement, value and reason, set up and solve equations from angle facts, add a helping line where the diagram needs one, and prove that two lines are parallel from their angles.
