Today we learn the names of the angle pairs that straight lines make, because every geometry argument from here to Grade 12 is built from them. Two lines that cross make vertically opposite angles and angles on a straight line; perpendicular lines make right angles; a transversal across parallel lines makes corresponding, alternate and co-interior angles. By the end you will name angles with three letters, recognise each pair in a diagram however it is turned, state the relationship — equal or adding to 180 degrees — and quote the reason in the exact words an examiner expects.
