Today we put a number on chance. A coin, a die, a spinner with equal sectors, a bag of marbles: in each, every outcome is as likely as every other, and that symmetry lets us calculate the probability of an event exactly, before anything is tossed or rolled. By the end you will list every outcome of a simple situation without missing or repeating one, write the probability of an event as the fraction favourable over total, convert it to a decimal or percentage, place it on the scale from impossible to certain, and use the fact that the probabilities of all outcomes add to one.
