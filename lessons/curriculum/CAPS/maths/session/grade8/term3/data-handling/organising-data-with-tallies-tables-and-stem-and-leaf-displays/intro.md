Today we take a heap of raw responses and give it order. Ninety ticked forms or twenty test marks on a page mean nothing until they are counted into a tally, tidied into a frequency table, laid out in a stem-and-leaf display or grouped into intervals; after that, the most common answer, the spread and the shape of the data can be read at a glance. By the end you will tally in fives, build frequency tables for categories and numbers, construct and read stem-and-leaf displays with a key, choose and use class intervals for grouped data, and check that every count adds up to the total.
