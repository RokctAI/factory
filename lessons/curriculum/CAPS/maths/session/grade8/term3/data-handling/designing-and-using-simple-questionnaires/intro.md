Today we build the tool that collects primary data: a short questionnaire whose questions have multiple-choice responses. A questionnaire is good when every respondent understands the same question the same way, finds exactly one box to tick, and finishes in two minutes; it is bad when a question leads, overlaps, leaves gaps or asks two things at once. By the end you will write clear closed questions, design response options that are complete and do not overlap, choose sensible intervals for numerical answers, order and trial a questionnaire, and record the responses in a form ready for tallying.
