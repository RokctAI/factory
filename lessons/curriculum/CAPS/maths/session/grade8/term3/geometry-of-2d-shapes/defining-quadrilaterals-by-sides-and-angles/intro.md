Today we define the six special quadrilaterals so precisely that each can be recognised from its marks alone and placed in a family tree. A quadrilateral has four sides and four angles; parallel sides, equal sides and right angles sort the quadrilaterals into trapezium, parallelogram, rectangle, rhombus, square and kite, and the definitions nest — every square is a rectangle and a rhombus, every rectangle is a parallelogram. By the end you will state each definition in exact words, read parallel arrowheads, equal-side dashes and right-angle squares to classify a figure, draw the family tree, decide which statements such as every rhombus is a square are true, and sketch a quadrilateral to a given description.
