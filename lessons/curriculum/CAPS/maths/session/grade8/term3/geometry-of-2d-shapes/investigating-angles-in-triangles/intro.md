Today we investigate the three angle facts every triangle obeys and then calculate with them. By constructing triangles accurately and measuring, we find that the interior angles always add to 180 degrees, that an equilateral triangle's angles are each 60, and that the base angles of an isosceles triangle are equal — and then we see why, using the parallel-line facts from the straight-lines topic. By the end you will construct and measure to test each fact, explain why each holds, and use them with reasons to find unknown angles in triangles, including unknowns given as expressions.
