Today we define the triangles precisely enough to argue with them. A triangle is named by its three vertices; it is classified by its sides as scalene, isosceles or equilateral and by its angles as acute-angled, right-angled or obtuse-angled, and every triangle has one name from each list. By the end you will state each definition in exact words, read the side dashes and angle marks that declare a triangle's type, decide which combinations of names are possible and which are impossible, sketch a triangle to a given description, and use the definitions as reasons in the written style of Grade 8 geometry.
