Today we make precise two words people use loosely: the same shape, and the same shape and size. Congruent shapes are identical copies — every corresponding side and angle equal — however they are turned or flipped; similar shapes have the same angles and sides in one fixed ratio, the scale factor, so one is an enlargement of the other. By the end you will test two figures for congruence and for similarity by comparing corresponding sides and angles, find a scale factor and use it to calculate missing lengths, state which families of shapes are always similar and which are not, and write the properties in the notation and vocabulary used from here through Grade 12.
