Today the whole topic comes together in problems. A figure — a triangle, a quadrilateral, or two of them sharing a side — carries some given sides and angles and some unknowns, and the job is to find the unknowns using the definitions and facts of the last six lessons, each step reasoned. By the end you will choose the right facts for a figure from its marks, combine angle sums, isosceles base angles, parallelogram properties, straight-line and parallel-line pairs and perimeters, set up and solve equations when the unknowns are expressions in x, handle figures built from more than one shape, and present a solution an examiner can mark line by line.
