Today we investigate two families of facts and then calculate with them. Every quadrilateral's four interior angles add to 360 degrees, which a single diagonal explains by cutting the figure into two triangles; and every parallelogram has opposite sides equal, opposite angles equal and adjacent angles adding to 180, which the parallel-line pairs explain. By the end you will test each fact by construction and measurement, explain it with a reason, and use the facts — with the written reasons an examiner expects — to find unknown sides and angles in quadrilaterals, including unknowns given as expressions in x.
