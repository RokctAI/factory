Today we learn to say why a rule works, not just that it works. A rule can be described in words — one starting stick and three more for every square — or in algebra — three n plus one — and a good description is one you can justify from the picture or the situation. By the end you will describe and justify the rule for chairs around a row of tables, two n plus two, and show that two bracket n plus one bracket is the same rule in different clothes.
