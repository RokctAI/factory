Today we investigate patterns: sequences of numbers and sequences of shapes, and the rules that make them grow. Some patterns add the same amount each time, some multiply by the same amount, and some do neither — and the first job is always to find out which. By the end you will extend three, seven, eleven, fifteen; two, six, eighteen, fifty-four; one, four, nine, sixteen; and a row of matchstick squares, and you will record each in a table of position and term.
