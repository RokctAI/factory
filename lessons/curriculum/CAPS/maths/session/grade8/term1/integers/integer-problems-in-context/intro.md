Today we take everything from the Integers topic into the real world: temperatures, altitudes, bank accounts, quiz scores and time zones. Each problem has several operations hiding inside a story, and the skill is to translate the story into one correct integer calculation before you touch a number. By the end you will handle a bank balance that starts at two hundred and fifty rand, takes three debit orders of one hundred and eighty rand and a deposit of five hundred rand, and you will know it ends at two hundred and ten rand.
