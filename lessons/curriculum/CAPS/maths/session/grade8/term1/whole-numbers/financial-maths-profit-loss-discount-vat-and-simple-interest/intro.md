Today we put whole-number arithmetic to work on money: profit and loss, discount, VAT at fifteen percent, a simple budget, simple interest on a loan, hire purchase and the exchange rate. Two small case studies run through the session — a spaza shop buying and selling cooldrink, and a R2 000 loan at eight percent — and by the end you will calculate every one of these quantities with a method that shows its working, because in financial questions the working is where the marks are.
