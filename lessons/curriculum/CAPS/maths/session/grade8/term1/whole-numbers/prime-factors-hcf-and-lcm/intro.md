Today we break numbers down to their prime factors and use that breakdown to find the two quantities every fraction, every timetable and every tiling problem depends on: the highest common factor and the lowest common multiple. One pair of numbers runs through the whole session — 180 and 300 — and by the end you will factorise both, read the HCF and the LCM straight off the factor trees, and know which one a word problem is asking for.
