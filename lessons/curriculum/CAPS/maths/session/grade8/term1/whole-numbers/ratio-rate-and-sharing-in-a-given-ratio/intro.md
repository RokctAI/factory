Today we work with ratio and rate — the two ideas behind every recipe, every taxi fare and every pay split you will ever argue about. One anchor runs through the session: R1 800 shared between two people in the ratio four to five. From it we build the parts method, use it to increase and decrease a number in a given ratio, and separate ratio from rate so that you never confuse the two in an examination. By the end you will share, scale and compare with a method that carries full marks.
