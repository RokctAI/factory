Today we revise the three fraction operations you already know — adding, subtracting and multiplying — and tighten them to Grade 8 standard, including mixed numbers and fractions of whole numbers. The one idea behind adding and subtracting is a common denominator; the one idea behind multiplying is top times top, bottom times bottom. By the end you will show that two thirds plus three quarters is one and five twelfths, and you will know exactly why.
