Today is a toolkit lesson: the techniques that make every fraction calculation faster and safer. Converting between mixed numbers and improper fractions, simplifying with the highest common factor, building equivalent fractions with the lowest common multiple, and cancelling in products. By the end you will simplify thirty-six over forty-eight in one move, order three fractions without a calculator, and multiply fourteen fifteenths by twenty-five twenty-firsts without a single large number.
