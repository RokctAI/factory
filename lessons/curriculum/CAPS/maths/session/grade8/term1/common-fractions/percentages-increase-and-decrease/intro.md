Today we revise percentages and then take them further: a percentage of an amount, one amount as a percentage of another, percentage increase and decrease, and the reverse problem of finding an original amount after a change. A percentage is just a fraction with denominator one hundred, and keeping that in view makes every calculation one line. By the end you will increase eight hundred by twelve percent in a single multiplication and find the original price of a jacket that costs three hundred and sixty rand after a twenty percent discount.
