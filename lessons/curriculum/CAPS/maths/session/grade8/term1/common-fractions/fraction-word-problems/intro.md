Today the fraction skills go to work on word problems: grouping, sharing, fractions of whole numbers and the trickier questions where a fraction of a remainder or an unknown whole is involved. The skill is recognising which operation a story is asking for before you calculate. By the end you will handle a tank that is two thirds full and loses a quarter of its capacity, and a shopper who spends a third of her money and then a quarter of what is left.
