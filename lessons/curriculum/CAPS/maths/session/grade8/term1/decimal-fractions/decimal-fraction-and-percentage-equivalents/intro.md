Today we revisit the equivalence of fractions, decimals and percentages from the decimal side: which fractions give terminating decimals and which recur, how decimals with three or four places become percentages with a decimal part, and how percentages such as two and a half percent become decimals and fractions. By the end you will know that zero comma zero six two five, one sixteenth and six and a quarter percent are the same number, and why one sixth never terminates.
