### subtopic_1 — Terminating or Recurring? Look at the Denominator
**Assistant:** Why do only denominators made of twos and fives give terminating decimals?

**Tutor:** Because a terminating decimal is a fraction whose denominator is a power of ten, and powers of ten are made entirely of twos and fives since ten is two times five.
