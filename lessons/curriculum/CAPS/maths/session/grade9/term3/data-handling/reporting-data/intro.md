Today we write the data report, the only part of an investigation a reader ever sees. We learn its eight parts in order, from the question and the method through the organised data, the chosen summary statistics, the findings, the predictions and the limitations to the recommendation, and we build the transport report from this term's two-way table of sixty Grade 9 learners. Every finding is a sentence with its number: walking is the most common mode at 33 of 60, which is 55 percent, and girls use taxis more than boys at 40 percent against 30. We scale predictions to the population with the word about, forecasting about 165 walkers in the grade and about 540 kilowatt hours in July from a rising trend, with the caution each needs. We report extremes and outliers honestly, including the 60 rands that moved the mean, and write the limitations with their directions.
