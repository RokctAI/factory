Today we turn organised data into graphs and learn which graph fits which data. We draw a bar graph and a double bar graph of sixty learners' modes of transport by gender, with gaps between the bars and a key, and a pie chart whose slices of 198, 126 and 36 degrees add to 360. We draw a histogram of thirty learners' distances to school with touching bars, learn to choose our own equal-width intervals from the range of the data, and read the modal interval and the skewed shape. We draw a broken-line graph of a household's electricity use rising from 320 to 480 kilowatt hours into winter, and a scatter plot of hours studied against marks that slopes upward without proving cause. Finally we match each data type to its graph and fit every graph with a title, labelled axes with units, an even scale from zero and a key.
