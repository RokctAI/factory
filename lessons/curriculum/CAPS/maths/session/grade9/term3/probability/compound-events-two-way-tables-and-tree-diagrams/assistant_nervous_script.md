Hey — if two coins, two dice and bags of marbles all blurred together today, here is the calm version: write every way, grid for two dice, branches for one after another.

Write every way. Two coins can land HH, HT, TH, TT: four ways, and HT and TH are different, so two heads is 1 in 4. The count of ways is ways for the first thing times ways for the second: two coins 4, coin and die 12, two dice 36.

Grid for two dice. First die down the side, second across the top, 36 squares each worth 1 over 36. Write the totals in. Six squares make 7, one square makes 2, so 7 is 1 in 6 and 2 is 1 in 36; the eleven totals are not equally likely. Doubles are the six squares on the other diagonal. Count the squares you want and put them over 36.

Branches for one after another. Draw a branch for each way the first thing can go with its chance on it, then branches for the second thing from each end. Multiply along a path, add across the paths that make your event, and check that all the tips add to 1. Spinner one third red twice: red-red is 1 over 9, one of each is 4 over 9. Bag of 3 red and 2 green without replacement: the second branches change because a marble is gone, so red-red is 3 over 5 times 2 over 4, which is 3 over 10. With replacement the bag resets: 9 over 25.

Write every way, grid, branches. You've got this!
