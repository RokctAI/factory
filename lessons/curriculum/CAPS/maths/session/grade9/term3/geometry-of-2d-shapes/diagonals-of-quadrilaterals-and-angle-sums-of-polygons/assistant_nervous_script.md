Hey — if the ruler, the protractor and all those polygons left you dizzy, here is the calm version: measure the sticks, slice from one corner, walk the block.

Measure the sticks. Draw the shape, put in both diagonals, mark the crossing O. In a rectangle 8 by 6 both sticks are 10 and the four pieces are 5 each, so they are equal and cut each other in half, but they do not cross at 90. A square adds the right angle and the 45s. A parallelogram only cuts the sticks in half. A rhombus cuts them in half at a right angle and halves the corners. A kite's middle stick is the only one that works. Write the shape in every reason.

Slice from one corner. Stand at one corner of a polygon and draw a line to every corner you are not touching. You get two fewer triangles than sides, each worth 180. Pentagon 540, hexagon 720, octagon 1080. Four known corners of a pentagon, 100, 110, 120 and 130, leave 80 for the fifth. Regular means share the total equally: hexagon 120, octagon 135.

Walk the block. Going round any polygon your outside turns add to 360. Regular with inside angles of 150 means outside angles of 30, and 360 shared by 30 gives 12 sides.

Sticks, slices, the walk. You've got this!
