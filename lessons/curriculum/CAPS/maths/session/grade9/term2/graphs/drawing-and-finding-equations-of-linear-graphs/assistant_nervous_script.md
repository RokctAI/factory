Hey — if drawing lines and finding their formulas felt like two different lessons, here is the calm version: it is one formula, y equals mx plus c, read in two directions.

Formula to picture. Safest way: three dots and a ruler. Pick x equals negative 1, 0 and 1, work out each y with brackets, plot the three dots, check they line up, rule through them with arrows, and write the formula next to the line. Faster way: start on the wall at c, then step the staircase m, one across and m up, or m down if m is negative. Two more dots, ruler, done. Flat line for y equals a number, upright line for x equals a number.

Picture to formula. Wall first: where the line touches the y-axis is c. Staircase next: two grid corners the line passes through, up over across, is m. A line through 0 semicolon negative 3 and 3 semicolon 3 has c equal to negative 3 and m equal to 6 over 3, which is 2, so y equals 2x minus 3. Falling line means negative m.

If the wall crossing is not on the picture, find m from two dots, then put one dot into y equals mx plus c and solve for c.

Always check with one more dot. Three dots and a ruler; wall then staircase; one more dot. You've got this!
