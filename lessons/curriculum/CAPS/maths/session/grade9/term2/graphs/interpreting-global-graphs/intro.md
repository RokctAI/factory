Today we read graphs before we draw them. A global graph is a picture of a whole relationship, and the Grade 9 skill is to describe it in exact words anchored to readings from the axes: linear or non-linear, increasing or decreasing or constant, where the maximum and minimum are and when they occur, and whether the quantity is discrete or continuous. We work through a day of Bloemfontein temperatures that falls to 8 degrees at 05:00 and peaks at 28 degrees at 14:00, a walk to school with a stop at the shop, a ball thrown to a maximum height of 20 metres, and a taxi fare that grows R15 per passenger in separate dots rather than a line. Every description carries a direction, an interval, a value and a unit.
