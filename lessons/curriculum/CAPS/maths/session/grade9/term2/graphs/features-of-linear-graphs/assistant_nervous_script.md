Hey — if the three features felt like three separate topics, here is the calm version: a straight line is a wall, a staircase and a floor, and each one gives you a number.

Wall: the vertical axis. Where the line touches it, x is zero. Put x equal to 0 into the equation and you get the y-intercept. For y equals 2x plus 1 that is 1. In a story it is the starting value, like a taxi's R20 fee before you have moved. In y equals mx plus c, it is the c.

Staircase: pick two grid corners on the line, count up, count across, divide up by across. From 0 semicolon 1 to 2 semicolon 5 is up 4, across 2, gradient 2. Downhill lines are negative. Flat lines are zero. In a story the gradient is the rate, like R12 per kilometre. In y equals mx plus c, it is the m.

Floor: the horizontal axis. On the floor, y is zero. Put y equal to 0 and solve for x. For y equals 3x minus 6 that gives x equals 2. Fractions are fine.

Write every intercept as a pair with the zero showing, 0 semicolon 1 for the wall and 2 semicolon 0 for the floor, and you will never swap them.

Wall, staircase, floor. Three numbers, one line. You've got this!
