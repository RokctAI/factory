Hey — if the graph words came at you all at once today, here is the calm version: a graph is a story, and five words plus two numbers describe any part of it.

First read the two labels and their units. Bottom is the thing that passes or gets counted, like time or passengers. Side is the thing that changes, like temperature or fare. Then pick a point and read it both ways, with the unit: 28 degrees at 14:00.

The five shape words. Up is increasing. Down is decreasing. Flat is constant, which means standing still, not going backwards. Straight is linear: the same step every time. Bendy is non-linear. Describe a graph section by section, each with its word and its interval.

The two numbers for a peak. The maximum is how high the graph gets and when; the minimum is how low and when. Give both numbers. And remember that steepest is not highest: steep is about how fast it changes, high is about the value.

Dots or a line: if the thing along the bottom can be a fraction, like kilometres, draw a line. If it comes in whole pieces, like passengers, draw dots and do not join them.

Labels, a reading with a unit, five words, two numbers, dots or a line. You've got this!
