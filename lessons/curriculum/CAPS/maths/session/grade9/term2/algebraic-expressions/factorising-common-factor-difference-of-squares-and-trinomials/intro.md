Today we run expansion backwards: factorising takes terms and rebuilds the brackets they came from. Grade 9 asks for three kinds. Taking out a common factor, where 6x squared plus 9x becomes 3x times the bracket 2x plus 3. Recognising a difference of two squares, where x squared minus 49 becomes the bracket x plus 7 times the bracket x minus 7. Splitting a trinomial, where x squared plus 8x plus 15 becomes the bracket x plus 3 times the bracket x plus 5 by hunting for two numbers with product 15 and sum 8. When a number in front of x squared is shared by every term, as in 3x squared minus 3x minus 18, it comes out first. Every answer is checked by expanding, and this is the skill that solves equations two lessons from now.
