Hey — if this lesson felt like three topics stacked on top of each other, here is the calm version: three short rules, and one check that guards all of them.

Rule one, like terms: same fruit adds, counts can be fractions, and a minus in front of a bracket takes away the whole bag — every sign inside flips. 3x squared minus 2x plus 5, take away the bag x squared plus 4x minus 1, gives 2x squared minus 6x plus 6.

Rule two, multiplying monomials: numbers times, little numbers add. 3x squared times 4x cubed is 12x to the fifth. Dividing: numbers divide, little numbers subtract. Negatives follow the integer rules.

Rule three, substituting: put the number in brackets first, square before you multiply by the number in front, and remember fractions and decimals are the same number — a half and 0,5 must give the same answer.

The check: put x equals 1 or 2 into the original and into your answer. If they match, you are right. If they do not, the slip is almost always a sign in a bag or a little number added when it should have stayed.

Open the practice set, flip the whole bag, and check with 1. You've got this!
