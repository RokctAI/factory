Today we return to equations with the whole Term 2 toolkit behind us. An equation is a locked box with x inside, and Grade 9 opens it three ways: by inspection, when the answer can be seen, as in x squared equals 49 with its two roots; by inverses, undoing each operation in reverse order on both sides, as in 3x minus 5 equals 16 giving x equals 7; and by the laws of exponents, writing both sides with the same base, as in 3 to the power of x plus 1 equals 81 giving x equals 3. We expand brackets first, clear fractions with the lowest common denominator, and turn word problems such as a R20 plus R12 per kilometre taxi fare into equations. Every solution is substituted back to check it.
