Hey — if the angle names piled up today, here is the calm version: three pictures and one habit.

Picture one, the half-turn. A straight line is a half-turn, 180 degrees, so angles side by side on a line add to 180. A full turn around a point is 360. A right angle is 90. If the angles have letters, add them up, set them equal to 180 or 360, and use your undo buttons: x plus 2x plus 30 equals 180 gives x equal to 50.

Picture two, the scissors. Two lines crossing make an X, and the angles opposite each other are equal. Not 180 together, equal. 3x minus 10 equals 2x plus 20 gives x equal to 30.

Picture three, the parallel alphabet. Two parallel lines, marked with arrowheads, and a line across them. F means corresponding, equal. Z means alternate, equal. C means co-interior, adds to 180. One angle of 125 makes its F and Z partners 125 and its C partner 55.

The habit: after every statement, write the reason in brackets. Angles on a straight line. Vertically opposite angles. Corresponding angles, with the parallel lines named. The reason is the mark.

Half-turn, scissors, F Z C, reason in brackets. You've got this!
