Hey — if the angle problems felt like a maze today, here is the calm version: dominoes, one letter, and a key.

Dominoes. You cannot find the last angle directly. Find one angle from one fact, write the fact in brackets, and use that angle to find the next. Two lines crossing with 2x and x plus 30 side by side: the half-turn gives 3x plus 30 equals 180, so x is 50. Then put x back: 100 and 80, and the X rule gives the other two. Check that all four add to 360.

One letter. Between parallel lines, say the letter shape before you write the equation. F or Z means twins: equals sign between the two expressions. C means a couple: add them and set the sum to 180. That one choice decides the whole question.

The key. x is not an angle; it is the key to the angles. When you have x, put it back into every expression the question asked about and write each angle with its degree sign and its reason.

And for proving lines parallel, run the letters backwards: equal Z or F angles, or C angles adding to 180, prove the lines parallel. Never use a parallel rule before the lines are proved parallel.

Dominoes in order, say the letter, put x back. You've got this!
