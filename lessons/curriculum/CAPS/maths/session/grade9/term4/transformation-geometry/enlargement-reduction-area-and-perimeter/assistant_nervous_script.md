Hey — if the difference between k and k squared felt slippery today, here is the calm version: same shape bigger or smaller, fence times k, carpet times k squared.

Same shape. Enlarging or reducing means multiplying every side by the same number, the scale factor k. Bigger than 1 grows the shape; between 0 and 1 shrinks it. Angles stay the same. Find k by dividing a new side by its old side. Adding the same amount to every side is not enlarging, because it changes the shape.

Fence times k. Perimeter is a length, the fence around the shape. Every side is multiplied by k, so the fence is multiplied by k. A 4 by 3 rectangle has a fence of 14; doubled to 8 by 6, the fence is 28.

Carpet times k squared. Area is the carpet inside, and it has a length and a width, both multiplied by k. So the carpet is multiplied by k twice. The 4 by 3 rectangle has area 12; the 8 by 6 rectangle has area 48, four times as much. Four small rectangles fit inside the big one. Going backwards from areas, take a square root: areas nine times bigger mean sides three times bigger.

Ask one question every time: is this a length or an area? You've got this!
