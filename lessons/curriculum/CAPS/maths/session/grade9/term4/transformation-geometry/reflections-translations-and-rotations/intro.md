Today we move figures around the Cartesian plane by exact rules, writing each rule as what happens to x semicolon y. Working with triangle ABC, with A at 1 semicolon 2, B at 4 semicolon 2 and C at 1 semicolon 4, we reflect it in the x-axis, where y changes sign, in the y-axis, where x changes sign, and in the line y equals x, where the co-ordinates swap. We translate it 3 left and 2 down by subtracting from each co-ordinate, and rotate it about the origin through 90 degrees each way and through 180 degrees, checking each answer by the quadrant it should land in. Then we rotate a point about a centre that is not the origin by counting from the centre. Throughout we note what each transformation keeps, lengths, angles and congruence, and what it changes: position always, and orientation only for reflections.
