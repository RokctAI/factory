Today we run transformations backwards: given a point and its image, we name the transformation. Each one leaves a fingerprint on the co-ordinates. Starting from 3 semicolon 5, a single sign change means a reflection in an axis, a swap with no sign change means a reflection in y equals x, both signs changed means a half turn, and a swap with one sign changed means a quarter turn, clockwise or anticlockwise depending on which sign. A constant shift, found as image minus original, means a translation. We meet points that are ambiguous, such as 2 semicolon negative 2 mapping to negative 2 semicolon 2, and settle them with a second vertex and with orientation. We describe each transformation fully, with its mirror line, its centre, angle and direction, or its shift, and finish with combined transformations, where two reflections make a rotation and the order of the moves can change the result.
