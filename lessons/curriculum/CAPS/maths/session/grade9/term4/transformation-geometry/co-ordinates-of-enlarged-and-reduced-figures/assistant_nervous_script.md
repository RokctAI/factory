Hey — if the co-ordinates of enlargements felt like a lot of multiplying today, here is the calm version: multiply both, halve to shrink, rays from the origin.

Multiply both. With the origin as the centre, multiply both numbers of every corner by the scale factor k. Times 2, the corner 1 semicolon 1 becomes 2 semicolon 2 and 3 semicolon 1 becomes 6 semicolon 2. Same multiplier for both numbers, or the shape gets stretched. Signs stay as they are.

Halve to shrink. A scale factor smaller than 1 shrinks the shape towards the origin. Halving 4 semicolon 6 gives 2 semicolon 3. A third means divide by 3. To undo a scale factor, divide by it: an image corner at 3 semicolon negative 6 after times 3 came from 1 semicolon negative 2.

Rays from the origin. Every corner slides along a line from the origin, ending k times as far away. To check whether a picture is a real enlargement, divide each new number by its old number: every answer must be the same k. 2 semicolon 3 going to 4 semicolon 9 fails, because 2 and 3 are different multipliers.

And area grows by k squared: double the sides, four times the area. You've got this!
