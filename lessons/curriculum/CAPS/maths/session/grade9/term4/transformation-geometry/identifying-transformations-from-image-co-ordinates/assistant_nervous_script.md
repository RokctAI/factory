Hey — if naming the move from just two points felt like guesswork today, here is the calm version: swap and signs, a second point, two moves in order.

Swap and signs. Line up the start and the finish and ask two questions. Did the numbers swap? Which signs flipped? No swap and one sign flipped: a mirror in an axis, and the axis that keeps its number is the mirror. Swap and no sign flipped: the slanted mirror y equals x. No swap and both signs flipped: a half turn. Swap and one sign flipped: a quarter turn, anticlockwise if the new first number is negative, clockwise if the new second one is. If none of that fits, it is a slide: finish take away start.

A second point. Some points fool you, like 2 semicolon negative 2 going to negative 2 semicolon 2, which a swap or a half turn could both do. Test the other corners of the shape until only one move fits. Mirrors also flip the order the corners read in; slides and turns do not.

Two moves in order. Two mirrors in a row make a turn. A mirror and a slide can give different answers in different orders, so always follow the order you are given.

Name every detail: the mirror line, the slide, or the pin, angle and direction. You've got this!
