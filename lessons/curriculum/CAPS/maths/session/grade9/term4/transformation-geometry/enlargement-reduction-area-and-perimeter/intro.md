Today figures change size while keeping their shape. An enlargement or reduction multiplies every length by the same scale factor k, greater than 1 to enlarge and between 0 and 1 to reduce, while every angle stays the same, so the original and the image are similar. We prove the two rules of the topic. Perimeter is a length, so it is multiplied by k: a 4 by 3 rectangle with perimeter 14 enlarges by scale factor 2 to an 8 by 6 rectangle with perimeter 28. Area involves two lengths, so it is multiplied by k squared: the same rectangle's area goes from 12 to 48, four times as much. We use both rules in proportion problems with murals, photographs, building plans and maps, find k backwards from perimeters and from areas with a square root, and finish with the five most common errors.
