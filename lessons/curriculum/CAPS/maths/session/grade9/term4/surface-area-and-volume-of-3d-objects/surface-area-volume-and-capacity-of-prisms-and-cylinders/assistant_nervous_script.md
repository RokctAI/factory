Hey — if formulas for areas and volumes started blurring together today, here is the calm version: wrap it, fill it, convert it.

Wrap it. Surface area is the wrapping paper: add up every face. Picture the net. A box 4 by 3 by 2 has three pairs, 12, 8 and 6; add them and double: 52 square centimetres. A tin is two circles and a label: two lots of pi r squared, plus the label, which is the distance round, 2 pi r, times the height. Use the radius, not the width, and check for a missing lid.

Fill it. Volume is floor times height, for every shape today. Box: 4 times 3, times 2, is 24 cubic centimetres. Tin: pi times 3.5 times 3.5, times 10, is about 385 cubic centimetres. Area units get a little 2, volume units a little 3.

Convert it. One cubic centimetre holds one millilitre; a thousand make a litre; one cubic metre holds 1 000 litres. But careful: a square metre is 10 000 square centimetres and a cubic metre is a million cubic centimetres, because you convert once for every direction. And change everything into one unit before you multiply.

Formula, substitute with units, calculate, unit on the answer. Wrap it, fill it, convert it. You've got this!
