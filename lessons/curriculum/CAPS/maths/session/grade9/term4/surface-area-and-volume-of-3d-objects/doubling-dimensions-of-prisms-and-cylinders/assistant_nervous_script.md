Hey — if all the doubling and multiplying felt like a lot today, here is the calm version: count the directions, the radius counts twice, and times k cubed.

Count the directions. Volume is length times width times height. Double one of them and the volume doubles: the 4 by 3 by 2 box goes from 24 to 48, two old boxes side by side. Double two of them and it is times 4. Double all three and it is times 8. Every doubled direction is another times 2.

The radius counts twice. A cylinder's floor is a circle, and the radius sets its width in both directions at once. Double the height: times 2. Double the radius: times 4. That is why a tank twice as wide holds four times as much water, but twice as tall only twice as much. Double both and it is times 8.

Times k cubed. Make everything k times bigger and lengths go times k, wrapping paper times k squared, space inside times k cubed. Triple everything: times 27. Going backwards, use the cube root: 8 times the volume means every length doubled. And remember the surface only goes up 4 times when the volume goes up 8, which is why big containers are cheaper per litre.

Count directions, radius twice, k cubed. You've got this!
