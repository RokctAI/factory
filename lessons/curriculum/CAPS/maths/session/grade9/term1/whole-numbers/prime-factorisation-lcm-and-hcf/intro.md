Today we take numbers apart into their prime factors and use the pieces to find the highest common factor and the lowest common multiple. The anchor pair is 360 and 84: we will break each into primes with the ladder method, read the HCF off the primes they share, build the LCM from the primes they need between them, and then settle the question that costs the most marks in this topic — which of the two a word problem is actually asking for. Taxis leaving a rank, tiles on a floor and party packs with nothing left over all hide one of these two numbers. By the end you will find both, check them against each other, and choose the right one every time.
