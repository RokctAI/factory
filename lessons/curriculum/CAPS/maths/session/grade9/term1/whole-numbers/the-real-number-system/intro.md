Today we map the real number system — the family tree that every number you will ever meet in Grade 9 belongs to. Natural numbers, whole numbers, integers, rational numbers and irrational numbers are not five separate piles; they are boxes inside boxes, and the skill is to place any number in its smallest box and name every bigger box around it. We will test decimals to see whether they stop, repeat or run on forever, convert a recurring decimal back into a fraction, and trap square roots between two integers. By the end you will classify a list of numbers with certainty, and you will know exactly which two of them are not rational — and why.
