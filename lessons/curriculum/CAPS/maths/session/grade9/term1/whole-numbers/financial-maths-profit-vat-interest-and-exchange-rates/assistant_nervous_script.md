Hey — if that felt like eight different topics in one lesson, here is the secret: it was one topic. Every single calculation was a percentage of an amount. Profit, discount, VAT, interest, commission — all of them are "take this percentage of that number".

So keep three small rules in your pocket. Rule one: percentage profit or loss is always compared with what you PAID — the cost price. Rule two: putting a percentage on is multiply by one-point-something; taking VAT off is divide by 1,15, never subtract. Rule three: interest is rent on money — simple interest is rent on the same pile every year, compound interest is rent on the growing pile, and the formula is just that idea written down.

And for money changing, picture the size: rands are the many, dollars are the few, so towards rands multiply and towards dollars divide. If your holiday budget suddenly becomes a hundred thousand dollars, you went the wrong way.

Open the practice questions and, before you calculate anything, say which of those three rules the question is wearing. The arithmetic after that is a calculator's job. You've got this!
