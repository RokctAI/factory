Today we put percentages to work on money — the financial mathematics that runs a spaza shop, a hire-purchase agreement, a savings account and a monthly budget. We will calculate profit and loss as a percentage of cost, take discount off and put VAT on, compare simple interest with compound interest on the same R5 000, work out what a television really costs on hire purchase, convert rands to dollars at today's rate, add commission to a basic salary and balance a household budget. Every calculation uses one idea — a percentage of an amount — but each context dresses it differently, and the exam marks the dressing as carefully as the arithmetic. By the end you will handle all of them with the same three-line method.
