Today we work with ratio, rate and the two kinds of proportion — the four tools behind sharing money fairly, comparing prices per kilogram, scaling a recipe and planning how long a job takes. One prize of R2 400 shared in the ratio 5 to 3 to 2 runs through the first half; a taxi covering 180 km in two and a half hours gives us a rate; the price of bread shows direct proportion; and a building crew that doubles in size shows the inverse kind. By the end you will simplify any ratio, build any rate, and tell the two proportions apart with one question — when one quantity goes up, does the other go up, or down?
