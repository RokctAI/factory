Hey — if the number family tree felt like a lot of symbols at once, let me shrink it to one picture: boxes inside boxes.

Counting numbers in the smallest box. Add zero, that's the whole numbers. Add the negatives, that's the integers. Add every fraction, that's the rationals. And the only things outside all of those boxes are the endless, patternless decimals — root 2, pi — the irrationals. Any number you meet goes in its smallest box and automatically belongs to every box around it. That's the entire topic.

For anything with a comma, ask one question: does it stop, or does it repeat? Yes to either means rational. No to both means irrational. And for anything with a root sign, work it out first — root 9 is just 3 wearing a costume.

The comforting part is that none of this needs calculation under pressure. It is definitions and a sorting habit, and you already sort things every day. Open the practice questions, say the smallest box out loud for each number, then list the boxes around it. Three or four numbers in, the sorting becomes automatic. You've got this!
