Today we investigate and extend patterns — sequences of numbers, rows of matchstick shapes, tables of positions and terms — and learn to write the rule that produces any term from its position. A numeric pattern with a constant difference, like 5, 8, 11, 14, has a rule of the form T n equals d n plus c, and that rule is a straight line in disguise. A geometric pattern with a constant ratio, like 3, 6, 12, 24, has a rule built from a power, and that is last week's exponents returning. We will extend patterns in diagram form, in tables and algebraically, find the general term, test it against the pattern, and use it to answer questions no amount of counting could reach: what is the 50th term, and is 100 a term at all? This is the doorway to Grade 10 functions.
