Hey — if the patterns that broke the rules left you feeling there were no rules, here is the calm version: there are four fingerprints, and you check them in order.

One: subtract neighbours. Same every time? Taxi meter — step times n plus flag fall. Two: divide neighbours. Same every time? Chain message — first term times multiplier to the power n minus 1. A negative multiplier just makes the signs flip.

Three: if the differences are not the same but they grow steadily — by 2, by 2, by 2 — the rule has n squared in it. Write 1, 4, 9, 16 underneath your terms and see what has been added or taken away. If the differences grow by 4 instead, the squares have been doubled.

Four: if each term is the two before it added together, it is a Fibonacci-style pattern. Say the rule and say where it starts, or it is not a description.

And the golden rule: three terms never decide. Use every term you are given.

When you make your own pattern, pick a rule, write five terms, check the fingerprint yourself, and describe it in words and in algebra. Open the practice set and run the four checks in order. You've got this!
