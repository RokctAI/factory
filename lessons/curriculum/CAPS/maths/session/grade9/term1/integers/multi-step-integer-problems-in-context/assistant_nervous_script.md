Hey — if the word problems felt like too many things happening at once, here is the calm version: every one of them is a walk along a number line, and you already know how to walk.

Step one, find the zero. Empty account, freezing point, ground floor, sea surface. Step two, read the words as directions: up, gain, deposit, rise are plus; down, lose, pay, fall are minus; and "overdrawn" or "below" means you start on the minus side. Step three, if the same thing happens several times — three debit orders, five hours of warming — multiply it, and do that multiplying before you start walking.

Then keep a running total. Negative 250, plus 1 200 is 950, minus 540 is 410, minus 45 is 365. One event, one move, one new balance. Nothing clever, just careful.

And give yourself the two safety nets: guess the sign before you calculate, and walk backwards afterwards to see if you land where you started. Those two habits catch almost every slip without any extra maths.

Open the practice questions, find the zero, and take the story one step at a time. You've got this!
