Today we make integers fully operational: adding and subtracting across zero, multiplying and dividing with the sign rules, squaring and cubing negatives, taking square and cube roots, and then combining all of it under the order of operations. The anchor calculation is 5 minus 2 times negative 3 squared, plus the cube root of negative 8 — a single line that hides every trap in the topic. By the end you will know why negative 3 squared and negative of 3 squared are different numbers, why a cube root can be negative but a square root cannot, and how to walk a mixed integer expression to the right answer every time.
