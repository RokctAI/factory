Today we take integers out of the textbook and into bank accounts, thermometers, lifts, diving depths and quiz scores — problems where several operations happen in one story and the signs must survive all of them. The anchor is a bank account that starts overdrawn at negative R250, receives a salary, pays three debit orders and a bank fee, and must end on the correct side of zero. We will translate every word of a story into a sign and an operation, chain the operations in the right order, estimate the sign of the answer before we calculate, and check it afterwards. By the end a word problem full of ups and downs will read like a single integer expression you already know how to evaluate.
