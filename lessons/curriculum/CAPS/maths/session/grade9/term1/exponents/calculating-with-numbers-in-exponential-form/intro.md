Today we calculate with numbers in exponential form using all four operations — and the first thing to learn is which operations have laws and which do not. Multiplying and dividing powers of the same base have the laws from last week. Adding and subtracting powers have no law at all: 2 cubed plus 2 to the power 4 is 8 plus 16, which is 24, and nothing shorter is true. We will settle when to evaluate and when to use a law, put powers into the order of operations alongside brackets, roots and multiplication, and finish with calculations on numbers in scientific notation, where the fronts and the powers of ten are handled separately. These are the calculations the Term 1 assignment and every science class will expect you to do without hesitation.
