Today we revise the laws of exponents — the five rules that let you multiply, divide and raise powers without ever writing out the repeated multiplication. Same base, multiply: add the exponents. Same base, divide: subtract. A power of a power: multiply the exponents. A power of a product: give the exponent to every factor. And anything non-zero to the power zero is one. None of these is a rule to memorise blindly; each one falls out of counting factors, and we will derive every one before we use it. By the end you will simplify expressions like 2 to the power 5 times 2 cubed divided by 2 to the power 6, and 2a squared b all cubed, with the confidence that Grade 10 exponential equations will demand.
