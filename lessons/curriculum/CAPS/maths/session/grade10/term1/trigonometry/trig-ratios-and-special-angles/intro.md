Today we open the gateway to the largest section of Paper 2: trigonometry, starting with the three ratios and the special angles. Two exam tasks carry the whole session — evaluating sine sixty times cosine thirty minus tangent forty-five without a calculator, and finding an unknown angle from a hypotenuse of thirteen and an opposite side of five. Along the way we build the two special triangles you can redraw in ten seconds instead of memorising six values, and we learn the inverse move that runs a ratio backwards into an angle. Three ratios, two triangles, one choosing routine — the complete starter kit for years of trigonometry marks.
