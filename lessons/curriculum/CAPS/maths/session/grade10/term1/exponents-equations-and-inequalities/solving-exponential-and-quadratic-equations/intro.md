Today we are solving the two families of equation that dominate the algebra section of Paper 1: quadratics and exponentials. Both give way to the same strategy — turn the unfamiliar into something you already handle. A quadratic is driven into standard form and factorised so the zero-product law can read the brackets apart; an exponential is rewritten onto a single base so the exponents can be equated. Six worked equations carry the session, and stationed around them are the two costliest errors in the topic — losing a solution by dividing by the unknown, and equating exponents across different bases — so you meet each trap here rather than in the examination.
