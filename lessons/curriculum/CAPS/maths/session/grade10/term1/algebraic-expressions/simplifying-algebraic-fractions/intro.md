Today every factorising method of the past three weeks goes to work on algebraic fractions — fractions whose tops and bottoms are polynomials. One instruction opens every operation on them: factorise first, cancel second, because only factors shared by the whole numerator and the whole denominator may ever be cancelled. We will simplify, multiply, divide, add and subtract four worked expressions, and alongside them build the discipline examiners reward and learners forget — stating the restrictions on the variable from the original denominators. Get the cancelling law and the restrictions right, and this topic becomes some of the most reliable marks in the paper.
