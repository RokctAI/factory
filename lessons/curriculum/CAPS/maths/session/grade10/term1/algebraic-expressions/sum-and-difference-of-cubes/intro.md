Today we are factorising the sum and difference of two cubes — an identity that looks intimidating on the page and becomes completely mechanical once one mnemonic is in place. The anchor problem is to factorise x cubed plus eight, a two-term expression that every familiar tool bounces off, and one that CAPS examiners test every single year precisely because so few learners own it properly. We will build the recognition list of perfect cubes, state both identities, tame their signs with SOAP — Same, Opposite, Always Positive — and finish with the exam's favourite trick, the cube hiding behind a common factor. Learn it tonight and it earns marks for three years.
