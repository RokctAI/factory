Today we are sketching and interpreting the parabola in its Grade 10 form, y equals a x squared plus q — the first curved graph you are asked to draw from an equation alone. We will build the mother function point by point, isolate exactly what the parameter a does and what q does, then execute a full five-step sketch and run the process backwards to recover an equation from a graph. This is guaranteed territory in the functions question of Paper 1, and the routine we drill today turns it into four quick lines and a curve.
