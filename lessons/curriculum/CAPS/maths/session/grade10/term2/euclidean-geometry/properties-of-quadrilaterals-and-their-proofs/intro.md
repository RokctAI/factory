Today we are working through the special quadrilaterals and the proofs behind their properties — the first place in Paper 2 where marks are earned by reasoning rather than arithmetic. We will build the family from trapezium to square and see exactly what each shape inherits, then prove the parallelogram's properties from its definition and learn the conditions that let you prove a figure is a parallelogram, rectangle or rhombus. The session closes with a full examination rider, written statement by statement with a reason beside every line, because that is precisely how these questions are marked.
