Today we are mastering the midpoint theorem — the most productive theorem in Grade 10 geometry, where one hypothesis about two midpoints buys you two conclusions at once: a parallel line and a half length. We will state it precisely, prove it through a cleverly constructed parallelogram, then turn it around into its converse, which examiners test just as often. The session closes on the riders where both versions chain together, and on the five errors that quietly empty a page of its marks.
