Today we are carrying a dataset all the way from a messy list of raw numbers to a finished statistical comment — the full journey the examiners call organise, summarise, represent and interpret. The vehicle is the frequency table and its picture, the histogram, and one set of thirty travel times runs through the whole session. By the end you will build the table with clean class boundaries, draw and describe the histogram precisely, read its peak, middle and tail, and write the centre-spread-shape sentences that collect the final marks.
