Today we are doing the arithmetic of typical: the mean, the median and the mode — the three measures of central tendency that compress a whole dataset into one representative number. One set of homework minutes runs through the first half of the session, a grouped frequency table takes over in the second, and by the end you will compute all three measures, watch an outlier drag the mean while the median stands firm, and estimate a mean you cannot compute exactly. The choosing-and-justifying sentence we build today is a guaranteed mark in the data-handling question.
