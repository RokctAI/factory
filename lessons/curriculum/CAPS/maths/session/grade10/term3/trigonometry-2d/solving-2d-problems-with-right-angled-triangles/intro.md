Today we are putting the three trigonometric ratios to work on real two-dimensional problems — heights of flagpoles, distances to boats, angles of ladders — every one of them a right-angled triangle wearing a story. The whole topic runs on two ideas: angles of elevation and depression are always measured from the horizontal, and the three-step method of sketch, label, choose the ratio turns any description into an equation. We build up to the distinction-level question where two triangles share one height, the exact structure examiners use to separate the top marks. Master the sketch and the triangle does the heavy lifting for you.
