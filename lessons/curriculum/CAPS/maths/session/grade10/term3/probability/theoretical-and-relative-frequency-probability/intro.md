Today we are putting a number on uncertainty. Probability is the mathematics of how likely, and this session builds its two engines: theoretical probability, computed by counting what could happen, and relative frequency, measured by counting what did happen. One ordinary die and one bag of coloured counters run through the whole session, and by the end you will define the vocabulary precisely, compute with both engines, use the complement shortcut, and know exactly when each engine is the right tool for an exam question.
