Today we are handling two events at once. One event was counting; two events are geography — regions, overlaps, and the rule that stops you counting anybody twice. This session builds the Venn diagram from a single survey of forty learners, derives the addition rule and the famous subtraction inside it, and sharpens the special vocabulary of mutually exclusive and complementary events. Fill the overlap first, check the total, and the whole two-event world of the exam becomes a map you can read.
