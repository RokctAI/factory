Today we build the machine behind every number-pattern question in the final paper: the linear pattern and its general term. One sequence carries the whole session — five, eight, eleven, fourteen — and from it we construct the formula, drive it forwards to term fifty, backwards to find a position, and use it to prove that a number never appears in the sequence at all. Then we undress the disguises examiners love: matchstick figures, tables pushed together, and tariffs that charge a flat fee plus a rate. The formula is difference times position plus a correction, and by the end it will build itself.
