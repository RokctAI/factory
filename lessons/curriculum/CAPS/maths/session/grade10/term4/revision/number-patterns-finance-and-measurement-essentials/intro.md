Today is the revision sweep for three topics the other revision sessions leave untouched: number patterns, finance, and measurement. Each runs on a tiny engine — patterns on one constant difference, finance on the two growth formulas that separate the flat ladder of simple interest from the compound snowball, and measurement on a short catalogue of solids governed by one scale-factor law. Everything is the exam-day version, worked through the classic examples: the general term built and tested, a hire purchase priced to the monthly payment, the full solid catalogue, and the k, k squared, k cubed law that gives every power its dimension. Four traps recur every November, and by the end you will recognise each one on sight.
