Today we rebuild the measurement machinery the final examination expects on demand: volume and surface area of right prisms and cylinders. One law carries the whole topic — volume is the area of the base times the perpendicular height, a stack of identical floors — while surface area comes from unfolding the solid flat into its net and adding the faces one by one. We work a juice box, a cooldrink can and a triangular prism from start to finish, keeping answers exact in pi until the last line. Get the base right and the units right, and every solid on the paper answers to the same five-step checklist.
