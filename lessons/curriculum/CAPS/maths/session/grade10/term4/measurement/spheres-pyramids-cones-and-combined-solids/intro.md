Today we complete the Grade 10 measurement toolkit with spheres, right pyramids, right cones, and the combined solids examiners build by gluing them together. The whole topic rests on one new number — the one third that turns a prism or cylinder formula into a pointy-solid formula — and one old friend, Pythagoras, who manufactures the slant height whenever surface area calls for it. We work a cone and an ice-cream shape from start to finish, then build a rondavel and close with the four traps that harvest marks every November. Get the two heights straight and the hidden faces crossed out, and these questions become steady, bankable marks.
