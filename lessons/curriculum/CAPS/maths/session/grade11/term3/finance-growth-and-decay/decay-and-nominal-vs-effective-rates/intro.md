Today finance runs downhill as well as up: simple and compound decay price every depreciating bakkie and ageing machine, and the same fifteen percent tells two completely different stories depending on whether it bites the original value or the current one. Then we sharpen the growth story with the question banks use to compete for your deposit — what monthly compounding really does to an advertised rate — and learn to convert any nominal headline into its honest effective equivalent. Add exchange rates and mixed timelines, and you hold the complete grade eleven finance toolkit for Paper 1.
