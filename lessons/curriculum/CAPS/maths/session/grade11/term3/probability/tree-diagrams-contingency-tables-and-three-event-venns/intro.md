Today we are mastering the three tools of grade eleven probability: the three-event Venn diagram, the tree diagram for events in sequence, and the contingency table. Every probability story in the examination arrives in one of these shapes, and the real skill being tested is diagnosing the shape from the wording alone and reaching for the matching tool. We will fill a three-circle survey from the centre outwards, build a without-replacement tree whose branches remember what was drawn, and put independence on trial with the product test. Get the tool choice right and the arithmetic that follows is short, structured and full marks.
