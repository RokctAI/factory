Today we measure what averages cannot see: spread. Two classes can share the same mean and be nothing alike — one steady, one wild — and this session builds the instruments that tell them apart. We revise the five number summary and the box-and-whisker diagram, then construct grade eleven's precision tools, variance and standard deviation, step by fixed step. From there we diagnose the shape of data — symmetric or skewed — and finish with the fence rule that turns outlier-spotting from a feeling into an exact written argument, which is precisely what the interpretation questions in Paper 2 pay for.
