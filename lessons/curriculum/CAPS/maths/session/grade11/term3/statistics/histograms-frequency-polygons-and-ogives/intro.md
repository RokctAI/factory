Today we take grouped data — the way real-world numbers actually arrive — and master its three pictures: the histogram, the frequency polygon, and the ogive, the cumulative frequency curve that grade eleven adds to your toolkit. One dataset of forty learners' travel times runs through the whole session, so you will see the same numbers become touching bars, a mountain profile, and a lazy-S curve. The payoff is the ogive's two-step reading — across, then down — which hands you medians, quartiles and percentiles straight off a graph, and those readings are guaranteed marks in Paper 2.
