Today we are solving quadratic equations by factorisation — the first and fastest of the three solving methods, and the one the examiners expect whenever an expression will factorise. Everything in this session rests on one principle: a product can only equal zero if one of its factors does, which is why every solution starts by arranging the equation to equal zero. We will drill the four factorising shapes, then push the same principle through equations with fractions, hidden quadratics, and a linear equation paired with a quadratic one. Master this and question one of Paper 1 becomes marks you collect, not marks you fight for.
