Today we open the exponent to fractions, and with that single move the root sign and the power sign become the same instruction written two ways. We will read every fractional exponent as bottom-for-root, top-for-power, push composite bases down to primes, solve equations where the unknown hides in the exponent, and treat surds as exact numbers that must be tidied, rationalised and checked. This topic runs straight through question one of Paper 1, and the marks go to the learner whose method is disciplined: one base before comparing exponents, factorise across a plus sign, and never leave a surd equation unchecked.
