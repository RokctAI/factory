Today we solve trigonometric equations, where one equation carries infinitely many answers because the trig functions repeat forever. The tool that captures them all in a single line is the general solution: a reference angle from the calculator, quadrants to place it, and a counter of full periods to multiply it without end. We will build the formulae for sine, cosine and tangent, harvest the particular answers an interval demands, and crack the equation that is secretly a quadratic in disguise — a package that examiners return to in Paper 2 every single year.
