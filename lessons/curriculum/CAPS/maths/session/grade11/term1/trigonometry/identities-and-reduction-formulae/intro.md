Today we take trigonometry past ninety degrees, and four pieces of equipment do it: the two identities that tie the three ratios together, the CAST rule that signs the four quadrants, the reduction formulae that fold any angle back to an acute one, and the co-function rules that let sine and cosine trade places at ninety degrees. This is the engine room of every trigonometry question in Paper 2 — simplification chains, proofs and no-calculator evaluations all run on these four tools. By the end you will decide every sign from the quadrant, never from guesswork, and collapse a five-line chain to a single ratio.
