Today we move from patterns that grow steadily to patterns that accelerate — quadratic number patterns, whose fingerprint is a constant second difference. You will learn the diagnosis, then build the general term from any such sequence using three facts read straight off the difference table, and run that formula as a machine in both directions: positions to terms, and term values back to positions, where a quadratic equation and the discriminant decide whether a number ever appears at all. We close with the tiled-figure contexts the examiners favour, where the structure of the picture can hand you the formula for free.
