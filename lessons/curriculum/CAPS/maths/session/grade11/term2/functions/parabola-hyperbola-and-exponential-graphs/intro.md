Today we take the three graph families from grade ten — the parabola, the hyperbola and the exponential — and hand each one its full set of controls: the number a that stretches and flips, the p that slides left and right, and the q that slides up and down. We will read turning points and asymptotes straight off the equation, pin down every intercept, and state domain and range with the exceptions that examiners check first. The session closes with the average gradient between two points on a curve — the first honest step towards the calculus of grade twelve.
