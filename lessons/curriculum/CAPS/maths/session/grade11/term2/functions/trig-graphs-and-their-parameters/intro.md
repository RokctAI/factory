Today the sine, cosine and tangent of term one become graphs — waves that repeat forever — and we take hold of the four controls that shape them: a for the height of the swing, q for the middle line, k for how fast the wave cycles, and the horizontal shift that decides where it starts. We will sketch from anchor points, read equations off pictures by averaging the top and bottom, and finish with the combined two-control sketch that examiners set in Paper 1 and Paper 2 alike. The controls are independent, and reading them one at a time is exactly what the marks reward.
