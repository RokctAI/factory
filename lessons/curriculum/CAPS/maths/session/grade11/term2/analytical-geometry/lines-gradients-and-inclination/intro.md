Today we do geometry with coordinates: every point becomes a pair of numbers and every geometric fact becomes a calculation. We consolidate the three instruments from grade ten — distance, midpoint and gradient — then build two new skills on top of them: writing the equation of any straight line from minimal information, and reading a line's angle of inclination straight out of its gradient. Around thirty marks of Paper 2 live in this territory, and they are among the most learnable marks in the paper, because every question reduces to the same short recipes.
