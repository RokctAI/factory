Today we complete the circle toolkit with its two remaining families: the cyclic quadrilateral, whose four corners all sit on one circle, and the tangent, the line that touches the circle exactly once. These supply the theorems that dominate the hardest riders in Paper 2 — opposite angles adding to one eighty, the three converse tests that prove a circle exists, the two equal tangents from a point, and the tangent-chord angle that echoes into the alternate segment. We will prove the theorems, run the standard calculations, and stitch everything into a full rider with a reason on every line.
