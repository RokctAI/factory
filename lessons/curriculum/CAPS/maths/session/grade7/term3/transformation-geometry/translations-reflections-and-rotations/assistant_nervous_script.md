Hey — it's me, your session host. Transformations are really just three moves: slide, flip and turn. If you can do them with a piece of paper, you can do them on a grid.

Slide: move every corner the same number of squares. Flip: count to the mirror line and the same again on the other side. Turn: use tracing paper and a pencil point on the centre.

Do one corner at a time, then join the corners in order. Check by asking if the shape is still the same size.

Go try the practice questions now. Slide, flip, turn. You've got this.
