Hey — it's me, your session host. Similar and congruent sound alike, so it is natural to mix them up at first.

Think of photos. Congruent is an identical copy — same shape, same size. Similar is an enlargement or reduction — same shape, but every side multiplied by the same number. If the sides are multiplied by different numbers, the shape is stretched, and it is neither.

Match the sides, then check the multiplying number for every pair.
