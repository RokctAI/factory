Hey — it's me, your session host. Integer sums are full of minus signs, and it is easy to feel like you are losing track. That is okay — there is a simple way to stay in control.

Picture a number line, or a bank account. Adding a positive moves you up; adding a negative moves you down. To subtract, flip the sign of the second number and add instead. Minus a minus becomes plus.

Put brackets around negative numbers so you can see their signs clearly, and check your answer with real-life sense.
