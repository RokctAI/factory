# Part 1 — Expert

This session covers writing and reading whole numbers in exponential form, aᵇ = a × a × … for b factors, identifying base and exponent, calculating powers with exponents up to 5, and comparing numbers in exponential form. The anchor is 2⁵ = 32.

## Subtopic: Base, Exponent and Power

Exponential form is a short way of writing repeated multiplication of the same number. Two times two times two times two times two is written as two with a small five: two to the power five. The two is the base, the number being multiplied. The five is the exponent, which counts how many times the base appears as a factor. The whole expression is called a power.

So a to the power b means a times a times a and so on, with b factors of a. Three to the power four is three times three times three times three, which is eighty-one. Ten to the power three is one thousand.

Special cases: any number to the power one is itself — seven to the power one is seven. Squares and cubes are just powers with exponents two and three. One to any power is one, because one times one is always one.

Reading powers aloud: two to the power five, or two to the fifth. Five squared is five to the power two. Four cubed is four to the power three.

The exponent is not a multiplier. Two to the power five is NOT two times five. It is thirty-two, not ten.

The questions for this section are ready: name the base and exponent, expand and calculate.

## Subtopic: Calculating Powers

To calculate a power, multiply step by step, keeping a running product. Two to the power five: two, four, eight, sixteen, thirty-two. Each step multiplies the previous result by the base.

Three to the power five: three, nine, twenty-seven, eighty-one, 243. Four to the power four: four, sixteen, sixty-four, 256. Five to the power four: five, twenty-five, 125, 625.

Powers of ten are especially useful: ten to the power one is ten, ten squared is one hundred, ten cubed is one thousand, ten to the fourth is ten thousand, ten to the fifth is one hundred thousand. The exponent equals the number of zeros. This lets us write large numbers neatly: 300 000 is three times ten to the power five.

Back to the message story. On day one, two people get the message. If each person who received it passes it on to two new people the next day, the number of new people on day five is two to the power five, thirty-two. Powers grow very fast: by day ten it would be two to the tenth, 1 024.

## Subtopic: Comparing Numbers in Exponential Form

To compare numbers written as powers, the safest method is to calculate each one.

Compare two to the power five and five squared. Two to the fifth is thirty-two. Five squared is twenty-five. So two to the fifth is larger, even though the digits are the same. Swapping the base and the exponent usually changes the value.

Compare three to the power four and four cubed: eighty-one and sixty-four. Three to the fourth is larger.

When the bases are equal, the larger exponent gives the larger power, provided the base is bigger than one: two to the sixth is bigger than two to the fifth. When the exponents are equal, the larger base gives the larger power: five cubed is bigger than four cubed.

Expressing a number as a power: sixty-four is two to the sixth, four cubed and eight squared. 125 is five cubed. 1 000 is ten cubed. 81 is three to the fourth and nine squared.

# Part 2 — Simplifier

The same 2⁵ now, as a message passed from friend to friend. Same powers, a story you can picture.

## Subtopic: The Message That Doubles

Imagine you send a funny message to two friends. The next day, each of them sends it to two new friends. The day after, each of those sends it to two more. How many new people get it on day five?

Day one: 2. Day two: each of those 2 tells 2, so 4. Day three: 8. Day four: 16. Day five: 32.

Look at what we did: two, times two, times two, times two, times two. Five twos multiplied together. Writing that out is long, so mathematicians write it short: a big 2 with a small 5 up high — two to the power five. The big number is the base — what we multiply. The small number is the exponent — how many times it appears.

It is NOT two times five, which is only ten. By day five the message has already reached thirty-two new people.

Keep going and it explodes: day ten is two to the power ten, 1 024 people! That is why things that double spread so fast.

## Subtopic: Which Power Is Bigger?

Which is bigger: two to the power five or five to the power two? They use the same two digits. Let us find out.

Two to the power five: two, four, eight, sixteen, thirty-two. Five to the power two: five times five, twenty-five. Two to the power five wins, thirty-two against twenty-five!

So you cannot just swap the numbers around — the answer changes. The easiest way to compare powers is to work them out.

Some shortcuts help. If the bases are the same, like two to the six and two to the five, the one with more twos is bigger. If the exponents are the same, like five cubed and four cubed, the one with the bigger base is bigger.

And one number can be written many ways. Sixty-four is two to the six, four cubed and eight squared — all the same number, dressed differently.
