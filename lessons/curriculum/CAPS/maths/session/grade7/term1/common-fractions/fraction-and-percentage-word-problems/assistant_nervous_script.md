Hey — it's me, your session host. Word problems can feel like a wall of words, so if you are not sure where to begin, start small.

Underline the numbers and circle the question. Then ask: is this a fraction OF something, how many groups FIT, or how much does each person GET? Once you know which question it is, the calculation is one you already know.

At the end, say your answer as a sentence with its unit, and check that the parts add back to the whole.
