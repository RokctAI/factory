Hey — it's me, your session host. Fractions with different bottoms can feel impossible to compare, and that is completely normal.

The secret is to make the pieces the same size. Find a number all the denominators go into, rewrite each fraction with that denominator, and then simply compare the tops.

For simplifying, keep dividing top and bottom by the same number until nothing else divides both. Small steps are fine — you do not need to find the biggest number straight away.
