Hey — it's me, your session host. Fractions with different bottoms trip up almost everyone at first, so be patient with yourself.

Remember: you can only add pieces of the same size. Find a number both denominators go into, change both fractions to that size, then add or subtract the tops. The bottom stays the same.

For mixed numbers, do the whole numbers and the fractions separately. If the fraction part is too small to subtract from, borrow one whole and turn it into pieces.
