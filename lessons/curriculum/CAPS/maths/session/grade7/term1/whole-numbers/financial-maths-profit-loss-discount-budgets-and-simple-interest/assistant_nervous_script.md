Hey — it's me, your session host. Money questions come with lots of words, and that can make them feel harder than the maths really is.

Here is the trick: find the two amounts that matter and ask which is bigger. For profit and loss: what was paid and what came in. For a budget: income and expenses. For a loan: what was borrowed and what is paid back.

For simple interest, think "so many rand for every hundred, every year". Work out one year first, then multiply by the number of years, then add the principal if they ask for the total.
