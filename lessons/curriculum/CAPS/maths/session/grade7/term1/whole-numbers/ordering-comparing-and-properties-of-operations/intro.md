Today we put big whole numbers in order and learn the rules that let you calculate them the smart way. One set of numbers runs through the session — the populations of three made-up metro cities, 1 384 000, 1 241 000 and 1 108 000 — and by the end you will compare them by place value, and use the commutative, associative and distributive properties, together with zero and one, to calculate faster and check your own work.
