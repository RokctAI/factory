Today we sharpen the written methods for whole numbers — column addition, subtraction and multiplication, and long division — and wrap each one in an estimate before and a check after. One shopping problem runs through the session: a school buys 38 boxes of exercise books at R247 a box and shares 9 386 books among classes. By the end you will estimate it, calculate it exactly, and check it, using a calculator only to confirm what you already worked out.
