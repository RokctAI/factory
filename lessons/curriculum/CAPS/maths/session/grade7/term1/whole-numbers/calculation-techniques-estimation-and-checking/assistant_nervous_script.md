Hey — it's me, your session host. Long division and long multiplication can feel like a lot of steps where one slip ruins everything. That fear is normal, and there is a cure for it.

Before you calculate, make a rough answer. Round the numbers, do the easy version, and write it down. Then do the real calculation slowly, one column at a time. When you finish, look at your rough answer: if they are close, relax; if they are miles apart, go hunting for the slip.

For long division, just keep the rhythm: divide, multiply, subtract, bring down. Repeat until there is nothing left to bring down. Then multiply back to check.

Go try the practice questions now. Estimate first, every time. You've got this.
