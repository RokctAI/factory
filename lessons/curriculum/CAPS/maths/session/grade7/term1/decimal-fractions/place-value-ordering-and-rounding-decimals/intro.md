Today we count with decimal fractions, read their place values to three decimal places, put them in order and round them off. One sports day runs through the session: three long-jump distances of 3,457 m, 3,5 m and 3,45 m. By the end you will know exactly who jumped furthest, why 3,5 beats 3,457 even though it has fewer digits, and how to round each distance to two decimal places.
