Hey — it's me, your session host. Decimals can feel tricky because the rule "more digits means bigger" suddenly stops working. That is not your fault — it is a real trap.

Here is your safety net: line up the commas and add zeros at the end until every number has the same number of decimal places. Then compare from the left, one place at a time, just like whole numbers.

For rounding, find the place you want, peek at the very next digit, and decide: five or more goes up, four or less stays.
