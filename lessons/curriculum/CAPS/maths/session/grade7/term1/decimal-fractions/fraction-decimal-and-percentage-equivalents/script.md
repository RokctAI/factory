# Part 1 — Expert

This session covers equivalence between common fractions, decimal fractions and percentages: converting fractions to decimals by equivalent fractions or division, decimals to fractions by place value, and both to and from percentages. The anchor is 3/8 = 0,375 = 37,5%.

## Subtopic: Fractions to Decimals

A decimal is a fraction whose denominator is a power of ten: tenths, hundredths, thousandths. So the first way to convert a common fraction to a decimal is to make an equivalent fraction with a denominator of ten, one hundred or one thousand.

Three fifths: multiply top and bottom by two to get six tenths, 0,6. Seven twentieths: multiply by five to get thirty-five hundredths, 0,35. Three eighths: eight goes into one thousand 125 times, so multiply top and bottom by 125: 375 thousandths, 0,375.

The second way works for every fraction: divide the numerator by the denominator. Three divided by eight: eight into three does not go, write zero and a comma; add zeros: eight into thirty goes three, remainder six; into sixty goes seven, remainder four; into forty goes five exactly. 0,375. The same answer.

Some fractions give recurring decimals. One third is 0,333 and so on, two thirds is 0,666 and so on. These cannot be written exactly with a finite number of places, so they are rounded, for example to 0,33 or 0,67.

Worth knowing by heart: one half is 0,5; one quarter is 0,25; three quarters is 0,75; one fifth is 0,2; one eighth is 0,125; one tenth is 0,1.

The questions for this section are ready: equivalent fractions with powers of ten, division and the benchmark facts.

## Subtopic: Decimals to Fractions

To convert a decimal to a common fraction, read its place value and write it over the matching power of ten, then simplify.

0,6 is six tenths: six over ten, which simplifies to three fifths. 0,35 is thirty-five hundredths: thirty-five over one hundred, divide by five, seven twentieths. 0,375 is 375 thousandths: 375 over 1 000, divide by 125, three eighths.

The last digit's place gives the denominator. In 0,375 the last digit, five, is in the thousandths place, so the denominator is one thousand.

Decimals greater than one become mixed numbers: 2,75 is two and seventy-five hundredths, which simplifies to two and three quarters.

A common error is reading 0,375 as 375 over one hundred, or ignoring zeros: 0,05 is five hundredths, one twentieth, not five tenths.

## Subtopic: Converting To and From Percentages

A percentage is a fraction with denominator one hundred, so hundredths are the bridge between all three forms.

Decimal to percentage: multiply by one hundred. 0,375 times one hundred is 37,5, so 37,5 percent. 0,6 is sixty percent; 1,25 is 125 percent.

Percentage to decimal: divide by one hundred. Forty-five percent is 0,45. Seven percent is 0,07 — notice the zero placeholder. 12,5 percent is 0,125.

Fraction to percentage: convert to a decimal, then multiply by one hundred, or make the denominator one hundred directly. Three eighths is 0,375, which is 37,5 percent. Seven twentieths is thirty-five hundredths, thirty-five percent.

Percentage to fraction: write over one hundred and simplify. Forty-five percent is forty-five hundredths, nine twentieths. 37,5 percent is 37,5 over one hundred, which is 375 over one thousand, three eighths.

So three eighths, 0,375 and 37,5 percent are three names for one number. Choose the form that suits the job: fractions for exact parts, decimals for calculating and measuring, percentages for comparing.

# Part 2 — Simplifier

## Subtopic: One Result, Three Names

In a class test, three out of every eight learners got a distinction. A teacher might say this three different ways. "Three eighths of the class." "Zero comma three seven five of the class." "Thirty-seven and a half percent of the class." They all mean the same amount.

How do we get from one to another? Picture a pizza cut into eight slices, and three slices are distinction slices. Now imagine cutting the whole pizza into 1 000 tiny pieces instead. Each eighth is 125 tiny pieces, so three eighths is 375 tiny pieces out of 1 000. That is 0,375 — 375 thousandths.

And percent means out of a hundred. If the pizza were cut into one hundred pieces, three eighths would be 37 and a half of them. So 37,5 percent.

Some you can learn by heart, like friends' names: one half is 0,5 and fifty percent. One quarter is 0,25 and twenty-five percent. Three quarters is 0,75 and seventy-five percent. One tenth is 0,1 and ten percent.

## Subtopic: Switching Between Forms

Here is a simple map for moving between the three forms.

From a percentage to a decimal: percent means out of a hundred, so divide by one hundred — every digit shifts two places smaller. Forty-five percent is 0,45. Seven percent is 0,07 — do not forget the zero in front!

From a decimal to a percentage: do the opposite, multiply by one hundred. 0,6 is sixty percent. 0,375 is 37,5 percent.

From a decimal to a fraction: read it out properly. 0,35 is "thirty-five hundredths", so write thirty-five over one hundred, then simplify to seven twentieths.

From a fraction to a decimal: a fraction is a sharing sum. Three eighths means three shared by eight: 3 divided by 8, which is 0,375.

So whenever you see a number in one form, you can switch it to whichever form makes the question easier. Comparing 0,4, two fifths and forty-five percent? Turn them all into percentages: forty, forty and forty-five. Now it is obvious.
