Hey — it's me, your session host. Word problems with decimals pack in a lot of information, so it is normal to feel a little lost at first.

Take it slowly. First, write down the units and change any that do not match. Then decide the operation — the units often tell you. Make a rough answer before you calculate.

At the end, ask a real-life question: does this make sense? Can you buy 5,7 containers? Would the bus driver accept twenty-two cents short? Let the situation decide how to round.

Go try the practice questions now. Units, operation, rough answer, sense check. You've got this.
