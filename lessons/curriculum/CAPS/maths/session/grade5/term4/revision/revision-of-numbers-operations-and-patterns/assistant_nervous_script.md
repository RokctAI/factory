Hey — if the whole year of number work feels like a lot, here is the calm version: it is one toolbox, and you have used every tool already.

Place value and rounding: look at one digit to the right.

Columns: carry, exchange, and check with the opposite.

Times and divide: split, estimate, and think about the remainder.

Fractions: one part first, and keep the bottom when you add.

Patterns: find the jump, follow the boxes.

Open the practice set and do one of each. You've got this!
