Hey — if all the different clocks feel confusing, here is the calm version: they all show the same moment in different ways.

Analogue clock: short hand for hours, long hand for minutes.

12-hour time: add a.m. for morning or p.m. for afternoon and evening.

24-hour time: after noon, add 12 to the hour.

Going back? Take away 12 and write p.m.

Open the practice set and read one clock at a time. You've got this!
