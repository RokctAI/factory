Hey — if grams and kilograms feel muddled, here is the calm version: there is only one fact to know.

1 kilogram is 1 000 grams.

Kilograms to grams? Times 1 000.

Grams to kilograms? Divide by 1 000.

Half a kilogram is 500 g, and a quarter is 250 g.
