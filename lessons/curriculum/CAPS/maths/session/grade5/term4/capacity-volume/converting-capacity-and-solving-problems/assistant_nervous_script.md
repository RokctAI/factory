Hey — if converting litres and millilitres feels tricky, here is the calm version: there is only one fact to know.

1 litre is 1 000 millilitres.

Litres to millilitres? Times 1 000.

Millilitres to litres? Divide by 1 000.

Half a litre is 500 ml, and a quarter is 250 ml.
