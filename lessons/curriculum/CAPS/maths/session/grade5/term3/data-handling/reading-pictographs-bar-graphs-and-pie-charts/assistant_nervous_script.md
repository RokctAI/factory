Hey — if graphs look like a puzzle, here is the calm version: read the labels first, and the numbers will make sense.

Pictograph? Find the key and multiply.

Bar graph? Slide across from the top of the bar to the scale.

Pie chart? Each slice is a fraction of the whole.

Something looks too big or too small? Check where the scale starts.

Open the practice set and read the key first. You've got this!
