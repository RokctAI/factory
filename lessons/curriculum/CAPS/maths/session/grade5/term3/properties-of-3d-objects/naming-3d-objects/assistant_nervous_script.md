Hey — if 3D shapes all look like boxes and tins, here is the calm version: look at the faces and the name follows.

Two matching ends joined by rectangles? A prism, named by its ends.

All faces equal squares? A cube.

Two circles and a curved side? A cylinder.

One circle and a point? A cone.

One base and triangles meeting at a point? A pyramid.

Open the practice set and check the faces first. You've got this!
