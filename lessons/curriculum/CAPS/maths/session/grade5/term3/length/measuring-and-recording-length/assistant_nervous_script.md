Hey — if measuring feels fiddly, here is the calm version: pick a tool, start at zero, read the mark.

Tiny or short? Ruler. Longer? Metre stick or tape measure. Very long? Trundle wheel.

Guess first, so you know if your answer is silly.

Write the unit every time.

To compare, look at the metres first, then the centimetres.
