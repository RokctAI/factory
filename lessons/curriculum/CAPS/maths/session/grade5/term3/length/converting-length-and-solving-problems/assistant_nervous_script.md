Hey — if converting units feels like a muddle, here is the calm version: there are only three facts to know.

10 mm is 1 cm. 100 cm is 1 m. 1 000 m is 1 km.

Going to a smaller unit? The number gets bigger, so multiply.

Going to a bigger unit? The number gets smaller, so divide.

In a problem, change everything to one unit first.

Open the practice set and convert before you calculate. You've got this!
