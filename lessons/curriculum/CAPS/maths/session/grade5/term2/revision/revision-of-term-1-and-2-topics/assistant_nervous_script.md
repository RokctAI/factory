Hey — if two whole terms feel like too much to hold at once, here is the calm version: it is one toolbox, and you already own every tool in it.

Whole numbers: line up, carry, exchange, and check with the opposite operation.

Division: think of the times table, and ask what the remainder means.

Fractions: keep the bottom when you add, and find one part first for fractions of amounts.

Patterns: look at the jumps. Flow diagrams: follow the boxes, or undo them in reverse.

Shapes: count the straight sides. Symmetry: fold in your mind.

Open the practice set and do one of each. You've got this!
