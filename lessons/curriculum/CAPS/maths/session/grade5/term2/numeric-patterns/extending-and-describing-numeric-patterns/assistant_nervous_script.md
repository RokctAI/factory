Hey — if number patterns feel like a guessing game, here is the calm version: look at two neighbours and ask what happened to get from one to the next.

Same amount added or taken away every time? That is a constant difference.

Multiplied or divided by the same number every time? That is a constant ratio.

Check at least three numbers before you decide.

Then say the rule out loud: where it starts and what happens each time.
