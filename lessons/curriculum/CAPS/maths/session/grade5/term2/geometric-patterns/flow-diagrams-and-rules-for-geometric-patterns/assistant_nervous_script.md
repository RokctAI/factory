Hey — if flow diagrams look like a maze of boxes and arrows, here is the calm version: a number goes in, you do each box in order, and a number comes out.

Going forwards? Follow the arrows.

Going backwards? Start at the end and undo each box: minus undoes plus, and divided by undoes times.

Finding the rule? See how much the outputs jump, then fix the start.

Not sure two rules match? Put the same number into both.

Open the practice set and follow the arrows one box at a time. You've got this!
