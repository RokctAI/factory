Hey — if patterns look like a jumble of shapes, here is the calm version: count, write the numbers down, and look at how they change.

Same jump every time? That is your rule: add the jump.

Jumps getting bigger? Look at the jumps themselves. They often make their own pattern.

Doubling? Each shape is twice the one before.

To jump far ahead, find a rule that uses the shape number.
