Hey — if sorting shapes feels like a messy pile, here is the calm version: pick one question and ask it of every shape.

Straight sides, curved sides, or both?

How many sides? Ignore the size and the colour.

Drawing on grid paper? Count the little squares along each side, not the lines.

Finished? Check that the shape is closed and count its sides.

Open the practice set and sort one shape at a time. You've got this!
