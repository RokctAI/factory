Hey — if equivalent fractions sound complicated, here is the calm version: you are just cutting the same cake into smaller pieces.

Half a cake is 2 quarters, and it is 4 eighths. Same cake.

To make an equivalent fraction, multiply the top and the bottom by the same number.

To simplify, divide the top and the bottom by the same number.

To compare two fractions, give them the same bottom number first.

Open the practice set and cut your pieces. You've got this!
