Hey — if adding fractions feels tricky, here is the calm version: with the same bottom number, you are just counting pieces of the same size.

Add or take away the top numbers. Keep the bottom number.

A whole is all the pieces: 12/12 for a pizza cut into 12.

Too many pieces? Turn them into a whole. 6/4 is 1 and 2/4.

Too few pieces to take away? Break a whole into pieces first.

Open the practice set and count your pieces. You've got this!
