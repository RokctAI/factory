Hey — if fractions feel slippery, here is the calm version: the bottom number is how many pieces, the top number is how many you have.

Counting in fractions is just counting pieces: 1/4, 2/4, 3/4, 4/4. And 4/4 is one whole.

Same bottom number? The bigger top wins.

Same top number? The smaller bottom wins, because fewer pieces means bigger pieces.

Half the egg box is 6 out of 12. Use a half to help you compare.

Open the practice set and picture the pieces. You've got this!
