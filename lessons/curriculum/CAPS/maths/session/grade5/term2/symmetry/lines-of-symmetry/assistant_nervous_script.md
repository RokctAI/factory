Hey — if symmetry seems tricky, here is the calm version: imagine folding the shape and ask whether the two halves land exactly on top of each other.

Yes? That fold is a line of symmetry.

Something sticks out? Not a line of symmetry.

Counting lines? Try every direction: up and down, across, and corner to corner.

Completing a drawing? Each corner gets a partner, the same number of squares away on the other side.

Open the practice set and fold in your mind. You've got this!
