Hey — if dividing by a two-digit number feels like too much, here is the calm version: you are only asking how many groups fit, and you can take big bites.

First bite: try 10 groups or 30 groups, whatever fits.

See what is left, and take another bite.

Add up the bites. That is your answer.

If a little is left over that is smaller than one group, that is the remainder.

Then multiply back to check.

Open the practice set and take big bites. You've got this!
