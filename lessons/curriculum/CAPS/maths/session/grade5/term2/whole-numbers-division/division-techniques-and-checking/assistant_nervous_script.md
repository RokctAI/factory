Hey — if long divisions make you nervous, here is the calm version: change the numbers until the division is easy, then multiply back.

Guess the size first with friendly numbers: 800 divided by 20 is 40.

Split the big number into parts that share easily, or build up with the times table of the small number.

Halve both numbers, or double both. The answer stays the same.

Then multiply your answer by the divisor. If you get back to the start, you are right.

Open the practice set and use two techniques on every division. You've got this!
