Today we pull the whole of Term 1 together before the test. We revisit six-digit numbers, place value and rounding, number sentences and the properties of operations, adding and subtracting big numbers, multiplying three-digit by two-digit numbers, multiples and factors, and ratio and rate. One running story, a school market day in Upington, shows every skill where it is actually used, and we practise the checking habits that turn a good answer into a sure one.
