Hey — if the whole term feels like too much to hold at once, here is the calm version: it is one toolbox, and you already own every tool in it.

Numbers: seats, comparing from the left, rounding by one digit.

Sentences: write the story, box the gap, undo to solve, put it back to check.

Adding and subtracting: line up, carry, exchange, add back to check.

Multiplying: split, multiply every part, add, estimate.

Factors in pairs, multiples go up, ratio multiplies both parts, per means for each.

Open the practice set and work through one of each. You've got this!
