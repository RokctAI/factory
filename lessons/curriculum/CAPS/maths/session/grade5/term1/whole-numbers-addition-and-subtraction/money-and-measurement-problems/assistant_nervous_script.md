Hey — if word problems about money and measuring make you freeze, here is the calm version: every problem walks the same five steps.

One: what is the story about, and what is the question?

Two: write a number sentence.

Three: work it out.

Four: check it, with the opposite operation or by asking if the answer makes sense.

Five: answer in a sentence, with rands, kilometres, kilograms or litres.

If there are two steps, the first answer is just a stepping stone.

Open the practice set and walk the five steps. You've got this!
