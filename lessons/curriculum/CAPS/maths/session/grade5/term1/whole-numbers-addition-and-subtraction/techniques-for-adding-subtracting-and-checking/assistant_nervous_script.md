Hey — if a sum feels too big to hold in your head, here is the calm version: make it smaller before you start.

Split it: thousands with thousands, hundreds with hundreds.

Round it: 9 998 is nearly 10 000. Add 10 000, then fix the extra 2.

Jump it: count up on a number line to a round number, then to the end, and add the jumps.

Then check it a different way. The opposite operation is the best check.

Open the practice set and use two techniques on every sum. You've got this!
