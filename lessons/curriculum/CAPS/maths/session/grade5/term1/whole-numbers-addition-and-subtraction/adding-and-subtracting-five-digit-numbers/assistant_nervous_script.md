Hey — if five-digit sums look like a wall of numbers, here is the calm version: you only ever deal with one column at a time.

Line the numbers up on the right. Start with the units.

Adding: if a column makes more than 9, carry the extra ten to the next column.

Subtracting: if the top digit is too small, exchange 1 from the next column.

Lots of zeros? Take away from 49 999 instead of 50 000, then add 1.

Then check: estimate for adding, and add back for subtracting.

Open the practice set and go column by column. You've got this!
