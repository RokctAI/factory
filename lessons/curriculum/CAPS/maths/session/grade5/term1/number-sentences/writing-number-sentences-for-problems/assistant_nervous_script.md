Hey — if word problems make your mind go blank, here is the calm version: you do not have to solve anything yet, you only have to write the story in numbers.

Ask what is happening. Putting together means add. Finding how many more or how many left means subtract. Equal groups mean multiply. Sharing or grouping means divide.

Put a box where the missing number is.

If there are two steps, put brackets around the first one.

Then read your sentence back as a story. If it matches, you are ready to solve.

Open the practice set and write the sentence first every time. You've got this!
