Hey — if the long words commutative, associative and distributive sound scary, here is the calm version: they are just three ways of making a sum easier.

Swap: for plus and times, the order does not matter.

Group: for plus and times, you can choose which pair to do first.

Split: break a number into parts, multiply each part, then add.

Never swap or regroup minus or divide.

And two special numbers: plus 0 changes nothing, times 1 changes nothing.
