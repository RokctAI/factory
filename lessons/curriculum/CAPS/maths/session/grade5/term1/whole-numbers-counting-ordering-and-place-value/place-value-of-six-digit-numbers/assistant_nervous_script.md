Hey — if a big number feels like a jumble of digits, here is the calm version: every digit has a seat, and the seat tells you what it is worth.

Six seats from the right: units, tens, hundreds, thousands, ten thousands, hundred thousands.

Each seat is worth ten times the one on its right. That is the whole secret.

Breaking a number up is just saying what each seat holds. Building it back is putting each part in its seat, with a zero in any empty seat.

For the biggest number, big digits go on the left. For the smallest, small digits go on the left, but never a zero first.

Open the practice set and give every digit its seat. You've got this!
