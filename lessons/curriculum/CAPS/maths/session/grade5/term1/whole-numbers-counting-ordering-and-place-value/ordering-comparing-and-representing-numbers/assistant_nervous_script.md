Hey — if six-digit numbers look like a long train of digits, here is the calm version: split the train into two carriages of three.

The left carriage is the thousands. The right carriage is the rest. 245 318 is 245 thousand and 318.

Empty seats get a zero. Three hundred thousand and six is 300 006.

To compare, count the digits first. If they match, start on the left and move right until two digits are different. The bigger digit wins.

Ascending goes up, descending comes down.

Open the practice set and split every number into its carriages. You've got this!
