Hey — if rounding to the nearest 5 sounds strange, here is the calm version: numbers that are multiples of 5 end in 0 or 5, so you just pick the closest one.

21 367 is between 21 365 and 21 370. It is closer to 21 365. Done.

For tens, hundreds and thousands, look at the digit just to the right of the place you want. 0 to 4 goes down. 5 to 9 goes up.

Round the number you were given, once. Do not round and round again.

Then use your rounded numbers to guess an answer before you work it out, so you can spot a slip.
