Hey — if ratio and rate sound like new monsters, here is the calm version: they are both just multiplying.

Equal groups? Multiply. 36 bags at R95 is 36 times 95.

Ratio compares the same kind, like buckets to buckets. To make more, multiply both parts by the same number. 1 to 3 becomes 12 to 36.

Rate compares different kinds, like rands per kilogram. Per means for each. R45 per kilogram for 12 kilograms is 12 times 45.

Write the units and the answer will tell you if it makes sense.
