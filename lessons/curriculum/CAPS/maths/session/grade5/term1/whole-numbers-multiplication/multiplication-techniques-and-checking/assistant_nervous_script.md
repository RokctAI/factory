Hey — if multiplying in your head feels impossible, here is the calm version: change the numbers into friendlier ones first.

Double one number and halve the other. 16 times 25 becomes 4 times 100.

Or split a number. 48 times 15 is 48 times 10 plus 48 times 5.

Or round it. R199 is nearly R200. Then take off the extra for every ticket.

Then check another way. Guess the size first, and divide the answer to undo it.

Open the practice set and use two techniques on every product. You've got this!
