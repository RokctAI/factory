Hey — if multiples and factors blur together, here is the calm version: multiples go up, factors go in.

Multiples go up the times table: 12, 24, 36, 48.

Factors go into a number exactly, and they come in pairs: 48 is 6 times 8, so 6 and 8 are factors of 48.

To find all the factors, start at 1 and pair up until the pairs meet.

To find when two things match, skip-count both until you hit the same number.
