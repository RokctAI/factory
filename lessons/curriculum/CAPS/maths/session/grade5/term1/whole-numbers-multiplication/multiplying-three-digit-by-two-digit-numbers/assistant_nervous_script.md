Hey — if multiplying big numbers feels like guesswork, here is the calm version: you only ever multiply small pieces, then add.

Split both numbers by place value. 345 is 300, 40 and 5. 28 is 20 and 8.

Multiply every piece by every piece. That is six small multiplications.

Add them up.

In columns, that becomes two rows: one for the units, one for the tens with a 0 first.

Then estimate. 300 times 30 is 9 000, so 9 660 makes sense.

Open the practice set and split every number. You've got this!
