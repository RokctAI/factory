Today we are learning the fundamental counting principle — the engine underneath all of Grade 12 probability and a reliable source of marks in Paper 1. When outcomes are too many to list, this principle counts them without listing: choices along a sequence multiply, and probability becomes one count divided by another. We will build the slot method, master factorial arrangements with restrictions — forced positions, glued pairs, and counting the complement — handle codes with and without repetition, and finish by turning every count into a probability.
