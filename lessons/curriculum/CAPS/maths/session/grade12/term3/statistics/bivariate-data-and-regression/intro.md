Today we are working with bivariate data and regression — statistics done on pairs, where two measurements taken on the same individual either move together or do not. This topic is dependable Paper 2 territory: read a scatterplot for direction, form and strength, fit the least squares regression line with the calculator, interpret its gradient in context, and quote the correlation coefficient with the right words attached. We will finish with the discipline of prediction — interpolating inside the data range, flagging extrapolation as unreliable, and refusing to turn correlation into causation.
