Today we are completing the growth and decay toolkit — the four formulae for simple and compound growth and decay, and then the two questions Grade 11 could not answer: finding the rate, and finding the time. Solving for time is the true novelty of the year, because an unknown sitting in the exponent calls for logarithms — this is the reason logarithms are in the syllabus at all. We will drill the formulae on one timeline, un-multiply our way to hidden depreciation rates, ask how long money takes to double, and finish with the timeline technique that tames accounts where rates change and deposits arrive midstream.
