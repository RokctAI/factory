Today we are working with annuities — streams of equal payments moving through compound interest, the mathematics behind every savings plan, car loan, and sinking fund, and a certainty in Paper 1. An annuity is geometric series from term one cashing its cheque: line the payments up, ask each one how long it earned, and the sum formula delivers both the future value of a saving stream and the present value of a loan. We will derive both formulas, price a car loan honestly, build a sinking fund with three rates doing three different jobs, and finish with the outstanding balance calculation that shows why a half-paid loan is nowhere near half settled.
