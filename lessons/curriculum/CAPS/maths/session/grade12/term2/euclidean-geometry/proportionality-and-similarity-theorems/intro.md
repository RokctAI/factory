Today we are studying the proportionality and similarity theorems — the heart of Grade 12 Euclidean geometry and a guaranteed presence in Paper 2, where the theorem proofs themselves are examinable. One parallel line cutting a triangle forces the sides into proportion, and from that single fact we will build the two similarity theorems and finish by proving the Theorem of Pythagoras using nothing but similar triangles. We will work the area-based proof line by line, drill the ratio technique that keeps part-to-part separate from part-to-whole, and lock down the letter-ordering discipline that turns similarity statements into correct ratios every time.
