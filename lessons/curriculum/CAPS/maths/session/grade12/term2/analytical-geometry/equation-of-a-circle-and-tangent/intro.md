Today analytical geometry gains its one curved character: the circle, and the tangent line that touches it at exactly one point. The circle's equation is nothing more than the distance formula holding a promise, and the whole tangent story runs on a single geometric fact — the radius to the touching point stands perpendicular to the tangent. We will read centre and radius straight from the equation, complete the square to unmask a disguised circle, and build the four-step routine that delivers any tangent's equation, all of it standard Paper 2 territory.
