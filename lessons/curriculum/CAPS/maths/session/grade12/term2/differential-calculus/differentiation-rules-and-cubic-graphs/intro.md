Today differentiation stops being handwork and becomes machinery: the power rule differentiates any power of x in one line, and with it the cubic function — the star graph of Grade twelve — opens up completely. Calculus is the biggest single block of marks in Paper 1, and the cubic sketch with its turning points is its centrepiece. We will drill the rule and the rewriting it demands, build the tangent routine, sketch a full cubic from its stationary points and intercepts, and learn to read a function's whole story through its first and second derivatives.
