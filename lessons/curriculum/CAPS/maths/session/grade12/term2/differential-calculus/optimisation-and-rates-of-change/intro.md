Today calculus stops being about sketching and becomes what it is for: measuring how fast things change, and finding the best possible value of anything you can write as a formula. Rates of change and optimisation carry the final, highest-mark questions of the calculus section in Paper 1 — the moving particle that comes to rest, the biggest camp a roll of fence can hold, the cheapest box that still holds enough. We will build the motion chain from position to velocity to acceleration, master the four-step optimisation recipe, work a full volume-and-material problem, and learn the judgement that turns a calculus answer into a real-world one.
