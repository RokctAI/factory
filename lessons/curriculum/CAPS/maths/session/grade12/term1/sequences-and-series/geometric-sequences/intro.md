Today we meet the arithmetic sequence's multiplying sibling: the geometric sequence, where every term is the one before it times a fixed ratio. This is the mathematics behind compound interest, population growth and radioactive decay, and it carries guaranteed marks in the sequences section of Paper 1. We will certify a sequence with the constant ratio test, build the general term with its exponent, handle geometric means and unknowns hiding inside the terms, and finish with the modelling questions where multiplication runs the world.
