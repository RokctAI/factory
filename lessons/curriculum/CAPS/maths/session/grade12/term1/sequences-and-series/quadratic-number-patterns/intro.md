Today we revise the quadratic number pattern — the sequence whose second difference is constant — because Paper 1 puts it at the front of the sequences section almost every year. The difference table is the whole diagnosis: constant first difference means linear, constant second difference means quadratic, and then three short equations recover a, b and c in T n equals a n squared plus b n plus c. We will build general terms, jump straight to term fifty, solve backwards for the position that holds a given value, flush out an unknown hiding inside the terms, and find the exact term where a falling pattern bottoms out.
