Today we arm the simplest number pattern of all: the arithmetic sequence, where every term climbs by the same fixed difference. Sequences and series open Paper 1 in Grade twelve, and the general term T n equals a plus n minus one times d is the machine behind almost every question in that section. We will certify a sequence with the constant difference test, jump straight to term two hundred, solve for unknowns hiding inside the terms, insert arithmetic means, and turn word problems about salaries and fares into term numbers.
