Today we take on the triangles of the real world — survey lines, towers and fields that are never right-angled — with the sine rule, the cosine rule and the area rule. Grade twelve pushes these three rules into three dimensions, where every examination question is secretly two flat triangles sharing one edge. We will build the decision procedure that chooses the right rule from the data you are given, work each rule through a full example, and finish with the two-triangle strategy that turns any 3D problem into bookkeeping.
