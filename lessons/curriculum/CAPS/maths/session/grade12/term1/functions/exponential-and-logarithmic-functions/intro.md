Today the inverse machinery meets the one graph ordinary algebra cannot untangle: the exponential. Inverting y equals b to the power x demands a brand-new word — the logarithm — and this session is where that word is defined, graphed and put to work. By the end you will hold four pieces of equipment: the exponential graph with its laws and asymptote, the definition of a logarithm as a rewritten exponential, the log graph as its mirror twin across the line y equals x, and the skill the finance chapters depend on — digging an unknown out of the exponent.
