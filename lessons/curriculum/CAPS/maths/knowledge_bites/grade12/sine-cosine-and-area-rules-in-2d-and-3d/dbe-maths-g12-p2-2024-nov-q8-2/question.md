## Question (7 marks)

Calculate how far the painter at E is from the top of the building.

## Method

cosine rule then right triangle

## Memo working

Cosine rule in ABC: 19^2 = x^2 + 15^2 - 2x(15)cos 105,61° (x = BC); x^2 + 8,07x - 136 = 0; x = (-8,07 + sqrt(8,07^2 - 4(1)(-136)))/2 = 8,30 m (negative root rejected); BÊC = 180° - 122° = 58° (or EB̂C = 32°); EC = 8,3/tan 58° = 5,19 m; DE = 16 - 5,19 = 10,81 m

(Memo note: Alternative memo method finds BC with the sine rule (CBA = 49,5°, BAC = 24,89°, BC = 8,3 m), then proceeds identically.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

DE = 10,81 m
