## Question (4 marks)

Which circle has a diameter of 3/128 cm?

## Method

geometric sequence solve n

## Memo working

radius = (1/2)(3/128) = 3/256; 3/256 = 6(1/2)^(n-1); (1/512) = (1/2)^(n-1); (1/2)^9 = (1/2)^(n-1); n - 1 = 9; n = 10

(Memo note: Alternative memo method works with diameters 12 ; 6 ; 3 ; ... and 3/128 = 12(1/2)^(n-1).)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

n = 10 (the 10th circle)
