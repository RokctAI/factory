## Question (4 marks)

Which circle (in the pattern with radii 6 ; 3 ; 1,5 ; ... cm, each half the previous) has a diameter of 3/128 cm?

## Method

geometric sequence solve n

## Memo working

A diameter of 3/128 means a radius of (1/2)(3/128) = 3/256. Set the general term equal to it: 6(1/2)^(n-1) = 3/256, so (1/2)^(n-1) = 1/512 = (1/2)^9. Equate exponents: n - 1 = 9 -> n = 10. (Memo alternative: work with diameters 12 ; 6 ; 3 ; ... and solve 3/128 = 12(1/2)^(n-1).)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

n = 10 (the 10th circle)
