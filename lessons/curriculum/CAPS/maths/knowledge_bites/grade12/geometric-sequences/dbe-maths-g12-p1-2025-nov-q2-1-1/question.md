## Question (3 marks)

Given the infinite geometric series: (t + 10) + (t - 2) + (t + 4) + ... Show that t = -2

## Method

equate common ratios

## Memo working

(t - 2)/(t + 10) = (t + 4)/(t - 2); cross multiply: (t - 2)^2 = (t + 4)(t + 10); t^2 - 4t + 4 = t^2 + 14t + 40; -18t = 36; t = -2

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

t = -2
