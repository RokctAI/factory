## Question (3 marks)

Given the infinite geometric series: (t + 10) + (t - 2) + (t + 4) + ... Show that t = -2

## Method

equate common ratios

## Memo working

In a geometric series the ratio between consecutive terms is constant, so (t - 2)/(t + 10) = (t + 4)/(t - 2). Cross multiply: (t - 2)^2 = (t + 4)(t + 10). Expand both sides: t^2 - 4t + 4 = t^2 + 14t + 40. The t^2 terms cancel, leaving -18t = 36, so t = -2. (With t = -2 the series is 8 - 4 + 2 - ..., a genuine geometric series with r = -1/2.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

t = -2
