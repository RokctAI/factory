## Question (2 marks)

A circle with radius 6 cm is drawn. A second, smaller circle is drawn through the centre of the first circle and also touches the first circle internally, as shown in the diagram. A third, smaller circle is drawn through the centre of the second circle and touches the second circle internally. The process of drawing circles continues and forms a geometric pattern. [Figure: three nested circles, each smaller circle passing through the centre of the previous circle and touching it internally at the top.] Write down the radius of the 3rd circle.

## Method

geometric sequence term

## Memo working

2nd circle: 6/2 = 3 cm; 3rd circle: 3/2 = 1,5 cm  (or 6(1/2)^2 = 1,5)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

1,5 cm
