## Question (3 marks)

Calculate the value of T25. Write your answer in the form Tn = b^x  (For the geometric series of Q2.1.1 with t = -2, i.e. 8 - 4 + 2 - ...)

## Method

geometric term exponential form

## Memo working

With t = -2 the series is 8 ; -4 ; 2 ; ... so a = 8 and r = -4/8 = -1/2. The general term is Tn = a·r^(n-1), so T25 = 8(-1/2)^24. Since 24 is even, (-1/2)^24 = (1/2)^24, and 8 = 2^3, so T25 = 2^3 · 2^(-24) = 2^(-21) = (1/2)^21.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

T25 = (1/2)^21  OR  T25 = 2^(-21)  OR  T25 = 8^(-7)  OR  T25 = 128^(-3)  OR  T25 = (2 097 152)^(-1)
