## Question (3 marks)

Calculate the value of T25. Write your answer in the form Tn = b^x

## Method

geometric term exponential form

## Memo working

Series is 8 ; -4 ; ... with r = -4/8 = -1/2; T25 = 8(-1/2)^24 = (1/2)^21

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

T25 = (1/2)^21  OR  T25 = 2^(-21)  OR  T25 = 8^(-7)  OR  T25 = 128^(-3)  OR  T25 = (2 097 152)^(-1)
