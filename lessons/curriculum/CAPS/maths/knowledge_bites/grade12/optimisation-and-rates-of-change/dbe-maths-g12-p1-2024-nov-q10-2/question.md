## Question (5 marks)

Calculate the distance between town P and town T.

## Method

antiderivative of rate

## Memo working

-3t^2 + 18t = 0; -3t(t - 6) = 0; t = 0 or t = 6; s(t) = at^3 + bt^2 with s'(t) = 3at^2 + 2bt: 3a = -3, 2b = 18 -> a = -1, b = 9; s(t) = -t^3 + 9t^2; s(6) = -(6)^3 + 9(6)^2 = 108

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

108 km
