## Question (3 marks)

A rectangular metal sheet has dimensions x and h units, with x > h, and a perimeter of 50 units. The metal sheet is rolled into a cylinder with two open ends (top and bottom) and height h units. (Figure: rectangle with width x and height h alongside an open cylinder of height h formed by rolling the sheet.) Show that the volume of the cylinder is given by V = 25x^2/(4π) - x^3/(4π)

## Method

build volume function

## Memo working

From the perimeter: 2x + 2h = 50, so h = 25 - x. When the sheet is rolled up, the side of length x becomes the circumference of the circular end: 2πr = x, so r = x/(2π). Substitute into the cylinder volume formula: V = πr^2 h = π(x/(2π))^2 (25 - x) = x^2(25 - x)/(4π) = 25x^2/(4π) - x^3/(4π).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

V = 25x^2/(4π) - x^3/(4π)
