## Question (3 marks)

A cyclist rode from town P and stopped at town T. The speed (in km/h) at which this cyclist rode, is represented by the equation s'(t) = -3t^2 + 18t. NOTE: Speed is the rate of change in distance with respect to time. Calculate the maximum speed that the cyclist reached on this ride.

## Method

optimisation set derivative zero

## Memo working

-6t + 18 = 0; t = 3; s'(3) = -3(3)^2 + 18(3) = 27

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

27 km/h
