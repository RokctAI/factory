## Question (1 mark)

If the circle is translated 2 units to the left, determine the minimum distance between the circle and the y-axis.

## Method

distance centre to axis minus radius

## Memo working

New centre (-8 ; 4); distance to y-axis 8 minus radius 5 = 3 units

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

3 units
