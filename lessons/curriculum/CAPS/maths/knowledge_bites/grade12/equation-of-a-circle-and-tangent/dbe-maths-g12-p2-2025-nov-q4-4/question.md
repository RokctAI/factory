## Question (1 mark)

If the circle is translated 2 units to the left, determine the minimum distance between the circle and the y-axis. (From QUESTION 4.3: the circle has centre M(-6 ; 4) and radius 5.)

## Method

distance centre to axis minus radius

## Memo working

Shifting 2 units left moves the centre to (-8 ; 4). The centre is then 8 units from the y-axis, and the nearest point of the circle sits one radius closer: 8 - 5 = 3 units.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

3 units
