## Question (4 marks)

Triangle ACD has vertices A, D(3 ; -9) and C(-9 ; 0), where A is a point in the second quadrant. B(-1 ; k) lies on side DC. (In the diagram, A lies above the x-axis in the second quadrant, C(-9 ; 0) is on the x-axis, D(3 ; -9) is below the x-axis, and B(-1 ; k) is marked on line DC between C and D.) If M is a point on AD such that AC || MB, calculate the ratio of Area triangle MBD / Area triangle ACD. (DB/DC = 1/3.)

## Method

area ratio with proportionality

## Memo working

Because MB || AC, the proportionality theorem in triangle ACD gives DM/DA = DB/DC = 1/3. Both triangles share the angle at D, so using the area rule: Area ΔMBD / Area ΔACD = [1/2 (DM)(DB) sin D] / [1/2 (DA)(DC) sin D] = (DM/DA)(DB/DC) = (1/3)(1/3) = 1/9.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Area ΔMBD / Area ΔACD = 1/9
