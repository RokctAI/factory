## Question (4 marks)

If M is a point on AD such that AC || MB, calculate the ratio of Area triangle MBD / Area triangle ACD.

## Method

area ratio with proportionality

## Memo working

DM/DA = DB/DC = 1/3 (prop theorem, MB || AC); Area ΔMBD / Area ΔACD = [½(DM)(DB)sin D] / [½(DA)(DC)sin D] = (1/3)(1/3) = 1/9

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Area ΔMBD / Area ΔACD = 1/9
