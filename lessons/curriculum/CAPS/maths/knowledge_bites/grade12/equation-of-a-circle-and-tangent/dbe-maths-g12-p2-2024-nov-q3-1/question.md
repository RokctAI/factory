## Question (2 marks)

In the diagram below, triangle ACD has vertices A, D(3 ; -9) and C(-9 ; 0), where A is a point in the second quadrant. B(-1 ; k) lies on side DC. [Figure: triangle with A above the x-axis in the second quadrant, C(-9 ; 0) on the x-axis, D(3 ; -9) below the x-axis; B(-1 ; k) marked on line DC between C and D.] Calculate the gradient of DC.

## Method

gradient formula

## Memo working

m_DC = (-9 - 0)/(3 - (-9)) = -3/4

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

m_DC = -3/4
