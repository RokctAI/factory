## Question (3 marks)

Calculate the area of parallelogram PQRS.

## Method

base times height

## Memo working

PT = sqrt((4 - (-1))^2 + (-2 - 8)^2) = √125 = 5√5 = 11,18 units; area PQRS = QR·PT = (8√5)(5√5) = 200 units^2

(Memo note: Alternative memo method: area ΔPQR = (1/2)(8√5)(5√5) = 100 units^2; area PQRS = 2 × 100 = 200 units^2.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

200 units^2
