## Question (4 marks)

Calculate the size of AĈB. (From QUESTION 4: tangents from C(-6 ; -9/4) touch the circle at A(-9 ; 0) and B(-3 ; 0); tangent CB has gradient 3/4.)

## Method

angle between lines from gradients

## Memo working

Use angles of inclination. For CB: tan β = 3/4 gives β = 36,87°. By symmetry the other tangent CA has gradient m_AC = -3/4, so tan α = -3/4 -> α = 180° - 36,87° = 143,13°. The angle between the tangents is the difference of the inclinations: AĈB = 143,13° - 36,87° = 106,26°. (Memo alternatives: (1) tan MÂB = 4/3 -> MÂB = 53,13°, AM̂D = 36,87°, MÂC = 90° since a tangent is perpendicular to the radius, AĈM = 53,13°, and AĈB = 106,26° by the kite's symmetry; (2) tan AĈD = 3/(9/4) = 4/3 -> AĈD = 53,13°, AĈB = 106,26°; (3) cosine rule with AC = BC = 15/4 and AB = 6: cos AĈB = -0,28 -> 106,26°; (4) AMBC is a cyclic quadrilateral, MĈB = 53,13° by angles in the same segment, AĈB = 106,26°.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

AĈB = 106,26°
