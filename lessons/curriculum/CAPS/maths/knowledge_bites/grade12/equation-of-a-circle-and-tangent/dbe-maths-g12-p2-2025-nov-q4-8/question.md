## Question (4 marks)

Calculate the size of AĈB.

## Method

angle between lines from gradients

## Memo working

tan β = 3/4 (inclination of CB); m_AC = -3/4; tan α = -3/4 -> α = 180° - 36,87° = 143,13°; AĈB = 143,13° - 36,87° = 106,26°

(Memo note: Alternative memo methods: (1) tan MÂB = 4/3 -> MÂB = 53,13°, AM̂D = 36,87°, MÂC = 90° (tangent ⊥ radius), AĈM = 53,13°, AĈB = 106,26° (property of kite); (2) tan AĈD = 3/(9/4) = 4/3 -> AĈD = 53,13°, AĈB = 106,26°; (3) cosine rule with AC = BC = 15/4 and AB = 6: cos AĈB = -0,28 -> 106,26°; (4) AMBC cyclic quad, MĈB = 53,13° (angles in same segment), AĈB = 106,26°.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

AĈB = 106,26°
