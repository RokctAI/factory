## Question (4 marks)

M(1 ; 3) is the centre of the circle. The circle cuts the x-axis at N. ST is a tangent to the circle at P(3 ; 9). R(d ; 1), with d > 0, and L lie on the circle. O and V are the x-intercepts of PL and RL respectively. (In the diagram, P(3 ; 9) is at the top right of the circle, R(d ; 1) on the right and L at the bottom left near the y-axis; the tangent ST touches the circle at P; chord PL passes through the centre M and cuts the x-axis at O, the origin; chord RL cuts the x-axis at V; N lies on the x-axis to the right of V.) Show that the equation of the circle with centre M is x^2 + y^2 - 2x - 6y - 30 = 0.

## Method

circle equation expand

## Memo working

Centre-radius form: (x - 1)^2 + (y - 3)^2 = r^2. Since P(3 ; 9) lies on the circle, r^2 = (3 - 1)^2 + (9 - 3)^2 = 4 + 36 = 40. Expanding: x^2 - 2x + 1 + y^2 - 6y + 9 = 40, which simplifies to x^2 + y^2 - 2x - 6y - 30 = 0.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x^2 + y^2 - 2x - 6y - 30 = 0
