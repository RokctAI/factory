## Question (4 marks)

Show that the equation of the circle with centre M is x^2 + y^2 - 2x - 6y - 30 = 0.

## Method

circle equation expand

## Memo working

(x - 1)^2 + (y - 3)^2 = r^2; r^2 = (3 - 1)^2 + (9 - 3)^2 = 40; expand: x^2 - 2x + 1 + y^2 - 6y + 9 = 40; x^2 + y^2 - 2x - 6y - 30 = 0

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x^2 + y^2 - 2x - 6y - 30 = 0
