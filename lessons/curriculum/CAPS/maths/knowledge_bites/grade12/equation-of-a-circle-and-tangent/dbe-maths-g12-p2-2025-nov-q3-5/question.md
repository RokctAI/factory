## Question (2 marks)

PQRS, in that order, is a parallelogram. Write down the coordinates of S.

## Method

parallelogram translation

## Memo working

Q -> R: (x ; y) -> (x + 16 ; y + 8); apply to P(-1 ; 8): S(15 ; 16)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

S(15 ; 16)
