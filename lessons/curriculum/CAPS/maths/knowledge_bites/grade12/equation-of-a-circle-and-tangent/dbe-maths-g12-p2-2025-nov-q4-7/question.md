## Question (2 marks)

Write down the coordinates of C. (From QUESTION 4: C lies on the vertical line MC through M(-6 ; 4), and from QUESTION 4.6 tangent BC has equation y = (3/4)x + 9/4.)

## Method

substitute x into tangent

## Memo working

C lies on the vertical line x = -6, and also on tangent BC. Substitute x = -6 into y = (3/4)x + 9/4: y = (3/4)(-6) + 9/4 = -18/4 + 9/4 = -9/4. So C(-6 ; -9/4).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C(-6 ; -9/4)
