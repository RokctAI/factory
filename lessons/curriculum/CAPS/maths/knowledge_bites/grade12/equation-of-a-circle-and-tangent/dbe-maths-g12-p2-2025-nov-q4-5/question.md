## Question (3 marks)

Calculate the coordinates of A and B.

## Method

substitute y zero into circle

## Memo working

(x + 6)^2 + (0 - 4)^2 = 25; (x + 6)^2 = 9; x + 6 = ±3; x = -3 or x = -9; A(-9 ; 0), B(-3 ; 0)

(Memo note: Alternative memo methods: expand to x^2 + 12x + 27 = 0, (x + 3)(x + 9) = 0; or DB = AD = 3 (line from centre ⊥ to chord) with D(-6 ; 0).)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A(-9 ; 0) and B(-3 ; 0)
