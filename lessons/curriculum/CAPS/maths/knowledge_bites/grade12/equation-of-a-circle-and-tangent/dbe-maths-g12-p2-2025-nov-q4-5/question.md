## Question (3 marks)

Calculate the coordinates of A and B. (From QUESTION 4: A and B are the points where the circle (x + 6)^2 + (y - 4)^2 = 25 cuts the x-axis.)

## Method

substitute y zero into circle

## Memo working

On the x-axis, y = 0. Substitute into the circle equation: (x + 6)^2 + (0 - 4)^2 = 25 -> (x + 6)^2 = 9 -> x + 6 = ±3 -> x = -3 or x = -9. So A(-9 ; 0) and B(-3 ; 0). (Memo alternatives: expand to x^2 + 12x + 27 = 0, (x + 3)(x + 9) = 0; or DB = AD = 3, since the line from the centre perpendicular to a chord bisects it, with D(-6 ; 0).)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A(-9 ; 0) and B(-3 ; 0)
