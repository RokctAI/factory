## Question (2 marks)

Calculate the ratio of DB/DC.

## Method

distance ratio

## Memo working

DB = sqrt((3 - (-1))^2 + (-9 - (-6))^2) = 5; DB/DC = 5/15 = 1/3

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

DB/DC = 1/3
