## Question (2 marks)

Triangle ACD has vertices A, D(3 ; -9) and C(-9 ; 0), where A is a point in the second quadrant. B(-1 ; k) lies on side DC. (In the diagram, A lies above the x-axis in the second quadrant, C(-9 ; 0) is on the x-axis, D(3 ; -9) is below the x-axis, and B(-1 ; k) is marked on line DC between C and D.) With k = -6, so that B(-1 ; -6), calculate the ratio of DB/DC. (DC = 15 units.)

## Method

distance ratio

## Memo working

DB = sqrt((3 - (-1))^2 + (-9 - (-6))^2) = sqrt(16 + 9) = 5. Therefore DB/DC = 5/15 = 1/3.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

DB/DC = 1/3
