## Question (1 mark)

In the diagram, M(p ; q) is the centre of the circle that intersects the x-axis at A and B. C is a point such that the line drawn from M to C is parallel to the y-axis and intersects the x-axis at D. MC intersects the circle at E(-6 ; -1). Tangents drawn from C touch the circle at A and B. AD = (q - 1) units. (Figure: circle with centre M(p ; q) above the x-axis, cutting the x-axis at A and B, both left of O; vertical line MDC through D on the x-axis meeting the circle again at E just below the x-axis and ending at C below E; tangent lines CA and CB.) Write down the value of p.

## Method

read coordinate from vertical line

## Memo working

MC is parallel to the y-axis, so every point on it — including M(p ; q) and E(-6 ; -1) — shares the same x-coordinate. MC is vertical through E(-6 ; -1), so p = -6.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

p = -6
