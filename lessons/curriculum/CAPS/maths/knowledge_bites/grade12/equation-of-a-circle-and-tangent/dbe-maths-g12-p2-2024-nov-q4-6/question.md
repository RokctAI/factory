## Question (3 marks)

TR is a tangent to the circle at R. Prove that PT ⊥ RT.

## Method

perpendicular gradients

## Memo working

m_RM = (1 - 3)/(7 - 1) = -1/3; m_RT = 3 (tangent ⊥ radius); m_PT = -1/3; m_RT × m_PT = -1; PT ⊥ RT

(Memo note: Alternative memo methods: PT || MR with MRT = 90° and co-interior angles; or tan-chord theorem with TP = TR giving PTR = 90°.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

m_RT × m_PT = -1, therefore PT ⊥ RT
