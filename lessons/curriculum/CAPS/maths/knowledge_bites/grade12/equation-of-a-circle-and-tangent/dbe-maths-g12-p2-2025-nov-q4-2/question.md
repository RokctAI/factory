## Question (4 marks)

Show that q = 4.

## Method

pythagoras with radius

## Memo working

MD̂B = 90° (MC || y-axis); AM = ME = q + 1 (radii); MD = q; Pythagoras: AM^2 = AD^2 + MD^2; (q + 1)^2 = (q - 1)^2 + q^2; q^2 + 2q + 1 = q^2 - 2q + 1 + q^2; q^2 - 4q = 0; q(q - 4) = 0; q ≠ 0, so q = 4

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

q = 4
