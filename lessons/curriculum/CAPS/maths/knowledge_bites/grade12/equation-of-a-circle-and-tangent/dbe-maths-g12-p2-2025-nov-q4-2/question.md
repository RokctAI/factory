## Question (4 marks)

Show that q = 4. (From QUESTION 4: M(-6 ; q) is the centre of a circle cutting the x-axis at A and B; the vertical line MC meets the x-axis at D and the circle at E(-6 ; -1); AD = (q - 1) units.)

## Method

pythagoras with radius

## Memo working

MD̂B = 90° because MC is parallel to the y-axis, so ΔADM is right-angled at D. AM = ME = q + 1 (radii — M is at height q and E is at height -1 on the same vertical line); MD = q (the vertical distance from M down to the x-axis). Pythagoras in ΔADM: AM^2 = AD^2 + MD^2 -> (q + 1)^2 = (q - 1)^2 + q^2 -> q^2 + 2q + 1 = q^2 - 2q + 1 + q^2 -> q^2 - 4q = 0 -> q(q - 4) = 0; q ≠ 0, so q = 4.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

q = 4
