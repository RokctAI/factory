## Question (4 marks)

Determine the equation of tangent BC. (From QUESTION 4: the circle has centre M(-6 ; 4); BC is the tangent touching the circle at B(-3 ; 0).)

## Method

tangent perpendicular to radius

## Memo working

The gradient of radius MB: m_MB = (4 - 0)/(-6 - (-3)) = 4/(-3) = -4/3. A tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact, so m_BC = 3/4 (negative reciprocal). Through B(-3 ; 0): 0 = (3/4)(-3) + c -> c = 9/4. So y = (3/4)x + 9/4.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

y = (3/4)x + 9/4
