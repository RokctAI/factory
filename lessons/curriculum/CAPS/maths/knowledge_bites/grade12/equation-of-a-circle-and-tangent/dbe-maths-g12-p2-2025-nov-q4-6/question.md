## Question (4 marks)

Determine the equation of tangent BC.

## Method

tangent perpendicular to radius

## Memo working

m_MB = (4 - 0)/(-6 - (-3)) = -4/3; m_BC = 3/4; through B(-3 ; 0): 0 = (3/4)(-3) + c -> c = 9/4; y = (3/4)x + 9/4

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

y = (3/4)x + 9/4
