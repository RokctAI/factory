## Question (1 mark)

Triangle ACD has vertices A, D(3 ; -9) and C(-9 ; 0), where A is a point in the second quadrant. B(-1 ; k) lies on side DC. (In the diagram, A lies above the x-axis in the second quadrant, C(-9 ; 0) is on the x-axis, D(3 ; -9) is below the x-axis, and B(-1 ; k) is marked on line DC between C and D.) The equation of DC is y = -3/4 x - 27/4. Show that k = -6.

## Method

substitute point in line

## Memo working

B(-1 ; k) lies on DC, so substitute x = -1: k = -3/4(-1) - 27/4 = 3/4 - 27/4 = -24/4 = -6.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

k = -6
