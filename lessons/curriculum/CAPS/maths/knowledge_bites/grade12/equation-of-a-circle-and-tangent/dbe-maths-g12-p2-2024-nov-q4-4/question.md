## Question (2 marks)

M(1 ; 3) is the centre of the circle. The circle cuts the x-axis at N. ST is a tangent to the circle at P(3 ; 9). R(d ; 1), with d > 0, and L lie on the circle. O and V are the x-intercepts of PL and RL respectively. (In the diagram, P(3 ; 9) is at the top right of the circle, R(d ; 1) on the right and L at the bottom left near the y-axis; the tangent ST touches the circle at P; chord PL passes through the centre M and cuts the x-axis at O, the origin; chord RL cuts the x-axis at V; N lies on the x-axis to the right of V.) The equation of the circle is x^2 + y^2 - 2x - 6y - 30 = 0. Show that d = 7.

## Method

substitute point in circle

## Memo working

R(d ; 1) lies on the circle, so substitute: d^2 + (1)^2 - 2d - 6(1) - 30 = 0, giving d^2 - 2d - 35 = 0, so (d - 7)(d + 5) = 0 and d = 7 (d = -5 is rejected since d > 0). (Alternative memo methods: substitute into (d - 1)^2 + (1 - 3)^2 = 40, or use angle PRL = 90°, the angle in a semi-circle, with gradients.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

d = 7
