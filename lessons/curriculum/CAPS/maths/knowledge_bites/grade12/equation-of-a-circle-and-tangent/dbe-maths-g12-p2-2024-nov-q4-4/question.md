## Question (2 marks)

Show that d = 7.

## Method

substitute point in circle

## Memo working

d^2 + (1)^2 - 2d - 6(1) - 30 = 0; d^2 - 2d - 35 = 0; (d - 7)(d + 5) = 0; d = 7 (d = -5 rejected since d > 0)

(Memo note: Alternative memo methods: (d - 1)^2 + (1 - 3)^2 = 40, or use angle PRL = 90° (angle in semi-circle) with gradients.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

d = 7
