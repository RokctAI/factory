## Question (5 marks)

T is a point on QR such that PT ⊥ QR. Calculate the coordinates of T.

## Method

foot of perpendicular intersection

## Memo working

m_PT = -2; equation of PT through P(-1 ; 8): y = -2x + 6; intersect with y = (1/2)x - 4: -2x + 6 = (1/2)x - 4; 5x = 20; x = 4; y = (1/2)(4) - 4 = -2; T(4 ; -2)

(Memo note: Alternative memo method: PQ = PR = √205, so ΔPQR is isosceles and the perpendicular height bisects the base QR; T is the midpoint of QR = (4 ; -2).)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

T(4 ; -2)
