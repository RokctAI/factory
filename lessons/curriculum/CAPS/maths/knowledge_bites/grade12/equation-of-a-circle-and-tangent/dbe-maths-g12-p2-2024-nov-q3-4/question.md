## Question (2 marks)

Triangle ACD has vertices A, D(3 ; -9) and C(-9 ; 0), where A is a point in the second quadrant. B(-1 ; k) lies on side DC. (In the diagram, A lies above the x-axis in the second quadrant, C(-9 ; 0) is on the x-axis, D(3 ; -9) is below the x-axis, and B(-1 ; k) is marked on line DC between C and D.) Calculate the length of DC.

## Method

distance formula

## Memo working

Using the distance formula with D(3 ; -9) and C(-9 ; 0): DC = sqrt((3 + 9)^2 + (-9 - 0)^2) = sqrt(144 + 81) = sqrt(225) = 15.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

DC = 15 units
