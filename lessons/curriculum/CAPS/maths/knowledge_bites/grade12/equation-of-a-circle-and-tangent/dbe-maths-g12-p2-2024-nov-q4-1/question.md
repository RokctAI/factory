## Question (2 marks)

M(1 ; 3) is the centre of the circle. The circle cuts the x-axis at N. ST is a tangent to the circle at P(3 ; 9). R(d ; 1), with d > 0, and L lie on the circle. O and V are the x-intercepts of PL and RL respectively. (In the diagram, P(3 ; 9) is at the top right of the circle, R(d ; 1) on the right and L at the bottom left near the y-axis; the tangent ST touches the circle at P; chord PL passes through the centre M and cuts the x-axis at O, the origin; chord RL cuts the x-axis at V; N lies on the x-axis to the right of V.) Write down the coordinates of L.

## Method

diametrically opposite point

## Memo working

PL passes through the centre M, so L is diametrically opposite P: M(1 ; 3) is the midpoint of PL. From P(3 ; 9): x_L = 2(1) - 3 = -1 and y_L = 2(3) - 9 = -3, so L(-1 ; -3).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

L(-1 ; -3)
