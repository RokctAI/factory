## Question (2 marks)

In the diagram, M(1 ; 3) is the centre of the circle. The circle cuts the x-axis at N. ST is a tangent to the circle at P(3 ; 9). R(d ; 1), with d > 0, and L lie on the circle. O and V are the x-intercepts of PL and RL respectively. [Figure: circle centred at M(1 ; 3) passing through P(3 ; 9) at the top right, R(d ; 1) on the right, L at the bottom left near the y-axis; tangent line S-T touching at P; chords PL and RL drawn, crossing the x-axis at O (the origin) and V; N on the x-axis to the right of V.] Write down the coordinates of L.

## Method

diametrically opposite point

## Memo working

L is diametrically opposite P through M: x = -1, y = -3

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

L(-1 ; -3)
