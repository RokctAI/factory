## Question (2 marks)

Determine the equation of the circle in the form (x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 = r^2.

## Method

circle equation centre radius

## Memo working

AM = 5 units (radius q + 1 = 5); (x + 6)^2 + (y - 4)^2 = 25

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

(x + 6)^2 + (y - 4)^2 = 25
