## Question (2 marks)

Determine the equation of the circle in the form (x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 = r^2. (From QUESTION 4: the circle has centre M(-6 ; 4) and passes through E(-6 ; -1).)

## Method

circle equation centre radius

## Memo working

The radius is the distance from M(-6 ; 4) down to E(-6 ; -1) on the same vertical line: AM = q + 1 = 5 units. Substitute centre (-6 ; 4) and r = 5: (x + 6)^2 + (y - 4)^2 = 25.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

(x + 6)^2 + (y - 4)^2 = 25
