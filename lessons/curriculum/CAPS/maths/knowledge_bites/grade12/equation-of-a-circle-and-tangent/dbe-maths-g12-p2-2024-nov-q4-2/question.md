## Question (4 marks)

M(1 ; 3) is the centre of the circle. The circle cuts the x-axis at N. ST is a tangent to the circle at P(3 ; 9). R(d ; 1), with d > 0, and L lie on the circle. O and V are the x-intercepts of PL and RL respectively. (In the diagram, P(3 ; 9) is at the top right of the circle, R(d ; 1) on the right and L at the bottom left near the y-axis; the tangent ST touches the circle at P; chord PL passes through the centre M and cuts the x-axis at O, the origin; chord RL cuts the x-axis at V; N lies on the x-axis to the right of V.) Determine the equation of tangent ST to the circle at P.

## Method

tangent perpendicular to radius

## Memo working

Gradient of the radius MP: m_MP = (9 - 3)/(3 - 1) = 3. The tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact, so m_ST = -1/3. Substitute P(3 ; 9): 9 = -1/3(3) + c gives c = 10. The tangent is y = -1/3 x + 10.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

y = -1/3 x + 10
