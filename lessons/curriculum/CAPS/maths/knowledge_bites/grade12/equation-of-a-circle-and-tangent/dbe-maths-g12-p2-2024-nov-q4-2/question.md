## Question (4 marks)

Determine the equation of tangent ST to the circle at P.

## Method

tangent perpendicular to radius

## Memo working

m_MP = (9 - 3)/(3 - 1) = 3; m_ST = -1/3 (tangent ⊥ radius); 9 = -1/3(3) + c -> c = 10; y = -1/3 x + 10

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

y = -1/3 x + 10
