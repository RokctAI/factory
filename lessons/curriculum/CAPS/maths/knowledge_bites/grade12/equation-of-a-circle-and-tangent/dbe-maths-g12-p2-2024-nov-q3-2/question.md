## Question (2 marks)

Determine the equation of DC in the form y = mx + c.

## Method

equation of line

## Memo working

0 = -3/4(-9) + c -> c = -27/4; y = -3/4 x - 27/4

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

y = -3/4 x - 27/4
