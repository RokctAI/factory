## Question (2 marks)

Triangle ACD has vertices A, D(3 ; -9) and C(-9 ; 0), where A is a point in the second quadrant. B(-1 ; k) lies on side DC. (In the diagram, A lies above the x-axis in the second quadrant, C(-9 ; 0) is on the x-axis, D(3 ; -9) is below the x-axis, and B(-1 ; k) is marked on line DC between C and D.) Determine the equation of DC in the form y = mx + c. (The gradient of DC is -3/4.)

## Method

equation of line

## Memo working

Substitute C(-9 ; 0) into y = -3/4 x + c: 0 = -3/4(-9) + c, so 0 = 27/4 + c and c = -27/4. The equation is y = -3/4 x - 27/4.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

y = -3/4 x - 27/4
