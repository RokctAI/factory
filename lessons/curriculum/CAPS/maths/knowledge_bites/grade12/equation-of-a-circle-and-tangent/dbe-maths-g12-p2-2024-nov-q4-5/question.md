## Question (5 marks)

Calculate the size of L̂ (the angle at L between LP and LR).

## Method

inclination angles

## Memo working

m_PO = 3, tan(POV) = 3 -> POV = 71,565...°; m_RL = (1 - (-3))/(7 - (-1)) = 1/2, tan(RVN) = 1/2 -> RVN = 26,565...°; L̂ = 71,565...° - 26,565...° = 45° (exterior angle of triangle)

(Memo note: Alternative memo methods: right angle at R (angle in semi-circle) with PR = 4√5, PL = 4√10, RL = 4√5 and a trig ratio; or the cosine rule giving cos L = √2/2.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

L̂ = 45°
