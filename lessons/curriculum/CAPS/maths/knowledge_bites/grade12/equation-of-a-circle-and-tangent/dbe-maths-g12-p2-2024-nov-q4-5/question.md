## Question (5 marks)

M(1 ; 3) is the centre of the circle. The circle cuts the x-axis at N. ST is a tangent to the circle at P(3 ; 9). R(d ; 1), with d > 0, and L lie on the circle. O and V are the x-intercepts of PL and RL respectively. (In the diagram, P(3 ; 9) is at the top right of the circle, R(d ; 1) on the right and L at the bottom left near the y-axis; the tangent ST touches the circle at P; chord PL passes through the centre M and cuts the x-axis at O, the origin; chord RL cuts the x-axis at V; N lies on the x-axis to the right of V.) With R(7 ; 1) and L(-1 ; -3), calculate the size of L̂ (the angle at L between LP and LR).

## Method

inclination angles

## Memo working

Line PL has gradient 3 and passes through the origin O, so tan(angle POV) = 3 and angle POV = 71,565...°. Gradient of RL: m_RL = (1 - (-3))/(7 - (-1)) = 4/8 = 1/2, so tan(angle RVN) = 1/2 and angle RVN = 26,565...°. By the exterior angle of a triangle, L̂ = 71,565...° - 26,565...° = 45°. (Alternative memo methods: use the right angle at R, the angle in a semi-circle, with PR = 4√5, PL = 4√10 and RL = 4√5 and a trig ratio; or the cosine rule, giving cos L = √2/2.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

L̂ = 45°
