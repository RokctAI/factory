## Question (6 marks)

For which values of t will y = -11x + t intersect f at 3 distinct points?

## Method

tangency bounds for three roots

## Memo working

Tangency where f'(x) = -11: 3x^2 - 16x + 5 = -11; 3x^2 - 16x + 16 = 0; (x - 4)(3x - 4) = 0; x = 4 or x = 4/3. At x = 4: t = 14; at x = 4/3: t = 634/27 = 23 13/27 = 23,48. Three distinct points for 14 < t < 634/27

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

14 < t < 634/27
