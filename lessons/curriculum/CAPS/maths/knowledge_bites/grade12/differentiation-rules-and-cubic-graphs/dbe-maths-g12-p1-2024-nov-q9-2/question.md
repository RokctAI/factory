## Question (2 marks)

Write down the x-intercepts of f', the derivative of f.

## Method

derivative zero at turning points

## Memo working

f'(x) = 0 at the turning points: x = 1 and x = 5/2

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

(1 ; 0) and (5/2 ; 0)
