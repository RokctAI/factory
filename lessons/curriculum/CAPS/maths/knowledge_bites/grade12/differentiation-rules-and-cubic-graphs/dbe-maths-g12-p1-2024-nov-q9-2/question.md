## Question (2 marks)

A(1 ; 9) and B(5/2 ; 8) are the turning points of the graph of a cubic function f. C(0 ; 7) is the y-intercept of f (the cubic curve rises steeply from below the x-axis on the left, passes through C(0 ; 7), reaches a local maximum at A(1 ; 9), dips to a local minimum at B(5/2 ; 8), then increases). Write down the x-intercepts of f', the derivative of f.

## Method

derivative zero at turning points

## Memo working

At a turning point the gradient of f is zero, so f'(x) = 0 exactly at the turning points. The turning points are at x = 1 and x = 5/2, so the graph of f' cuts the x-axis at these two values.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

(1 ; 0) and (5/2 ; 0)
