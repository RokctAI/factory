## Question (3 marks)

The graph of f(x) = -(1/2)x^2 + 3x + 7/2 passes through the point D(5 ; 6). Determine the equation of the tangent to f at D.

## Method

tangent line via derivative

## Memo working

The gradient of the tangent is the derivative at the point of contact. Differentiate: f'(x) = -x + 3, so the gradient at D is m = f'(5) = -5 + 3 = -2. The tangent passes through D(5 ; 6), so substitute into y = -2x + c: 6 = -2(5) + c, giving c = 16. The tangent is y = -2x + 16.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

y = -2x + 16
