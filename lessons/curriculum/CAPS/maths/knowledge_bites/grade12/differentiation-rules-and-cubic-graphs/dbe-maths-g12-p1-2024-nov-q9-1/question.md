## Question (2 marks)

A(1 ; 9) and B(5/2 ; 8) are the turning points of graph f below. C(0 ; 7) is the y-intercept of f. [Figure: cubic curve f rising steeply from below the x-axis on the left, through C(0 ; 7), local maximum at A(1 ; 9), local minimum at B(5/2 ; 8), then increasing.] For which values of x is f decreasing?

## Method

decreasing between turning points

## Memo working

f decreases between the local maximum and local minimum

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

1 < x < 5/2
