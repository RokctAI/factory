## Question (2 marks)

A(1 ; 9) and B(5/2 ; 8) are the turning points of the graph of a cubic function f. C(0 ; 7) is the y-intercept of f (the cubic curve rises steeply from below the x-axis on the left, passes through C(0 ; 7), reaches a local maximum at A(1 ; 9), dips to a local minimum at B(5/2 ; 8), then increases). For which values of x is f decreasing?

## Method

decreasing between turning points

## Memo working

A cubic with a positive leading coefficient rises, turns down at its local maximum, falls to its local minimum, and rises again. So f decreases exactly between the two turning points: from the local maximum at x = 1 to the local minimum at x = 5/2.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

1 < x < 5/2
