## Question (4 marks)

Sketched below is the graph of f(x) = x^3 - 8x^2 + 5x + 14. A, B and C are the x-intercepts of f. D and E are the turning points of f. (Figure: cubic curve with x-intercepts A (negative), B and C (positive); local maximum D near the y-axis and local minimum E between B and C, below the x-axis.) Calculate the coordinates of E.

## Method

turning points via derivative

## Memo working

Turning points occur where the derivative is zero. Differentiate: f'(x) = 3x^2 - 16x + 5. Set it equal to zero and factorise: (3x - 1)(x - 5) = 0, so x = 1/3 or x = 5. E is the local minimum, at the larger value x = 5. Substitute back: f(5) = 125 - 200 + 25 + 14 = -36. So E(5 ; -36).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

E(5 ; -36)
