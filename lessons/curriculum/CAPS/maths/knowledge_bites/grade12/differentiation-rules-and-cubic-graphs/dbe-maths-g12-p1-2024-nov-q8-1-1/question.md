## Question (2 marks)

Determine:  d/dx [3x - 5x^2]

## Method

power rule

## Memo working

Differentiate term by term with the power rule: the derivative of 3x is 3, and the derivative of -5x^2 is -10x. d/dx[3x - 5x^2] = 3 - 10x.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

3 - 10x
