## Question (2 marks)

A(1 ; 9) and B(5/2 ; 8) are the turning points of the graph of a cubic function f. C(0 ; 7) is the y-intercept of f (the cubic curve rises steeply from below the x-axis on the left, passes through C(0 ; 7), reaches a local maximum at A(1 ; 9), dips to a local minimum at B(5/2 ; 8), then increases). Determine the value of k for which y = f(x) + k will have THREE positive x-intercepts.

## Method

vertical shift root analysis

## Memo working

Adding k slides the whole graph vertically. For three x-intercepts, the x-axis must cut the curve three times, which happens when the axis lies strictly between the shifted local minimum value (8 + k) and the shifted local maximum value (9 + k): 8 + k < 0 < 9 + k. This gives -9 < k < -8. For such k the y-intercept 7 + k is negative, so all three x-intercepts lie to the right of the y-axis — all three are positive.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

-9 < k < -8
