## Question (2 marks)

Determine the value of k for which y = f(x) + k will have THREE positive x-intercepts.

## Method

vertical shift root analysis

## Memo working

The x-axis must lie strictly between the local minimum value 8 and local maximum value 9 after shifting: -9 < k < -8

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

-9 < k < -8
