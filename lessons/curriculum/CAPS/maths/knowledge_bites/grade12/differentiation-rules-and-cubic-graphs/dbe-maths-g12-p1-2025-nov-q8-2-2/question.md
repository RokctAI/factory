## Question (4 marks)

Determine:  dy/dx if y = (2x^4 + 1)/x^2

## Method

simplify then power rule

## Memo working

y = 2x^2 + x^(-2); dy/dx = 4x - 2x^(-3)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

dy/dx = 4x - 2x^(-3)
