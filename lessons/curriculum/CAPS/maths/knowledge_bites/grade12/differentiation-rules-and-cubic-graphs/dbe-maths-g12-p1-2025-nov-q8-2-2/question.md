## Question (4 marks)

Determine:  dy/dx if y = (2x^4 + 1)/x^2

## Method

simplify then power rule

## Memo working

First rewrite the quotient as a sum of powers of x by dividing each term in the numerator by x^2: y = 2x^4/x^2 + 1/x^2 = 2x^2 + x^(-2). Now apply the power rule term by term: the derivative of 2x^2 is 4x, and the derivative of x^(-2) is -2x^(-3). So dy/dx = 4x - 2x^(-3).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

dy/dx = 4x - 2x^(-3)
