## Question (2 marks)

A(1 ; 9) and B(5/2 ; 8) are the turning points of the graph of a cubic function f. C(0 ; 7) is the y-intercept of f (the cubic curve rises steeply from below the x-axis on the left, passes through C(0 ; 7), reaches a local maximum at A(1 ; 9), dips to a local minimum at B(5/2 ; 8), then increases). For which values of x will f be concave up?

## Method

concavity from inflection point

## Memo working

For a cubic, the point of inflection lies midway between the two turning points: x = (1 + 5/2)/2 = 7/4. To the left of the inflection point the curve bends downwards (it holds the local maximum); to the right it bends upwards (it holds the local minimum). So f is concave up for x > 7/4.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Concave up for x > 7/4
