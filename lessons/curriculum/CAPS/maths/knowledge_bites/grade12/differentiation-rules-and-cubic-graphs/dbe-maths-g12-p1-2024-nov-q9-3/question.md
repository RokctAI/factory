## Question (2 marks)

For which values of x will f be concave up?

## Method

concavity from inflection point

## Memo working

Point of inflection midway between turning points: (5/2 + 1)/2 = 7/4; concave up for x > 7/4

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Concave up for x > 7/4
