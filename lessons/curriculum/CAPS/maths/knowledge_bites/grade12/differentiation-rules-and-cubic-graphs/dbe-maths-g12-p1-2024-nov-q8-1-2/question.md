## Question (4 marks)

Determine g'(x) if g(x) = 2/x^2 - cuberoot(x^7)

## Method

power rule negative fractional exponents

## Memo working

First rewrite each term as a power of x: 2/x^2 = 2x^(-2) and cuberoot(x^7) = x^(7/3), so g(x) = 2x^(-2) - x^(7/3). Apply the power rule to each term: the derivative of 2x^(-2) is -4x^(-3), and the derivative of -x^(7/3) is -(7/3)x^(4/3). g'(x) = -4x^(-3) - (7/3)x^(4/3).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

g'(x) = -4x^(-3) - (7/3)x^(4/3)
