## Question (2 marks)

Determine:  g'(x) if g(x) = -3x^4 + 2x

## Method

power rule

## Memo working

Differentiate term by term with the power rule: the derivative of -3x^4 is -3 · 4x^3 = -12x^3, and the derivative of 2x is 2. So g'(x) = -12x^3 + 2.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

g'(x) = -12x^3 + 2
