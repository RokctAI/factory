## Question (3 marks)

Determine the equation of the tangent to f(x) = x^3 - 4x^2 + 2x + 3 at x = 2.

## Method

tangent line at point

## Memo working

The gradient of the tangent is the derivative at x = 2: f'(x) = 3x^2 - 8x + 2, so m = f'(2) = 3(4) - 8(2) + 2 = -2. The point of contact: y = f(2) = 8 - 16 + 4 + 3 = -1, so the tangent touches at (2 ; -1). Substitute into y - y1 = m(x - x1): y + 1 = -2(x - 2), giving y = -2x + 3.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

y = -2x + 3
