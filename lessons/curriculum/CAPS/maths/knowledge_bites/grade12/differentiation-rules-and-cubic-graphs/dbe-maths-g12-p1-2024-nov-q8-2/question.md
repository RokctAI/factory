## Question (3 marks)

Determine the equation of the tangent to f(x) = x^3 - 4x^2 + 2x + 3 at x = 2.

## Method

tangent line at point

## Memo working

f'(x) = 3x^2 - 8x + 2; m = f'(2) = -2; y = f(2) = -1; y + 1 = -2(x - 2); y = -2x + 3

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

y = -2x + 3
