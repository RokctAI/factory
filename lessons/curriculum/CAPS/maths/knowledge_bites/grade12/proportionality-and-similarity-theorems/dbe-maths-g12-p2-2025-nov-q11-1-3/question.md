## Question (4 marks)

Calculate, with reasons, the value of: Area of ΔGCF / Area of GFDA

## Method

area ratio sine formula

## Memo working

Area GFDA = Area ΔCDA - Area ΔGCF; with GC = 3k, CA = 5k, CF = 3p, CD = 5p: ratio = (1/2)(3k)(3p)sin Ĉ / [(1/2)(5k)(5p)sin Ĉ - (1/2)(3k)(3p)sin Ĉ] = 9kp/(25kp - 9kp) = 9/16

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Area ΔGCF / Area GFDA = 9/16
