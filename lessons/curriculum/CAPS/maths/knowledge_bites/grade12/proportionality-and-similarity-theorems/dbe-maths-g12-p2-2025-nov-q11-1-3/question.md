## Question (4 marks)

Calculate, with reasons, the value of: Area of ΔGCF / Area of GFDA (From QUESTION 11.1: in ΔABC, F and D lie on BC with AD || EF; AC and EF intersect at G; CF/FB = 2/5 and CG/GA = 3/2; from QUESTION 11.1.1: FD/CF = 2/3.)

## Method

area ratio sine formula

## Memo working

Quadrilateral GFDA is what remains of triangle CDA after triangle GCF is removed, so Area GFDA = Area ΔCDA - Area ΔGCF. Express the sides from C in parts: CG/GA = 3/2 gives CG = 3k and CA = 5k; FD/CF = 2/3 gives CF = 3p and CD = CF + FD = 5p. Both triangles share the angle Ĉ, so use the sine area formula: Area ΔGCF = (1/2)(3k)(3p)sin Ĉ and Area ΔCDA = (1/2)(5k)(5p)sin Ĉ. Then the ratio = (1/2)(3k)(3p)sin Ĉ / [(1/2)(5k)(5p)sin Ĉ - (1/2)(3k)(3p)sin Ĉ] = 9kp/(25kp - 9kp) = 9/16.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Area ΔGCF / Area GFDA = 9/16
