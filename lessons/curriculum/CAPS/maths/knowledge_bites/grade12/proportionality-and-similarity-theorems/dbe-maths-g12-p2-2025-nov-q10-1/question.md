## Question (3 marks)

In the diagram, PQRS is a cyclic quadrilateral. T is a point on the circle such that QT is perpendicular to SR at V. PT and ST are drawn. Q̂2 = 35° and R̂ = Ŝ1. [Figure: circle through P (upper left), Q (top), R (right) and S (left); chord SR extended through V where QT crosses it at right angles; T at the bottom of the circle; angles numbered at P (1, 2), Q (1, 2), V (1, 2, 3, 4), S (1, 2), T (1, 2); Q̂2 = 35° marked.] Calculate, with reasons, the size of QT̂S.

## Method

triangle sum same segment

## Memo working

R̂ = 55° (sum of angles in ΔQVR with V̂ = 90° and Q̂2 = 35°); QT̂S = R̂ = 55° (angles in the same segment)

(Memo note: Alternative memo method: Ŝ2 = 35° (angles in the same segment), then QT̂S = 55° (sum of angles in Δ).)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

QT̂S = 55°
