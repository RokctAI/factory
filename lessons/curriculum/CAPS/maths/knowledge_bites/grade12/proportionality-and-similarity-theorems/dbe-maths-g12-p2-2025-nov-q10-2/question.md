## Question (3 marks)

Prove that PQ || SR.

## Method

co interior angles supplementary

## Memo working

SP̂Q = 125° (opp angles of cyclic quad, R̂ = 55°); Ŝ1 = R̂ = 55° (given); SP̂Q + Ŝ1 = 180°; PQ || SR (co-interior angles supplementary)

(Memo note: Alternative memo method: Ŝ1 = R̂ = 55° (given); PQ̂R = 125° (opp angles of cyclic quad); Q̂1 = 125° - 35° = 90°; Q̂1 + V̂1 = 180°; PQ || SR (co-interior angles supplementary).)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

PQ || SR
