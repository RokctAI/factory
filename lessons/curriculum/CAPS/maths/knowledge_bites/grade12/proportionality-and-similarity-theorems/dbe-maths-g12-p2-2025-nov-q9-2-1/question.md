## Question (2 marks)

In the diagram, O is the centre of the circle. POF is the diameter of the circle and MF is a tangent to the circle at F. OM cuts the circle at L. P̂ = 32°. [Figure: circle with centre O; diameter from P at the top to F at the bottom; PL chord to lower-left point L; OM drawn from O through L to external point M at bottom-left; MF tangent along the bottom; angles at O labelled 1 (above OM) and 2 (below OM); angle at P is 32°.] Calculate, with reasons, the size of: Ô2

## Method

central angle twice inscribed

## Memo working

Ô2 = 2 × 32° = 64° (angle at centre = 2 × angle at circumference)

(Memo note: Alternative memo method: PL̂O = P̂ = 32° (angles opp equal radii); Ô2 = 64° (ext angle of Δ).)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Ô2 = 64°
