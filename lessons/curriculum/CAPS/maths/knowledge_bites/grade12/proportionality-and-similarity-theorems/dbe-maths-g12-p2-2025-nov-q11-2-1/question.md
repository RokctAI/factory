## Question (2 marks)

In the diagram, WVZR is a cyclic quadrilateral. RZ is produced to Q. A tangent is drawn from Q to touch the circle at S. WV is produced to E, a point on ZQ. RW produced meets ZV produced in P. PQ || WE. RS and ZS are drawn. (In the figure the circle passes through W at the top, V on the left, Z lower left and R on the right; P lies outside the circle above left where RW and ZV produced meet; Q lies outside at bottom left; E is on ZQ; S is at the bottom of the circle with tangent QS.) Prove, with reasons, that: PR = PW.QR/QE

## Method

proportionality theorem

## Memo working

Work in triangle PQR (or equivalently triangle RQP): W lies on side RP and E lies on side RQ, and WE || PQ is given. By the proportionality theorem (a line parallel to one side of a triangle divides the other two sides proportionally), QE/QR = PW/PR. Cross-multiply: PR.QE = PW.QR, and divide both sides by QE to get PR = PW.QR/QE.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

PR = PW.QR/QE
