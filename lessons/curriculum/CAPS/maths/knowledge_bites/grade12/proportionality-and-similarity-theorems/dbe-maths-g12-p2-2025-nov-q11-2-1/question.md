## Question (2 marks)

In the diagram, WVZR is a cyclic quadrilateral. RZ is produced to Q. A tangent is drawn from Q to touch the circle at S. WV is produced to E, a point on ZQ. RW produced meets ZV produced in P. PQ || WE. RS and ZS are drawn. [Figure: circle through W (top), V (left, inside chord region), Z (lower left) and R (right); P above left outside the circle at the meet of RW and ZV produced; Q bottom left outside the circle; E on ZQ; S at the bottom of the circle with tangent QS; angles numbered at P, W, V, Z, R, S and Q.] Prove, with reasons, that: PR = PW.QR/QE

## Method

proportionality theorem

## Memo working

QE/QR = PW/PR (prop theorem; PQ || WE / line || one side of Δ); therefore PR = PW.QR/QE

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

PR = PW.QR/QE
