## Question (5 marks)

In the diagram, O is the centre of the circle. Triangle PWS is drawn with P, W and S on the circle. OR ⊥ PS. PRS is produced to T. SW and OT intersect at V. OV : OT = 1 : 4. (Diagram: circle centred at O with P at the top, S at the upper right and W at the bottom; R on chord PS with a right-angle mark where OR meets PS; line P-R-S extended to T outside the circle on the right; SW and OT crossing at V inside the circle.) Prove, with reasons, that OR : WS = 1 : 2.

## Method

midpoint theorem

## Memo working

PR = RS [line from centre ⊥ to chord]; PO = OW [radii]; OR = ½WS [midpoint theorem]; therefore OR : WS = 1 : 2 (Memo alternatives: PŜW = 90°, angle in semi-circle, so RO || SW, then the proportion theorem; or similar triangles ΔPRO ||| ΔPSW with PW = 2PO.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

OR : WS = 1 : 2
