## Question (4 marks)

Prove, with reasons, that: PW = QE.PZ/sqrt(QR.QZ)

## Method

combine similar triangles

## Memo working

PQ/RQ = PZ/PR (ΔPQZ ||| ΔRQP); PR = QR.PZ/PQ; but PR = PW.QR/QE (proved in 11.2.1); PW.QR/QE = QR.PZ/PQ; PW = QE.PZ/PQ; but PQ^2 = RQ.QZ (proved in 11.2.2), so PQ = sqrt(RQ.QZ); therefore PW = QE.PZ/sqrt(QR.QZ)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

PW = QE.PZ/sqrt(QR.QZ)
