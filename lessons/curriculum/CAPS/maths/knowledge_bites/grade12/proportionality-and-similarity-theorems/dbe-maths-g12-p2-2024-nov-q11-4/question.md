## Question (6 marks)

Prove that GF^2 = BC.FC.AF / AD

## Method

combine similar triangles

## Memo working

GF/BC = AF/AC [ΔAGF ||| ΔABC], so GF = BC.AF/AC; ΔACD ||| ΔFGC, so AC/GF = AD/FC, giving AC = AD.FG/FC; substitute: GF = BC.AF.FC/(AD.FG); therefore GF^2 = BC.FC.AF/AD

(Memo note: Alternative memo route multiplies GF = AF.BC/AC and GF = AF.CD/AD, then uses ΔFCD ||| ΔFAC to replace FC = CD.AF/AC.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

GF^2 = BC.FC.AF / AD
