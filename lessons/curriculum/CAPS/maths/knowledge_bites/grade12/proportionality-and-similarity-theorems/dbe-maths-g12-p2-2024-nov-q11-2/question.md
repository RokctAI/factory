## Question (4 marks)

Prove that AG.AD = AC.AF

## Method

parallel lines proportion

## Memo working

D̂1 = AF̂G = x, so DC || FG [corresp angles equal]; AG/AC = AF/AD [prop theorem; DC || FG]; therefore AG.AD = AC.AF

(Memo note: Alternative memo route: ΔACD ||| ΔAGF (common Â3, AF̂G = D̂1 = x) giving AC/AG = AD/AF.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

AG.AD = AC.AF
