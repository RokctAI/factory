## Question (4 marks)

Calculate the length of PT if ST = 15 units.

## Method

proportion theorem calculation

## Memo working

OV/VT = RS/ST = 1/3 [prop theorem; RO || SW]; RS/15 = 1/3 -> RS = 5; PR = RS = 5 [line from centre ⊥ to chord]; PT = PR + RS + ST = 5 + 5 + 15 = 25 units

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

PT = 25 units
