## Question (3 marks)

Calculate, with reasons, the size of: M̂

## Method

tangent perpendicular diameter triangle sum

## Memo working

PF̂M = 90° (tangent ⊥ diameter); M̂ = 180° - 90° - 64° = 26° (sum of angles of Δ)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

M̂ = 26°
