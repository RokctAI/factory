## Question (1 mark)

Prove, with reasons, that: If ΔPQZ ||| ΔRQP, then PQ^2 = RQ.QZ

## Method

similar triangles ratio

## Memo working

PQ/RQ = QZ/QP (ΔPQZ ||| ΔRQP); PQ^2 = RQ.QZ

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

PQ^2 = RQ.QZ
