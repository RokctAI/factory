## Question (1 mark)

Prove, with reasons, that: If ΔPQZ ||| ΔRQP, then PQ^2 = RQ.QZ (From QUESTION 11.2: WVZR is a cyclic quadrilateral, RZ is produced to Q, and P lies where RW produced meets ZV produced.)

## Method

similar triangles ratio

## Memo working

Similar triangles have their corresponding sides in proportion, matching the order of the letters: ΔPQZ ||| ΔRQP gives PQ/RQ = QZ/QP. Cross-multiplying yields PQ.QP = RQ.QZ, that is PQ^2 = RQ.QZ.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

PQ^2 = RQ.QZ
