## Question (6 marks)

In the diagram, triangle RST is drawn. Line AB intersects RS and RT at A and B respectively such that AB || ST. (Diagram: triangle RST with apex R; line AB inside the triangle parallel to base ST, with A on RS and B on RT.) Prove the theorem which states that a line drawn parallel to one side of a triangle divides the other two sides proportionally, i.e. RA/AS = RB/BT.

## Method

proportionality theorem proof

## Memo working

Construction: join SB and TA; draw h1 from B ⊥ AR and h2 from A ⊥ RB. area ΔRAB/area ΔASB = (½RA·h1)/(½AS·h1) = RA/AS; area ΔRAB/area ΔABT = (½RB·h2)/(½BT·h2) = RB/BT; area ΔASB = area ΔABT [same base AB, same height, AB || ST]; therefore RA/AS = RB/BT

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

RA/AS = RB/BT
