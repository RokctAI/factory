## Question (4 marks)

Calculate, with reasons, the value of: BA/EA

## Method

proportionality theorem combined ratios

## Memo working

Let CF = 2x, FB = 5x; FD = (2/3)(2x) = (4/3)x; BA/EA = BD/FD (prop theorem; AD || EF); BD = 5x - (4/3)x = (11/3)x; BA/EA = (11/3)x ÷ (4/3)x = 11/4

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

BA/EA = 11/4
