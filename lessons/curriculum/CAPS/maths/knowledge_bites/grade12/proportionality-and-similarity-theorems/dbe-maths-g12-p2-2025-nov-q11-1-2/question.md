# Past-Paper Worked Example — Q11.1.2

## Question (4 marks)

Calculate, with reasons, the value of: BA/EA (From QUESTION 11.1: in ΔABC, BA is produced to E; F and D lie on BC with AD || EF; AC and EF intersect at G; CF/FB = 2/5 and CG/GA = 3/2; from QUESTION 11.1.1: FD/CF = 2/3.)

## Method

proportionality theorem combined ratios

## Memo working

Introduce a variable to combine the two ratios along BC. Let CF = 2x and FB = 5x (from CF/FB = 2/5). From QUESTION 11.1.1, FD = (2/3)CF = (2/3)(2x) = (4/3)x. Now use triangle BEF: A lies on BE and D lies on BF, with AD || EF, so by the proportionality theorem BA/EA = BD/FD. Compute BD = FB - FD = 5x - (4/3)x = (11/3)x. Therefore BA/EA = (11/3)x ÷ (4/3)x = 11/4.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

BA/EA = 11/4
