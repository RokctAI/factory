## Question (2 marks)

In the diagram, ΔABC is drawn. BA is produced to E. F and D are points on BC such that AD || EF. AC and EF intersect at G. CF/FB = 2/5 and CG/GA = 3/2. [Figure: triangle ABC with B top right and C at the bottom; E to the left of A on BA produced; D on BC between A's foot and B; EF drawn from E crossing AC at G to F on BC below D; AD || EF marked with arrows.] Calculate, with reasons, the value of: FD/CF

## Method

proportionality theorem

## Memo working

FD/CF = GA/CG (prop theorem; AD || EF / line || one side of Δ); FD/CF = 2/3

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

FD/CF = 2/3
