## Question (2 marks)

In the diagram, ΔABC is drawn. BA is produced to E. F and D are points on BC such that AD || EF. AC and EF intersect at G. CF/FB = 2/5 and CG/GA = 3/2. (In the figure, B is top right and C at the bottom; E lies to the left of A on BA produced; D is on BC between the foot of A and B; EF runs from E through G on AC to F on BC below D; AD || EF is marked.) Calculate, with reasons, the value of: FD/CF

## Method

proportionality theorem

## Memo working

Look at triangle CDA: the line GF is parallel to side AD (given AD || EF) and cuts the other two sides at G and F. By the proportionality theorem (a line parallel to one side of a triangle divides the other two sides in the same ratio), FD/CF = GA/CG. Since CG/GA = 3/2, the reciprocal gives GA/CG = 2/3, so FD/CF = 2/3.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

FD/CF = 2/3
