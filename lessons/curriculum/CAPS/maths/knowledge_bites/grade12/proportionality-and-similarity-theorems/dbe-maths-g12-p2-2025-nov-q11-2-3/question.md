## Question (3 marks)

Prove, with reasons, that: ΔQSZ ||| ΔQRS (From QUESTION 11.2: WVZR is a cyclic quadrilateral, RZ is produced to Q, and QS is a tangent touching the circle at S; RS and ZS are drawn.)

## Method

similar triangles aaa

## Memo working

Compare the two triangles angle by angle. First, Q̂2 = Q̂2: the angle at Q is common to both triangles, since Z lies on the line QR. Second, Ŝ1 = R̂2 by the tan-chord theorem — the angle between tangent QS and chord SZ equals the angle that SZ subtends in the alternate segment, which is the angle at R. Third, the remaining angles must be equal because the angles of a triangle sum to 180°: Ẑ1 = QŜR (third angle of the triangle). With three pairs of equal angles, ΔQSZ ||| ΔQRS (AAA).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

ΔQSZ ||| ΔQRS
