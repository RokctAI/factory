## Question (3 marks)

Prove, with reasons, that: ΔQSZ ||| ΔQRS

## Method

similar triangles aaa

## Memo working

In ΔQSZ and ΔQRS: Q̂2 = Q̂2 (common angle); Ŝ1 = R̂2 (tan chord theorem); Ẑ1 = QŜR (3rd angle of Δ); therefore ΔQSZ ||| ΔQRS (AAA)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

ΔQSZ ||| ΔQRS
