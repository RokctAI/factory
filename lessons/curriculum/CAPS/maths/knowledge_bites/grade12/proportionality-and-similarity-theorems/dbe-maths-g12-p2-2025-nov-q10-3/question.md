## Question (2 marks)

Prove that PT is a diameter of the circle.

## Method

converse angle in semicircle

## Memo working

Q̂1 = 90° (co-interior angles; PQ || SR); PT is a diameter (converse angle in semi-circle: chord subtends 90°)

(Memo note: Alternative memo method: Ŝ2 = 35° (ext angle of ΔSVT or angle sum); PŜT = 90°; PT is a diameter (converse angle in semi-circle).)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

PT is a diameter
