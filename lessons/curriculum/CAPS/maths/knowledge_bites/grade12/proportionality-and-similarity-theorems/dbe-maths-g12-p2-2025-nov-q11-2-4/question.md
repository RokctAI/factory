## Question (3 marks)

Prove, with reasons, that: PQ = QS (From QUESTIONS 11.2.2 and 11.2.3: PQ^2 = RQ.QZ, and ΔQSZ ||| ΔQRS.)

## Method

combine similarity results

## Memo working

From the similarity ΔQSZ ||| ΔQRS, corresponding sides are proportional: QS/QR = QZ/QS. Cross-multiplying gives QS^2 = QZ.QR. But QUESTION 11.2.2 established PQ^2 = RQ.QZ — the same product. So PQ^2 = QS^2, and since both are lengths (positive), PQ = QS.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

PQ = QS
