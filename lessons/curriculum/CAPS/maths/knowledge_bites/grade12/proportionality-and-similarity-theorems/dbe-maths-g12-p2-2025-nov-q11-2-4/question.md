## Question (3 marks)

Prove, with reasons, that: PQ = QS

## Method

combine similarity results

## Memo working

QS/QR = QZ/QS (ΔQSZ ||| ΔQRS); QS^2 = QZ.QR; but PQ^2 = RQ.QZ (proved in 11.2.2); therefore PQ = QS

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

PQ = QS
