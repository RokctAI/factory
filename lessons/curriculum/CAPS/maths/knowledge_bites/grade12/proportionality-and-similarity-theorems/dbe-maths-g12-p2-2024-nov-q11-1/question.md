## Question (6 marks)

In the diagram, A, B, G and F lie on the larger circle. A smaller circle is drawn to touch the larger circle internally at A. EA is a common tangent to both circles. EBCF is a tangent to the smaller circle at C. AC is produced to G. AF cuts the smaller circle at D. AB, CD and GF are drawn. [Figure: two circles touching internally at A (top); E outside to the lower left on tangent EA; line E-B-C-F tangent to the smaller circle at C, with B on the larger circle between E and C, F on the larger circle beyond C; G at the bottom of the larger circle on AC produced; D on AF inside the smaller circle; angles at A numbered 1, 2, 3; at B numbered 1, 2; at C numbered 1, 2, 3, 4; at D numbered 1, 2; at F numbered 1, 2.] If EÂG = x, determine with reasons, FOUR other angles that are equal to x.

## Method

tan chord theorem angle chase

## Memo working

D̂1 = EÂG = x [tan-chord theorem]; Ĉ1 = D̂1 = x [tan-chord theorem]; Ĉ4 = Ĉ1 = x [vert opp angles]; AF̂G = EÂG = x [tan-chord theorem]

(Memo note: Alternative memo route starts with EA = EC [tangents from common point] and Ĉ1 = EÂG [angles opp equal sides].)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

D̂1 = x, Ĉ1 = x, Ĉ4 = x and AF̂G = x
