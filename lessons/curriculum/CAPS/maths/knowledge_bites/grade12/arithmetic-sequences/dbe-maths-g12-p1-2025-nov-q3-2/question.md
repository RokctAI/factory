## Question (3 marks)

Show that the depth of the torpedo at the end of n seconds was Tn = -n^2 + 38n - 1

## Method

quadratic pattern general term

## Memo working

2a = -2 -> a = -1; 3(-1) + b = 35 -> b = 38; -1 + 38 + c = 36 -> c = -1; Tn = -n^2 + 38n - 1

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Tn = -n^2 + 38n - 1
