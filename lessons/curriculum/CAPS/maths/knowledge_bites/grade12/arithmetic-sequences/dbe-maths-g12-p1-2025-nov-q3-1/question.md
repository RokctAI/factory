## Question (2 marks)

The depth of a torpedo below sea level forms a quadratic pattern, where 0 metres is at sea level. A submarine tracked a torpedo in one-second intervals. [Figure: drawing of a submarine with a torpedo below it.] The depth (in metres) that the torpedo reached is given in the table below. At the end of the first second: 36; At the end of the first 2 seconds: 71; At the end of the first 3 seconds: 104. Calculate the depth of the torpedo at the end of the first 5 seconds.

## Method

extend quadratic pattern

## Memo working

36 ; 71 ; 104 ; 135 ; 164 with first differences 35 ; 33 ; 31 ; 29 and second difference -2; T5 = 164

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

T5 = 164 (metres)
