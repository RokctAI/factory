## Question (3 marks)

Calculate the maximum depth that the torpedo reached.

## Method

quadratic turning point

## Memo working

n = -38/(2(-1)) = 19; T19 = -(19)^2 + 38(19) - 1 = 360

(Memo note: Alternative memo method: T'n = -2n + 38 = 0 -> n = 19.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

360 (metres, at n = 19)
