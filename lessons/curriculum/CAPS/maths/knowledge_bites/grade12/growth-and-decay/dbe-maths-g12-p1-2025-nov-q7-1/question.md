## Question (2 marks)

A travel company informs Robert that a holiday to a certain destination costs R40 000 now. The company predicts that the cost of this holiday will increase by 7,8% per annum. What will this holiday cost in 5 years' time?

## Method

compound growth

## Memo working

A = P(1 + i)^n = 40 000(1 + 7,8%)^5 = R58 230,94

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R58 230,94
