## Question (2 marks)

After 4 years, the value of a printer was half of its original value. Determine the rate at which the value of the printer depreciated over this period, if depreciation was calculated according to a straight-line method.

## Method

straight line depreciation

## Memo working

Straight-line depreciation uses A = P(1 - in). Half the original value after 4 years means (1/2)P = P(1 - 4i). Divide by P and solve: 4i = 1/2 -> i = 1/8, so r = 12,5%. (Or directly: the printer loses 50% of its value in 4 years, so r = 50%/4 = 12,5% per year.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

r = 12,5%
