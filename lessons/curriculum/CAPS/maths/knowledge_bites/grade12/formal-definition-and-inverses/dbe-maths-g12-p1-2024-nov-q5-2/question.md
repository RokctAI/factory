## Question (2 marks)

Determine the equation of the horizontal asymptote of f.

## Method

symmetry line through asymptote intersection

## Memo working

Axis of symmetry passes through the point of intersection of the asymptotes: y = g(1) = 1 - 3 = -2. Answer only: 2/2

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

y = -2
