## Question (3 marks)

Show that the equation of the line through A and C is given by g(x) = 2x + 2.

## Method

straight line through two points

## Memo working

x^2 - 4x - 5 = 0; (x-5)(x+1) = 0; A(-1 ; 0); m = (8-0)/(3+1) = 2; y - 0 = 2(x + 1); y = 2x + 2

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

g(x) = 2x + 2
