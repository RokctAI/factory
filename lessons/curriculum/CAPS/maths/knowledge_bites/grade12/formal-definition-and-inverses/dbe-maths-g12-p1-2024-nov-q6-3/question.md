## Question (4 marks)

The graphs of f(x) = -x^2 + 4x + 5 and the straight line g(x) = 2x + 2 are drawn. C(3 ; 8) is a point of intersection of f and g. EH is drawn parallel to the y-axis, with E a point on f and H a point on g (concave-down parabola f with x-intercepts A on the negative x-axis and D on the positive x-axis, turning point B, y-intercept 5; increasing line g through A and C(3 ; 8); vertical segment EH between the two graphs, to the left of C). Calculate the maximum length of EH for f > g.

## Method

maximise vertical distance

## Memo working

The length of the vertical segment EH is the height of f minus the height of g at the same x-value: EH = f(x) - g(x) = -x^2 + 4x + 5 - (2x + 2) = -x^2 + 2x + 3. This is a concave-down parabola in x, so its maximum sits where the derivative is zero: -2x + 2 = 0 -> x = 1. Substitute back: EH = -(1)^2 + 2(1) + 3 = 4. (Memo marking note: the answer alone, without working, earns 1/4.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Maximum length of EH = 4
