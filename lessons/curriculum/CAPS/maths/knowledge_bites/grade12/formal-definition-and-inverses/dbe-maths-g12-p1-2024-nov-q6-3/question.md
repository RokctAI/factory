## Question (4 marks)

Calculate the maximum length of EH for f > g.

## Method

maximise vertical distance

## Memo working

EH = -x^2 + 4x + 5 - (2x + 2) = -x^2 + 2x + 3; max at -2x + 2 = 0 -> x = 1; EH = -(1)^2 + 2(1) + 3 = 4. Answer only: 1/4

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Maximum length of EH = 4
