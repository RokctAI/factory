## Question (3 marks)

Determine the equation of the tangent to f at D.

## Method

tangent line via derivative

## Memo working

f'(x) = -x + 3; m = f'(5) = -2; 6 = -2(5) + c -> c = 16; y = -2x + 16

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

y = -2x + 16
