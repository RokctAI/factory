## Question (2 marks)

Determine the values of x for which f(x) < 6

## Method

graph inequality reading

## Memo working

f(x) = 6 at x = 1 and x = 5 (symmetric about x = 3); f below 6 outside these values

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x < 1 or x > 5  OR  x ∈ (-∞ ; 1) or x ∈ (5 ; ∞)
