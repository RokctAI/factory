## Question (2 marks)

Determine the equation of f^(-1), the inverse of f, in the form y = ...

## Method

inverse of logarithm

## Memo working

y = log_(1/3) x; swop x and y: x = log_(1/3) y; y = (1/3)^x = 3^(-x)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

y = (1/3)^x = 3^(-x)
