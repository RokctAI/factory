## Question (2 marks)

Describe the transformation that g must undergo to become an axis of symmetry of f that cuts f at two points.

## Method

describe transformation

## Memo working

The increasing axis of symmetry of f is y = x - 3 (through (2 ; -1)); g: y = x - 2 must shift 1 unit down or 1 unit right

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Translation of 1 unit down OR translation of 1 unit to the right
