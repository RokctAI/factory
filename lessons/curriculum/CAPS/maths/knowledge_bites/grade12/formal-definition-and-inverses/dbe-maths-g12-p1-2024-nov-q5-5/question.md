## Question (2 marks)

Graph f undergoes a transformation to h where: (a) the domain and range of h are the same as that of f; (b) h'(x), the derivative of h, is negative on its domain. Describe a possible transformation that f could have undergone to result in h.

## Method

describe transformation

## Memo working

Any one of: reflection in y-axis then translation 2 units right; reflection in x-axis then translation 4 units down; reflection about the line x = 1; reflection about the line y = -2

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

f is reflected in the y-axis, then translated 2 units to the right
