## Question (3 marks)

Determine the equation of f^(-1) for f^(-1)(x) <= 0. Write your answer in the form y = ...

## Method

inverse of parabola

## Memo working

y = -6x^2; swap x and y: x = -6y^2; y^2 = -x/6; y = ±sqrt(-x/6); take y = -sqrt(-x/6) for x <= 0

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

y = -sqrt(-x/6), x <= 0
