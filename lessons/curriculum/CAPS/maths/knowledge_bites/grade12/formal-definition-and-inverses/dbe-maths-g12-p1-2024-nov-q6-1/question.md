## Question (3 marks)

In the diagram below, the graphs of f(x) = -x^2 + 4x + 5 and g, a straight line, are drawn. C(3 ; 8) is a point of intersection of f and g. EH is drawn parallel to the y-axis, with E a point on f and H a point on g. [Figure: concave-down parabola f with x-intercepts A (negative) and D (positive), turning point B, y-intercept 5; increasing line g through A and C(3 ; 8); vertical dashed segment EH between the curves left of C.] Calculate the coordinates of B, the turning point of f.

## Method

turning point formula

## Memo working

x = -4/(2(-1)) = 2 (or f'(x) = -2x + 4 = 0 -> x = 2); y = -(2)^2 + 4(2) + 5 = 9

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

B(2 ; 9)
