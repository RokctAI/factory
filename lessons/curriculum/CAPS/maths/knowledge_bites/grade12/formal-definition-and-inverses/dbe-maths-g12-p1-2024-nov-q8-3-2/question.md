## Question (1 mark)

Given: f(x) = -6x^2. Write down how you will restrict the domain of f such that f^(-1), the inverse of f, is a function.

## Method

restrict domain for inverse

## Memo working

The parabola fails the horizontal line test: each negative y-value comes from two x-values, one on each side of the turning point. Keeping only one half of the parabola — either the right half or the left half — makes the inverse a function. Either restriction is accepted.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x >= 0  OR  x <= 0
