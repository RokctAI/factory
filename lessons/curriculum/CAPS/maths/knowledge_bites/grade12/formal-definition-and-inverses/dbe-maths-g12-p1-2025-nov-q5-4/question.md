## Question (3 marks)

Show that the equation of the parabola is f(x) = -(1/2)x^2 + 3x + 7/2

## Method

turning point form substitute point

## Memo working

y = a(x - 3)^2 + 8; substitute D(5 ; 6): 6 = a(5-3)^2 + 8 -> a = -1/2; y = -(1/2)(x - 3)^2 + 8 = -(1/2)x^2 + 3x + 7/2

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

f(x) = -(1/2)x^2 + 3x + 7/2
