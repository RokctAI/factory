## Question (2 marks)

Calculate the value of a.

## Method

substitute point solve parameter

## Memo working

Substitute (0 ; 1): 1 = a/(0 - 1) - 2; 3 = a/(-1); a = -3

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

a = -3
