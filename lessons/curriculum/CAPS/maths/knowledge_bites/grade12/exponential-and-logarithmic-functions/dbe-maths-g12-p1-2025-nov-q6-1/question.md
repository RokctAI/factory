## Question (1 mark)

The graphs of g(x) = x + c and f(x) = a/(x + p) + q are drawn below. Graph g and the vertical asymptote of f intersect at the x-axis. [Figure: increasing line g through the origin area cutting the x-axis right of O where the dotted vertical asymptote of f meets it; hyperbola f with one branch above the horizontal asymptote (dotted, below the x-axis) to the right of the vertical asymptote and the other branch below it to the left.] Write down the coordinates of the x-intercept of g in terms of p.

## Method

read intercept from asymptote

## Memo working

The vertical asymptote of f is x = -p, and g cuts the x-axis there

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

(-p ; 0)
