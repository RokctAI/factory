## Question (2 marks)

Given: f(x) = a^x - 1 for a > 0. B(2 ; -5/9) is a point on f. Calculate the value of a.

## Method

substitute point solve parameter

## Memo working

-5/9 = a^2 - 1; a^2 = 4/9; a = 2/3

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

a = 2/3
