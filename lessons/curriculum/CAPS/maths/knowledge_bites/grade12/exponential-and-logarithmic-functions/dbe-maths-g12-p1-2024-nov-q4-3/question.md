## Question (3 marks)

Sketch the graph of f, where f(x) = (2/3)^x - 1. Clearly show the intercepts with the axes and asymptotes, if any.

## Method

sketch exponential graph

## Memo working

Memo sketch awards three features: the graph passes through the origin (0 ; 0), which is both intercepts since f(0) = (2/3)^0 - 1 = 0; the horizontal asymptote is y = -1; and because the base 2/3 is between 0 and 1, the curve is decreasing — coming down from the upper left, through the origin, and flattening towards y = -1 on the right.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Decreasing exponential through (0 ; 0) with horizontal asymptote y = -1
