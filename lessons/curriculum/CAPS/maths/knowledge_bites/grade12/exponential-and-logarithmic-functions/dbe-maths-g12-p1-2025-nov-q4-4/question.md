## Question (1 mark)

Write down the equation of the asymptote of f^(-1)  (f^(-1)(x) = (1/3)^x)

## Method

asymptote of exponential

## Memo working

The inverse f^(-1)(x) = (1/3)^x is an exponential function. An exponential of this form gets closer and closer to the x-axis but never reaches it, so its horizontal asymptote is y = 0.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

y = 0
