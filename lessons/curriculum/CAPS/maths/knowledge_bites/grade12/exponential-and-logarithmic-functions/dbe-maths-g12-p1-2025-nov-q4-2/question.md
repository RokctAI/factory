## Question (1 mark)

Write down the coordinates of A. (A is the x-intercept of f(x) = log_(1/3) x)

## Method

x intercept of log

## Memo working

At the x-intercept, y = 0: log_(1/3) x = 0 means x = (1/3)^0 = 1. So A is the point (1 ; 0).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A(1 ; 0)
