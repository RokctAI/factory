## Question (2 marks)

Determine the equation of f^(-1), the inverse of f, in the form y = ...  (Given f(x) = log_(1/3) x)

## Method

inverse of logarithm

## Memo working

Start from y = log_(1/3) x. For the inverse, swop x and y: x = log_(1/3) y. Rewrite in exponential form: y = (1/3)^x, which can also be written as y = 3^(-x).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

y = (1/3)^x = 3^(-x)
