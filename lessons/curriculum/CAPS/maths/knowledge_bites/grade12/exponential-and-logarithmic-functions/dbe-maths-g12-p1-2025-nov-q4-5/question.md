## Question (3 marks)

Draw the graph of f^(-1) on the set of axes provided in the ANSWER BOOK. Clearly indicate the intercepts with the axes, the coordinates of ONE other point and the asymptotes.

## Method

sketch exponential graph

## Memo working

Memo sketch awards: decreasing exponential shape with asymptote y = 0; y-intercept (0 ; 1); any other point (e.g. (-1 ; 3))

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Decreasing exponential through (0 ; 1) and (-1 ; 3) with horizontal asymptote y = 0
