## Question (2 marks)

The graph of h is obtained when f^(-1) is translated 5 units to the right. Determine the y-values of h where x > 4.

## Method

translate exponential evaluate range

## Memo working

(4 ; 3) is a point on h (h(x) = (1/3)^(x-5), h(4) = 3); for x > 4 the decreasing curve gives 0 < y < 3

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

0 < y < 3  OR  y ∈ (0 ; 3)
