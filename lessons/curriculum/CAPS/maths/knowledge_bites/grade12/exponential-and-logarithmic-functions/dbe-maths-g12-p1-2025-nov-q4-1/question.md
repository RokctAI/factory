## Question (1 mark)

The graph of f(x) = log_(1/3) x is drawn below. Point A is the x-intercept of f and (3 ; t) lies on f. [Figure: decreasing logarithmic curve f in the first/fourth quadrants, cutting the x-axis at A and passing through the labelled point (3 ; t) below the x-axis.] Calculate the value of t

## Method

evaluate logarithm

## Memo working

t = log_(1/3) 3 = -1

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

t = -1
