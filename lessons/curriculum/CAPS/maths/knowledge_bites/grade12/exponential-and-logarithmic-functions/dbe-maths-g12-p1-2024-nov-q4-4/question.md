## Question (3 marks)

It is further given that C is a point on f at y = 19/8. Determine the coordinates of C', the image of C, when C is reflected about the line y = x.

## Method

solve exponential then reflect

## Memo working

19/8 = (2/3)^x - 1; (2/3)^x = 27/8; (3/2)^3 = (3/2)^(-x); x = -3; C(-3 ; 19/8); reflection about y = x gives C'(19/8 ; -3)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C'(19/8 ; -3)
