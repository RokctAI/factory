## Question (1 mark)

Write down the range of f.

## Method

range of exponential

## Memo working

Horizontal asymptote y = -1; exponential part positive, so y > -1

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

y > -1  OR  y ∈ (-1 ; ∞)
