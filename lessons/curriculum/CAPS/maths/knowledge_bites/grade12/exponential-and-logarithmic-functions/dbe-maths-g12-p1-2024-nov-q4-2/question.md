## Question (1 mark)

Write down the range of f, where f(x) = (2/3)^x - 1.

## Method

range of exponential

## Memo working

The horizontal asymptote is y = -1, and the exponential part (2/3)^x is always positive, so the graph lies strictly above the asymptote: y > -1

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

y > -1  OR  y ∈ (-1 ; ∞)
