## Question (2 marks)

The first term of an arithmetic series is 7. The common difference of this series is 5 and the series contains 20 terms. Calculate the sum of this series.

## Method

arithmetic series sum

## Memo working

Use the arithmetic sum formula Sn = n/2[2a + (n-1)d] with a = 7, d = 5, n = 20: S20 = 20/2[2(7) + (20-1)(5)] = 10[14 + 95] = 1 090

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

S20 = 1 090
