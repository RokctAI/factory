## Question (4 marks)

The original arithmetic series is extended to 75 terms. The sum of these 75 terms is 14 400. Using sigma notation, write down an equation for the sum of the terms added to the original series.

## Method

sigma notation

## Memo working

Tn = 7 + (n-1)(5) = 5n + 2; S75 - S20 = 14 400 - 1 090 = 13 310; therefore Σ_{n=21}^{75} (5n + 2) = 13 310

(Memo note: Alternative memo answer: Σ_{n=1}^{55} (5n + 102) = 13 310, using Tn = 102 + 5n for the added terms.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Sum from n=21 to 75 of (5n + 2) = 13 310
