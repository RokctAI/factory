## Question (2 marks)

Given: Sum from p=k to 117 of (4p - 1) = 26 675. Write down the difference between T6 and T14

## Method

arithmetic sequence term difference

## Memo working

The terms 4p - 1 form an arithmetic sequence with common difference d = 4. From the 6th term to the 14th term is 14 - 6 = 8 steps of the common difference, so T14 - T6 = 4·8 = 32. (Memo alternatives: (4(14) - 1) - (4(6) - 1) = 55 - 23 = 32, or (4(k+13) - 1) - (4(k+5) - 1) = 32.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

32
