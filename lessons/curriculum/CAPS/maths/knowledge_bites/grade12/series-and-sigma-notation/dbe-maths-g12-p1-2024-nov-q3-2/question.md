## Question (4 marks)

Calculate the sum of the areas of the first 10 circles (radii 6 ; 3 ; 1,5 ; ... cm, each half the previous).

## Method

geometric series sum

## Memo working

The areas form a geometric series: 36π ; 9π ; 9π/4 ; ... with first term a = 36π and ratio r = 1/4 (halving the radius quarters the area). Apply the geometric sum formula: S10 = 36π((1/4)^10 - 1)/(1/4 - 1) = 150,80

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

S10 = 150,80
