## Question (4 marks)

Calculate the sum of the areas of the first 10 circles.

## Method

geometric series sum

## Memo working

Areas: 36π ; 9π ; 9π/4 ; ... with a = 36π, r = 1/4; S10 = 36π((1/4)^10 - 1)/(1/4 - 1) = 150,80

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

S10 = 150,80
