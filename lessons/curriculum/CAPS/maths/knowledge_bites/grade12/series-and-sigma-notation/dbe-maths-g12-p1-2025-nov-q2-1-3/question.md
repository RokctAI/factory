## Question (2 marks)

Calculate the sum of the infinite series. (The geometric series of Q2.1.1 with t = -2, i.e. 8 - 4 + 2 - ...)

## Method

infinite geometric series sum

## Memo working

The series has a = 8 and r = -1/2. Since -1 < r < 1, the infinite sum converges: S∞ = a/(1 - r) = 8/(1 - (-1/2)) = 8/(3/2) = 16/3 = 5,33.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

S∞ = 16/3 = 5,33
