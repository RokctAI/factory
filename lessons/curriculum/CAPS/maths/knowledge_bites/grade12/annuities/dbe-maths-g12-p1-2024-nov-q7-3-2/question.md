## Question (7 marks)

Tshepo paid R22 300,98 (his monthly instalment and an additional R20 000) on 1 March 2024 into the loan account. He continues to pay the original monthly instalment thereafter. How many months earlier will Tshepo repay the loan?

## Method

annuity outstanding balance solve n

## Memo working

Balance after 2 years (36 payments left): P = 2 300,98[1 - (1 + 13,5/1200)^(-36)]/(13,5/1200) = R67 804,94; after extra payment: 67 804,94 - 20 000 = R47 804,94; solve 47 804,94 = 2 300,98[1 - 1,01125^(-n)]/0,01125; 0,766 = 1,01125^(-n); n = 23,83, so 24 payments needed instead of 36 -> 12 months earlier

(Memo note: Alternative memo methods use A - F (outstanding balance R67 805,20) or set the whole loan equal to the present values of the payments; all give 12 months earlier.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

12 months earlier
