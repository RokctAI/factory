## Question (7 marks)

Tshepo was granted a loan of R100 000 on 1 March 2022 at an interest rate of 13,5% p.a., compounded monthly, repayable over 5 years in monthly instalments of R2 300,98 starting on 1 April 2022. Tshepo paid R22 300,98 (his monthly instalment and an additional R20 000) on 1 March 2024 into the loan account. He continues to pay the original monthly instalment thereafter. How many months earlier will Tshepo repay the loan?

## Method

annuity outstanding balance solve n

## Memo working

On 1 March 2024, 24 payments have been made and 36 remain. The outstanding balance is the present value of the remaining payments: P = 2 300,98[1 - (1 + 13,5/1200)^(-36)]/(13,5/1200) = R67 804,94. The extra R20 000 reduces this to 67 804,94 - 20 000 = R47 804,94. Now solve for the number of instalments n that settles this balance: 47 804,94 = 2 300,98[1 - 1,01125^(-n)]/0,01125. Rearranging: 1 - 1,01125^(-n) = 47 804,94 × 0,01125/2 300,98 = 0,234, so 1,01125^(-n) = 0,766 and n = 23,83. He therefore needs 24 more payments instead of 36, repaying the loan 36 - 24 = 12 months earlier. (Alternative memo methods use the outstanding balance via A - F, R67 805,20, or set the whole loan equal to the present values of all payments; all give 12 months earlier.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

12 months earlier
