## Question (5 marks)

The bank granted Rajesh a loan of R900 000 on 28 February 2024 at an interest rate of 6,8% p.a., compounded monthly. Rajesh was unable to make the first three payments. He made his first repayment of R10 000 on 30 June 2024. He continued to make monthly repayments of R10 000 at the end of each month thereafter. How long, in completed months, will it take Rajesh to repay the loan from the time the loan was granted?

## Method

annuity present value solve n with deferred start

## Memo working

For the three months with no payments, the loan grows with compound interest: A = 900 000(1 + 0,068/12)^3 = R915 386,86. This grown balance is then repaid by an ordinary annuity of R10 000 per month: 915 386,86 = 10 000[1 - (1 + 0,068/12)^(-n)]/(0,068/12). Rearranging gives (1 + 0,068/12)^(-n) = 0,4812..., so n = log_(1,005...) (1/0,4812...) = 129,419... months of paying. Add back the three payment-free months: 129,419... + 3 = 132,419... months, so it takes 133 completed months since the loan was granted.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

133 months since the loan was granted
