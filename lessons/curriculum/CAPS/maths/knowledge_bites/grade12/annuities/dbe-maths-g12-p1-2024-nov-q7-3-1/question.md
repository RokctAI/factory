## Question (2 marks)

Tshepo was granted a loan of R100 000 on 1 March 2022 at an interest rate of 13,5% p.a., compounded monthly. Tshepo agreed to repay the loan over 5 years in monthly instalments of R2 300,98, starting on 1 April 2022. Calculate the total interest that he will pay over the 5 years.

## Method

total interest on annuity

## Memo working

Over 5 years Tshepo makes 60 monthly payments. Total paid: 2 300,98 × 60 = R138 058,80. The interest is everything paid beyond the original loan amount: 138 058,80 - 100 000 = R38 058,80.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R38 058,80
