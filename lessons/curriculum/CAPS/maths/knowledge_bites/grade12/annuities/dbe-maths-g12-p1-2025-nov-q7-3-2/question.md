## Question (4 marks)

Calculate the value of the final payment.

## Method

annuity final partial payment

## Memo working

Balance for the fractional period: P = 10 000[1 - (1 + 0,068/12)^(-0,419...)]/(0,068/12) = R4 173,55...; final payment = 4 173,55...(1 + 0,068/12)^1 = R4 197,21

(Memo note: Alternative memo method: A - F after 129 payments: A = 915 386,86(1 + 0,068/12)^129 = R1 897 482,712; F = 10 000[(1 + 0,068/12)^129 - 1]/(0,068/12) = R1 893 309,16; balance = R4 173,552; final payment = 4 173,552(1 + 0,068/12) = R4 197,21.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R4 197,21
