## Question (4 marks)

Rajesh repays the loan of Q7.3.1 (R900 000 at 6,8% p.a., compounded monthly, grown for 3 payment-free months, then R10 000 monthly repayments; the repayment count worked out to 129,419... payments). Calculate the value of the final payment.

## Method

annuity final partial payment

## Memo working

After 129 full payments of R10 000, a fraction 0,419... of a payment period remains. The outstanding balance at that point is the present value of the fractional payment: P = 10 000[1 - (1 + 0,068/12)^(-0,419...)]/(0,068/12) = R4 173,55... This balance still earns one month of interest before the final payment is made: final payment = 4 173,55...(1 + 0,068/12)^1 = R4 197,21. (Alternative memo method computes the balance as A - F after 129 payments: A = 915 386,86(1 + 0,068/12)^129 = R1 897 482,712 and F = 10 000[(1 + 0,068/12)^129 - 1]/(0,068/12) = R1 893 309,16, so the balance is R4 173,552 and the final payment is 4 173,552(1 + 0,068/12) = R4 197,21.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R4 197,21
