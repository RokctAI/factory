## Question (4 marks)

Sarah opened a savings account that paid interest at a rate of 5,8% p.a., compounded quarterly. She deposited R2 300 into the account on 1 January 2020 and continued to make deposits of R2 300 at the beginning of each quarter thereafter. She made her last deposit on 1 October 2025. Calculate the accumulated amount in the account on 1 January 2026.

## Method

future value annuity due

## Memo working

Count the deposits: from 1 January 2020 to 1 October 2025 there is one deposit at the start of each quarter for 6 years, so 24 deposits. The quarterly rate is i = 0,058/4. Because each deposit is made at the beginning of a quarter, every payment earns one extra quarter of interest by 1 January 2026, so multiply the ordinary future value by (1 + i): F = 2 300[(1 + 0,058/4)^24 - 1]/(0,058/4) × (1 + 0,058/4) = R66 411,60. (Alternative memo method: treat it as 25 deposits and remove the extra one: F = 2 300[(1 + 0,058/4)^25 - 1]/(0,058/4) - 2 300 = R66 411,60.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R66 411,60
