## Question (5 marks)

Given: f(x) = -6x^2. Determine f'(x) from first principles.

## Method

first principles

## Memo working

f'(x) = lim_{h->0} [f(x+h) - f(x)]/h = lim_{h->0} [-6(x+h)^2 + 6x^2]/h = lim_{h->0} (-12xh - 6h^2)/h = lim_{h->0} (-12x - 6h) = -12x

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

f'(x) = -12x
