## Question (4 marks)

Determine f'(x) from first principles if it is given that f(x) = -2x + 3.

## Method

first principles

## Memo working

f'(x) = lim_{h->0} [f(x+h) - f(x)]/h = lim_{h->0} [-2(x+h) + 3 - (-2x + 3)]/h = lim_{h->0} (-2h)/h = lim_{h->0} (-2) = -2

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

f'(x) = -2
