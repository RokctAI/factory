## Question (2 marks)

Use the correlation coefficient to show whether the prediction made in QUESTION 1.2 is valid or not. (The data is the age and selling price of the 11 cars from QUESTION 1.1.)

## Method

correlation coefficient interpretation

## Memo working

From the calculator, the correlation coefficient is r = -0,95. The strong correlation (r = -0,95, close to -1) suggests that the data points lie close to the regression line; therefore the prediction will be valid.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

r = -0,95 (strong correlation), therefore the prediction will be valid
