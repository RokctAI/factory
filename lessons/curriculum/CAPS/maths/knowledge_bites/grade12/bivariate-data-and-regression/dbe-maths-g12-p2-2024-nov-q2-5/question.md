## Question (2 marks)

The company manager decided that all employees who travel for an hour or more will be allowed to work from home for part of the day. What percentage of the employees will be allowed to work from home for part of the day?

## Method

percentage from ogive

## Memo working

Number of employees who travel 60 minutes or more = 60 - 26 = 34 (or 35); 34/60 × 100 = 56,67% (or 35/60 × 100 = 58,33%)

(Memo note: The memo accepts either reading of the ogive (34 or 35 employees).)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

56,67% (using 34 employees) OR 58,33% (using 35 employees)
