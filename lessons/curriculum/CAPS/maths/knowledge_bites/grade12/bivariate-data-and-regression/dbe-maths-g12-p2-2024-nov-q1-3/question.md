## Question (2 marks)

The coach uses the least squares regression line to set the target for the minimum number of push-ups by each team member according to their weight. Predict the number of push-ups that a member of the team, who weighs 29 kg, should do to meet the target.

## Method

regression prediction

## Memo working

y = 39,46 - 0,59(29) = 22,35

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

y = 22,35
