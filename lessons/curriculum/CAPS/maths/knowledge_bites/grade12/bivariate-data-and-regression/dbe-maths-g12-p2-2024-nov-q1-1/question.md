## Question (3 marks)

At the beginning of a season, the coach of a junior boys' rugby team recorded the weight (in kg) of the 15 players in his team and the number of push-ups that each player was able to do in one minute. The data is represented in the table and scatter plot. The least squares regression line for the data is drawn. Weight (in kg) (x): 34, 32, 40, 27, 33, 28, 27, 55, 39, 44, 30, 57, 28, 32, 31. Number of push-ups per minute (y): 26, 21, 6, 20, 16, 26, 23, 7, 18, 14, 17, 8, 28, 25, 20. (The scatter plot shows push-ups per minute against weight in kg, with a decreasing least squares regression line drawn through the points.) Determine the equation of the least squares regression line for the data.

least squares regression calculator

## Memo working

Enter the 15 data pairs into a calculator in statistics (regression) mode. The calculator gives the intercept a = 39,456001... and the gradient b = -0,590018... Rounded to two decimal places, the least squares regression line is ŷ = 39,46 - 0,59x. (Correct answer only: full marks.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

ŷ = 39,46 - 0,59x
