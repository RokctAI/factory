## Question (1 mark)

The cumulative frequency graph (ogive) shows the time taken (in minutes) for 60 employees to travel to work each morning. [Figure: ogive from (0 ; 0) rising in an S-shape through approximately (20 ; 5), (40 ; 13), (60 ; 26), (80 ; 42), (100 ; 57) and levelling off at (120 ; 60); time axis 0-140 minutes, cumulative frequency axis 0-65.] Estimate the median travel time.

## Method

median from ogive

## Memo working

Read off at cumulative frequency 30: 65 minutes

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Median = 65
