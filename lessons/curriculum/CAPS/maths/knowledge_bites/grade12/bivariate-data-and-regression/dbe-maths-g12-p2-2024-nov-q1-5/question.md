## Question (1 mark)

The players trained hard during the season. At the end of the season, the coach reported that each player was able to do 5 more push-ups per minute than they did at the beginning of the season. How does the increase in the number of push-ups influence the standard deviation of the data?

## Method

effect of constant shift on spread

## Memo working

Adding the same constant (5) to every data value shifts the whole data set up by 5 but leaves the distances between the values unchanged, so the spread does not change.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

No influence; the standard deviation stays the same
