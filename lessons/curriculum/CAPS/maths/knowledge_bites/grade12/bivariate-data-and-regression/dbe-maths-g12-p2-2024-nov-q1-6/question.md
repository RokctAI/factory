## Question (2 marks)

At the beginning of the season, the coach used the least squares regression line as the minimum target for a player to aim for. Determine the maximum possible increase in the number of push-ups that a team member must obtain to reach the minimum target.

## Method

residual from regression line

## Memo working

6 is the furthest y-value below the least squares regression line; an increase of 10 push-ups will get that team member to (40 ; 16), the minimum number of push-ups for a player weighing 40 kg

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

An increase of 10 push-ups
