## Question (2 marks)

At the beginning of the season, the coach used the least squares regression line ŷ = 39,46 - 0,59x as the minimum target for a player to aim for. Determine the maximum possible increase in the number of push-ups that a team member must obtain to reach the minimum target. (In the scatter plot, the point (40 ; 6) lies furthest below the regression line.)

## Method

residual from regression line

## Memo working

The player at (40 ; 6) lies furthest below the least squares regression line, so this player needs the largest increase. The minimum target for a player weighing 40 kg is ŷ = 39,46 - 0,59(40) ≈ 16 push-ups, the point (40 ; 16) on the line. An increase of 16 - 6 = 10 push-ups will get that team member to the minimum target.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

An increase of 10 push-ups
