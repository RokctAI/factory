## Question (2 marks)

The minimum and maximum times taken for an employee to travel to work are 5 and 120 minutes respectively. On the scaled line in the ANSWER BOOK, draw a box and whisker diagram to indicate the distribution of the data as represented in the ogive above.

## Method

box and whisker diagram

## Memo working

Box from 44 to 84 with median at 65; whiskers ending at 5 and 120

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Box and whisker diagram with five-number summary 5 ; 44 ; 65 ; 84 ; 120
