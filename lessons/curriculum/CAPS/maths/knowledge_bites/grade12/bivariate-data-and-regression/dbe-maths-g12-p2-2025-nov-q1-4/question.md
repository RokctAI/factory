## Question (1 mark)

Use the answer to QUESTION 1.1 to write down the estimated average yearly decrease in the selling price of these 11 cars. (Regression line from QUESTION 1.1: ŷ = 331 397,20 - 22 988,32x, where x is the age of the car in years.)

## Method

interpret regression gradient

## Memo working

The gradient b = -22 988,32 means the predicted price drops by 22 988,32 rand for every extra year of age. So the average decrease per year is R22 988,32 (the magnitude of the gradient b).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R22 988,32 (average decrease per year)
