## Question (2 marks)

Predict the selling price of a similar car at this car dealership that is 5 years old. (Regression line from QUESTION 1.1: ŷ = 331 397,20 - 22 988,32x, where x is the age of the car in years.)

## Method

regression prediction

## Memo working

Substitute x = 5 into the regression line: ŷ = 331 397,20 - 22 988,32(5) = 216 455,60. (Memo alternative: ŷ = 216 455,61 straight from the calculator earns both marks.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R216 455,60
