## Question (1 mark)

Write down the mean number of push-ups for the given data (push-ups per minute: 26, 21, 6, 20, 16, 26, 23, 7, 18, 14, 17, 8, 28, 25, 20).

## Method

mean calculator

## Memo working

The sum of the 15 y-values is 275, so the mean is 275 ÷ 15 = 18,33 (this is also read directly from the calculator's statistics output).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

ȳ = 18,33
