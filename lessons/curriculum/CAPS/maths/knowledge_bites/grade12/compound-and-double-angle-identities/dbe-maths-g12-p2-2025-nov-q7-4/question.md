## Question (1 mark)

Write down the range of h. (From QUESTION 7.3: h(x) = -sin 2x.)

## Method

range of sinusoid

## Memo working

h(x) = -sin 2x is a sine curve with amplitude 1 about the line y = 0; reflecting and compressing a sine graph does not change how high or low it reaches, so the output still runs from -1 up to 1.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

y ∈ [-1 ; 1]  OR  -1 ≤ y ≤ 1
