## Question (6 marks)

Prove that: [tan(180° - x)](1 - cos^2 x) + cos^2 x = ((sin x - cos x)(1 + sin x.cos x))/(-cos x)

## Method

identity proof difference of cubes

## Memo working

LHS = (-tan x)(sin^2 x) + cos^2 x = -(sin x/cos x)(sin^2 x) + cos^2 x = -sin^3 x/cos x + cos^2 x = (sin^3 x - cos^3 x)/(-cos x) = (sin x - cos x)(sin^2 x + sin x.cos x + cos^2 x)/(-cos x) = (sin x - cos x)(1 + sin x.cos x)/(-cos x) = RHS

(Memo note: Alternative memo method works from RHS: expand (sin x - cos x)(cos^2 x + sin^2 x + sin x.cos x), simplify to (sin^3 x - cos^3 x)/(-cos x), split the fraction and use quotient and square identities to reach LHS.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

LHS = RHS (identity proved)
