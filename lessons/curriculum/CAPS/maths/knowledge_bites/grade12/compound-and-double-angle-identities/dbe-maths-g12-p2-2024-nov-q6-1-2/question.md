## Question (6 marks)

Hence, or otherwise, show that: [cos(90° - x)cos y + sin(-y)cos(180° + x)] / [cos x cos(360° + y) + sin(360° - x)sin y] = tan(x + y). (Context from Q6.1.1: cos(x + y) = cos x cos y - sin x sin y.)

## Method

reduction formulae identity

## Memo working

cos(90° - x) = sin x; sin(-y) = -sin y; cos(180° + x) = -cos x; cos(360° + y) = cos y; sin(360° - x) = -sin x; LHS = [sin x cos y + cos x sin y]/[cos x cos y - sin x sin y] = sin(x + y)/cos(x + y) = tan(x + y)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

LHS = tan(x + y) = RHS
