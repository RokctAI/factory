## Question (4 marks)

Given: (sin(540° + x).cos(90° + x))/sin(-x). Simplify the expression above fully to a single trigonometric ratio.

## Method

reduction formulae

## Memo working

sin(540° + x) = -sin x; cos(90° + x) = -sin x; sin(-x) = -sin x; expression = (-sin x)(-sin x)/(-sin x) = -sin x

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

-sin x
