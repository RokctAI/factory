## Question (4 marks)

Given: (sin(540° + x).cos(90° + x))/sin(-x). Simplify the expression above fully to a single trigonometric ratio.

## Method

reduction formulae

## Memo working

Reduce each factor one at a time. For the first factor, 540° is a full revolution plus 180°, so sin(540° + x) = sin(180° + x) = -sin x. For the second factor, the co-function shift gives cos(90° + x) = -sin x. In the denominator, sine is an odd function, so sin(-x) = -sin x. The expression becomes (-sin x)(-sin x)/(-sin x): the numerator is sin^2 x, and dividing by -sin x cancels one factor of sin x and flips the sign, leaving -sin x.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

-sin x
