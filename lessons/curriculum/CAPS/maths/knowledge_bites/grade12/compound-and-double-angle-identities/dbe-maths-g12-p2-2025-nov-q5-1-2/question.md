## Question (5 marks)

Express in terms of k:  (2 sin 25°.cos 25°)/(-2 + 4 sin^2 25°)

## Method

double angle identities

## Memo working

Numerator = sin 50°; denominator = -2(1 - 2 sin^2 25°) = -2 cos 50°; expression = sin 50°/(-2 cos 50°) = -(1/2)tan 50° = -(1/2)k

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

-(1/2)k
