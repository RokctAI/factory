## Question (5 marks)

Express in terms of k:  (2 sin 25°.cos 25°)/(-2 + 4 sin^2 25°)  (Given, from QUESTION 5.1: tan 50° = k.)

## Method

double angle identities

## Memo working

The numerator is a double angle: 2 sin 25°.cos 25° = sin 50°. Factorise the denominator: -2 + 4 sin^2 25° = -2(1 - 2 sin^2 25°) = -2 cos 50° (double angle for cosine). So the expression = sin 50°/(-2 cos 50°) = -(1/2)tan 50° = -(1/2)k.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

-(1/2)k
