## Question (4 marks)

If cos α = p, express the following expression in terms of p:  [cos(α/2 - 45°) sin(α/2 - 45°)] / 2

## Method

double angle and cofunction

## Memo working

Multiply by 2/2: = [2cos(α/2 - 45°) sin(α/2 - 45°)]/(2·2) = sin(α - 90°)/4 = -cos α / 4 = -p/4

(Memo note: Alternative memo method expands both factors with compound angles and special angles, reaching -cos α / 4.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

-p/4  OR  -1/4 p
