## Question (1 mark)

In the diagram, the graph of f(x) = tan x is drawn for the interval x ∈ [-180° ; 180°]. (Diagram: standard tan graph with dashed vertical asymptotes at x = -90° and x = 90°, passing through (-180° ; 0), (0 ; 0) and (180° ; 0); y-axis marked from -3 to 3.) Write down the equation of the asymptote of f in the interval x ∈ [0° ; 180°].

## Method

asymptote of tan

## Memo working

x = 90°

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x = 90°
