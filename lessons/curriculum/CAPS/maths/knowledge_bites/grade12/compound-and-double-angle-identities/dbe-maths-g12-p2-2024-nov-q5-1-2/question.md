## Question (2 marks)

Determine, without using a calculator, the value of cos 2A.

## Method

double angle formula

## Memo working

cos 2A = 2cos^2 A - 1 = 2(-3/5)^2 - 1 = -7/25

(Memo note: Memo also accepts cos^2 A - sin^2 A or 1 - 2sin^2 A forms.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

cos 2A = -7/25
