## Question (1 mark)

Graph f is translated 45° to the left to form graph h. Determine the equation of h in its simplest form.

## Method

horizontal translation co function

## Memo working

h(x) = cos 2(x + 45°) = cos(2x + 90°) = -sin 2x

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

h(x) = -sin 2x
