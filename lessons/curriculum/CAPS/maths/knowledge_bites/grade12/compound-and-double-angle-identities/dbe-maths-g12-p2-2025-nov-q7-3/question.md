## Question (1 mark)

Graph f is translated 45° to the left to form graph h. Determine the equation of h in its simplest form. (From QUESTION 7: f(x) = cos 2x.)

## Method

horizontal translation co function

## Memo working

A translation 45° to the left replaces x with x + 45°: h(x) = cos 2(x + 45°) = cos(2x + 90°). By the co-function reduction, cos(90° + angle) = -sin(angle), so cos(2x + 90°) = -sin 2x.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

h(x) = -sin 2x
