## Question (3 marks)

On the set of axes provided in the ANSWER BOOK, draw the graph of g(x) = tan 2x - 1 for x ∈ [-135° ; 135°].

## Method

sketch tan graph

## Memo working

Memo sketch awards: asymptotes; shape; intercepts with axes. Key points: y-intercept (0 ; -1); x-intercepts where tan 2x = 1 (x = 22,5°, 112,5°, -67,5°); vertical asymptotes where 2x = ±90° + k.180° (x = ±45°, ±135°)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

tan graph with period 90° shifted 1 unit down: vertical asymptotes at x = ±45° and x = ±135°; y-intercept (0 ; -1); x-intercepts at x = -67,5°, 22,5° and 112,5°
