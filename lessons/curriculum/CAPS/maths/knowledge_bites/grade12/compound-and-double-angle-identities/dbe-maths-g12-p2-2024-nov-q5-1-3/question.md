## Question (4 marks)

Determine, without using a calculator, the value of sin(A - B), if it is further given that sin B = 4/5 and 90° < B < 360°.

## Method

compound angle formula

## Memo working

B in second quadrant (sin B > 0): cos B = -3/5 [memo sketch: point (x ; 4) with x = -3]; sin(A - B) = sin A cos B - cos A sin B = (-4/5)(-3/5) - (-3/5)(4/5) = 12/25 + 12/25 = 24/25

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

sin(A - B) = 24/25
