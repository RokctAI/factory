## Question (4 marks)

Use the graphs to determine the general solution of 2cos^3 x - sin x = 0.

## Method

graphical intersection general solution

## Memo working

2cos^3 x = sin x; divide by cos x: 2cos^2 x = tan x; 2cos^2 x - 1 = tan x - 1; cos 2x + 1 = tan x, i.e. g(x) = f(x); graphs intersect at x = 45°; general solution x = 45° + k.180°, k ∈ Z

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x = 45° + k.180°, k ∈ Z
