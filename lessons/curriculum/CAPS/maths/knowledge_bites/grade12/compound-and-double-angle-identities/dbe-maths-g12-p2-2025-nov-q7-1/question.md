## Question (1 mark)

In the diagram, the graph of f(x) = cos 2x is drawn for x ∈ [-135° ; 135°]. [Figure: cosine curve with maximum (0 ; 1), zeros at ±45° and minima at ±90°, drawn from -135° to 135°.] Write down the period of f.

## Method

period of cos bx

## Memo working

Period = 360°/2 = 180°

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

180°
