## Question (1 mark)

In the diagram, the graph of f(x) = cos 2x is drawn for x ∈ [-135° ; 135°] (cosine curve with maximum (0 ; 1), zeros at ±45° and minima at ±90°). Write down the period of f.

## Method

period of cos bx

## Memo working

For y = cos bx the period is 360° divided by b. Here b = 2, so the period = 360°/2 = 180°: the graph completes a full wave every 180°.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

180°
