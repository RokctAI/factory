## Question (3 marks)

On the grid given in the ANSWER BOOK, draw the graph of g(x) = cos 2x + 1 for the interval x ∈ [-180° ; 180°]. Clearly show the intercepts with the axes as well as the coordinates of the turning points.

## Method

sketch trig graph

## Memo working

Memo sketch awards: turning points on x-axis at x = -90° and 90°; correct shape; turning point on y-axis at (0 ; 2)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Cosine curve between y = 0 and y = 2: maxima at (-180° ; 2), (0 ; 2), (180° ; 2); minima on the x-axis at x = -90° and x = 90°
