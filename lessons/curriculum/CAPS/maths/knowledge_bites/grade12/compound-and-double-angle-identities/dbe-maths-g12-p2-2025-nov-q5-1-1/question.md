## Question (2 marks)

It is given that tan 50° = k. Express EACH of the following in terms of k:  cos 40°

## Method

right triangle co function

## Memo working

Right triangle with tan 50° = k/1; hypotenuse r = sqrt(k^2 + 1) (Pythagoras); cos 40° = sin 50° = k/sqrt(k^2 + 1)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

cos 40° = k/sqrt(k^2 + 1)
