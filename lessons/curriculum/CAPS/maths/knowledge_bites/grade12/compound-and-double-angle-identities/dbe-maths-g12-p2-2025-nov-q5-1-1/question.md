## Question (2 marks)

It is given that tan 50° = k. Express EACH of the following in terms of k:  cos 40°

## Method

right triangle co function

## Memo working

Draw a right triangle with a 50° angle where tan 50° = k/1: the side opposite 50° is k and the adjacent side is 1. By Pythagoras the hypotenuse is r = sqrt(k^2 + 1). Since 40° and 50° are complementary, cos 40° = sin 50° = opposite/hypotenuse = k/sqrt(k^2 + 1).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

cos 40° = k/sqrt(k^2 + 1)
