## Question (2 marks)

Given the identity: cos(x - y) = cos x cos y + sin x sin y. Use the compound angle identity given above to derive a formula for cos(x + y).

## Method

derive compound angle

## Memo working

cos(x + y) = cos(x - (-y)) = cos x cos(-y) + sin x sin(-y) = cos x cos y - sin x sin y

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

cos(x + y) = cos x cos y - sin x sin y
