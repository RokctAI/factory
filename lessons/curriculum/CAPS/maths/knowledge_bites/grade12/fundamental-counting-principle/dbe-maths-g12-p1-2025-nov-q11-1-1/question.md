## Question (3 marks)

A survey was conducted among female and male learners at a school about which type of cold drink they preferred. The data from the survey is presented in the table below. [Table: rows Female (JUICE a, ENERGY DRINKS b, TOTAL c), Male (JUICE 36, ENERGY DRINKS 54, TOTAL f), Total (JUICE e, ENERGY DRINKS d, TOTAL 210).] The events male and preferring juice are independent. Show that e = 84.

## Method

independence condition solve

## Memo working

P(Male and Prefer juice) = P(Male) × P(Prefer juice); 36/210 = (90/210)(e/210); e = 84

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

e = 84
