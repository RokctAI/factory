## Question (4 marks)

Calculate the probability that TWO OR MORE runners finish the race after Andrew and before Bongi.

## Method

count gap arrangements probability

## Memo working

Favourable outcomes: Andrew and Bongi separated by 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6 runners: 5×6! + 4×6! + 3×6! + 2×6! + 1×6! = 6!(15); P = 6!(15)/8! = 15/56 = 0,27

(Memo note: Alternative memo method (complement): [8! - (7!·2 + 2·6·6!)]/(8!·2) = 21 600/80 640 = 15/56.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

15/56 = 0,27
