## Question (3 marks)

Calculate the probability that a female learner, chosen at random from the group, will like energy drinks.

## Method

two way table probability

## Memo working

d = 210 - 84 = 126; b = 126 - 54 = 72; P = 72/210 = 12/35 = 0,34

(Memo note: Alternative memo method: c = 120, b = 72, then P = 72/210.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

P(Female and like energy drink) = 72/210 = 12/35 = 0,34
