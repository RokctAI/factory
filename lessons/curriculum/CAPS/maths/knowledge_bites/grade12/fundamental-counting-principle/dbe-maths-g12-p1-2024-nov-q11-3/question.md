## Question (4 marks)

Determine if the events: sitting for examinations in Mathematics and sitting for examinations in Tourism are independent. Support your answer with the necessary calculations.

## Method

independence test

## Memo working

P(M) × P(T) = (22/40)(16/40) = 11/50 = 0,22; P(M and T) = 7/40 = 0,175; P(M) × P(T) ≠ P(M and T), so not independent

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Events M and T are not independent
