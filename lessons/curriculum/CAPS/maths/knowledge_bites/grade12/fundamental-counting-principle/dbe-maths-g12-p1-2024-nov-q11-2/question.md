## Question (2 marks)

Calculate the probability that a learner, chosen at random, will sit for examinations in at least TWO of the subjects.

## Method

probability from venn

## Memo working

P = (4 + 2 + 5 + 3)/40 = 14/40 = 0,35

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

P(at least two subjects) = 14/40 = 7/20 = 0,35
