## Question (4 marks)

At a kiosk, 120 people buy either a cup of coffee or a bottle of water. The chance of rain on any given day is 75%. The chance of a person buying a cup of coffee on a rainy day is three times the chance of the person buying coffee on a non-rainy day. The probability of a person buying coffee on any given day is 7/12. Calculate the number of cups of coffee that will be sold on a non-rainy day.

## Method

tree diagram total probability

## Memo working

(3/4)(3x) + (1/4)x = 7/12; 27x + 3x = 7 (×36); 30x = 7; x = 7/30; 28 cups

(Memo note: Alternative memo methods work with counts: (75/100)(3x/120) + (25/100)(x/120) = 7/12 -> x = 28 cups; or with 120 - x bottles of water giving x = 92 water, so 28 cups of coffee.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

28 cups
