## Question (2 marks)

A company generates a 4-character code using the 26 letters of the alphabet and the 10 digits, from 0 to 9. The code is in the form: letter digit letter digit. Determine how many different codes can be formed if letters and digits may be repeated.

## Method

fundamental counting principle

## Memo working

26 × 10 × 26 × 10 = 67 600

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

67 600
