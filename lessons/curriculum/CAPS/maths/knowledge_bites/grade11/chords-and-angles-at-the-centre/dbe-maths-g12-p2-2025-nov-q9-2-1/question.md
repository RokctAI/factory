## Question (2 marks)

In the diagram, O is the centre of the circle. POF is the diameter of the circle and MF is a tangent to the circle at F. OM cuts the circle at L. P̂ = 32°. (In the figure, P is at the top and F at the bottom of the diameter; chord PL runs to lower-left point L; OM passes through L to the external point M at bottom-left; the angles at O are labelled 1 above OM and 2 below OM.) Calculate, with reasons, the size of: Ô2

## Method

central angle twice inscribed

## Memo working

The centre angle Ô2 and the circumference angle P̂ both stand on the same arc LF. By the theorem that the angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference: Ô2 = 2 × 32° = 64°. (Alternative memo method: PL̂O = P̂ = 32°, angles opposite equal radii in triangle OPL; then Ô2 = 32° + 32° = 64° as the exterior angle of the triangle.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Ô2 = 64°
