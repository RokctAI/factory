## Question (4 marks)

Solve for x:  2.2^(2x) - 9.2^x + 4 = 0

## Method

exponential equation factorise

## Memo working

(2.2^x - 1)(2^x - 4) = 0; 2^x = 1/2 or 2^x = 4; 2^x = 2^(-1) or 2^x = 2^2; x = -1 or x = 2

(Memo note: Alternative memo method substitutes k = 2^x: 2k^2 - 9k + 4 = 0; (2k - 1)(k - 4) = 0; k = 1/2 or k = 4.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x = -1 or x = 2
