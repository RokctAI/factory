## Question (4 marks)

Solve for x:  8x^2 > 2x

## Method

quadratic inequality

## Memo working

Bring everything to one side: 8x^2 - 2x > 0. Factorise: 2x(4x - 1) > 0. The critical values are x = 0 and x = 1/4. The expression is a concave-up parabola in x, so it is positive outside its roots and negative between them. Sign analysis: for x < 0 both factors are negative, so the product is positive; between 0 and 1/4 the product is negative; for x > 1/4 both factors are positive, so the product is positive. Therefore x < 0 or x > 1/4.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x < 0 or x > 1/4
