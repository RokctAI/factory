## Question (2 marks)

Solve for x:  x(2x + 1) = 0

## Method

zero product property

## Memo working

x(2x+1)=0 -> x=0 or 2x+1=0 -> x=-1/2

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x = 0 or x = -1/2
