## Question (5 marks)

Solve for x and y simultaneously:  2x + y = 3  and  y^2 + xy = 2

## Method

simultaneous substitution

## Memo working

From the linear equation: y = 3 - 2x. Substitute into the second equation: (3 - 2x)^2 + (3 - 2x)x = 2. Expand and simplify: 2x^2 - 9x + 7 = 0. Factorise: (2x - 7)(x - 1) = 0 -> x = 7/2 or x = 1. Substitute back: y = 3 - 2(7/2) = -4 or y = 3 - 2(1) = 1. (Memo alternative: solve for y first via y^2 + 3y - 4 = 0, giving y = -4 or y = 1.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x = 7/2 or x = 1; y = -4 or y = 1
