## Question (2 marks)

Solve for x:  (x + 5)(x - 2) = 0

## Method

zero product property

## Memo working

The product of two factors is zero, so at least one factor must be zero: x + 5 = 0 or x - 2 = 0. This gives x = -5 or x = 2.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x = -5 or x = 2
