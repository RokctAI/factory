## Question (6 marks)

Calculate the values of x and y if: x is the sum of 2 and y; Five times the product of x and y, is 6 more than the square of x

## Method

simultaneous substitution

## Memo working

x = y + 2 ...(1); 5xy = x^2 + 6 ...(2); substitute: 5(y+2)y = (y+2)^2 + 6; 4y^2 + 6y - 10 = 0; 2y^2 + 3y - 5 = 0; (2y + 5)(y - 1) = 0; y = -5/2 or y = 1; x = -1/2 or x = 3

(Memo note: Alternative memo method substitutes y = x - 2 into 5xy - 6 = x^2: 2x^2 - 5x - 3 = 0; (2x + 1)(x - 3) = 0.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x = -1/2 or x = 3; y = -5/2 or y = 1
