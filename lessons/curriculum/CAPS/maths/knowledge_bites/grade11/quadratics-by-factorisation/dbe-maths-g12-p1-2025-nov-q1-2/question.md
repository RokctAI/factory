## Question (6 marks)

Calculate the values of x and y if: x is the sum of 2 and y; Five times the product of x and y, is 6 more than the square of x

## Method

simultaneous substitution

## Memo working

Translate the sentences into equations: x = y + 2 ...(1) and 5xy = x^2 + 6 ...(2). Substitute (1) into (2): 5(y + 2)y = (y + 2)^2 + 6. Expand both sides: 5y^2 + 10y = y^2 + 4y + 4 + 6, so 4y^2 + 6y - 10 = 0, which divides by 2 to give 2y^2 + 3y - 5 = 0. Factorise: (2y + 5)(y - 1) = 0, so y = -5/2 or y = 1. Substitute back into (1): y = -5/2 gives x = -1/2, and y = 1 gives x = 3. (Memo alternative: substitute y = x - 2 into 5xy - 6 = x^2 to get 2x^2 - 5x - 3 = 0; (2x + 1)(x - 3) = 0.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x = -1/2 or x = 3; y = -5/2 or y = 1
