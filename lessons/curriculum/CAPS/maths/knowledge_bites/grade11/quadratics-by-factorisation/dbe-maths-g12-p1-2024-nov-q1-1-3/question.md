## Question (4 marks)

Solve for x:  x^2 - 2x - 3 > 0

## Method

quadratic inequality

## Memo working

(x-3)(x+1) > 0; critical values x = 3 or x = -1; sign analysis -> x < -1 or x > 3. Answer only: 3/4

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x < -1 or x > 3  OR  x ∈ (-∞ ; -1) ∪ (3 ; ∞)
