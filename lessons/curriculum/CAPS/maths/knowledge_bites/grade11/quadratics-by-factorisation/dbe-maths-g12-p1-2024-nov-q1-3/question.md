## Question (3 marks)

Consider the product (1 + 1/2)(1 + 1/3)(1 + 1/4)... Each factor in the product is of the form (1 + 1/n) for n >= 2. Determine ALL the values of n for which the product will be an integer value.

## Method

telescoping product

## Memo working

Product = (3/2)(4/3)(5/4)...((n+1)/n) = (n+1)/2. Integer when n+1 is divisible by 2, i.e. n odd, n > 2.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

n ∈ {3; 5; 7; ...}; n must be an odd number, greater than 2
