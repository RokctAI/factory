## Question (2 marks)

Calculate the length of AC.

## Method

pythagoras

## Memo working

AC^2 = AB^2 + BC^2 = 18^2 + 36^2 (Pythagoras); AC = √1620 = 18√5 = 40,25 cm

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

AC = √1620 = 18√5 = 40,25 cm
