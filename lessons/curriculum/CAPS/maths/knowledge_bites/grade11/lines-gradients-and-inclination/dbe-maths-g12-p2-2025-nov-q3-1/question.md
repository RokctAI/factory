## Question (2 marks)

In the diagram, P(-1 ; 8), Q(-4 ; -6) and R(12 ; 2) are the vertices of ΔPQR. The angle of inclination of QR is θ. (Figure: triangle PQR with P above the x-axis left of the y-axis, Q in the third quadrant, R in the first quadrant; θ marked at the x-axis where QR crosses it.) Calculate the length of QR. Leave your answer in simplified surd form.

## Method

distance formula

## Memo working

Apply the distance formula to Q(-4 ; -6) and R(12 ; 2): QR = sqrt((-4 - 12)^2 + (-6 - 2)^2) = sqrt(256 + 64) = √320 = 8√5.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

QR = √320 = 8√5 units
