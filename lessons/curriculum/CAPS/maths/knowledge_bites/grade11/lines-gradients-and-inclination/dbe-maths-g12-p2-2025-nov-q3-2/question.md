## Question (2 marks)

Calculate the gradient of QR. (From QUESTION 3: P(-1 ; 8), Q(-4 ; -6) and R(12 ; 2) are the vertices of ΔPQR.)

## Method

gradient formula

## Memo working

Gradient is change in y over change in x between Q(-4 ; -6) and R(12 ; 2): m_QR = (-6 - 2)/(-4 - 12) = -8/-16 = 1/2.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

m_QR = 1/2
