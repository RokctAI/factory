## Question (5 marks)

T is a point on QR such that PT ⊥ QR. Calculate the coordinates of T. (From QUESTION 3: P(-1 ; 8), Q(-4 ; -6) and R(12 ; 2); from QUESTION 3.4 the equation of QR is y = (1/2)x - 4.)

## Method

foot of perpendicular intersection

## Memo working

PT ⊥ QR, so m_PT = -2 (negative reciprocal of 1/2). Equation of PT through P(-1 ; 8): 8 = -2(-1) + c -> c = 6, so y = -2x + 6. Intersect with QR's equation y = (1/2)x - 4: -2x + 6 = (1/2)x - 4 -> 10 = (5/2)x -> 5x = 20 -> x = 4; then y = (1/2)(4) - 4 = -2. So T(4 ; -2). (Memo alternative: PQ = PR = √205, so ΔPQR is isosceles and the perpendicular height bisects the base QR; T is the midpoint of QR = (4 ; -2).)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

T(4 ; -2)
