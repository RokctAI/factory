## Question (2 marks)

Calculate the size of θ. (From QUESTION 3: θ is the angle of inclination of QR, and from QUESTION 3.2 the gradient of QR is 1/2.)

## Method

inclination from gradient

## Memo working

The gradient of a line equals the tangent of its angle of inclination: tan θ = m_QR = 1/2, so θ = 26,57°.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

θ = 26,57°
