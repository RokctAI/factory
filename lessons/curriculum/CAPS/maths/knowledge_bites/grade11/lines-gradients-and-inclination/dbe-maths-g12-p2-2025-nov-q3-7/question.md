## Question (3 marks)

Calculate the area of parallelogram PQRS. (From QUESTION 3: P(-1 ; 8), Q(-4 ; -6), R(12 ; 2), S(15 ; 16); from QUESTION 3.1 QR = 8√5; from QUESTION 3.6 T(4 ; -2) is the foot of the perpendicular from P to QR.)

## Method

base times height

## Memo working

The height of the parallelogram on base QR is PT: PT = sqrt((4 - (-1))^2 + (-2 - 8)^2) = sqrt(25 + 100) = √125 = 5√5 = 11,18 units. Area of parallelogram = base × height: area PQRS = QR·PT = (8√5)(5√5) = 200 units^2. (Memo alternative: area ΔPQR = (1/2)(8√5)(5√5) = 100 units^2; area PQRS = 2 × 100 = 200 units^2.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

200 units^2
