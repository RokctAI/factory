## Question (2 marks)

Calculate the length of diagonal KC. (From QUESTION 8: BKLC is the horizontal base with KB̂C = 104° and BK̂C = 52,6°; BC = 36 cm.)

## Method

sine rule

## Memo working

In triangle BKC we know two angles and the side BC = 36 cm, which lies opposite the angle at K. The side KC lies opposite the angle at B. By the sine rule: KC/sin KB̂C = BC/sin BK̂C, so KC/sin 104° = 36/sin 52,6°. Therefore KC = 36 sin 104°/sin 52,6° = 43,97 cm.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

KC = 43,97 cm
