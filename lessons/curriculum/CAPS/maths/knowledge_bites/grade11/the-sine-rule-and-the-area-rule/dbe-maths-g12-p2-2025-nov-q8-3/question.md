## Question (2 marks)

Calculate the length of diagonal KC.

## Method

sine rule

## Memo working

KC/sin KB̂C = BC/sin BK̂C; KC/sin 104° = 36/sin 52,6°; KC = 36 sin 104°/sin 52,6° = 43,97 cm

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

KC = 43,97 cm
