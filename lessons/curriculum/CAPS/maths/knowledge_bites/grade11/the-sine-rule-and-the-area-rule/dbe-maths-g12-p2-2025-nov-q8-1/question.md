## Question (2 marks)

As part of a school project, learners are required to design a portable stage for a puppet show. The design must fulfil the following requirements: BKLC is a horizontal base having KB̂C = 104° and BK̂C = 52,6°; the rectangular backdrop, ABCD, is vertical to the horizontal base and must have an area of 648 cm^2; the sides of ABCD must be in the ratio AB : BC = 1 : 2; the stage must be partly enclosed with triangular sides ABK and DCL. (In the 3-D drawing, the vertical rectangle ABCD — A top-left, D top-right, B bottom-left, C bottom-right — stands on the shaded horizontal base BKLC, with K front-left, L front-right and diagonal KC drawn on the base.) Show that AB = 18 cm.

## Method

area ratio equation

## Memo working

The area of rectangle ABCD is length times breadth, BC × AB. The ratio AB : BC = 1 : 2 means BC = 2AB, so the area condition becomes 648 = 2AB × AB, that is 2AB^2 = 648. Then AB^2 = 324, and taking the positive square root gives AB = 18 cm.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

AB = 18 cm
