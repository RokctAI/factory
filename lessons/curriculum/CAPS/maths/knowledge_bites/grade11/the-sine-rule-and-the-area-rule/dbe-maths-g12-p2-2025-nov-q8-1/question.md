## Question (2 marks)

As part of a school project, learners are required to design a portable stage for a puppet show, as shown in the diagram below. The design must fulfil the following requirements: BKLC is a horizontal base having KB̂C = 104° and BK̂C = 52,6°; The rectangular backdrop, ABCD, is vertical to the horizontal base and must have an area of 648 cm^2; The sides of ABCD must be in the ratio AB : BC = 1 : 2; The stage must be partly enclosed with triangular sides ABK and DCL. [Figure: 3-D drawing of vertical rectangle ABCD (A top-left, D top-right, B bottom-left, C bottom-right) standing on shaded horizontal quadrilateral base BKLC, with K front-left and L front-right; angle at B is 104° and angle at K is 52,6°; diagonal KC drawn on the base.] Show that AB = 18 cm.

## Method

area ratio equation

## Memo working

Area of ABCD = BC × AB; 648 = 2AB × AB; AB^2 = 324; AB = 18 cm

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

AB = 18 cm
