## Question (2 marks)

Calculate the length of AC. (From QUESTION 8.1: ABCD is a vertical rectangle with AB = 18 cm and BC = 36 cm; AC is its diagonal.)

## Method

pythagoras

## Memo working

AC is the diagonal of rectangle ABCD, so triangle ABC is right-angled at B. By Pythagoras: AC^2 = AB^2 + BC^2 = 18^2 + 36^2 = 324 + 1296 = 1620. Therefore AC = √1620 = 18√5 = 40,25 cm.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

AC = √1620 = 18√5 = 40,25 cm
