## Question (3 marks)

Calculate, with reasons, the size of: M̂ (From QUESTION 9.2: O is the centre of the circle, POF is the diameter, MF is a tangent to the circle at F, OM cuts the circle at L, P̂ = 32°, and from QUESTION 9.2.1: Ô2 = 64°.)

## Method

tangent perpendicular diameter triangle sum

## Memo working

A tangent is perpendicular to the diameter (radius) at the point of contact, so PF̂M = 90° — the tangent MF meets the diameter POF at F at a right angle. Now look at triangle OFM: it contains the right angle at F and the angle Ô2 = 64° at O. The angles of a triangle sum to 180°, so M̂ = 180° - 90° - 64° = 26°.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

M̂ = 26°
