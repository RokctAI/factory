## Question (3 marks)

In the diagram, PQRS is a cyclic quadrilateral. T is a point on the circle such that QT is perpendicular to SR at V. PT and ST are drawn. Q̂2 = 35° and R̂ = Ŝ1. (In the figure the circle passes through P upper left, Q at the top, R on the right and S on the left; QT crosses SR at right angles at V; T is at the bottom of the circle; the angles at each vertex are numbered, with Q̂2 = 35° marked.) Calculate, with reasons, the size of QT̂S.

## Method

triangle sum same segment

## Memo working

Start in triangle QVR: the angle at V is 90° (QT ⊥ SR) and Q̂2 = 35°, so by the angle sum of a triangle R̂ = 180° - 90° - 35° = 55°. Now QT̂S and R̂ (that is, QR̂S) are angles in the same segment — both subtend chord QS from the same side — so QT̂S = R̂ = 55°. (Alternative memo method: Ŝ2 = 35°, angles in the same segment, then QT̂S = 55° by the angle sum in triangle SVT.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

QT̂S = 55°
