## Question (3 marks)

Prove that PQ || SR. (From QUESTION 10: PQRS is a cyclic quadrilateral, QT ⊥ SR at V, Q̂2 = 35°, R̂ = Ŝ1, and from QUESTION 10.1: R̂ = 55°.)

## Method

co interior angles supplementary

## Memo working

PQRS is a cyclic quadrilateral, so its opposite angles are supplementary: SP̂Q = 180° - R̂ = 180° - 55° = 125°. It is given that Ŝ1 = R̂ = 55°. Now SP̂Q and Ŝ1 lie between the lines PQ and SR on the same side of the transversal PS, and SP̂Q + Ŝ1 = 125° + 55° = 180°. Since the co-interior angles are supplementary, PQ || SR. (Alternative memo method: Ŝ1 = R̂ = 55° given; PQ̂R = 125°, opposite angles of the cyclic quadrilateral; Q̂1 = 125° - 35° = 90°; then Q̂1 + V̂1 = 180°, so PQ || SR by co-interior angles.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

PQ || SR
