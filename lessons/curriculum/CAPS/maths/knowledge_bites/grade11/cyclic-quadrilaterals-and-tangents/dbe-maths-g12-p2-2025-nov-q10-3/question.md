## Question (2 marks)

Prove that PT is a diameter of the circle. (From QUESTIONS 10.1 and 10.2: PQRS is a cyclic quadrilateral, QT ⊥ SR at V, and PQ || SR.)

## Method

converse angle in semicircle

## Memo working

Because PQ || SR and QT is a transversal meeting SR at 90°, the co-interior angles give Q̂1 = 180° - 90° = 90° — that is, PQ̂T = 90°. So the chord PT subtends a right angle at Q, a point on the circle. By the converse of the angle-in-a-semicircle theorem, a chord that subtends 90° at the circumference must be a diameter; therefore PT is a diameter. (Alternative memo method: Ŝ2 = 35° by the exterior angle of triangle SVT or the angle sum; then PŜT = 55° + 35° = 90°, so PT is a diameter by the converse angle in semi-circle.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

PT is a diameter
