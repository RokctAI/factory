## Question (3 marks)

The sequence of the first differences of a quadratic pattern is: 1 ; 3 ; 5 ; ... If T99 of this quadratic pattern is 9 632, calculate the value of T98.

## Method

first differences

## Memo working

The first differences themselves form the linear pattern Tn = 2n - 1. The difference between T98 and T99 of the quadratic pattern is the 98th first difference: 2(98) - 1 = 195. Step back from T99: T98 = 9 632 - 195 = 9 437

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

T98 = 9 437
