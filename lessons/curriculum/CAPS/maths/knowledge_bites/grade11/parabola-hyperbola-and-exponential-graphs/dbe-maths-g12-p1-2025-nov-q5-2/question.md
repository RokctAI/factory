## Question (1 mark)

Write down the range of f. (Concave-down parabola f whose turning point P is the intersection of the asymptotes of g(x) = -4/(x - 3) + 8)

## Method

range of parabola

## Memo working

P is the intersection of the asymptotes x = 3 and y = 8 of g, so P is the point (3 ; 8). Since f is concave down, P(3 ; 8) is a maximum turning point: the parabola takes every value up to and including 8, so the range is y ≤ 8.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

y ≤ 8
