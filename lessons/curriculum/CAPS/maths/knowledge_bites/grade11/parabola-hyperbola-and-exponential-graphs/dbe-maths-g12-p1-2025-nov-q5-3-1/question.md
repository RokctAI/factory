## Question (2 marks)

Determine the values of x for which g(x) ≤ f(x)  (Concave-down parabola f with maximum turning point P(3 ; 8); hyperbola g(x) = -4/(x - 3) + 8; the graphs intersect at D(5 ; 6))

## Method

graph inequality reading

## Memo working

Read the graphs: the lower branch of the hyperbola lies on or below the parabola only between the vertical asymptote x = 3 and the intersection point D(5 ; 6). To the left of the asymptote g sits above its horizontal asymptote y = 8, which is the maximum of f, and to the right of D the hyperbola climbs back above the falling parabola. So g(x) ≤ f(x) for 3 < x ≤ 5 (x = 3 excluded because g is undefined there; x = 5 included because the graphs are equal at D).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

3 < x ≤ 5  OR  x ∈ (3 ; 5]
