## Question (1 mark)

The graphs of g(x) = x + c and f(x) = a/(x + p) + q are drawn. Graph g and the vertical asymptote of f intersect at the x-axis. (Figure: increasing line g cutting the x-axis where the vertical asymptote of f meets it; hyperbola f with one branch above its horizontal asymptote to the right of the vertical asymptote and the other branch below it to the left.) Write down the coordinates of the x-intercept of g in terms of p.

## Method

read intercept from asymptote

## Memo working

The vertical asymptote of f(x) = a/(x + p) + q is the line x = -p. The question states that g cuts the x-axis exactly where this asymptote meets it, so the x-intercept of g is the point on the x-axis with x = -p.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

(-p ; 0)
