## Question (2 marks)

Determine the equation of the horizontal asymptote of f, the hyperbola f(x) = a/(x - 1) + q whose line of symmetry is g(x) = x - 3.

## Method

symmetry line through asymptote intersection

## Memo working

A hyperbola's axis of symmetry passes through the point where its asymptotes intersect. That point lies on the vertical asymptote x = 1, so its y-value is g(1) = 1 - 3 = -2, giving the horizontal asymptote y = -2. (Memo note: answer only earns 2/2.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

y = -2
