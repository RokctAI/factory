## Question (2 marks)

Determine the values of x for which f(x) < 6  (Concave-down parabola f with maximum turning point P(3 ; 8), passing through D(5 ; 6))

## Method

graph inequality reading

## Memo working

The parabola reaches the height y = 6 at x = 5 (point D). By symmetry about the axis x = 3, it is also at height 6 at x = 1, since 5 and 1 are equally far from 3. Between these two values the concave-down parabola is above 6 (its maximum is 8 at x = 3), so f(x) < 6 outside them: x < 1 or x > 5.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x < 1 or x > 5  OR  x ∈ (-∞ ; 1) or x ∈ (5 ; ∞)
