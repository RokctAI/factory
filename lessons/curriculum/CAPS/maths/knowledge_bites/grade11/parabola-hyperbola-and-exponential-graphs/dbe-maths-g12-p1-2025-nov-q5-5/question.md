## Question (6 marks)

The graphs of f(x) = -(1/2)x^2 + 3x + 7/2 and g(x) = -4/(x - 3) + 8 are drawn. M and T are x-intercepts of f and g respectively, with M left of the origin. Calculate the length of MT.

## Method

x intercepts distance

## Memo working

Find M from f: -(1/2)x^2 + 3x + 7/2 = 0. Multiply by -2 to clear the fraction: x^2 - 6x - 7 = 0, which factorises as (x - 7)(x + 1) = 0, so x = 7 or x = -1. M is the x-intercept left of the origin, so M(-1 ; 0). Find T from g: 0 = -4/(x - 3) + 8. Multiply through by (x - 3): -8x + 24 = -4, so 8x = 28 and x = 7/2, giving T(7/2 ; 0). Both points lie on the x-axis, so the length MT is the difference of the x-values: MT = 7/2 - (-1) = 7/2 + 1 = 9/2 = 4,5.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

MT = 9/2 = 4,5
