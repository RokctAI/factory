## Question (3 marks)

The graphs of f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c and g(x) = -4/(x - 3) + 8 are drawn. P is the turning point of f and also the point of intersection of the asymptotes of g. The graphs intersect at D(5 ; 6). (Figure: concave-down parabola f with turning point P on the vertical asymptote x = 3; hyperbola g whose asymptotes cross at P; f and g intersect at D(5 ; 6).) Show that the equation of the parabola is f(x) = -(1/2)x^2 + 3x + 7/2

## Method

turning point form substitute point

## Memo working

The asymptotes of g are x = 3 and y = 8, so their intersection — which is also the turning point of f — is P(3 ; 8). Write f in turning point form: y = a(x - 3)^2 + 8. Substitute the point D(5 ; 6): 6 = a(5 - 3)^2 + 8, so 4a = -2 and a = -1/2. Then y = -(1/2)(x - 3)^2 + 8 = -(1/2)x^2 + 3x - 9/2 + 8 = -(1/2)x^2 + 3x + 7/2.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

f(x) = -(1/2)x^2 + 3x + 7/2
