## Question (1 mark)

The graphs of f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c and g(x) = -4/(x - 3) + 8 are drawn below. P is the turning point of f and the point of intersection of the asymptotes of g. The graphs intersect at D(5 ; 6). M and T are x-intercepts of f and g respectively. (Figure: concave-down parabola f with x-intercept M left of the origin and turning point P on the dotted vertical asymptote x = 3; hyperbola g with branches above-left and below-right of the asymptote intersection, dotted horizontal asymptote through P, g cutting the x-axis at T; f and g intersect at D(5 ; 6).) Write down the domain of g.

## Method

domain of hyperbola

## Memo working

The hyperbola g(x) = -4/(x - 3) + 8 is undefined where its denominator is zero, that is at x = 3 (the vertical asymptote). Every other real x is allowed, so the domain is x ∈ R, x ≠ 3.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x ∈ R, x ≠ 3
