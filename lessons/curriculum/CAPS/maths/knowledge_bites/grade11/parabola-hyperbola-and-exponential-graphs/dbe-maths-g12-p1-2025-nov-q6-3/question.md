## Question (2 marks)

The graphs of g(x) = x - 2 and f(x) = 2/(x - 2) - 1 are drawn. Describe the transformation that g must undergo to become an axis of symmetry of f that cuts f at two points.

## Method

describe transformation

## Memo working

The asymptotes of f cross at (2 ; -1). The axis of symmetry of f with gradient +1 — the one that actually cuts the hyperbola in two points — passes through this centre point: y = x - 3. Graph g is y = x - 2, one unit above this line. Shifting g one unit down gives y = x - 3, and shifting g one unit to the right gives y = (x - 1) - 2 = x - 3 as well, so either translation works.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Translation of 1 unit down OR translation of 1 unit to the right
