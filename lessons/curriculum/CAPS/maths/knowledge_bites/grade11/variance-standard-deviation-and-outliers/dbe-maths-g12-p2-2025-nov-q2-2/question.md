## Question (5 marks)

There are 9 players in a basketball team. The coach calculated that on average, each player scored 12 points during a game. The points scored by 8 of the 9 players from the team is given below: 11, 14, 19, 20, 8, 10, 2, 14. How many players' points score was outside ONE standard deviation of the mean points score?

## Method

find missing value then standard deviation interval

## Memo working

First find the missing ninth score from the mean: (11 + 14 + 19 + 20 + 8 + 10 + 2 + 14 + x)/9 = 12 -> x + 98 = 108 -> x = 10 (the 9th player scored 10 points). Then use the calculator on all nine scores: σ = 5,23 (5,22812...). One standard deviation of the mean is the interval (x̄ - σ ; x̄ + σ) = (6,77 ; 17,23). Comparing each score against this interval, 3 players' points (19 ; 20 ; 2) were outside one standard deviation of the mean.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

3 players
