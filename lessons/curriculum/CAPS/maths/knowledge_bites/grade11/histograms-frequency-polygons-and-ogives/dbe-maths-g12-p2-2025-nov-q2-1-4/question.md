## Question (1 mark)

Comment on the skewness of the data. (The histogram from QUESTION 2.1.3 has bar heights 16, 24, 19, 8 and 3 across the intervals from 0 to 100 minutes.)

## Method

describe skewness

## Memo working

The bar heights rise to a peak of 24 in the second interval and then fall away — 19, then 8, then 3 — so the histogram tails off to the right.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Skewed to the right OR positively skewed
