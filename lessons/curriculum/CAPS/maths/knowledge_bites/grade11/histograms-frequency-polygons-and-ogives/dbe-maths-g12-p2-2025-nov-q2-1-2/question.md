## Question (2 marks)

How many people spent more than 40 and up to 80 minutes on the website? (Cumulative frequency table from QUESTION 2.1: 0 < t ≤ 20 -> 16; 0 < t ≤ 40 -> 40; 0 < t ≤ 60 -> 59; 0 < t ≤ 80 -> 67; 0 < t ≤ 100 -> 70.)

## Method

cumulative frequency difference

## Memo working

The cumulative frequency up to 80 minutes counts 67 people, and the cumulative frequency up to 40 minutes counts 40 people. Subtracting removes everyone at 40 minutes or below: number of people = 67 - 40 = 27.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

27
