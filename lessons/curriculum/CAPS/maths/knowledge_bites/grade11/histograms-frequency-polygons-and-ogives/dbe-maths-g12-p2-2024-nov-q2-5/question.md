## Question (2 marks)

The cumulative frequency graph (ogive) shows the time taken (in minutes) for 60 employees to travel to work each morning. (The ogive rises from (0 ; 0) in an S-shape through approximately (20 ; 5), (40 ; 13), (60 ; 26), (80 ; 42) and (100 ; 57), levelling off at (120 ; 60); the time axis runs from 0 to 140 minutes and the cumulative frequency axis from 0 to 65.) The company manager decided that all employees who travel for an hour or more will be allowed to work from home for part of the day. What percentage of the employees will be allowed to work from home for part of the day?

## Method

percentage from ogive

## Memo working

From the ogive, about 26 employees travel less than 60 minutes, so the number who travel 60 minutes or more is 60 - 26 = 34. Percentage = 34/60 × 100 = 56,67%. (The memo accepts either reading of the ogive: 34 or 35 employees, so 35/60 × 100 = 58,33% is also accepted.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

56,67% (using 34 employees) OR 58,33% (using 35 employees)
