## Question (1 mark)

The cumulative frequency graph (ogive) shows the time taken (in minutes) for 60 employees to travel to work each morning. (The ogive rises from (0 ; 0) in an S-shape through approximately (20 ; 5), (40 ; 13), (60 ; 26), (80 ; 42) and (100 ; 57), levelling off at (120 ; 60); the time axis runs from 0 to 140 minutes and the cumulative frequency axis from 0 to 65.) Estimate the median travel time.

## Method

median from ogive

## Memo working

There are 60 employees, so the median is the value at cumulative frequency 60 ÷ 2 = 30. Reading across from 30 on the cumulative frequency axis to the ogive and down to the time axis gives 65 minutes.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Median = 65
