## Question (2 marks)

The cumulative frequency graph (ogive) shows the time taken (in minutes) for 60 employees to travel to work each morning. (The ogive rises from (0 ; 0) in an S-shape through approximately (20 ; 5), (40 ; 13), (60 ; 26), (80 ; 42) and (100 ; 57), levelling off at (120 ; 60); the time axis runs from 0 to 140 minutes and the cumulative frequency axis from 0 to 65.) Estimate the interquartile range. (From the ogive, the lower quartile is 44 minutes.)

## Method

interquartile range

## Memo working

The upper quartile is the value at cumulative frequency 45 (three quarters of 60); reading off the ogive gives Q3 = 84 minutes. Then IQR = Q3 - Q1 = 84 - 44 = 40.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

IQR = 40
