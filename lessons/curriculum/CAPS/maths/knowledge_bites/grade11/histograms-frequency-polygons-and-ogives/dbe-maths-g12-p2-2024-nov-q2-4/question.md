## Question (2 marks)

The cumulative frequency graph (ogive) shows the time taken (in minutes) for 60 employees to travel to work each morning. (The ogive rises from (0 ; 0) in an S-shape through approximately (20 ; 5), (40 ; 13), (60 ; 26), (80 ; 42) and (100 ; 57), levelling off at (120 ; 60); the time axis runs from 0 to 140 minutes and the cumulative frequency axis from 0 to 65.) The minimum and maximum times taken for an employee to travel to work are 5 and 120 minutes respectively. On the scaled line in the ANSWER BOOK, draw a box and whisker diagram to indicate the distribution of the data as represented in the ogive above.

## Method

box and whisker diagram

## Memo working

Use the five-number summary read from the ogive: minimum 5, lower quartile 44, median 65, upper quartile 84, maximum 120. Draw the box from 44 to 84 with the median line at 65, and whiskers ending at 5 and 120.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Box and whisker diagram with five-number summary 5 ; 44 ; 65 ; 84 ; 120
