## Question (3 marks)

In the survey table of Q11.1.1 (210 learners; Male: JUICE 36, ENERGY DRINKS 54; juice total e = 84), calculate the probability that a female learner, chosen at random from the group, will like energy drinks.

## Method

two way table probability

## Memo working

Complete the table from the known totals. The energy-drinks total is d = 210 - 84 = 126. The female energy-drinks count is b = 126 - 54 = 72. The required probability is the number of female learners who like energy drinks out of the whole group: P = 72/210 = 12/35 = 0,34. (Alternative memo method: first find the female total c = 210 - 90 = 120, then b = 120 - (84 - 36) = 72, then P = 72/210.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

P(Female and like energy drink) = 72/210 = 12/35 = 0,34
