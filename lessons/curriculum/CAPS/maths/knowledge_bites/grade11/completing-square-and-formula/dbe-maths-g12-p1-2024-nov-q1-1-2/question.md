## Question (4 marks)

Solve for x:  2x^2 + 1 = 4x  (correct to TWO decimal places)

## Method

quadratic formula

## Memo working

Standard form 2x^2 - 4x + 1 = 0; x = (-(-4) ± sqrt((-4)^2 - 4(2)(1)))/(2(2)); x = 1,71 or x = 0,29. Answer only: 4/4

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x = 1,71 or x = 0,29
