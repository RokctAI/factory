## Question (4 marks)

Solve for x:  2x^2 + 1 = 4x  (correct to TWO decimal places)

## Method

quadratic formula

## Memo working

Write in standard form: 2x^2 - 4x + 1 = 0. Apply the quadratic formula with a = 2, b = -4, c = 1: x = (-(-4) ± sqrt((-4)^2 - 4(2)(1)))/(2(2)) = (4 ± sqrt(8))/4. This gives x = 1,71 or x = 0,29 (correct to two decimal places). (Memo note: answer only earns 4/4.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x = 1,71 or x = 0,29
