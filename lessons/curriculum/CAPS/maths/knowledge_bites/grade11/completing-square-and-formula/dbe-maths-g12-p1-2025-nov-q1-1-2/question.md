## Question (4 marks)

Solve for x:  5x^2 + 2 = -9x  (correct to TWO decimal places)

## Method

quadratic formula

## Memo working

Write in standard form: 5x^2 + 9x + 2 = 0. Apply the quadratic formula with a = 5, b = 9, c = 2: x = (-9 ± sqrt(9^2 - 4(5)(2)))/(2(5)) = (-9 ± sqrt(41))/10. Since sqrt(41) is approximately 6,40, this gives x = -0,26 or x = -1,54 (correct to two decimal places).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x = -0,26 or x = -1,54
