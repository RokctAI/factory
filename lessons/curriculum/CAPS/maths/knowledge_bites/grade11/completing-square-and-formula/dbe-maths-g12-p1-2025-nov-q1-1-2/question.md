## Question (4 marks)

Solve for x:  5x^2 + 2 = -9x  (correct to TWO decimal places)

## Method

quadratic formula

## Memo working

Standard form 5x^2 + 9x + 2 = 0; x = (-9 ± sqrt(9^2 - 4(5)(2)))/(2(5)) = (-9 ± sqrt(41))/10; x = -0,26 or x = -1,54

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x = -0,26 or x = -1,54
