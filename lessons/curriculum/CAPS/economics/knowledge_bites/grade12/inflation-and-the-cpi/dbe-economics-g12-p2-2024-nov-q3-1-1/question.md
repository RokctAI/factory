## Question (2 marks)

Name any TWO types of consumer inflation. (2 x 1)

## Method

recall listing

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Teachable steps: consumer inflation is measured on the prices households pay, and the syllabus distinguishes it by what is included in the basket — headline (the full CPI basket), core (the basket with volatile items such as food and energy stripped out) and administered prices (prices set by government or public entities, such as electricity). Any TWO of these earn 1 mark each.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Headline / CPI inflation
- Core
- Administered prices
