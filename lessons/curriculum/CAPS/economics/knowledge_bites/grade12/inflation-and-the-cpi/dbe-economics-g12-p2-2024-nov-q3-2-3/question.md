## Question (2 marks)

Briefly describe the term stagflation. (Refers to a cartoon titled IMPACT OF INFLATION ON THE CONSUMER, showing a man dragging a ball and chain labelled INFLATION.)

## Method

definition

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Teachable steps: the word combines stagnation and inflation, and a full definition must carry both sides — the stagnating economy (low growth, high unemployment) and the rising prices happening at the same time. Mentioning only one side is an incomplete definition.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Occurs when the economy experiences low levels of economic growth with high levels of unemployment as well as high rate of inflation.
