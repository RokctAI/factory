## Question (4 marks)

How can natural disasters contribute to inflation in the economy? (2 x 2) (Refers to a cartoon titled IMPACT OF INFLATION ON THE CONSUMER, showing a man dragging a ball and chain labelled INFLATION.)

## Method

explanation

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Teachable steps: natural disasters work on the supply side. Trace each disaster to either higher production costs (cost-push: rebuilding costs, damaged inputs get recovered through prices) or reduced supply of goods while demand stays the same, which pushes the general price level up. Give TWO developed links for 2 marks each.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Unfavourable weather conditions such as droughts, floods and global warming may increase the cost of production, resulting in an increase in prices for goods and services.
- Natural disasters may lead to unplanned expenditure such rebuilding cost by businesses which may be recovered by increasing the prices of goods and services.
- Natural disasters may reduce the supply of goods and services which may increase the general price level if demand remains the same.
