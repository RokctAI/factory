## Question (1 mark)

Identify a positive impact of inflation on the consumer in the cartoon.

(Cartoon titled IMPACT OF INFLATION ON THE CONSUMER: a man drags a huge ball and chain labelled INFLATION shackled to his ankle; three thought bubbles rise from the ball reading 'Less real debt value', 'Less real savings value' and 'Less real income value'.)

## Method

data response identification

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Teachable steps: two of the three bubbles are clearly bad for the consumer — savings and income losing real value. The remaining one, less real debt value, is the positive: inflation erodes the real burden of what a borrower owes, so debtors repay with money that is worth less.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Less real debt value
