## Question (1 mark)

Name the institution that uses interest rates to control inflation in South Africa. (Refers to a cartoon titled IMPACT OF INFLATION ON THE CONSUMER, showing a man dragging a ball and chain labelled INFLATION.)

## Method

recall

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Teachable steps: monetary policy — and specifically the repo rate — is the tool used against inflation, and monetary policy in South Africa is run by the central bank, not the government. The central bank is the South African Reserve Bank.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

South African Reserve Bank / SARB
