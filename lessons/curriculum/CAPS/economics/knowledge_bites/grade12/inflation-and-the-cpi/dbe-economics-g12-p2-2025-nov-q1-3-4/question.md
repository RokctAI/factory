## Question (1 mark)

Give ONE term: A general rise in prices caused by increases in the cost of the factors of production.

## Method

terminology recall

The cause named is rising costs of the factors of production — higher costs push prices up from the supply side. That is cost-push inflation, as opposed to demand-pull inflation, which starts on the spending side.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Cost-push inflation
