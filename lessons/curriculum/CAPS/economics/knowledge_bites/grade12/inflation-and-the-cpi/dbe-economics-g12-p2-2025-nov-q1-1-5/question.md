## Question (2 marks)

When the general price level increases, the real value of savings will …

A rise.
B decline.
C remain the same.
D increase.

**Note: the question paper prints options A ('rise') and D ('increase') as synonymous alternatives — a documented anomaly in the source paper, transcribed verbatim. The memo's accepted answer, B, is unaffected.**

## Method

multiple choice recall

Reason with real value: inflation erodes what a fixed sum of money can buy. If the general price level rises, the same amount of saved money buys fewer goods and services, so the purchasing power — the real value — of those savings declines.
