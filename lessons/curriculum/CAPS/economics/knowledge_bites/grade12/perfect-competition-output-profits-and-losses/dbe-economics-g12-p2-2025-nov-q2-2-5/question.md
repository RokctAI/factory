## Question (4 marks)

Use the information in the table above to calculate the marginal cost (A) if 10 units are produced. Show ALL calculations.

(Refers to a table for Q2.2.1 to Q2.2.5 titled COST AND REVENUE SCHEDULE, with columns Quantity, Total revenue in rands, Marginal revenue in rands, Marginal cost in rands and Total cost in rands. The rows read: at quantity 0 — total revenue 0, no marginal figures, total cost 100; at quantity 2 — total revenue 40, marginal revenue 20, marginal cost 10, total cost 120; at quantity 4 — 80, 20, 8, 136; at quantity 6 — 120, 20, 10, 156; at quantity 8 — 160, 20, 20, 196; at quantity 10 — 200, 20, A, 256.)

## Method

marginal cost calculation

Marginal cost is the change in total cost divided by the change in quantity. Read the last two rows of the table: total cost rises from 196 rand at 8 units to 256 rand at 10 units — a change of 60 rand for 2 extra units. Divide 60 by 2 to get 30 rand.

## Memo working

Marginal cost (A) = ΔTC / ΔQ = (R256 - R196) / (10 - 8) = 60 / 2 = 30

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Marginal cost (A) = 30
