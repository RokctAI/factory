## Question (2 marks)

Briefly describe the term implicit cost. (Refers to the COST AND REVENUE SCHEDULE table in Q2.2.1.)

## Method

definition

Implicit costs are the opportunity costs of the entrepreneur's own resources used in production — inputs the owner already possesses, such as their own building or their own labour, for which no money changes hands but which still carry a value.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The value of inputs owned by the entrepreneur and used in the production process.
