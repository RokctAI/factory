## Question (2 marks)

The price that is set by the government above the equilibrium price to allow producers to make a fair profit is called ...

A market price.
B selling price.
C maximum price.
D minimum price.

## Method

multiple choice recall

Listen for two clues: the price is set above equilibrium, and it protects producers. A price set above equilibrium is a floor below which the price may not fall — a minimum price. A maximum price would sit below equilibrium and protect consumers instead.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

D - minimum price
