## Question (1 mark)

State the nature of products sold in a perfect market. (Refers to graphs of a perfectly competitive market showing the industry and the individual firm.)

## Method

recall

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Teachable steps: in perfect competition every seller offers exactly the same product — there is no branding or differentiation, which is one reason no single firm can influence the price. The technical word for this is homogeneous (identical) products.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Homogeneous / Identical
