## Question (1 mark)

Name the concept that refers to income earned by a business for each unit sold. (Refers to the COST AND REVENUE SCHEDULE table in Q2.2.1.)

## Method

recall

Income earned for each unit sold is total revenue divided by quantity — average revenue. For a perfectly competitive firm this also equals the price, and the table confirms it: total revenue divided by quantity is 20 rand at every output level.
