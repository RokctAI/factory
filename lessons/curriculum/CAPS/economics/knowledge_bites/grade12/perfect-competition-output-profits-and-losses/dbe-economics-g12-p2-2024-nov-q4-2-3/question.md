## Question (2 marks)

Briefly describe the term marginal revenue. (Refers to graphs of a perfectly competitive market showing the industry and the individual firm.)

## Method

definition

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Teachable steps: "marginal" in economics always means "from one extra unit". So marginal revenue is the extra income earned when one more unit of the product is sold. In perfect competition it equals the market price, which is why the firm's demand, average revenue and marginal revenue lines coincide.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The additional amount of income gained from selling one extra unit of a product
