## Question (1 mark)

Give ONE term: A market in which decisions are left to individual sellers and buyers without government interference. (Abbreviations, acronyms and examples will NOT be accepted.)

## Method

terminology recall

The clue is the absence of government interference: buyers and sellers make decisions freely on their own. That is the free market — the memo also accepts unregulated market or perfect market.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Unregulated market / Perfect market / Free market
