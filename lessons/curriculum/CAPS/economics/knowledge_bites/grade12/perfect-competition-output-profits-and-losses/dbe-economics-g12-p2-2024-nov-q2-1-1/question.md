## Question (2 marks)

Give any TWO examples of markets that may be regarded as perfect competition. (2 x 1)

## Method

recall listing — think of markets where there are very many buyers and sellers trading a homogeneous product, with prices set purely by demand and supply, so that every participant is a price taker.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Any TWO of:

- Johannesburg Securities Exchange (JSE)
- Foreign exchange market
- Agricultural products market
