## Question (2 marks)

Why is the individual firm's demand curve horizontal? (Refers to graphs of a perfectly competitive market showing the industry and the individual firm.)

## Method

explanation

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Teachable steps: the firm is a price taker — with a very large number of sellers offering identical products, no single firm can influence the market price. It can sell any quantity it likes, but only at that one price, so its demand curve is a horizontal line at the market price.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The firm can only sell different quantities at the same price because it is a price taker / A large number of sellers in the market makes the individual firm not to have an influence over the market price.
