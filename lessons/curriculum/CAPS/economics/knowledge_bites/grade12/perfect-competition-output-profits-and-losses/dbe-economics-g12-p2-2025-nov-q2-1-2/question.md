## Question (2 marks)

Why would a perfectly competitive firm consider shutting down its operations? (1 x 2)

## Method

explanation

Apply the shut-down rule. In the short run a firm keeps producing as long as its revenue at least covers its variable costs, because the fixed costs are lost either way. Once average revenue falls below average variable cost — equivalently, total revenue below total variable cost — every unit produced deepens the loss, so the firm shuts down.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A perfectly competitive firm will shut down because its average revenue cannot cover all the average variable cost or total revenue is not able to cover its variable cost of production.
