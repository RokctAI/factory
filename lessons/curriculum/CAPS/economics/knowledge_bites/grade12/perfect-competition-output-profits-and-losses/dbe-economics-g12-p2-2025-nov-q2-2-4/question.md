## Question (2 marks)

Why is the marginal revenue the same for a perfectly competitive firm when production increases? (Refers to the COST AND REVENUE SCHEDULE table in Q2.2.1.)

## Method

explanation

Link marginal revenue to price-taking. A perfectly competitive firm can sell any quantity at the going market price, so every extra unit sold adds exactly that same price to total revenue. That is why the marginal revenue column stays constant at 20 rand as production increases.
