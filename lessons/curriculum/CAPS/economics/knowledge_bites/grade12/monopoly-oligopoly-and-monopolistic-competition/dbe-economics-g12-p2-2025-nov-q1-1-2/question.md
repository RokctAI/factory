## Question (2 marks)

The demand curve of a monopolistic competitor is …

A relatively elastic.
B relatively inelastic.
C perfectly elastic.
D perfectly inelastic.

## Method

multiple choice recall

Place monopolistic competition between the two extremes. A perfectly competitive firm faces a perfectly elastic, horizontal demand curve; a monopoly faces the whole downward-sloping market demand curve. A monopolistic competitor sells a differentiated product with many close substitutes, so its demand curve slopes downward but only gently — relatively elastic.
