## Question (1 mark)

Name the type of profit earned by a monopolistic competitor in the long run. (Refers to an extract on monopolistic competition among restaurants competing on food quality, prices, ingredients, location and services.)

## Method

recall — in the long run there are no barriers to entry in monopolistic competition, so new businesses enter whenever economic profit is being made, competing it away until only one type of profit remains.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Normal profit
