## Question (1 mark)

Study the extract below and answer the question that follows.

MONOPOLISTIC COMPETITION: "In any city, many restaurants compete based on the quality of their food and prices. The basic concept of providing hospitality services differs when restaurants change their strategies. One restaurant might charge R150 for a pizza and burger combo, while another might charge R180 for the same order. The price of the dish can depend on other factors, such as the quality of the ingredients used, the restaurant's location, its popularity and other services they offer. This product differentiation is a key element of the restaurant business." (Source: www.educba.com)

Identify an example of non-price competition in the extract above.

## Method

data response identification — non-price competition means competing on anything other than price, so scan the extract for ways the restaurants distinguish themselves without changing what they charge.
