## Question (2 marks)

Briefly describe the term monopolistic competition. (Refers to an extract on monopolistic competition among restaurants competing on food quality, prices, ingredients, location and services.)

## Method

definition — a good description names the two ingredients the term combines: many competing sellers (like perfect competition) and differentiated products that give each seller some price-setting power (like a monopoly).

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Exists in a market where there are relatively many businesses selling differentiated products / A market structure that combines the features of perfect competition and monopoly.
