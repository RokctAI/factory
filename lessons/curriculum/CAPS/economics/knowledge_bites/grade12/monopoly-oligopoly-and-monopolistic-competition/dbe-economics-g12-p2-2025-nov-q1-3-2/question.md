## Question (1 mark)

Give ONE term: The percentage of total sales in an industry that a particular business controls.

## Method

terminology recall

A percentage of the industry's total sales controlled by one business is that firm's slice of the market — its market share. In imperfect markets, a large market share is what gives a firm power over price.
