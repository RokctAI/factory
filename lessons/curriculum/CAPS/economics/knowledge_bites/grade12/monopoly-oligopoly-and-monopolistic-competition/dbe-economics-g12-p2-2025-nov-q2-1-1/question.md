## Question (2 marks)

Give any TWO examples of oligopoly markets. (2 x 1)

## Method

recall listing

An oligopoly is a market dominated by a few large, interdependent firms. Scan the South African economy for industries run by a handful of big players — banking, mobile networks, fuel, airlines, car manufacturing — and name any two.
