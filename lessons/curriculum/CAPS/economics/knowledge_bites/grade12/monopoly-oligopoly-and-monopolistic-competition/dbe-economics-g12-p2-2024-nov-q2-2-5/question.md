## Question (4 marks)

Why does a monopolist enjoy greater profits than a monopolistic competitor? (2 x 2) (Refers to an extract on monopolistic competition among restaurants competing on food quality, prices, ingredients, location and services.)

## Method

explanation — compare the two structures point by point: how much competition each faces, how much control each has over price, and how elastic the demand for each one's product is. Each contrast, fully explained, earns two marks.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Any TWO of:

- A monopolist has no competition whereas the monopolistic competitor has a higher degree of competition which lowers prices, hence less profit
- A monopolist can manipulate prices to higher levels whereas the monopolistic competitor has little control over prices due to many sellers
- A monopolist can charge higher prices and make higher profit because demand is inelastic whereas a monopolistic competitor cannot charge higher due to elastic demand which leads to lower profit
