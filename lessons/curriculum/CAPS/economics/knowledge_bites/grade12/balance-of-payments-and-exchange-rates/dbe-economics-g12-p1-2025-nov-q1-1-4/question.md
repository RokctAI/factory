## Question (2 marks)

When a country produces more output than other countries given the same input, it is referred to as a/an … advantage.

A competitive
B relative
C absolute
D comparative

## Method

multiple choice recall

Compare the two classic trade concepts. Absolute advantage is about raw productivity: more output from the same input. Comparative advantage is about opportunity cost: producing at a lower opportunity cost even without being the most productive. The question describes raw productivity, so it is absolute advantage.
