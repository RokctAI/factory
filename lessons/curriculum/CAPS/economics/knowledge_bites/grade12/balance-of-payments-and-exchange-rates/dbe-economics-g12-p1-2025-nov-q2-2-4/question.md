## Question (2 marks)

Explain the purpose of unrecorded transactions in the Balance of Payments. (Refers to the Q2.2.1 table on the financial account of the BOP; the table shows Unrecorded transactions of -18 613 R millions.)

## Method

explanation

The Balance of Payments must balance as a matter of accounting, but the real-world data never adds up perfectly — some transactions are mistimed, missed or misvalued. The unrecorded transactions item (errors and omissions) exists to absorb those discrepancies so the accounts balance.
