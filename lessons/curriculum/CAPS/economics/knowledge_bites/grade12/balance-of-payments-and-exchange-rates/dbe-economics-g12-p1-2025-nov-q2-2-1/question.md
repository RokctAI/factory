## Question (1 mark)

(Table titled FINANCIAL ACCOUNT OF BALANCE OF PAYMENTS (BOP) – 2024, adapted from the SARB Quarterly Bulletin, December 2024, items in R millions: Net direct investment 68 622; Net portfolio investment -23 348; Net financial derivatives 4 311; Net other investment 13 481; Reserve assets A; Balance on financial account 62 869; memo item: balance on financial account excluding reserve assets 63 066; Unrecorded transactions -18 613. A note below the table states that an increase in reserve assets is indicated by a negative sign and a decrease by a positive sign.)

Identify the item that records the purchase of shares in the table above.

## Method

data response identification

Run through what each financial account item records. Direct investment is buying control of a business or fixed property; portfolio investment is buying paper assets — shares and bonds — without control; financial derivatives and other investment cover the rest. The purchase of shares therefore sits under net portfolio investment.
