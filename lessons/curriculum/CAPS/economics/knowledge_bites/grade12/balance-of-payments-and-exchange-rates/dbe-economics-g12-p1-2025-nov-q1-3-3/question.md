## Question (1 mark)

Give ONE term: The exchange of goods and services between different countries.

## Method

terminology recall

The definition has two parts: exchange of goods and services, and between different countries. Trade within one country is domestic trade; the moment it crosses borders it becomes international trade, also called foreign trade.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

International trade / Foreign trade
