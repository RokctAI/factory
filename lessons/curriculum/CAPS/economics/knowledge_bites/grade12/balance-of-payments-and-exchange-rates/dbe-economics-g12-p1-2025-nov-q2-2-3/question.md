## Question (2 marks)

Briefly describe the term direct investment. (Refers to the Q2.2.1 table on the financial account of the BOP.)

## Method

definition

The distinguishing feature of direct investment is control: the investor acquires a meaningful stake — ten percent or more — or outright control of a business, or invests in fixed property. Contrast it with portfolio investment, where shares are bought purely for returns without any say in running the business.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

An investment in real estate or fixed property and obtaining a meaningful share (10%+) or control of such business / It includes transactions relating to investments in foreign businesses and fixed property.
