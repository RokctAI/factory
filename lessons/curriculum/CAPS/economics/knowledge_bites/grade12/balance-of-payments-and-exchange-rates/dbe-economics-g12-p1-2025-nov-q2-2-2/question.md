## Question (1 mark)

Name the sub-account of the Balance of Payments that record transactions relating to debt forgiveness. (Refers to the Q2.2.1 table on the financial account of the BOP.)

## Method

recall

Recall the structure of the Balance of Payments: the current account, the capital transfer account and the financial account. One-way transfers of capital with nothing received in return — such as debt forgiveness and migrants' transfers of assets — are recorded in the capital transfer account.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Capital transfer account
