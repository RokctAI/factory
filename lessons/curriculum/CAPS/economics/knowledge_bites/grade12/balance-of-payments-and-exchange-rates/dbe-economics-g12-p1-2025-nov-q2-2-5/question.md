## Question (4 marks)

Use the information above to determine whether there is an increase or decrease in the reserve assets (A). Show ALL calculations. (Refers to the Q2.2.1 table, FINANCIAL ACCOUNT OF BALANCE OF PAYMENTS (BOP) – 2024, items in R millions: Net direct investment 68 622; Net portfolio investment -23 348; Net financial derivatives 4 311; Net other investment 13 481; Reserve assets A; Balance on financial account 62 869; memo item: balance on financial account excluding reserve assets 63 066. A note states that an increase in reserve assets is indicated by a negative sign and a decrease by a positive sign.)

## Method

bop calculation

The reserve assets figure is the missing piece that makes the financial account add up. The quickest route: the balance on the financial account includes reserve assets, and the memo item excludes them, so subtracting the one from the other isolates reserve assets. Alternatively, start from the balance on the financial account and strip out each listed item one by one. Either way you land on -197, and the note under the table tells you how to read the sign: a negative sign means reserve assets increased.

## Memo working

Method 1: Balance on financial account less balance on financial account excluding reserve assets -> 62 869 - 63 066 = -197.

Method 2: Reserve assets = 62 869 - 13 481 - 4 311 + 23 348 - 68 622 = -197.

The value of A is -197 (R millions); per the sign convention in the table, the negative sign indicates an increase in reserve assets.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

There is an increase in the reserve assets because the value of reserve assets has a negative sign (-).
