## Question (2 marks)

Name any TWO demand reasons for international trade. (2 x 1)

## Method

recall listing

One mark each for any two demand-side reasons why countries trade: the size of the population, income levels, changes in the wealth of the population, preferences and taste, or differences in consumption patterns.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Size of the population
- Income levels
- Change in the wealth of the population
- Preferences and taste
- The difference in consumption patterns
