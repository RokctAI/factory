## Question (2 marks)

Explain the impact of a weaker rand on foreign tourist arrivals. (Refers to a bar graph titled REGIONAL CHANGES IN TOURIST ARRIVALS.)

## Method

explanation

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Teachable steps: when the rand weakens, a foreign tourist's own currency buys more rand, so a South African holiday becomes cheaper for them. Cheaper travel and spending attracts more visitors, so foreign tourist arrivals tend to increase. State the direction of the effect and the reason to earn both marks.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Foreign tourist arrivals may increase as it becomes cheaper for them to visit or spend money in South Africa.
