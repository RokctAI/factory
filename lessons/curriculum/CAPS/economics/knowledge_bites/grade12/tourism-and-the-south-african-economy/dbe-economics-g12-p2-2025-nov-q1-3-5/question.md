## Question (1 mark)

Give ONE term: Places listed by the United Nations as having cultural, historical and scientific significance.

## Method

terminology recall

Places listed by the United Nations — through UNESCO — for their cultural, historical and scientific significance are World Heritage Sites; South African examples include Robben Island and the Cradle of Humankind.
