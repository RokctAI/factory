## Question (2 marks)

Why is the tourism industry regarded as labour-intensive? (1 x 2)

## Method

explanation

Labour-intensive means production relies more on people than on machines. Tourism is built on human interaction — guides, hospitality staff, drivers, cleaners — so it needs a large workforce, and for every unit of capital invested it creates more jobs, formal and informal, than most other industries. One fully explained reason earns the 2 marks.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Tourism activities require a large workforce because the industry involves human interaction and personal services.
- Tourism creates more jobs opportunities, in both formal and informal sector, for every unit of capital invested compared to any other industry.
