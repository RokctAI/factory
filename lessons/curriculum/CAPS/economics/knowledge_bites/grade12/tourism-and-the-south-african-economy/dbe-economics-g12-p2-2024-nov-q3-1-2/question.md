## Question (2 marks)

Why does the government levy or impose taxes on tourism activities? (1 x 2)

## Method

explanation

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Teachable steps: think about what tourists use and what they cost the host country. Tourists use roads, water, airports and other public amenities, and their activities create external costs. Taxing tourism lets the government recover those costs and raise revenue to fund spending and development. ONE well-explained reason earns the 2 marks.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- To raise revenue to finance government expenditure.
- To recover the external costs incurred due to tourism activities.
- To compensate the host community for providing infrastructure and amenities that they use.
- To raise revenue to achieve developmental goals.
