## Question (2 marks)

Briefly describe the term domestic tourism. (Refers to a bar graph titled REGIONAL CHANGES IN TOURIST ARRIVALS.)

## Method

definition

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Teachable steps: the defining feature is who travels where — residents of a country visiting places inside their own country's borders. Contrast it with inbound tourism (foreigners visiting) and outbound tourism (residents leaving), which is what makes "within the boundaries of their own country" the mark-earning phrase.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The activities of people visiting places within the boundaries of their own country.
