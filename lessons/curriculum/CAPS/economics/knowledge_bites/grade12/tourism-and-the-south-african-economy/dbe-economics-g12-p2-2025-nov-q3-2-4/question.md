## Question (2 marks)

Explain the importance of public-private partnerships (PPPs) in developing tourist destinations.

(Refers to the TOURISM TRANSFORMATION FUND extract in Q3.2.1.)

## Method

explanation

The heart of a PPP is sharing: government and private businesses share the resources, costs and risks of developing a destination. A strong answer shows the split — government typically funds infrastructure (roads, utilities) or offers financial incentives, while private businesses invest capital in the tourism businesses themselves.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Public-private partnerships allow the government and private sector businesses to share resources and costs in developing tourist destinations in the country.
- The government may provide capital to develop infrastructure in tourist destinations while private sector businesses provide capital to establish tourism businesses.
- The public sector may provide financial incentives to the private sector for tourism investments.
