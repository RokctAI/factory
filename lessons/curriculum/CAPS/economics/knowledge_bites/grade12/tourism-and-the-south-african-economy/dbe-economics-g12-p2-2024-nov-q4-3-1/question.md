## Question (1 mark)

Identify the image that relates to eco-tourism.

(Two images titled SOUTH AFRICAN TOURISM. IMAGE A: a nature scene with wildlife — trees, birds, a waterfall and an elephant in a natural landscape. IMAGE B: a tourist with a backpack, hat and luggage standing on a beach among palm trees.)

## Method

data response identification

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Teachable steps: eco-tourism means responsible travel to natural areas to experience the environment and wildlife. The image showing untouched nature and animals — trees, birds, a waterfall and an elephant — matches that, so the answer is Image A, not the beach-holiday scene in Image B.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Image A
