## Question (4 marks)

How can natural disasters negatively impact the tourism industry? (2 x 2)

(Refers to the TOURISM TRANSFORMATION FUND extract in Q3.2.1.)

## Method

explanation

Think about what a flood or cyclone destroys and what that destruction means for visitors: unsafe conditions stop tourism activities, damaged infrastructure (roads, airports, hotels) discourages travel, and lost natural and cultural assets — wildlife, beaches, forests, heritage sites — remove the very attractions tourists come for. Two fully explained impacts earn the 4 marks.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Natural disasters, such as floods and cyclones, may create unfavourable conditions for tourists to participate in certain tourism activities.
- Damaged infrastructure due to natural disasters may discourage people to visit tourists' destinations.
- Natural disasters can result in loss of wildlife, beaches, forests and cultural sites which may reduce tourism activities.
