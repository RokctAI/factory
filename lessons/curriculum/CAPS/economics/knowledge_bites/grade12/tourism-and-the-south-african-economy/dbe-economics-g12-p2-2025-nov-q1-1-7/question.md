## Question (2 marks)

Internet facilities needed by tourists is called … infrastructure.

A transport
B energy
C communication
D basic services

## Method

multiple choice recall

Classify infrastructure by its function. Internet and telephone facilities carry information, so they belong to communication infrastructure. Transport infrastructure moves people, energy infrastructure powers facilities, and basic services cover water and sanitation.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C - communication
