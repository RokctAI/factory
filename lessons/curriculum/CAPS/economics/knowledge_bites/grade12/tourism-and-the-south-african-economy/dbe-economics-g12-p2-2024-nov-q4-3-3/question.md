## Question (2 marks)

Briefly describe the term paleo tourism. (Refers to two images titled SOUTH AFRICAN TOURISM.)

## Method

definition

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Teachable steps: "paleo" points to the ancient past — fossils and archaeology. Paleo tourism is travel to sites of great archaeological significance to learn about fossils, such as the Cradle of Humankind. The definition needs both the visiting of such sites and the fossil/archaeological focus.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The activities of tourists visiting sites of great archaeological significance to learn about fossils
