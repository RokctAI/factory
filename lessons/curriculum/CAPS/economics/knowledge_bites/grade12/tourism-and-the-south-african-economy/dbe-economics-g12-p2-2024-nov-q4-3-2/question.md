## Question (1 mark)

Name the institution that is responsible for promoting tourism in South Africa. (Refers to two images titled SOUTH AFRICAN TOURISM.)

## Method

recall

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Teachable steps: the marketing body — as opposed to the policy-making Department of Tourism — is South African Tourism, historically known as SATOUR. It markets the country internationally and domestically as a tourist destination.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

South African Tourism (SAT/SATOUR)
