## Question (2 marks)

Briefly describe the term cultural tourism.

(Refers to the TOURISM TRANSFORMATION FUND extract in Q3.2.1.)

## Method

definition

A good definition names both the activity and its purpose: travelling to places such as museums and art galleries, in order to learn about and engage with the culture of the place being visited. Keep both halves for the full 2 marks.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The activities of people travelling to museums and art galleries to learn and engage more in the culture of the place they are visiting.
