## Question (1 mark)

Name an economic sector under which tourism is classified.

(Refers to the TOURISM TRANSFORMATION FUND extract in Q3.2.1.)

## Method

recall

Work through the three sectors: primary extracts raw materials, secondary manufactures goods, tertiary provides services. Tourism sells services — accommodation, transport, entertainment — so it belongs to the tertiary sector.

(see marking guidelines)
