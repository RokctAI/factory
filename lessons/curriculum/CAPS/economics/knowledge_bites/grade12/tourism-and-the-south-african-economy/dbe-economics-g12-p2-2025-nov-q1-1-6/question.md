## Question (2 marks)

The activities of people visiting places for meetings and conferences is known as … tourism.

A paleo
B business
C leisure
D eco-

## Method

multiple choice recall

Sort tourism by the purpose of travel. Travelling for meetings, conferences and exhibitions is business tourism. Leisure tourism is holiday travel, ecotourism centres on enjoying the natural environment, and paleo-tourism visits fossil and archaeological sites.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

B - business
