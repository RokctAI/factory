## Question (2 marks)

Explain the impact of households' income levels on tourism. (Refers to two images titled SOUTH AFRICAN TOURISM.)

## Method

explanation

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Teachable steps: tourism is a luxury bought out of disposable income. When disposable income rises, more households can afford to travel, so tourism participation increases; when it falls, leisure travel is among the first spending cut. Explaining either direction of the link earns the marks.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Increase in households' disposable income may increase the number of people who participate in tourism activities / Decrease in disposable income reduces the number of people who travel for leisure and recreation
