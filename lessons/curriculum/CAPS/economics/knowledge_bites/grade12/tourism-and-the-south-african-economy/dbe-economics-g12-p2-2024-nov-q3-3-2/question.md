## Question (1 mark)

Name any ONE environmental World Heritage Site in South Africa. (Refers to a bar graph titled REGIONAL CHANGES IN TOURIST ARRIVALS.)

## Method

recall

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Teachable steps: the question asks for an environmental (natural) site, not a cultural one — so sites like Robben Island or the Cradle of Humankind would not fit. Any ONE natural World Heritage Site from the list earns the mark.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- iSimangaliso wetland park / Greater St Lucia wetland park
- Cape Fynbos region
- Maloti / uKhahlamba Drakensberg park
- Vredefort Dome
- Richtersveld Cultural and Botanical Landscape
- Barberton Makhonjwa
