## Question (1 mark)

Identify the number of SADC tourist arrivals in the 4th quarter of 2023.

(Bar graph titled REGIONAL CHANGES IN TOURIST ARRIVALS, adapted from Statistics South Africa: vertical axis in millions, 0 to 2. Overseas arrivals: 0,8 million in Q4 2019, 0,5 in Q4 2022, 0,6 in Q4 2023. SADC arrivals: 1,9 million in Q4 2019, 1,3 in Q4 2022, 1,7 in Q4 2023.)

## Method

data response identification

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Teachable steps: find the SADC group of bars, then pick the bar for the 4th quarter of 2023 — the most recent of the three. Its height reads 1,7 on the millions axis, so the answer is 1,7 million.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

1,7 million
