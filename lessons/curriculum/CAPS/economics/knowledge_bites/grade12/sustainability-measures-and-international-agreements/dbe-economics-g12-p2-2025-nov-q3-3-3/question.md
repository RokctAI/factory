## Question (2 marks)

Briefly describe the term marketable permit.

(Refers to the INTERNATIONAL PROTOCOLS AND AGREEMENTS ON ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY cartoon in Q3.3.1.)

## Method

definition

Capture the three elements: it is a licence, sold by the government to a business, and it allows pollution only up to a certain degree. Because the permit can be traded, firms that pollute less can sell their unused allowance — that is what makes it 'marketable'.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A license that is sold by the government to a business to allow it to pollute the environment to a certain degree.
