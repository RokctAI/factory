## Question (1 mark)

Match COLUMN A item 'Preservation' to a description from COLUMN B. (COLUMN B options for Q1.2.1 to Q1.2.8: A - method used to prevent the extinction of non-renewable resources; B - people who enjoy goods and services without paying for them; C - the activities of people travelling to and staying in places outside their usual environment; D - a firm that has no influence over the market price; E - goods that are identical in terms of appearance, packaging and quality; F - monetary policy approach used by the SARB to keep price changes within 3–6%; G - an agreement among firms within the same industry to reduce competition through price fixing; H - a firm that has control over the market price; I - activities of people visiting places to enjoy the natural environment.)

## Method

matching items

Distinguish preservation from conservation. Preservation sets resources aside so they are not used up at all — the method used to prevent the extinction of non-renewable resources, option A. Conservation, by contrast, allows careful, sustainable use of resources.
