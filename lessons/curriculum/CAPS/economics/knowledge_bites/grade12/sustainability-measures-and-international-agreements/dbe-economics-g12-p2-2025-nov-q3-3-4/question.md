## Question (2 marks)

Explain the impact of the loss of indigenous knowledge on the environment.

(Refers to the INTERNATIONAL PROTOCOLS AND AGREEMENTS ON ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY cartoon in Q3.3.1.)

## Method

explanation

Indigenous knowledge is the accumulated understanding of how to live with the environment — which resources to use, when, and how much. Losing it breaks that co-existence: future generations no longer know how to manage natural resources sustainably, so environmental damage and over-exploitation increase.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Environmental damage may increase as future generations will not be able to co-exist with the environment without indigenous knowledge.
- Loss of indigenous knowledge may lead to over-exploitation of natural resources.
