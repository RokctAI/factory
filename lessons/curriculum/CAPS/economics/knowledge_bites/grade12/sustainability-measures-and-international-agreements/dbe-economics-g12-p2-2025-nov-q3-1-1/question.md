## Question (2 marks)

Name any TWO approaches used in command and control systems for ensuring environmental sustainability. (2 x 1)

## Method

recall listing

Command and control means the government sets rules and enforces them. The rules come in three standard forms: quality standards (how clean air or water must be), quantity standards (how much of a resource or pollutant is allowed), and social impact standards (limits on harm to people, such as noise or exposure levels). Name any two of the three for the 2 marks.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Any TWO of:

- Quality standards
- Quantity standards
- Social impact standards
