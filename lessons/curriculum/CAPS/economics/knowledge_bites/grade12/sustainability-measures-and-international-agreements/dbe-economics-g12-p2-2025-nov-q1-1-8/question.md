## Question (2 marks)

The process of converting waste material into economic goods is known as …

A recycling.
B re-using.
C littering.
D resourcing.

## Method

multiple choice recall

Focus on the word converting. Recycling processes waste material into new economic goods; re-using simply uses the same item again without converting it into something new. Littering and resourcing do not describe turning waste into goods at all.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A - recycling
