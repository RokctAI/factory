## Question (1 mark)

Give ONE term: The gradual increase in the temperature of the Earth's atmosphere due to a increase in carbon emissions.

**Note: the question paper misprint 'a increase' (for 'an increase') is transcribed verbatim from the source; the answer is unaffected.**

## Method

terminology recall

A gradual rise in the temperature of the Earth's atmosphere driven by rising carbon emissions is global warming — the result of greenhouse gases trapping more of the sun's heat.
