## Question (1 mark)

Identify, in the cartoon above, the international agreement that refers to the world summit on sustainable development.

(Cartoon for Q3.3.1 to Q3.3.5, titled INTERNATIONAL PROTOCOLS AND AGREEMENTS ON ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY, source Caglecartoons.com: a group of hikers with backpacks walks through a forest, looking up at direction signboards; a board with directional arrows at the top right reads 'STOCKHOLM PROTOCOL 1972', a tilted placard in the middle reads 'JOHANNESBURG SUMMIT 2002', and a board at the bottom left reads 'RIO+20 SUMMIT 2012'.)

## Method

data response identification

Three agreements appear on the signboards. The World Summit on Sustainable Development was held in Johannesburg in 2002 — Stockholm 1972 was the first UN conference on the human environment, and Rio+20 in 2012 was the UN Conference on Sustainable Development. The signboard that matches is the Johannesburg Summit 2002.
