## Question (1 mark)

Identify the environmental problem that relates to the reduction of plants and animal species in the extract above.

(Extract for Q4.3.1 to Q4.3.5, titled MANAGING WASTE: 'Managing hazardous waste and chemical waste requires proper identification, handling, storage and transportation in order to prevent biodiversity loss. Storage of waste in designated well-ventilated areas and the use of appropriate containers prevent leaks and reactions. Companies should train employees on safe handling procedures and labelling containers clearly with hazard symbols and waste descriptions.' Adapted from https://www.vetorstock.com.)

## Method

data response identification

Biodiversity means the variety of plant and animal life, so a reduction of plant and animal species is biodiversity loss — named in the extract's first sentence as the problem that proper waste management is meant to prevent.
