## Question (2 marks)

How can soil erosion impact agricultural production? (1 x 2)

## Method

explanation

Link the erosion to the farm's output: eroded soil loses its fertile top layer, so fertility falls and output decreases; erosion can also damage the landscape and silt up water sources such as dams, disrupting production. One fully explained impact earns the 2 marks.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Soil erosion may reduce soil fertility which will lead to a decrease in agricultural production output.
- Soil erosion may damage the landscape and close water sources, such as dams, which disrupts agricultural production.
- Soil erosion may encourage farmers to adopt soil conservation techniques, such as terracing, which improves long-term productivity.
