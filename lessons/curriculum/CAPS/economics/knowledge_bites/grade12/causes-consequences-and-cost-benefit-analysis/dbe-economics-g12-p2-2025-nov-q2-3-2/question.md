## Question (1 mark)

Give any ONE example of a direct tax. (Refers to the TAXES ON PRODUCTION graph in Q2.3.1.)

## Method

recall

A direct tax is levied straight onto the income or wealth of a person or business and cannot be shifted onto someone else. Standard examples are personal income tax collected as Pay As You Earn, company tax and capital gains tax. Any one earns the mark.
