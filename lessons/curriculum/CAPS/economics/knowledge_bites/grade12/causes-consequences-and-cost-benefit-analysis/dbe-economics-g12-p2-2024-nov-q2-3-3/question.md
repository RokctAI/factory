## Question (2 marks)

Briefly describe the term externalities. (Refers to a graph titled POSITIVE EXTERNALITIES OF EDUCATION showing supply S with demand curves D and D1.)

## Method

definition — the key idea is that some costs and benefits spill over onto third parties and are not reflected in the market price; a full description mentions both the third parties and the fact that the market price leaves these effects out.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Cost and benefits to third parties which are not included in market prices / Spill-over effects to individuals and businesses that are not directly involved in the consumption and production of goods and services
