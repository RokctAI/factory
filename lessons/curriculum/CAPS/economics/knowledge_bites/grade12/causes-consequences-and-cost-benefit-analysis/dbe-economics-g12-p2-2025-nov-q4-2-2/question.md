## Question (1 mark)

Give an economic term which refers to the lowest remuneration of labour set by the government.

(Refers to the IMPERFECT DISTRIBUTION OF INCOME AND WEALTH cartoon in Q4.2.1.)

## Method

recall

Break the description into its parts: 'lowest remuneration of labour' is the least a worker may legally be paid, and 'set by the government' makes it a legislated floor — the minimum wage.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Minimum wage
