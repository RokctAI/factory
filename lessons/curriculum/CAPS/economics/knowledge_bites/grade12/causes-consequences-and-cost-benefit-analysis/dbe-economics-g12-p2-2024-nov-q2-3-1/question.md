## Question (1 mark)

Study the graph below and answer the question that follows.

(Graph titled POSITIVE EXTERNALITIES OF EDUCATION: PRICE on the vertical axis and QUANTITY on the horizontal axis; an upward-sloping supply curve S; two downward-sloping demand curves — D, the inner curve, intersecting S at price P and quantity Q, and D1, the outer curve to the right of D, intersecting S at the higher price P1 and larger quantity Q1.)

Identify the curve that represents the marginal private benefit.

## Method

data response identification — with a positive externality, the demand curve based only on buyers' own benefit lies inside the one that adds the benefit to society, so the inner demand curve is the marginal private benefit and the outer one is the marginal social benefit.
