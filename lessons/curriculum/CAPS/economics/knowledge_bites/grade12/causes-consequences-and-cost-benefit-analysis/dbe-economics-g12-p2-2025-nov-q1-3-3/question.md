## Question (1 mark)

Give ONE term: A technique used to evaluate the social costs and benefits associated with public projects.

## Method

terminology recall

A technique that weighs the social costs of a public project against its social benefits — used by government to decide on projects such as dams and highways — is cost-benefit analysis.
