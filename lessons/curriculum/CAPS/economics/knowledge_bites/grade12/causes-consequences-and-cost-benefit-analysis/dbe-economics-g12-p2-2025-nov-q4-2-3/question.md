## Question (2 marks)

Briefly describe the term progressive income tax.

(Refers to the IMPERFECT DISTRIBUTION OF INCOME AND WEALTH cartoon in Q4.2.1.)

## Method

definition

The key word is 'progressive': the tax rate itself rises as income rises. A complete description contrasts both ends — higher earners pay higher rates, lower earners pay lower rates — which is what makes the tax a tool for redistributing income.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

People who earn higher income pay higher tax rates while those who earn lower incomes pay lower tax rates.
