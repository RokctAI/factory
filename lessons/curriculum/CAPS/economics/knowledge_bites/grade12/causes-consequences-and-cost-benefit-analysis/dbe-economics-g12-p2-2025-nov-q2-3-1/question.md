## Question (1 mark)

Identify the original equilibrium price in the graph above.

(Refers to a graph for Q2.3.1 to Q2.3.5 titled TAXES ON PRODUCTION: price on the vertical axis and quantity on the horizontal axis; a downward-sloping demand curve D; the original upward-sloping supply curve S cuts D at point e, giving price P and quantity Q; a new supply curve S1, shifted upward and to the left of S, cuts D at point e1, giving the higher price P1 and the smaller quantity Q1; an arrow on the price axis shows the rise from P to P1, with dashed guide lines from e and e1 to both axes.)

## Method

data response identification

Original equilibrium sits where the first supply curve S crosses the demand curve D, at point e. Trace across from point e to the price axis: the original equilibrium price is P. After the tax shifts supply to S1, the new, higher price is P1 — but the question asks for the original price.
