## Question (2 marks)

Give any TWO examples of private costs associated with the building of a school. (2 x 1)

## Method

recall listing

Private costs are the costs actually paid by the party undertaking the project — as opposed to social costs borne by third parties. For building a school, think of what the builder or funder must pay for directly: materials, labour, land, overheads, water and electricity. Any two earn the 2 marks.
