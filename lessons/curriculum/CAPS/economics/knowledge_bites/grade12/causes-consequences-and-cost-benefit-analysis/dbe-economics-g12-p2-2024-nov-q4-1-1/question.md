## Question (2 marks)

Name TWO types of inefficiencies caused by market failures. (2 x 1)

## Method

recall listing

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Teachable steps: inefficiency comes in two syllabus forms — productive (also called technical) inefficiency, where goods are not produced at the lowest possible cost, and allocative inefficiency, where resources are not allocated to the goods and services society values most. Name both for 1 mark each.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Productive / technical
- Allocative
