## Question (4 marks)

How can producer subsidies positively influence the economy? (2 x 2)

(Refers to the TAXES ON PRODUCTION graph in Q2.3.1.)

## Method

explanation

A producer subsidy works in the opposite direction to the production tax shown in the stimulus graph: instead of shifting supply left and raising the price, a subsidy lowers producers' costs and shifts supply to the right. Trace the chain from lower costs outwards — lower production costs ease inflation, more output means economic growth, more output needs more workers so unemployment falls, and lower prices leave consumers with more spending power. Any two chains, fully explained, earn the 4 marks.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Producers will incur lower cost of production which may reduce inflation in the economy
- Businesses may be encouraged to produce more goods and services which stimulates economic growth
- Increase in production may create more job opportunities thereby reducing unemployment in the economy
- Consumers may pay lower prices for goods and services leading to higher consumers spending.
