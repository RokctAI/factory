## Question (1 mark)

Give any ONE example of negative externalities. (Refers to a graph titled POSITIVE EXTERNALITIES OF EDUCATION showing supply S with demand curves D and D1.)

## Method

recall — a negative externality is a cost that lands on third parties who were not part of the transaction, so name any spill-over harm suffered by people other than the buyer and seller.

(see marking guidelines)

- Illnesses and diseases
- Traffic congestion
