## Question (2 marks)

Why is cost-benefit analysis (CBA) important for public projects? (1 x 2)

## Method

explanation — recall that public projects create social costs and social benefits that market prices do not capture, so the government needs a tool to weigh them objectively before committing resources.

- To bring greater objectivity in decision making concerning the execution of public project
- To ensure efficient allocation of resources
