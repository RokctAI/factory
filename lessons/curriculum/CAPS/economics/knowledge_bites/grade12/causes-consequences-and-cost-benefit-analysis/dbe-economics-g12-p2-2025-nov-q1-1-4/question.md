## Question (2 marks)

Failure by businesses to produce goods and services at the lowest possible average cost is called … inefficiency.

A allocative
B market
C productive
D distributive

## Method

multiple choice recall

Match the wording to the type of inefficiency. Productive inefficiency means failing to produce at the lowest possible average cost. Allocative inefficiency is about producing the wrong mix of goods relative to what society wants. The question quotes the cost-based definition, so it is productive inefficiency.
