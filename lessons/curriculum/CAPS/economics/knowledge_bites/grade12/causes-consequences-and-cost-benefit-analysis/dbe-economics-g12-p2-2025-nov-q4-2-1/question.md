## Question (1 mark)

Identify the reason for the unequal distribution of income and wealth in the cartoon above.

(Cartoon for Q4.2.1 to Q4.2.5, titled IMPERFECT DISTRIBUTION OF INCOME AND WEALTH, adapted from https://www.bostonglobe.com/: two cliff tops are separated by a deep chasm. On the lower left cliff a group of ordinary people stands next to a signpost reading DISCRIMINATION; one of them says 'HEY, THERE IS INCOME INEQUALITY IN THE MARKET.' and another replies 'YES, BUT IT'S MAN-MADE.' On the much higher right cliff a wealthy couple stands beside a sign reading WEALTH TAXES and answers 'NO — IT'S TOTALLY NATURAL!')

## Method

data response identification

The cartoon places the ordinary people on the lower cliff next to a signpost that names the cause directly: discrimination. That signpost, standing on the poorer side of the chasm, is the reason the cartoon gives for the unequal distribution of income and wealth.
