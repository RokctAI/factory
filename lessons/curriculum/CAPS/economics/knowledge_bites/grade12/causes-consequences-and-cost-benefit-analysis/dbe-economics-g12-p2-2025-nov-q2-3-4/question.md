## Question (2 marks)

Why does the government impose excise duties, such as sin tax, on demerit goods? (Refers to the TAXES ON PRODUCTION graph in Q2.3.1.)

## Method

explanation

Connect demerit goods to negative externalities. Consuming goods such as alcohol and tobacco imposes costs on society beyond the buyer, so the government adds excise duties both to recover those external costs and to raise the price enough to discourage consumption.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- To recover the external costs that are caused by the consumption of demerit goods.
- To discourage the consumption of demerit goods by making them more expensive
