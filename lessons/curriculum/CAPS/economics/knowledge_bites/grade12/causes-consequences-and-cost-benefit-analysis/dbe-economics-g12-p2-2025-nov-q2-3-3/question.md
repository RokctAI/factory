## Question (2 marks)

Briefly describe the term Pareto efficiency. (Refers to the TAXES ON PRODUCTION graph in Q2.3.1.)

## Method

definition

Pareto efficiency describes an allocation of resources with nothing left to gain for free: resources are allocated in such a way that it is impossible to make anyone better off without making someone else worse off.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A situation whereby resources are allocated in such a way that no one can be made better off without making someone else worse off.
