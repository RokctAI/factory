## Question (2 marks)

Why is quantity Q1 regarded as socially efficient? (Refers to a graph titled POSITIVE EXTERNALITIES OF EDUCATION: supply S with demand curves D and D1, where D1 lies outside D and intersects S at price P1 and quantity Q1.)

## Method

explanation — on the graph, D1 is the demand curve that includes the external benefits of education, so the quantity where D1 meets supply is the one at which society's full benefit from the last unit just matches its cost.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The price at quantity Q1 takes into consideration the external benefits of education / Quantity Q1 is exchanged at a price where the marginal private cost is equal to the marginal social benefit
