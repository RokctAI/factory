## Question (2 marks)

Explain the impact of poor infrastructure on economic growth. (Refers to the Q4.3.1 extract on the Expanded Public Works Programme.)

## Method

explanation

One explained impact earns both marks: poor infrastructure — aging roads, unreliable energy — disrupts the movement of inputs and final goods and raises operating costs, so production falls, investment is discouraged and economic growth slows.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Poor infrastructure such as aging road networks may disrupt the distribution of production inputs and final goods leading to low production and a decline in economic growth.
- Poor infrastructure may discourage foreign and domestic investments which limits the production capacity of the economy because it will be expensive for entrepreneurs to operate their businesses.
- Poor energy supply will make businesses to produce less output which decreases economic growth.
