## Question (1 mark)

(Graph titled PHILLIPS CURVE: the vertical axis shows the inflation rate in percent, marked 1 to 9, and the horizontal axis shows the unemployment rate in percent, marked 2 to 22. A single downward-sloping convex curve is labelled PC. Three labelled points lie on the curve with dashed guide lines: point C at 10% unemployment and 6% inflation, point B at 16% unemployment and 2% inflation, and point A at 20% unemployment where the curve touches the horizontal axis, meaning zero inflation.)

Identify the level of inflation where the unemployment rate is 10% in the graph above.

## Method

data response identification

Read the graph from the horizontal axis upward: find 10% on the unemployment axis, follow the dashed guide line up to the curve at point C, then trace across to the inflation axis. The dashed line meets the vertical axis at 6%, so inflation is 6% when unemployment is 10%.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)
