## Question (8 marks)

Briefly discuss the Broad-Based Black Economic Empowerment Programme (BBBEE) and the National Skills Development Strategy (NSDS) as South African growth and development policies and strategic initiatives. (2 x 4)

## Method

discussion

Teachable steps: two headings, four marks each. BBBEE: a legal framework for transforming the South African economy by promoting inclusive economic participation for black South Africans — redress and affirmative action in the workplace pursued through preferential procurement, enterprise development, skills development and the promotion of ownership and management. NSDS: a strategy to improve the skills of the labour force so as to meet economic needs, reduce unemployment and promote equity — radically transforming education and training, targeting skills in high demand, building partnerships between educational institutions, employers and SETAs, and prioritising historically disadvantaged groups.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

**Broad-Based Black Economic Empowerment Programme (BBBEE)**

- BBBEE promotes inclusive economic participation for black South Africans to address historical inequalities.
- The strategy provides a legal basis for the transformation of the South African economy.
- The objectives are redress and affirmative action in the workplace and business environments.
- BBBEE promotes growth and development through preferential procurement, enterprise development, skills development, and the promotion of ownership and management.

(Accept any other correct relevant response)

**National Skills Development Strategy (NSDS)**

- The NSDS aims to improve the skills of the South African labour force to meet economic needs, reduce unemployment, and promote equity.
- The NSDS is intended to radically transform education and training in South Africa.
- The strategy identifies and provides training for skills that are in high demand to boost economic productivity.
- The NSDS improves the quality training programs, through partnerships between educational institutions, employers, and SETAs.
- The strategy focuses on providing skills acquisition opportunities to historically disadvantaged groups, to transform racial and gender inequalities in the labour force.
