## Question (4 marks)

How can the Expanded Public Works Programme help to improve the standard of living? (2 x 2) (Refers to the Q4.3.1 extract on the Expanded Public Works Programme.)

## Method

explanation

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Teachable steps: give two channels, two marks each: the EPWP provides temporary jobs and therefore income for disadvantaged households; it develops skills that help participants get absorbed into the labour market; and those skills can be used to start small businesses, alleviating poverty.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Expanded Public Works Programme (EPWP) provides temporary employment opportunities which create sources of income for the disadvantaged households.
- EPWP provides opportunities for skills development for the participants to be absorbed into the labour market.
- EPWP contributes to poverty alleviation by allowing households to start small businesses using skills acquired.
