## Question (2 marks)

Explain the relationship between unemployment and inflation as illustrated by the graph above. (Refers to the Q2.3.1 Phillips curve graph: a downward-sloping curve with inflation at 6% when unemployment is 10%, inflation at 2% when unemployment is 16%, and zero inflation at 20% unemployment.)

## Method

explanation

Describe what the downward slope of the curve tells you: as you move along the curve, lower unemployment comes with higher inflation, and higher unemployment with lower inflation. The two variables move in opposite directions — an inverse, or trade-off, relationship.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

There is an inverse/ trade-off/ negative relationship between unemployment and inflation.
