## Question (1 mark)

(Extract titled EXPANDED PUBLIC WORKS PROGRAMME (EPWP), adapted from https://www.gov.za/news/media: 'The implementation of the EPWP focuses on key service delivery interventions, such as waste management, paving of roads, patching of potholes and the maintenance of public facilities. The programme will be intensified to create job opportunities for the youth, women and rural poor communities that bear the burden of low economic growth and unemployment.')

Identify in the extract above a socio-economic problem addressed by the Expanded Public Works Programme.

## Method

data response identification

Scan the extract for a socio-economic problem it names directly: it speaks of communities that bear the burden of low economic growth and unemployment — unemployment is the problem the EPWP addresses.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Unemployment
