## Question (1 mark)

Give the concept that relates to the level of unemployment where the inflation rate is zero. (Refers to the Q2.3.1 Phillips curve graph, where the curve touches the horizontal axis at point A at 20% unemployment, meaning zero inflation.)

## Method

recall

On the Phillips curve, the point where the curve meets the horizontal axis is where inflation is zero. The unemployment rate at that point — the level of unemployment that remains even when prices are stable — is called the natural rate of unemployment.
