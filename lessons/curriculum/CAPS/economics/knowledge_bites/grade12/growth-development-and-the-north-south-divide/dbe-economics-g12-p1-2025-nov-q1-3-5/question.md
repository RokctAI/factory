## Question (1 mark)

Give ONE term: A land reform programme that aims to compensate people who lost their land due to discriminatory laws of the past.

## Method

terminology recall

Distinguish the three land reform programmes: redistribution transfers land to broaden ownership, tenure reform secures people's rights on land they occupy, and restitution compensates or returns land to those who lost it under discriminatory laws of the past. "Compensate people who lost their land" points to land restitution.
