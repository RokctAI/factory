## Question (4 marks)

How can the government improve efficiency of markets in the economy? (2 x 2) (Refers to the Q2.3.1 Phillips curve graph of inflation against unemployment.)

## Method

explanation

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Teachable steps: efficiency questions ask how government can make markets work better. Take two distinct interventions and explain each for two marks: deregulation — removing unnecessary laws and regulations so that markets become more flexible; and promoting competition — encouraging new businesses, inviting foreign direct investment, removing power imbalances and levelling the playing field so that no market participant is unfairly disadvantaged.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- The government should remove laws and regulations (deregulation) to improve the flexibility of markets.
- Encourage competition by promoting the establishment of new businesses, inviting foreign direct investments and removing power imbalances.
- Levelling the playing field to ensure that all market participants have equal opportunities and no business is unfairly disadvantaged.
