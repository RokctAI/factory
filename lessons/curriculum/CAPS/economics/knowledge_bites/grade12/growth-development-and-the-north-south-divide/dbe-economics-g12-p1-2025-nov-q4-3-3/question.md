## Question (2 marks)

Briefly describe the term labour-intensive. (Refers to the Q4.3.1 extract on the Expanded Public Works Programme.)

## Method

definition

Define labour-intensive as processes or tasks that rely more on human effort than on capital to produce goods and services.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Processes or tasks that require more human efforts than capital to produce goods and services.
