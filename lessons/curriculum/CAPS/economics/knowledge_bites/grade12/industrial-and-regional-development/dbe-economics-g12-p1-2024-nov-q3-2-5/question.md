## Question (4 marks)

How can South African businesses benefit from the National Research and Development Strategy (NRDS)? (2 x 2) (From the data-response set on South Africa's industrial development strategies; the extract notes a target of 1,5% of GDP expenditure on research and development, with actual investment decreasing from 0,75% in 2018/19 to 0,60% in 2021/22.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Promotes innovation which allows businesses to use new production techniques that are more efficient and minimise their average cost of production.
- New production techniques with higher productivity may help businesses to produce more output and satisfy higher demand from customers.
- Businesses may produce better quality products and gain a competitive advantage, especially on global markets.
- Lower cost of production and higher output due to innovation may increase the profitability of South African businesses.
