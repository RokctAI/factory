## Question (2 marks)

Explain the importance of corridors in promoting regional development. (From the data-response set on South Africa's industrial development strategies.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Allows mining, agriculture and manufacturing industries to have access from one area to another.
- Promotes efficient urbanisation through establishment of new towns in underdeveloped regions.
- Corridors make regions more attractive to both domestic and foreign investors, fostering economic growth.
