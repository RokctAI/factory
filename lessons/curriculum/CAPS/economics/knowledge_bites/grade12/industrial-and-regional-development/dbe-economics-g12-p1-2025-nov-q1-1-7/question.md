## Question (2 marks)

The industrial development strategy that focuses on improving competitiveness in production is known as the …

A Integrated Manufacturing Strategy.
B National Industrial Policy Framework.
C National Research and Development Strategy.
D Industrial Policy Action Plan.

## Method

multiple choice recall

Link each strategy to its focus. The Integrated Manufacturing Strategy is the one aimed at improving the competitiveness of production in manufacturing. The National Industrial Policy Framework sets the broad vision for industrialisation, the Industrial Policy Action Plan is its implementation plan, and the National Research and Development Strategy deals with research and innovation.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A - Integrated Manufacturing Strategy
