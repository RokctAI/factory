## Question (1 mark)

Match COLUMN A item 'Industrial hub' to a description from COLUMN B. (COLUMN B options for Q1.2.1 to Q1.2.8: A - the value of a currency is determined by the forces of supply and demand only; B - developing countries undergoing rapid economic growth and industrialisation; C - the relationship between real output and factor input; D - a geographical area that shows a cluster of businesses in a specific sector, such as manufacturing and technology; E - consists of a three-year rolling expenditure and revenue projection of the government; F - changes in interest rates and money supply to influence the economy; G - the amount of money spent on final goods and services within the borders of a country; H - the value of a currency is pegged by the government; I - shows the general direction in which the economy is moving.)

## Method

matching items

A "hub" is a central point where activity clusters together. An industrial hub is therefore a geographical area where businesses in a specific sector — such as manufacturing and technology — group together to share infrastructure and skills. That is option D.
