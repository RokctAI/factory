## Question (2 marks)

Explain the relationship between marginal propensity to save (MPS) and the value of the multiplier (K). (Refers to the Q2.3.1 graph of the multiplier effect in a two-sector economy: expenditure lines E = 20 + 0,5Y and E1 = 30 + 0,5Y against the 45-degree line E = Y.)

## Method

explanation

Recall the formula K = 1/MPS (equivalently K = 1/(1 - MPC)). Because MPS sits in the denominator, the relationship is inverse: a bigger MPS means more of each rand leaks into saving, so the spending chain dies out sooner and the multiplier is smaller; a smaller MPS keeps more spending circulating, giving a bigger multiplier.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- There is an inverse or a negative relationship between marginal propensity to save (MPS) and the multiplier
- The bigger the MPS, the smaller the value of the multiplier / The smaller the MPS, the bigger the value of the multiplier
