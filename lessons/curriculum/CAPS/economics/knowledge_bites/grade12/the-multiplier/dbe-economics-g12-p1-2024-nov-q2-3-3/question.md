## Question (2 marks)

Briefly describe the term autonomous consumption. (Refers to the Q2.3.1 graph of the multiplier effect in a two-sector economy: expenditure lines E = 20 + 0,5Y and E1 = 30 + 0,5Y against the 45-degree line E = Y.)

## Method

definition

Tie the definition to the graph: in E = 20 + 0,5Y the intercept of 20 is spending that happens even when income is zero. That is the key phrase the memo rewards — consumption that does not depend on the level of income.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The level of consumer spending that is independent of the level of income
