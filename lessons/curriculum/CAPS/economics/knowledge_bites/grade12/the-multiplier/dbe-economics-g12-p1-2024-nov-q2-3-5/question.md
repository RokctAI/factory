## Question (4 marks)

Use the information in the above graph to calculate the change in national income (ΔY). Show ALL calculations. (Graph titled MULTIPLIER EFFECT IN A TWO-SECTOR ECONOMY: expenditure on the vertical axis in billion rands, income on the horizontal axis, with a dashed 45-degree line E = Y. The expenditure line E = 20 + 0,5Y, with vertical intercept 20, cuts E = Y at income Y; it shifts up to the parallel line E1 = 30 + 0,5Y, with vertical intercept 30, which cuts E = Y at the higher income Y1.)

## Method

multiplier calculation

## Memo working

The memo accepts any of four routes; all give the same result.

Method 1 — multiplier times the change in injections: the autonomous spending injection rises from 20 to 30, so ΔJ = 30 - 20 = 10. The multiplier K = 1/(1 - MPC) = 1/(1 - 0,5) = 2. Therefore ΔY = ΔJ x K = 10 x 2 = 20.

Method 2 — compare the two equilibrium incomes directly: ΔY = Y1 - Y = (30/0,5) - (20/0,5) = 60 - 40 = 20.

Method 3 — start from the multiplier formula: K = 1/(1 - mpc) = 1/(1 - 0,5) = 2. Since K = ΔY/ΔJ, it follows that 2 = ΔY/(30 - 20), so ΔY = 20.

Method 4 — solve the equilibrium condition Y = E for each expenditure line: Y = 20 + 0,5Y gives Y = 40; Y = 30 + 0,5Y gives Y1 = 60; therefore ΔY = Y1 - Y = 60 - 40 = 20.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

ΔY = 20 (billion rand)
