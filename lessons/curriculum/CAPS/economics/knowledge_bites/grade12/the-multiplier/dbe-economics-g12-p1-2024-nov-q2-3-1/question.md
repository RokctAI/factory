## Question (1 mark)

(Stimulus for Q2.3.1-Q2.3.5, graph titled MULTIPLIER EFFECT IN A TWO-SECTOR ECONOMY: vertical axis Expenditure (E) in billion rands, horizontal axis Income (Y). A dashed 45-degree line labelled E = Y. Two parallel upward-sloping expenditure lines: E = 20 + 0,5Y with vertical intercept 20, cutting E = Y at point e with income Y; and E1 = 30 + 0,5Y with vertical intercept 30, cutting E = Y at point e1 with income Y1. Arrows show the upward shift from E to E1 and the rightward movement from Y to Y1.)

Identify the value of the marginal propensity to consume (MPC) in the graph above.

## Method

data response identification

In an expenditure equation of the form E = a + bY, the intercept a is autonomous spending and the slope b — the coefficient of Y — is the marginal propensity to consume. Both lines on the graph, E = 20 + 0,5Y and E1 = 30 + 0,5Y, have slope 0,5, so the MPC is read straight off the equation as 0,5.
