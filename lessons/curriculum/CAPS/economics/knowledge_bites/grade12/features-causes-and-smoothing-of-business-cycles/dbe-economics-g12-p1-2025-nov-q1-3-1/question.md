## Question (1 mark)

Give ONE term: A decline in real gross domestic product for at least two consecutive quarters. (Abbreviations, acronyms and examples will NOT be accepted.)

## Method

terminology recall

Anchor on the technical threshold: two consecutive quarters — that is six months — of falling real GDP is the standard definition of a recession. If the decline continues much longer and deeper, it becomes a depression, but two quarters is the recession benchmark.
