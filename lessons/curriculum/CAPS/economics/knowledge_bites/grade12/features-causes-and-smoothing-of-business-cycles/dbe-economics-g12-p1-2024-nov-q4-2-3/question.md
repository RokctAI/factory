## Question (2 marks)

Briefly describe the term amplitude. (From the data-response set on South Africa's business cycles since 1999.)

## Method

definition

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The vertical difference between a trough and the next peak or a peak and the next trough of a business cycle.
