## Question (2 marks)

Briefly describe the concept new economic paradigm in relation to business cycles. (Refers to the Q2.3.1 Phillips curve graph of inflation against unemployment.)

## Method

definition

The new economic paradigm is the idea that the old trade-off between growth and inflation can be escaped: by combining demand-side and supply-side policies, business cycles can be smoothed and the economy can grow without provoking inflation, achieving price stability at the same time.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

It is the smoothing of business cycles (achieving price stability) through the use of demand-side and supply-side policies / It is possible to promote economic growth without provoking inflation by using demand-side and supply-side policies.
