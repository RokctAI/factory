## Question (2 marks)

Give any TWO examples of leading indicators. (2 x 1)

## Method

recall listing

Leading indicators are the data series that turn before the economy does — they change direction ahead of the business cycle and so give early warning of upswings and downswings. Name any two examples, one mark each; classic choices are job advertising space and the number of building plans approved.
