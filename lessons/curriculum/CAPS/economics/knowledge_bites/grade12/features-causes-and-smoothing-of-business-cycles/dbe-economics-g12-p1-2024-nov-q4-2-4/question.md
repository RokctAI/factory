## Question (2 marks)

Explain the importance of the trend line in the forecasting of business cycles. (From the data-response set on South Africa's business cycles since 1999.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Trend line shows the general direction in which the economy is heading.
- A rising trend line indicates that the economy is gradually growing / A declining trend line indicates that the economy is gradually contracting.
