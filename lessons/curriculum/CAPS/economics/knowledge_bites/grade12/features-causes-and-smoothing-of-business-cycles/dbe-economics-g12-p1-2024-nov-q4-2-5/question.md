## Question (4 marks)

How can an economic recession influence households in the economy? (2 x 2) (From the data-response set on South Africa's business cycles since 1999.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- During an economic recession households lose their jobs and other sources of income.
- Households may be forced to look for alternative ways of generating income for survival, such as informal businesses.
- Households buy less goods and services due to low income level leading to low standard of living.
- Households may have to import goods and services due to product shortages on local markets.
- Some households may be forced to migrate to other countries for employment opportunities and better standard of living.
