## Question (2 marks)

Fluctuations in economic activity that are caused by forces inside the market system are regarded as a/an … business cycles.

A endogenous explanation of
B exogenous explanation of
C monetarist approach to
D classical approach to

## Method

multiple choice recall

Listen for the key phrase "forces inside the market system". Endogenous means arising from within the economy itself — internal factors such as changes in demand, supply and prices. Exogenous means the opposite: shocks from outside the system, like weather or war. Monetarist and classical are schools of thought, not the inside/outside label the question asks for.
