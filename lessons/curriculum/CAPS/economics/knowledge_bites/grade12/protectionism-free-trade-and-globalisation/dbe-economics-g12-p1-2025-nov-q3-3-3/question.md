## Question (2 marks)

Briefly describe the term trade liberalisation. (Refers to the Q3.3.1 extract on trade agreements.)

## Method

definition

Define trade liberalisation as the removal of government restrictions on trade — tariffs, quotas and other controls on both imports and exports — so that goods and services flow freely between countries.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The abolishment of government intervention in trade flows on both the import and the export side. / The removal of trade restrictions such as tariffs and quotas on exports and imports to allow free flows of goods and services between countries.
