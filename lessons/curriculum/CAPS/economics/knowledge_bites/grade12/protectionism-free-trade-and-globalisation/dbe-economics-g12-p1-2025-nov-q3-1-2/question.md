## Question (2 marks)

How can export promotion lead to economic growth? (1 x 2)

## Method

explanation

Link the policy to output in one causal chain worth two marks: export subsidies and incentives encourage producing goods and services for foreign markets, which raises gross domestic product and so stimulates economic growth.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Export subsidies and incentives may encourage the production of goods and services for exporting which increases the gross domestic product and stimulates economic growth.
