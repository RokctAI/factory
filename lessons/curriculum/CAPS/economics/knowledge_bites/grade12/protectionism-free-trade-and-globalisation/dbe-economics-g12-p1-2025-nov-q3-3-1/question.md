## Question (1 mark)

(Extract titled TRADE AGREEMENTS, adapted from SARS, Trade Agreements, September 2024: 'The South African Revenue Service (SARS) administers a number of trade agreements or protocols as well as other international instruments in terms of the Customs and Excise Act, 1964 (Act 91 of 1964). Among them is the Southern African Customs Union (SACU) agreement between the governments of some Southern African Development Community (SADC) states.')

Identify in the extract above the trade protocol that aims to reconstruct and develop Southern Africa's social and economic framework.

## Method

data response identification

The extract names two bodies, SACU and SADC. The protocol whose aim is to reconstruct and develop Southern Africa's social and economic framework is the Southern African Development Community (SADC).

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Southern African Development Community / SADC
