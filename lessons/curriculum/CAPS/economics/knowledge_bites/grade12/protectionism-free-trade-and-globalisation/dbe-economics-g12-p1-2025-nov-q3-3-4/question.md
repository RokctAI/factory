## Question (2 marks)

Explain a benefit of the African Growth and Opportunity Act (AGOA) for South Africa. (Refers to the Q3.3.1 extract on trade agreements.)

## Method

explanation

Pick one benefit and explain it for two marks: AGOA gives South African exporters duty-free access to the USA market, which makes their goods more competitive — supporting industrial growth, diversification and employment.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Local producers may enjoy duty-free access on their exports to the USA market which makes their goods more competitive.
- AGOA promotes industrial growth and diversification in South Africa.
- Increase in exports production may lead to an increase in employment in South Africa.
