## Question (1 mark)

Give ONE term: A trade policy that governments use to safeguard local industries against unfair foreign competition.

## Method

terminology recall

The key words are "safeguard local industries" against foreign competition. The policy of shielding domestic producers — using instruments like tariffs, quotas and subsidies — is protectionism. Free trade is its opposite.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)
