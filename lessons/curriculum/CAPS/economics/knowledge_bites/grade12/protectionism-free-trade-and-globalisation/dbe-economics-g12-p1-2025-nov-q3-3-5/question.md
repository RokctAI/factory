## Question (4 marks)

How can dumping impact producers in importing countries? (2 x 2) (Refers to the Q3.3.1 extract on trade agreements.)

## Method

explanation

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Teachable steps: dumping is selling exports at very low prices, below cost or home-market price. Explain two effects on producers in the importing country, two marks each: unfair competition from artificially cheap foreign goods; forced price cuts to match the imports, squeezing profit margins; and, at the extreme, shutdowns as demand for local products collapses.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Producers in importing countries may face unfair competition as foreign enterprises sell their product at very low prices.
- Local producers may be forced to shut down their operations due to low demand for their products.
- Businesses in importing countries may be forced to reduce their prices to match prices of foreign products which may lead to low profit margins.
