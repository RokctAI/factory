## Question (2 marks)

Briefly describe the term public sector failure. (Refers to the Q4.2.1 cartoon of the sinking 'South African public sector' ship.)

## Method

definition

Define public sector failure as the government failing to manage the economy and the resources under its control optimally, or failing to provide public goods in the desired quantities and quality.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Public sector failure is when the government fails to manage the economy and the resources under its control optimally.
- Occurs when the government fails to provide public goods in the desired quantities and quality.
