## Question (1 mark)

Name the type of public goods that are supplied free of charge. (Refers to the Q4.2.1 cartoon of the sinking 'South African public sector' ship.)

## Method

recall

Public goods that are supplied free of charge are community goods — think of services such as defence and street lighting, which are financed from tax revenue rather than user charges.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Community goods
