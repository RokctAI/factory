## Question (2 marks)

How can the government exclude free riders from the consumption of public goods? (1 x 2)

## Method

explanation

Start from the free-rider problem: because public goods are non-excludable, people can benefit without paying. The government breaks this by making users pay — either directly, through user charges such as toll fees and stadium entrance fees, or indirectly, by financing the goods through taxes so that everyone is forced to contribute. Give one such measure, explained, for the two marks.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- The government may impose user charges/fees/levies for using collective goods such as stadiums and national roads.
- The government may use taxes to prevent free-riders from consuming public goods by forcing everyone to contribute financially.
