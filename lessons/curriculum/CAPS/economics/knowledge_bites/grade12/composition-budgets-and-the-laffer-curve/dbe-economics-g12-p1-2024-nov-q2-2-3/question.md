## Question (2 marks)

Briefly describe the term accountability. (Refers to the Q2.2.1 extract on public sector failure.)

## Method

definition

A full two-mark description needs both halves of the idea: explaining or justifying decisions and spending, and accepting responsibility for the outcome. Anchor it to a person or organisation having a duty to answer for their actions.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The duty of an individual or organisation to explain their decisions, actions, expenditure and accept responsibility for their behaviour.
