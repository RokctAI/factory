## Question (2 marks)

When government departments follow too many rules and procedures at the cost of quality service delivery, it is known as …

A apathy.
B efficiency.
C rent seeking.
D bureaucracy.

## Method

multiple choice recall

The clue is "too many rules and procedures". That is the textbook description of bureaucracy — official red tape slowing down service delivery. Apathy is a lack of interest, rent seeking is using influence to gain benefits without producing anything, and efficiency is the opposite of what is described.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

D - bureaucracy
