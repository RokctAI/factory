## Question (1 mark)

Give ONE term: The curve that illustrates the relationship between tax rates and tax revenue for the government.

## Method

terminology recall

Recall the curve from public sector economics that plots tax rates against tax revenue: revenue rises as rates increase, peaks at an optimal rate, and then falls when rates get so high that they discourage work and production. That inverted-U shape is the Laffer curve.
