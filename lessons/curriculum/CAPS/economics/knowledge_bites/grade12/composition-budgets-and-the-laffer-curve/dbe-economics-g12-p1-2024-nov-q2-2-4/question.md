## Question (2 marks)

Explain the effect of public sector failure on the allocation of resources. (Refers to the Q2.2.1 extract on public sector failure.)

## Method

explanation

Link the failure to a misallocation outcome: when the public sector fails, scarce resources do not go to their best use — they are wasted on failed projects and inefficient departments instead of reaching communities. One well-explained effect earns the two marks.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Resources will not be optimally allocated which leads to some valuable resources being wasted.
- Allocation of funds to failed projects and inefficient government departments results in wasteful use of tax-payers' money.
