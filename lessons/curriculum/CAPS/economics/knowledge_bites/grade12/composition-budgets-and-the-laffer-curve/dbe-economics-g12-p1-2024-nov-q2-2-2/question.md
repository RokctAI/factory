## Question (1 mark)

Name the level of the government that is responsible for providing services, such as refuse removal. (Refers to the Q2.2.1 extract on public sector failure.)

## Method

recall

Work through the three levels of government — national, provincial and local — and match the service to the level closest to households. Day-to-day community services like refuse removal, water and streetlights are delivered by municipalities, the local level.

(see marking guidelines)
