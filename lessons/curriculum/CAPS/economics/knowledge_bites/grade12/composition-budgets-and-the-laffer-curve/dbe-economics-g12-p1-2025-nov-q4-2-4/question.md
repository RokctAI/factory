## Question (2 marks)

Explain the effect of an increase in value-added tax (VAT) on businesses. (Refers to the Q4.2.1 cartoon of the sinking 'South African public sector' ship.)

## Method

explanation

One explained effect earns both marks: businesses incur extra costs when re-pricing VAT-linked goods and services, or they face lower demand and falling sales revenue as the tax pushes prices up.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Businesses may incur additional cost when adjusting the prices of goods and services that are VAT linked.
- Businesses may be faced with lower demand for goods and services which may lead to a decline in sales revenue.
