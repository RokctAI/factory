## Question (1 mark)

(Cartoon adapted from Dall-e, January 2025: a large passenger ship sinking stern-first into the sea. Its funnel is labelled PUBLIC and the hull carries a banner reading SOUTH AFRICAN; a man in a suit on the deck shouts through a megaphone, with a large speech bubble reading 'SOUTH AFRICAN PUBLIC SECTOR'; the tilting hull bears the word ACCOUNTABILITY, a life-buoy shape at the waterline reads 'PUBLIC SECTOR FAILURE', and a South African flag beside the sinking stern is labelled TAXATION; passengers crowd the decks and people are visible in the water.)

Identify ONE factor in the cartoon above that contributes to poor public sector provisioning.

## Method

data response identification

Listen for the labels painted on the sinking ship — PUBLIC, SOUTH AFRICAN, ACCOUNTABILITY, PUBLIC SECTOR FAILURE and TAXATION. The factor the memo accepts as contributing to poor public sector provisioning is accountability.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Accountability
