## Question (2 marks)

The South African government ensures a more equitable redistribution of income by using a … tax system.

A proportional
B progressive
C regressive
D multiple

## Method

multiple choice recall

Match the tax system to its effect on income distribution. A progressive tax takes a higher percentage from higher earners, which narrows the income gap — that is what redistributes income more equitably. Proportional takes the same percentage from everyone, and regressive takes a bigger share from the poor, which worsens inequality.
