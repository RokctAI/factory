## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- The government uses a progressive personal income tax system where higher income earners pay higher tax rates to reduce the income gap.
- The government may use redistribution programs, such as social welfare and unemployment benefits to redistribute income.
- The government may use redress methods, such as BBBEE and affirmative action to redress the income inequalities.
