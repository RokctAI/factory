## Question (4 marks)

Why is it necessary for the government to impose taxes on demerit goods? (2 x 2) (Refers to the Q2.2.1 extract on public sector failure.)

## Method

explanation

The mark allocation (2 x 2) asks for TWO separate reasons, each explained for two marks. Start from what a demerit good is: a good that harms the consumer or society, like alcohol or tobacco. Then reason out why taxing it helps — a tax raises its price and discourages consumption, it recovers the external costs the good imposes on people and the environment, and the revenue can fund public goods. Give two of these, each fully explained.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- To make demerit goods more expensive in order to discourage consumers from buying them.
- To recover the external costs that are caused by demerit goods to human beings and the environment.
- To raise revenue that can be used to finance the provision of public goods
