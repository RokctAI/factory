## Question (1 mark)

Give ONE term: The social indicators that relate to the information about the size and structure of a population.

## Method

terminology recall

"Demography" is the study of populations — their size, growth, age structure and composition. Social indicators that describe the size and structure of a population are therefore called demographic indicators, for example population growth and life expectancy.
