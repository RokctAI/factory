## Question (2 marks)

Briefly describe the term sanitation. (Refers to the Q3.2.1 bar graph of household access to services.)

## Method

definition

Define sanitation as the provision of facilities and services — toilets, garbage disposal and sewerage systems — that ensure the safe disposal of human waste, hygiene and a clean environment that protects people's health.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The provision of facilities and services, such as toilets, garbage disposal and sewerage system, to ensure safe disposal of human waste, hygiene and cleans environment to protect people's health.
