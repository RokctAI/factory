## Question (2 marks)

Explain the impact of an increase in the population on a country's labour force. (Refers to the Q3.2.1 bar graph of household access to services.)

## Method

explanation

One well-explained effect earns both marks: a larger population means more people are available to work, so the labour force grows — with knock-on effects such as downward pressure on wages, a wider variety of skills, or unemployment if there are not enough jobs.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- An increase in population increases the number of people who are available to work increasing the labour force.
- An increase in supply of labour may lead to lower wages and salaries for the labour force.
- A larger population brings a variety of skills and experiences to the labour market.
- An increasing population may cause unemployment because there might not be enough jobs for everyone seeking work.
- Social and economic inequalities gap may widen as some groups cannot access education, training, and job opportunities.
