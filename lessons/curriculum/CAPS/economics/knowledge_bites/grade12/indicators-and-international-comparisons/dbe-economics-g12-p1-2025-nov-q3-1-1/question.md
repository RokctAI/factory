## Question (2 marks)

Name any TWO international organisations that require member countries to standardise economic and social indicators. (2 x 1)

## Method

recall listing

One mark per organisation: name any two of the big multilateral bodies that require member countries to standardise their economic and social indicators — the International Monetary Fund (IMF), the World Bank or the United Nations (UN).

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- International Monetary Fund (IMF)
- World bank
- United nations (UN)
