## Question (2 marks)

How can literacy levels be improved in developing countries? (1 x 2)

## Method

explanation

Offer one developed measure for two marks: build more schools and widen access to education; run programmes that teach reading for meaning at elementary level; make funding accessible to marginalised communities; or make schooling compulsory for all children.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- The government must build more schools and increase access to education.
- Implement policies and programmes that encourage learners to read for meaning at elementary levels.
- Make funding available and accessible to all marginalised communities.
- Implement policies that make it compulsory for all the children to attend schooling.
