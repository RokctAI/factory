## Question (2 marks)

The economically active population consists of people between the ages of …

A 15 and 54.
B 15 and 34.
C 15 and 64.
D 15 and 74.

## Method

multiple choice recall

This is a definition to memorise: the economically active population (EAP) covers everyone of working age who is able and willing to work — people between the ages of 15 and 64. Employment and unemployment rates are calculated against this group.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C - 15 and 64
