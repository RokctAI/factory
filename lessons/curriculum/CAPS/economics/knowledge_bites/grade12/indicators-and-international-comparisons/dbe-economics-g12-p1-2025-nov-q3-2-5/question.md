## Question (4 marks)

How can access to clean water boost a country's economic performance? (2 x 2) (Refers to the Q3.2.1 bar graph of household access to services.)

## Method

explanation

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

Teachable steps: give two separate channels from clean water to economic performance, two marks each: healthier workers (fewer diseases and sick days, higher productivity); better school attendance leading to a better-educated, higher-earning workforce; more tourism; and greater attractiveness to investing businesses.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Clean water helps prevent diseases thus keeping workers healthy, reduces sick days, and boosts productivity.
- Access to clean water promotes regular school attendance that leads to a better-educated workforce with higher earning potential.
- Access to clean water may attract tourists to the country which increases economic activity.
- Countries with reliable supply of clean water are more attractive to businesses that want to invest.
