## Question (2 marks)

Why is access to healthcare services important to the labour force? (From the data-response set on South Africa's labour productivity growth.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

- Access to healthcare services helps to improve the mental and physical ability of workers which increases the productivity of the labour force.
- Access to affordable health care helps to minimise medical costs and contain the workers' cost of living.
- Access to good health care can make an area more attractive to potential workers and retain the skilled labour force.
