## Question (1 mark)

(Bar graph titled SOUTH AFRICA'S GENERAL HOUSEHOLD SURVEY ON ACCESS TO SERVICES – 2024, adapted from StatsSA GHS, May 2024: the vertical axis shows the percentage of households with access, from 0 to 100; four bars: Sanitation 83,3%, Drinking water 87%, Electricity 89,8%, Refuse removal 60%.)

Identify in the graph above the service to which households have the least access.

## Method

data response identification

Read the four bars and pick the shortest one. Refuse removal, at 60%, sits well below sanitation at 83,3%, drinking water at 87% and electricity at 89,8%.

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Refuse removal
