## Question (1 mark)

Name any ONE participant in a two-sector economy. (Refers to the Q2.3.1 graph of the multiplier effect in a two-sector economy: expenditure lines E = 20 + 0,5Y and E1 = 30 + 0,5Y against the 45-degree line E = Y.)

## Method

recall

A two-sector economy is the simplest circular-flow model: it strips the economy down to just the two core participants who trade with each other — households supplying factors of production and businesses producing goods and services. Naming either one earns the mark.

(see marking guidelines)

- Businesses / Producers / Firms
