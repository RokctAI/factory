## Question (2 marks)

Name any TWO methods used to calculate gross domestic product (GDP). (2 x 1)

## Method

recall listing

Remember that the same circular flow can be measured at three points: where goods are made (production), where factor rewards are paid out (income), and where spending happens (expenditure). Naming any two of these three methods earns the two marks.
