## Question (2 marks)

The sum of marginal propensity to consume (mpc) and marginal propensity to save (mps) in a two-sector model is equal to …

A 2.
B 1.
C 0,5.
D 0,75.

## Method

multiple choice recall

In a two-sector model every extra rand of income can only go two ways: it is either spent (consumption) or not spent (saving). So the fraction consumed plus the fraction saved must account for the whole rand: mpc + mps = 1.

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)
