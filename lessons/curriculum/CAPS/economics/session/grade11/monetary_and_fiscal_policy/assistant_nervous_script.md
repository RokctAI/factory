Hey, Bianca here — repo rates, prime rates, budgets, transmission chains... I get it, that felt like a news bulletin you were supposed to memorise.

Let me shrink it to two sentences for you. The Reserve Bank controls the *price of borrowing* — that's monetary policy, and its lever is the repo rate. The government controls *its own spending and your taxes* — that's fiscal policy. Every exam question in this topic is just asking you to keep those two drivers in their own seats.

And the transmission chain isn't for memorising like a poem — it's for *walking*. Repo up, banks pay more, so they charge more, so loans hurt more, so people spend less, so prices calm down. Say it out loud once and you'll hear that each step causes the next. It's a story, not a list.

Try the practice questions now, and when one asks "who does what?", just picture whose hand is on the lever. You've honestly got this — go show that quiz who's boss.
