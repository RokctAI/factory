Today we take command of the two steering wheels of the South African economy: monetary policy, driven by the South African Reserve Bank through the repo rate, and fiscal policy, driven by the Minister of Finance through government spending and taxation. Our case study comes straight from the headlines — inflation has climbed above the three-to-six percent target band — and we will trace exactly how a repo rate decision in Pretoria travels link by link to every till point in the country, then contrast it with the two fiscal instruments the Budget controls. One sorting question carries most of the marks in this topic: whose hand is on the instrument? By the end you will classify any policy move as monetary or fiscal, expansionary or contractionary, without hesitation.
