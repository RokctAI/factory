Hey, Bianca here — development, six characteristics, three plans, IKS... that lesson covered half a textbook, so if you're feeling buried, fair enough. Let's dig you out with three keepers.

Keeper one: taller versus better. Growth is the tape measure — more stuff produced. Development is whether life actually got better — longer, healthier, more schooled. South Africa in one sentence: richer than we are healthy or educated. If you remember nothing else, remember that sentence — it unlocks the HDI, the comparisons, everything.

Keeper two: the walk. Don't memorise six characteristics as a list — walk them down a street. Thin wallets, spades where machines should be, one pension feeding a household, matriculants with nowhere to go, ore out and phones in, power that cuts. Six scenes, and you can rebuild the textbook list from the scenes any time.

Keeper three: three plans, three firsts. RDP — needs first. GEAR — stability first. NDP — capability first. And one honest verdict: services delivered, jobs missed.

IKS? That's just the reminder that the street already had working systems — stokvels, healers, drought crops — and smart development builds on them instead of bulldozing them.
