Today we move from how much an economy produces to what that production does for human lives: economic development. We separate development from growth, measure it with the Human Development Index and the poverty lines beneath it, and work through the six classic characteristics of developing countries with South Africa held honestly up to each one. From there we survey the strategies countries use to develop — including South Africa's own succession of plans from the RDP through GEAR to the NDP 2030 — and close with a source of development thinking the exam increasingly rewards: Indigenous Knowledge Systems, from stokvels to the hoodia case for protection.
