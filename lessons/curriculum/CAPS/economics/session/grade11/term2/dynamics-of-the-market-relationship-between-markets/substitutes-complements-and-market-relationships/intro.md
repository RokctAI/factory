Today we point the demand and supply machinery at a bigger truth: markets talk to each other. A price change in one market shifts demand in another, which is the world of substitutes and complements; what happens in the shops decides what happens to jobs, which is the loop between product markets and factor markets through derived demand; and the number of sellers in a market decides how prices get made at all, which is the map of the four market structures. Each of the three relationships is a reliable source of exam marks, and each comes with one rule you can say in a single breath — we will build all three on the board with the shifts drawn and the rules boxed.
