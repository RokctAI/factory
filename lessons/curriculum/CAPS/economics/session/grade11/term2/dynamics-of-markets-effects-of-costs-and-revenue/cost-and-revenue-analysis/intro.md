Today we build cost and revenue analysis — the working method behind every market-structure question Grade 12 will ask. Starting from a plain table of output and total cost, we derive average and marginal cost, build the revenue side at a market price of R30, and then answer the one question every firm asks: how much should I produce to maximise profit? The whole session turns on two verbs — divide for averages, subtract for marginals — and on the rule that a firm produces every unit whose marginal revenue covers its marginal cost and refuses the first unit that does not. Master these six columns now and next year's perfect markets, monopolies and oligopolies only change the shape of the revenue side, never the method.
