Today we turn the law of demand from a direction into a number: price elasticity of demand, the percentage change in quantity demanded divided by the percentage change in price. That single number decides whether a spaza raises or drops its prices, why the Minister of Finance taxes cigarettes and not bread, and a reliable eight to twelve marks in every Grade 11 paper. We will calculate it for a taxi fare and a chocolate bar, place every demand curve on the five-zone scale, work through the determinants, and then cash the number in on the question businesses actually ask — what a price change does to total revenue.
