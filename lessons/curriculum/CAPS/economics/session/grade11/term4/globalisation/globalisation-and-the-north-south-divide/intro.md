Today we tackle globalisation: what it means for economies to become interconnected and interdependent, the technology and policy forces that caused it, and the genuinely two-sided scorecard of consequences it delivers — from South Africa's world-scale motor industry to Newcastle's silent sewing machines. Then we go beneath the scorecard to the divide globalisation has not healed: the persistent gap between the global North and the global South, driven by the terms of trade, foreign debt and unequal voice in the world's rulebooks. South Africa sits inside every part of this story as an open economy on the southern side of the divide, so the examples come home fast — and so do the exam questions.
