Today we put numbers on the circular flow's arrows: the national accounts. We start from the identity that production, expenditure and income must give one and the same total, then build the expenditure side item by item — household consumption C with its four classes of goods, government consumption G with its two examinable exclusions, and gross fixed capital formation I with the gross-to-net calculation. From there we assemble the three aggregates the syllabus names — gross national expenditure, gross value added and gross national income — running the exact chain of conversions in rand billions, in both directions, the way the examiner will ask for it.
