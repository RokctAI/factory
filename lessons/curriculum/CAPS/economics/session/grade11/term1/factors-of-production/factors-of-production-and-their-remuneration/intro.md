Today we take the four factors of production off Grade 10's list and put them to work: natural resources, labour, capital and entrepreneurship, each with the exact reward it earns — rent, wages, interest and profit — and the reasoning that determines each payment. We will compute value added for a real firm and share it out among the factors, showing why profit is the residual that can turn negative while everyone else is paid first. From there the topic turns to South Africa itself: who can actually reach these factors, and how integrated development planning, community participation and empowerment procurement are built to widen that reach. Examiners reward the exact pairing and the precise mechanism, and this session drills both.
