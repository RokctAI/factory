Today we open up the public sector — the participant that takes roughly one rand in every three moving through South Africa's economy. Paper 1 asks four things about it: its composition and why markets genuinely need it, why providing public services is hard, how the February budget and fiscal policy work — including the one curve this topic owns, the Laffer curve — and what happens when the state itself fails. We pair each market failure with the public-sector role that answers it, work through the budget's revenue and spending sides, draw the Laffer hump and read it on both slopes, and close with the balanced conclusion the twenty-mark essays reward. This is where state-owned enterprises, the deficit and the grant system stop being headlines and become examinable material.
