Today we open the foreign sector fully: why nations trade at all, the balance of payments on which every cross-border rand is recorded, and the market where the rand itself is priced against the dollar. We work comparative advantage from a two-country table, walk the accounts of the country's bank statement with the world, build the foreign exchange diagram curve by curve, and finish with the four channels a country uses to correct a persistent deficit. This is the topic behind every headline about the rand weakening, and the examiners' favourite place for data-response questions with real figures.
