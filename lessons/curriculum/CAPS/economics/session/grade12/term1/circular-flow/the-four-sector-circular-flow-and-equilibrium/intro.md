Today we take the circular flow you drew in Grade 10 and turn it into the arithmetic that carries the whole of Paper 1's macroeconomics — the four-sector model with households, businesses, government and the foreign sector. We line up the three leakages against the three injections, prove equilibrium with S plus T plus M equal to I plus G plus X, then measure the flow three ways and convert between market prices, factor cost, GNI and real GDP per capita. We finish by reading a disequilibrium — what actually happens in the economy when the totals stop balancing — because every essay on business cycles, fiscal policy or the balance of payments is written on top of this diagram.
