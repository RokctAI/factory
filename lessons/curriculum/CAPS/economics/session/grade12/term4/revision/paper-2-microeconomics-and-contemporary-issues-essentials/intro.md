Today we revise the whole of Paper 2 — Microeconomics and Contemporary Economic Issues, the graph-heavy 150 marks. We walk the market structures from the perfectly competitive price taker with its flat revenue line through monopoly, oligopoly's kinked demand curve and monopolistic competition, then the market failures with their externality diagrams, the state's price controls with their shortages and surpluses, and cost-benefit analysis. The contemporary half sweeps inflation, tourism and environmental sustainability, each a full essay possibility. Every graph is told as a story in words you can reproduce under pressure, because that is exactly how the paper asks for it.
