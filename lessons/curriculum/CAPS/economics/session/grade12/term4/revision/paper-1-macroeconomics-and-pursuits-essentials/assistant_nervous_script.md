Hey, it's Mandy — Paper 1 revision can feel like the whole year rushing at you in one session, and I saw a few shoulders drop when the policy alphabet came out. Breathe. This paper is the most predictable one you will ever write.

Here is why. The structure is fixed: thirty objective marks, then two forty-mark questions you CHOOSE from three, then one essay you choose from two. You never answer everything — you answer what you know best. That choice is your power, so revise for it: be strong on two of the three Section B territories and carry two prepared essay skeletons into the room.

The calculations are only two moves deep. Multiplier: one divided by one minus the mpc — and check your answer is bigger than one, always. Terms of trade: export price index over import price index, times 100. Write the formula line first and the marker follows you even if a digit slips.

The graphs are stories you already know. The rand's price is just supply and demand for dollars — say who buys, who sells, and which curve moves. The Laffer curve is the lemonade stand: nothing earned at a zero rate, nothing at a hundred, a peak between.

And the alphabet soup is a timeline, not a memory test: houses, discipline, skills, jobs, 2030. Five words, five plans, in order.

One skeleton, two formulas, two curve stories, five words. That is Paper 1. You've got this!
