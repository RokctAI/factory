Today we revise the whole of Paper 1 — Macroeconomics and Economic Pursuits, 150 marks — by walking the skeleton the examiners rebuild every year: the circular flow and the multiplier, business cycles, the public sector and fiscal policy, foreign exchange and the balance of payments, then trade policy, the growth plans and the indicators that measure them. At every stop we flag the definitions, calculations and curve stories that keep returning, from the multiplier arithmetic to the Laffer curve and the market for the rand told in words. Everything is drawn from the official examination guideline, so treat each item as a promise about what the paper can ask. The skeleton never changes — only the numbers do.
