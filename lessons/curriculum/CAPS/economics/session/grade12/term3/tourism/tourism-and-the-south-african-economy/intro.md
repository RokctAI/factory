Today we study the export that walks in: tourism, the industry where the customer travels to the product, spends foreign money inside our borders, and leaves the product standing for the next visitor. We pin down the precise definitions the paper tests — the tourist, the day visitor, and the domestic, inbound and outbound flows — then explain why world tourism exploded, what it does to GDP, jobs, households and the state through the multiplier, and where the costs and leakages hide. We finish with South Africa's own shop window, from the Big Five to indigenous knowledge systems, and the policy fixes that could grow it. This topic feeds the growth and unemployment essays directly, so count every busload like an economist.
