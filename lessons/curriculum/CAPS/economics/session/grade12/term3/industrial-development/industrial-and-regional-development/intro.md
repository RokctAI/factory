Today we tackle industrial and regional development — why a country that ships out iron ore and buys back machinery is selling the cheapest link in the chain, and what government does to close that gap. We build the case for industrialisation through value added, employment, stability and linkages, then anchor South Africa's named strategies: the National Industrial Policy Framework, the IPAPs, the automotive programme, the IDC, and the special economic zones and corridors that try to spread growth across the map. The session ends with the skill examiners prize most — judging whether these strategies are appropriate, with wins and failures cited by name. This is the backbone of the economic-pursuits essay, so every acronym here is a mark.
