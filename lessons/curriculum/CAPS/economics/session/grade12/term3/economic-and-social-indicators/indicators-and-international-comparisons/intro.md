Today we assemble the economy's full dashboard — the economic and social indicators that tell you how production, prices, money, labour and human lives are moving, and that anchor the data-response questions of Paper 1. We will sort indicators by timing into leading, coincident and lagging, work through every gauge from real GDP and the money aggregates to life expectancy, the HDI and the Gini coefficient, and then learn the three corrections — per capita, purchasing power parity and coverage — that keep international comparisons honest. We close with the IMF and the World Bank, the two Bretton Woods institutions that read these dashboards for the whole world. Read the gauges correctly and the numbers stop being trivia and start being arguments.
