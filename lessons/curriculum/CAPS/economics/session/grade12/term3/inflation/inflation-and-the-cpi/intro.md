Today we take apart the single most reliable number in the Economics paper: the inflation rate, and the Consumer Price Index it is manufactured from. We start with the definition and its three tests, build the CPI basket the way Statistics South Africa builds it, and then drill the calculations — constructing the index, computing the inflation rate, weighting a basket, and running the formula backwards. From there we separate demand-pull from cost-push inflation, trace who wins and who loses when prices run, and end with inflation targeting, the repo rate, and the honest limits of that weapon. Every step here is a mark somewhere in Paper 1, from the two-mark definition to the forty-mark essay.
