Today we reach the capstone of the course: how a country grows richer, and — a different question — how its people come to live better. We separate economic growth from economic development and measure each properly, then weigh the demand-side approaches against the supply-side ones, with the four South African walls any stimulus runs into and the sequencing verdict the big essays reward. From there we judge South Africa's own plans — RDP, GEAR, ASGISA and the NDP — honestly, and widen out to the north-south divide with the mechanisms that keep the global gap open. Everything from the circular flow to the multiplier to the public sector converges here, and this topic stands behind the biggest essays on Paper 1.
