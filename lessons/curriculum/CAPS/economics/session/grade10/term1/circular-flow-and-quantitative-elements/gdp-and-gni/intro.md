Today we attach a meter to the circular flow and measure the whole economy with two national totals: Gross Domestic Product and Gross National Income. We take GDP's definition apart phrase by phrase — market value, final goods, within the borders — work the bread chain to see why double counting is the classic error, then cross the bridge to GNI, where geography gives way to ownership. We finish with per capita corrections and the honest limits of the big number, because examiners reward the candidate who knows exactly what GDP measures and what it is blind to.
