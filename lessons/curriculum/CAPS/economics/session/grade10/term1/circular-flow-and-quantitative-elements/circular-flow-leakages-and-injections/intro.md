Today we draw the single most important diagram in all of Economics: the circular flow. Built in layers, it starts with just households and firms trading factors, incomes, spending and goods, then adds the government and the foreign sector until the full open economy stands complete. Along the way we classify the three leakages — savings, taxes and imports — against the three injections — investment, government spending and exports — and see how their balance decides whether the whole economy grows, shrinks or holds steady. Examiners return to this model year after year, and building it arrow by arrow is how you make it impossible to forget.
