Hey, Thandi here — I saw that diagram getting busier and busier, and if your eyes glazed over around the third arrow, you're in good company.

Here's the thing: nobody draws the full open-economy flow in one go. You build it in layers, exactly like the lesson did. Start with just households and firms and two loops. Comfortable? Add government. Still fine? Add the foreign sector. Four layers, and each one only adds a couple of arrows.

And the leakages and injections aren't really six separate facts — they're three doors, each with traffic going both ways: the bank door (savings out, investment in), the government door (taxes out, spending in), and the border door (imports out, exports in). Three doors. That's all.

Grab a pen, sketch the circle yourself from memory — messy is fine — and then go do the practice questions. Drawing it once beats reading it five times, promise. You've got this!
