Today we define the discipline itself: what Economics studies, before any curve or cycle appears. We take the definition apart — limited resources against unlimited needs and wants — then map the subject's structure: the elements and processes of economic activity, microeconomics against macroeconomics, positive statements against normative ones, and the scientific method economists run on society. Examiners open Paper 1 with exactly these foundations, so precise definitions here are marks banked before the harder questions arrive.
