Today we study the pattern the economy's rises and falls make over time: business cycles — the recurring wave of activity around its long-term growth path. We name the four phases from upswing to trough, learn to read the leading, coincident and lagging indicators the Reserve Bank compiles, separate trend, cycle, season and shock inside one time series, and weigh the exogenous and endogenous causes with their very unequal effects. This is the topic that turns a news headline about recession into something you can define, date and defend in the examination.
