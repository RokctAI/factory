Hey, it's Thandi — phases, three kinds of indicators, four kinds of movement, two families of causes... this topic loves lists, and lists blur. So let's hang everything on the two pictures, because the pictures don't blur.

Picture one: the BREATH. In, full, out, empty — upswing, peak, downswing, trough. Every phase question is just "where in the breath are we?" — and each phase has street signs you know: hiring everywhere (in), prices creeping and everything strained (full), retrenchments spreading (out), cheap-and-quiet-but-turning (empty). The recession definition is the one exact phrase to memorise: two consecutive quarters of falling real GDP. Word for word.

Picture two: the DASHBOARD. Headlights bend before the road turns — anything that's a decision about the FUTURE: building plans, orders, confidence. Speedometer is now: today's production and sales. Mirror is after: unemployment, always late, because bosses fire late and hire late. When you get an indicator to classify, don't memorise — ask "is this a plan, a pulse, or an after-effect?"

The four movements trick: before calling anything a cycle, ask "is this just December?" (season), "just a flood?" (shock), "the slow decades-long climb?" (trend). What remains is the wave.

And causes: outside stones versus inside sloshing — drought and world prices versus mood and credit. Real answer: usually both.

Two pictures, one exact phrase, one sorting question each. That's the whole topic. You've got this!
