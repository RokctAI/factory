Today's topic is labour relations: the system South Africa built to turn the confrontation between employers and workers into regulated negotiation. We will see why labour law exists at all, then match the three foundational statutes — the Labour Relations Act, the Basic Conditions of Employment Act and COIDA — to their jobs, before working through the machinery of collective bargaining and the CCMA's ladder of conciliation, mediation and arbitration. Examiners test this topic as institutions matched to functions, so the whole session drills one habit: know which body does what, and why.
