Today we trace how the economy came to be: the journey from self-sufficient households, through surplus and barter, to money, growing markets, the state's entry as regulator, tax collector and builder, and finally industrialisation. This is the opening topic of term three, and examiners love it because it tests whether you can explain each stage as the solution to the previous stage's problem — barter's four failures answered by money's four functions, market limitations answered by the state. We will work through the full sequence twice, first with precise definitions and then as the story of one village growing up, ending with South Africa's own route into the industrial age through mining.
