Today we face South Africa's defining economic problem: unemployment. We sharpen the strict and expanded definitions and the rate's correct denominator, then work through the five types economists distinguish — frictional, structural, cyclical, seasonal and technological — because each has its own cause and therefore its own cure. From there we sort causes into layers and consequences across the individual, the economy and society, before weighing the three approaches to solving it: growth of production, public works programmes and the UIF, each with its strengths and honest limits. The whole topic examines as diagnosis and prescription: name the type, then match the remedy.
