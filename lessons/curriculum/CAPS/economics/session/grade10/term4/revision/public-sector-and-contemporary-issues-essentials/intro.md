Today is the second revision sweep for the examination: terms two, three and four gathered into the four structures the paper tests. We revise the state's instruments in the market — taxes, subsidies, price ceilings that create shortages and price floors that create surpluses — then the evolution of markets from barter through money to industry, then the labour force, the three Acts and the dispute ladder, and finally the twin contemporary issues of unemployment and economic redress. Every definition is sharpened to exam form, every diagram described so you can rebuild it, and every list attached to the mechanism that makes it earn marks.
