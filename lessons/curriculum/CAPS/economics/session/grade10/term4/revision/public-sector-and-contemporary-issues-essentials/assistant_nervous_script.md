Hey, it's Thandi — three terms in one sweep is a lot of ground, but look closer and it's really just four structures wearing different clothes.

Structure one: the referee. Tax shifts supply left, subsidy shifts it right, ceiling below equilibrium makes a SHORTAGE, floor above equilibrium makes a SURPLUS. Memory hook: ceilings make queues, floors make piles. And the minimum wage always gets both halves — protection for those who keep jobs, pressure on how many jobs there are.

Structure two: the story. Self-sufficiency, then surplus and barter, then money, then the state, then the machines. The exam's favourite corners: the double coincidence of wants as barter's brake, and money's four jobs — go-between, measuring stick, storage jar, promise-keeper.

Structure three: the gate. Fence the labour force first — working or actively seeking, fifteen to sixty-four. Demand for labour is DERIVED. Then the three Acts: basic conditions is the floor, labour relations is the collective game, employment equity is fairness — and the dispute ladder climbs conciliation, arbitration, Labour Court.

Structure four: the queue and the starting line. Five types of unemployment, each with its own cure — diagnose before prescribing. Redress runs through the four factors, and every instrument needs its partner: the field needs the seed money.

That's the whole sweep: referee, story, gate, starting line. The practice questions open to those four keys — go turn them.
