Today's topic is economic redress: the deliberate correction of historical economic injustice, and the question every earlier topic this year has been circling. We identify the economically marginalised groups with the mechanisms that excluded them, then structure redress through the four factors of production — land reform, employment equity and skills, B-BBEE and access to capital, and entrepreneurship support. From there we examine how economic decision-making itself was democratised, through labour law, public participation, NEDLAC's four constituencies and self-regulating bodies, and close by judging redress the way examiners reward: on both ledgers, achievements and shortfalls, with mechanisms rather than slogans.
