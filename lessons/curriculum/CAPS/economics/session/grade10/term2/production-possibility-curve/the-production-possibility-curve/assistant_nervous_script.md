Hey, it's Thandi — one curve carrying scarcity, choice, opportunity cost, unemployment AND growth... that's a lot of jobs for one line, and it's normal if the meanings blurred. Let's pin them with the bakery, because the bakery never blurs.

Three places, three meanings. ON the line: full day — best possible, pick your mix. INSIDE: lazy day — you had the hands and oven and didn't use them; that's waste, and nationally, unemployment. BEYOND: dream day — impossible until the bakery itself changes. When a question shows you a point, just ask: full, lazy, or dream?

Three movements, three names — this is where the exam trap lives. Walking ALONG the line: changing your mix, no growth. Walking from inside BACK TO the line: recovery — more of everything with the same resources. The LINE ITSELF stepping outward: growth, the only one that deserves the word. Say the three aloud once: along, back to, outward.

The bowed shape? One sentence: the best brown-bread bakers get used first, so each extra brown costs more whites — resources aren't all-purpose.

And the movers: internal (more resources, better skills, technology) versus external (weather, world prices, shocks). Sort them like drains and taps — two lists, examples ready.

One picture, three places, three movements, two lists. The whole topic folds into your pocket. You've got this!
