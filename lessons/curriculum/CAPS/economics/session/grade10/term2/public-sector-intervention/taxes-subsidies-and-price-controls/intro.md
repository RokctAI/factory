Today we study what happens when society overrules the market: public sector intervention through indirect taxes, subsidies, welfare, and price controls. Each instrument gets the same honest treatment — what it is for, what the demand and supply diagram predicts, and what else follows: the tax that shifts supply left, the subsidy that mirrors it, the ceiling that breeds queues and black markets, and the floor that piles up surpluses, ending with the minimum wage argued fairly in both directions. This three-question discipline is exactly how examiners want every intervention analysed.
