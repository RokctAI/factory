Today is a pure skills session: turning a demand and supply schedule into a proper market graph, and reading answers straight off it. Working with prices from R2 to R10, we interrogate the table first, build the axes with even scales and full labels, plot both curves point by point, and confirm equilibrium at R6 and 60 units. Then the graph becomes a measuring instrument as we read off the excess supply of 40 units at R8. These are the quiet presentation and reading marks that appear in every Economics paper, and they are won by routine, not talent.
