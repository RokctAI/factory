Hey, it's Thandi — if graphs make your stomach drop a little, listen up, because this is honestly the most learnable skill in all of Economics.

There's no theory to wrestle with here. It's a recipe: price up the side, quantity along the bottom, even steps on each axis, one dot per table row, join the dots, label. That's the whole thing. Nobody is born good at plotting — people just get neat at it by doing it three or four times.

And here's the encouraging part: this is where marks hide. Labels, dotted lines, even scales — those are marks the examiner *wants* to give you, before you've answered anything clever.

So please don't just read the lesson — grab graph paper (or the practice screen), plot the R2-to-R10 table yourself, and find that R8 surplus with your own ruler. One careful graph today and this becomes your banker question for the next three years. Go on — dots first, brilliance later!
