Today we learn the two forces that set every price you have ever paid: demand and supply. We state both laws, draw the market diagram with its downward and upward curves, and find equilibrium — the only price at which the market clears. Then we put the method to work on a painfully South African case study: a drought that halves the maize harvest, shifting supply left and driving the price up. Master the distinction between a movement along a curve and a shift of the whole curve, and half the marks in every market-dynamics question are already yours.
