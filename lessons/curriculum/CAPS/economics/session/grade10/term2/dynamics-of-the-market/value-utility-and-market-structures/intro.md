Today we lay three foundations under demand and supply: what value is and where it comes from, what kinds of markets exist, and what markets actually do for an economy. We resolve the famous water-and-diamonds paradox through scarcity and utility, classify markets from the perfect benchmark through monopoly, oligopoly and monopolistic competition, and weigh how electronic and world markets have rewritten the rules for South African buyers and sellers. Each foundation carries its own family of exam questions — definitions, classifications and functions — and precision in all three is what this session builds.
