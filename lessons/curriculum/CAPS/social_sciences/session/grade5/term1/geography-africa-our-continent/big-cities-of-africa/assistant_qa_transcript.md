### subtopic_1 — What Makes a Big City
**Assistant:** Which is the biggest city in Africa?

**Tutor:** It depends on how you measure it, which is why books sometimes disagree. Cities spread out, and it is hard to decide exactly where a city ends and the towns around it begin. If you count the whole area around the city, Cairo and Lagos are usually at the top of the list, each with many millions of people, and Kinshasa, the capital of the Democratic Republic of the Congo, is also one of the biggest. Counting people in a fast-growing city is also difficult, because people arrive every day and some live in informal settlements that are hard to count. In South Africa, Johannesburg is the biggest city, and together with the cities around it in Gauteng, such as Tshwane and Ekurhuleni, it forms one of the biggest built-up areas in Africa.

### subtopic_2 — Cairo and Lagos
**Assistant:** What is Nollywood?

**Tutor:** Nollywood is the nickname for the film industry of Nigeria, and much of it is based in and around Lagos. The name mixes the N of Nigeria with Hollywood, the famous film area in the United States. Nollywood grew quickly from the 1990s, when film-makers began making films cheaply on video, and selling them in markets. Today, Nigeria makes a huge number of films every year, more than most countries in the world, and they are watched across Africa and by Africans living overseas. Many are about family life, love, comedy, history and village and city life. Nigerian music is also very popular around the world. This shows that cities are not only about factories and offices. They are also places where people create music, films, art and fashion, and share them with others.

### subtopic_3 — Johannesburg and Nairobi
**Assistant:** How can lions live in a national park next to a big city like Nairobi?

**Tutor:** Nairobi National Park was set up in 1946 on the southern edge of the city, and it is one of the few national parks in the world right next to a capital city. It has grassland, bush and a river, with lions, buffalo, giraffes, zebras and rhinos. A fence runs along the side of the park that faces the city, to keep the animals and people safe, but the southern side is open, so animals can move in and out to a wider area where they find food and water at different times of year. As Nairobi grows, there are more roads, houses and farms around the park, and it is getting harder for the animals to move. People in Kenya are working to find ways for the city and the wildlife to share the land.
