Hey — this topic fits in three pictures: boxes that do not exist, a lie that paid well and a game with unfair rules.
The boxes. Race divides people by visible features, but all humans are one species sharing about 99.9 percent of DNA, so race is a social construct with real effects. Prejudice is prejudging; a stereotype is a fixed, oversimplified idea; discrimination is unfair action. Racism is the belief that some races are superior, with prejudice and discrimination, most harmful when combined with power.
The lie. Modern racism grew with the Atlantic slave trade and colonialism to justify exploitation. Scientific racism and social Darwinism justified conquest. Sarah Baartman was exhibited in Europe; her remains returned in 2002. Nazi racism led to the Holocaust; UNESCO rejected racial ranking in 1950.
The rules. Personal racism is individual attitudes and actions; institutional racism is built into laws and systems, like apartheid's Population Registration Act. Its legacy continues. The Constitution's non-racialism, Section 9, the Equality Act and the Human Rights Commission challenge racism, and so can individuals.
