Why are some countries rich and others poor? This lesson examines historical and economic reasons for differences in development. We begin with colonialism: how European powers divided Africa at the Berlin Conference of 1884 to 1885 and shaped colonial economies to export raw materials, leaving borders, railways and economies that served the colonisers. We then look at unfair trade, where countries that export raw materials such as cocoa, coffee or copper earn little while the profits of processing go elsewhere. Finally we study technology and industrialisation, from Britain's Industrial Revolution to South Africa's mining-based economy and the digital divide today. These ideas connect to Grade 10 and 11 History on colonialism and to Grade 11 Geography's development studies.
