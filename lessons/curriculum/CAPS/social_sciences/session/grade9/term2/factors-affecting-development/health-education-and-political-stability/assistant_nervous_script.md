Hey — this lesson is about people, and it fits in three pictures: a worker with a fever, a ladder of learning, and a calm house.

A worker with a fever. Illness stops people working and learning. Malaria killed more than 600 000 people in 2022, mostly African children. South Africa has the biggest HIV treatment programme in the world. Welfare, like child support grants and school meals for about nine million learners, protects poor families.

A ladder of learning. Each extra year of school can raise earnings by around a tenth. Educating girls helps whole families: healthier children, who also go to school. But quality matters: in 2021 about 81 per cent of South African Grade 4 learners could not read for meaning.

A calm house. War and corruption destroy schools, clinics and trust. Botswana, very poor in 1966, used diamond money wisely with stable democracy and developed fast.

All three connect in cycles of poverty or development. Open the practice set when you are ready. You've got this!
