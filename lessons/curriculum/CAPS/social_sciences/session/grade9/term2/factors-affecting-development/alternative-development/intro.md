For two hundred years, many people assumed that the only road to development ran through factories, smokestacks and heavy industry. This lesson asks whether there are other roads. We compare top-down development, such as large dams and industrial zones planned by governments, with bottom-up development led by communities. We explore alternatives to industrialisation: appropriate technology, small loans through microfinance, community-based tourism and ecotourism, agriculture-led development, services and digital innovation such as mobile money, and public works that restore the environment. South African examples include stokvels, the Makuleke community in the Kruger National Park and the Working for Water programme. These ideas lead into sustainable development next, and into Grade 11 Geography.
