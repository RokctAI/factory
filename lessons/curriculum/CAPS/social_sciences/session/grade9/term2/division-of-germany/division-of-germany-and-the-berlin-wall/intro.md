For twenty-eight years a concrete wall cut through the middle of a great European city, separating families, friends and neighbours. This lesson explains how Germany, defeated in 1945, was divided into four occupation zones and then into two states, and why its capital, Berlin, became the front line of the Cold War. We follow the Berlin Blockade and Airlift of 1948 to 1949, the creation of West Germany and East Germany in 1949, the flight of millions of East Germans to the West, and the overnight building of the Berlin Wall on 13 August 1961. We also hear the human stories of escape and loss. Grade 12 History returns to Berlin as a Cold War crisis.
