Hey — this story fits in three pictures: a cake cut into four, planes every few minutes, and a wall overnight.

A cake. In 1945 Germany was divided into four zones: American, British, French and Soviet. Berlin, deep inside the Soviet zone, was also split into four. The Allies argued; in 1947 the American and British zones joined, and Marshall Plan money went to the west.

Planes. In June 1948 the West introduced the Deutsche Mark. On 24 June Stalin blockaded West Berlin. The Berlin Airlift flew in food and coal for eleven months: more than 270 000 flights and about 2,3 million tonnes. Stalin gave up in May 1949. In 1949 Germany split into West Germany and East Germany.

A wall. About 2,7 million East Germans left between 1949 and 1961, many through Berlin. On 13 August 1961 East German forces sealed the border with barbed wire, then concrete. At least 140 people died at the Wall. Kennedy said "Ich bin ein Berliner" in 1963.
