The new driver. In March 1985 Gorbachev took over, aged 54. Glasnost meant openness; perestroika meant restructuring. In 1987 he and Reagan signed the INF Treaty. In 1988 he told the United Nations that every nation could choose its own path.

The gate. In 1989 Poland elected Solidarity, Hungary opened its border, and tens of thousands of East Germans marched in Leipzig. On 9 November 1989, Schabowski announced new travel rules "immediately", crowds rushed to the Wall, and the guards opened the gates. Germany reunified on 3 October 1990. In South Africa, de Klerk unbanned the ANC on 2 February 1990.
