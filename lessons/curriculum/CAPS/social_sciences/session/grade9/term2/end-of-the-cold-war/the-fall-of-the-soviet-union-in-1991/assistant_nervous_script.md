Hey — this story fits in three pictures: a house held together with straps, a man on a tank, and a flag coming down on Christmas night.

The house. The Soviet Union was fifteen republics, held together by the Communist Party, the army and the KGB. Gorbachev's glasnost loosened the straps, and nationalism grew. In August 1989 the Baltic Way human chain demanded independence, and Lithuania declared independence in March 1990. The economy collapsed into shortages and inflation.

The tank. On 19 August 1991 hardliners put Gorbachev under house arrest in Crimea and sent tanks into Moscow. Boris Yeltsin, elected President of Russia in June 1991, climbed onto a tank and called for resistance. The coup failed after three days, and the Communist Party was blamed.

The flag. On 1 December 1991 Ukraine voted for independence. On 8 December Russia, Ukraine and Belarus declared the Union over. On 25 December 1991 Gorbachev resigned and the Soviet flag came down. Fifteen countries replaced it, and the United States was the only superpower left.
