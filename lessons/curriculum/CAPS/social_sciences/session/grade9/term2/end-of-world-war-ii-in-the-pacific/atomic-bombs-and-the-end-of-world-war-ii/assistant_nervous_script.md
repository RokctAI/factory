Hey — this is how the war ended, and it fits in three pictures: an island ladder, a secret desert laboratory, and a flash over two cities.

An island ladder. American forces island-hopped towards Japan. Iwo Jima and Okinawa in 1945 cost huge numbers of lives. Kamikaze pilots crashed into ships. A firebombing raid on Tokyo in March 1945 killed about 100 000 people. Japan's generals still refused to surrender.

A secret laboratory. The Manhattan Project, led by Oppenheimer, built the atomic bomb. The first test was on 16 July 1945 in New Mexico. The Potsdam Declaration warned Japan of prompt and utter destruction.

A flash over two cities. Hiroshima on 6 August 1945: about 140 000 dead by the end of the year. The Soviet Union declared war on 8 August and invaded Manchuria. Nagasaki on 9 August: about 74 000 dead. The emperor announced surrender on 15 August, and Japan signed on 2 September 1945. In 1949 the Soviet Union tested its own bomb, and the nuclear age began.
