At 8:15 on the morning of 6 August 1945, an American aircraft dropped a single bomb on the Japanese city of Hiroshima. In an instant, most of the city was destroyed, and by the end of the year about 140 000 people had died. Three days later a second bomb struck Nagasaki. This lesson explains when, where, why and how World War II ended in the Pacific. We follow the bloody island campaigns of 1945, the secret Manhattan Project that built the bomb, the bombings themselves and their human cost, the Soviet declaration of war, and Japan's surrender on 2 September 1945. We also see how these events opened the nuclear age, which shaped the Cold War studied in Grade 12 History.
