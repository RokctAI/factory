How do we know whether one country is more developed than another, or whether South Africa is developing faster today than twenty years ago? Geographers answer with indicators: measurable facts that show something about people's lives. In this lesson we work with economic indicators such as GDP per capita and GNI per capita, and calculate them ourselves; social and demographic indicators such as life expectancy, infant mortality, literacy, access to clean water and doctors per person; and measures of inequality such as the Gini coefficient. We also ask what every indicator leaves out, and why a single number never tells the whole story. These skills lead directly to the Human Development Index in the next lesson and to Grade 11 Geography's data work.
