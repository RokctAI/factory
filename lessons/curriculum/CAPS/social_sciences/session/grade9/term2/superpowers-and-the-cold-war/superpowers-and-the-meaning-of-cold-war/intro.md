After 1945 two countries towered over the rest of the world: the United States and the Soviet Union. They were called superpowers, and for more than forty years they competed in a struggle known as the Cold War. It was cold because the two superpowers never fought each other directly in a full-scale war, yet it shaped almost every corner of the globe, including Southern Africa. In this lesson we define a superpower, explain the meaning of the term Cold War and its main methods, from alliances such as NATO and the Warsaw Pact to propaganda, espionage and proxy wars, and see how the Cold War affected Africa and apartheid South Africa. Grade 12 History studies the Cold War in depth.
