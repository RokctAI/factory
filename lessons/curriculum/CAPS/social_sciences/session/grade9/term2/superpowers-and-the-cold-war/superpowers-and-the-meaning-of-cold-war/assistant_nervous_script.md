Hey — the Cold War sounds huge, but it fits in three pictures: two boxers who never punch, fighting through others, and a battlefield close to home.

Two boxers. A superpower has worldwide military, economic and political power. After 1945, only the United States and the Soviet Union qualified. The Cold War, from about 1947 to 1991, was cold because they never fought each other directly. Both had nuclear weapons, so attacking would mean mutually assured destruction, MAD.

Fighting through others. They formed alliances, NATO in 1949 and the Warsaw Pact in 1955. They used propaganda, spies, an arms race and a space race. The worst were proxy wars, where they backed opposite sides, as in Korea, Vietnam and Afghanistan. Millions died.

Close to home. In Angola from 1975, the Soviet Union and Cuba backed one side; America and apartheid South Africa backed the other. The apartheid government used anti-communism to ban its opponents. Many countries joined the Non-Aligned Movement in 1961.
