This lesson brings the development topic together with one powerful idea: sustainable development, defined in 1987 as development that meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs. We examine its economic, social and environmental dimensions and why all three must be balanced. We follow the global response, from the Rio Earth Summit of 1992 to the seventeen Sustainable Development Goals adopted in 2015, and we look at South Africa's challenges and opportunities, including Cape Town's Day Zero water crisis, our dependence on coal and the move towards renewable energy. These ideas connect with Term 4's lessons on resources and with Grade 11 and 12 Geography.
