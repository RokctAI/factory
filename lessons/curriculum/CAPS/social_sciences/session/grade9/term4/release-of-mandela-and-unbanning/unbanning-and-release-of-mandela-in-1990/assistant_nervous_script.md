Hey, this topic fits in three pictures: a speech that surprised everyone, a walk through a prison gate and a long road home.
The speech. F.W. de Klerk became party leader in February 1989 and State President in September. Pressure came from the uprisings, the economy, sanctions, the fall of the Berlin Wall, Namibia and secret talks. On 2 February 1990, he unbanned the ANC, PAC and SACP and lifted restrictions on the UDF and COSATU. He also freed non-violent prisoners, eased media rules and suspended executions. The CP and AWB were furious.
The gate. Mandela was arrested in 1962 and sentenced to life after Rivonia in 1964. He was held on Robben Island, then Pollsmoor from 1982, then Victor Verster from 1988. On 11 February 1990, he walked out with Winnie. He spoke at Cape Town City Hall as a humble servant of the people, and said the armed struggle would continue for now.
The road. Sisulu and others had been freed in October 1989, and Tambo returned in December 1990. The Groote Schuur Minute came in May 1990, and in the Pretoria Minute of August 1990 the armed struggle was suspended. Group Areas and Population Registration were repealed in 1991.
