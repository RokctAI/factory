On Friday 2 February 1990, State President F.W. de Klerk stood up in Parliament in Cape Town and made a speech that very few people had expected. He announced that the ANC, the PAC, the South African Communist Party and many other organisations were no longer banned, and that Nelson Mandela would soon be released. Nine days later, on Sunday 11 February, Mandela walked out of Victor Verster Prison, hand in hand with his wife Winnie, after twenty-seven years in prison. This lesson explains why De Klerk took this step, what he announced, how Mandela's release unfolded, and how the release of other political prisoners and the return of exiles opened the way to negotiations.
