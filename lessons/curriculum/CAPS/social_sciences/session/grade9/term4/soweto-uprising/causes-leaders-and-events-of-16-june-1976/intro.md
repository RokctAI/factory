On the morning of Wednesday 16 June 1976, thousands of school students in Soweto put on their uniforms, picked up placards and set out to march to Orlando Stadium. They were protesting against a government order that half of their school subjects had to be taught in Afrikaans. Before the day was over, the police had opened fire, and a twelve-year-old boy named Hector Pieterson lay dying in the arms of an older student. This lesson explains the causes of the Soweto uprising, from Bantu Education and the Afrikaans Medium Decree to the influence of Black Consciousness; the student leaders and organisations behind the march; and what happened on 16 June 1976, the day that young people changed the course of South African history.
