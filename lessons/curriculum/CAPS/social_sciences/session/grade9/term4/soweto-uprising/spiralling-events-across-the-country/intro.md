The shooting in Soweto on 16 June 1976 did not end the protest. It spread it. Within days, students in townships across the Witwatersrand were marching and burning government buildings. By August, the uprising had reached Cape Town, where African and coloured students marched side by side, and over the following months it touched more than a hundred towns, from Port Elizabeth to the universities of the north. Students called on their parents to stay away from work, and hundreds of thousands did. This lesson follows how the uprising spiralled across the country between June 1976 and 1977, how students, workers and communities took part, and how the government responded with force, detention and death.
