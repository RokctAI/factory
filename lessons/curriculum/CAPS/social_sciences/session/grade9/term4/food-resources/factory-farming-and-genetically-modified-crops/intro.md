Chicken is the most popular meat in South Africa, and most of it comes from huge sheds that each hold tens of thousands of birds, raised to market weight in about six weeks. Much of the maize in South African maize meal comes from plants whose genes were changed in a laboratory so that they make their own insecticide. These two technologies, factory farming and genetic modification, produce large amounts of affordable food, and both are hotly debated. This lesson explains what factory farming is and how it works, its benefits and its costs for animals, people and the environment, what genetically modified crops are and how they are used in South Africa, and the arguments for and against them, so that you can weigh the evidence and form your own view.
