South Africa produces enough food to feed everyone who lives in it, and it exports large amounts of fruit, wine, maize and other products. Yet in many South African homes, there are days when children go to bed hungry, and roughly a quarter of young children are too short for their age because they have not had enough nutritious food. How can a country be food secure and food insecure at the same time? This lesson explains the concept of food security and its four pillars, the situation in South Africa and the world, and the role that science and technology have played in producing more food, from the Green Revolution of the 1960s to irrigation, mechanisation, fertilisers and modern technology, together with the benefits and costs of these changes.
