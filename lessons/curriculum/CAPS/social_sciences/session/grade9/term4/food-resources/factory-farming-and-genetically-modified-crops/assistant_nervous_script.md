The city. Factory farming raises many animals in confined spaces for cheap meat, eggs and milk. In South Africa, poultry is the largest farming sector; broilers reach market weight in about five to six weeks; many hens are in battery cages; most beef cattle finish in feedlots. Benefits: affordable protein, efficiency, jobs. Costs: animal welfare, antibiotic resistance, disease such as bird flu in 2023, manure pollution, feed and emissions, corporate dominance.
The recipe. Genes carry instructions. Selective breeding is old; genetic modification changes DNA directly. Bt crops resist insects; herbicide-tolerant crops survive weedkiller; Golden Rice adds beta-carotene. South Africa began Bt cotton in the late 1990s and Bt maize in 1998, regulated by the GMO Act of 1997, and is Africa's largest GM grower.
The scale. For: yields, fewer sprays, conservation agriculture, safety findings of major scientific bodies. Against: gene flow, stalk borer resistance, patented seed and dependence, hunger rooted in poverty, labelling.
