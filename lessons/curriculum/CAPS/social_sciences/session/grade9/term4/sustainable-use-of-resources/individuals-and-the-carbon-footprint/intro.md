In early 2018, the four million people of Cape Town were told that their taps might soon run dry. The city called it Day Zero. Within months, residents had cut their daily water use roughly in half, by taking short showers, reusing bath water and fixing leaks, and Day Zero never came. That crisis showed what millions of individual choices can achieve. This lesson explains the role of individuals in using resources more sustainably. It covers how individuals put pressure on resources, the idea of a carbon footprint and why it matters for climate change, and practical ways that learners and families can reduce, reuse and recycle, save water and energy, waste less food and travel more sustainably, as well as the limits of individual action and why governments and businesses must act too.
