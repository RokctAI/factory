In a fish shop in Cape Town or Durban, you might see a small card with a green, orange or red label next to the fish on sale. Green means the fish is from a healthy stock and fished responsibly; red means it should not be bought at all. That simple card is one of the tools South Africans use to keep fish in the sea for the future. This lesson explains what sustainable use of resources means and how it applies to fishing. It looks at how governments manage fish stocks with catch limits, closed seasons, size limits and marine protected areas, at better fishing methods and fish farming, and at how consumers and communities, including South Africa's small-scale fishers, can help make fishing sustainable.
