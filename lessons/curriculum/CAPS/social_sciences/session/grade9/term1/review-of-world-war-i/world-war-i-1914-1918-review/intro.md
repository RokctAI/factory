Grade 9 History begins with the war that ended in 1918, because everything in this term grows out of it. Today we briefly review Grade 8's World War I: the long-term causes of militarism, alliances, imperialism and nationalism, the assassination in Sarajevo that lit the fuse, the two alliances, and the trench warfare of the Western Front with its machine guns, gas and tanks. We look at South Africa's part, from the campaign in German South West Africa to the sacrifice at Delville Wood and the sinking of the SS Mendi. Then we count the cost and see the empires that collapsed in 1918, leaving a defeated, angry Germany. Along the way we practise the historian's tool, the source. Grade 10 History builds its source-based and essay skills on exactly this kind of review.
