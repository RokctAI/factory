This lesson deals with the most terrible crime of the twentieth century, and we approach it with care, accuracy and respect for those who were murdered. The Holocaust was the systematic, state-organised murder of about six million Jewish men, women and children by Nazi Germany and its collaborators between 1941 and 1945. We follow how persecution became genocide: the ghettos of occupied Poland, the mass shootings in the Soviet Union, the Wannsee Conference of January 1942 that coordinated the so-called Final Solution, and the extermination camps such as Auschwitz-Birkenau and Treblinka. We remember the other victims of Nazi mass murder, the rescuers who risked everything, and why we remember today. Grade 11 History studies the Holocaust in depth, and Term 3 links it to human rights.
