Hitler wrote in Mein Kampf that Germany needed living space in the east. Between 1933 and 1939 he set out to take it, one step at a time, testing whether Britain and France would stop him. Today we follow those steps: leaving the League of Nations, rearming, marching into the Rhineland in 1936, the union with Austria and the Munich Agreement over the Sudetenland in 1938, and the seizure of Czechoslovakia in March 1939. We weigh the policy of appeasement and why Britain and France chose it. Then the Nazi-Soviet Pact clears the way, Germany invades Poland on 1 September 1939, and World War II begins: the Axis against the Allies. South Africa's parliament votes narrowly to join the war. Grade 12 History's Cold War topic begins where this war ends.
