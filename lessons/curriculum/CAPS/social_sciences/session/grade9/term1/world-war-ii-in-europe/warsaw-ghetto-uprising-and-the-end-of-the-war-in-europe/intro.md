This lesson brings World War II in Europe to its end, and it begins with courage in the darkest place imaginable. In April 1943 a few hundred poorly armed young Jewish fighters in the Warsaw Ghetto rose against the German army and held out for almost four weeks, the largest Jewish revolt of the war. We then follow how the tide of the war turned against Nazi Germany: Stalingrad, El Alamein, the campaign in Italy where South African soldiers fought, the D-Day landings of June 1944 and the Soviet advance on Berlin. We finish with Hitler's death, Germany's unconditional surrender on 8 May 1945 and the terrible cost of the war. Grade 12 History begins with the Cold War that grew out of this ending.
