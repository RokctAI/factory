Hey — if the minutes and seconds felt like a maze today, here is the calm version: row and seat, find the box, measure, times 60.

Row and seat. Latitude is the row, longitude is the seat. Latitude first. South Africa is south and east, so every answer ends with S and E. A degree has 60 minutes, a minute has 60 seconds.

Find the box. The ticks on the edge of the map mark every minute. Join them in pencil and find the box your feature sits in. That gives the degrees and minutes.

Measure. For latitude in the south, start at the top line of the box, because south numbers get bigger going down. For longitude in the east, start at the left line, because east numbers get bigger going right. Measure to the middle of the symbol, and measure the whole box too.

Times 60. Seconds equal your distance divided by the box, times 60. 1,5 centimetres in a 3,7 centimetre box is about 24 seconds: 25 degrees 45 minutes 24 seconds south.

Check: halfway is 30 seconds, a quarter is 15.
