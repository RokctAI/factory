In November 1918 Germany lost a war, an emperor and its old system of government in the same week. Today we follow what came next. We see the Weimar Republic, Germany's first democracy, born in defeat in 1919, and the Treaty of Versailles that the victors signed with Germany in June 1919, with its four kinds of punishment: land, army, money and blame. We look at how Germans reacted, with anger, the stab-in-the-back myth and attempted uprisings from left and right, and at the crisis year of 1923, when money became worthless. We also find South Africa at the peace table, because German South West Africa became a mandate run by the Union. In Grade 11 History, this background opens the case study of Nazi Germany and the Holocaust.
