The Nazis never won a majority of German votes in a free election. Yet within eighteen months of Hitler becoming chancellor, Germany was a one-party dictatorship. Today we follow how that happened. We read the results of the Reichstag elections of July and November 1932 and March 1933, see the Nazis become the largest party with 37 percent, and see who voted for them and why. We follow the backroom deal that made Hitler chancellor on 30 January 1933, the Reichstag fire, the emergency decree that suspended civil rights, and the Enabling Act of 23 March 1933, by which parliament voted away its own power. Then we watch the dictatorship being built: parties and unions banned, opponents imprisoned, and Hitler made Führer in 1934. Grade 11 History studies this Nazi state in depth.
