Hey — this is a big story, but it fits in three pictures: an empty fuel tank, a Sunday morning, a giant waking up.

An empty fuel tank. Japan had factories and a big navy but almost no oil. It invaded Manchuria in 1931 and China in 1937. When Japan moved into Indochina in 1941, the United States stopped selling it oil, and about 80 per cent of Japan's oil came from America. Japan decided to take oil from South-East Asia by force.

A Sunday morning. To stop the American navy interfering, Japan attacked Pearl Harbour in Hawaii on 7 December 1941. Its 353 planes sank or damaged all eight battleships, and 2 403 Americans died. But the American carriers were at sea and escaped.

A giant wakes up. Roosevelt called it "a date which will live in infamy". America declared war on Japan, and Hitler declared war on America on 11 December. Japan won for six months, then lost four carriers at Midway in June 1942.
