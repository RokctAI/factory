This lesson moves World War II from Europe to Asia and the Pacific Ocean. On the morning of Sunday 7 December 1941, Japanese aircraft launched from six aircraft carriers attacked the United States naval base at Pearl Harbour in Hawaii, killing more than 2 400 Americans and turning a European and Asian conflict into a truly global war. We trace why Japan's military leaders chose to strike: expansion in China, the need for oil and raw materials, and an American oil embargo. We examine the attack itself, the American response, and the rapid Japanese advance that followed, until the Battle of Midway turned the tide in June 1942. Term 2 continues the Pacific story with the atomic bombs, and Grade 12 History examines the Cold War world that followed.
