Grade 9 Geography opens with the two maps that every later Geography lesson leans on: the 1:50 000 topographic map and the 1:10 000 orthophoto map. We start with what Geography in Social Sciences asks of you this year and how it is assessed, then meet the two maps side by side: who makes them, what area one sheet covers, how a sheet is numbered from latitude and longitude, what each scale means in real distance, and why one is a drawing while the other is a corrected aerial photograph. By the end you will be able to pick up either map, read its margin, and say what it can and cannot show. In Grade 10 Geography these same two maps return in the very first topic, Consolidation of Grade 8 and 9 map skills, and then in every Paper 2 mapwork question.
