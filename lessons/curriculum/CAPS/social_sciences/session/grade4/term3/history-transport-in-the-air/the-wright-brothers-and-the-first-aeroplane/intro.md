Today we join a Grade 4 class in East London that is folding paper planes and testing which ones fly furthest. Their teacher tells them the story of Wilbur and Orville Wright, two brothers who ran a bicycle shop in the United States, and who built and flew the first successful aeroplane in 1903. We learn how the brothers studied, tested and solved the problems of flight, and how the first flight in South Africa took place, right there in East London.
