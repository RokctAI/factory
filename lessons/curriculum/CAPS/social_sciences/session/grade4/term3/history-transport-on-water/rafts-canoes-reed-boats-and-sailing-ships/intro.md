Today we visit a museum in Mossel Bay, on the southern coast of the Western Cape, where a full-size copy of a Portuguese sailing ship stands inside a hall. We learn about the earliest kinds of water transport, rafts, canoes and reed boats, about the mokoro still used in the Okavango Delta today, and about the first sailing ships, from dhows on the east coast of Africa to the ships that sailed around the southern tip of Africa.
