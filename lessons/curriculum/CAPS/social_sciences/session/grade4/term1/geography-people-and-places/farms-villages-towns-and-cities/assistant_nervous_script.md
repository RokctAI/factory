Hey — if all the different places feel mixed up, here is the calm version: think of a ladder from small to big.

A village is small, with a school and a shop.

A town is bigger, with banks, offices and a hospital.

A city is the biggest, with many thousands of people.

Open the practice set and climb the ladder one step at a time. You've got this!
