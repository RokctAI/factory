Today a Grade 4 class in Gqeberha, in the Eastern Cape, visits a soup kitchen run by a grandmother in their community. They learn that leadership can start with a pot of soup. We learn three qualities of a good leader: a good leader listens to people, is a servant of the people, and works for the good of others. We meet leaders who showed these qualities, including Albertina Sisulu and Desmond Tutu.
