Hey — this is a hard topic, so take it slowly. Here is the calm version.

Slavery means people are owned like property and not free. The transatlantic slave trade carried more than twelve million Africans to the Americas between the 1500s and the 1800s.

Slavery existed in West Africa before, mostly of war captives, and their children could often become part of the community. It was not based on race.

The Atlantic trade made slavery much bigger, permanent and linked to race. Traders paid with guns and cloth, which led to more wars.
