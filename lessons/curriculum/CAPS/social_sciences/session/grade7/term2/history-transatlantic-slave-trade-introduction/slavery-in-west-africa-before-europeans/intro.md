Between the sixteenth and nineteenth centuries, more than twelve million African men, women and children were forced onto ships and carried across the Atlantic Ocean to the Americas. Today we begin the history of the transatlantic slave trade. We define slavery and the key terms we need, place the trade on a map and a timeline, and ask how we know about it. Then we look at West Africa before Europeans arrived, at the different forms of slavery and servitude that existed there, and at how the coming of the Atlantic trade changed them.
