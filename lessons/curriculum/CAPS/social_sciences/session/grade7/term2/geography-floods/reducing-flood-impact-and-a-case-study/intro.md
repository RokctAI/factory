We cannot stop heavy rain, but we can do a great deal to stop rain from becoming a disaster. Today we learn how communities prepare for floods, with weather forecasts and warnings, dams and drains, wetlands and wise land use, and how people should respond when a flood comes. Then we study one flood in depth, the KwaZulu-Natal floods of April 2022, examining its causes, effects and the response, and asking what lessons it teaches for the future.
