Hey — this lesson is about staying safe from floods. Here is the calm version.

The Weather Service gives yellow, orange and red warnings. Warnings only help if they reach people and people know what to do.

Hard ways to reduce floods: dams, walls and drains. Soft ways: trees, wetlands and leaving room for rivers. Use both.

In a flood, go to high ground and never cross floodwater.

In April 2022, very heavy rain in KwaZulu-Natal killed more than four hundred and thirty people. Homes on dangerous land and blocked drains made it worse.
