Floods are the most common natural disaster in the world and in South Africa. Today we find out what causes them. We start with unusually heavy rain, and then see how human activities, such as farming, building towns, starting fires and clearing vegetation, make floods bigger and faster. We also look at floods from the sea caused by tsunamis. Then we study the effects of floods on people and the land: loss of life, injury, disease, homelessness, soil erosion, and damage to fields, buildings, roads and bridges.
