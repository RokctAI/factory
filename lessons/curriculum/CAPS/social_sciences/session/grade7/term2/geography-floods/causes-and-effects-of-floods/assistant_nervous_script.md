Hey — floods are about too much water arriving too fast. Here is the calm version.

Rain either soaks into the ground slowly or runs over the top quickly. If too much runs off, rivers overflow.

Very heavy rain, from cut-off lows or cyclones, causes most floods. Tsunamis and storms can flood the coast.

People make floods worse by clearing plants, overgrazing, fires, covering ground with roofs and roads, blocking drains, and building next to rivers.

Floods cause drowning, disease, homelessness, damage to roads and bridges, lost crops and soil erosion.
