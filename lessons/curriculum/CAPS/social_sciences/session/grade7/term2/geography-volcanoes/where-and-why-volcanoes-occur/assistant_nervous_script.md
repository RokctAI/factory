Hey — volcanoes are exciting, and here is the calm version.

A volcano is a hole in the crust where melted rock comes out. Underground it is magma; above ground it is lava.

Most volcanoes are where plates meet, especially around the Pacific Ocean in the Ring of Fire. In Africa they are along the East African Rift.

Runny magma gives gentle eruptions and wide shield volcanoes. Sticky magma traps gas and explodes, building steep cones.

Volcanoes can be active, dormant or extinct.
