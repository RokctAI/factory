About fifty volcanoes erupt somewhere on Earth in a typical year. Today we find out where they are and why. We look at a world map of volcanoes and see that most of them form long chains, especially around the Pacific Ocean in the Ring of Fire. We learn what magma is, how it forms and rises, and how a volcano is built, from the magma chamber to the crater. We compare gentle and explosive eruptions, active, dormant and extinct volcanoes, and Africa's own volcanoes, from Kilimanjaro to Nyiragongo.
