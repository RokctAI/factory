Enslaved people in the American South were denied freedom, but they were never just victims. Today we look at daily life on a plantation: work from sunrise to sunset, food, housing, punishment and the constant fear of families being sold apart. Then we see how enslaved people built a culture of their own, with families, faith, songs and stories that kept hope alive. Finally we look at the many everyday ways in which they resisted slavery, from working slowly to learning to read in secret and running away.
