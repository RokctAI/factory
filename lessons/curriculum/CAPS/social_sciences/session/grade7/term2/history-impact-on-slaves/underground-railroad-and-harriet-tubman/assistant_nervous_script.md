Hey — this lesson is a story of courage. Here is the calm version.

In the North and in Canada, slavery was not allowed, so some enslaved people escaped there, walking at night and following the North Star.

The Underground Railroad was a secret network of helpers and safe houses, not a real railway. Conductors were guides; stations were hiding places.

Harriet Tubman escaped in 1849, then went back about thirteen times and led about seventy people to freedom. People called her Moses. She never lost a passenger.
