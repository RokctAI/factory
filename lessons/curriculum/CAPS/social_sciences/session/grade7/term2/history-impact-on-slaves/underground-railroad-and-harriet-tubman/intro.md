It was not underground and it was not a railroad, yet it carried thousands of people to freedom. Today we learn about the Underground Railroad, a secret network of people and safe houses that helped enslaved people escape from the American South to the free northern states and Canada. We find out how it worked, who took part and the dangers involved. Then we follow the remarkable life of Harriet Tubman, who escaped slavery in 1849 and returned again and again to lead others to freedom.
