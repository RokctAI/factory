Hey — this lesson is about how people survived slavery. Here is the calm version.

Enslaved people worked from sunrise to sunset, lived in crowded cabins and lived in fear of being sold away from their families.

But they built strong families and communities. They sang work songs and spirituals like Go Down, Moses, told stories about Brer Rabbit, and worshipped in secret.

They resisted every day: working slowly, learning to read in secret, running away, and keeping their culture alive.
