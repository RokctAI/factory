Hey — this lesson is about how slavery in America finally ended. Here is the calm version.

Abolitionists, Black and white, like Frederick Douglass, used speeches, newspapers and books to fight slavery.

John Brown, a white abolitionist, believed only force would work. In 1859 he raided the armoury at Harpers Ferry, failed, and was hanged.

The South saw him as a terrorist; many in the North saw him as a hero.

His raid helped lead to the Civil War. In 1865 the Thirteenth Amendment ended slavery in the United States, freeing about four million people.
