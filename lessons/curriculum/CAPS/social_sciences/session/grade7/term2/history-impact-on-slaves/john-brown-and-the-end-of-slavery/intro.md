In 1859 a white abolitionist named John Brown led a small armed band in a raid on a government armoury at Harpers Ferry, Virginia, hoping to start an uprising that would destroy slavery. He failed, was captured and hanged, yet his raid helped push the United States towards civil war. Today we learn about the abolition movement, John Brown's life and beliefs, the raid on Harpers Ferry, and the very different ways people saw him. Then we follow the road from the raid to the Civil War and the ending of slavery in the United States in 1865.
