Hey — this lesson is about staying safe. Here is the calm version.

A hazard can hurt people; a disaster is when it does. Weak buildings and poverty make people more vulnerable.

Strong buildings, building laws, and not building on faults save the most lives.

If the ground shakes indoors: drop, cover under a strong table, and hold on. At the coast, if the sea pulls back, go to high ground.

Afterwards: fast rescue, doctors, tents, clean water, and rebuilding stronger than before.
