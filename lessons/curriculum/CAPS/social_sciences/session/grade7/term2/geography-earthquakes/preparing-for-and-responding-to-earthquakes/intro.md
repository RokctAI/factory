Nobody can stop an earthquake, and nobody can yet predict exactly when one will strike. But people can make sure that far fewer lives are lost when it does. Today we learn how communities reduce the impact of earthquakes. We look at earthquake-resistant buildings, warning systems, land-use planning, education and drills, including what to do during shaking: drop, cover and hold on. Then we study the response after an earthquake, from search and rescue to rebuilding, and compare how different countries cope.
