In a few seconds, an earthquake can destroy a city. Today we find out where earthquakes happen and why. We map them around the world and see how closely they follow the edges of the tectonic plates. We learn what happens underground when rocks snap along a fault, what the focus and the epicentre are, and how earthquakes are measured. Then we study their effects on people: injury and loss of life, disease, homelessness, damage to roads, pipes and power lines, fires, and the giant sea waves called tsunamis.
