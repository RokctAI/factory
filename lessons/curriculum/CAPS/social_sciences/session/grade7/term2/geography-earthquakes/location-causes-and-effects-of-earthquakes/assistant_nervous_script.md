Hey — earthquakes sound scary, but here is the calm version.

Plates get stuck, stress builds up, and the rocks suddenly snap. That releases shaking called seismic waves.

The focus is where it starts underground; the epicentre is right above it on the surface.

Most earthquakes happen where plates meet, especially around the Pacific.

Effects include falling buildings, broken roads and pipes, fires, disease, homelessness and sometimes tsunamis, giant sea waves.
