Hey — the inside of the Earth sounds scary, but here is the calm version.

The Earth is like a peach. The crust is the thin skin we live on. The mantle is the thick, hot rock underneath that flows very slowly. The core is iron and nickel: liquid outside, solid inside.

The crust is cracked into giant pieces called tectonic plates. They move a few centimetres a year, pushed by heat inside the Earth.

Plates move apart, push together or slide past each other. Where they meet, we get volcanoes and earthquakes.

South Africa is in the middle of a plate, so it is fairly safe.
