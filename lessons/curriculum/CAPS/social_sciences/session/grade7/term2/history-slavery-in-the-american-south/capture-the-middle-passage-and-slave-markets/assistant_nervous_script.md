Hey — this is a painful lesson, so go gently. Here is the calm version.

People were captured in wars, raids and kidnappings, like Olaudah Equiano, and marched to the coast.

They were held in dungeons in forts like Elmina and Cape Coast Castle, then put on ships.

The Middle Passage across the Atlantic took six to ten weeks, chained below deck. About one in seven or eight died. Many resisted.

In the Americas, they were sold at auctions and scrambles, and families were split up. Most went to the Caribbean and Brazil.
