Behind the numbers of the slave trade are the journeys of individual people. Today we follow that journey step by step: capture in West Africa, the forced march to the coast, imprisonment in coastal forts, the terrible voyage across the Atlantic known as the Middle Passage, and sale in the slave markets of the Americas. We use the testimony of Olaudah Equiano and others who survived, and we look at the numbers: how many people were taken, how many died, and where they went.
