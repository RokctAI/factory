Hey — this lesson is about why the slave trade happened. Here is the calm version.

A plantation was a giant farm growing one crop to sell: tobacco, rice, sugar cane or cotton.

Owners used enslaved Africans because they did not have to pay them, so they made more profit. Laws and racist ideas were used to excuse it.

Ships sailed a triangle: Europe to Africa with goods, Africa to the Americas with captives, and the Americas to Europe with crops.

Owners, traders and factory owners got rich. The enslaved got nothing.
