Why did plantation owners in the Americas want millions of enslaved workers? Today we visit the plantations of the American South and the Caribbean, where tobacco, rice, sugar cane and cotton were grown. We find out what a plantation was, why the crops needed so much labour, and why owners chose enslaved Africans rather than paid workers or local people. Then we follow the crops across the ocean, through the triangular trade, to the factories and shops of Europe, and see who grew rich from the labour of the enslaved.
