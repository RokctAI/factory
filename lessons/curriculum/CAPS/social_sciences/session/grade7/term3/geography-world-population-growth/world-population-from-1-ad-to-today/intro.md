For most of human history, the world's population grew so slowly that it barely changed over centuries. Then, in the last two hundred years, it shot upwards: from one billion people around 1800 to eight billion in 2022. Today we study the pattern of world population growth from the year 1 AD to today, using a line graph. We learn to read and describe the graph, divide it into stages, explain the changes, and look at what experts expect for the future.
