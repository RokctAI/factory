Hey — this lesson is about reading one famous graph. Here is the calm version.

Years go along the bottom, people in billions up the side. Flat means slow growth; steep means fast growth.

From 1 AD to about 1750, the line is almost flat: many babies were born, but many people died too.

After 1750, fewer people died thanks to more food, clean water, vaccines and medicine, so the line shot up: one billion around 1804, eight billion in 2022.

Now families have fewer children, so growth is slowing.
