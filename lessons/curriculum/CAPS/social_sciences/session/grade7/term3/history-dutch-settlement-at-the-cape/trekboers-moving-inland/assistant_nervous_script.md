Hey — this lesson is about the trekboers. Here is the calm version.

Trekboers were farmers who moved inland from the Cape with wagons, cattle and sheep.

They wanted grazing land and freedom from the Company's rules.

They lived simply and made most things themselves. Khoikhoi servants and some enslaved people did much of the work.

The San fought back when their land was taken, and many were killed by commandos. In the east, the trekboers met the Xhosa.
