In 1657, five years after the station was founded, the VOC released nine of its employees to farm for themselves. These were the first free burghers. Over the next decades, more settlers arrived from the Netherlands and Germany, and in 1688 a group of French Protestant refugees, the Huguenots. In this lesson we study who the free burghers were, why the Huguenots came, how wine and wheat farming developed, and how the colony's frontier expanded inland.
