Hey — this lesson is about the first mission and the !Xam stories. Here is the calm version.

In 1738 Georg Schmidt started the first mission station for Khoikhoi people. It became Genadendal, the Valley of Grace.

It was a refuge where people could learn, work at trades and live with their families. The first teachers' college opened there in 1838.

In the 1870s, Bleek and Lloyd wrote down the language and stories of the !Xam San, a language that later died out.
