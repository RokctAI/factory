Hey — this lesson is about how the Khoikhoi and San lost their land. Here is the calm version.

The Khoikhoi and San shared land as groups. Colonists believed land could be owned, fenced and sold.

Land was taken step by step: farms, wars, cattle raids, smallpox, trekboers, commandos and laws.

The Khoikhoi lost their independence and had to work on farms. Many San communities were destroyed.

Land is still an important issue today.
