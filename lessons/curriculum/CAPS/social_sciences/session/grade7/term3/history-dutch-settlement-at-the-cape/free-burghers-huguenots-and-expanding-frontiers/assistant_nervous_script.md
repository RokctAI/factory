Hey — this lesson is about the settlers who turned a station into a colony. Here is the calm version.

In 1657, nine VOC workers became free burghers, free farmers, on Khoikhoi land.

From 1688, about two hundred French Huguenots, Protestant refugees, settled at Franschhoek.

Wheat and wine farms grew around Stellenbosch, worked by enslaved people.

As families grew, settlers moved inland, and the frontier spread, taking Khoikhoi and San land.
