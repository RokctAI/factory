In 1738, a German missionary named Georg Schmidt arrived in a valley near today's Caledon and began teaching a small group of Khoikhoi people. The mission he started, later called Genadendal, the Valley of Grace, became the first mission station in southern Africa. More than a century later, in Cape Town, a linguist named Wilhelm Bleek and his sister-in-law Lucy Lloyd recorded the language and stories of the !Xam San. In this lesson we study both: Genadendal and its significance, and the remarkable Bleek and Lloyd archive.
