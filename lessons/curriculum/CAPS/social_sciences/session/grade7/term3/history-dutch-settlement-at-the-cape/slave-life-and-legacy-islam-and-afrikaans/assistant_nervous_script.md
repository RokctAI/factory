Hey — this lesson is about life under slavery and what it left behind. Here is the calm version.

Enslaved people worked on wine and wheat farms and in Cape Town houses. They had no shoes, could not marry legally, and could be punished or sold. But they still built families and communities.

Islam came with enslaved people and exiles. Sheikh Yusuf arrived in 1694, and the first mosque was built in Bo-Kaap in 1794.

Afrikaans grew from Dutch as enslaved people, Khoikhoi and settlers all used it.
