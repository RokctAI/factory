In the eighteenth century, a new kind of colonist moved deep into the interior of southern Africa: the trekboers, stock farmers who travelled with their families, wagons, cattle and sheep, and with enslaved people and Khoikhoi servants, looking for grazing land. In this lesson we learn who the trekboers were, why they moved, how they lived, how they related to the people whose land they entered, and how their stories have been told.
