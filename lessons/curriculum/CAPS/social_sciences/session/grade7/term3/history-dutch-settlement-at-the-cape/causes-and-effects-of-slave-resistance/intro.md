Enslaved people at the Cape never simply accepted their condition. They resisted in many ways, from working slowly and running away, to building hidden communities in the mountains, to open rebellion. In this lesson we study the causes of slave resistance, the different forms it took, two major uprisings, the Hangklip runaways and the 1808 rebellion led by Louis of Mauritius, and the effects of resistance on enslaved people and the colony.
