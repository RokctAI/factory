Hey — this lesson is about four illnesses that affect populations. Here is the calm version.

HIV weakens the body's defences. ARV pills keep people well. It is not spread by hugging.

TB spreads by coughing and attacks the lungs. It can be cured with six months of pills.

Malaria comes from mosquito bites, mostly in hot, wet places. Nets and spraying help.

Diarrhoea comes from germs in dirty water. Clean water, soap and a sugar-salt drink save lives.

All four raise death rates and lower life expectancy. Open the practice set and try a few. You've got this!
