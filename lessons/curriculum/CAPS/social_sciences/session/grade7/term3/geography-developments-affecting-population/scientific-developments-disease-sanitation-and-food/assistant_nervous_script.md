Hey — this lesson is about the discoveries that stopped people dying young. Here is the calm version.

Jenner made the first vaccine in 1796. Pasteur showed germs cause disease. Fleming discovered penicillin in 1928.

John Snow mapped cholera deaths around a water pump in 1854. Cities then built sewers and clean water supplies.

Canned food from 1810, and later fridges, kept food safe for longer.

Fewer deaths while births stayed high made populations grow fast.
