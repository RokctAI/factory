Hey — this lesson links money and war to population. Here is the calm version.

Wealthier places usually mean better food, water and hospitals, so people live longer and families are smaller.

Poorer places often have more child deaths and bigger families.

Wars kill people directly, but hunger and disease after fighting often kill even more.

People who flee war into another country are refugees. Over a hundred million people are forced from home today.
