A pandemic is an outbreak of disease that spreads across many countries and continents. In this lesson we study the Spanish flu of 1918 to 1920, one of the deadliest pandemics in history, including its terrible impact on South Africa, and then compare it with the COVID-19 pandemic that began in 2019 and that you lived through yourselves. The COVID-19 comparison helps understanding, but it is not examinable; the Spanish flu is.
