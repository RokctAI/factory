Hey — two important numbers today. Here is the calm version.

Infant mortality: how many babies die before their first birthday, for every thousand born alive. In South Africa it is about twenty-four.

Most baby deaths can be prevented with clean water, nurses at births, vaccinations, breastfeeding, and sugar-and-salt water for diarrhoea.

Life expectancy: how long, on average, a newborn can expect to live. Worldwide it is about seventy-three. In South Africa it fell because of AIDS and recovered with free HIV medicine.
