Hey — population maths is just a few simple steps. Here is the calm version.

A census counts all the people. South Africa had about sixty-two million in 2022.

Birth rate: births divided by the population, times a thousand. Death rate: the same with deaths.

Natural increase: birth rate minus death rate. Divide by ten to get a percentage.

Migration, people moving in and out, also changes population.
