Hey — this lesson is about where African farmers lived and why. Here is the calm version.

African farmers came into southern Africa about two thousand years ago. They lived in villages, kept cattle and worked iron.

Their crops, sorghum and millet, needed summer rain, at least about five hundred millimetres a year.

The east of South Africa gets more summer rain, so that is where they settled.

Cattle were their wealth.
