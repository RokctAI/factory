When the Dutch arrived at the Cape in 1652, the San and Khoikhoi were not the only people of South Africa. Further east and north, African farming communities had lived for well over a thousand years, growing crops, herding cattle, working iron and building chiefdoms and kingdoms. In this lesson we learn where these farmers settled and why: in the summer rainfall areas, east of the Cape, where at least about five hundred millimetres of rain falls a year, enough to grow sorghum and millet.
