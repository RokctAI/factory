This term we study the colonisation of the Cape in the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries: how the Dutch East India Company arrived, how slavery and settlement grew, and what this meant for the people who already lived there. In this first lesson we learn how to write a history essay, a skill we will use all term, and we revise who the indigenous inhabitants of the Cape were in the 1600s: the San hunter-gatherers and the Khoikhoi herders.
