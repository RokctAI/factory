On the northern frontier, missionaries and traders often travelled the same routes and lived in the same places. The most famous missionary of the region was Robert Moffat, a Scottish gardener who spent about fifty years at Kuruman, among the Batlhaping, and translated the whole Bible into Setswana. In this lesson we study the role of missionaries and traders on the northern frontier, and then Robert Moffat as a case study: his life, the Kuruman mission, his translation and printing work, his relationships with Tswana and other leaders, and how his legacy is viewed today.
