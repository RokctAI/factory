While wars were being fought on the eastern frontier, the northern frontier, along the Orange River, was more a zone of trade and movement. Here lived the Kora and the Griqua, communities who had moved north from the Cape Colony, with horses, guns and wagons. In this lesson we learn who the Kora and Griqua were, how they settled along the Orange River, and how they became traders and middlemen, carrying manufactured goods, tobacco and pack oxen from the Cape to the peoples of the interior, and bringing ivory, cattle and skins back.
