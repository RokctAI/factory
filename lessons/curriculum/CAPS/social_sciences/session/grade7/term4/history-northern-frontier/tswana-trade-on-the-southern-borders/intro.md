North of the Orange River lay the world of the Batswana, Sotho-Tswana-speaking farmers who lived in some of the largest towns in southern Africa. On the southern edge of this world, around today's Kuruman and Dithakong, the Batlhaping and Barolong met the Kora and Griqua. In this lesson we learn about Tswana society and its great towns, the goods the Tswana produced, including ivory, hides, skins, furs and iron and copper work, and how trade with the Kora and Griqua linked them to the Cape and changed their world.
