Hey — this lesson is about the Tswana and their trade. Here is the calm version.

The Tswana lived north of the Orange River in big towns like Dithakong, led by chiefs with a kgotla assembly.

They hunted for ivory, skins and furs, sewed fur cloaks called karosses, and made iron and copper goods.

They traded with the Kora and Griqua for beads, tobacco, cloth and later guns.

Trade brought wealth, but also new dangers.
