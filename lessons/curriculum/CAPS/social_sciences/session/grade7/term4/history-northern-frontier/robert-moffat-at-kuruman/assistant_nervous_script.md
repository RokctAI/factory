Hey — this lesson is about missionaries and Robert Moffat. Here is the calm version.

Missionaries spread Christianity, started schools and translated the Bible. Missions also became trading centres.

Robert Moffat, a Scottish gardener, built a mission beside the Kuruman Eye in 1824. He dug furrows, built a stone church and started a school.

With Tswana help, he translated the whole Bible into Setswana by 1857.

His work spread literacy, but also changed Tswana customs.
