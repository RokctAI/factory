Hey — this lesson is about the Kora and Griqua traders. Here is the calm version.

The Kora were Khoikhoi herders who moved north to the Orange River. Many had horses and guns.

The Griqua were people of mixed descent who left the colony to be free. Their main town became Griquatown in 1813.

They carried guns, beads, cloth, tobacco and pack oxen from the Cape into the interior, and brought back ivory, cattle and skins.
