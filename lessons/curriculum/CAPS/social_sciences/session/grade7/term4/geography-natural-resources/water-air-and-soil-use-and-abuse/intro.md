Everything we use, from the water we drink and the air we breathe to the food we eat and the phone in your pocket, comes from natural resources. In this lesson we learn what natural resources are, how they can be classified as renewable or non-renewable, and why three of them, water, air and soil, are essential for life. Then we look at how people use these resources, and how they abuse them through pollution, waste and overuse, with South African examples.
