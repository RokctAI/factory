Hey — this lesson is about nature's supplies. Here is the calm version.

Natural resources come from nature. Renewable ones, like wind and trees, can be replaced. Non-renewable ones, like coal and gold, run out.

Water, air and soil are essential for life. Most water is salty, and soil forms very slowly.

We damage them with pollution, leaks, smoke and overgrazing.

Using them sustainably means leaving enough for the future.
