South Africa is a water-scarce country, so it matters a great deal who uses its water and how. In this lesson we learn to read and draw a pie chart, and use one to show the main users of South Africa's water: irrigation for farming, municipalities for homes and businesses, industry, mining, power generation, forestry plantations and livestock. We then compare the needs of these users and think about how water could be shared more fairly and used more wisely.
