A healthy river is clear, flowing and full of life, and it provides clean water for people, farms and nature. But many of South Africa's rivers are under stress from pollution, alien plants, dams, sand mining and the loss of wetlands. In this lesson we learn what a catchment is, what makes a river healthy, how to check river health using the miniSASS method, what threatens rivers, and how communities, farmers, industries and governments can care for catchment areas.
