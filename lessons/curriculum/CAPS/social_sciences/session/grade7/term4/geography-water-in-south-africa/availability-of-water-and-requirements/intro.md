South Africa is one of the thirty driest countries in the world. Its rain is unevenly spread, unreliable and falls mostly in the east, while its biggest cities and mines are often far from large rivers. In this lesson we learn where South Africa's water comes from, how its availability varies across the country and from year to year, how water is stored and transferred between rivers, how much water different areas need, and what can be done to close the gap between supply and demand.
