Hey — this lesson is about how much water South Africa has. Here is the calm version.

South Africa gets about half the world's average rain, mostly in the east, and a lot evaporates.

Most water comes from rivers and dams. Small towns often use groundwater from boreholes.

Gauteng has no big river, so water is brought from Lesotho through tunnels.

Demand is growing, so we need to find more water and use less.
