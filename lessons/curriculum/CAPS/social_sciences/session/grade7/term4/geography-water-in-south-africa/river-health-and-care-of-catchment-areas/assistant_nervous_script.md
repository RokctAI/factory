Hey — this lesson is about keeping rivers healthy. Here is the calm version.

A catchment is all the land that drains into one river, so what happens on the land affects the river.

Healthy rivers are clear and full of life. With miniSASS, finding mayflies under stones means the water is probably clean.

Rivers are harmed by sewage, chemicals, litter, alien plants and drained wetlands.

We can help with clean-ups, reporting pollution and planting indigenous trees.
