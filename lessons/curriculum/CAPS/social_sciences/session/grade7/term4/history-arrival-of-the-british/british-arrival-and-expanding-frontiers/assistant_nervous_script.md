Hey — this lesson is about the British taking over the Cape. Here is the calm version.

The British wanted the Cape to protect their sea route to India. They took it in 1795, returned it in 1803, and took it again in 1806 at Blaauwberg.

They made English official and brought new laws: passes for Khoikhoi in 1809, then freedom of movement in 1828 with Ordinance 50.

The frontiers kept moving east into Xhosa land and north to the Kora, Griqua and Tswana.
