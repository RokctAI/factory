Hey — this lesson is about soldiers, officials and settlers. Here is the calm version.

The British guarded the frontier with soldiers and forts. Grahamstown was their headquarters. Many soldiers were Khoikhoi.

Governors and magistrates ran the frontier, and their policies kept changing.

In 1820 about four thousand British settlers landed at Algoa Bay. Farming was hard, so many became traders.

Their arrival put more pressure on Xhosa land.
