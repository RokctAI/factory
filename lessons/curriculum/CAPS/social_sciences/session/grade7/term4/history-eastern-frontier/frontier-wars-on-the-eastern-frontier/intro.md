For one hundred years, from 1779 to 1879, the eastern frontier of the Cape Colony was the scene of nine wars between the Xhosa and the colonists, later the British. These are often called the Frontier Wars, or the Wars of Dispossession. In this lesson we learn about the Xhosa kingdom, why the wars broke out, what happened in the most important wars of the early nineteenth century, and what the consequences were for the Xhosa and the colony.
