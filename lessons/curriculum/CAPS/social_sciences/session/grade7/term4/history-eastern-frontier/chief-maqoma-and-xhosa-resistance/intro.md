Maqoma was one of the most remarkable leaders in South African history: a brilliant military commander, a skilled speaker, a friend and then an enemy of the colony, and a prisoner on Robben Island who died there in 1873. His life spans much of the struggle on the eastern frontier. In this lesson we study Maqoma as a case study of Xhosa resistance to British rule: his background, his expulsion from the Kat River valley, his leadership in the wars of 1834 to 1835 and 1850 to 1853, and his imprisonment and legacy.
