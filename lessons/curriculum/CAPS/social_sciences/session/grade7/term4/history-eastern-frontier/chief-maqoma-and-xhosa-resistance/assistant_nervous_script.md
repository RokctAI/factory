Hey — this lesson is a case study of Chief Maqoma. Here is the calm version.

Maqoma, born in 1798, was the son of the Xhosa chief Ngqika. At first he tried to live peacefully near the colony.

In 1829 he was driven out of the Kat River valley. He then led Xhosa forces in the wars of 1834 and 1850 to 1853, fighting from forests and mountains.

He was imprisoned on Robben Island and died there in 1873. He is honoured as a hero of resistance.
