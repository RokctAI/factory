British rule on the eastern frontier depended on soldiers, forts and officials, and from 1820 on thousands of British immigrants who came to farm the Zuurveld. In this lesson we learn about the role of British soldiers and officials on the frontier, including the forts, the Cape Regiment and magistrates; why about four thousand British settlers arrived in 1820, what they experienced, and how they changed the Eastern Cape; and how all this affected the Xhosa and Khoikhoi.
