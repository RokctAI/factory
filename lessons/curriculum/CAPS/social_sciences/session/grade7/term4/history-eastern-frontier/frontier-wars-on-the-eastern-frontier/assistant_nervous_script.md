Hey — this lesson is about the frontier wars. Here is the calm version.

The Xhosa were cattle farmers in the Eastern Cape. From the 1770s, trekboers moved into the Zuurveld and both needed the same land.

Nine wars were fought between 1779 and 1879, mainly over land and cattle.

In 1819 Makhanda attacked Grahamstown. In 1835 King Hintsa was killed.

The Xhosa resisted bravely but lost most of their land and independence.
