On 1 December 1834, slavery was abolished at the Cape. About thirty-nine thousand enslaved people were legally freed, although they had to serve a further four years as apprentices before full freedom on 1 December 1838. In this lesson we learn why slavery was abolished in the British Empire, how abolition happened at the Cape, how slave owners and the formerly enslaved responded, and what freedom meant in practice for people who had been enslaved.
