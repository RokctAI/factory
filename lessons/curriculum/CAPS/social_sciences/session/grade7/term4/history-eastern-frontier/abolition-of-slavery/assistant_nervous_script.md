Hey — this lesson is about the end of slavery. Here is the calm version.

Campaigners in Britain and rebellions by enslaved people helped end slavery. Britain ended the slave trade in 1807 and passed a law against slavery in 1833.

At the Cape, slavery ended on 1 December 1834, but freed people had to work as apprentices until 1 December 1838.

Owners got money; the enslaved got nothing. Many freed people moved to towns and mission stations.
