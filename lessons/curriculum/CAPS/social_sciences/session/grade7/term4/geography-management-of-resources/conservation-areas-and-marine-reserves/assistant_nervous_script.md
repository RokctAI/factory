Hey — this lesson is about places that protect nature. Here is the calm version.

Conservation areas include national parks, reserves and marine protected areas. They protect plants, animals and water, and bring tourists.

Kruger has the Big Five. Hluhluwe-iMfolozi saved the white rhino. Addo saved elephants.

Marine reserves stop fishing so fish can grow, and some spill over to fishers nearby.

Tsitsikamma, from 1964, is Africa's oldest marine reserve.
