Conservation is not only about fences and rangers. Some of the most successful conservation projects in South Africa are led by communities, who protect natural resources because they benefit from them. In this lesson we learn what community conservation means, look at examples such as the Makuleke community in the Kruger, Working for Water, and local recycling and food garden projects, and then study eco-tourism: what it is, how it differs from mass tourism, and how it can benefit both nature and people.
