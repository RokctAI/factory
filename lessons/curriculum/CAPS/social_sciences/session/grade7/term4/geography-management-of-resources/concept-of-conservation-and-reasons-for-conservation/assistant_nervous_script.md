Hey — this lesson is about why we look after nature. Here is the calm version.

Conservation means using nature wisely so it lasts.

South Africa has an amazing variety of plants and animals, many found nowhere else.

They are threatened by habitat loss, alien plants, pollution, too many people using too much, and poaching.

We conserve for nature, for jobs like tourism, for health, and because nature is our heritage.
