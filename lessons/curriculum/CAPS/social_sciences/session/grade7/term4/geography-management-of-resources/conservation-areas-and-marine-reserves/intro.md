South Africa protects its natural heritage in a network of conservation areas: national parks, provincial and private nature reserves, World Heritage Sites, transfrontier parks, and marine protected areas along its coastline. In this lesson we learn about the types and purposes of conservation areas, where the main ones are located, how marine protected areas work, and we study a case study: the Tsitsikamma section of the Garden Route National Park, home of Africa's oldest marine protected area.
