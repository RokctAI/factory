Every news bulletin is full of places: a cyclone in Mozambique, an earthquake in Japan, a sports final in France. Today we learn to put those places on a world map. We use continents, oceans and the grid of latitude and longitude to find and describe news places in degrees, and we start a class news map that grows all year. Then we come home to our own area and practise showing compass directions on a simple local sketch map, so that we can say which way the school lies from the shops, or the clinic from the taxi rank.
