Hey — news places and directions are easier than they sound. Here is the calm version.

There are seven continents and five oceans. South Africa has the Atlantic on the west and the Indian Ocean on the east.

When you hear a place in the news, find it on a world map: the continent, the country, then its numbers, latitude first. Nairobi is almost on the equator.

At home, find north first. In South Africa your shadow points south at lunchtime. Draw the north arrow, add landmarks, and use compass points instead of left and right.
