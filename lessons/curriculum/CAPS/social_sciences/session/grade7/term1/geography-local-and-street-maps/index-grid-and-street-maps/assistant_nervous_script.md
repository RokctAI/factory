Hey — finding a street on a big map is a simple trick once you know it. Here is the calm version.

Look up the street name in the index, which is in alphabetical order. It gives you a page and a square, like page forty-seven, D3.

The map has letters across the top and numbers down the side. Go across to D, down to 3. Your street is in that square. Letter first, number second.

Map apps on phones do the same job with a search box and a blue dot. Paper maps still work when there is no data.

Open the practice set and try a few grid questions. You've got this!
