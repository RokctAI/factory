How do you find one small street among thousands of streets in a city? Today we learn how a street map and a street guide are organised. We use the index, an alphabetical list of street names, and the grid of lettered and numbered squares, to find any street in seconds. We practise reading a grid reference such as C4. Then we compare printed street maps with map apps on a phone, such as Google Maps, and see what each does well and where each can let us down.
