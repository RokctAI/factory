Hey — this lesson is about a great African empire. Here is the calm version.

Sundiata Keita founded Mali in about 1235 after winning the Battle of Kirina. Mansa means king.

Mali became huge, about two thousand kilometres across, and rich from gold, trade taxes, farming and tribute.

The mansa ruled with officials, loyal local kings, a strong army and Muslim scholars. Roads were very safe.

Mansa Musa, who ruled from about 1312 to 1337, was its most famous king. A map from Spain shows him holding a gold nugget.
