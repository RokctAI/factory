Hey — this is a great story about a king's journey. Here is the calm version.

The hajj is the pilgrimage to Mecca. Mansa Musa went in 1324, for his faith, for fame, for friendship with other rulers, and to bring back scholars.

He travelled with thousands of people and lots of gold. In Cairo he spent and gave away so much gold that it became less valuable there.

He came home with scholars, books and an architect, al-Sahili.

He built the Great Mosque of Timbuktu, the Djinguereber, from mud bricks. The community replasters it every year.
