In 1324 a king from West Africa arrived in Cairo with thousands of followers and so much gold that the price of gold in Egypt fell for years afterwards. Today we follow Mansa Musa's pilgrimage to Mecca: why he went, how the journey was organised, what happened in Cairo, and what the pilgrimage achieved for Mali. Then we see what he brought home, including a scholar and architect who helped build the Great Mosque of Timbuktu, the Djinguereber, which still stands nearly seven hundred years later.
