In the early fourteenth century, one of the largest and richest states in the world lay in West Africa. Today we study the kingdom, or empire, of Mali at its height. We learn how Sundiata Keita founded Mali in the thirteenth century, how far the empire stretched, how it was governed and where its wealth came from. Then we meet its most famous ruler, Mansa Musa, who ruled from about 1312 to 1337, and find out why his reign is remembered as Mali's golden age.
