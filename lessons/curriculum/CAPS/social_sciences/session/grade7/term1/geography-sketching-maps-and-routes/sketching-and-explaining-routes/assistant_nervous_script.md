Hey — giving directions is something you already do. Here is the calm version.

To draw a route map: north arrow, start and end, the roads, landmarks at the turns, a bold route line with arrows, a key and a title.

To say a route: go step by step. Where to start, which way to go, what you will see at each turn, and how far.

To guess distance: about fifteen hundred steps or fifteen minutes walking is one kilometre. A soccer field is about a hundred metres.
