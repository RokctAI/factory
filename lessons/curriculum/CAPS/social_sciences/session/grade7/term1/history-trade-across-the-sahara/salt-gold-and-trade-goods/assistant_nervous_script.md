Hey — this lesson is about swapping. Here is the calm version.

The desert had salt. West Africa had gold. North Africa had cloth, metal goods, horses and books. So they traded.

Salt was precious in West Africa, because people needed it to stay healthy and to keep food from going bad, and there was little salt there.

Gold went north, along with ivory, ostrich feathers, kola nuts, and sadly also enslaved people.

The kings of Mali got rich by taxing the trade.
