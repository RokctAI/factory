Hey — this lesson is about how a religion travelled. Here is the calm version.

Islam is a religion with one God. It began in Arabia with the Prophet Muhammad and spread across North Africa.

From about the ninth century, Muslim traders crossed the Sahara with their caravans. West African traders and then kings became Muslim, because a shared faith built trust in business.

Islam brought writing in Arabic, schools, scholars, judges and mud-brick mosques.

West Africans mixed Islam with their own customs and made it their own.
