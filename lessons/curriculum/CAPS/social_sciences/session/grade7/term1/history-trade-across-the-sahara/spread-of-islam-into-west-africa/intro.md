Along with salt and gold, the camel caravans carried something that could not be weighed: ideas. Today we learn how the religion of Islam spread from Arabia across North Africa, and then, from about the ninth century, travelled south across the Sahara with Muslim traders. We see why West African merchants and kings chose to become Muslim, how Islam brought writing in Arabic, schools and new laws, and how West Africans blended the new faith with their own traditions.
