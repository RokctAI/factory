Nobody drives as the crow flies. Roads turn corners, bend around hills and follow rivers. Today we learn to measure indirect distances, the real distances along roads, railways and rivers. We use three methods: breaking a route into straight sections and adding them up, walking a strip of paper along the curves, and laying a piece of string along the route and measuring it against the scale. We practise on a street map and compare the indirect distance with the direct distance between the same two places.
