A map of South Africa fits on one page, and so does a map of your school. How can both be true? The answer is scale. Today we revise what scale means: how much smaller the map is than the real world. We look at two ways of showing scale, the word scale and the line scale, and learn to read and use each. Then we sort maps into large-scale maps, which show a small area in great detail, and small-scale maps, which show a large area with little detail, and we learn the trick for remembering which is which.
