Hey — measuring a winding road is easier than it looks. Here is the calm version.

The indirect distance is the distance along the real route, and it is longer than the straight line.

If the route has straight bits, measure each, add them up, and multiply by the scale.

If it curves, use thin string. Hold the start, follow every bend, pinch the end, straighten it, and read it on the line scale from zero.

Don't let the string slip off the bends, or your answer will be too short.
