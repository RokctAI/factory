Hey — scale is just about shrinking. Here is the calm version.

Scale tells you how much the world was shrunk to fit on the map.

A word scale says it in words, like one centimetre represents five kilometres. Measure in centimetres and multiply.

A line scale is a little ruler on the map. Mark the distance on a paper strip, put it on the line scale at zero, and read.

Large-scale maps show small areas with lots of detail. Small-scale maps show big areas with little detail.
