Hey — measuring distance on a map is a short recipe. Here is the calm version.

A direct distance is a straight line between two places, like a bird flying.

First guess using the scale bar. Then put your ruler's zero on the centre of the first dot, measure in centimetres, and multiply by the scale.

Check that your answer is close to your guess and has a unit.

For full marks, write the map distance, the scale, the sum and the answer.
