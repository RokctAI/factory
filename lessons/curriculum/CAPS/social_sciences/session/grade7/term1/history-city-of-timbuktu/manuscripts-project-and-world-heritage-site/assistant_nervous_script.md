Hey — this lesson is about saving old books. Here is the calm version.

Timbuktu has hundreds of thousands of old handwritten books called manuscripts, mostly kept by families.

Heat, dust, damp, termites, theft and war can destroy them.

In 2001 President Thabo Mbeki offered South Africa's help. South Africa built a new library, opened in 2009, and trained Malian experts in Pretoria.

In 2012 and 2013, when armed groups took Timbuktu, people secretly moved most of the manuscripts to Bamako.

Timbuktu is a World Heritage Site because of its mud mosques, tombs and history of learning.
