Hey — this lesson is about a traveller and a trading city. Here is the calm version.

Leo Africanus, whose real name was al-Hasan al-Wazzan, visited Timbuktu in about 1510 and wrote Description of Africa, published in 1550.

He described weavers, merchants, a rich king and many scholars, and said books were the most profitable goods. Salt was scarce.

His book is useful but must be checked, because he wrote years later, for European readers.

Timbuktu was rich because camels from the desert met boats from the Niger River there.
