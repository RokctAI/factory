Hidden in wooden chests, mud-brick homes and family libraries, hundreds of thousands of handwritten books survived in Timbuktu for centuries. Today we learn about the Timbuktu manuscripts: what they are, why they matter, and the threats they face. We follow the South Africa-Mali project, which built a new library for the manuscripts in Timbuktu and trained Malian conservators, and the brave rescue of the manuscripts during the conflict of 2012 and 2013. Then we find out why Timbuktu is a World Heritage Site and what that status means.
