Hey — this lesson is about a city full of books. Here is the calm version.

Trade made Timbuktu rich, Islam brought reading and writing, and kings respected scholars. So learning grew.

Children learned in Quranic schools. Older students studied with masters at mosques like Sankore and earned certificates called ijazas.

They studied Islam, law, maths, astronomy, medicine, history and geography.

Ahmad Baba was the most famous scholar. In 1591 Morocco conquered Timbuktu and he was taken away, but he came back.
