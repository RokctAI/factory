Hey — if maps and directions sound tricky, here is the calm version.

The compass has eight points: north, north-east, east, south-east, south, south-west, west, north-west. North or south always comes first in the name.

To say which way a place is, stand at the place after the word from, face north, and see which way you turn.

Latitude lines go across, measuring north or south of the equator. Longitude lines go from pole to pole, measuring east or west of Greenwich.

Write latitude first, then longitude, with the letters. Cape Town is about thirty-four degrees south, eighteen degrees east.
