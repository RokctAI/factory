Grade 7 Geography begins with the tools every geographer carries: direction and position. Today we look at what Social Sciences in Grade 7 covers and how the year's work is assessed. Then we revise two skills from earlier grades. First, the eight points of the compass, and how to use them to describe where one place lies from another. Second, latitude and longitude, the imaginary grid of lines that lets us give any place on Earth an exact address in degrees. By the end you will be able to say that Pretoria lies north-east of Kimberley, and that Cape Town is at about thirty-four degrees south and eighteen degrees east.
