Hey — the map project is just careful looking and neat drawing. Here is the calm version.

Use point symbols for one spot, line symbols for long things like roads, and area symbols for spaces like fields. Put every symbol in the key.

Draw a compass rose with eight points and make sure north is real north. Pace a street to make a rough scale.

Walk your area safely with others, and write down what the land is used for and what grows there.

Then describe your map: the big pattern first, then details, with compass directions.

Open the practice set and try a few questions. You've got this!
