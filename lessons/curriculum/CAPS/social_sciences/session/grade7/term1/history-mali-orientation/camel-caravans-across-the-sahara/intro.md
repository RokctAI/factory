Seven hundred years ago, the richest trade route in Africa ran straight across the largest hot desert on Earth. Today we begin the history of the kingdom of Mali and the city of Timbuktu. We learn how History is studied and assessed in Grade 7, and the special words used in history questions. We revise how people moved goods over land before engines, and then we meet the camel caravan: the long lines of camels, traders and guides that crossed the Sahara Desert, and the reasons why the camel made that crossing possible.
