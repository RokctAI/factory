Hey — this new history topic starts with a story about camels. Here is the calm version.

History is studying the past using evidence. Primary sources come from the time; secondary sources were written later.

Long ago, goods were carried by people and animals. Horses and oxen need water every day, so they could not easily cross the Sahara.

Camels could. They go a week or more without water, their humps store fat, and their wide feet walk on sand.

Traders crossed together in caravans, led by guides who used the stars and knew every well. The trip took about two months.
