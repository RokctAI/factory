Hey — this lesson is about rural settlements, places in the countryside.

There are three kinds: an isolated farmstead, a hamlet and a village. Villages have some services, like a school or a shop.

Most people in rural areas work in farming, mining, forestry or fishing.

Patterns can be dispersed, with houses far apart, or nucleated, with houses close together.
