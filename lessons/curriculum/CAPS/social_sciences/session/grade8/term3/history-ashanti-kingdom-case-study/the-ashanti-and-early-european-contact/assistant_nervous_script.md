Hey — this lesson starts the story of the Asante kingdom in Ghana.

Around 1700, Osei Tutu united the Asante with the Golden Stool, which held the spirit of the nation. Kumasi was the capital.

The Asante were rich from gold and trade. Europeans had forts on the coast but did not rule the interior.

In the 1820s, the Asante and the British traded, talked and fought. In 1824 the Asante won a big battle.
