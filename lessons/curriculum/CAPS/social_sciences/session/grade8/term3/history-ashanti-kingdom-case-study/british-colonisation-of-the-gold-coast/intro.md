In March 1900, the British governor of the Gold Coast, Sir Frederic Hodgson, stood before the assembled chiefs of Asante in Kumasi and demanded to know where the Golden Stool was. He said that, as the representative of the Queen, he should be sitting on it. The chiefs listened in silence. Days later, the Asante rose in war, led by a queen mother of about sixty, Yaa Asantewaa. Today we study how Britain colonised the Gold Coast and conquered the Asante: from the burning of Kumasi in 1874, through the arrest of the Asantehene Prempeh I in 1896, to the War of the Golden Stool in 1900 and the annexation of Asante in 1901.
