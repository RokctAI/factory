Hey — this lesson is about what colonisation meant for the Asante and for Britain.

The Asante lost their independence and were ruled through chiefs under British officials. In 1935 the Asantehene was restored.

African farmers made the Gold Coast the biggest cocoa producer in the world. British companies mined gold. Railways ran to the coast.

Schools trained new leaders, and in 1957 the Gold Coast became independent as Ghana.
