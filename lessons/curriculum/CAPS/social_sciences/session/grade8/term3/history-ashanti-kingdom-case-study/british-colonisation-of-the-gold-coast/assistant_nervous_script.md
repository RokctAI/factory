Hey — this lesson is about how Britain conquered the Asante.

In 1874 the British burned Kumasi and made the coast a colony.

In 1896, Prempeh I did not fight, to save his people. He was arrested and exiled.

In 1900, the British governor demanded to sit on the Golden Stool. Yaa Asantewaa led a war. The British won, and Asante became a colony, but they never got the Golden Stool.
