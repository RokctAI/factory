Hey — this lesson is about the problems that come when cities grow very fast.

There are not enough houses, so informal settlements grow, often on unsafe land where fires and floods are dangers.

Water, toilets, electricity and rubbish collection fall behind, which spreads disease.

Clinics and schools get crowded. Solutions include upgrading settlements, mobile clinics and new classrooms.
