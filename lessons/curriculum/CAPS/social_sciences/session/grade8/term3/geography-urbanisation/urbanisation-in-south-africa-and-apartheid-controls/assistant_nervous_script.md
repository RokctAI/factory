Hey — this lesson is about how South Africa's cities grew, and how apartheid tried to control who lived there.

Mines and factories pulled people to the cities.

Pass laws and influx control tried to keep African people out, or there only to work. The Group Areas Act forced people out of mixed areas.

Homelands pushed people far away, so many travelled for hours to work. In 1986 the pass laws ended.
