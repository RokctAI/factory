Every day, buses and minibus taxis arrive at Park Station in Johannesburg from Limpopo, the Eastern Cape, KwaZulu-Natal, and from Zimbabwe, Mozambique and Malawi. Among the passengers are young people carrying everything they own, coming to the city for the first time to look for work, to study or to join family. Why do they leave home? Why do they choose the city? Today we study why cities in Africa, and especially in South Africa, are growing so fast. We learn the meaning of urbanisation and migration, and we sort the reasons people move into push factors, which drive them away from rural areas, and pull factors, which attract them to cities.
