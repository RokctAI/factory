Hey — this lesson is about finding land use zones on aerial photos and maps.

A topographic map at one to fifty thousand uses symbols. An orthophoto map at one to ten thousand is a photo from above with labels.

The CBD has tall buildings where roads meet. Industry has big roofs, tanks and railway lines. Rich areas have big gardens and pools.

Always say the land use, give your evidence, then explain.
