Hey — this lesson is about aerial photographs, photos taken from above.

A vertical photo looks straight down, like a map. You see only roofs and treetops, and you can measure on it.

An oblique photo is tilted. You see the sides of things, and sometimes the horizon. It is easy to recognise things, but you cannot measure.

Natural features are made by nature; constructed features are made by people.
