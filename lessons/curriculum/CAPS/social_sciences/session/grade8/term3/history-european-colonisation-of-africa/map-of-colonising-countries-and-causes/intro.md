Look at a map of Africa in 1914 and it looks like a patchwork quilt in seven colours. Large areas in one colour stretch from Egypt to South Africa. Another colour covers most of north-west Africa and the Sahara. Smaller patches in other colours lie in the Congo, Angola and Mozambique, East Africa, South West Africa, Libya and the Horn of Africa. Only two areas are left in their own colours: Ethiopia and Liberia. Today we read this map: which European countries colonised which parts of Africa. Then we ask the big question: why did they do it? We sort the causes of colonisation into economic, political, social and strategic causes, and practise explaining them with evidence.
