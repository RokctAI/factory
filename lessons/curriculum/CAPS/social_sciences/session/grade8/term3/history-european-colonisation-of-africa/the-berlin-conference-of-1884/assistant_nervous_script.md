Hey — this lesson is about the Berlin Conference of 1884 to 1885.

European countries were competing for Africa. Bismarck hosted a meeting of fourteen countries in Berlin. No Africans were invited.

They agreed rules: free trade on the Congo, Leopold II's Congo Free State, and that you must really occupy land to claim it.

It did not draw all the borders, but it sped up the scramble. By 1914, only Ethiopia and Liberia were independent.
