Hey — this lesson is about which countries colonised Africa, and why.

By 1914, seven European countries ruled almost all of Africa. Only Ethiopia and Liberia were free.

The causes were economic, like raw materials and markets; political, like rivalry and prestige; and social, like missionaries and racist ideas.

New technology, like steamships, quinine and machine guns, made it possible.
