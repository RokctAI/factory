Cut a globe in half along the equator and you get two hemispheres, the Northern and the Southern. Cut it along the prime meridian and the hundred and eightieth meridian and you get two more, the Eastern and the Western. Today we revise the four hemispheres from Grade 6 and use them precisely. We place every continent and ocean in its hemispheres and find that Africa is the only continent crossed by both the equator and the prime meridian, so it lies in all four. We see how unevenly land and people are shared between north and south, with most of the world's land and most of its people in the Northern Hemisphere. We learn the special parallels that divide the Earth into heat zones, and we preview why the seasons in the two halves of the world are always opposite.
