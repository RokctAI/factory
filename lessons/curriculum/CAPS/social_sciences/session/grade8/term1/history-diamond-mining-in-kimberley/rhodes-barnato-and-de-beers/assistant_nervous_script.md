Hey — this is really a story about two people, so let's keep it simple.

Cecil Rhodes came from England in 1870 for his health. He made money pumping water from flooded mines, bought claims, and in 1880 started the De Beers Mining Company.

Barney Barnato came from a poor part of London in 1873 with a box of cigars. He bought diamonds from diggers, gambled on the blue ground and got rich. His company was Kimberley Central.

Too many diamonds made prices fall. Each man wanted to control all the mines.

Rhodes, helped by the Rothschild bank, bought more and more of Barnato's company. In 1888 they merged into De Beers Consolidated Mines. It controlled about ninety percent of the world's diamonds.

Rhodes used the wealth to push north and became Prime Minister of the Cape. His legacy is still debated.
