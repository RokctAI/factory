Hey — a few names in this one, so let's line them up.

1867: a boy finds a diamond near Hopetown.

1871: diamonds are found in the dry ground, including on a hill called Colesberg Kopje. The diamonds go deep, so mining can last a long time. That hill becomes the Big Hole of Kimberley.

Four groups claim the land: the Orange Free State, the Transvaal, the Griqua leader Nicolaas Waterboer, and Tswana chiefs.

The Keate Award in 1871 rules against the Transvaal. Waterboer asks Britain for protection, and Britain takes the area in October 1871. It is called Griqualand West.

Why? Britain wanted the diamond wealth.

Afterwards, the Free State gets ninety thousand pounds, and the Griqua lose most of their land.
