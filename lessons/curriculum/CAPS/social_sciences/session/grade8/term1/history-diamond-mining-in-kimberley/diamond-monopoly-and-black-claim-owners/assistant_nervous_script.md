Hey — this lesson is about how things changed, so let's go step by step.

1871: the Kimberley Mine is cut into hundreds of small claims. Each is about nine and a half metres on a side.

Some black and coloured men owned claims too. White diggers accused them of buying stolen diamonds, rioted in 1872, and pushed for rules about licences that shut black owners out.

The mine got deeper. Rock walls fell in and water flooded it. Small diggers could not afford machines and sold out. Companies formed from 1880.

African workers had to carry passes from 1872. From 1885 they were locked in compounds next to the mine for months and searched before going home. White workers were not.

By 1888 one company, De Beers, controlled almost everything. That is a monopoly.
