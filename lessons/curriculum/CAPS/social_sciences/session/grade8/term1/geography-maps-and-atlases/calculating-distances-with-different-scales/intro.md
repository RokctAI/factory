How far is Johannesburg from Cape Town, and how far is it from London? Today the ruler and the atlas answer both. We calculate distances between settlements at three scales: a local map at one to fifty thousand, regional maps of South Africa at one to five million and one to ten million, and a world map at one to one hundred million. We use one method for every scale: measure in centimetres, convert with the scale, and state the answer in kilometres. We compare straight-line distance with distance by road, work backwards from a real distance to a map distance, and see why flat world maps stretch long distances so that a globe and a piece of string give a better answer. We finish with the layout that earns full marks and the slips that lose them.
