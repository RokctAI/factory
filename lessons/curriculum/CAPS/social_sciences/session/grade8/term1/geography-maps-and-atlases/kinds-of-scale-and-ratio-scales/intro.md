Open an atlas and the same page size shows the whole world on one spread and a single city on the next. The difference is scale. Today we sort atlas maps into world, regional and local maps and see how their scales change the detail they can show. We review word scales and line scales, then meet the third and most powerful kind, the ratio or number scale, written as one to fifty thousand. We learn what the number after the one means, why a ratio scale works in any unit, and how to convert it into kilometres by dividing by one hundred thousand. Then we convert between all three kinds of scale, compare two ratio scales to decide which is larger, and finish with the five mistakes that cost marks in every scale question.
