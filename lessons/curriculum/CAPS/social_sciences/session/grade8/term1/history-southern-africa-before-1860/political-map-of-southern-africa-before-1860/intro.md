Before diamonds were found in 1867, the map of southern Africa looked very different from the map of South Africa today. There were two British colonies, two Boer republics, and many independent African kingdoms and chiefdoms, from the Zulu kingdom and the Swazi kingdom to Moshoeshoe's Basotho and the Pedi of Sekhukhuneland. Today we draw the political map of southern Africa around 1860. We place each state and kingdom, explain how it came to be there, who governed it and how, and how they related to one another through trade, diplomacy and war. This map is the starting point for everything that follows this year, because the mineral discoveries would change it completely within fifty years.
