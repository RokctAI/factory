In 1860 there was no South Africa yet.

Two British colonies: the Cape, British since 1806, and Natal, British since 1843.

Two Boer republics, set up by trekkers who left the Cape from 1835: the Transvaal north of the Vaal River, recognised in 1852, and the Orange Free State between the Orange and Vaal, recognised in 1854.

Many African kingdoms that held most of the land: the Zulu under Mpande, the Swazi under Mswati, the Basotho under Moshoeshoe at Thaba Bosiu, the Pedi, the Tswana, the Xhosa chiefdoms east of the Kei, the Mpondo and the Venda. The Griqua had their own leaders too.

They traded and made treaties, and they fought over land and cattle.
