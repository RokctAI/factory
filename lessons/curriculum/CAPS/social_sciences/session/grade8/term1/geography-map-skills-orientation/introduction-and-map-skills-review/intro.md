Grade 8 Social Sciences is two subjects in one: Geography, the study of places, people and the land, and History, the study of how the past shaped the world we live in. Today we set out the year, term by term, and the tasks that earn the marks: map skills, source-based questions, paragraphs and essays. Then we pick up the map tools from Grade 7 and make them sharp again. We read a street map with its grid squares and index, we compare word scales and line scales, we sort small-scale maps from large-scale maps, and we turn a measurement on paper into a real distance on the ground. One rule carries the whole term: every map distance is only half an answer until it has been multiplied by the scale and given its unit.
