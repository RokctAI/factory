Hey — let's keep this simple.

Land heats up fast and cools down fast. Water heats up slowly and cools down slowly.

So the sea keeps places near the coast mild: not too hot in summer, not too cold in winter, and not much difference between day and night.

Places far from the sea get bigger differences. That is called continentality.

Johannesburg is far inland. In July, it goes from about seventeen degrees in the afternoon to about four at night, with frost.

Cape Town is on the coast. In July, it goes from about eighteen to about seven, and frost is rare.

Johannesburg is also higher and further north, so remember other factors matter too.
