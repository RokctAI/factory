Someone in Johannesburg might say: it is raining today, so I will take an umbrella. That is about the weather. Someone else might say: Johannesburg has summer rainfall and dry winters, so the best time to plant maize is October. That is about the climate. Today we learn the difference between weather and climate, and we study the elements that describe the weather: temperature, humidity, wind and precipitation. We learn how each element is measured, with thermometers, hygrometers, wind vanes, anemometers and rain gauges, how to calculate averages and ranges, and how the South African Weather Service turns millions of measurements into forecasts and climate records that farmers, pilots, engineers and families rely on every day.
