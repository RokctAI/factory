Hey — climate can sound technical, but this one is simple.

Latitude means how far you are from the equator.

Near the equator, the sun shines almost straight down, so its heat is concentrated. Further away, it shines at a slant, so the heat is spread thin and passes through more air. So places closer to the equator are usually warmer.

Mbombela is at about twenty-five and a half degrees south. George is at about thirty-four degrees south. In January, Mbombela averages about twenty-four degrees and George about twenty.

Mbombela is higher above sea level, which should make it cooler, but it is still warmer. That shows latitude is strong.

Rain: Mbombela gets summer thunderstorms. George gets rain all year.
