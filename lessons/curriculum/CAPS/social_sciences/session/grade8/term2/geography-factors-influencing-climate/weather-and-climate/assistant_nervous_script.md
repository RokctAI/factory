Hey — two words to sort out today, weather and climate.

Weather is what is happening now or this week: sunny, rainy, windy. It changes fast.

Climate is what usually happens in a place, worked out from about thirty years of weather.

Weather has four main elements:

Temperature: how hot or cold, measured with a thermometer in a white box called a Stevenson screen.

Humidity: how much water vapour is in the air, measured with a hygrometer.

Wind: a wind vane shows where it comes from, and an anemometer measures its speed.

Precipitation: rain, hail and snow, measured in millimetres with a rain gauge.
