Hey — this lesson just connects things you already know.

Each climate is explained by a few factors: latitude, pressure belts and winds, the sea, height, currents and mountains.

Rising air brings rain. Sinking air brings dry weather. That alone explains rainforests and deserts.

The belts move with the seasons, which is why the savanna has summer rain and Cape Town has winter rain.

Higher means colder: about six and a half degrees per thousand metres.

Warm currents mean humid coasts. Cold currents mean dry, foggy coasts.

When you explain, link the factor to the process to the effect.
