Why is Cape Town wet in winter while Pretoria is wet in summer? Why does the Namib Desert reach right down to the Atlantic shore while Durban's coast is green and humid? Why can there be snow on Kilimanjaro, almost on the equator? Every climate region on the map is the result of the same small set of factors you studied at the start of the term: latitude, distance from the sea, altitude, ocean currents, mountains and the winds and pressure belts that move with the seasons. Today we put those factors to work. We take each climate region in turn, explain its temperature and rainfall using the factors, and then practise the kind of linked explanation that earns full marks, using South African towns as our main examples.
