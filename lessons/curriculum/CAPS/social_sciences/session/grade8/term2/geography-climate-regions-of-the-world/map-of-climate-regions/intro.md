If you colour a world map by climate, a striking pattern appears. A band of tropical rainforest straddles the equator. Belts of savanna lie on either side of it. Then come the great deserts, centred near the tropics, then the Mediterranean and subtropical zones, the temperate and continental belts, and finally the tundra and ice of the polar regions. The pattern is roughly repeated north and south of the equator, but it is twisted by oceans, currents and mountains. Today we learn to read a world map of climate regions, to locate each region on each continent, and to describe the pattern in words. We also zoom in on southern Africa and place South Africa's own climate regions on the map.
