Hey — a climate map is just a coloured picture of the world. Here is how to read it.

First, read the key: it tells you which colour is which climate.

Then use the equator and the tropics to say where things are.

From the equator outwards: rainforest, savanna, desert near the tropics, then Mediterranean and subtropical, temperate and continental, and tundra and ice near the poles.

West coasts and east coasts can be different because of cold and warm currents.

In South Africa, rain decreases from east to west: humid on the KwaZulu-Natal coast, dry on the west coast, and Cape Town gets winter rain.
