Hey — this lesson is about the people who owned the gold mines.

Gold was found on the Witwatersrand in 1886. It was in hard rock deep underground, so only big companies with lots of money could mine it.

The rich men who controlled the mines were called the Randlords. Many had made money in Kimberley first.

In 1887 they formed the Chamber of Mines. Because the price of gold was fixed, they worked together to keep costs low, especially black workers' wages.

They also recruited workers from all over southern Africa and pushed the government for what they wanted.
