Hey — this lesson is about how workers and communities were divided.

Skilled white miners came from places like Cornwall. They were well paid and demanded a colour bar to keep certain jobs for white workers.

Many Afrikaners lost their farms and became poor in the towns. This was called the poor white problem.

The mine owners wanted cheap labour and brought in Chinese workers from 1904.

Indian South Africans faced laws on land, tax, the vote and immigration, like Natal's three-pound tax and the Transvaal's Black Act.
