Hey — this lesson is about three wars in three years.

After diamonds were found, Britain wanted to control all of southern Africa. Strong African kingdoms stood in the way.

The Xhosa were defeated in 1878.

The Zulu won a famous battle at Isandlwana in January 1879, but lost the war at Ulundi in July 1879.

The Pedi under Sekhukhune were defeated at the end of 1879 by the British and Swazi.

After these wars, people lost land and paid taxes, so more men had to work on the mines.
