Hey — this lesson is about what it was like to work underground.

At first Kimberley was an open hole. From about 1885, the mines went underground with shafts and tunnels.

Underground was dangerous: falling rock, dynamite, fires, heat and dust. In 1888, a fire in the De Beers Mine killed about two hundred miners, most of them black workers.

Black workers did the hardest jobs for low pay. White workers had the skilled and boss jobs. That was the colour bar.

Dust and poor food caused diseases, and many workers died.
