Hey — this lesson is about how power shifted in South Africa from 1902 to 1910.

Britain beat the Boer republics in a war that ended in 1902, mostly because of the gold.

The peace left black people without the vote. People organised: the APO in 1902, Indians in the Transvaal in 1903.

In 1906 the Bambatha Rebellion in Natal was crushed.

In 1910 the Union of South Africa was formed, run by white men only.
