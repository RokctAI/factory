Hey — this lesson is about the founding of the SANNC in 1912.

After the Union of 1910 kept Africans out of Parliament, African leaders decided to unite.

On 8 January 1912 in Bloemfontein, they formed the South African Native National Congress. John Dube was president and Sol Plaatje secretary-general.

They used peaceful methods like petitions and delegations. In 1923 the name changed to the ANC.
