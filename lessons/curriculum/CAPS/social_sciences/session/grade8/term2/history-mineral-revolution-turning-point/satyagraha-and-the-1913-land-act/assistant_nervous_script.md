Hey — this lesson is about two big events of 1913.

The Natives Land Act said Africans could only own or rent land in the reserves, about seven per cent of the country. Thousands of families were evicted.

Indian South Africans, led by Gandhi, used satyagraha: breaking unfair laws peacefully and going to jail. In 1913 there were strikes and a big march.

In 1914, the three-pound tax was ended and Indian marriages were recognised.
