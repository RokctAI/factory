A climate graph packs thirty years of weather into one small picture: a line for temperature, twelve bars for rainfall, and the months along the bottom. Once you can read it, you can tell in seconds whether a place has summer rain or winter rain, hot or mild summers, cold or gentle winters, and which climate type it belongs to. Today we learn to read, draw and interpret climate graphs step by step. We work with two stations, one with a winter-rainfall pattern like Cape Town's and one with a summer-rainfall pattern like Pretoria's, and we calculate annual totals, ranges, means and seasonal percentages. Then we practise writing the kind of description and explanation that examiners reward.
