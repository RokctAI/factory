In Kisangani, in the rainforest of the Democratic Republic of the Congo, it is hot and humid every month of the year, and it rains almost every afternoon. In Durban, summers are hot and wet, but winters are mild and drier. In London, it rarely gets very hot or very cold, and light rain can fall in any month. These are three different kinds of climate: tropical, subtropical and temperate. Today we learn how geographers group the world's climates into types, what defines tropical, subtropical and temperate climates, where they are found, and what their temperature and rainfall patterns look like. We also see how each climate shapes the vegetation, farming and ways of life of the people who live in it.
