Hey — climate graphs look busy, but there are only two things to look at, and you already know how to read both.

The line is temperature. Find its highest and lowest months. Subtract to get the range.

The bars are rainfall. Add them all to get the total. Then see where the tallest bars are.

Tallest bars under the low part of the line means winter rain, like Cape Town.

Tallest bars under the high part of the line means summer rain, like Pretoria.

Always write numbers with units: degrees Celsius for temperature, millimetres for rain.
