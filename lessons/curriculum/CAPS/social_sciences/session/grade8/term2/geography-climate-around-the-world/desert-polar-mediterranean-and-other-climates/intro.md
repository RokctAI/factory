Cape Town's rain falls mainly in winter, while its summers are dry and sunny, a pattern shared by Rome, Los Angeles and Perth. Upington, in the Northern Cape, gets less than two hundred millimetres of rain a year. Moscow's winters average below freezing, and in Antarctica temperatures have dropped below minus eighty degrees. Today we complete our tour of the world's climates. We study desert and semi-desert climates, continental climates deep inside the northern continents, the Mediterranean climate of the south-western Cape, the polar and tundra climates of the far north and south, and high mountain climates. For each, we learn its temperature and rainfall pattern, where it is found and why, and how plants, animals and people live with it.
