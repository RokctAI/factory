Hey — this lesson is about sorting climates into families. Here is the short version.

Tropical climates are hot all year. Rainforest: hot and wet every month, like the Congo. Savanna: hot, with a wet summer and a dry winter, like the Kruger Park.

Subtropical: hot summers and mild winters, like Durban, with most rain in summer.

Temperate: clear seasons without big extremes, like London, with light rain in every month.

To sort a place, ask: how hot, is there a cold season, how much rain, and when does it fall?

One twist: Johannesburg is high up, so its climate is temperate, not subtropical.
