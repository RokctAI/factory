Hey — lots of climate names here, so here is a calm list. You do not have to learn them all at once; just match each name to one picture.

Desert: less than about two hundred and fifty millimetres of rain a year, like the Sahara and the Namib.

Semi-desert: a bit more rain, like the Karoo and Upington.

Mediterranean: dry summers and wet winters, like Cape Town.

Continental: warm summers and very cold winters, far from the sea, like Moscow.

Polar: below freezing all year, like Antarctica.

Tundra: a short, cool summer, frozen ground underneath, no trees.

High mountain: colder the higher you go, like Lesotho's mountains.
