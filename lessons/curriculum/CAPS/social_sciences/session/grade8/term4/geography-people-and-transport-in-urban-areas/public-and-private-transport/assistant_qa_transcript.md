### subtopic_1 — Public Transport: Buses and Trains
**Assistant:** Why are taxis used more than buses and trains?

**Tutor:** Mostly because they are available where people live and when they need them. Taxis run on a huge number of routes, including into townships and informal settlements where buses and trains do not go. They leave frequently, start early and stop almost anywhere along a route, so passengers walk shorter distances. Many train services have also become unreliable, so commuters who once took trains have switched to taxis. Buses usually follow fewer, fixed routes. Even though a taxi fare can be higher than a subsidised bus or train ticket, convenience and reliability make taxis the first choice for millions of people.

### subtopic_2 — Minibus Taxis
**Assistant:** What are taxi hand signals?

**Tutor:** They are a system of hand signs that passengers use at the roadside to show a taxi driver where they want to go. For example, a finger pointing up can mean going into town, and other signs show particular areas or routes. The signs differ from city to city, and people learn them from family and friends. They work because many taxis do not have fixed stops, so drivers need to know quickly whether a person waiting by the road is going their way. The signs are part of the everyday culture of South African cities.

### subtopic_3 — Private Transport: Cars, Bicycles and Walking
**Assistant:** Is it better to drive a car than use public transport?

**Tutor:** It depends on whose point of view you take. For one person, a car can be faster, more comfortable and more flexible. For the city as a whole, many cars with one person each cause congestion, pollution, road accidents and a need for huge parking areas. Good public transport moves many more people in the same road space at a lower cost per person. Many cities therefore try to make public transport safe, reliable and affordable so that people choose to use it even if they own a car.

### subtopic_4 — Apartheid Geography, Calculation and the Error Museum
**Assistant:** How do I answer a question on urban transport?

**Tutor:** First, identify the mode or modes in the question and say whether each is public or private. Then give advantages and disadvantages, linked to cost, time, safety, comfort, flexibility and the environment. Use South African examples such as Rea Vaya, MyCiTi, Metrorail, the Gautrain and minibus taxis. Where it fits, explain the historical reason for long commutes, namely apartheid planning that placed many people far from work. If a calculation is given, show your steps, for example fare times two trips times the number of working days.
