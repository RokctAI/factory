Before sunrise in Soweto, Khayelitsha or Umlazi, long queues form at taxi ranks and bus stops. Some workers squeeze onto a Metrorail train, some climb into a bus and many take a minibus taxi. Others drive alone in a car, ride a bicycle or walk for an hour to reach work. Every one of these journeys is a choice shaped by cost, distance, safety and what is available. Today we study how people move around South African cities, using public transport and private transport.
