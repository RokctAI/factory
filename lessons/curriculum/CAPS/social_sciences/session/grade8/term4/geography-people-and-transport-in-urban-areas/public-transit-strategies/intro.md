A red MyCiTi bus glides past a long line of cars on Cape Town's West Coast road, using a lane of its own. In Johannesburg, a Rea Vaya bus pulls into a station in the middle of the road, and passengers step straight on without climbing stairs. On a Sunday, a street in a city centre is closed to cars and filled with cyclists, walkers and market stalls. These are not accidents. They are strategies, chosen by city planners to move more people with less traffic. Today we study strategies to improve public transport and reduce the problems of urban transport.
