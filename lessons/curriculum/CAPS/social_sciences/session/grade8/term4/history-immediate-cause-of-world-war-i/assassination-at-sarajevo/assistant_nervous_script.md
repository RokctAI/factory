Hey — this lesson is about the spark that started the First World War.

On 28 June 1914, Gavrilo Princip shot Archduke Franz Ferdinand and his wife in Sarajevo.

Germany backed Austria-Hungary, Austria-Hungary declared war on Serbia, Russia helped Serbia, and Germany marched through Belgium.

On 4 August Britain joined. It took just 37 days.
