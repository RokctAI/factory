### subtopic_1 — The Allied Powers
**Assistant:** Why did the Ottoman Empire join the Central Powers?

**Tutor:** The Ottoman leaders had several reasons. They had close military and economic links with Germany, which had trained parts of their army and invested in projects such as the Berlin to Baghdad railway. They feared Russia, their old enemy, which wanted control of the straits near Constantinople. They had also been angered when Britain took over two warships that were being built for them in British shipyards. In 1914 two German warships escaped to Constantinople and were transferred to the Ottoman navy, and at the end of October they took part in a raid on Russian ports in the Black Sea. Russia, Britain and France then declared war on the Ottoman Empire.

### subtopic_2 — The Central Powers
**Assistant:** Why were black South Africans not allowed to fight as soldiers?

**Tutor:** The South African government of the time was controlled by white South Africans, and racial segregation was part of its policy. Many white leaders feared that if black men were trained and armed as soldiers, they would later use those skills to challenge white rule. So black South Africans were recruited into the South African Native Labour Contingent, which did hard labour such as loading ships, building roads and cutting timber in France, but they were not given weapons. Many served bravely in difficult and dangerous conditions, and their contribution was not properly recognised for many years.

### subtopic_3 — A World War: Fronts and Empires
**Assistant:** Was the United States an ally?

**Tutor:** The United States fought on the side of Britain, France and their partners from April 1917, but it called itself an associated power rather than an ally. This was because it wanted to keep its freedom of action and did not want to be bound by the secret treaties that the Allies had made among themselves. In practice, American troops, money, food and weapons were extremely important to the Allied victory in 1918. In exams, it is acceptable to say that the United States joined the Allied side in 1917.

### subtopic_4 — Mapping the Sides, Calculation and the Error Museum
**Assistant:** How do I remember who was on which side?

**Tutor:** For the Central Powers, remember four countries in a line through the middle of the map: Germany, Austria-Hungary, Bulgaria and the Ottoman Empire. Everyone else who fought was on the Allied side. For the Allies, start with the Triple Entente, Britain, France and Russia, add Serbia and Belgium, which were invaded, and then add the late joiners in order: Japan in 1914, Italy in 1915, Romania and Portugal in 1916, and the United States and Greece in 1917. Remember that Italy switched from the Triple Alliance and that Russia left in 1918.
