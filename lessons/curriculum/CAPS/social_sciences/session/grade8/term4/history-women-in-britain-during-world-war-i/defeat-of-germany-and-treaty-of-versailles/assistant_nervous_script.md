Hey — this lesson is about how the First World War ended.

In 1918 Germany's last big attack failed, the Allies struck back, and Germany ran out of food and allies.

The fighting stopped at 11 a.m. on 11 November 1918.

The Treaty of Versailles in 1919 blamed Germany, made it pay, cut its army and took its land and colonies. South Africa got South West Africa.
