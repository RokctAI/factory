Hey — this lesson is about South Africa's main roads, railways, airports and ports.

The N1 goes from Cape Town to Zimbabwe, the N2 along the coast, and the N3 from Durban to Johannesburg.

Railways were built to carry minerals to the ports. O. R. Tambo is the busiest airport, and Durban is the busiest port.

Most routes link inland Gauteng to the coast, like the spokes of a wheel.
