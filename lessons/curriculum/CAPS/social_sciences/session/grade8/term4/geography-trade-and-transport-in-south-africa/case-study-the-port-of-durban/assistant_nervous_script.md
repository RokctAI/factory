Hey — this lesson is a case study of the Port of Durban.

It sits on a natural bay protected by the Bluff, and it has to be dredged because sand builds up.

It is the busiest port in South Africa: containers, cars, fuel and sugar each have their own terminals.

Goods travel inland by the N3, by train to Johannesburg, and fuel by pipeline.
