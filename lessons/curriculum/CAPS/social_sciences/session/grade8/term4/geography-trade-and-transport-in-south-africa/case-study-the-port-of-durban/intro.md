Stand on the Bluff above Durban harbour and look down. Giant cranes lift containers off a ship that has just arrived from Asia. On another quay, thousands of new cars made in South Africa wait in rows to drive onto a car carrier bound for Europe. Tankers unload fuel at a terminal, a bulk carrier takes on sugar, and tugboats guide a ship through the narrow entrance channel. Beyond the harbour, trucks queue on the roads and trains wait in the yards. This is the Port of Durban, the busiest port in South Africa. Today we use it as a case study: its location, its exports and imports, the specialised ships it handles, and its links with other forms of transport.
