Stand beside the N3 near Van Reenen's Pass on a weekday and count the trucks. Thousands of heavy vehicles climb the escarpment every day, carrying containers, fuel, food and machinery between Durban and Gauteng. Beside the highway runs a railway line that could carry much of that cargo. So why does so much freight travel by road? And what should South Africa's transport network look like in the future? Today we compare road and rail transport, weigh their advantages and disadvantages, and ask what a good future network needs.
