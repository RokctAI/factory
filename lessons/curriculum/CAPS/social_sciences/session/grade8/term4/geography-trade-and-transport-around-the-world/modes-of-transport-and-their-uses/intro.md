Iron ore from Sishen in the Northern Cape travels more than eight hundred kilometres to the coast on trains so long that they take minutes to pass a level crossing. Roses grown in Kenya are cut in the morning and sold in Europe the next day, after a night flight. Petrol from the refinery at Durban flows to Gauteng through a pipeline buried under the ground. A furniture shop in Polokwane receives its sofas on a truck that drives right up to its door. Each mode of transport has its own strengths and weaknesses, and each is suited to particular goods and journeys. Today we study the five main modes of transport: sea, air, road, rail and pipeline, and how to choose between them.
