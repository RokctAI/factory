Hey — this lesson is about the five ways to move goods: sea, air, road, rail and pipeline.

Ships are slow but cheap for heavy loads. Planes are fast but expensive, good for flowers and medicines.

Trucks go door-to-door. Trains carry heavy loads like coal and iron ore. Pipelines carry liquids and gases.

Many journeys use more than one mode.
