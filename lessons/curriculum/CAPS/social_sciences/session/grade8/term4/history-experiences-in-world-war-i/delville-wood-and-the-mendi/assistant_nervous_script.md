Hey — this lesson is about two South African stories from the First World War.

At Delville Wood in 1916, white South African soldiers held a wood in France for six days, with terrible losses.

In 1917 the Mendi sank after a crash in fog, and 616 South Africans died, most of them black labourers.

For years only Delville Wood was honoured; after 1994 the Mendi was too.
