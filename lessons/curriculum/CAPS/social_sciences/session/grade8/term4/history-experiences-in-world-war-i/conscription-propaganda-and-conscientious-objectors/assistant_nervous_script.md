Hey — this lesson is about how Britain got soldiers and support in the First World War.

In 1914 huge numbers volunteered. In 1916 Britain made men join: that is conscription.

Posters, films and stories, called propaganda, built support, and the news was censored.

Some men refused to fight because of their conscience, and many were sent to prison.
