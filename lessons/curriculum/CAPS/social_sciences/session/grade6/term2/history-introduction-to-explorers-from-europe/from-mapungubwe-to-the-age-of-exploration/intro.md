Today a Grade 6 class in Cape Town compares a modern world map with a copy of an old European world map from the 1400s, where the southern end of Africa is missing. We briefly revise Mapungubwe and the Indian Ocean trade from Term 1, and then start the new topic: explorers from Europe who sailed around southern Africa, why their voyages mattered, and why the people who already lived here did not need to be found.
