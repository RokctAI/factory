Today a Grade 6 class from Cape Town walks through the Company's Garden in the middle of the city, which began as a vegetable garden for passing ships. We follow Vasco da Gama on the first European sea voyage from Portugal around southern Africa to India in 1497 to 1499, and then learn how the Dutch East India Company later set up a refreshment station at the Cape in 1652, and what this meant for the Khoikhoi.
