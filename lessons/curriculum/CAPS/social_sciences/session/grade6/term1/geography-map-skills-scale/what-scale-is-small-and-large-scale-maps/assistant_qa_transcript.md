### subtopic_1 — What Scale Means
**Assistant:** Why is it called large scale when the map shows a small area?

**Tutor:** This confuses many people, so you are in good company. The word large describes the scale itself, not the area. Think of the scale as a fraction. One over ten thousand is a bigger fraction than one over one hundred million, just as one over two is bigger than one over a hundred. So a street map at one to ten thousand has a larger scale than a world map at one to one hundred million. A larger scale means things are shrunk less, so they look larger on the paper, and you can see more detail. But because things are shrunk less, only a small area fits onto the page. So large scale means large drawings of a small area. If you remember that the street map is large scale because the houses look large, you will get it right.

### subtopic_2 — Small Area, Big Detail
**Assistant:** How do mapmakers decide what to leave out?

**Tutor:** Mapmakers must make choices on every map, because there is never enough space for everything. This is sometimes called generalising. On a small-scale map, like a world map, they leave out small towns, small rivers, minor roads and buildings, and they keep only the most important features, such as big cities, major rivers, mountain ranges and borders. They also make coastlines and rivers smoother, because every little bend would not show anyway. On a large-scale street map, they can include almost everything, such as street names, parks, schools and even single buildings. Mapmakers also think about who will use the map. A tourist map of a city might show hotels and museums, while a map for firefighters shows fire hydrants. So two maps of the same place can include different details.

### subtopic_3 — From World Maps to Street Maps
**Assistant:** What scale are the maps on a phone?

**Tutor:** Map apps are clever, because their scale is not fixed. When you open the app and see the whole of South Africa, you are looking at a small-scale view, with only big cities and main roads. When you pinch to zoom in, the app changes to a larger scale and adds more detail: towns, then suburbs, then street names and even buildings. Most map apps show a small line scale in one corner, which changes as you zoom, so you can still measure distances. The app is really holding many maps at different scales and showing you the right one for how far you have zoomed. Printed maps cannot do this, so atlases include several maps of the same place at different scales, such as a map of South Africa and a separate map of each province.
