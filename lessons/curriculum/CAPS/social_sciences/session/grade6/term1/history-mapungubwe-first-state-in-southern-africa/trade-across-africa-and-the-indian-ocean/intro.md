Today a Grade 6 class in Thohoyandou stretches pieces of string across a big floor map, from Mapungubwe to the coast of Mozambique, and then across the Indian Ocean to Arabia, India and China. We learn how Mapungubwe was linked to a huge trading network, which goods went out and which came in, how goods were carried on foot to the coast, and why this is an early example of globalisation.
