### subtopic_1 — What Population Means
**Assistant:** How did people count the population before computers?

**Tutor:** Counting people is very old. Thousands of years ago, rulers in places such as ancient Egypt, China and the Roman Empire counted people, mainly to collect taxes or find men for their armies. These counts were written by hand on papyrus, clay, wood or paper, and adding them up took a very long time. In the eighteen hundreds and nineteen hundreds, many countries began holding regular censuses, with large teams of census workers going from house to house with paper forms. The results were added up by hand or, later, with machines that read holes punched into cards. Today, census workers often use tablets, and in South Africa's 2022 census, people could also fill in the form online or by phone, which made counting faster.

### subtopic_2 — Natural Reasons for Where People Live
**Assistant:** Why are some census results disputed?

**Tutor:** Counting every single person in a country is extremely difficult. Some people are not at home when census workers visit, some live in places that are hard to reach, some move around a lot, and some do not want to answer questions. So almost every census misses some people, which is called an undercount. Statistics agencies try to measure how many people were missed, for example by doing a second, smaller survey afterwards, and then adjust their figures. In South Africa's 2022 census, the undercount was higher than usual, so some experts have questioned parts of the results. That is why population numbers are often given as estimates, and why you should check the date and source of any population figure you use.

### subtopic_3 — Human Reasons for Where People Live
**Assistant:** Which country has the most people?

**Tutor:** For a long time, China was the country with the most people in the world. According to United Nations estimates, India overtook China in 2023 and now has the largest population, with more than one point four billion people. China has a similar number. Together, these two countries hold more than a third of all the people on Earth. The next most populous countries are much smaller in population, including the United States, Indonesia, Pakistan and Nigeria, which is Africa's most populous country. South Africa, with about sixty-two million people, is in the top thirty or so countries by population, and it is one of the most populous countries in Africa, although several, such as Nigeria, Ethiopia and Egypt, have many more people.
