Today a Grade 6 class from Mitchells Plain, in Cape Town, visits the Heart of Cape Town Museum at Groote Schuur Hospital, in the very rooms where the world's first human heart transplant took place in December 1967. We learn about Christiaan Barnard, the patient Louis Washkansky, the donor Denise Darvall, how the operation was done, what happened afterwards, and how heart transplants save lives today.
