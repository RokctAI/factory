Hey — if weather and climate seem to blur together, here is the calm version: today versus many years.

Weather is the air today: hot, wet, windy or cloudy.

Climate is the usual weather over about thirty years.

One storm is weather. A dry pattern over many years is climate.

Climate decides which plants, animals and crops can live in a place.
