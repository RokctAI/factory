Hey, if SWOT sounded like a code word, here is the calm version: four boxes, inside and outside.

Strengths and weaknesses are inside your business, so you can change them. Strengths are what you do well; weaknesses are what you lack.

Opportunities and threats are outside, so you cannot control them, only prepare. Opportunities are chances to benefit; threats could harm you.

Then turn the boxes into actions: use strengths, fix weaknesses, grab opportunities and plan for threats.

Open the practice set and sort a few points into the right box. You've got this!
