Hey, if SMARTER felt like a lot of letters, here is the calm version: make the goal clear, then check it.

S is specific: say exactly what. M is measurable: put a number on it. A is achievable: make it realistic. R is relevant: make it matter. T is time-bound: give it a deadline.

E is evaluated: check progress along the way. R is reviewed: look back and learn.

Then break the goal into small tasks with a name and a date for each, and tick them off.

Open the practice set and turn a vague wish into a SMARTER goal. You've got this!
