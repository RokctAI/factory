Hey, if AIDA sounded like a name, here is the calm version: notice, understand, want, do.

Attention: grab their eye or ear with a bold headline, a picture or a sound.

Interest: tell them what it is and why it is good for them. Benefits beat features.

Desire: make them want it, with tasty descriptions, testimonials or a special offer.

Action: tell them exactly what to do: price, place, time, buy now.

Use the four letters as a checklist for every advert.
