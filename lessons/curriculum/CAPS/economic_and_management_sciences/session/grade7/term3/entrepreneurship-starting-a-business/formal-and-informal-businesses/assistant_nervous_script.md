Hey, if formal and informal businesses felt confusing, here is the calm version: registered or not.

Formal businesses are registered with the CIPC and SARS, pay tax, follow labour laws and can get loans and big contracts, but they have more paperwork and costs.

Informal businesses are not registered. They are easy to start and flexible, but income is uncertain, loans are hard to get and workers have little protection.

The informal sector gives millions of South Africans an income.

And every business, formal or informal, starts by meeting a need or a want that people will pay for.
