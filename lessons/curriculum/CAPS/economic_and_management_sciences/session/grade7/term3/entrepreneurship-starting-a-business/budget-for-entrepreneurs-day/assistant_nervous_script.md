Hey, if the Entrepreneur's Day budget looked like a lot of sums, here is the calm version: two columns and two divisions.

Column one, fixed costs: they stay the same however many you sell, like table hire and posters.

Column two, variable costs: they grow with every unit, like ingredients and cups.

Division one: total cost divided by number of units gives the cost price per unit. Set the selling price higher than that.

Division two: fixed costs divided by selling price minus variable cost per unit gives the break-even point: how many you must sell to be safe.

Open the practice set and budget a small stall. You've got this!
