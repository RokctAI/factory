Today we learn one of the most useful planning tools in business: the SWOT analysis. SWOT stands for strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats. We use it to look honestly at a business idea from the inside and the outside before risking money on it. We do a SWOT analysis for a Grade 7 team planning a vetkoek stall for Entrepreneur's Day, and we see how to turn the four boxes into a better plan.
