Hey, if manufacturing sounded like big factories only, here is the calm version: make it, cost it, price it.

Manufacturing means turning raw materials into products, like beads into bracelets or flour into muffins. The extra value comes from your skill and time.

Costs come in three groups: materials, labour and overheads. Add them up and divide by the number you made to get the cost per unit.

Set a selling price above that cost that customers will pay. Price minus cost is the profit per unit.

Trading buys and resells; manufacturing makes. Both need you to know your costs.

Open the practice set and cost a small batch. You've got this!
