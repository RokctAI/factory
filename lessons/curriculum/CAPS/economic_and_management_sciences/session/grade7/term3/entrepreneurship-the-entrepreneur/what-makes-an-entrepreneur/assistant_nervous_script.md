Hey, if entrepreneurship sounded like something only famous business people do, here is the calm version: spot, idea, risk, reward.

An entrepreneur spots a need, has an idea to meet it, takes a risk by putting in money and time, and earns a profit if it works.

Characteristics are who you are: creative, brave but careful, determined, honest, responsible.

Skills are what you learn: budgeting and pricing, keeping records, communicating, negotiating, planning and solving problems.

Entrepreneurs create jobs and new products, and anyone can start building these qualities now.
