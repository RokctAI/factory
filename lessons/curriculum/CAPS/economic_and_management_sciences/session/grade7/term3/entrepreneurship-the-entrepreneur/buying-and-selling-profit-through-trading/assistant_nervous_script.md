Hey, if mark-ups and cost prices felt like a maths test, here is the calm version: buy low, sell a bit higher.

A trader buys goods and sells them without changing them.

Cost price is what the trader paid. Selling price is what the customer pays. Mark-up is the difference.

Gross profit is total sales minus the cost of the goods sold. Then other costs, like transport and electricity, still have to come out.

Competition, customers and costs decide how high the price can go.
