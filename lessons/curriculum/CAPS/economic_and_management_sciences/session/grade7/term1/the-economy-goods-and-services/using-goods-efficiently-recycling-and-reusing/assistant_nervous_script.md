Hey, if efficiency and recycling felt like a lecture, here is the calm version: waste less, use again, remake.

Efficient means getting the most out of something with the least waste. Effective means it actually does the job.

The waste hierarchy goes in order: reduce first, by buying and using less; then reuse, by using things again; then recycle, by turning waste into new materials.

Recycling also creates income: waste pickers sell cans and cardboard to buy-back centres, and some entrepreneurs turn waste into products.
