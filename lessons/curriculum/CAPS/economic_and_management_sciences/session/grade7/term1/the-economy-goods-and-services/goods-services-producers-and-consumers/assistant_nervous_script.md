Hey, if goods, services, producers and consumers felt like four new words at once, here is the calm version: touch it, and who makes it.

Can you touch it and take it home? Then it is a good: bread, shoes, a phone.

Did someone do something for you? Then it is a service: a haircut, a taxi ride, a lesson.

Producers make goods or provide services. Consumers buy and use them. Most people are both: they produce at work and consume when they spend.

Open the practice set and sort a few things into goods or services, and producer or consumer. You've got this!
