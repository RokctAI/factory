Hey, if primary and secondary needs felt like hair-splitting, here is the calm version: first and second.

Primary means first: food, water, shelter and clothing. You need these to stay alive, so families pay for them first.

Secondary means second: education, transport, a phone, furniture, recreation. They make life better once the first things are sorted.

Then come wants, the extras.

Income decides how far up the ladder a family can climb, and free services help poorer families reach the middle rungs.

Open the practice set and sort a few items into first, second or extra. You've got this!
