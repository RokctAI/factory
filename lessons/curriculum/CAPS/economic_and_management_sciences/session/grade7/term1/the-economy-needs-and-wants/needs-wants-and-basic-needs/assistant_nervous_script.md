Hey, if needs and wants felt confusing, here is the calm version: one question.

Ask: what happens if I do not have it? If you would be hungry, sick, unsafe or unable to cope, it is a need. If you would just be disappointed, it is a want.

Food, water, shelter, clothes, health care and safety are needs. Chips, branded sneakers and the newest phone are wants.

Needs grow as the circle grows: a family needs a home and income, a community needs a clinic and a road, the country needs electricity, police and jobs.

Open the practice set and run a few items through the trolley test. You've got this!
