Hey, if scarcity sounded like a big word, here is the calm version: long list, short money.

Wants never stop. As soon as you get one thing, you want the next. That is unlimited wants.

Money, time, water and land always run out. That is limited resources.

When the list is longer than the money, that is scarcity, and it means we must choose. Put needs at the top.

Whatever you choose, the best thing you gave up is the opportunity cost.

Open the practice set and find the opportunity cost in a few choices. You've got this!
