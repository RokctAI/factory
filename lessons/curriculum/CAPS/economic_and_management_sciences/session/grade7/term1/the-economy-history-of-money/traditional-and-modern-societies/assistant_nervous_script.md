Hey, if traditional and modern societies felt like a lot of history at once, here is the calm version: make it or buy it.

In a traditional society, families made most things themselves. They farmed, herded, built and shared, and cattle were their savings. That is called being self-sufficient.

In a modern society, people specialise. Each person does one job, earns money and buys almost everything from other people. That is called interdependence.

Life without money is hard because you need someone who wants exactly what you have, and it is hard to agree on value or to save.

So the rule is simple: the more people specialise and trade, the more they need money.
