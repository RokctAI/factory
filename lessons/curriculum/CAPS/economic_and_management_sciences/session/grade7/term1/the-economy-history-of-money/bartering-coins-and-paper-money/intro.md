Today we follow the story of money from a straight swap of a goat for a sack of grain to the shiny rand coins in your pocket. We see why bartering was difficult, how communities chose special items like cattle, salt and beads as early money, how metal coins and then promissory notes and paper banknotes made trade easier, and how the South African Reserve Bank issues the rand today. By the end you will know the qualities that make something good money, and why a piece of paper can be worth one hundred rand.
