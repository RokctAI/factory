Hey, if the history of money felt like a long list, here is the calm version: every step fixed a problem.

Barter was a straight swap, but you needed someone who wanted exactly what you had.

Commodity money, like cattle, salt and beads, fixed that, but it could die, rot or be hard to carry.

Coins fixed that, because metal lasts and can be counted, but heavy coins were easy to steal.

Promissory notes and paper money fixed that, because paper is light. Today the Reserve Bank issues the rand.

Good money is accepted, lasting, portable, divisible, scarce and uniform.
