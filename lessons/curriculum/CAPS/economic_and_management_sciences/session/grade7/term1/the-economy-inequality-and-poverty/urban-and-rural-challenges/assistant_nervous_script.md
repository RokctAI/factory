Hey, if urban and rural challenges felt like a long list, here is the calm version: too little and too much.

Rural areas often have too little: too few jobs, roads, clinics, schools and internet. Many families live on grants and money sent home from the city.

Cities often have too much pressure: too many people for the jobs, houses, water and electricity. That shows up as informal settlements, unemployment, crime and traffic.

People move because of push factors, like no jobs at home, and pull factors, like work and schools in the city. That movement is urbanisation.
