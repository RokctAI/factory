Hey, if this topic felt heavy, here is the calm version: two words and two kinds of causes.

Poor means a household does not have enough to meet basic needs. Unequal means there is a big gap between those with the most and those with the least. South Africa has both.

Past causes: land taken in colonial times, the 1913 Land Act, the Group Areas Act, Bantu Education and job reservation.

Present causes: very high unemployment, unequal schools, living far from jobs and slow growth.

The good news is that education, skills and jobs can narrow the gap, and that is what the next lessons are about.
