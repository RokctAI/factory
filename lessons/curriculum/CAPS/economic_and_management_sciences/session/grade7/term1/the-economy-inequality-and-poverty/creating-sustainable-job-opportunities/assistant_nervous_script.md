Hey, if job creation sounded like a problem only presidents can solve, here is the calm version: jobs that last.

A sustainable job lasts, pays a fair income, is safe and does not harm the environment.

Public works jobs help for a while, but lasting jobs come from a growing economy, especially small businesses, called SMMEs.

Government builds roads, power and skills. Business invests and employs. Communities buy local. Green jobs, like solar and recycling, protect the planet and create work.

And entrepreneurs, people who spot a need and start a business, create jobs for others.
