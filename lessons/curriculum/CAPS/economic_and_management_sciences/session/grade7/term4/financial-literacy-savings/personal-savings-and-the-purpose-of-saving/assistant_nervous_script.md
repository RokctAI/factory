Hey, if saving felt hard, here is the calm version: why, what stops you, and one trick.

Why: people save for emergencies, for goals like a bag or a trip, and for the far future like studies and retirement.

What stops you: low income, debt, small daily wants, peer pressure and no plan.

The trick: pay yourself first. Put savings aside the moment money arrives, then spend.

And when millions of people save, banks can lend for investment that creates jobs.
