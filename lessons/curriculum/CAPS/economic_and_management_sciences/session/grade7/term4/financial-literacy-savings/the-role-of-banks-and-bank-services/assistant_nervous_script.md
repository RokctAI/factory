Hey, if banking felt like a lot of products, here is the calm version: go-between, accounts, and the price of money.

A bank is a go-between. It takes deposits from savers and lends them to borrowers.

It pays savers a lower interest rate and charges borrowers a higher one. The difference, plus fees, pays its costs and makes a profit.

Accounts: transactional for everyday payments, savings and fixed deposits for saving. Loans: home, car, personal and business. Services: cards, EFTs, app banking and foreign exchange.
