Hey, if production felt like a big topic, here is the calm version: in, change, out.

Production means making goods and services. Inputs go in, a process changes them, and outputs come out.

The four factors of production are natural resources, labour, capital and entrepreneurship. Their rewards are rent, wages, interest and profit.

The three sectors are primary, taking from nature; secondary, making things; and tertiary, providing services.
