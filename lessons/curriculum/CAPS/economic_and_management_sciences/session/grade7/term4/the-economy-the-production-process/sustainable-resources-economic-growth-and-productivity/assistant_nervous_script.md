Hey, if productivity, growth and sustainability sounded like three big words, here is the calm version: more, bigger, lasting.

Productivity means getting more output from the same inputs, through skills, better tools and better organisation.

Economic growth means the whole country producing more goods and services each year, measured by GDP. It creates jobs and income.

Sustainability means producing in a way that does not destroy the water, soil, air and resources that the future needs.
