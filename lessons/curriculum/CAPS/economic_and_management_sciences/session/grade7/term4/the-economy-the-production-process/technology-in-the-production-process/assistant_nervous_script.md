Hey, if technology and production sounded like robots taking over, here is the calm version: tools that help people produce more.

Technology is knowledge used to make tools, machines and methods, from a wheelbarrow to a robot.

Labour-intensive production uses many workers and few machines. Capital-intensive production uses many machines and fewer workers.

Technology raises productivity, quality and safety, and lowers costs, but it can replace some jobs and needs money, power and skills.

The best protection is learning: maths, reading, digital skills and problem-solving.
