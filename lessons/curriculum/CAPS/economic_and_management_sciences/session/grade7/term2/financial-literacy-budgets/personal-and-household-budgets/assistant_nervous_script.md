Hey, if budgets sounded boring, here is the calm version: plan it, then check it.

A budget is a plan for money before you spend it. Income is what you expect to come in. Expenditure is what you plan to spend, including savings.

Put needs first, then savings, then wants. If spending equals income, the budget balances. Extra is a surplus; too little is a deficit.

At the end of the month, compare what really happened with the plan and adjust next month.
