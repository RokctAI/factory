Hey, if business budgets felt like a lot of numbers, here is the calm version: guess the sales, list the costs, subtract.

Budgeted income is the number you expect to sell times the price.

Fixed costs, like rent and table hire, stay the same. Variable costs, like ingredients, go up with every item.

Expected profit is income minus all the costs.

Afterwards, compare plan with reality: better than planned is favourable, worse is unfavourable. Then improve next time.

Open the practice set and plan a popcorn stall. You've got this!
