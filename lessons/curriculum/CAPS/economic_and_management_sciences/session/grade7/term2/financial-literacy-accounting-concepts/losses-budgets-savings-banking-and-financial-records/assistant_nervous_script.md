Hey, if this lesson felt like six new words, here is the calm version: plan, save, bank, record.

A loss happens when expenses are bigger than income. Records help you find out why.

A budget is a plan for your money before you spend it. Savings are money kept for later; pay yourself first.

A bank keeps money safe, makes payments and pays interest.

A transaction is any money exchange, a source document like a receipt proves it, and financial records keep track of everything.
