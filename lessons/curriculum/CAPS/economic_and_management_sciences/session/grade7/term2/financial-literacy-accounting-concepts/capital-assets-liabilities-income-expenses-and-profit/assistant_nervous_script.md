Hey, if these accounting words felt like a new language, here is the calm version: own, owe, earn, spend.

Capital is the owner's own money put into the business.

Assets are what the business owns. Liabilities are what it owes to others.

Income is what it earns. Expenses are what it spends to run.

Profit is income minus expenses. If expenses are bigger, it is a loss.
