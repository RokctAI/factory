Hey, if business income and expenses felt like a long list, here is the calm version: in, out, and the owner's own.

Income is money the business earns: sales of goods, fees for services, rent and interest. Capital and loans are not income.

Expenses are money spent to earn that income: stock, wages, rent, electricity, data, advertising.

Fixed expenses stay the same every month; variable expenses change with how busy the business is.

Profit is income minus expenses. And when the owner uses business money for personal things, that is drawings, not an expense.
