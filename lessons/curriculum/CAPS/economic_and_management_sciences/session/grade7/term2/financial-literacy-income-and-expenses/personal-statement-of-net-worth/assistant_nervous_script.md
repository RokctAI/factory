Hey, if the statement of net worth looked like a big form, here is the calm version: own minus owe.

List what the person owns, their assets, at today's values: house, car, furniture, savings, cash.

List what the person owes, their liabilities: bond, car loan, store accounts, credit card.

Subtract: assets minus liabilities equals net worth. It can be positive or negative.

To grow net worth, save and invest, and pay off debt. And remember, looking rich is not the same as being rich.

Open the practice set and do a few own-minus-owe sums. You've got this!
