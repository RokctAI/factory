Hey, if savings and investments felt like a lot of jargon, here is the calm version: safe jar, growth jar, and time.

Saving is putting money aside safely for later. Investing is putting money to work so it grows.

Interest is what the bank pays you for using your money. Compound interest means your interest earns interest too, so starting early makes a huge difference.

Low risk usually means low return; high return always comes with higher risk. And anything that promises huge guaranteed returns is probably a scam.
