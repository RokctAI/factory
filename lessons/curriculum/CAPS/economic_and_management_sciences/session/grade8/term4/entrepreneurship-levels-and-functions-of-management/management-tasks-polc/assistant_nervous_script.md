Hey, if management tasks sound abstract, here is the calm version: POLC.
P is planning: decide the goal and how to get there. Make it SMART: specific, measurable, achievable, realistic and time-bound.
O is organising: divide the work, give jobs to the right people, and get the resources ready.
Delegating means giving someone the authority and responsibility for a task, but the manager stays accountable.
L is leading: give clear instructions, communicate both ways, and motivate people.
C is control: set standards, measure, compare, and correct.
Example: target four hundred deliveries, actual three hundred and twenty, which is eighty percent. Find the cause, fix it.
It is a cycle. Control feeds the next plan.
Control can happen before the work, during the work and after the work. Earlier is cheaper.
All four tasks happen at every level, just in different amounts. Top managers plan more; supervisors lead and control more.
Open the practice set and try a few. You've got this!
