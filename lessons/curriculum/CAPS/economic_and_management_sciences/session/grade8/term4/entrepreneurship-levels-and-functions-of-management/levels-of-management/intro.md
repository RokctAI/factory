Today we meet management and the three levels at which it happens. Our business is a family-owned supermarket in Mthatha with sixty people: two top managers who decide where the business is going, six department managers who turn those plans into action, ten supervisors who run the shop floor day by day, and forty-two workers. We define management, see why growing businesses need levels, and compare top, middle and lower management: who they are, what they do, and the strategic, tactical and operational decisions each one makes. Then we read an organisational chart and follow one idea, a home delivery service, from the owner's office down to the delivery van, and spot the same three levels in a school and a municipality.
