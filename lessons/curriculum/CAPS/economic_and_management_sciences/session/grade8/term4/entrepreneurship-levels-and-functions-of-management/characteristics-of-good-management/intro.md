Today we ask what makes a good manager. Two supervisors at a Mthatha supermarket each lead eight people with the same pay, equipment and rules, yet one team loses one worker in a year and the other loses four. We look at the qualities of good managers, such as integrity, fairness, communication and decision-making; the technical, human and conceptual skills needed at each level; the autocratic, democratic and laissez-faire leadership styles and when each fits; a step-by-step method for making decisions; ethical and responsible management under South African law; and how to recognise good management in results, including calculating staff turnover. By the end, you will have a checklist you can use to judge any manager, from a supermarket supervisor to a sports coach or the leader of your own group project.
