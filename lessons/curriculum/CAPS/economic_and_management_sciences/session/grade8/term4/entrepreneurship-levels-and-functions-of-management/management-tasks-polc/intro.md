Today we find out what managers actually do, using four tasks you can remember as POLC: planning, organising, leading and control. Our example is a Mthatha supermarket launching home deliveries. We plan with a SMART goal of four hundred deliveries a month and a budget, organise the van, the driver and the delivery team and see how delegation works, lead the team through clear instructions, communication and motivation, and then control the service. The first month brings only three hundred and twenty deliveries, so we compare results with the standard, find the cause, take corrective action, and watch the service grow to four hundred and fifty deliveries, more than paying its way.
