Hey, if the trial balance looks scary, here is the calm version.
It is just a list of every ledger balance on one date, with a debit column and a credit column.
Assets, expenses and drawings go in the debit column.
Capital, liabilities and income go in the credit column.
List the balance sheet accounts first, then the nominal accounts, in folio order.
Add both columns. For the repair business, both come to one hundred and thirty-four thousand eight hundred and eighty rand.
If they do not match, look for a missing half, a wrong side or swapped digits.
Remember, matching totals do not catch a forgotten transaction or a wrong account on the correct side.
