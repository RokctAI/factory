Today we carry the appliance repair business's April cash journals into the general ledger. We open each account with its folio number and bring down the balances from the end of March, including seventy-four thousand one hundred and eighty rand in the bank. Then we post the cash receipts journal and the cash payments journal: bank totals to the bank account, analysis totals to their own accounts, and every sundry amount separately, each with the date, the contra account and the folio. We balance the bank account step by step, carrying down a favourable balance of eighty thousand one hundred and eighty-five rand, total the other accounts, and check that debits posted equal credits posted.
