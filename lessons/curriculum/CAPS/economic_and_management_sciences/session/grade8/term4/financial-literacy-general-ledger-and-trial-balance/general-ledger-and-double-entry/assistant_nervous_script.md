Hey, if debits and credits make your head spin, here is the calm version.
The ledger is a set of folders, one for each item. Each folder is a T: debit on the left, credit on the right.
Every transaction changes two folders, one on the left and one on the right, with the same amount.
Team debit grows on the left: assets, expenses and drawings.
Team credit grows on the right: capital, liabilities and income.
To make something grow, write on its own side. To make it shrink, use the other side.
Money in: bank on the left. Money out: bank on the right.
In each folder, write the name of the other folder in the details column. That is the contra account.
If you get two lefts or two rights, check again.
Every receipt is debit bank; every payment is credit bank. Then the other account takes the other side.
Open the practice set and try a few. You've got this!
