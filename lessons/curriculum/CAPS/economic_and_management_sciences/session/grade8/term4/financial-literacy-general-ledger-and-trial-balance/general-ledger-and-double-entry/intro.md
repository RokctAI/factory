Today we open the general ledger properly. Using the appliance repair business in April, we see how the ledger is split into balance sheet accounts and nominal accounts, how each account is drawn as a T with a debit side on the left and a credit side on the right, and what goes in the date, details, folio and amount columns. We learn the double-entry principle, that every transaction is recorded with an equal debit and credit, and the rules for which side each kind of account increases on. Then we analyse April's receipts and payments, from additional capital and rent income to a diagnostic tool and the owner's drawings, naming the account debited and the account credited every time.
