Hey, if posting to the ledger feels fiddly, here is the calm version.
The ledger is a filing cabinet with one folder per item. Each folder is a T: left debit, right credit.
First open the folders and bring down last month's balances, on their normal side.
CRJ: bank total on the left of bank; current income and each sundry amount on the right of their own folders.
CPJ: bank total on the right of bank; wages, repair parts and each sundry amount on the left of their own folders.
In every entry write the date, the other folder's name and the folio, CRJ 4 or CPJ 4.
To balance bank: add both sides, find the difference, write it as balance c/d on the smaller side, then bring it down as b/d.
April's bank: one hundred and five thousand five hundred and ten minus twenty-five thousand three hundred and twenty-five is eighty thousand one hundred and eighty-five.
Open the practice set and try a few. You've got this!
