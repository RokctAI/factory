Today we stand in a maize field in the Free State and finish the factors of production with natural resources: the soil, the rain, the borehole water and the sunshine that no factory can make. We separate renewable from non-renewable resources, look at South Africa's mixed inheritance of rich minerals and scarce water, and see how farms, mines and fisheries can use nature sustainably. Then the harvest money arrives, five hundred thousand rand, and we split it between the four factors of production: rent for the leased field, wages and salaries for the workers, interest for the bank and, last of all, profit for the farmer who carried the risk. We see what happens in a drought year, and how all this remuneration flows home to households in the circular flow.
