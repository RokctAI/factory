Hey, if capital and the factors of production felt like economics vocabulary, here is the calm version: one coffee trailer.

Four factors of production: natural resources, like the spot and the beans; labour, the barista; capital, the trailer and the money to buy it; and entrepreneurship, the person who spots the chance and takes the risk.

Their rewards: rent, wages, interest and profit. Profit is what is left over, and it can be a loss.

Fixed capital lasts for years, like the trailer. Working capital keeps the business running day to day, like money for beans and wages.

Own capital is the owner's money: savings, family gifts, profits left in the business. No repayment, no interest, but the owner's savings are at risk.

Borrowed capital is from outsiders: bank loans, overdrafts, supplier credit. It must be repaid with interest, even in a bad month.

Simple interest is amount times rate times time: sixty thousand at twelve percent for a year is seven thousand two hundred rand.
