Today we help a young entrepreneur raise one hundred thousand rand for a mobile coffee trailer outside a busy taxi rank. She has thirty thousand rand saved, her aunt offers ten thousand rand, a bank will lend sixty thousand rand at twelve percent, and a coffee supplier will let her pay for beans after thirty days. We meet the four factors of production, natural resources, labour, capital and entrepreneurship, with their rewards of rent, wages, interest and profit, and separate physical from financial capital and fixed from working capital. Then we compare own capital with borrowed capital, their sources, costs and risks, calculate simple interest on the loan, and finish with gearing and the rules for borrowing wisely.
