Hey, if natural resources and remuneration felt like two separate topics, here is the calm version: one maize farm.

Natural resources are gifts of nature: soil, water, sunshine, minerals, forests and fish.
Renewable ones can come back if used carefully, like sun, wind, rain and well-kept soil.
Non-renewable ones are used up for good, like coal and gold.

Using them sustainably means using nature today without stealing it from tomorrow: protect the soil, save water, rehabilitate mines.

Every factor of production gets paid:
Natural resources earn rent.
Labour earns wages and salaries.
Capital earns interest.
Entrepreneurship earns profit, which is whatever is left over, and it can be a loss.

Farm example: five hundred thousand income, minus rent sixty thousand, wages two hundred and twenty thousand and interest thirty thousand, leaves one hundred and ninety thousand profit.

All these payments become household income, which households spend at the shops. That is the circular flow.
