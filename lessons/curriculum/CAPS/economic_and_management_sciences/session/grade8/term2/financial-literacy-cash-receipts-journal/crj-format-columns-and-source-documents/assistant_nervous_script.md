Hey, if the cash receipts journal looked like a wall of columns, here is the calm version: a notebook with lanes.

On the left: document number, day and who paid.

Then the money lanes. The bank lane gets every receipt, every time. The current income lane is for money earned from the business's services. The sundry lane is for everything else, like capital, a loan, rent income or interest, and each sundry amount must have its account name next to it.

Every receipt runs in two lanes: bank, and the lane that says what kind of money it was. That is the double entry.

The analysis of receipts lane is for days when several amounts are deposited together; the bank lane then shows just the total.

The papers: duplicate receipts, the cash register roll, the bank statement for EFTs, loans and interest, and the deposit slip to check the daily bank total.

Open the practice set and drop a few receipts into the right lanes. You've got this!
