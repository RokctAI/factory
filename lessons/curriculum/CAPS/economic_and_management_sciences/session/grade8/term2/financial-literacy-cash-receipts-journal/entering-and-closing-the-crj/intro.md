Today we fill in a whole month of the cash receipts journal for an appliance repair business, one source document at a time. April brings cash register rolls, a washing machine repair banked together with the till takings, extra capital from the owner, storeroom rent, a customer's EFT and a little interest from the bank. We follow a five-step entering routine, handle the combined deposit with the analysis of receipts column, and see why an EFT for a repair is still current income. Then we close the journal off: total each column, cross-cast to prove that eighteen thousand six hundred and fifty rand of current income plus twelve thousand six hundred and eighty rand of sundry receipts equals the bank total of thirty-one thousand three hundred and thirty rand, rule off, and post the totals to the general ledger.
