Hey, if filling in and closing the CRJ felt like a lot of steps, here is the calm version: two lanes and one check.

For every receipt, in date order: write the document number, the day and who paid. Put the amount in the bank lane. Then put it again in current income if it was earned from the business's services, or in sundry with the account name if it is anything else, like capital, rent or interest.

If two amounts are banked together, list each in the analysis column and put the total in the bank column once.

How the money arrived, cash or EFT, does not change what kind of money it is.

At month-end, add each lane. The check: current income plus sundry must equal bank. In April, eighteen thousand six hundred and fifty plus twelve thousand six hundred and eighty is thirty-one thousand three hundred and thirty.

Rule one line above the totals and two below. Then post: bank total to the debit side, everything else to the credit side.

Open the practice set and try filling in a few receipts. You've got this!
