Hey, if the accounting cycle felt like a long list of steps, here is the calm version: a road with six stops.

Stop one, the transaction: something happens with money.
Stop two, the source document: paper proof, like a receipt or an EFT confirmation.
Stop three, the journal: the diary where transactions are first written, in date order. Money in goes to the CRJ, money out to the CPJ.
Stop four, the ledger: folders, one per item, where the journal totals are posted at month-end.
Stop five, the trial balance: a check that debits equal credits.
Stop six, the financial statements: the report cards.

Remember T, S, J, L, T, F.

Posting means moving totals from the journal into the ledger, always on two sides: money in is debited to bank, money out is credited to bank.

In our repair shop, one hundred and three thousand five hundred and fifty rand in, twenty-nine thousand three hundred and seventy out, leaves seventy-four thousand one hundred and eighty in the bank.

Open the practice set and put the stops in order. You've got this!
