Hey, if job creation and sustainability sound like big national problems, here is the calm version: one small town with four businesses.
South Africa needs jobs: about a third of people who want work cannot find it.
Small businesses, like sole traders and partnerships, are cheap to start and create many jobs close to home, though some are insecure or seasonal.
Big companies create thousands of jobs, indirect jobs at suppliers, and training, but can retrench many at once.
Every business uses natural resources. Sustainable use means using them today without spoiling them for the future.
Farms can save water, factories can use cleaner kilns, mines must repair the land.
Laws, environmental assessments and the carbon tax push businesses in the right direction.
The triple bottom line: people, planet and profit.
A mine's suppliers and the shops where miners spend wages are indirect jobs.
