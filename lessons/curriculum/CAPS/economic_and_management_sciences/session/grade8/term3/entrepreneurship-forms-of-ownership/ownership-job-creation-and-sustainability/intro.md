Today we visit a small town in a dry Limpopo river valley where four businesses work side by side: a sole trader's tyre repair stall, two brothers farming tomatoes in partnership, a private company making bricks, and a public company mining platinum. Each creates jobs and each uses natural resources, but in very different ways. We look at why unemployment matters, why small businesses create so many jobs, what large companies add, what makes a job sustainable, and how each business can protect the water, soil and land it depends on. We meet the laws, the carbon tax and the triple bottom line of people, planet and profit, and finish by comparing how each form of ownership helps the town's future.
