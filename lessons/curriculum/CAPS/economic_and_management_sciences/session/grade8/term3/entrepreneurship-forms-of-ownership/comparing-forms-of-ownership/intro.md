Today four people visit a business advice desk on the same morning: a young man starting a car wash, three electricians going into business together, a family furniture factory planning for the next generation, and a grocery chain that needs two billion rand. Each needs a different form of ownership. We line up the sole trader, the partnership, the private company and the public company and compare them on the same criteria: owners, legal personality, liability, capital, management, profit, continuity, formation and regulation. Then we choose a form for each visitor and justify it properly. By the end you can build the comparison table and defend your choice, which is exactly what this term's project asks.
