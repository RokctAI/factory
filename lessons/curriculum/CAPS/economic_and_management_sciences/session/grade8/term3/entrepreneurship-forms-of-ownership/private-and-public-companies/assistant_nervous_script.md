Hey, if companies sound like a world of suits and stock prices, here is the calm version: one bakery that becomes its own person.
When a business registers as a company with CIPC, it becomes a separate legal person. It owns its things and owes its own debts.
That gives limited liability: owners can lose only what they invested, not their houses.
It also gives continuity: the company carries on if an owner retires or dies.
Owners are shareholders. Directors run the company.
Private company: (Pty) Ltd, at least one director, cannot sell shares to the public.
Public company: Ltd, at least three directors, can sell shares to anyone, often on the JSE, with audited results.
A dividend is a share of profit: one hundred thousand rand over a thousand shares is one hundred rand a share.
