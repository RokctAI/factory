Today the Soweto bakery grows up. The two partners want more shops, more capital and protection for their homes, so they register a private company, and we imagine the day it might become a public company on the Johannesburg Stock Exchange. We learn what incorporation with CIPC means, how a company becomes a separate legal person with limited liability and continuity, and the roles of shareholders, directors and the Memorandum of Incorporation. We compare private companies, (Pty) Ltd, with public companies, Ltd, and finish with numbers: dividends per share, retained profit, and how much a shareholder can really lose. By the end you will know why so many growing businesses become companies.
