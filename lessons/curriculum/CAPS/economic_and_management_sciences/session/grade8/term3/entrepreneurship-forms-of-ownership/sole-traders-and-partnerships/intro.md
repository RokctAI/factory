Today we follow a home baker in Soweto who sells scones and birthday cakes from her own kitchen, and who is then invited by a friend, a trained pastry chef with savings, to open a bakery shop together. That choice is a choice between two forms of ownership: the sole trader and the partnership. We learn what legal personality, unlimited liability and continuity mean, list the characteristics, advantages and disadvantages of each form, see why a written partnership agreement matters, and share a year's profit between two partners in a ratio. By the end you will know who owns, who decides, who keeps the profit and who pays when things go wrong in each of these two simple business forms.
