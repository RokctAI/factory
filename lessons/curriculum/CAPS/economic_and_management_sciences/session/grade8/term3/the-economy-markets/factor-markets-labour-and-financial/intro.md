Today we explore markets, and two that every business depends on: the labour market and the financial market. A growing bakery in Polokwane needs two more assistant bakers and a new oven. To hire, it enters the labour market, where workers sell their skills and the price is the wage. To pay for the oven, it enters the financial market, where banks bring savers and borrowers together and the price is interest. We look at who the buyers, sellers and role players are, from trade unions and the CCMA to the Reserve Bank and the JSE, at labour laws and unemployment, and at the difference between the money market and the capital market.
