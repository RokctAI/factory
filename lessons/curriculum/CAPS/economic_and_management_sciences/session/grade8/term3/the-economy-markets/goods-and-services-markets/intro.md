Today we follow the money that households earn into the markets where they spend it: goods and services markets. Our story is a box of tomatoes, from a farm in Limpopo to a fresh produce market in Johannesburg, and on to a supermarket, a spaza shop, a pavement stall and a shopping app. We sort goods into durable, semi-durable and non-durable, classify markets as local or international, physical or online, wholesale or retail, formal or informal, and use a tomato price schedule to find the equilibrium price where demand meets supply. Then we see how surpluses, shortages and a hailstorm move the price, and what the Consumer Protection Act and the Competition Commission do for you as a consumer.
