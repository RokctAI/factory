Today we finish the accounting equation for cash. In Term 2 we showed money received; now we add money paid out, using the repair business's April CRJ and CPJ. Paying wages, buying a diagnostic tool, repaying a loan and the owner's drawings each shake the equation differently: some reduce owner's equity, some swap one asset for another, and some reduce a liability. We analyse every April payment in an equation table with reasons, then put the whole month on the scale: assets up by eleven thousand two hundred and five rand, owner's equity up by the same amount, and a profit of three thousand seven hundred and five rand that is not the same as the change in the bank.
