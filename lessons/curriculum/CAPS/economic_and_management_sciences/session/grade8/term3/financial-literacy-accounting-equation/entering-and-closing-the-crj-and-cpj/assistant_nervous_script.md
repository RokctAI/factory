Hey, if two journals at once feels like a lot, here is the calm version.
Ask one question of every document: did money come in, or go out?
In goes to the cash receipts journal. Out goes to the cash payments journal.
The bank statement feeds both: customer EFTs and interest earned go in; bank charges go out.
Every line appears twice in its journal: once in the bank column, once in the column that says what it was.
Capital, loans and interest are sundry receipts. Equipment, drawings and loan repayments are sundry payments.
At month-end, total every column and check that the other columns add up to the bank column.
