Hey, if the cash payments journal sounds like a new set of rules, here is the calm version: it is the CRJ's twin, for money going out.
Every payment from the bank gets one line, in date order.
Doc: the EFT number, or B/S for the bank statement. Day, then the payee: who got the money.
Bank column: how much left. Then write it again in its own column: wages, repair parts, or sundry with the account's name.
Source documents: EFT payment confirmations, supplier invoices or slips, payslips, and the bank statement for bank charges and debit orders.
Drawings are not an expense, and equipment is an asset, but both are payments, so both go in the CPJ.
At month-end, add the columns: wages plus parts plus sundry must equal the bank total. In April: twenty-five thousand three hundred and twenty-five rand.
Open the practice set and place a few payments. You've got this!
