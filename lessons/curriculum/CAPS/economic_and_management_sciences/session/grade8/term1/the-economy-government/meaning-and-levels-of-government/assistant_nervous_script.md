Hey, if government felt like a long list of departments and big words, here is the calm version: one street, three governments.

The police on the corner, your ID book and the social grants come from the national government. The country's leader is the President, with the ministers.

The school and the clinic are run by the province. South Africa has nine provinces, and each one is led by a Premier with MECs.

The water in the tap, the refuse truck and the potholes belong to the municipality, led by a mayor and councillors. Your ward councillor is the one closest to your street.

Every government has three jobs: make the laws, do the work and judge disputes. That is Parliament, the executive and the courts, kept apart so that nobody is both player and referee.

Government also buys from businesses, employs teachers and nurses, and pays grants from the taxes it collects.

When a service fails, ask one question first: which sphere is responsible?
