Today we follow one small building company from a tender envelope to a finished classroom and count every way it deals with government. The provincial education department buys its building service with taxpayers' money, the national road carries its bricks, the municipality connects its site to water and power, and SARS collects its company tax, VAT and its workers' PAYE. We draw the four flows between businesses and government, see how each sphere acts as a consumer through procurement and as a producer of infrastructure, and learn why section 217 of the Constitution demands fair tenders. We finish with government as the marshal of the economy, regulating businesses to protect consumers, workers and fair competition, with a worked VAT example.
