Today we follow one household of five through one month and count every flow between that home and government. Mama is a nurse paid by the province, Gogo collects an older person's grant, Thabo attends a no-fee school and the baby is vaccinated free at the clinic. We separate real flows, like labour and services, from money flows, like wages, taxes and grants, and we see how government acts as a consumer when it employs household members and as a producer when it supplies schooling, health care, water and safety. We finish with what households owe in return, from PAYE and VAT to paying the municipal account and using water carefully, and we add up the social wage that makes all of this a tool against poverty.
