Today we spend one week at a small hair salon and follow every rand that moves. A customer taps her card for braids, cash comes in for haircuts, the rent goes out by EFT and the stylists' wages leave on Friday. Each of these is a transaction, an event that changes the business's financial position and can be measured in money, and each one needs a source document as proof. We look at what the bank does for a business, from EFTs and debit orders to bank statements and charges, and why cheques have disappeared. Then we separate cash receipts from cash payments, calculate the week's bank balance, and meet the subsidiary journals, the books of first entry, starting with the cash receipts journal and the cash payments journal.
