Hey, if government spending felt like a list of huge numbers, here is the calm version: follow one rand to six stops.

Stop one, education: teachers' salaries, textbooks, school meals and NSFAS for students.
Stop two, health: clinics, hospitals, nurses and medicines, free at primary health care.
Stop three, social grants: the child support grant, the older person's grant and the disability grant, paid by SASSA.
Stop four, housing: subsidised houses and water and toilets for informal settlements.
Stop five, transport: roads, trains and buses.
Stop six, security: police, courts and the defence force.

There is also a seventh stop, interest on government's debt, and it keeps growing.

Most of the money is divided by law between national government, the nine provinces and the municipalities.

Two kinds of spending to remember: current, used up this year, like salaries; capital, lasting many years, like a new school.

And one big idea: the pot is limited, so more for one stop means less for another. That is opportunity cost.
