Today we follow one rand of tax out of SARS and watch it split into six stops: a classroom, a clinic, a grandmother's grant, a new house, a commuter train and a police station. Those are the six areas of government spending in CAPS: education, health, housing, social grants, transport and security. We separate current, capital and transfer expenditure, see how the Division of Revenue Act shares the money between national government, the nine provinces and the municipalities, and look at what each area actually pays for, from NSFAS to the child support grant. We finish with the hard part of every budget, opportunity cost and rising interest on debt, and with share and per-person calculations of the kind exam papers ask.
