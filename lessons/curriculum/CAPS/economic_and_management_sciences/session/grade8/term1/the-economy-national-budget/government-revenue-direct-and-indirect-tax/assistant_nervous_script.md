Hey, if taxes felt like a pile of strange names, here is the calm version: one payslip and one till slip.

The payslip shows a direct tax. Income tax is taken from your own income and paid to SARS, by you or by your employer through PAYE. Company tax at twenty-seven percent is direct too.

The till slip shows an indirect tax. VAT at fifteen percent is added to the price of things, and the shop pays it to SARS. Fuel levy, sin taxes and customs duty are indirect too.

To add VAT, multiply by fifteen percent. To find VAT hidden in a price, multiply by fifteen over one hundred and fifteen.

Progressive means the richer you are, the bigger the share you pay, like income tax. Regressive means poorer people pay a bigger share of their income, like VAT, which is why basic foods are zero-rated.

The budget is government's plan for money in and money out, presented every February.
