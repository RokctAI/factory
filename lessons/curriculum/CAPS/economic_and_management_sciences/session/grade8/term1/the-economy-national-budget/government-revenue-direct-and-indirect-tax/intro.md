Today we read one payslip and one till slip, and between them we find most of the money the South African government spends. On the payslip, PAYE income tax has gone to SARS before the salary arrives: a direct tax. On the till slip, fifteen percent VAT is hidden in the price of washing powder but not maize meal: an indirect tax. We define the national budget, the financial year and a deficit, list the sources of government revenue, and work through an income tax calculation with brackets and the rebate. Then we calculate VAT both ways, meet excise, fuel and customs duties, and compare progressive, proportional and regressive taxes, finishing with the difference between tax avoidance and tax evasion.
