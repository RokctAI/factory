Hey, if growth and inequality sounded like big newspaper words, here is the calm version: one pie.

The whole economy is a pie, everything the country produces in a year. Its size is GDP. Economic growth means baking a bigger pie, which brings more jobs and more tax money.

Inequality means some people get huge slices and others tiny ones. South Africa's gap is one of the widest in the world, with a Gini number of about zero point six three, where zero is fully equal and one is completely unequal.

The budget grows the pie by building roads, power and schools and by keeping workers healthy and skilled.

It shares the pie by taxing higher incomes more and spending more on poorer households, through grants, free schools, clinics and housing.

The hard part: money is limited, so sharing and growing compete. Spend everything on sharing and nothing is left to build; borrow for both and interest eats next year's pie. And the best way to share the pie is a job.
