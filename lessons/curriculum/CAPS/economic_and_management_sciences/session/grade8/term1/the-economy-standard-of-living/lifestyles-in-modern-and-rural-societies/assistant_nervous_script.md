Hey, if standard of living and lifestyles felt fuzzy, here is the calm version: two cousins.

Standard of living is about what you can buy and use: income, electricity, water, schools, clinics, internet. The city cousin usually has more of these, so a higher standard of living.

Quality of life is bigger. It includes safety, health, clean air, family and community. The village cousin may have a better quality of life in some ways, with her own home, space, neighbours and food from the field.

City life: wages, buying everything, better services, but high rent, transport costs, crowding and crime.

Village life: farming, grants and money sent home, fewer jobs and services, but strong community and lower costs.

When people move from villages to cities, that is urbanisation. No jobs and drought push them out; jobs and services pull them in. The money they send home is called remittances.

Remember: a higher standard of living does not always mean a better quality of life.
