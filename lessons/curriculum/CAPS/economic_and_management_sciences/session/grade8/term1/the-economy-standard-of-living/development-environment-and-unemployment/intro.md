Today we visit a coal town on the Mpumalanga Highveld that powered the whole country for fifty years, and read its hidden bill: grey winter air, acid water seeping from old mines, and, as the mines close, workers whose skills no longer match the jobs on offer. This session covers two CAPS subtopics together, the impact of development on the environment and unemployment, and the productive use of resources. We separate growth from development, list the main kinds of environmental damage, and calculate the unemployment rate using the labour force, comparing the narrow and expanded definitions and four types of unemployment. Then we define sustainable development, read section 24 of the Constitution, climb the waste ladder of reduce, reuse and recycle, and meet the green jobs that link a healthy environment to employment.
