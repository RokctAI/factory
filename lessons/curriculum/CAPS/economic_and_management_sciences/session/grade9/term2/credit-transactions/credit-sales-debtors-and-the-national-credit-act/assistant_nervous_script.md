Hey, credit just means take now, pay later. Here is the calm version.

A credit sale: the goods go now, the money comes later. The customer who owes is a debtor, and debtors are an asset of the business.

Why sell on credit? More sales and loyal customers. The risk: waiting for money, late payers and bad debts. So businesses check customers with a credit bureau, set a credit limit and agree terms like 30 days.

Documents: the invoice for a credit sale, a receipt when the debtor pays, and a monthly statement.

The National Credit Act protects consumers: the right to apply, reasons if refused, the full cost in plain language before signing, and a free credit report once a year. Lenders must check affordability; reckless lending is not allowed. Debt counsellors help people with too much debt.

The cost of credit: 12 payments of R650 is R7 800 for a R6 000 television, R1 800 extra.
