Hey, take a breath. This lesson is three books that fit together neatly.

Book one, the debtors journal: one line per credit invoice. Sales column, cost of sales column. At 50 percent mark-up, cost is selling price times 100 divided by 150. April totals: sales 18 600, cost 12 400.

Book two, the debtors ledger: one page per customer. Invoices go on the debit side, payments from the CRJ go on the credit side, and you keep a running balance. Hilltop owes 4 500, Ace owes 1 800, Sunset owes 2 700.

Book three, the general ledger: only totals arrive here. Debtors control debit 18 600, Sales credit 18 600. Cost of sales debit 12 400, Trading stock credit 12 400. Customer payments of 9 600 are credited to Debtors control, leaving 9 000.

The big check: the debtors list adds to 9 000, and so does Debtors control. Match? Done.

Work one book at a time in the practice set. You've got this!
