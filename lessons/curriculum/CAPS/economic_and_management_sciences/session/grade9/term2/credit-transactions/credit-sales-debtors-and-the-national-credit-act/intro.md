Today we start credit transactions. A credit sale means the customer takes the goods now and pays later, and becomes a debtor of the business, an asset because the money will come in. We weigh up why a business like Ridgeview Hardware sells on credit to builders, what can go wrong, and the credit policy and documents it uses: the credit check, the credit limit, the invoice and the monthly statement. Then we turn to the National Credit Act of 2005, which protects consumers in South Africa through the right to clear information, affordability checks and protection from reckless lending. Finally, we work out what credit really costs: a R6 000 television that costs R7 800 on instalments. This leads into Grade 10 Accounting.
