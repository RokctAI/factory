Today we learn the books a business uses when it sells on credit. The debtors journal records every credit sale from the duplicate invoice, with one column for the selling price and one for the cost price. The debtors ledger keeps a separate page for each customer, so the business knows exactly who owes what. At the end of the month, the totals of the debtors journal are posted to the general ledger, to Debtors control, Sales, Cost of sales and Trading stock. Finally, we prove the work by preparing a debtors list and checking that its total agrees with the Debtors control account. We use Ridgeview Hardware's five credit invoices for April.
