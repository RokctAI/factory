Hey, equilibrium is just where the demand slide meets the supply ladder. Here is the calm version.

Put both schedules together. Muffins: at R12, 120 demanded and 120 supplied. That is the equilibrium price and quantity. On the graph, it is where D and S cross; label it E.

Price too high, like R16, means supplied 160, demanded 80, a surplus of 80. Leftovers push the price down.

Price too low, like R8, means demanded 170, supplied 80, a shortage of 90. Queues push the price up.

Four moves. Demand up: price up, quantity up. Demand down: price down, quantity down. Supply down: price up, quantity down. Supply up: price down, quantity up.

Trick: when demand moves, price and quantity follow it. When supply moves, quantity follows it but price goes the opposite way.
