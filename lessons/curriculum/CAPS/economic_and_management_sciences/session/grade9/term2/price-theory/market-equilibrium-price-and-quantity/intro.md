Today we bring demand and supply together to find the market price. Putting the muffin schedules side by side, we find the equilibrium: the price where the quantity demanded equals the quantity supplied, R12 and 120 muffins a day, where the downward demand curve crosses the upward supply curve. We see what happens when the price is wrong: too high causes a surplus that pushes the price down, too low causes a shortage that pushes it up, until the market clears. Then we work through the four moves: increases and decreases in demand and in supply, and how each changes the equilibrium price and quantity. This is the heart of the price mechanism in Grade 10 Economics.
