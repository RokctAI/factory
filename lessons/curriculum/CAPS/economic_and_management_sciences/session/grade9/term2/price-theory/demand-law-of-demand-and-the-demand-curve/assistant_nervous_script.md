Hey, demand is something you already do every day at the tuck shop. Here is the calm version.

Demand means wanting a good and being able to pay for it, at each price, over a period of time.

The law of demand: price up, quantity demanded down. Price down, quantity demanded up. Other things staying the same.

Why? You switch to substitutes, your money stretches less far, and each extra unit is less satisfying.

Demand schedule: a table of prices and quantities. Muffins: R20, 50; R16, 80; R12, 120; R8, 170; R4, 230.

Demand curve: price up the side, quantity along the bottom. It slopes down from left to right. Label it D.

Own price changes: slide along the curve, a change in quantity demanded. Anything else changes, like income, tastes, related prices or population: the whole curve shifts, right for more, left for less.

Open the practice set and ask on every question: own price, or something else? You've got this!
