Hey, this lesson is about two things: looking after resources and building skills. Here is the calm version.

Renewable resources can grow back if we do not overuse them: water, soil, fish, forests, sun and wind. Non-renewable ones are gone once used: coal, oil, gold, platinum.

Sustainable development: meet today's needs without spoiling the chances of future generations. From a United Nations report in 1987.

Each sector can help. Farms: drip irrigation, crop rotation, no overgrazing. Fishing: quotas. Mines: fix the land afterwards. Factories: save energy, solar panels, recycle, clean waste water. Shops and offices: less paper, less plastic, recycle e-waste.

The three Rs in order: reduce, reuse, recycle.

Skills: primary needs farmers, geologists and mining engineers; secondary needs artisans, engineers and technicians; tertiary needs people skills, accountants, IT and caring skills. Every job needs literacy, numeracy, teamwork and learning.

Routes: university, TVET colleges, learnerships and apprenticeships. Open the practice set and match each skill to its sector. You've got this!
