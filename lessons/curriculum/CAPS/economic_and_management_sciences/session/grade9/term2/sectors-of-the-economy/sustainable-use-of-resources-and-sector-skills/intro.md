Today we look at how each sector can use resources sustainably and what skills each sector needs. We separate renewable resources, like water, soil, fish and sunlight, from non-renewable ones, like coal, gold and platinum, and learn the 1987 United Nations definition of sustainable development: meeting today's needs without spoiling the future's. We look at sustainable practices on farms, at sea, in mines, factories, shops and offices, and why reduce comes before reuse and recycle. Then we match skills to sectors, from geologists and artisans to accountants and nurses, and look at the routes to those skills, including TVET colleges and learnerships, and the new green jobs. These choices shape your subjects for Grade 10.
