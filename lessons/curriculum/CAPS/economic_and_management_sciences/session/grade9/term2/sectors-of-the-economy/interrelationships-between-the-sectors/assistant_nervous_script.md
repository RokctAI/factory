Hey, this lesson has one big idea: the three sectors need each other. Here is the calm version.

Value chain: the steps a product goes through from nature to you. T-shirt: cotton R10, primary; factory sells for R60, adding R50, secondary; shop sells for R120, adding R60, tertiary. 10 + 50 + 60 = 120, the price you pay.

Everyone needs everyone. Farms need tractors and fertiliser from factories, and trucks and banks from services. Factories need raw materials from farms and mines, and shops to sell their goods. Shops need goods from farms and factories.

Roles: primary gives raw materials, food and export earnings; secondary adds value and makes electricity and buildings; tertiary moves and sells goods, provides banking, schools and health, and employs most people.

Ripple effects: when a mine closes, suppliers and local shops suffer too. When a new factory opens, the good news spreads.

Open the practice set and trace each example through all three sectors. You've got this!
