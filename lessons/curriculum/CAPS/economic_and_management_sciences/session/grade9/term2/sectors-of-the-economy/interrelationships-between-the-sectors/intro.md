Today we see how the three sectors of the economy depend on each other. We follow a T-shirt along its value chain, from a cotton farm, through a clothing factory, to a shop, and show that the value added at each stage, R10, R50 and R60, adds up to the R120 you pay. We trace how the primary sector needs machinery and banks, how factories need raw materials and shops, and how services need goods to sell. Then we look at the role of each sector in South Africa, from export earnings to jobs, and at ripple effects, such as what happens to a whole town when a mine closes or load-shedding strikes. These links are central to Grade 10 Business Studies and Economics.
