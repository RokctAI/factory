Hey, three sectors, one question. Here is the calm version.

Primary sector: takes things straight from nature. Farms, mines, fishing boats, forests.

Secondary sector: makes things. Factories, mills, bakeries that bake, builders, power stations like Eskom's.

Tertiary sector: provides services or sells things it did not make. Shops, banks, taxis, hairdressers, hotels, schools, hospitals, cellphone companies.

The one question: what does this business mainly do? Take from nature, make, or serve?

Follow a loaf of bread: wheat farm, primary; mill and bakery, secondary; truck and corner shop, tertiary.

Watch the traps. A bakery that bakes is secondary, but a shop that resells bread is tertiary. Electricity generation is secondary, not a service.

In South Africa the tertiary sector is the biggest, about two-thirds of what the economy produces. But the other two still matter: mining earns much of our export money, and farms and factories employ many people.
