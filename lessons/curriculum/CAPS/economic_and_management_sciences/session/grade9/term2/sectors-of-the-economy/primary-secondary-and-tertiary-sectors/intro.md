Today we sort the whole economy into three sectors. The primary sector takes resources straight from nature: farming, mining, fishing and forestry. The secondary sector makes things: manufacturing, construction, and electricity and water supply. The tertiary sector provides services: shops, transport, banks, tourism, schools and hospitals. We look at the types of businesses in each sector with South African examples, follow a loaf of bread from a wheat field through a mill and a bakery to the corner shop, and learn one question that classifies any business: what does it mainly do? We also see why the tertiary sector is the largest in South Africa. These ideas run straight into Grade 10 Business Studies.
