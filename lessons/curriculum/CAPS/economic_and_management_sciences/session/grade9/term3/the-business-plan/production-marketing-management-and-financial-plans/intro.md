Today we write the four plans at the heart of a business plan. The production plan explains how the product is made or the service delivered: premises, equipment, materials, suppliers, process and quality. The marketing plan applies the four Ps: product, price, place and promotion. The management plan shows who runs the business and who does what. The financial plan adds up the start-up costs of R13 500, shows where the money comes from, and projects a monthly profit of R5 500 for Shine on Wheels. Finally, we find the break-even point: 59 washes a month before the business makes a profit.
