Hey, take a breath. Four plans, one simple question each.

Production plan: how will we do it? Equipment, materials at about R30 a wash, a step-by-step process and a quality checklist.

Marketing plan: the four Ps. Product, price, place, promotion. R120 a wash, we go to the customer, flyers and WhatsApp.

Management plan: who runs it? The owner, a helper at R4 000 a month, and help for the skills that are missing.

Financial plan: what does it cost and will it pay? Start-up R13 500. A month at 120 washes: income R14 400, minus R3 600 products, minus R5 300 fixed costs, profit R5 500.

Break-even: each wash leaves R90. 5 300 divided by 90 is 58.9, so 59 washes. Always round up.

One step at a time. You've got this!
