Today we learn two parts of the business plan that show whether an idea can really work. The SWOT analysis looks inside the business at its strengths and weaknesses, and outside at the opportunities and threats in its environment. We build a SWOT for Shine on Wheels, the mobile car wash, using the results of a survey of 50 car owners, and we turn the four lists into strategies. Then we write the description of the product or service: what is sold, its features and benefits, the target market and the unique selling point that makes customers choose this business over its competitors.
