Hey, breathe. A business plan is just a map for a new business. Here is the short version.

Why write one? To think before spending money, to get a loan, and to check progress later.

The sections, in order: cover page and contents, executive summary (written last, read first), business description, product or service, market analysis with a SWOT, marketing plan, production plan, management plan, financial plan, appendices.

Vision: the big dream. Mission: what we do now, for whom and how. Goals: SMART, which means specific, measurable, achievable, realistic and time-bound. "Wash 120 cars a month by month six" is SMART.

Sole trader: one owner, unlimited liability. Partnership: two or more owners. Company: limited liability, registered with CIPC.

Presentation: clear headings, tables for numbers, the same figures everywhere, and no exaggeration.

Plan on paper first; it is far cheaper than learning by losing money. Open the practice set and write a vision, a mission and three SMART goals for your own idea. You've got this!
