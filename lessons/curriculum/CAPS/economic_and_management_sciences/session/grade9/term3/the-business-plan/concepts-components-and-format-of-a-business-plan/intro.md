Today we begin planning a business by learning what a business plan is and how it is set out. A business plan is a written document that explains a business idea and how the entrepreneur will make it succeed. It helps the entrepreneur think, obtain finance, measure progress and communicate. We go through the components in order, from the cover page and executive summary to the marketing, production, management and financial plans and the appendices. We define the vision, mission and SMART goals, and the legal forms of ownership. Finally, we look at the format, language and presentation that make a plan convincing, using a mobile car-wash business as our example.
