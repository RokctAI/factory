Hey, breathe. This lesson is just four boxes and one good sentence.

Two boxes are inside your business: strengths and weaknesses. You can change those. Two boxes are outside: opportunities and threats. You cannot change those, only prepare for them. Test: if the business closed tomorrow, would it still be there? Then it is outside.

Shine on Wheels' survey: 32 of 50 car owners said yes. 32 divided by 50, times 100, is 64 percent.

Then mix the boxes. Strengths grab opportunities. Strengths block threats. Using 20 litres instead of 150 means water restrictions hurt less.

For the product: a feature is a fact, a benefit is what the customer gets. Customers buy benefits. And your USP is the one sentence that says why they should choose you.

Try it with your own idea. You've got this!
