Hey, breathe. This is the debtors lesson in a mirror.

Creditors journal: every credit purchase, one line per supplier invoice. Full amount in Creditors control, the same amount again under what was bought.

September: control 20 000. Stock 15 000, stationery 600, equipment 3 500, advertising 900. They add to 20 000.

Creditors ledger: a page per supplier. Invoices on the credit side, payments on the debit side. BuildMart owes 5 800.

General ledger: Creditors control credit 20 000, debit 5 000 for the payment. Balance 15 000. Supplier pages add to 15 000. Match!
