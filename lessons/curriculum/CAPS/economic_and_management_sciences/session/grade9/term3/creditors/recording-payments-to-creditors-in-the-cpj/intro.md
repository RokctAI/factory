Today we complete the creditors cycle by paying the suppliers. Payments to creditors are recorded in the cash payments journal, in the Bank column and a new Creditors control column, from the EFT proof of payment or cheque counterfoil. We record Ridgeview Hardware's October payments of R19 600, of which R13 500 went to creditors, and cross-cast the journal. Then we post each payment to the debit side of the creditor's account in the creditors ledger and the total to Creditors control. Finally, we reconcile: creditors fall from R15 000 to R11 100, and the creditors list agrees.
