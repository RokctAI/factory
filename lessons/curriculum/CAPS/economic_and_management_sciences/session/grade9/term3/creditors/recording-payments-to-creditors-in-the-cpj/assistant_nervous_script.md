Hey, breathe. Paying a supplier is just money out.

Money out goes in the CPJ. A payment to a creditor goes in Bank and in Creditors control. Never in Trading stock, because that stock was already recorded in the CJ.

Stock bought and paid for today is different: that one goes in the Trading stock column.

October: Bank 19 600. Creditors 13 500, wages 2 800, cash stock 3 300. They add up.

Control: 15 000 plus 9 600 minus 13 500 is 11 100. Pages add to 11 100. Match.
