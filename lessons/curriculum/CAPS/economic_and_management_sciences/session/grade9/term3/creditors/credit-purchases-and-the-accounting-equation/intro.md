Today we turn the debtors story around. When a business buys on credit, it takes the goods now and pays the supplier later, and that supplier becomes a creditor: a liability of the business. We follow the accounting cycle for credit purchases, from the supplier's original invoice to the creditors journal, the creditors ledger and the general ledger. Then we analyse four of Ridgeview Hardware's September transactions in the accounting equation: stock, stationery and equipment bought on credit, and a payment to a supplier. We prove the equation balances, with assets up R6 500, owner's equity down R600 and liabilities up R7 100.
