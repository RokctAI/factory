Today we learn the books for credit purchases. The creditors journal records every credit purchase from the supplier's original invoice, with a Creditors control column for the total and analysis columns for trading stock, stationery, equipment and sundry accounts. We record Ridgeview Hardware's six credit purchases for September, totalling R20 000, and cross-cast the journal. Then we post each invoice to the supplier's account in the creditors ledger and the totals to the general ledger. Finally, we prepare a creditors list of R15 000 and prove that it agrees with Creditors control.
