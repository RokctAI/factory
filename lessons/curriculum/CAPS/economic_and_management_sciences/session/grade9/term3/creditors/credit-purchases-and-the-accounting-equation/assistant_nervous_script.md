Hey, breathe. Creditors are just debtors, turned around.

Debtors owe us: asset. We owe creditors: liability.

Buying on credit always makes liabilities go up. Then ask what you bought. Stock or equipment: assets go up. Stationery: it is an expense, so owner's equity goes down.

Paying a creditor: bank goes down and liabilities go down. No expense.

Ridgeview's four transactions: assets plus 6 500, owner's equity minus 600, liabilities plus 7 100. Minus 600 plus 7 100 is 6 500. Balanced.

One line at a time. You've got this!
