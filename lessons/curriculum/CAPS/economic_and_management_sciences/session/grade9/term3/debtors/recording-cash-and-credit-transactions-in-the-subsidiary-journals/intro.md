Today we take a whole month of a sole trader's transactions, cash and credit mixed together, and record each one in the correct subsidiary journal. Ridgeview Hardware has twelve transactions in August. We sort them with three questions: did money come in, did money go out, or was something sold on credit? Then we record them in the cash receipts journal, the cash payments journal and the debtors journal, total and cross-cast each journal, and check the results: R22 600 received, R9 450 paid out, R7 500 sold on credit and debtors down from R12 500 to R10 400.
