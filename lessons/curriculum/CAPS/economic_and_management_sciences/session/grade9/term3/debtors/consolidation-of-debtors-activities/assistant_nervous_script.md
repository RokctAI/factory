Hey, take a breath. This is a lesson you already know most of.

Credit sale: invoice, debtors journal, debit the customer's page. Payment: receipt, cash receipts journal, credit the customer's page.

July for Ridgeview: debtors owed 8 400 at the start. The DJ adds 12 000 of credit sales, cost 8 000. The CRJ shows 7 900 received from debtors.

Customer pages: Hilltop 5 400, Ace 4 200, Sunset 2 900. Total 12 500.

Control account: 8 400 plus 12 000 minus 7 900 is 12 500. They agree.

And the business habit: statements every month, credit limits, and follow up late payers. One page at a time. You've got this!
