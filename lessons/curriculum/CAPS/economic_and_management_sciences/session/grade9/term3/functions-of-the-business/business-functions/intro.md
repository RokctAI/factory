Today we study the nine business functions that CAPS names: general management, administration, purchasing, production, marketing, financing, human resources, public relations and risk management. We explain what each function does, using a small furniture workshop as our example. We see how management plans, organises, leads and controls, how purchasing follows the five rights, how production turns inputs into outputs, and how marketing uses the four Ps. We look at how finance obtains and manages money, how HR follows labour law, how public relations builds goodwill, and how risk management avoids, reduces, transfers or accepts risk. Finally, we see why a small business owner must often perform all nine functions alone, and why one weak function can sink a strong business.
