Hey, no stress. This lesson has one big idea: the business functions are a team.

Each function needs the others. Marketing wins the order, purchasing buys materials, HR supplies workers, production makes the goods, finance pays the bills, administration keeps the records, risk management protects, public relations builds the good name, and general management coordinates.

An organogram is a diagram of boxes and lines. The lines show the chain of command. The span of control is how many people report to one manager.

When one function fails, the damage spreads. Late wood means late desks, unhappy customers, fewer orders and less cash.

Small business: the owner does nearly everything and may outsource some jobs. Large business: separate departments with specialists, but they must keep talking.

Quick check: nine workers report to a foreman. Span of control? Nine.

Open the practice set, draw an organogram and trace one failure through the business. Every function counts, even the quiet ones. You've got this!
