Hey, relax. A business is just nine jobs that someone has to do. Here they are.

General management: plan, organise, lead, control.
Administration: records and information.
Purchasing: buy the right quality, quantity, price, time and supplier.
Production: turn inputs into finished goods, and check quality.
Marketing: the four Ps, product, price, place and promotion.
Financing: get money and manage it, so there is cash when bills arrive.
Human resources: hire, train, pay and treat workers fairly under labour law.
Public relations: a good name with the public, which is not the same as advertising.
Risk management: spot what could go wrong, then avoid, reduce, transfer or accept it.

Quick test: no timber? Purchasing. Fire with no insurance? Risk management. Bad newspaper story? Public relations.

In a small business, one person does all nine. Remember: functions work together, so one weak function can sink a strong business. Open the practice set and match each problem to its function. You've got this!
