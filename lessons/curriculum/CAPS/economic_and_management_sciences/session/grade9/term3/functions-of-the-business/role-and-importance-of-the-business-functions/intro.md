Today we look at why the nine business functions matter and how they work together. We see how the functions depend on each other, so that a business is only as strong as its weakest function, and how management balances conflicting goals between departments. We learn to read and draw an organogram, showing levels of management, the chain of command and the span of control. We then explore what happens when each function fails, and how failures spread. Finally, we compare how small and large businesses perform the functions, and why planning for every function matters when you start a business of your own.
