Today we draw the circular flow diagram of a closed economy. There are two rivers in it, flowing in opposite directions. The real river carries factors of production from households to businesses, and goods and services from businesses to households. The money river carries wages, rent, interest and profit to households, and spending back to businesses. We build the diagram step by step, add the government with its taxes, salaries, purchases and grants, and learn to label every arrow. Then we look at leakages, savings and taxes, and injections, investment and government spending, and calculate why the flow stays steady when they are equal. This diagram is a core tool in Grade 10 Economics.
