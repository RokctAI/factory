Hey, there are only three players to learn today, and each has a simple job. Here is the calm version.

Closed economy: no trade with other countries. Three participants: households, businesses, government.

Households: families. They own the factors of production, their work, land, savings and business ideas. They sell these and receive wages, rent, interest and profit. Then they spend that income on goods and services.

Businesses: the makers and sellers. They hire workers, rent buildings and borrow money, and they sell goods and services. Households and businesses are on opposite sides in each market.

Government: collects taxes like income tax and VAT, pays salaries to teachers and nurses, buys textbooks and builds roads, pays social grants, and makes rules like the minimum wage.

A grant is a transfer payment: money given with nothing given back.

The trick for any story: who pays, who receives, and what do they get in return?

Open the practice set and use those three questions on every item. You've got this!
