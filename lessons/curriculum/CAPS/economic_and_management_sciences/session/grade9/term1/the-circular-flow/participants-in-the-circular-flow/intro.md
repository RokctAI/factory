Today we meet the participants in the circular flow of a closed economy. A closed economy is one that does not trade with other countries, and it has three participants: households, businesses and the government. Households own the factors of production and are the consumers. Businesses produce goods and services and hire the factors. The government collects taxes, buys goods and services, employs teachers and nurses, pays social grants and makes the rules. We follow a single rand coin around a day to see that every rand spent by one participant becomes income for another, which is why the flow is circular. Then we practise spotting who pays whom, and for what. The full circular flow returns in Grade 10 Economics.
