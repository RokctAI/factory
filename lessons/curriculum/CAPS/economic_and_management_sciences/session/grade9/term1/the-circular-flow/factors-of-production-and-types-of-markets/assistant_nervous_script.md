Hey, lots of new words today, but they fit into two neat lists. Here is the calm version.

Four factors of production: natural resources, like land, minerals and water; labour, the work people do; capital, the machines, tools and buildings, not money; and entrepreneurship, the person who puts it all together and takes the risk.

Four rewards, in the same order: rent, wages or salaries, interest, profit. Chant it: rent, wages, interest, profit.

Profit is what is left over. Sales R50 000, minus rent R8 000, wages R22 000, interest R2 000 and ingredients R12 000, leaves R6 000.

Three markets: goods and services market, where households buy from businesses; factor market, where households sell their labour and other factors to businesses; financial market, where people save and borrow, money market for short-term and capital market for long-term.

Markets can be physical or online, local, national or international, formal or informal.
