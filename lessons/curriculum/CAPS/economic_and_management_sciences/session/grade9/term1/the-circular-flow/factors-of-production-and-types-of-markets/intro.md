Today we meet the building blocks of every economy. There are four factors of production: natural resources, labour, capital and entrepreneurship, and every business, from a vetkoek stand to a platinum mine, uses all four. Each factor earns its own reward: rent, wages and salaries, interest and profit, and we will calculate a bakery's profit as the reward that is left for the entrepreneur. Then we look at markets: the goods and services market, where households buy; the factor market, where households sell their labour; and the financial market, where money is saved and borrowed. We also sort markets by place, reach and type. These ideas are the parts of the circular flow, and they come back in Grade 10 Economics.
