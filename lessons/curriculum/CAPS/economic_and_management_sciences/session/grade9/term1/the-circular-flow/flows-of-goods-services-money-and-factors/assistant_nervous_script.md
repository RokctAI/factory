Hey, the circular flow diagram looks busy, but it is built from one simple rule. Here is the calm version.

The rule: real things and money always move in opposite directions. Work goes from households to businesses; wages come back. Goods go from businesses to households; spending goes back.

The boxes: households on the left, businesses on the right, goods and services market at the top, factor market at the bottom, government in the middle.

The government: taxes come in from households and businesses; salaries, grants and spending go out. Taxes and grants are direct arrows, not through a market.

Leakages take money out: savings and taxes. Injections put money in: investment and government spending.

Numbers: income R1 000 million, tax 200, saving 100, so spending 700. Investment 100 plus government 200 makes 300, the same as the leaks. 700 + 100 + 200 = 1 000. Steady flow.
