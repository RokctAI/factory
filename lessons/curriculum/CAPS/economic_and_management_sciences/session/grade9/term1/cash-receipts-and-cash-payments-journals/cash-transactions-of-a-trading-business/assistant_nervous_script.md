Hey, a trading business just buys goods and sells them for more. Everything today follows from that. Here is the calm version.

Trading stock is an asset. Buying it is a swap: stock up, bank down. No expense yet.

Selling has two halves. Selling price: bank up, owner's equity up for sales. Cost price: trading stock down, owner's equity down for cost of sales. The gap is gross profit.

Mark-up is a percentage of cost. Cost R600, 50 percent mark-up: 600 + 300 = 900.

Going backwards: selling price times 100, divided by 100 plus the mark-up. R1 200 at 50 percent: 1 200 × 100 ÷ 150 = 800. Not 600!

Owner takes stock home: drawings, at cost price, because nothing was sold and no profit was earned.

Source documents: cash register roll for cash sales, duplicate receipt for other money in, EFT proof or bank statement for money out.
