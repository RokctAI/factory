Today we revise the accounting equation from Grade 8 before we start the books of a trading business. The equation is assets equal owner's equity plus liabilities: everything the business has was paid for either by the owner or by outsiders. We analyse seven cash transactions of a new stationery shop in four steps: which accounts, which element, increase or decrease, and the reason. We see how income raises owner's equity, how expenses and drawings lower it, and why drawings are never an expense. Then we add up both sides and prove that R62 500 of assets equals R47 500 of owner's equity plus R15 000 of liabilities. This is the base for everything in Grade 10 Accounting.
