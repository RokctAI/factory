Today we complete the cash payments journal, the CPJ, the book where a trading business records every rand it pays out. The columns are document, day, name of payee, folio, bank, trading stock, wages and sundry accounts. Using Ridgeview Hardware's payments for March, we record stock bought, wages, rent, new shelving, the owner's drawings, the telephone account and bank charges from the bank statement, and we learn two questions that find every payment's home. Then we total and cross-cast the journal and combine it with last lesson's CRJ: receipts of R140 250 minus payments of R56 590 leave R83 660 in the bank. This is the second book of first entry in Grade 10 Accounting.
