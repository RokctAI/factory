Hey, the CRJ looks like a big table, but every line follows one simple pattern. Here is the calm version.

The CRJ is the notebook for money coming in. Money going out goes in the CPJ, never here.

Every line: document number, day, who paid, and the amount in the bank column.

Then one more column. Cash sales go in sales, with the cost of sales next to it. Everything else, capital, rent income, a loan, interest, goes in sundry with its name written next to it.

Cost of sales at 50 percent mark-up: divide by 150, times 100. R12 000 sales means R8 000 cost.

At month end, add every column. Then cross-cast: sales plus sundry must equal bank. For Ridgeview: 27 600 + 112 650 = 140 250. Leave cost of sales out of the check.

Source documents: CRR for cash sales, duplicate receipt for other money, bank statement for interest or EFTs.

Open the practice set and do one line at a time. You've got this!
