Today we move from a service business to a trading business, one that buys goods and sells them again for more. We learn the language of trading: trading stock, cost price, mark-up, selling price, cost of sales and gross profit. We calculate selling prices from cost, and the harder one, cost from selling price, where dividing by 150 instead of taking half off makes all the difference. We meet the source documents for cash transactions, from the cash register roll to the EFT proof of payment. Then we show every cash sale as two effects on the accounting equation, one at selling price and one at cost. These ideas run straight into the cash journals and into Grade 10 Accounting.
