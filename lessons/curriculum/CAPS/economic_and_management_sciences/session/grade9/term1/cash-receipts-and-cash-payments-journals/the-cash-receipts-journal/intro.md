Today we complete the cash receipts journal, the CRJ, the book where a trading business records every rand it receives. We meet the columns from left to right: document, day, details, folio, analysis of receipts, bank, sales, cost of sales and sundry accounts. Using Ridgeview Hardware's receipts for March, with a mark-up of 50 percent on cost, we record capital, cash sales with their cost of sales, rent income, a bank loan and interest. Then we total each column and cross-cast: sales of R27 600 plus sundry receipts of R112 650 must equal the bank total of R140 250, with cost of sales left out of the check. This journal is the starting point of the accounting cycle in Grade 10.
