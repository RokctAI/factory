Hey, the global economy sounds enormous, but it boils down to a few simple ideas. Here is the calm version.

Globalisation: countries joined up through trade, money, technology and people. One cellphone can pass through ten countries.

Why trade? Countries have different resources, climates and skills. Each does what it is good at and swaps. That is specialisation.

Exports: we sell to other countries, money comes in. Imports: we buy from other countries, money goes out. Exports bigger than imports is a surplus; imports bigger is a deficit.

Exchange rate: the price of a dollar in rand. At R18 per dollar, a 50 dollar item costs 900 rand. At R20 it costs 1 000 rand. Weak rand: imports dearer, exports cheaper for foreigners.

Organisations: WTO makes trade rules; SACU and AfCFTA cut tariffs between neighbours; South Africa is in BRICS.

Good: jobs from exports, more choice, investment. Bad: local factories can close, and shocks overseas hit us too.
