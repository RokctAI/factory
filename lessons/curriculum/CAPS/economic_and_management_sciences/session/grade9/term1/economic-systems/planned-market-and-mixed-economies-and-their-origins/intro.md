Today we look at the three major economic systems and where they came from. Every society faces the same problem: wants are unlimited, resources are scarce, so somebody must decide what to produce, how to produce it and for whom. In a planned economy the state decides, an idea that grew from Karl Marx in 1848 and was first put into practice in the Soviet Union. In a market economy buyers and sellers decide through prices, the idea Adam Smith described in 1776 as the invisible hand. In a mixed economy, like South Africa's, private businesses and the state both play a part. We will place real countries on a line from planned to market and see where South Africa sits. Grade 10 Economics opens with exactly these ideas.
