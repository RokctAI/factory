Today we look at the global economy: how the countries of the world are joined together through trade, investment, technology and people. We follow a cellphone through about ten countries to see globalisation in action, and we ask why countries trade at all: because resources, climate and skills differ, so specialising and swapping makes everyone better off. We work out a balance of trade, and we use exchange rates to see why a weaker rand makes imports more expensive but helps South African exporters. We meet the WTO, SACU, SADC, the AfCFTA and BRICS, and we weigh up who wins and who loses from globalisation in South Africa. These ideas run straight into Grade 10 Economics and Business Studies.
