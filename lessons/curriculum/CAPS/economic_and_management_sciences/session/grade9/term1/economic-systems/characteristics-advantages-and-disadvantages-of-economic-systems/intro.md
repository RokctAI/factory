Today we compare the three economic systems: what makes each one a planned, a market or a mixed economy, and what is good and bad about each. We use a table with five rows for every system: who owns the resources, who decides, how prices are set, what the motive is and how big the state's role is. Then we look at the advantages and disadvantages and discover a pattern: the strengths of the market economy, efficiency and choice, are the weak spots of the planned economy, and the strengths of the planned economy, equality and security, are the weak spots of the market. The mixed economy, which South Africa has, tries to get both. We also practise writing full-sentence answers with reasons, which is what Grade 10 Economics expects.
