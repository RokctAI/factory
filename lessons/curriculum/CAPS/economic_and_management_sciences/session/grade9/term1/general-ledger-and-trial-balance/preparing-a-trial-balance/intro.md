Today we prepare a trial balance: a list of every account balance in the general ledger, in two columns, debit and credit, that must add up to the same total. Using Ridgeview Hardware's ledger at the end of March, we list the balance sheet accounts and then the nominal accounts, put each balance in the correct column and prove that both columns total R140 250. We learn which errors make a trial balance fail and which errors it cannot see, and we practise hunting for a difference, including the divide-by-nine trick for swapped digits. Finally, we read the trial balance to find a gross profit of R9 200 and a first-month net loss. This is where Grade 10 financial statements begin.
