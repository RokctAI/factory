Hey, posting looks fiddly, but it follows one mirror pattern. Here is the calm version.

From the CRJ, money in: bank is debited with the total receipts, R140 250. Everything else is credited: sales, and each sundry line to its own account, like capital and rent income.

From the CPJ, money out: bank is credited with total payments, R56 590. Everything else is debited: trading stock, wages, and each sundry line to its own account.

Cost of sales goes to two accounts: debit cost of sales, credit trading stock.

Details column: write the name of the other account. Folio: CRJ1 or CPJ1.

Balancing: find the bigger side, take the difference, write balance c/d on the smaller side so both totals match, underline twice, then balance b/d on the bigger side on the first of the next month. Bank: 140 250 - 56 590 = 83 660. Trading stock: 29 000 - 18 400 = 10 600.

Open the practice set and post one journal at a time. You've got this!
