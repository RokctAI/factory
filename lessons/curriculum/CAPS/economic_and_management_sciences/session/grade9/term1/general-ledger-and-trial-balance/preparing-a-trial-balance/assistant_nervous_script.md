Hey, the trial balance is just a register of accounts with two columns that must agree. Here is the calm version.

List every account from the ledger: balance sheet accounts first, then nominal accounts, in folio order.

Write only the balance. Debit column: assets, expenses and drawings. Credit column: capital, liabilities and income.

Ridgeview at the end of March: debits total R140 250, credits total R140 250. Balanced.

If they do not agree, hunt: find the difference; look for that amount; look for half of it, in case something is in the wrong column; check if it divides by 9, for swapped digits like 850 written as 580.

Even a balanced trial balance can hide errors: a forgotten transaction, or the right amount in the wrong account of the same kind.

Bonus: sales 27 600 minus cost of sales 18 400 is a gross profit of 9 200. After other income and expenses, a loss of 6 240 in the first month.

Open the practice set and tick each account as you list it. You've got this!
