Today we post Ridgeview Hardware's March cash journals to the general ledger and balance the accounts. Posting means carrying each journal total and each sundry amount to its account, on the correct side, with the date, the folio of the journal and, in the details column, the name of the other account in the double entry. From the CRJ, bank is debited with total receipts of R140 250 and the other accounts are credited; from the CPJ, bank is credited with total payments of R56 590 and the other accounts are debited. Then we balance the bank account to R83 660 and trading stock to R10 600 using balance carried down and brought down. These are the core ledger skills of Grade 10 Accounting.
