Hey, breathe. This lesson is about two sides of one coin.

Good effects on a business: one partner to negotiate with, clear ways to handle complaints, happier workers who stay longer, safer workplaces and more training.

Bad effects: higher wage costs, strikes that stop production, rigid rules, and sometimes conflict or violence, which is never legal.

The numbers: 6 percent of R8 000 is R480. Times 40 workers is R19 200 a month. A 10-day strike at R60 000 a day loses R600 000, and each worker loses R4 000.

Sustainable growth is growth that lasts: fair to people, kind to the environment, stable. Unions help through fair wages, skills, safety and talks at NEDLAC.

The golden rule: wages and productivity should rise together. If both rise 6 percent, the cost of each product stays the same.

Remember: unions are not automatically good or bad. It depends on how both sides behave.

In an essay, always give both sides, explain each point, and finish with a balanced conclusion. You've got this!
