Today we look at what trade unions mean for businesses and for the economy as a whole. Unions can help a business: one organised partner to negotiate with, clear grievance procedures, better morale, safer workplaces and more training. They can also hurt it: higher wage costs, rigid rules and strikes that stop production, like the five-month platinum strike of 2014. We use a canning factory with 40 workers to calculate the cost of a 6 percent wage increase and of a 10-day strike. Then we see how unions can support sustainable growth through fair wages, skills and social dialogue at NEDLAC, and why wages and productivity must rise together.
