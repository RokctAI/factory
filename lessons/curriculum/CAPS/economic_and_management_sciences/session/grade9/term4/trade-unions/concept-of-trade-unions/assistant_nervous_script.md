Hey, breathe. A trade union is simply workers standing together.

One voice is easy to ignore; thousands are not. That is bargaining power.

What unions do: negotiate wages and conditions, push for safety, represent members, and speak for workers nationally through federations like COSATU, FEDUSA, NACTU and SAFTU.

Key dates: 1973 Durban strikes, 1979 black unions recognised, 1985 COSATU, 1995 Labour Relations Act.

The maths: 7 percent of R8 000 is R560, so the new wage is R8 560.

Strike rules: CCMA first, then 30 days, then 48 hours' notice. Protected, but no work, no pay.
