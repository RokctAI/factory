Hey, breathe. Posting is just copying, carefully, to two places.

Every amount goes on one debit side and one credit side. In the details, write the other account. In the folio, write the journal page.

Cash journals: Bank gets the totals, R21 000 in and R16 700 out. Bank ends at R18 300.

Credit journals: DJ sales R7 500 to Debtors control, CJ R11 000 to Creditors control.

Then the people pages. Debtors ledger: Mzansi R4 500, Greenway R3 000, total R7 500. Creditors ledger: Bike Parts R7 000, Trail R4 000, total R11 000. Both match the control accounts.

Trial balance: debits R95 500, credits R95 500. Balanced!

If yours does not balance, look at the difference. If it equals one amount, that amount was probably posted only once. If it divides by 9, two digits may be swapped.

Balance sheet accounts first, then nominal accounts.
