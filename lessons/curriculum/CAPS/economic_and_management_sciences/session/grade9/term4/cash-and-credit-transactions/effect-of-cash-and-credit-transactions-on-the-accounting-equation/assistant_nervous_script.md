Hey, breathe. The accounting equation is a scale that always balances: assets = owner's equity + liabilities.

Ask three questions for every transaction: which accounts, what type, up or down?

Cash sale of R9 000: bank up 9 000, stock down 6 000, owner's equity up 3 000.

Credit sale: the same, but debtors go up instead of the bank. Profit is earned when you sell, not when you are paid.

Buying stock on credit: stock up, creditors up. No profit effect.

Debtor pays: bank up, debtors down. Not income! Paying a creditor: bank down, creditors down. Not an expense!

Expenses like wages, rent and bank charges: bank down, owner's equity down.

Kasi Cycles starts November at 70 000 = 62 000 + 8 000 and ends at 73 800 = 62 800 + 11 000. Owner's equity grew by R800, which is the profit: 7 500 gross profit minus 6 700 expenses.

Check the balance after every line, and you will never get lost. You've got this!
