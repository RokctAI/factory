Today we finish Kasi Cycles' November books. We post the four journals, the CRJ, CPJ, DJ and CJ, to the general ledger, writing the other account in the details and the journal page as the folio. We post the individual lines to the debtors and creditors ledgers and check that the lists of debtors and creditors, R7 500 and R11 000, agree with the control accounts. Then we prepare the trial balance on 30 November, with debit and credit totals of R95 500 each, check the profit of R800 against the accounting equation, and learn how the difference in an unbalanced trial balance can point to the error.
