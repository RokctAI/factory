Hey, breathe. Four journals, four questions.

Money came into the bank? CRJ. Money went out? CPJ. Stock sold on credit? DJ. Bought on credit? CJ.

One transaction, one journal. Never two.

The traps: stock bought and paid for at once goes in the CPJ under Trading stock. Paying a supplier for an old purchase goes in the CPJ under Creditors control. A debtor paying goes in the CRJ under Debtors control, with no sales and no cost of sales.

Cost price at 50 percent mark-up: times 100, divided by 150. R9 000 becomes R6 000.
