Today we record a full month of cash and credit transactions in the four subsidiary journals: the cash receipts journal, the cash payments journal, the debtors journal and the creditors journal. We use the fourteen November transactions of Kasi Cycles, a bicycle shop in Soweto with a mark-up of 50 percent on cost. First we sort each transaction with four questions: money in, money out, sold on credit, or bought on credit? Then we enter each one in its journal, total the columns and cross-cast the cash journals. Finally we check the month: R22 500 of sales from the CRJ and DJ together, and R13 000 of stock bought for cash and on credit.
