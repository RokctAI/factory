## Question (3 marks)

(Context: Mrs Xulu used recycled materials to make a tree decoration to hang on the wall — a triangular tree pattern built from small coloured circles pasted onto a cardboard template of three sections: Part A at the top, Part B in the middle and Part C at the bottom.) In Part A, there are seven rows of circles. The first row consists of one circle at the top and thereafter, each successive row has an additional circle added to complete the rest of the rows. Calculate the total number of circles in Part A.

## Method

sum of arithmetic pattern

## Memo working

Row 1 has one circle and every row below it has one more circle than the row above, so the seven rows contain 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 7 circles -> 28. (Memo option 2: use the sum shortcut for the pattern — 7(1 + 7)/2 = 28.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

28
