## Question (7 marks)

Miecke purchased prepaid electricity for R1 300,00, of which R1 130,43 is the electricity amount excluding VAT (per her till slip). A partial residential electricity tariff table (excluding VAT) is shown (TABLE 3: Block 1, consumption 0-350 kWh, tariff R2,19 per kWh; Block 2, consumption more than 350 kWh, tariff R2,91 per kWh). Determine the number of kWh units Miecke purchased.

## Method

block tariff reverse calculation

## Memo working

Work out how far the money stretches, block by block. Amount for Block 1 (the first 350 kWh): 350 kWh x R2,19 = R766,50 (1MA, 1CA). Money left over for Block 2: R1 130,43 - R766,50 = R363,93 (1MCA). Units bought in Block 2 at the higher tariff: R363,93 ÷ R2,91 = 125,0618557 kWh (1MCA, 1CA). Total units = 350 kWh + 125,0618557 kWh = 475,06 kWh (1MCA adding, 1CA simplification). (Memo alternative with VAT-inclusive tariffs: R2,19 x 1,15 = R2,5185 and R2,91 x 1,15 = R3,3465; 350 x R2,5185 = R881,475; R1 300 - R881,475 = R418,525; 418,525 ÷ 3,3465 = 125,06 kWh; total 475,06 kWh. NPR)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

475,06 kWh
