## Question (2 marks)

For Lee's 21st birthday party the DJ options (TABLE 4) were: DJ Rain — R4 000 for 5 hours or less, thereafter R1 250 per hour; DJ 5-Star — R1 000 per hour or part thereof; DJ Cool — one fixed price for the whole party. Lee's parents chose DJ 5-Star. Give ONE reason why Lee's parents did not choose DJ Cool.

## Method

reasoned opinion

## Memo working

Compare the pricing structures: DJ Cool's price is a flat, fixed amount no matter how long the party lasts, while the other DJs charge according to time. If the party ends early, a flat rate means paying for hours of music that were never played, which is not economical. (2O correct reason)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

He charges a flat/fixed rate, which is not economical if the party ends early. OR He has a bad reputation.
