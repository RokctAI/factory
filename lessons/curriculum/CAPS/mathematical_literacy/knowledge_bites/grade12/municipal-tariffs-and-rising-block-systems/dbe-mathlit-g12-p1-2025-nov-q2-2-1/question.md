## Question (3 marks)

TABLE 4 shows Metrobus fares in Johannesburg for three passenger categories: for a single daily trip an adult (15-59 years) pays R14,20, a child (3-14 years) pays R10,30 and a pensioner (60+ years) pays R4,20. Calculate the percentage savings a pensioner receives per bus trip when compared to the amount paid by an adult for the same bus trip.

## Method

percentage saving calculation

## Memo working

Work out what fraction of the adult fare the pensioner actually pays, then take that away from 100%: % paid = R4,20/R14,20 × 100% (1MA dividing correct values) = 29,58% (1A simplification); % savings = 100% - 29,58% = 70,42% (1CA simplification). (Memo option 2: % savings = (R14,20 - R4,20)/R14,20 × 100% (1MA subtracting correct values, 1A correct denominator) = 70,42% (1CA simplification).)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

70,42%
