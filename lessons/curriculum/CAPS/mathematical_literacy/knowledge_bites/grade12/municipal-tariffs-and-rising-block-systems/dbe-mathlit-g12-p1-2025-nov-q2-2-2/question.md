## Question (5 marks)

TABLE 4 shows Metrobus trip packages for adults: a single daily trip costs R14,20, while a monthly package of 44 trips costs R465,60. Muriel travels to work by bus. Determine the amount Muriel will save per trip if she buys the monthly package instead of the daily trip package.

## Method

unit cost comparison

## Memo working

First find what one trip costs on the monthly package: Cost per trip = R465,60/44 (1RT R465,60, 1MA dividing by 44) = R10,58 (1CA simplification). Then compare with the daily fare: Saving per trip = R14,20 - R10,58 (1MCA subtracting values) = R3,62 (1CA simplification). (Memo option 2: total cost of 44 daily trips = R14,20 × 44 = R624,80; difference = R624,80 - R465,60 = R159,20; saving per trip = R159,20/44 = R3,62.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R3,62
