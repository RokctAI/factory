## Question (7 marks)

A worker is replacing both of her double garage doors with double-brick walls (bricks are sold per pallet of 525 bricks at R6,45 per brick; 2 pallets are needed). The opening of a garage door has dimensions of 2,13 m x 3 m. Other costs to build the double-brick wall: Material, other than bricks, cost R2 000,00; Labour is R500,00 per square metre. The owner states that the total cost to replace both garage doors with walls would be R15 200,00. Verify her statement by means of calculations. You may use the following formula: Area = length x width

## Method

area based cost verification

## Memo working

Work out each cost separately, then compare the total with the claim. Area of the 2 door openings = 2 x 2,13 x 3 = 12,78 m^2. Labour is charged per square metre: 12,78 x R500 = R6 390. Bricks: 2 pallets x 525 bricks x R6,45 = R6 772,50. Total cost = R2 000 + R6 390 + R6 772,50 = R15 162,50. This is not equal to the claimed R15 200,00, so her statement is NOT VALID. (The number of pallets carries over from Q4.2.1. A third memo alternative rounds the area to 13 m^2, giving labour R6 500 and a total of R15 272,50 — also Not VALID.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Area of 2 doors = 12,78 m^2; labour = R6 390; bricks (2 pallets) = R6 772,50; total = R2 000 + R6 390 + R6 772,50 = R15 162,50. Her statement is NOT VALID.
