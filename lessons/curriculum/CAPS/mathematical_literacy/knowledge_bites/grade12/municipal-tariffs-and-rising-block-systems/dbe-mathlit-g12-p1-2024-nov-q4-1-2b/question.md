## Question (4 marks)

TABLE 5 gives the cost to hire DJ Rain for up to 9 hours: R4 000 for each of hours 1 to 5, then R5 250 (6 hours), R6 500 (7 hours), R7 750 (8 hours) and R9 000 (9 hours). (b) Use TABLE 5 to draw a line graph of DJ Rain's cost on the answer-sheet grid (cost in rand 0-10 000 against number of hours playing 0-9), on the same grid as the DJ 5-Star and DJ Cool graphs.

## Method

draw line graph

## Memo working

Plot cost against hours from the table. From 0 to 5 hours the cost never changes, so the graph is a horizontal line at R4 000: start at the point (0 ; 4 000) (1A starting point) and run flat to (5 ; 4 000) (1A). From 5 hours onwards the cost climbs by R1 250 per hour, so the points (6 ; 5 250), (7 ; 6 500), (8 ; 7 750) lie on a straight slanted line ending at (9 ; 9 000) (1A end point). Join ALL the plotted points on the slanted part of the graph (1A). (CA from Question 4.1.1(b).)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

[Drawn graph] Horizontal line at R4 000 from 0 to 5 hours, then rising straight line to (9 ; 9 000).
