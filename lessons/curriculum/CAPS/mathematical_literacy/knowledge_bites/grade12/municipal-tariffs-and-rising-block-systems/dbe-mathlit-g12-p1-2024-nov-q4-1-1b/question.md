## Question (4 marks)

DJ Rain charges R4 000 for 5 hours or less, thereafter an additional tariff of R1 250 per hour. TABLE 5 shows the cost to hire DJ Rain per hour — number of hours: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9; cost (in R): 4 000, 4 000, P, 4 000, 4 000, Q, 6 500, 7 750, R. (b) Complete the table by calculating the missing values P, Q and R.

## Method

evaluate cost formula

## Memo working

P is the cost for 3 hours: that is still within the first 5 hours, so the flat fee applies and P = 4 000 (1A). Q is the cost for 6 hours: one hour beyond 5, so Q = R4 000 + R1 250 x 1 = 5 250 (2A). R is the cost for 9 hours: four hours beyond 5, so R = R4 000 + R1 250 x 4 = R4 000 + R5 000 = 9 000 (1A). As a check, the printed values follow the same pattern: 7 hours gives 4 000 + 2 x 1 250 = 6 500 and 8 hours gives 4 000 + 3 x 1 250 = 7 750.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

P = 4 000; Q = 5 250; R = 9 000
