## Question (4 marks)

Johannesburg Metrobus introduced a compulsory card payment system. All passengers must purchase the Metrobus card for R35 to activate it, and then upload trip fares according to the available trip packages (the weekly package of 12 trips costs R127,10). Muriel purchased a Metrobus card together with an uploaded trip package to the total value of R416,30. Calculate the maximum number of weekly trips Muriel can take using this card.

## Method

remaining amount division

## Memo working

First remove the once-off card cost to see how much is left for trip packages: Package amount left = R416,30 - R35,00 (1RT R35) = R381,30 (1A simplification). Then see how many weekly packages fit into that amount: Number of weekly trips = R381,30 ÷ R127,10 (1MCA dividing values) = 3 (1CA simplification). (Memo option 2: R416,30 - R127,10 - R127,10 - R127,10 (1RT R127,10, 1MA subtracting values) = R35 (1A), so 3 (1CA). Note: the memo interprets "weekly trips" as the number of 12-trip weekly packages, at R127,10 each, that fit into the remaining R381,30 — giving 3.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

3
