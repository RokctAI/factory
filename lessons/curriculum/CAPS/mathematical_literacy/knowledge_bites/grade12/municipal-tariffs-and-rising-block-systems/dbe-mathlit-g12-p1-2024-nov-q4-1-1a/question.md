## Question (3 marks)

Lee's parents are planning his 21st birthday party. One of the disc jockey options is DJ Rain, who charges R4 000 for 5 hours or less, and thereafter an additional tariff of R1 250 per hour. A table (TABLE 5: cost to hire DJ Rain per hour, for 1 to 9 hours) shows the cost staying at R4 000 up to 5 hours and then increasing per extra hour. (a) Write a formula to calculate the cost to hire DJ Rain for more than 5 hours in the following form: Cost = ...

## Method

construct cost formula

## Memo working

The charge has two parts: a fixed amount of R4 000 that covers the first 5 hours, plus a variable amount of R1 250 for every hour beyond the fifth. Put these together as fixed cost plus rate times extra hours: 1A fixed cost (R4 000); 1A multiply hours with tariff (R1 250); 1A number of hours more than 5. (Memo alternative: Cost = R4 000 + R1 250 x n, where n = number of hours exceeding 5.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Cost = R4 000 + R1 250 x (number of hours exceeding 5)
