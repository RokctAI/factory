## Question (5 marks)

Lee's 21st birthday party starts at 18:00 at a hotel. The budgeted expenses (TABLE 4) are: cost of the venue — R750 deposit and R6 185 for the day; food and drinks — R18 000; birthday cake — R1 250. Lee's parents decide to hire DJ 5-Star, who charges R1 000 per hour or part thereof and will provide the music entertainment from 18:00 until 01:30. Determine the total budgeted expenses for the party.

## Method

total budget with time based cost

## Memo working

First work out the playing time: from 18:00 to 01:30 is 7 hours 30 minutes. Because DJ 5-Star charges per hour "or part thereof", the extra 30 minutes counts as a full hour, so charge for 8 hours (1A calculating hours). Cost for the DJ = 8 x R1 000 (1MCA) = R8 000 (1CA). Now add every budgeted item: food and drinks, venue deposit, venue day fee, cake and DJ. Total cost = R18 000 + R750 + R6 185 + R1 250 + R8 000 (1MCA adding all values) = R34 185 (1CA simplification).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R34 185
