## Question (2 marks)

For Lee's 21st birthday party there are three DJ options: DJ Rain (R4 000 for 5 hours or less, then R1 250 per extra hour), DJ 5-Star (R1 000 per hour or part thereof) and DJ Cool (a single price for the whole party). The ANSWER SHEET grid, "Comparison of the cost for the different DJs" (cost in rand 0-10 000 against number of hours playing 0-9), already shows DJ 5-Star plotted as an increasing graph rising R1 000 per hour, and DJ Cool as a horizontal dash-dot line. (a) Name the type of graph used to represent the cost to hire DJ 5-Star.

**Note: the official answer sheet plots DJ Cool as a constant line at R6 500, although TABLE 4 in the question paper prices DJ Cool at R6 000 for the party — a source discrepancy transcribed as printed. It does not affect this question, which asks only about DJ 5-Star's graph.**

## Method

identify graph type

## Memo working

DJ 5-Star charges R1 000 per hour or part thereof, so the cost stays constant within each hour and then jumps by R1 000 as soon as a new hour starts. A graph of flat pieces with sudden jumps between them is a step graph. (2A correct name)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Step graph / Stepwise graph
