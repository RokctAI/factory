## Question (2 marks)

An extract of Muriel Davids' monthly salary slip for the tax year ending February 2025 shows a gross salary of R35 000 and employee deductions that include UIF of R177,12, PAYE tax and medical aid. Write the acronym UIF in words.

## Method

expand acronym

## Memo working

UIF stands for the Unemployment Insurance Fund — the fund that both employee and employer contribute to, and that pays short-term benefits to workers who become unemployed. (Memo: 2A correct acronym.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Unemployment Insurance Fund
