## Question (2 marks)

Muriel Davids earns a gross salary of R35 000 per month, and her salary slip shows the PAYE tax deduction only as a missing value A. ANNEXURE A in the ANSWER BOOK shows the monthly PAYE deduction table for the 2024/2025 tax year. (a) Write down the missing value A from the monthly PAYE deduction table in ANNEXURE A.

**Note: ANNEXURE A is printed in the 2025 Special Answer Book, which is not in the source archive. The value below is the memo's definitive reading of the table, and it is used consistently in the memo for questions 2.1.4(b) and 2.1.4(c), but the table reading itself cannot be independently re-checked until the answer book is available.**

## Method

read value from tax table

## Memo working

Find Muriel's salary level (R35 000 per month) in the monthly PAYE deduction table and read off the monthly PAYE tax for a taxpayer of her age: A = R6 298. (Memo: 2RT correct value.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A = R6 298
