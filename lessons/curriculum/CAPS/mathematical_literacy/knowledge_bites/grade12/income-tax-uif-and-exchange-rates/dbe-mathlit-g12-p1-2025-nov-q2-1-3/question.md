## Question (2 marks)

Muriel Davids' salary slip shows an employee medical aid deduction of R2 531,54; she pays the medical aid for herself only. Determine the employer contribution towards the medical aid if the employer contributes one third of the total contribution.

## Method

fraction of total contribution

## Memo working

If the employer pays one third of the total contribution, then Muriel's deduction of R2 531,54 is the other two thirds. One third is half of two thirds, so the employer's share is half of Muriel's deduction: Employer medical aid contribution = R2 531,54 ÷ 2 (1MA divide by 2) = R1 265,77 (1A simplification). (Memo option 2: total contribution = 3/2 × R2 531,54 = R3 797,31; employer share = R3 797,31 ÷ 3, or R3 797,31 - R2 531,54 = R1 265,77. AO)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R1 265,77
