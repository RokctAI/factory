## Question (2 marks)

Muriel Davids' salary slip shows a PAYE tax deduction, since employees in South Africa pay income tax based on their earnings. Name the institution that collects tax on behalf of the government of South Africa.

## Method

name institution

## Memo working

Income tax (including PAYE deducted by employers) is collected by SARS, the South African Revenue Service — the institution that administers tax collection on behalf of the government. (Memo: 2A correct government institution.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

SARS OR South African Revenue Services
