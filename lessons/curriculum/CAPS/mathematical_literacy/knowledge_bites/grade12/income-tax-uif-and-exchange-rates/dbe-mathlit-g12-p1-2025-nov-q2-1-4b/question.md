## Question (2 marks)

Muriel Davids' monthly salary slip shows: gross salary R35 000; employee deductions of UIF R177,12, PAYE tax R6 298 and medical aid R2 531,54; and a net salary shown only as a missing value B. (b) Calculate the missing value B.

## Method

net salary calculation

## Memo working

Net salary is the gross salary with every deduction taken off: B = Gross salary - UIF - PAYE tax - medical aid = R35 000 - R177,12 - R6 298 - R2 531,54 (1MCA subtracting values) = R25 993,34 (1CA simplification). (Memo option 2: B = R35 000 - (R177,12 + R6 298 + R2 531,54) = R35 000 - R9 006,66 = R25 993,34. AO. CA from Question 2.1.4(a).)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R25 993,34
