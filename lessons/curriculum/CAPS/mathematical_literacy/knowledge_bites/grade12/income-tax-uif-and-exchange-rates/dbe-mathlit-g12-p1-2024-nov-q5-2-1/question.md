## Question (2 marks)

In 2024, South Africa received R302,4 billion from corporate income tax. NOTE: R1 000 000 = 4 447 989,1 rupees; 4 447 989,1 rupees = 44,479891 lakhs. Convert R302,4 billion to million rands.

## Method

billion to million conversion

## Memo working

One billion is one thousand million, so to change billions into millions, multiply by 1 000. R302,4 billion x 1 000 (1MA multiplying by 1 000) = R302 400 million (1A simplification). AO

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R302 400 million (or R302 400 000 000)
