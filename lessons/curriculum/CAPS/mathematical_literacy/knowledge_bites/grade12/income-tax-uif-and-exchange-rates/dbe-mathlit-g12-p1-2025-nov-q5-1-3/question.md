## Question (5 marks)

Candice started a small delivery business in London and chose car purchase OPTION A, whose deposit and first-month instalment is £1 173,66. Candice's aunt in Switzerland offers to pay the deposit and first-month instalment. TABLE 9 shows the currency conversion factors: for the Swiss franc (CHF), 20,48 ZAR per unit and 0,049 units per ZAR; for the pound sterling (£), 23,20 ZAR per unit and 0,043 units per ZAR. Calculate, in Swiss franc (CHF), the deposit and first-month instalment for OPTION A.

## Method

two step currency conversion

## Memo working

Deposit and first-month instalment = £1 173,66 (1RT). Amount in rand = £1 173,66 / 0,043 (1MA exchange rate) = R27 294,42 (1CA). Amount in CHF = R27 294,42 / R20,48 (1MCA exchange rate) = CHF 1 332,74 (1CA) OR R27 294,42 x 0,049 = CHF 1 337,43. OR via £1 173,66 x R23,20 = R27 228,91; /20,48 = CHF 1 329,54 OR x 0,049 = CHF 1 334,22. OR conversion rate £1 = CHF 23,20/20,48 = 1,1328125 (1MA, 1CA); 1 173,66 x 1,1328125 = CHF 1 329,54. (The memo accepts several answers depending on which of the rounded conversion factors are used: 1 329,54 / 1 332,74 / 1 334,22 / 1 337,43.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

CHF 1 332,74 (alternatives in memo: CHF 1 337,43; CHF 1 329,54; CHF 1 334,22)
