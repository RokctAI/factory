## Question (5 marks)

Miecke is 45 years old with an annual taxable income of R471 310,20 (R39 275,85 per month) in the 2023/2024 tax year; she does not belong to a medical aid. Per ANNEXURE A, tax bracket C (taxable income R370 501-R512 800) charges R77 362 + 31% of taxable income above R370 500, and the primary rebate is R17 235. Calculate Miecke's annual tax payable for the 2023/2024 tax year.

## Method

income tax with rebate

## Memo working

Substitute the annual income into the bracket C rule: Tax before rebate = R77 362 + 31% of (R471 310,20 - R370 500) (1SF correct substitution) = R77 362 + 31% of R100 810,20 = R77 362 + R31 251,162 (1MCA adding values) = R108 613,162 (1CA). Miecke is 45, so only the primary rebate applies: Annual tax payable = R108 613,162 - R17 235 (1RT rebate: R17 235) = R91 378,16 (1CA simplification). She gets no medical tax credits because she has no medical aid. (CA from Question 2.3.1.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R91 378,16
