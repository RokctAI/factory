## Question (3 marks)

Miecke is 45 years old and earns a monthly taxable income of R39 275,85 in the 2023/2024 tax year. She does not belong to a medical aid. ANNEXURE A shows the annual tax table for individuals for 2023/2024 (tax brackets by annual taxable income in rand: A: 1-237 100; B: 237 101-370 500; C: 370 501-512 800; D: 512 801-673 000; E: 673 001-857 900; F: 857 901-1 817 000; G: 1 817 001 and above). Identify the tax bracket that Miecke will use to calculate her income tax payable.

## Method

annualise income and identify tax bracket

## Memo working

The tax table works on ANNUAL taxable income, but Miecke's income is given per month, so first annualise it: Annual taxable income = R39 275,85 x 12 (1MA multiplying by 12) = R471 310,20 (1A simplification). Now place this amount in the table: R471 310,20 falls between R370 501 and R512 800, which is Tax Bracket C (1MCA). AO

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Tax Bracket C
