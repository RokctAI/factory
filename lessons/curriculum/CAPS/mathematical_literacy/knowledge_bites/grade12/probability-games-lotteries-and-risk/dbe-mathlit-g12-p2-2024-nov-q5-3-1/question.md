## Question (2 marks)

TABLE 3 gives the area sizes of the mainland, islands and total for each Australian state or territory, with each state's percentage of the total area (Western Australia total 2 527 013 km^2, 32,9%; Queensland total 1 729 742 km^2, 22,5%; Northern Territory total 1 347 791 km^2, 17,5%; South Australia total 984 321 km^2, 12,8%; New South Wales total 801 150 km^2, 10,4%; Victoria total 227 444 km^2, 3,0%; Tasmania total 68 401 km^2, 0,9%; Australian Capital Territory total 2 358 km^2; TOTAL for Australia 7 688 220 km^2). A meteorite (rock from the sky) fell somewhere in Australia. Write down the probability that it randomly fell in Queensland.

## Method

probability from proportion

## Memo working

The probability that a random landing point is in Queensland equals Queensland's share of Australia's total area — which the table already gives as a percentage: P = 22,5%. (Memo alternative: compute it from the areas, P = 1 729 742 ÷ 7 688 220 = 0,224986..., which is 22,5%.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

22,5% (or 1 729 742 / 7 688 220 = 0,224986...)
