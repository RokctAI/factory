## Question (2 marks)

ANSWER SHEET 3.2 shows a stacked bar graph of the number of LTE devices for each of the nine provinces of South Africa over the three-year period 2021 to 2023. Determine, as a fraction, the probability of randomly selecting a province that has a total number of LTE devices less than 8 million for the three-year period. (On the graph, two of the nine provinces have three-year stack totals under 8 million.)

**Note: ANSWER SHEET 3.2 lives in the 2025 Special Answer Book, which is not in the repo. Two of the nine provinces have three-year totals under 8 million on the answer-sheet graph, but which two cannot be verified until the answer book arrives; the probability structure (favourable outcomes over total outcomes) is verified.**

## Method

probability as fraction

## Memo working

P = 2/9 (1A numerator, 1A denominator)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

2/9
