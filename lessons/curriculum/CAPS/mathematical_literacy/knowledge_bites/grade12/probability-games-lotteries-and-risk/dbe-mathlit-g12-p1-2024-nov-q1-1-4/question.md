## Question (2 marks)

In an SGB election at School X, the probability of randomly selecting a voter from School X is 0,56 during the 17:30-18:00 time slot. Write 0,56 as a simplified fraction.

## Method

decimal to simplified fraction

## Memo working

0,56 has two decimal places, so write it over 100: 0,56 = 56/100 (1A writing as a fraction). Divide the top and bottom by their highest common factor, 4: 56 ÷ 4 = 14 and 100 ÷ 4 = 25, giving 14/25 (1A simplification).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

14/25
