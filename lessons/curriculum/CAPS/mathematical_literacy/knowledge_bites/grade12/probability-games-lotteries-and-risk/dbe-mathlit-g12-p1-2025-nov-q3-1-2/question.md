## Question (3 marks)

TABLE 5 shows the use of six social media platforms categorised by gender, with users in millions (male | female): Facebook 1 732,8 | 1 307,2; Instagram 1 020 | 980; Snapchat (male users not shown, 46% of users male) | 405; X/Twitter 346,64 | 272,36; TikTok 645 | 855; YouTube 1 350 | 1 150. Determine, as a decimal, the probability of randomly selecting a social media platform where the female users were fewer than the male users.

## Method

probability as decimal

## Memo working

Count the favourable outcomes: platforms where female users are fewer than male users are Facebook, Instagram, X/Twitter and YouTube — 4 of the 6 platforms. (Snapchat and TikTok both have more female than male users.) Probability = favourable outcomes over total outcomes: P = 4/6 (1A numerator, 1A denominator) = 0,67 (1CA simplification).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

0,67
