## Question (3 marks)

A hotel has three smaller venues, each with a maximum number of guests per function: cinema-style venue 400 guests; sit-down venue without dancing space 250 guests; sit-down venue with dancing space 200 guests. TABLE 6 shows the actual number of guests per function during a specific month — 2023: 180, 350, 400, 200, 215, 90, 150, 230, 160; 2022: 360, 160, 300, 70, 160, 200, 240. Write, as a decimal, the probability that a cinema-style venue was chosen as a suitable function venue for all the functions held during 2022 and 2023.

## Method

probability as decimal

## Memo working

Only the cinema-style venue (maximum 400) can host a function with more than 250 guests, so count the functions that needed it: 350 and 400 in 2023, plus 360 and 300 in 2022, giving 4 functions. The total number of functions over both years is 9 + 7 = 16. So P = 4/16 (1A numerator, 1A denominator) = 0,25 (1CA simplification).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

0,25
