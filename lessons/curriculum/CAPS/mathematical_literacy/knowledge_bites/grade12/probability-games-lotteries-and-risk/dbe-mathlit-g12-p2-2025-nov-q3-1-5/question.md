## Question (4 marks)

Mrs Xulu placed all the coloured circles for her tree decoration in a container as she cut them out. TABLE 2 shows the number of circles per colour (Red 9; Green 7; Gold 28; Dark denim 8; Mixed colour 22). Determine, as a percentage, the probability of randomly choosing a green circle or a red circle from the container.

## Method

probability of either event as percentage

## Memo working

Total number of circles = 9 + 7 + 28 + 8 + 22 = 74. Favourable outcomes: green or red = 7 + 9 = 16. P(green or red) = 16/74 x 100% = 21,62%. (Memo option 2: add the separate percentages — P(red) + P(green) = 12,16% + 9,46% = 21,62%.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

21,62%
