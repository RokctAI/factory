## Question (2 marks)

(Context: Simon the carpenter's bookcase, shown on ANNEXURE D, has three shelf openings, of which one has a height greater than 30 cm.) Write down the probability of randomly placing a book on a shelf with a height greater than 30 cm.

**Note: ANNEXURE D is printed in the 2025 Special Answer Book, which is not in the archive; the fact that one of the three shelf openings is taller than 30 cm is reconstructed from the marking guidelines. The memo answer is definitive.**

## Method

probability as fraction

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) There are 3 shelf openings in total (the denominator) and only 1 of them has a height greater than 30 cm (the numerator), so P = 1/3. No penalty for the form of the answer — the fraction, decimal or percentage form is accepted.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

P = 1/3 OR 0,333 OR 33,3%
