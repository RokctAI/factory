## Question (3 marks)

There are 35 different coloured sweets in each birthday packet. TABLE 2 shows the number of sweets per colour: Blue 4; Purple 5; Pink 8; Orange 11; Yellow 7. Determine the probability, as a decimal, of randomly choosing a pink or a yellow sweet from the packet.

## Method

probability or events as decimal

## Memo working

Count the favourable sweets: pink and yellow together give 8 + 7 = 15 sweets out of the 35 in the packet. So P = 15/35 = 0,42857... which rounds to 0,43. (Memo alternative: add the two probabilities separately, P = 8/35 + 7/35 = 0,22857... + 0,2 = 0,43.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

0,43 (0,42857...)
