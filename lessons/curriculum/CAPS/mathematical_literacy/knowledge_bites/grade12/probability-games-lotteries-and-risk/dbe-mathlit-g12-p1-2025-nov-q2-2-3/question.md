## Question (2 marks)

TABLE 4 shows Metrobus trip packages with fares per passenger category (adults 15-59 years, children 3-14 years, pensioners 60+ years). The weekly package of 12 trips is offered at an adult fare of R127,10 only — the table shows no child fare and no pensioner fare for it. Write down the probability of a 10-year old child purchasing a weekly package with 12 trips.

## Method

identify impossible event

## Memo working

A 10-year old falls in the children category (3-14 years), but the 12-trip weekly package has no child fare at all — a child cannot buy it. An event that cannot happen is an impossible event, and its probability is 0. (Memo: 2A correct probability; accepted forms: 0, 0%, impossible, and also 0/4 or 0/3.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

0 / 0% / impossible (Accept: 0/4 or 0/3)
