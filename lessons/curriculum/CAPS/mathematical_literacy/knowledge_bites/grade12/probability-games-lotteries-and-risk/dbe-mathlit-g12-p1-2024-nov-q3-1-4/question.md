## Question (3 marks)

ANNEXURE B, GRAPH 1 shows the trends in the total number of Shoprite and Pick n Pay stores for each year from 2014 to 2023 (ten years in total). On the graph, the Shoprite line starts at 1 581 stores in 2014 and only climbs above 2 000 stores from 2017 onwards, so Shoprite had fewer than 2 000 stores in three of the ten years shown (2014 to 2016). Determine, as a percentage, the probability of randomly selecting a year where the number of Shoprite stores is fewer than 2 000.

## Method

probability as percentage

## Memo working

Probability = favourable outcomes over total outcomes. The favourable outcomes are the years with fewer than 2 000 Shoprite stores — reading the graph, that is 3 years (2014, 2015 and 2016). The total outcomes are all 10 years shown on the graph. So P = 3/10 x 100% (1RT correct numerator, 1RT correct denominator) = 30% (1CA simplification). AO

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

30%
