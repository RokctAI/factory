## Question (5 marks)

A home solar system unit can be paid for in two ways (OPTION 1: RENT-TO-OWN - pay for 7 years at R1 549 p/m, plus a R782 initiation fee and a buy-out of R7 820 after the rental period. OPTION 2: CASH PRICE - R78 200 including VAT). After seven years, Miecke decides to buy-out the rent-to-own option. Calculate the extra amount she has to pay compared to buying the home solar system unit for cash.

## Method

total cost comparison

## Memo working

First convert the rental period to months: 12 x 7 = 84 months (1A). Then total up everything the rent-to-own route costs: (R1 549 x 84) + R782 + R7 820 = R130 116 + R782 + R7 820 (1MCA adding ALL correct values) = R138 718 (1CA). Finally compare with the cash price: Difference = R138 718 - R78 200 (1MCA subtracting values) = R60 518 (1CA simplification).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R60 518
