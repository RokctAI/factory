## Question (2 marks)

Miecke plans to install a home solar system unit consisting of solar panels, a battery and an inverter. A newspaper advertisement shows two payment options for the same unit (OPTION 1: RENT-TO-OWN - pay for 7 years at R1 549 p/m, plus additional costs of a R782 initiation fee and a buy-out of R7 820 after the rental period. OPTION 2: CASH PRICE - R78 200 including VAT). Write down the monthly instalment for the rent-to-own option.

## Method

read value from document

## Memo working

The monthly instalment is the amount labelled "p/m" (per month) under the rent-to-own option: R1 549. The initiation fee and buy-out amount are once-off extras, not the monthly instalment. (Memo: 2RT correct amount, NPU.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R1 549
