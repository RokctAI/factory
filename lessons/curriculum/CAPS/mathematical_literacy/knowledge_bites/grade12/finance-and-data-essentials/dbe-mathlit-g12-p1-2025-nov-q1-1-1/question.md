## Question (2 marks)

TABLE 1 shows the prices of some food items in three cities of South Africa, namely Johannesburg, Durban and Cape Town (white sugar, 10 kg: Johannesburg R223,23; Durban R197,07; Cape Town R229,49). Name the city where the cost of white sugar is the highest.

## Method

read value from table

## Memo working

Read along the white sugar row and compare the three city prices: Johannesburg R223,23, Durban R197,07 and Cape Town R229,49. The largest of the three amounts is R229,49, which is the Cape Town price. (Memo: 2RT correct city; the answer alone earns full marks.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Cape Town
