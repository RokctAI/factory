## Question (3 marks)

TABLE 7 shows the VAT payments made to SARS by vendors in certain economic sectors for 2023/2024. For each sector it lists the number of vendors, the VAT payments in R million, and the average payment per vendor in R million (Financial: 197 178 vendors, 221 322, average 1,12245; Manufacturing: 46 901, 73 893, 1,57548; Wholesale and retail trade: 86 610, 76 826, 0,88703; Construction: 39 279, 25 593, average V; Transport: 15 258, 29 418, 1,92804; Community and social: 33 825, 32 458, 0,95959; Utilities: 2 658, 22 141, 8,32995). NOTE: A vendor is a person or company offering goods or services for sale. Determine, rounded to the nearest thousand, the missing value V.

**Note: The question says 'rounded to the nearest thousand' but the value is in R million, so the memo rounds to three decimal places (the nearest thousandth of a million is the nearest thousand rand): 0,652.**

## Method

division and rounding

## Memo working

V = 25 593 ÷ 39 279 (1MA dividing values) = 0,651569541 (1CA simplification) = 0,652 (1R correct rounding). AO

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

V = 0,652
