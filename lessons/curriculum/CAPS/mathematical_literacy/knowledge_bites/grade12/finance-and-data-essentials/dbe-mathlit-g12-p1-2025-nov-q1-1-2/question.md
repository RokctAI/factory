## Question (2 marks)

TABLE 1 shows the prices of some food items in three South African cities; carrots are sold in 5 kg packs and cost R41,41 in Johannesburg. Calculate the cost of carrots, per kilogram, in Johannesburg.

## Method

unit rate from table

## Memo working

The table gives the price of a 5 kg pack, so divide by 5 to get the price of one kilogram: Cost per kilogram = R41,41 ÷ 5 (1RT R41,41) = R8,282, or R8,28 rounded to the nearest cent (1A simplification). (Memo option 2: 5 kg : R41,41, so 1 kg = 1/5 × R41,41 = R8,282 OR R8,28.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R8,282 OR R8,28
