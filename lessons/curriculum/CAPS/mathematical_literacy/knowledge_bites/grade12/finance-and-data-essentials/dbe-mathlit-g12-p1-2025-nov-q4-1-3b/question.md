## Question (4 marks)

One of Monwabisi's rental trailers was returned with a damaged rear light; the refundable deposit he holds per trailer is R1 000. (b) The following quotation for the repair and replacement of the rear light was received: New rear light R242; Labour at R480 per hour or part thereof; Time required to complete the repair and replacement is 1 hour 30 minutes. Verify, showing ALL calculations, whether the deposit amount will be sufficient to cover the total cost of the entire repair and replacement. (Because labour is charged per hour or part thereof, 1 hour 30 minutes is charged as 2 hours.)

## Method

cost vs deposit verification

## Memo working

Labour cost = R480 x 2 (1MA multiplying by 2) = R960 (1CA simplification). Total cost = R960 + R242 = R1 202 (1CA simplification). The deposit of R1 000 will not be sufficient (1O conclusion). OR R1 000 - R480 - R480 - R242 = -R202 (1A R480, 1MCA, 1CA); not sufficient (1O)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Labour cost = R480 x 2 = R960; total cost = R960 + R242 = R1 202. The deposit of R1 000 will not be sufficient.
