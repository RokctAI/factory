## Question (2 marks)

TABLE 1 shows the Johannesburg prices of ten food items: salt R18,07; onions R111,59; carrots R41,41; butternuts R105,24; curry powder R41,91; white sugar R223,23; apples R52,38; oranges R104,96; eggs R163,31; canned beans R101,94. Calculate the total price of ALL the food items in Johannesburg.

## Method

sum of column

## Memo working

Add every value in the Johannesburg column: Total price = R18,07 + R111,59 + R41,41 + R105,24 + R41,91 + R223,23 + R52,38 + R104,96 + R163,31 + R101,94 (1MA adding all values) = R964,04 (1A simplification).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R964,04
