## Question (3 marks)

Monwabisi's trailer rental costs are: R1 000 refundable deposit per trailer, R350 per day per small trailer and R500 per day per large trailer. TABLE 6 shows the rental costs, including the deposit, for 1, 3, 5, 7, 10 and 15 days; the cost for a small trailer reads R1 350, R2 050, B, R3 450, R4 500, R6 250, so B is the 5-day cost of a small trailer. Calculate the missing value B.

## Method

evaluate cost formula

## Memo working

B = R1 000 + (R350 x 5) (1A, 1MA multiplying values) = R2 750 (1CA simplification). OR B = R2 050 + R350 + R350 (1A R2 050, 1MA adding values) = R2 750. AO

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

B = R2 750
