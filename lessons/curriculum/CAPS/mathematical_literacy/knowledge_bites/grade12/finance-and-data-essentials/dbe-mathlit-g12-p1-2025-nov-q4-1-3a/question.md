## Question (6 marks)

Monwabisi operates a trailer rental business with these costs: R1 000 refundable deposit per trailer if there is no damage to the trailer; R350 per day per small trailer; R500 per day per large trailer (so a one-day rental including deposit costs R1 350 for a small trailer and R1 500 for a large trailer). On 12 December 2024, Monwabisi rented out TWO small trailers and SIX large trailers to different customers for a day. One of the trailers was returned with a damaged rear light. (a) Monwabisi stated that his total income, after refunding the deposits, amounted to R4 550. Verify, showing ALL calculations, whether his statement is VALID. (Seven of the eight R1 000 deposits are refunded; the deposit of the damaged trailer is retained.)

## Method

income after refund verification

## Memo working

Income = (R1 350 x 2) + (R1 500 x 6) (1MA multiplying values) = R2 700 + R9 000 (1A) = R11 700 (1CA). Total income after refund = R11 700 - R7 000 (1MCA subtracting R7 000) = R4 700 (1CA). Statement NOT VALID (1O conclusion). OR rental income only = (R350 x 2) + (R500 x 6) = R700 + R3 000; plus retained deposit R1 000 = R4 700; NOT VALID

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Income for both trailer types = (R1 350 x 2) + (R1 500 x 6) = R11 700; total income after refunding 7 deposits = R11 700 - R7 000 = R4 700. Statement NOT VALID.
