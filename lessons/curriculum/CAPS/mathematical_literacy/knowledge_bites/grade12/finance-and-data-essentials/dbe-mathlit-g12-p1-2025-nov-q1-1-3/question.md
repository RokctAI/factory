## Question (3 marks)

TABLE 1 shows the prices of some food items in three South African cities; eggs (60 eggs) cost R165,52 in Cape Town and R169,62 in Durban. Determine, as a unit ratio rounded to THREE decimal places, the price of eggs in Cape Town compared to the price of eggs in Durban in the form 1 : ...

**Note: the memo's explanation column prints "1RT 169,92" — a typo for 169,62. The memo's solution column correctly uses 169,62, and the arithmetic checks out: 169,62 ÷ 165,52 = 1,02477..., which rounds to 1,025 at three decimal places.**

## Method

unit ratio

## Memo working

Write the two prices as a ratio in the order asked, Cape Town to Durban: 165,52 : 169,62 (1RT 165,52; 1RT 169,62). To turn this into a unit ratio, divide both sides by the first amount, 165,52, so that the first term becomes 1: 1 : 169,62/165,52 = 1 : 1,02477..., which rounds to 1 : 1,025 at three decimal places (1A simplification).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

1 : 1,025
