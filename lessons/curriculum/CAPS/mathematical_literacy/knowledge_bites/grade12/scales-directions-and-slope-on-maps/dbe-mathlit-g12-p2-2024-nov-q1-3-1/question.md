## Question (2 marks)

The fold-up chair is a beach chair that folds into itself. A 3D sketch, a side view and a material list are given (3D sketch labels crosspieces of 36 cm at the back and 32 cm on the seat, and chair supports; side view labels the chair back with 1,27 cm spacing between crosspieces and a 66 cm chair support; MATERIAL LIST: 4 chair supports of 1,9 x 3,8 x 66 cm; 13 crosspieces of 1,9 x 3,8 x 36 cm; 1 seat back, bottom crosspiece of 1,9 x 3,8 x 32 cm; the thickness of the wood is the smallest dimension; adapted from instructables.com). Use the information above. QUESTION: Write down the total number of wooden items needed to assemble this chair.

## Method

sum items from material list

## Memo working

Read the quantity column of the material list and add the three item counts together: 4 chair supports plus 13 crosspieces plus 1 seat back bottom crosspiece gives 4 + 13 + 1 = 18 wooden items. (Memo: 2A correct number.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

18
