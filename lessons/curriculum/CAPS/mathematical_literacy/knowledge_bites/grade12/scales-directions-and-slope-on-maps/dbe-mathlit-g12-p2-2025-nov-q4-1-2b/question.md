## Question (3 marks)

(Context: ANNEXURE C shows a map of central South Africa with the tracked flight of a GPS-fitted flamingo, including towns and numbered national roads.) Name the towns found on the N12.

**Note: ANNEXURE C is printed in the 2025 Special Answer Book, which is not in the archive; the map description is reconstructed from the marking guidelines. The memo answer is definitive.**

## Method

read locations from map

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Trace the road labelled N12 across the map and read off every town the road passes through: Kimberley and Britstown.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Kimberley and Britstown
