## Question (2 marks)

(Context: ANNEXURE C shows a map of central South Africa with the tracked flight of a GPS-fitted flamingo from point A to point E.) National roads are indicated with a number enclosed in a pentagon, e.g. the N1 pentagon symbol. Write down how many different national roads are shown on this map.

**Note: ANNEXURE C is printed in the 2025 Special Answer Book, which is not in the archive; the map description is reconstructed from the marking guidelines. The memo answer is definitive.**

## Method

count map features

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Count each differently numbered pentagon road symbol on the map once — the map shows 8 different national roads.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

8
