## Question (2 marks)

TABLE 1 contains a list of explanations and definitions of concepts used in Mathematical Literacy (A: a measuring system using metres, litres and kilograms; B: the amount of space occupied by an object; C: a system measuring in inches, gallons and pounds; D: the amount of space available to hold something; E: the side of a building you see when you are facing south; F: the side of a building you see when you are facing north; G: the amount of space that is enclosed by the perimeter of an object). Use TABLE 1 and match an explanation or a definition with the concept below. Write only the letter (A-G). QUESTION: North elevation

## Method

match definition

## Memo working

The north elevation is the side of a building that faces north. To look at that side you must stand to the north of the building and look back towards it — which means you, the observer, are facing south. That matches option E, "the side of a building you see when you are facing south". Option F is the trap: facing north shows you the south elevation. (Memo: 2A correct option.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

E
