## Question (3 marks)

ANNEXURE A shows an aerial view of the Baviaanskloof Uitspan camping site with the number of campers allowed at each campsite (Campsite 1: 6; Campsite 2A: 15, 2B: 4; Campsite 3A: 15, 3B: 4, 3C: 4; Campsite 4: 5; Campsite 5A: 15, 5B: 8; Campsite 6: 10; Campsite 7: maximum 6; Campsite 8: maximum 6; Campsite 9: 20). Calculate the maximum number of campers that can be accommodated at Baviaanskloof Uitspan camping site.

## Method

sum values from plan

## Memo working

Read every camper number off the annexure, taking the maximum where a range is given (campsites 7 and 8 each allow 4 to 6 campers, so use 6), and add them all: 6 + 15 + 4 + 15 + 4 + 4 + 5 + 15 + 8 + 10 + 6 + 6 + 20 = 118. A quick way to check is to group equal values: 20 + 15 x 3 + 10 + 8 + 6 x 3 + 5 + 4 x 3 = 118. (Memo: 1RT all correct values; 1MCA adding values; 1CA simplification; answer only full marks.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

118
