## Question (4 marks)

The layout plan of the ablution facilities shows a rectangle whose actual length is 8,2 metres; on the printed paper the plan's length measures 90 mm. (b) Determine, rounded to the nearest whole number, the scale of the plan.

## Method

calculate scale from measurement

## Memo working

A number scale compares a measured length on the plan with the real length, in the SAME units. Measure the plan's length with a ruler: 90 mm. The real length is 8,2 m, which is 8,2 x 1 000 = 8 200 mm. The scale is then 90 : 8 200; divide both sides by 90 to get the form 1 : n, so n = 8 200 ÷ 90 = 91,11..., which rounds to 91. The scale of the plan is 1 : 91. (Memo option 2: work in centimetres — 9 cm : 8,2 m = 9 cm : 820 cm = 1 : 91,11 = 1 : 91.) The memo accepts a deviation of ± 1 mm on the ruler measurement, since it is taken on the printed paper. (Memo: 1A measured distance; 1C convert to mm; 1M divide by 90; 1R rounded answer.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

1 : 91
