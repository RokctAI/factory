## Question (2 marks)

ANNEXURE A shows an aerial view of the Baviaanskloof Uitspan camping site with a north arrow, the numbered campsites 1-9 and the dam. Determine the general direction of the dam from campsite 8.

## Method

compass direction from map

## Memo working

Stand at campsite 8 on the map and use the north arrow to orient yourself: with north pointing up the page, the dam lies down and to the left of campsite 8 — that is, towards the bottom-left of the map. Down the page is south and left is west, so the general direction of the dam from campsite 8 is south-west. Always measure the direction FROM the given point (campsite 8) TO the feature (the dam). (Memo: 2A compass direction.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

South West / SW
