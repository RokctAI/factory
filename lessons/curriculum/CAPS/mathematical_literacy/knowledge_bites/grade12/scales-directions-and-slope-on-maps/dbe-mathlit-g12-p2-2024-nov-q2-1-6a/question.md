## Question (2 marks)

The layout plan of the ablution facilities (toilets and showers) in the middle of campsites 3, 4, 5 and 6 shows a rectangle 8,2 metres long: along the top wall a row of 9 shower cubicles; along the bottom wall a row of 6 toilet cubicles and, at the left, benches; a door on the right wall; a key identifies the shower, toilet and bench symbols. (a) Write down the total number of showers.

## Method

count symbols on plan

## Memo working

Use the key to identify the shower symbol, then count every shower symbol on the plan. The row along the top wall contains 9 shower cubicles, so there are 9 showers in total. (Memo: 2A correct number.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

9 showers
