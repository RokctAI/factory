## Question (2 marks)

ANNEXURE A in the ANSWER BOOK shows a route map of a round trip and the 18 stops made by a tour bus (a Cape Town tour-bus route map with 18 numbered stops and a bar scale; the annexure is printed in the 2025 Special Answer Book). Use ANNEXURE A: write down the name of the southern-most stop on this route.

## Method

read location from map

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Camps Bay (Memo: 2RT correct stop. On the route map, south is towards the bottom of the map per the compass indicator, and of the 18 numbered stops Camps Bay lies furthest south.)
