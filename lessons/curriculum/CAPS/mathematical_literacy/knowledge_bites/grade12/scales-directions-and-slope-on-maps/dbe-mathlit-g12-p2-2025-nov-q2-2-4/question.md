## Question (5 marks)

ANNEXURE A in the ANSWER BOOK shows a route map of a round trip and the 18 stops made by a tour bus (Cape Town tour-bus route map with a bar scale on which a measured 13 mm represents 500 m; measurements of 12 mm to 13 mm are accepted; the annexure is printed in the 2025 Special Answer Book). The distance of a round trip is 19,2 km. Use the given scale on the map to determine the map distance.

## Method

real distance to map distance via bar scale

## Memo working

Measure the bar scale: 13 mm = 500 m (1A measured distance, 1RT correct ratio). Put the real distance into the same unit as the scale: 19,2 km = 19 200 m (1C convert to m). Work out how many 500 m blocks fit into the trip and give each block 13 mm on the map: map distance = 19 200 ÷ 500 x 13 (1MCA divide and multiply) = 499,2 mm (1CA simplification). (Memo option 2: 1,3 cm = 500 m = 0,5 km; map distance = 19,2 x 1,3/0,5 = 49,92 cm. Accept measurements of 12 mm - 13 mm, so 460,8 mm is also accepted for a 12 mm reading. The bar-scale measurement comes from ANNEXURE A in the Special Answer Book; the scaling arithmetic 19 200/500 x 13 = 499,2 is verified.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

499,2 mm OR 49,92 cm (Accept 460,8 mm for a 12 mm bar-scale measurement)
