## Question (2 marks)

The layout plan of the ablution facilities (toilets and showers) shows benches at the left along the bottom wall. (c) Give ONE suitable reason why benches are found in the ablution facilities.

## Method

reasoned opinion

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

To allow people to sit while waiting for a toilet or shower to become available. OR To place your clothes or belongings on while you are showering. OR To sit while you change your outfit, or getting dressed or apply body lotion or for baby nappy change. (Memo: 2O reason.)
