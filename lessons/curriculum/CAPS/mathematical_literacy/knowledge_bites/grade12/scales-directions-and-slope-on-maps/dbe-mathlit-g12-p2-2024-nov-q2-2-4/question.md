## Question (2 marks)

ANNEXURE B includes the elevation map of the Leopard Trail Run route. On the Day 1 profile there are twin peaks near 1 183 m around the 5-8 km marks, then a long descent from about 8 km (with one small bump at about 15 km) down to a low section around 20 km. John's best average speed (in km/h) for the run was his average speed from the 8 km mark up to the 18 km mark on Day 1. Use the elevation map to explain why this was the case.

## Method

interpret elevation profile

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The part shows a continuous downward slope, it is downhill. OR That part does not have many uphills. (Memo: 2O correct explanation. On the profile, the stretch from 8 km to 18 km slopes steadily downwards with only one small bump at about 15 km, so a runner covers it faster than the climbing sections.)
