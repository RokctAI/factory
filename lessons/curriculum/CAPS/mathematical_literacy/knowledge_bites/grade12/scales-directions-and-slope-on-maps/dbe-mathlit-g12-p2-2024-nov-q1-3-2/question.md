## Question (3 marks)

The fold-up beach chair is built from 4 chair supports, 13 crosspieces and 1 seat back bottom crosspiece (the 3D sketch shows the crosspieces on the seat and at the back; the seat of one chair has 6 crosspieces). Determine the number of crosspieces found on the seats of THREE chairs.

## Method

count and multiply

## Memo working

First count the crosspieces on the seat of ONE chair from the sketch: there are 6. Then multiply by the number of chairs: 6 x 3 = 18 crosspieces on the seats of three chairs. The key skill is reading the count from the diagram before scaling it up. (Memo: 1RT reading 6 from the sketch; 1A multiplying by 3; 1A pieces.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

18
