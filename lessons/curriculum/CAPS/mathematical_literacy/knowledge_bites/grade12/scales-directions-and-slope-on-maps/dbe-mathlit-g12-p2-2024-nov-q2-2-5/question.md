## Question (3 marks)

ANNEXURE B includes the elevation map of the Leopard Trail Run route. On the Day 2 profile there is a peak at about the 17,5 km mark on the 1 050 m gridline, and the trail drops to about 900 m at its end (21,9 km). John stated that on Day 2, while he was running from the 17,5 km mark to the end of the trail, he had moved more than 100 metres closer to sea level. Show, by means of calculations, whether or not he is correct.

## Method

elevation difference verification

## Memo working

Read the two heights off the Day 2 profile: at the 17,5 km mark John is at 1 050 m above sea level, and at the end of the trail he is at 900 m. The drop in height is the difference: 1 050 m - 900 m = 150 m. Since 150 m is more than 100 m, John did move more than 100 metres closer to sea level, so his statement is correct. (Memo: 1RT first correct value; 1RT second correct value; 1O conclusion.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Difference = 1 050 m - 900 m = 150 m. He is CORRECT.
