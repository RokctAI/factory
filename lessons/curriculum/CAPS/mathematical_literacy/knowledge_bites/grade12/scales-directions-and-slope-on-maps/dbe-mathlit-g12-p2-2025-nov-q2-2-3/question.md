## Question (2 marks)

ANNEXURE A in the ANSWER BOOK shows a route map of a round trip and the 18 stops made by a tour bus (Cape Town tour-bus route map with 18 numbered stops; the annexure is printed in the 2025 Special Answer Book). State whether the bus will be driving in a clockwise or an anticlockwise direction from stop 11 to stop 18.

## Method

determine rotation direction on map

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Clockwise (Memo: 2A correct direction. Following the numbered stops 11, 12, 13, ... , 18 along the loop on the route map, the bus travels around the loop in the same sense as the hands of a clock.)
