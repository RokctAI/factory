## Question (2 marks)

The Leopard Trail Run is a two-day event, run on the outskirts of the camp, starting and ending at the Cedar Falls Home Base. ANNEXURE B shows the map of the Leopard Trail Run route: two loops joined at the Cedar Falls Home Base — the western loop is labelled Day 2 with Rhebok Valley, Gabriel's Pools and Reflection Pools, and the eastern loop is labelled Day 1 with Birdsong Valley, Fond Farewell, The Cauldron, Honeybush Hill and Inconvenient Truth. Use ANNEXURE B. QUESTION: Write down the day during the run when you would pass Gabriel's Pools.

## Method

read feature from map

## Memo working

Find Gabriel's Pools on the route map: it lies on the western loop. The western loop is the one labelled Day 2, so a runner passes Gabriel's Pools on Day 2 of the run. (Memo: 2A correct description.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Day 2
