## Question (2 marks)

(Context: ANNEXURE C shows a map of central South Africa with the tracked flight of a GPS-fitted flamingo.) Hanover is a town in the Northern Cape, while Middelburg is a town in the Eastern Cape. Describe how the provincial borders are indicated on this map.

**Note: ANNEXURE C is printed in the 2025 Special Answer Book, which is not in the archive; the map description is reconstructed from the marking guidelines. The memo answer is definitive.**

## Method

describe map symbol

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Compare the line style used between the two provinces with the other lines on the map: the provincial border is not a solid line.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Provincial borders are indicated by a broken line / dotted line / dashed line.
