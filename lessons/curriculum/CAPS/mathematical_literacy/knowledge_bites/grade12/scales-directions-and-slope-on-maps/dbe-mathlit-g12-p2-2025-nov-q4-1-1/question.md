## Question (2 marks)

A flamingo (bird) was fitted with a Global Positioning System (GPS) tracking device. ANNEXURE C in the ANSWER BOOK shows the map with the tracked flight of the flamingo, starting at point A and ending at point E. (The map covers central South Africa and shows towns including Colesberg, De Aar — directly west of Colesberg — Kimberley, Britstown, Hanover, Middelburg and Queenstown, with numbered national roads.) Use ANNEXURE C: name the closest town directly west of Colesberg.

**Note: ANNEXURE C is printed in the 2025 Special Answer Book, which is not in the archive; the map description above is reconstructed from the marking guidelines. The memo answer is definitive.**

## Method

read location from map

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) On the map, west is towards the left-hand side. Reading directly left from Colesberg, the nearest town is De Aar.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

De Aar
