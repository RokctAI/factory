## Question (2 marks)

ANNEXURE A shows an aerial view of the Baviaanskloof Uitspan camping site (campsite huts and camper numbers: 1: 6; 2A: 15, 2B: 4; 3A: 15, 3B: 4, 3C: 4; 4: 5; 5A: 15, 5B: 8; 6: 10; 7: 4-6; 8: 4-6; 9: 20; the dam lies immediately beside campsites 2 and 9). The description below is found on their website: "This campsite is situated near the dam and can accommodate a maximum of 19 campers." Write down which campsite is being described. Write down only the number of the campsite.

## Method

identify site from description

## Memo working

Two clues must BOTH be satisfied: the campsite is near the dam, and its maximum capacity is 19. The campsites beside the dam are 2 and 9. Campsite 9 holds 20 campers, so it does not fit. Campsite 2 has two huts, 2A taking 15 campers and 2B taking 4, so together 15 + 4 = 19 campers — it matches both clues. (Memo: 2O identifying correct site.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

2
