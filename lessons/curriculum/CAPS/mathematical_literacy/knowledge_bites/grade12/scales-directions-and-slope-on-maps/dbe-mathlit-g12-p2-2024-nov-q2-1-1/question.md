## Question (2 marks)

John and five other family members decided to participate in the Baviaanskloof Leopard Trail Run and to camp at the Baviaanskloof Uitspan camping site. ANNEXURE A shows an aerial view of Baviaanskloof Uitspan with campsites and the number of campers allowed (an aerial photograph with a north arrow, numbered campsites 1-9, the dam, the ablution facilities, the main house and the Houtkloof mountains). Use ANNEXURE A and the information above. QUESTION: Explain what is meant by aerial view.

## Method

define aerial view

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

View from the top. OR View of the landscape from a certain height above ground like from a satellite or drone. OR View from an elevated height. OR Birds-eye view. (Memo: 2A correct explanation.)
