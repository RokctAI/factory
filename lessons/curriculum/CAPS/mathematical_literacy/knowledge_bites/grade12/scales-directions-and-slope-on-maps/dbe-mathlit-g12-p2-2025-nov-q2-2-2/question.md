## Question (2 marks)

ANNEXURE A in the ANSWER BOOK shows a route map of a round trip and the 18 stops made by a tour bus (Cape Town tour-bus route map; the annexure is printed in the 2025 Special Answer Book). Name the type of scale used on this map.

## Method

identify scale type

## Memo working

The scale on this map is not written as a number ratio like 1 : 50 000. Instead it is drawn as a marked line segment labelled with the real distance that length represents (a measured 13 mm on the printed scale stands for 500 m). A scale drawn as a labelled line is called a bar scale — also known as a line scale, linear scale or graphic scale. (Memo: 2RT correct scale.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Bar scale / Line scale / Linear scale / Graphic scale
