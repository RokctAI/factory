## Question (2 marks)

The material list of the fold-up beach chair reads: 4 chair supports of 1,9 x 3,8 x 66 cm; 13 crosspieces of 1,9 x 3,8 x 36 cm; 1 seat back, bottom crosspiece of 1,9 x 3,8 x 32 cm. Name the item in the material list with the longest dimension.

## Method

identify max dimension item

## Memo working

Compare the largest measurement of each item: the chair support is 66 cm long, the crosspieces are 36 cm and the seat back bottom crosspiece is 32 cm. The biggest of these is 66 cm, so the chair support is the item with the longest dimension. (Memo: 2RT correct option — 66 cm is the longest dimension.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Chair support
