## Question (2 marks)

ANNEXURE B, GRAPH 2 shows the trends in the total number of employees at Shoprite and Pick n Pay stores for the period 2005 to 2023 (line graph; the Pick n Pay line rises gently from about 60 000 employees in 2005 to about 73 000 in 2014, drops to about 70 000 the following year, then rises again to plateau at 90 000 in 2023). Identify the year in which there was a decrease in the number of employees in the Pick n Pay stores for the first time.

## Method

read trend from line graph

## Memo working

Trace the Pick n Pay line from 2005 onwards: it climbs steadily until 2014, then dips for the first time in the next year, 2015, before recovering. So the first year showing a decrease is 2015. (Memo: 2RT correct year.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

2015
