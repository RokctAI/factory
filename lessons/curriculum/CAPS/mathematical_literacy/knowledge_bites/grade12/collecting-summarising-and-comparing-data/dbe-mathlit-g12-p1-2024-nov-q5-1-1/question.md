## Question (2 marks)

The pie charts on ANNEXURE C show the Indian Union budgets for income and expenditure for 2023/2024 (both totalling 45,03 lakh crores). Choose the correct word from those given in brackets. Write down only the word next to the question number. A budget is a (deficit/surplus/balanced) budget when the income is less than the expenditure.

## Method

budget terminology choice

## Memo working

Match the situation to the budget terminology: when income is less than expenditure the budget falls short — a deficit. (A surplus is when income exceeds expenditure, and a balanced budget is when the two are equal.) (2A correct word)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Deficit
