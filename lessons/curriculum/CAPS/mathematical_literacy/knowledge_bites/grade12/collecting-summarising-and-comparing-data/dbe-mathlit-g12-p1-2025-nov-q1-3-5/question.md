## Question (2 marks)

TABLE 3 shows the population data for South Africa by population group and gender for 2022. The total number of black South Africans is forty-nine million seventy thousand eight hundred and nine. Write this number in numerals without using words.

## Method

words to numerals

## Memo working

Break the number words into place-value groups: forty-nine million = 49 000 000; seventy thousand = 70 000; eight hundred and nine = 809. Add the groups: 49 000 000 + 70 000 + 809 = 49 070 809. Note the zero in the hundred-thousands place and the zero in the tens place — the words skip those place values, so numerals must show them as 0. (Memo: 2A correct number.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

49 070 809
