## Question (2 marks)

TABLE 7 shows the VAT payments made to SARS by vendors in certain economic sectors for 2023/2024. The number of vendors per sector reads: Financial 197 178; Manufacturing 46 901; Wholesale and retail trade 86 610; Construction 39 279; Transport 15 258; Community and social 33 825; Utilities 2 658. Write down the sector that has the third largest number of vendors.

## Method

rank values in table

## Memo working

2A correct sector

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Manufacturing
