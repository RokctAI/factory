## Question (2 marks)

School X and School Y conducted an election for new members to serve on the school governing body (SGB). A dual bar graph shows the number of voters voting during different time slots at School X and School Y (School Y bars read: 15:00-15:30 = 18; 15:30-16:00 = 12; 16:00-16:30 = 5; 16:30-17:00 = 9; 17:00-17:30 = 26; 17:30-18:00 = 24; 18:00-18:30 = 5; 18:30-19:00 = 2). Write down the number of voters at School Y from 16:00-16:30.

## Method

read value from bar graph

## Memo working

Find the 16:00-16:30 time slot on the horizontal axis, then read the height of the School Y bar in that slot: it reaches 5 on the vertical "Number of voters" axis. (Memo: 2A correct number; answer only full marks.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

5 / Five
