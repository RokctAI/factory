## Question (4 marks)

TABLE 5 shows the use of popular social media platforms categorised by gender. The percentage of male users per platform is: Facebook 57%; Instagram 51%; Snapchat 46%; X/Twitter 56%; TikTok 43%; YouTube 54%. Calculate the median percentage for male social media users.

## Method

median of data

## Memo working

The median is the middle value of an ordered data set, so first arrange the six percentages in ascending order: 43% 46% 51% 54% 56% 57% (1MA arranging values). With an even number of values (six), the median is the average of the two middle values, the third and fourth: Median = (51% + 54%)/2 (1RT 51% and 54%, 1MA concept of median) = 52,5% (1CA simplification). AO

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

52,5%
