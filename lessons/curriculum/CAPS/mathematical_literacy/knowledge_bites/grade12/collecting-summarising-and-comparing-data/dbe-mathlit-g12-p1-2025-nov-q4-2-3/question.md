## Question (3 marks)

TABLE 7 shows the VAT payments made to SARS by vendors in certain economic sectors for 2023/2024. The VAT payments per sector, in R million, read: Financial 221 322; Manufacturing 73 893; Wholesale and retail trade 76 826; Construction 25 593; Transport 29 418; Community and social 32 458; Utilities 22 141. Calculate (in R million) the mean VAT payment amount per economic sector.

## Method

mean of data

## Memo working

Mean = (221 322 + 73 893 + 76 826 + 25 593 + 29 418 + 32 458 + 22 141)/7 (1MA adding values) = 481 651/7 (1MA concept of mean) = 68 807,29 (1CA simplify). NPU

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

68 807,29 (R million)
