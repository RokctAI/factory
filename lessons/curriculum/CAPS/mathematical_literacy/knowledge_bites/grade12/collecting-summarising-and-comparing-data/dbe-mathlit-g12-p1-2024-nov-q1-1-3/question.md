## Question (2 marks)

School X and School Y conducted an SGB election. A dual bar graph shows the number of voters per time slot at each school (School Y bars, in time order from 15:00 to 19:00: 18; 12; 5; 9; 26; 24; 5; 2). Choose the statement (A-C) below that BEST describes the trend of the voters at School Y. A: As time went by, the voter numbers increased to a maximum and then decreased. B: The voter numbers started high, then decreased and then picked up again. C: The number of voters decreased, then increased and finally decreased with time.

## Method

identify trend multiple choice

## Memo working

Follow the School Y bars through the evening: they fall from 18 to 12 to 5 (a decrease), then climb from 5 through 9 to a peak of 26 (an increase), then fall again through 24 and 5 down to 2 (a final decrease). Decrease, then increase, then decrease matches statement C. Statement A misses the opening decrease, and statement B misses the final decrease. (Memo: 2A correct option.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C
