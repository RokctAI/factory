## Question (2 marks)

TABLE 3 shows the population data for South Africa by population group and gender for 2022 (adapted from www.statssa.gov.za). Name the type of instrument used to collect the data above.

## Method

identify collection instrument

## Memo working

Population data like this is gathered directly from people, using a data-collection instrument. The memo accepts any one of the standard instruments: a questionnaire, a survey, a census or an interview. (For national population data specifically, Statistics South Africa uses a census — a survey of the whole population.) (Memo: 2A correct instrument.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Questionnaire / Survey / Census / Interview
