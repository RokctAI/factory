## Question (3 marks)

Shown below, in rank order, is the time spent (in minutes) by 32 customers in a store, together with an incomplete box-and-whisker plot of the same data (drawn on a 0-140 number line with minimum 6, lower quartile 15 and maximum 127 marked; the upper quartile is unlabelled). Ranked data: 6 8 9 10 12 15 15 15 15 15 17 / 18 19 22 23 23 23 24 25 25 25 26 / 27 28 29 33 37 40 43 43 67 127. (a) Calculate the value of the upper quartile (Q3).

## Method

calculate upper quartile

## Memo working

With 32 values, the upper quartile is the median of the top half of the ordered data, which falls between the 24th and 25th values. Counting along the ranked list, the 24th value is 28 and the 25th value is 29. Quartile 3 = (28 + 29)/2 (1RT correct values, 1MA concept of quartile) = 28,5 (1CA simplification). AO

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

28,5
