## Question (2 marks)

There has been an increase in the number of long-term evolution (LTE) devices in South Africa over the last three years. ANSWER SHEET 3.2 in the ANSWER BOOK shows a graph for the number of LTE devices per province over a three-year period; some of the information on the graph has been omitted. (The graph shows one bar for each of the nine provinces, and each bar is built up of three portions — for 2021, 2022 and 2023 — placed one on top of the other, with the vertical axis counting the number of devices in gridline steps of 10 000 000 up to 60 000 000.) NOTE: LTE is a term used for a particular type of 4G that delivers a fast mobile internet experience. Name the type of graph drawn on ANSWER SHEET 3.2.

## Method

identify graph type

## Memo working

2A correct graph

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Stacked bar graph OR Compound bar graph
