## Question (3 marks)

The income pie chart on ANNEXURE C shows the Indian Union Budget income sources in 2023-2024: Borrowings 34%; Corporation tax 15%; Income tax 15%; GST 17% (calculated in the previous part); Excise duties 7%; Non-tax receipts 6%; Customs 4%; Non-debt capital receipts 2%. List THREE sources of income shown on the pie chart where the total percentages add up to the borrowing percentage value.

## Method

combine percentages to target

## Memo working

The target is the Borrowings slice, 34%. Look for three slices whose percentages sum to exactly 34: Corporation tax 15% + Income tax 15% + Customs 4% = 34%. (1RT correct source; 1RT correct source; 1RT correct source adding to 34%.) (Memo alternatives: Corporation tax 15% + GST 17% + Non-debt capital receipts 2% = 34%; or Income tax 15% + GST 17% + Non-debt capital receipts 2% = 34%. CA from Question 5.1.2 for GST.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Corporation tax + Income tax + Customs (15% + 15% + 4% = 34%). OR Corporation tax + GST + Non Debt Capital Receipts (15% + 17% + 2% = 34%). OR Income tax + GST + Non Debt Capital Receipts (15% + 17% + 2% = 34%).
