## Question (5 marks)

TABLE 6 shows the actual number of guests per function at a hotel during a specific month — 2023: 180, 350, 400, 200, 215, 90, 150, 230, 160; 2022: 360, 160, 300, 70, 160, 200, 240. The manager stated that the range of the number of guests per function during 2022 is the same as the range of the number of guests per function during 2023. Verify, showing ALL calculations, whether his statement is valid.

## Method

range comparison verification

## Memo working

The range is the highest value minus the lowest value. For 2022 the highest is 360 and the lowest is 70: Range 2022 = 360 - 70 (1RT both correct values, 1MCA concept of range) = 290 (1CA). For 2023 the highest is 400 and the lowest is 90: Range 2023 = 400 - 90 = 310 (1A range). Since 290 is not equal to 310, the two ranges differ, so the manager's statement is NOT VALID (1O conclusion).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Range 2022 = 360 - 70 = 290; Range 2023 = 400 - 90 = 310. His statement is NOT VALID.
