## Question (3 marks)

A study relating to the amount of time spent (in minutes) by a selected number of customers in a store was conducted on a particular Saturday. On that Saturday, a total of 12 342 customers visited the store. The recorded times are (32 values in a data table): 12 15 25 25 40 28 18 23 / 43 15 22 19 15 8 37 9 / 24 23 15 67 25 29 6 17 / 15 43 23 27 33 26 127 10. Write down the number of customers that represent the sample size and the number of customers that represent the population size for this study.

## Method

identify sample and population

## Memo working

The sample is the group actually measured: counting the recorded times gives 32 customers, so Sample = 32 (1A counting to 32, 1A sample). The population is the whole group the study is about: every customer who visited the store that Saturday, so Population = 12 342 (1A correct population). (Memo also accepts: 32 and 12 342 stated in the correct order without the words.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Sample = 32; Population = 12 342
