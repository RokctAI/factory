## Question (3 marks)

The expenditure pie chart on ANNEXURE C shows the Indian Union Budget 2023-2024 expenditure of 45,03 lakh crores, with Defence taking an 8% slice. Calculate, in lakh crores, the amount spent on defence in India.

## Method

percentage of amount

## Memo working

The defence amount is 8% of the total expenditure. Defence = 8% x 45,03 lakh crore (1RT correct percentage, 1MA multiply by 45,03) = 0,08 x 45,03 = 3,6024 lakh crore (1CA simplification). NPR, AO

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

3,6024 lakh crore
