## Question (4 marks)

TABLE 5 shows the use of popular social media platforms categorised by gender. For Snapchat the table gives 46% male users (number omitted) and 54% female users, numbering 405 million. From part (a), the total number of Snapchat users is 750 000 000. (b) Fezeka stated that the difference between the number of female and male Snapchat users was 61 300 000. Verify, showing ALL calculations, whether her statement is VALID.

## Method

difference verification

## Memo working

Male users = 750 000 000 x 46/100 (1MCA calculating 46%) = 345 000 000 (1CA). Difference = 405 000 000 - 345 000 000 = 60 000 000 (1CA difference). NOT VALID (1O conclusion). OR percentage difference = 54% - 46% = 8% (1CA); 8% x 750 million (1MCA) = 60 000 000 (1CA); NOT VALID (1O). CA from Question 3.1.3(a)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Male users = 345 000 000; difference = 405 000 000 - 345 000 000 = 60 000 000. Her statement is NOT VALID.
