## Question (5 marks)

The time spent (in minutes) by 32 customers in a store is given in rank order: 6 8 9 10 12 15 15 15 15 15 17 / 18 19 22 23 23 23 24 25 25 25 26 / 27 28 29 33 37 40 43 43 67 127. The value 127 is an outlier. (b) Vuyo stated that if the outlier value was removed from the data set, the new interquartile range (IQR) value would be 13. Verify Vuyo's statement, showing ALL calculations.

## Method

interquartile range verification

## Memo working

Remove the outlier 127, leaving 31 ordered values. The median is now the 16th value, the lower quartile is the middle of the bottom 15 values (the 8th value) and the upper quartile is the middle of the top 15 values (the 24th value). Counting along the list: New Quartile 1 = 15 (1RT); New Quartile 3 = 28 (1RT). Then apply the formula IQR = Q3 - Q1 (1A correct formula): IQR = 28 - 15 (1MCA subtracting values) = 13. This matches Vuyo's value, so he is CORRECT (1O conclusion).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

New Q1 = 15, new Q3 = 28, IQR = 28 - 15 = 13. He is CORRECT.
