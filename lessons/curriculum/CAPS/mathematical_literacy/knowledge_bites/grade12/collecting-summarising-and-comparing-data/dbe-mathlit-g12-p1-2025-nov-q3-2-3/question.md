## Question (4 marks)

ANSWER SHEET 3.2 shows a stacked bar graph of the number of LTE devices per province in South Africa, stacked by year 2021/2022/2023; the KwaZulu-Natal and Mpumalanga bars are incomplete because their 2023 values were omitted. The data below represents the number of LTE devices for two provinces during 2023 that has been omitted in the graph: KwaZulu-Natal 11 000 000; Mpumalanga 5 500 000. Use the data above to complete the graph on ANSWER SHEET 3.2. (On the answer-sheet graph, KwaZulu-Natal's stack reaches 20 500 000 after 2022 and Mpumalanga's reaches 8 500 000 after 2022, so completing each bar means drawing the 2023 portion on top of those heights.)

**Note: ANSWER SHEET 3.2 lives in the 2025 Special Answer Book, which is not in the repo. The cumulative 2021+2022 readings (KwaZulu-Natal 20 500 000; Mpumalanga 8 500 000) are implied by the marking guidelines' end points and cannot be independently verified until the answer book arrives; the addition itself is verified.**

## Method

complete stacked bar graph

## Memo working

1A KwaZulu-Natal end point 31 500 000; 1A KwaZulu-Natal complete stacked bar graph; 1A Mpumalanga end point 14 000 000; 1A Mpumalanga complete stacked bar graph

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

[Drawn on graph] KwaZulu-Natal stack completed to end point 31 500 000; Mpumalanga stack completed to end point 14 000 000.
