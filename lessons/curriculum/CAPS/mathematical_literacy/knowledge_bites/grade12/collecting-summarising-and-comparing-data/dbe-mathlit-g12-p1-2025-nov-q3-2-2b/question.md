## Question (3 marks)

ANSWER SHEET 3.2 shows a stacked bar graph of the number of LTE devices per province in South Africa, one bar per province stacked by year 2021/2022/2023. On the graph, Limpopo's bar reads 4 000 000 at the top of the 2021 portion and 11 000 000 at the top of the 2022 portion, and the full three-year stack ends at 18 500 000. (b) Determine the number of LTE devices in Limpopo during 2022.

**Note: ANSWER SHEET 3.2 lives in the 2025 Special Answer Book, which is not in the repo. The graph readings above (4 000 000; 11 000 000; 18 500 000; 2023 portion 7 500 000) are reconstructed from the marking guidelines and cannot be independently verified until the answer book arrives; the subtraction itself is verified.**

## Method

difference of cumulative graph values

## Memo working

Number of devices in Limpopo 2022 = 11 000 000 - 4 000 000 (1RT both correct values, 1MA subtracting values) = 7 000 000 (1CA simplification). OR 18 500 000 - 4 000 000 - 7 500 000 = 7 000 000. AO

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

7 000 000 OR 7 million
