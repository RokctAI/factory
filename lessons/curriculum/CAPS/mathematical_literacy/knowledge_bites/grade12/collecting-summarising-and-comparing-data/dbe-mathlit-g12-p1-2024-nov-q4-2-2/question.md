## Question (3 marks)

TABLE 6 shows the actual number of guests per function at a hotel's three smaller venues during a specific month in 2023: 180, 350, 400, 200, 215, 90, 150, 230, 160. Determine the median number of guests accommodated during 2023.

## Method

median of ordered data

## Memo working

First arrange the nine values in ascending order: 90 150 160 180 200 215 230 350 400 (1A arranging). With nine values, the median is the middle (fifth) value — four values lie below it and four above it. Counting to the fifth value gives Median = 200 (2A median). AO

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

200
