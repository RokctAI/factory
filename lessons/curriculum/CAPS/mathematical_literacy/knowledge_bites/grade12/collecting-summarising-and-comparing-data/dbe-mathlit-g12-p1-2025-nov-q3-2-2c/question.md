## Question (4 marks)

ANSWER SHEET 3.2 shows a stacked bar graph of the number of LTE devices per province in South Africa for 2021 to 2023. Reading the 2021 portions of the nine provincial bars, the highest 2021 value on the graph is 16 000 000 devices and the lowest is 1 000 000 devices. (c) Calculate the range of LTE devices in the provinces during 2021.

**Note: ANSWER SHEET 3.2 lives in the 2025 Special Answer Book, which is not in the repo. The graph readings above (highest 16 000 000; lowest 1 000 000 in 2021) are reconstructed from the marking guidelines and cannot be independently verified until the answer book arrives; the range calculation itself is verified.**

## Method

range from graph values

## Memo working

Range = Highest Value - Lowest Value = 16 000 000 (1RT) - 1 000 000 (1RT) (1MA concept of range) = 15 000 000 (1CA simplification). AO

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

15 000 000 OR 15 million
