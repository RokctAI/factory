## Question (3 marks)

TABLE 3 shows the population data for South Africa by population group and gender for 2022; there are 2 242 589 white males, and the total number of males is 29 624 882. Express the number of white males as a percentage of the total number of males.

## Method

percentage of total

## Memo working

Percentage = part divided by whole, times 100. Here the part is the number of white males and the whole is the total number of males: Percentage = 2 242 589 / 29 624 882 × 100% (1RT both correct values, 1MA percentage calculation) = 7,569950827% (1A simplification).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

7,569950827%
