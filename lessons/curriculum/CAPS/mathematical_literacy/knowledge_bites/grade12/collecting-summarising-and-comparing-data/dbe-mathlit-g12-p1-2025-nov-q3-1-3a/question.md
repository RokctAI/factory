## Question (2 marks)

TABLE 5 shows the use of popular social media platforms categorised by gender: for each platform it lists the percentage of male users, the number of male users in millions, the percentage of female users and the number of female users in millions (Facebook 57% male, 1 732,8 million male, 43% female, 1 307,2 million female; Instagram 51% and 1 020 million male, 49% and 980 million female; Snapchat 46% male with the number of male users omitted, 54% female and 405 million female; X/Twitter 56% and 346,64 million male, 44% and 272,36 million female; TikTok 43% and 645 million male, 57% and 855 million female; YouTube 54% and 1 350 million male, 46% and 1 150 million female). Fezeka is interested in the number of male and female Snapchat users. (a) Determine the total number of Snapchat users.

## Method

total from percentage part

## Memo working

Total = 405 000 000 x 100/54 (1MA percentage calculation) = 750 000 000 (1CA simplification). OR male users = 46/54 x 405 000 000 = 345 000 000 (1A); total = 405 000 000 + 345 000 000 = 750 000 000 (1CA). AO

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

750 000 000 / 750 million
