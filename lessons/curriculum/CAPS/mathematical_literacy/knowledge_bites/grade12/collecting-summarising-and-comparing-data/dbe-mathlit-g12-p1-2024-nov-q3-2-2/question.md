## Question (2 marks)

Choose the correct option (A, B, C, D or E) that represents the stage in the data handling process that uses the measures of central tendency and spread. A: Collecting data. B: Classifying and organising data. C: Summarising data. D: Representing data. E: Analysing data.

## Method

data handling process multiple choice

## Memo working

Work through the data handling cycle: data is first collected, then classified and organised, then summarised, then represented in graphs or tables, and finally analysed. The measures of central tendency (mean, median, mode) and spread (range, quartiles) are the tools used when analysing the data to draw conclusions from it. (2A correct option)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Option E
