## Question (3 marks)

TABLE 3 gives the area sizes of the mainland, islands and total for each Australian state or territory. The total island area of Australia is 32 159 km^2, and the mainland area of Tasmania is 64 519 km^2. Show, with calculations, why it could be stated that the total area of the islands of Australia is approximately half of the area of the mainland area of Tasmania.

## Method

ratio verification

## Memo working

Divide the total island area by Tasmania's mainland area: 32 159 ÷ 64 519 = 0,498, which is approximately 0,5 — that is, about half. (Memo alternatives: half of Tasmania's mainland = 64 519 x 1/2 = 32 259,5 km^2, which is very close to the island total of 32 159 km^2; or as a ratio, 64 519 : 32 159 ≈ 2 : 1.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

32 159 / 64 519 = 0,498 ≈ 0,5
