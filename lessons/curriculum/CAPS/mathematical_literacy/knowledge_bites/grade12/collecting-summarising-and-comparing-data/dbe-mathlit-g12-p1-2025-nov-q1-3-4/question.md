## Question (2 marks)

TABLE 3 shows the population data for South Africa by population group and gender for 2022; its TOTAL row reads 29 624 882 males, 30 980 110 females and 60 604 992 people in total. Identify the total number of the South African population for 2022.

## Method

read value from table

## Memo working

Go to the TOTAL row of the table and read the value in the total-number-of-people column: 60 604 992. (Check: 29 624 882 males + 30 980 110 females = 60 604 992.) (Memo: 2RT correct population total.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

60 604 992
