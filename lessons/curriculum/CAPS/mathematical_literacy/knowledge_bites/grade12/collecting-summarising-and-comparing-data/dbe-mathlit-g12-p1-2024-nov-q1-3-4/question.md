## Question (2 marks)

TABLE 2 gives definitions of terminology used in Mathematical Literacy (A: an increase in the price of a basket of goods or services over time that is representative of the economy as a whole; B: sum of all the values divided by the number of values; C: the income received from the item sold and the cost price of the item is the same; D: value or values appearing most often in a data set; E: the difference between the highest and lowest values in a data set; F: an amount paid regularly at a particular rate for the use/loan of money; G: amounts that must be paid every month and which stay the same, like rent, school fees and transport costs; H: middle value in an ordered data set; I: expenses that change over time or from one week/month to the next, e.g. telephone and electricity costs; J: the point where the income exceeds the expenses). Match the definition with the term: Mean. Write only the letter (A-J).

## Method

match definition

## Memo working

The mean (average) is found by adding every value in the data set and dividing by how many values there are. That is definition B, "sum of all the values divided by the number of values". Keep it apart from the other averages in the table: D defines the mode (most frequent value) and H defines the median (middle value when ordered). (Memo: 2A correct letter.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

B
