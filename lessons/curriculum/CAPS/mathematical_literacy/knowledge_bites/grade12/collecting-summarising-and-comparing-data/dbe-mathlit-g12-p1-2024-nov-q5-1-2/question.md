## Question (3 marks)

The income pie chart on ANNEXURE C shows the Indian Union Budget income sources in 2023-2024 (45,03 lakh crores) with slices: Borrowings 34%; Corporation tax 15%; Income tax 15%; Excise duties 7%; Non-tax receipts 6%; Customs 4%; Non-debt capital receipts 2%; GST with its percentage omitted. General sales tax (GST) is a tax that is similar to VAT in South Africa. Calculate the missing percentage for GST on the pie chart.

## Method

missing percentage from pie chart

## Memo working

All the slices of a pie chart together make 100%, so the GST slice is 100% minus everything else. GST % = 100% - (15% + 15% + 4% + 7% + 6% + 2% + 34%) (1RT ALL correct values, 1MA adding and subtracting) = 100% - 83% = 17% (1CA simplification). AO

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

17%
