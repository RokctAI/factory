## Question (2 marks)

School X and School Y conducted an SGB election. A dual bar graph shows the number of voters per time slot at each school (School X bars read: no voters before 16:00; 16:00-16:30 = 10; 16:30-17:00 = 12; 17:00-17:30 = 26; 17:30-18:00 = 30; 18:00-18:30 = 18; 18:30-19:00 = 8). Identify the time slot when 30 people voted at School X.

## Method

read value from bar graph

## Memo working

Scan the School X bars for the one that reaches 30 on the vertical axis: only one bar does, and it sits in the 17:30-18:00 time slot. (Memo: 1A for 17:30 / 5:30 pm / half past five; 1A for 18:00 / 6:00 pm / 6 o'clock.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

17:30 - 18:00 (also accepted: 5:30 pm - 6:00 pm; Half past five until 6 o'clock in the afternoon/evening)
