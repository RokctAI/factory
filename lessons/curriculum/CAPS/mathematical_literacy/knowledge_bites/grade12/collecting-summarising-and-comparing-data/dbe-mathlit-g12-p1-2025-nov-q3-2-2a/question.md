## Question (2 marks)

ANSWER SHEET 3.2 shows a stacked bar graph of the number of LTE devices per province in South Africa for 2021 to 2023 — one bar per province, each bar stacked by year, with the 2021 portion at the bottom of each stack. (a) Use the graph on ANSWER SHEET 3.2 to write down the names of TWO provinces that had the same number of LTE devices during 2021. (ANSWER SHEET 3.2 is printed in the 2025 Special Answer Book, which is not in the repo; on the graph, two pairs of provinces start their stacks at the same 2021 height.)

## Method

read equal values from graph

## Memo working

2A correct pair of provinces

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Limpopo (LP) and North West (NW) OR Mpumalanga (MP) and Eastern Cape (EC)
