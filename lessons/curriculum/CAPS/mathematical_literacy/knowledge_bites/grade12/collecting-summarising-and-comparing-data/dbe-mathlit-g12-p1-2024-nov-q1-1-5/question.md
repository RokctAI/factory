## Question (3 marks)

School X and School Y conducted an SGB election. A dual bar graph shows the number of voters per time slot at each school (in the 17:00-17:30 time slot both bars read 26: School X = 26 and School Y = 26). Calculate the total number of voters at both schools who voted during the 17:00-17:30 time slot.

## Method

read and add graph values

## Memo working

Read the 17:00-17:30 bar for each school: School X = 26 voters and School Y = 26 voters (1RT correct values). Add them: Total number = 26 + 26 (1MA adding correct values) = 52 (1A simplification).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

52
