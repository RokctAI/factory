## Question (2 marks)

TABLE 3 shows the population data for South Africa by population group and gender for 2022. The number of females per group is: Black 25 085 330; Coloured 2 737 987; Indian/Asian 760 114; White 2 396 679. Write down the population group with the lowest number of females.

## Method

read value from table

## Memo working

Compare the values in the "number of females" column: Black 25 085 330, Coloured 2 737 987, Indian/Asian 760 114 and White 2 396 679. The smallest of these is 760 114, in the Indian/Asian row. (Memo: 2RT correct population.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Indian / Asian
