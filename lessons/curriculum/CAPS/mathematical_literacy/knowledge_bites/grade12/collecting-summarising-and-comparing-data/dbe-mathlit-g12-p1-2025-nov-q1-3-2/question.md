## Question (2 marks)

TABLE 3 shows the population data for South Africa by population group and gender for 2022, with a total of 60 604 992 people. State whether the total number of people in the table represents discrete data or continuous data.

## Method

classify data type

## Memo working

A number of people is a count: it can only take whole-number values — you cannot have a fraction of a person. Counted whole-number data is discrete; continuous data comes from measurement and can take any value in a range (like height or mass). So the total number of people is discrete data. (Memo: 2A correct classification.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Discrete
