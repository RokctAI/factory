## Question (4 marks)

(Context: a GPS-tracked flamingo flew non-stop from area B to area E in 14 hours and 25 minutes.) The flight from area B to area E is 770 km. Calculate (in km/h) the average speed of the bird as it flew from area B to area E. You may use this formula: Distance = speed x time

## Method

speed from distance and time

## Memo working

Substitute into the formula: 770 km = speed x 14 hours 25 min. Change the subject: speed = 770 km ÷ 14 hours 25 min. Convert the minutes to a decimal of an hour: 25 min = 25/60 hour, so the time is 14,4166... hours. Speed = 770 ÷ 14,4166... = 53,41 km/h.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

53,41 km/h
