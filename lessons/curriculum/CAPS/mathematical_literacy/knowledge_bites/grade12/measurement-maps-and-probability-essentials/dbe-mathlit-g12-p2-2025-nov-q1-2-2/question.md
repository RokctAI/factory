## Question (2 marks)

Shown below is the information regarding a South African Airways flight from Cape Town to Johannesburg (flight information card: Wednesday 8 January 2025; distance 1 278 km; maximum number of passengers 189; departure 12:15 Cape Town; arrival 14:15 Johannesburg). Only 33 1/3 % of the maximum number of passengers were on board this flight. Calculate the number of passengers on this flight.

## Method

percentage of amount

## Memo working

Number of passengers = 33 1/3 % x 189 (1MA multiply with 33 1/3 %) = 63 (1A simplification). The quick route: 33 1/3 % is exactly one third, so 1/3 x 189 = 63. (Memo option 2: 1/3 x 189 = 63.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

63
