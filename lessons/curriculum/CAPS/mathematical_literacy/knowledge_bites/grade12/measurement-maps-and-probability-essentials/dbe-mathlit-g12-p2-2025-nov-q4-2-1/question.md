## Question (2 marks)

On 13 January 2022, a GPS-tracked flamingo was in area A on the map. On 13 June 2023, it started moving southwards from area A, the starting point of tracking the bird. Write down the number of months the bird spent in area A.

## Method

months between dates

## Memo working

From 13 January 2022 to 13 January 2023 is 12 months. From 13 January 2023 to 13 June 2023 is another 5 months. Total = 12 + 5 = 17 months.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

17 months
