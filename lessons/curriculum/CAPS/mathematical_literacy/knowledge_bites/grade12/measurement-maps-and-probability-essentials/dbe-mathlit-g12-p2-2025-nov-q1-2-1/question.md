## Question (2 marks)

Shown below is the information regarding a South African Airways flight from Cape Town to Johannesburg (flight information card: Wednesday 8 January 2025; distance 1 278 km; aircraft Boeing 737-800; codeshare NZ 3120; flight number SA332; maximum number of passengers 189; departure 12:15 Cape Town; arrival 14:15 Johannesburg). State, in words, the arrival time of the flight using the 12-hour format.

## Method

convert time to words 12 hour

## Memo working

The arrival time on the card is 14:15 in the 24-hour format. Subtract 12 hours to move into the 12-hour format: 14:15 becomes 2:15. Since it is after midday, it is in the afternoon. Fifteen minutes past the hour is "quarter past", so 2:15 in the afternoon is quarter past two in the afternoon. (Memo: 1RT correct time in words; 1A correct time of day.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Quarter past two in the afternoon. OR Fifteen minutes after two in the afternoon.
