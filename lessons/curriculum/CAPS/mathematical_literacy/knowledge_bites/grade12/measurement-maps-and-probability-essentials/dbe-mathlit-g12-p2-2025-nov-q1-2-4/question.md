## Question (2 marks)

Shown below is the information regarding a South African Airways flight from Cape Town to Johannesburg (flight information card: Wednesday 8 January 2025; departure 12:15 Cape Town; arrival 14:15 Johannesburg). State which day of the week was 26 December 2024.

## Method

calendar day calculation

## Memo working

Anchor on the date given on the flight card: Wednesday 8 January 2025. Step back in whole weeks — 7 days earlier, 1 January 2025, was also a Wednesday, and 7 days before that, 25 December 2024, was again a Wednesday. One day after 25 December is 26 December 2024, which is therefore a Thursday. (Memo: 2A correct day; verified against the real calendar.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Thursday
