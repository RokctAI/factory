## Question (5 marks)

(Context: a GPS-tracked flamingo flew 770 km from area B to area E.) The direct distance between area B and area E is 311,72 miles. The person tracking the bird's GPS stated that the bird flew an extra distance of 268,13 km when compared to the direct distance. 1 mile = 1,60934 km. Verify, with calculations, whether this statement is VALID.

## Method

mile conversion and difference verification

## Memo working

Convert the direct distance to kilometres: 311,72 miles x 1,60934 = 501,6634... km. Extra distance actually flown = 770 km - 501,6634... km = 268,336... km, which is not the claimed 268,13 km. The statement is INVALID. (Memo option 2: 501,6634... + 268,13 = 769,7934... km, which is not 770 km, so INVALID.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

311,72 miles = 311,72 x 1,60934 = 501,6634... km; extra distance = 770 - 501,6634... = 268,336... km, not 268,13 km. INVALID.
