## Question (2 marks)

Shown below is the information regarding a South African Airways flight from Cape Town to Johannesburg (flight information card: Wednesday 8 January 2025; distance 1 278 km; departure 12:15 Cape Town; arrival 14:15 Johannesburg). Write down which ONE of the methods below is the CORRECT method to calculate the speed in km/h of the aircraft. Write only the letter (A-C). A: 1 278 / 14:15. B: 1 278 / 2. C: 2 / 1 278.

## Method

select correct speed formula

## Memo working

Speed = distance ÷ time. The distance is 1 278 km. The time is NOT the clock reading 14:15 — it is the duration of the flight: from departure 12:15 to arrival 14:15 is 2 hours. So speed = 1 278 km ÷ 2 h, which is option B. Option A wrongly divides by a clock time, and option C divides the wrong way around (time by distance). (Memo: 2A correct letter.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

B
