## Question (2 marks)

Mrs Kgomo is a Grade 2 teacher at Happy Valley Primary School. She has a reading corner at the back of her classroom where learners who wait for their transport after school usually sit and read a book. The school day ends at 12:55. Tendani's father was late and only came to fetch him at 14:13. Determine how long Tendani sat in the reading corner.

## Method

elapsed time calculation

## Memo working

Work out the elapsed time from 12:55 to 14:13. A reliable way is to break it at the hour: from 12:55 to 13:00 is 5 minutes, and from 13:00 to 14:13 is 1 hour 13 minutes; together that is 1 hour 18 minutes. Equivalently, subtract the times directly: 14:13 - 12:55 = 1 hour 18 minutes, which is 78 minutes or 1,3 hours. (Memo: 1MA subtracting time; 1A simplification; answer only full marks.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

1 hour 18 minutes (OR 78 minutes OR 1,3 hours)
