## Question (3 marks)

(Context: Amanda Xulu's circular earrings — the radius of the large circle is 14 mm.) Calculate, rounded to the nearest mm, the circumference of the large circle. You may use the following formula: Circumference of circle = 3,142 x 2 x radius

## Method

circle circumference

## Memo working

Substitute the radius into the formula: Circumference = 3,142 x 2 x 14 mm = 87,976 mm. Rounded to the nearest millimetre this gives 88 mm.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

88 mm
