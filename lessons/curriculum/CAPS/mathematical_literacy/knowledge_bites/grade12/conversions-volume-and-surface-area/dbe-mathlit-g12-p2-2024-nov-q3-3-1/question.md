## Question (4 marks)

On each learner's birthday, the learner receives a cubic-shaped packet of homemade sweets (shown in a line drawing of a cube and a photo of a cube-shaped gift packet with handles). The volume of the cubic-shaped packet is 2 744 cm^3. Calculate the side length of the cubic-shaped packet. You may use the following formula: Volume of a cube = side x side x side

## Method

cube root from volume

## Memo working

Substitute the known volume into the formula: 2 744 cm^3 = side x side x side, so (side)^3 = 2 744 cm^3. Find the number that multiplied by itself three times gives 2 744: 14 x 14 x 14 = 2 744, so the side is 14 cm.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

14 cm
