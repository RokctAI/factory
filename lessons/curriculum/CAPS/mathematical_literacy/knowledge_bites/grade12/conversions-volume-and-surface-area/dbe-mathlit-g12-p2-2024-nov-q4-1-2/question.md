## Question (2 marks)

Crude oil is separated in a vertical, cylindrical tower (the tower diagram lists the products and their boiling temperatures from top to bottom: petroleum gas 20 °C, gasoline (petrol) 150 °C, kerosene (lamp oil) 200 °C, diesel 300 °C, industrial oil 370 °C, paraffin wax 400 °C). Write down the product extracted at 150 °C.

## Method

read value from diagram

## Memo working

Read the tower diagram at the 150 °C level: the product listed there is gasoline (petrol).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Gasoline or petrol
