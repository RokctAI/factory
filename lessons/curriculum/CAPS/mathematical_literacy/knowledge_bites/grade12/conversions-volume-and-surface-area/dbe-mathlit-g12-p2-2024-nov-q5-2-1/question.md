## Question (3 marks)

Chris and Boipelo plan to visit Uluru. This oval-shaped big rock rises 1 142 feet above the surrounding desert plain. A diagram compares the height of Uluru with other tourist attractions (Sydney Harbour Bridge 134 m; ULURU 348 m; The Eiffel Tower 324 m; The Great Pyramid of Giza 139 m; Big Ben 96 m). Determine, rounded to THREE decimal places, the conversion factor from feet to metres used in this diagram, in the form 1 foot = ... m.

## Method

derive conversion factor

## Memo working

The same height is given in both units: 1 142 feet (in the text) equals 348 m (in the diagram). To find what 1 foot is worth in metres, divide the metres by the feet: 1 foot = 348 ÷ 1 142 = 0,304728546..., which rounds to three decimal places as 0,305 m.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

1 foot ≈ 0,305 m
