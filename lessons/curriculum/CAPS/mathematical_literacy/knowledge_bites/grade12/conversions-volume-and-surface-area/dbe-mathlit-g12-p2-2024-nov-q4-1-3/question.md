## Question (4 marks)

In the crude oil distillation tower, diesel boils at 300 °C. Convert the boiling point of diesel to degrees Fahrenheit. Use the formula: °C = (°F - 32) / 1,8

## Method

celsius to fahrenheit conversion

## Memo working

The boiling point of diesel from the diagram is 300 °C. Substitute into the formula: 300 = (°F - 32) / 1,8. Change the subject of the formula to °F by multiplying both sides by 1,8 and then adding 32: °F = 1,8 x 300 + 32 = 540 + 32 = 572.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

572 °F
