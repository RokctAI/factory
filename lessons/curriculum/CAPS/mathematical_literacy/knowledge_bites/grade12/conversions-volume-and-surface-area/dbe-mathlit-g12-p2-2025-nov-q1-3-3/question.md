## Question (2 marks)

One way to save money during winter is to wrap the geyser and waterpipes with a geyser blanket made of aerolite (insulating material). The length of the geyser is 1,2 m. Convert this measurement to mm.

## Method

metric length conversion

## Memo working

Going from metres to millimetres means moving to a smaller unit, so the number gets bigger: 1 m = 1 000 mm. Length of geyser = 1,2 m x 1 000 (1MA multiply by 1 000) = 1 200 mm (1A conversion).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

1 200 mm
