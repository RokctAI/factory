## Question (3 marks)

Hexagonal paving bricks (equal side lengths L = 90 mm, height H = 50 mm, width W = 220 mm) will be used to cover a rectangular ground surface, laid in rows with the width W of each brick running along the row. Calculate the number of bricks needed for a single row along a length of 2 860 mm.

## Method

length division

## Memo working

Each brick takes up its width, 220 mm, along the row. To find how many bricks fit along 2 860 mm, divide the total length by the width of one brick: 2 860 mm ÷ 220 mm = 13. The division comes out exactly, so 13 whole bricks fill the row with nothing left over. (Memo: 1RT correct values; 1MA dividing; 1A number of bricks.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

13
