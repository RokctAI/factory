## Question (2 marks)

(Context: Amanda Xulu makes circular-shaped earrings to sell at the local flea market. A diagram of one earring shows a small circle above a large circle; the radius of the large circle is 14 mm.) Determine, in mm, the radius of the small circle if it is 4/7 of the radius of the large circle.

## Method

fraction of length

## Memo working

Radius of small circle = 4/7 x 14 mm = 8 mm.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

8 mm
