## Question (4 marks)

On the map of Australia's A1 motorway (ANNEXURE C), the leg between Cairns and Brisbane is marked as 1 705 km with a driving time of 20 h 40 min. Calculate the average speed, in km/h, from Brisbane to Cairns. You may use the following formula: Distance = speed x time

## Method

average speed from distance and time

## Memo working

Substitute the leg's values into the formula: 1 705 km = speed x 20 h 40 min. The time must be in hours only: 40 minutes is 40/60 of an hour, so 20 h 40 min = 20,67 h. Change the subject of the formula: Speed = 1 705 ÷ 20,67 = 82,5 km/h.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

82,5 km/h
