## Question (2 marks)

Hexagonal paving bricks are made up of 6 equal triangles (brick dimensions: equal side lengths L = 90 mm, height H = 50 mm, width W = 220 mm). Choose, from the options (A, B or C) below, the correct one that could be used to calculate the volume of the hexagonal paving brick. A: Volume = 6 x area of triangle x height. B: Volume = side length + height + width. C: Volume = 6 x perimeter x height.

## Method

volume formula multiple choice

## Memo working

Volume of any prism is the area of its base multiplied by its height. The base here is a hexagon made of 6 equal triangles, so the base area is 6 times the area of one triangle, and the volume is 6 x area of triangle x height — option A. Option B just adds three lengths together, which gives a length, not a volume; option C uses the perimeter, which measures the distance around the edge, so multiplying it by height cannot give a volume either. (Memo: 2A correct option.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

A
