## Question (6 marks)

The wooden pieces for two identical bookcases are cut from ONE complete rectangular piece of wood which is 2 cm thick, 42 cm wide and 430 cm long. (Each bookcase needs six pieces: two side pieces of 90 cm x 20 cm, a top and a bottom piece of 60 cm x 20 cm each, and two shelves of 56 cm x 20 cm — dimensions as printed in the marking guidelines from ANNEXURE D.) Determine the area of the remaining part of the wood after cutting the required material. You may use the following formula: Area of rectangle = length x breadth

## Method

remaining area after cutting

## Memo working

Area of the complete piece = 42 cm x 430 cm = 18 060 cm². For ONE bookcase, pair the pieces: one side (90 cm) plus one top/bottom (60 cm) plus one shelf (56 cm) laid along the length gives 90 + 60 + 56 = 206 cm, and two such strips of 20 cm width give 20 + 20 = 40 cm, so area per bookcase = 206 x 40 = 8 240 cm². Two bookcases use 2 x 8 240 = 16 480 cm². Area of unused wood = 18 060 - 16 480 = 1 580 cm². (Memo option 2, piece by piece per bookcase: E and D 60 x 20 x 2 = 2 400; F and G 56 x 20 x 2 = 2 240; A and B 90 x 20 x 2 = 3 600; total 8 240 cm², x 2 = 16 480 cm²; 18 060 - 16 480 = 1 580 cm². The piece dimensions come from ANNEXURE D but are printed in the memo working; the arithmetic is verified.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Area of complete piece = 42 x 430 = 18 060 cm²; area of pieces of one bookcase = (90 + 60 + 56) x (20 + 20) = 8 240 cm²; area of unused wood = 18 060 - 2(8 240) = 1 580 cm²
