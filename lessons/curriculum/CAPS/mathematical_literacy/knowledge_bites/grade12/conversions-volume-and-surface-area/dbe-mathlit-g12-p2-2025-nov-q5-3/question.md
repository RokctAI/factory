## Question (6 marks)

The density of the wood is 0,75 g/cm³. A volume of approximately 0,4 m³ unused wood is accumulated each month. Simon states that it will take more than three months to accumulate one ton of unused wood. Verify his statement, showing ALL calculations. You may use the following formula: Density = mass/volume

## Method

density mass volume verification

## Memo working

Convert the monthly volume to cm³ so it matches the density unit: 0,4 m³ x 100³ = 400 000 cm³. Mass per month = density x volume = 0,75 g/cm³ x 400 000 cm³ = 300 000 g = 300 kg. One ton = 1 000 kg. Number of months = 1 000 kg ÷ 300 kg = 3,33 months, which is more than three months, so the statement is VALID. (Memo option 2: in 3 months he accumulates 0,3 ton x 3 = 0,9 ton, which is less than 1 ton, so it must take more than three months — VALID.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Volume per month = 0,4 m³ = 400 000 cm³; mass per month = 0,75 x 400 000 = 300 000 g = 300 kg; number of months for 1 ton (1 000 kg) = 1 000 ÷ 300 = 3,33 months. VALID.
