## Question (2 marks)

Bricks for the double-brick garage walls are delivered stacked on a wooden pallet (a photo shows bricks stacked on a wooden pallet). Give ONE suitable reason why the bricks have to be placed on a wooden pallet.

## Method

reasoned opinion

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines — 2 marks for any ONE suitable reason)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Any ONE of: To be easily lifted by a fork lift. OR Storage/loading/transportation are made easier. OR It keeps them from breaking. OR It makes counting easier. OR To protect the bricks from damage. OR To keep it tight and compact.
