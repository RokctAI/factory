## Question (2 marks)

Crude oil is heated in a vertical, cylindrical tower to separate and extract products which boil at different temperatures (the tower diagram lists the products and their boiling temperatures from top to bottom: petroleum gas 20 °C, gasoline (petrol) 150 °C, kerosene (lamp oil) 200 °C, diesel 300 °C, industrial oil 370 °C, paraffin wax 400 °C). Extraction occurs because different liquids have different boiling points. Identify the crude oil product that will be third to boil.

## Method

order by boiling point

## Memo working

The product with the lowest boiling temperature boils first. Rank the boiling temperatures from lowest to highest: petroleum gas boils first at 20 °C, gasoline (petrol) second at 150 °C, and kerosene (lamp oil) third at 200 °C.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Kerosene or lamp oil
