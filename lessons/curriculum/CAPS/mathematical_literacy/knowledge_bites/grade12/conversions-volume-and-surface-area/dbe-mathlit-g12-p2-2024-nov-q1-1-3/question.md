## Question (2 marks)

TABLE 1 contains a list of explanations and definitions of concepts used in Mathematical Literacy (A: a measuring system using metres, litres and kilograms; B: the amount of space occupied by an object; C: a system measuring in inches, gallons and pounds; D: the amount of space available to hold something; E: the side of a building you see when you are facing south; F: the side of a building you see when you are facing north; G: the amount of space that is enclosed by the perimeter of an object). Use TABLE 1 and match an explanation or a definition with the concept below. Write only the letter (A-G). QUESTION: Imperial system

## Method

match definition

## Memo working

The imperial system is the older British measuring system that uses inches for length, gallons for capacity and pounds for weight — option C. Option A (metres, litres and kilograms) describes the metric system, which South Africa uses, so it is the distractor to avoid. (Memo: 2A correct option.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C
