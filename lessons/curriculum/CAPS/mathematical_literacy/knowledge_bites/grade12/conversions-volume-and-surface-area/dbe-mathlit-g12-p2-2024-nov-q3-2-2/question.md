## Question (2 marks)

Mrs Kgomo's circular wooden birthday tags are shown in a diagram as a circle 8 cm high and 8 cm wide. Determine, in cm, the radius of ONE circular wooden tag.

## Method

radius from diameter

## Memo working

The radius is half the diameter. The tag's diameter is 8 cm, so Radius = 8 cm ÷ 2 = 4 cm.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

4 cm
