## Question (4 marks)

Mrs Kgomo stores the pillows from her classroom reading corner in zip-up storage bags (each bag is 46 cm long, 30 cm wide and 48 cm high; the bag diagram shows 4 pillows stacked in the bag). The height of one pillow is 11 cm. Determine the difference between the total height of the pillows in ONE bag and the height of the bag.

## Method

stacked height difference

## Memo working

Four pillows are stacked on top of each other, so the total height of the pillows is 11 cm x 4 = 44 cm. The bag itself is 48 cm high, so the difference is 48 cm - 44 cm = 4 cm. (Memo alternative: subtract the pillows one at a time, 48 - 11 - 11 - 11 - 11 = 4 cm.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

4 cm
