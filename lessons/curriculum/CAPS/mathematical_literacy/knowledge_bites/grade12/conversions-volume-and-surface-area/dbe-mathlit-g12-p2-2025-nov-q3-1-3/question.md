## Question (4 marks)

(Context: Mrs Xulu's tree decoration is pasted onto a cardboard template of three sections. Part A is the top triangular section, with a vertical height of 240 mm and a triangle base of 28 cm.) Calculate, in cm², the area of Part A. You may use the following formula: Area of triangle = 1/2 base x height

## Method

triangle area with conversion

## Memo working

The height and the base are in different units, so first convert the height: 240 mm = 24 cm. Then Area = 1/2 x (28 cm x 24 cm) = 336 cm². (Memo option 2: work in millimetres — 1/2 x 280 mm x 240 mm = 33 600 mm², then convert: 33 600 mm² = 336 cm².)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

336 cm²
