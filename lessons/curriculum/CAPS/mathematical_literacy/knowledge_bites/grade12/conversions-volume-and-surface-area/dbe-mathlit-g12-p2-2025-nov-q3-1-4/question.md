## Question (5 marks)

(Context: Mrs Xulu's cardboard tree template is made up of three sections — Part A, Part B and Part C. From the previous part, the area of Part A is 336 cm².) The area of Part B is 30 240 mm². The area of Part C is 1,6 times the area of Part B. Calculate, in mm², the total area of the cardboard template.

## Method

scaled area and total

## Memo working

Area of Part C = 30 240 mm² x 1,6 = 48 384 mm². The answer must be in mm², so convert Part A: 336 cm² = 33 600 mm² (multiply by 100, since 1 cm² = 100 mm²). Total area = 48 384 mm² + 30 240 mm² + 33 600 mm² = 112 224 mm². (Consistent accuracy carried from Q3.1.3.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

112 224 mm²
