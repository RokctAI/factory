## Question (2 marks)

The side view of the fold-up beach chair shows a spacing of 1,27 cm between the crosspieces of the chair back. Write down, in millimetres, the length of the spacing between the crosspieces of the back of the chair.

## Method

cm to mm conversion

## Memo working

There are 10 millimetres in one centimetre, so to change centimetres to millimetres you multiply by 10. The spacing is 1,27 cm, so 1,27 x 10 = 12,7 mm. (Memo: 1RT correct value; 1MCA simplification; no penalty for units.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

12,7 mm
