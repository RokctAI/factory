## Question (2 marks)

Paving bricks are available in different shapes and sizes. Hexagonal paving bricks are made up of 6 equal triangles and will be used to cover a rectangular ground surface (sketch of a hexagonal paving brick with equal side lengths L = 90 mm, height H = 50 mm and width W = 220 mm, plus a picture of three bricks in a row with W marked across one brick; source: econbrick.co.za). Use the information above. QUESTION: Convert 220 mm to metres.

## Method

mm to m conversion

## Memo working

There are 1 000 millimetres in one metre, so to go from millimetres to metres you divide by 1 000. Take the width of the brick, 220 mm, and divide: 220 ÷ 1 000 = 0,22. So the width is 0,22 of a metre. (Memo: 1MA dividing by 1 000; 1A conversion.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

0,22 m
