## Question (2 marks)

TABLE 1 contains a list of explanations and definitions of concepts used in Mathematical Literacy (A: a measuring system using metres, litres and kilograms; B: the amount of space occupied by an object; C: a system measuring in inches, gallons and pounds; D: the amount of space available to hold something; E: the side of a building you see when you are facing south; F: the side of a building you see when you are facing north; G: the amount of space that is enclosed by the perimeter of an object). Use TABLE 1 and match an explanation or a definition with the concept below. Write only the letter (A-G). QUESTION: Capacity

## Method

match definition

## Memo working

Capacity asks how much a container can hold, so listen for the words "space available to hold something". Option B describes volume — the space an object itself takes up — while option D describes the space available inside a container to hold something, which is exactly what capacity means. (Memo: 2A correct option; answer only full marks.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

D
