## Question (2 marks)

Mrs Kgomo created a birthday calendar for her 2024 class. She will write each learner's name and birthday on a small circular wooden tag and attach the tag to the correct month. The circular wooden tag has a circular hole to attach a ring that is used to hang the tag (the diagram shows a circle 8 cm high and 8 cm wide with a small hole at the top). Determine, in cm, the circumference of the circular wooden tag. You may use the following formula: Circumference of a circle = 3,142 x diameter

## Method

circumference from diameter

## Memo working

The tag is 8 cm across in every direction, so its diameter is 8 cm. Substitute into the formula: Circumference = 3,142 x 8 cm = 25,136 cm.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

25,136 cm
