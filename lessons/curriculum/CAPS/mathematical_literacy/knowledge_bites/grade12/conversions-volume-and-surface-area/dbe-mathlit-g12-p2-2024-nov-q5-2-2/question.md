## Question (4 marks)

A diagram compares the heights of tourist attractions around the world: Sydney Harbour Bridge 134 m; ULURU 348 m; The Eiffel Tower 324 m; The Great Pyramid of Giza 139 m; Big Ben 96 m. Write, as a simplified ratio, the heights of Uluru : Eiffel Tower : Big Ben.

## Method

simplify three part ratio

## Memo working

Read the three heights from the diagram in the order asked: 348 : 324 : 96. All three numbers divide by 12 (their highest common factor): 348 ÷ 12 = 29, 324 ÷ 12 = 27, 96 ÷ 12 = 8. So the simplified ratio is 29 : 27 : 8.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

29 : 27 : 8
