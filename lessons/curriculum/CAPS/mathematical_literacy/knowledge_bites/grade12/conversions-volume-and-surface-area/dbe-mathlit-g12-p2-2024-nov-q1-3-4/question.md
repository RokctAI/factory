## Question (2 marks)

The material list of the fold-up beach chair gives the chair support as 1,9 x 3,8 x 66 cm, with a note that the thickness of the wood is the smallest dimension. Identify which dimension of the chair support will represent the thickness of the wood.

## Method

identify smallest dimension

## Memo working

The note tells you the thickness is the smallest of the three dimensions. Compare 1,9 cm, 3,8 cm and 66 cm: the smallest is 1,9 cm, so the thickness of the wood is 1,9 cm. (Memo: 2A correct dimension; no penalty for units.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

1,9 cm
