## Question (2 marks)

TABLE 1 of comparative food prices lists eggs sold as a 1,5 dozen tray for R52,97 in May 2022. Calculate the price of a dozen eggs during May 2022.

## Method

proportional scaling

## Memo working

The tray holds 1,5 dozen, so one dozen costs the tray price divided by 1,5: Price per dozen = R52,97 ÷ 1,5 (1A dividing by 1,5) = R35,31 (1A simplification). (Memo alternatives: 1 egg = R52,97/18, then x 12 = R35,31; or half a dozen = R52,97/3 = R17,65666, then x 2 = R35,31. NPR)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R35,31
