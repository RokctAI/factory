## Question (2 marks)

TABLE 1 shows the average comparative food prices for some lunch items in May 2022 and May 2023 (May 2022 prices in rand: 410 g beans 12,60; 250 g instant coffee 45,56; eggs 1,5 dozen tray 52,97; 750 ml sunflower oil 40,68; oranges per kg 22,07; chicken giblets per kg 37,73; chicken portions per 2 kg 86,80; the May 2022 total is shown as the missing value A, while the May 2023 total is R316,37). Determine the missing value, A.

## Method

sum of column

## Memo working

A is the total of the May 2022 price column, so add all seven prices: A = R12,60 + R45,56 + R52,97 + R40,68 + R22,07 + R37,73 + R86,80 (1MA adding ALL correct values) = R298,41 (1A simplification, NPU).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R298,41
