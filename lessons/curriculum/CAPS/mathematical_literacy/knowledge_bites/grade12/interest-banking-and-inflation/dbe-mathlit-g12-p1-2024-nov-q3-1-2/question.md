## Question (3 marks)

ANNEXURE B, GRAPH 1 shows the trends in the total number of Shoprite and Pick n Pay stores for 2014 to 2023 (the Pick n Pay line ends at 2 204 stores in 2023). It is projected that the number of Pick n Pay stores will increase by 95,39% from 2023 to 2032. Determine, by means of calculations, the projected number of Pick n Pay stores in 2032.

## Method

percentage increase projection

## Memo working

Start from the 2023 value of 2 204 stores. The increase is 95,39% of that: 2 204 x 95,39/100 = 2 102,3956 (1RT correct value 2 204, 1MA percentage calculation). Add the increase to the original: 2 102,3956 + 2 204 = 4 306,3956, which rounds to 4 306 stores (1CA simplification). (Memo alternative in one step: 2 204 x 195,39/100, i.e. 2 204 x 1,9539 = 4 306. AO)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

4 306 stores (Accept: 4 307)
