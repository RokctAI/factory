## Question (3 marks)

TABLE 10 shows the monthly inflation rates for the period Jul. 2024 to Oct. 2024: Jul. 2024: 2,2; Aug. 2024: 2,2; Sep. 2024: C (from Question 5.2.1, C = 1,7); Oct. 2024: 2,3. Describe the trend in the inflation rate for the period Jul. 2024 to Oct. 2024.

## Method

describe trend

## Memo working

1A remains the same; 1A decreases; 1A increases. (CA from Question 5.2.1)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

It remains the same (constant) for July to August; it decreases from August to September; it increases from September to October.
