## Question (3 marks)

The newly elected SGB planned to have lunch with the staff. TABLE 1 shows the average comparative food prices for May 2022 and May 2023 for some lunch items, with the percentage change year on year and month on month (percentage changes read: 410 g beans 20,2 and 0,5; 250 g instant coffee 6,4 and -1,0; eggs 1,5 dozen tray 8,2 and 1,6; 750 ml sunflower oil -12,7 and -2,3; oranges per kg -8,9 and -14,5; chicken giblets per kg 23,9 and 2,2; chicken portions per 2 kg 7,2 and 0,6). Identify the food items that showed a decrease in both the month-on-month and year-on-year percentage change.

## Method

identify negative values in table

## Memo working

A decrease shows as a negative percentage change. Go down the table looking for items with a minus sign in BOTH columns: sunflower oil has -12,7 (year on year) and -2,3 (month on month), and oranges have -8,9 and -14,5. Instant coffee only decreased month on month (-1,0) while increasing year on year (6,4), so it does not qualify. (Memo: 2RT first correct product; 1RT second correct product.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Sunflower oil / Oil AND Oranges
