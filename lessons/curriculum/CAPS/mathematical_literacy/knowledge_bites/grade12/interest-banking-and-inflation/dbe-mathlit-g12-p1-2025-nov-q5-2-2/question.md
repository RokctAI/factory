## Question (5 marks)

Candice's husband has a car with a 40 litre petrol tank capacity. ANNEXURE B in the ANSWER BOOK shows the graph of the average petrol price per month in pounds (£) for a four-month period during 2024; on the graph, Jul. 2024 reads £1,45 and Aug. 2024 reads about £1,42. Candice stated that the difference in the cost of a full tank of petrol from Jul. 2024 to Aug. 2024, was £1,5. Verify, showing ALL calculations, whether her statement is VALID.

**Note: ANNEXURE B lives in the 2025 Special Answer Book, which is not in the repo. The Aug. 2024 price is read from that missing graph (the memo accepts readings 1,41-1,43); the arithmetic itself is verified.**

## Method

full tank cost difference verification

## Memo working

Difference = (£1,45 (1A) - £1,42 (1RT, graph reading; accept 1,41-1,43)) x 40 ℓ (1MA multiply difference with capacity) = £1,20 (1CA simplification). NOT VALID (1O conclusion). OR (£1,45 x 40) - (£1,42 x 40) = £58 - £56,80 (1MA) = £1,20 (1CA); NOT VALID (1O)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Difference = (£1,45 - £1,42) x 40 ℓ = £1,20. Her statement is NOT VALID.
