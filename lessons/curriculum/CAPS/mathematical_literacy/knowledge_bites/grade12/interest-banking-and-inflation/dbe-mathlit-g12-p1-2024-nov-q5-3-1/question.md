## Question (2 marks)

TABLE 7 shows the inflation rates in India for house prices for the last five years: 2020: 6%; 2021: 6,5%; 2022: 7%; 2023: 7,5%; 2024: 8%. Describe the trend in the inflation rates in India.

## Method

describe trend

## Memo working

Read the table from the earliest year to the latest: the rate moves from 6% in 2020 up through 6,5%, 7% and 7,5% to 8% in 2024 — it rises every year. A trend statement must link both variables: as the years increase (1O years increase), the inflation rate increases (1O rate increases).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

As the years increase the inflation rate increases. OR The inflation rate increases from 2020 to 2024.
