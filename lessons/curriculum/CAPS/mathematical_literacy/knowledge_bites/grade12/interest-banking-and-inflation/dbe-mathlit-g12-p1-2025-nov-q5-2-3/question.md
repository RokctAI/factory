## Question (3 marks)

ANNEXURE B shows the average petrol price per month in pounds (£) for a four-month period during 2024; the Jul. 2024 price on the graph reads £1,45. TABLE 10 gives the monthly inflation rate for Jul. 2024 as 2,2%. Calculate, in £ per litre, the cost of petrol during Jun. 2024, if the petrol price from Jun. 2024 to Jul. 2024 changed in line with inflation.

**Note: ANNEXURE B lives in the 2025 Special Answer Book, which is not in the repo. The £1,45 Jul. 2024 reading comes from that missing graph; the reverse-inflation arithmetic (1,45/1,022 = 1,42) is verified.**

## Method

reverse inflation calculation

## Memo working

Petrol price for June = £1,45 (1RT) x 100/102,2 OR £1,45/1,022 (1MA percentage calculation) = £1,42 (1CA simplification). AO. CA from Question 5.2.2

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

£1,42
