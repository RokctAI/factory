## Question (6 marks)

A house in Chennai, India, is valued at 5 000 000 rupees at the end of 2024. TABLE 7 shows the inflation rates in India for house prices: 2024: 8%; 2023: 7,5%; 2022: 7%; 2021: 6,5%; 2020: 6%. Calculate how much the house was worth at the end of 2022.

## Method

reverse inflation calculation

## Memo working

Work backwards one year at a time, dividing by each year's growth factor instead of multiplying. The 2024 value grew by 8% from the end-of-2023 value, so undo it: Price at end of 2023 = 5 000 000 / 1,08 (1A 1,08 or 108%, 1MA dividing) = 4 629 629,63 rupees (1CA). That value grew by 7,5% during 2023 from the end-of-2022 value, so undo it too: Price at end of 2022 = 4 629 629,63 / 1,075 (1MCA 1,075 or 107,5%, 1MA dividing) = 4 306 632,214 rupees (1CA simplification). (Memo alternative in one line: 5 000 000 x 100/108 x 100/107,5 = 4 306 632,214 rupees. NPU, NPR)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

4 306 632,214 rupees
