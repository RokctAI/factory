## Question (3 marks)

TABLE 1 of comparative food prices lists oranges at R22,07 per kg in May 2022 and R20,10 per kg in May 2023. Write down the unit ratio, in the form 1 : ..., for the price of the oranges in May 2022 to the price of the oranges in May 2023.

## Method

unit ratio

## Memo working

Set up the ratio in the order asked, May 2022 price to May 2023 price: 22,07 : 20,10 (1RT + 1RT correct values). To make the first number 1, divide both sides by 22,07: 20,10 ÷ 22,07 = 0,9107385591, so the ratio is 1 : 0,9107385591 ≈ 1 : 0,91 (1A simplification in correct order, NPR).

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

1 : 0,91
