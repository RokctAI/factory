## Question (3 marks)

TABLE 10 shows the monthly inflation percentages for a four-month period during 2024: Jul. 2024: 2,2; Aug. 2024: 2,2; Sep. 2024: C; Oct. 2024: 2,3. The average inflation rate over the four-month period during 2024 was 2,1%. Calculate the missing value C.

## Method

missing value from mean

## Memo working

2,1 = (2,2 + 2,2 + 2,3 + C)/4 (1MA concept of mean; 1MA adding all values); 2,1 x 4 = 6,7 + C; C = 8,4% - 6,7% = 1,7% (1CA simplification). OR sum = 2,1 x 4 = 8,4% (1MA); C = 8,4% - 6,7% (1MA) = 1,7% (1CA). AO

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

C = 1,7%
