## Question (2 marks)

The seating plan for a tourist bus that travels around Cape Town is shown (upper deck: 59 seats, rows G to V; lower deck: 22 seats and the driver seat, rows A to F, with a toilet at the front left of the cabin and stairs at the rear). The seats are numbered according to the following numbering system: A to F is for the lower deck; G to V is for the upper deck; the seats are numbered 1 to 4 from right to left if you are facing the back of the bus. Write down the seat number which has the easiest access to the toilet.

## Method

locate seat on plan

## Memo working

The toilet is on the lower deck, at the front left of the cabin, so the answer must be a lower-deck row (A to F). On the plan, row F is the lower-deck row right next to the toilet, and applying the numbering rule (seats numbered 1 to 4 from right to left when you face the back of the bus) the seat in row F beside the toilet is seat number 2. So the seat with the easiest access to the toilet is F2. (Memo: 1A correct row; 1A correct seat.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

F2
