## Question (4 marks)

John lives in a flat in Cape Town. ANNEXURE B in the ANSWER BOOK shows the floor plans of two flats in John's block of flats: Flat 1 is on the ground floor and Flat 2 is on the first floor (the annexure is printed in the 2025 Special Answer Book). Write down TWO differences between Flat 1 and Flat 2.

## Method

compare floor plans

## Memo working

Compare the two plans room by room and look for features one has that the other lacks. First difference: where Flat 1 has a living room, Flat 2 has a bedroom in that position. Second difference: Flat 2, on the first floor, has a balcony, while Flat 1 on the ground floor does not. (Memo: 2RT identify first difference; 2RT identify second difference.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

One has a living room and the other has a bedroom; the balcony is on the first floor (Flat 2 has a balcony, Flat 1 does not).
