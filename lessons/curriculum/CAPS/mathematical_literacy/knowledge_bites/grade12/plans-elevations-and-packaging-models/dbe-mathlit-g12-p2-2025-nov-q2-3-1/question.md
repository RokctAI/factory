## Question (2 marks)

John lives in a flat in Cape Town. ANNEXURE B in the ANSWER BOOK shows the floor plans of two flats in John's block of flats: Flat 1 is on the ground floor and Flat 2 is on the first floor (the annexure is printed in the 2025 Special Answer Book). Use ANNEXURE B: state whether the bathroom door opens to the (left/right) as you enter the bathroom.

## Method

read door swing from plan

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Right (Memo: 2A correct direction. On a floor plan a door is drawn with a quarter-circle arc showing its swing; on this plan the arc sweeps to the right-hand side as you enter the bathroom.)
