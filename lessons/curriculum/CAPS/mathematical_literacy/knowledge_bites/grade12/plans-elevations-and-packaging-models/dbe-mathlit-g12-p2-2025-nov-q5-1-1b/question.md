## Question (2 marks)

Choose the letter (A-G) in DIAGRAM 2 that matches the item in DIAGRAM 1 given below: (b) Back of bookcase

**Note: ANNEXURE D is printed in the 2025 Special Answer Book, which is not in the archive; the diagram labelling above is reconstructed from the marking guidelines. The memo answer is definitive.**

## Method

match component on diagram

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) The back of the bookcase is the single hardboard piece, the large 60 cm x 90 cm rectangle labelled C on DIAGRAM 2.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)
