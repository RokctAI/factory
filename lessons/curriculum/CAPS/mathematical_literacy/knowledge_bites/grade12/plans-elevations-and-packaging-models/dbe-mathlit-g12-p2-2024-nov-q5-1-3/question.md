## Question (3 marks)

The A1 motorway around Australia is the world's longest continuous road of 14 655 km. ANNEXURE C marks the distances per leg: Melbourne-Adelaide 738 km; Sydney-Melbourne 1 062 km; Brisbane-Sydney 922 km; Cairns-Brisbane 1 705 km; Darwin-Cairns 2 850 km; Broome-Darwin 1 871 km; Perth-Broome 2 811 km. The Perth to Adelaide leg is marked with a driving time of 29 hours but no distance. Determine the distance from Perth to Adelaide.

## Method

subtract leg distances from total

## Memo working

The whole A1 ring is 14 655 km, and every leg except Perth-Adelaide has a marked distance. Add the known legs: 738 + 1 062 + 922 + 1 705 + 2 850 + 1 871 + 2 811 = 11 959 km. Subtract from the total ring distance: 14 655 - 11 959 = 2 696 km.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

2 696 km
