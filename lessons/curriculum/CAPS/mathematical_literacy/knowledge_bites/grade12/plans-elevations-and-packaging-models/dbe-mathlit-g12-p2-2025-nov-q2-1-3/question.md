## Question (4 marks)

The seating plan for a tourist bus that travels around Cape Town is shown (upper deck: 59 seats, rows G to V, stairs mid-deck; lower deck: 22 seats and the driver seat, rows A to F, toilet at the front left, stairs at the rear; seats are numbered 1 to 4 from right to left if you are facing the back of the bus). Complete the following paragraph with the directions to move from seat C2 to seat J3 by using the words provided in the list: aisle; second; left; right; first. Write only the word next to the question numbers (a) to (d). Paragraph: Move forward towards the front set of stairs on your (a) ... Use this set of stairs to get to the upper deck. Once on the upper deck, turn (b) ... and you will find your seat on the (c) ... in the (d) ... row from the stairs.

## Method

give directions on plan

## Memo working

Track the walk on the plan from seat C2 on the lower deck. Walking forward, the front set of stairs is on your right — (a) Right. At the top of the stairs on the upper deck you must turn left to head towards row J — (b) Left. Seat J3 sits next to the walkway between the seat pairs, so it is on the aisle — (c) Aisle. Counting rows from the stairs, row J is the second row — (d) Second. (Memo: 1A correct choice for each of (a)-(d).)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

(a) Right; (b) Left; (c) Aisle; (d) Second
