## Question (2 marks)

Chris and Boipelo are planning a year-long trip, mostly along the A1 motorway, around Australia — the world's longest continuous road of 14 655 km. ANNEXURE C is a map of Australia showing the A1 motorway with the distances and driving times between the major cities (Melbourne-Adelaide 738 km, 9 h 6 min; Sydney-Melbourne 1 062 km, 13 h 40 min; Brisbane-Sydney 922 km, 10 h 30 min; Cairns-Brisbane 1 705 km, 20 h 40 min; Darwin-Cairns 2 850 km, 33 h 55 min; Broome-Darwin 1 871 km, 21 h 13 min; Perth-Broome 2 811 km, 30 hours). Write down the names of the cities where the distance between them is 738 km.

## Method

read distance from map

## Memo working

Scan the legs marked on the map for the value 738 km: it labels the leg between Adelaide and Melbourne. (Memo note in the source transcription: verified against Annexure C — the 738 km, 9 h 6 min leg is marked between Melbourne and Adelaide. One mark for each correct city.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Adelaide and Melbourne
