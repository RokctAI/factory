## Question (2 marks)

John lives in a flat in Cape Town. ANNEXURE B in the ANSWER BOOK shows the floor plans of two flats in John's block of flats, which form part of a block of flats; neither plan has windows in the north- or south-facing walls (the annexure is printed in the 2025 Special Answer Book). Explain why there are no windows in the north- and south-facing walls of both plans.

## Method

reasoned opinion

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

There must be more flats adjacent to it. (Memo: 2O explanation. These flats sit inside a block of flats, so the north- and south-facing walls are shared party walls with the neighbouring flats — a wall shared with another flat cannot have a window in it.)
