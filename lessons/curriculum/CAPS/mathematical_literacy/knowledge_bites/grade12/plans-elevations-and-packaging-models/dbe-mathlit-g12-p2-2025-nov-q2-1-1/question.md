## Question (2 marks)

The seating plan for a tourist bus that travels around Cape Town is shown (upper deck: 59 seats in rows of forward-facing seats, rows G to V, with stairs mid-deck; lower deck: 22 seats and the driver seat, rows A to F, with a toilet at the front left and stairs at the rear; the plan's key marks the driver seat, toilet, forward-facing seats and stairs). Determine the number of forward-facing passenger seats on this bus.

## Method

count seats on plan

## Memo working

Number of seats = 59 + 22 - 4 (1MA addition and subtraction of correct values) = 77 seats (1A simplification). The plan gives 59 upper-deck seats plus 22 lower-deck passenger seats, which is 81 passenger seats in total — but four of those 81 seats on the plan are not forward-facing, so they must be subtracted. Answer only earns full marks.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

77 seats
