## Question (3 marks)

Chris and Boipelo are planning a year-long trip around Australia. ANNEXURE C includes the plan of their trip: an outline map of Australia with monthly arrows JAN through DEC around the coast (JAN up the east coast, FEB in Tasmania, MAR-APR back up the east coast, MAY-JUN across the north via Queensland/Northern Territory, JUL at the top of Western Australia, AUG, SEP, OCT and NOV down and around Western Australia, DEC returning east along the south coast). Name the FOUR months when Chris and Boipelo will be in Western Australia.

## Method

read schedule from plan

## Memo working

Follow the monthly arrows on the planned-trip map and read off which arrows lie in the state of Western Australia. (Memo note in the source transcription: verified against Annexure C's planned-trip panel — the AUG, SEP, OCT and NOV arrows all lie in Western Australia. Marks: one for the first correct month, one for the second, one for ALL correct months.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov
