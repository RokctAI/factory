## Question (2 marks)

Chris and Boipelo's planned trip around Australia (ANNEXURE C) shows monthly arrows JAN through DEC around the coast: JAN up the east coast, FEB in Tasmania, MAR-APR back up the east coast, MAY-JUN across the north, JUL-NOV in and around Western Australia, DEC returning east along the south coast. Name the state where they plan to be during February.

## Method

read schedule from plan

## Memo working

Find the FEB arrow on the planned-trip map and read off the state it lies on. (Memo note in the source transcription: verified against Annexure C's planned-trip panel — the FEB arrow is on Tasmania.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Tasmania
