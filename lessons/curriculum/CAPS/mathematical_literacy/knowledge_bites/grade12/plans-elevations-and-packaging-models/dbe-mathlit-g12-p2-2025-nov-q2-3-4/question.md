## Question (2 marks)

John lives in a flat in Cape Town. ANNEXURE B in the ANSWER BOOK shows the floor plans of two flats in John's block of flats: Flat 1 is on the ground floor and Flat 2 is on the first floor (the annexure is printed in the 2025 Special Answer Book). Give ONE possible reason why Flat 1 does not have a balcony.

## Method

reasoned opinion

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

It is on the ground floor. (Memo: 2O explanation. A balcony is a raised platform that projects from an upper storey; a ground-floor flat opens directly onto the ground, so there is no need for one.)
