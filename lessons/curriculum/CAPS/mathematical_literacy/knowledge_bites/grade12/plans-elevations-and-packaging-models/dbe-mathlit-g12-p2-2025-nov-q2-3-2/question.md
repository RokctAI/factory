## Question (5 marks)

John lives in a flat in Cape Town. ANNEXURE B in the ANSWER BOOK shows the floor plans of two flats in John's block of flats, with dimensions given in imperial units (on the plan the longer dimension of the living room reads 12'8", that is 12 feet 8 inches; the annexure is printed in the 2025 Special Answer Book). Convert the longer dimension of the living room to centimetres.

## Method

feet inches to centimetres

## Memo working

Read the longer dimension off the plan: 12'8", meaning 12 feet 8 inches (1RT). Convert each part separately. Feet: 12 x 30,48 (1MCA multiply by 30,48) = 365,76 cm (1C convert feet to cm). Inches: 8 x 2,54 = 20,32 cm (1C convert inches to cm). Add the two parts: total = 365,76 + 20,32 = 386,08 cm (1CA simplification).

**Note:** The memo's second alternative prints "365,6 cm" — a documented typo for 365,76 cm; the accepted final answer of 386,08 cm is unaffected. The 12'8" reading comes from ANNEXURE B in the Special Answer Book (not in the repo); the conversion arithmetic (12 x 30,48 + 8 x 2,54 = 386,08) is verified.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

386,08 cm
