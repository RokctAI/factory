## Question (2 marks)

Simon is a carpenter who builds wooden bookcases using wooden pieces for the shelves and the frame, and hardboard for the back. ANNEXURE D shows two diagrams: DIAGRAM 1 shows the wooden pieces for the top, bottom, sides and shelves of a bookcase, with hardboard for the back; DIAGRAM 2 shows the hardboard and SIX pieces of wood, with dimensions, labelled with letters A to G (A and B are the side pieces of 90 cm x 20 cm; E and D are the top and bottom pieces of 60 cm x 20 cm; F and G are the shelves of 56 cm x 20 cm; C is the hardboard back of 60 cm x 90 cm). Choose the letter (A-G) in DIAGRAM 2 that matches the item in DIAGRAM 1 given below: (a) Shelf

**Note: ANNEXURE D is printed in the 2025 Special Answer Book, which is not in the archive; the diagram labelling above is reconstructed from the marking guidelines. The memo answer is definitive.**

## Method

match component on diagram

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) The shelves are the two internal horizontal pieces of 56 cm x 20 cm, labelled F and G on DIAGRAM 2 — either letter earns the marks.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

F OR G
