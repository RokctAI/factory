## Question (2 marks)

(Context: Simon the carpenter's bookcase on ANNEXURE D — the frame is 60 cm wide and 90 cm high, and the back is a single piece of hardboard.) The back of the bookcase is made out of hardboard. (a) Explain why the dimensions of the hardboard is 60 cm by 90 cm.

**Note: ANNEXURE D is printed in the 2025 Special Answer Book, which is not in the archive; the bookcase dimensions are reconstructed from the marking guidelines. The memo answer is definitive.**

## Method

explain dimensions

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines) Link the hardboard's dimensions to the bookcase frame it must close off: the back sheet must span the full length and full height of the bookcase.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

The length of the bookcase is 60 cm and the height of the bookcase is 90 cm. OR It covers the entire back of the bookcase.
