## Question (6 marks)

(Context: the back of Simon's bookcase is a piece of hardboard measuring 60 cm by 90 cm.) (b) Draw a scaled diagram of the hardboard according to scale 1 : 20.

## Method

scale drawing

## Memo working

A scale of 1 : 20 means every real length is divided by 20 on the drawing. Width on the drawing = 60 cm ÷ 20 = 3 cm. Height on the drawing = 90 cm ÷ 20 = 4,5 cm. Draw a rectangle of 3 cm wide by 4,5 cm high — marks are awarded for the correct width, the correct height and the rectangular shape.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

Width = 60 cm ÷ 20 = 3 cm; height = 90 cm ÷ 20 = 4,5 cm; a rectangle of 3 cm x 4,5 cm is drawn.
