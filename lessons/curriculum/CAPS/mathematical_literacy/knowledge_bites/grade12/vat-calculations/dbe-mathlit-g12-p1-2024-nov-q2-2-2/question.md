## Question (3 marks)

A home solar system unit is advertised at a cash price of R78 200 (including VAT). Calculate, excluding VAT, the cash price of the home solar system unit.

## Method

reverse vat calculation

## Memo working

The advertised price already contains 15% VAT, so it is 115% of the price excluding VAT. Work backwards by dividing: Price excluding VAT = R78 200 ÷ 1,15 (1RT for R78 200, 1MA dividing by 1,15) = R68 000 (1A simplification). (Memo alternatives: R78 200 x 100/115 = R68 000; or VAT amount = R78 200 x 15/115 ≈ R10 200, then R78 200 - R10 200 = R68 000.) The common trap is subtracting 15% of the inclusive price - dividing by 1,15 is the correct reverse step.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R68 000
