## Question (2 marks)

Miecke purchased prepaid electricity and received a till slip; some information has been omitted (the slip reads: TAX INVOICE, VAT NO. 4420106777; PREPAID ELECTRICITY, Electricity Municipal Prepaid; VAT REGISTRATION NO. 4370102313; Meter: 07032985769; DOMESTIC HOUSEHOLD TARIFF; Transaction Sequence No.: 56332515; Amount R1 300,00; Units *** kWh; Electricity R1 130,43; VAT 15% B; TOTAL R1 300,00). Write down the meter number.

## Method

read value from document

## Memo working

Find the line on the till slip labelled "Meter" and copy its number exactly: 07032985769. Do not confuse it with the VAT numbers or the transaction sequence number, which are different labelled values on the same slip. (Memo: 2RT correct number.)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

07032985769
