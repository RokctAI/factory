## Question (3 marks)

Miecke purchased prepaid electricity and received a till slip on which some information has been omitted (the slip reads: Amount R1 300,00; Electricity R1 130,43; VAT 15% B; TOTAL R1 300,00). Determine the missing value, B.

## Method

vat amount calculation

## Memo working

B is the VAT portion of the total. The total of R1 300,00 is made up of the electricity amount plus VAT, so subtract: B = R1 300,00 - R1 130,43 (1RT correct value, 1MA subtracting values) = R169,57 (1A simplification). (Memo option 2: B = R1 130,43 x 15/100 = R169,56. Memo option 3: B = R1 300 x 15/115 = R169,57. AO)

**Note (documented memo quirk):** the official memo's second alternative prints R169,56 (0,15 x 1 130,43 = 169,5645, rounded down), while the subtraction and 15/115 methods give R169,57. Both values appear in the official memo and both are accepted.

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

R169,57 (the memo also shows R169,56 via the 15% multiplication route)
