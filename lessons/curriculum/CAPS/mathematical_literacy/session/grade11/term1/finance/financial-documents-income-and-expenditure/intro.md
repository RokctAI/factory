Today we are reading the financial documents that run every South African household — the municipal account, the payslip, the budget and the loan agreement. We will learn the vocabulary examiners test, check a bill's arithmetic line by line, sort income and expenditure into fixed, variable and occasional, and build a budget that ends in a surplus or a deficit. Then we follow the money one step further, to simple interest, and work out what a fridge bought on credit truly costs. Every mark in this section comes from reading the document carefully and finishing with an answer in context — that is exactly the habit we build today.
