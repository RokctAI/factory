Today we are turning perimeter and area into rand amounts — fencing a yard, paving it, tiling a floor and painting the walls of the room behind it. We will use the formulae the curriculum requires for rectangles, triangles and circles, subtract gates, doors and windows before multiplying by any price, and apply the hardware shop's own rules: wastage allowances, whole tins and boxes, and rounding up whenever running short would stop the job. Examiners set exactly this chain in Paper 2, and the marks sit in the middle steps and the final costed sentence, not just the arithmetic.
