Today we are working with conversions and estimating — the measurement skills behind a recipe in Fahrenheit, a tape measure in inches, a paint tin promising square metres per litre, and a bus timetable on the twenty-four-hour clock. We will move within the metric ladder, cross into imperial with a supplied table, convert temperature and time by formula, and scale rates like paint coverage and medicine doses to a decision about what to buy. These conversions carry marks all through Paper 1 and Paper 2, and the direction rule plus a real-world sense check will keep every one of them honest.
