Today we are working with number patterns — lists that change by a rule — and the one skill the examination pays for: find the rule, then use it. We will meet the three families the papers use, constant difference, constant ratio and the growing step, and the difference-row test that identifies them in seconds. Then we take on the stacked-rows question straight from the latest national paper: rows growing by one each time, and the total counted safely with a table and checked by pairing. Patterns arrive dressed as tariffs, rumours and supermarket displays, and by the end you will see through the clothing every time.
