Today we follow one data investigation from start to finish: a class surveying what learners spend on airtime and data each week, and whether the tuck shop should stock data vouchers. We will sharpen the research question, choose a sample without bias, tally sixty answers into a frequency table with honest intervals, summarise them with mean, median, mode and range, and finish with graphs — including the ones designed to mislead. Every stage of this cycle is examined directly, and the marks favour the learner who can name what could go wrong at each step and say why.
