Today we examine the numbers that pass through every South African wallet unnoticed: the fifteen percent VAT inside a till slip, the one percent UIF on a payslip, the tariff tables behind phone and bank charges, and the exchange rate that prices the rand itself. We will add and remove VAT correctly, follow the two R84 contributions to the unemployment fund, convert rands to dollars and pounds in the right direction, and compare two cellphone deals to find exactly where they trade places. Each of these carries guaranteed marks in the finance section, and each has one classic trap we will name and disarm.
