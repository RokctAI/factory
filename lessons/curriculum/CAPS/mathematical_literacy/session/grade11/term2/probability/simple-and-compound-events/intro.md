Today we are measuring chance — the mathematics behind a full taxi, a lucky-draw spinner and a coin game at the school fete. We will place every probability on the scale from zero to one, separate the chances you calculate from symmetry from the ones you estimate by watching, and then master the two machines for double events: the tree diagram that lists every route, and the two-way table that turns a crowd into a grid. Probability questions reward the learner who draws the picture and chooses the right denominator, and both habits are exactly what we practise in this session.
