Today we are reading assembly instructions and diagrams — the skill of turning a box of ninety parts and a wordless folded sheet into a finished bookshelf, or a safely wired three-pin plug. We work through the language of exploded diagrams, the parts-list audit that must happen before any building, why the numbered sequence is a one-way street, and the cost comparison that decides whether you assemble it yourself or pay someone. It is the final maps-and-plans skill, and the exam tests it with counting, ordering and a decision you must defend in rands.
