Today we are reading building plans and models — the Grade 11 journey from millimetres on paper to bricks, flooring and rands. We follow one bedroom extension in Kimberley: reading its floor plan at a scale of one to one hundred, matching its elevations, costing its flooring, skirting and paint, and finally packing cans into a carton the way a spaza shop actually does. Every mark in this section comes from the same discipline — convert with the scale, count in wholes, and round in the direction reality demands.
