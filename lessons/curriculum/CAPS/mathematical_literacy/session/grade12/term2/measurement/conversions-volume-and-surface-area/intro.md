Today we are measuring a whole working month: unit conversions, volume, surface area and rates, the way Paper 2 packages them. An American recipe arrives in pounds and Fahrenheit, so we convert with supplied factors and the temperature formula; a new water tank gets its capacity checked against the supplier's promise; the tank then needs paint, which is surface area and the round-up rule of the shop; and the delivery trip gets timed and costed with rates. Every calculation here ends in a sentence about the context — the litres, the tins, the time on the clock — because in Mathematical Literacy the decision is the answer.
