Today we are reading maps the way Paper 2 demands: scales, directions and the Grade 12 headline skill — slope. We plan the December run from Johannesburg to Durban on a 1 : 2 000 000 road map, price a bar scale, build a scale of our own, and use grid blocks and the eight compass words to say where things are and which way they lie. Then we walk off the road into the Drakensberg, where contour lines and gradient tell you how steep a path is before your legs find out. Distance, direction, slope and time — one flat page answers all four, and the memo pays for every method line you show.
