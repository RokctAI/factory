Hey, it's Mandy — the finals are close and Finance plus Data can feel like a mountain, but here's the secret: Paper 1 is about 90 marks of Finance and 53 of Data, and almost all of them come from a handful of moves you already own.

Tariffs: split the units across the blocks like a staircase. First 350 units at R2,00 is R700, the next 70 at R2,50 is R175 — R875, then times 1,15 for VAT, R1 006,25. The trap is charging everything at the top rate; the staircase protects the early steps.

VAT has two directions. Excluding means multiply by 1,15 to add it. Including means DIVIDE by 1,15 to remove it — never take 15 percent of the total, because the tax was charged on the smaller number.

Interest: simple looks only at the original deposit, the same R300 every year. Compound earns interest on interest — R5 000 at 6 percent becomes R5 955,08 in three years, not R5 900. Say it in the exam: the balance grows, so the interest grows.

Tax is five steps in a fixed order: find the bracket, subtract for the excess, base plus percentage, MINUS the rebate, then divide by twelve if they want monthly. The rebate step is the forgotten mark — circle it.

Data: arrange first, always. Median is position, mean is balance, and one terrible mark drags the mean while the median stands still — that sentence, with the numbers in it, IS the Level 4 answer.

Blocks, both VAT directions, interest that grows, five tax steps, arrange-then-answer. Five habits and Paper 1 opens like a door. You've got this!
