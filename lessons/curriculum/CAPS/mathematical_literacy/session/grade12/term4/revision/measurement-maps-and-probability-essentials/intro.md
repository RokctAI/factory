Today we revise the physical half of the syllabus — the essentials Paper 2 is built from: Measurement at roughly fifty-five percent of the marks, Maps and Plans at about forty, and Probability threading through for the rest. We run conversions with perimeter, area and the cylinder walked all the way to litres, then scale chains and travel planning, plans and elevations matched by counting, and probability in the three dialects the memo accepts. Then the same skills carry one family wedding — a hall to set up, a gogo to fetch on time, and a thirty percent chance of rain to argue about — because that is exactly how the paper will hand them to you.
