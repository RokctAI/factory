Hey, it's Mandy — a full Paper 1 practice run can feel like standing at the bottom of a mountain, but here's what changes everything: the paper's shape never changes. Learn the shape and half the fear is gone before the first question.

Question 1 is your friend. Thirty marks, Level 1 only, straight from Finance and Data: VAT on R240 is R36, net pay is gross minus deductions, the mode is the value you see most. Bank those thirty like a warm-up lap — but write the one line of working every time, because a slip with no working loses both marks and a slip beside visible method usually keeps one.

Then trust the mark allocations to tell you how much to write. Two marks: one operation, one answer. Six marks: a staircase — like the water bill: free block written down, R162, R182, subtotal R344, times 1,15, R395,60. Each line pays.

If a number goes wrong mid-question, do NOT stop. Carry it forward and keep the operations clean — consistent accuracy pays every correct later step. A finished chain with one slip beats an abandoned chain every single time.

And the comment questions? A sentence with a computed number inside it. Mean 59, median 58, quarter of the class below 49 — say the numbers, add because, collect the marks.

Thirty free marks, allocations as instructions, never abandon a chain, numbers in every comment. Walk the practice run twice and the real paper will feel like the third rehearsal. You've got this!
