Hey, it's Mandy — loans, hire purchase and insurance questions all secretly ask the same thing: what does this deal REALLY cost? One habit answers every one of them: add up everything that will ever be paid, then compare it with the price or the amount received.

Hire purchase: deposit plus every instalment. The R11 999 suite with a R1 199,90 deposit and 24 payments of R589,50 totals R15 347,90 — so the deal costs R3 348,90 more than cash. Write each piece on its own line; each line is usually a mark.

Balloon deals: three pieces, not two. Deposit, plus all the instalments, plus the balloon waiting at the end — R18 990 plus R209 940 plus R56 970 gives R285 900 for the R189 900 car. Never judge the deal by the monthly payment; the balloon is debt too.

Loans: total cost of credit is everything paid minus the cash received, and everything means everything — repayments, the monthly service fee, and the once-off initiation fee. Then the affordability check is just income minus expenses: does the surplus cover the instalment, and with how much room to spare?

Insurance: name the three numbers first — premium, cover, excess. The insurer pays the damage minus the excess, and the comparison questions are premium totals over time against the cover or the claim.

Total everything paid, compare, and finish with a sentence saying what the difference means for the household. You've got this!
