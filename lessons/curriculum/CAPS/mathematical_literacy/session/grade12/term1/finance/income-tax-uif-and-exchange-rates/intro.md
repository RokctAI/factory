Today we follow a salary through the two forces that reach into every South African wallet: the taxman and the exchange rate. A Polokwane radiographer earning R28 500 a month takes us from gross salary through the tax table, the rebate and UIF to the net pay that actually lands in the bank — and then her rands go abroad, measured against the dollar. Matric Paper 1 tests every step: find the bracket, tax only the slice above the line, cap the UIF, and know which way to multiply when currencies swap.
