Today we open the contracts behind almost every big purchase in South Africa: the lounge suite on hire purchase, the car financed with a balloon payment, the R25 000 personal loan, and the insurance policies that pay out on the worst day. Every one of these deals hides its true price, and matric papers test whether you can find it — total everything you would ever pay, stand it next to the cash price, and name the gap in rands. By the end you will judge any deal by its total cost, never by the friendly monthly number it advertises.
