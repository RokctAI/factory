Today we watch money move in both directions: interest pulling it up, inflation dragging its buying power down, and a bank statement full of quiet fees sitting in the middle. One Umlazi family carries the whole session — a gogo investing a R8 000 stokvel payout at simple and then compound interest, a grandson decoding his first bank statement, and a loaf of bread climbing from R19,50 year after year. Matric papers test all three movements, and the winning habit is the same each time: build the year-by-year table and compare every growth rate against inflation.
