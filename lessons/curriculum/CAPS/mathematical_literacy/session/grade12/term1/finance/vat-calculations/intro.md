Today we hunt the fifteen percent hiding in almost every South African price: VAT. One Saturday of spending in Mitchells Plain carries the session — a builder's quote that needs VAT added on, a pair of R299 school shoes that needs it stripped out, and a till slip split between taxed goods and the zero-rated basics the law protects. Matric papers test both directions, and the backwards one is where the marks are lost, so the rule of the day is simple: multiply by 1,15 going forward, divide by 1,15 coming back, and never subtract fifteen percent from an inclusive price.
