Today we are working with probability — the mathematics of uncertainty that prices every lottery ticket, insurance premium and weather forecast in the country. We start with the language of chance and the 0-to-1 scale, separate theoretical probability from relative frequency, and then read the lottery and insurance with honest arithmetic. We finish with tree diagrams and two-way tables, the two tools matric uses to tame events that happen one after another. Every question in this section is counting and dividing — the marks go to whoever reads the denominator out of the question's own words.
