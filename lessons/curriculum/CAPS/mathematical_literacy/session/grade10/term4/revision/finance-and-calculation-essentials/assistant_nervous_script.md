Hey, it's Thandi — a whole year of finance in one revision session sounds heavy, but here is the secret: it is the same four moves wearing different price tags. Percent. Per unit. Two lists. Fixed plus rate.

Percent: 25 percent off R480 is 0,25 times 480 — R120. Percentage change? The word FROM names the bottom of the fraction: from 15 to 18 is 3 over 15, twenty percent. And including VAT means divide by 1,15 — never subtract 15 percent from the tag.

Per unit: best-buy questions are settled by division. R109,50 over 5 kilograms is R21,90 a kilogram; R45,90 over 2 is R22,95. Divide both, then say the winner out loud with its numbers — that sentence is a mark.

Two lists: every budget and every profit question is money in, money out, subtract, name the answer. Surplus, deficit, profit, loss — the name is part of the answer.

Fixed plus rate: bank fees and tariffs are one shape — a fixed part, plus a rate times the usage. R2 plus 1,2 percent of R500 is R8. R50 plus R2,50 times 300 units is R800. On a graph, the fixed part is where the line starts and the rate is the slope.

Last thing: reverse every answer. Price before VAT times 1,15 must hit the tag. Surplus plus expenses must hit the income. One line of proof, every time. Go run the practice questions with those four moves. You've got this!
