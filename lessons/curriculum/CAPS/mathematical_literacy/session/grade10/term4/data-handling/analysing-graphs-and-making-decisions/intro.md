Today we are analysing graphs — the skill that outscores merely drawing them, because analysis is where data handling meets real decisions. Our material is one household's municipal year in three pictures: an electricity line that peaks at 560 units in July, water bars that drop from 28 to 19 kilolitres after one plumbing repair, and a pie chart splitting a R2 400 bill. We will read values and ranges accurately, compute percentage changes with the original safely in the denominator, turn every reading into rand, and finish by exposing the truncated axis — the most common graphical lie in real media. By the end you will conclude only what the numbers support, and check where every axis starts before believing any picture.
