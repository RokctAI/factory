Hey, it's Thandi — analysis questions look wordy and scary, but here is the secret: they are all the same three moves in a trench coat. Read. Multiply. Judge.

Read: finger on the axis, up to the dot, across to the number — and say the unit out loud. 560 units. 28 kilolitres. The unit is half the mark.

Multiply: whatever you read, one multiplication turns it into money or meaning. 560 units times R2,50 is R1 400. Nine kilolitres saved times R32 is R288 a month. Half of a R2 400 bill is R1 200. One multiplication each — nothing deeper hiding underneath.

Judge: end with a sentence that answers the actual question. Worth it? Yes — the R650 repair is repaid by month three. Which slice to attack? The big one — that is where 10 percent saves R120, not R36. The verdict sentence is a mark sitting on the table; do not walk past it.

Percentage change? The word FROM tells you the bottom of the fraction. From 500 to 560: change 60, over 500, is 12 percent. And if a graph's axis does not start at nought — trust nothing, read the real values, redo the comparison yourself.

That is the whole topic: read, multiply, judge, and check the axis. Go run the practice questions with those four moves. You've got this!
