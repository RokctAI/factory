Today we are mastering percentage calculations — the most-used tool in the whole of Mathematical Literacy, running through VAT, discounts, interest, inflation and tariffs, and carrying marks in Question 2 of every Paper 1. Our case study is a jacket priced at R850, discounted by fifteen percent and then hit with fifteen percent VAT — and it hides a famous surprise, because the two fifteens do not cancel. By the end you will command five moves: the decimal trick, the single multiplier, chained changes, percentage change, and running the chain backwards to recover a price before VAT.
