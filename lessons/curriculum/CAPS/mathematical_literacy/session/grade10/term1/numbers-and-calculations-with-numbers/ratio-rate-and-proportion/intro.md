Today we are working with ratio, rate and proportion — three friends splitting R2 400 from a car wash, a shopper choosing between maize-meal bags, and a builder mixing concrete, all running on the same mathematics. You will learn to share any amount in a given ratio, to calculate a unit rate and let it price a journey or a water bill, to tell direct proportion from indirect, and to settle a best-buy argument honestly with a price per kilogram. These four moves anchor tariff, finance and measurement questions right through to matric.
