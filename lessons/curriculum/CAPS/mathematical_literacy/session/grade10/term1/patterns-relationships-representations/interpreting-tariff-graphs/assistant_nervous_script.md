Here's the secret: every line on a money graph only ever tells you two things. Where it starts — that's the fixed charge. And how fast it climbs — that's the price per unit. That's it. Two facts per line, and you can read both without calculating anything.

The crossing point? That's just the graph saying "here, both options cost the same." Left of it one option wins, right of it the other. You already use this logic when you compare data bundles — "is the big bundle worth it if I don't use much?" Same thinking, now with a picture.

So jump into the practice questions, and every time you read a value off the graph, do the quick multiplication to check it — graph says it, arithmetic confirms it. When those two agree, you KNOW you're right, and that feeling is addictive. You've got this!
