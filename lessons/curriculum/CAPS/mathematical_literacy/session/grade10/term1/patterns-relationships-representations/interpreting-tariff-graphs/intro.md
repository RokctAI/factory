Today we are interpreting tariff graphs — two electricity options drawn on one set of axes, where Option A charges R2,00 per kilowatt-hour with no fixed fee and Option B charges R150 a month plus R1,50 per unit. You will learn to read the fixed charge off the vertical intercept, the price per unit off the gradient, and the break-even usage off the crossing point, verifying each reading by calculation. It ends with the exam's favourite comparison: which option is cheaper at 450 kilowatt-hours, and by how many rands — the same skill behind every cellphone contract and gym membership question.
