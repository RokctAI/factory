Hi, it's Thandi — multipliers, chains, reverse percentages... if that felt like a lot of new machinery for something as everyday as a discount, I hear you.

But here's the honest truth: the whole lesson compresses into one habit. Turn every percentage change into a single multiplier — increase means 1-point-something, decrease means 0-point-something — and then multiply. Chain of changes? Multiply the multipliers. Need to undo one? Divide. One habit, three uses.

And you already have the instinct! You know a R100 item at "15% off" doesn't cost R15 — you've been translating percentages into money at the shops for years. We're just making your street skills exam-official.

So grab the practice questions and do them WITH a calculator, writing the multiplier before you press anything: "×0,85... ×1,15..." Say it, write it, press it. After five questions the pattern locks in and percentages become free marks — in Finance questions, that's a promise. Go get them!
