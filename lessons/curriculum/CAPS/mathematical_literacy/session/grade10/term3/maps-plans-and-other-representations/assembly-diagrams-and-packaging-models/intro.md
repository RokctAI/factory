Today we are reading two humble but examinable documents: the assembly diagram that falls out of a flat-pack box, and the packaging problem of fitting round cans into rectangular crates. Both reward slow reading and small multiplications — translating exploded views and symbols into words, checking that a parts list's quantities interlock, and counting can spots one dimension at a time instead of dividing volumes. We will also learn the two rounding worlds of this topic: buying rounds up, fitting rounds down, and the question's verb tells you which. By the end you will be able to catch a misprinted parts list and prove which crate is the cost-effective choice for a real job.
