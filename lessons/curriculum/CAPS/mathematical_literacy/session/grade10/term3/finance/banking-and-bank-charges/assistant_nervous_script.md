Hey, it's Thandi — fee tables can look like the back of a cereal box, all small print and traps. Let me hand you the three keys that unlock every bank-charges question.

Key one: every fee has a shape. Flat, like the R1,60 cashback. Fixed-plus-rate, like R2,00 plus R1,00 per hundred. Or the once-a-month admin fee that charges you for simply existing. Name the shape first and the calculation writes itself.

Key two: "per R100 or part thereof" means count the hundreds UP. R250 is three hundreds, not two and a half. The bank always rounds in its own favour — expect it and it can never trick you.

Key three: comparing two accounts is just costing out the SAME month twice. Four withdrawals? EasySave R34,50, Bundle R55 — EasySave wins by R20,50. Twelve big withdrawals? R150,50 against R55 — Bundle wins. Then say the winner, the margin, and the advice in one sentence. That sentence is a mark.

The vocabulary is free marks too: on a statement, debit is money out, credit is money in. Ten seconds to memorise, guaranteed to appear.

Now go cost out the practice months — shape, count, total, sentence. You've got this!
