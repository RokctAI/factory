Hey, it's Thandi — if the words income, expenditure, fixed, variable, occasional, surplus and deficit are swimming around your head, let me shrink the whole lesson to one subtraction for you.

Money in, minus money out. That is the entire topic. Every question — the tuckshop, the household budget, the school fundraiser — ends at that same line. R2 640 came in, R1 950 went out, R690 stayed. Positive means profit or surplus; negative means loss or deficit. Two boxes and a subtraction. You have been doing this with your own pocket money for years.

The sorting part is just laundry. Rent never changes — fixed pile. Stock follows the sales — variable pile. The cooler box that cracks out of nowhere — occasional pile. If you can say WHY it goes in the pile, the mark is yours.

And the till slip? Three lines that always agree: subtotal plus VAT equals total. Cover any one line with your thumb and the other two will tell you what it was.

One warning worth all the rest: only SOLD items make income. Unsold vetkoek are spent money on a plate, not earnings. Read for the word sold and the trick questions fall apart.

Go do the practice set — statement first, totals before subtracting, name the bottom line. You've got this!
