Today we are doing probability in everyday contexts — a jar of sweets, a two-coin game at a school fun day, and a clinic survey linking age to transport, all answered with one small toolkit. You will learn the scale from 0 to 1 and its three ways of writing a chance, the difference between theoretical probability and relative frequency, how to read probabilities out of a two-way table, and how a tree diagram exposes the full sample space. It ends with the question that makes the topic a life skill: is the game fair, and what prize would make it so?
