Today we are building a personal budget from the ground up — a Katlehong household with a payslip, rent from a back room, weekend sewing income, and eleven bills that all want paying. You will learn to read gross and net income off a payslip and budget only on what actually arrives, to sort expenditure into fixed, variable and occasional, and to find the bottom line and name it a surplus or a deficit. Then we turn the same logic on a vetkoek stall to find its break-even point — the calculation that anchors finance questions in both papers.
