Today we are working with VAT, discount and simple interest — the three money tools behind every till slip, sale sign and savings account in the country. Using a real Mthatha shopping basket, you will learn to add VAT forwards, remove it backwards by dividing by 1,15, spot the zero-rated goods the taxman never touches, and see why successive discounts refuse to add up. We finish with simple interest and a fridge-on-sale decision that pulls all three tools into one answer — the exact reasoning Paper 1 finance questions reward.
