Today we are using perimeter, area and volume where CAPS actually tests them — a Seshego household fencing a garden, tiling and painting a back room, and working out how long a water tank will last. You will learn to convert units first, keep them on every line, and make the two rounding judgements examiners watch for: materials round up, days of supply round down. By the end, a tape measure and a price list will give you a full costed answer — rolls of mesh, boxes of tiles, tins of paint and days of water.
