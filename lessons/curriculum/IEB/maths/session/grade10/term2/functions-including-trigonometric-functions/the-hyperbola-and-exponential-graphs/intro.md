Today we complete the Grade 10 function family with the hyperbola and the exponential graph — the two curves built around an asymptote, a line they approach forever without ever touching. We build each shape point by point from a table, watch the parameters a and q move the graph and carry its asymptote along, and work full sketches with every intercept, restriction and range in place. These two graphs sit at the heart of the functions work in your exams, and the marks live in exactly the features we drill today: the moved asymptote, the strict inequality in the range, and intercepts found honestly by substitution.
