Today we sketch and interpret the parabola in its Grade 10 form, y equals a x squared plus q — the first curved graph you build from an equation alone. We construct the mother function point by point, isolate exactly what the parameter a does and what q does, then run a complete five-step sketch and reverse the process to recover an equation from a drawn graph. This is core territory in the functions work of your exams, and the routine we drill today — shape, turn, crossings, extra point — turns every parabola question into a few quick lines and one clean curve.
