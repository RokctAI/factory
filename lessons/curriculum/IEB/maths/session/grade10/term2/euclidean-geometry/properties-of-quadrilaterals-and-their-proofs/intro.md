Today we take on the special quadrilaterals and the proofs behind their properties — the first part of the course where marks are earned by reasoning rather than by arithmetic. We build the family from trapezium to square and trace exactly what each shape inherits from its parent, then prove the parallelogram's properties from its definition and learn the conditions that let you prove a figure is a parallelogram, rectangle or rhombus. The session ends with a complete worked rider, written statement by statement with a reason beside every line — because that is precisely how these questions are marked.
