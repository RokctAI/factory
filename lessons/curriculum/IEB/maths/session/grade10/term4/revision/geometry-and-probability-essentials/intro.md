Today is the revision sweep for the geometry and data half of the year — quadrilaterals and the midpoint theorem, analytical geometry, probability and statistics, compressed into four small toolkits. The quadrilaterals become a family tree read downwards for properties and upwards for cheapest-ticket proofs; two points on the grid yield distance, midpoint and gradient; the addition rule subtracts the Venn overlap exactly once; and any data set sits for its five-number portrait, drawn as a box plot. Four topics, four pictures, and every one of them checks its own totals.
