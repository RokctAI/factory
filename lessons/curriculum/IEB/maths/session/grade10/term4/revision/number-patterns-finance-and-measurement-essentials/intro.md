Today is the revision sweep for the three topics the other revision sessions leave untouched — number patterns, finance and measurement — each running on a tiny engine. Patterns run on one constant difference: build term n from the spacing, drive it forwards, and let a fractional position say no. Finance runs on two growth formulas: the flat fee of simple interest and the compound snowball, with hire purchase, inflation and exchange rates as costumes. Measurement runs on a short catalogue of solids and one law — lengths by k, areas by k squared, volumes by k cubed. Three engines, four classic traps, and a free proof attached to every answer.
