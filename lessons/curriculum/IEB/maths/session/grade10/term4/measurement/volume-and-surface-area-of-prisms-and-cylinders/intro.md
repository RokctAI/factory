Today we restore the measurement toolkit that end-of-year questions assume is ready: volume and surface area of right prisms and cylinders. One law carries the entire topic — volume is the area of the base times the perpendicular height, a stack of identical layers — while surface area comes from unfolding the solid flat into its net and totalling the faces. We work a tea box, a paint tin and a triangular prism from start to finish, holding every answer exact in pi until the final line. Get the base right and the units right, and every solid a paper can offer yields to the same five-step checklist.
