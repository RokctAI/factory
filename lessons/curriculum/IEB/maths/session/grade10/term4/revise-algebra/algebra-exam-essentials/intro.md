Today is the algebra revision sweep for the end-of-year examinations — the whole year's algebra reorganised into four decisions: how to factorise, how to solve each equation species, how to handle an inequality, and how to drive the exponent laws. We run the factorising order until it is automatic, solve one equation of every species, defend the inequality flip at the mirror, and chain the exponent laws through integrated questions. Then we patrol the four classic traps — cancelled terms, the divided-away solution, the unflipped sign, and invented laws for sums — and install the free proof: substitute back, expand back, test a value. Exam-day algebra, compressed and ordered.
