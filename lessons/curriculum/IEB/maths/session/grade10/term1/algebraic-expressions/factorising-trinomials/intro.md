Today we are factorising trinomials — the busiest factorisation tool in all of algebra, and the one exam setters lean on more than any other. Our anchor problem carries the classic complication: a leading coefficient that is not one, which changes the famous product-and-sum hunt in exactly one place. We will read expansion backwards to discover the rule, let the signs shape the pair before any searching starts, split the middle term, finish with grouping, and prove every answer by expanding it back. Master this one procedure and every trinomial the syllabus can offer breaks open along the same seam.
