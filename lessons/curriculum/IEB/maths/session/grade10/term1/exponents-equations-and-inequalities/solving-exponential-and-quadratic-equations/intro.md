Today we are solving the two families of equation that rule the algebra questions at this level: quadratics and exponentials. Both give way to one strategy — turn the unfamiliar into something already solved. A quadratic gets driven into standard form and factorised, so the zero-product law can read its brackets apart; an exponential gets rewritten onto a single base, so its exponents can be equated. Six worked equations carry the session, and standing guard around them are the two costliest errors in the topic — the root lost by dividing through by the unknown, and exponents equated across different bases. Meet both traps here, in daylight, and they will never take a mark from you again.
