Hey, it's Thandi — new topic, strange words, a calculator button you've never used... if trigonometry feels like a lot right now, let me shrink it to its actual size, because underneath the vocabulary there are only three fractions.

Sine is opposite over hypotenuse. Cosine is adjacent over hypotenuse. Tangent is opposite over adjacent. SOH CAH TOA — that's the entire subject at this level. Every question is just: stand at your angle, name the two sides that matter, and let their letters pick the fraction.

The special angles? You never memorise them. Two ten-second drawings hold all six values: cut a triangle with sides of two down the middle for thirty and sixty degrees, fold a square of side one corner to corner for forty-five. Pythagoras fills in root three and root two, and you READ the answers off the picture.

And the inverse button is just reverse gear: angle in, ratio out is forward; ratio in, angle out is reverse — shift, then sine. Ask "am I hunting a side or an angle?" and the gear chooses itself.

One habit before you start: check your calculator shows the D for degrees, and test it with sine thirty — you know it must say zero comma five.

Now go do the practice questions. Three fractions, two drawings, one reverse gear. That's the whole gateway, and you're already through it. You've got this!
