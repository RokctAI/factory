Today we open the gateway to the biggest single source of marks in senior mathematics: trigonometry, beginning with the three ratios and the special angles. Two exam-style tasks carry the session — evaluating sine forty-five times cosine forty-five plus tangent sixty times tangent thirty without touching a calculator, and recovering an unknown angle from a hypotenuse of twenty-five and an opposite side of seven. Along the way we build the two special triangles you can redraw in ten seconds instead of memorising six values, and we learn the inverse move — the reverse gear that turns a known ratio back into its angle. Three ratios, two triangles, one choosing routine: the complete starter kit for years of trigonometry.
