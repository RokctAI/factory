Today we master the arithmetic of "typical": the mean, the median and the mode — three different ways of compressing a whole dataset into one representative number. A set of reading times drives the first half of the session, a grouped frequency table takes over in the second, and by the end you will compute all three measures, watch an outlier expose the difference between them, justify your choice in one clean sentence, and estimate a mean that cannot be computed exactly. Learn which measure to trust and why, and you will read data the way an analyst does for the rest of your life.
