Today we learn to put a number on chance itself. Probability runs on two engines — theoretical probability, where you count what could happen, and relative frequency, where you count what actually did happen — and this session builds both. One fair eight-sector spinner and one bag of coloured marbles travel with us the whole way, and by the end you will define the vocabulary precisely, calculate with both engines, and know exactly when each one is the right tool. Get the two counts and one divide under control today, and this topic becomes some of the most reliable ground you will ever stand on.
