Today we learn to handle two events at once. One event was counting; two events are territory — regions, overlaps, and the rule that stops anyone being counted twice. Our tools are the Venn diagram, the addition rule with its famous subtraction, and the precise vocabulary of mutually exclusive and complementary events. One survey of fifty learners runs through the whole session, and by the end you will build a two-event diagram from scratch, pull any probability out of it, and know exactly when the subtraction is owed and when it is not. Map the territory once, properly, and every two-event question becomes a walk across known ground.
