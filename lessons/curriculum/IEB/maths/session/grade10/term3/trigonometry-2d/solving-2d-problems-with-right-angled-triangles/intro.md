Today we turn the three trigonometric ratios you already know into a genuine problem-solving machine. Heights of masts, distances to cars below a cliff, the angle of a leaning ladder — every one of them is a right-angled triangle wearing a story. One communications mast travels through the whole session, a second triangle joins near the end for the famous two-observation problem, and by the close you will convert any written description into a labelled sketch, choose the right ratio by what you have and what you want, and finish with an exact answer that carries its unit. Sketch, label, choose — three steps that never change.
