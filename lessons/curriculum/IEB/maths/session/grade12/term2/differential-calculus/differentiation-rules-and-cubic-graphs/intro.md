Today differentiation graduates from handwork to machinery: the power rule handles any power of x in one line, and with it the cubic function — the headline graph of Grade twelve — opens up completely. Calculus questions anchor the algebra half of your final examinations, and the full cubic sketch with its turning points is their centrepiece. We will drill the rule and the rewriting it insists on, build the tangent routine, sketch a complete cubic from its stationary points and intercepts, and learn to read a function's entire story through its first and second derivatives.
