Today calculus stops being about sketching and becomes what it exists for: measuring how quickly things change, and finding the best possible value of anything you can capture in a formula. Rates of change and optimisation supply the culminating calculus questions of your final examinations — the moving particle that comes to rest, the biggest camp a roll of fencing can enclose, the cheapest tin that still holds enough. We will build the motion chain from position to velocity to acceleration, master the four-step optimisation recipe, work a complete volume-and-material problem, and practise the judgement that turns a calculus answer into a real-world one.
