Today we pour the two foundation stones of calculus: the limit, which lets a quantity approach a value it never has to reach, and differentiation from first principles, which turns that limit into a machine for measuring change at a single instant. First principles is a skill your final examinations return to directly, and every differentiation rule you will use for the rest of the year is born in it. We begin with the gap in a graph and the value its neighbourhood points at, shrink an average gradient into an instantaneous one, and finish by applying the four-step definition of the derivative to each of the syllabus functions.
