Analytical geometry finally gets a curve today: the circle, together with the tangent line that touches it at exactly one point. The circle's equation turns out to be the distance formula with a condition attached, and every tangent question runs on one geometric theorem — the radius to the touching point meets the tangent at ninety degrees. We will read centre and radius directly from the equation, complete the square to recover a circle from its expanded disguise, and build a four-step routine that produces any tangent's equation — skills your final examinations return to year after year.
