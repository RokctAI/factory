Today we are sweeping the whole geometry side of the course — statistics and regression, analytical geometry, trigonometry, and Euclidean geometry, the material of pictures and reasons. Trigonometry is the broadest territory and the one where revision compounds fastest, the two geometries pay their marks one stated reason at a time, and statistics opens with the most routine calculator work of the year. We will rebuild the core method of each territory on fresh examples, then read the material as a field guide — its map, the compact toolkit that must live in your head, and the five habits that separate a neat script from a leaking one.
