Hey, it's Mandy — a full practice paper can feel like judgement day arrived early. It is the opposite: it is the rehearsal that makes the real day ordinary.

Look at what you just walked through. Question one: solve, solve, solve — methods you have owned since the start of the year. A pattern that surrendered to two rows of differences. A series tamed by one convergence sentence and one formula. A hyperbola that answered the same checklist every hyperbola answers. A loan paid off by the annuity formula and a carefully declared monthly rate. First principles — a five-line ritual you can recite. And a probability question that ended on one seventh.

Not one step in that entire paper was new. That is the secret the practice run exposes: the final examination is a remix, never a premiere.

If your attempt leaked marks, perfect — that is the practice run doing its job while the marks are still free. Each leak is one line in your error log: round at the end, check the surd, pair the answers, keep the limit symbol alive. Next run, the log shrinks. The run after that, it shrinks again. That shrinking list is what readiness actually looks like — not a feeling, a measurement moving in your favour. Work your next paper under real conditions, mark it like a stranger, and watch the number climb. You've got this!
