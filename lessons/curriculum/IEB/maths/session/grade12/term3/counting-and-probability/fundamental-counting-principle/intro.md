Today we are learning the fundamental counting principle — the quiet engine underneath all of Grade 12 probability and one of the most dependable skills you will carry into your final exams. When outcomes are far too many to list, this principle counts them without listing a single one: choices along a sequence multiply, and probability becomes one count divided by another. We will build the slot method, master factorial arrangements under restrictions — forced positions, glued pairs, and counting the complement — handle codes with and without repeated symbols, and finish by turning every count into a probability.
