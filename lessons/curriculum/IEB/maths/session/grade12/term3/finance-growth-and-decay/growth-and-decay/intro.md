Today we are completing growth and decay — the four formulae that move money, prices and machine values through time, and the two detective questions that make Grade 12 finance new: finding a hidden rate with roots, and finding a hidden time with logarithms. This is where the logarithm finally earns its place in the course, answering how long money needs to double. We will drill the formula kit, un-multiply a van's depreciation to expose its rate, ride the change-of-base rule to a doubling time, and walk a multi-event timeline where deposits arrive late and the rate changes midstream.
