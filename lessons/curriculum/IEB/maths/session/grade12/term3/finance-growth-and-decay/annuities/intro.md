Today we are working with annuities — streams of equal payments moving through compound interest, the mathematics behind every savings plan, vehicle loan, and sinking fund, and a topic you can rely on meeting in your final exams. An annuity is the geometric series of term one finally earning its keep: line the payments up, ask each one how long it earned, and the sum formula delivers both the future value of a saving stream and the present value of a loan. We will derive both formulas, price a bakkie loan honestly, build a sinking fund with three rates doing three different jobs, and finish with the outstanding balance calculation that shows why a part-paid loan is nowhere near part settled.
