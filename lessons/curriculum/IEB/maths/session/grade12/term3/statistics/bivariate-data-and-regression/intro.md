Today we are working with bivariate data and regression — statistics grown up from single lists to pairs, asking whether two measurements taken on the same person travel together. We will pin paired data onto a scatterplot and read its direction, form and strength; let the calculator fit the least squares regression line and interpret its gradient in context; score the tightness of the cloud with the correlation coefficient r; and finish with the discipline of prediction — interpolating with confidence inside the data, flagging every extrapolation beyond it, and never mistaking a strong correlation for proof of cause.
