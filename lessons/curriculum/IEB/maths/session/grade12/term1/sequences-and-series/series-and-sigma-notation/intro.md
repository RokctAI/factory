Today sequences learn to add themselves up. Sigma notation gives us one line that commands a hundred additions, the arithmetic sum formula folds a long list into pairs, the geometric sum formula collapses like a telescope, and then comes the strangest result of the year: an infinite list of positive numbers whose total is a single finite value. We will read and write the sigma instruction, prove both sum formulas, hunt the number of terms behind a given total, and use convergence to turn every repeating decimal into the fraction it secretly is.
