Today we take the simplest pattern in mathematics and turn it into serious equipment: the arithmetic sequence, where every term climbs by the same fixed difference. The general term T n equals a plus n minus one times d is the engine behind almost every question this topic can ask, right through to your final exams. We will certify a sequence with the constant difference test, leap straight to term two hundred, dig out unknowns hiding inside the terms, insert arithmetic means, and translate word problems about seedlings and scooter fares into term numbers.
