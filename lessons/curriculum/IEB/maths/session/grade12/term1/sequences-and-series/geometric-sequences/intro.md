Today the sequence family swaps addition for multiplication: the geometric sequence, where every term is the previous one times a fixed ratio. This single change powers compound interest, population growth and radioactive decay, and it all rests on T n equals a times r to the power n minus one. We will certify sequences with the constant ratio test, leap to distant terms through the exponent, insert geometric means, solve for unknowns hiding inside the terms, and translate bouncing balls and doubling bacteria into sequence language.
