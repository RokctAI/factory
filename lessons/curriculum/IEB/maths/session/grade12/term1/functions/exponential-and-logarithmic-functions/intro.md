Today we reverse the graph that refuses ordinary algebra: the exponential. To undo y equals b to the power x we need a new word entirely — the logarithm — and this session defines it, draws it and sets it to work. We will read the exponential graph with its anchor point and asymptote, translate freely between log statements and exponential statements, sketch the log curve as the exponential's reflection in the line y equals x, and master the skill that carries straight into finance: pulling an unknown down out of an exponent.
