Today trigonometry escapes the right angle. The area rule and the sine rule work in any triangle at all, and together they can price a wedge of farmland or reach across a river nobody can swim. Everything hangs on one labelling convention — small letters facing capitals — and two short proofs you must own, because both are asked. We will build the area rule from a manufactured height, tip it over into the sine rule, learn to hunt sides and angles with one fixed exchange rate, interrogate the second suspect that hides behind every inverse sine, and finish by chaining the rules through the multi-step problems where the route is planned before the driving starts.
