Today finance learns to run downhill: simple and compound decay put a value on every ageing minibus and fading laptop, and the same twelve percent tells two completely different stories depending on whether it bites the original price or the current one. Then we turn the magnifying glass on growth itself — what quarterly or monthly compounding really does to an advertised rate — and learn to convert any nominal headline into the honest effective rate behind it. With exchange rates and chained timelines added, you leave holding the complete grade eleven finance toolkit.
