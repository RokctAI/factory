Today we take charge of the three great containers of grade eleven probability: the three-event Venn diagram, the tree diagram for events in sequence, and the contingency table. Every probability story you will meet arrives in one of these shapes, and the skill genuinely under test is diagnosing the shape from the wording alone and reaching for the right tool. We will fill a three-circle survey from the centre outwards, grow a without-replacement tree whose branches remember what has already been drawn, and put independence on trial with the product test. Choose the container correctly and the arithmetic inside it is short, structured and safe.
