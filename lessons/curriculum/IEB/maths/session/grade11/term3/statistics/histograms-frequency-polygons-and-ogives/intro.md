Today we take grouped data — the form in which real-world numbers actually arrive — and master its three pictures: the histogram, the frequency polygon, and the ogive, the cumulative frequency curve that grade eleven adds to your toolkit. One dataset of fifty learners' daily study times runs through the entire session, so you will watch the same numbers become touching bars, a mountain profile, and a lazy-S curve. The payoff is the ogive's two-step reading — across, then down — which hands you medians, quartiles and percentiles straight off the graph, with nothing but a ruler and the word approximately.
