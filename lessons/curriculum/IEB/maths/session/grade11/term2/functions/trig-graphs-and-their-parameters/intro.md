Today the sine, cosine and tangent numbers of term one are set in motion and become graphs — waves that repeat forever — and we take command of the four controls that shape them: a for the height of the swing, q for the middle line, k for how fast the wave cycles, and the horizontal shift that decides where it starts. We will sketch from anchor points, recover equations from pictures by averaging the top and bottom of the swing, and finish with the two-control combined sketch, closed off by playing the first note — the y-intercept check that catches errors before a marker ever sees them. The controls are independent, and reading them one at a time is the whole skill.
