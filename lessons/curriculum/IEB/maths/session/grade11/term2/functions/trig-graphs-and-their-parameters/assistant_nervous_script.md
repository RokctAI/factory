Hey, it's Bianca — if your page turned into a spaghetti of overlapping waves today, breathe. This whole lesson is a sound system with four knobs, and you already knew three of them from the other graphs.

Knob one, the volume: the a outside multiplies every height — three sine x swings three times as high. Knob two, the shelf: the q lifts or lowers the whole monitor — the middle line simply moves to q. Knob three, the fast-forward: the k inside squeezes the wave — new period is three sixty over k, and heights are completely untouched, because faster is never louder. Knob four, the start time: the bracket delays the song — and you never argue with its sign, you just ask where the bracket equals zero. That x is where the wave begins.

Reading a picture is the same three questions every time: where is the calm middle — that is q; how far is a peak from the middle — that is a; how many waves fit the window — that is k. Top two, bottom minus four: middle minus one, half-swing three. Done.
