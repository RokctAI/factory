Today the three graph families of grade ten — parabola, hyperbola and exponential — each receive their full control panel: the a that stretches and flips, the p that pushes sideways against its sign, and the q that hoists or lowers. We will read turning points and asymptotes directly off equations, pin down every intercept, and write domain and range with the exceptions and strict inequalities that markers check first. The session closes with the average gradient between two points on a curve, and with the shrinking-interval table that quietly opens the door to grade twelve calculus.
