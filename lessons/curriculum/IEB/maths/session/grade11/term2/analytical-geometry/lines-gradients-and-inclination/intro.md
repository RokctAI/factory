Today geometry hands its work to algebra: every point becomes a pair of coordinates, and every claim about lengths, midpoints and angles becomes a calculation you can check. We sharpen the three instruments from grade ten — distance, midpoint and gradient — and then add the two new powers of grade eleven: building the equation of any straight line from whatever information a question offers, and reading the angle of inclination straight out of a gradient. The recipes are short, they repeat, and once they are yours this topic becomes one of the most dependable places to collect marks in your final exams.
