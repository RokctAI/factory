Today we finish the circle toolkit with the two families still missing: the cyclic quadrilateral, whose four vertices all touch one circle, and the tangent, the line that grazes the circle at a single point. These bring the theorems that decide the toughest riders — opposite angles totalling one eighty, the three converse tests that prove four points share a circle, the twin tangents from an outside point, and the tangent-chord angle that echoes unchanged into the alternate segment. We will prove the theorems, run the standard Pythagoras calculations, and close with a full rider solved along two independent roads that land on the same answer.
