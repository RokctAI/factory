Today geometry steps inside the circle, and the rules of the game change: theorems must now be proved, not just used, and every line of a solution must carry the reason that makes it true. We build the first half of the circle toolkit — the chord theorems and the perpendicular symmetry that finds the centre, the angle at the centre that doubles every angle at the circumference, the right angle waiting inside every semicircle, and the equal angles that share a segment. Then we practise the craft the marks are really paid for: writing a rider as a chain of claims, each with its reason, that a marker can audit line by line.
