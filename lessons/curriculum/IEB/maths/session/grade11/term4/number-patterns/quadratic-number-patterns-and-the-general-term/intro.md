Today we step up from patterns that grow steadily to patterns that accelerate — quadratic number patterns, identified by a constant second difference. You will learn to run the diagnosis, then to assemble the general term from any such sequence using three facts read directly off the difference table, and to drive that formula in both directions: positions forward into terms, and term values backward into positions, where a quadratic equation and its discriminant decide whether a number ever appears in the pattern at all. We finish inside the brick-and-tile picture patterns that assessments favour, where the structure of the figure can hand you the formula for free.
