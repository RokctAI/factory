Hey, it's Bianca — circle geometry, statistics AND finance in one revision lesson can look like three different subjects, but each one is a picture with a procedure attached, and you've run all three this year.

Circles: a crime scene with three witnesses. The centre testifies at ninety degrees to any chord — Pythagoras closes every chord case. The arc tells the same story doubled at the centre and identically from every rim seat on its side. The tangent testifies at ninety degrees to the radius and whispers into the alternate segment. Mark the givens, chase one theorem at a time, and file a reason with every statement — the reason is half the mark.

Analytical geometry adds one link: gradient equals tan of the inclination. Positive gradient, acute angle; negative gradient, one eighty minus the reference. Sketch first and the signs police themselves.

Statistics is a recipe: mean, deviations, square, average, root. Square because raw deviations always cancel to zero. Ogives read across, touch, down; outliers live beyond the one-comma-five-IQR fences — by definition, not by debate.

Finance: two downhill slopes. Straight line loses the same chunk of the original every year; reducing balance bites a shrinking remainder. The wording picks the formula. And nominal rates are labels — compound the monthly bite to find the true effective contents.
