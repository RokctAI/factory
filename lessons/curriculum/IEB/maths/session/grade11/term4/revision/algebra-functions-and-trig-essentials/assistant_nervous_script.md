Trig: one circle. Sine is height, cosine is shadow, CAST records the signs, reduction is just the circle's symmetry, and general solutions are the circle refusing to stop spinning — the base angles plus k full turns. Triangles: matching pair, sine rule; two sides gripping an angle, cosine rule; area, half a b sine of the included angle.
