This revision session pulls the human half of your Grade 11 year into a single argument. We start with development — the difference between growth and genuine improvement in lives, the indicator toolkit from GDP per capita through Gini to the HDI, and the core-periphery and Rostow frames with their criticisms. Then trade and aid: the primary-product trap, the sliding terms of trade, beneficiation as the escape, and the aid debate argued honestly in both directions. From there we power the country — coal's chain from seam to socket, Koeberg, the water batteries, and the sun and wind that now undercut new coal — and finish on soil, the erosion ladder and its remedies, all bound by the definition worth knowing word for word: sustainability. One session, the whole human year, arranged so the connections that win essay marks in your final exams are already drawn.
