This revision session reassembles the physical half of your Grade 11 year into one connected machine. We start with unequal heating and follow it through pressure belts, cells and the Coriolis bend to Africa's climates — the wandering ITCZ, the three highs that box in southern Africa, and the precise vocabulary of drought and desertification. Then we turn to rock: the tabletop family carved from flat Karoo strata, the tilted ridges from cuesta to hogsback, and granite's domes and tors, before dissecting the slope into its four elements and grading mass movement from creep to rockfall. Everything lands on the map sheet at the end, because in your final exams each of these landforms is also a contour pattern, a gradient calculation and a sight line. One session, the whole physical year, organised so it stays organised under pressure.
