This session takes the other lane at the energy fork: non-conventional sources — solar, wind and their renewable siblings — and why South Africa holds sun and wind territory ranked among the finest measured anywhere. The topic rounds off the energy unit and suits evaluation questions perfectly: precise definitions, named South African and world examples, and effects on economy and environment argued in both columns. We will define the family and the two properties that rule it, tour photovoltaic farms and molten-salt storage plants across the Northern Cape, work the wind turbine's cube law along the coasts from Gibson Bay to Sere, and finish by weighing the future honestly — grid capacity, storage and a just transition for coal country. Command this material and the whole energy debate closes in your favour.
