This session closes the energy unit with its most practical theme: management — how South Africa steers its energy use toward a greener economy and sustainable lifestyles. It is a natural home for evaluation questions, because the syllabus assigns responsibility by name to government, business and individuals, and full-credit answers give each actor its own specific tools rather than vague good intentions. We will build the case for managing energy at all — supply, climate and trade, and equity — then work through government's plans, taxes and standards, business's audits, rooftop solar and carbon-footprint pressure, and the household choices that assemble the evening peak. The master idea runs through everything: the cheapest, cleanest unit of energy is the one never used.
