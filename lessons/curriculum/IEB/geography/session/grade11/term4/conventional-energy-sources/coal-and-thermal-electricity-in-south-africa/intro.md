This session covers South Africa's conventional energy sources — the coal that supplies roughly eight in every ten units of our electricity, the small hydro-electric share a dry country can manage, and nuclear power as the case study for whether conventional sources can carry the decades ahead. Energy is a cornerstone of the resources and sustainability theme, and in your final exams the strongest answers explain processes in strict order and weigh both sides of every argument with specifics. We will map the energy mix and its coalfield geography, follow the energy chain through a coal-fired power station from conveyor belt to transmission line, audit coal's genuine benefits against its mounting environmental costs, and close at the fork in the road where Koeberg and the logic of a diversified mix wait. Command this material and the whole energy debate becomes yours to argue.
