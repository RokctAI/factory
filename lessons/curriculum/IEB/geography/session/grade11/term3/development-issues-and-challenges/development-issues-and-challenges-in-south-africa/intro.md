This session asks why development is so hard to achieve, and refuses the easy answer of a single villain. We trace the tangle of interrelated factors — resources and the resource curse, energy, colonial and apartheid history, trade imbalances, population growth, education and environmental limits — then turn to South Africa's own burdens: the spatial legacy, extreme inequality and unemployment, leaking skills pipelines, strained services and energy insecurity. From there we look at community-based development, where residents plan, build and own the solutions, with real rural and urban cases to name, and we close with the two-way relationship between development and the environment — damage as a loan against the future, poverty as a driver of degradation, and sustainable development as the answer to both. Every idea here feeds the term's essay questions, so settle in and build the full picture.
