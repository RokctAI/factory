The development topic ends on its hardest question: does aid actually help? Today we define development aid and official development assistance, trace its three channels — bilateral, multilateral and non-governmental — and see why the newer language of development co-operation, partnership with duties on both sides, matters so much, with South Africa sitting on both sides of the table. We sort aid into its three types: technical aid that transfers skills, conditional aid with its two kinds of strings, and humanitarian relief that must be judged as relief, not as development. Then the honest weighing: smallpox eradicated, South Africa's antiretroviral programme, malaria beaten back, rapid disaster relief — against dependency, market distortion, the debt trap, structural adjustment's costs, corruption and fragmentation. By the end you will carry the scaffolding verdict: aid as essential support that must be designed, from day one, to come down.
