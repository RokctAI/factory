This session lays the foundation for the whole development term: what development truly means, and why it can never be reduced to money. We separate development from economic growth and standard of living from quality of life, then build the measuring kit — GDP and GNP told apart by soil versus passport, the per capita correction, the Gini coefficient that shows how the income cake is actually cut, and the Human Development Index that grades health, education and income on one report card. We end by trading the old rich-North-poor-South boxes for a continuum, and placing South Africa on it honestly with evidence on both sides. Every development question this term draws on these definitions and indicators, so this is the session to master word for word.
