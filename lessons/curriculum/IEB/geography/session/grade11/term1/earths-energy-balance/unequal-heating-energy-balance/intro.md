Today we are working out why the planet heats unevenly and how it settles its energy accounts — the engine that every wind, current and weather system in this term runs on. We begin with the geometry that concentrates sunlight in the tropics and dilutes it toward the poles, then follow the tilted axis and the revolution that swing the seasons, the five named processes that move heat once it lands, and the surplus-and-deficit ledger that forces energy to stream poleward without rest. The midday sun-angle calculation is built in here too, worked for Johannesburg at both solstices, and once the method is second nature those are dependable marks in your final exams. Get this session solid and the rest of the climate work stops being a list of facts and becomes one connected machine.
