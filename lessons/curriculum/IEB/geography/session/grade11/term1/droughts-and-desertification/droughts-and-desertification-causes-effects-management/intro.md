Today we are following what happens when the rains stay away for years at a time: drought, defined against each place's own normal and tracked through its four deepening stages, and desertification, the human-driven degradation that drought drags into the open. We trace the causal chain from overgrazing to the self-feeding erosion spiral, count the costs from failed harvests to drought migration, and walk through the Nelson Mandela Bay water crisis — a modern city that came within one season of the bottom of its dams. We finish with the three families of management — seeing it coming, spending less and healing the land — carrying named programmes like Working for Water and the Great Green Wall, so that every drought question finds you with a complete toolkit in hand.
