Today we map South Africa's four core industrial regions and work two of them as full case studies — Gauteng, the old PWV, and Port Elizabeth–Uitenhage on Algoa Bay — using a standard grid of location factors, activities, successes, hindrances and impacts. One sentence organises the entire map: minerals built the inland region, harbours built the coastal three. We finish with the policies meant to spread industry beyond the four — Spatial Development Initiatives with the Maputo Corridor as flagship, and Industrial Development Zones like Coega inside the Special Economic Zone framework — and weigh honestly how far they have worked. This is one of the most dependable question lines in economic geography, and today you collect its whole structure in one sitting.
