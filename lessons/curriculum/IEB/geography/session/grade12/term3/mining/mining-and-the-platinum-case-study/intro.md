Today we cover mining and the platinum case study — the industry modern South Africa was assembled around, from Kimberley's diamonds to the Witwatersrand's gold to the richest platinum deposits on earth in the Bushveld. We will defend the claim that mining is the foundation of the economy, walk the Bushveld Igneous Complex with its three limbs and two famous reefs, weigh platinum's contribution against its hindering factors, and finish with the mapwork skills that let you catch a mine red-handed on any topographic sheet. This is a topic where the theory and the mapwork pay you twice for the same knowledge, so every picture we build today earns in two places.
