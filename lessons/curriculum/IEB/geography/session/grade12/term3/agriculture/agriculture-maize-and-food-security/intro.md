Today we work through agriculture, the maize case study and food security — a sector earning barely three per cent of GDP that still powers three of the term's most dependable question families. We will argue both sides of the small-percentage paradox, compare the two farming worlds that share one fence line, walk maize from the highveld triangle to the export terminal, and take the food security definition apart clause by clause until its famous riddle — full silos, empty plates — solves itself. Hold one constraint throughout: only about twelve per cent of South Africa's land is arable, and that single fact explains half of everything ahead. Master this session and the agriculture question becomes marks you collect, not marks you chase.
