Today we complete the mapwork calculation toolkit with two recipes: magnetic declination, the correction between the compass needle's north and the map's north, and area, which turns two measured map distances into square metres or square kilometres. We will define the two norths and the growing angle between them, update a topographic sheet's declination note from 2018 to the present year step by step — including the carry that turns eighty-five minutes into one degree and twenty-five minutes — then convert and multiply our way through an orthophoto area calculation, and finish by naming every trap that costs marks in these questions. Both calculations are pure method: count, multiply, convert, and set out your working line by line.
