Today we secure the conversion that every other mapwork calculation stands on: turning a distance measured on paper into a distance across real ground. We will read the 1:50 000 ratio literally and feel its size — one centimetre of paper, half a kilometre of country — then run the full worked calculation from a 7,4 centimetre measurement to a 3,7 kilometre answer, extend the method to winding roads with the string technique, to 1:10 000 orthophoto sheets, and to the reverse question, and finish with the hand-span sense check and the four traps that catch careless answers. One conversion, mastered once, and gradient, cross-section and area all inherit it.
