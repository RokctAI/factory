Today we assemble the most powerful storm the atmosphere can build: the tropical cyclone. We will set out the four birth conditions and the regional names, follow the staged growth from a nameless cluster of thunderstorms to a named giant with an eye, dissect the anatomy of eye, eye wall and spiral rain bands together with the exact weather sequence they drag across a coastal town, and close with the case study of Cyclone Idai, the one-look satellite identification, and the management strategies that complete any answer in your final exams. Keep a single idea burning underneath everything: a tropical cyclone is an engine that converts warm ocean water into wind — where it is born, how it strengthens and why it dies are all chapters of that one fuel story.
