Today we investigate how a city manufactures its own climate. We will compare urban and rural air element by element — surfaces, moisture, wind, rain and sunshine — each difference tied to its mechanism; define the urban heat island and account for it through five causes, from low-albedo tar to the urban canyon; weigh its effects on health, energy, weather and ecology and match a reduction strategy to every cause; and finish with the pollution dome and plume — how winter's inversion lid seals the city's smoke, who breathes the consequences, and the strategies that shrink the dome. The command words ruling this topic are define, account for and suggest strategies, and every section arms all three.
