Today we run a complete mapwork exercise at examination pace on this session's own practice map: a one to fifty thousand extract of the town of Kransfontein below the Uitkyk escarpment, with its orthophoto companion of the town centre. Every question is walked with its mark allocation named, and the centrepiece calculations — the average gradient from contour to trigonometrical beacon and the vertical exaggeration of a cross-section — are worked and marked step by step, tolerance included. Since no map sheet is in front of you, we build each map moment on the board as it comes, so keep the terrain assembling in your mind. The habit this session drills is the one that banks mapwork everywhere: formula before numbers, working before answers, units always.
