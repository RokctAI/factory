Today we take a timed practice run through the theory half of Geography, using this session's own practice set: a quick-fire warm-up of ten retrieval marks, then one fully walked structured question from each pillar — climate, geomorphology, settlement and economic geography. Every question comes with its mark allocation named, because the allocation is the instruction — two marks buys one developed fact, and the paragraph task at the top is always four ideas at two marks each in full sentences. Answer each question in your head before the walkthrough gives it away, and by the end the staircase of a structured theory question — definition, source-reading, explanation, insight — will be one you have already climbed.
