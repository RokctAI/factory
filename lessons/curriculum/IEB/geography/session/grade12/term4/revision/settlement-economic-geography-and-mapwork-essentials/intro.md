Today we sweep the human side of the subject and its toolkit in one revision session: settlement geography from site and situation to the South African city model, the economic geography of sectors, farms, mines and the four industrial regions, and the mapwork toolkit of calculations, interpretation and GIS. Each topic is compressed to its concepts, its South African examples and the shapes that questions about it tend to take in your final exams, ending with the five calculation recipes that make mapwork the most practisable marks in Geography. By the end, the story of where South Africans live, work and travel — which is really what these topics examine — should feel like a story you already know how to retell.
