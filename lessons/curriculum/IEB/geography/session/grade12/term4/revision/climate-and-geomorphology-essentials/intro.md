Today we compress a year of Climate and Weather and Geomorphology into one revision sweep of the essentials — the two great pillars of physical geography. We set the three weather systems side by side, follow the three anticyclones through their seasons, spend a winter night in a valley, and then walk the geomorphology chain from drainage basins through patterns, profiles and fluvial landforms to rejuvenation, river capture and catchment management. The method is deliberate: working mechanism first, precise vocabulary second, then the shapes that questions about each idea tend to take in your final exams. Keep a pen moving while you listen, because revision that only listens is revision half done.
