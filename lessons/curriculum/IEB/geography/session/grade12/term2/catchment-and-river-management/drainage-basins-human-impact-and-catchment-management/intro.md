Today we close the river section with its management half, because in one of the driest countries on Earth every tap ultimately opens onto a distant catchment. We begin with two special drainage patterns whose courses ignore the rock they cross — superimposed and antecedent drainage — then establish why drainage basins and catchment areas matter and why they are so often badly managed. From there we work through the four major human impacts on river systems, with eutrophication leading the list, and we finish with management strategies and a South African case study of catchment repair in action.
