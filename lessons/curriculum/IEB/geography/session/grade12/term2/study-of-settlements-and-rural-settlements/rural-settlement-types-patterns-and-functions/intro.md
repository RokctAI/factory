Today we build the toolkit that opens every settlement question: what a settlement is, where it sits, and why it took its shape. We separate the two location questions — site, the ground under a settlement's feet, and situation, its position among everything around it — including the wet-point and dry-point special cases. We climb the classification ladder from farmstead to metropolis, contrast dispersed with nucleated patterns and connect each to land ownership, learn the four rural village shapes — round, linear, T-shaped and crossroad — with the cause behind each, and finish with the land-use functions of farming, forestry and conservation, keeping the examples South African throughout.
