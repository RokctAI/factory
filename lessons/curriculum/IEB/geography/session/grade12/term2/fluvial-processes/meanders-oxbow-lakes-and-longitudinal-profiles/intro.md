Today we master the two drawings that carry most of the fluvial marks: the river seen from the side and the river seen from above. From the side we build the longitudinal profile — the graded curve, the waterfalls and reservoirs that interrupt it, and the base levels that control it. From above we build the meander: the two-speed current that makes one bank a collapsing cliff and the other a gentle beach, and the full story of how a tightening loop is cut off in flood and left behind as an oxbow lake that ages into a meander scar. We finish by training the eye on sketches, photographs and map extracts, so that every version of these questions pays out.
