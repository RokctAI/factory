Today we descend through the whole planet without leaving the room — crust, mantle and core, the discontinuities that separate them, and the two kinds of crust whose density difference explains why subduction happens at all. The deepest borehole ever drilled stopped a little past twelve kilometres of the six thousand three hundred and seventy-one to the centre, so this session also answers the question strong candidates are expected to handle: how we know, through the seismic shadow zones that reveal a liquid outer core and a solid centre. Get the layers, the evidence and the four classic traps under control, and the plate tectonics that follows becomes almost automatic.
