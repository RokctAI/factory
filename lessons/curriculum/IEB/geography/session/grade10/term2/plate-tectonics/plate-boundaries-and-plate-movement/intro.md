Today the map stops being furniture and starts moving — because the continents drift, and we can prove it. This session assembles the four kinds of evidence Alfred Wegener gathered for continental drift, finds the engine he could not find in the slow convection of the mantle and the spreading sea floor, and then sorts every plate boundary on earth into three moves: apart, together, and past. Each move builds its own catalogue of landforms, from the East African Rift to the Andes to the San Andreas Fault, and together they explain why earthquakes and volcanoes cluster exactly where they do — and why South Africa mostly escapes them.
