Today we take an earthquake apart. First the mechanism — strain stored in bending rock until the fault snaps back, with a focus below ground and an epicentre above it. Then the three waves that carry the energy and the elegant trick of triangulation that locates any epicentre from three recording stations. Then the two rulers everyone confuses: Richter, one number for the energy released, and Mercalli, many numbers for what people actually felt. And finally the question this topic is really about — why the same size of earthquake kills a few hundred in one country and a few hundred thousand in another, and what engineering, planning and practised people can do about it.
