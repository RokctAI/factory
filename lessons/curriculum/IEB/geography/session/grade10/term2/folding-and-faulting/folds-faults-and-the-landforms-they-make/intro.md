Today we watch rock answer force. Squeeze it slowly and deep, and it bends — anticlines, synclines and whole families of folds stacking into fold mountains. Stress it cold, fast or too far, and it breaks — normal, reverse and shear faults building scarps, block mountains and rift valleys. This session ties all of it to the three tectonic forces and their plate boundaries, then brings it home through the Cape Fold Mountains, whose parallel ranges and fruit-filled valleys were pressed into shape while Gondwana was being assembled. By the end you will describe every structure in one fixed order — force, response, shape, landform — and tell folded country from faulted country at a single glance.
