Today we are studying the composition and structure of the atmosphere — the precisely mixed envelope of gases above you and the four layers it is stacked into. Every weather and climate topic you will meet from now until Grade 12 is built on this foundation: the percentages of dry air, the temperature reversals that divide troposphere, stratosphere, mesosphere and thermosphere, and the thin ozone shield that filters ultraviolet before it reaches your skin. We will fix the gas figures, build the layer stack and its zigzag temperature graph, trace the CFC story from an old fridge to the Antarctic thinning, and close with a working method for answering structure and ozone questions in full.
