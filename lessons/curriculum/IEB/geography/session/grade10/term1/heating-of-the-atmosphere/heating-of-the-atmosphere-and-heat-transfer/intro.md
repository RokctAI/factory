Today we are studying the heating of the atmosphere — the engine that drives every wind, cloud and temperature pattern you will meet in this subject. We will follow one hundred units of insolation through reflection and absorption down to the fifty that actually warm the ground, define the four transfer processes — radiation, conduction, convection and advection — and establish the principle that the atmosphere is heated from below. Then we build the greenhouse mechanism in its strict five-step order, separate the natural effect that keeps the planet habitable from the enhanced warming that human activity is driving, and finish with the five factors that explain why Musina swelters on the Limpopo while Molteno freezes on its plateau.
