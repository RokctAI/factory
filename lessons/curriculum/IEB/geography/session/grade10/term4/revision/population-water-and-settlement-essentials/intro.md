Today we are revising the human geography of the Grade 10 year as one connected story. We start with where people are — distribution against density, the calculation and its average trap, and why Gauteng crowds while the Northern Cape empties. We read population pyramids in three looks, from the wedge to the block, with South Africa's own scarred, mid-journey shape. We follow the movers — push and pull, the homeland-to-city flow and the regional magnet — into the informal settlements where the housing backlog lives. And we end where the people meet the water: a dry country's dams, transfers and free basic allowance, and the floods that strike hardest exactly where the poorest settled — the Jukskei in 2022 and Natal in 1987. One story, three names, exam-ready.
