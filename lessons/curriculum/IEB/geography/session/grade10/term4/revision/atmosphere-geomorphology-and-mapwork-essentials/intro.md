Today we are revising the physical geography of the whole Grade 10 year in one sweep. First the atmosphere: its gases and four layers, the rule that air is heated from below, and the chain from water vapour through dew point to cloud — ending with the three lifts that make convectional, orographic and frontal rain. Then the restless crust: Wegener's evidence, the three plate boundaries and their landforms, folds against faults, and how earthquakes and volcanoes are measured and survived. And finally the mapwork toolkit that turns paper into terrain — scale, contours, the gradient calculation worked to 1 in 15, the cross-section, and the synoptic chart's station model with a cold front on the march. One sitting, the year's essentials, exam-ready.
