Today chemistry moves into its true home ground: water. We will see why the polar water molecule levers ionic solids apart, which solutions light a conductivity bulb and which leave it dark, and how two clear liquids can meet and snow a solid precipitate — in white, and once in spectacular gold. One puzzle carries the whole session: a storeroom bottle whose label has washed off, holding a clear solution whose hidden ion we will name with two reagents and a fizz. Dissolution equations, the electrolyte distinction and the standard ion tests are staples of your final exams, and they lay the exact foundations Grade 11 chemistry builds on.
