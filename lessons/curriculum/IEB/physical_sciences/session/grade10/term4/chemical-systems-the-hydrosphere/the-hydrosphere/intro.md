Today we close the year's chemistry by pointing every skill you own at one planetary system: the hydrosphere — all the Earth's water, in every state and every hiding place. We will inventory it and discover that a soaked planet leaves humanity only tablespoons to drink, follow one drop across the atmosphere, the rocks and the living world, weigh honestly what a dam gives a city against what it costs a valley, and finish at the laboratory bench — testing a water sample with the ion drill you already know and purifying it stage by stage. This topic is examined through application and interpretation, so today is about thinking with water, not memorising it.
