Today is the chemistry revision sweep for the final examination — the year's matter and chemical change reorganised into four toolkits. We will read the atom and the periodic table, including the isotope arithmetic behind the decimal masses; choose between ionic, covalent and metallic bonding and predict properties from the choice; represent reactions with balanced equations and honest state symbols; and count with the mole from the scale to the measuring jug to the balloon, finishing in solution with electrolytes and a golden precipitate. Nothing is new — everything is compressed, ordered, and drilled through fresh worked examples chosen to spring the classic traps here, where springing them is free.
