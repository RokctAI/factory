Today we build magnetism from one strange observation — a steel pin leaping an air gap toward a magnet — all the way to the planet-sized magnet you are standing on. We will define the magnetic field precisely and set it beside the gravitational and electric fields you already know, prove that magnetic poles come only in pairs no matter how finely you cut, learn to draw the field lines that carry most of this topic's marks, and end with the Earth's own field: the working of the compass, the two different norths, and the glowing auroras that are our magnetic shield made visible. No formulas today — in this topic, precise words and correct drawings are the calculation.
