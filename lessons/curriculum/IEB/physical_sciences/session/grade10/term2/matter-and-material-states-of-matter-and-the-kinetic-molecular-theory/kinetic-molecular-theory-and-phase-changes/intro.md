Today we build the model behind every physical property you have ever noticed: the Kinetic Molecular Theory. Five statements about particles, motion, energy, spaces and forces explain why solids hold their shape, liquids pour and gases fill the room — and diffusion and Brownian motion supply the evidence that invisible particles really move. We name the six changes of state, sort them into energy-in and energy-out, separate evaporation from boiling, and then read the heating curve of half a kilogram of ice all the way to steam — answering the question this topic always turns on: why the temperature stops rising at the plateaus while the stove keeps pouring energy in.
