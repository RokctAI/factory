Today we build the two circuit patterns every electricity question is assembled from: series and parallel. One battery and two resistors — 18 volts, 3 ohms and 6 ohms — get wired both ways, and we calculate every current, every potential difference and every total resistance in each. You leave with the definitions of current, potential difference and resistance, Ohm's law working in all three arrangements, the reciprocal formula with its easily forgotten invert step, the paired rule sets that must never be mixed, and the reason the wiring in your own home chooses parallel — plus the price it pays in amps.
