Today we build chemistry's counting unit — the mole — around one problem: a camping stove canister leaks 8,8 grams of propane, and we turn that single measurement into a count of moles, a count of molecules, and a volume in litres at STP. Along the way you meet Avogadro's number, the molar mass read straight off the periodic table, the molar volume of a gas at standard conditions, and the mole map — three roads in and out of the same centre — that turns every conversion in this topic into one short, navigable trip.
