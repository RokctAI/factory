Today we assemble the short list of quantities that describes every wave in this course: amplitude, wavelength, period and frequency, all welded together by the wave equation v equals f lambda. We start with a single pulse on a rope, grow it into a travelling wave, define each quantity with its symbol and SI unit, and work the standard calculations in the three-line layout that earns full credit — formula, substitution, answer with unit. Then we cover the four classic traps: measuring amplitude crest to trough, swapping period and frequency, forgetting to convert centimetres and milliseconds, and misreading a graph's horizontal axis. The physics is friendly; the discipline with symbols and units is where the marks are won.
