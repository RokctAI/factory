Today we meet the second family of waves — the longitudinal family, where the medium vibrates back and forth along the same line the wave travels — and we follow it straight to sound. We define compressions and rarefactions, carry over wavelength, amplitude, period and frequency unchanged, and establish why sound needs a medium and dies in a vacuum. Then the three dials of hearing: pitch set by frequency, loudness set by amplitude, quality set by waveform. We close with echoes and sonar, where one factor decides everything — the sound travels there and back, so every distance must be halved. Halve the round trip, convert the kilohertz, and this topic is yours.
