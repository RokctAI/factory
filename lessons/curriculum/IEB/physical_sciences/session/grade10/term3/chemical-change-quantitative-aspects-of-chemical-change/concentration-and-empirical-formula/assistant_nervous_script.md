Concentration is cooldrink strength: moles over litres. Two conversions first — grams become moles by dividing by the molar mass, and cubic centimetres become cubic decimetres by dividing by a thousand — then one division. Our whole calculation was 10,6 over 106, then 0,1 over 0,2. That's it.

Empirical formula is a recipe read backwards, in three moves: pretend 100 grams so the percentages become masses, divide each mass by its atomic mass to get moles, then divide everything by the smallest. If a 1,5 shows up, double the whole recipe — never round it away.

And keep the fun fact for motivation: the maths you just learned proves that "dry" washing soda crystals are nearly two-thirds water. Formulas make predictions a kitchen scale could check.
