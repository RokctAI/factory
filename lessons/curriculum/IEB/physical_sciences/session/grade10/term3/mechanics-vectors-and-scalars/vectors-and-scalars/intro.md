Today we lay the foundation stone of all mechanics: the split between vectors and scalars, and the different arithmetic each family obeys. Two tasks organise the session — classifying displacement, speed, force, energy and velocity, and then finding the resultant when 40 newtons east meets 70 newtons west, where the instinctive answer of 110 newtons turns out to be exactly wrong. We will build the direction test, the sign-convention method, and the lifelong habit of answering with magnitude and direction together. Small questions on paper — but every mechanics and electricity topic through to Grade 12 stands on getting them right.
