So here is your safety net: hunt in the same order every time. Gravity first — it never takes a day off, always straight down. Then simply ask, "what is physically touching my object?" A floor? Normal force, and maybe friction. A strap or rope? Tension along its line. A hand? Applied force. Nothing else earns an arrow — if you can't point at the culprit, the force isn't real.

And don't think of the dot-and-arrows picture as baby physics. It's the exact sketch engineers make before they trust a crane or a bridge. Four clean, labelled arrows from one dot is professional work.
