Today we take on acceleration and the three motion graphs — the tools that turn any journey into a picture you can read marks straight off. One bus trip powers the whole session: pulling away, cruising, then braking, and we will lift both accelerations and the total displacement of 570 metres out of a single velocity-time graph using nothing but a gradient and an area. Along the way we disarm the classic traps — the rising line that gets misread as moving away, and the units that divide off the axes. Learn to read these pictures properly and half of Grade 10 mechanics becomes looking, not calculating.
