Today we assemble the vocabulary of motion — frame of reference, position, distance, displacement, speed and velocity — the six ideas every mechanics question quietly assumes you own. One short walk powers the whole session: 60 metres north, 20 metres south, 80 seconds in total, and from it we will pull four different answers, two of which ambush almost everyone the first time. The scalar-vector split we drill here is the most examined distinction in this topic, and it keeps deciding marks all the way to the end of Grade 12. Get this alphabet right and everything that follows in mechanics is just spelling.
