Hey, it's Thandi — if that formula with its little squared symbol is making you nervous, good news: the whole topic is three moves, done in the same order every time.

Move one: square the speed, on its own line. Fifteen squared is 225 — write it down before anything else, and the most common mistake in this topic can never touch you. Move two: halve the mass. Half of 800 is 400. Move three: multiply. Four hundred times 225 is 90 000 joules. That's it — that's the entire calculation the lesson was built on.

Two guards to keep by the door: if the speed arrives in kilometres per hour, divide by 3,6 FIRST, before squaring. And never bolt a direction onto an energy answer — joules don't point.

The big idea to carry in your pocket is the square: double the speed, four times the energy. It's why the road safety message and the physics marks are the same thing here.

Now go to the practice questions and run the three moves — square, halve, multiply — until they feel like brushing your teeth. You've got this!
