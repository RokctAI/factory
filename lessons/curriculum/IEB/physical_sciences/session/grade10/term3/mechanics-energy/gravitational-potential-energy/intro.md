Today we build gravitational potential energy — the energy an object banks purely because of where it sits — and the formula that prices it: E p equals m g h, the lifting bill of weight times height. One problem carries the session: a painter hoisting a 4 kilogram paint tin to a 3 metre platform and then on to 10 metres, where two different roads must agree on the further gain of 274,4 joules. We will also settle the subtlety that catches the most marks: the reference level, where your zero lives, and why nature only ever cashes in energy differences. Every idea in the energy chapter starts with this one.
