And the formula is just the bill for the deposit: mass times 9,8 gives you the weight — how hard gravity pulls — and weight times height is the cost of the climb. Two little guards keep you safe. If the question gives newtons, that's already the weight, so divide by 9,8 before using m. And height means straight-up height — ramps and staircases don't count their own length, only their rise.
