Today we are learning the two discoveries behind every generator on Earth: a current makes a magnetic field, and a changing magnetic field makes a current. We will master the Right Hand Rule for straight wires, loops and solenoids, define magnetic flux as B A cosine theta with the angle taken from the normal, and state Faraday's law in wording that earns full credit. The session builds to the calculation this topic always returns to — the emf induced in a multi-turn coil while the field through it changes — together with the opposition rule that makes every induced current fight the change creating it. Count the flux first, find its change second, apply the law last, and this topic pays reliably.
