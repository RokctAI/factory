Today we are looking between the molecules rather than inside them — at the intermolecular forces that decide why light little water is a liquid at room temperature while heavier carbon dioxide is a gas, and why surgical spirits vanishes off your skin while oil sits for hours. We will separate these weak attractions from the strong covalent bonds inside molecules, meet the whole family — London forces, dipole-dipole attractions, hydrogen bonding and ion-dipole forces — and then put them to work on the four properties every exam probes: boiling point, melting point, vapour pressure and solubility. The showcase duel between water and hydrogen sulfide, and the machinery hiding inside like dissolves like, both live in this session.
