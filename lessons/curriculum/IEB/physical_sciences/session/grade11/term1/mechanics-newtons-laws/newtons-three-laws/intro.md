Today we take on the three laws that govern every push and pull in the universe. We state each one in wording a marker can tick, then put them to work: the four-step free-body method that turns any force problem into one clean equation, a crate dragged across a workshop floor, a learner on a scale in a moving lift where the reading rises and falls while the true weight never changes, and a rocket whose thrust must first pay off its weight before a single metre of climb. Along the way we settle the two classic confusions — why moving things need no force to keep moving, and why equal-and-opposite forces never cancel each other, because they always act on different objects.
