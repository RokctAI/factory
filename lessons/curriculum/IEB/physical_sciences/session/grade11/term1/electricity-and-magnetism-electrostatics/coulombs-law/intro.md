Today the attractions and repulsions you met last year get their exact price in newtons. We state Coulomb's law in examinable wording, work the substitution carefully through microcoulombs and centimetres — magnitudes into the formula, signs kept outside to decide pull or push — and then build the two arrangements assessments love: three charges in a straight line, solved as a tug-of-war of arrows, and three charges at a right angle, finished with Pythagoras and the inverse tangent. We close with touching identical spheres that share their charge and sometimes swap attraction for repulsion, and a deliberate hunt through the five traps: units, signs, centre-to-centre distances, inverse-square ratios and the identical-spheres condition.
