One unit underpins every calculation in this year's chemistry: the mole. Today we rebuild it at Grade 11 depth and equip you with the three conversion tools everything else depends on — molar mass for substances you weigh, molar volume for gases, and concentration for solutions. We close with percentage composition and the two-step route from an empirical formula to a molecular formula, every example worked in full. Three tools, one discipline: whatever a question hands you, convert it to moles before anything else happens — master that habit and the quantitative chemistry marks look after themselves.
