Today every chemical reaction becomes an energy audit: breaking bonds always costs energy, forming bonds always pays it back, and the closing balance of that account is the heat of reaction, delta H. We will sort all reactions into exothermic and endothermic with their signature examples, pay the activation energy admission that guards even downhill reactions, and meet the activated complex balanced at the summit. Then we compress everything into the one diagram this topic is assessed on — the potential energy graph, with and without a catalyst. Learn to read its three levels correctly and the marks in this section become routine.
