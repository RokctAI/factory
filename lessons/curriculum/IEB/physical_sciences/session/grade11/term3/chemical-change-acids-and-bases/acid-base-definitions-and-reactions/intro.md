Today we dig underneath the labels acid and base and ask what earns a substance either name. We begin with the Arrhenius definitions and the two famous cracks in them, then rebuild the theory the Brønsted-Lowry way, where everything reduces to one proton changing hands. From that single idea we extract conjugate acid-base pairs, meet the ampholytes that can play either role, master the three reactions of acids with metal hydroxides, oxides and carbonates, and lock down the four indicator colours that let you watch invisible chemistry with your own eyes. The definitions, equations and colours in this session are dependable marks in any chemical change question you will face.
