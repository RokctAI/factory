This session is the chemistry revision sweep for the final examination: the year's quantitative chemistry and acids and bases rebuilt as four working toolkits. We run the mole conversions for mass, solution and gas through one hub, price any gas state with the ideal gas equation, cross the stoichiometry bridge with its limiting reagents and honesty percentages, and finish with the acid-base machinery from proton donor to titration. Nothing here is new — everything is the exam-day version, compressed, put in working order, and drilled through fresh worked examples chosen to spring the classic traps while springing them still costs nothing.
