Today we open the full energy ledger: mechanical energy — the sum of kinetic and gravitational potential energy — and what becomes of it in a world with friction. We will separate conservative forces from non-conservative ones, state the conservation principle with its conditions, and then put the equation W nc equals delta E k plus delta E p to work on slides, ramps and projectiles, reading its sign the way you read a bank statement. The session closes with power, the rate of doing work, from a stopwatch on a staircase to the borehole pump and the cruising bus. The method is the same four steps every time, and once it settles in, these become some of the most dependable marks in mechanics.
