Today we open the second great mechanics block of the year: work and energy, where instead of chasing forces instant by instant we audit the energy that flows into and out of a moving object across a whole journey. We will pin down the definition of work and let the cosine sort every force into positive, negative or zero work, then assemble net work from the free-body diagram. The destination is the work-energy theorem — net work equals the change in kinetic energy — proved on level ground and then on an incline, where the weight has to be split into components. Get the sign discipline right here and questions about forces, distances and speeds all collapse into one short energy audit.
