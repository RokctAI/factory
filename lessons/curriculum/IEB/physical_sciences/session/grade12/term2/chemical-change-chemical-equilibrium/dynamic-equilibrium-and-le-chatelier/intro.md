Today we study the reactions that never finish: reversible reactions in closed systems, settling into dynamic equilibrium — forward and reverse rates exactly equal, concentrations frozen while the particle traffic never stops. We will build the definitions word-perfectly, then let Le Chatelier's principle predict how the balance answers every disturbance: concentration, temperature and pressure, each opposed but never fully undone, with the catalyst speeding both directions equally and shifting nothing. We will learn to read concentration-time and rate-time graphs by their jumps, curves and flats, and finish by scoring the contest with the equilibrium constant K c — products over reactants, coefficients as exponents, a number loyal to temperature alone.
