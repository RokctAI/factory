Today is a full run through a physics practice set written specially for this session — seven questions in a deliberate order, from Newton's laws on an incline to the photoelectric effect. Every question gets the treatment a final examination deserves: state it in full, plan the attack before any substitution, work it line by line, and price each step against our own marking guideline. Along the way we flag the classic forfeits — the missing direction on a vector answer, the sign slip in the Doppler denominator, the internal resistance left out of the circuit total, and the nanometres never converted to metres. The goal is examination pace: read, plan, execute, audit, move on.
