Today is a full run through a chemistry practice set written specially for this session — seven questions spanning the year's work: organic naming and isomers, a three-step reaction scheme, stoichiometry with percentage purity, rates and collision theory, the equilibrium constant, titration with pH, and the galvanic cell. Every question gets the same treatment a final examination deserves — state it, plan the attack before writing, work it line by line, and price each step against our own marking guideline. Along the way we flag the classic forfeits: skipping the concentration step before K c, dropping the double negative in the cell emf, and answering without the reason a marker is paid to find. The goal is examination pace — read, plan, execute, audit, move on.
