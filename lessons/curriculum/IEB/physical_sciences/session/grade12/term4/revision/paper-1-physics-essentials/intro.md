Today is the final sweep of the year's physics — the whole territory in one session. First the mechanics core: Newton's three laws stated word for word, the free-body method, momentum with its sign discipline, and the elastic-or-inelastic decision made by arithmetic. Then projectile motion where gravity never switches off, the work-energy theorem and the energy budget that closes to the joule, and the power calculations. From there, electrostatics and the inverse square, circuits with the battery's hidden internal resistance and the audit that balances every branch, and finally electrodynamics and the photoelectric effect, where intensity counts photons and frequency sizes them. Every definition in its mark-earning form, every calculation rebuilt from scratch, and the classic traps named before they can bite.
