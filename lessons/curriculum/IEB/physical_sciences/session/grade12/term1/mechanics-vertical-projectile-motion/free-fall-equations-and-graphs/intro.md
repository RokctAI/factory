Today we take command of vertical projectile motion — the most learnable calculation topic in mechanics, because a single force runs the show and three equations describe everything. We fix the sign convention that opens every solution, work the standard calculations for objects dropped and thrown upward, and then turn to the graphs of position, velocity and acceleration, where the deepest questions live. By the end you will read gradients and areas off a velocity-time graph as fluently as you substitute into an equation, and the bouncing-ball sawtooth will look like an old friend.
