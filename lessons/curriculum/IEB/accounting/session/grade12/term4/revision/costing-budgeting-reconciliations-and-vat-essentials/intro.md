Today we revise the four pillars of managerial accounting at examination speed: the production cost statement with its five-level funnel, contribution and the break-even argument, cash budgets set against the projected income statement, and the housekeeping trio of reconciliations, age analysis and the VAT settlement. One factory — Marang Apparel, fifteen thousand tracksuits a year — anchors the costing, and smaller businesses carry the rest, with every trap named as we pass it: misfiled costs, bad debts written into a cash budget, and the fifteen-over-one-fifteen reading of inclusive amounts. In this territory the marks are workings marks, so everything is spoken and shown as workings.
