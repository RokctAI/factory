Today we read the fixed asset note the way an accountant does — not just building it, but interrogating it: how old the assets are, how long they will last, and whether the business is replacing them fast enough. Then we follow the people who check that those assets actually exist, working through internal audit's evidence, sampling techniques and reports, and drawing the sharp line between the internal auditor and the external auditor who signs the annual report. Asset interpretation and the audit process turn up together again and again, and one set of figures will carry us through both sides.
