Today we are interpreting reconciliations — the bank reconciliation, the creditors' statement reconciliation, and the debtors' control account with its age analysis. Earlier grades taught you to prepare these; grade 12 asks what they mean: what an outstanding deposit whispers when it stays too long, what a disputed statement item proves, and what an aging debtors' book is costing the business. We will rebuild each bridge to the rand, then read every one of them as the fraud-detection instrument it is. Reconciliation questions are where arithmetic and suspicion work together, and they are among the most reliably earnable marks in your final exams.
