With the financial statements built, this lesson turns them over to interrogation. The grade 11 indicator families — profitability, liquidity, the operating cycle, solvency — return in company form, and grade 12 adds the shareholder's own instruments: return on shareholders' equity, return on total capital employed, earnings per share, dividends per share, net asset value and the pay-out rate. Every figure runs through one company, Bokamoso Limited, and every calculation is followed by the sentence that actually earns the marks: gearing argued as eighteen percent earned against eleven percent paid, a share price of 620 cents weighed against a book value of 500, and a dividend policy judged from both the grandmother's and the expansionist's chair. The discipline throughout is figure, comparison, judgement — because interpretation, not arithmetic, is the skill this topic exists to teach.
