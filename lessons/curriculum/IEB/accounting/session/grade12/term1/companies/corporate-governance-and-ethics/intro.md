A company runs on the savings of strangers, and this lesson is about the system that makes trusting strangers reasonable. It maps the powers of shareholders against the fiduciary duties of directors, the structure of a board from executive to independent non-executive, and the King Code's four demands — responsibility, accountability, fairness and transparency — turned into working committees. It then moves to the individual: the five principles of professional conduct, the correct handling of a conflict of interest, and the twin share offences of insider trading and price manipulation. Finally it stacks the three layers of checking — internal control, internal audit and the independent audit — into one chain in which every arrow points back to the owners. All of it is practised on Masakhane Limited, and again at street level through a netball club and its committee, because the scenario skill is always the same: problem, principle, remedy.
