A company's year closes into three documents, and this lesson builds all three until they interlock. The statement of comprehensive income plays the year back from sales to net profit after tax. The statement of financial position photographs the final midnight, balanced to the rand. And the cash flow statement — the true grade 12 addition — explains why a company can earn over four hundred thousand rand of profit while its bank balance shrinks, tracing operating, investing and financing activities until they land exactly on the closing cash figure. We work every number through one business, Zamani Limited, a clothing retailer, then close with the audit report: the three possible verdicts of the independent auditor, and why an opinion that can be bought is worth nothing at all.
