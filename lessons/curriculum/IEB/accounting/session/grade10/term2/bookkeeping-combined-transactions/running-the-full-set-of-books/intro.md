Last term built six journals, two subsidiary ledgers, a general ledger and a trial balance — each in its own lesson, each in isolation; today we run them together as one system on a full combined month. The skill that now matters is routing: reading any transaction — a cash sale, a credit purchase, a return, a receipt from a debtor, a write-off — and sending it through the right journal to the right ledgers without hesitation. We journalise a complete month at Zanele's Traders event by event, post it, balance every account, and prove the whole thing with a level trial balance and control accounts that agree with their lists. Then we thread internal control and ethics through the same month, because integrated scenarios are where strong answers are built.
