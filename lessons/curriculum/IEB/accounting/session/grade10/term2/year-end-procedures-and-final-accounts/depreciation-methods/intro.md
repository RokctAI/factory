Fixed assets work for years, and every working year wears them down — depreciation is the books saying so. Today we take Zanele's Traders' new delivery bakkie, bought for R150 000 on the first day of the financial year, and charge its wearing two honest ways: straight-line, the level slice of cost that writes off R30 000 every year, and diminishing balance, the shrinking slice of carrying value that charges R30 000, then R24 000, then R19 200. We handle the mid-year arrival — a R24 000 computer that served only four months and earns only a four-twelfths charge — and build the asset register page that keeps every figure auditable. Cost, accumulated depreciation, carrying value: three figures, kept strictly apart, in the most examined adjustment of them all.
