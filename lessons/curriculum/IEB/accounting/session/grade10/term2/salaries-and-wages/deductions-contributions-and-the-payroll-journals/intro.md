Last lesson built gross pay; nobody takes gross home. Today the payroll machinery completes its work: the five deductions that shrink the administrator's eleven thousand rand gross to a net of eight thousand and forty — PAYE, pension, UIF, medical aid and the union subscription — and then the contributions the employer adds on top, including the one-sided Skills Development Levy, lifting the true cost of the job to twelve thousand five hundred and sixty-five rand. We build the Salaries Journal and Wages Journal rows column by column, reason the double entry from gross expense to net payment to the creditors owed, settle each fund through the CPJ, and close with the ethics of payday: the contract, nepotism, pay matching responsibility, and the trade union's voice.
