The books have paid suppliers and collected from customers; now they must pay the people who do the work. This is the first of two payroll lessons: today the earning side — the difference between a wage and a salary, the salary scale with its starting notch, annual increment and ceiling, and the overtime multipliers that price evening, Saturday, Sunday and public-holiday hours. We run a full week at Zanele's Traders to gross pay for two hourly workers, place the salaried administrator on her scale, and drill the labelled-line technique that payroll working always demands. Every payroll question begins with a gross figure you must build yourself — after today, you can build it.
