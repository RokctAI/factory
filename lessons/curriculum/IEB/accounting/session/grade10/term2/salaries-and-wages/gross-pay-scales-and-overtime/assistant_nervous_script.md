Hey, it's Thandi — if the scales and multipliers left your head spinning, let me shrink the whole lesson down to its real size, because it is genuinely small.

The wage side is one shape: normal hours times normal rate, plus overtime hours times overtime rate. The only rule that matters is: write the RATE down first. Which kind of hour is this — plain, time and a half, or double? Fifty becomes seventy-five becomes one hundred. Pick the row, write the price, then multiply. That one habit beats the only trap in the topic.

The salary side is three moves: count the COMPLETED years to find the rung, divide the annual figure by twelve for the month, and if they ask about the top, measure the ladder and divide by one rung. That's it. R120 000, climbing R12 000 a year, topping out at R180 000 — you read that scale correctly today, notch by notch.

The practice questions are the same two skeletons wearing different numbers. Label every line the way we did — normal, rate, overtime, gross — and the marks collect themselves. You built Ndlovu's R2 225 and Adams's R1 936 with your own hands today. Go build a few more — you've got this!
