Today we step back from single entries and study the whole production line: the accounting cycle — the fixed six-stage route every transaction travels, from source document through journals, ledger and trial balance to the final accounts and financial statements. Questions about how information flows through the books always walk this exact path, and the order and outputs of the stages are tested again and again. We will follow the route stage by stage through a real month of trading at a small store, prove why no stage can happen without the one before it, and then ride a single R240 till slip through all six stops. By the end the sequence will sit in your memory not as a list but as a journey you can retrace from any point.
