Hey, it's Thandi — don't let the word "posting" intimidate you.

All you're really doing is carrying column totals from the diaries to the filing cabinet: each total goes to its drawer, on its growing side, with a return ticket saying where it came from. Money and possessions grow left; the owner's stake, debts and earnings grow right. You've known those sides since the double-entry lesson — the ledger just makes them permanent.

Two things earn most of the marks. First, the cost-of-sales pair: it posts twice — debit Cost of Sales, credit Trading Stock — and the credit belongs with that pair, not with Bank. Second, balancing: put the difference on the smaller side as Balance c/d, rule off, bring it down as Balance b/d. That's the whole trick.

And when your trial balance won't agree in practice, breathe and read the gap: equal to an amount, halves neatly, or divides by nine — each one names its own error. Go work through the questions now; carry one total at a time and the month will land level.
