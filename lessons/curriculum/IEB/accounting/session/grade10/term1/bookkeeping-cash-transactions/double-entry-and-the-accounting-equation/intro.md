Today we pour the foundation under everything else this subject will ever ask of you: the accounting equation — assets equal owner's equity plus liabilities — and the double-entry principle that keeps it permanently true. We follow four real transactions at a small stationery business: the owner's R80 000 capital deposit, a R24 000 scooter bought for cash, R15 000 of stock bought on credit, and a R6 000 payment to a creditor. For each one we trace what grew, what shrank, which account is debited and which is credited, and we prove the equation still balances — finishing with the five-step routine that answers every transaction you will ever meet.
