Today the month comes home. The journals have done their sorting; now we post their totals to the general ledger — one T-account per item, balance sheet section and nominal section — balance the accounts that carry forward, and prove the whole month with a trial balance. We post a real month at a general store: R67 700 of receipts, R25 300 of payments, the double posting of cost of sales that keeps the stock account honest, and the folio references that let any figure be traced back to its journal page. Then the weigh-in: debit balances against credit balances, 67 700 against 67 700 — and a clear-eyed look at what level scales prove, what they cannot see, and how to read the gap when they refuse to level.
