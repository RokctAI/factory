Debits and credits feel like a secret language at first, I know. But you already understand the hard part without realising it: everything anyone owns was paid for either with their own money or with borrowed money. That's the whole equation — A equals O plus L is just that sentence wearing a uniform.

When a question looks scary, don't dive for the words "debit" and "credit" straight away. First ask the simple version: which two places does this event touch, and which way does each one move? Bank up? Stock down? Owner's pocket fatter or thinner? Once you can say that in plain words, the debits and credits are just translation — assets up on the debit side, owner's equity and liabilities up on the credit side.

And always finish with the check: is the table still level? If both your entries are equal and opposite, it will be. Go try the practice questions now — slowly, two places at a time. You're more ready than you feel.
