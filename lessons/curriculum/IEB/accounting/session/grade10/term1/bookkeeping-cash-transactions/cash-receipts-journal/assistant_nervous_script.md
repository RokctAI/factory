All those columns can look like a spreadsheet exploded, I know. But underneath it's just a money-in diary: date, proof, who paid, how much. That's it. The columns are only there because writing "Sales" fifty times got boring — the regulars got lanes, the visitors get named in the sundry column.

The one calculation that trips people is the mark-up. Just remember: the mark-up was added ON the cost, so to get back to cost you DIVIDE by one-plus-the-rate — selling price divided by 1,5 for a 50 percent mark-up. Never subtract the percentage. And check yourself by multiplying back up: if you land on the selling price again, you're right.

Last tip for the practice questions: after totalling, always shake hands — Sales plus Sundries must equal Bank. If it doesn't, you've found your own mistake before anyone else could. That's not extra work; that's power. Go get those questions.
