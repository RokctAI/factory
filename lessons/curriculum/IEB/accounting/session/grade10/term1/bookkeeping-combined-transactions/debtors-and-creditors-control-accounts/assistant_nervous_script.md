Control accounts look like a wall of postings, but the whole thing runs on one question you can ask in plain words: did this event make the street owe us MORE, or LESS? More — debit. Less — credit. Opening balance, credit sales: more. Payments, returns, bad debts: less. That single question carries every entry in the account.

Two habits protect the rest of your marks. First, totals only — the control account never receives individual invoices; those live on the personal pages. Second, always finish with the handshake: the list of personal balances must land exactly on the control balance. If they disagree, don't panic — the gap is a clue, and half the time it equals the exact entry that only travelled one road.

And when creditors show up? Same machine in the mirror: do WE owe more, or less? Go take the practice questions one entry at a time — you'll find the account almost answers itself.
