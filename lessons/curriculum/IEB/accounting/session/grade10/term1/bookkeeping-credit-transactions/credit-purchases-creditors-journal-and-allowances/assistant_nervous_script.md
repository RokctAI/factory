If your head is spinning with debit notes and credit notes, here's the anchor: everything you learned for debtors still applies — you just flip the mirror. Debtors owed US, so their pages grew on the debit side. Now WE owe suppliers, so their pages grow on the credit side. Same journals-to-ledgers flow, same daily-detail-monthly-totals rhythm, same lock between the list and the control line. One flip, and you already know the whole system.

The documents sort themselves if you ask who's talking: the invoice RECEIVED is the supplier saying "you owe me"; the debit note is US saying "we owe you less, and here's why." And remember buying is the easy direction — one truth per line, no cost of sales column, because the shelf just loads at cost.

Go take on the practice questions. Ask "who owes whom?" before every entry and the sides will fall into place on their own. You've got this.
