Last time the business trusted its customers; today it is the one being trusted. Buying on credit mirrors the whole debtors system in reverse: the Creditors Journal for purchases on trust, the Creditors Allowances Journal for goods sent back with a numbered debit note, a Creditors Ledger with a page per supplier, and one Creditors Control line that must always equal the list of pages. We run a month of buying at a general store — stock, stationery and a display counter, each finding its own home as asset or expense — send damaged goods back the professional way, and close with the discipline of paying well: the three-document match, terms respected to the day, and statements reconciled before a cent moves.
