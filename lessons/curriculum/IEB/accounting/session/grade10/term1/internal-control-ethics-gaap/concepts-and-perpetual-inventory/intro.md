Before the bookkeeping year begins, the vocabulary must be exact. Today we nail down the working words of the subject — the sole trader and the business entity rule, assets, liabilities and the running story called owner's equity, debit and credit as nothing more mystical than left and right, the pipeline from journal to ledger to trial balance to final accounts, and the two discounts that are treated completely differently. We split accounting into its two branches — the backward-looking report card and the forward-looking game plan — and then master the system every entry will run on: perpetual inventory, where each sale writes two truths at once and a stocktake checks the records instead of discovering them. One small backpack shop carries every word.
