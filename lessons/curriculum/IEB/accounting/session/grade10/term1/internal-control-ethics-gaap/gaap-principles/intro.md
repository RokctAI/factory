A business buys land for R650 000; years later an agent swears it is worth R1 100 000 — yet the books stubbornly show R650 000. Lazy bookkeeper? No: a principle at work, and today you learn all six of them — historical cost, prudence, materiality, the business entity rule, going concern and matching — the GAAP rulebook that makes every set of financial statements trustworthy and comparable. For each principle we take the rule, the reason and the tell-tale sign that identifies it inside a scenario, then meet the whole rulebook again as six unforgettable relatives, from the grandfather who only quotes what he paid to the cousin who pins every cost to its month. Scenario in, principle out, reason attached — that is the skill this lesson builds.
