The second revision session closes the loop: what happens when the year ends, and what guards the books all year long. Four stations for one business with a February year-end. First the adjustments — both depreciation methods, the accrued and prepaid expenses, the accrued income — matching each figure to the year it truly belongs to. Then the closing sequence into the two statements: gross profit checked against the mark-up policy, net profit closed to capital, and a statement of financial position that balances to the rand. Third, the gauges: profitability, liquidity, solvency and return on equity, each commented with a comparison. Finally the guard-rails — internal control, ethics and GAAP — plus the unit cost and cash budget skeleton revised in one breath. Six tools, one business, every figure balancing on the way through.
