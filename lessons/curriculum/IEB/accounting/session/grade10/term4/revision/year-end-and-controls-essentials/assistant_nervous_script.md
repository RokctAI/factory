Hey, it's Thandi — second revision session, and if year-end feels like the heaviest part of the course, here's the reframe: it's photo day, and you already know how to tidy a room. Four movements, always the same: admit the wearing — depreciation; pull in what was used or earned but not yet paid — accrued expenses and accrued income; push out what was paid but belongs to next year — prepayments. Every adjustment is half a move on the income side and half a parking spot on the balance sheet. Do both halves and the marks come in pairs.

The statements are a story you've told all year: sales down to gross profit — check it against the mark-up policy — down to net profit, closed to capital. Then the photograph: assets on one side, equity and liabilities on the other, and they MUST meet. If they don't, don't panic — recheck the carrying values and the four parking spots first; that's where the slips hide.

Ratios? One sentence, three parts: figure, comparison, judgement. Never leave a number standing naked, and aim each gauge at its own benchmark — liquidity at the safe lines, returns at what the bank pays.

And controls: risk plus fix, in pairs, tied together. Split hands, numbered slips, second signatures, independent counts.

Four movements, three-part sentences, pairs of locks. Work the practice set slowly once, and the whole year-end clicks into place. You've got this!
