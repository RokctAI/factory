Revision begins where the year began: the recording machine, rebuilt end to end as one system instead of ten loose topics. The accounting equation and its debit-credit compass govern every entry; six journals capture a month of transactions from their source documents; posting gathers the month into the ledger; the trial balance weighs it; and the control accounts count the debtors and creditors a second time to catch what the scale cannot see. One small month for a trading business runs through the entire machine — capital deposited, cash and credit sales with their cost of sales, purchases, returns, receipts and payments — every figure balancing on the way. Master the machine once, and every exam question becomes the same machine running with different slips.
