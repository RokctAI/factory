Today we finish the year-end machine itself: the trading stock surplus that mirrors the deficit, the correction of errors and omissions through the General Journal, the post-adjustment trial balance in its prescribed format, and the final accounts run with every adjusted figure in place. Then comes the destination Grade 10 has not yet seen — the post-closing trial balance, the one list that survives midnight and opens the new year. Along the way we master the three families of error that questions love, each corrected by exactly the gap between what happened and what should have, and the sharpest format trap in the topic: a nominal account appearing after closing is wrong by definition. Six stations, in order, and the accounting cycle closes into itself.
