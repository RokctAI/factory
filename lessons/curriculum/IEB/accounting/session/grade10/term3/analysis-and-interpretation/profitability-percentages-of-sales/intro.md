Today the financial statements you built start talking: profitability analysis turns absolute figures into percentages that can be compared against last year, against the pricing policy, and against other businesses. We work through the five profitability tools on your formula summary — the two gross profit percentages, operating expenses on sales, operating profit on sales and net profit on sales — computing each one from the year's statement of comprehensive income. The marks sit not in the arithmetic but in choosing the right formula, labelling the substitution, and then commenting in the four-move anatomy: state, judge, explain, recommend. By the end you will read the five percentages as one dashboard telling the whole earning story of every hundred rand of sales.
