Today we move from how well the business earns to whether it survives: liquidity, solvency and return on owner's equity. Three questions drive the session — can the bills of the coming year be paid as they arrive, do total assets cover total debts at all, and did the owner's money do better in this business than it would have in the bank? We will compute the current ratio, the acid-test ratio, the solvency ratio and the return on average equity straight from the statement of financial position, then judge each against its norm and comment in both directions — too low is dangerous, but too high can be idle money. By the end you will hold the full nine-gauge dashboard of the term's analysis and read it the way a decision-maker does.
