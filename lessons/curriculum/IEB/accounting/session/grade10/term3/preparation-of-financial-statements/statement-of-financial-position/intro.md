Today we photograph the business at midnight on the last day of June: the Statement of Financial Position, which shows what is owned, what is owed and what the owner's stake stands at the instant the year closes. It is the accounting equation dressed formally — assets on one side, equity and liabilities on the other — and its prescribed headings and sub-headings carry marks line for line. We build both sides from the year's figures, watch net profit arrive from the income statement into the equity movement, file every adjustment's balance-sheet twin at its exact address, and close with the two totals meeting at R55 600. The one-year test that sorts current from non-current is where markers strike first, so we drill it throughout.
