Today we open the pages behind the financial statements: the notes, where each summarised line is unpacked item by item for the reader who wants the detail. Grade 10 work covers seven of them — interest, fixed assets, inventories, trade and other receivables, cash and cash equivalents, owner's equity, and trade and other payables — and we build every one from the year's figures, holding the discipline that line and note must agree to the rand. Along the way we gather the GAAP principles the statements have been applying all term, and set the formal formats beside South Africa's own tradition of accountable record-keeping in stokvels and savings clubs. Cross-referencing is exactly what markers test, so the habits here are direct marks.
