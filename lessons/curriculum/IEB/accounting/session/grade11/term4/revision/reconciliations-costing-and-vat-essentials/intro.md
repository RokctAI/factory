Today we revise the grade 11 toolkit — the four skills that stand guard around any set of books. Reconciliation makes two records of one truth admit the same figure; costing finds the contribution per unit and the break-even line where production starts earning; the stock systems and forward documents value the shelf and write the months before they happen; and VAT is the tax a business gathers without ever owning it. Every station carries a fully worked example, from the bank balance of R14 100 proved from both directions to the R900 of VAT hiding inside a R6 900 slip. These four skills anchor the short questions you will meet in your final exams, and by the end each one should fire on reflex.
