Today we revise the heart of the grade 11 year: the partnership, rebuilt end to end through one business — Lerato and Karabo, trading as LK Traders. We run the pair of accounts each partner owns, share R300 000 of profit through the appropriation account until it empties to the rand, assemble the financial statements with the four-step asset disposal inside them, and finish by reading the indicators the way a creditor, a lender and a partner each would. Every mark in a partnership question hangs on two distinctions staying razor sharp — capital against current, and expenses against appropriations — so this session drills both until they run on reflex.
