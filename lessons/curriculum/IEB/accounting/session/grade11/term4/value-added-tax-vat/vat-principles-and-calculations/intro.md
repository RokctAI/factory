Today the quiet line at the bottom of every South African receipt gets explained in full: Value Added Tax — why the state levies it, how it gathers in slices along the whole production chain, and the four calculations you must be able to run without hesitation. We will separate standard-rated, zero-rated and exempt supplies using the one question that never misfires — may the seller recover its own input tax — and disarm the favourite trap of extracting VAT from an inclusive figure with fifteen over one hundred and fifteen. We close with returns, discounts and bad debts, and the ethics of a tax that is trust money: gathered from customers for the state and never the vendor's to spend. The sums are short; the classification is where marks are made or lost.
