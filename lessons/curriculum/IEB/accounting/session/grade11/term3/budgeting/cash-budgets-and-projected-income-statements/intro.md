Today we build the two forecasts a business prepares for the very same months — the projected Statement of Comprehensive Income, which predicts profit on the accrual basis, and the cash budget, which predicts the bank balance on the cash basis — and we learn why the two constantly disagree. The engine room of the topic is the debtors' collection schedule: a single month's receipts drawing on three earlier months' sales, with the settlement discount confined to the prompt payers who earn it and the bad-debt slice routed to the other document entirely. We work a full quarter, explain how a business can be profitable and cash-strapped in the same month, and finish with variance analysis and the ethics of forecasting — because a budget nobody checks is a wish, and a budget built wrong on purpose is a lie.
