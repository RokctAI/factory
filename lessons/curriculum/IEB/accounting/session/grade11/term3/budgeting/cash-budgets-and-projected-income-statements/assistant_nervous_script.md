Hey, it's Bianca — two forecasts, a grid of percentages, money landing from three different months at once... I know this topic looks like admin gone wild. It's actually three moves, so let me hand them to you.

Move one: file every item under the right document. One question per line — does money move this month? Cash budget. Does the business earn or use up value this month? Profit forecast. A few items live in both, in different months; two famous groups live in only one — depreciation (profit forecast only, because no rand ever moves for wearing) and drawings, loans and asset purchases (cash budget only, because they move money without being income or expenses).

Move two: the collection grid. Never in your head — sales down the side, months across. Fill in one arrival month at a time: a big slice of last month's credit sales, a smaller slice of the month before, a sliver of the month before that. Only two traps exist: the discount comes off ONLY the prompt-payer slice, and the bad-debt slice never enters the cash budget at all.

Move three: the closing balance chain. Receipts minus payments, plus the opening balance, equals the closing balance — and that closing figure walks forward to open the next month. The same five lines, every month.

And the idea worth keeping long after the test: profitable and broke at the same time isn't a contradiction — it's a timing gap, and an owner who spots it in April has cheap, calm options that are gone by June. You've got this!
