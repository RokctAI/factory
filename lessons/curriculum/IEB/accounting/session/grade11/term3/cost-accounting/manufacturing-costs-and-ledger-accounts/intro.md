Today we cross from the business that buys its stock to the business that makes it — manufacturing costs and the ledger accounts that trace them. Every rand in a factory walks one unbroken chain: direct materials and direct labour form prime cost, factory overheads join them in work-in-progress, and the finished goods that emerge carry a unit cost on which every pricing and break-even decision stands. We will sort costs by traceability and by behaviour, run the three stock accounts a manufacturer keeps where a trader kept one, and walk Mokoena Backpack Works through a full year, from canvas on the storeroom shelf to gross profit. Master the chain and the classic exam move — hiding one figure for you to recover — becomes routine marks.
