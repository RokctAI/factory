Today we take the unit cost from the previous lesson and sharpen it into the most useful decision tool in this course: break-even analysis. The question is the one every owner actually loses sleep over — how many units must be sold before the business stops losing money — and the answer arrives through contribution per unit, the amount each sale throws at the wall of fixed costs. We will make fixed and variable behaviour precise, build the four-line contribution statement, compute Mokoena Backpack Works' break-even point in units and in rand, and then drag the line around with what-ifs on price and costs. Learn why the answer always rounds up, and the calculation, interpretation and discussion marks all come home together.
