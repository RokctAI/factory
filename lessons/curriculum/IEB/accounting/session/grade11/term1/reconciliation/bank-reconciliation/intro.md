Today we put last year's trust in the Bank account on trial. Two independent records exist of the same money — the business's cash journals and the bank's monthly statement — and they will not agree, so we learn the three-way sort that rules the whole topic: timing differences, our omissions, and errors. We work the February statement of Amatola Building Supplies item by item, journalise what belongs in our books, correct the balance to the figure the financial statements will carry, and then build the Bank Reconciliation Statement that bridges our number to the bank's. One question decides every add and subtract on that bridge — what will the bank still do to this balance — and reasoning it out, rather than reciting a rule, is what earns the marks.
