Today the partnership goes public with its year: the Statement of Comprehensive Income assembled line by line from sales down to net profit, the Statement of Financial Position raised as two towers that must weigh exactly the same, and the notes — fixed assets, capital accounts and current accounts — where every headline figure proves itself. Format discipline earns the marks in this work: FOR the year ended on the income statement, ON the date for the position statement, net values on the face, reconciliations in the notes, and face and note always meeting to the rand. Every figure belongs to one business, Msunduzi Paint and Tile, run in partnership by Ngcobo and Venter — so this session is where a whole year of bookkeeping stands up and reports in public.
