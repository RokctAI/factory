Today we learn to read financial statements the way markers reward: turning figures into indicators and indicators into comments. We revise the profitability percentages, then add Grade 11's four operating-cycle stopwatches — stock turnover rate, stock holding period, debtors' collection period and creditors' payment period — and the risk-and-reward pair, the debt-equity ratio and the return on average partners' equity. Every figure comes off the statements of Msunduzi Paint and Tile built in the last lesson, and every indicator is only as good as its comparison: last year, the policy, the norm, the rival. The session ends with the four-part comment discipline — figure, comparison, cause, advice — because interpretation carries more marks than calculation, and it is rewarded to exactly that pattern.
