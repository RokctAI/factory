This session strips down the most dependable number in Economics: the inflation rate, and the Consumer Price Index it is built from. We begin with the definition and its three hidden tests, assemble the CPI basket the way Statistics South Africa assembles it, and then master the calculations — constructing an index, computing the inflation rate, weighting a basket, and running the formula in reverse. From there we separate demand-pull inflation from cost-push inflation, trace who quietly gains and who quietly loses when prices run, and finish with inflation targeting, the repo rate, and the honest limits of that weapon. Everything here converts directly into marks in your final exams, from the short definition to the long discussion essay.
