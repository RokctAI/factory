This session studies the one export whose customer does the travelling. We begin with the precise definitions — who counts as a tourist, day visitors, and the domestic, inbound and outbound flows with their export-import logic. We then explain why world tourism exploded, from rising incomes and cheap flight to open borders and South Africa's own 1994 turning point, before measuring what the industry does to GDP through the multiplier, to employment through its labour-intensity, and to households, businesses, infrastructure and the state — costs and leakages included. We close with South Africa's shop window, the special role of indigenous knowledge systems with their authenticity and benefit-sharing duties, and the policy fixes matched to named obstacles. Definitions, effects and paired suggestions here are dependable mark-earners in your final exams.
