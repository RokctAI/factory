This session follows the distance between two invoices — the modest one a country earns selling raw ore, and the heavy one it pays buying that ore back as finished products. We build the case for industrial development through value added, beneficiation, employment, stability and linkages; then learn South Africa's named strategies — the industrial policy framework and its action plans, the automotive programme, the clothing programme, the IDC, localisation. From there we cross to regional development: why industry clumps, the failed homeland decentralisation, the Maputo Corridor, and the special economic zones from Coega to Dube TradePort. We end with the grade-twelve skill of evaluating it all — best-practice benchmarks, honest results, binding constraints and cost per job. Strategy names, zone names and the evaluation moves all convert into marks in your final exams.
