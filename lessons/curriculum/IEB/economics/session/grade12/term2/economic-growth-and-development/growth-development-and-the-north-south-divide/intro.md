Today we reach the capstone that every earlier topic has been feeding: how a country grows richer, and — a different question entirely — how its people come to live better. We separate economic growth, measured by real GDP against population, from economic development, measured by life expectancy, learning and living standards through the Human Development Index. Then we weigh the two families of policy: demand-side approaches that heat up spending fast but hit four South African walls, and supply-side approaches that build lasting capacity slowly. We walk South Africa's own strategy road from the RDP through GEAR and ASGISA to the National Development Plan, judging the record with both hands, and finish on the world map with the north-south divide — the mechanisms that hold the gap open and the counter-currents redrawing it. Bring everything you have learnt this year; this topic is where all of it gets spent.
