Today we assemble micro-economics' measuring instrument: the perfect market, the benchmark that every other market structure this term will be judged against. We begin with the five assumptions and the price-taking business whose demand curve collapses into one flat line, then slide the cost curves underneath it to find the profit-maximising output where marginal revenue equals marginal cost. From there we handle losses and the shut-down decision, follow free entry and exit into the long run where both efficiencies arrive together, and close with South Africa's Competition Act and the institutions that push real markets toward the model. Keep a pencil moving throughout — every idea in this lesson is a diagram, and in your final exams those diagrams are marks waiting to be drawn.
