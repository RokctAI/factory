Today we open the public sector — the participant that handles roughly one rand in every three moving through the South African economy. The topic asks four questions: what the public sector consists of and why markets genuinely need it, why delivering public services is hard, how the February budget and fiscal policy operate — including the one diagram this topic owns, the Laffer curve — and what happens when the state itself fails. We pair every market failure with the public-sector role that answers it, work both sides of the budget, narrate the Laffer crest and read it on both slopes, and finish with the balanced conclusion that the long essays reward. This is where state enterprises, the deficit and the grant system stop being headlines and become material you can be tested on.
