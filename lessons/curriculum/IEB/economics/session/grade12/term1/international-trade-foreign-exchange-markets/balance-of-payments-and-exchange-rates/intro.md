Today we open the foreign sector completely: why nations trade in the first place, the balance of payments on which every cross-border rand is recorded, and the market where the rand itself carries a price against the dollar. We extract comparative advantage from a two-country table, walk through the accounts of the country's bank statement with the world, build the foreign exchange diagram curve by curve, and close with the four channels a country uses to correct a persistent deficit. This is the machinery behind every headline about the rand weakening, and one of the richest sources of data-response practice in the whole course.
