Today we are mastering the multiplier — the tool that tells you by how much national income rises when a rand of new spending enters the circular flow, and the answer is never rand for rand. Each rand becomes income, is partly re-spent, becomes income again, and echoes through the economy until the leakages silence it. We assemble the formula from the marginal propensities to consume and save, run it forwards and backwards with rand figures, extend it to the three-leakage open economy that South Africa really is, and finish with the honest conditions — spare capacity, time and finance — that decide whether the multiplier delivers output or only inflation. This tool returns in business cycles, in fiscal policy and in every growth essay you will write this year.
