Today we take the circular flow you first drew in Grade 10 and turn it into working arithmetic — the four-sector model with households, businesses, government and the foreign sector. We line up the three leakages against the three injections, state equilibrium as S plus T plus M equal to I plus G plus X and test it with figures, then measure the flow three ways and convert between market prices, factor cost, GNI and real GDP per person. We finish by reading a disequilibrium — what actually unfolds in the country when the totals stop balancing — because every essay on business cycles, fiscal policy or the balance of payments is written on top of this one diagram.
