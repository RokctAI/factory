Today we build the diagram at the heart of all economics: the circular flow. We start with its skeleton — households supplying factors of production, firms paying incomes and selling goods back, real flows and money flows driving in opposite lanes. Then we open the drains and taps: savings leaking out and investment injecting back through the banks, taxes out and government spending in through the state, imports out and export earnings in across the border. Three leakages, three injections, three doors — and the balance between them decides whether the whole economy grows, shrinks or holds steady. Draw it in layers with me and it will never overwhelm you.
