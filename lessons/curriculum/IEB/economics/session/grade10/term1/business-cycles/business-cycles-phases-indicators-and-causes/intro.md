Today the economy gets a pulse. We study business cycles — the recurring rise and fall of economic activity around its long-term growth path — and we learn to read them like professionals. First the four phases: upswing, peak, downswing and trough, each with its signature conditions, plus the exact definition of a recession. Then the indicators that reveal the cycle — leading, coincident and lagging — and the Reserve Bank's role in compiling and dating them. We separate the four movements tangled inside any economic time series, weigh exogenous causes against endogenous ones, and trace who carries the heaviest costs when the wave breaks. By the end, an economic headline will read like an instrument panel.
