Today we take on the problem that created the entire subject: scarcity — limited resources standing against unlimited wants — and the choosing it forces on every household, business and government. We will draw a sharp line between scarcity and a mere shortage, then meet opportunity cost, the true price of every decision, measured not in rands but in the next best alternative surrendered. From there, the three questions every economy must answer — what, how and for whom to produce — and the ethical thread that runs through it all: how economic activity can promote or violate human rights and the environment. Master these ideas now and the rest of the year becomes a series of answers to one familiar problem.
