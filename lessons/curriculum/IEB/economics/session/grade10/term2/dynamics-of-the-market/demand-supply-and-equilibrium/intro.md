Every price you have ever paid was settled by two forces: demand pulling one way, supply pulling the other. In this session we state both laws, build the market diagram with its downhill and uphill curves, and find equilibrium — the one price at which the market clears. Then the method goes to work on a case South Africa lived through: a bird flu outbreak that culls millions of laying hens, shifts egg supply leftward and sends the price climbing. Master the difference between moving along a curve and shifting the whole curve, and market questions become a routine you can run every time.
