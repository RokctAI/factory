This session builds the quiet skill behind every strong Economics answer: turning a table of numbers into an accurate market graph and reading answers straight off it. Working with a schedule of prices from five to twenty-five rand, we interrogate the table before drawing, build axes with even scales and full labels, plot both curves, and confirm the equilibrium at fifteen rand where demand and supply both read one hundred and fifty. Then the graph becomes a measuring instrument: one level sweep at twenty rand exposes an excess supply of one hundred units. Master the routine here and every curve the subject throws at you later obeys the same rules.
