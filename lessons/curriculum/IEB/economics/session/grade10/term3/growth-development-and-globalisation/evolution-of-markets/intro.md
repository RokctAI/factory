Today we ask where the economy actually came from. The route runs from households that made everything for themselves, through the first agricultural surplus, into barter and its four famous defects, then to money — the invention that repairs each defect in turn — and onward as markets deepen through specialisation, transport, law and companies, the state steps in to regulate, tax and build, and finally machines and harnessed energy launch the industrial age. The skill this topic tests is causal storytelling: explaining every stage as the solution to the problem the previous stage created. We travel the road twice — first with precise definitions, then as the story of one river-valley village growing up — and we end with South Africa's own industrial beginning in the diamond and gold mines.
