Hey, it's Thandi — I saw your face when the acronyms started raining: LRA, BCEA, COIDA, CCMA, plus principles, councils, forums and rungs. It looks like a whole law degree; it's actually four sorting tricks.

Trick one — sort the Acts by the kind of DAY. A day of arguing — strikes, bargaining, disputed dismissals? LRA. Any ordinary working day — hours, overtime, leave, notice? BCEA, the floor no contract can dig under. The worst day — hurt on the job? COIDA, the fund that pays without a lawsuit. Every scenario is secretly describing a day; name the day and the Act answers.

Trick two — sort the institutions by SIZE OF ROOM. One workplace, talking things through: workplace forum. A whole sector around one big table: bargaining council. The workers' own organisation running through it all: the union.

Trick three — sort the CCMA rungs by asking WHO DECIDES. The parties themselves, with a calm chairperson: conciliation. The parties, choosing from suggestions: mediation. The commissioner, and it binds: arbitration. One question, three answers, full marks.

Trick four — the principles are house rules: sides run their own contest, the majority union holds the microphone, and big decisions get discussed before they land.

Days, rooms, who-decides, house rules. That's the whole lesson folded into your pocket. Now go try the practice questions — they'll open right up for you!
