Hey, it's Thandi — three terms folded into one sweep sounds heavy, but here's the secret: it's four pictures, and you already own all four.

Picture one: the referee. Tax pushes supply left, subsidy and zero-rating push it right. Ceiling pinned below equilibrium — shortage; floor pinned above — surplus. Say it as the memory hook: ceilings make queues, floors make piles. And the minimum wage always gets both halves — protection for those who keep the job, pressure on how many jobs there are.

Picture two: Sipho at the riverbank. His goat finds no trading partner — that's the double coincidence of wants, barter's anchor. Money cuts it loose with four jobs: go-between, measuring rod, storage jar, promise-keeper. Then the state walks in, then the machines — and in our story the machines were pulled inland by diamonds and gold.

Picture three: the factory gate. Fence the team first: fifteen to sixty-four, working or genuinely seeking. Demand for labour is derived — the factory hires when orders grow. Three statutes: the floor of basic conditions, the collective rules of labour relations, the fairness of employment equity. Deadlocks climb the ladder: conciliation, arbitration, Labour Court.

Picture four: the queue and the starting line. Five types of unemployment, each with its own fix — diagnose before prescribing. Redress runs through the four factors, and every instrument needs its partner: the field still needs the seed money.

Four pictures, and every practice question opens with one of them. Go through them slowly — you know this ground better than you think.
