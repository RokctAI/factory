Today we take on the problem that shadows every other number in South African economics: unemployment. We fix the strict and expanded definitions and the rate's correct denominator, then work through the five types — frictional, structural, cyclical, seasonal and technological — because each has its own cause and therefore its own cure. From there we stack the causes in layers, sort the consequences across the individual, the economy and society, and weigh the three standing approaches: growth of production, public employment programmes and the Unemployment Insurance Fund, each with real strengths and honest limits. One sentence governs the whole topic: diagnose before you prescribe — name the type, then match the remedy.
