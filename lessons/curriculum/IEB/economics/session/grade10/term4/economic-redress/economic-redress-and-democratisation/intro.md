Today's topic is economic redress: the deliberate correction of the economic injustice the past built into ownership, work and opportunity. We identify the economically marginalised groups together with the mechanisms that excluded each of them, then organise redress through the four factors of production — land reform on its three tracks, employment equity and skills development, broad-based ownership and access to finance, and support for new entrepreneurs. From there we look at how economic decision-making itself was opened up, through labour law, public participation, NEDLAC's four constituencies and self-regulating bodies, and we close by judging the whole project the way an economist must: with both ledgers open, achievements and shortfalls, mechanisms rather than slogans.
