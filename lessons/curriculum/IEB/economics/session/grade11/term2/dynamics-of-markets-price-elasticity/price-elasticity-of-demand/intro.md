Today we upgrade the law of demand from a direction into a measurement: price elasticity of demand, the percentage change in quantity demanded divided by the percentage change in price. That single number separates the spaza that prices boldly from the one that prices itself out of business, explains why the Budget taxes cigarettes and fuel rather than maize meal, and delivers dependable marks in your final exams. We will calculate it for a commuter bus pass and a neighbourhood car wash, place every demand curve on the five-zone scale, work through the determinants, and then cash the number in on the question businesses actually ask — what a price change does to total revenue.
