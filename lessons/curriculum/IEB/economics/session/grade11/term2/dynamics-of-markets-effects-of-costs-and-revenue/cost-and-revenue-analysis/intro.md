Today we assemble cost and revenue analysis — the toolkit that every market-structure discussion in Grade 12 will stand on. From a plain table of output and total cost, we derive average and marginal cost, build the revenue side at a market price of R40, and settle the question every business owner asks: how many units deliver the largest possible profit? The whole session hangs on two verbs — divide for averages, subtract for marginals — and on one rule: make every unit whose marginal revenue covers its marginal cost, and refuse the first unit that fails the test. Master these six columns now, and next year's perfect markets, monopolies and oligopolies only redraw the revenue side — the method never changes.
