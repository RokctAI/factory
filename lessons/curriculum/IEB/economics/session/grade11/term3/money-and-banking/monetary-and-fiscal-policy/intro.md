Today we take the controls of the two panels that steer South Africa's economy. Monetary policy sits with the independent South African Reserve Bank, guarding the value of the rand through one powerful number — the repo rate — and its three-to-six percent inflation target. Fiscal policy sits with the government, working through the Budget's two great instruments: spending and taxation. We run a single scenario through both panels — inflation breaking above the band — tracing the repo decision link by link from Pretoria to your family's bond repayment, setting the minister's two taps beside it, and finishing with the comparison that unlocks every policy question: fast but blunt against slow but aimed.
