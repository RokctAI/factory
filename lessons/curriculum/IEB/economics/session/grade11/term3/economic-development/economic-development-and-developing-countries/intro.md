Today the question changes from how much an economy produces to what all that production does for the people living inside it: economic development. We separate development from growth, measure it with the Human Development Index and the poverty lines beneath it, and hold South Africa up against the six classic characteristics of developing countries to see where we fit cleanly and where we fit only partly. Then we study how countries develop on purpose — the big strategic choices, and South Africa's own run of plans from the RDP through GEAR to the NDP 2030 — before closing with the development knowledge communities built for themselves: Indigenous Knowledge Systems, from the stokvel to the hoodia case for legal protection.
