Today we revise the markets and macroeconomy essentials of the early terms — economic systems and the four factors of production, the three tape measures that must agree on GDP, elasticity with costs and revenue, and the connected markets steered by monetary and fiscal policy. Nothing new arrives in this session; everything arrives sharpened into four structures you can carry into any exam room. We rebuild each one — who answers the three questions, which factor collects which reward, how the three GDP methods reconcile, and which policy driver holds which lever at which speed — until the standard comparison questions become marks you collect on sight.
