This session takes the four factors of production off the Grade 10 list and sets them working: natural resources, labour, capital and entrepreneurship, each paired exactly with the income it earns — rent, wages, interest and profit — and each payment explained by a defining characteristic of the factor itself. We compute value added for a real firm and divide the pot among the factors, showing why profit is the residual that can turn into a loss while every other factor is paid first at an agreed amount. Then the topic comes home to South Africa: who can actually reach these factors, and how integrated development planning, community participation and empowerment procurement are engineered to widen that reach. Precision in the pairing and in every mechanism is what this session drills.
