This session is about economic systems — the machinery a society builds to answer the three questions scarcity forces on it: what to produce, how, and for whom. We test the free market, where the price mechanism co-ordinates millions of decisions without a single instruction, against the centrally planned economy, where one authority answers all three questions — judging both by their mechanisms, not by slogans. Then we place South Africa exactly: a mixed economy, combining private markets with state enterprises, regulators and heavy redistribution, and a dualistic one, where a modern formal economy and a survivalist informal economy share one country. The strongest answers explain that dualism through its structural causes, and by the end you will be able to.
