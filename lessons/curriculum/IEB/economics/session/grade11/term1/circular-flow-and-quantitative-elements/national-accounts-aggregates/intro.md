This session puts a price on every arrow of the circular flow. We open with the identity that production, expenditure and income are three doors into the same room, then assemble the expenditure side piece by piece — household consumption C and its four classes, government consumption G with the two items that must be kept out of it, and gross fixed capital formation I with the gross-to-net subtraction done in full. Finally we chain the three headline aggregates — gross national expenditure, gross value added and gross national income — working every conversion in rand billions, forwards and backwards, until each one is just a total plus one honest correction.
