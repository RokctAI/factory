Today we walk one data investigation from its first question to its final picture: a class surveying what learners spend on tuck-shop snacks and cool drinks each week, to decide whether a R25 lunchtime combo would sell. We will sharpen a vague question into a measurable one, choose fifty learners without bias, tally their answers into a frequency table with honest intervals, compress everything into mean, median, mode and range, and close with graphs — including the kind engineered to mislead. Every stage of this cycle is assessed directly, and the marks go to the learner who can name what breaks at each step and explain why.
