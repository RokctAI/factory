Today we put a number on chance — the mathematics behind a full minibus, a lucky-draw wheel and a coin game at the school fair. We will pin every probability onto the scale from zero to one, separate the chances you calculate from symmetry from the ones you estimate by watching and counting, and then take command of the two machines for double events: the tree diagram that lists every journey, and the two-way table that turns a crowd into a grid. Probability questions reward the learner who draws the picture and chooses the right denominator, and both habits are exactly what this session drills.
