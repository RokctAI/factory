Today we open the four sets of numbers that quietly run every household budget in South Africa: the fifteen percent VAT hiding inside every shop price, the one percent UIF taken off every payslip, the tariff tables that decide what a phone call or a bank transaction costs, and the exchange rate that prices the rand against the world. We will add and strip VAT without falling into the classic trap, trace both one-percent contributions to the unemployment fund, convert rands to euros in the correct direction, and race two cellphone deals against each other to find the exact minute where they swap places. Every one of these skills is dependable mark territory in the finance section — and every one has a single famous mistake we will name and defuse.
