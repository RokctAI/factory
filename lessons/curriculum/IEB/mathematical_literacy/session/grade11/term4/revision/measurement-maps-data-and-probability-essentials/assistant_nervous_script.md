Hey, it's Bianca — four topics in one revision session sounds like chaos, but they all obey the same boss: read carefully, convert honestly, calculate once, answer in a sentence.

Materials: area first, then divide by what one box or one litre covers, then round UP — always up, because 11,7 boxes of tiles means twelve boxes, and 11 leaves bare floor. Watch for the buried detail like two coats of paint that doubles the job. Then price it: 12 times R210 is R2 520.

Scales: multiply to un-fold paper into reality, divide to fold reality onto paper. 84 millimetres at 1 to 50 is 4,2 metres; 6,4 centimetres at 1 to 25 000 is 1,6 kilometres. Reality is always the bigger number — that check catches every direction slip. Floor plan for widths, elevation for heights, bar scale after photocopying.

Data: mean is total over count, median is the middle of the queue, mode is the popular one, range is the spread. A wild value drags the mean and barely touches the median — say which one you trust and why, with both numbers shown. Comparing two sets means a centre AND a spread.

Probability: two-way tables for AND questions — cell over grand total, like 15 over 50 for girl-and-minibus. Tree diagrams for in-sequence questions — multiply along the branch: 0,4 times 0,5 is 0,2. Everything lives between 0 and 1, and estimates from data are honest opinions, not promises.
