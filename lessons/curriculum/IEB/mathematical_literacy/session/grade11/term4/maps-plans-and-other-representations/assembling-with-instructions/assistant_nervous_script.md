The pictures are commands. The exploded view floats every part where it belongs — follow the thin line from bolt to hole and the drawing has answered for you. Numbers are the sequence, arrows are the movements, the crossed-out mallet forbids force, and the tick-and-cross pairs show right and wrong side by side. When a question asks what a symbol means, give the command it issues AND what ignoring it would break — two halves, one complete answer.

Count before you build. The parts list is a register: tick every piece. The classic: nine cam locks where twelve are listed — say THREE are missing and say what stops: two shelves lock, the third waits. Shortfall plus consequence is the full answer. Scaling up?
