Today we are reading assembly instructions and diagrams — the skill that turns two flat boxes and a wordless folded sheet into a finished double-door cupboard, or a safely wired three-pin plug on an iron's cord. We work through the picture language of exploded diagrams, the parts-list audit that must come before any building, why the numbered sequence only runs one way, and the cost comparison that decides whether you assemble it yourself or pay someone. It is the closing maps-and-plans skill, and it is tested with counting, ordering and a decision you must defend in rands.
