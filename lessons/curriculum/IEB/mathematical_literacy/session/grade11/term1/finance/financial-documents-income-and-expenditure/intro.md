Today we follow the paper trail that money leaves through every South African home — the municipal statement, the payslip, the budget and the credit agreement. We will master the vocabulary these documents use, audit a bill's arithmetic line by line, sort income and expenditure into fixed, variable and occasional, and build a budget that closes on a surplus or a deficit. Then we push one step further, into simple interest, and calculate what a washing machine bought on credit genuinely costs. Every mark in this section is earned by reading the document carefully and finishing with an answer in context — and that is precisely the habit we practise today.
