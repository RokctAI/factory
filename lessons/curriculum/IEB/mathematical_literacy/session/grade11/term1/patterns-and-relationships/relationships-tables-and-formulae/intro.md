Today we are pulling the rules out of tables of real rand amounts — a plumber's charges, a bakkie hire split between friends, a stokvel account earning interest, and a municipal water bill. We will name the independent and dependent variables, run the three tests that identify constant difference, inverse proportion and constant ratio, move a single relationship between formula, table and graph, and then work a combination tariff backwards from the bill to the water used. This is the heart of the patterns and relationships strand, and the marks go to the learner who names the relationship and shows the substitution.
