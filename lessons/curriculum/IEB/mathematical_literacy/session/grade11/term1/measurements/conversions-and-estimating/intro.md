Today we take on conversions and estimating — the measurement skills hiding behind a recipe in Fahrenheit, a tape measure marked in inches, a paint tin promising square metres per litre, and a bus schedule on the twenty-four-hour clock. We will climb the metric ladder, cross into imperial with a supplied conversion table, convert temperature and time by formula, and scale context rates like paint coverage and medicine doses all the way to a buying decision. Conversion marks appear throughout your assessments this year, and the direction rule plus a real-world sense check will keep every one of them safe.
