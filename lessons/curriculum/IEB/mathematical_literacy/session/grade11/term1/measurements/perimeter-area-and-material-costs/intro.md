Today we are converting perimeter and area into rand amounts — fencing a yard, paving it, tiling a floor and painting the walls of the room behind it. We will apply the standard formulae for rectangles, triangles and circles, subtract gates, doors and windows before multiplying by any price, and obey the hardware shop's own rules: wastage allowances, whole tins and boxes, and rounding up whenever running short would stop the job. Costing chains exactly like this appear all through your assessments, and the marks live in the middle steps and the final costed sentence, not just the arithmetic.
