Today we are reading building plans and models — the journey from millimetres on paper to bricks, flooring and rands. We follow one home study extension in Gqeberha: reading its floor plan at a scale of one to fifty, matching its elevations, costing its flooring, skirting and paint, and finally packing juice cans into a carton the way a tuck shop actually does. Every mark in this section comes from the same discipline — convert with the scale, count in wholes, and round in the direction reality demands.
