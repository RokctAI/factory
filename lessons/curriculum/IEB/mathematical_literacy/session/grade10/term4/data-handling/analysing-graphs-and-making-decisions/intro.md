Today we are analysing graphs — the skill that outranks merely drawing them, because analysis is where data handling becomes decision-making. Our material is one Makhanda household's municipal year told in three pictures: an electricity line that peaks at 575 units in June, water bars that fall from 25 to 18 kilolitres after one tap repair, and a pie chart carving up a R3 200 bill. We will read values and ranges precisely, compute percentage changes with the original safely in the denominator, convert every reading into rand, and finish by exposing the truncated axis — the most common graphical lie in real media. By the end you will conclude only what the numbers support, and check where every axis starts before believing any picture.
