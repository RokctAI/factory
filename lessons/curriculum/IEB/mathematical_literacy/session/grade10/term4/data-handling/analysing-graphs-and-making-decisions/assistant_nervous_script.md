Hey, it's Thandi — analysis questions look long and wordy, but here is the secret: every one of them is the same three moves wearing a disguise. Read. Multiply. Judge.

Read: finger on the axis, up to the dot, across to the number — and say the unit out loud. 575 units. 25 kilolitres. The unit is half the mark.

Multiply: whatever you read, one multiplication turns it into money or meaning. 575 units times R2,40 is R1 380. Seven kilolitres saved times R35 is R245 a month. 45 percent of a R3 200 bill is R1 440. One multiplication each — there is nothing deeper hiding underneath.

Judge: finish with a sentence that answers the actual question. Worth it? Yes — the R580 repair is repaid by month three. Which slice to attack? The big one — that is where 10 percent saves R144, not R32. The verdict sentence is a mark lying on the table; do not walk past it.

Percentage change? The word FROM points at the bottom of the fraction. From 500 to 575: change 75, over 500, is 15 percent. And if a graph's axis does not start at nought — trust nothing, read the real values, and redo the comparison yourself.

That is the whole topic: read, multiply, judge, and check the axis. Go tackle the practice questions with those four moves. You've got this!
