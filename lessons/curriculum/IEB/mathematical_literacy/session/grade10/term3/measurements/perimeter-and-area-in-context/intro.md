Today we are measuring a community play park to master perimeter and area the way exam questions ask for them — always in work clothes, with a fence to price and a lawn to plant. The formulae are printed on the formula sheet; the marks are for choosing between edge and inside, halving diameters before using them, and remembering that the perimeter of a part-circle keeps its straight edges, the trap that catches more candidates than any other in this section. We will work a rectangle, a triangle and a semicircle in full, then turn every measurement into money using the golden rule of materials: divide by the pack size and always round up. By the end you will run the whole pipeline from shape to formula to metres to rolls to rand.
