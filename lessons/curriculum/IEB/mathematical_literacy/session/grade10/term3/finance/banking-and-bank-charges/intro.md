Today we are working out what a month of banking really costs — the vocabulary on a bank statement, the fee table, and the arithmetic that decides between a pay-per-transaction account and a flat monthly bundle. Fee questions reward careful reading and careful counting, especially the phrase "per R100 or part thereof", where the bank always rounds the hundreds up in its own favour. We will read a fee table line by line, cost out a quiet month and a busy month for both accounts, and find the break-even point where the advice flips. By the end you will be able to pick the cheaper account for any month of use and prove your choice with two totals.
