Today we are working with interest the Grade 10 way — no formulae, just percentages taken one time period at a time. The whole topic turns on one distinction that questions test relentlessly: the interest rate is a percentage, a speed, while the interest itself is an amount of rand, and the units tell you which is which. We will grow a R4 000 saving year by year, cost out a bank loan against a moneylender charging per month instead of per year, and run the calculation backwards to find a rate from the interest it produced. Get the balance right and the time period right, and every mark in this topic is a straightforward percentage away.
