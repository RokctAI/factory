Today we are following one month of a Saturday market stand to master profit, loss and the documents that record them — one of the most dependable question settings in all of Finance. Everything rests on one subtraction, money in minus money out, but the marks live in the details around it: reading a till slip's VAT line, checking an invoice's multiplication, and sorting every expense into fixed, variable or occasional. We will build a full income-and-expenditure statement, watch it show a healthy surplus, and then watch a short rainy month tip the same statement into a deficit. By the end you will be able to build that page from any trading story and explain its bottom line in the sentence the marker wants.
