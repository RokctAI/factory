Today we are using perimeter, area and volume where they actually earn their keep — a Mahikeng household fencing a vegetable garden, tiling and painting a spare room, and working out how long a rainwater tank will last. You will learn to convert units first, keep them on every line, and make the two rounding judgements assessors watch for: materials round up, days of supply round down. By the end, a tape measure and a price list will hand you a fully costed answer — rolls of mesh, boxes of tiles, tins of paint and days of water.
