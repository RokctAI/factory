Today we are building a household budget from scratch — a Tembisa family with a payslip, rent from a back room, Saturday income from cutting and braiding hair, and a dozen accounts that all want paying. You will learn to read gross and net income off a payslip and budget only on what actually arrives, to sort expenditure into fixed, variable and occasional, and to find the bottom line and name it a surplus or a deficit. Then we point the same logic at a koeksister stall to find its break-even point — the calculation that anchors household finance questions in your final exams.
