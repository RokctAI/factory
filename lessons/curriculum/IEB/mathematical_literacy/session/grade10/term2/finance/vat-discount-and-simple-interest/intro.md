Today we are working with VAT, discount and simple interest — the three money tools behind every till slip, sale sign and savings account in the country. Using a real Polokwane shopping basket, you will learn to add VAT forwards, strip it out backwards by dividing by 1,15, recognise the zero-rated goods the taxman never touches, and see why successive discounts refuse to simply add. We finish with simple interest and a washing-machine-on-sale decision that pulls all three tools into a single answer — exactly the consumer reasoning your final exams reward.
