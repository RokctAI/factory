Today we are reading scale, floor plans and elevation plans — the two sheets of paper a builder leaves with a George family adding a bedroom, and the arithmetic that turns them into a quotation. You will learn what number scales and bar scales each promise, how to move between the page and reality by multiplying or dividing, and how to read the symbols and millimetre dimensions on a floor plan and the heights on an elevation. It all comes together in the finish assessors love: using both drawings at once to price carpet, skirting, paving and the wall area to be plastered.
