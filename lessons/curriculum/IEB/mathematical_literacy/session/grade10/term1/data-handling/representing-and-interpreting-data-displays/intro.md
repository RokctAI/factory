Today we are representing and interpreting data displays — the bar graph, the histogram, the pie chart and the line graph, each built from real Kimberley survey data and each with a different job to do. You will learn the rule for choosing between them, how to convert pie-chart percentages to degrees and back to rands, and how to read a trend off a line graph and price it in rands. We finish with the most satisfying skill in the topic: catching a misleading graph, naming the trick, and supplying the honest figure.
