Today we are interpreting tariff graphs — two prepaid electricity options sharing one set of axes, where Option A charges R2,50 per kilowatt-hour with no fixed fee and Option B charges R200 a month plus R1,70 per unit. You will learn to lift the fixed charge off the vertical intercept, the price per unit off the gradient, and the break-even usage off the crossing point, checking every reading by calculation. The lesson closes on the comparison that decides real bills: which option is cheaper at 400 kilowatt-hours, and by how many rands — the same skill behind every data-bundle and gym-membership decision.
