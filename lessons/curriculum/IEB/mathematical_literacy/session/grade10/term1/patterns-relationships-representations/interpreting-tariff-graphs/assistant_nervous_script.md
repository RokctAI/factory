Every line on a money graph only ever admits two facts. Where it starts — that's the fixed charge. How fast it climbs — that's the price per unit. Two facts, read straight off the picture, zero calculation required.

And the crossing point? That's just the graph whispering "right here, both deals cost the same." On one side of it the cheap-to-start option wins; on the other side the cheap-per-unit option takes over.
