Hey, it's Thandi — multipliers, chains, reverse VAT... if that felt like a lot of machinery for something as ordinary as a sale price, I promise it shrinks.

The entire lesson folds into one habit: turn every percentage change into a single multiplier — up means 1-point-something, down means 0-point-something — and multiply. Two changes in a row? Multiply the two multipliers. Need to undo one? Divide by it. One habit, three uses, that's the whole toolbox.

And you already own the instinct. You know "15% off" a R800 pair of sneakers doesn't mean they cost R15 — you've been converting percentages into money at the shops your whole life. We're just tidying your street maths into full-credit written answers.

So open the practice questions with your calculator next to you, and write the multiplier down before you press anything: "times 0,85... times 1,15..." Say it, write it, press it. Five questions in, the pattern clicks and percentages turn into free marks in every finance question after this. Go get them!
