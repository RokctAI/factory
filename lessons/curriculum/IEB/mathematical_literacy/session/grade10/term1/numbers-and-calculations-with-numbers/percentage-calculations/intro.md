Today we are mastering percentage calculations — the hardest-working tool in Mathematical Literacy, running through VAT, discounts, interest, inflation and tariffs, and waiting in every finance question between now and your final exams. Our case study is a pair of sneakers priced at R800, discounted by fifteen percent and then hit with fifteen percent VAT — and it hides a famous surprise, because the two fifteens refuse to cancel. By the end you will command five moves: the decimal trick, the single multiplier, chained changes, percentage change, and running the chain backwards to recover a price before VAT.
