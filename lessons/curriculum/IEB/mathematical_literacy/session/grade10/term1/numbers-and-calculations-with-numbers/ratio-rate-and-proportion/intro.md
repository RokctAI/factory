Today we are working with ratio, rate and proportion — three friends splitting R3 600 from a vetkoek stall, a shopper weighing up two bags of rice, and a builder mixing concrete, all running on the same mathematics. You will learn to share any amount in a given ratio, to calculate a unit rate and let it price a road trip or a water bill, to tell direct proportion from indirect, and to settle a best-buy argument honestly with a price per kilogram. These four moves anchor tariff, finance and measurement questions all the way to your final exams.
