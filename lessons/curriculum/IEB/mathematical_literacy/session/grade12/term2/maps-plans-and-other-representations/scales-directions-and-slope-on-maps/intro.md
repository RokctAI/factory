Today we are reading maps the way the final exams demand: scales, directions and the Grade 12 headline skill — slope. We plan the holiday run from Cape Town to Knysna on a 1 : 1 500 000 road map, price a bar scale, build a scale of our own, and use grid blocks and the eight compass words to say where things are and which way they lie. Then we leave the tar for the Outeniqua mountains, where contour lines and gradient tell you how steep a trail is before your boots find out. Distance, direction, slope and time — one flat page answers all four, and the marker pays for every method line you show.
