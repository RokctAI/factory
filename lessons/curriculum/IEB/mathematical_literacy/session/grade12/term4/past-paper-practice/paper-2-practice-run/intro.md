Today is a full practice run of the second practice paper built for this session — Measurement with Maps and Plans, 150 marks — walked question by question at examination pace. This is the practical paper: a road trip on the N2, a youth centre to paint, a drum to fill, a floor plan to read, with probability's few marks hiding inside the contexts. Its special demand is units — nearly every chain crosses a conversion, and the marking plan places a mark directly on the crossing. We work each specimen in staged detail, name where every mark sits, and close with the habits that turn a practice run into reflex on the day.
