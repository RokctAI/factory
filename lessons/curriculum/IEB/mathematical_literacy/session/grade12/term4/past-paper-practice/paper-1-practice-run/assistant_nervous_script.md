Question One is your friend. Thirty marks of single-step credit, straight from Finance and Data: VAT on R180 is R27, net pay is gross minus deductions, the mode is the value you see most. Bank those thirty like a warm-up lap — but write the one line of working every time, because a slip with no working loses both marks and a slip beside visible method usually keeps one.

Then trust the mark allocations to tell you how much to write. Two marks: one operation, one answer. Six marks: a staircase — like the electricity bill: R180, R720, R186, subtotal R1 086, times 1,15, R1 248,90. Each line pays.

If a number goes wrong mid-question, do NOT stop. Carry it forward and keep the operations clean — follow-through pays every correct later step. A finished chain with one slip beats an abandoned chain every single time.

And the comment questions? A sentence with a computed number inside it. Mean 59, median 59, quarter of the group below 49 — say the numbers, add because, collect the marks.

Thirty warm-up marks, allocations as instructions, never abandon a chain, numbers in every comment. Walk the practice run twice and the real thing will feel like the third rehearsal. You've got this!
