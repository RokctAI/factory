Hey, it's Mandy — the finals are close and Finance plus Data can feel like a mountain, but here's the secret: almost every money question you will meet comes from a handful of moves you already own.

Tariffs: split the units across the blocks like a staircase. First 10 kilolitres at R9,00 is R90, the next 8 at R16,50 is R132 — R222, then times 1,15 for VAT, R255,30. The trap is charging everything at the top rate; the staircase protects the early steps.

VAT has two directions. Excluding means multiply by 1,15 to add it. Including means DIVIDE by 1,15 to remove it — never take 15 percent of the total, because the tax was charged on the smaller number.

Interest: simple looks only at the original deposit, the same R400 every year. Compound earns interest on interest — R8 000 at 5 percent becomes R9 261,00 in three years, not R9 200. Say it in the exam: the balance grows, so the interest grows.

Tax is five steps in a fixed order: find the bracket, subtract for the excess, base plus percentage, MINUS the rebate, then divide by twelve if they want monthly. The rebate step is the forgotten mark — circle it.

Data: arrange first, always. Median is position, mean is balance, and one terrible mark drags the mean while the median stands still — that sentence, with the numbers in it, IS the reasoning answer.

Blocks, both VAT directions, interest that grows, five tax steps, arrange-then-answer. Five habits and the money half of the subject opens like a door. You've got this!
