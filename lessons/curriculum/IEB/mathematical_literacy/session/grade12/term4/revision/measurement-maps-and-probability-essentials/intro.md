Today we revise the practical half of the subject — Measurement, Maps and Plans, and Probability, the skills that price a building job, plan a journey and weigh a forecast. We run conversions with perimeter, area and the cylinder walked all the way to litres, then scale chains and travel planning, plans and elevations matched by counting, and probability in the three dialects a marking plan accepts. Then the same skills carry one family reunion — a farm shed to set up, an umkhulu to fetch on time, and a forty percent chance of rain to argue about — because that is exactly how the questions will hand them to you.
