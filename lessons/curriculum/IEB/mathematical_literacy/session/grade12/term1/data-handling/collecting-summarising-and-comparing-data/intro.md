Today we are collecting, summarising and comparing data — the craft that settles everyday South African arguments with honest numbers. Our investigation asks how long patients wait to be seen at a clinic, with two clinics answering: Ntabeni Clinic, deep rural in Limpopo, and Umbilo Clinic in central Durban. We move from the question and the fair sample, through frequency tables, mean, median, quartiles and percentiles, to the box-and-whisker plots that put both clinics on one scale — where a single average would smooth the real story away.
