Today we watch money move in both directions: interest pulling it up, inflation dragging its buying power down, and a bank statement full of quiet fees sitting in the middle. One Mitchells Plain family carries the whole session — Mrs Adams investing a R12 000 savings-club payout at simple and then compound interest, her niece decoding a first bank statement, and a two-litre bottle of milk climbing from R32,00 year after year. Final exams test all three movements, and the winning habit is the same each time: build the year-by-year table and compare every growth rate against inflation.
