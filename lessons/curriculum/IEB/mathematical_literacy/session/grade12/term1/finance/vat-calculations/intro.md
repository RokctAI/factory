Today we hunt the fifteen percent hiding in almost every South African price: VAT. One Saturday of spending in Mamelodi carries the session — an electrician's quote that needs VAT added on, a R391 school tracksuit that needs it stripped out, and a till slip split between taxed goods and the zero-rated basics the law protects. Final exams test both directions, and the backwards one is where the marks are lost, so the rule of the day is simple: multiply by 1,15 going forward, divide by 1,15 coming back, and never subtract fifteen percent from an inclusive price.
