Today we work with a number that sits on every till slip you have ever been handed: VAT. The price on the shelf already includes it, and the exam loves asking you to work backwards from that inclusive price. One case study runs through the whole session: a television priced at R6 899 including VAT at 15%. We start with the till slip in your hand — estimate first, exact calculation after — and by the end you will split that R6 899 into the price excluding VAT and the VAT amount, using the reverse calculation and the rounding the exam demands, so a backwards VAT question becomes money in the bank on paper two.
