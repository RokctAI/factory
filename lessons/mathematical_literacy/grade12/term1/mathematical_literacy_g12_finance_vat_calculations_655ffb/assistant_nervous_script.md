# Mandy Nervous Script - VAT Calculations

Hey! I know VAT can seem tricky, especially when numbers get confusing and we have to remember formulas. But don't stress! You actually deal with VAT every single time you buy a cool drink or a packet of chips.

The biggest secret here is just remembering that the price on the shelf is *already* 115%. It's the whole price plus the extra tax. So if you ever need to find the price *before* tax, you can't just minus 15%, because you're starting from 115, not 100!

Just remember Big John's trick: to go backwards to the original price, divide by 1,15. Take a deep breath, grab your calculator, and let's try one together. You've totally got this!
