It really just comes down to one big idea: how steep is the hill? That’s all gradient is trying to figure out.

If you get stuck, just write down VI / HE.
VI is just the up-and-down part — subtract the smaller height from the bigger one. Easy, right?
HE is just the flat walking part across the map.

The only sneaky trap they set for you is the units. Just remember: before you divide, make sure they are both in meters. If your HE is in kilometers, just times it by 1,000.
