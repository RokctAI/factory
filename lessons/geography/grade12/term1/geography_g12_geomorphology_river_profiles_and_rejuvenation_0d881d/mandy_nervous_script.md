Hey! It's Mandy. Okay, I know Dr Molefe can sound a bit intense with all the terms like "dynamic equilibrium" and "eustatic change," but let's take a breath. It's actually a lot simpler than it sounds.

Think of a river just trying to get comfortable. A graded river is like a river that's finally made its bed perfectly smooth—it just flows easily down to the sea. But an ungraded river is like a bed with a bunch of lumps in it; the water trips over waterfalls and rapids (which Dr Molefe calls knickpoints) because it's still trying to smooth things out.
