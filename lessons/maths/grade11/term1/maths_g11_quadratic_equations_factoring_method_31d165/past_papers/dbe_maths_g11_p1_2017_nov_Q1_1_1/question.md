## Question (2 marks)

Solve for x:  (2x - 3)(x + 7) = 0

## Method

zero product property

## Memo working

(2x-3)(x+7)=0 -> 2x-3=0 or x+7=0 -> x=3/2 or x=-7

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x = 3/2 or x = -7
