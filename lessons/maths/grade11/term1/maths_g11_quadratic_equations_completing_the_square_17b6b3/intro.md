Today we master completing the square — the technique that rewrites any quadratic into a perfect square plus a constant, and the method the examiner specifies by name when factorising will not serve. One equation runs through the whole session: x² + 6x + 2 = 0, chosen precisely because it does not factorise over the integers. We set out the method formally — isolate, halve the coefficient, square it, balance both sides — with every step justified in exam-marking terms, because in this question the method marks are earned line by line. By the end you will solve the equation in exact surd form and know exactly when this technique is the one the question demands.
