## Question (3 marks)

Solve for x (correct to TWO decimal places):  5x^2 + 2x - 6 = 0

## Method

quadratic formula

## Memo working

a=5, b=2, c=-6; x = (-2 ± sqrt(2^2 - 4(5)(-6)))/(2·5) = (-2 ± sqrt(124))/10

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x = 0,91 or x = -1,31
