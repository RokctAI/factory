## Question (3 marks)

Solve for x (leave your answer correct to TWO decimal places):  7x^2 + 3x - 2 = 0

## Method

quadratic formula

## Memo working

a=7, b=3, c=-2; x = (-3 ± sqrt(3^2 - 4(7)(-2)))/(2·7) = (-3 ± sqrt(65))/14

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

x = -0,79 or x = 0,36
