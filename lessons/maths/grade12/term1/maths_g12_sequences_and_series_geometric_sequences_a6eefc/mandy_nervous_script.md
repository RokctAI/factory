The most important thing he said was about that exponent. When you see $a \cdot r^{n-1}$, think of it like this: the $r$ has a little hat on it, the $n-1$. The $a$ is standing next to it, but it doesn't get to wear the hat.

If you try to multiply $a$ and $r$ together *before* doing the exponent, it's like putting the hat on both of them. And that's going to give you the wrong answer.

So, when you type it into your calculator, always do the exponent part first, or just use brackets exactly the way he showed. You've got this! Just take it one step at a time.
