Today we establish the arithmetic sequence — the foundation of the entire Sequences and Series section, worth guaranteed marks in Paper 1 for the candidate whose notation is precise. One problem runs through the whole session: find the 20th term of the sequence 3; 7; 11; and so on. We proceed formally — define the first term, the common difference and the general term, state the formula, then apply it to the example step by step so that every line earns its mark. By the end you will compute any term of any arithmetic sequence, and, at distinction level, solve for whichever of the four quantities the examiner hides, given the other three.
