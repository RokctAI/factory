Today we sharpen the factoring method on quadratic equations — the fastest route to the roots when it applies, and in Grade 12 the technique that must be automatic before calculus and nature-of-roots questions build on it. One equation runs through the whole session: 2x² + 5x + 3 = 0, a leading coefficient greater than one, which is exactly where marks are dropped. We work formally — standard form first, target product and sum, split the middle term, factorise by grouping, then the zero-product property — with every line justified in exam-marking terms. By the end you will factorise it cleanly, state both roots, and know precisely when factoring is the method the examiner expects.
