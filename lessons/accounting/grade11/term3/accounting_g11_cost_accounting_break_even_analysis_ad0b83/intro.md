Today we are working out the one number every business owner watches: the break-even point — the number of units you must sell before the shop stops eating money and starts making it. One case study runs through the whole session: a manufacturer paying R60 000 in fixed costs every month, selling each unit for R50 while each unit costs R30 to make. We start at the spaza till, watch each sale contribute its little piece toward the rent, and only then write the formal formula the exam wants. By the end you will calculate the break-even point in units, show it in the marked layout, and explain what it means for the business.
