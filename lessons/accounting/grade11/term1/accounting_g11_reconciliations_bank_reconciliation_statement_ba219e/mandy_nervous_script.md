Hey! Don't stress if the whole "debit/credit inversion" thing feels completely backward right now. When I first did this, I kept putting the favourable bank balance on the debit side because, well, an asset is a debit, right? But Prof Mokoena's trick is the lifesaver: you have to look at it from the *bank's* perspective. If you have money in the bank, the bank *owes* you that money. So to them, you are a liability—a credit! Once you lock that inversion into your head, the rest of the statement just falls into place. Just trust the format and post those numbers exactly where Prof shows you. You've got this!
