# Mandy Nervous Script: VAT Calculations

Mandy: Hey, if you're feeling stuck on the 115% part, you're not alone! It used to confuse me so much.

I always wanted to just hit "times 15 percent" on my calculator. But think of it this way: the price tag on the shelf is like a big pie that’s already been baked with extra ingredients. The original pie was 100%, and SARS added an extra 15% on top of that. So the whole pie you are looking at is 115% of the original size!

If you just do times 15%, you are taking 15% of the *already big pie*, which gives you the wrong number. You have to divide by 115 to find out what 1% looks like, and *then* times by 15 to find the tax slice.

Just remember Aunty Grace's formula: what you *want* over what you *have*. You *want* the 15% VAT, and you *have* the 115% total. 15 over 115. You've got this!
