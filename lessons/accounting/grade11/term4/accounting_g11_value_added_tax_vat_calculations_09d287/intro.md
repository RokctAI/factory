Today we take apart a price tag. Almost every price you see already has VAT baked inside it, and the skill this session builds is pulling that tax back out — the 115% rule. Our one worked case for the whole lesson: an item marked R2 875 including VAT at 15%. We start at the till, where the marked price is really two amounts travelling together — the shop's money and SARS's money — and once that picture is solid we write the formal fraction the exam wants. By the end you will split any VAT-inclusive price into the VAT amount and the price excluding VAT, and show the calculation the way the marker expects it.
