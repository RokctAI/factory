# Mandy Nervous Script

Hey! It's Mandy here. I saw you hesitating a bit on the last question. Take a deep breath. Conservation of Energy can look really scary because of all the letters and subscripts—$E_k$, $E_p$, $i$, $f$. It looks like alphabet soup!

But here is the secret: it's just a before-and-after picture. That big long equation is just saying, "The energy you start with equals the energy you end with."

When Ms Dlamini says "explicit substitution", she just wants you to plug the numbers into the spots without doing the math in your head first. Think of it like filling out a form. Put the mass where the 'm' is, put the velocity where the 'v' is, and don't worry about solving it until the very next step.

You don't have to be a math genius to do this. You just have to be neat and follow the recipe. You've got this! Try the next one step-by-step with me.
