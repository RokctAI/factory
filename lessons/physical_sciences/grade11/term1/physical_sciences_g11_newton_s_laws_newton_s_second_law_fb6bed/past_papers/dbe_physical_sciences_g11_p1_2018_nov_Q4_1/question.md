## Question (2 marks)

State Newton's Second Law of Motion in words.

## Method

state newtons second law

## Memo working

(see marking guidelines)

## Answer (per marking guidelines)

When a resultant/net force acts on an object, the object will accelerate in the direction of the force. The acceleration is directly proportional to the net force and inversely proportional to the mass of the object.
