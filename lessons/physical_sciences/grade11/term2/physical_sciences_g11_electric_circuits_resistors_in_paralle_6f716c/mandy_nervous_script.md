# Mandy Script: Nervous Student (Resistors in Parallel)

Hey! Mandy here.

Okay, if you're looking at that parallel resistance formula and your brain just went "nope," take a deep breath. You are definitely not the only one. Adding things together to get a *smaller* number feels completely backwards, I know!

Think about it like being at a supermarket. If there's only one checkout open, the line is long—that's high resistance. But if they suddenly open three more checkouts? It's way easier for everyone to get through, right? The "resistance" to the crowd goes down. That's all the parallel circuit is doing. It's opening more paths for the current.

And with that scary-looking formula? Just take it one tiny step at a time. Write the formula. Substitute your numbers. Find a common denominator. And the absolute biggest thing: remember to flip it at the end! 1 over Rp is not your final answer. You have to turn it upside down.
