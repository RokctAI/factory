Today we measure something you already feel at the taxi rank and the spaza: when a price goes up, how hard do buyers pull back? That measurement is price elasticity of demand. One case runs through the whole session: a product whose price rises from R10 to R12 while the quantity demanded falls from 100 units to 80. We start with the story — who keeps buying, who walks away — and only then turn it into the percentage-change calculation and the classification the memorandum expects. By the end you will calculate the elasticity coefficient for this case, classify the demand, and explain in plain words what that answer tells the seller.
