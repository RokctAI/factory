I know looking at all those intersecting lines and labels like D1 and S1 can feel a little overwhelming at first. Take a deep breath.

Remember, these graphs are just a way of drawing a picture of how real people behave. Think about when you buy airtime or a snack at the shop. If you suddenly got a bigger allowance or a new job—meaning more income—you might buy more of the things you like, right? That’s all that the shift from D1 to D2 is showing. It’s just showing that people have more money, so they are willing to buy more beef at any price.

And because everyone suddenly wants more beef, the price naturally goes up a bit at the butchery, and the butchery orders more to keep up. That’s your new equilibrium point!
