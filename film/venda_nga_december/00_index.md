- [ ] Confirm Mizo Phyll — agreement, fee, release form

- [ ] Confirm Tman Gavin — agreement, fee, identity preference
- [ ] Confirm Nicodemic — agreement, fee, identity preference
- [ ] Confirm DJ Davic — agreement, fee (relationship basis)
- [ ] Identify faith witness — through Mizo or community networks
- [ ] **Ricky Rick archive search** — all public social media
  for any mention of Mizo Phyll or Venda nga December
- [ ] **Community archive collection** — Facebook, Twitter, Instagram,
  YouTube comments — minimum 20 items for closing sequence
- [ ] Dark room studio — identify, rent, dress
- [ ] Multi-camera setup — lock cameras, lighting design confirmed
- [ ] Music licensing conversation with Mizo Phyll —
  title track and EP licensing as part of participant agreement
- [ ] All release forms prepared and ready to sign

### Early Production
- [ ] Populate cut floor bubbles in character files after each shoot day
- [ ] Curate community archive down to 12–15 final items
- [ ] Confirm Ricky Rick archive findings — bubble or silence decision

## The Three Things This Film Is

1. A celebration of Thohoyandou and VenRap
   — the hidden gem eTV is looking for

2. An honest portrait of what it costs
   to carry your identity through an industry
   that wants to negotiate it

3. A document of permanence
   — what stays when everything else is negotiable

---

## The One Thing This Film Is Not

A verdict.

The film does not judge the dreads decision.
The film does not judge the industry.
The film does not judge the taxi drivers.
The film does not judge anyone.

It simply holds the evidence
and lets the tattoo speak for itself.

**VENDA NGA DECEMBER.**
*It was always going to say what it says.*
