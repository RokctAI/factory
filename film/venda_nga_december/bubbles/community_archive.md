# Community Archive — Research Tasks & Collected Material

## Status
TO BUILD — Pre-production research task

## Purpose
Source public social media material for Type 3 Community Bubbles
used during the closing performance sequence.

Facebook doesn't forget. Neither does Twitter/X, Instagram, or YouTube comments.

---

## Research Sources

### Primary Sources
- Mizo Phyll official Facebook page — post comments and reactions
- Venda Nga December platform Facebook page — community posts
- Mizo Phyll Instagram — comment sections on milestone posts
- Twitter/X — mentions and quote tweets
- YouTube — comment sections on Venda Anthem and other videos
- Tshivenda Music Awards social media — audience reactions

### Secondary Sources
- Fan pages and community groups (Venda music, Limpopo music, VenRap)
- Local news articles with reader comments
- Tagged posts from fans at live events

---

## Collection Rules

1. Public posts only — no private messages, no DMs
2. Collect exact text — do not paraphrase fan words
3. Note platform and approximate date for each
4. Attribute with first name or handle only in the film
   Full names require separate consent form
5. No negative comments — this is community celebration
6. Priority: comments that mention Thohoyandou, Venda,
   pride, home, recognition — the themes of the film
7. Ricky Rick's public statements about Mizo Phyll
   are collected here and flagged for Type 2 (Cut Floor)
   use — not Type 3 (Community)

## Ricky Rick Archive — Priority Research

Search all public Ricky Rick social media for mentions of:
- Mizo Phyll by name
- Venda nga December (track or platform)
- VenRap or Venda music
- Any post referencing their collaboration

These become **Type 2 Cut Floor Bubbles** — his own words,
from the archive, surfacing at the right moment in the film.

*He cannot speak for himself anymore.
But he already spoke. We find it.*

Platforms to search:
- Ricky Rick Twitter/X archive
- Ricky Rick Instagram
- Any recorded interviews where Mizo Phyll is mentioned
- Collaborative project posts and credits

Minimum 20 community bubbles collected for the closing sequence.

The closing sequence runs approximately 90 seconds.
One bubble every 6–8 seconds = 12–15 bubbles maximum.
