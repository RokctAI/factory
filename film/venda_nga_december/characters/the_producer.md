# The Producer
## DJ Davic

## Status
TO CONFIRM — Supporting Witness — The Origin Story

## Role
He is the person who believed first.

Before Mizo Phyll had a tattoo that meant anything to anyone.
Before Tman Gavin had any reason to doubt or believe.
Before the record company, before Ricky Rick, before the national stages.

DJ Davic sat across from a young man from Maniini Block J
with dreads and a sound nobody had a name for yet
and said: *yes.*

That decision changed everything.
He is the only person in this film who can speak
to what Mizo was before Mizo knew what he was.

He is also the director's friend.
That relationship is this film's most significant access point.
He will speak honestly because he trusts the room.

## Why His Testimony Is Irreplaceable

Everyone else in this film knew Mizo Phyll
after the world had already started paying attention.

DJ Davic knew him before.

He can answer the question nobody else can:
*What did you see in him that made you say yes?*

That answer — whatever it is — is the film's deepest
piece of evidence that the tattoo was always going to be true.

He believed it before Mizo tattooed it.
The tattoo was confirmation, not declaration.
DJ Davic is the proof.

## Interview Questions

1. Take me back to the first time you heard Mizo.
   What did you hear?

2. What made you say yes to him when most people
   hadn't said anything yet?

3. What was it like producing that early music —
   what was the sound trying to become?

4. You also worked with Tman Gavin.
   Same sessions, same house, same time.
   How do you explain what happened differently
   for each of them?

5. When the record company came for Mizo —
   when things started moving nationally —
   what did you feel watching that happen
   to someone you'd believed in from the beginning?

6. What do you think the tattoo means?
   You knew him before it existed and after.
   What does VENDA NGA DECEMBER mean to you?

7. What do you want people to understand about
   where this music came from —
   about what Thohoyandou gave him
   that nobody else could have given him?
