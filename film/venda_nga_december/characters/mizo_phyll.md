## The Tattoo

This is not a detail. This is the film.

He got it before the world agreed.
Before the record deal. Before the national stages.
Before Ricky Rick. Before the dreads came off.
He tattooed three words on his chest
when the only evidence they were true was that he believed them.

The tattoo is the film's first image.
The tattoo is the film's last image.
The tattoo appears at every ad break.
The tattoo is the film's only narrator.

## The Faith

Mizo Phyll was a committed member of the Rastafari faith.
His role within his faith community to be confirmed
in the shoot day interview — he will name it himself.
We do not label it for him.

The dreads were not fashion. They were covenant.
They meant something specific within the faith.
They had rules. They had weight.

Then the record company came.
The dreads came off.

What that cost — spiritually, relationally, personally —
is the emotional centre of Act Two.

## Interview Questions — Shoot Day 1

These questions walk the same path the film walks.
Ask in order. Do not rush.
The answers to questions 1–5 become Act One.
Questions 6–9 become Act Two.
Questions 10–12 become Act Three and Four.
Question 13 becomes the closing of the interview chair.

1. Take me back to Maniini Block J.
   What did Thohoyandou sound like when you were growing up?

2. When did you first know music was not just something you did —
   it was something you were?

3. Tell me about the faith. What did the dreads mean to you —
   not to anyone else. To you.

4. Who believed in you first? Before anyone else?
   Before any stage, any recording, any platform?

5. Walk me through how the Venda nga December tattoo happened.
   Not the story — the moment. What were you thinking?

6. When the record company came — what did they ask you to change?
   What did you say yes to? What did you say no to?

7. The dreads. Tell me how that decision happened.
   Who was in the room? What was said?

8. After they came off — what did you feel?
   Not what you told people you felt. What you actually felt.

9. What did your faith community say?
   What did the people who knew what those dreads meant — say?

10. Tell me about Ricky Rick. Not the collaboration —
    the person. What do you carry from that?

11. The tattoo never moved. Everything else negotiated.
    The tattoo didn't. Why?

12. What does Thohoyandou mean to you now —
    standing here, with everything you've built?
    Does it still fit on your chest?

13. What would you say to the young man in Maniini Block J
    who hadn't tattooed anything yet?
    Who was still deciding whether to believe himself?

- **Day 3:** Return session — situational questions + closing performance
