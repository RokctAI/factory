## Role
Same streets. Same start. Driving a taxi.

He is the second witness to the same truth Tman Gavin carries.

the same production house, the same early scene —
are both now driving taxis while Mizo Phyll is the King of VenRap —
is the film's clearest measurement of distance.

## The Distinction Between Tman and Nicodemic

These are two different witnesses, not the same witness twice.

**Tman Gavin** was directly in the same production house.
Same producer, same recordings, credited on the Venda Anthem.
His testimony is about the *professional* proximity.

**Nicodemic** brings a different angle —
the street-level, community-level view.
What the scene felt like from slightly further out.

In the edit they complement each other, not duplicate.
If they say the same thing, only the stronger version survives.
If they say different things, both stay.
