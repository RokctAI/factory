# Ricky Rick
## Ricardo Moloi (1987–2023)

## Status
ABSENT — The Permanent Presence

## Role
He is not a character who sits in the dark room.
He is a chapter. A presence. A loss.

He does not get a seat in the interview chair
because that chair is for people who can still speak.
He already spoke. We find it.

His role in this film is handled with complete respect
and complete restraint.
He gets one section. One photograph.
His own words — sourced from the public archive.
Then silence. Then the film moves forward.

The way he had to.

---

## Why He Is In This Film

Mizo Phyll worked with Ricky Rick.
That collaboration was not a footnote.
It was evidence that a man from Thohoyandou
had traveled far enough for the national stage
to reach back and find him.

Ricky Rick was a nationally recognised artist.
His name in this film is not name-dropping.
It is geography.
It tells the audience how far Mizo went.

And the fact that Ricky Rick is gone
adds a weight to that chapter of the story
that no living collaborator could carry.

---

## What The Film Does NOT Do

- Does not dwell on his death
- Does not use his death for emotional manipulation
- Does not speculate about his life or choices
- Does not place words in his mouth
- Does not make him a supporting character in someone else's trauma narrative

He gets one mention. One photograph. His own words if found.
Handled with the restraint it deserves.

---

## Archive Bubbles

No archive bubble appears during the Ricky Rick section.
This space is sacred.
Silence and one photograph only.

Archive bubbles related to Ricky Rick may appear
at other moments in the film — during Act Three —
when Mizo mentions the national stage broadly:

> **Collaboration with the late Ricky Rick**
> *Thohoyandou to the national stage*

This bubble appears briefly and without elaboration.
It is a fact, not a tribute. The tribute is the silence.

## Pre-Production Task — The Archive Search

Search all public Ricky Rick social media and interview
archives for any mention of:

- Mizo Phyll by name
- Venda nga December (track, EP, or platform)
- VenRap or Venda music broadly
- Any caption, post, or interview statement about
  their collaboration or about Mizo's work

**Platforms:**
- Ricky Rick Twitter/X (archived)
- Ricky Rick Instagram
- YouTube interviews featuring both artists
- Music publication features mentioning the collaboration

**If found:**
His own words become a Type 2 Cut Floor Bubble.
They surface during the Ricky Rick section of the film.
The audience reads what he said.
The film holds silence.

That is more powerful than any narration.

**If not found:**
The section runs without a bubble.
Mizo speaks. The photograph appears behind him.
Silence holds.
That is also enough.

---

## The Photograph

One photograph. Selected during pre-production.
The criteria:

- Both of them together if possible
- If not, a photograph of Ricky Rick alone
  that he would have chosen — not a grief photo,
  a life photo
- Appears behind Mizo in the dark room
  as he speaks — not cutting away,
  floating in the background the way memory surfaces
- Holds for the duration of that section
- Fades with the section

No caption on the photograph.
No lower third naming him.
The audience knows who he is.
If they don't — they will feel why he matters
from the way Mizo speaks about him.

---

## The Section in the Film

**Duration:** 2 minutes maximum. Possibly less.

Mizo speaks about him from the dark room.
We hear what their relationship was —
not the collaboration, the person.
What he carried from knowing him.
What is different now that he is gone.

Then:
One photograph behind him.
His own words if found — surfacing as a bubble.
Mizo finishes speaking.
Silence.
The photograph fades.
The film continues.
