1. **Core Relational Circle**: Primary family, close confidants, and **Close family members, key professional mentors, and core co-builders in RokctAI.**.
2. **Mentors and Coaches**: Professional advisors that support strategic career and personal shifts.
3. **Health Partners**: Medical professionals, training partners, and nutritionists that guard physical capital.

1. **Deep Work**: Blocks of focused cognitive efforts to build enduring IP, code, and systems.
2. **Wellness Rituals**: Dynamic exercise, deep recovery, and nutrition protocols that sustain life force.
