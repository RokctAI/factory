# ROKCT (Pty) Ltd — Business Plan on a Page

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Mission
ROKCT (Pty) Ltd is a high-growth business operating under the primary classification of **Spazafy POS (offline-robust POS), Juvo Logistics (gig-fleet platform), ROKCT AI Core (predictive engine)**. Our strategic vision is to bridge informal supply chains and digitize transaction flows through automated, high-fidelity operations. Guided by **Rendani Sinyage (Director/CTO, 2 years tenure)**, we are deploying a robust multi-tenant model that delivers modern technology to underserved markets.
