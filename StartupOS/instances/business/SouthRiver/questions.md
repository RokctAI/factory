## 2. Operational & Machinery Specifics
*   **Sachet Machine Capabilities**: What packaging specs does the sachet machine support?
    *   **Answer**: Seals 4-side packs, maximum roll width is 320mm, high-speed automated output.
*   **Water Sourcing**: What is the capacity of the current water sourcing setup?
    *   **Answer**: 3,500 liters per hour borehole pump, connected to three 5,000L JoJo storage tanks.
*   **Power Continuity**: How does the business handle loadshedding?
    *   **Answer**: Generac 20kVA diesel generator backup plus direct solar panels on franchise depots.
*   **Purification Capacity**: What is the daily rated purification capacity?
    *   **Answer**: Reverse Osmosis (RO) membranes rating 10,000 liters per day under SANS 241 standards.
