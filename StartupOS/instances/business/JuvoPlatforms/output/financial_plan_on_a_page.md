# Juvo Platforms (Pty) Ltd — Financial Plan on a Page

## 1. Corporate Financial Assumptions
Our financial strategy is built on a high-velocity, low-overhead operational model. By decoupling server infrastructure and leveraging dynamic local gig-fleets, we avoid heavy fixed CAPEX. 

*   **Gross Margin Target**: > 40% across physical products; > 80% on digital services.
*   **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: Minimized via strategic referral loops.
*   **B-BBEE Alignment**: High-scoring supplier status enables direct eligibility for Enterprise and Supplier Development (ESD) funding pools.

## 3. Cost Control & Mitigation Plan
1.  **Infrastructure Elasticity**: Docker-based hosting scales dynamically, ensuring that server costs mirror actual user transaction volume.
2.  **Gig-Fleet Flexing**: Delivery commissions are paid purely per successful delivery, turning a historically massive fixed delivery cost into a strictly variable cost.
3.  **Local Supply Sourcing**: Sourcing raw materials or technology from certified local vendors reduces currency and cross-border shipping risks.
