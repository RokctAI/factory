## 2. Fleet & Operations Model
*   **Fleet Model**: What is the vehicle sourcing and ownership model for the fleet?
    *   **Answer**: Independent local youth bakkie owners acting as a last-mile gig-fleet.
*   **Routing Algorithm**: How are orders routed and managed?
    *   **Answer**: Dynamic routing and micro-depot allocations powered by the ROKCT AI Engine.
*   **Pilot Region**: What geographic region is targeted for launch?
    *   **Answer**: Vhembe District (Musina, Thohoyandou, Louis Trichardt) in Limpopo.

## 3. Commercial & Monetization Mechanics
