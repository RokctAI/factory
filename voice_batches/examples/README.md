# voice_batches/examples/

Example voice batches. Nothing here runs: the "Voice batch" workflow only
picks up JSONs in `voice_batches/inbox/`. Moving one there (on `main`) is
the owner's call.

- `r3_phonics_probe.json`: 6 Grade R phonics lines rendered from their
  respellings in `voice_batch/r3_respellings.json`, in Voice A, only to hear
  whether the voice says the letter sound (not the letter name).
