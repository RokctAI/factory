## Results

### India's biggest microdrama players (Sensor Tower downloads, Jan to Sep 2026)

1. **Story TV (EloElo Group):** 98.3M downloads; $80M Series C on 4 Nov 2026 (Hornbill Capital, NBCUniversal), about $500M valuation; own AI studio "Dolphin"; partners Balaji Telefilms, Zee, Applause, Round Table Media (AI shows).
2. **QuickTV (ShareChat / Mohalla Tech):** 57.8M downloads; Rs 293 crore revenue FY26; Rs 100 crore AI microdrama budget for 2026.
3. **Kuku TV (Kuku):** 55.1M downloads; $85M raised Oct 2025; IPO filed; AI-generated slate; creator contests (Rs 10 lakh plus production deal).
4. Also FastTV (T-Series + Hungama, $32M), Chai Shots, Stage. Pocket FM shut Pocket TV in mid-2026.

No open call for content was running on 2026-11-20. No large creator collective found.

### How Story TV's engine works

- Data-led slate planning plus a script helper; about 5 shows a day.
- Dolphin AI: chat in, finished episode out. It turns a script into a shot timeline, keeps a character and location library for consistency across the ~8 s clip limit of video models, picks an outside model per step, and stitches clips. No own video model. About 150 to 200 shows made (company claim). Being opened to outside creators; no price published.
- Story TV's own cost per episode and its models are not public.

### Mapping to this factory

- Ready: VibeVoice voices and `radio_ads/` (JSON script in, mixed and mastered audio out, line cache).
- To build: an episode script format, a cast folder (fixed picture per actor), a shot step (LTX-Video drafts on CPU, Seedance 2.0 finals via fal.ai), and a video assembler (9:16, subtitles, cliffhanger end card).
- Suggested pilot: 5 episodes of about 1.5 minutes; estimated $300 to 450 in Seedance fees with retakes.
- Blocker: fal.ai account and key for Seedance.
