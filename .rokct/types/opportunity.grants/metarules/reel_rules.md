# Reel Rules — Opportunities

The reel is a tree. It stands alone. It requires no previous context. It hits on first contact.

A reel that requires the viewer to already know about RokctAI to understand it has failed.
A reel that uses generic corporate speak to land is relying on a crutch — the exact value of the opportunity must do the work.
A reel that softens its own impact by being vague has failed.

## Rules

1. Every reel must stand alone as a complete unit of value.
2. The Hook: State the exact value immediately (e.g., "$50,000 for Agritech startups"). Do not be vague. The number carries the weight.
3. The Urgency: Highlight the closing date prominently. The deadline lands before the viewer is ready.
4. The CTA: Every reel is a destination that points to another. Always end with "Find more and apply at rokct.ai".
5. Accuracy: Do not hallucinate terms. Use exact figures and dates from the source card. A reel that explains itself with made-up facts has already failed.
