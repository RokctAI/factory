# Impact Metrics — Forbidden Questions

An episode scores on three axes:

Axis 1: Textual Integrity (0-10)
Is every claim grounded in the text? Are Hebrew/Greek terms verified? Does the argument hold without importing meaning the text does not contain?

Axis 2: The Punch (0-10)
Does the Detail Most People Miss actually reframe the standard reading? Does it hit before the reader is ready? Would someone who thought they knew this text stop and reconsider?

Axis 3: Thread Alignment (0-10)
Can this episode be located on the master thread? Does it strengthen the overall narrative? Does it leave the reader wanting to read the next episode?

Scoring Rules:
1. A score below 7 on any axis triggers a suggested revision.
2. Axis 1 failures are non-negotiable — a theologically inaccurate episode is never published regardless of impact.
3. The evaluator never wrote the episode it evaluates.
4. The existing 15 episodes are the quality benchmark. New content must match or exceed them.
