# Episode Rules — Forbidden Questions

Every episode in this series follows this exact structure:

1. Core Argument — the thesis in plain language. One paragraph maximum. No hedge language.
2. Key Verses — NASB translation with Hebrew/Greek terms where critical to the argument.
3. The Detail Most People Miss — the single observation that reframes the standard reading. This is the punch.
4. Cross-references — links to related files in the series.
5. Translation Notes — flagged terms that lose meaning in English. Always verify in Hebrew/Greek.
6. Episode/Visual Angle — production notes for scripting and AI visual generation.

## Rules

1. The Core Argument must be arguable from the text alone. No tradition substitutes for the verse.
2. The Detail Most People Miss is always the impact point. It must hit before the reader is ready.
3. Every episode adds to the master thread. It does not contradict it.
4. New sections may be added. Existing sections are never replaced — reasoning history is preserved.
5. Every key term that matters to the argument must have its Hebrew or Greek original verified.
6. The Visual Angle must give enough direction for an AI image generation system to produce the scene.
7. An episode that could exist in a different series has failed to belong to this one.
